Tovorafenib (DAY101)

19.4.2.2. Exposure-Response (efficacy) Assessment Summary

Applicant’s Position

Exposure-efficacy analysis using data from Arm 1 of FIREFLY-1 study was performed to evaluate
the relationship between tovorafenib exposure and efficacy endpoints (ie, tumor response and
tumor size). Tumor response was assessed by an IRC based on the RANO-HGG criteria and
tumor size was assessed by the SPPD at each visit. The key findings of these analyses include:
