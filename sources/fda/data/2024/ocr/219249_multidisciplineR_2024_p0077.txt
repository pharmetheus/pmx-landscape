NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

Per the population PK (popPK) analysis, moderate renal impairment (creatinine clearance [CrCL]
30 to <60 mL/min) increased inavolisib exposure (AUCo~;; by 42%). During review of the NDA,
on August 7, 2024, the Applicant submitted the interim CSR for the ongoing clinical renal
impairment study. Participants with moderate renal impairment (eGFR 30 to <60 mL/min; n=7)
or normal renal function (eGFR >90 mL/min; n=7) received a single dose of inavolisib 6 mg.
Inavolisib AUCo. was 60% higher in participants with moderate renal impairment than
participants with normal renal function (Table 11). The predicted median steady state AUCox
for patients with moderate renal impairment at 6 mg PO QD dosage was similar to that for
patients with normal renal function at 9 mg PO QD dosage (Table 12). Based on these results,
the Applicant submitted revised labeling proposing to reduce the proposed starting dosage of
inavolisib to 6 mg PO QD in patients with moderate renal impairment. FDA agrees with this
recommendation.
