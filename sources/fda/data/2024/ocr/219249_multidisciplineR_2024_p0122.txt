NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

The investigator-assessed PFS event-free rate at 6 months, 12 months, and 18 months
demonstrated early and durable benefit as shown by consistently and substantially higher PFS
rates in the Inavot+Palbo+Fulv arm compared with the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm starting from the
first tumor assessment and throughout the follow-up. Further evidence of consistent efficacy is
supported by the interim OS analysis, which although not statistically significant, is highly
supportive of the primary endpoint. In addition, the confirmed objective responses were also
durable. Of the patients who had a confirmed objective response, the median duration of
response was approximately 8.8 months longer in the Inavo+Palbo+Fulv (18.4 months) arm
than in the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm (9.6 months).

Patient-reported outcomes showed that adding inavolisib to palbociclib and fulvestrant extends
time without clinically meaningful worsening of pain severity without contributing additional
burden to patients’ day-to-day function and overall quality of life.

Supportive Study GO39374: In Study GO39374, the triplet combination of inavolisib plus
palbociclib and fulvestrant or inavolisib plus palbociclib and letrozole resulted in consistent and
promising clinical activity in terms of investigator-assessed endpoints of ORR, DOR, CBR, and
PFS, regardless of endocrine backbone (letrozole or fulvestrant) and across multiple treatment
lines in patients with P/K3CA-mutated HR+, HER2— LA/mBC.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that INAVO120 (Study WO41554) met its primary endpoint of PFS by investigator.
Furthermore, the sensitivity analysis for PFS by BICR was also supportive of the primary
endpoint. The interim analysis of OS at the time of PFS analysis was not statistically significant
but does appear to be supportive of the primary endpoint results. Results for the additional
secondary endpoints of ORR and DOR also support the primary endpoint results; however,
since the interim analysis of OS at the time of PFS analysis was not statistically significant, none
of the remaining secondary endpoints in the pre-specified testing hierarchy were formally
tested.

Data from a phase 1 trial, GO39374, was submitted by the Applicant |

GO39374 is an ongoing, open-label,
multicenter, Phase | study designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and PK of inavolisib
administered orally as a single agent in patients with locally advanced or metastatic PIK3CA-
mutated solid tumors, including breast cancer, and in combination with standard-of-care
endocrine and/or targeted therapies in patients with PIK3CA-mutated locally advanced and
metastatic breast cancer. Not all patients enrolled on GO39374 had endocrine resistant breast
cancer. Arms B (inavolisib in combination with palbociclib and letrozole), E (inavolisib in
combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant), and F (inavolisib in combination with palbociclib,
fulvestrant, and metformin) were identified by the Applicant to be the most pertinent to the
current NDA submission.
