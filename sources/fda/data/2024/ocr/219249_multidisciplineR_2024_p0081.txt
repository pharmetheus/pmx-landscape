{Inavolisib}

5) DDI-Inavolisib as a Precipitant

Per in vitro Study 16-0806, inavolisib was not a direct inhibitor of CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C8,
CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, and CYP3A, and was not a time-dependent inhibitor of CYP1A2,
CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, or CYP2D6; however, inavolisib showed potential for time-
dependent inhibition of CYP3A. Per in vitro Study 16-0131, inavolisib increased CYP3A4 mRNA
up to 40-fold, CYP2B6 mRNA up to 4-fold, and CYP1A2 mRNA up to 2.5-fold at 30 uM inavolisib
in each case.

The Applicant provided the following
(b) (4)

Based on FDA assessment, available data cannot rule out potential induction effect of inavolisib
on sensitive CYP3A substrates. See Section 19.4.4 for details. PMCs will be issued to conduct a
clinical study or studies to assess inavolisib induction potential on substates of CYP3A and
CYP2B6. If the clinical study with a CYP3A substrate identifies inavolisib as at least a weak
inducer of CYP3A in vivo, additional studies may be warranted to evaluate inavolisib induction
potential on CYP2C enzymes and the potential for inavolisib to interact with COCs.

6.2.2.3. Outstanding Issues

The Applicant's Position: Not applicable.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The following clinical pharmacology PMRs and PMCs will be issued.

1) Dosage Optimization PMR: Inadequate justification and supportive data were provided
to show that inavolisib 9 mg PO QD is the optimized dosage for the target patient
population in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant due to: 1) high rates of
adverse events (including severe cases of hyperglycemia, stomatitis and diarrhea) and
dosage modifications in patients receiving inavolisib 9 mg PO QD + palbociclib +
fulvestrant, and 2) multiple positive E-R relationships for safety endpoints. In addition,
there are limited clinical data at lower inavolisib starting dosages (i.e., <9 mg PO QD)
and a lack of E-R relationships for efficacy using clinical data from one dose level.
Additional dosage optimization is needed to identify whether an alternative inavolisib
dosage would provide an improved benefit-risk profile.
