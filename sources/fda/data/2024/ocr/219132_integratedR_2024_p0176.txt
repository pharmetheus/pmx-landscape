The fetal visceral developmental variation of absent accessory lung lobe was observed in four
of six litters (67%) at the 2500 mg/kg/day dose level (Table 60).

Fetal skeletal malformations observed in 2500 mg/kg/day included misshapen maxilla (two
of six litters), misshapen premaxilla (two of six litters, 33%), and small interparietal bone
(one of six litters, 17%). Skeletal developmental variations indicative of delayed ossification
(e.g., incomplete ossification of frontal bone, palatine, and sternebrae) were observed in the
1250 or 2500 mg/kg/day. The above findings in the 2500 mg/kg/day correlated with lower

fetal body weights (Table 60).
