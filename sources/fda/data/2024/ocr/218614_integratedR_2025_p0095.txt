NDA 218614
Tryngolza (olezarsen sodium)

Conclusion

Yes, the olezarsen 80 mg dose demonstrated efficacy in reducing TG levels in subjects with FCS
in Trial CS3.

In the FCS population, who have severely elevated TG (typically >1,000 mg/dL, sometimes
exceeding 2,000-10,000 mg/dL), the goal is to reduce TG levels as much as possible to minimize
the risk of AP. Although there is no universally defined "target" TG level for patients with FCS,
the general goal is to keep TG below 500 mg/dL. However, because FCS patients tend to have
persistently high TG due to genetic factors, achieving levels below 1,000 mg/dL is often
considered a success in clinical practice.

In Trial CS3, the placebo-subtracted mean percent change in TG (primary endpoint) showed a
statistically significant mean TG reduction of -44%. In the olezarsen 80 mg group, the mean TG
at baseline was 2613 mg/dL which decreased at Month 6 to a mean TG of 1732 mg/dL; the
within treatment arm TG reduction with olezarsen 80 mg from baseline to Month 6 was
approximately -33% (95% CI -49.03, -14.94). Therefore, olezarsen 80 mg dose showed both a
statistically significant and clinically meaningful reduction in mean TG levels. Furthermore, this
TG reduction is associated with a nominal reduction in the rate of AP (see Section 6.3.3).
