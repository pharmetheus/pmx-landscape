6.3.2.1 Dosage Recommendations for Restarting Therapy after Dose Delay

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with Applicant’s proposed recommendations for restarting therapy after
dose delay, as outlined in Table 21, based on clinical data and PK simulations. Although
CRS associated with tarlatamab treatment occurred primarily with the first dose, risk of
CRS re-emerged when a patient was off treatment for a prolong period. This is due to
re-sensitization of the immune system and potential rebound of the target cells.
Therefore, repeating step-up dose 1 mg at the proposed cutoffs following dose delays is
needed to mitigate the risk of rebound CRS.
