7.6. Safety Results

7.6.1. Overview of Treatment-Emergent Adverse
Events

In Part 1 of the PRECISION study, TEAEs and serious AEs (SAEs) were reported in a higher
proportion of aprocitentan-treated subjects than placebo-treated (Table 25). AEs leading to
permanent discontinuation were also reported in a slightly numerically greater proportion of
aprocitentan- as compared to placebo-treated subjects. Although AEs leading to dose interruption
were reported in a slightly numerically greater proportion of subjects in the aprocitentan 12.5 mg
dose group as compared to placebo, a similar finding was not seen at the higher dose.
