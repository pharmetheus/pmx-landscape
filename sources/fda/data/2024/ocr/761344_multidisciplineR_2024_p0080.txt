Pivotal and supportive
evidence of effectiveness

The primary evidence of effectiveness comes from Study
20200491 which demonstrated ORR of 40% (95% Cl:
31%, 51%) with the proposed 1/10 mg dosing regimen.

General dosing
instructions

The 1/10 mg is selected with the following rationales:

The step-up dose 1 mg mitigated the risk of cytokine
release syndrome (CRS), especially > Grade 2 CRS for
the subsequent full doses.

CRS grade and incidence for each dose event did not
differ significantly across body weight (BW) quartiles,
ages or DLL-3 cut-points.

The 1/10 mg dosage showed a numerically higher ORR
than 1/100 mg in Study 20200491.

E-R analyses for efficacy with doses up to 100 mg
showed ORR plateaued at the selected 1/10 mg.

E-R analyses for safety did not identify major safety
concerns with 1/10 mg. A positive E-R safety trend was
observed between tarlatamab exposure and any
grade neutropenia, Grade 1 and Grade 2
neurotoxicity.

Dosage
recommendations for
restarting therapy after
dose delay

The proposed dosage recommendation for restarting
therapy after dose delay aims to mitigate CRS risk and is
supported by:

Rebound CRS risk is low and no clear trend for
rebound CRS increase with longer dose delays.
However, data is limited for longer duration of dose
delays.

Based on the totality of data including PK simulation
and clinical data, FDA agrees with Applicant’s
proposed restart therapy recommendation.
