BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with Applicant’s general demographics summary, above. The two arms were
balanced overall with the exception of a higher percentage of patients in the tislelizumab arm
having PD-L1 expression >= 10% (which could favor the tislelizumab arm), as noted above.

The age of patients enrolled in Study 302 was slightly younger than the overall population of
patients with esophageal cancer in the US. The BMI of patients appeared lower than the general
BMI of adults in the US; however, mean/median BMI data for US patients with esophageal
cancer are not available. Of note, although a key secondary endpoint, ascertainment of PD-L1
expression status is missing in 19% of patients. As the trial was enrolled mostly in Asia, race and
ethnicity differ from the US population suffering from ESCC. However (see conclusions on
effectiveness), race and ethnicity do not appear to have an impact on prognostic factors in ESCC.

Other Baseline Characteristics (e.g., disease characteristics, important concomitant drugs)
Data:

Comorbidities at Baseline:

Most patients (468 patients [91.4%]) in the ITT Analysis Set had multiple comorbidities at
baseline. The most frequently comorbidities (by MedDRA System Organ Class (SOC)) were
comparable in the 2 arms and included gastrointestinal disorders (Tislelizumab and ICC: 35.5%
each), vascular disorders (Tislelizumab: 35.2%; ICC: 34.0%), and metabolism and nutrition
disorders (Tislelizumab: 26.6%; ICC: 29.7%).

Disease History and Characteristics:

Overall, disease history of patients was similar between the 2 arms, except for metastatic
diagnosis at study entry (Table 18). Over 95% of patients had metastatic disease at study entry
(Tislelizumab: 98.0%; ICC: 92.2%), the remaining patients (4.9%) had locally advanced disease
at study entry (Tislelizumab: 2.0%; ICC: 7.8%). The median sum of diameters of the target
lesions, as assessed by the investigator, were similar in the 2 arms; 42.0 mm (range: 10.0 to
261.0 mm) for the tislelizumab Arm and 42.00 mm (range: 10.0 to 238.0 mm) for the ICC Arm.
The location of target and nontarget lesions at study entry in the 2 arms was similar, and the most
commonly reported distant metastatic visceral organs were lung (Tislelizumab: 116 [45.3%];
ICC: 119 [46.5%)), liver (Tislelizumab: 76 [29.7%]: ICC: 69 [27.0%]), and

bone (Tislelizumab: 30 [11.7%]; ICC: 32 [12.5%]).
