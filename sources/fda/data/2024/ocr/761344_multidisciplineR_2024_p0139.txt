NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761344}
{Tarlatamab}

The Applicant's Position:

Overall, the subjects with SCLC enrolled in Study 20200491 had baseline characteristics
indicative of subjects with advanced SCLC. Thus, the patient population evaluated in the
efficacy studies is considered representative of the overall population of patients with
advanced SCLC and supportive of the proposed indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s characterization of the baseline characteristics of
patients who were enrolled in Study 20200491. Through information requests, the Applicant
clarified that all patients who received 10 mg tarlatamab from Parts 1 and 2 of Study 20200491
had ES-SCLC, 97% of patients had metastatic disease and 73 patients (74%) had received prior
anti-PD-(L)1 therapy. Of these 73 patients, 58 (59%) received anti-PD-(L)1 therapy in
combination with platinum-based chemotherapy in the first line setting which is representative
of frontline standard of care treatment in the US. In addition, 50 patients (51%) received prior
topoisomerase-l inhibitors including 20% of patients who received prior topotecan. Of the 49
patients who did not receive a topoisomerase | inhibitor, the majority (89%) received
treatments that are considered standard of care options in the US, including platinum-based
chemotherapy rechallenge, lurbinectedin, CAV, temozolomide, paclitaxel. Other treatments
received include amrubicin, PD-(L)1 in combination with experimental agents and gemcitabine
in combination with paclitaxel. A table of all second line therapies received in patients who did
not receive a prior topoisomerase-| inhibitor is summarized below.
