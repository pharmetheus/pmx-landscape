The Applicant's Position:
Data from these studies allow for an informed assessment of the safety profile of

tarlatamab and an evaluation of the overall benefit-risk in adult subjects with advanced
SCLC with disease progression on or after platinum-based chemotherapy. This safety
population is also considered appropriate for the detection and characterization of
common adverse events and to provide guidance on toxicity management.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Safety analyses were conducted on the complete datasets provided by the Applicant for
the pooled population which includes Study 20200491 and Study 20160323 based on an
October 2, 2023 data cut-off. FDA notes that the primary safety population from Study
20200491 included 99 patients in Parts 1 and 2 of protocol which required
hospitalization for 48 hours post tarlatamab infusion for Cycle 1 Day 1 and Cycle 1 Day 8
doses. In Part 3 of the protocol (n=34), patients were hospitalized for 24 hours post
tarlatamab infusion for cycle 1 day 1 and day 8 doses. In supportive Study 20160323
(n=54) patients were hospitalized for 48 hours post tarlatamab infusion on Cycle 1 Day 1
