19.4.4.4, Conclusion

The FDA’s Assessment: PBPK analysis cannot rule out the induction potential of inavolisib on
the sensitive CYP3A substrate midazolam. The magnitude of inavolisib induction on the
exposure of substrates of CYP3A cannot be confidently predicted due to the uncertainties
about the induction parameters and methodology.

19.5. Additional Safety Analyses Conducted by FDA

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA conducted an analysis to review major adverse cardiac events (MACE) in both treatment
arms based on the expanded 5-point MACE criteria (Table 57). The 5-point MACE criteria

includes acute myocardial infarction (AMI), stroke, cardiovascular death, unstable angina and
heart failure.
