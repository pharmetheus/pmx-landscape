Rabbit Embryofetal Development Toxicity Study

A preliminary embryo-fetal toxicity study was conducted in rabbits. Administration of
macitentan (0, 2.5, 5, 12.5 and 25 mg/kg/day) from Day 6 to Day 19 of the gestation period did
not result in maternal toxicity but embryo-fetal toxicity was observed at all the doses tested.
