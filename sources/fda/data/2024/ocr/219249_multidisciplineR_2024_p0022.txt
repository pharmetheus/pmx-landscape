NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
ITOVEBI {inavolisib}

In addition, the Applicant further explored safety and tolerability through assessment of
patient-generated data. A list of expected symptomatic adverse events from the combination of
inavolisib, palbociclib, and fulvestrant (diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, fatigue, mouth sores,
decreased appetite, and rash), was selected from the patient reported outcome (PRO)-CTCAE
item library, along with an additional single item assessing bother due to side-effects of
treatment. These PRO data were assessed appropriately with acceptable data quality. Analysis
of patient-reported symptoms that worsened from baseline were generally observed to be
worse in the inavolisib arm, with the greatest difference observed for diarrhea, mouth sores,
decreased appetite, and rash. The patient-reported data provide additional insight into the
safety and tolerability of the approved regimen and were included in section 6 of the USPI to
further inform patients and providers.

Overall, results from INAVO120 showed a statistically significant and clinically meaningful
improvement for the primary endpoint of PFS when inavolisib was added to palbociclib and
fulvestrant for the population enrolled. Results for secondary endpoints of ORR and OS were
supportive of the primary endpoint. The addition of inavolisib to palbociclib and fulvestrant
resulted in increased toxicity that was appropriately characterized by both clinician- and
patient-reported assessment. Overall, the safety profile was acceptable for a patient population
with a serious and life-threatening condition and is manageable with appropriate monitoring,
dose modifications, and/or supportive care as described in the labeling.

Therefore, the review team recommends granting regular approval to inavolisib + palbociclib +
fulvestrant for endocrine-resistant, PIK3CA-mutated, HR-positive, HER2-negative, locally
advanced or metastatic breast cancer. This represents a new therapeutic option in combination
with palbociclib + fulvestrant for an endocrine resistant population. The indication for approval
Is:

Inavolisib (ITOVEBI), in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant, is indicated for the
treatment of adults with endocrine-resistant, PIK3CA-mutated, hormone receptor (HR)-
positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative, locally advanced or
metastatic breast cancer, as detected by an FDA-approved test, following recurrence on or
after completing adjuvant endocrine therapy.

A supplemental Pre-Market Approval (PMA) Application (sPMA P190032-S023) was submitted

to CDRH for the FoundationOne Liquid CDx (F1LCDx) The sPMA will receive contemporaneous

approval with this NDA as a companion diagnostic device for inavolisib. A PMC has been issued
for tissue testing, as the PMA is planned to be submitted at the end of 2024.
