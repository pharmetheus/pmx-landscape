Overall, the ORR and durable responses observed with tarlatamab in Study 20200491 are consistent with a meaningful advantage over
available therapies for the proposed indication. Tarlatamab has an acceptable safety profile when assessed in the context of this life-
threatening disease and current available therapy. Based on these results, tarlatamab has a favorable risk-benefit profile in the context of a
life-threatening disease with an unmet medical need, and the submitted evidence meets the statutory evidentiary standard for accelerated
approval. The review team’s regulatory recommendation is to grant tarlatamab accelerated approval for the following indication: Treatment of
adult patients with extensive-stage small cell lung cancer (SCLC) with disease progression on or after platinum-based chemotherapy.

Continued approval for this indication may be contingent upon verification and description of clinical benefit in a confirmatory trial, conducted
as a PMR. The Applicant proposes to verify the clinical benefit of tarlatamab in the treatment of patients with SCLC who have disease
progression on or after prior platinum-containing therapy, based on the results of an ongoing randomized, controlled trial, DeLLphi-304 (Study
20210004), comparing tarlatamab to investigator’s choice standard of care therapy with a primary endpoint of overall survival (OS). The status,
including current enrollment and appropriateness of the proposed completion date, of the post-marketing trial, Dellphi-304, was assessed
during the review of this application and the trial was considered to be well underway.
