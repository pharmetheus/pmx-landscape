Comment on study validity

The criteria for study validity were met. The
negative and positive control values were within the
facility's range of historical control values.

Dose analysis indicated slightly higher
concentration than target 25 mg/ml (actual 28.25 &
28.24 mg/mL), 80 mg/mL (actual 87.66 &

85.73 mg/mL) 250 mg/mL (actual 265.59 and

Results

Levacetylleucine was not clastogenic in vivo in rat bone marrow micronucleus assay tested at
0, 250, 800 and 2500 mg/kg/day (Table 55). The positive control produced significant
cytogenetic damage compared to vehicle control.
