The FDA's Assessment:

In FDA’s analysis of the pooled safety population, adverse events leading to dose interruptions
occurred in 55 patients (27%). The most frequently reported adverse events leading to dose
interruptions in >2% of patients included fatigue (3.2%), cytokine release syndrome (2.7%) and
respiratory tract infection (2.1%). All events were coded as dose reduced but based on the
narratives, all were dose interruptions (dose reductions were not permitted).
