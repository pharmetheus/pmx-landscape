BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

There were no clinically meaningful changes from baseline in systolic or diastolic blood
pressure, pulse rate, temperature, or body weight over time in each arm in Study 302. Analyses
of vital signs, physical examination findings, and other observations related to safety were not
integrated for Studies 302, 102, 001, 303, 208, 204, and 203. No additional analyses were
conducted for this assessment. Any clinically relevant changes in vital signs, physical
examination, or other safety-related observations associated with tislelizumab use and those that
led to substantial interventions were reported as adverse events.

The Applicant’s Position:

Tislelizumab did not appear to have clinically meaningful adverse effects on patient vital signs
during any clinical study.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA concurs with the Applicant’s analysis.

Electrocardiograms (ECGs)/ QT

Data:

The ECG values were calculated and summarized by different methods, including corrected QT
interval by Fridericia’s formula (QTcF), corrected QT interval by Bazett’s formula (QTcB), and
the unspecified method. For the patients who had > 1 postbaseline measurement, few patients
had a postbaseline value of > 480 msec in the analysis by QTcF method (1 patient in the
Tislelizumab Arm) and by QTcB method (1 patient in the ICC Arm). In the analysis by the
unspecified method, 3 patients in the Tislelizumab Arm and 3 patients in the ICC Arm had a
postbaseline value of > 480 msec. For the patients who had a baseline QTc measurement and

> 1 postbaseline QTc measurement, few patients had QTc prolongation of > 60 msec in the
analysis by QTcF method (1 patient in the ICC Ann) and by QTcB method (1 patient in the ICC
Arm). In the analysis by the unspecified method, 6 patients in the Tislelizamab Arm and 3
patients in the ICC Arm had QTc prolongation of > 60 msec.

The Applicant’s Position:
The total number of patients in each arm who had a postbaseline value of > 480 msec (4 patients

in the Tislelizumab Arm and 4 patients in the ICC Arm) or QTc prolongation of > 60 msec (6
patients in the Tislelizumab Arm and 5 patients in the ICC Arm) irrespective of QTc
measurement method was small, and the differences between the 2 arms were not considered
clinically meaningful.
