{Inavolisib}

7 Sources of Clinical Data

7.1. Table of Clinical Studies

Data and the Applicant’s Position: All studies pertinent to the evaluation of efficacy and safety
are summarized in Table 18. The evidence in support of the proposed indication is primarily
derived from the pivotal Phase III Study WO41554 (INAVO120) (CCOD: 29 September 2023).
Additional evidence is derived from the Phase | Study GO39374 (CCOD: 27 March 2023).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees that the primary efficacy and safety analyses for this application are based on
results from the INAVO120 (WO41554) trial.

Data from GO39374 was submitted as supportive evidence. GO39374 was a phase 1 study with
7 different treatment arms and no comparator arm. The patient population varied in each of
the 7 arms due to different eligibility criteria and differed from the patient population enrolled
in INAVO120. In addition, the treatment regimen in most of the 7 arms was different than the
triplet regimen from INAVO120. The Applicant submitted data from 3 of the 7 treatment arms

© that included use of any endocrine therapy for the triplet
combination and to allow treatment with the triplet combination in later lines of therapy
(compared to the patient population in INAVO120). The three treatment arms of focus include:
Arm B (inavolisib + palbociclib + letrozole), Arm E (inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant), and
Arm F (inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant + metformin).
