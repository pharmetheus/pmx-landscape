TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

Exploratory Endpoints

¢ Disease control rate (DCR) in both the ITT analysis set and the PD-L1 positive analysis
set - defined as the proportion of patients who have CR, PR and SD assessed by the
investigator per RECIST v1.1

e Pharmacokinetic endpoints: summary of serum concentration of tislelizumab to include
but not limited to Ctrough

e Assessments of immunogenicity of tislelizumab to determine the incidence of ADA

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the description of the study endpoints above and that an improvement in OS
represents a direct measure of clinical benefit.

Statistical Analysis Plan and Amendments

The Applicant’s Description:

Analysis Sets

ITT Analysis Set included all randomized patients and was the primary analysis set for all
efficacy analyses. Patients were analyzed according to their randomized arms. Patients who died
before the randomization but who were inadvertently randomized into the study were excluded
from the ITT Analysis Set.

PD-L1-Positive Analysis Set included all randomized patients whose tumor PD-L1 vCPS was
> 10% as assessed by Ventana PD-L1 (SP263) CDx Assay.

Per-Protocol (PP) Analysis Set included all randomized patients who received > 1 dose of their
assigned study treatment and had no critical protocol deviations that significantly impacted
efficacy or safety evaluation.

Safety Analysis Set included all randomized patients who received > 1 dose of study treatment
and was the primary analysis set for all safety analyses. Patients were analyzed by actual
treatment received.

PK Analysis Set included all patients who received > 1 dose of tislelizumab per the protocol, for
whom any postdose PK data were available.

ADA Analysis Set included all patients who received > 1 dose of tislelizumab, had non-missing
baseline ADA result, and > 1 postbaseline ADA result.
