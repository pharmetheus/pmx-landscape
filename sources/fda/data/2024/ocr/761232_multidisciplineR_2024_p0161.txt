TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA’s analysis of laboratory findings is summarized in Table 40, below. Patients treated with
tislelizumab had a greater incidence of increased AST, ALT, alkaline phosphatase, and creatine
kinase levels compared to patients in the ICC while patients receiving chemotherapy had a
greater incidence of myelotoxicity. These differences are consistent with mechanism of action of
each drug class.
