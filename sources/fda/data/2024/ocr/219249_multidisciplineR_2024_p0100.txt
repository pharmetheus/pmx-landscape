The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s description of the design and protocol aspects of the INAVO120
trial as stated above.

FDA also notes that the statistical test for PFS was based on 320 patients randomized in a 1:1
ratio, where 194 PFS events would provide a power of approximately 85% to detect a hazard
ratio (HR) of 0.65 with a 2-sided type | error of 5%. One interim analysis for futility was to be
conducted after approximately 75 PFS events (33% of information). The futility boundary was
non-binding. One interim analysis was to be performed for OS at the time of the primary
endpoint analysis, when 105 OS events were expected to have occurred (68% information
fraction).

Protocol Amendments

The Applicant's Description: The first version of the protocol was issued on 12 August 2019 and
the protocol version in effect at the CCOD was Version 8. None of the implemented changes
impacted the integrity of the trial or the interpretation of the results. The key changes relevant
to the evaluation of efficacy and safety are summarized below:

Protocol Version 2 (16 October 2019) included new information about interstitial lung disease
(ILD)/pneumonitis with palbociclib (IBRANCE®) based upon a label update (USPI). The
pneumonitis management guidelines were updated to align with the updated label.

Protocol Version 3 (Voluntary harmonization procedure [VHP]) (4 August 2020) was amended
to address feedback from VHP. Contraception requirements for women and men were
amended in response to fulvestrant label change. Interim safety analysis of the first 25 enrolled
patients was added.

Protocol Version 4 (Global) (3 November 2020) was primarily amended to merge the VHP and
global versions and to add China-specific language. In addition,

than 6 months

enrolled in the study in China were a current resident of mainland China and of Chinese
ancestry. A China-specific pharmacokinetic objective was added

Protocol Version 5 (Canada) (21 May 2021) replaced Version 4 of the protocol only in Canada.
