1 INDICATIONS AND USAGE

Indication statement edited to restrict use of sulopenem based on
feedback obtained from the 9/9/2024 Antimicrobial Drugs
Advisory Committee (AMDAC) panel regarding the most
appropriate use of sulopenem etzadroxil/probenecid:
“ORLYNVAH is indicated for the treatment of uncomplicated
urinary tract infections (uUTI) caused by the designated
microorganisms Escherichia coli, Klebsiella pneumoniae, or
Proteus mirabilis, in adult women who have limited or no
alternative oral antibacterial treatment options.” This revised
indication statement was reviewed with the Office of New Drug
Policy (ONDP) on 10/1/2024. Concurrence was obtained from
ONDP regarding the revised indication statement during a
meeting held on 10/16/2024.

Deleted “Staphylococcus saprophyticus” as an indicated
pathogen due to insufficient microbiological and clinical
information. Refer to Section 19.2 of the IA for additional
information.

Deleted Oy
as this is not relevant to this product labeling.

Added the following Limitations of Use: “ORLYNVAH is not
indicated for the treatment of complicated urinary tract infections
(cUTI) or as step-down treatment after intravenous antibacterial
treatment of cUTI” and “ORLYNVAH is not indicated for the
treatment of complicated intra-abdominal infections (clAl) or as
step-down treatment after intravenous antibacterial treatment of
cIAI.” These were added to mitigate the risk of off-label use as
efficacy of sulopenem etzadroxil/probenecid has not been
established for cUTI or clAI. Refer to Sections 6.2.3, 16.4 and
16.5 of the IA for additional information.

The Usage heading was edited from “Usage” to “Usage to
Reduce Development of Drug-Resistant Bacteria.” In discussion
with ONDP, the Usage heading was edited so as not to imply that
one could use sulopenem etzadroxil/probenecid for another
indication given the strong concerns by AMDAC regarding
antibacterial resistance with off-label use and to inform
healthcare providers about the restricted indication.
