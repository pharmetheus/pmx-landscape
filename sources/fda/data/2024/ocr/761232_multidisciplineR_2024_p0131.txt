BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

The Applicant’s Position:
The demographics and baseline characteristics were balanced between arms, except for PD-L1

expression status, where a higher proportion of patients in the Tislelizumab Arm had a PD-L1
expression status of vCPS > 10%. Patients included in the safety datasets are representative of
US patients with advanced or metastatic ESCC following prior systemic therapy

The FDA’s Assessment:

Table 17 in Section 8 described the demographics of the overall population. However, of the
randomized patients, a total of 17 patients (one randomized to receive tislelizumab [0.4%] and 16
[16.3%] randomized to receive ICC) did not receive any study treatment. The most common
primary reason for not receiving study treatment in the ICC Arm was “withdrawal by subject”
(11 patients [4.3%]) which may be related to patients not wanting to receive standard cytotoxic
chemotherapy on this open-label study. The demographics of the safety population were similar
to that of the efficacy population and was generally balanced between the 2 arms as shown in
FDA’s Table 28, below.
