NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

Supportive Study GO39374: Inavolisib as monotherapy and in combination therapies with
palbociclib, letrozole, fulvestrant, and/or metformin was generally well tolerated, toxicities
were manageable, and AEs were in line with the anticipated safety profile of inavolisib, the
known safety profiles of the individual components of combination therapies, and the
underlying disease. At the time of CCOD, there were no new or unexpected safety signals
identified for inavolisib in this study. A summary of key AE-related safety results are presented
in Table 37.

For the purpose of labeling the Applicant is proposing Table 38 for the USPI to reflect the safety
profile of inavolisib.

The FDA’s Assessment:

All grade adverse events occurred in almost all patients in both arms of INAVO120. A higher
proportion of patients experienced SAEs in the inavolisib treatment arm compared to the
placebo arm and more patients in the inavolisib treatment arm had treatment discontinuations
and dose modifications (both interruptions and dose reductions). Grade 5 adverse events, SAEs
and dose modifications are discussed in more detail in the respective sections above.
Modifications have been made to the Applicant Table 38 by FDA for inclusion in the USPI, as
noted in Table 39, as FDA grouped additional PT terms for certain adverse reactions which the
