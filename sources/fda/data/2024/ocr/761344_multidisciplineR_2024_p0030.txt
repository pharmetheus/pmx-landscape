In addition to topotecan and lurbinectedin, other
therapeutic options that are not FDA approved include

There is no therapy with traditional FDA approval for
patients with platinum-resistant SCLC who have no
response to or disease progression after first-line
chemotherapy or for patients with SCLC after
progression on two prior lines of therapy.

The evaluation of efficacy of tarlatamab was based on
results from Study 20200491 (DeLLphi-301), an ongoing,
multicenter, open-label, multi-cohort trial evaluating the
efficacy and safety of tarlatamab as a single agent in adult
patients with relapsed/refractory SCLC with disease
progression after receiving previous treatment with
platinum-based chemotherapy and at least one other line
of prior therapy.

The primary efficacy population consists of 99 patients
enrolled in Parts 1 and 2 of Study 20200491 who received
tarlatamab at the dosage proposed for marketing. Of the
99 patients, 73 patients (74%) had received prior anti-PD-

In Study 20200491, tarlatamab administered to patients
with relapsed/refractory SCLC who progressed on or after
platinum-based chemotherapy and at least one other prior
therapy demonstrated an ORR and DOR that was clinically
meaningful and was also seen across most subgroups of
patients including different DLL3 cutoffs. Review of
subgroup data by prior therapy support the justification
for tarlatamab use in an earlier line (after platinum-based
chemotherapy) than what was studied.

It is not expected that treatment with additional therapies
following platinum-based chemotherapy would increase
the likelihood of response to tarlatamab relative to
treatment with tarlatamab used as second-line therapy
