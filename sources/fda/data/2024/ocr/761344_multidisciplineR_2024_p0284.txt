T-cell redistributions after first dose of tarlatamab infusion were characterized by in study
20160323 and 20200491(Figure 31 and Figure 32). In Study 20160323, following initial infusion,
peripheral CD8+CD69+ T-cell increased transiently 7.4% to 12.2% from baseline within 24 hours
after infusion. Based on Study 20160323’s result, there is no clear dose dependent effect in the
T cell activation.

In study 20200491, both 1/10 and 1/100 mg increased the activation of CD8+CD69+T-cells, with
1/100 mg demonstrating a higher mean increase from baseline (27.8 %), as compared to 1/10
mg (16.3%). However, the increase in T cell activation from 1/10 to 1/100 mg dosages did not
translate to a clinically meaningful increase in response rate and this PD effect is not considered
to be clinically relevant.
