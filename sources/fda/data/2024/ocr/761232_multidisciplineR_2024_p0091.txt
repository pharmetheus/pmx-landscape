The Applicant’s Position:

Only 2.1% of patients enrolled in Study 302 had critical protocol deviations which were balanced
across the arms. Data of patients with critical protocol deviations were excluded from a
sensitivity analysis of OS based on the PP Analysis Set.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the results as presented in Table 15 and Table 16. Irrespective of small
differences between arms, because of the nature of these protocol deviations, they are unlikely to
have had an impact on the observed efficacy outcomes or assessment of the safety of
tislelizumab for the proposed indication.

Table of Demographic Characteristics

Data:

In the ITT Analysis Set, demographics and baseline characteristics were balanced between the

2 arms, except for the PD-L1 expression status. The median age of all randomized patients was
62.0 years (range: 35 to 86 years), with 194 patients (37.9%) > 65 years of age. Most patients
were male (Tislelizumab: 84.8%; ICC: 84.0%) and had a baseline ECOG PS score of

1 (Tislelizumab: 74.2%; ICC: 76.6%). In each arm, 26.6% and 24.6% of (Tislelizumab Arm and
the ICC Arm, respectively) had never smoked (Table 17).
