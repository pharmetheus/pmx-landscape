Trials for olezarsen enrolled sixteen participants with mild HI based on the National Cancer
Institute Organ Dysfunction Working Group criteria. Two subjects were from the healthy subject
single ascending dose/multiple ascending dose study (ISIS 678354-CS1), one subject from
healthy Japanese subject single ascending dose/multiple ascending dose study (ISIS AKCEA-
CS1), six patients from dose-ranging study in patients with HTG and established or at high risk
for CVD (ISIS 678354-CS2), four patients from confirmatory efficacy phase 2b study in patients
with HTG (ISIS 678354-CS8) and three patients were from phase 3 study in patients with FCS
(ISIS 678354-CS3 or Balance). One subject with moderate HI was enrolled in healthy subject
study AKCEA-CS1. No patients with moderate or severe HI were enrolled in the studies.

The population PK analysis did not find baseline AST, ALT, or bilirubin as statistically
significant covariates impacting olezarsen PK. Furthermore, the range of post hoc estimates of
steady-state exposure, including Cmax, AUC, and Cyough, Were overlapping between participants
with normal hepatic function (N=332) and participants with mild (N=16) or moderate (N=1) HI
(based on National Cancer Institute Organ Dysfunction Working Group classification category)
(Figure 35). Consistent with this, steady-state ApoC-III and TG reduction were similar between
participants with normal hepatic function (N=330) and participants with mild (N=16) or
moderate (N=1) HI (Figure 36 & Figure 37).
