Based on its mechanism of action, the labeling includes a warning for embryo-fetal toxicity,
administration of PD-1 blocking antibody is likely to disrupt the maintenance of normal
pregnancy (e.g., increased rates of abortion and stillbirth). The labeling also advises females of
reproductive potential to use contraception during treatment with tislelizumab and for at least
four months after the last dose.

5.5.5. Other Toxicology Studies

The Applicant’s Position:

e There was no specific staining observed in the human or cynomolgus tissue cross
reactivity studies with tislelizumab.

e  Tislelizumab had no hemolytic or aggregatory effects in rabbit red blood cells (RBCs) in
the in vitro hemolysis test.

e  Tislelizumab did not induce cytokine release in the peripheral blood cells from healthy
donors or in human whole blood cells study. The results suggested that tislelizumab has
low risk of causing acute cytokine release syndrome (CRS) arising from PBMCs.

e The potential of enhanced immune recall responses was observed in an in vitro
immunologic recall response assay using human PBMC cells, but not in a T-cell
dependent antibody response to keyhole limpet hemocyanin (KLH) antigen in a human
PD-1 transgenic mouse study.
