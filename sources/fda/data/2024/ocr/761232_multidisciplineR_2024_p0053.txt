BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — BLA 761232
TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

The Applicant’s Position:
The recommended dose of tislelizumab is 200 mg administered as an intravenous infusion once

every 3 weeks until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Deciding whether to keep a
patient on tislelizumab beyond initial signs of disease progression should be based on factors
such as the extent of disease progression and the clinician’s assessment of the patient’s clinical
status.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the recommended dosage of 200 mg
every 3 weeks (Q3W) is acceptable from a clinical pharmacology perspective. However, FDA
does not agree that there is sufficient data to evaluate differences in efficacy and safety for dose
levels between 2 mg/kg and 5 mg/kg and dosing regimens between once every 2 weeks (Q2W)
and Q3W.

Tn the clinical development program of tislelizumab, 280 patients with ESCC received 200 mg
Q3W and 26 patients with ESCC received 5 mg/kg Q3W. Body weight is a significant covariate
to tislelizumab clearance though the exposure change is small. The exposure of 200 mg Q3W is
slightly lower than that of 5 mg/kg Q3W and slightly higher than 2 mg/kg Q3W. In addition,
there is no efficacy data in patients with ESCC who received tislelizumab Q2W. Overall, there
are limited data to evaluate efficacy of tislelizumab in patients with ESCC between 2 and 5
mg/kg doses and Q2W and Q3W dosing regimens.

In addition, a slight trend of higher incidence of imAE and adverse events of special interest
(AESD was observed in patients with advanced solid tumors receiving 5 mg/kg Q3W as
compared with those receiving 200 mg Q3W (Figure 8). Due to limited number of patients with
solid tumors (n=21) evaluated at the 2 mg/kg dose, a formal comparison of safety between 2
mg/kg and 5 mg/kg is limited. There are also limited safety data for comparison between the
Q2W and Q3W dosing regimens because only a small portion of patients with solid tumors
received tislelizumab at the Q2W dosing regimens (n=26 for 2 mg/kg Q2W; n=26 for 5 mg/kg
Q2W). Furthermore, there are limited data to rule out any potential differences in safety across
different tumors as final safety analyses of other supportive clinical trials for other cancers are
pending. Thus, there is limited data to compare safety between 2 and 5 mg/kg doses and dosing
regimens (Q2W and Q3W) for patients with ESCC.
