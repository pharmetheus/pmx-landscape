Patient ID# --Applicant Attribution: Cerebrovascular accident

75-year-old White female experienced a stroke with right hemiparesis on Study Day 3 and
presented to the emergency department in a semi-comatose state. A CT scan showed a
thrombus in the left middle cerebral artery, and she then underwent thrombolysis and
thrombectomy. The patient died on the same day.

The Investigator considered COVID-19 to be unrelated to inavolisib, fulvestrant, palbociclib and
related to other cause (unspecified).

Pertinent medical history includes hyperglycemia and hypertension. FDA agrees with the
diagnosis of cerebrovascular accident.

(b) (6)

Patient ID# --Applicant Attribution: COVID-19 pneumonia

58-year-old White female randomized to the placebo treatment arm was diagnosed with Grade
1 COVID-19 pneumonia. On Study Day 57, she was hospitalized. No known treatment was
administered for the event as it was reported that medical documents were not available as
participant was hospitalized in another hospital. On Study Day 67, the participant died due to
COVID-19 pneumonia. An autopsy was not performed.

The Investigator considered COVID-19 pneumonia to be unrelated to placebo, fulvestrant and
palbociclib and unrelated to other cause (unspecified).

FDA agrees with the diagnosis of COVID-19 and agrees the death due to pneumonia was likely
due to COVID-19.

(b) 6)

Patient ID# --Applicant Attribution: Cardiac Arrest

57-year-old Asian female randomized to the placebo treatment arm was diagnosed with Grade
4 cardiac arrest on Study Day 4. The patient died later the same day due to cardiac arrest. An
autopsy was not performed.

The Investigator considered cardiac arrest to be unrelated to placebo, fulvestrant and
palbociclib and related to disease under study.

Pertinent concurrent conditions at the time of study entry include hypertension and
hyperlipidemia. Given the limited information, it is difficult to assess the cause of cardiac arrest.
