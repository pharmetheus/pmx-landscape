NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

For the dose range tested (3, 6, 9, and 12 mg, with limited data from 3, 6, and 12
mg), ORR, BOR, DOR and PFS were not associated with inavolisib exposure, after
adjustment to baseline covariates. Measurable disease was identified as
covariate in the final model of ORR and BOR (the presence of measurable
disease was associated with a higher probability of response); Combit
status (Triplet), Liver Metastasis and Baseline Tumor Burden were identified as
covariates in the final model of PFS (liver metastasis and higher baseline tumor
burden led to shorter PFS, and triplet combination was associated with longer
PFS).

The analyses outcome indicated better efficacy in Triplet combination (with a

CDK4/6 inhibitor and endocrine agent), and worse efficacy for higher tumor

burden and Liver Metastasis.
