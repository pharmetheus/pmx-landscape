DHN Assessment

This case is assessed as highly likely due to aprocitentan treatment based on the positive
rechallenge which strongly suggests a causal relationship with aprocitentan and not the
atorvastatin. The statin was not restarted. The latencies from aprocitentan start, dose increase and
rechallenge were 208, 179 and 61 days, respectively. The shorter latency on rechallenge would
