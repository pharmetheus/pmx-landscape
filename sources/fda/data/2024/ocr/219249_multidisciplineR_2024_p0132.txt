Patient ID# --Applicant Attribution: Gastrointestinal hemorrhage

42-year-old Asian female randomized to the inavolisib treatment arm was diagnosed with
gastrointestinal hemorrhage on Study Day 491 (symptoms not reported). Cardiopulmonary
resuscitation was performed however the patient died. The patient had progression of disease
in non-target lesions on Study Day 443 and treatment was permanently discontinued on Study
Day 460 with the last dose of palbociclib administered on Study Day 453, fulvestrant on Study
Day 454, and inavolisib on Study Day 460. An autopsy was not performed.

The Investigator considered acute coronary syndrome to be unrelated to placebo, fulvestrant,
and palbociclib and related to disease under study.

FDA agrees that the event of gastrointestinal hemorrhage was unlikely related to inavolisib as
study treatment had been discontinued more than 30 days prior. The patient had no reported
adverse event of thrombocytopenia that may have contributed to bleeding.

(b) 6)

Patient ID# --Applicant Attribution: Death

74-year-old White female randomized to the inavolisib treatment arm was reported to have
poor condition and not get up on Study Day 276. On Study Day 277, the participant died, and
the reason of death was unknown as per death certificate. An autopsy was not performed.

The Investigator considered death to be unrelated to inavolisib, fulvestrant, palbociclib and
related to other cause (disease progression).

Given the limited information, FDA considers it difficult to assess the cause of death. Although
the death may be due to progression of disease, no clear evidence has been presented, even
after FDA review of information requests to the Applicant that were unable to provide
additional clinical information for attribution.

(b) (6)

Patient ID# --Applicant Attribution: COVID-19

72-year-old Asian female randomized to the inavolisib treatment arm was diagnosed with
Grade 1 COVID-19 on Study Day 253. On Study Day 256, she had a low oxygen saturation of 88%
and was hospitalized for symptomatic treatment, COVID-19 had worsened to Grade 4. On Study
Day 260, the patient died due to COVID-19. It was unknown if an autopsy was performed.
