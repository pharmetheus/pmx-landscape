6.3.5.4 Seizure

Seizure is an adverse reaction for ESAs, therefore, this risk was further evaluated in Studies Cl-

0036 and CI-0039. Results are difficult to interpret due to the rarity of event (Table 40 and
Table 41) and do not inform further on this risk with vadadustat.

Conclusion: Seizures are a risk with ESAs. While seizures were not listed as a deficiency in the
CRL for vadadustat, it was noted that this risk should be studied in future clinical trials. Studies
Cl-0036 and Cl-0039 had too few events of seizures to further inform this risk. Given the rate of
seizures in the vadadustat clinical development program (see original NDA review for further
details) is similar to that with ESAs, this risk will be described in a Warning in the vadadustat
USPI.

6.3.5.5 Rhabdomyolysis

No adverse events of rhabdomyolysis were reported in Studies Cl-0036 or Cl-0039. Elevated
creatine phosphokinase (CPK) >5x ULN were observed in only vadadustat arms.
