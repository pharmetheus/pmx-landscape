5.5.5. Other Toxicology Studies

Phototoxicity

The Applicant's Position: The phototoxic potential of inavolisib was assessed in a GLP neutral
red uptake phototoxicity assay in BALB/c 3T3 mouse fibroblasts (16-1227 / 4.2.3.7.7). Inavolisib
did not reduce the viability of 3T3 cells exposed to ultraviolet radiation as compared with that
in the absence of ultraviolet radiation up to the highest concentration tested of 31.6 j1g/mL.
Therefore, it was concluded that inavolisib did not show phototoxicity potential in this assay.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position.
