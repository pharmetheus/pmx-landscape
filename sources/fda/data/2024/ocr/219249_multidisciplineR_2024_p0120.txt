INAVO120 were required to have advanced breast cancer with a PIK3CA mutation, considered
to be endocrine resistant, and could not have received prior systemic therapy in the advanced
or metastatic setting for breast cancer. PIK3CA mutation was determined through testing of
blood plasma-derived ctDNA with a next-generation sequencing (NGS) assay performed at the
Sponsor-designated central laboratory, or through local testing of ctDNA or tumor tissue with
an appropriately validated NGS or PCR assay. Primary endocrine resistance was defined as
relapse while on the first 2 years of adjuvant endocrine therapy (ET) and secondary endocrine
resistance was defined as relapse while on adjuvant ET after at least 2 years or relapse within
12 months of completing adjuvant ET.

The primary efficacy endpoint for INAVO120 was PFS as assessed by investigator. A

prospectively planned sensitivity analysis of PFS by BICR assessment was also performed on all
patients. Key secondary endpoints included OS, ORR, and TTCD in worst pain severity, physical
functioning, role functioning, and health-related quality of life. DOR was a secondary endpoint.

INAVO120 met its primary endpoint with a median PFS of 15 months (95% CI 11.3, 20.5) vs 7.3
months (95% Cl: 5.6, 9.3) favoring inavolisib (HR 0.43, 95% Cl: 0.32, 0.59; p-value <0.0001). PFS
by BICR results were supportive of the primary endpoint. OS is the key secondary endpoint per
the hierarchical testing procedure pre-specified in the SAP. The interim OS analysis at the time
of PFS analysis with 63% information fraction was not statistically significant. Interim OS results
showed a HR of 0.64 (95% Cl: 0.43, 0.97) with median OS not estimable (95% Cl: 27.3, NE) on
the inavolisib with palbociclib plus fulvestrant arm and 31.1 months (95% Cl: 22.3, NE) on the
placebo + palbociclib + fulvestrant arm. Thus, there appears to be no detriment in OS at this
time. The final analysis of OS is expected in the second quarter of 2025. Results for the
secondary endpoints of ORR and DOR appear to support the primary endpoint results.
However, since the interim analysis of OS at the time of PFS analysis was not statistically
significant, none of the remaining secondary endpoints in the pre-specified testing hierarchy
were formally tested at this time.

Time to confirmed deterioration (TTCD) in worst pain severity, physical functioning, role
functioning, and health-related quality of life were key secondary endpoints included in the
testing hierarchy. In general, FDA has concerns about patient-reported time-to-deterioration
endpoints, and this was communicated to the Applicant prior to submission. Further, these
results are considered descriptive at this time because the first key secondary endpoint of OS
was not statistically significant as noted above.

As noted in the Integrated Assessment of Effectiveness (Section 8.1.4), the results of the
G039374 trial were not considered supportive of expanded indication beyond the population
enrolled in INAVO120.
