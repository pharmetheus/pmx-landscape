Results

Levacetylleucine, at up to 2000 g/mL, was evaluated to be non-genotoxic because it did not
produce chromosomal, or chromatid damage in cultured human lymphocytes in the presence
(4-hour incubation) or absence (4- and 24-hour incubations) of S9 compared to vehicle
control (Table 53). Mitomycin positive control produced significant increases in
chromosomal aberrations compared to vehicle control.
