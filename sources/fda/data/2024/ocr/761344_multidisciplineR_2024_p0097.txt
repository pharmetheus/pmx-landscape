Higher tarlatamab exposures (e.g., Cavg,first cycle) Were associated
with higher incidences of any grade neutropenia, grade 1 and
grade 2 neurotoxicity including ICANs. E-R for safety analysis did
not identify major safety concerns for the proposed dosage.

Additional Pharmacokinetics Summary
The popPK analysis of tarlatamab was performed using pooled data from Study
20160323 and Study 20200491. Sparse PK samples were collected following end of
infusion on C2D15, C3 D1 and C4D1 and pre-infusion on C2D15, C3D1 through C6D1.
Based on the PK simulation, geometric mean (% CV) of PK parameters are summarized

in Table 17. The popPK simulated tarlatamab PK profile is presented in

Figure 7. For additional details, refer to section 19.4.1.
