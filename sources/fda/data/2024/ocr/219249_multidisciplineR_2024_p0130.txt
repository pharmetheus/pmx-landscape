The Applicant’s Position:

Pivotal Study WO41554: At the time of CCOD (29 Sep 2023), a total of 97 patients (29.9%) had
died due to any cause. The proportion of patients who died in the Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm
(26.5%) was lower in comparison with the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm (33.3%). The most common
cause of death in both treatment arms was progressive disease (Table 30). Grade 5 AEs were
reported in 6 patients (3.7%) in the Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm and 2 patients (1.2%) of patients in
the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm (Table 31). None of the Grade 5 AEs were reported as related to any
study drug by the investigator and no Grade 5 AE by PT was observed in >1 patient in either
arm.
