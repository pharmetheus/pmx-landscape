BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — BLA 761232
TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

No treatment related toxicity, including inflammation and immunotoxicity, was identified in
monkeys at repeated doses up to 30 mg/kg every two weeks for 13 weeks (total 7 doses).

No specific safety concerns were identified in the vital functions of the cardiovascular,
respiratory, or central nervous systems in monkeys. No specific cross reactivity between tissue
cell surface epitopes and tislelizumab was noted in monkey or human tissues. No apparent
cytokine releases were noted in assays conducted with human whole blood cells or peripheral
blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs). The dose of 30 mg/kg in monkeys is approximately 10-fold
of the recommended human dose (200 mg) based on body weight and resulted in exposures
(AUC) approximately 5-fold to 8-fold higher than those in patients receiving the recommended
human dose of 200 mg.

Neutralizing ADA were detected in most of monkeys in all studies, however, there was no
immune related toxicity or apparent effect on systemic exposure.

The systemic exposure appeared to increase dose proportionally without apparent sex difference.
No drug accumulation or changes in systemic exposure were noted after repeated doses every
two weeks for 13 weeks.

The toxicology studies and resulting data are considered adequate to support the clinical
development and marketing authorization application of tislelizumab.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees. Immune cell infiltration was observed in various tissues including the
heart and skin. One high dose female has mild myocardial degeneration.

Study title/ number: A 13-week Intravenous Infusion Toxicity Study of A Monoclonal
Antibody BGB-A317 with A 6-Week Recovery Period in Cynomolgus Monkeys/P14-
057-CD

GLP compliance: Stated compliance with US GLP (21 CFR Part 58).

FDA considers this to be a non-GLP study based on results of study inspection. A follow-
up GLP-compliant 13-week repeat-dose toxicology study in cynomolgus with
intravenous (IV) injection every 2 weeks (Q2W) did not further inform the safety profile.
Considering the toxicology profile observed in Study No. P14-057-CD and the
pharmacology data are consistent with the class, the totality of the data supports the

nonclinical safety of tislelizumab.
Methods
Dose and frequency of dosing: 0, 3, 10, or 30 mg/kg once every two weeks for 13
weeks
Route of administration: Intravenous infusion
Fonnulation/Vehicle: 0.9% sodium chloride injection
Species/Strain: Cynomolgus monkeys
