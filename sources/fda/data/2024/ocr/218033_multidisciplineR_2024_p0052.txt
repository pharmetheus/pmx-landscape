Evaluation of antitumor activity was conducted in several subcutaneous xenograft models and
one intracranial model. Tovorafenib had antitumor activity in xenograft mouse models bearing
subcutaneous melanoma tumors with the AGK-BRAF fusion, BRAF V600E or V600D mutations,
and BRAF WT when administered orally ranging from once daily to once every 4 days. Similar
antitumor activity was observed in subcutaneous mouse models of colorectal tumors bearing
BRAF V600E mutations and pancreatic tumors with BRAF WT. Tovorafenib antitumor activity
peaked around a dose of 12.5 mg/kg with lower or higher doses having less antitumor activity
when given orally once daily or once every 2-3 days for up to 28 days. Tovorafenib led to
inhibition of phosphorylation of ERK in the tumor tissue harvested from these treated mice. In
mice bearing intracranial tumors of KIAA1549:BRAF model cells or BRAF V600E model cells
(both p53-/- neuroprogenitor cells) expressing luciferase, treatment with tovorafenib dose-
