The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA acknowledges that TTCD in worst pain severity, physical functioning, role functioning, and
health-related quality of life were key secondary endpoints included in the testing hierarchy.
Completion rates for all PROs were high at baseline, above 90%, and remained above 80% at
subsequent assessment timepoints during which the majority of patients remained on
treatment in each arm (Cycle 9 and Cycle 5 for the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv versus Pbo + Palbo +
Fulv arms, respectively. In general, FDA has concerns about patient-reported time-to-
deterioration endpoints, and this was communicated to the Applicant prior to submission.
Further, as noted in the secondary endpoints section, these results are considered descriptive
at this time because the first key secondary endpoint of OS was not statistically significant. The
Applicant did collect PRO-CTCAE and patient-reported overall side effect impact data, which is
described in Section 8.2 below.

8.1.2.2. Supportive Study: GO39374, Phase I

The primary endpoint of Study GO39374 is safety. The study makes a preliminary assessment of
the anti-tumor activity of inavolisib administered as a single agent in patients with locally
advanced or metastatic P/K3CA-mutated solid tumors or in combination with other therapies in
patients with P/K3CA-mutated HR+, HER2— LA/mBC. The data provided herein focuses on Arms
B (Inavo+Palbo+Letro), E (Inavot+Palbo+Fulv), and F (Inavo+Palbo+ Fulv+ Metf).

Patient Disposition

Data and the Applicant’s Position: All patients enrolled in Arm B (N =33), Arm E (N = 20), and
Arm F (N =21) were treated except for 1 patient in Arm F. This patient withdrew consent and
discontinued from the study prior to receiving any study drug. As of CCOD (27 March 2023), the
majority of patients in Arm B (84.8%), Arm E (70.0%), and Arm F (95.2%) had discontinued from
the study, mostly due to progressive disease (Arm B: 69.7%, Arm E: 50.0%, and Arm F: 61.9%).
A total of 191 patients were enrolled across Arms A-F. At the CCOD, a total of 176 patients
(92.1%) discontinued from the study. The majority of study discontinuations were due to
progressive disease (141 patients [73.8%]) and symptomatic deterioration (clinical progression
of study disease; 15 patients [7.9%]).
