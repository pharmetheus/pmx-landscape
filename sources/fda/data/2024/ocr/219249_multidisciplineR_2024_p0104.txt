{Inavolisib}

A lower proportion of patients in the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv arm (35.4%) had discontinued from

The Applicant's Position: The pivotal Study WO41554 enrolled a globally diverse population
through the multi-center, multinational nature of the study.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. This was a multinational trial with 16 patients
enrolled from the United States as outlined in Table 19 below. Refer to FDA assessment below
regarding the assessment of diversity in INAVO120 below, under demographic characteristics,
and the PMC issued by FDA for diversity in Section 13.

Protocol Violations/Deviations

Data: As of the CCOD (29 Sep 2023), 28.3% of patients of the FAS had at least one major
protocol deviation. Overall, major protocol deviations were comparable between arms
(Inavo+Palbo+Fulv: 26.1% vs. Pbho+Palbo+Fulv: 30.5%). The most common protocol deviations
were procedural violations (17.8%). Major protocol deviations due to COVID-19 were
comparable across arms (3.7% vs. 3.0%). The majority of major protocol deviations related to
COVID-19 were medication related (3.1% vs. 2.4%). The major protocol deviations related to
the Ukraine-Russia conflict were comparable across arms (1.9% vs. 1.2%), and were medication
related (1.9% vs. 1.2%) or procedural related (1.2% vs. 0.6%).

The Applicant's Position: Overall, none of the major protocol deviations were considered to
have impacted the monitoring of patient safety, the study outcome, or the interpretation of the
study results.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the major protocol deviations likely did not
impact the outcome or interpretation of the study results.

The most common protocol deviations categorized under procedural violations were labs not
done per protocol (5.0% vs 7.9%); tumor assessment not done per protocol (6.2% vs 4.3%) and
ICF not signed prior to any study-specific procedure (1.9% vs 6.1%) in the inavolisib vs placebo
treatment arm, respectively.

The most common protocol deviations categorized under medication were Incorrect
inavolisib/placebo dose modification/interruption or delay (4.3% vs 1.8%); Incorrect palbociclib
and dose modification/interruption or delay (1.2% vs 3.0%) in the inavolisib vs placebo
treatment arm, respectively.
