The review team identified the key review issues, listed in Sections 3.1.1 and 3.1.2 below,

and risk issues can be found in Sections 6.3 and 7.7, respectively.

3.1.1.1. Efficacy of Triglyceride Reduction of 80 mg
Olezarsen in Trial CS3 in the FCS Population

The review team concluded that the results of the primary endpoint of TG reduction of olezarsen
80 mg dose was statistically significant compared to placebo and was sufficient to establish the
efficacy of olezarsen 80 mg dose for TG reduction in the FCS population.

3.1.1.3. Acceptability of the Secondary Endpoint of Acute
Pancreatitis Risk Reduction

Trial CS3 assessed key secondary endpoints of a reduction in prospectively adjudicated AP; it is
the first outcome trial submitted to the FDA to measure adjudicated AP in an HTG population.
The nominal finding of a reduction in the rate of AP is consistent with the pathophysiology that
links elevated levels of TG with AP and the reduction of TG with a subsequent reduction in AP.
