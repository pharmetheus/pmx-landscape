NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

Serious Adverse Events

Data and the Applicant's Position:
Pivotal Study WO41554: A numerically higher proportion of patients experienced SAEs in the

Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm compared with the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm (24.1% vs. 10.5%, respectively).
The SAEs were distributed across multiple PTs and no SAE was reported with a >2% difference
between treatment arms. The majority of SAEs were Grade 3 in severity in both the
Inavot+Palbo+Fulv arm (16.7%) and the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm (7.4%).

The incidence of SAEs of any grade in the Infections and Infestations System Organ Class (SOC)
was higher in the Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm (11 [6.8%]) compared with the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm
(3 [1.9%]) and was primarily driven by PTs of COVID-19 (4 [2.5%] vs. 1 [0.6%]), pneumonia

(3 [1.9%] vs. 0) and urinary tract infection (2 [1.2%] vs. 0), which were all assessed by
investigator as not related to any study drug.

The SAEs occurring in > 2 patients in either arm were: COVID-19 (4 [2.5%] Inavo+Palbo+Fulv vs.
1 [0.6%] Pbo+Palbo+Fulv), pneumonia, anemia, febrile neutropenia (3 [1.9%] vs. 0 each),
urinary tract infection, diarrhea, pyrexia, and acute coronary syndrome (2 [1.2%] vs. 0 each),
vomiting (1 [0.6%] vs. 2 [1.2%]), and pulmonary embolism (0 vs. 2 [1.2%]).

Supportive Study GO39374: In the overall safety population, 85 SAEs were reported in 27.4% of
patients. SAEs occurring in >2 patients (1.1% overall) included: hyperglycemia (4 [2.1%]),
cellulitis, hypercalcemia, and urinary tract infection (3 each [1.6%]), and back pain, headache,
osteomyelitis, pneumonia, rash, small intestinal obstructions, superior vena cava syndrome,
and vomiting (2 each [1.1%]); all other events were reported in one patient each. SAEs assessed
by the investigator as related to any study treatment (except metformin) were reported in

14 patients (7.4%).

Amongst Arms B, E, and F,

e In Arm B (Inavo+Palbo+Letro; n=33 patients), 25 SAEs were reported in 42.4% of patients
(14 of 33 patients). Cellulitis occurred in 2 patients (7.4%) in Arm B; the remainder of SAEs
were single occurrences observed in one patient each (3.7%).

e InArm E (Inavot+Palbo+Fulv; n=20 patients), 9 SAEs were reported in 30.0% of patients (6 of
20 patients). No SAE occurred in more than one patient.

e In Arm F (Inavot+Palbo+Fulv+Met; n=20 patients), 15 SAEs were reported in 40.0% of
patients (8 of 20 patients). No SAE occurred in more than one patient.

INAVO120 trial (WO41554). The highest incidence of SAEs on the inavolisib treatment arm
