The Applicant’s Position:

Study 302 was designed and initiated when single-agent chemotherapy was considered standard
of care and the study design enrolled patients with advanced unresectable/metastatic ESCC that
has progressed during or after first line therapy.

A potential weakness in the design of Study 302 is the open-label identification of the specific
drug, whether tislelizumab or one of the 3 chemotherapeutic agents, assigned to the patient. This
element of the study may bias the reporting of adverse events and may have influenced the
higher withdrawal rate by patient and by investigator seen in the ICC Arm compared with the
Tislelizamab Arm. However, within the analyses of safety data, no obvious impact on the safety
results was noted.

Regardless of the limitations noted, the totality of the safety package is sufficient to characterize
the safety profile of tislelizumab and to guide the management of risks associated with
tislelizumab as a treatment for ESCC.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees that the safety database is adequate to evaluate the safety of tislelizumab compared
to single-agent chemotherapy in this setting. See FDA’s comments in Section 8.2.1, above.

8.2.5. Adequacy of Applicant’s Clinical Safety Assessments
Issues Regarding Data Integrity and Submission Quality

Data:

The clinical sites for the safety analysis groups were monitored by clinical research associates
following study-specific monitoring plans for consistency. Data were queried per study-specific
data management plans. Additionally, external and internal Safety Monitoring Committee (SMC)
reviewed safety data for all studies.

The Applicant’s Position:
No issues were identified regarding data integrity or submission quality that had an effect on the

safety review.
