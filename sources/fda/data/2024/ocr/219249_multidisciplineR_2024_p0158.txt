NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

The incidence of neutropenia AEs in the Inavo+Palbot+Fulv arm of Study WO41554 was
consistent with the palbociclib-containing Arms B, E, and F of Study GO39374. Across both
studies, the majority of neutropenia events were Grade 3-4 with no Grade 5 events reported.
Two SAEs, 1 event in the placebo arm of Study WO41554 and 1 event in Arm B of

Study GO39374, were reported. Among common selected AEs in Study WO41554, the incidence
and severity of neutropenia were well balanced between the two treatment arms and
consistent with the incidence of this AE reported with palbociclib (IBRANCE® USPI); thereby,
indicating that inavolisib has minimal impact on the known risk of neutropenia associated with
palbociclib.

Across both studies, most of the stomatitis and mucosal inflammation events were Grade 1-2
with no Grade 5 events. One SAE was reported in Study GO39374. The AEs of stomatitis and
mucosal inflammation were manageable with standard clinical measures and dose
modifications/interruptions.

The median dose intensity of triplet combination of Inavo+Palbot+Fulv was high across both
studies thereby showing that the addition of inavolisib did not compromise the dose intensity
of palbociclib and fulvestrant (current standard of care). At the time of CCOD for Study
W041554, the majority of patients in the Inavot+Palbo+Fulv arm had been on treatment for
longer than 6 months in comparison to the majority of the patients in the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm
being on treatment for less than 6 months.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The safety profile for inavolisib in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant is primarily
based on data from the safety population from the INAVO120 trial (WO41554). This includes
162 patients treated with inavolisib+palbociclib+fulvestrant. Data from Study GO39374 was not
considered supportive of the intended indication and was not reviewed in depth. The patient
population in Study GO39374 was different than the patient population for the granted
indication.

The overall safety profile for the triplet combination was consistent with the anticipated safety
profile for inavolisib and the known safety profile for palbociclib and fulvestrant.

Adverse events known to occur with PI3K inhibitors include hyperglycemia, stomatitis, diarrhea,
and rash. Hyperglycemia (increased laboratory glucose) occurred at much higher rates on the
inavolisib treatment arm (85%) compared to the placebo arm (43%). Hyperglycemia was
managed with dose modifications and implementation of anti-hyperglycemic therapy as
needed. Only two patients required permanent discontinuation of inavolisib due to
hyperglycemia. Grade 3 increased laboratory blood glucose (FPG > 250 to 500 mg/dL) occurred
in 12% of patients, and 0.6% with Grade 4 (FPG > 500 mg/dL) events. There was no grade 5
