NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

CYP Substrates: In vitro studies suggest a low likelihood of time-dependent inhibition and
induction of CYP3A4, and no potential to inhibit or induce the other CYP enzymes tested
(CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, or CYP2D6) at clinically relevant concentrations.
Endogenous biomarker data, palbociclib PK, and PBPK simulations together suggest that
inavolisib is unlikely to have a clinically relevant impact on the exposure of CYP3A4 substrates.
Transporters: In vitro studies have shown that inavolisib does not appear to have the potential
to inhibit any of the transporters tested (P-glycoprotein [P-gp], BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3,
OCT1, OCT2, MATE1, MATE2K, OAT1, or OATS) at clinically relevant concentrations.

Effects of other drugs on inavolisib

CYP Inhibitors/Inducers: Clinical study results show that the predominant metabolites of
inavolisib are not mediated by CYP enzymes, suggesting a low likelihood of interaction between
inavolisib and CYP inhibitors or inducers.

Transporters: In vitro studies have shown that inavolisib is not a substrate of OATP1B1,
OATP1B3, OCT1, OCT2, OAT1, OAT2, MATE1, or MATE2K, but is a substrate of P-gp and BCRP.
However, based on the pharmacokinetic profile of inavolisib, inhibitors or inducers of P-gp
and/or BCRP are not expected to have a clinically relevant DDI with inavolisib.

Acid-reducing Agents: |n clinical studies, concomitant use of proton pump inhibitors did not
have a clinically meaningful effect on inavolisib exposure.

The Applicant's Position:

Food-drug interaction: These data suggest that administration of inavolisib in the presence of
food did not impact the rate or the extent of absorption of inavolisib following a single or
multiple doses of inavolisib.

Drug-drug interactions: No clinically relevant DDIs are identified or expected with inavolisib as a
victim or perpetrator.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position on food-effect on inavolisib PK. FDA does not agree
with the Applicant’s positions on inavolisib DDI potential. In addition, FDA agrees with the
Applicant’s approach of bridging different formulations using dissolution studies; refer to the
Biopharmaceutics review for details.

1) Food-effect:

inavolisib was evaluated after a single dose (Day 1) and following QD dosing (Day 8) in Arm D of
Study GO39374 (Table 17). Study results showed that AUCo-24 and Cmax of inavolisib given with a
high-fat meal decreased by 12% and 9%, respectively. The food effect study was conducted
