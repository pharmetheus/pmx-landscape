{Inavolisib}

Most patients in Arm B (72.7%), Arm E (80%), and Arm F (95.2%) had received prior Al, with
letrozole as the most common aromatase inhibitor therapy reported in all arms. Fourteen out

In the overall population, 97.4% of patients had at least one site of metastatic disease at
baseline. A total of 94.8% of patients had received prior cancer therapy in the metastatic
setting. Among the patients who received therapy in the metastatic setting, the median line of
prior therapy in the metastatic setting was 2 (range: 1-10).

The Applicant's Position: The population from Arms B, E, and F of Study GO39374 are
representative of P/K3CA-mutated, HR+, HER2— LA/mBC patients receiving an endocrine-based
combination therapy in a later line setting. There were no eligibility restrictions with respect to
patients’ endocrine sensitivity or resistance; however, the majority of patients enrolled in Arm
B, Arm E, and Arm F were endocrine-resistant.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. The majority of patients in Arms B, E and F had
received prior systemic therapy in the advanced/metastatic setting. In addition, patients
enrolled in GO39374 were not required to have endocrine resistant disease.

Treatment Compliance, Concomitant Medications, and Rescue Medication Use

Data and The Applicant’s Position: At the CCOD, the median cumulative inavolisib dose intensity
was high at 95.3%, 90.5% and 89.1% in Arms B, E, and F, respectively. The median cumulative
palbociclib dose intensity was 80.6% in Arm E and 92.8% in Arm F, and the median cumulative
fulvestrant dose intensity was 95.4% in Arm E and 95.7% in Arm F. In the overall population, the
median cumulative inavolisib dose intensity across all arms was 96.0% (Table 28).

Overall, 93.7% of patients had at least one medication ongoing at baseline. The most commonly
used (230% of patients overall) medication classes were: analgesics (59.7%); vitamins (44.5%);
psycholeptics (44.5%); mineral supplements (40.8%); topical products for joint and muscular
pain (36.1%); drugs for acid related disorders (35.1%); ophthalmologicals (31.9%); other
dermatological preparations (30.4%).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
