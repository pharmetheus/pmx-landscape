Disk Content Studies

The Applicant submitted data from disk diffusion susceptibility testing with sulopenem disk
masses of 5, 10, and 20 pg verses broth microdilution MIC testing by using concurrent inoculum
of the following pathogens: 110 S. aureus (including methicillin-resistant staphylococcus
aureus), 63 CONS, 81 S. pneumoniae, 26 GAS, 27 GBS, 27 VGS, 105 enterococci, 116
Enterobacterales (including extended spectrum beta-lactamase), 31 lactose non-fermenting
gram-negative bacilli and 91 H. influenzae (Study a 615). The disk zone diameters and MIC
values of the tested isolates were correlated in scatterplots to develop provisional interpretive
criteria for the disk diffusion testing of sulopenem. QC ranges have been established using the 2
Lg sulopenem disk.

Correlation of Broth Microdilution vs. Agar Dilution MIC Values

Susceptibility testing was conducted in parallel by broth microdilution and agar dilution using
concurrent inoculum of 398 isolates (106 staphylococci, 102 Enterobacterales, 102 S.
pneumoniae and 88 H. influenzae isolates) by following the CLSI guidelines (CLS! M07).

Essential agreement between broth microdilution and agar dilution MIC values did not exceed
95% for any of these tested groups of organisms, however, the maximum agreements of 93.8%,
and 94.1% were achieved for methicillin-susceptible staphylococcus aureus (n=64) and
Enterobacterales (n=102), respectively.

Quality Control Studies

Quality control (QC) studies for sulopenem have been conducted using CLSI M23 guidelines for
the establishment of QC ranges for broth microdilution susceptibility testing of aerobes (Study
| 631), and disk diffusion susceptibility testing of Enterobacterales (Study | ITM-004-18).
