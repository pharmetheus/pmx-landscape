Based on published literature, among patients with estrogen receptor-positive (ER+)/HER2-
breast cancer, the prevalence of PIK3CA mutations was around 37 — 48% across all ancestry
groups, and the distributions of mutation types was also similar across all ancestry groups
(Chen et al. 2022); therefore, inavolisib may be utilized in patients from racial or ethnic groups
with limited current data. Due to the limited data from patients from underrepresented races
and ethnicities, there are insufficient data to assess any potential differences in PK or PD for
patients who were not White or Asian. A PMC will be issued to study inavolisib in a diverse
patient population representative of the US population (see Section 8.1.1). Collection of clinical
pharmacology data to assess potential differences in PK and PD will be included in the PMC.
