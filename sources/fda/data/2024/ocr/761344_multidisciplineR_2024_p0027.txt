NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761344}
IMDELLTRA - Tarlatamab

Administration sections of the tarlatamab U.S. Prescribing Information (USPI), and additional mitigation strategies for CRS and neurologic
toxicity, including ICANS, are discussed below. The observed safety profile is considered acceptable when assessed in the context of a life-
threatening disease.

In the pooled safety population (n = 187), CRS occurred in 55% of patients treated with tarlatamab, including 34% Grade 1, 19% Grade 2, 1.1%
Grade 3 and 0.5% Grade 4. Recurrent CRS occurred in 24% of tarlatamab-treated patients (18% Grade 1 and 6% Grade 2). Most events (43%) of
CRS occurred after the first dose with 29% of patients experiencing any grade CRS after the second dose and 9% of patients experiencing CRS
following the third dose or later. Following the Day 1, Day 8, Day 15 infusions, 16%, 4.3%, and 2.1% of patients experienced 2 Grade 2 CRS,
respectively. The median time to onset of all grade CRS from most recent dose of tarlatamab was 13.5 hours (range: 1 to 268 hours). The
median time to onset of > Grade 2 CRS from most recent dose of tarlatamab was 14.6 hours (range: 2 to 566 hours).

Neurologic toxicity including ICANS occurred in 47% of patients in the pooled safety population, including 10% Grade 3; no Grade 4 events were
reported. The most frequent neurologic toxicities were headache (14%), peripheral neuropathy (7%), dizziness (7%), insomnia (6%), muscular
weakness (3.7%), delirium (2.1%), syncope (1.6%) and neurotoxicity (1.1%). ICANS occurred in 9% of patients and recurrent ICANS occurred in
1.6% of patients. Most patients experienced ICANS following cycle 2 day 1 (24%). Following Day 1, Day 8, and Day 15 infusions, 0.5%, 0.5% and
3.7% of patients experienced 2 Grade 2 ICANS, respectively. The median time to onset of ICANS from the first dose of tarlatamab was 29.5 days
(range: 1 to 154 days). The median time to resolution of ICANS was 33 days (range: 1 to 93 days).

Per the protocol for Study 20200491, patients were hospitalized for 48 hours post tarlatamab infusion for Cycle 1 Day 1 and Cycle 1 Day 8
doses, except for patients in Part 3 of Study 20200491, who were hospitalized for 24 hours post tarlatamab infusion for Cycle 1 Day 1 and Cycle
1 Day 8 doses. Based on review of all currently available safety data, it was determined that monitoring of patients for 22 to 24 hours on Cycle 1
Day 1 and Cycle 1 Day 8 in an appropriate healthcare setting is acceptable to ensure safe use, and this recommendation is incorporated in
labeling.

Based on our review, we have determined that the safety risks of tarlatamab can be adequately conveyed through labeling. The USPI will
include Boxed Warnings to communicate the risks of CRS and neurologic toxicity, including ICANS. In addition, enhanced pharmacovigilance and
a Dear Healthcare Provider letter are non-REMS adjuncts recommended to help convey these risks. A post-marketing requirement (PMR) will
