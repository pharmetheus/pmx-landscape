The FDA’s Assessment:

The safety analyses were based primarily on FDA’s independent review of data from the phase
3 INAVO120 (WO41554) trial. The FDA’s safety analysis focused on deaths and treatment
emergent adverse events (TEAEs) including serious adverse events (SAEs), TEAEs leading to trial
drug discontinuation, dose reduction, or dosage interruption, grade 3-4 TEAEs, and all-grade
TEAEs. The FDA also reviewed laboratory data. AEs of special interest included hyperglycemia,
stomatitis, and diarrhea.

Topline safety results were reviewed for GO39374 by FDA. In general, no new safety signals
were noted from the GO39374 data compared to the safety data from INAVO120. In depth
safety analyses were not conducted by FDA for the GO39374 trial, as this trial was not
considered to be supportive of expanded indication.

8.2.1. | Safety Review Approach

The Applicant's Position: The safety and tolerability assessments consisted of the monitoring
and recording of AEs, including SAEs and protocol-specified selected AEs, performing
protocol-specified safety laboratory assessments, and assessing protocol-specified vital signs,
ECG, ECOG performance status and PRO -CTCAE assessments.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees that the safety review approach was appropriate to evaluate the safety profile for
inavolisib.
