NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

Laboratory abnormalities were not reviewed in the GO39374 Trial.
Vital Signs

Data and the Applicant's Position:
Pivotal Study WO41554: There were no clinically relevant differences between the treatment
arms with respect to vital sign parameters during the study.

Supportive Study GO39374: Although there were vital sign parameters outside normal limits
noted among patients without an abnormality at baseline, these were not considered to be
clinically relevant.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment for Study WO41554.

Vital signs were not reviewed for Study GO39374.

Electrocardiograms (ECGs)

Data and the Applicant’s Position:
Pivotal Study WO41554: The baseline ECG data were comparable between the arms. No

clinically meaningful trends post-baseline vs. baseline in mean ECG parameters were identified
in either of the arms.

Supportive Study GO39374: Overall, the ECG data showed no clinically meaningful trends post-
baseline vs. baseline in mean ECG parameters.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. No clinically meaningful change (>20msec mean
increase in QTc interval) was noted. Refer to FDA QT-IRT review.

QT

Data:

Pivotal Study WO41554: PTs in the Investigations SOC included ECG QT prolonged (6 patients
[3.7%] Inavo+PalbotFulv vs. 1 patient [0.6%] Pbot+Palbo+Fulv) and ECG T wave abnormal

(1 patient [0.6%] vs. no patient [0%]). In the Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm, one patient (0.6%)
experienced a Grade 3 event of ECG QT prolonged, 2 patients (1.2%) a Grade 2 event and

3 patients (1.9%) a Grade 1 event. The one event of ECG T wave abnormal in the
Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm was reported as Grade 1 in the single patient (0.6%). The Grade 3 event
of ECG QT prolonged was considered related to inavolisib, required treatment, led to dose
reduction of inavolisib and was considered recovering/resolving at the CCOD (29 Sep 2023).
There were no SAEs reported.
