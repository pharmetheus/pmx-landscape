BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

17. Division Director (Clinical)

T agree with the overall approval recommendations for tislelizumab-jsgr (tislelizumab) from the
review teams. Study RATIONALE-302 demonstrated an improvement in OS for tislelizumab
when compared to investigators’ choice of chemotherapy. RATIONALE-302 was a multi-
tegional trial; however, most patients were enrolled in Asia (consistent with the lower incidence
tate of ESCC in the US and Europe). The study enrolled a limited number of patients in Asia
outside of China and close to 20% of patients in Europe or North America. The point estimate
for the treatment effect in Europe and North America was favorable (0.55) and the PK of
tislelizumab across races (Asian, White patients, and Black patients — with data collected beyond
RATIONALE-302) appeared comparable.

Because FDA inspectors were not able to travel to China due to COVID-19 restrictions in China,
action on this application was delayed (i.e., past the PDUFA deadline). FDA carefully
considered the potential impact of the delayed action. Notably, this application does not address
an unmet medical need as there are other treatment options in the same therapeutic class
available to patients with unresectable advanced, recurrent or metastatic esophageal squamous
cell carcinoma (ESCC) after prior fluoropyrimidine- and platinum-based chemotherapy and also
for patients with unresectable advanced or metastatic esophageal squamous cell carcinoma as
first-line treatment in combination with chemotherapy (See Section 2.2).
