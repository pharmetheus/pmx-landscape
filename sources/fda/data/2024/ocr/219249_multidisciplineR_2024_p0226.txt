Induction effect of inavolisib on CYP3A

Inavolisib increased mRNA and activity of CYP3A in hepatocytes from three donors (namely,
FOS, 1198 and 1421, report 16-0131). The induction parameters of inavolisib were estimated
based on the response of mRNA expression in these hepatocytes to inavolisib, and then
calibrated to the induction parameters of rifampin generated from the same hepatocytes
(Table 54). Using the calibrated parameters, inavolisib was predicted to have a weak induction
effect on CYP3A based on the parameters generated from lot FOS (Table 54). However, there is
uncertainty about using the calibrated parameters to estimate induction effect. Although the
induction calibration method was published by Simcyp (PMID: 27026679), in the Simcyp PBPK
models of CYP3A inducers except for phenobarbital, CYP3A induction parameters optimized
with clinical DDI data were used instead of calibrated parameters published in the
aforementioned paper, indicating that clinical DDI data were needed to adequately predict the
induction effects of CYP3A inducers.
