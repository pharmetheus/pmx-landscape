{Tarlatamab}

Information Request issued Jan. 11, 2024.
19.4.4. Exposure-Response Analysis
19.4.4.1. ER (efficacy) Executive Summary
The FDA’s Assessment:

The E-R efficacy analysis generally supports the proposed dosage of tarlatamab (10 mg) in
patients with advanced SCLC with disease progression on or after platinum-based
chemotherapy.

The exposure efficacy analysis suggested that higher tarlatamab exposure associated with
higher ORR across the dose range of 3 to 100 mg, evaluated in studies 20160323 and 20200491.
The ORR appeared to plateau at doses of 10 mg and above. The secondary endpoints (DCR,
PFS, OS, BTSR) also demonstrated similar efficacy across the evaluated dose range of 3 to 100
mg. These analyses based on secondary endpoints are considered to be exploratory due to
difficulty of interpretation in the absence of an appropriate control arm. Except for the sum of
tumor diameter at baseline (BTSR) being identified as a covariate associated with higher hazard
for OS, no other covariates were identified to have a clinically meaningful impact on ORR, DCR,
PFS, OS and BTSR.

Overall, there are no clinically significant exposure-response relationships for efficacy over the
exposure range observed between 10 mg and 100 mg. This flat ER relationship for efficacy is
consistent with similar ORRs observed in 10 mg and 100 mg treatment arm in the pivotal trial.

19.4.4.2. ER (efficacy) Assessment Summary

The Applicant's Position:

Using pooled data across Studies 20160323 and 20200491, the relationship between
model-estimated averaged concentration over the first cycle and key efficacy endpoints were
explored. These analyses confirmed that the exposures associated with the proposed dosing
regimen of 10 mg Q2W maximizes the efficacy of tarlatamab.
