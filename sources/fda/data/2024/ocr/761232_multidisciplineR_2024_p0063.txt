BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — BLA 761232
TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

6.3.2.4 Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions, and what is the
appropriate management strategy?

Data:
No dedicated DDI study was conducted.

The Applicant’s Position:

A PK based drug-drug interaction of tislelizumab with other therapeutics is not anticipated given
that the primary elimination pathways are protein catabolism via the reticuloendothelial system
or target-mediated disposition. Tislelizumab is not expected to induce or inhibit the major drug
metabolizing CYP pathways (Evers et al 2013), as tislelizumab is neither a proinflammatory
cytokine nor a cytokine modulator. Therefore, no dedicated drug-drug interaction studies have
been conducted with tislelizumab as of the data cutoff date of the Study 302 clinical study report.

Tislelizumab is intended for intravenous infusion administration; therefore, food-drug
interactions are not expected.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position.
