8.2.5.3. Eosinophilia and Eosinophil-Related Disorders

Placebo-Controlled Induction Period (Weeks 0 to 16

During the placebo-controlled period, the frequency of subjects who reported eosinophilia
TEAEs was similar in the LEB 250 mg Q2W group (n = 5, 0.6%) and the placebo group (n = 3,
0.8%). All events were nonserious and did not lead to treatment discontinuation. There were
no reported events of eosinophil-related disorders.
