The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment of treatment compliance and concomitant
medications. A higher percentage of patients in the inavolisib treatment arm were initiated on
dexamethasone, paracetamol and metformin during the study, these treatments are reflective
of the toxicities observed more commonly on the inavolisib treatment arm.

Efficacy Results — Primary Endpoint (Including Sensitivity Analyses)

Data and the applicant’s position: The primary endpoint was met with a statistically significant
and clinically meaningful improvement in investigator-assessed PFS (Table 20 and Figure 7). The
results of the sensitivity analyses of BICR-assessed PFS (stratified HR = 0.50; 95% Cl: 0.36, 0.68),
investigator-assessed PFS in patients with centrally determined P/K3CA mutation-positive status
(stratified HR: 0.44; 95% Cl: 0.31, 0.62), and additional sensitivity analyses to assess the impact
of stratification on PFS (unstratified HR = 0.50; 95% Cl: 0.38, 0.67) and missing data (stratified
HR = 0.44; 95% Cl: 0.32, 0.60) were consistent with the primary analysis of
investigator-assessed PFS, demonstrating the robustness of investigator-assessed PFS results.
The PFS benefit by investigator-assessment per RECIST v1.1 favoring the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv
arm was generally consistent across pre-defined patient subgroups.
