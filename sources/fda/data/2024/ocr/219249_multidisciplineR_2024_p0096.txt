NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

Trial Location: The study was conducted at 123 centers across 28 globally distributed countries,
with a total of 325 patients enrolled from across Asia, Europe, Australia, North America, and
Central/South America. Among them, 16 patients were enrolled from 9 U.S. sites (see

Section 8.1.1 for further details).

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. The control arm treatment regimen of palbociclib
+ fulvestrant was an appropriate regiment for the first line treatment of patients with HR
positive/HER2 negative MBC when the INAVO120 trial was initiated in 2019. This combination
is still an appropriate option for first line treatment; however, based on results from the
MONALEESA-2 and MONARCH 3 trials, ribociclib and abemaciclib are now used more commonly
than palbociclib in the first line setting in the US.

Key Inclusion Criteria

e If female, patients had to meet at least one of the following definitions:

o Postmenopausal, as defined by criteria specified in the protocol

Oo Premenopausal or perimenopausal (i.e., not meeting the criteria for

= Treatment with luteinizing hormone—releasing hormone (LHRH) agonist

e Histologically or cytologically confirmed adenocarcinoma of the breast that was locally
advanced or metastatic and was not amenable to surgical or radiation therapy with curative
intent
e Documented estrogen receptor (ER)—positive and/or progesterone receptor (PR)—positive
tumor according to American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO)/College of American
Pathologists (CAP) guidelines
Documented HER2—negative tumor according to ASCO/CAP guidelines
Confirmation of biomarker eligibility: valid results from either central testing of blood or
local testing of blood or tumor tissue documenting P/K3CA-mutated tumor status
e Patients had to have progressed during adjuvant endocrine treatment or within 12 months
of completing adjuvant ET with an aromatase inhibitor or tamoxifen.

oO. Ifa CDK4/6 inhibitor was included as part of neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy,
progression event had to be > 12 months since completion of CDK4/6 inhibitor
portion of neoadjuvant or adjuvant therapy

e Measurable disease per Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1
