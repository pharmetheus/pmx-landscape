Immunogenicity: The immunogenicity profile of tislelizumab has been characterized in all
clinical studies using validated assays. Overall, a 16.5% incidence of treatment-emergent ADA
was observed (295 of 1785 evaluable patients). Neutralizing antibodies (Nabs) were detected in
11 (0.6%) patients. At the recommended dose of 200 mg once every three weeks, 16.3% (232 of
1424 evaluable patients) tested positive for treatment-emergent ADA. NAbs were detected in
0.8% (11 of 1424 evaluable patients). PopPK analysis showed that ADA status was a significant
covariate; however, based on the flat exposure-response relationships and the assessment of
overall clinical response and safety events, the presence of treatment-emergent ADA did not
impact PK, efficacy, or safety to a clinically meaningful extent.

The Applicant’s Position:

The clinical pharmacokinetics and immunogenicity profile of tislelizumab have been sufficiently
characterized and demonstrated that no dose modifications are needed based on age, weight,
tace, sex, hepatic impairment (mild to moderate), renal impairment (mild to moderate), and other
intrinsic or extrinsic factors.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. In the FDA’s assessment, analysis of
immunogenicity was limited to patients with ESCC because of the potential for differences in
immunogenicity across differences in tumor types. See Table 9 for highlights of FDA’s
assessment of the clinical pharmacology information.
