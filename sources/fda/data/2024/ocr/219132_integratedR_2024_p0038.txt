6.1.2. Dose Selection Rationale for Clinical Trials

The dose of levacetylleucine used in the clinical trials — approximately 0.1 g/kg/day — was
chosen based on extrapolation from experience with the racemate N-acetyl-DL-leucine and in
vivo studies in relevant animal models (including the NPC mouse model). The Applicant
claimed that this dose was supported by the exposure safety margins demonstrated in nonclinical
studies. The clinical dose selected was well-tolerated in the trials (i-e., a lower dose was not
needed due to any toxicity concerns). The Applicant claimed that long-term clinical data from
oral use of the racemate up to 8 g/day in subjects with NPC have shown the treatment to be safe
and well-tolerated. Therefore, the Applicant chose the dose of up to 4 g/day.
