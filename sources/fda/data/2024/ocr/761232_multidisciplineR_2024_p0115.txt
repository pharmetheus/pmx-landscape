FDA reviewed the Applicant’s presentation of PRO results in the RATIONALE-302 CSR. FDA
did not conduct independent analyses of the PRO endpoints. FDA does not agree with the
Applicant’s claim that treatment with tislelizumab was associated with a better overall HRQoL.
No conclusions can or should be drawn regarding HRQoL given that no alpha was allocated to
these endpoints and results are to be considered exploratory only. In addition, while FDA
acknowledges that the 10-point threshold used to define clinically meaningful worsening has
been cited in the Osoba et al. (1998) publication, FDA does not agree with this threshold as there
are several limitations to the approach that was used to derive it, and it has not been justified for
this specific disease setting. Results of exploratory time to deterioration endpoints should further
be interpreted with caution given these limitations. Comparative claims are unsupported.

The FDA’s Assessment:

As stated in Section 6, there are limited data to evaluate the efficacy of tislelizumab in patients
with ESCC at doses other than the studied in RATIONALE-302. Tislelizumab exposure-
response relationship for efficacy was inconclusive. The finding of effectiveness of tislelizumab
was supported by a favorable overall survival of tislelizumab monotherapy against ICC with a
stratified hazard ratio (HR) of 0.70 (95% CI 0.57, 0.85).

FDA’s assessment of durability of response is based on the exploratory analysis of DoR in all
