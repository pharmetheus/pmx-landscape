NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

SUMMARY AND CONCLUSIONS
8.3. Statistical Issues

The FDA’s Assessment:

There were no major statistical issues with this application. The INAVO120 study met its
primary endpoint of INV-assessed PFS, showing a statistically significant improvement in PFS
with the treatment of inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant. The results were generally
consistent across sensitivity analyses and subgroup analyses, except for Region, where the
difference in survival between arms is diminished in the North America/Western Europe region.
However, subgroup analyses should be interpreted with caution due to small sample sizes in
each subgroup. The interim OS analysis at the time of PFS analysis with 63% information
fraction was not statistically significant. Interim OS results showed a HR of 0.64 (95% Cl: 0.43,
0.97) with median OS not estimable (95% Cl: 27.3, NE) on the inavolisib + palbociclib +
fulvestrant arm and 31.1 months (95% Cl: 22.3, NE) on the placebo + palbociclib + fulvestrant
arm. Thus, there appears to be no detriment in OS at this time. The final analysis of OS is
expected to occur in the second quarter of 2025.

8.3.1. | Conclusions and Recommendations

The FDA’s Assessment:

INAVO120, a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind, multicenter trial in 325 patients
with endocrine resistant, PIK3CA mutated, HR-positive, HER2-negative (defined as IHC 0 or 1+,
or IHC 2+/ISH-), advanced or metastatic breast cancer, following recurrence on or after
completing adjuvant endocrine therapy and who have not received prior systemic therapy for
locally advanced or metastatic disease, demonstrated statistically significant and clinically
meaningful improvement in PFS by investigator which was supported by an interim OS that
showed no detriment. Inavolisib is associated with increased hyperglycemia, stomatitis, and
diarrhea compared to placebo and are included as Warnings and Precautions, as these were
considered severe, and occurred in a significant proportion of patients as noted in labeling.

The review team recommends granting regular approval to inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant
for the following indication:

Inavolisib (ITOVEBI), in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant, is indicated for the
treatment of adults with endocrine-resistant, PIK3CA-mutated, hormone receptor (HR)-positive,
human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative, locally advanced or metastatic
breast cancer, as detected by an FDA-approved test, following recurrence on or after completing
adjuvant endocrine therapy.
