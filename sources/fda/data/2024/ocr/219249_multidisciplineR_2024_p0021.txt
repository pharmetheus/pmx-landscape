G039374 is an ongoing, open-label,
multicenter, trial designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and PK of inavolisib administered
orally as a single agent in patients with locally advanced or metastatic PIK3CA-mutated solid
tumors, including breast cancer, and in combination with standard-of-care endocrine and/or
targeted therapies in patients with PIK3CA-mutated locally advanced and metastatic breast
cancer. The Applicant submitted data from 3 cohorts. Arm B (N=33; inavolisib in combination
with palbociclib and letrozole), Arm E (N=20; inavolisib in combination with palbociclib and
fulvestrant), and Arm F (N=21; inavolisib in combination with palbociclib, fulvestrant, and
metformin). Arms B, E and F did not require that patients have endocrine resistant breast
cancer. The confirmed ORR results in patients with measurable disease in Arm B (N=25), Arm E
(N=15), and Arm F (N=18) were 52% (95% Cl: 31%, 72%), 40% (95% Cl: 16%, 68%), and 17% (95%
Cl: 0%, 41%), respectively. While activity was observed across the three treatment arms based

Patients treated with inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant experienced more toxicities than
those treated with palbociclib + fulvestrant. The most common (2 20%) adverse reactions,
including laboratory abnormalities, were decreased neutrophils, decreased hemoglobin,
increased fasting glucose, decreased platelets, decreased lymphocytes, stomatitis, diarrhea,
decreased calcium, fatigue, decreased potassium, increased creatinine, increased ALT, nausea,
decreased sodium, decreased magnesium, rash, decreased appetite, COVID-19 infection, and
headache. A higher proportion of patients experienced serious adverse events (SAEs) in the
inavolisib arm (24.1% vs. 10.5%), Grade > 3 adverse events (86% vs. 82%), and dose
discontinuation (6% vs. 1%). Fatal adverse reactions occurred in 3.7% of patients receiving
inavolisib. Hyperglycemia, stomatitis, and diarrhea are included as Warnings and Precautions, as
these were considered severe, and occurred in a significant proportion of patients as noted in
labeling.
