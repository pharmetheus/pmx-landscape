19.4.3.4. Overall benefit-risk evaluation based on E-R analyses

The Applicant's Position: Exposure-efficacy analyses supported 9 mg QD inavolisib as an
efficacious dose in the context of the positive topline efficacy results for treatment
(Inavo+Palbo+Fulv) versus reference (Pbo+Palbo+Fulv) groups.

Inavolisib exposure was not significantly associated with ORR, BOR, DOR, CBR, PFS, or TFST
based on Study WO41554, after adjustment to baseline covariates, indicating that patients
benefited similarly across the range of observed exposures following 9 mg QD. For OS,
exposures of inavolisib achieved at the 9 mg QD were associated with similar effect on OS with
the exception of the upper quartile of Cmin (Q4) which showed a lower probability of survival.
This should be interpreted with caution; indeed, OS was not mature yet and could have been
impacted by treatments after study treatment discontinuation.

In Study WO41554, higher inavolisib exposure was associated with a higher risk for Grade > 2
anemia, but not with any of the other AE of interest (i.e., Grade > 2 hyperglycemia,
neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, diarrhea, stomatitis; any Grade 2 3 AE; inavolisib dose
modification due to AE), after adjustment to baseline covariates.
