The Applicant’s Position:

The totality of the safety package is sufficient to characterize the safety profile of tislelizumab
monotherapy and to guide the management of risks associated with tislelizumab as a treatment
for ESCC. The safety review is focused on patients who received the dose of 200 mg once every
3 weeks (Table 26) as recommended by the proposed label.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that the size of the safety database in this submission is adequate to characterize the
safety profile of tislelizumab for the treatment of ESCC. For pivotal Study 302, the safety
population consists of 255 patients who received tislelizumab 200 mg IV q3 weeks and 240
patients who received ICC. For the ISS, the safety population consists of 1,972 patients who
received tislelizumab across seven studies: Study 102, Study 203, Study 204, Study 208, Study
302, Study 303, and Study 001, as described in the Applicant’s table above. Although FDA’s
assessment of the safety of tislelizumab for the proposed indication is based on the overall safety
data submitted in this Application, the review is focused on the results of RATIONALE-302.

The Applicant included analyses of relatedness of AEs. Following standard FDA review
practices, FDA review did not include relatedness in the safety analyses as the safety profile is
best assessed through the comparison with the control arm. Thus, the FDA will not comment on
the Applicant’s analysis of “drug-related” TEAEs, except for Grade 5 TEAEs.

Overall Exposure

Data:

In Study 302, a near 2-fold longer median duration of exposure was observed in the Tislelizumab

proportion of patients who received > 6 months of exposure was higher in the Tislelizumab Arm
(25.5%) compared with the ICC Arm (8.8%). The median relative dose intensity in the
