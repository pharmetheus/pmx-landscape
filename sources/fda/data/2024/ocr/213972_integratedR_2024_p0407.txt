ADME

Study A8811006

Study A8811006 evaluated the metabolic profile, safety, tolerability, and routes of excretion of
sulopenem in 4 healthy adult male subjects following administration of a single 2000 mg oral
dose of [14C]sulopenem etzadroxil containing ~200 pCi of radiocarbon under fasted conditions.
Blood samples were collected pre-dose and at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 12, 24, 48, 72 and 96h
post-dosing into heparin-coated tubes for determination of plasma CP-70429 (sulopenem)
concentrations. At these same timepoints, blood samples were also obtained for measurement
of total plasma radioactivity. For profiling and identification of [**C]sulopenem etzadroxil
metabolites in plasma, blood samples were obtained pre-dose and at the 0.5, 1,3,6and12h
post-dosing.

Urine samples were collected pre-dose and pooled (per subject) at intervals of 0-8, 24-48, 48-
72, 72-96, 96-120, 120-144 and 144-168 h post-dosing for analysis of total radioactivity and
identification of metabolites. The daily urine collections were considered complete if the 24h
urinary excretion of creatinine was 20 to 25 mg/kg. A pre-dose fecal sample was obtained on
