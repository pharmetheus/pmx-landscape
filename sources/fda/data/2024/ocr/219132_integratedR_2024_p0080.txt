Abbreviation: fSARA = functional Scale for the Assessment and Rating of Ataxia

While there can be predominant visceral manifestations with very early symptom onset, when
symptoms begin in the late infantile period or afterward, they are predominantly
neurodegenerative. Because NPC is a genetic disease with the same underlying disease
pathophysiology across age groups, levacetylleucine is expected to exert its treatment effect on
the neurological symptoms of NPC similarly across age groups. Given the shared underlying
disease pathophysiology and primary manifestations of NPC in subjects <10 years old with those
>10 years of age, the review team determined that it would be appropriate to extrapolate the
efficacy data from subjects >10 years to the younger population from Trial IB1001-301 by
comparing PK data between subjects in these two age groups from Trials IB1001-201, IB1001-
202, and IB1001-301.
