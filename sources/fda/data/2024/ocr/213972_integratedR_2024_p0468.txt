QC Ranges for the Disk Diffusion Testing of Sulopenem Against Enterobacterales

A Tier 2 study was performed to establish the QC range for E. coli ATCC 25922 for sulopenem
using 2 pg disks by following the M23 guidelines ( | ITM-004-18). Two lots of sulopenem 2 pg
disks were used in this study, both manufactured by Liofilchem (Roseto delgli Abruzzi, Italy) and
three lots of Mueller-Hinton agar media were used in this study: BBL (Becton-Dickinson, Sparks,
MD), Hardy (Santa Maria, CA) and Remel (Lenexa, KS). Eight laboratories participated in the
study, and each site tested ten replicates over a three-day period according to the CLSI disk
diffusion method (CLSI MO2-A13).

Results were similar by disk lot (mean zones were 26.5 and 26.8 mm) and media (mean zones
were 26.3 to 27.0 mm). Disk diffusion susceptibility testing using a disk mass of 2 ug was
selected as the optimum disk mass for the establishment of QC ranges.
