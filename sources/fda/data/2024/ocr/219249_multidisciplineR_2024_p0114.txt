NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. Among patients achieving an objective response,
longer median DOR was observed in the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv arm, 18.4 months, compared with
9.6 months in the Pbo + Palbo + Fulv arm.

Persistence of Effect

Data: The investigator-assessed PFS event-free rate at 6 months, 12 months, and 18 months
demonstrated early and durable benefit as shown by consistently and substantially higher PFS
rates in the Inavot+Palbo+Fulv arm compared with the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm starting from the
first tumor assessment and throughout the follow-up. Further evidence of early onset and
persistence of efficacy is supported by the interim OS analysis, which although not statistically
significant, is highly supportive of the primary endpoint.

The Applicant's Position: Overall, the consistently prolonged PFS, OS and DOR data support that
inavolisib, combined with palbociclib and fulvestrant, is durable and persists after treatment.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

Efficacy Results — Secondary and exploratory COA (PRO) endpoints

Data: PRO analyses were conducted for the BPI-SF Worst Pain Severity item and the EORTC
QLQ-C30 PF, RF, and GHS/QoL scales. High compliance rates (>80%) for the BPI-SF Worst Pain
Severity item and EORTC QLQ-C30 were seen in both treatment arms at baseline and through
Cycle 15.

TTCD in Worst Pain Severity: Median TTCD in worst pain severity, as assessed by the BPI-SF
questionnaire worst pain severity item, was longer for the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv arm

(30.9 months) compared to the Pbo + Palbo + Fulv arm (18.1 months), indicating patients
receiving inavolisib experienced a longer duration of time without worsening pain severity.

TICD and Mean Change in Physical Functioning, Role Functioning and HRQoL: Median TTCD was
23.7 months and 22.7 months for physical functioning, 25.7 months and 24.2 months for role
functioning, and 29.0 months and 27.4 months for HRQoL, as assessed by the EORTC QLQ-C30
function and GHS/QoL scales, in the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv arm and Pbo + Palbo + Fulv arm,
respectively, indicating patients in both arms maintained their day-to-day functioning and
HRQoL for a similar duration of time while on treatment. Mean change scores of less than 10
points observed in these scales during treatment demonstrate that patients’ baseline levels of
functioning and HRQoL were maintained while on treatment in both arms.

The Applicant’s Position: These findings demonstrate that adding Inavo to Palbo and Fulv
prolongs time without clinically meaningful deterioration in pain severity and does not
adversely impact patients’ day-to-day functioning and HRQoL, further supporting a positive
benefit/risk profile for inavolisib.
