3) Limited Clinical Data at Lower Dosages

In stage 1 of Arm A (inavolisib monotherapy) in Study GO39374, patients received 6, 9, and 12
mg PO QD dosage levels. There were 2 dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) at the 12 mg PO QD dose
level and therefore 9 mg PO QD was identified as the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of
inavolisib monotherapy. Only 7 patients received a starting inavolisib monotherapy dosage of 6
mg PO QD and no patients received a starting inavolisib monotherapy dosage <6 mg PO QD
(Table 10). Multiple doublet and triplet combination regimens were subsequently studied in
stage 1 of Study GO39374. For inavolisib in combination with palbociclib and letrozole (Arm B),
there were three patients each who received inavolisib at 3 mg PO QD and 6 mg PO QD. In
total, there were only 20 patients across all regimens who received inavolisib starting dosages
lower than the proposed 9 mg PO QD dosage. Of note, all patients who received inavolisib in
combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant received the proposed 9 mg PO QD dosage.
