TRYVIO (aprocitentan)

Interindividual variability was modelled assuming a log-normal distribution for subject level
random effects. The interindividual variability was considered for total body clearance of drug
(CL), volume of distribution for central compartment (Vc), tlag, absorption rate constant (ka),
volume of distribution of peripheral compartment (Vp) and intercompartmental drug transfer

Q.

Intra-individual variability was tested as combined additive and proportional error model on the
dependent variable.

Model evaluation and selection were based on the point estimates of PK parameters, their
respective relative standard errors and standard statistical criteria of goodness-of-fit such as a
decrease in the minimum objective function value, successful model convergence, and diagnostic
prediction-corrected visual predictive check.

Covariate Analysis

Covariate parameters such as body weight, sex, age, formulation, race, etc. shown in Table 128,
were investigated.

Agency Assessment

Per FDA General Clinical Pharmacology Considerations for Pediatric Studies for Drugs and
Biological Product (Wang et al. 2012; September 2022), there are enough samples for subjects
in renal groups and hepatic groups to target a 95% CI [confidence interval] of the geometric
mean estimates of clearance and volume of distribution in each subgroup with at least 80%
power.
