BLA 761306 Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Ebglyss (lebrikizumab) injection

Table 9295. Summary of PopPK analyses

General Information

Objectives of PopPK analysis

To characterize the pharmacokinetics (PK) of lebrikizumab in adult
and adolescent participants with atopic dermatitis (AD) and healthy
participants

¢ To identify patient factors and laboratory parameters that may
influence lebrikizumab disposition in this population, and

© To derive individual PK parameters/exposure metrics for the future
assessment of exposure-response relationships.

Study and population included

The PopPK analysis dataset contained data from 11 studies: 4 were in healthy
participants and 7 were in participants with AD. The dataset includes a total
of 1607 subjects from phase 1, 2, and 3 studies. Both adults and adolescents
(12 to <18 years, >40 kg) were included in the phase 3 studies and included in
the analysis. See Table 93 for details.

Dose(s) included

Single SC doses: 37.5 — 375 mg
Single IV or SC dose: 1 mg/kg
Multiple SC dose: 250 mg Q2W or Q4W preceded by 250— 500 mg loading
doses.
