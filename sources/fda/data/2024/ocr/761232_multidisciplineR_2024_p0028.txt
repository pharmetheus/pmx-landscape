3. Regulatory Background

3.1. U.S. Regulatory Actions and Marketing History

The Applicant’s Position:

Currently, tislelizumab is not approved or marketed in the US. Tislelizumab has received the
following marketing authorization approvals in China:

27 Dec 2019: Treatment of patients with relapsed or refractory classical Hodgkin’s
lymphoma who received at least two prior lines of systemic
chemotherapy regimens.

10 Apr 2020: Treatment of patients with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial
carcinoma with PD-L1 high expression and whose disease progressed
during or following platinum-containing chemotherapy or within
12 months of neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment with platinum-
containing chemotherapy.

13 Jan 2021: Treatment in combination with two chemotherapy regimens as a first-
line treatment for patients with advanced squamous non-small cell lung
cancer (NSCLC).

23 Jun 2021: First-line treatment of patients with advanced non-squamous NSCLC in

combination with chemotherapy.

23 Jun 2021: Conditional approval for the treatment of patients with hepatocellular
carcinoma (HCC) who have received at least one systemic therapy.

The clinical development program for tislelizumab is being conducted under Investigational New
Drug (IND) 126875 with Division of Oncology Products 2, and the relevant regulatory history is
included in Section 1.

The FDA’s Assessment:
In addition to the parent IND 126875, the clinical development for tislelizumab for the treatment
of ESCC is under IND 135699 with the Division of Oncology 3.
