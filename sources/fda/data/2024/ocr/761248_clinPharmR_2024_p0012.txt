Participants who achieved amyloid plaque reduction (<11 centiloid at one scan or
between 11 to 24.1 centiloid in successive scans) based on amyloid PET scans at Week
24 or Week 52 were switched to receive a placebo for the remainder of the double-blind
period (Table 1). Approximately 74% (321/437) of the low/medium tau population and
69% (429/620) of the combined population met the criteria for placebo reduction at week
76, based on amyloid PET levels.
