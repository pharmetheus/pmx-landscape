Data Quality and Integrity

Data and the Applicant’s Position: Information requested by the Office of Scientific
Investigations (OSI) is provided in Module 5.3.5.4. No issues were identified with the data
quality or integrity which could affect the efficacy results.

A comprehensive assessment of the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic was conducted looking
at planned study procedures, data collection, and data analyses. Overall, the impact of the
COVID-19 pandemic for these studies was minor and there was no significant impact on the
study results and the overall conclusions. Similarly, an assessment on the impact of the
Russia-Ukraine conflict on Study WO41554 was also performed and assessed as minor. For
Study GO39374, there were no patients enrolled at investigational sites in Russia or Ukraine;
therefore, an assessment of the Russia-Ukraine conflict was not applicable for this study.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Refer to Section 4.1 of this Assessment Aid regarding details for OSI inspections conducted by
FDA. FDA OSI inspected 3 clinical sites for INAVO120 (site codes 323897, 323629, and 323146).
INV-assessed PFS, OS, and BICR-assessed PFS were evaluated excluding these study sites to
assess any possible effect on HR. Overall, the results were consistent with those of the ITT
population. No action was indicated for the sites.
