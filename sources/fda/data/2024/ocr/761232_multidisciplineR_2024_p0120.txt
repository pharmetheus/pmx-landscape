BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

The efficacy results based on secondary endpoints from Studies 001 and 102 provided supportive
evidence for the efficacy of tislelizumab monotherapy in patients with advanced or metastatic
ESCC who had been heavily treated previously.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA acknowledges the applicant’s presentation of results for secondary endpoints for studies
101 and 102. FDA did not independently verify the results since these are supportive and
considered exploratory. Furthermore, time to event endpoints like OS, PFS, and DCR results
from single arm trials are not interpretable.

Subpopulations

Data:
Subgroup analyses were not performed across studies as Studies 001 and 102 were single arm
studies.

8.1.5. Integrated Assessment of Effectiveness

Data:

As a global multicenter study, Study 302 was designed in accordance with ICH guidelines
(including ICH-E17) and international treatment guidelines for patients with ESCC. Key
parameters of the study (ie, patient population, stratification factors, choice of comparator,
primary endpoint, statistical analysis plan, and safety management plan) were designed in
consultation with the US FDA, the European Medicines Agency (EMA), the Center for Drug
Evaluation of the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA), and the Pharmaceuticals
and Medical Devices Agency (PMDA).
