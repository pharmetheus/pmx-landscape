BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — BLA 761232
TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA generally agrees.

. The T-cell dependent antibody response to keyhole limpet hemocyanin (KLH)
antigen was assessed in a human PD-1 transgenic mouse model. All mice produced a
primary and secondary humoral anti-KLH IgG response after immunization with KLH on
Days 2 and 23. Tislelizumab (10 mg/kg) was intraperitoneally administrated on Days 1,
8, and 15 to assess the effect on the primary TDAR, and on Days 22, 29, and 36 to assess
the effect on the recall TDAR. Tislelizumab did not induce significant changes in the
primary or secondary anti-KLH responses relative to control (Study BGB-A317-IVP-
Tox-OT-0001).

. Cytokine release responses in human whole blood cells did not identify a safety
signal (Studies: CRAA317- 1; CRA-A317-2)
