responses were observed across subgroups of patients

the proposed dosage in Study 20200491, 69 patients had
available data regarding platinum sensitivity. In this
subgroup of patients, ORR was 31% (95% Cl: 18%, 47%) in
42 patients with platinum-sensitive SCLC and 52% (95% Cl:
32%, 71%) in 27 patients with platinum-resistant SCLC.

FDA considers response rate supported by DoR an
evaluable endpoint for SCLC because it is a direct measure
of the intervention; in the absence of therapy, tumors do
not typically regress on their own. With a favorable risk-
benefit profile in the context of a life-threatening disease
with an unmet medical need, the submitted evidence
meets the statutory evidentiary standard for accelerated
approval.

Continued approval for this indication may be contingent
upon verification and description of clinical benefit in a
confirmatory trial, conducted as a PMR. The Applicant
proposes to verify the clinical benefit of tarlatamab in the
treatment of patients with SCLC who have disease
progression on or after prior platinum-containing therapy,
based on the results of an ongoing, randomized controlled
trial, DeLLphi-304 (Study 20210004), comparing
