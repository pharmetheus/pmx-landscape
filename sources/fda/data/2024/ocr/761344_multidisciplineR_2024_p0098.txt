Cytokine levels
FDA assessed of the potential for drug interactions between tarlatamab and CYP

substrates and considered this risk is low. In Study 2020049, cytokine levels were
transiently elevated and mostly peaked around 20-24 hours after step-up dose (1 mg)
on Cycle 1 Day 1 and returning to baseline around the next target dose (10 mg) on Cycle
1 Day 8 (Figure 8 and Table 18). The concentrations of IL-6 for the total samples,
samples without tocilizumab and samples with tocilizumab were summarized in

Table 18.

Prior literature and validated physiologically based pharmacokinetic model (PBPK)
derived the EC50 values for IL-6 on CY3A4 and CYP3A5 to be 75.2 pg/mL and 51 pg/mL,
respectively (Jiang 2016). The median and mean value of peak IL-6 concentrations are
26 pg/mL (Table 18). This peak concentration did not exceed the literature reported
EC50 of IL-6, suggesting a clinically relevant interaction between tarlatamab and CYP3A
substrates is not expected, and the mean value of IL-6 was lower for tarlatamab than
other bispecific drugs including elranatamab and epcoritamab (Table 19). Based on the
predicted low drug interaction risk in the general patient population, the information is
not included in Section 7 of the USPI.
