NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

The addition of inavolisib to palbociclib and fulvestrant resulted in a statistically significant and
clinically meaningful improvement in PFS based on results from INAVO120. Results for
secondary endpoints were supportive of the primary endpoint. However, racial and ethnic
minorities reflective of the U.S. population of patients with breast cancer were significantly
underrepresented in INAVO120. INAVO120 was a multinational trial with just 16 patients
(4.9%) enrolled from the US. Almost all patients were female (98%) and the median age of
enrolled patients was 54 years. The majority of patients on study were White, with only two
Black patients and 20 Hispanic/Latino patients enrolled on study. Due to the lack of diversity in
the enrolled population, a Post Marketing Commitment (PMC) is being issued to further
characterize the efficacy and safety of inavolisib with palbociclib and fulvestrant for the
treatment of breast cancer in study populations whose demographics are more representative
of the population of U.S. patients for the proposed indication.

8.1.4. Assessment of Efficacy Across Trials

Data and the Applicant’s Position: Because the study designs, patient populations, and the
combination regimens differed between Studies WO41554 and GO39374, the efficacy data are
presented for each study separately, no pooling of efficacy data between the two studies was
conducted.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

8.1.5. | Integrated Assessment of Effectiveness

Data: See Section 8.1.1 Pivotal Study: WO41554 (INAVO120), Phase III and Supportive Study:
GO39374, Phase | for efficacy data.

The Applicant's Position:
Pivotal Study WO41554: Study WO41554 met its primary endpoint. The addition of inavolisib to

the standard of care regimen of palbociclib and fulvestrant reduced the risk of disease
progression or death by 57% and more than doubled the median PFS (15.0 months in the
Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm vs. 7.3 months Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm). The results of the sensitivity
analysis of BICR-assessed PFS, the sensitivity analysis of investigator-assessed PFS in patients
with centrally determined P/K3CA mutation-positive status, and additional sensitivity analyses
to assess the impact of stratification and missing data on PFS, were consistent with the primary
analysis, and thus overall support the robustness of the observed PFS benefit of
Inavo+Palbo+Fulv over Pbo+Palbo+Fulv. The PFS benefit by investigator-assessment favoring
the Inavo+Palbo+Fulv arm was generally consistent across pre-defined subgroups.
