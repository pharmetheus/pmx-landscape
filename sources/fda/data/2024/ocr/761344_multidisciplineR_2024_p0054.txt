Source: Module 2.6.2 Pharmacology Written Summary (or Module 4.2.1.1 Study 122717)

Activated cytotoxic T cells secrete inflammatory cytokines following TCR engagement. The
ability of tarlatamab to promote cytokine release was investigated in co-cultures of human
peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMC) with DLL3-expressing target cells (Study Number:
122717/ eCTD Location: Module 4.2.1.1). Human T cell-induced target cell killing was
accompanied by the dose-dependent release of interferon gamma (IFN-7), interleukin (IL)-6,
IL-10, tumor necrosis factor (TNF) as well as IL-2 in the presence of DLL3-positive SHP-77 cells
(Figure 2B). Cytokine release was highly specific for the co-cultures that contained DLL3-
expressing cells, as no cytokines were detected in co-cultures of human effector cells with
DLL3-negative HCT-116 cells after exposure to tarlatamab (Figure 2A; Study 122717).
