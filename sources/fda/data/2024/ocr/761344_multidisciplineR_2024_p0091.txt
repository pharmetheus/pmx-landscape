3) The E-R safety analyses did not identify major safety concerns with the
proposed dosage. Positive E-R for safety trends were observed for any
grade neutropenia, grade 1 and grade 2 neurotoxicity including ICANs,
supporting the lower dosage 1/10 mg. A positive trend was also
observed for Grade > 1 CRS and tarlatamab exposure; however, this
trend is not conclusive and may be confounded by the effect of step-up
dose to subsequent treatment doses. No trends of E-R for safety were
identified for Grade 3 AEs, Grade 3 TEAEs, or Grade 22 CRS. There were
limited data for other AEs. Refer to Appendix 19.4.4 for detailed E-R for
safety analyses.

Efficacy and safety data from Study 20200491 together with the E-R analyses for safety
and efficacy support the recommended dosage 1/10 mg in patient with SCLC.
