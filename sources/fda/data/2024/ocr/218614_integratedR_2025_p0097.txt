Since the review team determined that the olezarsen 80 mg dose would be approved, it was
decided © the AP incidence for olezarsen 80 mg would be listed in the label. Specifically,
the numerical incidence of AP in subjects treated with olezarsen 80 mg (n=1, 5%) was lower
than placebo (n=7, 30%).

Conclusion

Yes, the secondary endpoint of reduction in the rate of AP is acceptable and clinically
meaningful and therefore can be described in the olezarsen labeling.

Because of the failure of efficacy in TG reduction of olezarsen 50 mg dose (co-primary
endpoint), all following endpoints, including AP did not meet statistical significance but were
considered to be of nominal significance. However, the reduction in the AP rate with olezarsen
80 mg can be considered clinically significant.
