NDA 217686
TRYVIO (aprocitentan)

13.1.3.4. Reproductive and Developmental Toxicity

The Applicant submitted a fertility and early embryonic development study (Segment I) with
aprocitentan alone and referred to NDA 204410 (Opsumit) for embryo-fetal development (EFD)
studies (Segment II) and pre- and postnatal developmental (PPND) study with macitentan. This
was acceptable because aprocitentan represented >50% of the combined exposure to macitentan
and aprocitentan in rats and rabbits, and the plan was agreed upon with the FDA prior to NDA
submission. The referenced EFD and PPND studies were reviewed previously under NDA
204410.

During the NDA review cycle, the Applicant submitted additional EFD studies with aprocitentan
alone to support their proposed © REMS. While the Agency was not
obligated to accept those data for review during the NDA review cycle, the data were
nonetheless reviewed. The adequacy of the submitted information to address Agency concerns
for embryo-fetal risk is discussed in Section 13.2.1. The general conclusion was that the data

compared to the other members of the endothelin receptor
antagonist (ERA) class, all of which demonstrate teratogenicity at clinically relevant exposures
in rats due to a shared mechanism of action.

13.1.3.4.1. Fertility and Early Embryonic Development

Study Number/Title: T-16-001/ACT-132577E — Fertility Toxicity Study by the Oral Route
(Gavage) in the Male Rat (Segment I)

Key Study Findings

¢ No aprocitentan-related effects on male gonadal function, mating behavior or virility were
observed based on reproductive indices and sperm parameters.

e Minimal dilation of the seminiferous tubules in the testes was observed in 1/22 rats at dose of
50 mg/kg/day and 4/22 rats at dose of 250 mg/kg/day.

e Dose-dependent enlarged liver and increased liver weight with associated centrilobular
hepatocellular hypertrophy was observed with increasing severity. Hepatocellular vacuolar
degeneration/necrosis (minimal to slight) of hypertrophic hepatocytes was observed at
250 mg/kg/day. A resultant dose-dependent minimal and/or slight follicular cell hypertrophy
in the thyroid glands was observed, which represents a rat-specific response to increased
turnover of thyroid hormone in the liver.

e The no observed effect level for male fertility was 250 mg.kg/day, based on lack of effect on
gonadal function, mating behavior and reproductive performance. The NOAEL for general
toxicity was determined at 50 mg/kg/day, based on hepatocellular vacuolar
degeneration/necrosis at 250 mg/kg/day, which was associated with AUC .24, values of
468 j1g-hr/mL in male rats, approximately 13-fold the AUC at the MRHD.
