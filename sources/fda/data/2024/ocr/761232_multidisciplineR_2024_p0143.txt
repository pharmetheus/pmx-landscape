FDA’s analysis concurs with that of the Applicant regarding the SAEs that occurred at >= 1% in
either arm with the following notes and/or exceptions below:

e Grouping terms for esophageal fistulas (including esophagomediastinal,
tracheoesophageal, and esophagobronchial), incidence for this event is 2.3% and 2.5% in
the tislelizumab and ICC arms, respectively. Given the nature of ESCC, esophageal
fistulas are an important AE to monitor for, regardless of treatment modality.

e FDA combined diarrhea and colitis into one grouped-term (GT) and calculated the
incidence of this event as 0.4% and 3.8% in the tislelizumab and ICC amms respectively.

e FDA combined aspiration pneumonia with pneumonia to calculated the incidence as 9%
and 8% in the tislelizumab and ICC arms, respectively.

e FDA combined hemorrhage terms to calculate the incidence as 4.3% and 4.2% in the
tislelizumab and ICC arms, respectively.

e FDA combined musculoskeletal pain terms to calculate the incidence as 1.2% and 0.4%
in the tislelizumab and ICC arms, respectively.

The SAEs noted above are generally consistent with ESCC or are often associated with cytotoxic
chemotherapy (e.g. diarrhea, nausea, febrile neutropenia, decreased neutrophil count, and
decreased white blood cell count). For both the tislelizumab arm and the ICC arm, pneumonia
was the most frequently reported SAE, as noted by the Applicant, above.
