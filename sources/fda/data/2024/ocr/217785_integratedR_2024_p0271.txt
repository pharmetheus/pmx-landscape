13.1.9.2.3. An Oral (Gavage) Study of the Effects of MGL-3623
on Embryo/Fetal Development With a Toxicokinetic
Phase in Rats, Study 3196-17-019

Key Study Findings

e Administration of MGL-3623 (a major metabolite in humans, also referred to as MGL-3196-
M1, VIA-3196-M1, or M1) had no effects on BW, BW gain, or food consumption.

e¢ MGL-3623 had no effects on post-implantation loss, number or percentage of viable fetuses,
mean fetal BW, or fetal sex ratio.

e No test article-related malformations or variations were observed in the visceral and skeletal
examinations.

e MGL-3623 at 3 and 100 mg/kg/day produced a significant decrease in free T3 (-10.5% for
both doses), with no dose-dependent effect. A significant decrease in TSH occurred at
3 mg/kg/day (-28.8%).

e Systemic exposure to MGL-3623 increased with dose level. There was no accumulation in
plasma following repeated doses.
