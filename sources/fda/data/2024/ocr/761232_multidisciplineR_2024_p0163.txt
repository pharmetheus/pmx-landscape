Immunogenicity

Data:

Monitoring of ADA to tislelizumab and titer determination for confirmed positive ADA samples
have been performed in all clinical studies included in this submission. NAbs were evaluated in
the confirmed positive ADA samples.

In Study 302, 14.5% (32/221) of evaluable patients tested positive for treatment-emergent ADA.
NAbs were detected in 0.5% (1/221) of patients. Exploratory analyses of clinical endpoints in
Study 302 showed that the patients who were treatment-emergent ADA-positive had comparable
OS to patients who were treatment-emergent ADA-negative, with an HR of 1.16 (95% CI: 0.77,
1.75). In addition, numerically higher ORR (18.8% versus 16.4%) and disease control rate
(56.3% versus 51.3%) were noted in the treatment-emergent ADA-positive group than those of
the ADA negative group, respectively. However, these minor numerical differences are not
considered to be clinically relevant. Overall, comparable safety profiles (incidence rate of
imAEs, AESIs, and AEs leading to dose modification or discontinuation) were observed, except
patients who were treatment-emergent ADA-positive had a slightly lower rate of safety events of
> Grade 3 AEs than ADA negative patients (62.5% versus 39.2%, respectively). These trends
were not seen when larger sample size from additional studies were compared.

Summary of immunogenicity from seven clinical studies (001, 102, 203, 204, 208, 302, and 303)
with total 1785 ADA evaluable patients indicated that 16.5% (295/1785) and 0.6% (11/1785)
incidence of treatment-emergent ADA and NAbs was observed, respectively, across all dose
regimens (0.5 to 10 mg/kg once every 2 weeks, 2.0 or 5.0 mg/kg once every 3 weeks, or 200 mg
once every 3 weeks). Of the recommended dose of 200 mg once every 3 weeks across all the
aforementioned studies, 16.3% (232/1424) patients had treatment-emergent ADA and NAbs
were detected in 0.8% (11/1424) patients. Population PK analysis as described in Section 6.3.1
showed that, although treatment-emergent ADA status was a significant covariate, the presence
of treatment-emergent ADA did not impact PK, efficacy, or safety to a clinically meaningful
extent.
