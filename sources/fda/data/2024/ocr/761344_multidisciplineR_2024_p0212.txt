The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA performed an independent analysis of the incidence of SAEs in the pooled safety
population of patients who received tarlatamab at the recommended dose of 10 mg in Studies
20200491 and 20160323. Refer to Table 50 for FDA’s analysis of SAEs in the pooled safety
population. SAEs occurred in 108 patients (58%). The most frequently reported SAEs (> 3% of
patients) were cytokine release syndrome (24%), pneumonia (6%), pyrexia (3.7%), and

hyponatremia 3.7%.
