Agency Assessment

The reviewer independently assessed covariates of the final model by Monolix (version 2021R1).
HI and eGFR were not identified as significant covariates, which implies that dose adjustment
for moderate hepatic impairment and renal impairment are not required. In contrast,
formulation (capsule, tablet A, tablet B) was identified as a significant covariate as shown in
Table 130. The optimized model parameter estimates are shown in Table 131. Model parameter
estimates based on this model were, as a whole, similar to those obtained in the Applicant’ final
model. Therefore, the Applicant’s final model is considered acceptable.
