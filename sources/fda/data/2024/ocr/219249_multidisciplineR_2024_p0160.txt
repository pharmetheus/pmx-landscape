In addition, the Applicant further explored safety and tolerability through assessment of
patient-generated data. A list of expected symptomatic adverse events from the combination
of inavolisib, palbociclib, and fulvestrant (diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, fatigue, mouth sores,
decreased appetite, and rash), was selected from the patient reported outcome (PRO)-CTCAE
item library, along with an additional single item assessing bother due to side-effects of
treatment. These PRO data were assessed appropriately with acceptable data quality. Analysis
of patient-reported symptoms that worsened from baseline were generally observed to be
worse in the inavolisib arm, with the greatest difference observed for diarrhea, mouth sores,
decreased appetite, and rash. The patient-reported data provide additional insight into the
safety and tolerability of the approved regimen and were included in section 6 of the USPI to
further inform patients and providers.
