density (a g/mL). Then the potential exposure (ug/dose) was determined by multiplying the
leachable concentrations (g/mL) by the maximum DS dose volume (4 mL). For human health
risk characterization, each PDE is divided by the estimated potential exposure to determine the
margin of safety (MOS). A MOS >1 is considered acceptable.
