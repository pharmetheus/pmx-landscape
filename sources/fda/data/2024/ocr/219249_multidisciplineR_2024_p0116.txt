Demographic Characteristics

Data: All patients enrolled in Arm B (N = 33), Arm E (N = 20), and Arm F (N = 21) were female
(Table 24). The median age of patients was 57 years (range: 37-80 years) in Arm B, 55 years
(range: 33-73 years) in Arm E, and 65 years (range: 33-77 years) in Arm F. Half of the patients in
Arm E (50%) and the majority of patients in Arm B (78.8%) and Arm F (76.2%) had self-reported
race recorded as White. The remaining patients in each arm self-reported as either Asian, Black
or African American or were of the race category ‘unknown’. All patients in Arm B, Arm E, and
Arm F had a baseline Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) status of either 0 or 1. A high
proportion of patients in Arm F had a baseline HbAic = 5.7% (10 patients; 50.0%) and

BMI > 30 kg/m? (15 patients; 75.0%) reflecting the inclusion criteria for enrollment in Arm F
(patients needed to meet at least one of these 2 eligibility criteria, which are considered risk
factors for hyperglycemia).

The overall population of GO39374 comprised of mostly female patients (99.5%) with breast
cancer and 1 male patient (0.5%) with colorectal cancer (CRC) (in Stage | Arm A), was
predominately self-reported as White (72.8%), and had a median age of 59 years (range:
31-85 years).
