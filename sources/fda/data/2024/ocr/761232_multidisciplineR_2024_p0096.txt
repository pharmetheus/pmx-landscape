In the 3 PD-L1 subgroups, patient-reported disease history was similar between all 3 subgroups
and between the 2 arms within each PD-L1 subgroup. The disease history profile was balanced
between the Asia and the Europe/North America subgroups, and between the 2 arms within each
region.

Prior Anticancer Systemic Therapies:

Nearly all patients had received 1 prior line of systemic therapy, with 2 patients (1 patient in each
arm) whose prior systemic anticancer therapy did not meet the definition of a line of systemic
therapy per protocol. Both cases were identified as important protocol deviations. .

Among the 510 patients who received 1 prior line of systemic treatment per protocol, 16.2%
received neoadjuvant/adjuvant treatment as first-line systemic therapy (tislelizumab: 19.9%;
ICC: 12.5%) and 11.3% received definitive chemoradiotherapy (tislelizumab: 10.2%; ICC:
12.5%) as first-line systemic therapy.

The Applicant’s Position:

Overall, comorbidities at baseline, disease history, and prior anticancer systemic therapies were
similar between the 2 arms, and representative of the target population. Together with the
demographic characteristics, patients characteristics in study 302 were similar to those of
patients in recently completed studies, that enrolled patients with ESCC for second-line
treatment (Kojima et al 2020; Kato et al 2019; Huang et al 2020), with a few minor variations
due to the difference in the regional distribution and the composition of histological types of
tumors in the enrolled populations.

Baseline characteristics (including prior anticancer therapies) had few regional variations.
Compared with patients in the Asia subgroup, the Europe/North America subgroup had a lower
proportion of patients who had undergone prior surgery for ESCC, and a higher proportion of
patients who had received prior definitive chemoradiotherapy. The regional variations were
similar with that observed in a retrospective study comparing clinicopathologic features of
Eastern and Western populations (Zhang et al 2015).
