PK Sampling and Assessments

Plasma PK samples were collected on Day 1 at pre-dose and at pre-specified times through 12 h
post-dosing. The following plasma PK parameters of sulopenem were calculated: Crax Tmax
AUC tasts AUC infp AUCextrap» Vz, V2/F, clearance (CL), and ty). Plasma samples were assayed for
sulopenem and formate using validated LC/MS/MS methods. Whole blood samples were also
assayed for PF-03678685 and PF-03709270 (sulopenem etzadroxil) using a validated LC-MS/MS
method.

The PK population was comprised of subjects who received any study drug and had sufficient
data to calculate at least 1 pre-planned PK parameter. Absolute bioavailability was calculated
for both the bilayer tablet and PIB under fasting conditions using 366 mg IV as reference. The
effect of food on the PK of sulopenem was also determined using a paired t-test on the natural-
log transformed C,,.xand AUCs of the fed versus fasted state. Similarly, for Cohorts 2 and 4,
paired t-test on the log transformed C,,,,, and AUCs was used for comparison of Periods 1 and 2.

Demographics

Forty-six healthy male and female subjects with mean age 35.8 years and BMI of 25.76 kg/m?
were enrolled in this study. The majority of the subjects (87%) were male and Black (67.4%). No
previous or concomitant medications were recorded for any subjects in this study. Although the
study’s inclusion criteria indicated that subjects with estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR)
285mL/min/1.73m? were to be enrolled in the study, three subjects in Cohort 1, one subject in
Cohort 2 and one subject in Cohort 3 with eGFR values in the range of 75 mL/min/1.73m? to 83
mL/min/1.73 m2 participated in the study.

Reviewer’s Comment: Of the five subjects indicated as having an eGFR in the range of 75
mL/min/1.73m2 to 83 mL/min/1.73 m2 under protocol deviations, individualized eGFR values
ranged from 94 mL/min to 113 mL/min. Individualization of eGFRs for all other subjects enrolled
in this study identified only two subjects (both in Cohort 1) whose eGFR fell within the range of
83 to 88 mL/min and would thus be categorized as having mild renal impairment according to
