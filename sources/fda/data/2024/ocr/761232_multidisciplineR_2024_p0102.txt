Subgroup Analyses for Primary Efficacy Endpoint

Overall, the benefit of tislelizumab in improving OS, when compared with ICC, was observed
across all the prespecified subgroups (Figure 13). For all prespecified subgroups, including
baseline PD-L1 expression status, age, gender, smoking status, ICC option, ECOG PS score,
region, and race, the unstratified HR favored the Tislelizumab Arm (ie, HR < 1).

The OS treatment effect estimated within both the Asia subgroup and Europe/North America
subgroup clearly favored the Tislelizumab Arm. In the Asia subgroup, the median OS was 8.5
months (95% CI: 7.1 to 10.3 months) for the Tislelizumab Arm and 6.3 months (95% CI: 5.3 to
7.4 months) for the ICC Amn, and the HR was 0.73 (95% CI: 0.59 to 0.90). In the Europe/North
America subgroup, the median OS was 11.2 months (95% CI: 5.9 to 14.8 months) for the
Tislelizumab Arm and 6.3 months (95% CI: 4.6 to 7.7 months) for the ICC Arm, and the HR was
0.55 (95% CI: 0.35 to 0.87).
