Assessment of Cases in the Cholestatic Quadrant of the Hepatocellular DILI Screen

In addition to evaluation of cases in the potential Hy’s law cases and Temple’s corollary,
narratives for cases in the cholestatic quadrant of the hepatocellular DILI screen were reviewed.
The majority of elevations of TB in this quadrant were attributable to Gilbert’s syndrome. None
of the cases were considered likely related to resmetirom.
