The Applicant’s Position:

Study 302 was conducted in accordance with international clinical research guidelines (NCCN
2017). The data cutoff occurred at the pre-determined point of at least 400 deaths. The study
met its accrual target, by accumulating sufficient death events (ie 410) to allow endpoint
assessment.

Nearly all patients were no longer receiving treatment as of the data cutoff. A higher proportion
of patients in the Tislelizumab Arm discontinued the treatment due to radiographic or clinical
progression than in the ICC Arm, due at least in part to the higher proportion of patients in the
ICC Arm who discontinued for an AE or voluntarily withdrew from the treatment. The primary
reason for study discontinuation in both arms was death, the primary endpoint. More patients on
the Tislelizumab Arm remained on study as of the data cutoff, consistent with the treatment
effect observed. More patients in the ICC arm discontinued the study before their first dose of
study treatment, mainly due to patient decision, suggesting a desire for treatment other than the
ICC to which they were assigned.
