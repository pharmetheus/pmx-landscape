The Applicant's Position: Secondary endpoint analyses of OS, ORR, BOR, CBR rate, and DOR
consistently showed a clinically meaningful improvement in Inavo+Palbo+Fulv treated patients
over the Pbo+Palbo+Fulv arm.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the efficacy results for the secondary endpoints of OS, ORR, BOR,
DOR, and CBR as presented by the applicant above. However, FDA notes that CBR is not an
endpoint evaluated for regulatory decision-making.

FDA also notes that, since the interim analysis of OS at the time of PFS analysis was not
statistically significant, none of the remaining secondary endpoints in the pre-specified testing
hierarchy were formally tested at this time and results should be considered descriptive. The
final analysis of OS is expected in the second quarter of 2025.
