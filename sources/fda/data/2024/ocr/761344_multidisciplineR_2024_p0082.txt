6.2. Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. | Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

In Study 20160323 and Study 2020491, tarlatamab exhibited approximate
dose-proportional increases in exposures in the evaluated dose range of 0.003 to

100 mg IV administered Q2W and 200 mg Q3W. The steady state in tarlatamab serum
concentrations were approximately achieved by the start of cycle 2. The mean (SD)
estimated terminal phase elimination half-life at steady state was 5.5 (1.6) days across
the evaluated dose range.

Following the proposed dosing regimen of 1 mg on day 1 and 10 mg on day 8, day 15,
and Q2W thereafter, tarlatamab geometric mean trough values at steady state (cycle 2
day 15 predose) and peak serum concentrations at steady state (cycle 2 day 15, end of
infusion) were 0.44 and 4.3 mg/mL, respectively, based on pooled data across phase 1
and phase 2 studies. There was no indication of time dependent kinetics.

Based on population PK analyses, tarlatamab serum clearance, central volume of
distribution, peripheral volume of distribution, and volume of distribution at steady
state were estimated to be 0.65 L/day, 3.44 L, 5.06 L, and 8.5 L, respectively for a typical
73 kg White individual. The inter-individual variability for tarlatamab clearance and
central volume of distribution were 43.7% and 38.7%, respectively.

At the proposed dosing regimen of 10 mg Q2W, the incidence of treatment-emergent
antitarlatamab antibodies was 12% (3 of 25 subjects) for the first-in-human (FIH)
Study 20160323 and 3.2% (4 of 124 subjects) for the pivotal phase 2 Study 20200491,
for a pooled total of 7 of 149 subjects (4.7%) across the 2 studies. In the pivotal phase 2
