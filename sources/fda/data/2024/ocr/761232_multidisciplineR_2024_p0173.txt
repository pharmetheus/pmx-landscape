TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

The Applicant’s Position:

Neither the effects of overdose of tislelizumab nor an antidote to overdose are known. The
potential for tislelizumab drug abuse and dependence is unknown.

The FDA’s Assessment:
Based on its pharmacological properties, there are no concerns regarding the potential for abuse,
withdrawal, or rebound with tislelizumab.

The Applicant’s Position:

The data available to BeiGene have been analyzed to provide a critical analysis of the benefit-

emerged during the relevant reporting period (26 December 2019 to 25 December 2020). The
