19.4.1. Pharmacokinetic

Summaries of PK parameters following other dosing regimens of tarlatamab in study 20160323
are presented in the Table 62. The peak and trough serum concentrations for the 1/10 mg and
1/100 mg are summarized in Table 63.

Excluding dosage cohorts with only 1 evaluable patient (ranges from 0.003 to 0.1 mg), the dose-
normalized AUC;au and Cmax are generally consistent across the dosage groups, which suggests
that tarlatamab exposure appears to be approximately dose proportional across the clinically
relevant dosage range of 1 mg to 100 mg Q2W in study 20160323 (Table 62). Applicant did not
report exposure for 200 mg Q2W due to limited data.
