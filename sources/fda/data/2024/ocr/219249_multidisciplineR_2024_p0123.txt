The addition of inavolisib to palbociclib and fulvestrant resulted in a statistically significant and
clinically meaningful improvement in PFS for the patient population enrolled on INAVO120.
Results for secondary endpoints were supportive of the primary endpoint. The safety profile for
inavolisib is reviewed in Section 8.2 of this Assessment Aid. Overall, the benefit-risk assessment
is favorable for the triplet combination treatment with inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant in
adults with endocrine resistant, PIK3CA mutated, HR-positive, HER2-negative, locally advanced,
or metastatic breast cancer, following recurrence on or within 12 months of adjuvant endocrine
therapy. There is not adequate data to support a benefit for use of the triplet combination in
patients that do not have PIK3CA mutated advanced breast cancer that is not considered
endocrine resistant or for use of a different endocrine therapy other than fulvestrant.

Currently, there are no FDA approved treatments specifically for the first line treatment of
patients with PIK3CA mutated locally advanced or metastatic HR-positive, HER2-negative breast
cancer considered to have endocrine resistant disease. Alpelisib and capivasertib are two drugs
approved by the FDA for use in patients with PIK3CA-mutated advanced breast cancer.
However, both are for second line and later treatment and are not specifically for patients with
endocrine resistant disease. Addition of CDK4/6 inhibitor to ET has improved the outcomes of
patients with advanced HR-positive breast cancer. The addition of inavolisib to this standard of
care doublet therapy further improves the outcome specifically for patients with endocrine
resistant, PIK3CA-mutated advanced breast cancer.

Patients should be selected for treatment based on the presence of PIK3CA mutation using the
FoundationOne Liquid CDx (F1LDx). The sPMA P190032-S023 will be contemporaneously
approved with this NDA for inavolisib. A PMC has been issued for tissue testing, as the PMA is
planned to be submitted at the end of 2024.
