The Applicant's Position:
Pivotal Study WO41554: The proportion of patients who experienced AEs leading to withdrawal

of any study drug was overall low in both arms (Inavot+Palbo+Fulv: 6.8% vs. Pbo+Palbo+Fulv:
0.6%), indicating manageability of AEs and good tolerability of inavolisib in combination with
palbociclib and fulvestrant (Table and Table ).

Supportive Study GO39374: In the overall safety population, AEs leading to withdrawal of any
study treatment were reported in 7 patients (3.7%) and all AEs were single occurrences
observed in one patient each. AEs leading to withdrawal of any study drug in Arm B, Arm E, or
Arm F were infrequent. Overall, 2 patients each in Arm B, Arm E, and Arm F withdrew from
treatment due to an AE; AEs that led to treatment withdrawals were: cerebrovascular disorder
and hyperglycemia (one patient each in Arm B), asthenia and diarrhea (one patient each in Arm
E), panniculitis (one patient in Arm F), esophageal candidiasis and hypocalcemia (both events in
one patient in Arm F).

The FDA’s Assessment:

More patients on the inavolisib treatment arm in the INAVO120 trial (WO41554) discontinued
treatment due to an AE compared to the placebo arm. The only adverse event that occurred in
more than one patient (2 patients) leading to treatment discontinuation on the inavolisib
treatment arm was hyperglycemia. All other adverse events occurred in one patient.

The most common reason for treatment discontinuation was progression of disease, which
occurred in a high proportion of patients on the placebo arm (62%) compared to the inavolisib
treatment arm (42%).

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment of discontinuations for GO39374.
