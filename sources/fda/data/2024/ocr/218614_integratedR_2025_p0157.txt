Reviewer Comment

Causal association with olezarsen cannot be ruled out given the temporal association with
olezarsen treatment and positive dechallenge.

e Subject #CS8- ®© CS8 drug interruption and DAE; ‘elevated ALT’, 50 mg olezarsen:
55-year-old man randomized to 50 mg olezarsen with ALT elevation >3x ULN on Study Day
176 (Week 25). Baseline values were notable for an elevated ALT at baseline of 90 U/L
(reference range 10 to 53 U/L) and GGT of 68 U/L (reference range 11 to 52 U/L). All other
liver enzyme values were within the reference range. On Study Day 176 (Week 25), the
subject’s ALT was 172 U/L and AST of 95 U/L. Study drug was interrupted until Week 33.
Treatment resumed until Week 41, when subject experienced another ALT elevation >3x
ULN (ALT 179 U/L and AST 91 U/L). Study drug was interrupted again. ALT and AST

remained elevated but were trending towards baseline. Treatment did not restart by the time
the study ended.
