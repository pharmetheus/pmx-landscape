Population PK: PK of inavolisib following oral administration was well-described by a
population PK model consisting of a three-compartment model with first-order rate of
absorption and elimination. eGFR effect on clearance (CL) and body weight and race (Asian)
effects on central volume of distribution (V-) were identified as significant with higher CL for
subjects with higher eGFR, higher V- for subjects with higher body weight and 25% lower V; in
Asian subjects compared to non-Asian subjects. However, PopPK model based simulations
indicated that body weight, age, race, mild hepatic impairment, and mild renal impairment
were not associated with clinically relevant differences in inavolisib exposures following 9 mg
QD dosing.

The Applicant's Position: No DDIs were identified or predicted with inavolisib. Given the lack of
clinically relevant impact of body weight, age, race (Asian vs. Non-Asian), hepatic impairment
(mild, moderate, severe), or renal impairment (mild) on inavolisib PK, dose adjustment of
inavolisib in these specific populations is not warranted.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s summary of ADME (absorption, distribution,
metabolism, and excretion) properties and dose proportionality of inavolisib. FDA disagrees
with the Applicant’s positions in the following areas:

* Total metabolites in the excreta accounted for 48% (38% in feces and 10% in urine) of
the dose, rather than the Applicant’s summarized 42%.

e Per the interim CSR of the ongoing renal impairment study submitted on August 7,
2024, inavolisib AUCo; was 64% higher and AUCo int was 73% higher in subjects with
moderate renal impairment compared to subjects with normal renal function after a
single 6 mg oral dose of inavolisib (Table 8). The Cmax values were similar between
subjects with normal renal function and subjects with moderate renal impairment.

e FDA disagrees with the Applicant’s conclusion that a dedicated hepatic impairment
study is not warranted (see Section 6.1.2.2). A PMR will be issued to request a dedicated
clinical hepatic impairment study.

@ The popPK analysis included sufficient White and Asian patients to assess for PK
differences between these two racial groups only. There was limited representation of
Black/African American, Hispanic/Latino, and other underrepresented minority patients
in the inavolisib development program. Clinical pharmacology data will be requested to
be included if a PMC on race and ethnicity to be issued by FDA.

The inavolisib steady state AUC is 1,010 h*ng/mL (25%) and Cmax is 69 ng/mL (27%) at the
recommended dosage (9 mg PO QD). Inavolisib steady state apparent (oral) volume of
distribution is 155 L (3.5%). The estimated total clearance of inavolisib is 5.5 L/hr (29%).
