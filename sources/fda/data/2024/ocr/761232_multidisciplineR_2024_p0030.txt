The FDA’s Assessment:

In addition to the summary for meetings under IND 135699 listed above, pertinent regulatory
history for Study 302 is summarized below.

In the letter issued September 20, 2017 for the Type B Pre-IND/Pre-Phase 3 meeting, FDA:

e encouraged the incorporation of an additional stratification factor for investigator’s
choice of chemotherapy.

e recommended that BeiGene consider including ECOG performance status (0 versus 1) as
a stratification factor in place of gender.

e stated that FDA has no objections to the anticipated geographic distribution provided that
the BLA contains adequate justification that the results observed in a predominantly Asian
population can be extrapolated to the U.S. population, as discussed in the International
Conference on Harmonization (ICH) E5 Ethnic Factors in the Acceptability of Foreign
Clinical Data, and the FDA Guidance for Industry ICH E5 Ethnic Factors in the
Acceptability of Foreign Clinical Data, Questions and Answers.

e stated that FDA has no objections to the chemotherapy options proposed for use in the
investigator’s choice control arm; however, the protocol should require investigators to
choose the control chemotherapy prior to patient randomization, so that this can be used as a
stratification factor for randomization.

e stated that the primary endpoint of OS is acceptable but that a decision regarding whether
the proposed magnitude of OS benefit, corresponding to an approximately 2.3-month
improvement in median OS, is sufficient to support approval will depend on the totality of
the data, including the overall benefit-risk assessment of the application.

e stated that the quality of the safety data and its applicability to the U.S. patient population
will be assessed upon submission of the data.

e asked BeiGene to provide case definitions for immune-mediated AEs (imAEs), include
more frequent laboratory monitoring, provide management guidelines for all Grade 2-4
imAEs, provide a monitoring plan for potential ocular toxicities, and revise the protocol and
consent forms regarding treatment beyond radiologic progression.
