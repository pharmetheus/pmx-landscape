As shown in Figure 73 above, LDL-C increased in subjects on olezarsen treatment as compared
to subjects on placebo. An increase in LDL-C following TG-lowering therapy often occurs
because treatments that reduce TG tend to reduce VLDL particles, which are rich in TG. As
VLDL is metabolized, some of it is converted into LDL particles, thus increasing the overall
amount of LDL-C in circulation. Because subjects with FCS have very low levels of LDL-C, the
increase in LDL-C more often remains within the normal range for LDL-C levels. However,
clinicians will need to evaluate and monitor subjects’ LDL-C levels, especially those at higher
cardiovascular risk.
