Drug-drug interactions
(DDI)

Two PMCs will be issued to conduct clinical study/studies to
address the induction potential of inavolisib on CYP3A and
CYP2B6. If the induction potential of inavolisib on CYP3A is
confirmed, the Applicant may need to address the induction
potential of inavolisib on CYP2C enzymes and DDI potential
between inavolisib and COCs.

Bridge between the to be
marketed and clinical
trial formulations

The Applicant utilized dissolution studies to bridge the to-be-
marketed and clinical trial formulations. Refer to the
Biopharmaceutics Review for details.
