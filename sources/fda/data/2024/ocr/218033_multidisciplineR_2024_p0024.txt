There are no approved targeted therapies for the treatment of pediatric
LGG with BRAF fusions, representing a patient population with high unmet
medical need.

Approved Type | BRAF inhibitors including dabrafenib do not inhibit
signaling from the KIAA1549:BRAF fusion, the most common oncogenic
driver in these tumors and which functions as dimers (Behling, 2019;
Schreck, 2019).

The primary efficacy data supporting these applications are derived from
76 patients enrolled in FIREFLY-1, a single-arm, open-label, multicenter
trial conducted globally in patients 6 months to 25 years of age with
relapsed or refractory LGG with an activating BRAF alteration or RAF
fusion, or locally advanced or metastatic solid tumor with an activating RAF
fusion.

The primary endpoint was overall response rate according to BICR as per
Response Evaluation in Neuro-oncology (RANO) criteria, which are used to
evaluate response in high-grade glioma based on contrast enhancement,
with ORR assessed by the Response Assessment in Pediatric Neuro-
oncology (RAPNO) LGG (RAPNO-LGG) criteria per BICR as a secondary
endpoint.
