16.5.1.9. Metabolites of Sulopenem etzadroxil

In addition to the prodrug sulopenem etzadroxil, five metabolites M1, M2, M3, M4 and
sulopenem were detected during incubation in human whole blood. Following incubation,
sulopenem etzadroxil levels were observed to decrease with time and by 120 minutes the
prodrug was barely detectable (Table 167).

16.5.2. Animal Infection Model Studies

Sulopenem and its oral prodrug sulopenem etzadroxil were evaluated for in vivo activity in
three animal infection models:

e The mouse systemic infection model against S. pneumoniae and K. pneumoniae;
@ The mouse pulmonary infection model against S. pneumoniae; and
e@ The Mongolian gerbil otitis media model against H. influenzae.
