The FDA’s Assessment:

The binding affinity of zanidatamab to human, cynomolgus monkey, dog, rat, and mouse HER2
ECD was evaluated via SPR. These data showed that zanidatamab bound human and monkey
HER2 with similar affinity (Kd = 0.74 and 0.47 nM, respectively), bound dog HER2 >10-fold less
(Kd = 4.07 nM) and did not bind rat or mouse HER2. These data support the use of cynomolgus
monkeys as a pharmacologically relevant species for toxicity assessments.
