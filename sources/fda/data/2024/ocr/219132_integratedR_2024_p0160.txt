In the 26-week toxicity study, administration of levacetylleucine (0, 250, 800, and

2500 mg/kg/day) by oral gavage in rats with a 4-week treatment-free recovery period
produced no adverse effects at doses of up to 800 mg/kg/day; thus, the NOAEL is

800 mg/kg/day. At 2500 mg/kg/day test article related adverse effects include decreased body
weight (males only), changes of clinical chemistry parameters (males and females), changes
of urine parameters (females only), increased kidney weights (males and females),
histopathological changes in the kidney (males only). Exposure (AUC...) in rats dosed at

800 mg/kg provide a safety margin of 2- to 3-fold when compared to the respective steady-
state levels in humans (Table 62).
