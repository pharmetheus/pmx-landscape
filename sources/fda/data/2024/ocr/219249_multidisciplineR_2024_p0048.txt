FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. To calculate the exposure multiples, FDA used the

ITOVEBI.

5.4. ADME/PK

The Applicant’s Position: The nonclinical PK and toxicokinetic (TK) studies demonstrate that
inavolisib has a favorable absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion (ADME) profile and
characteristics consistent with a compound that can be delivered orally to achieve the targeted
clinical exposure. Inavolisib was well absorbed across preclinical species, with bioavailability
ranging from 57.5% to 100%. Metabolism of inavolisib was minimal and no human-specific
metabolites were observed in in vitro studies and in plasma. /n vitro studies suggested a low
potential for drug-drug interaction (DDI) involving cytochrome P450s (CYPs) or drug
transporters at clinically relevant concentrations.
