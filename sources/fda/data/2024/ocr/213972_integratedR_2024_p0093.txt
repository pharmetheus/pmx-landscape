7.6.4.1. Overview of Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events
Summary, Pooled Analyses, Trials 301 and 310

An overview of TEAEs, SAEs and drug discontinuations in the ISP (Trials 301 and 310) is
provided in Table 53. Six (0.3%) subjects in the sulopenem arm had a SAE. All six subjects were
enrolled in Trial 301. One SAE, angioedema, was judged as related to sulopenem
etzadroxil/probenecid and led to treatment discontinuation; the remaining SAEs are described in
Section 7.6.4.3. The single death was due to underlying lung adenocarcinoma and was not
telated to study drug (see Section 7.6.4.2). TEAEs resulting in treatment discontinuation were
reported in 21 (1.1%) subjects in the sulopenem group with the majority of the TEAEs being
gastrointestinal AEs, including diarrhea, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, and gastroesophageal
reflux disease (see Section 7.6.4.4). The overall incidence of TEAEs was higher (21.5%) in the
sulopenem group than in the comparator groups (amoxicillin/clavulanate, 12.3%; ciprofloxacin,
14.0%). In all three groups, the majority of TEAEs were mild or moderate with severe AEs being
uncommon.
