The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. The criteria for fasting glucose and HbA:- are
stricter than the criteria in the trial leading to the approval of alpelisib and capivasertib. There
were no eligibility criteria regarding body mass index (BMI).

All patients were required to have endocrine resistant HR-positive HER2-negative
advanced/metastatic breast cancer. Endocrine resistance was further categorized as primary
endocrine resistance or secondary endocrine resistance. Primary endocrine resistance was
defined as relapse while on the first 2 years of adjuvant endocrine therapy (ET) and secondary
endocrine resistance was defined as relapse while on adjuvant ET after at least 2 years or
relapse within 12 months of completing adjuvant ET.
