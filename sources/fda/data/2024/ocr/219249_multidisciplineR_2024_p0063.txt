Impurities (Including Mutagenic Impurities)

The Applicant’s Position: Impurities in the drug substance (inavolisib) and drug product are
controlled according to ICH Q3A and ICH Q3B, respectively. All specified and unspecified
impurities in the drug substance are controlled with individual acceptance criteria lower than or
equal to the ICH Q3A qualification threshold, except for impurities |

©® Were qualified in a 28-day GLP toxicity study (15-3288/4.2.3.2
Repeat-Dose Toxicity) that justifies higher limits than the ICH Q3A qualification threshold.
Degradation products are controlled in the drug product with individual acceptance criteria
lower than or equal to the ICH Q3B qualification threshold. Refer to Module 3, 3.2.P.5.6
Justification of Specification(s).

In silico mutagenicity assessments were conducted with inavolisib and impurities of inavolisib
using standard in silico structure—activity relationship software programs Derek for Nexus®
(Version 6.1.0) and Leadscope Statistical bacterial mutation model (Version 2) in combination
with expert review. One impurity © was shown to be experimentally non-mutagenic
in a 5-strain non—GLP compliant bacterial reverse mutation assay (Study 15-3392/4.2.3.7.6).

FDA agrees with Applicant’s position. Additional details are provided below.
