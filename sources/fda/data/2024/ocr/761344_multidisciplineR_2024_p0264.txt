NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761344}

{Tarlatamab}

Study 20200491 was conducted globally and only 3% of the efficacy population in this
application was enrolled in the U.S. In addition, racial and ethnic minorities reflective of
the U.S. population of patients with SCLC, particularly Black/African American and
Hispanic/Latino patients, were significantly underrepresented in the efficacy and safety
population that supported this application, with no Black/African American patients and
only one Hispanic/Latino patient enrolled. Therefore, it is important to evaluate data
further characterizing the efficacy and safety of tarlatamab for the treatment of SCLC in
study populations whose demographics are more representative of the population of

U.S. patients for the proposed indication.
