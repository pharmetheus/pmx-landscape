BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

The FDA’s Assessment:

Prior to conducting the safety review, FDA’s Office of Computational Science (OCS) generated
a CoreDF report of data quality which did not identify any significant or unusual data issues.
During the safety review, FDA did not identify any significant data issues that would impact the
safety review or overall conclusions.

Categorization of Adverse Event

Data:

All adverse event summaries were based on the Safety Analysis Set. A treatment-emergent
adverse event was defined as an adverse event that had an onset date or a worsening in severity
from baseline (pretreatment) on or after the first dose of study treatment up to 30 days after study
treatment discontinuation or initiation of a new anticancer therapy, whichever occurred first.
Treatment-emergent adverse events also included all immune-mediated adverse events (for the
Tislelizumab Arm) up to 90 days after the last dose of study treatment regardless of whether a
patient started a new anticancer therapy. Only treatment-emergent adverse events were included
in the summary tables.

The adverse event verbatim descriptions (investigator’s description from the eCRF) were
classified into standardized medical terminology using MedDRA. Adverse events were coded to
MedDRA (Version 23.0) lower-level term closest to the verbatim term. The linked MedDRA
System Organ Class (SOC) and PT were also classified.

The incidence of adverse events was summarized as the number (percentage) of patients with
adverse events by SOC, PT, severity, and relationship to study drug. Patients with multiple
occurrences of the same adverse event within a specific SOC and PT were counted only once
within the SOC and PT, and the maximum grade per NCI-CTCAE (Version 4.03) was used.
Serious adverse events, adverse events of > Grade 3, adverse events leading to treatment

discontinuation or dose modification, and adverse events leading to death were also summarized
by SOC, PT, and relationship to study drug.

All deaths and causes of death were summarized, including those that occurred during the study
treatment period and those reported during the survival follow-up period after treatment
completion/discontinuation.

Immune-mediated adverse events were identified according a 2-step process which included
generation of a potential immune-mediated adverse event list and medical evaluation of potential
immune-mediated adverse events. The incidence of immune-mediated adverse event (for all
grades and > Grade 3) was summarized by category and worst grade per NCI-CTCAE (Version
4.03). The immune-mediated adverse events were also summarized by outcome and category.
The immune-mediated adverse events leading to treatment discontinuation, dose modification,
and death were summarized by category.
