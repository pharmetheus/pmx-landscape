Secondary Pharmacology

The Applicant’s Position: No off-target responses (>50% inhibition or stimulation of
control-specific binding) were identified when inavolisib was evaluated in radioligand binding
and enzyme assays at a concentration of 10 uM against 88 targets (including those associated
with abuse liability), including major classes of biogenic amine receptors, neuropeptide
receptors, ion channels, neurotransmitter transporters, and enzymes (Studies 15-0292 and
15-1941/4.2.1.2).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position.
