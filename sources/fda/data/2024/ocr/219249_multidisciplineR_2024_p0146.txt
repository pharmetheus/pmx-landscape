NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

Supportive Study GO39374: Six patients experienced AEs within the Investigations SOC related
to ECG abnormalities, which included ECG QT prolonged (2.6%) and ECG ST segment depression
(0.5%).

The Applicant's Position:
Although in Study GO39374, there is limited ECG data with the triplet combination (inavolisib+

palbociclib+ fulvestrant in Arms E and F) that was selected for the Phase III study, the data from
single agent inavolisib arms (Arm A), doublet arms (Arm C: inavolisib+ letrozole and Arm D:
inavolisib+ fulvestrant), and the other triplet arm (Arm B: inavolisib+ palbociclib+ letrozole), can
be extrapolated to the inavolisib+ palbociclib+ fulvestrant triplet combination due to following
reasons:
e  Inavolisib exposure is comparable between all the arms, hence no additional potential
for QT prolongation is expected with the triplet combination.
¢ Norisk of QT prolongation has been reported for the combination agents, palbociclib
and fulvestrant.
Based on the concentration-QTc analysis from study GO39374, it would not be expected that
inavolisib would have a clinically relevant QTc prolongation effect.
No clinically significant trends in post-baseline vs. baseline mean ECG parameters were
identified in both pivotal and supportive studies.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Interdisciplinary Review Team for Cardiac Safety Studies at the FDA completed a QT review
and concluded that inavolisib at the proposed dosage of 9mg daily is unlikely to cause >20msec
mean increase in QTc interval based on the alternative QT assessment pathway. See Section
6.3.1 for additional details.

See the full review by the IRT for Cardiac Safety Studies (QT-IRT) for further details.
Immunogenicity

Data and the Applicant’s Position: Not applicable; immunogenicity assessments were not
performed in both studies.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
