NDA 219132
Aqneursa (levacetylleucine)

Evaluation of Dosing Regimen

The Applicant’s proposed dosing regimen is based on | body weight. |

To estimate steady state PK profiles of
levacetylleucine following the proposed dosing regimen, PopPK simulations were conducted
using the reviewer’s PopPK model, which is similar to the Applicant’s. As body weight was the
only significant covariate included in the PopPK model, simulations were conducted in virtual
populations, each of which includes 1000 subjects whose body weight was sampled from
uniformly distributed values within each body weight group. To generate the reference range for
comparison, PopPK simulation was conducted based on a virtual NPC population (n=1000)
sampled from the subjects with NPC from Trials IB1001-201 and IB1001-301. The red lines in
Figure 28 to Figure 30 present 5“, mean, and 95" percentiles of the reference PK exposures
(Cmax and daily AUC).

The simulations show that PK exposure metrics (Cyax, and daily AUC) are generally similar
across body weight groups 15 to <25 kg (dose group 3), 25 to <35 kg (dose group 4), and >35 kg
(dose group 5). The proposed dosing regimens for patients weighing >15 kg are deemed
adequate and supported by the efficacy and safety findings from Trial IB1001-301, and the
similar PK exposures across body weight groups.
