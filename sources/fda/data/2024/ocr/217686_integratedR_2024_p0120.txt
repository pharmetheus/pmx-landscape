TRYVIO (aprocitentan)

13.1.2. Pharmacokinetics/ ADME/Toxicokinetics

General pharmacokinetic (PK) assessments, including absorption, distribution, metabolism, and
excretion (ADME), and toxicokinetic evaluations with aprocitentan were conducted in rodents
(rats) and nonrodents (rabbits and dogs) following oral (PO) or intravenous (IV) administration.
The species and strains were those used in the toxicology program. Overall, the animal data
predicted the PK/ADME profile in humans and no significant limitations on the interpretability
of animal toxicology studies for predicting human safety were identified.

Absorption

Single-dose PK studies were conducted in male rats (0.5 mg/kg, IV or 3 mg/kg, PO) (B-05.130)
and dogs (0.3 mg/kg, IV or 3 mg, PO) (B-13.249) by administering the ©® form of
aprocitentan. PK parameters are summarized in the tables below. Aprocitentan was identified as
a low-clearance drug in rats (1.3 mL/min.kg) and dogs (0.4 mL/min.kg). In rats, oral absorption
was slow with a median time to maximum concentration (T,,.x) of 8 h and oral bioavailability
was 41% ( ©® form in suspension). The slow uptake observed in this assessment was likely
due to poor solubility and/or slow dissolution of the drug substance. In dogs when administered
with a more clinically relevant ©® aprocitentan, oral bioavailability was ~90% and the
Tmax Was 3 hours.
