BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

The Applicant’s Position:
The primary evidence supporting the efficacy and safety of tislelizumab in patients with

advanced or metastatic ESCC is based on pivotal study 302 (RATIONALE-302). Study 302 is a
randomized, controlled, open-label, global study assessing the efficacy and safety of tislelizumab
in patients with advanced or metastatic ESCC after prior systemic treatment. Study 302 was
designed in accordance with international treatment guidelines.

Supportive efficacy evidence for tislelizumab in the treatment of advanced or metastatic ESCC
comes from patients enrolled in Studies 001 and 102.

The remaining studies (BGB-A317-203, BGB-A317-204, BGB-A317-208, and BGB-A317-303;
hereafter referred to as Studies 203, 204, 208, and 303, respectively) investigated tislelizumab
monotherapy in patients with other advanced cancers and provide additional safety and PK data
to support this BLA.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with Applicant’s general description of the clinical trials considered in this review.

8. Statistical and Clinical Evaluation

8.1. Review of Relevant Individual Trials Used to Support Efficacy
8.1.1. BGB-A317-302

Trial Design

The Applicant’s Description:
Study 302 is an open-label, multicenter, randomized Phase 3 study comparing the efficacy and

safety of tislelizumab versus ICC (paclitaxel, docetaxel, or irinotecan) in patients with advanced
or metastatic ESCC after prior systemic treatment. The study design schematic is presented in
Figure 11.

As of the data cutoff date of 01 December 2020, a total of 512 patients were randomized to the
Tislelizumab Arm (256 patients) and the ICC Arm (256 patients), with the last patient
randomized on 04 March 2020.
