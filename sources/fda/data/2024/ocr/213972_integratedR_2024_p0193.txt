7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

Subsection 7.1 Potential for ORLYNVAH to affect other drugs:
Organized drug interactions into a tabular format to improve
readability and clarify mitigation strategies. Information on the
negative finding from the valproic acid drug-drug interaction study
was also added given the observed class effect with other penem
antibacterials.

Subsection 7.2 Potential for Other Drugs to Affect ORLYNVAH:
Added this subsection to describe the potential for OAT3
inhibitors to affect the PK of ORLYNVAH.

Subsection 7.3 Drug/Laboratory Interactions: Clarified the type of
interference (i.e., with copper sulfate) is the basis for the noted
drug/laboratory interaction with urine glucose tests.

8 USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS
(e.g., Pregnancy, Lactation, Females
and Males of Reproductive Potential,
Pediatric Use, Geriatric Use, Renal
Impairment, Hepatic Impairment)

Subsection 8.1 Pregnancy: This section was modified to provide
additional details regarding effects of sulopenem etzadroxil in
pregnant females in embryo-fetal development (EFD) studies in
mice, rats, and rabbits. Information about EFD studies in)

rabbits with intravenous sulopenem was deleted. Also,
additional details were added to the description of the pre-
postnatal study with sulopenem etzadroxil in rats. Refer to
Section 7.4 of this IA and the NDA 213972 Multidisciplinary
Review dated July 22, 2021, for additional details.

Subsection 8.2 Lactation: This subsection was modified to
include information about human and animal data regarding the
presence of sulopenem etzadroxil, sulopenem, and probenecid in
lactation milk.

Subsection 8.4 Pediatric Use: This subsection was modified to
include a brief description of the methods and results of the
toxicology study in juvenile rats with sulopenem etzadroxil.

Subsection 8.6 Renal Impairment: Revised to streamline the
description of findings from the dedicated renal impairment study
and recommend against use in patients with CrCl <15 mL/min
and patients on hemodialysis due to the lack of PK data in these
patients.
