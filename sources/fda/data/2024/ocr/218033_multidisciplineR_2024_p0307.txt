19.4.3.3.1. Effects of CYP2C8 Perpetrators on the PK of Tovorafenib.

PBPK analysis was considered inadequate to predict the effects of CYP2C8 inhibitors and
inducers on the PK of tovorafenib, because there is uncertainty about the contributions of AO
and CYPs to the metabolism of tovorafenib due to the variability of AO activity in the human S9
system, the low turnover rate of tovorafenib, and the potentially overlapping substrate
specificity of AO and CYPs. In addition, the contribution of CYP2C8 in the tovorafenib model was
