NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
ITOVEBI {inavolisib}

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

Currently, there are no FDA approved treatments specifically for the first line treatment of
patients with PIK3CA mutated locally advanced or metastatic HR-positive, HER2-negative breast
cancer considered to have endocrine resistant disease.

3 Regulatory Background

3.1. U.S. Regulatory Actions and Marketing History

The Applicant's Position: Inavolisib is not currently marketed in the US or in any other country.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
