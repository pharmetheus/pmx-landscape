BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — BLA 761232
TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

The Applicant’s position:

The results from the Study 302 and the supportive studies provide evidence of the positive
benefit-risk supporting the efficacy claim of tislelizumab 200 mg Q3W in 2L ESCC patients.
While clinical pharmacology evaluation does not include a direct assessment of benefit-risk, the
exposure-response models in safety and efficacy highlight a flat dose — response relationship
between 2 mg/kg and 5 mg/kg Q3W from earlier studies, as well as in patients who received 200
mg Q3W tislelizumab from the 302 study. Taken together, these results support tislelizamab 200
mg Q3W to be an appropriate dosage regimen for this population.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

6.3.2.2 Is the proposed dosing regimen appropriate for the general patient population for
which the indication is being sought?

Data:
Refer to section 6.2.2.

The Applicant’s Position:
The totality of clinical efficacy and safety data in patients with advanced or metastatic ESCC

along with a lack of clinically relevant E-R relationships between PK, efficacy, and safety
support the proposed tislelizumab dose of 200 mg administered intravenously once every 3
weeks.

FDAs Assessment:
FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position.

6.3.2.3 Is an alternative dosing regimen or management strategy required for
subpopulations based on intrinsic patient factors?

Data:
Refer to section 6.2.2.2.

The Applicant’s Position:

In the population pharmacokinetic analysis, potential covariates influencing the
pharmacokinetics of tislelizumab have been evaluated . No dose adjustment is required for race,
hepatic and renal function-related covariates (eg, ALT, AST, bilirubin, eGFR), and disease-
telated covariates (eg, LDH, ECOG PS score).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position.
