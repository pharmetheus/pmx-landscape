Carbapenem-Resistant Enterobacterales

Sulopenem had very little to no activity against CRE isolates with known carbapenemases
(study IHMA_3234). Sulopenem MICso/99 value of 216/>16 pg/mL was observed for CRE
expressing isolates such as IMP (N=50), KPC (N=50), NDM (N=50), VIM (N=50), and OXA (N=50)
carbapenemases, compared to 1.0/4.0 g/mL for CRE isolates (N=50) that were negative for
known carbapenemases. A breakdown of isolates by species and resistance mechanism is
shown in Table 160 and the sulopenem MIC distribution against these isolates is shown in
Figure 21.
