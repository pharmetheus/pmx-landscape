TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

1. Executive Summary

1.1. Product Introduction

Tislelizumab-jsgr (tislelizumab) is a recombinant humanized immunoglobulin G4 (IgG4)
monoclonal antibody that binds to the programmed death 1 (PD-1) receptor and blocks the
interaction between PD-1 with its ligands PD-L1 and PD-L2, releasing PD-1 pathway-mediated
inhibition of the immune response, including the antitumor immune response. Tislelizumab is
supplied in single vials of 100 mg/mL (10 mg/mL) clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to
slightly yellow solution. Tislelizumab is not currently approved in the U.S. In China, it is
approved for the treatment of:
e patients with relapsed or refractory classical Hodgkin’s lymphoma who received at least
2 prior lines of systemic chemotherapy regimens.
¢ patients with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma with PD-L1 high
expression and whose disease progressed during or following platinum-containing
chemotherapy or within 12 months of neoadjuvant or adjuvant treatment with platinum-
containing chemotherapy.
e in combination with two chemotherapy regimens as a first-line treatment for patients with
advanced squamous non-small cell lung cancer

1.2. Conclusions on the Substantial Evidence of Effectiveness

Substantial Evidence of Effectiveness (SEE) was established with one adequate and well-
controlled clinical investigation and confirmatory evidence.

The Applicant submitted the results of a single adequate and well-controlled study to support the
claims of effectiveness for the proposed indication. RATIONALE-302 (NCT03430843) is a
randomized, international, open-label trial in patients with locally advanced unresectable or
metastatic esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) with disease progression on/after
previous therapy. Patients were eligible if they had histologically confirmed predominant
squamous cell histology, irrespective of PD-L1 status, and ECOG PS of 0 or 1. Prior
chemotherapy in the neoadjuvant/adjuvant setting was permitted if completed before enrollment
and patients had a recurrence-free period of less than 6 months. Patients with prior therapy with
PD-1 or PD-L1 monoclonal antibodies were not eligible for the trial. Tumor cell PD-L1 was
determined by a central lab using the Ventana SP263 assay (vCPS).

(75 mg/m? IV Q3W), or irinotecan (125 mg/m? IV on Days | and 8 of every 3-week
cycle). Regional variations on dosing regimen for taxanes were allowed.
