NDA 219132
Aqneursa (levacetylleucine)

PMRs for evaluating toxicity of levacetylleucine on juvenile animals will be issued because
onset of NPC ranges from infancy into adulthood, and treatment with levacetylleucine may
range from infancy into adulthood. In addition, nonclinical studies available at the times of
NDA submission used doses that lacked an adequate justification, as they were not clearly
based on achieving a maximum tolerated dose, maximum feasible dose, or saturation of
exposure with dose, nor did they achieve a sufficient safety margin relative to the clinical
dose. Further, the rat and rabbit EFD studies indicate levacetylleucine crossed placenta and
modulated growth of bones, and it is uncertain if levacetylleucine will modulate the growth
of bones in juvenile animals. Considering leucine causes defects in ossification of the skull,
torsion of the neck and depression of the brain waves (Bergstrom et al. 1967),
levacetylleucine has potential to influence growth, neurological development (learning and
memory), sexual development, and general health. Hence, there is need for a juvenile toxicity
study to determine the toxic effects of levacetylleucine on juvenile animals and inform
patients of potential risks of medication.
