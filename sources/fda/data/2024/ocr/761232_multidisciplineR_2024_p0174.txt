8.2.13. Integrated Assessment of Safety

Data:

This comprehensive review, based primarily on the large, well-controlled, pivotal Phase 3 Study
302 and supported by collective data from 1972 tislelizumab-treated patients, establishes that
tislelizumab provides a favorable safety profile compared to chemotherapy in patients with
advanced or metastatic ESCC after prior systemic therapy. Study 302 allows for a clear
comparison of tislelizumab safety against the safety of single-agent chemotherapy, the standard
of care at the time of study initiation. Patients in Study 302 are representative of the general
ESCC population in terms of age, sex, race, geographic region, disease status, and overall health,
