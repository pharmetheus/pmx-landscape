FDA also carefully considered the need for Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies (REMS)
given the major identified safety finding of CRS and have determined that a REMS is not
needed. Based on our review, we have determined that the toxicity profile of the tarlatamab
can be adequately conveyed through labeling (including a Boxed Warning for serious or life-

pharmacovigilance and a Dear Healthcare Provider letter are non-REMS adjuncts recommended
to help convey these risks. Refer to Section 12 for rationale for not requiring a REMS.
