TRYVIO (aprocitentan)

Case Level Analysis

To further evaluate the risk of hepatotoxicity, subject level data from the PRECISION study and
an additional Phase 2 study (Study 201) were reviewed by DHN. Per the DHN review:

e Based on hepatocellular scatterplots (eDISH), there was no imbalance in liver analytes nor
cases concerning for significant liver injury in Study 201.

e Based on hepatocellular scatterplots (eDISH), there was no imbalance in liver analytes
during the 4-week double-blinded placebo-controlled period (Part 1) in Study 301.

e DILI adjudication of high interest cases in the PRECISION study identified four cases in Part
2 (aprocitentan 25 mg arm), which included two highly likely cases (based on positive
rechallenge), and two possible cases (Table 36).

According to the DHN review, the mean latency period for the highly likely and possible DILI
events after initiation of aprocitentan was 134 days (approximately 4 months; range 84-205
days). Of note, DILI with a long latency period has been observed with other ERAs (bosentan,
macitentan, ambrisentan). In general, the elevations in liver enzymes resolved/improved with
discontinuation of aprocitentan. As previously mentioned, in two cases, elevations reoccurred
with aprocitentan rechallenge; these two highly likely cases of DILI are further described below.

who experienced elevated liver enzymes on Study Day 206; ALT was reported as 284 U/L
(8.4 x ULN) and AST was reported as 210 U/L (6.2 x ULN). The subject’s baseline
medication history included levothyroxine (0.125 mg daily), atorvastatin (20 mg daily). Her
atorvastatin dose was increased to 40 mg daily on Study Day 189 (17 days prior to the event).
