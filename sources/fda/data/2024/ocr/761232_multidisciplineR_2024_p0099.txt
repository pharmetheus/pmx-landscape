Rescue Medication
No rescue medications were used or needed in Study 302.

The Applicant’s Position:

Overall, the drug exposure results showed a good treatment compliance of both tislelizumab and.
ICC, and the concomitant treatments administered were representative of those commonly
prescribed for patients in the target population and were not expected to have impacted study
results.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s general assessment above. FDA concurs with the Applicant’s
data. Although it is stated above that immune suppressive regimens and steroids were restricted
or prohibited during tislelizumab treatment, corticosteroids were recommended and used for the
management of imAEs in the tislelizumab arm. Other immunosuppressive drugs (e.g.
mycophenolate) were not used in Study 302.

Efficacy Results — Primary Endpoint (Including Sensitivity/Supportive Analyses)

Data:

Primary Efficacy Endpoint: Overall Survival in the ITT Analysis Set

Tislelizumab demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in
OS versus ICC in patients with advanced or metastatic ESCC with disease progression after prior
systemic treatment.

With 410 death events (80.1% of all patients randomized) reported, the stratified HR of OS was
0.70 (95% CI: 0.57 to 0.85) with 1-sided p-value of 0.0001, indicating a 30% reduction in the
tisk of death events in the Tislelizumab Arm compared with the ICC Arm. A 2.3-month
improvement in median OS was observed (8.6 months in the Tislelizumab Arm versus 6.3
