The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s presentation of the primary efficacy results for OS in
the ITT population (N = 512). The primary analysis of OS in the ITT population demonstrated a
statistically significant improvement favoring the tislelizumab arm (stratified HR = 0.70; 95%
CI: 0.57, 0.85; one-sided p-value = 0.0001). This result was robust across the various sensitivity
analyses conducted by the applicant. However, FDA notes that the landmark OS rates at 3, 6, 9,
and 12 months by arm presented by the applicant should be considered exploratory. In general,
the point estimate of event rates at a fixed time point for a time-to-event endpoint can be
misleading because it does not represent the entire effect of the treatment and the chosen
landmark time is arbitrary.

Regarding the subgroup analyses presented by the Applicant, FDA notes that all subgroup
analyses are considered exploratory except for the subgroup of OS in PDL1 vCPS >10% which
was the key secondary endpoint tested in hierarchy.
