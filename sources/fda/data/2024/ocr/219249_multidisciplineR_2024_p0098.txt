NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

The Applicant's Description: For the primary and secondary efficacy objectives, a comparison of
the treatment arms was performed in the full analysis set (FAS). The primary efficacy endpoint
was PFS as assessed by the investigator according to RECIST v1.1. Secondary efficacy endpoints
include: OS; ORR; BOR rate; DOR for patients with objective response; CBR; time to confirmed
deterioration (TTCD) in worst pain severity; TTCD in physical functioning (PF); TTCD in role
functioning (RF); and TTCD in Global Health Status/Health-Related Quality of Life (GHS/HRQoL).
ORR, BOR, DOR, and CBR were based on tumor assessment by the investigator. Exploratory
efficacy endpoints include time to end of next-line treatment (proxy for PFS2), time to first
skeletal related event (SRE), mean and mean change from baseline scores in function (physical,
role, cognitive, emotional, and social), HRQoL, and disease and treatment-related symptoms.

Safety objective of the study was to evaluate the safety of inavolisib plus palbociclib and
fulvestrant compared with placebo plus palbociclib and fulvestrant (for further details see
Section 8.2.1). PK objective of the study was to characterize the PK of inavolisib, palbociclib,
and fulvestrant, when administered in combination in this population.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. The primary efficacy endpoint for the INAVO120
trial was PFS as assessed by investigator. A prospectively planned sensitivity analysis of PFS by
BICR assessment was also performed on all patients.

Statistical Analysis Plan and Amendments

The Applicant’s Description: The Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) was finalized prior to the conduct
of any efficacy analysis and unblinding of the database. SAP Version 1 was finalized on 25

April 2023. A subsequent amendment was finalized on 18 September 2023, to clarify the
statistical testing procedure of key secondary efficacy endpoints and the power of OS analyses.

Analysis Populations

All efficacy analyses were performed in the FAS (unless otherwise specified), defined as all
randomized participants; participants were included in the analyses according to the treatment
they were assigned. The safety set analysis (SAS) included all participants exposed to study
treatment and were analyzed according to the treatment that they actually received. The PK-
evaluable population included all randomized participants in the inavolisib arm who had at least
one evaluable inavolisib plasma concentration.

Hypothesis Testing
The primary analysis was a comparison of the investigator-assessed PFS between the two
treatment arms using a stratified log-rank test at an overall 0.05 significance level (two-sided).
The statistical hypothesis of this study was as follows:

¢ HO: PFS Inavo+Palbo+Fulv = PFS Pbo+Palbo+Fulv

¢ H1: PFS Inavo+Palbo+Fulv # PFS Pbo+Palbo+Fulv
