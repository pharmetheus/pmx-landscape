This study was conducted in two phases: an initial dose-ranging tolerability phase at doses of
up to 2500 mg/kg/day, and a definitive dosing phase at doses of up to 1925 mg/kg/day.

Observations and Results

FO Dams

Mortality

There was no treatment-related mortality in phase 1. A single 1250 mg/kg/day female
(Animal R0201) died following dosing on GD 13. The death was considered a gavage error
based on the observed presence of red discoloration and perforation of the lung lobes and
red/tan contents in the trachea.

Clinical Signs

Four of six rabbits administered 2500 mg/kg/day appeared thin at one or two instances and
this was considered incidental. No dose related clinical signs were observed.

Body Weight

Body weight loss and decreased body weight gain were observed primarily around GD 22:
however, the effect was not dose dependent (Figure 21).
