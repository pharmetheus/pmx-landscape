8.2.8. Specific Safety Studies/Clinical Trials

The Applicant is conducting Study KGAK (ADopt-VA), a phase 3, randomized, double-blind,
placebo-controlled, parallel group study to evaluate the impact of lebrikizumab on vaccine
responses in adult subjects with moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis. The study was ongoing
at the time of BLA submission and could not be incorporated into this review.
