8.2.5.3. Neutropenia

Data:

Across the monotherapy studies | subjects © had adverse events of
neutropenia based on the AMQ broad search (ISS Table 14-6.1.7). Using this search, the most
frequently reported (> 5% of subjects) preferred terms were neutropenia (7.9%), decreased
neutrophil count (6.1%), and decreased white blood cell count (5.3%) (ISS Table 14-6.2.65).
Using the narrow search, 63 subjects (13.8%) had adverse events of neutropenia, 37 subjects
(8.1%) had grade > 3 events, and 12 subjects (2.6%) had grade > 4 events. Serious neutropenia
events were reported for 9 subjects (2.0%), and 2 subjects (0.4%) had neutropenia events
leading to tarlatamab discontinuation. No neutropenia adverse events were fatal. Results for
the 10 and 100 mg dose groups were similar for neutropenia, with the exception of a higher
incidence of grade > 3 events with 100 mg (11.7%) than with 10 mg (6.3%).
