Immunogenicity
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position that the incidence of ADA was low. In Study

20200491 1/10mg dosage cohort, 3% (4/124) of patients tested positive for ADA and
none of the patients developed Nab. All ADAs in 1/10 mg cohort were treatment-
induced (Table 20).
