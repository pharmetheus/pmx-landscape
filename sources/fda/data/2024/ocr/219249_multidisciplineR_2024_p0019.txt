NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
ITOVEBI {inavolisib}

1 Executive Summary

1.1. Product Introduction

Inavolisib (Itovebi) is a small molecule kinase inhibitor of the phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase
(PI3K) with inhibitory activity predominantly against PI3Ka. Inavolisib is a new molecular entity.
It does not have an approval for any indication worldwide. Itovebi film-coated tablets are
supplied for oral administration with two strengths that contain 3 mg and 9 mg of inavolisib.

The Applicant proposed the following indication for NDA 219249:

(b) (4)

The recommended indication for regular approval is:

Inavolisib, in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant, is indicated for the treatment of
adults with endocrine-resistant, PIK3CA-mutated, hormone receptor (HR)-positive, human
epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative, locally advanced or metastatic breast
cancer, as detected by an FDA-approved test, following recurrence on or after completing
adjuvant endocrine therapy.

The recommended dose for inavolisib is 9 mg taken orally once daily in combination with
palbociclib and fulvestrant until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. The
recommended dosage of palbociclib is 125 mg taken orally once daily for 21 consecutive days
followed by 7 days off treatment to comprise a cycle of 28 days. The recommended dosage of
fulvestrant is 500 mg administered intramuscularly on Days 1, 15, and 29, and once monthly
thereafter.

For premenopausal and perimenopausal women, a luteinizing hormone releasing hormone
(LHRH) agonist should be administered in accordance with local clinical practice.

For men, administration of an LHRH agonist should be considered in accordance with local
clinical practice.
