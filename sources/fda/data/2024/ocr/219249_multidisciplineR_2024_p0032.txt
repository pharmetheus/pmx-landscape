NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
ITOVEBI {inavolisib}

2 Therapeutic Context

2.1. Analysis of Condition

The Applicant's Position: Breast cancer is the most commonly diagnosed cancer in women, with
an estimated global incidence of 2.2 million new cases and almost 700,000 deaths reported in
2020 (Sung et al. 2021). Breast cancer in men is rare; accounting for less than 1% of all breast
cancer cases in the US. Hormone receptor positive (HR+), human epidermal growth

factor receptor 2-negative (HER2—) breast cancer is the most common subtype accounting for
approximately 70% of all breast cancers (Breast Cancer, Facts and Figures 2022-2024).

In the U.S., the median age at diagnosis for HR+, HER2— breast cancer patients is 64.0 years. For
metastatic HR+, HER2— breast cancer, the annual incidence was observed to be highest for non-
Hispanic Blacks (5.1 per 100,000 women), followed by non-Hispanic Whites (4.0 per 100,000),
Hispanics (3.0 per 100,000), and Asian/Pacific Islanders (2.8 per 100,000 women) (SEER 2023).

Locally advanced and metastatic breast cancer (LA/mBC) remains a virtually incurable disease,
with 29.2% of these patients likely to survive at least 5 years from the time of diagnosis. The
5-year survival of distant disease also remains low for HR+, HER2—LA/mBC at 34% (SEER 2023).

Approximately 35-40% of patients with HR+ breast cancer have tumors that harbor mutations
in PIK3CA, the gene encoding the catalytic subunit of the alpha isoform of PI3K (i.e., p110a)
(Fillbrunn et al. 2022). There are mixed reports on whether there are differences in PIK3CA
mutation prevalence in HR+ breast cancer by race. In studies of Asian and Hispanic patients, the
mutation prevalence range (i.e., 35-40%) was similar to non-Hispanic Whites, while for African
American women and those with African ancestry, some data suggest PIK3CA mutation
prevalence may be up to 10% lower (Li et al. 2021; Blesinger et al. 2018; Chen et al. 2022; Pitt et

al. 2018; Baselga et al. 2017).

PIK3CA mutations result in upregulation of PI3K pathway activity (Saal et al. 2005; Stemke-Hale
et al. 2008) and are a negative prognostic factor (Fillbrunn et al. 2022). Specifically, it has been
estimated that patients with HR+, HER2— mBC whose tumors harbor one or more P/K3CA
mutations exhibit approximately 2 months shorter progression-free survival (PFS) and 8 months
shorter overall survival (OS), when treated with standard of care options compared with their
wildtype counterparts (Fillbrunn et al. 2022).

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. FDA considers advanced and metastatic HR-
positive, HER2-negative breast cancer that is endocrine resistant to be incurable, and the goal
of therapy is palliative.
