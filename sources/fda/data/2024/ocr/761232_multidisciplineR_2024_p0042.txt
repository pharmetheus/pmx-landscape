Unlike many other human IgG antibodies, tislelizumab has diminished binding capacity to
human Fe gamma receptors, including FeyRI, FeyRIAnis1, FeyRIArisi, FeyRIB, FeyRIIAviss,
FeyRIIAriss, and FeyRIIB, which may result in diminished Fe gamma receptor mediated
effector functions. In addition, tislelizumab has diminished binding to Clq, the initiating
component of complement complex formation, involved in complement-dependent cytotoxicity
(CDC) induction.

Cell-based functional assays were conducted to assess whether tislelizumab could induce
antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) or CDC. Results showed that tislelizamab had
no detectable ADCC or CDC activity.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA generally agrees.

Safety Pharmacology

Stand-alone safety pharmacology studies were not conducted nor required for this product in
accordance with International Conference on Harmonization (ICH) S9 Nonclinical Evaluation
for Anticancer Pharmaceuticals. The effects of tislelizumab on vital organ functions (including
cardiovascular, respiratory and central nervous systems) were evaluated as part of single-dose
and 13-week repeat dose toxicity studies in monkeys under Good Laboratory Practice (GLP)
conditions.

In the single dose study, there were no treatment-related changes in electrocardiogram
parameters at doses up to 100 mg/kg, administered via intravenous infusion. Similarly, there
were no changes in electrocardiogram parameters after 13 weeks of bi-weekly dosing at doses up
to 30 mg/kg. There were also no clinical signs or histopathological changes indicative of any
cardiac, respiratory, or central nervous system toxicity.

The Applicant’s Position:
No effect was observed on electrocardiogram (ECG), heart rate, blood pressure, or respiratory or

central nervous system safety pharmacology parameters evaluated as part of the general
toxicology studies.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA generally agrees; however, See FDA’s Assessment under 5.5.1.
