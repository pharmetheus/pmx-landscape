19.4.3.3. E-R (safety) Assessment Summary

The Applicant's Position: Exposure-safety analyses were performed based on a total of

165 patients with model-predicted exposure parameters from Study GO39374 (Arm F was not
part of the safety analysis because anti-hyperglycemic medication was given to all patients in
Arm F from Day 1).

Based on exposure-safety analysis of adverse events of interest using logistic regression
models, the probability of AE Grade 23, hyperglycemia Grade 22, and inavolisib dose
modification due to AE increased as a function of exposure parameter AUC or Cmax. In addition,
time to first instance of AE Grade 23, hyperglycemia Grade 22, and inavolisib dose modification
due to AE was shorter in patients with higher inavolisib AUC.

Exposure-safety analyses were performed based on a total of 149 patients with
model-predicted exposure parameters in the inavolisib arm from Study WO41554.

No exposure-response relationship was observed for most adverse events of interest (grade 23
AE, neutropenia Grade 22, hyperglycemia Grade 22, thrombocytopenia Grade 22, inavolisib
dose modification due to AE, diarrhea Grade 22, stomatitis Grade 22) based on logistic
regression models; lower and higher exposures of inavolisib achieved at the clinical dose (9 mg
PO QD) were associated with similar safety outcomes. The probability of anemia Grade >2 was
higher with increasing inavolisib Cmax values while there was no relationship between time to
onset of anemia and inavolisib exposure.
