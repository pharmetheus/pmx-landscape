TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

Absorption

A series of in vivo studies were conducted to characterize the pharmacokinetic (PK) and/or
toxicokinetic (TK) properties of tislelizumab in cynomolgus monkeys. A PK study was
conducted at single and repeated doses via intravenous infusion (IV). TK studies were conducted.
in single, and 13-week repeat dose toxicity studies. In addition, two single dose studies were
conducted to evaluate the comparability of PK profiles among batches produced by different
manufacturing sites during the clinical development of tislelizumab.

The results demonstrated that tislelizumab mean terminal half-life (ti2) was approximately 3 to 8
days (74 to 183 hours); maximum serum concentration (Cmax) ranged from 90 to 999 jg/mL;
area under the concentration-time curve from 0 to 1008 hours (AUC)o-1008sn ranged from 12,322
to 163,755h*\1g /mL; the volume of distribution (Va) was approximately 22 to 52 mL/kg; and the
clearance (CL) was approximately 0.18 to 0.31 mL/h/kg in monkeys at a single dose of 3, 10, or
30 mg/kg. The half-life of tislelizumab in cynomolgus monkeys supported biweekly dosing in
the repeat dose toxicology study.

The systemic exposure (Cmax and AUC) increased dose proportionally from 3 to 30 mg/kg
without apparent sex differences. No apparent drug accumulation or changes in systemic
exposure were noted after repeated doses weekly for 5 doses or every two weeks for 6 doses.

Two single dose PK studies were conducted in cynomolgus monkeys to compare the PK profiles
among batches produced by 3 different manufacturing sites during the clinical development. The
PK profiles of these batches were comparable.

Anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) were detected in most monkeys at each dose level in all studies but
did not have apparent effects on the systemic exposure.

Distribution, metabolism, and excretion studies were not conducted per ICH S6 (R1) Preclinical
Safety Evaluation of Biotechnology-Derived Pharmaceuticals.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA generally agrees.

5.5. Toxicology

5.5.1. General Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

The nonclinical toxicity and toxicokinetic profile of tislelizumab was characterized in single dose
toxicity studies in both mice and cynomolgus monkeys and in a 13-week repeat dose study in
cynomolgus monkeys. In addition, studies were conducted to evaluate tislelizumab tissue cross
reactivity, neutralizing ADA activity, and cytokine release responses.

No mortality or treatment related toxicities were noted in mice or cynomolgus monkeys at a
single dose up to 100 mg/kg via intravenous infusion.
