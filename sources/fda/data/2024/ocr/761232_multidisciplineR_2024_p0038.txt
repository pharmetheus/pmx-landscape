A431 tumor cells (2.5% 10%) and freshly prepared human PBMCs (510%) from healthy donor were co-implanted
subcutaneously in NOD/SCID mice. On the day of implantation. mice were randomly allocated into 4 groups and
treated with PBS (vehicle) or BGB-A317 ip. for 5 weeks (on day 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29) as indicated. Tumor volume
was measured twice weekly. Data are presented as mean tumor volume ~ standard error of the mean (SEM) of 10-12
animals in each group. P value was calculated using Student's t-test.
