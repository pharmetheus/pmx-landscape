The FDA’s Assessment:

Hyperglycemia is a known toxicity with PI3K inhibitors. Patients were required to have
FBG<126 mg/dL and HbA1C<6% at study entry for INAVO 120. Increased fasting glucose (based
on laboratory abnormality) occurred in 85% of patients treated with inavolisib, including Grade
3 events in 12% of patients. There were no grade 4 or 5 events or cases of diabetic ketoacidosis
(DKA) or hyperosmotic hyperglycemic nonketotic (HHNK) states. Sixty-six patients received
treatment for hyperglycemia, this included 62 patients receiving metformin and 11 patients
receiving insulin. Hyperglycemia was also managed with dose modifications, including dose
reduction in 3% of patients and dose interruption in 28% of patients. Permanent treatment
discontinuation was required in 1% of patients (2 patient) due to hyperglycemia.

Stomatitis occurred in 51% of patients treated with inavolisib, Grade 3 events in 6% of patients.
There was no grade 4 or 5 events or SAE associated with stomatitis. Stomatitis led to
interruption inavolisib in 10%, to dose reduction in 3.7%, and to discontinuation of inavolisib in
