Approximately 35-40% of patients with HR-positive breast cancer have tumors that harbor mutations in PIK3CA, the gene encoding the catalytic
subunit of the alpha isoform of PI3K. PIK3CA mutations result in upregulation of PI3K pathway and are considered a negative prognostic factor.
Alpelisib (PI3Ka inhibitor) plus fulvestrant and capivasertib (AKT inhibitor) plus fulvestrant are two treatment regimens approved by the FDA for
use in patients with PIK3CA-mutated advanced breast cancer. However, both are for second line and later treatment and are not specifically
for patients with endocrine resistant breast cancer.

The efficacy and safety assessment for inavolisib in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant is primarily based on data from INAVO120, a
randomized (1:1), placebo-controlled, double-blind, multicenter trial in 325 patients with PIK3CA mutated, HR-positive, HER2-negative (defined
as IHC 0 or 1+, or IHC 2+/ISH-), advanced or metastatic breast cancer, following recurrence on or within 12 months of adjuvant endocrine
therapy and who have not received prior systemic therapy for locally advanced or metastatic disease. PIK3CA mutation was determined
through testing of blood plasma-derived ctDNA with a next-generation sequencing (NGS) assay performed at the Sponsor-designated central
laboratory, or through local testing of ctDNA or tumor tissue with an appropriately validated NGS or PCR assay. Randomization was stratified by
presence of visceral disease (yes or no), endocrine resistance (primary or secondary), and geographic region (North America/Western Europe,
Asia, other). Primary endocrine resistance was defined as relapse while on the first 2 years of adjuvant endocrine therapy and secondary
endocrine resistance was defined as relapse while on adjuvant ET after at least 2 years or relapse within 12 months of completing adjuvant ET.
Patients were required to have a HbA1c<6% and fasting blood glucose<126 mg/dL. The study excluded patients with Type 1 diabetes mellitus or
Type 2 diabetes mellitus requiring ongoing systemic therapy at the start of study treatment.

Patients received either inavolisib 9 mg (n=161) or placebo (n=164) orally once daily, in combination with palbociclib 125 mg orally once daily
for 21 consecutive days followed by 7 days off treatment to comprise a cycle of 28 days and fulvestrant (500 mg) administered intramuscularly
on Cycle 1, Days 1 and 15, and then on Day 1 of every 28-day cycle. Patients received treatment until disease progression or unacceptable
toxicity. The primary efficacy endpoint for INAVO120 was PFS as assessed by investigator. A prospectively planned sensitivity analysis of PFS by
BICR assessment was also performed on all patients. Key secondary endpoints included OS and ORR. DOR was a secondary endpoint.
