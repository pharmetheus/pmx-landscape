REVIEW ISSUE RECOMMENDATIONS/COMMENTS
Pivotal evidence of The primary evidence of effectiveness comes from the Phase 3
effectiveness randomized, placebo-controlled trial (WO41554) in patients with

PIK3CA-mutant, HR+/HER2- locally advanced or metastatic
breast cancer. The study met the primary endpoint for
improvement in progression-free survival (PFS) with a 7.7 month
improvement in median PFS (15.0 months for inavolisib +
palbociclib + fulvestrant vs 7.3 months for placebo + palbociclib
+ fulvestrant). See Section 8.1.2 for details.

General dosing The recommended dosage is inavolisib 9 mg PO QD with or

instructions without food in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant at
their approved recommended dosages.

Dosing in patient e Renal impairment: Reduce the inavolisib starting dosage to 6

subgroups (intrinsic and mg PO QD in patients with moderate renal impairment

extrinsic factors) (estimated glomerular filtration rate [eGFR] 30 to <60

mL/min). No dosage modification is recommended in
patients with mild renal impairment (eGFR 60 to <90
mL/min). A dedicated renal impairment study is ongoing to
evaluate the safety and PK of inavolisib in participants with
severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min); a PMR will be
issued for the CSR and datasets from this study.

e Hepatic impairment: No dosage modification is
recommended in patients with mild hepatic impairment
(total bilirubin > upper limit of normal (ULN) to < 1.5 x ULN
or AST > ULN and total bilirubin < ULN). The PK and safety of
inavolisib in patients with moderate or severe hepatic
impairment has not been characterized. A PMR will be issued
for a hepatic impairment study to identify a safe dosage of
inavolisib in patients with moderate or severe hepatic
impairment.

race. Few patients were Black/African American (4/336;
1.2%), Hispanic/Latino (14/336, 4.2%), or other
underrepresented minority populations. A PMC will be
