14.5.2.2.1. SHBG at Week 52

The Applicant developed a logistic regression model to assess the potential relationship between
resmetirom exposure and ASHBG (%) at Week 52. The E-R relationship between resmetirom
Caves and the percent change from baseline SHBG (% SHBG) at Week 52 is shown in Figure 50.

production. Higher exposure to resmetirom, a THR agonist is associated with a higher level of
