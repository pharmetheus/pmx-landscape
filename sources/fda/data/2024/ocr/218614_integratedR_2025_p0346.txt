Significant reductions in white blood cells, lymphocytes, and neutrophils may lead to increased
susceptibility to infections and infestations. There were no significant differences across
treatment groups in the SOC of ‘Infections and infestations’ in Trials CS3 or CS8.

Conclusion

Small imbalances were noted with olezarsen treatment for AEs related to anemia and when
evaluating categorical changes in WBC, lymphocytes, and neutrophils in Trials CS3 and CS8.
No significant differences were noted in mean CFB in hemoglobin and WBC over time in Trials
CS3 and CS8. No clinically meaningful consequences were observed in that there were no
significant differences across treatment groups for evaluation of infections in CS3 or CS8.

Supplementary Safety Review-Renal Function

Background

Renal toxicity has been observed after administration of volanesorsen and other SC and IV
administered antisense oligonucleotides (Akcea Therapeutics 2024b).

Assessment

Renal impairment was defined per Acute Renal Failure Standardised Medical Query (narrow and
broad) and Acute Kidney Injury FMQ. In both CS3 and CS8, a higher proportion of placebo-
treated subjects compared to olezarsen-treated subjects reported a renal-impairment related
TEAE.
