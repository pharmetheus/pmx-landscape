Treatment-Related Adverse Events

In Study 302, the incidence of treatment-related adverse events, by PT, for all categories
including all grades, > Grade 3, serious, leading to treatment discontinuation or leading to dose
modification, were consistent with the overall adverse event incidences (ie, those assessed
regardless of relatedness). For all categories, a lower incidence of treatment-related adverse
events was reported in the Tislelizumab Arm than in the ICC Arm.

The Applicant’s Position:

The TEAEs with a higher incidence in the Tislelizumab Arm were consistent with the profile of
immune-mediated adverse events for tislelizumab, such as hypothyroidism and transaminase
elevation. The TEAEs with a higher incidence in the ICC Arm were consistent with the safety
profiles of paclitaxel, docetaxel, and irinotecan. The adverse events > Grade 3 findings were
consistent with the safety profile of paclitaxel, docetaxel, and irinotecan in the ICC Arm.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the overall stated incidence of TEAEs and that patients in the tislelizumab arm
had a lower incidence of Grade > 3 events compared to the patients in the ICC arm (46% vs.
68%). FDA also concurs with the description of Grade 5 TEAEs, events leading to permanent
discontinuation and dose interruptions, SAEs, imAEs, and IRRs with the exceptions already
described throughout this section. As noted above, FDA did not consider Applicant’s attribution
(“treatment-related”) when reviewing AEs. Also, based on FDA assessment, deaths due to
adverse events occurred in 18 patients in the tislelizumab arm (see Table 29, above).

See FDA Table 38, below, for FDA-calculated TEAEs occurring in Study 302. The TEAEs,
including laboratory findings, with the highest frequency (>= 20%) in the tislelizumab arm were:
increased glucose, decreased hemoglobin, decreased lymphocytes, decreased sodium, decreased
albumin, increased alkaline phosphatase, anemia, fatigue, increased AST, musculoskeletal pain,
decreased weight, increased ALT, and cough.

Additional TEAEs that were significantly elevated in the ICC arm compared to the tislelizumab
arm include alopecia (18% compared to 0%), peripheral neuropathy (13% compared to 3%),
decreased neutrophil count (39% compared to 2.4%), and decreased white blood cell count (41%
compared to 3.5%). These TEAEs are consistent with AEs from cytotoxic chemotherapy.
