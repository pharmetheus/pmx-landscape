Source: Module 2.6.2 Pharmacology Written Summary (or Module 4.2.1.1 Study 122717)

Tarlatamab bound to cynomolgus monkey DLL3 and CD3 with similar binding affinity as to
human DLL3 and CD3, and induced T-cell activation, transient cytokine production and T cell-
mediated lysis of target cells in co-cultures of cynomolgus monkey effector cells and target cells
that expressed cynomolgus monkey DLL3 comparable to that in the human system (Study
Number: 122717/ eCTD Location: Module 4.2.1.1). Furthermore, the cynomolgus monkey was
considered a relevant animal model based on similarity of DLL3 expression in cynomolgus
monkey tissues as compared with human.

Following the proposal of the FDA, a murine surrogate molecule of tarlatamab, designated
muS757, was developed to evaluate potential effects of a DLL3-targeting BITE molecule on
embryofetal development in mice. The comparability assessment of muS757 demonstrated
similar binding affinity, bioactivity (Study Numbers 122717 (tarlatamab) and 154479 (muS757) /
eCTD Location: Module 4.2.1.1), pharmacokinetic properties (Study Number 154480 / eCTD
Location: Module 4.2.2.2), and tolerability profile to tarlatamab (Study Number 153960 / eCTD
Location: Module 4.2.2.3), indicating that it is a suitable surrogate molecule for embryofetal
toxicology assessment. The FDA, ts

greed that, based on these data,
muS757 is a valid surrogate for tarlatamab for embryofetal development toxicity assessment.
