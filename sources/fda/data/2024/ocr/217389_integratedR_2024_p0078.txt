On November 27, 2019, written responses were provided to the Applicant in response to a Type
C Guidance meeting request where the Applicant was seeking guidance on their proposal for
maintaining the blind in phase 3 studies. FDA had concerms with the proposal to use a clear,
colorless vehicle as placebo due to the potential for unblinding. FDA recommended the
following: 1) the use a dose of the product that is clinically ineffective but visually similar to the
dose planned for use in phase 3 trials as placebo or 2) the development of a placebo formulation
that is similar in appearance to the drug product.
