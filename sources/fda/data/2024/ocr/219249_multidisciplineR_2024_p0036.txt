The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment, with the following caveats. At the July
2019 meeting, FDA recommended further dose optimization for inavolisib, as limited data had
been generated at doses less that 9mg. At the July 2023 meeting, FDA stated that supporting
data and adequate justification must be provided for the proposed indication as it was broader
than the patient population enrolled into the INAVO120 trial. At the February 2024 meeting,
FDA stated that adequate justification for dose selection needs to be provided and reiterated
that adequate justification must be provided for the proposed indication.

In addition, Breakthrough Therapy Designation (BTD) was granted on May 20, 2024, for the
following indication “inavolisib is in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant for the
treatment of adult patients with PIK3CA-mutated, hormone receptor (HR)-positive, human
epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative, locally advanced or metastatic breast
cancer, following recurrence on or within 12 months of completing adjuvant endocrine
treatment.” Of note, the indication granted BTD is slightly different than the Applicant
proposed indication in this NDA application.
