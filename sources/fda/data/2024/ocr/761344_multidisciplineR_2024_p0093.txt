The FDA’s Assessment:

There is limited PK data to assess the impact of PK differences relating to race (e.g.,
African American) or ethnicity (e.g., Hispanic or Latino) minorities. As described in the
PMC that will be issued with the Approval letter, the Applicant should conduct an
integrated analysis of data from clinical trial(s) to further characterize the safety,
efficacy, PK of tarlatamab among US racial and ethnic minority patients with SCLC. The
population should be representative of US population.
