Severe Renal Impairment PMR: A dedicated renal impairment study is currently
ongoing. A PMR will be issued to submit the full CSR and datasets, including data from
participants with severe renal impairment, after completion of the ongoing study.

Moderate and Severe Hepatic Impairment PMR: The effects of moderate and severe
hepatic impairment on inavolisib safety and PK have not been characterized (see
Section 6.1.2.2). Given the potential involvement of liver in inavolisib excretion, a PMR
will be issued to evaluate the effects of moderate and severe hepatic impairment on the
PK and safety of inavolisib.

Diversity PMC (see Section 8.1.1 for additional information): Patients from different
racial and ethnic groups relevant to the US patient population were not well
represented in the inavolisib development program. A PMC to evaluate inavolisib in a
diverse patient population will be issued. The PMC will include collection of clinical
pharmacology data to evaluate any potential differences in PK and PD based on race or
ethnicity.

CYP3A Induction PMC: Inavolisib increased CYP3A mRNA up to 40-fold in vitro and
available data cannot rule out potential induction effect of inavolisib on sensitive CYP3A
substrates in vivo. A clinical pharmacokinetic trial is needed to evaluate the effect of
repeat doses of inavolisib on the single dose pharmacokinetics of a sensitive CYP3A
substrate and inform appropriate management strategies for clinically relevant drug
interactions.

CYP2B6 Induction PMC: Inavolisib increased CYP2B6 mRNA up to 4-fold in vitro and
available data cannot rule out potential induction effect of inavolisib on sensitive
CYP2B6 substrates. A clinical pharmacokinetic trial is needed to evaluate the effect of
repeat doses of inavolisib on the single dose pharmacokinetics of a CYP2B6 substrate
and inform appropriate management strategies for clinically relevant drug interactions.
