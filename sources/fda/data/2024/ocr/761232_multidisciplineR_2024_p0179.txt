BLA 761232 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
TEVIMBRA - tislelizumab-jsgr

9. Advisory Committee Meeting and Other External Consultations

The FDA’s Assessment:

No advisory committee meeting was convened for this Application because the application did
not raise significant public health questions regarding the role of tislelizumab for this indication,
and outside expertise was not necessary as there were no issues that warranted the Committee’s
input. This is the second-in class product to be approved for the treatment of patients with
advanced unresectable or metastatic ESCC with disease progression after first-line treatment.
