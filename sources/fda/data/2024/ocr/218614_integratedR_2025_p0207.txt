13. Pharmacology Toxicology

Olezarsen (ISIS 678354) and/or volanesorsen (GalNAc-unconjugated form of olezarsen) were
characterized in a series of nonclinical pharmacology, pharmacokinetic, and toxicology studies.
The pharmacologic activity of olezarsen was evaluated in a series of in vitro and in vivo studies,
receptor binding activity was evaluated in a secondary pharmacology study, and safety
pharmacology studies were evaluated in cell lines and in cynomolgus monkeys.

The absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion profile was primarily characterized as a
component of the repeat-dose toxicity studies. Additionally, a series of single dose studies were
also performed in rats at various ages, in achondroplastic and wild-type mice, and by various
routes of administration.

The toxicity profile was established in a series of in vitro and in vivo studies. Repeat-dose
toxicity, fertility and early embryonic development, and genotoxicity were conducted with
olezarsen, while carcinogenicity, embryofetal development, and pre-/postnatal development were
evaluated in studies with volanesorsen, the unconjugated active antisense oligonucleotide (ASO).
