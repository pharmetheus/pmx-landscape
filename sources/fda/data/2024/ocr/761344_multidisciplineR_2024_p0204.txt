NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761344}
{Tarlatamab}

The Applicant's Position:

Review of fatal adverse events across the tarlatamab clinical development program as of the
data cutoff dates did not identify any meaningful trends suggesting a causal association
between tarlatamab use and fatalities.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA confirmed the information above and reviewed all narratives for on-treatment deaths in
the pooled safety population. There were 22 fatal events which were attributed to disease
progression. Narratives were provided for 10 patients who had fatal adverse events not due to
disease progression who were treated with tarlatamab at the recommended dose of 10 mg; 5
of these events occurred more than 30 days after discontinuing study therapy (all n=1,
pneumonia, necrotizing pneumonia, dyspnea, hepatic failure and myocardial infarction).

The narratives for the five patients with on-treatment deaths not due to disease progression

that were within 30 days of discontinuation of study treatment are reviewed in further detail
below.
