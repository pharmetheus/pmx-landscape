NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761344}
{Tarlatamab}

12 Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies (REMS)

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Division of Oncology 2 (DO2) worked closely with the Division of Risk Management (DRM)
and were aligned in the conclusion that a risk evaluation and mitigation strategy (REMS) was
not necessary to mitigate the risks of cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and neurologic toxicity
including immune-effector cell associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS) associated with
tarlatamab-dlle. The Applicant did not propose a REMS; however, the serious risks associated
with tarlatamab warranted further review. Similar to other BiTE (bispecific T-cell engager)
molecules, tarlatamab is associated with CRS and neurotoxicity including ICANS..

Background

Imdelltra (tarlatamab-dlle), a new molecular entity submitted by Amgen Inc., is a bispecific
delta-like ligand 3 (DLL3)-directed CD3 T-cell engager proposed for the treatment of adult
patients with extensive stage small cell lung cancer (SCLC) with disease progression on or after
platinum-based chemotherapy. Tarlatamab is administered as an intravenous (IV) infusion over
one hour. The first full dose is administered following a step-up dose to reduce the incidence
and severity of CRS. After the step-up dose, tarlatamab is administered every 2 weeks until
disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
