NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761344}
{Tarlatamab}

6.3.2.3 Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions, and
what is the appropriate management strategy?

Data:

Based on molecular size and route of administration of tarlatamab, no formal food-drug
or drug-drug interaction studies were required and therefore not performed.

The Applicant's Position:

Not applicable

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with Applicant on that a food effect is not anticipated given tarlatamab is a
large molecule and is administered intravenously.

FDA agrees with Applicant’s position on that no formal drug-drug interaction studies are
required. Based on FDA’s evaluation of literature and understanding of postulated
mechanism of IL-6 mediated CYP suppression, the drug interaction risk between CYP
substrates and tarlatamab is low. Cytokine levels peaked 24 hours after the tarlatamab
first dose administration with peak median concentration around 26 pg/mL. However,
the risk of drug interaction during and after any CRS cannot be ruled out. Therefore,
additional labeling language is provided in Section 12.3 of USPI that tarlatamab may
increase exposure of concomitant CYP substrates during and up to 14 days after
