Drug-drug interactions

Tarlatamab causes transient release of cytokines
during CRS that may suppress cytochrome (CYP) 450
enzymes and may result in an increased exposure of
concomitant CYP substrate; however, based on FDA’s
evaluation of literature and IL-6 concentrations, the
risk of drug interaction for concomitant CYP
substrates with tarlatamab appears to be low in the
overall patient population. For addition detail, refer
section 6.3.1.

Immunogenicity

In Study 20200491, 3.2% (4/124) of patients
developed ADA and none of the patients developed
neutralizing antibodies (Nab). Because of the low
occurrence, the effect of ADA on the PK, PD, safety,
and effectiveness of tarlatamab is inconclusive.

Labeling

Overall, the proposed labeling recommendations are
acceptable upon Applicant’s agreement with the FDA
recommended labeling revisions.
