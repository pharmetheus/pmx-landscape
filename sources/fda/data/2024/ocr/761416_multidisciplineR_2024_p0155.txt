Model Structure

The final model for the probability of confirmed OR was
a logistic regression model on the logit scale including
an intercept and a power function of Cycle 1 Cmin.

The final model for PFS was a Cox proportional hazard
model including the effect of daily Cag and the
covariate effect of HER2 status (3+ versus 2+/1+/0).
