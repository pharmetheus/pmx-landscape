Increases in scores for index score are deteriorations for OES18.

Median time to clinically meaningful worsening in GHS and physical functioning per EORTC
QLQC30 and dysphagia, eating, reflux, and pain per EORTC QLQOES18 was not reached in
either arm using the >10 point threshold. However, in the ITT population, the TTD analysis
showed patients treated with tislelizumab were at a lower risk for clinically meaningful
worsening of key symptoms of dysphagia (stratified HR of 0.76 [95% CI: 0.53, 1.07], P=
0.0562), reflux (0.50 [95% CI: 0.32, 0.80], P = 0.0014) as well as worsening of physical function
(0.67 [95% CI: 0.45, 1.00], P = 0.0239) through the course of treatment. Meanwhile, the PD-L1
vCPS > 10% patients treated with tislelizumab showed to be at lower risk for pain (0.52 [95%
CI: 0.24, 1.13], P = 0.0460).
