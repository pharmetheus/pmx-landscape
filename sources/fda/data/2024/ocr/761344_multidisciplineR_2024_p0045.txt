Overall, the large number of underreported AEs by the Korean sites, not captured during the
Applicant’s monitoring activities, was a major concern and put into question the integrity of the
safety data submitted to support the approval of tarlatamab for the proposed indication.

unreported AE’s were < Grade 2 with Grade 1 events of CRS and ICANS in 2 patients each
