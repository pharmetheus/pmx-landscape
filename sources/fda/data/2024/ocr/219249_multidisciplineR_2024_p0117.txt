The Applicant's Position: The population from Arms B, E, and F of Study GO39374 are
representative of P/K3CA-mutated, HR+, HER2— LA/mBC patients receiving an endocrine-based
combination therapy in a later line setting.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the demographic characteristics presented for Arms B, E, and F of GO39374
presented in Table 9. Similar to INAVO120, there were very few Black or Hispanic patients
enrolled on GO39374.

Other Baseline Characteristics (e.g., disease characteristics, important concomitant drugs)

Data: All patients enrolled in Arm B (N = 33), Arm E (N = 20), and Arm F (N = 21) had to have
histologically documented, P/K3CA-mutated, HR+, HER2— LA/mBC.

At baseline, the majority of patients in Arm B (93.9%), Arm E (95.0%), and all 21 patients (100%)
in Arm F had at least one site of metastatic disease. The most common sites of metastatic
disease were bone (Arm B: 66.7%; Arm E: 80.0%; Arm F: 76.2%), liver (Arm B: 54.5%; Arm E:
30.0%; Arm F: 61.9%), and lung (Arm B: 39.4%; Arm E: 30.0%, Arm F: 19.0%).

The majority of patients in Arm B (97.0%), Arm E (60.0%), and all 21 patients in Arm F had
received prior systemic anti-cancer therapy in the metastatic setting before study enrollment.
Among these patients, the median line of prior therapy in the metastatic setting was 2 lines
(range: 1-4 lines) for Arm B, 1 line (range: 1-4 lines) for Arm E, and 3 lines (range: 1-9 lines) for
Arm F. In Arm B, 21.2% had received prior treatment with a CDK4/6 inhibitor. No patients in
Arm E had received prior CDK4/6 inhibitor as per eligibility requirement, while 66.7% of
patients in Arm F had received a prior CDK4/6 inhibitor.
