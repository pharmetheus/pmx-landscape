populations and was also the most common reason for patients requiring a dose reduction (N=2
Cohort 1 Study 203, N=3 pooled safety). Five patients (2.1%) required dose reduction in the

decreased weight [Cohort 1 Study 203], N=1 ejection fraction decreased [pooled safety
population]).
