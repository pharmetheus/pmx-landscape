Validation of a Method for the determination of BGB-A317 in Human Serum using
Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay (ELISA) (Report 8354-363)

hyperlinks
This assay method employs the quantitative indirect enzyme immunoassay technique.
The tislelizumab antigen, human PD-1 (PDCD1) protein, is coated on a 96-well
immunoplate and then blocked using a nonspecific protein-blocking buffer. Samples
for calibration and quality control, and clinical study samples are pipetted into the
Method description wells. Tislelizumab is bound to the immobilized human PD-1 (PDCD1) in the

immunoplate. After washing away any unbound substrate, a horseradish peroxidase
(HRP)-conjugated goat antihuman IgG (fragment crystallizable [Fc] region specific)
secondary antibody is added to the wells. Then after a second washing, HRP conjugate
bound complexes are quantified by catalysis of color development using the
chromogenic substrate, tetramethylbenzidine, with a spectrophotometer.
