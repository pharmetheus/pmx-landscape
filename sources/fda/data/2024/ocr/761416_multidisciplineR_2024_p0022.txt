Study 203 enrolled 80 patients in Cohort 1; 62 (77.5%) patients had
ISH-amplified, IHC3+ tumors and 18 (22.5%) patients had IHC2+
tumors. Study participant demographics were as follows: median age 64
years (range 32-79) and 49% were age 65 or older; 56% female and 44%
male; 65% Asian, 29% White. ECOG performance status was 0 (28%)
or 1 (73%). Fifty-one percent of patients had gallbladder cancer, 29%
had intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma, and 20% had extrahepatic
cholangiocarcinoma. Nine (11%) patients had Stage 3 disease and the
remaining 71 (89%) patients had metastatic disease at study entry. Forty-
one percent of patients had received more than one prior line of therapy
for metastatic or locally advanced disease.
