5) Formulation Bridging
Multiple formulations were used during the course of development, including Phase 1, Phase 1
resupply, and Phase 3 formulations. The proposed to-be-marketed formulation is identical to
the Phase 3 formulation with the exception of the coating. Minor changes were made between
these formulations as well as the manufacturing process. The Applicant bridged these changes
by in vitro dissolution studies. Human bioequivalence studies were not needed to support the
bioequivalence of these formulations, particularly the Phase 3 formulation and proposed
commercial formulation. Refer to the Biopharmaceutics review for details.
