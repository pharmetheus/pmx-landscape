The Applicant's Position:

In both Studies 20160232 and 20200491, tarlatamab exhibited approximate
dose-proportional increases in exposures in the evaluated dose range. The steady state
in tarlatamab serum concentrations were approximately achieved by the start of cycle 2.
The mean (SD) estimated terminal phase elimination half-life at steady state was 5.5
(1.6) days, across the evaluated dose range. The incidence of antitarlatamab antibody
development was low. There was no meaningful clinical impact of antitarlatamab
antibody development on efficacy and safety.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position regarding the characteristics of
clinical pharmacology and pharmacokinetics of tarlatamab as summarized in Table 16.
