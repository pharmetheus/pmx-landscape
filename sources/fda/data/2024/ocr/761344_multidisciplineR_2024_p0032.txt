The pooled safety population included 187 patients
from Study 20200491 and Study 20160323 with
extensive stage SCLC who were treated with
tarlatamab at a step dose of 1 mg on Cycle Day 1
followed by the proposed dose of 10 mg on Cycle 1
Day 8, Cycle 1 Day 15 and every 2 weeks thereafter.

The observed safety profile is considered acceptable when
assessed in the context of a life-threatening disease. The
