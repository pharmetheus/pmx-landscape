Model Structure

The relationships between safety and inavolisib exposure were explored
using Kaplan-Meier curves and Cox proportional hazard models for the
time to event endpoints (time to onset for the AE endpoints), and logistic
regressions for the binary endpoints (the AE endpoints). The Firth
correction was implemented in the logistic regression when required to
reduce the bias of maximum /likelihood estimates where small samples
