Drug-related Findings:

malformations, increased skeletal and visceral variations, delayed skeletal ossifications).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s summary of the study results described above.

FDA also agrees that fertility and early embryonic development and pre- and postnatal
toxicology studies are not warranted to support the proposed indication in patients with

advanced cancer in accordance with ICH S9 guidance.
