The Applicant’s Position:
The safety profile of tislelizumab is similar across demographic subgroups (age, gender, race,

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s analysis. However, data based on demographic subgroups
should be interpreted with caution as these analyses are exploratory in nature. Additionally, 21%
of patients in the study were enrolled outside of Asia, which further reduces strength of
conclusions based on demographic groups.
