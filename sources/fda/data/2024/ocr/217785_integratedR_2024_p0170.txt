Assessment

The review team considered this case to be at least possible, if not probable, DILI due to
resmetirom; this case might also fit with DI-ALH. Whereas IgG levels can be elevated in
cirrhosis, the 50% increase in IgG, seroconversion to a positive ASMA (albeit low titer) and
decline in liver analytes even before immunosuppression are suggestive of a mild DILI with AIH
features rather than de novo AIH. Indeed, the subject may not have required immunosuppression
as her liver enzymes had improved substantially before it was initiated (Figure 10). A liver
biopsy would have been helpful but was not performed. The local provider initially chose
azathioprine for immunosuppression, and subsequently switched to mycophenolate without
explanation.
