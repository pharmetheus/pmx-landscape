19.4.3. Population PK Analysis
19.4.3.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The PPK analysis supports the proposed dose of 1 mg on day 1, followed by 10 mg on day 8, 15
and every 2 weeks thereafter in patients with advanced SCLC with disease progression on or
after platinum-based chemotherapy. No clinically relevant differences in exposure were
identified according to patient characteristics, including age, bodyweight, sex, race (Asian and
White), mild or moderate renal impairment, or mild hepatic impairment. The effect of severe
renal impairment and moderate to severe hepatic impairment on the PK of tarlatamab are
unknown due to data limitation.
