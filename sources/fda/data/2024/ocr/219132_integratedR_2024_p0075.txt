change threshold, the Agency disagrees with proposed clinically meaningful within-subject
change threshold @ in the mSARA.

The Agency conducted an independent assessment of clinical meaningfulness of the primary
efficacy endpoint results based on the fSARA by performing responder analyses, quantitative
analyses at the individual subject level, and evaluating exploratory endpoint evidence from exit
surveys.
