2) PPI

As described in section 6.1.2.2, based on popPK analysis of inavolisib PK in patient with self-
reported PPI use or no PPI use, no clinically significant difference in steady state inavolisib
pharmacokinetics were observed based upon concomitant use of a proton pump inhibitor
(lansoprazole, omeprazole, esomeprazole, pantoprazole, or rabeprazole). Refer to Section
6.2.2.2 for details.

3) DDlas an Object and Precipitant

Based on the mass balance study and in vitro evaluations, DDI potential for inavolisib as an
object is low given the lack of CYP metabolism observed in vivo. Inavolisib increased CYP3A and
CYP2B6 mRNA in vitro. Induction effect on CYP3A and CYP2B6 in vivo cannot be ruled out;
therefore, PMCs will be issued to conduct a clinical study or studies to assess inavolisib
induction potential on substates of CYP3A and CYP2B6. If inavolisib is confirmed to be at least a
weak inducer of CYP3A, the induction potential on CYP2C enzymes will need to be addressed.
Refer to Section 6.1.2.2.
