Findings From In Vitro Hollow-Fiber Study

To further support the proposed dosage of 500 mg sulopenem etzadroxil/500 mg probenecid BID
for the treatment of uUTI, the Applicant evaluated the bactericidal activity of urinary
concentrations associated with the proposed dosage. The simulated urinary concentrations were
demonstrated to suppress the growth of four £. coli isolates (with sulopenem MIC values in the
clinically relevant range of 0.03 to 0.5 g/mL) and prevented the development of resistance for
the entire duration of a 5-day study in a hollow-fiber infection model (see Section 14).

6.1.2. Support for Proposed Dose/Contribution of
Probenecid

Probenecid lacks antimicrobial activity; instead, it inhibits OAT3-mediated renal clearance of B-
lactams such as sulopenem, thus resulting in increased plasma drug concentrations. Multiple-
dose administration of 500 mg probenecid increased sulopenem plasma area under the
concentration-time curve (AUC) by 1.8-fold with a corresponding 1.9-fold reduction in renal

dosage in the treatment of uUTIs.
