NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

In the clinical trial GO39374, palbociclib was co-administered with 9-mg QD inavolisib along
with letrozole. The steady state PK parameters of palbociclib in this study were mostly within
30% of the values published in the Palbociclib FDA Clinical Pharmacology Review (NDA 207103
Review, 2014). During inavolisib model verification, the Applicant simulated palbociclib PK in
this study using the induction parameters generated from lot 1198 together with CYP3A TDI
parameters generated in human liver microsomes. The ability of the simulations to capture the
steady state palbociclib PK profile was used as the evidence that the CYP3A interaction
parameters were adequately established (Figure 21). The reviewer performed additional
simulations to examine the effects of inavolisib on palbociclib PK when inavolisib behaved as a
CYP3A inducer. These simulations were performed using the Applicant’s palbociclib model or
the reviewer-modified model (see Methods) and the induction parameters generated from lot
FOS but without the CYP3A TDI parameters. The results showed that the steady-state
palbociclib PK profiles and parameters were not significantly changed (Figure 21 and Table 55)
even when inavolisib was predicted to reduce midazolam AUC close to 50% under these
conditions (Table 56). Therefore, capturing the steady state palbociclib PK profiles is not
sufficient evidence to verify the CYP3A interaction parameters.

Of note, although cytotoxicity was not observed in hepatocytes from lot 1198 and lot 1421,
crystallization was observed from cell morphology at 100 1M inavolisib, which may be the
potential reason for the reduced response observed at 100 1M compared to 10 1M, the second
highest concentration (Report 16-0131). The maximum induction responses of the hepatocytes
from lot 1198 and lot 1421 to inavolisib were not reached, which may affect the accuracy of
induction parameter estimation.

Taken together, the effect of inavolisib on CYP3A substrates cannot be confidently predicted.
The potential induction effect of inavolisib on CYP3A cannot be ruled out.
