A. Efficacy data in subjects who met the criteria for early amyloid clearance

(<24.1 CL) at Week 24: A statistically significant reduction in clinical decline on
both iADRS and CDR-SB at Week 76 was observed in a subset of patients who
met the criteria for early amyloid clearance (<24.1 CL) at Week 24 (Figure 8). The
magnitude of donanemab treatment effect in this subset at week 76 was
comparable to the overall population. However, this subset of patients also
included participants who continued to receive donanemab treatment atleast until
week 52.
