Gastrointestinal Tract

Sulopenem etzadroxil-related findings in gastrointestinal (GI) tissues were observed in the 14-
day combination-toxicology study with sulopenem etzadroxil plus probenecid in rats (mixed cell
infiltrates, hyperplasia of glandular epithelium and edema in the large intestine) and the 1-month
study in monkeys (minimal mucosal hyperplasia). The sulopenem etzadroxil doses at which GI
effects were observed in the 14-day combination toxicology study in rats (800 mg/kg/day) and
the 1-month study in monkeys (400 mg/kg/day) are approximately 7- and 6-times, respectively,
the clinical sulopenem exposure for the maximum recommended human dose of sulopenem
etzadroxil based on plasma AUC comparison (see Sections 5.4, 5.5.1 and 5.5.2 in the NDA
213972 Multidisciplinary Review dated July 22, 2021).

Immune/Hematopoietic Systems

In the 1-month toxicology study, monkeys also exhibited positive Coombs test results that tended
to increase with the sulopenem etzadroxil dose and likely contributed to antibody mediation of
minimal erythrocyte loss and compensatory erythropoiesis in monkeys. The lowest sulopenem
etzadroxil dose (400 mg/kg/day) at which these effects were observed is approximately 6-times
the clinical sulopenem exposure for the maximum recommended human dose of sulopenem
etzadroxil based on plasma AUC comparison (see Sections 5.4 and 5.5.1 in the NDA 213972
Multidisciplinary Review dated July 22, 2021).
