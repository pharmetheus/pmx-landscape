Effect of Test Parameters on MIC Values

The effect of different test parameters on the activity of sulopenem was evaluated using QC
isolates from American Type Culture Collections (ATCC). The evaluated parameters included:
test medium cation content, test medium pH, inoculum density, inclusion of lysed horse blood
in test medium, and incubation atmosphere (ambient vs. 5% CO,).

Effect of Divalent Cations on the Activity of Sulopenem

The in vitro activity of sulopenem was tested using cation-adjusted Mueller-Hinton broth
(CAMHB) containing varying concentrations of calcium and magnesium cations against the four
QC strains, E. coli ATCC 25922, P. aeruginosa ATCC 27853, E. faecalis ATCC 29212, and S. aureus
ATCC 29213. Sulopenem did not appear to show significant variation in the in vitro MIC values.
(Table 161).

Effect of Medium pH on the Activity of Sulopenem

Sulopenem activity was maintained against the four QC strains over the pH range from 6.0 to
8.0 with little variation compared to the standard MIC testing at pH 7.2 — 7.4 (Table 162).
