19.4.4.3. Results
The FDA’s Assessment:
1. Can the PBPK model adequately describe the PK profiles of inavolisib?

Yes. The model could reasonably well describe the plasma concentration-time profiles of
inavolisib following single and multiple oral doses of inavolisib in patients with cancer, and the
model-estimated AUC and Cmax were mostly within 0.8- and 1.25-fold of the observed values.
Simulated and observed inavolisib PK profiles and parameters are summarized in Figure 19
Figure 20 and Table 52.
