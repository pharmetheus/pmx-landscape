19.4.1. Bioanalytical Methods

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA finds the Applicant’s bioanalytical method validation and performance acceptable to
determine the concentration of inavolisib, fulvestrant, palbociclib, and letrozole.

The Applicant submitted the following method validation and cross-validation reports:
1) Validation of a Method for the Determination of GDC-0011 in Human Plasma by HPLC
with MS/MS Detection; report No. 21-0686

2) Validation of a Method for the Determination of Fulvestrant in Human Plasma by HPLC
with MS/MS Detection; report No. 11-0261

3) Validation of a Method for the Determination of Palbociclib in Human Plasma by HPLC
with MS/MS Detection; report No. 15-3983

4) Validation of a Method for the Determination of Letrozole in Human Plasma by HPLC
with MS/MS Detection; report No. 8264525

5) Cross Validation of Two Methods ©® forthe
analysis of GDC-0077 in Human Plasma by HPLC with MS/MS Detection, report No. 22-
