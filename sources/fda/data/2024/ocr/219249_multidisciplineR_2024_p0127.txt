The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA’s review of safety population for this application is primarily from the INAVO120 Trial
(W0O41554). FDA agrees that the exposure is adequate to characterize safety of inavolisib. The
median relative dose intensity of inavolisib in the Inavot+Palbo+Fulv arm (95.8%) of the
INAVO120 (WO41554) trial was high. In addition, the median relative dose intensity of
palbociclib and fulvestrant was similar between the two treatment arms of the INAVO120
(W041554) trial; therefore, the triplet combination therapy including inavolisib did not have a
significant impact on the ability to administer full dose of palbociclib+fulvestrant.

In depth exposure analyses were not conducted by FDA for the GO39374 trial.

Relevant characteristics of the safety population:

Data and the Applicant’s Position: The safety population in WO41554 was similar to FAS. Refer
to Section 8.1.1 for the summary of demographics and baseline characteristics in the FAS
population for WO41554 and SAS population for GO39374, and Section 8.1 for an overview of
the eligibility criteria. Study GO39374 enrolled patients with broader HbAic and fasting blood
glucose (FBG) criteria to allow for the characterization of the risk of hyperglycemia in patients
with underlying risk factors.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the safety population for the INAVO120 trial
(W0O41554) was similar to the FAS. FDA agrees the safety population for GO39374 is the same
as the SAS population summarized in Section 8.1.1.
