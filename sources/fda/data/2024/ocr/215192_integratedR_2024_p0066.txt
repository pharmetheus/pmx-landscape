In Study Cl-0036, the incidence rate of HF was higher in the vadadustat arms (both QD and TIW)
compared to the darbepoetin arm (Table 36). In Study Cl-0039, the incidence rate of HF was
higher in the vadadustat arms (both 600 mg and 900 mg) compared to the Epoetin beta arm
(Table 37). The relatively small number of patients enrolled in Study Cl-0036 and CI-0039 limits
conclusions. Heart failure events were identified using the cardiac failure MedDRA SMQ
narrow (similar approach used for Studies Cl-0016 and CI-0017).

Conclusion: The rates of HF were higher in the vadadustat groups compared to the ESA groups
in Studies Cl-0036 and CI-0039. Results are difficult to interpret given the small sample size and
alternative dosing regimens. The risk of HF leading to hospitalization was not observed in larger
pivotal trials (studies Cl-0016 and Cl-0017) therefore, it is unlikely that heart failure is a risk
related to vadadustat (See Section 5.2 for further details). More likely, events of heart failure in
Studies Cl-0036 and Cl -0039 are due to comorbidities and underlying condition of CKD.
