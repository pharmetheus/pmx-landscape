The primary efficacy endpoint in the Phase 3 study (PRECISION)* was the change from
baseline at Week 4 of the double-blind treatment period in mean trough SiSBP. The reviewer
conducted an E-R analysis between AUC,, (estimated for the 12.5 mg and 25 mg dose based on

shown in Figure 27, the E-R relationship appears to be flat over the 12.5 mg and 25 mg dose
