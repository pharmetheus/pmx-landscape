The patient was a 70-year-old male received

Adverse events that occurred within a +/- 7-

Grade 2 dyspnea. Concomitant medications

and up to 3 days prior to event onset
included dexamethasone, and sodium
chloride. The patient died on Day 17 from
Grade 5 respiratory acidosis.

This patient was a 68-year-old male who
died on Day 2 of treatment from Grade 5
broncoaspiration. Adverse events that also
occurred within the onset of the SAE
included Grade 3 asthenia, Grade 2 balance
disorder, Grade 2 cytokine release
syndrome, and Grade 1 hyperuricaemia.
Concomitant medications taken at the onset
of the aspiration and up to 3 days prior to
event onset included dexamethasone,
enalapril, intravenous solutions,
levothyroxine, midazolam, morphine,
simvastatin, tinzaparin, and urea.

The patient died on Day 2 after receiving
treatment from Grade 5 bronchoaspiration.

The history and
