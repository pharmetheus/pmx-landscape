8.2.8. | Specific Safety Studies/Clinical Trials

Data and the Applicant’s Position: The clinical safety data supporting evaluation of the intended
indication is primarily derived from the pivotal Phase III Study WO41554, with supportive data
derived from the Phase | Study GO39374. No studies were conducted to evaluate a specific
safety concern.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the safety data supporting the intended
indication is primarily from the INAVO120 trial (WO41554). GO39374 was not reviewed in
depth by the FDA, as it was not considered to support the intended indication.

8.2.9. | Additional Safety Explorations
Human Carcinogenicity or Tumor Development

The Applicant's Position: No carcinogenicity studies with inavolisib have been conducted. No
carcinogenicity studies are currently planned because such studies are not required for drugs
intended for advanced cancer patients per ICH-S9 (DHHS 2010).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

Human Reproduction and Pregnancy

The Applicant’s Position: There were no pregnancies reported in patients treated with inavolisib
in either of the two studies. No fetus should be exposed to inavolisib. Because inavolisib will
likely cross the placental membrane, inavolisib should not be administered to pregnant women.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

Pediatrics and Assessment of Effects on GrowthData and the Applicant’s Position: The
Applicant received the Agreed Initial Pediatric Study Plan (iPSP) Agreement for Study WO41554
from the Agency on 12 September 2022. Further details are in Module 1 of the dossier.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
