The grand majority (42/47; 89%) of patients received standard treatment as the following are
considered either preferred or other recommended regimens in the US: CAV, gemcitabine plus
paclitaxel, lurbinectedin, paclitaxel, anti-PD-(L)1 antibody, temozolomide, and platinum
retreatment with or without anti-PD-(L)1 therapy. The ORR is 36% in these 42 patients.
Although amrubicin is not approved in the US, clinical trials have shown comparable efficacy
between amrubicin and topotecan (von Pawel, 2014). In addition, upon excluding patients with
either unknown platinum sensitivity or platinum resistant disease who received platinum +/-
PD-L1 rechallenge (n=3 and n=3, respectively) and patients who received amrubicin (n=5), the
ORR is 31% in the remaining 36 patients.

From this analysis, the majority of patients received second line treatments that are consistent
with standard of care in the United States. Analysis of the ORR in patients who received prior
topoisomerase | inhibitor, as well as in patients who did not receive prior topoisomerase |
inhibitor (including and not including patients who received other standard of care therapies)
were generally consistent with the response rate observed for the overall study population.
Furthermore, it is not expected that treatment with additional therapies following platinum-
based chemotherapy would increase the likelihood of response to tarlatamab relative to
treatment with tarlatamab used as second-line therapy following platinum-based
chemotherapy. In light of the observed results and particularly considering the ORR observed
with tarlatamab in patients with platinum-resistant SCLC, which far exceeds ORR reported with
standard of care therapies, the available data for tarlatamab supports the proposed indication
for treatment of SCLC with disease progression on or after platinum-based chemotherapy.

Lastly, FDA notes that subgroup analyses for time-to-event endpoints such as PFS and OS, and
PRO endpoints, are difficult to interpret in the absence of an appropriate control arm.
