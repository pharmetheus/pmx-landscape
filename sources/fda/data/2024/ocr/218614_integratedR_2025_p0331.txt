Abbreviations: HDL-C, high-density lipoprotein cholesterol

HDL-C helps transport cholesterol levels from the bloodstream and tissues back to the liver,
where it can be processed and removed from the body. In observational studies, higher levels of
HDL-C are associated with a lower risk of heart disease. However, human genetics and failed
clinical trials have created uncertainties about targeting HDL-C to reduce CV risk (Rader and
Hovingh 2014). As shown in Figure 76 above, treatment with olezarsen (both doses) increased
levels of HDL-C as compared to placebo. However, the clinical implications of the increases in
HDL-C in the FCS population are unknown.
