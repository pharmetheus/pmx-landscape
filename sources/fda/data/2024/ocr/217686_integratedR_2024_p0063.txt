NDA 217686
TRYVIO (aprocitentan)

7. Safety (Risk and Risk Management)

7.1. Potential Risks or Safety Concerns Based on
Nonclinical Data

Nonelinical studies, including safety pharmacology studies, general toxicology studies, a full
battery of genetic toxicology studies, and a partial battery of developmental and reproductive
toxicology studies were conducted with aprocitentan. The Applicant also provided a letter of
authorization referencing relevant data submitted to NDA 214410 for macitentan. Key
information on the reproductive safety of aprocitentan was provided by the developmental and
reproductive toxicology studies conducted previously with macitentan. Similarly, the
carcinogenicity assessment for aprocitentan was based on studies conducted with macitentan.
