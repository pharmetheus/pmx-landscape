The FDA's Assessment:

FDA reviewed the financial disclosures for all study investigators. Of the 689 investigators
participating in Study 20200491 (as stated above by the Applicant), 679 investigators (99%) did
not have significant financial disclosures to report; 2 investigators (0.3%) reported significant
financial disclosures, while the Applicant did not have complete financial disclosure information
on 8 investigators (1.2%).

Regarding the two investigators from the same site who reported significant financial
disclosures, both indicated disclosable interest according to 21 CFR 54.2(f) (significant
payments of other sorts means payments made by the Applicant of a covered study to the
investigator or the institution to support activities of the investigator that have a monetary
value of more than $25,000, exclusive of the costs of conducting the clinical study or other
clinical studies, (e.g., a grant to fund ongoing research, compensation in the form of equipment
or retainers for ongoing consultation or honoraria) during the time the clinical investigator is
carrying out the study and for 1 year following the completion of the study). However, the
