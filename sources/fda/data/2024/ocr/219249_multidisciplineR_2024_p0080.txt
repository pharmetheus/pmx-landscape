{Inavolisib}

In the mass balance study, >99% of the drug-related material in plasma was parent inavolisib
and the only CYP-mediate metabolite identified in vitro (M2) was not observed. Therefore, DDI
potential between inavolisib and CYP inhibitors or inducers is considered low given the lack of
CYP metabolism observed in vivo.

ii. Transporter inhibitors or inducers

In vitro, inavolisib is a substrate of P-gp and BCRP but not a substrate of MATE1, MATE2-K,
OAT1, OAT3, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OCT1, and OCT2. FDA agrees with the Applicant that the DDI
potential of inavolisib with P-gp or BCRP inhibitors is low given that the absolute oral
bioavailability of inavolisib was 76%.

iii. | Proton pump inhibitors

Inavolisib exhibits pH-dependent solubility with reduced solubility at higher pH conditions.
Based on reviewer’s repeat analysis, patients with self-reported concurrent proton pump
inhibitor (PPI) use in Study GO339374 (n=19) showed similar inavolisib steady state Cmax and
AUCo.24 to patients who self-reported no PPI use (n=56 for Cmax; n=54 for AUCo24) (Figure
6).This analysis supports the conclusion that concomitant administration of a PPI does not have
clinically meaningful impact on inavolisib PK exposure.
