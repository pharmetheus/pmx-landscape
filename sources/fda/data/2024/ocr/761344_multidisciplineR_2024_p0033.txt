Conclusions and Reasons

The key safety concerns for tarlatamab are cytokine
release syndrome (CRS) which occurred in 55% of
patients and neurologic toxicity, including immune
effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome
(ICANS) which occurred in 47% of patients. Other
safety concerns include hepatotoxicity, infections,
cytopenias, and hypersensitivity.

pharmacovigilance and a Dear Healthcare Provider letter
are non-REMS adjuncts recommended to help convey
these risks.

A post-marketing requirement (PMR) will also be issued to
submit the final study report and datasets from a
multicenter randomized clinical trial intended to verify and
describe the clinical benefit of tarlatamab in patients with
ES-SCLC who have had disease progression on or after
platinum-based chemotherapy. An additional PMR to
characterize the long-term incidence, severity, and
outcome of the known serious risks of CRS, ICANS and
neurologic toxicity will also be issued.
