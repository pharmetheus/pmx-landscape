The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, the two arms were well balanced across baseline characteristics. This was a
multinational study with just 16 patients (4.9%) enrolled from the US. Almost all patients were
female (98%) and the median age of 54 years for the patient population is younger than the
median age (62 years old) at the time of breast cancer diagnosis in the US (cancer. org).

The majority of patients on study were White. Racial and ethnic minorities reflective of the U.S.
population of patients with breast cancer were significantly underrepresented in INAVO120
(W041554), with only two Black patients and 20 Hispanic/Latino patients enrolled on study.
Due to the lack of diversity in the enrolled population, a Post Marketing Commitment (PMC) is
being issued to further characterize the efficacy and safety of inavolisib for the treatment of
breast cancer in study populations whose demographics are more representative of the
population of U.S. patients for the proposed indication.

Baseline Characteristics

The majority of patients (60%) were post-menopausal; the remainder (38.2%) were “not post-
menopausal” (i.e., pre-/peri-menopausal); six males (1.8%) enrolled and all were recorded with
menopausal status of “missing”. Approximately half of patients (47.1%) had a body mass index
(BMI) in the normal range (>18.5 kg/m? to <25.0 kg/m2). The majority of patients had a
baseline HbAic of <5.7% (88.0%) and fasting glucose of <100 mg/dl (71.1%).
