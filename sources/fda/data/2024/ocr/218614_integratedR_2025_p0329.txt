Non-HDL-C is a measure of all the cholesterol carried by lipoproteins other than HDL. Non-
HDL-C includes LDL-C, VLDL-C, and intermediate-density lipoprotein cholesterol. As shown
in Figure 74 above, non-HDL-C levels decreased with olezarsen 80 mg to a greater degree than
placebo. Although non-HDL-C does not measure TG directly, high levels of TG often correlate
with elevated VLDL cholesterol, and lower levels of TG correlate with lower levels of VLDL-C,
which is part of the non-HDL-C value.
