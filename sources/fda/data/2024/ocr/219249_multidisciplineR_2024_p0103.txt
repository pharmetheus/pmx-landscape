8.1.2.1. Pivotal Study: WO41554 (INAVO120), Phase III
Patient Disposition
Data: Patients were recruited from 123 centers across 28 countries (Table 19).

The FAS population was comprised of a total of 325 patients: 161 patients in the

Inavo + Palbo + Fulv arm and 164 patients in the Pbo + Palbo + Fulv arm. One patient was
randomized to the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv arm but withdrew consent to participate in the study
prior to dosing with any study drug. At the CCOD (29 Sep 2023) for the primary analysis, the
median duration of follow-up for all patients was 21.3 months and was similar in both arms. A
lower proportion of patients in the Inavo + Palbo + Fulv arm (58.4%) in comparison to the
Pbo + Palbo + Fulv arm (70.1%) had discontinued from all study treatment. Across individual
study treatments, the most common reason for study drug discontinuation was progressive
disease, which occurred at higher frequency in the Pbo + Palbo + Fulv arm.
