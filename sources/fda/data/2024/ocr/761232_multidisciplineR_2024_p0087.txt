Patient Disposition

Data:

A total of 512 patients were randomized 1:1 to receive tislelizumab or ICC (256 patients each) in
the ITT Analysis Set (Table 13). More patients were randomized but not treated in the ICC Arm
(16 patients) than in the Tislelizumab Arm (1 patient), possibly due to the open-label nature of
the study.

Over 90% of patients discontinued treatment by the data cutoff date, most frequently due to
radiographic progression (Table 13). More patients in the ICC Arm discontinued treatment due
to adverse events or withdrawal by the patient.

Overall, approximately 80% of patients discontinued the study due to death. Withdrew by patient
and lost to follow-up were more common from the ICC Arm (6.3%) than the Tislelizamab Arm
(2.0%). Approximately 20% of patients in the Tislelizumab Arm and 10% of patients in the ICC
Arm remained on study as of the data cutoff date.

The median duration of study follow-up was 8.5 months in the Tislelizumab Arm (range: 0.2 to
31.7 months) and 5.8 months in the ICC Arm (range: 0.0 to 30.8 months).
