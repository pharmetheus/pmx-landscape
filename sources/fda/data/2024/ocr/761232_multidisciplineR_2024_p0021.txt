The study enrolled 512 patients (256 in each arm). Upon randomization,
investigator’s choice of chemotherapy in the ICC arm was irinotecan in
118 (46%) patients, paclitaxel in 85 (33%) patients, and docetaxel in 53
(21%) patients. Baseline characteristics in all randomized subjects were
balanced between the tislelizumab and the ICC anms. In Study
RATIONALE-302, tislelizumab provided a modest but statistically
significant improvement in OS compared with chemotherapy. The OS
HR in the ITT was 0.70 (95% CI 0.57, 0.85; one-sided p-value = 0.0001)
with a median OS of 8.6 months (95% CI 7.5, 10.4) in the tislelizumab
arm and 6.3 months (95% CI 5.3, 7) in the ICC arm. Although the
secondary endpoint of OS in the PD-L1 vCPS >10% subpopulation (a:
157 patients, 31% of the overall population) was statistically significant
at the prespecified alpha level (stratified HR 0.53, 95% CI: 0.37, 0.77;
one-sided p-value = 0.006), ascertainment of PD-L1 status was missing
in 19% of patients, less than a third of patients had PD-L1 >10%, and
