14.5. Pharmacometrics Assessment

14.5.1. Executive Summary

The Applicant is seeking approval of aprocitentan, a dual ERA, for the treatment of hypertension
in subjects who are not adequately controlled on other drugs. A population pharmacokinetic
(PopPK) model was developed using data from 902 subjects receiving active treatment,
including data from 251 subjects in Phase 1 studies, 226 subjects with essential hypertension in
Phase 2, and 425 subjects with resistant hypertension in the Applicant’s Phase 3 studies. The key
