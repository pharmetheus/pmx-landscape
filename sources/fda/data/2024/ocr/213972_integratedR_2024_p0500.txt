16.5.6. Applicant’s Proposed Interpretive Criteria for Sulopenem: uUTI Target
Pathogens

The Applicant has proposed sulopenem broth microdilution MIC and disk diffusion susceptibility
testing interpretive criteria or breakpoints (BPs) against Enterobacterales based on the
following sulopenem summary data:

1. MIC frequency distributions for surveillance study and clinical study isolates for the
indicated pathogens;

2. PK/PD information including target attainment analysis from Monte-Carlo simulations
conducted with Phase 3 patient PK data;

3. Asummary of correlation between microbiologic outcome and MIC; and

4. The generation of proposed disk diffusion interpretive criteria by correlation between zone
diameter and broth microdilution MIC values in scatterplot following the CLSI error-rate
bounding analysis.

16.5.7. Recommended BPs for Sulopenem Against Target uUTI Pathogens

The following is a summary of the Clinical Microbiology team’s breakpoints (BPs) rationale and
labeling recommendations:

16.5.7.1. Setting the MIC BPs for Sulopenem Against uUTI Target Pathogens

For setting the MIC BPs for sulopenem against uUTI target pathogens E. coli, K. pneumoniae,
and P. mirabilis, the Clinical Microbiology team primarily relied on:

1. Overall MICg9 data from clinical and surveillance study isolates; and
2. Microbiological eradication rates at each MIC dilution of uUTI clinical study isolates.

Of note, the FDA Clinical Pharmacology team has a concern with the validity of PK/PD study
results.
