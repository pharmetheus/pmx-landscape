Efficacy results from patients with advanced or metastatic ESCC in Studies 001 and 102 provide
supportive evidence of the efficacy of tislelizumab monotherapy in patients with advanced or
metastatic ESCC who had been heavily pre- treated, the most patients (65.4% in Study 001 and
76.9% in Study 102) who were enrolled in these 2 studies had received > 2 prior lines of therapy.

The Applicant’s Position:
The efficacy results from the pivotal Study 302, together with the results from the 2 supportive

Studies 001 and 102, provide evidence that tislelizumab is an effective treatment for patients
with advanced or metastatic ESCC after prior systemic treatment in support of the proposed
indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that the primary OS results of RATIONALE-302 provide substantial evidence of the
effectiveness of tislelizumab for the treatment of patients with advanced or metastatic ESCC
with disease progression after one prior line of treatment. See FDA’s assessment in Section 8.1.3
(“Integrated Review of Effectiveness”), above.

8.2.1. Overall Safety Evaluation Plan

The safety analysis for this BLA is based primarily on the pivotal study, Study 302. The Study
302 dataset presents a clear comparison of the safety data of tislelizumab with the standard of
care at the time of study initiation, which was single-agent chemotherapy. The safety data from
the ESCC population represents the total experience of tislelizumab as treatment for ESCC at the
time of BLA submission.
