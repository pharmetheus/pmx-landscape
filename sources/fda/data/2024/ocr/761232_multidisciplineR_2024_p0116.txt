The FDA’s Assessment:

Persistence of effect is a term better suited for continuous variables (e.g., hypertension,
biomarker assessments, etc.) and is not intended to characterize or compare treatment effects on
selected endpoints. Treatment effect and study outcomes are described elsewhere in this section.
