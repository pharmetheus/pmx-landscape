Interference and specificity

The percentage cross-reactivity of ISIS 489608 (19-mer), ISIS
489609 (10-mer), and ISIS 489611 (5-mer) with olezarsen is
130.7%, < 0.100%, and < 0.100%, respectively.

No interference from metabolites ISIS 489609 (10-mer) and ISIS
489611 (5-mer) up to 3.000 nM (20% of olezarsen concentration),
and metabolite ISIS 489608 (19-mer) at 5% of olezarsen, on the
quantification of olezarsen in human plasma. However, there is an
interference from metabolite ISIS 489608 (19-mer) at 0.009 nM
(20% of olezarsen concentration) when in the presence of 0.045
nM olezarsen and at 3 nM (20% of olezarsen concentration) when
in the presence of 15 nM olezarsen. Therefore, any interpretation
of plasma concentrations of olezarsen will need to consider the
type and level of metabolites present when accounting for
potential interference.

There was no interference on the quantification of olezarsen in
K2EDTA human plasma when fortified with ADA at 10 pg/mL.
