7.3.2. Expectations on Safety

It is anticipated that olezarsen’s safety profile in the postmarket setting, when used in the

Given that this application seeks a treatment indication in a rare disease population, one would
expect the number of subjects exposed to olezarsen in the postmarket setting to be small. A
higher-than-expected amount of olezarsen patient exposure could suggest off-label treatment in
other HTG populations. Because the clinical trials of olezarsen in other HTG populations are
blinded and ongoing, the benefits and risks in an off-label population are unknown.
