NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

The Applicant's Position: The evaluation of safety is primarily derived from the pivotal Study
W041554, with supportive data derived from Study GO39374 to support the adequate
characterization of efficacy, safety, and tolerability of inavolisib in combination with palbociclib
and an ET in patients with PIK3CA-mutated HR+, HER2— LA/mBC.

Data from Study GO39374 will supplement the safety database for inavolisib based
combinations (aggregate data from Arms A-F), including additional data for patients, who were
enrolled with higher baseline FBG (< 140 mg/dL) and HbAic levels (HbAic <7%) compared to the
eligibility criteria for Study WO41554 trial. This will further support the applicability of the
inavolisib triplet combination in patients with underlying risk factors for hyperglycemia.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA conducted in depth safety analyses based on data from the phase 3 INAVO120 trial.
Topline safety results were reviewed by FDA for GO39374.

Issues Regarding Data Integrity and Submission Quality

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

Categorization of Adverse Event

The Applicant’s Position: Verbatim descriptions for AEs and diseases entered by the
investigators were assigned to Preferred Terms (PTs) by using MedDRA v26.1 (in
Study WO41554) and v26.0 (in Study GO39374).

The AE severity grading scale for the NCI CTCAE v5.0 (in Study WO41554) and v4.0 (in

Study GO39374) was used for assessing AE severity and routine laboratory parameters. For
events of varying severity, the highest grade was used in the summaries, unless specified
otherwise. In the summary tables, patients who experienced the same event on more than one
occasion were counted once and at the highest grade in the calculation of the event frequency
and severity.

Selected AEs for inavolisib were defined based on known and evolving safety profile of
inavolisib, known risks for the combination agents and protocol-defined AESI. These selected
events are grouped by medical concepts as described in Table 29.
