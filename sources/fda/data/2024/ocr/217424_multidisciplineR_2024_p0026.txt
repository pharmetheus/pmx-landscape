NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation (NDA 217424)
ZELSUVMI (berdazimer sodium gel)

Dimension Evidence and Uncertainties Conclusions and Reasons

e@ The results for the primary efficacy endpoint for the Phase 3 trials NI-
MC304, NI-MC-302, and NI-MC301 for subjects treated with berdazimer
gel, compared to vehicle, respectively, were the following:

— Trial NI-MC-304: 32.4% v. 19.7% (treatment difference of 12.8%, 95%
Cl [7.1%, 18.6%])

— Trial NI-MC-302: 30.0% v. 20.3% (treatment difference of 9.2%, 95%
Cl [-0.04%, 18.4%]).

— Trial NI-MC-301: 25.8% v. 21.6% (treatment difference of 4.3%, 95%
Cl [- 5.0%, 13.6%]).

e The results for the key secondary efficacy endpoint (the proportion of
subjects with complete clearance of all treatable MC lesions at Week 8)
for the Phase 3 trials NI-MC304, NI-MC-302, and NI-MC301 for subjects
treated with berdazimer gel, compared to vehicle, respectively, were the
following:

- Trial NI-MC-304: 19.6% v. 11.6% (treatment difference of 7.5%, 95%
Cl [3.0%, 12.0%])

- Trial NI-MC-302: 13.9% v. 5.9% (treatment difference of 7.8%, 95%
Cl [1.8%, 13.8%]).

- Trial NI-MC-301: 15.3% v. 10.3% (treatment difference of 4.7%, 95%
Cl [- 2.2%, 11.5%]).
