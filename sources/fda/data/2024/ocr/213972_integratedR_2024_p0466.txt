Effect of Testing in Medium With Lysed Horse Blood and Varied Incubation

The effects of supplementing test medium with 3 — 5% lysed horse blood compared to the
standard CAMHB and the impact of incubating at 5% CO, as opposed to the standard ambient
atmosphere on the activity of sulopenem were evaluated against the QC strains (Table 164).
The sulopenem geometric mean MIC values increased slightly in the presence of lysed horse
blood compared to CAMHB. Against all four QC strains, sulopenem geometric mean MIC values
in the presence and absence of CO, were within + one log2 dilution.
