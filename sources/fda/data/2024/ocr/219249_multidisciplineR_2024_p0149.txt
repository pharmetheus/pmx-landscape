Diarrhea occurred in 48% of patients treated with inavolisib, including Grade 3 events in 3.7% of
patients. There were no grade 4 or 5 events or cases of colitis on the INAVO120 Trial. There
were two SAEs associated with diarrhea, both of which resolved. In addition, there was a case
of acute kidney injury associated with diarrhea and cases of dehydration associated with
diarrhea. Dose interruptions were required in 7% of patients, and dose reductions occurred in
1.2%. Anti-diarrheal medicines were used in 28% of patients treated with inavolisib to manage
symptoms.

Ocular toxicity occurred in 22% of patients treated with inavolisib compared to 13% on the
placebo arm. The most common toxicity was dry eye (9%). There were no grade 3-5 events, no
SAEs and ocular toxicity did not lead to dose modifications (discontinuation, reduction or
interruption).

Neutropenia is a known toxicity with treatment of palbociclib and occurred at similar rates in
both treatment arms.

Pneumonitis occurred in 3 patients on inavolisib treatment arm (compared to 0 on the placebo
arm). There were no grade 3-4 events or SAEs. One patient had a dose interruption, there
were no dose reductions or drug discontinuations.

The Applicant included hyperglycemia in the Warnings and Precautions section of the proposed
USPI. In addition, FDA required inclusion of stomatitis and diarrhea in the Warnings and
Precautions section based on the incidence of these events and the occurrence of severe cases
for both stomatitis and diarrhea in the INAVO120 trial.
