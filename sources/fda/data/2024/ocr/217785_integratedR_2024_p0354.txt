Thyroid Parameters

Little changes in thyroid parameters were observed from Day 14 to Day 22 following dosing
with 100 mg/day resmetirom. The mean (SD) change from Day 14 in TSH, total T4, free T4,
TT3, and free T3 were -0.23 (0.34) IU/L, -1.35 (0.51) g/dL, -0.12 (0.05) ng/dL, -0.02 (0.07)
pg/L, and -0.03 (0.21) ng/L, respectively.
