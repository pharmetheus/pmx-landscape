DLL3 expression status
In study 20200491, DLL3 expression was evaluated by immunohistochemistry (IHC) at

baseline in fresh or archival tumor tissue samples. For patients with response
assessments determined by BICR (N=185), DLL3 expression data was available for 83%
of patients (82/99) in the 1/10 mg cohort and 86% of patients (74/86) in the 1/100 mg
cohort. Overall response rates in the subset of patients with DLL3 expression data
available were consistent with response rates observed in the full Safety Analysis Set for
Part 1 and Part 2 (see section 8.1.2). Although there was a general trend toward higher
DLL3 H-scores in patients who responded (Figure 12), 1 patient in the 1/10 mg cohort
with an H-score of 0 achieved a PR. Additionally, no difference in ORR was observed at
the DLL3 cut-points of < 75% vs 2 75% and < 25% vs = 25% at moderate (2+) and strong
(3+) staining intensities, as assessed by the Applicant (see section 8.1.6). These results
support treatment of patients irrespective of baseline DLL3 expression status.
