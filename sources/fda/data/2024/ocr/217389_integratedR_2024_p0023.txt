Pharmacologic Class

Ensifentrine is a small molecule inhibitor of both PDE3 and PDE4.

Mechanism of action

Ensifentrine is a small molecule inhibitor of the PDE3 and PDE4 enzymes. In vitro studies showed that ensifentrine
inhibited recombinant enzyme PDE3 activity with subnanomolar potency. The inhibitory potency at PDE4 ranged
from approximately 50 to 1479 nM. PDE3 primarily hydrolyzes the second-messenger molecule cAMP but is also
capable of hydrolyzing cGMP. PDE4 hydrolyzes cAMP only. Inhibition of PDE3 with ensifentrine results in smooth
muscle relaxation which causes bronchodilation. Inhibition of PDE4 results in an anti-inflammatory effect.

Proposed dose and
administration route

The proposed dose is 3 mg BID. The administration route is oral inhalation using a standard jet nebulizer with a
mouthpiece.

Active moieties

Unchanged ensifentrine is the pharmacologically active moiety.

QT prolongation Following a single dose of 9 mg ensifentrine (three times the proposed unit dose), no clinically relevant effects on
prolongation of the QTc interval were observed in the thorough QT study.
