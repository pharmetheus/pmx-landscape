Routine Clinical Tests

The Applicant’s Position:

Clinical safety tests included clinical laboratory values, physical examination findings (including
vital signs, body weight, and lung auscultation), cardiac function (12-lead ECG, ECHO/MUGA
scan), ECOG performance status, and ADAs and NAbs.

The FDA’s Assessment:

All patients received a baseline echocardiogram/MUGA prior to starting treatment with
zanidatamab in Study 203 and Study 101. In Study 203, repeat assessment of LVEF occurred at
6 weeks and then every 12 weeks. Patients had hematology and serum chemistry assessment
prior to each infusion (i-e., Day 1 and 15 of each cycle).
