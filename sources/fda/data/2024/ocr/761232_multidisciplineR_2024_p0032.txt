4.2. Product Quality

The Office of Pharmaceutical Quality, CDER, recommends approval of BLA 761232 for
tislelizumab-jsgr manufactured by BeiGene USA, Inc. Based on OPQ’s evaluation of the
submission, the Applicant provided adequate information on the proposed drug product to ensure
the identity, strength, purity, and quality of the proposed drug product. The overall
manufacturing inspection recommendation is approval for all the facilities associated with this
application. The proposed labeling and labels include adequate information to meet the
regulatory requirements. Refer to the OPQ’s review of this application for additional
information.

4.3. Clinical Microbiology

This BLA was reviewed by OPQ’s Division of Microbiology Assessment. The microbiology
reviewers did not identify issues that would preclude approval of tislelizumab-jsgr; however, two
post-marketing commitments (PMCs) will be included in the approval letter for BeiGene to 1)
develop a reliable endotoxin detection method that can overcome the LER phenomenon for the
tislelizumab drug product and 2) to implement an in-line pressure monitoring of sterile filtration
for the tislelizumab drug product.
