VAFSEO (vadadustat)
6.3.5.3 Gastric Erosion

Gastric erosion was an adverse event of special interest as daprodustat (another HIF-PH
inhibitor) contains a warning for increased risk of gastric erosions.

Overall, GI erosions were rare in Studies Cl-0036 and Cl-0039. In Study Cl-0036, the incidence
rate of GI erosion was higher in the vadadustat TIW arm compared to the darbepoetin arm
(Table 38). In Study Cl-0039, the incidence rate of GI erosion was higher in the vadadustat arms
(both 600 mg and 900 mg) compared to the epoetin beta arm (Table 39). The very low event
rates limit conclusions. GI erosion was defined as a grouped medical query, see Section 12.3 for
further details (this approach was also used for Studies CI-0016 and CI-0017).

Conclusion: An increased risk of gastric erosions among patients treated with vadadustat
compared to those treated with darbepoetin alfa was identified in the INNO2VATE trials (see
Section 5.1 for further details). The very low event rates of GI erosions in Studies Cl-0036 and
Cl-0039 limit conclusions from these trials and do not meaningfully impact the conclusions from
INNO2VATE. GI erosions will be listed as a Warning in the vadadustat USPI.
