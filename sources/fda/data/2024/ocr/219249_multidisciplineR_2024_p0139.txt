Significant Adverse Events

Data and the Applicant’s Position:

Pivotal Study WO41554: The incidence of Grade 3-4 AEs by highest grade was comparable
between the Inavo+Palbo+Fulv (85.8%) and Pbo+Palbo+Fulv (82.1%) arms (Table 36). The
incidence rates were driven by Grade 3-4 AEs (by PTs) of neutropenia (47.5% vs. 48.1%) and
neutrophil count decreased (34.6% vs. 32.7%). The majority of Grade 3-4 AEs in both treatment
arms were associated with myelosuppression. Other common Grade 3-4 AEs (by PTs) were
hyperglycemia (Inavo+Palbo+Fulv: 5.6% and Pbo+Palbo+Fulv: 0%), diarrhea (3.7% vs. 0%) and
stomatitis/oral mucositis (3.1% vs. 0%). The proportion of patients with Grade 3-4 AEs related
to any treatment were comparable between the arms (85.2% vs. 80.2%). See Section 8.2.4 for
further information on Grade 5 AEs.

Supportive Study GO39374: In the overall GO39374 safety population the most frequent Grade
3-4 AEs (=>10%) were hyperglycemia (24.2%) and neutropenia (23.7%). All Grade 3-4 AEs for
neutropenia were reported from palbociclib-containing arms (Arm B, Arm E, and Arm F). The
most frequently reported Grade 3-4 AE ( > 50% in each arm) among patients in Arm B and Arm
E was neutropenia (66.7% and 70.0%, respectively). In Arm F, neutropenia (45.0%) and
hyperglycemia (45.0%) were the most frequently reported Grade 3-4 AEs.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. Hyperglycemia, diarrhea, and stomatitis/mucositis
are discussed further in Section 8.2.5 (Submission Specific Safety Issues).
