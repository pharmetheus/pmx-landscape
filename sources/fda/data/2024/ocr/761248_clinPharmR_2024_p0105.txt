Metabolism/Excretion

Monoclonal antibodies are not known to be metabolized by
the cytochrome P450 system or affected by drug
transporters. As a human IgG1 monoclonal antibody,
donanemab is expected to be degraded to small peptides
and amino acids by ubiquitous proteolytic enzymes in the
same manner as endogenous IgG.
