Subject completion, discontinuation, or withdrawal:

Subjects were considered to have completed the study when they were no longer actively being
followed for survival. Subject completion could occur for the following reasons: patient
withdrawal of consent, lost to follow-up, death, or study termination by BeiGene, Ltd. After
patients had completed the study, no further study procedures or assessments were performed.
No patients were replaced, regardless of the reason for study discontinuation or withdrawal.
