-> = step dose to target dose (eg, 1 -> 10 mg = 1 mg step dose to 10 mg target dose); KM = Kaplan-Meier; NE = not estimable; OS = overall survival;
RECIST = Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors

The safety analysis set included all subjects who received at least 1 dose of tarlatamab.

Overall survival was defined as the interval from the date of first dose of tarlatamab to the event of death due to any cause. Subjects still alive was censored at the
date last known to be alive. If the date last known to be alive was after the date that triggered the analysis (ie, the data cutoff date), the subject was censored at
the analysis trigger date.

Censor indicated by vertical bar |.

The survival curves and median OS were estimated using KM method and their 95% Cl were estimated using log-log transformation of KM survival estimate by
Brookmeyer and Crowley (1982) method.

Data snapshot date: 01 March 2024. Data cutoff date: 27 June 2023.
