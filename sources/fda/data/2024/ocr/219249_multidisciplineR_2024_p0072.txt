{Inavolisib}

LEY (i.e., inavolisib in combination

with palbociclib and fulvestrant).

FDA does not consider that the Applicant has provided sufficient justification that inavolisib 9
mg PO QD is the optimized dosage for the target patient population based on the following
reasons:

1) High Rate of AEs and Dosage Modifications

Multiple common AEs in the pivotal study (i.e., increase blood glucose, stomatitis/mucosal
inflammation, and diarrhea) were observed at high rates in patients who received inavolisib +
palbociclib + fulvestrant and at higher incidence in the treatment arm than those in the control
arm (Table 8). Neutropenia, a known toxicity of palbociclib, was similar between the two arms.
In addition, 69% of patients in the inavolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant arm had at least one
inavolisib interruption with 84% (595/711) of the inavolisib dose interruptions due to AEs
(Table 9). These results suggest that the proposed dosing regimen for inavolisib in combination
with palbociclib and fulvestrant is poorly tolerated.
