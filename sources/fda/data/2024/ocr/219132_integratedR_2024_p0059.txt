6.2.2.4. Results of Analyses

6.2.2.4.1. Subject Disposition and Baseline Characteristics

A total of 64 patients were screened, of whom 60 were enrolled and treated. Four patients had
screen failures due to violation of one or more inclusion criteria. Of the 60 treated subjects, 59
(98.3%) subjects completed both treatment periods, and 58 (96.7%) subjects had assessments at
the end of both treatment periods (Visit 4 and Visit 6).
