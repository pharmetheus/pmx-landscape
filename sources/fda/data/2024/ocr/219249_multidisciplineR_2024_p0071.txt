e High dose intensity (>90%) seen across all exposure quartiles of inavolisib supports the

discontinuation rate of inavolisib due to AEs was low (6.2% in WO41554).

The Applicant’s Position: At the proposed 9 mg QD dose of inavolisib, Study WO41554
demonstrated a compelling clinical benefit of inavolisib in combination with palbociclib and
fulvestrant, and the treatment regimen demonstrated good tolerability. These results were
further supported by the anti-tumor activity and manageable safety and tolerability in

Study GO39374. Based on the totality of this information, the recommended dosing regimen
for inavolisib is 9 mg QD dose, administered orally, with or without food.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that inavolisib 9 mg PO QD in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant is
approvable in adult patients with HR+/HER2-, locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer

following recurrence on or after adjuvant therapy Lah
