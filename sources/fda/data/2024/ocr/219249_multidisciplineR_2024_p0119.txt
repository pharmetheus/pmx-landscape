The Applicant's Position: The triplet combination of inavolisib plus palbociclib and an endocrine
agent (fulvestrant or letrozole) across Arm B, Arm E and Arm F from the Phase | GO39374 study
resulted in promising clinical activity in terms of investigator-assessed endpoints of ORR, DOR,
CBR, and PFS, in patients with P/K3CA-mutated HR+, HER2— LA/mBC.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The confirmed ORR results in patients with measurable disease in Arm B (N=25), Arm E (N=15),
and Arm F (N=18) were 52% (31%, 72%), 40% (16%, 68%), and 17% (0%, 41%), respectively,
using the Clopper-Pearson exact method to calculate the 95% confidence intervals. While
activity was observed across the three treatment arms based on the confirmed ORR and DOR,
given the small sample sizes and wide Cl of ORR, the contribution of inavolisib to efficacy was
unclear in these single arm cohorts. |
