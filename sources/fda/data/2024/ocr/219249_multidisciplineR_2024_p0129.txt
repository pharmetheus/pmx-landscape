The FDA’s Assessment:
In general, FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment for categorization of adverse events.

FDA’s grouped term (GT) for rash differed slightly from the one used by the Applicant. This
difference resulted in identifying a few additional patients. The adverse events for the
additional patients included eczema, erythema multiforme, palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia,
and skin exfoliation. The incidence of rash was updated by FDA in the USPI reflecting this
grouping of PTs.

Routine Clinical Tests

The Applicant's Position: Patients were closely monitored for safety and tolerability throughout
the conduct of studies. The assessments performed were considered adequate for the expected
toxicities associated with inavolisib and the combination agents. Patients were assessed for
toxicity at each clinic visit and prior to each in-clinic dose of study treatment; dosing occurred
only if the clinical assessments and local laboratory test values were acceptable. The
assessments included, complete physical exam, ECOG performance status, vital signs, tumor
and response evaluations, laboratory tests (includes but not limited to hematology, chemistry,
fasting insulin, fasting lipid profile, amylase and lipase, HbAic, serum fibrinogen and
coagulation, urinalysis, pregnancy test, PK analysis, and biomarker assays), ophthalmic
examinations, electrocardiograms, and clinical outcome assessments.
