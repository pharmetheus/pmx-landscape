6.2.1 Study Title: 26-week chronic toxicity study of IB1001 by repeated oral
administration to Wistar Han rats (multi-site study)

Study no.: 36242

Study report location: SN 31 Module 4.2.3.2. — 26-week rat
study

Study initiation date: February 19.2020

Conducting laboratory and location: ]

Duration: 26
Duration Units: weeks
GLP compliance: Y
Drug, lot #, and % purity: LT 50 and 99.9% purity
Target Organ*: KIDNEY

Methods
Doses: 0 (vehicle), 250, 800, and 2500 mg/kg/day
Frequency of dosing: Once daily for 182 days
Number/Sex/Group: 20/sex/group
Dose volume: 10 mL/kg b.w.
Formulation/Vehicle: NaCl 0.9%, wiv, pH 8.0
Route of administration: ORAL GAVAGE
Species: RAT
Strain. WISTAR
Age / Sexual Maturity: ~ 60 days of age, sexually matured
Comment on Study Design and Recovery (5/sex/group)
Conduct:
Dosing Solution Analysis: Dosing solution was correctly prepared
(within the range between 96% and 103%).

Observations and Results
Mortality
None.

Clinical Signs
Salivation and soft feces were observed in both males and females at 2500 mg/kg/day.

Body Weights

Decreased body weight (-6%) was noted in males at 2500 mg/kg/day. This was
subsided at the end of the 4-week recovery period. No body weight effect was noted in
females.
