NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761344}

{Tarlatamab}

General Information

Objectives of PPK Analysis

The objectives of the analysis were to characterize the
population pharmacokinetics of tarlatamab in subjects with
small cell lung cancer (SCLC), to evaluate the effects of
demographic variables (covariates) on pharmacokinetic (PK)
parameters, and to assess the impacts of identified
covariates on tarlatamab exposures.

Study Included

20160323 and 20200491

Dose(s) Included

In these studies, tarlatamab was administered with short or
extended intravenous infusion, and using no-step, one-step or
two-step dosing regimens. Evaluated target doses included
0.003mg, 0.01mg, 0.03mg, 0.1mg, 0.3mg, Img, 3mg, 10mg,
30mg, and 100mg Q2W or 200mg Q3W (Table 2 and Table 5
of Amgen Pharmacometrics Report: 157688)

Population Included

Patients with small cell lung cancer
