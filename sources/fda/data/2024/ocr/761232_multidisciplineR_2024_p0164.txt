8.2.8. Clinical Outcome Assessment (COA) Analyses Informing Safety/Tolerability

Data:

Validated and most frequently used PROs instruments in oncology studies, esp. in PD-L1
studies, were used measure and to assess HRQoL. These included the EORTC QLQ-C30 to
measure general cancer signs and symptoms, its esophageal cancer-specific module, the EORTC
QLQ-OES18, and the EQ-5D-SL to measure general health, were used to further characterize
treatment benefits of tislelizumab beyond radiographic measures.

The assessment of the HRQoL were based on of the comparison of the key PRO endpoints in
esophageal cancer. The PRO key endpoints in this study included the global health status/QoL
(GHS) and physical function as measured by the EORTC QLQ-C-30 and esophageal cancer
main symptoms of pain, eating, dysphagia and reflux as measured by EORTC QLQ-OES18.

Compliance

The adjusted completion rates (the number of patients who completed the PRO questionnaires
divided by the number of patients who were still in treatment at that visit) from baseline through
Cycle 6 were > 95% for each questionnaire and were similar in both treatment arms.
