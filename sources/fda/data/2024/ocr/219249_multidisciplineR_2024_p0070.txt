NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {NDA 219249}
{Inavolisib}

@ Pharmacokinetic (PK) properties following single dose and multiple doses of inavolisib
administered as a single agent and with combination agents in subjects with cancer.

PK properties following a single dose administered as a single agent in healthy subjects.
Population PK analyses of single agent and combination studies included in the dossier.

e The results from clinical pharmacology assessments conducted in healthy subjects (mass
balance and absolute bioavailability) to describe the ADME properties of inavolisib in
humans.

e@ The results from clinical pharmacology assessments conducted in subjects with cancer
to assess intrinsic (mild hepatic impairment and mild renal impairment) and extrinsic
factors (food effect and effect of acid reducing agents) that may affect the PK of
inavolisib.

e In addition, the outcomes of the exposure-efficacy and exposure-safety analyses
conducted using data from Phase III Study WO41554 and supportive Phase | Study
G0O39374, which were used for the Phase III dose selection and justification in cancer
subjects, support the use of the recommended dose of 9 mg inavolisib, administered
orally, QD, on a continuous schedule.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s summary of submitted clinical pharmacology data.

In addition, during review of NDA 219249, the Applicant submitted an interim CSR for Study
GP44944, an ongoing single-dose study to evaluate the effect of renal impairment on inavolisib
PK. The interim CSR included data from participants with moderate renal impairment and
normal renal function.

FDA agrees that the proposed dosage of inavolisib 9 mg PO QD in combination with palbociclib
and fulvestrant is approvable for the granted indication; however, FDA does not agree that the
proposed dosage is optimized. See Section 6.1.2 for details on the FDA’s position and
recommendation for further dosage optimization of inavolisib.

6.2.2. General Dosing and Therapeutic Individualization
6.2.2.1. General Dosing

Data: A dose of 9 mg QD is the recommended dose for oral administration of inavolisib in
combination with palbociclib and endocrine therapy in patients with P/K3CA-mutated, HR+,
HER2— LA/mBC.
e Data from Phase | Dose Escalation/Expansion Study GO39374:
© 9mgQD was the MTD of inavolisib as identified during dose escalation (3 - 12 mg
QD) in the dose escalation and expansion Phase 1b Study GO39374 (Stage 1 Arm A).
