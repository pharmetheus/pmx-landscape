Acceptable

Model Structure

Nonlinear mixed-effects modeling approach using a first-
order conditional estimation with interaction method. The
Jinal PK model was a 2-compartment model with zero-order
drug input (using actual IV infusion duration times) and with
parallel linear and nonlinear CL pathways to describe
elimination kinetics.

Acceptable

Model Parameter Estimates

Predicted CL, Ve, Vp, Km and Vmax appear in Section 6.2.1
and Table 40.

Acceptable

Uncertainty and Variability
(RSE, IIV, Shrinkage,
Bootstrap)

All fixed effect parameters (representing PK parameters and
covariate effects) and random effect parameters (describing
IIV and RV) were estimated with < 39.4 %RSE and < 28.7
%RSE, respectively. The magnitudes of estimated IIV in CL
and V. were relatively modest (27.9 %CV and 21.9 %CV,
respectively); the magnitude of estimated IIV was larger for
Vp (66.1 %CV). Bayesian shrinkage in the estimates of the
IIV for CL, Ve, and Vp was 8.8%, 10.0%, and 22.0%
respectively. Residual variability was estimated to be 21.1
%CYV, with low Bayesian shrinkage (7.97%) in the estimate
of the RV term. The final model condition number was 34.4.

Acceptable

BLQ for Parameter Accuracy

Eight postdose PK records were BLO (0.215%). The BLO
samples were excluded from the analyses, according to MI
method, ie, when BLO samples represented <5% of the total
samples in the dataset, and if the occurrence of BLO samples
did not appear to be treatment-group dependent.

Significant Covariates and
Clinical Relevance

The final PopPK model covariates appear in Section 6.2.2.2.
The influences of body weight, ALB, and number of lesions
are not likely to have a clinically relevant impact on
zanidatamab AUCss, Cavgss, aNd Cmaxss exposures, as the
GMR values for all covariate groups were maintained within
the bounds of 0.8 to 1.25, with only the 90% CI overlapping
these bounds for certain covariate subgroups (Figure 5).
Gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma was the only cancer type
that exhibited considerably lower zanidatamab exposures
relative to BTC.
