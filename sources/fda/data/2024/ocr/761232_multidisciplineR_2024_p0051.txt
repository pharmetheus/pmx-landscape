TEVIMBRA (tislelizumab)

Review Issue Recommendations and Comments

Dosing in Based on PopPK analyses using pooled PK data across clinical trials, no
patient clinically significant differences in the pharmacokinetics of tislelizumab
subgroups were observed based on age (18 to 90 years), weight (32 to 130 kg), race
(intrinsic and (White, Asian, or Black), mild to moderate renal impairment (CLcr > 30
extrinsic mL/min, estimated by Cockcroft-Gault), mild to moderate hepatic

factors) impairment (total bilirubin < 3 times ULN and any AST, estimated by NCI

criteria). The effect of severe hepatic impairment (total bilirubin > 3 times
ULN and any AST), severe renal impairment (CLcr 15-29 mL/min), or end
stage renal disease (CLer <15 mL/min) on the pharmacokinetics of
tislelizumab is unknown.

In patients who received tislelizumab-jsgr in Study 302 for up to 22
months, the incidence of anti-tislelizumab antibodies (ADA) was 14.5%
(32/221). Among the ADA positive patients, the incidence of neutralizing
antibodies (NAb) was 3.1% (1/32). Tislelizumab ADA development has no
clinically meaningful impact on the PK of tislelizumab. The effect of ADA
on the efficacy and safety of tislelizumab in patients with unresectable or
metastatic esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) could not be fully
characterized as the number of patients with positive ADA and neutralizing
ADA were small.

Labeling Overall, the proposed labeling recommendations are acceptable upon the
Applicant’s agreement to the FDA revisions to the labeling. Clinical pharmacology
labeling recommendations are detailed in Section 12.2, 12.3 and 12.6.

6.2. Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:

The clinical pharmacology information in this submission includes characterization of
pharmacokinetics, exposure-response (E-R) analyses, and immunogenicity profile of
tislelizumab.

The PK profile of tislelizumab was characterized using noncompartmental analysis and
population PK (popPK) analysis. Exposure-response (E-R) analyses were performed to
understand the relationships between PK and efficacy, as well as safety parameters. These
analyses support the proposed dosing regimen of 200 mg once every 3 weeks and demonstrated
that no dose modifications are needed based on age, weight, race, sex, hepatic impairment (mild
to moderate), renal impairment (mild to moderate), and other intrinsic or extrinsic factors. The
immunogenicity profile of tislelizumab has been characterized and its lack of clinically relevant
impact on PK, safety, and efficacy in the ESCC population is also discussed in this section.

Additional information are reported in section 6.3.1.
