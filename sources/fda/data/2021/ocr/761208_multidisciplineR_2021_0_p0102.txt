BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {Biologics License Application (BLA) 761208}
{tisotumab vedotin}

The Applicant's Position:

Treatment with tisotumab vedotin 2.0 mg/kg 1Q3W resulted in clinically meaningful and
durable responses in subjects with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer with disease
progression on or after doublet chemotherapy with bevacizumab (if eligible). Confirmed ORR of
23.8% was generally consistent across subgroups, with 95% Cls overlapping with the confirmed
ORR of the overall trial population. Importantly, confirmed ORR was consistent across
histologies, prior vs no prior bevacizumab as part of frontline treatment.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA conducted an independent analysis of ORR. The confirmed ORR assessed by IRC was 23.8%
(95% Cl: 15.9%, 33.3%) out of 101 patients, including 7 (6.9%) CRs and 17 (16.8%) PRs, while the
confirmed ORR assessed by investigator was 20.8% (95% Cl: 13.4%, 30.0%), including 5 (5.0%)
CRs and 16 (15.8%) PRs. Characteristics of patients who experienced responses on GCT1015-04
are shown in Table 14.
