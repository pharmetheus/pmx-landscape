First Co-Primary Endpoint: Percent Change from Bas: in Weight at Week 24
The Applicant’s primary analysis results (Table 30) for the first co-primary endpoint were

obtained with ANCOVA with baseline weight as a covariate and the factors of Treatment group,
Age group, and Race. Post-baseline missing values were handled by the multiple imputation
(MI) method.

The Applicant presented the hypothesis testing results based on both the CHW test statistic

(primary) and the unadjusted test statistic (supportive). Both tests led to almost identical p-
values (0.003 with the CHW test vs. 0.002 with the unadjusted test).
