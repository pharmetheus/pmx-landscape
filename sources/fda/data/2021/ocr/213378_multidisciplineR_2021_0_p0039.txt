e Warnings and Precautions Section:

Patients Receiving Opioids: Two sections were added to the label to warn about risks
of ALKS 3831 and concurrent opioid use: one about precipitating opioid withdrawal
and a second regarding the potential vulnerability to opioid overdose when trying to
overcome samidorphan’s opioid receptor blockade or because of changes in opioid
tolerance when ALKS 3831 is interrupted.
