Safety Pharmacology

Data (presented by the Applicant):

Limited inhibition (<50%) of potassium conductance via the human ether-a-go-go-related gene
(hERG) channel was observed with the highest concentration of monomethy! auristatin E
(MMAE) (100 pM, 71.8 pg/mL), which was “12,000-fold higher than the clinically observed
Cmax of MMAE. At 10 #M MMAE (7.18 g/mL) there was no significant inhibition of hERG
channel activity. No effect was observed on the Fridericia corrected QT interval and RR-interval
in cynomolgus monkeys treated with MMAE. Tisotumab vedotin was not evaluated in the hERG
channel assay as the antibody component is too large to cross plasma membranes and
therefore would be unable to access and block the promiscuous inner pore of the hERG
channel.

No effect was observed on electrocardiogram (ECG), heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory, or
central nervous system (CNS) safety pharmacology parameters evaluated as part of the general
toxicology studies performed in cynomolgus monkeys with tisotumab vedotin and tisotumab.

The Applicant's Position:
Tisotumab vedotin has no effects on safety pharmacology endpoints. In addition, there was no

evidence of clinical effects relating to safety pharmacology endpoints. No effect was observed
on EGG, heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory or CNS safety pharmacology parameters
evaluated as part of the general toxicology studies performed in cynomolgus monkeys with the
antibody-drug conjugate (ADC). No effect was observed on cynomolgus monkeys at
physiological concentrations of MMAE. It is unlikely that MMAE derived from tisotumab
vedotin would block hERG K+ channels in vivo.

The FDA's Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. There were no tisotumab vedotin
related effects on the safety pharmacology evaluations of the nervous, cardiovascular or
respiratory systems at doses up to 5 mg/kg (4.2-fold the exposures at the recommended dose
based on AUC) (Study# 522531) nor MMAE-related effects at a dose of 0.058 mg/kg
(exposures similar to those at the recommended dose based on Cmax) (Study# 163.16).
However, in a non-GLP dose range finding study in monkeys (Study# _ 521988), one animal
given 6 mg/kg tisotumab vedotin (4.9-fold the exposure at the recommended dose based on
AUC) experienced respiratory symptoms described as gasping, wheezing, shallow, and labored
breathing that led to early euthanasia.
