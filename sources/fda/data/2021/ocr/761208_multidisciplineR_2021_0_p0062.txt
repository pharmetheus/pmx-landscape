6 Clinical Pharmacology

6.1 Executive Summary
The FDA’s Assessment:

Tisotumab vedotin is an antibody drug conjugate (ADC) consisting of a human tissue factor (TF)-
specific monoclonal antibody linked to monomethy! auristatin E (MMAE), an anti-microtubule
small molecule that induces cell cycle arrest and apoptosis, via a protease-cleavable valine-
citrulline (vc) linker. The applicant is seeking accelerated approval of tisotumab vedotin bay

The proposed dosing regimen of tisotumab vedotin is 2 mg/kg (up to a
maximum of 200 mg in patients > 100 kg) as an IV infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks
(Q3W) until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
