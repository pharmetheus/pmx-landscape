Given that there is no substantial decrease in the tolerability of tisotumab vedotin in patients
with mild hepatic impairment, and as lowering the dose of tisotumab vedotin to match the
exposure of MMAE may lead to lower ADC concentrations which could lead to lower efficacy
(based on the exposure-response analysis with efficacy), no tisotumab vedotin dose adjustment
is recommended for this population.

The effect of moderate or severe hepatic impairment on the PK and safety of tisotumab vedotin
has not been assessed. However, unconjugated MMAE exposures are expected to further
increase in the setting of moderate and severe hepatic impairment. As the proposed dosage of
tisotumab vedotin was identified as the MTD and unconjugated MMAE exposure is corelated
with increased SAEs based on exposure-safety analyses, tisotumab vedotin should be avoided
in patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment. This guidance is conveyed in Section
8.6 of product labeling.
