Premature Discontinuations and Missing Data
Overall, nearly 90% of the subjects completed the double-blind phase. The top discontinuation
reason was “withdrawal by subject.”

To assess the robustness of the primary analysis of the primary endpoint and impact of missing
data, the Applicant performed a sensitivity analysis using a delta-adjusted Pattern Mixture
Model. This model incorporated the clinical assumption that ALKS 3831 subjects who
discontinued at a given time point would have, on average, their unobserved PANSS worsened
by some amount 6 compared with the observed PANSS of subjects on the same treatment arm
who continue to the next time point, and subjects who discontinued from the olanzapine arm
would have the same PANSS trajectory as the olanzapine subjects who stay on the study. Shift
parameters used in the sensitivity analysis accounted for 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, and 50% of the
observed treatment difference between ALKS 3831 and olazapine. In this trial, 50% of the
observed treatment difference was equivalent to 3.2 points. The sensitivity analysis results
were very robust in support of the primary anlaysis even if the ALKS 3831 dropouts were
assumed to have PANSS outcomes worsened, on average, by up to 3.2 points of those of ALKS
3831 subjects whose PANSS scores were collected.
