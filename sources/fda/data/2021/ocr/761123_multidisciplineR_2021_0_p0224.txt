13 NONCLINICAL TOXICOLOGY

13.1 Carcinogenesis, Mutagenesis, Impairment of Fertility

Blocking the IFNAR1 pathway is expected to have increased carcinogenic potential. In
2014, the Applicant proposed to address the carcinogenic potential by product labeling
and clinical risk management practices without performing additional in vivo animal
studies. The agency agreed with their plan on 10/15/2014 under IND 101849 (End of
Phase 2 meeting minutes). The Applicant also reiterated the same conclusion regarding
carcinogenicity risk assessment in Modules 2.4 and 2.6.6 of the BLA submission.

The Applicant proposed the following language for this section on 7/31/2020 (SDN 1):

“The carcinogenic and genotoxic potential of anifrolumab-xxxx have not been
evaluated”.

In a nonclinical information request (IR) dated 05/07/2021 the Applicant was asked to
provide appropriate labeling to address the potential carcinogenic risk, as discussed
above. The Applicant provided updated language for this section on 5/14/2021 (SDN
32). They proposed to add the following language:

“In rodent models of IFNAR1 blockade, increased carcinogenic potential has been
observed. The clinical relevance of these findings is unknown”.

A request for an evaluation of the proposed labeling for Section 13.1 was made by
email on 06/08/2021 to the Executive Carcinogenicity Assessment Committee (Exec
CAC). The Exec CAC considered the above language sufficient to reveal carcinogenic
potential.
