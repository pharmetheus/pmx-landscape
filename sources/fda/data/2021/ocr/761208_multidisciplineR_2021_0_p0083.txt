Renal impairment:

No dose modification is recommended in patients with mild or moderate renal impairment. No
formal clinical trial has been conducted to assess the effect of renal function on the PK of
tisotumab vedotin. The effect of mild and moderate renal impairment on tisotumab vedotin
and unconjugated MMAE exposure was assessed using the population PK model. The
population PK analysis included patients with mild (n=142) and moderate (n=42) renal
impairment, in addition to patients with normal (n=215) renal function. There was no marked
difference in model-predicted Cycle 1 tisotumab vedotin and unconjugated MMAE exposures
between patients with normal renal function and those with mild and moderate renal
impairment (Figure 5).

In addition, comparison of safety by baseline renal function (CLcr per the Cockcroft-Gault
formula) across all subjects who received tisotumab vedotin at 2.0 mg/kg Q3W IV in studies
GCT1015-04, GEN701, SGNTV-001, GCT1015-03, and GEN702 (Table 6) indicated no marked
differences in tolerability observed between patients with mild or moderate renal insufficiency
at baseline and those with normal renal function.
