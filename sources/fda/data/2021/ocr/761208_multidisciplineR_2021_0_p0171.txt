Although patients 2 65 years experienced higher grade TEAEs and SAEs slightly more frequently
than patients <65 years, this analysis is limited by the small number of patients 265 years
enrolled to GCT1015-04. The details of the modest difference in AE incidence according to age
are described in Section 8.5 of product labeling.

FDA did not analyze safety by sex as all patients with cervical cancer enrolled included in
GCT1015-04 and Pool 2 were female. FDA did not analyze safety by race or ethnicity due to the
small numbers of patients representing racial or ethnic minority groups enrolled to receive
tisotumab vedotin.

FDA did not conduct independent analyses of safety by number of prior lines of therapy, prior
bevacizumab therapy, or prior radiotherapy but did review the Applicant’s analyses. In general,
these subgroups are small, and the clinical relevance of differences in safety in these subgroups
is uncertain. FDA does not consider the differences in safety to change the risk-benefit of
tisotumab vedotin for any of these subgroups defined by prior therapy.

FDA agrees that no tisotumab vedotin dose modifications are needed in patients with mild
hepatic impairment and in patients with mild or moderate renal impairment. Tisotumab
vedotin should be avoided in patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment. Please
refer to Clinical Pharmacology section 6.3.2.3 for further information.
