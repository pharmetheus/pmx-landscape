{tisotumab vedotin}

Logistic regression analysis determined a positive relationship between tisotumab vedotin
Cycle 1 Cmax and ORR. Tisotumab vedotin Cycle 1 AUC was also a predictor of ORR in the base
model but not significant in the full covariate model. There was a relationship of increased
probability of response with an increase in tisotumab vedotin Cmax, but responses were
observed across the exposure tertiles. In the lowest exposure tertile, an ORR of 14.7% was
observed, indicating that even patients within the lowest tertile of exposure have the potential
to derive benefit from tisotumab vedotin (Report No. SR1015-249). DOR and PFS were not
correlated with ADC exposure in the full covariate model. The relationships between tisotumab
vedotin exposure and efficacy endpoints suggest that treatment with 2.0 mg/kg tisotumab
vedotin was associated with clinically meaningful efficacy across the entire range of tisotumab
vedotin concentrations in recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer (m2.7.2, Section 3.6.4.4).

The Applicant's Position:
The relationships between tisotumab vedotin exposure and efficacy endpoints show that

treatment with 2.0 mg/kg tisotumab vedotin 1Q3W was associated with clinically meaningful
efficacy across the entire range of concentrations in subjects with recurrent or metastatic
cervical cancer who have progressed on or after chemotherapy.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA agrees that the clinical pharmacology program provides evidence of efficacy at the 2
mg/kg Q3W IV dosage in the proposed indication.

The Applicant’s exposure-response analyses support the proposed dose. Exposure-safety
analysis included the data from 272 subjects administered tisotumab vedotin 2.0 mg/kg Q3W IV
in studies GEN701 (expansion cohort), GEN702 (expansion cohort), and GCT1015-04. Exposure-
efficacy analysis included the data from 101 subjects from Study GCT1015-04. As the
population PK analysis showed that there was no appreciable accumulation of ADC and MMAE
with repeated dosing, Cycle 1 exposure measures were used for all exposure-response
analyses. The exposure-response analyses indicate relationships with both safety and efficacy
at the range of exposures achieved after the first cycle of treatment at the 2 mg/kg Q3W IV
dosage (please refer to the pharmacometrics review in section 19.4.2 for a detailed analysis).
