Anti-drug Antibodies

After the first dose of HuMax-TF-ADC, anti-drug antibodies were detected in
7/10, 10/10, 1/3, and 6/10 animals administered 1, 3, 4, and 5 mg/kg,
respectively. Following subsequent doses, nearly all animals produced anti-drug
antibodies.

An increase in antibody titers was reported over time.

No gender differences were noted in anti-drug antibody formation.

Reproductive
Investigations

No remarkable changes in the duration of menstrual cycles were noted.

A decrease in testicular size was reported for all dose levels and the 25 mg/kg
HuMax-TF group.

A reduction in sperm count and motility was noted at the 3 and 5 mg/kg dose
levels. These findings were still present in recovery animals consistent with
macroscopic and microscopic toxicities in the male reproductive organs.
