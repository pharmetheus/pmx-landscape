BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {Biologics License Application (BLA) 761208}
{tisotumab vedotin}

The Applicant's Position:
Tisotumab vedotin, administered at the recommended dose of the 2.0 mg/kg 1Q3W, does not

have a clinically meaningful effect on the QTc prolongation.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Based on the FDA QT IRT’s analysis, tisotumab vedotin treatment at 2 mg/kg Q3W IV does not
cause large mean increases (>20 msec) in the QTc interval. Please refer to the FDA QT IRT
review dated 4/21/21 for further information.

Immunogenicity

Data:

Serum samples of subjects from the GCT1015-04, as well as ADA Pool 1 and ADA Pool 2, were
analyzed for ADAs to tisotumab vedotin during the course of clinical development. ADA Pool 2
also included data from 56 subjects in the SGNTV-001 trial to enlarge the dataset to 305
subjects and include subjects that were a part of the PopPK analysis.

In GCT1015-04, approximately 2% of subjects were positive for ADAs at baseline. Across all ADA
Pools, the percentage of subjects positive for ADAs at baseline ranged from 2.1% to 3.9% (ISS
Table 10.4.14). In GCT1015-04, approximately 5% of the subjects were confirmed to have
treatment emergent ADAs. 4.3% of subjects were confirmed to be positive for treatment-
emergent ADA in the ADA Pool 2.

A total of 305 subjects were tested for ADA; 13 subjects (4.3%) were confirmed to be positive
for ADA at any post-baseline timepoint (ISS Table 10.4.14).

The Applicant's Position:
Overall, the rate of immunogenicity to tisotumab vedotin was low. The presence of ADA had no

clinically meaningful effect on tisotumab vedotin pharmacokinetics. There is no evidence of a
significant safety risk associated with immunogenicity.

The FDA’s Assessment:

In Study GCT1015-04, 5 of 93 (5.4%) patients who had immunogenicity assessment at baseline
and at least one time point post-dose, developed treatment-emergent ADA against tisotumab
vedotin. Two of these 5 patients in Study 04, tested positive for neutralizing antibodies against
tisotumab vedotin. Across studies, 145 patients with cervical cancer treated at the proposed 2
mg/kg Q3W IV dosage in Studies GEN701, GEN702, 04 had immunogenicity assessment at
baseline and at least one time point post-dose. Eight of 145 patients (5.5%) developed
treatment-emergent ADA against tisotumab vedotin. Due the limited number of patients who
were positive for ADA during treatment with tisotumab vedotin, the impact of ADA on the PK,
safety and efficacy of tisotumab vedotin is not fully characterized.
