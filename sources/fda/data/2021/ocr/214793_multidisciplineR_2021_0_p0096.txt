Arefined imputation that incorporated patient-specific factors including baseline tumor stage,
baseline Gleason score, and baseline PSA was performed in the Intent-To-Image Set. Two
patients ( a) were imputed with 0.75 probability of pelvic lymph node
histopathology positive for prostate cancer because they had bone biopsies positive for distant
metastatic disease and were therefore considered at higher risk for pelvic lymph node
metastases. Following discussion with the clinical team, the remaining patients without
reference standard information were imputed with 0.5 probability of pelvic lymph node
histopathology positive for prostate cancer if they were categorized as very high risk by NCCN
criteria and with 0.25 probability of pelvic lymph node histopathology positive for prostate
cancer if they were categorized as high risk by NCCN criteria. The results of this refined
imputation are presented side-by-side with the Evaluable Set results in Table 45.
