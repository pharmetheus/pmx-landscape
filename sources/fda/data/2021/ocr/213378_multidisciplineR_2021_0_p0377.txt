Dosing Solution Analysis

The concentrations of RDC-0313 in the dosing formulations were within 7% of the
nominal concentration throughout the study (tested at weeks 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, and 12), with
the exception of week 3 for the 0.60 mg/ml formulation (dose level 3 mg/kg/day) which
was +11.3% (just slightly outside the acceptance range of +10%).
