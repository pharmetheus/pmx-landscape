Subgroups

The following figures present the subgroup analyses for the percent change in body weight at
Day 92 for each of the olanzapine + samidorphan doses vs. olanzapine + placebo and for the
combined olanzapine + samidorphan group vs. olanzapine + placebo. Of particular interest are
the lead-in weight gain strata and the baseline BMI subgroups. A trend for increased treatment
effect with olanzapine + samidorphan (10 mg, 20 mg, and overall) was observed in two
subgroups of interest: 1) the subgroup of subjects with weight gain of at least 1 kg in the
olanzapine lead-in week, and 2) the subgroup of subjects with baseline BMI less than 25 kg/m?.
This is consistent with the observed weight gain with olanzapine (most weight gain observed
early and in patients with lowest BMIs) and could potentially identify a patient population more
likely to achieve benefit with the addition of samidorphan in future trials.
