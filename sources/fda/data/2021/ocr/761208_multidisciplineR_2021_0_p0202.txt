interest: body weight on CL, V1, V2, and Q; albumin and sex on CL, V1 and V2; age, ECOG, hepatic
impairment, eGFR, tumor size, and tumor type on CL; ECOG, hepatic impairment, and eGFR on V1 were
studied in the development of the ADC covariate model. All other covariates including race were tested
by diagnostic plots of random effects versus covariates. No dependencies on race were noticeable to be
clinically meaningful. The effects of body weight on CL, V1, V2, and Q, and the effects of albumin and sex
