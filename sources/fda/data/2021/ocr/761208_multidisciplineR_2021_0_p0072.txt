Immunogenicity

Please see OBP review regarding the bioanalytical performance of the
ADA and neutralizing antibody assays.

In Study 04, 93 patients with cervical cancer treated at the proposed
dosage had immunogenicity assessment at baseline and at least one time
point post-dose. Five of 93 (5.4%) patients developed treatment-
emergent ADA against tisotumab vedotin. Of these 5 patients, 2 patients
first tested positive for treatment-emergent ADA at C4, 1 patient first
tested positive at C7 and 2 patients first tested positive at the treatment
discontinuation visit. Two of these 5 patients in Study 04, tested positive
for neutralizing antibodies against tisotumab vedotin.
