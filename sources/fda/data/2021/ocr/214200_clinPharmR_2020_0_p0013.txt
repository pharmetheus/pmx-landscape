Anti-Proliferative Activity on Lymphocytes and Bone Marrow

The anti-proliferative activity of trilaciclib was assessed in healthy subjects following a single
infusion of trilaciclib (G1T28-1-01). A single IV infusion of trilaciclib resulted in a dose-
dependent decrease in the percentage of EdU+ cells within the T-lymphocyte population,
reflecting an inhibition of the proliferation of T-lymphocytes (Figure 1). A maximum mean
inhibition of T-lymphocyte proliferation of 37.2% (at a dose of 96 mg/m?) and 60% (at dose of
192 mg/m”) was observed at 4 h after the end of infusion. Although T-lymphocyte proliferation
started to recover from then on, inhibition of proliferation persisted until the last sampling time
point at 24 hours after end of infusion.
