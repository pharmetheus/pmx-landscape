Study A303
There were 63 subjects (11.5%) that experienced an AEDC. The most common AEs were
increased glycosylated hemoglobin, somnolence, neutrophil count decreased, weight
increased, schizophrenia, and drug abuse. There were two instances of mental status change:
One subject randomized to ALKS 3831, upon treatment initiation, experienced disorientation
along with somnolence, nausea, and vomiting; another subject randomized to olanzapine
treatment experienced decreased level of consciousness—this subject admitted to continuing
his prior psychiatric medications (divalproex, mirtazapine, and paliperidone) despite study drug
initiation.
