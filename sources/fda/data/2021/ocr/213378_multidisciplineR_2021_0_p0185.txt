As this analysis was conducted on the safety population, there was no analysis conducted for
FAS 1 and FAS 2. An exploratory subgroup analysis suggests a trend in favor of ALKS 3831 over
olanzapine in changes in fasting triglyceride, glucose, and insulin values for the olanzapine 1-

week run-in with weight gain >1 kg subgroup (data not shown).

Mean supine systolic and diastolic blood pressure values were normal at baseline and changed
small amounts over the 12-week randomized period.
