Chemocentryx provided a response on July 7, 2014. Historical control data from the
conducting laboratory ®® was provided and is presented in Table 7.
The incidences of lymphocytic infiltration and single cell necrosis are within the range of
historical controls. The acinar atrophy finding in 3/12 HD females is outside of the
historical control range for females at the testing facility. However, the sponsor provides
reference to a recently published paper by Chadwick et al.' in which 26/36 (72%) control
Sprague-Dawley rats had evidence of acinar cell atrophy/inflammation.

The sponsor has provided adequate historical control and literature-based evidence that
the pancreatic lesions observed in this study were not related to CCX168 treatment.
Therefore, the no observed adverse effect level (NOAEL) in the rat is 100 mg/kg/day.
