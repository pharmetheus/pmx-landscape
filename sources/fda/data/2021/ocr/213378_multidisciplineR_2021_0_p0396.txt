Key Study Findings

e Not carcinogenic up to 500 mg/kg/day in male and female Tg.rasH2 mice
e Statistically significant increase in mortality for low and mid dose males, and high
dose females compared to respective controls

Adequacy of Carcinogenicity Study

Adequate. There was a statistically significant increase in the incidence of lung and
spleen tumors in the positive control group, 1000 mg/kg/day urethane, demonstrating a
response in the test system.

Appropriateness of Test Models

The Tg.rasH2 mouse model is an acceptable model to use in short-term carcinogenicity
assays as an alternative to the traditional two-year mouse carcinogenicity (ICH S1B).

Evaluation of Tumor Findings

A separate statistical review was conducted by Dr. Hepei Chen from the Division of
Biometrics, her conclusion agrees with that of the Applicant that there was no
statistically significant increase in tumors in the drug-treated groups compared to
controls. The statistical review can be found in DARRTs under NDA
