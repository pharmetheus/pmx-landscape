Limitations associated with the anifrolumab IV safety database
include the lack of concomitant IV cyclophosphamide or other
biologics including those that target B cells (e.g., rituximab or

central nervous system disease. The small numbers of patients
available for subgroup analysis of gender (males), age (2 65 years of

determination of the product’s safety profile in these subgroups.
