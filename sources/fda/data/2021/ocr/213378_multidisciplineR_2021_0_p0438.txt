Key Study Findings

e No drug-related adverse effects on any maternal Fp parameters. NOAEL for
maternal toxicity is 100 mg/kg/day.

e 100 mg/kg/day: decrease in F, pup survival from birth to PND 4, decreased F,
pup birth weights and body weight gain during the pre-weaning and post-weaning
phases.

e No drug-related effects on F; sexual maturation, reproduction, neurobehavioral
function, and no effects on Fz embryo survival.

e NOAEL for F, survival and growth is 30 mg/kg/day, which is approximately 36
times the MRHD of 10 mg SAM based on AUC.

e NOAEL for F, reproductive and Fz embryonic toxicity is 100 mg/kg/day, which is
approximately 188 times the MRHD of 10 mg SAM based on AUC.
