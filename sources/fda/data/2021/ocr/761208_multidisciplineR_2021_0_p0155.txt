FDA agrees with the Applicant that 55% of patients experiencing ocular toxicity in Pool 2 had
resolution to baseline, and this frequency was even higher in GCT1015-04 with 73% of patients
having resolution to baseline. Among the n=6 patients who developed Grade 3 ocular toxicity in
Pool 2, n=4 patients experienced resolution. Data on resolution are not available for the n=2
patients with grade 3 keratitis detected in the non-treatment-emergent period in GCT105-04.
The patient with Grade 4 toxicity initially had grade 3 ulcerative keratitis which worsened and
required corneal transplantation. This toxicity was only considered resolved following this
procedure.
