8.1.2 Study Results
Compliance with Good Clinical Practices

Data:

Investigator sites and service provider audits were conducted by Genmab A/S to assess
compliance with Good Clinical Practice (GCP) and the GCT1015-04 protocol. A consolidated
audit certificate is included in Appendix 16.1.8 of the protocol.

The Applicant's Position:

The protocol for this study was designed in accordance with the general ethical principles
outlined in the Declaration of Helsinki (World Medical Association 2013). The conduct of all
aspects of the study, including methods for obtaining informed consent, were also in
accordance with principles enunciated in the declaration, the ICH GCP, and applicable regional
regulations/guidelines.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that this study was conducted in compliance with
principles outlined in the Declaration of Helsinki, ICH GCP, and other local guidelines.

Financial Disclosure

Data:
No investigators had significant equity interests (eg, stock and/or stock options) in Genmab A/S
or Seagen Inc. as defined in 21 the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) 54.2b.

The Applicant's Position:
No financial interests were disclosed by the investigators. Additionally, the potential impact of

financial interests is minimized by multi-center study design, assessment of the primary
endpoint of ORR by IRC, and safety review by the DMC.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant that no financial interests were disclosed by investigators and
that financial interests were unlikely to impact GCT1015-04. See details in Appendix 19.2.

Patient Disposition

Data:
At the GCT1015-04 data cutoff, 96.0% of subjects had discontinued from treatment (Table 9).
