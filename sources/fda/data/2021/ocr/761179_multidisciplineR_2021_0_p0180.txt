Study sample
analysis/ stability

In response to IR dated February 24, 2021, the applicant stated that the SAA
samples for study JZP458-101 were stored for up to 16 days at the clinical
sites and up to 38 days at the bioanalytical laboratory at -60°C to -85°C prior
to analysis. Accordingly, the maximum long-term stability needed to cover
sample storage is 54 days. Therefore, the long-term stability assessment for
183 days at -60°C to -80°C covers the storage condition and duration of the
study samples.
