The Division concluded that a drug discrimination study with atogepant was not necessary;
however, it requested that the Sponsor conduct a self-administration study in rats.

Following the June 2017 meeting, Allergan submitted a revised protocol to Study CGP-MD-01,
and a revised Statistical Analysis Plan in response to the Division’s feedback. The subsequent
changes made to both the protocol and Statistical Analysis Plan adequately addressed the
Division’s concerns regarding the proposed analysis of the primary endpoint, the addition of a
secondary endpoint to assess the use of rescue medication, the Sponsor’s overall study design,
and the plan for testing the proposed doses.

In a submission dated June 29, 2018, the Sponsor requested a meeting to discuss the suitability of
patient-reported outcome instruments for atogepant clinical studies. In the FDA preliminary
meeting comments, the Division expressed concerns regarding the lack of definition for
“headache,” and the lack of clear instructions for patients in the instructions for the Sponsor’s
novel patient-reported outcome, the Activity Impairment in Migraine Diary (AIM-D). The
Division stated that it was unclear how the Sponsor planned to analyze AIM-D scores during
headache and nonheadache days. The Division provided additional recommendations concerning
the firm’s proposed psychometric study protocol synopsis and statistical plan. The Sponsor
canceled the meeting, indicating that the preliminary comments sent by the Division were
sufficiently clear and complete. The Division issued Final Written Responses to Allergan on
September 11, 2018.
