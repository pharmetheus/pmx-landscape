Patient ID: GCT1015-04- ©

This patient is a 68 yo woman with metastatic SCC of the cervix involving the cervix, pelvic and
extrapelvic lymph nodes, bone, right ventricle, and liver. She received three cycles of tisotumab
vedotin. From study days 42 to 67, she was treated with dalteparin sodium for a cardiac
thrombus (which was subsequently determined to be a cardiac metastasis). On study day 68,
she developed grade 3 vaginal hemorrhage and was admitted. She received treatment with
vaginal compression, tranexamic acid, and metholprednisolone. She had concurrent grade 3
anemia and thrombocytopenia and received transfusions of red blood cells and platelets. Her
vaginal hemorrhage improved to grade 1 and she was discharged. On study day 70, she
resumed dalteparin sodium. On study day 81, vaginal hemorrhage worsened to grade 3, she
received transfusions with red blood cells and platelets and receive tranexamic acid. On study
day 84, tisotumab vedotin was discontinued because of radiographic progression of disease. On
study days 85-90, she received palliative radiation for hemorrhage. On study day 101, she died
due to underlying disease.

Patient ID: GCT1015-04- °°

This patient is a 42 yo woman with SCC of the cervix involving extra-pelvic lymph nodes.
Following five cycles of tisotumab vedotin, she developed grade 1 lower GI hemorrhage.
Following eight cycles of tisotumab vedotin, on study day 153, she developed grade 3 rectal
hemorrhage, received a transfusion, and hemorrhage resolved. She continued to receive 13
cycles of tisotumab vedotin until treatment was discontinued due to radiographic progression
of disease. It appears the patient received enoxaparin sodium from study day -14 to 106 for a
DVT and may have also briefly received warfarin. Her platelets were between 170 and 250x
1049/L for the duration of the trial.

Patient ID: GCT1015-04-"©

This patient is a 51 yo woman with SCC involving the bone and extrapelvic LN. On study day 3
she experienced grade 1 epistaxis which resolved on study day 105. She received seven cycles
of tisotumab vedotin. On study day 133, she developed grade 3 hemorrhagic cystitis. She was
admitted and a urinary catheter was placed. She received TMP/SMx for treatment of cystitis
and hematuria resolved. She developed grade 2 anemia. On study day 134, hematuria resolved
and she was discharged. On study day 157, the patient underwent cystoscopy due to hematuria
which was reported as normal. She received 12 cycles of tisotumab vedotin until treatment was
discontinued due to radiographic progression of disease. She died on study day 496 due to
underlying disease. Her aPTT, PT, and INR were within normal limits throughout the trial. Her
platelet count was between 180 and 350x10%9/L throught the trial.
