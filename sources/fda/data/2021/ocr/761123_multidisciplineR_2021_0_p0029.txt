A post-marketing, required pediatric study to
assess the safety, efficacy, and
pharmacokinetics of anifrolumab plus
background standard therapy in pediatric
subjects ages 5 years to 17 years of age with
active systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) will
be conducted. Based on the high proportion of
women of child-bearing potential among SLE
patients, a post-marketing required
prospective pregnancy registry to evaluate the
effects of anifrolumab on pregnancy and
maternal and fetal/neonatal outcomes and a
retrospective cohort study in a claims-based
database to evaluate the effects of
anifrolumab on pregnancy-related outcomes
will be conducted. The pregnancy registry
study may be conducted as part of a multiple-
product or disease-based registry. A lactation
study, milk only, in lactating women who
receive anifrolumab to assess concentrations
of anifrolumab in breast milk using a validated
assay will be conducted. A mother-infant pair
study may be required in the future depending
on the results of the milk-only study.
