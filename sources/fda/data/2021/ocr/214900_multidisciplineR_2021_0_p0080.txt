6.2.2. General Dosing and Therapeutic Individualization

The Applicant’s proposed dosage regimen of ibrexafungerp is 300 mg (two 150 mg tablets)
twice daily for one day for a total treatment dosage of 600 mg. This regimen is supported by
the efficacy and safety results from the clinical trials submitted in the NDA.
