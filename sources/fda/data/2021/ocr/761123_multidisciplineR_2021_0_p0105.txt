Study 04

BICLA

There were 34 patients on the anifrolumab 300mg arm and 50 patients on the placebo arm that
had missing week 52 data. Of these, 31 initiated restricted medication, and are considered non-
responders for the tipping point analyses. Therefore, the tipping point analyses considers the
53 patients, 25 on anifrolumab 300mg, and 28 on placebo, as having missing data.

Table 33 below displays the number of additional placebo responders needed to tip results per
additional number of added responders on anifrolumab. For example, given 0 additional
responders on anifrolumab 300mg, it would require at least 12 additional responders on
placebo.

Based on placebo completers, an estimated response rate is 39.4%, and the estimated response
rate among anifrolumab completers in this study is 55.5%. All scenarios that result in tipping
the conclusions would require at least 43% of the placebo subjects with missing data to be
responders. If we further consider that at least some additional anifrolumab 300mg responders
could be expected based on these observed rates, an even higher response rate would be
required among missing placebo subjects to tip the results, making such a scenario less likely.
