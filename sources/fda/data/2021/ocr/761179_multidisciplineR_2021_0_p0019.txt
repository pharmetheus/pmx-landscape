Maintaining adequate NSAA levels is critical to successful treatment of ALL. The result of the
PopPK analyses and simulations in Study JZP458-201 showed that administration of JZP-458 at
25 mg/m? 1M every 48 hours (48-hour dosing) or at 25-25-50 mg/m? MWF (MWF dosing) would
maintain adequate NSAA levels. Although the MWF dosing would be more convenient,
concerns were raised regarding the safety of the high dose of JZP-458 given on Fridays in the
latter regimen. At the present time, there is no established biomarker for safety of
asparaginase class products, so safety can be assessed only with clinical trial experience.

The adverse events of special interest (AESI) for JZP-458 included hepatotoxicity,
hypersensitivity, pancreatitis, and thrombosis. In the exposure-safety analyses, there was a
