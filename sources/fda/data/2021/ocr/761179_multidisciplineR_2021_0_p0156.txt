AESI: Pancreatitis

and lipase), and the grouped term pancreatitis, which includes the PT “acute pancreatitis,”
“pancreatitis,” and “amylase increased.” Patients with grade 1 increases in amylase or lipase at
baseline at excluded. FDA Table 46 shows this analysis.
