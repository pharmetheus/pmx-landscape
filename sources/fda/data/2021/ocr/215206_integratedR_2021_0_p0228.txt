6.0Addendum 1: After completing this consultation on May 16, 2021, the sponsor sent

a 120-Day Safety Report (Sq 17, SDN 17, 1.11.3) on May 19, 2021. The report
covered safety data through Jan 31, 2021.

Report Review Summary: After review of the report, our assessment and
recommendations in Section 5.0 are largely unchanged. However, the report raises
questions about an increased DILI risk in Japanese, or Asians in general. It appears
that the proportion of patients with elevation in transaminases is higher in Japanese
in a Phase 3 study that is not included in the ISS Group 1 or 2 of the NDA. Precise
proportions of patients with elevated transaminases by race are not provided, and
we would like such data in this Phase 3 and the ISS. Details of our report review are
below. Upon our suggestion, DN2 sent an information request on Jun 2, 2021.

Full Report Review: The Safety Report has two areas of interest: (1) Updated data
for the ISS Group 2 and (2) New cases of liver enzyme elevations in 2 studies
outside the ISS Groups.

6.1ISS Group 2 OLE Studies: Updated eDISH plot did not find any cases in Hy’s
Law quadrant. Proportions of patients with transaminases over 3x ULN and
higher were not significantly changed from earlier data sent with the NDA.
Just 1 new case (IP, Study 3101-309-002) required review with AST
elevation over 3x ULN. We reviewed the case narrative and graphic. This
was not DILI. AST elevation occurred 324 days after AGPT start (60 mg/d).
The rise was concurrent with increased physical activity and CPK of 4806
U/L. ALT was 38 U/L and bilirubin 0.2 mg/dL. Patient also reported recent
increase in alcohol intake. CPK and AST fell to normal within 30 days. AGPT
was continued without dose change.

6.2 Cases from Studies Not Included in Group 2 OLE Studies: The report
describes 5 new case of liver enzyme elevations. No cases met Hy’s Law
liver test elevation criteria. No case developed jaundice. All recovered.
