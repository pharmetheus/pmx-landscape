Liver Enzymes
Liver enzyme increase is a known antipsychotic class effect, and hepatotoxicity has been

associated with naltrexone, an opioid antagonist with a similar mechanism of action to
samidorphan. Subjects in the ALKS 3831 group experienced mean shifts from baseline similar to
the olanzapine group. Markedly abnormal liver enzyme results were infrequent and similar
between the treatment groups as follows:
