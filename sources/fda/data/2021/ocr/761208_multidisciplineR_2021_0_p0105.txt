Overall, the disease burden in patients experiencing tumor responses was low. Only one patient
experiencing a response had tumor sum of diameters >5 cm in diameter, and no patients
experiencing a response had bulky metastatic disease. However, based upon the mechanism of
action of tisotumab, as an antibody-drug conjugate, there does not seem to be reason to
expect that patients with bulkier disease may not respond to therapy with tisotumab vedotin,
and the results observed in Study GCT1015-104 may have been due to a small sample size or
chance. The review team did not think that restrictions on use of tisotumab vedotin, according
to disease burden, were warranted.
