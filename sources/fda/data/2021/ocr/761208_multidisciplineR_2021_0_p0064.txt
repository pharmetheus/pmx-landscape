6.2 Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1 Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:

Following tisotumab vedotin IV infusion, peak tisotumab vedotin concentrations were attained
at the end of infusion. Plasma concentrations of free MMAE increased until ~2-3 days after
tisotumab vedotin dosing. No accumulation of tisotumab vedotin or free MMAE was observed
on repeat dosing.

Based on population PK, the typical value of nonspecific linear clearance (CL) of tisotumab
vedotin was 1.42 L/day. Following a tisotumab vedotin dose of 2.0 mg/kg, * 60% of tisotumab
vedotin was eliminated by linear CL. Terminal median half-lives were 4.04 days for tisotumab
vedotin and 2.56 days for MMAE, respectively. Following 2.0 mg/kg tisotumab vedotin IV, the
typical values for tisotumab vedotin central volume of distribution and peripheral volume of
distribution were 3.10 L and 4.47 L, respectively.
