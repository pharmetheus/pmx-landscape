8.1 Pregnancy and 8.2 Lactation

General edits were made to Sections 8.1 and 8.2 to remove unnecessary text. The post
marketing requirement (PMR) for pregnancy and lactation, including Pregnancy
Registry was recommended by the Division of Pediatrics and Maternal Health (DPMH)
on 5/24/2021. The Applicant agreed with the recommendations on 07/26/2021 (SDN
47). The language for Pregnancy Exposure Registry was added to Section 8.1 (not
shown in this review because it is not relevant to nonclinical labeling text).

For Risk Summary in Section 8.1, the Applicant estimated that the exposures in
pregnant monkeys without maternal and developmental toxicity was up to approximately

| times the exposure at the MRHD on an AUC basis. Since the AUCp-14q for the
NOAEL of 60 mg/kg once every 2 weeks was 20900 yg.d/mL, and the highest mean
AUCo.28a in patients with SLE receiving repeat IV dosing of 300 mg anifrolumab-fnia
(once every 4 weeks for 52 weeks) was 1472 yg.d/mL between Weeks 48 to 52 for low
Type 1 IFN gene signature subjects, the safety margin was calculated to be 28 (=
20900 x 2/1472). A factor of 2 is applied to match the duration of AUC values before the
comparison, i.e., 14 days vs. 28 days.

For Animal Data in Section 8.1, language was added to indicate that there is no
anifrolumab-fnia related effect on T-cell-dependent antibody response in the infants up
to Day 180 after birth. Additional language was added to indicate the potential of
placenta transfer of anifrolumab-fnia based on the detection of anifrolumab-fnia in the
serum of neonates up to one month after birth.

For Risk Summary in Section 8.2, additional standard language was added. Although
anifrolumab-fnia was detected in the milk of female cynomolgus monkeys, we added
that “due to species-species differences in lactation physiology, animal data may not
reliably predict drug levels in humans”.
