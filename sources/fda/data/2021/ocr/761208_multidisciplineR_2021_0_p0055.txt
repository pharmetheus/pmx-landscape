The Applicant's Position:

The toxicity profile of tisotumab vedotin was established in a comprehensive series of
nonclinical safety studies in cynomolgus monkeys, the only cross-reactive toxicology species.

o®

Target-mediated uptake of tisotumab vedotin led to dose limiting skin toxicity at approximately
4-fold the human systemic exposure (AUC) at the clinically recommended dose. Skin toxicity, in
addition to conjunctival changes, correlated with TF expression. Bone marrow hypocellularity
and testicular toxicity were the primary target-independent toxicities and were observed with
both tisotumab vedotin and MMAE. Additional target organs identified following MMAE
treatment included lymphoid organs, gastrointestinal tract, and liver. The target organs of skin,
bone marrow and conjunctiva correlated with clinically observed toxicities and were reversible
following cessation of treatment. No toxicities were observed with the unconjugated antibody
(tisotumab), suggesting no impact from antigen binding alone.
