6.3.2.2 Is the proposed dosing regimen appropriate for the general
patient population for which the indication is being sought?

Yes, tisotumab vedotin administered by IV infusion at 2.0 mg/kg, up to a maximum dose of
200 mg 103W is appropriate for patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer. The
proposed dosing regimen is supported by the observed efficacy, safety, and PK data.

Data:

During dose escalation in the Phase 1 trial GEN701, the highest dose evaluated was 2.2 mg/kg
103W, but due to 3 DLTs (diabetes mellitus type I, mucositis and neutropenic fever, all grade
3) reported in 3 subjects, the dose was reduced to 2.0 mg/kg 103W, which was defined as the
maximum tolerated dose and RP2D. Tisotumab vedotin doses were administered based on
subject body weight, except for subjects >100 kg.

Logistic regression analysis, performed as part of the efficacy-response analysis, determined a
