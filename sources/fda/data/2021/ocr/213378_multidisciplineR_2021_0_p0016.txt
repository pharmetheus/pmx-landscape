e Study A303 demonstrated that, among people who were

eLimited data are available about the effects on pregnancy
and lactation. ALKS 3831 is likely to be used by women
of child-bearing potential and labeling with reflect this.
Any future approval would be accompanied with the
postmarketing requirement for a milk-only lactation
study.
