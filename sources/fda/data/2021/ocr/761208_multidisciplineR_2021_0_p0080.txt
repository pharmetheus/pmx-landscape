The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees there is no clinically meaningful effect of age (21 to 81 years), sex, race (White vs
Non-White) or ethnicity (Hispanic vs Non-Hispanic) on tisotumab vedotin or unconjugated
MMAE exposure. Population PK analysis identified body weight to be a statistically significant
covariate on clearance and volume of the distribution of tisotumab vedotin, which supports the
bodyweight-based dosing regimen. ADC and MMAE Cave were similar in patients with body
weight < 100 kg and > 100 kg (Figure 3), supporting the dose cap of 200 mg for patients > 100

kg.
