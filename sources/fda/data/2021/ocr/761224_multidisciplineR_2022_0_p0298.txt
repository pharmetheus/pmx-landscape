1 Executive Summary

1.1. Introduction

AstraZeneca submitted a 351(a) BLA 761224 application on May 7, 2021 for the use of
Tezepelumab (Trade Name: Tezespire) subcutaneous injection for the add-on
maintenance treatment of adult and pediatric patients aged 12 years and older with
severe asthma. The BLA was filed on July 6, 2021 as a priority review with a PDUFA goal
date of January 7, 2022.

Tezepelumab drug product for subcutaneous (SC) injection is a sterile, single-dose,
preservative-free solution presented at 110 mg/mL concentration in a 1.91 mL deliverable
volume in a vial and accessorized prefilled syringe (APFS). The proposed clinical dose is
210 mg tezepelumab to be administered once every four weeks.

On August 30, 2021, the pharmacology-toxicology reviewer received an informal consult
request via email from the assigned drug product reviewer, Dr. Andrea Franco (Office of
Pharmaceutical Quality, Office of Biotechnology Products). Dr. Franco requested
nonclinical safety assessment of the extractables and leachables in general and the levels
of following four organic compounds in particular-

This review evaluates the safety profile of all the extractables and leachables identified in
tezepelumab drug substance container closure system, and tezepelumab drug product
container closure systems- vial and accessorized prefilled syringe (APFS).

1.2 Brief Discussion of Nonclinical Findings

Controlled extractables and leachables studies conducted using the components of the
primary container closure system and tezepelumab drug substance and drug product
were evaluated to determine the identity and patient exposure levels to potential
extractables and leachables. All the extractable and leachable compounds and
elements were qualified from safety perspective.

1.3. Recommendations
1.3.1 Approvability

Based on the potential patient exposure levels and a review of available information, the
levels of each of these potential extractables and leachables in tezepelumab drug
substance and drug product are considered qualified from the nonclinical perspective.
