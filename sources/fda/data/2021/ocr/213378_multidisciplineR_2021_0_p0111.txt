Protocol Violations/Deviations

In this study, 29 subjects (5.3%) had “important” protocol deviations (15 receiving ALKS and 14
receiving olanzapine). The deviations included not meeting inclusion/exclusion criteria (<1%),
study drug non-compliance defined as taking <70% specified amount of medication (1.3%), and
receiving prohibited medications (3.6%). There were similar small numbers of protocol

deviations between the two treatment groups, so these were not likely to have markedly
affected efficacy results.
