BLA 761123 Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Saphnelo (anifrolumab-fnia) for adults with SLE

There were 9 patients on the anifrolumab 300mg arm and 20 on the placebo arm that had
missing data at week 24. Of these, 19 of them failed to meet the no excess medication and OCS
tapering criteria and are considered non-responders. Therefore, based on the definition of the
primary endpoint which incorporates use of excess medication and OCS tapering, only 10
patients, 1 on anifrolumab 300 mg and 9 on the placebo arm, are considered as having missing
data.

Table 30 below displays the number of additional placebo responders needed to tip results per
additional number of added responders on anifrolumab. For example, if we consider the single
patient on anifrolumab 300mg with missing data to remain a non-responder, the results do not
tip, i.e., does not change the conclusion, until at least 6 of the 9 placebo patients with missing
data are responders. If we consider the single patient in the anifrolumab 300mg arm to be a
responder, the results do not tip until at least 7 of the 9 placebo patients are responders.

To consider if such scenarios are plausible, we note that among placebo completers at Week
24, the response rate is 20.7%, and the estimated response rate among anifrolumab completers
is 37.8%. All scenarios that result in tipping the conclusions would require at least 67% of the
placebo subjects with missing data to be responders. Such a response rate would represent
almost a doubling of the estimated response rate of anifrolumab 300mg and over three times
the response rate observed among placebo completers. Such an increase in response rates for
placebo patients who do not have week 24 data seems implausible, and we therefore consider
these results to be robust to missing data assumptions.
