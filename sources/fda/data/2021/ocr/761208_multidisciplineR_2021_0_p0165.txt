8.2.4.4 Infusion-related Reactions

Data:

Infusion-related reactions were first programmatically identified in case report forms via a
check box for such reactions and must have occurred the day of, or after, an infusion of
tisotumab vedotin, followed by individual case clinical review to assess whether the event
represented a potential infusion-related reaction (IRR.) Clinical review of each event identified
took into consideration the temporal relationship to dose infusion, reported signs and
symptoms, action taken with dosing (eg, interruption of infusion, reduced rate of infusion on
resuming or subsequent infusion and/or introducing pre-medications), treatments or
management used for the event, concurrent conditions, medical history, and recurrence of
similar events on rechallenge, if available. In addition, exploratory analyses using MedDRA
SMQs of Anaphylaxis and Hypersensitivity were performed.

Infusion-related reactions (IRRs) meeting the programmatic search strategy were identified in
1 subject (1.0%) in GCT1015-04 (ISS Table 10.3.57). This subject developed grade 2 fatigue and
grade 2 pyrexia. The subject received subsequent doses of tisotumab vedotin with
premedication without recurrence of IRR.

A total of 7 subjects were assessed as having events representing possible IRR (Table 10.3.57).
