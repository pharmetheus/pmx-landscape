Study 1013

There were two versions of the statistical analysis plan (SAP), with the final version finalized in

Primary Endpoint:

The primary endpoint of SRI-4 + OCS tapering at week 24 was tested in the 1000mg and 300mg
arms against placebo in the whole population using the Cochran-Armitage trend test, and
likewise both doses were tested in the Type | IFN High subgroup. Subsequently, each dose was
nominally tested separately against placebo in each population. Below are described the pre-
specified methods used in the analyses.
