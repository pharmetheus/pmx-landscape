Study# ©® 4015-102: Intra-tumoral hemorrhages were observed in 1/5 xenograft models
(BxPC-3 pancreatic adenocarcinoma) when treated with tisotumab at 50 mg/kg followed by 25
mg/kg weekly and in 1/31 xenograft models (NCI-H441 lung adenocarcinoma) when treated
with tisotumab vedotin at 3 mg/kg twice weekly for 2 weeks or a single administration in the
range of 0.5 to 4.5 mg/kg. In the NCI-H441 model, hemorrhages were seen in the absence of
treatment as well but were enhanced with tisotumab vedotin treatment. Additionally, tumors
treated with a cMet inhibitor were also observed to hemorrhage; therefore, it’s likely that the
tumor model itself is prone to hemorrhaging. In general, hemorrhages were restricted to the
tumor area and did not appear to be dependent on the level of TF expressed by tumor cells,
resolved spontaneously, and were not associated with clinical signs of illness or weight loss.

Secondary Pharmacology data

In vitro inhibition of activated factor X (FXa) Generation by Tisotumab: Tisotumab (“6.6 nM, “1
pg/mL) inhibited FXa generation by 19% ( ® 1015-086). Tisotumab vedotin was not tested.

In vitro coagulation: The effects of tisotumab (unconjugated antibody) on outcomes related to
clot formation were investigated in four in vitro studies: standard prothrombin time (PT),
modified PT, thromboelastography (TEG), and thrombin generation assay (TGA)(264005,

1015-086, HxTFADC N Study Report 0361, HxTFADC N Study Report 0364). Results show
that tisotumab (10-20 pg/mL) can increase time to clot formation and surrogate substrate
generation when standard assay concentrations for TF are drastically reduced (2 1000-fold
dilution). Tisotumab vedotin (up to 20 pg/mL) was only tested in the standard PT study (no TF
dilution) and no effect was observed on clotting time. Tisotumab did not affect the strength of
clot formation.

Skin Bleeding Time in Cynomolgus Monkey: A non-GLP intra-animal dose escalation study was
conducted in cynomolgus monkeys to explore potential effects of tisotumab on coagulation and
bleeding via a skin incision test (©®514850). Tisotumab was administered as weekly doses of 0
(Phosphate Buffered Saline), 1, 10 and 100 mg/kg on days 1, 8, 15 and 22, respectively. There
was no relationship between tisotumab dose level (1-100 mg/kg) and bleeding times. No effects
on coagulation parameters were observed, and there were no toxicologically meaningful effects
with supra-pharmacologic exposures.
