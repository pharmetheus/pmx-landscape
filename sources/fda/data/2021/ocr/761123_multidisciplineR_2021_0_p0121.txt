The focus of this safety review is on the 52-week safety data for the 300 mg IV dose
summarized in the clinical study reports for studies 1013, 04 and 05—52-week Safety Pool—
including the Integrated Summary of Safety and electronic datasets for adverse events (AES),
lab data, and vital signs. The safety review is also supported by a P3 Safety Pool, comprising 52-
week 300 mg data for studies 04 and 05 only, a Phase 3 LTE Pool, consisting of up to 4 years of
data for the 300 mg dose in the long-term extension study recruiting from studies 04 and 05,
two 52-week dose-specific pools—150 mg Pool (from study 05) and 1000 mg Pool (from study
1013)—and an All Anifrolumab Pool, consisting of 52-week and long-term extension data for all
3 doses of anifrolumab. A summary of the Safety Pools is shown in Table 42.
