Clinical/Statistical Review
NDA 213378

Lybalvi (Olanzapine/Samidorphan, ALKS 3831)

Demographic Characteristics

In overall enrollment, there was a higher percentage of white males and subjects who were
overweight; slight male preponderance is typical for a population with schizophrenia. Although
this was an international study, sites outside of the United States were located in Europe, and
minority representation was inadequate and lower than their rates in the general United States
population. Therefore, this study’s findings may be of limited generalizability to those groups.
There were slight demographic imbalances between treatment arms for sex, race, and region:
more men were randomized to the ALKS 3831 group, whites to the olanzapine group, and
subjects in the United States to the ALKS 3831 group. These demographic imbalances were
small and, therefore, unlikely clinically significant. Additionally, subjects randomized to the
olanzapine group had higher mean baseline weight and BMI and a greater proportion were
obese (BMI >30 kg/m?) compared to the other two treatment groups. This weight discrepancy
may impact weight mitigation efficacy analysis, although weight was not a prespecified
endpoint in this study.
