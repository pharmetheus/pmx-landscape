4. APPENDICES

4.1 Summary of Bioanalytical Method Validation and Performance

Bioanalytical methods were developed and validated, following the US FDA guideline (Center
for Drug Evaluation and Research 2001), for the quantification of OLZ, SAM, and two
metabolites of SAM (RDC-9986 and RDC-1066) in human plasma and urine, utilizing LC-
MS/MS analysis. All of these validated methods met the performance acceptance criteria in the
guidance.

Bioanalysis in Plasma
Validation results demonstrated each method to be sensitive, selective, accurate, and

reproducible. Stability of the analytes was demonstrated on long-term storage and all study
samples were analyzed within validated long-term stability.

Bioanalysis in Urine

Validation results demonstrated each method to be sensitive, selective, accurate, and
reproducible. Stability of the analytes was demonstrated on long-term storage and all study
samples were analyzed within validated long-term stability.
