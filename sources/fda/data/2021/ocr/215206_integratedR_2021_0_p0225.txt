4.6.4 Case [IM (Study CGP-MD-01): Probable DILI due to AGPT

Summary: This is a 39 year-old white woman who developed elevated
transaminases w/o jaundice 12 days after starting Atogepant 60mg QD.

No mention of pre-existing liver disease. She drinks one alcoholic
beverage per week. Liver tests were normal at Day 0, but they had
climbed during screening. She had no symptoms when ALT and AST
rose on Day 12. No ALP or bilirubin rise. Drug was stopped 5 days after
DILI onset. Viral hepatitis serology testing was "negative". Autoimmune
hepatitis (AIH) markers negative. Ultrasound (US) unrevealing.

Patient was taking Excedrin Migraine "frequently" (every other day) but
precise dosing was not provided. This over the counter was continued
through the washout after stopping study drug.

Assessment: This case is a weak probable DILI due to the study drug.
APAP (acetaminophen) use competes some, but use continued through
the washout. It was also part of her baseline medications. Unclear if
APAP dosing changed. The sponsor suggests NAFLD, but that disease
would not present with such an acute rise and fall of ALT (peak of >9x
ULN). Sponsor notes inadvertent double dosing of IP, but this occurred
the day of DILI onset when ALT was already >300. The main argument
against DILI is that her ALT and AST had increased by 2.1x and 1.67x her
baseline even before drug start, so something else may have been
brewing (Figure 8). No other prior baseline liver enzyme data provided.
