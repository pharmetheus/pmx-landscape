{tisotumab vedotin}

Only 1 subject from the pivotal GCT1015-04 trial experienced an episode of potential IRR
reported as pyrexia and fatigue (both grade 2). The remaining 6 subjects were all from the
GEN701 trial and included 2 subjects with events of hypersensitivity, 2 subjects with events of
pyrexia, and 1 subject each of lethargy and hypotension. None of these events were life-
threatening, and only 3 subjects received treatment with antihistamines, steroids, and/or
acetaminophen. Three subjects received additional subsequent doses of tisotumab vedotin
without recurrence of IRR; only 1 of which received premedication on subsequent doses.

The incidence and severity of IRRs was similar across safety analysis groups.

No events consistent with anaphylaxis/anaphylactoid reactions or severe, life threatening
hypersensitivity were identified. There is no safety signal for anaphylaxis or systemic
hypersensitivity with tisotumab vedotin to date.

The Applicant's Position:
Few subjects experienced infusion-related reactions and these were of low clinical impact and

manageable by standard of care.

The FDA's Assessment:

FDA conducted an independent analysis of IRRs associated with tisotumab vedotin which were
infrequent. Given the low frequency of IRRs, FDA relied on the Pool 3 population for the
assessment and agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. The overall frequency of IRRs was low
with 1 patient enrolled to GCT1015-04 who experienced a grade 2 IRR, and 6 additional patients
in the Pool 3 population who experienced IRRs. Only one patient experienced a grade 3 IRR.
Four of the 7 patients experiencing IRRs were able to get subsequent treatment with tisotumab
vedotin, including the patient who experienced the grade 3 IRR.

8.2.4.5 Pneumonitis

The FDA’s Assessment

The Applicant did not include an assessment of pneumonitis in their submission. However, FDA
conducted an independent analysis of pneumonitis as pneumonitis has been observed with
other antibody-drug conjugates containing vedotin.

In the Pool 2 population, there were 2 patients who experienced pneumonitis, including 1
patient who died. Both patients were enrolled to GCT1015-04 and narratives are summarized
below.

As antibody-drug conjugates containing vedotin are associated with pneumonitis, and there
was one patient who received tisotumab vedotin, experienced pneumonitis and died, FDA
included this toxicity in Section 5: Warnings and Precautions of the USPI.
