NDA 213378
Lybalvi (Olanzapine/Samidorphan, ALKS 3831)

Lipid and glycemic laboratory values were normal at baseline and changed small amounts over
the 12-week randomized period, generally not in a dose-related fashion. Potentially adverse
findings were seen with increases in fasting glucose (16% of patients on olanzapine vs.
approximately 32% of patients on OLZ/SAM 10 and OLZ/SAM 20 reported increases in fasting
glucose of at least 10 mg/dL at any post-baseline visit).
