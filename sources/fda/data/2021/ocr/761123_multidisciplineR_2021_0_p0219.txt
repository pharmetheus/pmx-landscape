1.1. Introduction

AstraZeneca submitted a 351(a) Biologics License Application (BLA) on July 22, 2020,
for Saphnelo™ (anifrolumab-fnia, MEDI-546) a proposed type | interferon receptor
subunit 1 (IFNAR1) antagonist for the treatment of adult patients with moderate to
severe systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE), ©® This is a review
of the nonclinical sections of the Applicant’s proposed labeling received on July 31,
2020 (Supporting Document Number [SDN] 1). This review evaluated the Applicant’s
proposed Indications and Usage section under Highlights of Prescribing Information and
the following sections under Full Prescribing Information: Section 8.1 (Pregnancy),
Section 8.2 (Lactation), Section 11 (Description; first paragraph only), Section 12.1
(Mechanism of Action), and Section 13 (Nonclinical Toxicology). An integrated review
and evaluation of the nonclinical pharmacology and toxicology studies to support the
safety of anifrolumab-fnia for approval was completed on March 31, 2021 and is
included in the BLA multidisciplinary review and evaluation document (i.e., the
unireview).
