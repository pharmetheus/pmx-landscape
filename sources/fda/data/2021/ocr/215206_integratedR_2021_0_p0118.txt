An End of Phase 2 chemistry, manufacturing, and controls meeting was requested on February

An Initial Agreement letter was issued on August 15, 2019, for the Sponsor’s proposed pediatric
study plan |

On October 23, 2019, the Sponsor requested a meeting to discuss considerations for their
planned new drug application (NDA). A Type C meeting was granted on November 13, 2019.
Discussion at this meeting focused on questions regarding the appropriateness of the pivotal
studies, and the AIM-D, and the Migraine-Specific Quality of Life Questionnaire Version 2.1.
FDA Written Responses were issued to the Sponsor on January 6, 2020.

The Sponsor requested a chemistry, manufacturing, and controls-focused meeting on December
19, 2019, to obtain FDA agreement regarding its proposed control and manufacture strategy of
the atogepant drug substance and adequacy the drug product’s dissolution method in the NDA. A
Type C Guidance meeting was granted on January 10, 2020, and written responses issued on
February 25, 2020.

A Type B preNDA meeting to discuss the Sponsor’s incoming NDA was held on September 22,
2020. During this meeting, the Sponsor was advised to include exposure of atogepant of at least
300 patients at 6 months and 100 patients at 1 year, with a substantial number of patients
exposed at the highest planned marketed dose at 1 year. The Division determined that the
receptor binding studies, nonclinical data, and abuse-related adverse event profile assessment did
not suggest an abuse potential signal associated with atogepant. The Division stated that a human
abuse potential study and clinical physical dependence study were not required for the proposed
NDA. Allergan agreed to include tables for each Phase 2 and Phase 3 study which specify the
number of unique patients for each dose level with duration of exposure, and to include safety
analyses to evaluate whether there was hepatotoxicity associated with atogepant.

Allergan and its subsidiaries were acquired by AbbVie Inc. in May 2020. AbbVie sent a
Notification of Intent to submit an NDA with a Material Threat Medical Countermeasure Priority
Review Voucher on October 30, 2020.
