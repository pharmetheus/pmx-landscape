BLA 761123 Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Saphnelo (anifrolumab-fnia) for adults with SLE

Dimension Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and Reasons

BICLA and SRI-4 at week 52 were pre-specified secondary endpoints in
study 1013 and 05.

¢ In study 04, a higher proportion of anifrolumab IV subjects (47.8%)
achieved the 52-week BICLA primary endpoint as compared to the

placebo group (31.3%)[risk difference (95% Cl): 16.3 (6.3, 26.3);

p=0.001].

e In study 1013, a higher proportion of anifrolumab IV subjects (53.5%)
achieved the 52-week BICLA secondary endpoint as compared to the
placebo group (25.7%)[risk difference (95% Cl): 28.4 (15.3, 41.5)].

¢ In study 05 a higher proportion of anifrolumab IV subjects achieved

the 52-week BICLA endpoint (37.2%) as compared to the placebo

group (26.6%) [risk difference (95% Cl): 10.1 (0.6, 19.7)].

© In a post-hoc analysis of the study 05 BICLA endpoint using the
amended restricted medication rules intended to align with study 04,
consistent effects were observed with a higher proportion of
anifrolumab IV subjects (47.2%) achieving the 52-week BICLA
endpoint as compared to the placebo group (29.9%)[risk difference
(95% Cl): 17.0 (7.2, 26.8)].

e For its primary 52-week SRI-4 endpoint, study 05 did not demonstrate

statistically significant superiority over placebo.

¢ Additional supportive evidence for efficacy is provided by the SRI-4
composite 52-week endpoint achieved in study 04 by a higher
proportion of anifrolumab IV subjects (55.6%) as compared to the
placebo group (37.4%)[risk difference (95% Cl):18.2 (8.1, 28.3)] and in
study 1013 by a higher proportion of anifrolumab IV subjects (62.6%)
as compared to the placebo group (40.2%)[risk difference (95%
C1):24.0 (10.9, 37.2)].
