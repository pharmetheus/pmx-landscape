Covariate Effects: The effects of covariates on steady-state exposure (in Cycle 6) is illustrated in Figure
18. ADC exposure was most influenced by weight, with higher exposure in heavier patients. MMAE
exposure depended on 9 covariates. Tumor size had the strongest effect, with higher exposure in
patients with higher tumor size. Several other covariates that are generally correlated with overall
patients’ condition (albumin, ECOG, decrease of eGFR and hepatic impairment) also increased MMAE
exposure. The combined effect of these covariates may more than double the exposure in patients with
poor overall health status compared to exposure of the reference (healthier) subject. This is consistent
with the high value of the upper bound of 90% prediction interval of MMAE exposure in the study
population.
