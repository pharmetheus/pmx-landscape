The Applicant's Position:
HRQOL was in general maintained or showed small but not clinically meaningful decreases on

most domains between baseline and Cycle 4. The larger decreases in some domains/items at
time of treatment discontinuation are expected as most subjects at the timepoint experienced
progressive disease or discontinuation of trial drug due to ongoing AEs.

Given the single-arm trial design, limited data collected, lack of published data on HRQOL in
cervical cancer, the clinical meaningfulness of these declines is hard to interpret.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant that it is difficult to interpret PROs, given the lack of a control
arm and the small sample size.
