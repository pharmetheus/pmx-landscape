Vital Signs

Data:

Vital sign measurements, including heart rate, diastolic and systolic blood pressure, and
temperature, are summarized or listed individually in each CSR for the trials included in the
safety analysis groups. Any clinically significant changes were captured as AEs.

The Applicant’s Position:
Tisotumab vedotin did not appear to have clinically meaningful adverse effects on vital signs
during any clinical trial.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA did not conduct an independent analysis of vital sign data and believes that any clinically
significant changes would be captured as TEAEs.

Electrocardiograms (ECGs)

Data:

High-quality triplicate ECG measures were collected in studies GCT1015-04 and SGNTV-001 at a
dose of 2.0 mg/kg 1Q3W. In GCT1015-04, no meaningful changes in ECG interpretation (normal
vs abnormal) were observed over time. In the SGNTV-001 trial, none of the subjects had any
ECG change from baseline of more than 60 ms.

The Applicant's Position:
No clinically significant changes in ECG readings were detected after treatment with tisotumab

vedotin.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Tisotumab vedotin treatment at 2 mg/kg Q3W does not cause large mean increases (>20 msec)
in the QTc interval. Please refer to the FDA QT IRT by Nan Zheng and Christine Garnett dated
4/21/21 review for further information.

QT

Data:

The effect of tisotumab vedotin on the duration of cardiac ventricular repolarization was
evaluated in GCT1015-04 and SGNTV-001. This analysis was conducted on PK and ECG data
from 153 subjects with solid tumors. The relationship between ADC and MMAE concentrations
and change in QTcF intervals from baseline (AQTcF) was explored using linear mixed effects
modeling. The concentration-AQTcF analysis did not show a clinically relevant AQTcF risk at
Cmax of both ADC and MMAE following the administration of tisotumab vedotin at a dose of
2.0 mg/kg 103W, as the upper bounds of one-sided 95% Cl were below 10 msec for all analytes.
