8.5.3. Suicide-Related Events

Overall, | did not identify a signal for suicide-related events based on the incidence of
affirmative responses on the Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) in aducanumab-
assigned subjects compared to placebo-assigned subjects and based on a review of narratives
of subjects with SAEs related to suicide.

Baseline Affirmative Responses on the C-SSRS

In the placebo-controlled period of Study 302, aducanumab-assigned subjects had lower
incidence of affirmative responses on the C-SSRS related to suicidal ideation compared to
placebo-assigned subjects at baseline (3.5% versus 4.6%, respectively). Affirmative responses
on the C-SSRS related to suicidal behavior and self-injurious behavior were reported at similar
incidence in aducanumab-assigned subjects compared to placebo (0.6% versus 0.7% and 0.2%
versus 0.2%, respectively).
