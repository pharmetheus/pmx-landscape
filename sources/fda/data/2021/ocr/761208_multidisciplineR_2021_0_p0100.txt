The Applicant's Posit
The distribution of histology subtypes in GCT1015-04 was representative of the natural

distribution of subtypes in a second-line and later population of patients with recurrent or
metastatic cervical cancer (NCI, 2020) and included squamous cell carcinoma (68.3%),
adenocarcinoma (26.7%), and adenosquamous carcinoma (5.0%).

GCT1015-04 enrolled 14.9% of subjects in the US. All other subjects were enrolled in Europe. As
the treatment guidelines for recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer and ethnic diversity are
generally similar in these regions, the trial population is considered representative of the US
patient population.

The majority of subjects (69.3%) had previously been treated with bevacizumab in GCT1015-04.
This is higher than health claims data indicating that approximately half of all US patients with
recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer receive bevacizumab as part of a first-line treatment
regimen (Kantar Health 2020). The inclusion of an appropriate number of subjects previously
treated with bevacizumab, an expected histologic distribution, and the fact that biomarker (TF)
enrichment was not required demonstrates that the GCT1015-04 trial population is
representative of the US patient population.
