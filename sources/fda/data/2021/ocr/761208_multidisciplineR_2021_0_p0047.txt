Study title/number: HuMax-TF-ADC: 13 Week Repeat Dose Toxicity Study in
Cynomolgus Monkeys with a 6 Week Recovery Period/522531

¢ The major clinical signs were skin toxicity (dry, red, flaky skin and skin sloughing) at >3
mg/kg and ocular toxicity at 5 mg/kg HuMax-TF-ADC and 25 mg/kg HuMax-TF without
microscopic correlates.

e Adecrease in erythropoiesis (hemoglobin, red blood cells, and hematocrit), total
leukocytes, neutrophils, lymphocytes, eosinophils, and basophils at >4 mg/kg; animals
received antibiotics throughout the study due to neutropenia.

¢ Major target organs included the spleen and testis/epididymis at >1 mg/kg, bone marrow
and skin at 23 mg/kg, and the eye at 5 mg/kg. All findings were reversible except in the
testes/epididymides and spleen.
