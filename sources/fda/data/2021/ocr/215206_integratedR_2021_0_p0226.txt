Summary: This is a 56-year-old white woman who developed elevated

On acetaminophen (APAP) at baseline. ALP mildly elevated at baseline.
Other LFTs and CPK normal. She developed asymptomatic elevation in
transaminases 24 weeks after study drug start, which peaked a week later
(ALT 177, AST 83). Evaluaton showed a (+) CMV IgM. She had a flu-like
illness but this came on after injury onset. Estrogen and rosuvastatin was
started just prior to injury but these were held and washout did not occur.
Study drug continued and her liver enzymes did decline by 50% from
peak. However, they stayed elevated over baseline for 22-27 weeks while
on drug. They were gradaully declining but then returned to normal 9
weeks after stopping study drug.

Other viral serologies negative. ANA was (+) at 1:160. Malar rash and
chelosis mentioned. No immunosuppression treatments mentioned. No
liver biopsy. Imaging negative.

immunosuppression and after drug stop. Latency is long for DILI at 24
