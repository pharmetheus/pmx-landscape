5.5. Toxicology
5.5.1. General Toxicology

General toxicity studies with anifrolumab were conducted in cynomolgus monkeys following
single and repeated IV and SC administration with dosing durations up to 39 weeks.
Cynomolgus monkey was selected as the pharmacologically relevant species based on
comparable binding affinity and neutralization ability in cynomolgus monkey cells (COS-7) but
not murine cells (MLE-12). All of the pivotal GLP-compliant studies were reviewed previously
under IND 101849 (DARRTS Reference ID: 2892411 and 4763101). Key findings for the 39-week
repeat-dose study are summarized below. Since no additional findings were noted in shorter-
term toxicity studies, results from single dose and 4-week repeat-dose studies are not included
in the table below.
