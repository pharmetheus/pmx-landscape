In the Phase 2 dose-finding study (SCY-078-204), ibrexafungerp was administered in the fasted
state. While the two Phase 3 studies (SCY-078-303, SCY- 078-306) did not collect information
regarding food intake in relation to study drug administration, it is likely that most subjects
followed the recommendation of taking the study drug with food, as was stated in the informed

consent form and in the dosing reminder section of the subject’s diary.

The overall efficacy results from two Phase 3 studies (assumed fed) and the Phase 2 study

(fasted) based on frequency of clinical cure (primary endpoint) and mycological eradication are
consistent (Table 6-6). However, it is important to note the small sample size of SCY-078-204 in
comparison to the two Phase 3 studies.
