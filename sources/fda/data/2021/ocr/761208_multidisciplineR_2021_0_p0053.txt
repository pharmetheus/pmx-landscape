General toxicology; additional studies

The main findings in the 13-week GLP toxicology study were reflective of toxicities seen with
early non-GLP toxicology assessments with tisotumab vedotin. A noteworthy observation in the
10-week dose range finding study (Study# _ 521988) involved one animal given the high dose
of 6 mg/kg tisotumab vedotin that experienced respiratory symptoms ten days after the first
dose described as gasping, wheezing, shallow, and labored breathing that led to early
euthanasia the same day. Necropsy revealed discolored spongy lungs with microscopic findings
of mild edematous congestion/hemorrhage. In Study# ©® 514850 bleeding time was assessed,
