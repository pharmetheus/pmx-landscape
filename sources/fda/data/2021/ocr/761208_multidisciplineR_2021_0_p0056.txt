BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {Biologics License Application (BLA) 761208}
{tisotumab vedotin}

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. Increased prothrombin time (10 to 20%)
and ocular observations of glassy appearance of the fundus were observed with unconjugated
tisotumab at a dose of 25 mg/kg (approximately 30-fold the exposure at the recommended
dose based on AUC). Toxicities observed in the bone marrow, lymphoid organs (thymus,
spleen), liver, gastrointestinal and male reproductive organs (tested/epididymides) are similarly
seen in other approved ADCs with MMAE as the toxic payload. Skin and ocular toxicities are
likely due to TF expression in the epithelial cells.

5.5.2 Genetic Toxicology

Data (presented by the FDA):
The genetic toxicology studies were initially reviewed by the FDA under the Applicant’s BLA
125388 (brentuximab vedotin) and summarized below.

In Vitro Reverse Mutation Assay in Bacterial Cells (Ames

Study title/number: Bacterial Reverse Mutation Assay: SGD-1010/AA66EH.503 Co

Key Study Findings:

e MMAE, the small molecule cytotoxic agent of tisotumab vedotin, was negative in the
bacterial reverse mutation assay under the conditions tested.

GLP compliance: Yes; except the stability of the drug and homogeneity, concentration, and

stability of the drug preparations were not determined.

Test system: MMAE was evaluated at 75, 200, 600, 1800 and 5000 ug per plate with Salmonella

typhimurium TA98, TA100, TA1535, TA1537, and Escherichia coli WP2uvrA tester strains in the

absence and presence of S9.

Study is valid: Yes

In Vitro Assays in Mammalian Cells

Study title/ number: L5178Y TK+/- Mouse Lymphoma Forward Mutation Assay with a

Confirmatory Assay/8204155

Key Study Findings:

e MMAE, the small molecule cytotoxic agent of tisotumab vedotin, was negative in the mouse
lymphoma forward mutation assay under the conditions tested.

GLP compliance: Yes; except, the stability and concentration of the dosing preparations were

not analyzed.

Test system: Mouse lymphoma L5178Y TK*“ cell line was treated with MMAE, <50 ng/mL in the

absence of S9 and <100 ng/mL in the presence of S9, for 4-hours.

Study is valid: Yes.
