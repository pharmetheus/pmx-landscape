The Applicant's Position:
A comprehensive review of safety data pertinent to tisotumab vedotin was conducted across

4 clinical studies including subjects with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer with disease
progression on or after doublet chemotherapy with bevacizumab as well as other tumor types.
Events of interest were well-characterized based on a thorough analysis of all available safety
data in subjects, including by prior radiotherapy, bevacizumab, and prior lines of systemic
regimen.

The safety data were consistent across the pooled analyses and demonstrate an acceptable
safety profile of tisotumab vedotin monotherapy for the treatment of patients with recurrent
and metastatic cervical cancer with disease progression on or after chemotherapy.

The FDA's Assessment:

FDA’s safety analysis is primarily based on results from GCT1015-04, a single arm trial of
tisotumab vedotin in 101 patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer with disease
progression on or after chemotherapy. One reason for this is that the GCT1015-04 protocol
contains the most up-to-date risk mitigation strategies and dose modification strategies for
tisotumab vedotin. To assess adverse events of special interest (AESIs), FDA additionally
reviewed data from the Pool 2 population which included a total of 158 patients with cervical
cancer treated at the recommended dose of tisotumab vedotin across trials. FDA chose this
