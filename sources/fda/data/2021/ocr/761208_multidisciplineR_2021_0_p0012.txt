1 Executive Summary

11 Product Introduction

Tisotumab vedotin-tftv is a tissue factor (TF)-directed antibody drug conjugate (ADC). It
contains a human anti-TF lgG1 kappa antibody, a protease-cleavable valine-citrulline linker, and
a monomethyl auristatin E (MMAE) payload which is a microtubule-disrupting agent. Tisotumab
vedotin is not approved for any indication worldwide.
