@ Study 301 did not demonstrate an effect of high-dose aducanumab on

the primary endpoint, raising doubts about the results observed in
Study 302. Overall, though, there is also some consistency between
the studies and the results of Study 301 are not a direct
contradiction of the positive results observed in Study 302. For
example, numerically favorable results of a similar magnitude were
observed for low-dose aducanumab in the two studies. Also,
patients in the high-dose treatment arm in Study 301 who had
greater levels of exposure had similar outcomes to patients in Study
302. Additional analyses were performed to understand the results
of the two studies and determine if this understanding either
supports or undermines the outcome of Study 302. The results of
these analyses suggest that the results of the primary endpoint in
Study 301 do not meaningfully detract from the persuasiveness of
Study 302.
