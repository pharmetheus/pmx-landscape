Efficacy Results — Secondary and other relevant endpoints

Study 1013

Testing of secondary endpoints was not pre-specified, hence the secondary endpoints
presented in this section are considered exploratory and any reported p-values less than 0.05
are considered nominally significant. Table 34 below display the results of the following
exploratory secondary endpoints: OCS reduction at week 52, Cutaneous Lupus Erythematosus
Disease Area and Severity Index (CLASI) response at week 12, joint reduction at week 52, and
BICLA in IFN test high patients.

Among patients whose baseline OCS use was > 10 mg/day, OCS reduction was measured by the
ability to reduce OCS use from 10 mg/day at baseline to <7.5mg/day by week 52. Note that in
this analysis, a responder is defined by the last dose <7.5mg/day and not having discontinued
investigational product. The difference in proportions comparing anifrolumab 1000mg and
300mg to placebo is 10.8 and 30.1, respectively. We see that anifrolumab 300mg is nominally
significant compared to placebo in the proportion of patients who achieve last known dose of
OCS < 7.5mg by week 52.

Among patients with a CLASI activity score > 10 at baseline, CLASI response was measured by
