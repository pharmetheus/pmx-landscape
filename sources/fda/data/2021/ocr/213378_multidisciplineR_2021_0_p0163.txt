There is an indication of small, unfavorable changes in fasting glucose with ALKS 3831 versus
olanzapine. There is also a small, unfavorable imbalance in HbA1c at 5.7% or more at any visit.
These findings are of unclear significance, particularly as there were few subjects in either
group with sustained increases in glucose. Furthermore, the numbers of subjects with any
postbaseline HbA1c 2 6.5% (one criterion that can be used for the diagnosis of diabetes

mellitus?) were very few.
