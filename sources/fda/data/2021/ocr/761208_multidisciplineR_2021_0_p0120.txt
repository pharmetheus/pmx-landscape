Tissue Factor Expression and Objective Response:

Biomarker-based subject selection was not required in GCT1015-04. Tumor TF expression was
retrospectively assessed using a validated immunohistochemistry (IHC) assay in tumor biopsies
from subjects with second line or greater recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer. TF expression
was evaluable in 80/100 (80%) of tumor biopsies collected, and the majority of these were
archival and collected prior to last systemic therapy (82.5% [66/80]).

Membrane TF expression (2 1% of tumor cells with TF staining) was observed in 96% of subjects
with an evaluable biopsy. Responses to tisotumab vedotin were observed over a wide range of
membrane TF expression levels in GCT1015-04, and comparable distribution of TF expression
was observed in the IRC-assessed confirmed best overall response categories
(m5.3.5.2/GCT1015-04/sec11.11.1). These data suggest that tisotumab vedotin (2.0 mg/kg
1Q3W) is a potential treatment option for patients with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer
with disease progression on or after chemotherapy, regardless of TF expression levels via IHC.

The Applicant's Position:
Results of subgroup analyses were consistent across trials, with no meaningful differences in

confirmed ORR observed for any of the subgroups assessed. The population for efficacy
analyses was an all-comers population; that is, TF expression was not required for enrollment.
In GCT1015-04, TF was expressed in the majority of subjects with cervical cancer tumors
(96.3%) and responses were observed regardless of TF expression levels.
