Renal Impairment

Renal elimination is a minor excretion pathway for atogepant (5% excreted in urine as
unchanged) and the Applicant did not conduct a dedicated renal impairment (RI) study. The
population PK analysis using pooled PK data from clinical studies CGP MD-01 and 3101-301-
002 did not reveal a significant difference in the PK of atogepant in patients with mild (creatinine
clearance [CLcr] 60 to 89 mL/min, N=161) or moderate (CLer 30 to 59 mL/min, N=13) RI,
telative to those with normal renal function (CLer >90 mL/min, N=1,182). Therefore, the
Applicant proposed that no dose adjustment is needed in subjects with mild or moderate RI.
Based on a lack of a clinically relevant impact of mild and moderate RI on atogepant exposures,
the review team agrees with the Applicant’s labeling proposal to not adjust doses in subjects with
mild or moderate RI.
