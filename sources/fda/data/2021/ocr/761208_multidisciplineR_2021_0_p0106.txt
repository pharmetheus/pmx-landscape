The Applicant's Positio'
Steps taken to ensure the accuracy and reliability of data included selection of qualified trial

sites and investigators, GCP training of trial personnel, and protocol training before initiation of
the trial. In addition, site monitoring visits to review source documents against the eCRF were
performed by the contract research organization (CRO), with sponsor oversight of CRO’s duties
as per standard operating procedure. Investigators were updated on an ongoing basis on trial
progress, important trial milestones, and specific protocol specified requirements via
newsletters and flash letters. Data collected by external vendors (eg, laboratory results, ECG
results etc. excluding central imaging data) were transferred to the sponsor on an ongoing basis
and reviewed, together with other data, for accuracy and completeness. Any discrepancies
were resolved with the trial site. In addition, prior to locking the database a thorough review
was performed to enhance the quality and ensure completeness.

The sponsor was blinded to the IRC data with the exception of a limited number of personnel,
according to the protocol-specific blinding plan, who were unblinded when preparing for
database lock in order to ensure data quality and completeness of the IRC dataset.

Audits were conducted undertaking a risk-based approach. Five sites (IT03, ES01, US11, US13,
and CZO1) were audited. In addition, vendors and central laboratories Cy
were audited.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA OSI conducted clinical inspections and did not identify issues with data quality or integrity.
