The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. FDA recommends that tisotumab vedotin be avoided
in patients with moderate and severe HI. This is because unconjugated MMAE exposures are
expected to increase in moderate and severe hepatic impairment and the proposed dosage of
tisotumab vedotin was identified as the MTD. Evaluation of lower doses in patients with
moderate and severe HI was not recommended, as lowering the dose of tisotumab vedotin to
match the exposure of MMAE may lead to lower ADC concentrations which could lead to lower
efficacy (based on known exposure-response relationships of the ADC with efficacy).
