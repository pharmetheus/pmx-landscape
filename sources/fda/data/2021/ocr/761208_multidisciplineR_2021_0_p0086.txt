Coadministration of CYP3A4 Inducers with Tisotumab Vedotin

Concomitant administration of rifampin with brentuximab vedotin led to a 46% decrease in free
MMAE AUC exposure (Han 2013). Brentuximab vedotin PK were unchanged by concomitant use
of rifampin. Concomitant use of strong inducers of CYP3A4 such as rifampin with tisotumab
vedotin would likely result in similar effects of free MMAE and tisotumab vedotin.

The Applicant's Position:
No formal DDI studies have been conducted with tisotumab vedotin. Tisotumab vedotin is an

antibody-based therapeutic and DDI involving antibodies are not anticipated. MMAE, the
microtubule disrupting agent of tisotumab vedotin, is not expected to produce DDIs via CYP
inhibition or induction, and concomitant use of strong inducers or inhibitors of CYP3A4 is not
predicted to result in clinically meaningful changes in MMAE exposure.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees that no tisotumab vedotin dose modifications are recommended in patients
who are receiving concomitant strong CYP3A inhibitors or inducers. Tisotumab vedotin contains
the same linker-payload combination, at the same average drug-to-antibody ratio of 4, as
brentuximb vedotin. The geometric mean (CV%) of single dose systemic exposures of
unconjugated MMAE at the proposed dosage of tisotumab vedotin [Cmax = 4.73 (51%) ng/mL
and AUCo inf = 39.5 (53%) day.ng/mL, model-predicted in Study 04] are similar to those observed
at the approved 1.8 mg/kg Q3W dosage of brentuximab vedotin [Cmax = 4.97(43%) ng/mL and
AUCo.inf= 37 (47%) day.ng/mL, per the Adcetris clinical pharmacology review]. Therefore, similar
effects of strong CYP3A inhibitors (34% increase in free MMAE AUC) and inducers on
unconjugated MMAE PK might be expected with tisotumab vedotin as those observed with
brentuximab vedotin. Due to the expected increase in unconjugated MMAE exposure with
concomitant use of strong CYP3A inhibitors (which is expected to be similar in magnitude to
that in patients with mild HI), patients receiving concomitant strong CYP3A inhibitors should be
closely monitored for signs of toxicity.
