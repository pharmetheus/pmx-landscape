Gcr1015-04. °°

This patient is a 68 yo woman with metastatic SCC of the cervix involving the cervix, pelvic
lymph nodes, extra-pelvic lymph nodes, bone, right ventricle, and liver. She received two cycles
of tisotumab vedotin. On study day 41, she was diagnosed with grade 2 pneumonitis based on
symptoms of dyspnea and fatigue, and a high-resolution CT scan showing bilateral diffuse
ground glass infiltrates consistent with pneumonitis. She received treatment with prednisone,
and on study day 78, pneumonitis was reported as resolved.

8.2.5 Clinical Outcome Assessment (COA) Analyses Informing
Safety/Tolerability

Data:
See Section 8.1.2 for the secondary endpoints relating to COA.

The Applicant's Position:
HRQOL was in general maintained or showed small but not clinically meaningful decreases on

most domains between baseline and Cycle 4. The larger decreases in some domains/items at
time of treatment discontinuation are expected as most subjects at the timepoint experienced
progressive disease or discontinuation of trial drug due to ongoing AEs. Given the single-arm
trial design, limited data collected, lack of published data on HRQOL in cervical cancer, the
clinical meaningfulness of these declines is hard to interpret.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA did not consider HRQOL PRO data in our assessment of safety or tolerability. FDA agrees
with the Applicant that interpretation of HRQOL is limited due to the lack of a control arm.
Refer to Section 8.1.2 for more details on HRQOL data.
