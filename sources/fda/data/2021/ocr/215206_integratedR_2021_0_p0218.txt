2.2AGPT is absorbed quickly (median Tmax =1-2 hrs). Volume of distribution is
estimated at 73 to 86.1 L. It is metabolized via CYP3A4 primarily. It is
predominantly (81%) hepatically eliminated via metabolites in bile and feces.
Renal clearance is minor (<10%). AGPT is a substrate for P-gp, BCRP,
OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OAT1.

2.3 Hepatic impairment studies: Cmax is +9, -12% and -4% for mild, moderate
and severe impairment compared to normal function (Table 1, Figure 3).
However, AUCs were higher in “subjects with hepatic impairment” (14-38%
increase). While AUCs were similar between mild and moderate impairment,
the highest AUCs at 0-4 hours and overall were for severe impairment (38%
increase compared to normal) (Table 1).
