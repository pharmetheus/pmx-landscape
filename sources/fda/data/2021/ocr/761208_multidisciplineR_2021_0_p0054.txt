Study/ID: HuMab-TF (IgG1-1015-011): Pilot Repeat Dose Toxicology Study in Cynomolgus
Monkeys © 514850 (non-GLP)

Dosing: In a non-GLP, intra-animal dose escalation study, cynomolgus monkeys (n=2/sex) were
given an intravenous infusion over a 30-minute time period (10 mL/kg) of phosphate buffered
saline on Day 1 and unconjugated HuMax-TF at 1, 10, and 100 mg/kg on Days 8, 15, and 22,
respectively. The animals were observed for an additional 5 days after the last dosing and
sacrificed on Day 27. Bleeding time was assessed in the study at 1, 24, and 120 hours post dose.
A Simplate Bleeding Time Device was used to make an incision on the muscular area of the
forearm, and the flow of blood was evaluated using a filter paper every 30 seconds until blood
did not stain the filter paper twice. A TEG analyzer was used to assess the mechanical
properties of the developing clot (clot formation, kinetics, strength, stability and resolution).
Results: Bleeding time increased in a dose dependent manner by 13%, 65% and 93% at 1, 10,
and 100 mg/kg, respectively, 1 hour after the infusion compared to the saline dosed Day 1
measurement. Statistical significance (p=0.012) was reached at 100 mg/kg. The mean serum
concentration at 1, 10, and 100 mg/kg 1-hour post infusion was 23.5, 213, and 2255 pg/mL,
respectively. Microscopic findings of up to moderate focal myocardial degeneration (females)
with some inflammation and minimal hemorrhage in the heart (one female), perivascular
cuffing of the brain (males), dark appearance of the urinary bladder with microscopic findings
of hemorrhage (one female) and up to moderate pylorus gastritis in the stomach (one male,
females) was noted at the end of the study. The Applicant did not attribute the findings as drug-
related but rather as spontaneous background findings in the cynomolgus monkey; however,
the study did not include a vehicle dosed control group for comparison.
