The Applicant's Positio'
Fertility and Early Embryonic development

Stand-alone fertility and early embryonic studies were not performed with tisotumab vedotin.
However, testicular toxicity was observed in the 13-week repeat dose study in monkeys which
manifested as reduced or absent sperm motility at exposures approximately 0.6- to 4-fold times
the human systemic ADC exposure (AUC) at the clinically recommended dose. There was partial
recovery of the testes and epididymis findings at 3 and 5 mg/kg; and full recovery at 1 mg/kg
after a 6-week postdose treatment-free period. The testicular toxicity is consistent with the
pharmacologic disruption of microtubules caused by MMAE. No adverse effects were evident in
the female reproductive system and menstrual cyclicity was not affected by treatment.

Embryofetal Development

Intravenous administration of MMAE to pregnant rats during organogenesis (gestational Days 6
and 13) resulted in malformations and embryo fetal toxicity at a dose of 0.2 mg/kg
(approximately 0.5 times the human AUC at the clinically recommended dose). MMAE was
transferred across the placenta and present in fetal serum. The embryo-fetal developmental
toxicity is consistent with the pharmacologic disruption of microtubules caused by MMAE.

Pre- and Postnatal Development
In accordance with ICH S9, pre- and postnatal development studies were not conducted for
tisotumab vedotin as it is intended for treatment of metastatic cervical cancer.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position.
