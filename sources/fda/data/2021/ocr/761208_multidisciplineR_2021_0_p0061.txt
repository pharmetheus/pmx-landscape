BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {Biologics License Application (BLA) 761208}
{tisotumab vedotin}

Cynomolgus monkeys were considered the relevant nonclinical test species for tisotumab
vedotin based on comparable antigen binding affinities to human TF. Tisotumab vedotin did not
bind to pig, dog, rabbit or rodent TF.

Tissue Cross-Reactivity:

Positive immunohistochemical staining was observed both on the membrane and in the cytosol
of normal human and cynomolgus monkey tissues and was generally consistent with reported
sites of TF expression. Staining patterns were generally similar between the tissue panels
examined, although overall, staining of fewer tissue elements was present in cynomolgus
monkey tissue compared to human.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position.
