Narratives:

Patient ID: GCT1015-04- ©:

This 41 yo woman had metastatic SCC of the cervix involving the cervix, liver, and rectum. On
study day -4, she had grade 1 rectal hemorrhage and grade 1 anemia. She received treatment
with tranexamic acid. On study day 8, she developed grade 3 rectal hemorrhage and grade 3
anemia. She received a blood transfusion. On study day 20, the patient had rectal bleeding and
a fever and was admitted to the hospital. She received another blood transfusion as well as
antibiotic treatment and tisotumab vedotin administration was delayed. On study day 28, she
received another blood transfusion and was discharged. On study day 30, she was readmitted
with rectal hemorrhage grade 3 and she continued treatment with tranexamic acid and blood
transfusions. On study day 36, rectal hemorrhage improved to grade 2 and she received cycle 2
of tisotumab vedotin. On study day 48, she was found to have radiographic progression of
disease and tisotumab vedotin was discontinued. Her aPTT was within normal limits and
platelets were 200 to 300 x1049/L throughout the trial. Her INR was increased (1.5-1.8) and PT
was slightly prolonged throughout the trial for unclear reasons.

Patient ID: GCT1015-04- ©

This 75 yo woman had metastatic adenosqamous carcinoma of the cervix involving the uterus,
extrapelvic lymph nodes, and lung. On day 1, after receiving tisotumab vedotin, she
experienced grade 2 vaginal hemorrhage and was hospitalized. On day 4, hemorrhage
improved to grade 1, and the patient underwent arterial transcatheter embolization. The
procedure was unsuccessful. On day 13, the patient underwent another unsuccessful arterial
transcatheter embolization. She received cycle 2 of tisotumab vedotin on study day 21. On
study day 38, she underwent an epicystostomy. On study day 39, she had worsening vaginal
hemorrhage to grade 3. She received red blood cell transfusion and tranexamic acid. On study
days 53 to 65, the patient received palliative radiation for vaginal bleeding. Tisotumab vedotin
had been discontinued due to clinical progression of disease. On study day 82, the patient died
due to underlying disease. Her aPTT, PT, and INR were within normal limits throughout the trial
and platelets were in the 200 to 400 x 1049/L range.
