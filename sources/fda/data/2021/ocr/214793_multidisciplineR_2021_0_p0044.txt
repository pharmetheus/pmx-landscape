NDA 214793 / Piflufolastat F 18 (PYLARIFY): Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation

The Applicant conducted post-hoc analyses to examine the effect of concomitant diuretic use
on the diagnostic performance of piflufolastat F18. There were 20/253 (7.9%) evaluable
patients in OSPREY Cohort A and 16/93 (17.2%) evaluable patients in OSPREY Cohort B with
concomitant diuretic use. The results for OSPREY Cohort A and B showed no clear observable
differences in diagnostic performance of piflufolastat F18 for pelvic lymph nodes between the
patients who received concomitant diuretics and patients who did not (Table 6). However,
these analyses should be interpreted with caution due to the post-hoc nature and small
number of patients in certain cohorts.

In conclusion, available data do not suggest that concomitant use of diuretics at the time of
dosing significantly impacted the diagnostic performance of piflufolastat F18. The Applicant’s

clinical trials were not conducted with a required diuretic, therefore we have no
recommendation for the use of diuretics.
