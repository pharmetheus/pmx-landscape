Risk

7.7.1. Infections

Issue

TEAEs related to infection were among the most frequently reported adverse events in Study
1704.

Background

The topic of infections was of special interest as the Applicant’s proposed label notes that
infections were among the most frequently reported AEs in clinical trials. Additionally,
efgartigimod results in a reduction of circulating IgG and reduced IgG levels have been
associated with increased risk of infection (Agarwal and Cunningham-Rundles 2007; Furst 2009;
Driessen et al. 2013).
