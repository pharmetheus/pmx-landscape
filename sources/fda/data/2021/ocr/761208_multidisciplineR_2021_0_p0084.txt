Tissue factor (TF) expression and treatment response

Membrane and cytoplasm-associated TF expression was retrospectively tested by IHC on pre-
treatment fresh or archival tumor biopsies from 87% (88/101) of patients enrolled in the
GCT1015-04 study. IHC results were scored as percent of positive tumor cells (0-100%), and as a
TF histology-score (H-score), which was calculated as described in Hong et al., 2020 (PMID:
31796521). Results from 80 samples were considered evaluable by the Applicant and were used
for an exploratory analyses of TF expression and treatment response with tisotumab vedotin.

Of the 80 evaluable biopsies, 66 (82.5%) were obtained prior to last systemic therapy and 14
(17.5%) after their last systemic therapy. Approximately 96% (77/80) had detectable membrane
TF expression. Median membrane H-score was 120 (range: 0-265) and median percentage of
tumor cells positive for membrane TF expression was 60% (range: 0-100%). In the subgroup of
patients who were evaluable for tumor response (N=76), confirmed objective responses (CR=6
and PR=14 by IRC) were observed over a wide range of membrane H-scores (Figure 6), including
a partial response in a patient whose biopsy had a membrane H-score of 0. Similar results were
observed when TF expression was analyzed as percent of tumor cells positive on the membrane
or was based on cytoplasm staining. Refer to Section 8.1.4 for additional details and discussion.
