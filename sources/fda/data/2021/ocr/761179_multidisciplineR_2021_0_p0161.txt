TEAEs by Age

The majority of patients enrolled on JZP458-201 were under the age of 18, and that is true of
the patients enrolled in Cohort 1A (25 mg/m7). Patients from 1 month to < 17 years of age were
compared to adult patients. Pediatric patients were more likely to have hematologic toxicities

and febrile neutropenia.
