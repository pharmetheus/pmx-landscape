| Chemistry Clinical chemistry parameters were evaluated at pre-test, during dosing (pre-dose,
1- and 13-days post dose), and week 1, 3, and 6 of the recovery period.

Changes in albumin, globulin, haptoglobin, and iron were observed at 5 mg/kg.

Changes in other parameters were noted throughout the dosing period but were

transient, within pretrial values, and/or independent of dose.

© Decrease in albumin up to -20% and an increase in globulin up to 28% led to
overall decrease in the albumin/globulin ratio up to -38% from Day 14 until the
end of dosing period in both genders. Changes were most pronounced after the
first dose.

¢ Transient increase in haptoglobin up to 160% in both sexes throughout the
dosing period with the most pronounced change after the first dose.

* Decrease in iron (males only) up to -48%, however, not reaching statistical
significance at every timepoint examined.
