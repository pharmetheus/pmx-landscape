Liver Test Data for Safety Population.

The Report also contains liver test elevation data for this overall safety population

in several tables (Appendix 2). No significant changes were seen. Study 3101-
306-002 which is an OLE study outside this NDA’s ISS is ongoing in Japan and

has 113 patients thus far enrolled. The amount of transaminase elevation is

higher than the overall ISS (see Table 14.3-3.3, SDN 22, SN 22, 5.3.5.3). We
already reviewed this issue via an information request (IR) and sponsor response.
Please see our review of the sponsor’s IR response (July 7, 2021) in DARRTs.

CONCLUSIONS

None of the four new cases raise concerns for DILI beyond what we have
already seen for this NDA. Tabular data of liver tests elevations for the overall
safety population in this report did not have significant changes.

RECOMMENDED REGULATORY ACTION(S)

We do not recommend regulatory action for this 120-Day Safety Update Report.

We will continue to collaborate with DN2 during their NDA review. Our full written

consult assessing DILI risk in this NDA is pending our further review and

communication with DN2.
