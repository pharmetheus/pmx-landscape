In the open-label extension Studies A306 and A304, there were few novel severe AEs, none of
which indicated a new trend. In Study A306, single incidents of the following severe AEs were
reported: road traffic accident and fractures, overdose, and suicide attempt. In Study A304, one
severe AE of glycosuria and one of seizure were reported.

8.2.6.5 Treatment Emergent Adverse Events and Adverse Reactions

The most common AEs reported in pivotal Studies A305 and A303 were somnolence, weight
gain, and dry mouth; only dry mouth was consistently more frequent with ALKS 3831
treatment.
