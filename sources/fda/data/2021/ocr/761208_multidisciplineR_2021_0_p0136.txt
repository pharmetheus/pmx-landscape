Patient ID: GCT1015-04-_ © pneumonitis

This 70 yo woman with history of type 2 diabetes mellitus had metastatic SCC of the cervix
involving lungs, bone, rectum, and pelvis. She received two cycles of tisotumab vedotin. On
study day 27, she was admitted to the hospital with fever and shortness of breath and was
diagnosed with pneumonitis based on chest CT. CT also showed lung metastases and a L-sided
pleural effusion, size unspecified. She received treatments with antibiotics, prednisone, and
furosemide. The narrative states that the patient was believed to be at the end-of-life and was
discharged from the hospital. It is not clear if she was discharged to hospice, but on day 42 she
received palliative sedation with morphine and on day 45 she died. FDA disagrees with the
investigator and Sponsor and thinks that pneumonitis and death are possibly related to
tisotumab vedotin.

Patient ID: GCT1015-04- °° Multiorgan failure and ileus

This 59 yo woman had metastatic SCC of the cervix involving the cervix, kidney, and extra-pelvic
lymph nodes. She received thirteen cycles of tisotumab vedotin. On study day 271, she
experienced nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, and diarrhea, and was diagnosed with ileus. She
was treated with a partial intestinal resection and during surgery, she developed multiorgan
failure and circulatory instability, and died. The investigator attributed her death to
deterioration of underlying cancer disease, ileus, multiorgan failure, and circulatory instability.
FDA agrees with this description and attributes this patient’s death to multiorgan failure.
