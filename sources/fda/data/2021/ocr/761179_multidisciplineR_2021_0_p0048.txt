Dosing in patient subgroups Pediatric Patients >1 month of age: BSA is a statistically significant
(intrinsic and extrinsic factors) covariate for JZP-458 apparent clearance (CL/F) and apparent volume
of distribution (V/F), supporting the BSA-based dosing regimen. The
recommended dosage regimen is predicted to meet the goal NSAA in
>90% of patients, regardless of age.

Race and Ethnicity: Black (n=10) and Asian (n=5) patients had a 29%
lower CL/F compared to White patients (n=61), but there were no
clinically significant differences in NSAA or safety between race
groups. There were no differences in CL/F between Hispanic (n=28)
and Non-Hispanic (n=53) patients. No dosage modification is
recommended based on race or ethnicity.

Renal and Hepatic Impairment: No dedicated studies were conducted
for patients with renal or hepatic impairment.

Immunogenicity Treatment-emergent ADA against JZP-458 were detected in 40/99
(40.49%) evaluable patients in Study JZ2P458-201; 4 patients (4%) had
neutralizing antibodies against JZP-458. However, the immunogenicity
assays used in the current BLA submission did not have adequate drug
tolerance for the drug concentration present at the time of ADA
sampling and therefore the observed results underestimate the true
incidence of ADA against JZP-458.

APMC will be issued to assess binding and neutralizing ADA responses
with a validated assay capable of sensitively detecting ADA responses
in the presence of JZP-458 levels that are present in the serum at the
time of patient sampling.
