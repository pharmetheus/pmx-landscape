reactions occurred in 55% of patients; the most frequent serious adverse reactions (in > 5%

hypersensitivity, infection, nausea, and viral infection. Permanent discontinuation due to an
adverse reaction occurred in 9%; adverse reactions resulting in permanent discontinuation
