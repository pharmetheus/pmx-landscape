SUMMARY AND CONCLUSIONS

8.3 Statistical Issues

The FDA’s Assessment:

There were no major statistical issues with the study design, statistical analysis plan, or efficacy
results of Study GCT1015-04. The study showed a confirmed IRC-assessed ORR of 23.8% (95%
Cl: 15.9%, 33.3%) in 101 subjects with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer, and the median
DOR among the 24 confirmed responders was 8.3 months (95% Cl: 4.2 month, NR).

No inferential procedures were used to evaluate results from this single arm study. Instead, the
efficacy evaluation was based on the magnitude of response rate and adequate duration of
response. Additionally, although PFS and OS results were summarized, time-to-event endpoints
are uninterpretable without a comparator arm.

8.4 Conclusions and Recommendations

The FDA’s Assessment:

In GCT1015-04, treatment with tisotumab vedotin resulted in an ORR of 23.8% (95% Cl: 15.9,
33.3) and a median DOR of 8.3 months (95% Cl: 4.2, NR) in patients with recurrent or
metastatic cervical cancer who had received <2 lines of systemic treatment in the recurrent or
metastatic setting, including doublet chemotherapy, with bevacizumab if eligible. Available
therapies in this treatment setting include single agent chemotherapies with ORRs ranging from
5%-21% and median DORs ranging from 2-4 months, but these data collected prior to
establishment of a chemotherapy doublet with bevacizumab as standard-of-care first-line
treatment for patients in the U.S. Given that all patients who received tisotumab vedotin on
GCT1015-04 received first-line doublet chemotherapy and 69% of patients had prior
bevacizumab as per U.S. standard of care, the review team considers that the efficacy of
tisotumab vedotin demonstrates an improvement compared to available therapies and is
reasonably likely to predict clinical benefit in the indicated population.

Tisotumab vedotin demonstrated acceptable safety and tolerability in the indicated patient
population with a serious and life-threatening condition and limited therapeutic options. The
safety of tisotumab vedotin is manageable through labeling, which includes a boxed warning for
ocular toxicity, premedication, dose modification, and supportive care.
