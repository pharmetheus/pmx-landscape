Observations and Results

Parameters Major findings

Mortality All animals survived to their scheduled necropsy except one dam (B57447) who
delivered on GD 21 and was sacrificed.

Clinical Signs paleness (both ears [5/25] and entire body [1/25]), red/black vaginal discharge
(2/25), and red fluid in cage pan (1/25)

Body Weights Decrease in body weight compared to control was observed starting GD 8 (-3%)

and up to -10% on GD 18; mean maternal body weight gain based on the corrected
weight (terminal body weight — gravid uterine weight) decreased by -14%
compared to control during GD 4 to 21. Food consumption decreased after the first
and second dose by -25% and -17%, respectively; the mean maternal food
consumption decreased by -12% compared to control during GD 6 to 21.

Mean male fetal weight was -5% lower than those of control; mean female fetal
weight was unremarkable.
