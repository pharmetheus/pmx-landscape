3.0Non-clinical data related to DILI:
3.1Animal studies: The sponsor did rat, mouse and monkey studies for 3 months

(100 mg/kg/d), 6 months (200 mg/kg/d) and 9 months (300 mg/kg/d),
respectively. No liver injury was found. Three-month and 9-month necropsy
studies in Rhesus monkeys did not show any liver abnormalities.

3.2In vitro testing: The sponsor did bile acid transporter inhibition, oxidative
stress and mitochondrial dysfunction studies for AGPT, telcagepant and MK-
3207. AGPT is an inhibitor of OCT1 and MATE1 but not BCRP. There is no
mention of BSEP inhibition. AGPT is oxidized predominantly by CY3A4 and
inhibits it as well. There is no mention of mitochondrial toxicity.

3.3 Quantitative systems toxicology (DILISym®) studies suggest a “reduced risk
of hepatotoxicity” for AGPT compared to telcagepant based on in vitro studies
outlined. (Seq 001, 4.2.3.7.3). No ALT elevations were predicted at the 60 mg BID
dosing. Mild elevations were predicted at 120 mg BID and “more significant”
elevations at 180 mg BID.
