The Applicant's Position:
Tisotumab vedotin resulted in clinically meaningful confirmed ORR per IRC of 23.8% (95% Cl

15.9% - 33.3%) in subjects with previously treated recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer. The
confirmed ORR as assessed by IRC was consistent across GCT1015-04 and GEN701/GCT1015-03
(SCE Table 14.02.01.01).

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA primarily based the efficacy assessment for tisotumab vedotin on results from GCT1015-
04. FDA did not replicate the Applicant’s analyses from GEN701/GCT-1015-03 given the small
sample size.

Secondary and Other Endpoints

Data:

DOR and TTR by IRC:

Confirmed responses to tisotumab vedotin were durable, with a median DOR of 8.3 months
(95% Cl 4.2 months — NR) in GCT1015-04 and 5.1 months (95% Cl 3.0 months — 9.7 months) in
GEN701/GCT1015-03 (Table 17 and Figure 9). Reasons for the observed difference in median
DOR across trials remain to be elucidated; however, it is noted that more comprehensive safety
management guidelines may have enabled subjects to stay on therapy longer in GCT1015-04,
and that GCT1015-04 also enrolled a population with generally better prognostic factors. An
estimated 62% of confirmed responders had a DOR of 6 months or longer in GCT1015-04.

Responses occurred early in treatment, with a median TTR of 1.4 months in GCT1015-04 and
2.8 months in GEN701/GCT1015-03 (Table 17 and Figure 10).
