Hepatic impairment:

No dose modification is recommended in patients with mild hepatic impairment. No formal
clinical trial has been conducted to assess the effect of hepatic function on the PK of tisotumab
vedotin. The effect of mild hepatic impairment on tisotumab vedotin and free MMAE exposure
was assessed using the population PK model. The population PK analysis included patients with
mild (n=58) hepatic impairment, in addition to patients with normal (n=341) hepatic function.
Hepatic impairment status was identified as a covariate for MMAE clearance in the Applicant’s
population PK model. Model-predicted Cycle 1 ADC exposure in patients with mild hepatic
impairment was similar to patients with normal hepatic function, while MMAE exposures were
37% higher in patients with mild hepatic impairment (Figure 4).
