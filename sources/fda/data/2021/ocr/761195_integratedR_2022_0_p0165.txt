5.2 Hypersensitivity Reactions
Hypersensitivity reactions, including rash,
angioedema, and dyspnea, were observed
in VYVGART-treated patients. In clinical
trials, hypersensitivity reactions were mild
or moderate, occurred within one hour to
three weeks of administration, and did not
lead to treatment discontinuation. Monitor
patients during administration and for 1
hour thereafter for clinical signs and
symptoms of hypersensitivity reactions
[see Dosage and Administration (2.3)]. If a
hypersensitivity reaction occurs during
administration, discontinue VYVGART
