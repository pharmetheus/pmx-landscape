In Vitro Functional Activity

Study# 1015-087: In TF expressing SK-OV-3 (ovarian, 50,000-175,000 TF molecules/cell)
and A431 (epidermoid carcinoma, >300,000 TF molecules/cell) cells an average of
approximately 50% of tisotumab co-localized with the lysosomal marker LAMP-1 after 16 hours
and was degraded over time. Tisotumab vedotin induced cytotoxicity in a panel of TF
expressing cancer cell lines; sensitivity to tisotumab vedotin was dependent on the level of cell
membrane TF expression. In three cervical cancer cell lines expressing different levels of TF, ICso
values ranged from approximately 1.63 ng/mL to 575 ng/mL; tisotumab vedotin produced 87%
cell kill at 10 ng/mL in the highest TF expressing and most sensitive cervical cancer (CaSki) cell
line.

Study# ©® 1015-084: Tisotumab induced antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC)
with ECso values ranging from 0.031 nM (4.7 ng/mL) to 0.103 nM (15.4 ng/mL) in three cancer
cell lines co-cultured with PBMCs from three to six healthy donors; tisotumab vedotin was able
to induce ADCC in a similar manner, suggesting that ADCC activity was preserved with
conjugation.

Study# TRN-5631: A concentration of 2 g/mL tisotumab and tisotumab vedotin mediated
antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis (ADCP) with 41% to 93% and 38% to 72% activity,
respectively, using BxPC3 or A431 target cells and one or two macrophage donors as effector
cells. Baseline activity up to 26% and 34% was detected in the no drug and isotype control
groups, respectively.

In Vivo Functional Activity

Study# ©® 1015-149: The cervical squamous cell carcinoma CEXF773 PDX showed 25-50% TF
positive high intensity (3+) staining in the tumor when evaluated via IHC. The tumors showed
regression (tumor volume <200 mm‘) or complete response (no measurable tumor noted as 0
mm?*) up to approximately day 42 when mice were given two intravenous doses (day 0, 7) of
tisotumab vedotin at 4 mg/kg or three intravenous doses (day 0, 7, 14) of paclitaxel at 20
mg/kg. Then, 3/8 tisotumab vedotin treated and 4/8 paclitaxel treated tumors began to show
re-growth and by day 60 had mean tumor volumes of 345 mm? and 392 mm’, respectively.
These animals were re-treated with 4 mg/kg HuMax-TF-ADC on Day 60 and 67. Re-treated
tumors showed regression to <65 mm? tumor volume between Day 63 and 84. One animal that
received HuMax-TF-ADC during the main study and was re-treated was euthanized on day 70
during the re-treatment phase due to 22% body weight loss; weight loss was not otherwise
observed in any of the groups.
