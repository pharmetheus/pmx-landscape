Clinical Reviewer’s Comment: Because JZP-458 is administered as part of multi-agent
chemotherapy, assessment of “related” versus “unrelated” is challenging. It is expected that
most patients will have hematologic toxicities, GI toxicities, and increased risk of infection

and other known toxicities of asparaginase class products.

Laboratory Findings
As JZP-458 is used as part of a multi-agent chemotherapy regimen for patients with ALL or LBL,

cytopenias occur in nearly 100% of patients. Other laboratory abnormalities are listed below.
There are very few grade 3-4 laboratory abnormalities in the 25 mg/m? JZP-458 dosing regimen.
