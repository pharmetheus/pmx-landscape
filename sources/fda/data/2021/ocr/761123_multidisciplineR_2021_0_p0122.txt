A summary of interim adverse events, including serious adverse event (SAE) data generated
from the ongoing placebo-controlled, double-blind phase 3 long-term extension study (09)
were also provided for the reporting period August 01, 2019 through March 26, 2020 in the
120-Day Safety Update submitted on November 02, 2020 and are included in the pertinent
areas of the following discussion.

Event categories for adverse events of special interest (AESIs) were selected based on the
mechanism of action of anifrolumab, non-clinical findings, factors related to biologic and
infusion-based therapies, and based on the prevalence of a particular adverse event in the SLE
population (as is the case for MACE). AESIs were protocol-specified and assessed by the
investigator in the electronic case report form based on case definitions. For the integrated
safety summary, AESIs were identified using the criteria detailed in the Integrated Safety
Analysis Plan. The AESls for the integrated safety analysis were:

Opportunistic infections

Herpes Zoster

TB (including latent TB)

Influenza
Anaphylaxis/Hypersensitivity/Infusion-related reaction

MACE

Vasculitis (non-SLE)
