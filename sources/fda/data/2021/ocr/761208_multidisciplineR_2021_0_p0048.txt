Observations and Results: Changes from control

Parameters Major findings
Mortality One female given 4 mg/kg (interim study) was euthanized on Day 11, one male and
two females given 5 mg/kg were euthanized on Day 10, 26, and 41, respectively. The
cause of death was due to adverse skin reactions (noted below for clinical signs).
Clinical Signs Observations for both 3 and 5 mg/kg groups were generally noted by day 6.
Animals dosed at 3 mg/kg generally recovered prior to the second dose, and the
observations did not recur with subsequent dosing. Observations in the animals
dosed at 5 mg/kg generally persisted or recurred with subsequent dosing. The
clinical signs fully recovered by the end of the recovery period.
