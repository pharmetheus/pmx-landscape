Key Study Findings

Reference ID: 4758438

In a pre- and postnatal development (PPND) study, mated FO female hamster
received CCX168 at doses of 0 (vehicle- PEG ©®) 40, 30, or
100 mg/kg once daily or 0 (vehicle- PEG ©®) and 500 mg/kg
twice daily (1000 mg/kg) from Gestation Day (GD) 6 to Lactation Day/Postnatal
Day (LD/PND) 20.

No treatment-related changes of body weight gain were noted in FO dams during
the gestation period (GD 6 to GD 14) or lactation period (LD1 to LD21).

FO dams showed no treatment related effects on reproductive parameters. There
were no treatment related effects on the gestation length, the number of
implantation sites, the number of live births, or on viability of F1 offspring.

There were no treatment related effects on body weight gains of F1 offspring
from birth to weaning (LD 1 to LD 21)

For F1 generation pups, growth, physical (motor evaluation) and neurological
development (passive avoidance) were unaffected with doses up to 1000 mg/kg
