Conclusions and Reasons

¢ Ocular toxicity is an important safety signal associated with tisotumab
vedotin, experienced by 55% of patients enrolled to GCT1015-04. In
GCT1015-04, 4% of patients developed corneal ulcers (Grade 3
keratitis) and 1% of patients developed a corneal ulcer with
perforation (Grade 4 keratitis) requiring corneal transplantation.

¢ There is a boxed warning to alert providers to the potential for severe
ocular toxicity in patients receiving tisotumab vedotin in the USPI.

e Additional important safety signals associated with tisotumab vedotin
include peripheral neuropathy, hemorrhage, pneumonitis, and
embryo-fetal toxicity which are listed under Warnings and
Precautions in the USPI.
