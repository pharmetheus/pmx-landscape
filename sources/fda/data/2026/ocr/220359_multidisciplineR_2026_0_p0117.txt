Study 4577SR (effect on cardiac pacemaker current)

Method: Effects of AZD9833 on cardiac pacemaker current Ir were studied using patch-clamp
electrophysiology. Acute in vitro effects were tested on sinoatrial node cells from untreated rats
using AZD9833 concentrations of 0.3-30 tM. For in vivo effects, male rats received oral gavage
of vehicle or 75 mg/kg/day AZD9833, and sinoatrial node cells were isolated 2 hours after dosing
on day 1 or day 7. Ir current properties were analyzed including voltage dependence of activation
(V1/2) and maximal conductance (gmax).

Results: Following single in vivo dose (day 1), AZD9833 induced a small positive shift in
voltage dependence of activation (+4.87 mV) without affecting maximal conductance. After 7
daily doses, both voltage dependence shifted positively (+5.8 mV) and maximal conductance
was reduced by 37.7%, indicating decreased channel expression. Plasma concentrations were
2.68 + 0.524 pmol/L on day 1 and 2.12 + 0.878 jtmol/L on day 7.

Impurity Qualification
The FDA did not agree with the Applicant’s assessment of impurit qualification
at the proposed drug product (DP) level of no more than (NMT) @%. The impurity was not

for a drug product related impurity that may distribute similarly to the active pharmaceutical

guideline. The Applicant agreed to tighten the acceptance criteria accordingly.
