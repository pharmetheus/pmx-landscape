Observations and Results

Mortality

There were no deaths considered drug-related. Two LD animals were found dead and 1 LD
animal was sacrificed moribund approximately 2.5 to 5.5 hours after dosing on GD 14 (B0118,
B0120, and BO117). No findings were noted at necropsy of the moribund sacrifice animal
(B0118), and the cause of death was undetermined. Histopathologic examination of the 2
animals found dead (B0120, B0117) determined the cause of death attributable to
inflammation/necrosis in the lungs. Mortality was considered unrelated to SRK-015 treatment
because all other animals survived to the terminal sacrifice (see Endotoxin Analysis below).
However, at terminal necropsy, 3 HD animals (Animals B0301, B0315, and B0308) were noted
with discolored lung lobes (red, dark red, and/or brown), the same macroscopic observations
noted in the 2 animals found dead following dosing on GD 14.

Clinical Signs
There were no drug-related clinical signs.

Body Weight

Increases in maternal BW gain were seen at all doses (10, 33, and 69% at LD, MD, and HD,
respectively; SS at MD and HD) during the dosing period (Table 110).
