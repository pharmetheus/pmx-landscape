6.3.2.2. Is the proposed dosing regimen appropriate for the general patient
population for which the indication is being sought?

Data and The Applicant’s Position:

Yes. Please see Section 6.2.2.1.
The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position that camizestrant 75 mg QD in combination
with a CDK4/6 inhibitor (abemaciclib, palbociclib, ribociclib) demonstrates a reasonable safety
and tolerability profile in adults with HR-positive, HER2-negative, locally advanced or
metastatic BC upon emergence of ESR1 mutation during 1L endocrine based therapy given the
following reasons:

1) InSERENA-1 Part A and B (monotherapy dose escalation and randomized dose
expansion), camizestrant exhibited nonlinear PK with greater than dose proportional
increase in camizestrant exposure with increasing dose over the dose range of 25 mg
to 450 mg QD with no determination of MTD.

2) In SERENA-1 Part C, D, G, H, K and L, camizestrant in combination with CDK4/6
inhibitor was escalated up to 300 mg QD (4 times the proposed recommended
dosage) with no determination of MTD.
