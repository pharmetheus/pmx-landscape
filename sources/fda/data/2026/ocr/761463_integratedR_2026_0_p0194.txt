Key Study Findings

Weekly IV (bolus) administration of SRK-015 (0 (vehicle), 10, 30, 100, or 300 mg/kg/week to
tats for 12 weeks (13 total doses) resulted in increased body weights, increased levels of
circulating latent myostatin, and increased skeletal muscle weights (biceps and gastrocnemius) at
all doses. At the terminal and recovery sacrifices, fissure at the femoral head physis was
observed by histopathology at all doses. In some animals, the fissure was associated with partial
or complete absence of the epiphysis, and degenerative (resorption) and reactive (fibrosis, woven
bone, hyperplastic osteoclasts) changes at the femoral neck and proximal metaphysis. Physeal
fissure correlated with missing femoral head observed on CT imaging. Qualitative findings of
missing femoral head epiphysis were also seen in SRK-015-treated recovery animals. These
