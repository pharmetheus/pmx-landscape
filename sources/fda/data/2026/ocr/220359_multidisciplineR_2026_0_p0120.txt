General dosing | The proposed recommended camizestrant dosage is 75 mg PO QD, taken

instructions with or without food, in combination with a CDK4/6 inhibitor
(abemaciclib, palbociclib, or ribociclib).

e No camizestrant dosage modification is recommended for patients
with mild, moderate, or severe HI when the indicated combination
CDK4/6 inhibitor is abemaciclib or palbociclib or for patients with
mild or moderate HI when the indicated combination CDK4/6
inhibitor is ribociclib given that safety and tolerability of
camizestrant 300 mg QD (5.7-fold higher exposure than 75 mg
QD) is similar to camizestrant 75 mg QD, indicating an adequate
safety margin.

e Dosage reduction of camizestrant to 75 mg QOD is recommended
in patients with severe HI when the indicated combination CDK4/6
inhibitor is ribociclib given that the predicted camizestrant AUC
exceeds the 5.7-fold safety margin.
