PK: In study PTG-300-20, following single SC administration of 20 mg and 45 mg LPF,
tusfertide concentrations were generally observed by 0.5 hours post-dose. The increase in Cmax
and AUCinf was dose-related but less than dose-proportional. The median Tmax was 24 hours
for the 20 mg dose and 6 hours for the 45 mg dose.

In Study PTG-300-22, following single SC administration of rusfertide LPF across the 10 to 60

observed. The median Tmax was 24 to 36 hours for doses up to 30 mg and 2 hours for the 60 mg
dose.
