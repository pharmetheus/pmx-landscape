Key Consideration and Design
Features

PMR

In vitro studies showed that camizestrant
inhibits CYP2B6. The PBPK analysis
intended to evaluate the effects of
camizestrant on the exposure of the
CYP2B6 substrate bupropion is considered
inadequate because of the uncertainties
about the fraction metabolized by CYP2B6
for bupropion. Based on the risk
assessment, the potential interaction of
camizestrant with substrates of CYP2B6
cannot be ruled out.

Conduct a clinical trial to assess the
effects of camizestrant on the PK of
substrates of CYP2B6.

PMR

In vitro studies showed that camizestrant
inhibits the transporters P-gp and BCRP.
Based on the risk assessment using the
PBPK approach, the potential interaction
of camizestrant with substrates of these
transporters cannot be ruled out.

Conduct a clinical trial to assess the
effects of camizestrant on the PK of
substrates of P-gp and BCRP.
