7.2.9. Additional Safety Explorations

Human Carcinogenicity or Tumor Development

The available safety data did not raise concern about carcinogenicity associated with
centanafadine ER capsules.

Human Reproduction and Pregnancy

Refer to the DPMH Maternal Health Review for full details regarding the information related to
human reproduction, pregnancy, and lactation. Refer to Section 12 for details of the necessary
pregnancy- and lactation-related PMRs.
