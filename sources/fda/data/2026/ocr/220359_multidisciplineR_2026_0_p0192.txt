The results of exploratory subgroup analyses of PFS are presented in Table 19. FDA agrees that
results of these exploratory subgroup analyses of PFS were generally consistent with the primary
PFS analysis results.

FDA notes that the Applicant’s proposed indication and justification for the design of SERENA-
6 hinge on the hypothesis that patients benefit the most if they switch to camizestrant as soon as
an ESR1m is detected. It is unclear whether the exploratory subgroup analyses support this
hypothesis.

Results in the subgroup of patients with ESR/m detected on the first test appear to show a more
favorable HR than the HRs of the subgroup of patients with ESR/m detected at subsequent test
and of the ITT population. However, it is noted that, since ESR/m testing was not performed at
baseline, we cannot rule out the possibility that patients who tested positive on the first test
already had detectable ESR/m for some time before enrolling to SERENA-6.

Further, results in the subgroup of patients with time from AI + CDK4/6i > 18 months appear to
show a more favorable hazard ratio than that of the subgroup with time from AI + CDK4/6i< 18
months and of the ITT population. Given that these patients should be further along their disease
course, the more favorable treatment effect appears contradictory to the Applicant’s hypothesis.
