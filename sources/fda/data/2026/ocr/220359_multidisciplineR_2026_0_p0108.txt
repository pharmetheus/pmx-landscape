Additional data (presented by the FDA):

Study title: AZD9833: Oral Dose Range Finding Embryofetal Development Study with Pre
and Post Natal Assessments in the Rat (GLP Study No.: 490005)

The FDA considers the Phase 2 portion of the study, when camizestrant was administered to
pregnant rats during organogenesis (gestation day [GD] 6 to 16) to be inconclusive rather than
demonstrating no adverse effects on embryo-fetal survival, growth, and development, as
concluded by the Applicant. This assessment is based on the following considerations:

1. Inadequate systemic exposure: The doses tested were substantially below clinically
relevant exposures, with the low dose falling below the limit of quantitation in
toxicokinetic assessments, and the mid and high doses achieving AUC values > 1000-fold
lower than the AUC observed at the clinical dose of 75 mg.
