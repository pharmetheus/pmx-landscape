19.4.4.4.2. Can PBPK analyses predict the effects of CYP3A perpetrators
on the PK of camizestrant?

Yes. The camizestrant PBPK model could be used to predict the effects of CYP3A inhibitors

or inducers, and the results are presented in Table 43. Moderate CYP3A inhibitors were
predicted to increase camizestrant exposure by approximately 1.6-fold, and the weak CYP3A
inhibitor cimetidine was predicted to have a minimal effect on camizestrant exposure. The strong
CYP3A inducers rifampin and carbamazepine were predicted to decrease camizestrant exposure
by approximately 70-80%. The moderate CYP3A inducers efavirenz and phenobarbital were
predicted to decrease camizestrant exposure by approximately 55%. Additional simulations were
performed to show that camizestrant exposure in the presence of both phenobarbital 100 mg QD
and ribociclib 400 mg QD or 600 mg QD was similar to that in the absence of these CYP3A
inhibitors and inducers (Table 44), thereby supporting no dose adjustment for these drug
combinations. The conclusions are similar regardless of which cancer population model was
used for the simulations. However, these results should be interpreted with caution when applied
to cancer patients. See the detailed discussion below.
