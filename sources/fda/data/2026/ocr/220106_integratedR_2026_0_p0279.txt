Following a single oral administration of 60 mg (300 nCi) **C-PF-06700841, the total recovery of
the orally administered radioactive dose over a period of 192 hours post dose was 96.7%+6.3%,
with 88.0%+8.0% in the urine and 8.7%+2.1% in the feces.

After oral dosing (period A) of PF-06700841, the major drug related components detected in
circulation were PF-06700841 (47.8% of circulating radioactivity) and metabolite M1 (37.1% of
circulating radioactivity). Minor circulating drug components were M3 (3.6% of circulating
radioactivity) and M4 (3.6% of circulating radioactivity). In urine, the major drug related
component detected was M1 (51.4% of administered dose). Minor drug components in urine
were M6b (10.4% of administered dose), PF-06700841 (7.7% of administered dose), M4 (5.6%
of administered dose) and M3 (5.1% of administered dose). In feces, the predominant drug
related compounds were M3 (2.7% of administered dose), M4 (1.1% of administered dose), PF-
06700841 (0.8% of administered dose), M7 (0.8% of administered dose) and M1 (0.7% of
administered dose).

Following PF-06700841 oral solution administration, the absolution bioavailability is
