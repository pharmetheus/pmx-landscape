Camizestrant is an inhibitor of CYP3A, CYP2C9, and CYP2C19.

e Midazolam (sensitive CYP3A substrate) Cmax increased 1.5-fold
and AUC increased 2-fold following concomitant administration
with camizestrant.

e Warfarin (sensitive CYP2C9 substrate) Cmax is predicted to increase
1.1-fold and AUC 13-fold following concomitant administration
with camizestrant.

e Omeprazole (sensitive CYP2C19 substrate) Cmax is predicted to
increase 3.3-fold and AUC 8.5-fold and lansoprazole (moderately
sensitive CYP2C19 substrate) Cmax is predicted to increase 1.5-fold
and AUC 5.7-fold following concomitant administration with
camizestrant.

Based on the above clinical drug-drug interaction (DDI) and model-
informed approaches, the review team agrees with the proposed labeling
recommendations to avoid concomitant use of camizestrant with strong
and moderate CYP3A inducers, and to adjust the dosage of CYP3A
substrates where minimal increases in concentration may lead to serious
adverse reactions, as necessary. FDA recommends additional instructions
for moderate CYP3A inducers and substrates of CYP2C9 and/or
CYP2C19:

e Ifconcomitant use of a moderate CYP3A inducer cannot be
avoided, increase the dosage of camizestrant to 150 mg QD when
the indicated CDK4/6 inhibitor is palbociclib or abemaciclib. Do
not increase the dosage of camizestrant when combined with
ribociclib.

e Avoid substrates of CYP2C9 and/or CYP2C19 (rather than only
sensitive substrates or substrates with a narrow therapeutic index).
