Key Study Findings

Weekly IV (bolus) administration of SRK-015 (0 (vehicle), 30, 100, or 300 mg/kg/week to SD
tats for 26 weeks (27 total doses) resulted in non-dose-related increases in BW, circulating latent
myostatin levels, and skeletal muscle weights associated with hypertrophy of muscle fibers at all
doses. These findings were consistent with the expected pharmacology of SRK-015 and were not
considered adverse. Changes in the femoral head/neck (femoral head loss, thinning, fracture,
fragmentation of the femoral neck) were observed by in vivo radiography at the end of the
dosing and recovery periods with dose-related increases in incidence and severity. This and the
previous findings in the 12-week study suggest an SRK-015-related effect on bone.
