6.3.2.4. Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions, and
what is the appropriate management strategy?

Data and The Applicant’s Position:

No clinically relevant effect of food was observed on camizestrant PK, thereby camizestrant can
be taken with no food restrictions. A clinically meaningful impact of smoking-related CYP1A2
induction upon camizestrant PK was not anticipated based on in vitro CYP-mediated metabolism
results.

Based on physiologically-based PK modelling no clinically meaningful interactions are predicted
for P-gp, CYP2B6, CYP2D6, BCRP, or OATP1B1. No dose adjustment is required in the
presence of weak, moderate, or strong CYP3A4/5 inhibitors, inhibitors of UGT1A4, or acid
reducing agents. No dose adjustment is required in the presence of weak inducers of CYP3A4/5.
Concomitant use of strong CYP3A4 inducers is not recommended. In combination with
ribociclib, plus a moderate CYP3A4/5 inducer, no dose adjustment of camizestrant is necessary.
When co-administering camizestrant with abemaciclib or palbociclib, consider avoiding the use
of moderate CYP3A4/5 inducers by switching to an alternate concomitant drug product.

Medicinal products that are sensitive substrates (eg, omeprazole) or substrates with narrow
therapeutic index of CYP2C9 and/or CYP2C19 (eg, warfarin and phenytoin) should be |

No clinically meaningful DDIs for palbociclib or abemaciclib in combination with camizestrant
are expected. Ribociclib increases the exposure of camizestrant ~2-fold based on popPK,
