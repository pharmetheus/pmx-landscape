The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA has the following clarifications regarding the Applicant’s presentation of key
regulatory issues.

e Type C Pre-Phase 3 meeting (February 3, 2021): The FDA provided preliminary
comments to the Applicant that the Applicant should justify conducting ESR/m
surveillance and switching therapy in patients who had not experienced disease
progression. At a minimum, the FDA required that patients be consented that they could
be forgoing a therapy from which they are benefiting by switching treatment prior to
radiographic progression.

e Type C Content and Format Meeting (November 4, 2024): The FDA cautioned in a
written response that PFS2 would not be acceptable as a regulatory endpoint for efficacy
in this trial design.
