The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. GLP-compliant 1-month daily oral repeat dose
toxicology studies were conducted in rats and dogs. The studies were initially reviewed by the
FDA under IND 138396. The main findings, including target organs of toxicity in the 1- and 3-
month rat and 1-month dog toxicology studies generally reflected those observed in the 6- and 9-
month studies in rats and dogs, respectively.

The 6-week toxicity study in mice was reviewed as part of a Special Protocol Assessment under
IND 138396 to support dose selection for a future carcinogenicity study. The results from the 6-
week mouse study were not considered to support labeling given the available data in rats with up
to 6 months of daily dosing with camizestrant.
