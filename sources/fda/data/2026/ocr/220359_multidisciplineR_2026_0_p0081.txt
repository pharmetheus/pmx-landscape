Other In vivo studies

The major human circulating camizestrant metabolites M4 and M14 have low potency or were
inactive in ERa degradation (ONC9833-003, ONC9833-009). In a rat uterine assay in vivo,
camizestrant significantly reduced uterine weight compared to vehicle control, consistent with a
lack of uterotrophic agonism (Pharmacology Report 06).
