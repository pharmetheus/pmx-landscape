The Applicant’s Position:

These findings support the overall clinical benefit of the treatment with camizestrant and provide
evidence on the benefit-risk profile from a patient’s perspective.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA acknowledges that the Applicant collected patient-reported outcomes (PROs) using
EORTC-QLQ-C30 (GHS/QoL, physical function, role function, fatigue and pain scales) and
EORTC-QLQ-BR23 (breast symptoms, arm symptoms) to support efficacy evaluation. These
PRO questionnaires were self-administered electronically at home by the patients at C1D1 before
dosing, every 4 weeks for the first 12 weeks and every 8 weeks thereafter until second
progression. Patients completed the ePROs prior to or at the sites if the assessment timepoint
coincided with a scheduled site visit.

On September 2, 2025, an information request for the PRO available data rate (for which the
denominator is the number of randomized patients for each treatment arm at each assessment) for
these questionnaires was sent. On October 2, 2025, the Applicant provided a response to the
information request, contained in Table 23 and Table 24.

As shown in the tables, the available data rate for both EORTC-QLQ-C30 and EORTC-QLQ-
BR23 remains above 70% in both treatment arms through Week 8 and decreases rapidly between
Week 20 and Week 28, eventually falling below 40% in both arms by Week 52. For other PRO
questionnaires informing safety and tolerability, refer to Section 8.2.6.
