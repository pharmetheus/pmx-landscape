Key Study Findings

Weekly IV administration of SRK-015 (0, 30, 100, or 300 mg/kg) to SD rats from implantation
to weaning (GD 6 through LD 20) resulted in BW effects (decreased during lactation and
increased postweaning at all doses), delayed sexual maturation (both sexes at all doses),
increased auditory startle response (SS at MD and HD, sexes combined), decreased mating and
fertility indices (HD males), and macroscopic liver abnormalities (discolored, abnormally-shaped
lobes in HD males and females). A no-effect dose for adverse effects on pre- and postnatal
development was not identified.

SRK-015 (0 (vehicle), 10, 30 or 300 mg/kg/day) was administered weekly (GD 6, 13, 20, LD 4,
11, 18) by IV bolus (6 mL/kg) to pregnant SD rats (24/grp). The following parameters were
evaluated: maternal mortality, clinical observations, BWs, litter/delivery data, and findings at
necropsy and offspring mortality, clinical observations, BWs, developmental landmarks,
neurobehavioral assessments (automated locomotor activity and auditory startle response,
learning and memory in Morris Water Maze), estrous cyclicity, C-section parameters,
macroscopic observations, and mating, fertility, and reproductive indices.
