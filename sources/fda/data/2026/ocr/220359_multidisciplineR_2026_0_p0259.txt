On April 30, 2026, the FDA convened an ODAC meeting to discuss and vote on whether
SERENA-6 had provided a clinically meaningful benefit for camizestrant in patients with
HR+HER2- MBC with a tumor ESR/ mutation detected while on AI and CDK4/6 inhibitor

FDA’s presentation to the Committee described the key issues identified in FDA’s review of
SERENA-6.

e Clinical meaningfulness of a PFS improvement measured from ESR/m detection is
uncertain

switching treatment at ESR/m detection
Chemotherapy/ADC-free survival and PROs do not provide evidence of clinical benefit
