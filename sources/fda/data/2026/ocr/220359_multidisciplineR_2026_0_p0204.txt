The FDA disagrees with the Applicant's assertion that the PRO results from SERENA-6 support
a benefit in terms of symptoms and functioning. Overall, the PRO data from SERENA-6 do not
provide support for switching to camizestrant prior to radiographic disease progression for the
following reasons:

1. Although FDA acknowledges inclusion of GHS/QOoL, this is a very broad concept and
subject to confounding by non-treatment/non-disease factors. FDA typically recommends
focusing more on PRO concepts that are proximal to the disease and treatment being
studied, as outlined in the FDA Guidance: Core Patient-Reported Outcomes in Cancer
Clinical Trials (2024). As such, the FDA did not really consider this particular measure in
our review.

2. Use of TTD analyses for PRO data: Interpretation of TTD endpoint results from
SERENA-6 are constrained by the following:

a. Low Event Rates: Deterioration events occurred in less than half of each trial arm
for most PRO domains, limiting the interpretability of the hazard ratios. For
example, in the primary TTD analysis for physical functioning, only 33 patients
(31%) in the camizestrant and CDK4/6 inhibitor arm and 29 patients (31%) in the
AI and CDK4/6 inhibitor arm experienced deterioration events. Similarly low
event rates were observed across other domains including role functioning, breast
symptoms, and arm symptoms.

less than 55% PRO compliance (defined as the proportion of patients who
