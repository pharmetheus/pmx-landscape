Camizestrant is a next generation oral selective estrogen receptor degrader (ngSERD) and
complete ER antagonist. Camizestrant binds to the LBD of ERa, antagonising the activity of
ERa encoded by both wild-type ESR/ and mutated ESR/, and inducing proteasome-dependent
degradation of ERa, without agonising ERa.
