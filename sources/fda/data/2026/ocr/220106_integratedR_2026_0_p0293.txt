As the study did not progress to Part 2, only the effect of moderate hepatic impairment on the
PK of brepocitinib and M1 was assessed. One participant with normal hepatic function
(Participant oe) had unusually low, outlying, brepocitinib total exposure (AUC values
that were approximately 10% of the geometric mean for the cohort). Sensitivity analysis
excluding Participant ©) showed that brepocitinib AUC and Cmax were 19% higher and
17% lower in subjects with moderate hepatic impairment compared to normal hepatic function.
M1 AUC and Cmax were 19% higher and 19% lower in subjects with moderate hepatic
impairment compared to normal hepatic function.
