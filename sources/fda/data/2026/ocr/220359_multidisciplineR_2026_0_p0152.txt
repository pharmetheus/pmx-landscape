FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that camizestrant is an inhibitor of P-gp and BCRP
at clinically relevant concentrations in vitro. A PMR will be issued to conduct clinical drug
interaction trials to assess the effect of camizestrant on the PK of substrates of P-gp and BCRP to
inform an appropriate drug interaction management strategy for coadministration with
camizestrant.

UGT1A4

In vitro studies have demonstrated that camizestrant is primarily metabolized by CYP3A4/5 and
UGT1A4. FDA requested the Applicant to provide pharmacogenetic analyses to assess the
relationship between polymorphic variants of UGT/44 on camizestrant PK, safety, and efficacy.
Pharmacogenetic analyses were not conducted by the Applicant. However, data from human
ADME studies and pre-clinical data indicate that UGT/A4 has a minor role in the metabolism of
camizestrant, contributing approximately 16% to its total metabolism. The Applicant also
conducted analysis on between-subject variability (ETA) in apparent clearance (CL/F) using the
base PopPK model for camizestrant monotherapy to assess the potential impact of UGT1A4
polymorphism on PK of camizestrant. The resulting ETA on CL/F histogram plot showed a
unimodal normal distribution centered around 0, indicating limited variability in drug clearance
across patient population.
