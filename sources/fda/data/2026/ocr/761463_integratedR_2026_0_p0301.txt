5 WARNINGS AND
PRECAUTIONS

The Applicant had not proposed inclusion of this section in labeling;
however, the Agency added a subsection titled Fractures. This
inclusion was based on an imbalance of fractures in patients receiving
apitegromab (both 10 mg/kg and 20 mg/kg doses) compared to
placebo. Additionally, the Agency’s assessment of the nonclinical data
also concluded drug-related adverse effects on bone in animals. These
findings are suggestive that the risk of fractures with apitegromab
treatment is greater than that of patients with spinal muscular atrophy in
general.

Therefore, the Agency added this clinically significant adverse reaction
as a Warning and Precaution with the limitations in use imposed by
fractures (i.e., use with caution in certain patients) and the steps that
should be taken if fractures occur (i.e., weigh risk benefit) per 21 CFR
201.57(c)(6)(i).
