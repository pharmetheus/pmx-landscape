Key Study Findings

When SRK-015 (0, 30, 100, or 300 mg/kg) was administered to male and female rats once
weekly to male and female SD rats from premating through mating (males) or early gestation
and embryonic implantation (females), drug-related increases in BW gain (all doses in both
sexes), increases in the estrous cycle length, decreases in sperm concentrations, and decreased
inating and fertility indices were observed at all doses. Postimplantation loss was increased and
live fetuses were decreased at all doses.
