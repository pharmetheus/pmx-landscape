Camizestrant is a P-gp substrate. However, P-gp may play an insignificant role

in camizestrant absorption because: (1) camizestrant is a BCS class 1 drug with high
pemneability; and (2) the human ADME study confirms that the fraction absorbed

of camizestrant is high, assuming that the parent drug identified in the feces was from
hydrolysis of camizestrant glucuronide as discussed above. Furthermore, the

current camizestrant PBPK model, which does not incorporate P-gp, could fully explain
the effects of itraconazole on camizestrant exposure. Given that itraconazole is also a P-
gp inhibitor, the fact that CYP3A inhibition alone accounts for the observed DDI
suggests that P-gp inhibition does not significantly contribute to camizestrant disposition.
If P-gp does contribute to the camizestrant disposition, the fmcyp3a may be overestimated
in the current model, as some of the clearance attributed to CYP3A metabolism may in
fact reflect P-gp-mediated efflux. Nevertheless, the simulated induction effects may still
teflect a worst-case scenario since CYP3A inducers have greater induction effects on
CYP3A than on P-gp (PMID: 29569723 and 29569712).
