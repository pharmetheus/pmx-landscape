NDA 218145 Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Simtriyo (Centanafadine)

Design

In this multi-arm study, Arm-4 was single doses of 328.8 mg centanafadine ER capsule (i.e., to-

be-marketed ER capsule) administered in soft foods (either sprinkled on 15 mL of apple sauce,

15 mL of yogurt, or in 15 mL of orange juice) compared to administration as intact capsule. The
drug product was administered after a 10-hr overnight fast.

Results

The key PK parameters and PK profiles of centanafadine remained unchanged when
centanafadine ER capsule was administered either as intact or sprinkled the contents of capsule
over soft-foods such as apple-sauce, yogurt or in orange juice.
