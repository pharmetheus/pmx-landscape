The review team agrees that the bioanalytical method validation and performance is adequate for
quantification of serum apitegromab concentrations in studies SRK-015-001, SRK-015-002,
SRK-015-003 and SRK-015-004 and are in accordance with the criteria for accuracy and
precision as outlined in the FDA guidance for industry Bioanalytical Method Validation
Guidance for Industry (May 2018). Overall, the serum apitegromab concentrations measured in
these studies are reliable.
