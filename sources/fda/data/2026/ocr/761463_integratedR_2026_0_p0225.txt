Key Study Findings

Weekly IV (bolus) administration of SRK-015 (0 (vehicle), 30, 100, or 300 mg/kg) to pregnant
SD rats on GD 6 and 13 resulted in increases in maternal BW gain and fetal BWs at all doses but
no apparent maternal or clearly drug-related developmental toxicity. Mean postimplantation loss
was increased at the LD and HD but was not SS because of high variability across litters in those
groups. The NOAEL (300 mg/kg) was associated with GD 13 maternal Cmax and AUC values of
5940 pg/mL and 297000 p.g*h/mL, respectively.

SRK-015 was administered to time-mated female SD rats by IV bolus on GDs 6 and 13. The
following parameters and end points were evaluated: mortality, clinical signs, body weights,
food consumption, maternal reproductive parameters (GD 21), fetal weights, and fetal
examinations (external, visceral, and skeletal).

Doses were based on the results of a dose range-finding (DRF) EFD study in SD rats
( 8454865) in which SRK-015 (0, 30, 100, or 300 mg/kg) was administered IV
on GDs 6 and 13 and no maternal or developmental effects were observed.
