Observations and Results
Mortality

The survival rates for males and females are presented in Figure 1 and Figure 2,
respectively. At Week 80, the survival rate in high-dose (HD) group males began to
diverge from the control group, and a significant decrease (to 32%) was observed at
Week 104 (Table 2 and Figure 1). Statistically significant decrease was observed in
females in the LD group (Table 2 and Figure 2). Increased deaths in males were
attributed to bacterial infection in the heart, kidney, and/or ascending urinary tract, and
benign Leydig cell tumors in testis.
