Key Study Findings

Weekly SC administration of SRK-015 (0 (vehicle), 30, 100, 275 mg/kg) to juvenile rats on
PNDs 7, 14, 21, and 28 resulted in the expected effects on latent myostatin and muscle weights
but no apparent effect on juvenile development. However, the assessment of possible bone
effects consisted only of femur length measurement and routine histopathology. The NOAEL
(275 mg/kg) was associated with Cmax and AUC values of 2550 j1g/mL and 382000 j1g*h/mL,
respectively, on PND 28.
