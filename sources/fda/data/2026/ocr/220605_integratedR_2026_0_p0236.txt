14.4. Immunogenicity Assessment

14.4.1. Summary of Clinical Studies for
immunogenicity analysis

Immunogenicity of rusfertide was evaluated in 4 Phase 1 trials conducted with the commercial
LPF formulation (Studies PTG-300-15, PTG-300-16, PTG-300-20, and PTG-300-22) in healthy
volunteers and 2 studies in participants with PV: the Phase 2 dose-finding Study PTG-300-04
with the liquid formulation, and the pivotal Phase 3 Study PTG-300-11 with the LPF. Therefore,
the information in the draft label comes from the pivotal Phase 3 Study PTG-300-11. Table 17
contains the assessment of immunogenicity information up to 108 weeks for Study PTG-300-11.
