The FDA’s Assessment:

Food Effect:

FDA agrees that administration of the camizestrant with or without food is supported by
comparison of Regimens D and G in a fasted state and Regimens E and H in a fed state in Study
D8530c00004.

The design of Study D8530c00004 adequately evaluated the effect of food on the oral tablet
formulation. There were total of 8 study treatments (Regimens A-H). Each treatment consisted of
a single dose of camizestrant 75 or 300 mg administered using 1 of 3 formulations (” tablet,
® tablet, oral solution). On Day 1 of each period, camizestrant was administered under 1 of 2
conditions (fasted or with high-fat meal). In regimens B — D, F and G (fasted state), subjects
were administered a single 75 or 300 mg dose following a 10-hour overnight fast. In regimens E
and H (fed), a high-fat meal (comprised of 1 fried egg, 2 strips of bacon, 2 slices of sliced bread
with butter, 1 hash brown, and 240 mL of whole milk) was consumed 30 minutes before
camizestrant dosing. Cohorts 1 and 2 explored the potential effect of food on the PK of 75 mg of
camizestrant using 25 mg strength” tablets. Although the 25 mg strength is not the to-be-
marketed strength, given that the absolute bioavailability of camizestrant 25 mg” tablet is low
(~32%), similar to the oral solution, and that a majority of the drug is excreted by the fecal route,
it is anticipated that the 25 and 75 mg strengths will behave similarly, thus the in vitro bridging
data between the 25 and 75 mg strengths is reasonable. Refer to the Product Quality Assessment
review dated 5/7/2026 in DARRTS (ref ID 5789790) for more details regarding the dissolution
properties of camizestrant.
