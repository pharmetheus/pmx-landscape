Conclusions and Reasons

e The median heart rate on the camizestrant and CDK4/6i arm
decreased by 14 beats per minute compared to no change on the
Al and CDK4/6i arm. Bradycardia occurred in 7% of patients on
the camizestrant and CDK4/6i arm compared to 0% on the AI
and CDK4/6i arm. There were no Grade 3 or 4 events.

e Camizestrant in combination with a CDK 4/61 is associated with
QTc interval prolongation; however, the available data are
insufficient to characterize the effect size of camizestrant on the
QTc interval.Ribociclib, one of the CDK4/6i options on
SERENA-6, has known QTc interval prolonging effects and also
increases camizestrant exposure approximately 2-fold through a
drug-drug interaction.

¢ One patient on SERENA-1 who was treated with camizestrant
75mg and ribociclib 600mg had QTc interval prolongation with
bradycardia resulting in Torsades de Pointes.

Visual disturbances occurred in 34% of patients on the
camizestrant and CDK4/6i arm compared to 16% on the AI and
CDK4/6i arm in SERENA-6. The most common preferred term
PT under visual disturbances was photopsia (20% in the
camizestrant and CDK4/6i arm and 8% in the AI and CDK4/6i
arm). All cases of visual disturbances were Grade 1 and 2,
except for one case of Grade 3 photopsia.

The available data are insufficient to fully
characterize the effect of camizestrant on the QTc
interval and a PMR will be issued for further
evaluation.

A Boxed Warning and ECG monitoring
recommendations are included in labeling for risk
mitigation of cardiac toxicity.

Otherwise, the safety profile is generally as
expected for an endocrine therapy in combination
with a CDK4/6i and acceptable for the treatment
of an incurable disease.
