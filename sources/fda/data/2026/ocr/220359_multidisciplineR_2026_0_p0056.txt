Site #7884: Dr. Issam Makhoul
Inspection Dates: 9/2/2025 — 9/5/2025, 9/8/2025

This was a comprehensive, BIMO review based inspection of Dr. Issam Makhoul. This was the
first FDA inspection of this CI.

At this site for Study D8534C00001, 19 subjects were screened, and 11 subjects were enrolled
on to Step 1. Four subjects were enrolled onto Step 2: 2 subjects of which were in the indicated
population (Group A), and 2 subjects were enrolled on the control arm (Group B). Of the 4
subjects enrolled; one subject withdrew due to “subjective disease progressive” and “needing an
orthopedic procedure and investigator determination that the subject was no longer benefiting
from study treatment”, another subject withdrew and did not have documented AE nor narrative,
with data line listing as discontinuation “subject decision”, one subject had PD, and Subject

# © was in long-term follow up at the time of the inspection.

During the inspection, subject records reviewed included ICDs, subject case history records,
eligibility criteria, protocol deviations, AE reporting, concomitant medications, and study visit
assessments. The regulatory documents reviewed were IRB submissions for initial approval and
continuing reviews, protocol amendments, financial disclosures, training records, and IP
accountability records.

The primary efficacy endpoint data were verifiable. There were no under-reported AEs or SAEs.
Two of four enrolled subjects were ineligible for the study due to use of prohibited medication
when they were randomized into the study. Protocol exclusion criteria 2.22b stated that subjects
treated with drugs that prolong the QTc interval and have a known risk of torsade de pointe
should be excluded from the study if the tequired withdrawal period is not completed prior t to
