4.4. Devices and Companion Diagnostic Issues

CDRH received an sPMA (P200010/S024) from Guardant Health, Inc. for the Guardant360 CDx
assay on May 29, 2025. Guardant360 is a qualitative, next generation sequencing-based in vitro
diagnostic device that uses targeted high throughput hybridization-based capture technology for
detection of single nucleotide variants, insertions and deletions in fifty-five genes, copy number
amplifications in two genes, and fusions in four genes from ctDNA. It was proposed as a CDx to
select patients with tumor ESR/m to receive treatment with camizestrant. In the SERENA-6 trial,
qualifying ESR/m included any of the following: E380Q, V422del, S463P, L536H, L536P,
L536R, Y537C, Y537D, Y537N, Y537S, and D538G. The Guardant360 CDx is not currently
authorized to monitor patients for detectable ESR/m during treatment with an AI and CDK4/6i.

The sPMA was under review concurrently with the NDA for camizestrant and contemporaneous
approval of the device as a CDx to select patients with tumor ESR/m to receive treatment with
camizestrant is planned. Drug and device labeling describe that patients should have received an
Al and CDK4/6i for at least 6 months to be tested for ESR/m, and testing should be performed
every 3 months.
