Key Study Findings

Weekly IV (bolus) administration of SRK-015 (0 (vehicle), 30, 100, or 300 mg/kg) to pregnant
New Zealand White (NZW) rabbits on GD 7 and 14 resulted no adverse test article-related
effects on maternal body weight or food consumption. Three LD animals were found dead or
sacrificed moribund following the GD 14 dose. At terminal sacrifice, 1 LD animal was pregnant
with no viable fetuses (all fetuses had been resorbed early in gestation) and 2 other LD animals
were not pregnant, leaving only 14 litters available for evaluation. Fetal BW was decreased (SS)
and postimplantation loss increased (13.5 versus 1.5% in C) in the LD group. All findings in the
LD group were thought to be attributable to endotoxin contamination of the LD formulation, but
this was not clearly established as a cause. The HD was considered a NOAEL in the study report
(GD 14 AUCo-16s and Cmax were 1670000 h*ug/mL and 23800 g/mL, respectively), but
interpretation of the study is confounded by the possible endotoxin contamination.

following parameters and end points were evaluated: mortality, clinical signs, body weights,
