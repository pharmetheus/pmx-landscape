Thymomas were observed in female rats in the LD and MD groups compared to the
control group. Thymomas are a common background tumor in female Wistar rats
(Taylor and Mowat, 2020). In addition, due to lack of dose response and statistical
significance by the FDA statistical review, thymomas were not considered related to the

test article.
