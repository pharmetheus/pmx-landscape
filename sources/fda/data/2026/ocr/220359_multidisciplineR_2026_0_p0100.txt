Study title: AZD9833: Nine Month Oral (Gavage) Toxicity Study in the Dog (GLP Study
No.: 509313)

e One female animal at low dose 5 mg/kg was terminated from the study on day 244 due to
welfare reasons. The death was considered related to AZD9833 due to enlarged ovaries causing
abdominal pain, which was the primary reason for premature termination.

e Clinical signs included decreased activity immediately following dosing in both males and
females at >10 mg/kg and vaginal discharge in one female was observed at 10 mg/kg from day
220-238.

e Major target organs included the reproductive system in both sexes at >5 mg/kg with ovarian
masses, uterine/cervical/vaginal atrophy, testicular interstitial cell hypertrophy/hyperplasia,
prostate gland enlargement, and seminiferous tubule degeneration/atrophy; and cardiovascular
system with dose- and time-dependent heart rate decrease and QTca prolongation at all dose
levels.
