Conclusions and Reasons

Breast cancer is the most common cancer in women with
approximately 321,910 new cases and 42,140 deaths in the
US. in 2026 based on SEER estimates. Although rare (<1%),
breast cancer may also occur in males (American Cancer

Society 2026).

e Approximately 70% of patients with breast cancer have

HR+HER2- disease.

e¢ HR+HER2- MBC is incurable.

HR+HER2- MBC, including with tumor ESR/m,
is a serious and life-threatening condition with
significant unmet need for effective therapies that
prolong survival and preserve quality of life.

The incurable nature of HR+HER2- MBC
underscores the importance of maximizing the
benefit of each line of therapy.

e All treatment for HR+HER2- MBC is palliative, with the goals

of reducing cancer-related symptoms, delaying disease
progression, and prolonging survival.

e Each treatment regimen for metastatic disease is typically given
until progression or intolerable side effects, and then a new
treatment regimen is started. The goal of therapy is to maximize

the benefit from each line of therapy.

e For most U.S. patients, 1L treatment is with an AI and CDK4/6i.

e Following progression on an AI with or without a CDK4/6i,
treatment options include ET as monotherapy or in combination
with targeted therapy, targeted therapy alone, chemotherapy, and

antibody-drug conjugates.

All treatment options are palliative.

Three FDA-approved oral ER antagonists are
already available for patients with ESRJ-mutated
HR+HER2- MBC after disease progression on
prior therapy, all indicated at the standard
timepoint of radiographic progression.

It is unknown whether switching treatment at

detection of ESR1m, prior to radiographic or
clinical progression, improves long-term outcomes.

MBC is to continue a well-tolerated treatment
