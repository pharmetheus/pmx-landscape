General Information

Bioanalysis

Bioanalytical information for camizestrant is provided in Section 19.4.4.

Dose range tested in
clinical trials

25 mg to 450 mg QD as monotherapy, 75 mg to 300 mg QD in
combination with palbociclib, 75 mg to 150 mg QD in combination with
abemaciclib, and 75 mg QD in combination with ribociclib.

Dose proportionality

AUC and Cmax increase in a greater than dose proportional manner over
the dosage range of 25 mg to 450 mg (0.3 to 6 times the proposed
recommended dosage) QD.

Accumulation

Accumulation was observed after multiple dosing with an accumulation
ratio of 1.4 for Cmax and 1.8 for AUC.

Absorption

Absolute oral bioavailability is 43%. Median time to maximum
concentration (Tmax) is 3 to 4 hours.

No clinically significant differences in camizestrant PK were observed
following administration of a high-fat meal in healthy postmenopausal
women.

Distribution

Apparent volume of distribution is 1,888 L.

Camizestrant plasma protein binding is 0.75 and is not concentration-
dependent in vitro. The blood-to-plasma concentration ratio is 0.99.

Metabolism

Camizestrant is primarily metabolized by CYP3A and UGT1A4.

Elimination

Terminal half-life is 23 hours with an apparent clearance of 69 L/h.
