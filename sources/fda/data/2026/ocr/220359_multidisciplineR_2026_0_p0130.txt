Excretion

Following a single oral 75 mg dose of radiolabeled camizestrant to
healthy subjects, 65% of the total radioactivity was recovered in feces
and 17% in urine.

Effect of Other Drugs on camizestrant

e Effect of Strong CYP3A Inhibitors on camizestrant:

o Camizestrant AUC increased 1.9-fold and Cmax increased 1.4-
fold following concomitant use of itraconazole (strong CYP3A
inhibitor) 200 mg QD.

o Camizestrant AUC and Cmax both increased 2-fold when
camizestrant is used in combination with ribociclib (strong
CYP3A inhibitor) 400 or 600 mg QD.

administration of phenobarbital (moderate CYP3A inducer)
100 mg QD for 17 days.
