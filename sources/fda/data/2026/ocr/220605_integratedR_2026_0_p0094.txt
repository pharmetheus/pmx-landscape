The observed increase in platelet count in the rusfertide arm is an anticipated consequence of the
mechanism of action of rusfertide as a hepcidin mimetic, which suppresses iron-restricted
erythropoiesis and may secondarily affect thrombopoiesis. A detailed analysis of thrombocytosis,
including clinical context and individual subject narratives, is presented in Safety Review

Issue #1: Thrombocytosis (Section 7.6.1).
