The Applicant’s Position:

The results of the key secondary efficacy variables supported the PFS benefit. Camizestrant +
CDK4/6i reduced the PFS2 risk by 48% relative to AI + CDK4/6i, indicating that PFS benefit
was sustained through the subsequent therapy and further substantiating the benefit of an early
switch of ET. Additionally, camizestrant treatment delayed time to subsequent therapies. Interim
OS data (12.4% mature with 39 OS events) showed a favorable trend for the camizestrant arm,
with additional follow-up planned.

The results of the key secondary efficacy variables supported the PFS benefit.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Although FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of secondary endpoint results at
DCO1, including the key secondary endpoints of PFS2 and OS, FDA disagrees with the
Applicant’s characterization that these results supported a finding of clinical benefit.
