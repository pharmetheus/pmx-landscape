Additional data (presented by the FDA):

Study BE001764-30 (effect of vagotomy on heart rate)

Methods: On Day 4, animals underwent anesthesia, vagotomy procedure, and baroreflex testing
with phenylephrine administration before and after vagotomy. Blood samples were collected for
pharmacokinetic analysis on Day 2 and at termination, with brain tissue collected for exposure
assessment. Statistical analysis used linear mixed-effects models with significance set at P < 0.05.
Results: AZD9833 caused initial tachycardia on Day 1 (50 bpm increase) followed by sustained
bradycardia on Days 2-3 with maximum reduction of 87 bpm (22%). The heart rate reduction
persisted after vagotomy (maximum 82 bpm decrease), suggesting the effect is independent of
parasympathetic nervous system activity. Vagotomy success was confirmed by significantly
reduced baroreflex response to phenylephrine (from -25.6 to -8.46 bpm in vehicle group, P <
0.001).
