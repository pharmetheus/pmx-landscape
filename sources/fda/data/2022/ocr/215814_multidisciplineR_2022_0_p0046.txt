Parameters Major findings

Mortality There was a total of 7 unscheduled deaths during the study.
70 mg/kg/day (MD): 1 male was euthanized on Day 35 due to a necrotic distal tail
and thin appearance.

150 mg/kg/day (HD): 3 males and 2 females were euthanized early on Days 22 —
37; 1 female was euthanized on recovery Day 2 (Day 38). The animals showed poor
clinical conditions (thin appearance, discolored feces) and olutasidenib-related

microscopic findings in the liver.

Clinical Signs Olutasidenib-related clinical observations in the HD group included tremors,
vomitus/emesis, low food consumption, jaundice, pale mucosa, liquid feces, and/or
thin body condition.
