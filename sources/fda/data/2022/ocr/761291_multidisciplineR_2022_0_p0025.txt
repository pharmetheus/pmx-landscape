Conclusions and Reasons

@ Multiple myeloma (MM) is the second most common hematological
malignancy.

e Therapy for patients with relapsed or refractory MM (RRMM) has improved
considerably over the years with approval of multiple new therapies with
improvement in response rate and progression-free survival (PFS).

e However, MM remains incurable, with a 5-year survival rate of 57.9%.

e RRMM is a serious and life-threatening
condition.

Current
Treatment

e@ Multiple drugs approved for use in MM and numerous combination
regimens are considered standard of care.

e Potential treatments include alkylating agents, corticosteroids,
immunomodulatory agents (IMiDs), proteasome inhibitors (Pls) and
monoclonal antibodies (mAbs).

Despite the availability of multiple
therapies, RRMM remains an incurable
disease.

Benefit

e Assessment of the clinical benefit of teclistamab was based on the efficacy
results of the MajesTEC-1 trial. The overall response rate (ORR) in the 110
patients who had received at least 3 prior lines of therapy, including a Pl, an
IMiD, and an anti-CD38 mAb and were treated at the RP2D was 61.8% (95%
Cl: 52.1, 70.9).

¢ At the time of the 09 Nov 2021 efficacy data cut-off, with a median follow-up
of 7.4 months among responders, the median duration of response (DOR) was|
not reached (95% Cl: 9.0, not estimable), and the estimated DOR rate was
90.6% (95% Cl: 80.3, 95.7) at 6 months and 66.5% (95% Cl: 38.8, 83.9) at 9
months

¢ The ORR and durability of response
represent a treatment benefit.

e A post-marketing requirement (PMR) to
verify and describe the clinical benefit of
teclistamab in a randomized clinical trial
in patients with RRMM will be issued.

Risk and Risk
Management

¢ Safety was evaluated in 165 patients in the phase 1 and phase 2 cohorts
treated at the RP2D of teclistamab.

e Fatal adverse reactions (ARs) occurred in 5% of patients who received
teclistamab. Serious ARs occurred in 54% of patients who received
teclistamab.

e The key safety concerns for teclistamab are cytokine release syndrome (CRS)
and neurologic toxicity, including immune effector cell-associated
neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS). CRS and neurologic toxicity were common

prescribers regarding the risk of CRS and
neurologic toxicity, including ICANS, with
