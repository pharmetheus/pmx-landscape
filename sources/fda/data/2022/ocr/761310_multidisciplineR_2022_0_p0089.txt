Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions, and what is the appropriate
management strategy?

As mirvetuximab soravtansine is administered intravenously, no clinical assessment of potential
food-drug interactions has been performed.

Dedicated clinical studies to assess potential drug-drug interactions (DDIs) have not been
performed. As such, DDIs involving antibodies are not anticipated. Unconjugated DM4 and s-
methyl DM4 are substrates of CYP3A4 and therefore, concomitant use of ELAHERE with strong
CYP3A4 inhibitors may increase unconjugated DM4 exposure and thereby AEs associated with
ELAHERE. The proposed label Section 7.1 recommends close monitoring of patietns for adverse
reactions with ELAHERE when used concomitantly with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors. The applicant
performed population PK analysis to evaluate the effect of concomitant CYP3A4 inihibitor with
ELAHERE on mirvetuximab soravtansine, free DM4 or s-methyl DM4 exposure. There were 22,
64, and 15 patients taking weak, moderate, or strong Cytochrome P450 3A4 (CYP3A4)
inhibitors, respectively, at any time during the study. However, based on the start and end
times, ciprofloxacin (a moderate CYP3A4 inhibitor; n = 5) and ranitidine (a weak CYP3A4
inhibitor; n = 6) were the only inhibitors that were administered to multiple patients at or near
the start of C1D1 dosing of mirvetuximab soravtansine at 6mg/kg AIBW.

The exposures parameters of mirvetuximab soravtansine, free DM4 or s-methyl DM4 were
compared for all of these patients (5 on ciprofloxacin and 6 on ranitidine) were compared with
all other patients (n = 231), which showed overlapping exposures (Table 13).
