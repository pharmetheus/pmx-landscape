20.4.6. Population PK Analysis for Tocilizumab and sIL-6R
20.4.6.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

In support of the use of tocilizumab (TCZ) for the management of CRS induced by anti- CD20 T
cell engaging (TCE) bispecifics antibodies (i.e., mosunetuzumab and glofitamab), the Applicant
submitted updated PopPK report with study GO29781 (Mosunetuzumab) and NP30179
(Glofitamab). The PopPK analyses were updated from the previous PK model of tocilizumab by
using data from multiple studies in adult patients with RA and pediatric patients with sJIA and
pcllA.

The PK of tocilizumab was described by a two-compartment model with parallel linear and
Michaelis-Menten elimination, and the first-order subcutaneous absorption. The PK of total sIL-
6R was characterized by equations of quasisteady-state (QSS) approximation of the target-
mediated drug disposition (TMDD) model. The final PopPK models for tocilizumab and sIL-6R
were validated and the updated PopPK model is generally in line with the previous model.

The covariate analysis showed CRS had significant effects on tocilizumab PK parameters and
sIL6R parameters. Different TCZ dosing regimen scenarios were simulated and the following TCZ
dosing recommendations were shown to maintain TCZ concentrations below the “safety
threshold” during the full treatment duration of mosunetuzumab or glofitamab treatment: Two
consecutive TCZ doses at least 8 hours apart, maximum two doses per CRS event; maximum
three TCZ doses within each time period of 6 weeks of mosunetuzumab or glofitamab.

The Applicant’s PopPK analyses for tocilizumab and sIL-6R were verified by the reviewer, with

no significant discordance identified. ‘ |
