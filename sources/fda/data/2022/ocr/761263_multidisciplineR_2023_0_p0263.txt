The serum concentration-time course of tocilizumab following single and multiple IV
administrations in adult patients with CRS was accurately described by a two compartment
pharmacokinetic model with parallel linear and Michaelis-Menten elimination. The final PK
model parameter estimates and the corresponding 95% confidence intervals (Cls) for TCZ and
sIL-6R are presented in Table 70. The goodness-of-fit plots for the final PK model are
presented in Figure 56. The prediction corrected visual predictive check (pcVPC) based on all
data (Figure 57) illustrated the prediction percentiles and corresponding 95% Cl of simulated
concentrations overlaid on the observed TCZ and sIL-6R concentrations and the corresponding
5 and 95" percentiles. CRS had significant effects on tocilizumab PK parameters and sIL6R
parameters. Specifically, linear clearance in patients with CRS grade 2 or higher was on
average 60% (95% Cl: 25-103%) higher than in patients with RA. Elimination rate of bound sIL-
6R, was 109% (95% Cl: 80-142%) higher in patients with CRS than in patients with RA.
