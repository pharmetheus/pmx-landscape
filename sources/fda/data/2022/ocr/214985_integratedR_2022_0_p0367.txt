The reviewer's primary analysis shows that for Drug Liking Emax:

1. The median difference between each positive control and placebo was significantly greater than
15 (p =0.0003).

2. The mean or median differences between each positive control and each dose of ACT-541468
were not significantly greater than zero (p 20.1955) except the comparison between each
positive control and 50 mg ACT-541468 (p<0.0004).

3. For exploratory purpose, the test for the comparison between 50 mg ACT-541468 and placebo
was performed. The median difference between 50 mg ACT-541468 and placebo was not
significantly smaller than 11 (p=0.9785). This large p-value indicates that in fact, the median
difference between 50 mg ACT-541468 and placebo was significantly greater than 11 with a p-
value of 0.0215.

In conclusion, drug liking produced by ACT-541468 was not smaller than that produced by either 30 mg
zolpidem or 40 mg suvorexant. The results were validated by the significant differences between both
positive controls and placebo.

Note: Several mistakes were found in Tables 15-33, 15-34 and 15-35 in sponsor’s report. The
median of paired difference and (Q1, Q3) of paired difference in these tables were wrongly
calculated using the difference in medians, difference in Q1, and difference in Q3 between
treatments. These mistakes were also made in other tables for secondary endpoints.
