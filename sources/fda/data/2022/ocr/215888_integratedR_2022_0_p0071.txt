7. Risk and Risk Management

7.1. Potential Risks or Safety Concerns Based on
Nonclinical Data

The nonclinical data submitted to support the safety evaluation of oteseconazole included
pharmacology (primary, secondary and safety pharmacology), pharmacokinetics (absorption,
distribution, metabolism, excretion with toxicokinetics for pivotal studies) and toxicology
(repeat-dose, genotoxicity, carcinogenicity, reproductive toxicity) studies in rats (up to two
years), dogs (up to 39 weeks), mice (up to 26 weeks) and rabbits (during pregnancy to evaluate
embryo-fetal toxicity). The identified risks should be manageable in the indicated population.
