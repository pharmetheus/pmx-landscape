FDA also evaluated the safety profile in the 30 patients that were rechallenged with one additional dose
of tremelimumab administered in combination with durvalumab. There were no adverse events that
appeared to occur disproportionately in this population. The most common (>10%) adverse events were
diarrhea 40%, fatigue 37%, pruritis 37%, rash 33%, musculoskeletal pain 30%, abdominal pain 27%,
ascites 17%, hypothyroidism 17%, dry skin 13%, edema 13%, and hemorrhage 13%. Gastrointestinal
hemorrhage (gastrointestinal ulcer hemorrhage) was observed in 2 patients.

The imAEs observed in the patients reported as having immune-related toxicity include hyperthyroidism
(n=3), hypothyroidism (n=3), diarrhea (n=3) and pancreatic events (n=2).
