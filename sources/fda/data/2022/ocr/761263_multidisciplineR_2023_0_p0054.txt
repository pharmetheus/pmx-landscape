Immunophenotyping Changes in lymphocyte populations were measured in the blood, spleen,
and lymph node. Sustained B-cell depletion in the blood and lymphoid
tissues, T-cell activation, and associated transient decreases in circulating
T-cells and natural killer cells were observed at 0.1 and 0.5 mg/kg dose
levels.

Gross Pathology Macroscopic findings included red cecum (1 animal at 0.5 mg/kg), small
spleen (2 animals at 0.5 mg/kg), small adrenal (1 animal each at 0.2 and
0.5 mg/kg), and dark pancreas (2 animals at 0.5 mg/kg).

Organ Weights Reduced adrenal weights in males at 0.1 and 0.5 mg/kg and spleen
weights in females at 0.5 mg/kg were observed.
