Effect of Daridorexant on the PK of Other Drugs

CYP3A4 Substrates: In healthy subjects receiving daridorexant and midazolam (a sensitive
CYP3A4 substrate), daridorexant at a dose of 25 mg did not affect the PK of midazolam,
indicating the absence of CYP3A4 induction or inhibition. PBPK modeling showed that similar
results can be expected with a dose of 50 mg; therefore, CYP3A4 substrates can be administered
with daridorexant without any dose adjustment.

BCRP Substrate: In healthy subjects receiving daridorexant and rosuvastatin (a BCRP Substrate),
daridorexant at a dose of 25 mg did not affect the PK of rosuvastatin indicating an absence of
inhibition of BCRP. PBPK modeling showed that similar results can be expected with a dose of
50 mg; therefore, BCRP substrates can be administered with daridorexant without dose
adjustment.

Citalopram: In healthy subjects, the PK of citalopram at steady state was not affected by
coadministration of 50 mg daridorexant.
