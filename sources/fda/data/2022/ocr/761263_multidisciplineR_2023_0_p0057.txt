General toxicology; additional studies

Study title/number: A Single Dose Toxicokinetic, Pharmacodynamic, and Toxicology Study of
BTCT4465A via Intravenous Infusion or Subcutaneous Administration in Cynomolgus Monkeys
with a 7-week Recovery Period/14-1246

A GLP-compliant single-dose toxicology study of BTCT4465A in monkeys was conducted with IV
infusion or subcutaneous (SC) injection once followed by a 7-week recovery period. Monkeys
were dosed with BTCT4465A at 0 (IV/SC), 0.01 (IV), 0.1(IV), or 1 mg/kg (IV/SC). Necropsy
procedures were performed on Day 8 (terminal; 3M/3F), Day 22 (first recovery necropsy; 2
M/2F per dose except 1M/2F at 1 mg/kg IV) and Day 57 (second recovery necropsy; 2M/2F
each control and HD). The Day 22 necropsy was performed on animals instrumented with a
telemetry device for collection of cardiovascular and body temperature data. Clinical signs
included emesis, decreased activity/hunched posture and mucoid feces on Day 1 post-dose at 1
mg/kg IV.

The cardiovascular and body temperature safety assessment on telemetered animals revealed
transient and dose-dependent increases in heart rate and body temperature at 0.1 mg/kg IV
and 1 mg/kg (IV or SC) and decreased blood pressure at 1 mg/kg IV on Day 1. Heart rate
increases (RR interval decreases) at these dose levels had correlating decreases in PR and QT
intervals (1 mg/kg IV and SC, only), but there was no effect on QTca. Cardiovascular findings
resolved by Day 7.
