3 COMPREHENSIVE CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY REVIEW

3.1 Overview of the Product and Regulatory Background

Omidenepag Isopropyl (OMDI) ophthalmic solution 0.002% is a selective EP2 receptor agonist
with OMDI, a prodrug of omidenepag, with a non-prostaglandin structure, as the active ingredient.
Omidenepag is an IOP-lowering agent that reduces elevated IOP by increasing the outflow of
aqueous humor through the trabecular and uveoscleral pathways.
