Clinical Reviewer Comment:

8.3.1. Juvenile Animal Study

Daridorexant was administered orally to juvenile rats from postnatal day (PND) 21 through PND
84 at doses of 0, 50, 150, and 450 mg/kg/day. Daridorexant at 450 mg/kg/day delayed sexual
maturation in males (~2 days) without affecting reproductive performance or sperm parameters.
Sexual maturation and reproductive capacity in females were not affected. Learning and memory
in the juvenile animals were not affected by daridorexant in either sex.

In addition, daridorexant caused hepatocellular hypertrophy in the liver in both sexes with
correlative increases in GGT levels (3x and 8x in males and females, respectively) and increased
liver weight at 450 mg/kg/day and follicular cell hypertrophy in the thyroid with correlative
increases in thyroid weight at >150 mg/kg/day. Findings in the liver and thyroid gland were
similar to those observed in studies in adult rats at the same or comparable doses.
