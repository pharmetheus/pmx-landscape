1.3. Benefit-Risk Assessment (BRA)

Ben Risk Summary and Assessment

177Lu-PSMA-617 (PLUVICTO) is a beta-emitting small molecule radioligand therapeutic agent that targets prostate-specific membrane antigen
(PSMA)-expressing prostate cancer cells.

The efficacy of 177Lu-PSMA-617 was evaluated in VISION (NCT03511664), a randomized (2:1), multicenter, open-label trial that evaluated ?77Lu-
PSMA-617 plus BSoC (N= 551) or BSoC alone (N = 280) in men with progressive, PSMA-positive mCRPC. Randomization was stratified by
baseline lactase dehydrogenase (LDH), presence of liver metastases, ECOG PS score and inclusion of an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor as
part of BSoC at the time of randomization. All patients received a GnRH analog or had prior bilateral orchiectomy. Patients were required to
have received at least one AR pathway inhibitor, and 1 or 2 prior taxane-based chemotherapy regimens. Eligible patients were required to have
PSMA-positive mCRPC defined as having at least one tumor lesion with gallium Ga 68 gozetotide uptake greater than normal liver. Patients
were excluded if any lesions exceeding size criteria in short axis [organs > 1 cm, lymph nodes 2 2.5 cm, bones (soft tissue component) > 1 cm]
had uptake less than or equal to uptake in normal liver.

The alternative primary endpoints were OS and rPFS (by BICR per PCWG3 criteria). Either could be positive to satisfy primary endpoint. The
primary endpoint of OS was met, with patients randomized to receive 1”77Lu-PSMA-617 having prolonged OS (median estimate 15.3 months)
compared to BSC/BSoC (median estimate 11.3 months), HR 0.62 (95% Cl: 0.52, 0.74, p<0.001).The primary endpoint of BICR assessed rPFS was
also met, with patients randomized to receive 17”Lu-PSMA-617 plus BSC/BSoC having prolonged rPFS (median estimate 8.7 months) compared
to BSC/BSoC (median estimate 3.4 months), HR 0.40 (95% Cl: 0.31, 0.52, p<0.001). Key secondary endpoints, including a 30% ORR with median
duration of response of 10 months assessed by RECIST v1.1, further supported the efficacy of 17”7Lu-PSMA-617. Disproportionate dropout in the
BSoC arm compared to the !”7Lu-PSMA-617 arm was a key review issue and several sensitivity analyses in conjunction with the consistency
observed across several other efficacy endpoints supports the demonstrated efficacy of 177Lu-PSMA-617 in VISION. This is discussed further in
Section 8 of this review.
