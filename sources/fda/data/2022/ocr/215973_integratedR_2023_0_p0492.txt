Regarding possible correlation between viral load and development of nodules (e.g., number,
onset, duration, resolution, associated symptoms) we note that data is provided regarding the
association of nodules/indurations with CD4 count:

Response to IR of 10/12/2021 received 10/26/2021, p 13:

The odds ratio of the participants experiencing the AEs of injection site induration or nodule
(as compared to not experiencing) for low/normal CD4 count (vs high) was 1.18 (95% CI:
0.42 to 3.30) for Study GS-US-200-4334 and 0.54 (95% Cl: 0.20 to 1.47) for Study
GS-US-200-4625; the 95% CT in both studies contained 1, indicating no statistical
significance. Although the sample sizes are small for robust analyses, these data suggest that

there is no association between low/normal CD4 count and AEs of injection site induration or
nodule.
