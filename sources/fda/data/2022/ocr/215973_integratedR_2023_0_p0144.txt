The Division of Dermatology and Dentistry was previously consulted because injection site
reactions (ISRs) were the most common adverse reaction with lenacapavir subcutaneous injection.
Based on preclinical histology in rats, rabbits and dogs these reactions were observed to be
inflammatory granulomas. One of DAV’s concerns is that injection site nodules and indurations
can possibly last several weeks/months, and the longer-term safety data included in the
resubmissions may help to further describe and understand injection site reactions associated with
LEN.

DAV is requesting DDD’S review and assessment of the Applicant’s Cumulative Review of
Injection Site Reactions, including the Applicant’s assessment of high pH formulations and ISRs.
DAV requests any recommendations for communication of ISRs in labeling, specifically a
response to the Applicant’s rationale to
