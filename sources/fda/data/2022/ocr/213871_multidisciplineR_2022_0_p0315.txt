1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

In this submission, the applicant is seeking approval of abrocitinib |

The applicant proposes specific targeted clinical outcome assessment (COA)-related labeling
claims from three phase 3 clinical trials (Studies B7451012, B7451013, and B7451029):
e Studies B7451012 and B7451013 are multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-
controlled, parallel group studies.
e Study B7451029 is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, double-dummy, placebo-
controlled, parallel group study.

To support these claims, the applicant submitted a COA evidence dossier!. The primary objective
of this review is to evaluate from a COA perspective if the submitted information supports the
COA-telated labeling claims.

The ranked secondary efficacy patient-reported outcome (PRO) endpoints proposed for labeling
are:

e Proportion of patients achieving at least 4 points improvement in the severity of Peak
Pruritus numerical rating scale (PP-NRS) from baseline at Weeks 2, 4, and 12 (Studies
B7451012, B7451013, and B7451029). A copy of the instrument is in Appendix A.

e Change from baseline in Pruritus and Symptoms Assessment for Atopic Dermatitis
(PSAAD) total score at Week 12 (Studies B7451012 and B7451013). A copy of the
instrument is in Appendix B.

The exploratory efficacy PRO endpoints ©® are:

e Proportion of patients achieving at least 4 points improvement in the NTIS Severity item
(NTIS Severity) from baseline at all time points (Studies B7451013 and B7451029). A
copy of the instrument is in Appendix C.

e Time from baseline to achieve at least 4 points improvement in the NTIS Severity item
(Studies B7451013 and B7451029).

The data from Studies B7451012, B7451013, and B7451029 demonstrated that abrocitinib had
statistically significant improvement in the selected secondary efficacy endpoints compared with
placebo.

The subject of this review was originally focused on the PSAAD at the request of the Division.
However, the Division later sought input regarding the adequacy of data collection of the PP-
NRS and the NTIS ©® ‘As such, this review also includes general
comments on the PP-NRS and NTIS Severity instruments.
