The FDA’s Assessment: FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. FDA review also included
evaluation of prior therapies received in all enrolled patients. Patients with at least 1 prior
prostate cancer-related surgery (177Lu-PSMA-617: 96% vs. BSC only: 97%) or cancer-related
radiotherapy (1”7Lu-PSMA-617: 75% vs. BSC only: 78%) were balanced between arms.

All patients in VISION received at least one prior cancer-related systemic therapy. The most
frequent systemic therapies were therapeutics for 77% of patients and adjuvant therapies for
31% of patients. All patients received at least one prior AR inhibitor and taxane based

chemotherapy.
