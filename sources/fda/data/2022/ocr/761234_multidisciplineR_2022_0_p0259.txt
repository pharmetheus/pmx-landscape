Uncertainty and Variability Relatlimab PPK Analysis: Relatlimab parameters were estimated with Acceptable
(RSE, IIV, Shrinkage, adequate precision (Table 77). Relative high RSE for parameter estimates

Bootstrap) CLace, CLrace-aa, CLrace-asian, CLlmono were observed, mainly due to the

relative small magnitude of effects or small sample size. The shrinkage
values were around 20-30% except for ETA_EMAX (52.4%) and ETA_VP

Bootstrap was not conducted during the PPK analysis mainly due to the
long run time. The 95% Cl were from asymptotic standard error reported
by NONMEM.

Nivolumab PPK Analysis: Nivolumab parameters were precisely
estimated (Table 78) except for effects on CLraaa, CLotn, and EMAXcomso,
which was due to the small magnitude of effects and consequently the
uncertainties in the estimated effects are small despite the high RSE.
