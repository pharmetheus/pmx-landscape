Reviewer’s Comments: FDA conducted an independent analysis of laboratory abnormalities for
patients in Study 0417 noted in the table above. FDA analysis was consistent with the
Applicant’s assessment in the Clinical Study Report. Grade 1-2 transaminase elevations were
the most common laboratory abnormality seen on Study 0417, but it is notable that there were
very few grade 3-4 laboratory abnormalities on any kind. The above laboratory abnormalities
are described in the USPI for mirvetuximab and incorporated in the overall safety analysis for
the drug.
