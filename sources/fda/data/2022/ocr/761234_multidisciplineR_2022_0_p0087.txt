Opdualag™ (nivolumab and relatlimab-rmbw)

e In Study CA224020 and CA224047, relatlimab was administered as monotherapy or in
combination with nivolumab over 60 minutes infusion duration. Model simulations
suggest that there is no significant exposure difference of relatlimab between 30 and 60
mins infusion duration.
