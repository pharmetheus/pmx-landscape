7.6.3.3. Serious Adverse Events

Since the Week 26 interim analysis (original NDA submissions), six additional subjects
experienced the following treatment-emergent SAEs: COVID-19 (two subjects), genital herpes
simplex (one subject), pneumonia (one subject), pneumonia mycoplasmal, pancytopenia,
dehydration, and acute respiratory failure (one subject), and septic shock, renal impairment, and
shock (one subject). In addition, two subjects in Cohort 1 who had previously experienced
treatment-emergent SAEs, experienced additional SAEs of anal squamous cell carcinoma,
impaired healing, anal cancer (one subject), and angina pectoris (one subject).

In total, from both cohorts of Study 4625, 10 subjects (13.9% [6 subjects in Cohort 1; 4 subjects
in Cohort 2]) who received LEN experienced a treatment-emergent SAE. None of the SAEs was
assessed as related to LEN by the investigator. The only treatment-emergent SAE that was
reported for more than one subject was COVID-19 (two subjects [2.8%]). All SAEs from Study
