Reviewer’s comment(s):
The applicant also proposes tables and figures in the label.

The Division is considering abrocitinib 100mg and 200mg once daily (OD) for approval. The
data from Studies B7451012, B7451013, and B7451029 demonstrated that abrocitinib 100mg
and 200mg had nominally significant improvement in the PRO efficacy endpoints compared with
placebo.

This reviewer has the following recommendations for the PRO labeling claims:

Reference ID: 4816610

Due to the single administration of the PP-NRS at the clinic visit and the large amount of
missing data (e.g., Studies B7451012 and B7451013 had missing PP-NRS data ranging
Jrom 20% to 36% for the study treatment arms at Week 12), it is difficult to interpret the
data. As such, this reviewer does not recommend including data from the PP-NRS alone
in the label despite the acceptability of the instrument. Based on discussion with Clinical
and Biostatistics, they share the same concerns and indicate that these issues can lead to
unreliable treatment estimates. To provide supportive information on itch, the PSAAD
itch item may be explored in conjunction with the PP-NRS as it was administered daily
and does not have high missingness.

Regarding the PSAAD, there are no concerns regarding the reliability and the validity of
the instrument. However, it is unknown whether the magnitude of the statistically
significant treatment effect is clinically meaningful to patients due to limitations of the
external anchor used in the anchor-based analyses. See Section 5.4.6 for more details
regarding score interpretability of the PSAAD. We do note that the treatment arms
appear to experience change in every item compared to the placebo arm; the total score
does not seem to be overly influenced by specific items. Further, change from baseline in
the PSAAD total score showed separation between the treatment and placebo arm across
a range that likely includes a clinically meaningful change threshold for Studies
B7451012 and B7451013.

Regarding the NTIS, in general, an 11-point NRS is acceptable to evaluate itch severity
at its worst. However, the NTIS was not formally tested in the statistical hierarchy and
does not add any additional information regarding itch severity beyond the PP-NRS and
PSAAD item 1 (itch assessed daily with a recall of 24 hours) |
