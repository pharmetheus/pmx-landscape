Immunohistochemistry:

Study Title/Number: Evaluation of FRa in normal human eye FFPE samples by
immunohistochemistry/IMH28-018

Methods: Grade 1-2 eye adverse reactions (blurred vision, keratopathy, and dry eye) have been
observed in some of the patients treated with mirvetuximab soravtansine in the Phase 1 clinical
trial. To help understand whether these adverse reactions are target related, the Applicant
evaluated FRa expression in a panel of nine normal human eye fixed paraffin embedded (FFPE)
samples. Multiple eye compartments present across all these samples including cornea, limbus,
conjunctiva, iris, retina, sclera, choroid, optic nerve, and ciliary body were evaluated.

Results:

The results showed that FRa was not present in eight of the eye structures studied, including
cornea epithelia, limbal cells, conjunctiva, iris, retina epithelia, sclera, choroid, and optic nerve.
However, FRa positivity was found in the epithelial cells of ciliary body.
