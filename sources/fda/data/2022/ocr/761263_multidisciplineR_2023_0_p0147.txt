Vital Signs

Data:

No clinically relevant effects of mosunetuzumab on absolute or change from baseline values for
diastolic or systolic blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, weight, and
oxygen saturation were observed across the safety population as a whole.

The changes in vital signs for individual patients in the B11 RP2D cohort, B11 FL RP2D cohort,
and Group B were consistent with the reported AEs.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees that the observed changes in vital signs that are clinically relevant are in the
context of patients experiencing adverse events. The most relevant vital sign changes were in
the setting of CRS following mosunetuzumab administration and included pyrexia, hypoxia, and
hypotension. Of 86 patients in the B11 RP2D cohort that experienced CRS per ASTCT criteria,
pyrexia, hypoxia, and hypotension occurred in 100%, 23%, and 36%, respectively.
