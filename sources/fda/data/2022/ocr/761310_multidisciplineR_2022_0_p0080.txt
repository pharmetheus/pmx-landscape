Overall, the safety data from the dose escalation and expansion trials suggest that the modified
weekly dosing regimen may improve the safety of ELAHERE. As such, there is limited clinical
data collected at dose-levels and/or schedules other than the recommended dosage of 6 mg/kg
AIBW Q3W. Therefore, the dose escalation part of the study did not provide complete
information regarding the safety and efficacy properties of ELAHERE.

Is the proposed dosing regimen appropriate for the general patient population for which the
indication is being sought?

The data and information are insufficient to determine whether the proposed dosage of
mirvetuximab soravtansine is appropriate. As detailed above, the 6 mg/kg dose was selected
for the pivotal study evaluation based on the efficacy and safety data and the exposure-
response analysis for efficacy and safety from the phase 1 trial. The pivotal trial IMGN853-0417
was an open-label, single arm study of mirvetuximab soravtansine in adult patients platinum-
resistant high-grade serous epithelial ovarian, primary peritoneal, or fallopian tube cancer,
whose tumors express a high-level of FRa, and who have received prior bevacizumab
