Of note, exploratory covariates analysis showed that histology and age are significant

e Loading doses and base doses

1) Positive Exposure-Response relationships were observed for efficacy endpoints, such as
CR, OR, and PFS.

2) No Exposure-Response Trends were observed for safety endpoints, higher
mosunetuzumab exposure up to the proposed dose of 1/2/60/30 mg did not lead to
earlier onset time or higher incidence of any Grade 21 AEs, any Grade 23 AEs, Grade 23
Neutropenia, Grade 23 Infections and infestations or any Grade Neurological AEs.

3) Tumor size continued to decrease after shifting from loading dose to base dose.

See details on Exposure-Response relationships in section 20.4.5.

e¢ Treatment duration
1) Majority of CRs (50 out of 54 cases) were achieved within 8 cycles of treatment based
on the IRF assessment. Median duration of CR has not been reached.
2) Only 16 out of 90 efficacy evaluable patients received more than 8 cycles of treatment.
