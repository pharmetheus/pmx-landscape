FDA noted an error in the Applicant’s calculation of 30-day and 60-day mortality in Table 48
above. There were 13/153 (8%), not 18%, deaths in the first 30 days and 24/153 (16%), not 33%,
deaths in the first 60 days. The corrected incidences are included in the Table below, which
reflects deaths at the time of the 90-day safety update. These early mortality rates are comparable
to those seen with the currently approved IDH1-inhibitor ivosidenib (Ivosidenib multi-discipline
review/summary, 2018).
