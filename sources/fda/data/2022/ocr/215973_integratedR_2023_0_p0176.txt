Conclusion regarding non-clinical data:

The Applicant concludes that the available non-clinical data from local tolerance studies of ARV
agents presented above suggest that chronic granulomatous inflammation and foreign body
response at the injection site (SC or IM) occurs due to the drug depot of the product and is
independent of glass particulates.

This reviewer agrees that this is a reasonable conclusion.

According to the applicant, preliminary data from an ongoing Phase 1 Study GS-US-200-5710
(titled, “A Phase 1 Study in Healthy Participant to Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, and
Pharmacokinetics of Subcutaneous and Intramuscular Lenacapavir”) provides some indication of
the extent to which ISRs are independent of glass particulates. In this study, the LEN injection

interaction of LEN injection formulation with borosilicate glass vials because the formulations are
