The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s information in the table below.

The review of efficacy was primarily based on an analysis of Study GO29781, an open-label,
multicenter, single-arm, multi-cohort study that included a cohort of 90 patients with relapsed
or refractory follicular lymphoma.

The review of safety is primarily based on data from 218 patients with B-cell non-Hodgkin
lymphoma from Study GO29781 who received the dose and schedule of mosunetuzumab
(1/2/60/30 mg) intended for registration.

All major efficacy and safety analyses were reproduced or audited based on a data cutoff of
August 27, 2021. Statistical analyses by the reviewers were performed using R version 4.1.2
(2021-11-01, The R Foundation for Statistical Computing Platform) and JMP 8.0 (SAS Institute,
Inc., Cary, NC).
