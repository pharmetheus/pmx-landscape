BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761263, Lunsumio (Mosunetuzumab)}

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position and the summary of laboratory-shift results for
the B11 RP2D cohort. Cytopenias were common during treatment with mosunetuzumab. The
evaluation of laboratory data indicates that the frequency of all grade cytopenias and Grade 3-4
cytopenias were higher than those reported as a TEAEs. Based on the laboratory data in the
B11 RP2D cohort, Grade 3-4 neutropenia, anemia, and thrombocytopenia occurred in 38%,
19%, and 12% of patients, respectively. Additionally, Grade 4 neutropenia occurred in 19% and
Grade 4 thrombocytopenia occurred in 5% of patients. Based on the incidence and severity of
cytopenias, FDA has included a Warning and Precaution for cytopenias in the USPI. The table
below provides the rates of treatment emergent laboratory abnormalities in the B11 FL cohort
per laboratory shift evaluation.
