9. Product Quality

The Quality Assessment team completed an integrated assessment from the drug substance, drug
product, manufacturing, microbiology, biopharmaceutics and environmental disciplines. The
team determined that NDA 215794, for oral LEN tablet, was approvable from the product quality
perspective; however, NDA 215793, for LEN injection, was found to be deficient from a product
quality perspective. One of the proposed commercial vial containers (borosilicate) was not
compatible with the drug product solution. Degradation of the vial surface was evident from
microscopic imaging, and glass particles were found in the solution. Considering

» and concer for glass particle
contamination, approval of the proposed alternative aluminosilicate glass vial will require a
comprehensive validated study. Further, data supporting the container closure integrity testing
remained outstanding despite numerous information requests to the Applicant. The Quality
Assessment team also notified the OND review team about the atypical nature of this product,
including the lack of intrinsic release controls and the concern about a subcutaneous product with
a pH >9. Please refer to the integrated reviews for both NDAs, 215973 and 215974, completed
January 12, 2022, for additional details.

Although each discipline recommended approval for NDA 215794, both NDA 215793 and NDA
215794 will receive a Complete Response action. As stated above, deficiencies were identified in
the product quality information for the LEN injection NDA. The approval of the oral LEN NDA
is contingent on the approval of the LEN injection NDA; therefore, the LEN injection NDA
deficiencies must be adequately addressed before the oral LEN NDA can be approved.
