daridorexant PK. Based on these exposure-response analyses, which include treatment and
placebo groups, there was no apparent correlation between next-day daridorexant plasma
concentration and the change from baseline in efficacy endpoints assessed during the previous
night. If an exposure-response relationship exists for these endpoints, such a relationship is
expected to be “flatter” when using the next-day daridorexant plasma concentration (collected 9

PSG recording; Tmax ~2.09 h). However, considering the high PK variability (i-e., percent
