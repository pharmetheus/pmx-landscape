This trial design shows that there is no arm where active injected drug is compared with placebo
injected drug. There are two enrolled cohorts: Cohort 1, if subjects had a < 0.5 logio HIV-1 RNA
decline compared to the screening visit and Cohort 2, if subjects had a > 0.5 logig HIV-1 RNA
decline compared to the screening visit or after Cohort 1 reached its planned sample size.
Regarding Cohort 1, in the 14-day functional monotherapy period, subjects in Cohort 1 were
randomized in a 2:1 ratio in a blinded fashion to receive either drug product or placebo, while
continuing their failing regimen.

e Cohort 1A subjects received subcutaneous (SC) LEN on day 1 SC (14 days after 1st dose
of oral LEN) and once every 6 months (26 weeks). Also initiate their OBR

¢ Cohort 1B subjects received placebo for 14 days, then oral LEN for and initiate their OBR
on Day 15, then SC LEN at Day 1 SC (14 days after 1st dose of oral LEN and 28 days into
trial) and once every 6 months (26 weeks).

e Cohort 2: received oral LEN for 14 days, then SC LEN at Day 1 SC (14 days after 1st dose
of oral LEN) and once every 6 months (26 weeks).

on how to report injection site reactions.
