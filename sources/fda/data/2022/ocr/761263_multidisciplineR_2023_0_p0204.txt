BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761263, Lunsumio (Mosunetuzumab)}

Cytokine levels

Cytokines (IL-2, IL-6, IL-10, TNF-y, IFN-a) were measured in clinical studies GO29781. Only IL-6
and INFy were cytokines that were measured in all cohorts, including the Group A and B step-
up dosing cohorts. For TNF-a, IL-2 and IL-10, data are only available for Group A and cohort B11
in Group B.

The general profile of all cytokines showed a consistent pattern of acute elevation after initial
mosunetuzumab exposure in both Group A (fixed-dosing) and Group B (step-up-dosing)
followed by a return to baseline levels later within Cycle 1. In Group A, no changes from
baseline were observed in later cycles (Figure 26). In the step-up-dosing Group B, a first
cytokine peak was observed after the first step dose on Cycle 1 Day 1, and a second cytokine
peak was observed with the first loading dose on Cycle 1 Day 15. However, two peaks were not
found to be proportional to mosunetuzumab dose (Figure 27). Limited data are available in
later cycles. This repeat dose and time-related decrease observed within tested cytokines that
coincide with CRS occurrence reflect the outcome of effective CRS mitigation by the proposed
step-up dosing regimen of 1/2/60/30 mg.
