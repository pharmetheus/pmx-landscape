The most commonly reported TEAEs occurring at >1% in any study arm, included diarrhea,
dysgeusia, and vulvovaginal mycotic infection. Diarrhea was more commonly experienced in the
lansoprazole triple therapy group (9.6%) compared with the vonoprazan dual and triple therapy
groups (5.2% and 4.0%, respectively). Dysgeusia was reported more commonly in both the
vonoprazan and lansoprazole triple therapy groups (4.6% and 6.1%, respectively) compared with
the vonoprazan dual therapy group (0.6%), which would be expected as this event is frequently
associated with clarithromycin use. Vulvovaginal mycotic infection was more commonly
reported in the vonoprazan triple therapy group (3.2%) compared with the lansoprazole triple
therapy group (1.4%); the incidence in the vonoprazan dual therapy group was 2%. There were
cases of coronavirus infection which were deemed unrelated to the study drugs.
