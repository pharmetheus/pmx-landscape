AR inhibitors such as enzalutamide and abiraterone have demonstrated a survival advantage in
patients with metastatic hormone sensitive prostate cancer patients and in patients with
mCRPC both before and after receipt of docetaxel chemotherapy. However, there is a lack of
prospective data to assess their efficacy in a patient population that has received both a taxane
chemotherapy and another prior AR inhibitor, such as the population enrolled in VISION.

FDA conducted a sensitivity analysis to assess the impact of the receipt of concurrent ARPI on
the efficacy outcomes. The results showed that rPFS and OS benefit of adding 1””7Lu-PSMA-617
to BSC/BSoC was maintained even after excluding patients who received androgen pathway
receptor inhibitors.
