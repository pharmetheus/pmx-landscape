The occurrence of dose delays due to adverse events was relatively high, at 39%. The most
common adverse events leading to dosage delays included visual impairment (15%),
keratopathy (11%), neutropenia (6%), dry eye (6%), cataracts (3%), and GGT elevation (3%).
Dose reductions were less common than delays, and the most common reasons for dose
reductions were visual impairment (9%) and keratopathy (7%). Infusion interruptions were
characterized by events resulting in temporary interruption of an infusion, mainly due to
infusion-related adverse reactions. In these events, infusions could be restarted upon
improvement/ resolution of symptoms.

Reviewer comment: Specific guidance to clinicians regarding dose interruption and dose
reductions in the management of adverse events is described in Section 2 of product labeling,
and these are considered adequate for the safe use of mirvetuximab. Criteria for the
management of infusion reactions are also provided in Table 3 of product labeling.
