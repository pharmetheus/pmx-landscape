13.2.5.1. Impurities/Degradants in Vonoprazan

The qualification of specified and unspecified impurities within the vonoprazan drug substances,
and degradants in the vonoprazan drug product are described below. Overall, the proposed
specifications, or lack of specifications, are considered acceptable from a
pharmacology/toxicology perspective. This conclusion is based on the general toxicology
studies, Ames tests, and/or quantitative structure—activity relationship analysis.
