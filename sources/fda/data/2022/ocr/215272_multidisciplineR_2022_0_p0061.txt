Exploratory analyses demonstrated no relationship between Tapinarof exposure and
application area. When normalized by percent body surface area (%BSA) of disease, the
concentration range for Cmax demonstrated a wide range of values (Figure 1). Exploration of
individual patient PK profiles further support this lack of relationship. The subject with the
highest %BSA (46%) had mostly undetectable concentrations while another subject at the low
end of the %BSA range (22%) had a near-complete PK profile.
