To interpret study results on IDSIQ Sleep domain endpoint, anchor-based analyses using pooled
phase 3 trial data were requested. The requested anchor scales to be used in anchor-based
analyses include PGI-S and PGI-C nighttime symptoms as well as PGI-S and PGI-C daytime
symptoms. The derived thresholds of clinical meaningful within patient change in IDSIQ
Sleepiness domain score at Month 1/Visit 6 and Month 3/Visit 8 from both phase 3 trials were
summarized in Table 139 and Table 140.
