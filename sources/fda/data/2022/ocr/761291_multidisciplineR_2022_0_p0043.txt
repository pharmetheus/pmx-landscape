5.5 Toxicology

The nonclinical program for teclistamab was conducted in accordance with the ICH guidelines S9
and S6(R1), as well as S7A, S2(R1), M3(R2), S3A, and S5(R3). All nonclinical studies were
conducted in accordance with best scientific principles. Pivotal nonclinical studies were
conducted in conformance with GLP, 21 CFR, Part 58 and/or the principles of OECD-GLP in
countries that are part of the OECD Mutual Acceptance of Data process and include the
appropriate documentation. The cynomolgus monkey is considered the pharmacologically
relevant species for the toxicology program of teclistamab (see Section 5.3).
