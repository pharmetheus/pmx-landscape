NDA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation - NDA 215814
REZLIDHIA, olutasidenib

The Applicant’s Position:

The most common Grade 3 or Grade 4 events were primarily related to myelosuppression and
included febrile neutropenia (20%), decreased RBC count (19%), decreased platelet count
(16%), and decreased neutrophil count (13%) which are expected in patients with R/R AML.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA performed an independent assessment of Grade 3 or 4 AEs using data from the 90-day
safety update.

Based on the 90-day safety update, the most frequent causes of Grade 3 or 4 TEAEs remained
relatively consistent. In general, the most common causes of Grade 3 or 4 AEs reflect common
consequences of patients with R/R AML. However, transaminitis, leukocytosis, differentiation
syndrome, and hypertension were the most common AEs at least be possibly related to treatment
with olutasidenib. Hypokalemia also occurred but was better captured in the laboratory data (see
below).
