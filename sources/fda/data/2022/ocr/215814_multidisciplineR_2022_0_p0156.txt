REZLIDHIA, olutasidenib

As of 18 June 2020, 73 (48%) patients in Phase 2 Cohort 1 had died from any cause. Forty-five
(29%) patients died on treatment defined as death that occurred after the start of olutasidenib
through 28 days after the last dose of olutasidenib. A summary of TEAEs leading to on-treatment
death reported in Phase 2 Cohort 1 is presented in Table 49.
