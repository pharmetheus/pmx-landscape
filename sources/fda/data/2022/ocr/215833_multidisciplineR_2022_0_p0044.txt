Excretion

The evaluation was included in the biodistribution study in rats (see above tissue distribution)
1) There were no differences in the blood clearance with different doses of non-radiolabeled
PSMA-617 peptide co-injected with 177Lu-PSMA-617;
2) The half-life of the 177Lu-PSMA-617 in blood was 0.5 h;
3) More than 95 %ID was eliminated in the urine 1-day post-injection, independent of the co-
injected peptide amounts;
4) 177Lu-PSMA-617 was almost totally cleared from the organism one week after injection.
