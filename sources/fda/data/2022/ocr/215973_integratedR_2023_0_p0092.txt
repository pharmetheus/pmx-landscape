in Male New

Zealand White Rabbits with a 28- or 90-Day Observation Period (TX-200-2074

The effects of three formulations of LEN on local tolerance were evaluated in male rabbits via a
single intramuscular injection followed by a 28- or 90-day observation period. The vehicle
control formulations were as follows: |

There were no marked differences among the different vehicle formulations.

Injection site reactions of granulomatous inflammation and minimal to slight mineralization were
detected in all animals across doses and controls 28 days postdose (necropsy 1). Slight to
moderated myofiber necrosis and minimal to slight hemorrhage was detected at 400 mg/kg LEN.

At necropsy 2, granulomatous inflammation at dosing-site B was still detected but at a lower
incidence and severity compared to necropsy 1. Other histopathology findings detected at
necropsy 1 were no longer detected at necropsy 2.
