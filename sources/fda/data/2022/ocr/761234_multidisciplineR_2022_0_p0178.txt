NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761234}
Opdualag™ (nivolumab and relatlimab-rmbw)

According to the ADAE dataset, fatal/Grade 5 AEs had an attribution to study drug in 7 (2%)
patients on nivolumab-relatlimab FDC and 11 (3.1%) patients on nivolumab. AEs resulting in
death included terms such as “malignant neoplasm progression” and “other” which may
confound the assessment of AEs resulting in death (see Table 54). FDA reviewed each patient
narrative for death not related to disease progression in patients treated with nivolumab-
relatlimab FDC and summarized in Table 56
