Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 214958 Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) tablets, 6 mg

On March 7, 2018, an End-of-Phase 2 (EOP2) was held during which comments were provided
regarding secondary endpoints, stratification factors at randomization, criteria for re-
randomization, definition of relapse, power of the Phase 3 trials, and the comparison against
apremilast (NDA 205437, approved for the treatment of adult patients with plaque psoriasis
who are candidates for phototherapy or systemic therapy). Specifically, meeting minutes issued
on June 21, 2018 communicated the following regarding the proposed Phase 3 studies and
prioritization of sPGA for judging efficacy:

e Although the division considered the co-primary endpoints [the proportion of subjects who
achieve a 75% improvement in Psoriasis Area and Severity Index from baseline (PASI 75) to
Week 16 and the proportion of subjects who achieve a static Physician’s Global Assessment
(sPGA) score of 0 (clear) or 1 (minimal) at Week 16] acceptable, priority is given to success
based on the sPGA for judging efficacy. Therefore, testing for such endpoint should be given
priority.

e@ Inthe randomized withdrawal trial (IM011047), re-randomization of subjects at Week 24
who originally received deucravacitinib 6 mg QD was based on PASI 75 response. The
division recommend that re-randomization be based on the sPGA responders instead of
PASI 75 responders. The Agency noted that the preference for maintenance is to be based
on success of the PGA, as such an endpoint is the recommended endpoint for establishing
efficacy. The Agency added that while randomization can be carried out for subjects with
success on the PGA, the Applicant can randomize subjects who achieve success on PASI 75
but are not successful on PGA if they desire to meet the regulatory requirements of other
Agencies.

e Additionally, in Trial IM011047, relapse was defined as a > 50% increase in the PASI score
compared with that at Week 24. The division recommends the definition of relapse to be
based on the sPGA instead of PASI.

e Many key secondary endpoints, (4)

) may not be clinically meaningful and
therefore may not be included in the label. The division recommend that the number of key
secondary endpoints be intended for labeling be limited to those that are clinically
meaningful.

e For the key secondary endpoints based on patient reported outcomes (PROs), the
instruments need to be validated and a clinically meaningful threshold level for treatment
response be identified for each endpoint, along with justification for such level, as mere
change from baseline may not translate to clinically meaningful treatment effect.

e@ The division confirmed that superiority of deucravacitinib over apremilast based on the
endpoints used to establish efficacy of apremilast (i.e., sPGA score 0/1 and PASI 75 at Week
16) was reasonable and comparative efficacy information for systemic psoriasis products
could be a useful addition to product labeling although replication of findings would be
required.
