o Conclusion regarding the Applicant’s assessment of high pH formulations:
Review of the Applicant’s presented nonclinical data for LEN, clinical data for LEN and
clinical data of other approved ARVs does not identify a clear association between high
formulation pH and duration of nodules and indurations.

o DDD reiterates that in ongoing or future trials, where possible, injection site nodules be
followed to resolution or stabilization. In addition, DDD continues to recommend that
human histopathology be obtained (e.g., punch biopsy in a few subjects). The presence of
findings such as necrosis, hypersensitivity, granulomas, foreign bodies, fibrous tissue, etc.
can aid in understanding the pathophysiology of nodule formation. This in turn can
increase understanding of which treatments are most likely to be useful.

2. Assessment of the extent to which glass particulate contamination, including sub-visible
particles, may be associated with injection site nodules and indurations or other clinical effects.

o Information submitted (literature and nonclinical and clinical evidence from injectable
ARVs) indicates that ISRs, including nodules and induration, most likely result from the
presence of the drug depot rather than the potential presence of subvisible glass particulates
in the closure system with borosilicate glass vials.
