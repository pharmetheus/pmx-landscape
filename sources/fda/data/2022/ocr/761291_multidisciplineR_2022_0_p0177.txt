NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761291}
teclistamab

19.4.2 Population PK Analysis

19.4.2.1 Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The population PK analysis supports the proposed dose of 0.06 mg/kg SC, followed 2 to 4 days
later by 0.3 mg/kg SC, followed 2 to 4 days later by 1.5 mg/kg SC, followed by weekly
administration at 1.5 mg/kg SC thereafter. No clinically relevant differences in exposure according
to patient characteristics were identified.
