Section 6 Adverse Reactions

FDA modified this section to align with current labeling practice
for inclusion of adverse reactions observed in the clinical trial.
Included a pooled population for the safety information
described in section 5.

Section 7 Drug Interactions

FDA modified this section to add a heading for the effect of
Lunsumio on CYP450 substrates. Included the mechanism of
the DDI with CYP450 substrates. Revised to include information
about the period following CRS as a potential risk for DDI based
on the mechanism of the DDI.

Added recommendation for monitoring for toxicity or
concentrations of drugs used concomitantly with Lunsumio
that are CYP450 substrates where minimal concentration
changes might result in increased serious adverse reactions.

Section 8 Use in Specific
Populations

FDA modified these sections to align with current PLLR labeling
practice in sections 8.1-8.3, including adding recommendations
for pregnancy testing and contraception, as well as adding a
statement that Lunsumio may cause fetal harm when
administered to a pregnant woman.

Section 11 Description

FDA modified to add the EPC and additional information about
the drug substance and drug product, as well as USP/NF
monograph titles for the inactive ingredients.

Section 12 Clinical
Pharmacology

FDA revised section 12.2 pharmacodynamics to include
language to describe the B-cell depletion and cytokine
elevations and to add information on
hypogammaglobulinemia.

In section 12.3 pharmacokinetics, a table to describe PK
exposures (AUC, Cmax, Ctrough) in the first 2 cycles and at
steady state was added, as was a figure to show the
mosunetuzumab concentration-time profile up to steady state.
Per FDA guidance Immunogenicity Information in Human
Prescription Therapeutic Protein and Select Drug Product
Labeling, immunogenicity information moved to a new section
12.6.

Section 13 Nonclinical
Toxicology

FDA modified this section to align with current labeling
practice.
