Effect of Age

The inference of the effect of age on the PK of lutetium (17/Lu) vipivotide tetraxetan based on
population PK analysis should be interpreted with caution due to the limited data set.
Nonetheless, summary safety analysis of patients who received 1”7Lu-PSMA-617 in the VISION
trial did not show any trends in the incidence of ARs and dose modification and
discontinuations due to ARs with age (Table C).
