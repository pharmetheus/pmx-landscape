Bioanalysis

Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA), chemiluminescence was used to quantify nivolumab and relatlimab serum
concentrations. The presence of nivolumab at 300 pg/mL (or a ratio of 2000:1 [300:0.15 g/mL] of nivolumab: relatlimab)
in serum did not interfere with relatlimab quantitation. The presence of relatlimab at 200 pg/mL (or a ratio of 333:1 [200:0.6
pg/mL] of relatlimab :nivolumab) in serum did not interfere with nivolumab quantitation. In Study CA224020 and CA224047,
nivolumab concentrations were > 300 g/mL and relatlimab concentrations were > 200 g/mL in some patient samples;
however, the concentration ratios of nivolumab and relatlimab were within the limits that the presence of nivolumab or
relatlimab did not interfere with quantitation of nivolumab or relatlimab.
