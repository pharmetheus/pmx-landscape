Daridorexant (Quviviq) for Insomnia

Applicant’s PBPK Modeling Effort

PBPK Software

Simcyp V17 (Simcyp Ltd, UK) was used to develop the daridorexant PBPK models and predict
the PK of daridorexant with and without CYP3A modulators.

Model Development
Daridorexant

The absorption of daridorexant was modelled using the Advanced, Dissolution, Absorption, and
Metabolism (ADAM) model in Simcyp. A solid immediate release formulation |

model were selected. The effective permeability in human jejunum (Pes) (5.29 x 107 cm/s)
was predicted based on the measured apparent permeability (Papp) values in MDCK-II wild-type

permeability predictor). The mass balance study indicated almost complete absorption of
daridorexant from the gut, as evidenced by less than 1% of the oral dose excreted in feces as
unchanged drug (D-15.653).

h for the 5, 25 and 50-mg doses and 7.5 h and 8.8 h for the 100 and 200-mg doses, respectively.
The absolute bioavailability was 62%.

The full PBPK distribution model was used to describe the distribution of daridorexant.
Daridorexant was highly bound to plasma proteins with fraction unbound in plasma of 0.003.
The volume of distribution at steady state (0.41 L/kg) was estimated using the Method 3 in
Simcyp and adjusted based on the clinical PK data (D-15.653) by applying a Ky scalar of 0.53.
Perfusion-limited distribution was assumed for all organs.
