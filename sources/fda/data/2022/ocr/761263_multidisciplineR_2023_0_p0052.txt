Clinical Signs

Body Weights

females (-5.6 to -16%) at 0.5 mg/kg.

Food Consumption

Poor food consumption was noted on Study Days 2 and 3 for all test
article-dosed animals and on Days 12 and 13 for females at 0.5 mg/kg.
