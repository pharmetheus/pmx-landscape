Recommended
Indications/Populations

XENOVIEW is a hyperpolarized xenon-129 contrast agent
indicated for inhalational use with magnetic resonance imaging
(MRI) for preoperative evaluation of lung ventilation prior to
lung surgery in adults and pediatric patients aged 12 years and
older. (Labeling review is ongoing.)

Recommended Dosing
Regimen

Dose equivalent (DE) of 75 mL to 100 mL (both adults and
children aged 12 years and up) of hyperpolarized Xe-129 in 250
mL to 750 mL of xenon gas filled to 1 L with nitrogen gas,
inhaled with breath hold of up to 15 seconds
