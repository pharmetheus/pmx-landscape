Question for DDD

e “Considering the length of time for resolution of injection site nodules and indurations with SC
LEN injections, and with particular concern for individuals with low body fat percentage, does
DDD recommend that additional clinical safety information be submitted regarding these
reactions? In addition, we request any recommendations for communication of ISRs in
labeling.”

DDD Response:

Reference ID: 4911738

DAV has proposed the following edits to section 6 of labeling for lenacapavir (EN):

DDD agrees with DAV proposed edits to labeling for LEN in Section 6. DDD also agrees
that inclusion of the adverse reaction of injection site nodules in the Warnings and
Precautions section of labeling is not required at this time. In addition, a Med Guide is not
needed at this time (see discussion below).
