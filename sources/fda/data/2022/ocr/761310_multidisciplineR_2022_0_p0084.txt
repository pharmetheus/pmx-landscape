Collectively, given the limitations noted above regarding the clinical data at alternative dosage
schedules, additional clinical safety and efficacy information on mirvetuximab soravtansine is
needed to determine a dosing regimen and schedule that may minimize the risk of ocular
toxicity without a clinically significant impact on efficacy. The Applicant will be asked to conduct
a post-marketing study to evaluate the alternative dosing schedules.

Is an alternative dosing regimen or management strategy required for subpopulations based
on intrinsic patient factors?

No, an alternative dosing regimen and management strategy is not required based on age
and BW. Refer to Section 19.4 for a detailed discussion.

Renal Impairment

mirvetuximab soravtansine. The effect of mild and moderate renal impairment on
