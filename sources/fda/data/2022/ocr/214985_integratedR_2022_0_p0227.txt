At the scheduled necropsy, all main study animals were evaluated for gross pathology, organ

Toxicokinetics

On Days 1 and 28 at 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, and 24 hours postdose, blood samples were collected from TK
animals. The TK profile of ACT-699581 were determined.

After oral administration, ACT-699581 was quickly absorbed with Tmax values of 1 to 2 hours.
The exposure to ACT-699581 increased greater than dose proportionally. Repeat administration
led to slightly increased drug exposure, with the Day28-to-Day 1 ratio of 1.3 to 1.6. Females had
higher exposure compared to males with the female-to-male ratio of ~4.
