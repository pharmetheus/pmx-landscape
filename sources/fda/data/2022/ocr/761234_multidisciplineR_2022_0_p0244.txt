Method description

This ELISA technology utilizes a chemiluminescent substrate that reacts
with horseradish peroxidase conjugate to generate a luminescent signal
that is proportional to the concentration of BMS-986016 in the serum.
Briefly, this method uses a monoclonal antibody specific for BMS-
986016 as the capture antibody, and uses BMS-986016 as the reference
standard. The standards, quality controls, and validation samples in
neat human serum are diluted to the minimum required dilution (MRD)
of 50 (i.e., 2% human serum) and then added to the ELISA plate. The
captured BMS-986016 is detected using a biotinylated monoclonal
antibody specific for BMS-986016, followed by NeutrAvidin conjugated
with horseradish peroxidase (NeutrAvidin-HRP). A luminol-based
substrate specific for peroxidase is added and the resulting
chemiluminescence is measured on a microplate reader.
