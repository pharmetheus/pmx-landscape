Reviewer comment: The majority of AEs were Grade 1-2, with gastrointestinal disorders being
the most common category of AEs. Ocular AEs were also common, which was consistent with
the eye being the key target organ for mirvetuximab-related toxicities. Thirty-four patients
(32%) had peripheral neuropathy with the majority being Grade 1-2 in severity (26%). In
addition, 28 patients (26%) who experienced peripheral neuropathy did not have resolution of
this toxicity. Five patients had infusion-related reactions and 14 patients had hypersensitivity
reactions with the distinction being the timing of the symptoms being at the time of the infusion
for infusion-related reactions and within 3 days of infusion for hypersensitivity reactions. The 5
patients noted to have hypersensitivity also had infusion reactions and 3 patients with
hypersensitivity were noted to have pneumonitis, which the Applicant considered to possibly
related to each other.
