BLQ for Parameter Accuracy

Since 0.72% postdose PK records were BLQ,
exclusion of the postdose BLQ PK
observations should not result in
appreciable bias in the parameter estimates
of the model because of the very few
samples with BLQ values (Beal 2001, Ahn
2008). Sensitivity analysis using the
likelihood based (M3) method was not
deemed necessary and was not performed,
as specified in the Analysis Plan.
