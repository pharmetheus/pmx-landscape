adjudicate these events to be adverse events of special interest. The Sponsor will continue
to require neurological examinations in the phase 3 studies. The Division agreed with the

The Agency stated that the Sponsor should prespecify thresholds for clinically meaningful
improvement in scores for each prespecified endpoint.

The Agency provided additional comments regarding the Sleep Diary Questionnaire. The
Agency had concerns regarding use of the subjective total sleep time as the first-ranked
secondary endpoint, in addition to the objective polysomnography (PSG) endpoint. The
Agency recommended that the Sponsor re-examine their qualitative data to determine
what is most important to patients in terms of improvement from treatment (e.g., latency
to sleep, total time awake, number of awakenings, etc.). The Sponsor indicated that they
selected the subjective total sleep time as the key secondary endpoint because it was
sensitive to treatment in phase 2 studies and the IDSIQ had not been used in phase 2/3
studies yet. The Sponsor agreed to provide a summary report in support of their sleep
diary endpoint(s) as a separate submission for Agency review and comment.
