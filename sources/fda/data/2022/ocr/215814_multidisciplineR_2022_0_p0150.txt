Please note that these numbers reflect the data from the 90-day safety update. For patients in
Cohort 1, the median duration of treatment was 4.7 months. 52 patients (35%) received treatment
for at least 6 months and 32 patients (21%) received treatment for at least 12 months. The
median duration of treatment and percentage of patients receiving treatment for at least 6 months
is similar to what was seen with ivosidenib in the R/R AML setting (Ivosidenib multi-discipline
review/summary, 2018).
