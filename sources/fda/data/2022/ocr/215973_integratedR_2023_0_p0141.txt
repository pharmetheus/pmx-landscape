DDD Consult #2133: Lenacapavir (NDA 215973, NDA 215974)

Lenacapavir (LEN) is currently under review as an NME under two NDAs, 215973
[injectable (SC) ra and 215974 (tablets). The proposed indication by
the Applicant is a]

The Division of Dermatology and Dentistry was previously consulted because
injection site reactions (ISRs) were the most common adverse reaction with
lenacapavir subcutaneous injection. Based on preclinical histology in rats, rabbits
and dogs these reactions were observed to be inflammatory granulomas. One of
DAV’s concems was that injection site nodules and indurations can possibly last
several weeks/months, and the longer-term safety data included in the
resubmissions may help to further describe and understand injection site reactions
associated with LEN.

DAV is requesting DDD’S review and assessment of the Applicant’s Cumulative
Review of Injection Site Reactions, including the Applicant’s assessment of high
pH formulations and ISRs. DAV requests any recommendations for communication
of ISRs in labeling.
