BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761263, Lunsumio (Mosunetuzumab)}

The frequency of adverse events by category reported during initial treatment with
mosunetuzumab IV monotherapy in Group B was generally similar between patients aged
18-65 years (N=246) and those aged >65 years (N=168), male (N=269) and female (N=145)
patients. The safety analysis by race subgroup was conducted on White (N=320) and Asian
(N=67) patients only, as the numbers of patients in other racial groups were too small to draw
any meaningful conclusions. AEs for the majority of AE categories were reported in a higher
proportion of patients in the White subgroup when compared to the Asian subgroup; however,
the difference in the sample sizes of these subgroups could potentially be a reason for the
observed imbalances. Of note, no Grade 5 PD was reported among these 67 Asian patients at
the time of CCOD.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA’s safety analysis based on age and sex is summarized in the tables below. Safety
analysis based on race subgroups was not completed due to the small numbers of racial and
ethnic minority patients enrolled in the safety population for whom conclusions about safety
and effectiveness could not be drawn. The numbers of patients older than 75 years of age were
also too small to conduct a meaningful analysis. The limited enrollment of older patients and
racial and ethnic minorities is a significant limitation. To address this, a PMR will be issued to
verify the clinical benefit of mosunetuzumab in a randomized trial in patients with relapsed or
refractory follicular lymphoma and the trial should enroll sufficient numbers of racial and ethnic
minority patients to reflect the racial and ethnic diversity of the U.S. patient population and
allow for interpretation of the results in these patients.
