The FDA’s Assessment:
In general, the FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. The safety data support that
mosunetuzumab demonstrated a tolerable safety profile in patients with relapsed or refractory
B-cell NHL, including follicular lymphoma. However, based on the FDA’s evaluation of the data,
the identified risks of neurotoxicity, including ICANS, and cytopenias are warranted to include

9.1. Statistical Issues

The FDA’s Assessment:

Complete response rate is pre-specified as the primary endpoint in the protocol and SAP;
however, FDA considers that the efficacy is established on the basis of ORR and duration of
response as assessed by an independent review facility according to standard criteria for NHL
(Cheson 2007). For the B11 FL RP2D expansion cohort, the ORR rate is 80% (95% Cl: 70.3%,
87.7%) and median DOR is 22.8 months (95% Cl: 9.7, not estimable) based on a CCOD of 15
March 2021.
