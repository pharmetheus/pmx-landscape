NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761234}
Opdualag™ (nivolumab and relatlimab-rmbw)

Elimination:
Terminal half-
life and CL

Nivolumab CL decreases over time, with a mean maximal reduction from baseline (CV%) of 21.1% (16.3%) [geometric mean
CL (CV%), 7.6 ml/h (40.1%) at steady state and 9.6 mL/h (40.3%) after the first dose] and the terminal t1/2 (CV%) is 26.5
days (36.4%).

Relatlimab CL decreases over time, with a mean maximal reduction from baseline (CV%) of 9.7% (26.2%) [geometric mean
CL (CV%), 5.5 mL/h (41.3%) at steady state and 6.1 mL/h (38.9%) after the first dose]. Approximately 97% of the maximum
steady-state concentration is predicted to be eliminated by 68.6 days.

Immunogenicity

Bridging ligand-binding electrochemiluminescence immunoassay was used to quantify ADAs against nivolumab and
relatlimab.

Assays for nivolumab ADA and NAb are drug tolerant up to 800 yg/mL and 400 pg/mL of nivolumab concentrations in
samples, respectively. The presence of relatlimab at 200 g/mL in serum did not interfere with the detection of the
nivolumab ADA and NAb. In Study CA224047 and CA224020, nivolumab trough concentrations > 800 ug/mL were not
observed in one sample analyzed. In Study CA224047, no relatlimab trough concentrations > 200 pg/mL were observed in
1068 analyzed pre-dose samples. In Study CA224020, relatlimab trough concentration > 200 pg/mL in 18 samples among
5899 analyzed pre-dose samples.

Assays for relatlimab ADA and NAb are drug tolerant up to 100 pg/mL of relatlimab concentrations in samples. The presence
of nivolumab at 300 pg/mL and 200 pg/mL in serum did not interfere with the detection of relatlimab ADA and NAb
respectively. In Study CA224047, arelatlimab trough concentration > 100 g/mL was observed in one sample (135 pg/mL).
In Study CA224020, a relatlimab trough concentration > 100 pg/mL was observed in 56 samples. Nivolumab trough
concentration was > 300 ug/ml in one sample in Study CA224047 (418 pg/mL) and Study CA224020 (304 pg/mL),
respectively.

In Study CA224047, following FDC and nivolumab monotherapy administration, the incidence of treatment emergent ADAs
against nivolumab and relatlimab are low and are lower than ADA+ rates reported in nivolumab USPI.
