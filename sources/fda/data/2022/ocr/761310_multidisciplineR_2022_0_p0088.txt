To assess the appropriateness of the FRa cutoff utilized by the Applicant to determine FRa High
(275%) in 0403 trial and subsequent 0417 trial, the reviewer conducted exploratory analyses
utilizing a more stringent 90% cutoff and assessing the relationship to efficacy within these
trials. For trial 0403, the ORR in patients receiving MIRV was similar across FRa expression
levels but was numerically higher in patients taking MIRV with 2 90% FRa expression (28%)
compared to the ITT (22%), FRa High (24%), and FRa >50% but <75% (20%) populations. In
trial 0417, patients taking MIRV with FRa expression 290% had slightly higher ORR (36%) than
the FRa High patients (32%). However, it should be noted, that while the ORR was slightly
higher utilizing the FRa = 90% threshold, the number of patients that met this criterion was
approximately 50% of the patients meeting the FRa > 75% cutoff (Table 12). As such, it appears
that the FRa = 75% cutoff utilized by the Applicant for enrollment in the 0417 trial and for
stratification into FRa High in trial 0403 identifies a population of patients who are responsive
to MIRV without excluding a large number of patients who may benefit.
