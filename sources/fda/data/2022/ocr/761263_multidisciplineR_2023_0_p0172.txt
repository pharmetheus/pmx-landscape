8.2.8. | Additional Safety Explorations

Human Carcinogenicity or Tumor Development
The Applicant's Po: n:

In accordance with the International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) guidelines ICH S9 (2009),
no carcinogenicity studies have been conducted because mosunetuzumab is intended to treat
patients with advanced cancer.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. FDA notes that no carcinogenicity studies were
conducted as mosunetuzumab is a therapeutic bispecific antibody being developed for
oncology indications. The FDA Nonclinical Review Team agreed that carcinogenicity studies are
not needed to support the approval of this BLA.

Human Reproduction and Pregnancy

The Applicant's Position:
No pregnancies were reported during the GO29781 study.
