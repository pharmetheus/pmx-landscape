(6) November 16, 2017 End of Phase 2 Type B meeting held (minutes December 14, 2017)
to gain agreement on the following:

Nonclinical program to support the NDA at a maximum human dose of 50 mg

With respect to drug discrimination studies, FDA indicated that the Sponsor should make
a good faith effort to train the animals to suvorexant and would like to see the data if the
Sponsor believes it is not possible to do so. If it is not possible to train the animals to
discriminate suvorexant from vehicle, the Sponsor can train the animals to a

M4 as suggested in the original protocol. FDA also indicated that that the
Sponsor should conduct a dose response curve to inform their training dose.

Clinical pharmacology program to support an NDA for daridorexant in insomnia disorder

With respect to the clinical pharmacology program, FDA provided advice on
pharmacokinetic (PK) studies, which was not further discussed at the meeting, indicated
the need for a thorough QT study, which the Sponsor agreed to conduct, discussed their
driving study plan, including 50% target enrollment of elderly subjects.
