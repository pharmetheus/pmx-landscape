Conclusions and Reasons

@ Ovarian cancer, including fallopian tube and primary peritoneal
cancer, is the fifth leading cause of cancer-related death for women
in the United States.

e There are 19,880 new cases of ovarian cancer and 12,810 deaths from
ovarian cancer estimated in the US for 2022.

¢ Ovarian cancer is often diagnosed at an advanced stage and has only
a 49% five-year overall survival.

e Almost all patients with advanced ovarian cancer develop platinum-
resistant disease which is incurable and has a poor prognosis.

Platinum-resistant ovarian cancer is incurable
and is a serious and life-threatening
condition.

¢ Platinum-resistant ovarian cancer is not curable and treatment is
palliative.

e Treatment options are limited and include single agent
chemotherapies associated with relatively low ORRs and short DORs.
From contemporary data, ORRs for available therapies are 12-13%
with median DORs of 4-7.4 months.

e There are no treatments approved specifically for patients with FRa-
positive, platinum-resistant ovarian cancer.

There is an unmet medical need for
treatments that improve outcomes for
patients with platinum-resistant ovarian
cancer, including for patients with FRa-
positive platinum-resistant ovarian cancer.

@ In Study 0417, there were 104 patients with FRa-positive, platinum-
resistant ovarian cancer who had received 1-3 prior lines of systemic
treatment, including 1 line containing bevacizumab, in the efficacy
evaluable population.

¢ Confirmed ORR as assessed by investigator according to RECIST v1.1
was 31.7% (95% Cl: 22.9, 41.6).

e Median DOR was 6.9 months (95% Cl: 5.6, 9.7).
