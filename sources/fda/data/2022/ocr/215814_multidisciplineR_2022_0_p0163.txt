The Applicant’s Position:
Olutasidenib was well-tolerated in R/R AML treated in pivotal Phase 2 Cohort 1 with an AE

profile which was manageable through dose modifications and supportive care. The majority of
patients who experienced AEs leading to treatment discontinuation had AEs which were assessed
as not related to olutasidenib and included disease progression (13%), pneumonia and febrile
neutropenia (2% each). Of the 21 patients with AEs of Differentiation syndrome (DS), only 3
patients discontinued treatment, indicating that DS is manageable with early recognition and
appropriate medical management.
