6.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

Mosunetuzumab is an anti-CD20/CD3 T-cell-dependent bispecific (TDB) antibody targeting both
CD3 (on the surface of T cells) and CD20 (on the surface of B cells). The Applicant is seeking
approval of mosunetuzumab for the treatment of patients with relapsed or refractory (R/R)
follicular lymphoma (FL) who have received at least two prior systemic therapies. The proposed
dosing regimen is shown in Table 9 below (hereafter referred to as 1/2/60/30 mg). The
proposed treatment duration is 8 cycles initially; if patients received PR or SD, continue
treatment for up to 1 year (17 cycles).
