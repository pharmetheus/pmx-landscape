Immunogenicity
Deucravacitinib is a small molecule which is not expected to be associated with
immunogenicity. Therefore, the Applicant did not assess the potential for antibody formation.

8.2.5. Analysis of Submission-Specific Safety Issues

Some AEs were considered to be areas of safety interest due to deucravacitinib’s understood
inhibition of signaling downstream of Type | IFNs, IL-12, and the IL-23/IL-17 pathway; unknown
mechanisms for the toxicities identified with other products targeting the JAK/STAT pathway;
observed signals during nonclinical and Phase 2 studies, as well as known comorbidities
associated with psoriasis.
