Simulation for Specific
Population

The ORR model based on
mirvetuximab soravtansine AUCO-d21,
and the ocular toxicity model based on
mirvetuximab soravtansine AUCO-d21
were used to predict the ORR response
rate and ocular AE incidence rate over
a wide range of exposures. Aligned
with the overlaid efficacy and safety ER
curves were the AUCO-d21 ranges of
mirvetuximab soravtansine for doses
from 3.3 mg/kg to 7 mg/kg TBW,
including 6 mg/kg AIBW, that were
administered in the three clinical
studies. In addition, simulated AUCO-
d21 range following 6 mg/kg AIBW and
6 mg/kg TBW dosing (n=I000 subjects)
is illustrated.
