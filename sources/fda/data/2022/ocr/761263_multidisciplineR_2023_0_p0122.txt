Relevant characteristics of the safety population:

Data:

The demographics of the primary safety population (B11 RP2D cohort, n=218) was similar to
the primary efficacy population (B11 FL RP2D, n=90; see Table 19) with respect to gender
(66.5% male) and race (82.1% White) while the median age (64.0 years) and proportion of
patients >65 years (43.1%) was slightly higher.

Patients with R/R NHL of the following histologies were represented in the B11 RP2D cohort: FL:
41.3%; DLBCL/trFL: 40.4%; MCL: 11.5%; Richter’s transformation: 6.4%; and other histologies:
0.5%. A higher proportion of patients in the B11 RP2D cohort compared to the B11 FL RP2D
cohort had ECOG PS 1 (54.1% vs. 41.1%), Stage IV disease at study entry (60.8% vs. 48.9%), and
had received prior CAR-T therapy (13.8% vs. 3.3%), which may reflect the approval status of this
therapeutic option at the time.

The Applicant's Position:
Overall, patients with R/R NHL in the primary safety population had similar demographic and

baseline characteristics as patients with R/R FL in the primary efficacy population (and expected
target population). The noted differences may be attributed to the more aggressive nature of
DLBCL/trFL, which was represented in 40.4% of patients in the B11 RP2D cohort.
