Subgroup Analysis by Sex

The mITT-C population in the ASCEND study included 13 male and 18 female subjects. Table
14 provides the MMRM analysis for the DLco endpoint at Week 52 for the male and female
subjects separately. In both the male and female cohorts, percentage and absolute change show
statistically significant improvement for patients treated with olipudase alfa over placebo. The
data show no marked difference in effect size between men and women. Of note, several

subgroups have very small sample sizes (n=4 women received olipudase alfa and had data at
Week 52; n=4 men received placebo and had data at Week 52).
