Table 3. An overview of validated bioanalytical methods for teplizumab

METHOD 1: Chemiluminescent enzyme immunoassay (Protégé and Encore studies)

Serum concentrations of teplizumab were determined using the validated
chemiluminescent assay (ELISA) with a calibration range of 0.24- 15.6 ng/mL and supporting
up to 1:1000 dilutional linearity. The working assay range for samples diluted 1:1000 is
244.2 to 15625 ng/mL.

e The assay was validated for precision, accuracy, sensitivity, dilutional recovery, cold storage
stability and interference from hemolysis, bilirubin and lipemia. All standards assayed during
validation were within their respective target ranges and demonstrated < 15% of deviation
from the nominal concentrations (< 20% for the LLOQ).
