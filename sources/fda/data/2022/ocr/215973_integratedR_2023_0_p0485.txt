Study Endpoints:

The primary efficacy endpoint was the proportion of subjects in Cohort 1 achieving > 0.5 logio
copies/mL reduction from baseline in HIV-1 RNA at the end of the functional monotherapy

period.

The secondary efficacy endpoint was the proportion of participants in Cohort 1 with plasma HIV-1
RNA < 50 copies/mL and < 200 copies/mL at Weeks 26 and 52 of treatment
based on the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA)-defined snapshot algorithm
