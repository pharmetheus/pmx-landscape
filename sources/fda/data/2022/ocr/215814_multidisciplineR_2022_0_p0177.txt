The Applicant’s Position:
The incidence of baseline Grade 3 and 4 hematological parameters and on treatment shifts to

Grade 3 or 4 values is expected for the RR AML patient population evaluated in Phase 2 Cohort
1. The most common Grade 3 or 4 chemistry shifts occurred for ALT (19 [12%]), AST (12

[8%]). As of protocol version 6, on-treatment increases of Grade 2 or higher elevations in ALT,
AST, or total bilirubin in patients were reported as AESIs which are further described in Section
