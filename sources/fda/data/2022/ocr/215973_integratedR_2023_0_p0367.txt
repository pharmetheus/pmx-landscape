13.2.5. Carcinogenicity

The carcinogenicity potential of LEN is ongoing in a 26-week study (single subcutaneous
injection given once every 13 weeks for a total of two doses) in 001178-T (hemizygous) RasH2
mice and a 2-year study (single subcutaneous injection given once every 13 weeks for a total of
eight doses) in Wister Hanover rats. These studies are ongoing at the time of NDA submission
and will be part of a postmarketing requirement (PMR).
