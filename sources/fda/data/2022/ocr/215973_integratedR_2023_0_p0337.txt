Absorption

In Vitro

e The bidirectional permeability of GS-6207 was investigated using monolayers of the

In Vivo

Oral single dose absorption studies were conducted in Sprague Dawley rats (5 mg/kg), Beagle
dogs (4 mg/kg), and Cynomolgus and Rhesus monkeys (4 mg/kg) following oral administration
of GS-6207.

e The time to maximum concentration (Tmax) ranged from 8.7 to 18.7 across species.

e An exposure (area under the concentration-time curve [AUCnr]) was not determined for
Cynomolgus monkeys. However the exposure in Rhesus monkeys was 1133nMeh. In rats
and dogs, the exposure was 25,200 and 16,533nMeh, respectively. In addition, the Cmax was
336, 471, 17.7, and 29.4nM in rats, dogs, Cynomolgus and Rhesus monkeys, respectively.
