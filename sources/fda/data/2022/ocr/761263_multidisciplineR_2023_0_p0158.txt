8.2.4.5 Hemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis

EDA Assessment:

Hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis was identified as an adverse event of special interest by
the Applicant for mosunetuzumab and was specifically monitored and evaluated in Study
GO29781. There were 4 identified cases of HLH in the overall safety population. One case
(0.4%, 1/218) occurred in the B11 RP2D cohort and the 3 other cases occurred in patients
receiving different doses or regimens of mosunetuzumab monotherapy. A brief summary of the
cases is provided below.

1. B11 RP2D Cohort (1/2/60/30 mg): A 74 year old White male with diffuse large B-cell
lymphoma who received the mosunetuzumab step up doses of 1 mg on Cycle 1 Day 1
and 2mg on Cycle 1 Day 8. The patients developed Grade 4 hemophagocytic
lymphohistiocytosis (HLH) on Day 8. Day 10 soluble IL2 receptor serum level was 20,000
pg/mL. By Day 13, the patient was reported to show “clinical disease progression,
worsening of encephalopathy, and multiorgan dysfunction,” which appeared consistent
with a clinical presentation of HLH. Mosunetuzumab treatment was discontinued due to
