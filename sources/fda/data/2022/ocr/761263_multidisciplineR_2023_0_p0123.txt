BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761263, Lunsumio (Mosunetuzumab)}

The FDA’s Assessment:

The patient characteristics for patients in the B11 FL cohort and the B11 RP2D cohort are shown
in the table below. In the 218 patients in the B11 RP2D cohort, the median age was 64, 67%
were male, the majority of patients were White at 82%, and all patients had an ECOG status of
0-1. In the 90 patients in the B11 FL cohort, the median age was younger at age 60, 61% were
male, 82% were White, and all patients had an ECOG status of 0-1 The median number of prior
therapies was 3 in both cohorts. Of note, 38% of patients in the FL cohort had received 2 prior
therapies. Fifty-two percent of patients in the FL cohort had progression of disease within 24
months (POD 24) disease.

Of note, there was limited representation regarding race and ethnicity with 3% to 4% Black or
African American and 5% to 8% Hispanic or Latino. In addition, the median age in the FL cohort
was 60. Despite this cohort consisting of patients with relapsed or refractory disease, the
population was younger than the median age of initial diagnosis in the U.S. at age 63 years
(SEER Cancer Statistics 2020).
