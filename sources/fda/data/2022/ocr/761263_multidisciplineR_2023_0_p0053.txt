Immunotoxicology

B-Cell Activating Factor (BAFF) Analysis: Serum concentrations of BAFF
were elevated ( > 5 times) in animals administered 0.2/0.8/0.1 and
0.2/0.8/0.5 mg/kg when compared with pre-treatment and control
animal results. Highest concentrations were observed post-dose on Study
Days 8 and 29 and appeared less elevated as the study progressed.

Cytokines: Mild to marked transient increases in serum concentrations
of cytokines including IFNy, TNFa, IL-17A, IL-2, MCP-1, IL-5, IL-6, MIP-1B,
IL-10 and CXCL8/IL-8 were observed after the first dose (0.2 mg/kg),
peaking at 2 to 6 hours. Minimal to mild increases or no changes were
present following the second dose (0.8 mg/kg) on Study Day 2 or
subsequent doses.
