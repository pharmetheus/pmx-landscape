3.2 General Pharmacology and Pharmacokinetic Characteristics

Pharmacology

Mechanism of The mechanism by which AMX0035 exerts its therapeutic effects in treating
Action ALS is unknown.
Active Moieties PB and TUDCA are considered as the active moieties. The pharmacological

activity of PAA, UDCA, and GUDCA, as the major metabolites of PB and
TUDCA, was not evaluated and is unknown.

QT Prolongation No large mean increases (i.e., >20 msec) in the QTcF interval was detected in
this QT assessment of AMX0035 at the therapeutic dose. Potential effects of
parent drugs and metabolites on QTc at higher exposure are unknown
because high clinical exposure scenario was not identified in the clinical
program (See QT-IRT Review dated 02/22/2022 ).

General Information
Bioanalysis The concentrations of PB, PAA, TUDCA, UDCA, and GUDCA in human plasma
were measured using two validated LC-MS/MS methods. Approximately 50%
samples from the Phase 2 CENTAUR study were stored beyond established
long-term stability duration for PB, PAA, and TUDCA. Therefore, only the
results for PB, PAA, and TUDCA from samples analyzed within the established
stability duration were considered reliable and used in subsequent data
analysis. Please refer to Section 4.1 for details.

Healthy Volunteers For PB and PAA, there was no significant difference in PK between ALS

vs. Patients patients and healthy subjects.

A PPK model for TUDCA and its metabolites was not established. Thus, a
comparison between ALS patients and healthy subjects is not established.
Dose Proportionality | Dose proportionality was not evaluated.

Accumulation For PB and its metabolite PAA, no accumulation is expected after repeated
once (QD) or twice daily (BID) dosing.

For TUDCA and its metabolites, the accumulation is unknown for QD or BID
dosing.

Variability For PB, the coefficient of variation (CV%) was approximately 28% for Cmax and
34% for AUC 24n.

For TUDCA, the CV% was approximately 52% for Cmax and 60% for AUC5 24n.
