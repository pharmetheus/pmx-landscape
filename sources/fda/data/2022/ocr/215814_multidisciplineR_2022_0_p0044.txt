REZLIDHIA, olutasidenib

Histopathology Olutasidenib-related microscopic findings were observed in the kidney, liver, and
thyroid. There were increased incidences of the basophilic tubules and karyomegaly
in the kidney following the recovery period. All the other findings were reversible
following the recovery period.
