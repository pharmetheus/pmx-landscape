Abbreviations: IDSIQ, Insomnia Daytime Symptoms and Impacts Questionnaire

The IDSIQ v2.0 has 3 domains assessing Alert/Cognition, Mood, and Sleepiness. For each
domain, the item responses in the respective domains are summed to provide a domain score. A
total score is obtained by summing across all item responses. Prior to summation, items 1, 2, 8,
10 and 14 are reverse scored. For Sleepiness domain, Daily domain score is the sum of Item 8*,
Ttem 11, Item 12, and Item 13; Weekly Average is the mean of the daily domain score over 7
days. The Sleepiness domain score ranges from 0 to 40 with higher score indicating greater
burden of illness.

Secondary efficacy endpoints of Trial ID-078A301 and Trial ID-078A302 based on IDSIQ were
defined as:

e Change from baseline to Month 1 in IDSIQ sleepiness domain score
e Change from baseline to Month 3 in IDSIQ sleepiness domain score

The Applicant has taken several approaches to explore the thresholds of clinically meaningful
within patient change in IDSIQ Sleepiness domain scores.

(1) An exit interview study was conducted among 41 insomnia subjects exiting the phase 2
AC-078A203 study

patient change in IDSIQ v2.0 scores using phase 2 (AC-078A203) data.
