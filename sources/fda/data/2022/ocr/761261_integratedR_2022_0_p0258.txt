Observations and Results (F0)

Pregnancy Status, Mortality, Abortion, and Animal Disposition

The numbers of pregnant animals with surviving pups available for complete postnatal
evaluation (birth through reproductive performance) were 23, 23, 12, 21, and 22. Some females
were never pregnant (1, 2, 2, 3, 0). As reported in the mouse EFD study, maternal and
developmental mortality were over-represented in the 3 mg/kg/day dose group. Two dams were
euthanized on GD 23 with 100% early resorptions. Five dams were found dead between GD 16
and LD 2; this was generally attributed to hypersensitivity. One dam aborted on GD 16 and was
euthanized. Three dams were euthanized during the lactation period after all pups died. A listing
of all premature decedents is reproduced below, in Table 170.
