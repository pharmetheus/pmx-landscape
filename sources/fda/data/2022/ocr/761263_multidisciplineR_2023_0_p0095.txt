BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761263, Lunsumio (Mosunetuzumab)}

Hypothesis testing for the primary endpoint was conducted once only at the time of the
primary analysis, based on a clinical cutoff date of 15 March 2021.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The pre-specified primary efficacy endpoint was CR rate per independent review facility per the
2007 international working group criteria (Cheson 2007). However, the FDA uses overall
response rate as an intermediate endpoint reasonable likely to predict clinical benefit,
supported by durability. In addition, the FDA disagrees with the Applicant’s statement that CR
rate may be a potential surrogate for PFS in patients with follicular lymphoma. The use of CR
rate in patients with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma is not an established surrogate
endpoint and has not been used as an intermediate endpoint to support a regulatory
determination in follicular lymphoma. Of note, complete response rate at 30 months is an
established surrogate endpoint in patients with previously untreated follicular lymphoma.

The proposed historical control CR rate of (3% was not agreed upon with FDA. Comparison of
response rates from single-arm trials against a historical rate is challenging because the
historical benchmark is difficult to verify. Due to differences in population, trial design and
other possible unknown confounders, the historical estimation is likely subject to biases which
cannot be resolved. Consequently, the proposal for a hypothesis test or comparison against

3% is not adequate for inferential purposes but instead is considered as supportive only, and
the corresponding p-value is nominal.

Statistical Analysis Plan and Amendments

The Applicant’s Description:
The analyses conducted were prospectively planned and described in the Statistical Analysis

Plan (SAP, dated 18 December 2020) which was reviewed by the FDA (Type B Pre-BLA Content
and Format meeting, 21 January 2021) prior to the CCOD for the primary analysis. The SAP was
amended (v2, 21 May 2021) to revise censoring rules for time-to-event endpoints for events
occurring after NALT (at the recommendation of the Agency). There were no changes made to
the planned analyses of the primary and key secondary endpoints according to the SAP after
the database snapshot for either the primary, or updated analyses.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The primary analysis for efficacy was conducted on the B11 pivotal cohort, which includes 90
patients with relapsed or refractory FL treated at the proposed registrational dose (i.e.,
1/2/60/30 mg) in Group B of Study GO29781.

Response rates (ORR and CR) are summarized with 95% Clopper-Pearson Confidence Interval
(Cl) in the B11 pivotal cohort. Patients with missing/no response assessments are counted as
non-responders.
