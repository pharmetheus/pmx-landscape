Compatibility of Drug Product Solution With Container Closure Components

The available optical microscopy, scanning electron microscopy, and elemental analysis data
from registration and clinical batches indicate that the proposed aluminosilicate glass vials are
compatible with LEN injection up to 18 months under long-term storage conditions. The Office
of Translational Research laboratory analysis and inspection by the FDA team of the Gilead La
Verne site also support the vial compatibility.

The proposed vial compatibility test methods were found acceptable. However, because Gilead
made significant updates to the proposed test methods during current review cycle, and limited
data is available as per the revised procedures, Gilead was requested to provide the vial
