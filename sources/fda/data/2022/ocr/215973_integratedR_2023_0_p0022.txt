Key Review Issues Related to Evaluation of Risk (First Cycle)

e Emergence of resistance was assessed in virologic failures (VFs) of a LEN-containing ARV
regimen. The review team concluded that LEN resistance emerges readily in VFs and thus it
is important to use LEN as a component of a complete active ARV regimen. LEN resistance
substitutions do not confer cross-resistance to other ARVs, but longer-term data from all
subjects are required to determine durability of LEN and the other ARVs in the optimized
background regimen (OBR).

e Injection site reactions (ISRs) were identified as the most common adverse drug reaction
(ADR) among subjects who received SC injections of LEN; however, long-term experience
of subjects after multiple injections was limited in Studies 4625 and 4334. The review team
concluded that additional information is needed to understand the contributing factors of
ISRs with LEN, particularly for subjects who have prolonged time to resolution of nodules
and indurations.

e The risk of supratherapeutic LEN exposures when LEN is combined with a background
regimen containing boosted protease inhibitors was not prospectively assessed in Study 4625.
The review team conducted a post hoc analysis and concluded that labeling for LEN should
include the recommendation against concomitant administration of atazanavir/cobicistat or
atazanavir/ritonavir. No clinically significant drug interactions were observed with
darunavir/cobicistat.

For the NDA resubmissions, the review team identified one review issue relevant to the
evaluation of benefit (Section 6.3) and two review issues relevant to the evaluation of risk
(Section 7.7).
