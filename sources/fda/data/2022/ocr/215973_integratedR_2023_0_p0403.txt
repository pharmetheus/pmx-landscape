Base PK Model

The PK of LEN was best described by a two-compartment distribution model with linear
elimination from the central compartment. The oral LEN absorption was best characterized by a
first-order absorption process, and the SC absorption was best characterized by a parallel direct
first-order absorption and a delayed absorption process with four transit compartments. The PK
model was parameterized in terms of clearance (CL), central and peripheral volumes of
distribution (V- and Vp, respectively), intercompartmental clearance (Q/F), oral (PO) absorption
rate constant (Ka), PO bioavailability relative to TV (Fpo), SC direct/rapid first-order absorption
tate constant (Kair), SC transit first-order absorption rate constant (Kir), fraction of the SC dose
absorbed through the rapid/direct process, and the SC bioavailability relative to IV (Fsc).
Between-subject variability or inter-individual variability (ITV) was estimated on CL, Vp,
dissociation rate constant (Ka), Kx, and Fsc. The residual error model consisted of a combined
additive and proportional error model. The final structural model for lenacapavir is presented in
