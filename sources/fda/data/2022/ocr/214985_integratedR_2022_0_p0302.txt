The p-values are both <0.0001, see Table 128.

Analysis for Primary Endpoint

On Day 2, the test for the 50-mg dose of daridorexant failed to reject (p=0.2991) the null
hypothesis that daridorexant had at least a ASDLP 22.6 cm, see Table 129 and Figure 61. The
50-mg dose had an increase in SDLP of 2.19 cm (97.5% CI: 0.46 to 3.93) compared to placebo.
The Day 2 testing stopped at this point. The 100-mg dose had an increase in SDLP of 4.43 cm
(97.5% CI: 2.72 to 6.15) compared to placebo.
