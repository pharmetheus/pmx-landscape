Subject IM011046- oe, Lymphopenia (Discontinued)

40-year-old white male with a PMH of a positive quantiferon test and “leukocytopenia” in |

developed mild, nonserious lymphopenia after treatment with deucravacitinib for 3 weeks
(0.45 x 109/L, reference range: 1.02-3.36 x 109/L) down from a baseline lymphocyte count of 0.8
x 10°. He did not receive treatment for this event. At Week 16, the subject discontinued from
the study due to lymphopenia—he received his last dose during Week 8 at which time his
lymphocytes were 0.58 x 10°/L. The safety update report did not provide information regarding
this subject.

The contribution of study drug cannot be definitively determined based on limited information
and prior history of remote diminished white cell count. However, given that the lymphocyte
count continued to fluctuate and eventually decreased from baseline with continuous exposure
to deucravacitinib, with lymphopenia as an AE which subsequently led to discontinuation of the
study drug, it is reasonable to consider the reported grade 3 lymphopenia AE as a possible AR,
attributable to the study drug.
