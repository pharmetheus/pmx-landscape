13.2.6.2. Embryo-Fetal Development

An Oral (Gavage) Dose Range-Finding Study of the Effects of GS-6207 on
Embryo-Fetal Development in Sprague-Dawley Rats (TX-200-2031)

GS-6207 was administered to Sprague-Dawley rats (3 rats/control and 6 rats/dose group) from
gestation day (GD) 6 to 17 at doses of 0 ( a

), 3, 30, and 300 mg/kg with a dose volume of 2 mL/kg via oral gavage
once daily. There were no test article-related deaths or effects on the endpoints evaluated
(mortality, clinical observations, body weights, body weight gains, gravid uterine weights, food
consumption, gross necropsy findings, intrauterine growth and survival, and fetal extemal and
visceral morphology). The NOAEL for maternal and embryo-fetal development was 300 mg/kg.
The Day 17 AUCo.24n at the NOAEL was 26,300 ng:h/mL.
