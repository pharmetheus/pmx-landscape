In addition, due to the limited sample size in races other than White and Asian, the Applicant
only evaluated the PK difference in Asian vs. non-Asian. A further breakdown in races was
shown in Figure 16. However, a comparison of the PK differences among race groups is limited
by the sample size. The FDA will issue a PMR to conduct a randomized clinical trial in patients
with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma to verify the clinical benefit of mosunetuzumab.
The trial should include sufficient representation of racial and ethnic minorities to adequately
reflect the U.S. patient population and allow for interpretation of the results in these patient
populations. The PopPK analysis will be updated when more information becomes available to
evaluate the PK differences among race groups.
