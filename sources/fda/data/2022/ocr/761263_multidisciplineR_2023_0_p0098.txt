Protocol Violations/Deviations

The Applicant's Position:

A total of 150 major protocol deviations were reported in 111 of the 414 patients (26.8%) in
Group B. By category, the most common (>5% patients) major protocol deviations were ‘failure
to report AEs, SAEs, AESIs, DLTs, and pregnancy to the IEC/sponsor/regulatory authorities’ and
‘incomplete/missing/out-of-window tumor-related assessments’.

One patient with a non-hematological malignancy of melanoma was enrolled in error into the
B11 DLBCL/trFL RP2D cohort. The patient’s exclusion from the efficacy-evaluable population did
not affect the results of the primary analysis in R/R FL patients supporting this application. The
patient was included in the safety evaluable population because she received one dose of
mosunetuzumab.
