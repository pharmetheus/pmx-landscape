In stepwise model selection procedure, a forward and backward, Akaike information criterion
(AIC) based, automatic selection process was used to identify important exposure relationships
and covariates. Therefore, the Applicant opined that the Ctrough and AUCo-214 are more
appropriate exposure parameters for the E-R analysis.

It should be noted that the final E-R analysis for efficacy was conducted with clinical data
obtained from Study IMGN853-0417 since the patients from the other studies (i.e., Study
IMGN853-0401 and Study IMGN853-0403) were heterogenous with regards to the FRa
expression levels.

Exposure-safety relationship

The E-R analysis for safety evaluated a relationship between the ELAHERE exposure and the key
safety endpoints including ocular AEs of Grade > 2, peripheral neuropathy (PN), pneumonitis
and interstitial lung disease (ILD). The analysis identified a positive correlation between the
AUCo.214 of mirvetuximab soravtansine and probability of ocular AEs of Grade 2 2 or PN (Figure
3). As such, the incidence of PN was very low (including Grade > 2) and the E-R for PN showed a
shallow relationship.
