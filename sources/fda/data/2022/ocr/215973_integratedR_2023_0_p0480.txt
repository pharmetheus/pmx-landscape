Review:

Histopathology:

Clinical Histopathology:
There is no human histopathology included in the pivotal Phase 2/3 trial (GS-US-200-4625) to

support NDA 215973.

Nonclinical Histopathology:
The Applicant notes that nonclinical studies of rats (TX-200-2014), rabbits (TX-200-2033) and

dogs (TX-200-2047) showed microscopic findings of granulomatous inflammation at
subcutaneous (SC) injection sites. The Applicant notes further that granulomatous inflammation
has been observed after SC and intramuscular implantation of bioactive material, including those
for HIV treatment and concludes that inflammation and foreign body response to the lenacapavir
(LEN) SC depot is an expected host response. (IR response received 9/23/2021, p. 12)
