Drug-drug No clinical drug interaction studies were conducted with teclistamab. Teclistamab causes
interactions release of cytokines (including IL-6 and IL-10) that may suppress activity of cytochrome

P450 (CYP) enzymes, resulting in increased exposure of CYP substrates. The highest risk of
drug-drug interaction is expected to occur from initiation of the step-up dosing schedule
up to 7 days after the first treatment dose and during and after CRS. Patients receiving
concomitant CYP substrates where minimal concentration changes may lead to serious
adverse reactions should be monitored for toxicity or CYP substrate concentrations. The
dose of the concomitant CYP substrate drug should be adjusted as needed.
