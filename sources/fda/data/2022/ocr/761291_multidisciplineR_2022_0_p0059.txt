Additional E-R analyses conducted with data from 150 patients treated with the recommended
SC dosage regimen during either Phase 1 or Phase 2 of MajesTEC-1 did not identify relationships
between teclistamab exposure (Cave,istdose ANd Ctrough,Adoses) and efficacy outcomes including ORR
(assessed by IRC based on IMWG 2016 criteria), progression-free survival (PFS), duration of
response (DOR), or overall survival (OS). This finding is not unexpected given that only one
dosage regimen was included in the analyses. Patient covariates including baseline sBCMA and
PD-1 expression had statistically significant associations with ORR. The interpretability of this
finding is confounded by correlations between multiple covariates including teclistamab
exposure, body weight, myeloma type, ISS, baseline sBCMA, and baseline PD-1 expression. Refer
to Section 19.4.3.2 for details.
