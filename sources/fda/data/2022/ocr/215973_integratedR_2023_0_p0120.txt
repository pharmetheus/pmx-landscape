WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS

A new Warnings and Precautions subsection has been added to discuss the long-acting
properties and potential associated risks with SUNLENCA. This new subsection addresses
the risk of viral resistance if not transitioned to alternative therapy as well as the potential for
adverse reactions through interaction with sensitive CYP3A substrates with narrow
therapeutic indices for up to 9 months after the last subcutaneous dose. Refer to Section 6.2
of the previous, February 28, 2022 review.

A new Warnings and Precautions subsection has been added to discuss the risk of injection
site reactions (ISRs). This new subsection has been added in consideration of the long
duration of nodules and indurations, relatively high incidence of ISRs and discontinuations
seen as a result in the clinical studies. Refer to Section 7.7.2 of this review.
