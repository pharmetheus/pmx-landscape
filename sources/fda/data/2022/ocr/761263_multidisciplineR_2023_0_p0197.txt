Mosunetuzumab PK in Group A

A total of 33 patients enrolled in Group A received mosunetuzumab IV q3w treatment with the
dosing range 0.05-2.8 mg. Of these, 32 patients were included in the PK analysis.
Mosunetuzumab concentrations in the only patient treated at the 0.05 mg dose were below
the assay lower limit of quantification (LLOQ) of 10 ng/mL. See the summary of PK parameters
