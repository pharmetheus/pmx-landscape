Screening Resistance in Study 4625

The target population for Study 4625 was HTE HIV-1 subjects with resistance to at least two
ARV medications from each of at least three of the four main classes of ARV medications
(INSTI, NNRTI, NRTI, PI). In the presence of the RT substitution M184V/L, resistance to
lamivudine and emtricitabine did not count towards the total number of drugs with resistance for
the NRTI drug class per protocol requirements. Resistance reports were obtained prior to
enrollment to confirm that the resistance criteria for enrollment in the study were met. These
reports were either generated from the screening visit (Monogram genotypic and phenotypic
assays, n=45 of 72) or from historical reports supplied by the investigators (various assays, n=27
of 72). For a subset of subjects, both historical reports and screening reports were used to
evaluate the baseline resistance (n=15).

Based on the phenotypic resistance data provided, 78% (28/36) of subjects in Cohort 1 (79% in
1A and 75% in 1B) had greater than three-class resistance (Table 52) with 51% (18/35) showing
resistance to the INSTI DTG and 50% (16/32) showing resistance to the PI DRV. Additionally,
84% (27/32) of the subjects in Cohort 1 had C-X-C motif chemokine receptor (CXCR)-4 tropism
impacting maraviroc efficacy. Fewer subjects showed resistance to the gp120-directed
attachment inhibitor fostemsavir, the CD4*-directed post-attachment inhibitor ibalizumab, or the
fusion inhibitor enfuvirtide (Table 52).

The presence of resistance substitutions to the four main ARV drug classes was consistent with
the high level of resistance observed from phenotypic data. No subject harbored lenacapavir-
associated resistance substitutions at study entry.
