Various dosing scenarios described in Table 71 were investigated to formulate clinical rules
for tocilizumab administration in patients with CRS. The distributions of maximum
concentrations predicted by the model for these scenarios are presented in Figure 59. Model-
based simulations indicated that up to two consecutives TCZ doses at least 8 hours apart per
CRS event and maximum three TCZ doses within each time period of 6 weeks of
mosunetuzumab or glofitamab allow to maintain the TCZ concentrations below the “safety
threshold” used by FDA to determine the TCZ dose recommendation to manage CRS during
CAR-T therapy.
