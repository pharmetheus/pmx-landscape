13.2.6.3. Pre- and Postnatal Development

A Subcutaneous Injection Study of the Effect of GS-6207 on Pre- and
Postnatal Development, Including Maternal Function in Sprague-Dawley Rats
(TX-200-2049)

Key Study Findings

e Thickening at the injection site was noted in animals dosed with 30 and 300 mg/kg.

e No major effects were observed in pups or dams at the highest dose tested.

e The NOAEL for maternal health (FO generation) and fetal toxicity (F1 generation) was
300 mg/kg which corresponded to an AUCox and AUCo-192n of 54,800 ng-h/mL and Cmax of
412 ng/mL for GD 6.

e The exposure margin, based on the lack of systemic effects and the predicted human
exposures based on the RHD, was 5x.
