8.1.4. Assessment of Efficacy Across Trials
Primary Endpoints

Data:

Study MEK-NF-201 serves as the primary basis for establishing the efficacy of mirdametinib in
the treatment of participants with NF1 PN. In addition, data from Study NF-106 are provided as
supportive evidence of efficacy. Data from MEK-NF-201 and NF-106 were not integrated. The
rationale for this strategy is provided below.

Both studies evaluated the efficacy of mirdametinib at a dose of 2 mg/m? BID on an
intermittent schedule (3 weeks on/1 week off in a 28-day cycle) in participants with NF1 PN, but
differences in the study designs do not allow for an integration of results of the two studies.
Specifically, participants in Study NF-106 were discontinued from treatment if they did not
meet certain tumor volume reduction benchmarks: treatment beyond Cycle 8 was allowed only
for participants who had 215% reduction from baseline in volume of the target tumor by the
end of Cycle 8 and treatment beyond 12 cycles was allowed for participants who achieved >20%
tumor reduction from baseline by volumetric analysis by the end of Cycle 12. In contrast, there
were no requirements for volumetric reduction of the target PN at any cycle to continue
receiving mirdametinib in Study MEK-NF-201; participants received study treatment unless they
met withdrawal criteria.

TTR results from Study MEK-NF-201 demonstrate that the rule excluding further treatment
beyond Cycle 8 in Study NF-106 likely precluded many participants from an opportunity to
achieve an objective response with longer durations of mirdametinib administration. Similarly,
time to best percentage change in tumor volume results in Study MEK-NF-201 suggest that
participants in Study NF-106 who did not achieve the tumor reduction requirements at Cycles 8
and 12 were discontinued from treatment before their tumors could likely reach their greatest
reduction in volume, thereby affecting the likelihood of achieving a response. Therefore,
efficacy results from these two studies were presented separately with Study NF-106 included
as a supportive study of the results in the pivotal Study MEK-NF-201.

The Applicant's Position:
Efficacy Data from Study MEK-NF-201 and NF-106 were not integrated as agreed upon with the

Agency.
