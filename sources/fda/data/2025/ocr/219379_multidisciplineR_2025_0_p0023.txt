Benefit

@ The efficacy of mirdametinib was evaluated in the ReNeu
study, a multicenter, single-arm trial investigating
mirdametinib in adult and pediatric patients with NF1 and
progressive or symptomatic plexiform neurofibromas not
amenable to complete surgical resection. The primary efficacy
population included 114 patients, including 56 pediatric and
58 adult patients with NF1-PN.

¢ In the pediatric cohort, the confirmed ORR per volumetric
analysis was 52% (95% Cl: 38, 65); the median DOR was not
reached. A total of 90% of responders had a DOR of 212
months and 48% of responders had a DOR of 224 months.

@ In the adult cohort, the confirmed ORR per volumetric
analysis was 41% (95% Cl: 29, 55); the median DOR was not
reached. A total of 88% of responders had a DOR of 212
months and 50% of responders had a DOR of 224 months.

The submitted evidence meets the statutory
evidentiary standard for regular approval for the
indicated population. The magnitude of the treatment
effect and durability of responses, coupled with
supportive COA data, provide evidence of a clinically
meaningful treatment effect.
