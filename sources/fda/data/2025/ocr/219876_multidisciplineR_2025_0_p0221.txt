The Applicant’s Position:

diffuse glioma showed no significant relationship between drug exposure and ORR with no
increased safety findings with higher exposures. This indicates minimal safety risk at the doses
evaluated and supports the recommended dordaviprone dose of 625 mg, or the equivalent body
