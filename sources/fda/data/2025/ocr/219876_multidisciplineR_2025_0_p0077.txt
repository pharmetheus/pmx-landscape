6 Clinical Pharmacology

6.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant is seeking accelerated approval of dordaviprone for the treatment of H3 K27M-
mutant diffuse glioma in adult and pediatric patients. The proposed recommended dose in adult
patients is 625 mg and in pediatric patients is based on body weight: 125 mg (10 to < 12.5 kg);
250 mg (12.5 to < 27.5 kg); 375 mg (27.5 to < 42.5 kg); 500 mg (42.5 to < 52.5 kg) and 625 mg
