The FDA’s Assessment:

Tn general, the FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. The data in combination with the
CDK4/6 inhibitor were not reviewed for this NDA application since the proposed indication is
for imlunestrant monotherapy.

The established pharmacologic class (EPC) for imlunestrant is estrogen receptor antagonist. In
vitro, imlunestrant induced degradation of ERa, leading to inhibition of ER-dependent gene (i.e.,
PGR, PDZK1, AREG, RASGRP1, WISP2, and GREB1) transcription and cellular proliferation
in ER* breast cancer cells.

The Applicant states that imlunestrant showed anti-tumor activity in ER* breast cancer PDX
models including those that were resistant to palbociclib |
, the
Applicant did not provide direct evidence from in vitro or in vivo studies demonstrating anti-
tumor activity following imlunestrant treatment in either palbociclib or other CDK4/6 inhibitor

(abemaciclib) resistant models.

In an orthotopic brain model, daily oral administration of imlunestrant (> 10 mg/kg, 28 days) to
mice implanted with luciferase expressing breast cancer cells (MCF7-fLuc) led to reduced tumor
growth and increased animal life span compared to control. Exposure to imlunestrant was
observed in the brain following oral administration to mice bearing ERa wild-type flank breast
cancer xenografts. Although the nonclinical data suggests the ability of imlunestrant to penetrate
the brain resulting in anti-tumor activity, there is insufficient clinical data to support anti-tumor
activity on brain metastases from primary breast tumors.

A summary of the uterotrophic effects of imlunestrant are included in the Secondary
Pharmacology section of the review.
