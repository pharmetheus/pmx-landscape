In study 3002, peak plasma concentrations at steady state in adolescents were achieved at 3 to

7 days postdose, with mean (SD) concentrations of 21.9 (15.4) g/mL on Day 3 and 21.7

(11.5) g/mL on Day 7. These values are approximately 1.4-fold greater than the observed
steady state Cmax following 200 mg SC Q4W from adults in trial 2001 (mean [SD] Cmax=15.9
[5.2] g/mL), and 2.6-fold lower than the observed steady state Cmax following 600 mg SC Q4W
from adults in trial 2001 (mean [SD] Cmax=56.4 [15.9] jtg/mL).
