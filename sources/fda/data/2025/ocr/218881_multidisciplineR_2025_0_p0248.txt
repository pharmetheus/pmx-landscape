19.4.2. Exposure-Response Analysis

19.4.2.1 ER (efficacy) Executive Summary
The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant conducted an exposure—-response (E-R) analysis for efficacy using data from
Study J2J-OX-JZLC (EMBER-3). Initially, the Applicant concluded that higher imlunestrant
exposures were associated with longer progression-free survival (PFS). This conclusion was
found to be incorrect as the Applicant identified a mistake in unit conversion during the review.
After correcting the errors and updating the exposure-PFS analysis, no significant exposure-PFS
telationship was identified. The corrected analysis is acceptable.

In general, the E-R relationship for efficacy was considered not fully characterized because the
analysis included data from only one dose level (400 mg QD) evaluated in the pivotal EMBER-3
study. Labeling language in Section 12.2 (Pharmacodynamics) was updated to reflect this
limitation.
