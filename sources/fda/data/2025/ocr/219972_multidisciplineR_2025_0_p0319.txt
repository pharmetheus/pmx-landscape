Exposure showed a statistically
significant association with diarrhea
Grade >3. Younger participants and
participants with ECOG=1 had a lower

A statistically significant association
between exposure and all grade rash
was observed. At baseline, participants
from Japan showed a lower risk of all
grade rash compared to participants
who were not from Japan, while
participants with higher bilirubin
concentration at baseline had a higher
risk compared to participants with lower
bilirubin levels

There was a significantly increased
probability for dose modifications due to
TEAEs with increasing exposure. White
participants, participants who used
medication for the treatment of
constipation, and participants with a
body weight <54 kg showed a higher
baseline probability for dose
modifications due to TEAEs
