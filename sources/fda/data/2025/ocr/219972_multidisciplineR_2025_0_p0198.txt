The Applicant’s Position:

As of the database cut-off date, the maximum time on sevabertinib treatment was 24 months
across the 3 safety populations. The median treatment duration was 6.2 months in the primary
safety population, Group D, and 4.8 months in the Whole Study 20 mg BID population. In the
Whole Study SAF, including all treated participants from the start of the study (encompassing
the DE/BF), the median treatment duration was 5.0 months.

The median daily dose was >39 mg in both Group D and the Whole Study 20 mg BID
population, and the median percentage of the actual administered dose was >98% of the planned
dose across the safety populations, indicating a high level of compliance with the study
treatment.
