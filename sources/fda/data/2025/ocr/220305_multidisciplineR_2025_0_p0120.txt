NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

CYP3A substrates: No dosage adjustments are recommended for
concurrent use of CYP3A substrates.

The Applicant has an ongoing study, Study KO-MEN-001 Sub-study 1,
to evaluate the impact of ziftomenib (in vitro CYP3A inhibitor) on
midazolam PK. Given that the study is ongoing, a PMR will be issued
for its completion.

Transporter DDIs

P-glycoprotein (P-gp) inhibitors: No dosage adjustments are
recommended for concomitant use of P-gp inhibitors.

Breast cancer resistance protein (BCRP) inhibitors: The impact of
concomitant use of BCRP inhibitors on ziftomenib PK is unknown. A
PMR will be issued to evaluate the impact of BCRP inhibitors on

ziftomenib PK.

ARA DDIs

Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs): Concomitant use of PPIs should be
avoided.

Concomitant use of PPIs decreases ziftomenib exposure by ~60 to 70%,
which may decrease efficacy.

Histamine-2 receptor antagonists (H2RAs): Administration of H2RAs
should be staggered 10 hours before or 2 hours after KOMZIFTI
administration.

Antacids: Administration of antacids should be staggered 2 hours before
or 2 hours after KOMZIFTI administration.

Other DDIs

QTc-prolonging drugs: Concomitant use of QTc-prolonging drugs
should be avoided. If concurrent use cannot be avoided, additional safety

monitoring is recommended. Interrupt administration if QTc interval is >
500 ms or change from baseline is > 60 ms.

Patients receiving ziftomenib 600 mg displayed a concentration-
dependent increase in QTc interval (largest mean increase in QTc
predicted to be 7.7 ms), with 8% of patients experiencing QTcF > 500
ms and QTc increase > 60 ms over baseline.
