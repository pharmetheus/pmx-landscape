6.3.2 Clinical Pharmacology Questions
Does the clinical pharmacology program provide supportive evidence of effectiveness?

No. The Agency was not able to independently verify the primary endpoint of EFS for Study MC-
FludT.14/L Trial II (Refer to Section 8). Therefore, the efficacy evaluation for the current
submission focused on overall survival (OS) results in Study MC-FludT.14/L Trial II. In Study MC-
FludT.14/L Trial !I, 570 adult patients planned to undergo allogeneic HSCT for AML or MDS were
randomized 1:1 to receive treosulfan 10 g/m?/day x 3 days + fludarabine 30 mg/m*/day x 5 days
or busulfan 0.8 mg/kg Q6H x 8 doses + fludarabine 30 mg/m?2/day x 5 days. Treatment with the
proposed dosing regimen of treosulfan + fludarabine improved OS with a HR (95% Cl) of 0.65
(0.48, 0.88) compared to treatment with busulfan + fludarabine in the overall population. In
adult patients with AML, treosulfan + fludarabine improved OS with a HR (95% Cl) of 0.61 (0.41,
0.92) compared to treatment with busulfan + fludarabine while in adult patients with MDS
treosulfan + fludarabine improved OS with a HR (95% Cl) of 0.62 (0.34, 1.10) compared to
treatment with busulfan + fludarabine. Refer to Section 8.1.1 for a detailed assessment of
treosulfan efficacy in Trial Il.

Is the proposed dosing regimen appropriate for the general patient population for which the
indication is being sought?

Yes. Treatment with the proposed dosing regimen resulted in improved overall survival
compared to treatment with busulfan + fludarabine in adult patients with AML or MDS (Refer to

Section 8.1.1).
