Efficacy Results —- Secondary and other relevant endpoints

Data:

An analysis of all secondary endpoints was performed at the time of the primary endpoint
analysis (14 OCT 2024). The secondary endpoints included ORR by investigator assessment,
DCR, DOR, and PFS by BICR and investigator assessments, and OS. The other efficacy
endpoint, TTR, was also analyzed by BICR and investigator assessments.

An analysis of ORR and DCR by investigator assessment and DOR using a database cut-off date
of 28 MAR 2025 was also performed in Groups D and E, when all ongoing responders in
Groups D and E had reached at least six months of follow-up on DOR. These results are
summarized below, with an extra section added to present the updated data for Groups D and E
for the FAS.
