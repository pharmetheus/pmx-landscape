The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment of the phototoxicity assay for taletrectinib. A
concentration-dependent decrease in neutral red uptake was reported in the presence and absence
of UVA with ICso values of 0.186 and 4.95 j1g/ml, respectively. The calculated photo-iritation
factor (PIF) was 26.6 and the mean photo effect value (MPE) was 0.278. In accordance with
OECD guidelines, taletrectinib is potentially phototoxic since the PIF score was > 5 and the
MPE value was > 0.15.
