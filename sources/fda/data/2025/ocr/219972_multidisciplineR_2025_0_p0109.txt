The proposed dosage of sevabertinib is 20 mg BID taken with food. The
dosage is supported based on the PK, PD. activity, and safety data from.
patients receiving 10 mg BID and 20 mg BID in Study 21607 and the
supportive E-R analyses. Given the decreased exposure with food intake
determined from a dedicated food effect study, the identified positive E-R
relationship for safety, and the observed high rate of gastrointestinal (GI)
adverse events (e.g.. diarrhea), the proposed fed dosing condition is
reasonable with the potential for toxicity mitigation.

Renal impairment: Per population PK analysis, no dosage adjustment is
recommended in patients with eGFR 30 to < 90 mL/min. Since renal
elimination only accounts for 10% of the administered dose with 1.3% as the
parent drug, the effect of severe renal impairment on sevabertinib exposure is
not expected to be clinically meaningful, and therefore a dedicated renal
impairment study in patients with severe renal impairment is not needed.

Hepatic impairment: Per population PK analysis, no dosage adjustment is
recommended in patients with mild hepatic impairment (per NCI -ODWG).
The PK and safety of sevabertinib in patients with moderate or severe hepatic
impairment have not been evaluated. A PMR study will be issued for a
hepatic impairment study to evaluate the potential serious risk of increased
drug exposure and determine an appropriate dosage of sevabertinib in patients
with moderate hepatic impairment. The dosage recommendation in patients
with severe hepatic impairment will be determined based on the results in
patients with moderate hepatic impairment or a subsequent PMR study if
needed.
