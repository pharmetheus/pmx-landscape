PK: The FDA evaluated the effect of strong CYP3A-inhibiting azole use on ziftomenib PK in
patients not receiving PPIs (to control for other known DDIs), as shown in

FDA Table 23. In patients receiving strong CYP3A-inhibiting azoles, ziftomenib single-dose
Cmax and AUC were 23% and 76% higher, respectively, and steady-state Cmax and AUC were
118% and 145% higher, respectively, than in patients not receiving azoles. Although variability
in the FDA analysis was high, results were consistent with PopPK and physiologically-based PK
(PBPK) analyses submitted by the Sponsor demonstrating ~2-fold increase in ziftomenib
exposure at steady state [Section 14.4.2, 14.4.4].
