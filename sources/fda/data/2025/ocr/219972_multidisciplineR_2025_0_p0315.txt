19.4.4.3. ER (Safety) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:
e Sevabertinib exposure is associated with increased risk of all-Grade diarrhea, Grade 3 or
worse diarrhea, all-Grade rash, and TEAEs leading to dose modification (p< 0.01).
¢ The following patient factors were identified as significant covariates (p value< 0.01) in
the multivariate E-R analysis for respective endpoints.

1. Participants from Japan were found to have an increased risk of diarrhea as did
those who had previously undergone HER2 targeted therapy.

2. For diarrhea of grade > 3, older participants were found to be at increased risk whilst
participants with an Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group score greater than 0 were
at decreased risk at baseline.

3. For dose modifications due to treatment emergent adverse events, participants
taking drugs for treating constipation, White participants, and participants with
weight at baseline < 54 kg were found to be at increased risk at baseline.

4. For rash, participants with higher levels of bilirubin at baseline were at higher risk
whilst participants from Japan were at decreased risk
