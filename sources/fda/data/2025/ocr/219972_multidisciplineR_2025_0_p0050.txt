In vivo antitumor efficacy of sevabertinib in the A431 (EGFRwt) human NSCLC xenograft
model in mice

In order to evaluate whether the in vitro selectivity vs. EGFRwt translates into reduced in vivo
activity in an EGFRwt tumor model, sevabertinib was investigated in the A431 (EGFRwt)
human lung cancer xenograft model. Female CB17-SCID mice, subcutaneously inoculated with
A431 (EGFRwt) human NSCLC cells, received either vehicle (control) or sevabertinib at 10
mg/kg QD, 25 mg/kg QD, or 50 mg/kg QD. Animal body weight and tumor size were measured
2-3 times weekly and at study end, the tumors were collected for tumor weight analyses.
