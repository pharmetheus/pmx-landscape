Adequacy of the safety database:

The Applicant’s Position:

The safety database comprising of all participants who received at least 1 dose of sevabertinib in
Study 21607 (N=357), as of the database cutoff date of 14 OCT 2024, including the target
population for the proposed indication, i.e. participants with pre-treated HER2-mutated NSCLC
naive to HER2 ex20ins-targeted therapy, who were treated with sevabertinib at the RP2D

(N=8 1), and participants treated with the RP2D dose (Whole Study 20 mg BID, N=268), is
considered by the Applicant to be adequate to assess the safety and benefit-risk profile of
sevabertinib. In addition, the Whole Study SAF safety population (N=357) allows for the
characterization of less common toxicities associated with HER2-targeted therapy.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position regarding the adequacy of the available safety
database; however, the FDA’s assessment is based on a primary safety population including 136
patients with non-squamous NSCLC treated with sevabertinib at 20 mg BID from the combined
Groups D + E who received prior systemic chemotherapy but were either naive to or had
progression on anti-HER2 therapy respectively, in the unresectable or metastatic setting. The
pooled safety population used to inform Section 5 of labeling includes 268 total patients. The
number of patients in the primary and pooled safety populations is adequate to perform a
comprehensive safety review.
