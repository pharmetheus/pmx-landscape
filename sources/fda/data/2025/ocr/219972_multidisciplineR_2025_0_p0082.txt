NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s conclusions, with additional pertinent findings
described below. FDA notes that animal:human exposure comparisons were made using total
AUC, as it is not our standard practice to consider protein binding when making AUC
comparisons.

Study title/ number: 13-Week Repeat Dose Systemic Toxicity Study in Rats with Once
Daily Oral Administration followed by a 4-Week Recovery Period / Study No. T105912-8,
Report No. B002293

* Skin toxicity was observed in males at >5 mg/kg and in females at 10 mg/kg, as
evidenced by clinical signs, gross pathology, and histologic findings that did not recover
(crust, hyperplasia, inflammation, ulceration, and atrophy).

* Mild hemorrhage in the eye at 10 mg/kg and minimal degeneration and infiltrate in the
lacrimal gland in recovery animals at 10 mg/kg were observed.
