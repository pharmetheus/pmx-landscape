In the final PD-0315209 model, n-shrinkage
for CLm/F, Vmc/F, Fm, Qm1/F, and Vmp1/F
were 21.6%, 31.0%, 18.1%, 34.2%, and
28.2%, respectively. Shrinkage was low for
CLm/F and Fm and moderate for the
remaining parameters.

IIV was included on CLm/F, Vmc/F, Fm,
Qm1/F, and Vmp1/F with estimates of
47.4% CV, 163% CV, 49.7% CV, 65.7% CV,
and 51.9% CV. IIV on all modeled
parameters was high (at or above 50%),
reflecting the substantial variability
observed in PD-0315209 plasma exposures.
Residual unexplained variability was
described by a proportional plus additive
error with estimates of 27.3% for the
proportional error and 7.4% for the
additive error.

BLQ for Parameter Accuracy The LLOQ for all methods was 0.1 ng/mL Acceptable
[A4581001 (Phase 1) and A4581004] or 1
ng/mL [A4581001 (Phase 2), 44581002,
MEK-NF-101, NF106 and MEK-NF-201].

Since BLQ frequency was relatively low,
missing values were unlikely to unduly
influence model development. As such,
likelihood-based approaches for handling
BQL data were not considered.
