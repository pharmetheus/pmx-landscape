4 Significant Issues from Other Review Disciplines Pertinent to Clinical
Conclusions on Efficacy and Safety

4.1. Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI)

Refer to the Clinical Inspection Summary uploaded in DARRTs on October 21, 2025, for full
details. Briefly, three clinical investigators, Dr. Ticiana Leal (Site #14003), Dr. Tae Min Kim
(Site #56002), and Dr. Herbert Ho Fung Loong (Site #65001), as well as the General Clinical
Imaging Services (GCIS) at Bayer Healthcare Pharmaceuticals, Inc were inspected. The
following findings were observed during the inspections.

Inspection of Dr. Kim’s site identified a protocol deviation regarding a patient with mixed
histology (sarcomatoid carcinoma with squamous cell and spindle cell components) identified in
the pathology report, but histology was identified as squamous cell in the data provided; patients
with mixed histology were excluded from the trial per eligibility criteria. This patient was
ultimately excluded from the efficacy population and the deviation was determined not to impact
the overall assessment of efficacy or safety.

Inspection of Bayer's Clinical Imaging Services identified a data discrepancy for subject “

©) which resulted in a patient’s best overall response being reclassified from progressive
disease to stable disease; this finding did not affect the calculation of the primary endpoint.

Except for the observations noted above, the inspections did not identify significant concerns
regarding study conduct, data discrepancies, Good Clinical Practice (GCP), or regulatory
compliance. The data generated by these clinical investigators appear acceptable in support of
the proposed indication.

4.2. Product Quality

Please refer to the Office of Pharmaceutical Quality (OPQ) review uploaded to DARRTs on
November 4, 2025, for full details. Briefly, OPQ has recommended approval of NDA 219972 for
sevabertinib. The review team determined that the Applicant has provided sufficient information
to assure the identity, strength, purity, and quality of the proposed drug product.

Not applicable.
