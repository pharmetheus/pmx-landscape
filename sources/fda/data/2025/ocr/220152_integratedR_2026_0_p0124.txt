13.2.11. Extractables and Leachables

Tradipitant 85 mg capsules are packaged in high-density polyethylene bottles with child-resistant
closures containing aluminum foil-lined induction seals. Each bottle also contains a 2-g silica gel
desiccant @. The materials of construction of the container closure system components are
routinely used in the pharmaceutical industry for solid oral dosage forms, and the components
comply with 21 CFR 211.94 requirements for drug product containers and closures. Based on the
oral formulation (capsules) and container closure system, there are no nonclinical safety
concerns regarding extractables and leachables.
