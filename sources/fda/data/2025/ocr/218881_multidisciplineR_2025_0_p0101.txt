Strong CYP3A inducers: Co-administration of imlunestrant with carbamazepine,
(100 mg BID on Days 6-8, 200 mg BID on Days 9-11, 300 mg BID Days 12-22, 300
mg QD on Day 23) (strong CYP3A inducer) for multiple days decreased
imlunestrant AUCo-inf by 42% and decreased Cmax by 29% (Table 6.8). FDA agrees
with the Applicant’s position and labeling proposal to avoid concomitant use of
strong CYP3A inducers and if concomitant use is unavoidable increase imlunestrant
dosage to 600 mg QD.
