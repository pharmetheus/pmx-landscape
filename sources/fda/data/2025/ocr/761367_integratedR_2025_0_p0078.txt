Abbreviations: CSL312, garadacimab; EOT, end of treatment; HAE, hereditary angioedema; OL, open-label

Key Inclusion Criteria
1. Successful completion of trial 2001 or 3001.

OR

2. For garadacimab-naive subjects to enter the Run-in Period of the trial, they must meet the
following criteria:

a. Age >12 years.

b. Diagnosed with C1-INH as defined by documented clinical history consistent with HAE,
C1-INH antigen concentration and/or functional activity <50% of normal, and C4 antigen
concentration below the lower limit of the reference range as documented in the subject’s
medical record.

c. Documentation of >3 HAE attacks during the 3 months before screening. For subjects
taking HAE prophylaxis, the >3 HAE attacks could be documented over 3 consecutive
months before commencing the prophylactic therapy.

3. For garadacimab-naive subjects to exit the Run-in Period and enter the Treatment Period,
they must meet the following criteria:

a. Participated in the Run-in Period for at least 1 month.

b. Experienced at least an average of one HAE attack per month during the Run-in Period.

c. No laboratory clinical abnormalities in the hematology, chemistry, or urinalysis
assessments performed during screening.

d. Verified C1-INH functional activity and antigen and C4 antigen concentration at
screening.
