PD Results

FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity was assessed as a pharmacodynamic (PD) marker.
Garadacimab showed dose-dependent inhibition of FXIIa-mediated kallikrein activity (Figure 27
and Figure 28). A clear inhibition trend was observed at the highest doses of 3 or 10 mg/kg in
both IV and SC cohorts when compared to placebo group. The duration of inhibition was also
longer for the higher doses with a return to baseline range at about Day 56 for 3 mg/kg IV or SC,
and at about Day 85 for 10 mg/kg IV. The FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity following

10 mg/kg SC was kept slightly lower than baseline at Day 85. In SC cohorts, the time of peak
response appears to correlate with the garadacimab Tmax, suggesting a real-time PK/PD
relationship.
