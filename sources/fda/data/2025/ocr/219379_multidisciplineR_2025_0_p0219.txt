Issues Regarding Data Integrity and Submission Quality

The Applicant's Posit
The quality and integrity of the submitted datasets and individual case narratives are

appropriate and adequate to allow for a thorough review of safety.
The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that no issues were identified regarding data integrity or submission quality of the
datasets and individual case narratives that had an effect on the safety review.

Categorization of Adverse Events

Data:

The number and proportion of participants reporting at least 1 TEAE in each category is
described in Table 50. There were more participants in the adult cohort than in the pediatric
cohort that reported TEAEs that led to study discontinuation (12 [16%] participants in the adult
cohort vs 4 (7%) participants in the pediatric cohort) and treatment discontinuation (16 [21%]
participants in the adult cohort vs 5 [9%] participants in the pediatric cohort).
