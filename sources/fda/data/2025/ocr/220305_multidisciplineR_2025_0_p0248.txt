The rates of DS by genetic subtype and dose level are shown in FDA Table 51. Although all DS
events in subjects with NPMJ-m AML occurred at the 600 mg QD dose, events occurred in the
participants with KM724-r AML at doses both higher and lower than the labeled dose for the
proposed indication, including fatal events that occurred in patients with KM72A-r at doses lower
than the recommended dose. That fatal adverse reactions occurred in patients with KM724-1 at a
higher rate (4/39) than the occurred for the labeled indication (2/112) was included in the
warning and precaution for differentiation syndrome in USPI. As noted by the Applicant, the
higher rate and grade of DS observed in patients with KM724-r treated with ziftomenib along
with the lower response rate observed led them to abandon ziftomenib monotherapy studies for
these patients in favor of exploring combination therapies. It remains unclear why this
differential effect was observed for ziftomenib and if any other menin inhibitor currently in
development will show similar results.
