The Applicant also provided results from a second study, NF-106, which were evaluated solely

on a benefit:risk assessment of the data from Study MEK-NF-201 (ReNeu).

During the review it was noted that the Applicant used a modified REINS criteria for disease
assessments, confirming response within 2-6 months as opposed to 3-6 months per the criteria.
Results using strict adherence to the criteria were consistent with the pre-specified analysis
(50% and 52% in pediatric cohorts, respectively; 35% and 41% in adult cohorts, respectively).
See Section 8.1 for more detail. Additional sensitivity analyses of ORR across BICR readers and
in the Per Protocol Set population confirm the observed ORR benefit is robust.

Median estimates of DOR per Kaplan-Meier methods were not met in either cohort even at the
time of the 90-day safety update, due to only a small number of events occurring after a
response. A total of 90% of pediatric and 88% of adult responders had a DOR of 212 months
and 48% of pediatric and 50% of adult responders had DOR 224 months indicating that the
observed responses are durable.

In summary, the primary efficacy data of confirmed ORR and DOR from the ReNeu study
demonstrate clinically meaningful durable anti-tumor activity of mirdametinib in the treatment
of pediatric and adult patients with NF1 PN.

8.4. Conclusions and Recommendations

The FDA’s Assessment:

Study MEK-NF-201 (ReNeu) is a multicenter, single-arm trial investigating mirdametinib in adult
and pediatric patients with NF1 and progressive or symptomatic plexiform neurofibromas not
amenable to complete surgical resection. Eligible patients were at least 2 years of age with PN
causing significant morbidity, and a target PN that was evaluable by volumetric analysis.
Patients were enrolled by age group into pediatric and adult cohorts. Treatment consisted of
mirdametinib at a dosage of 2 mg/m? orally twice daily for the first 21 days of each 28-day cycle.
The study included a treatment phase lasting 24 cycles and an optional long-term follow-up
phase. The primary endpoint to demonstrate the efficacy of mirdametinib for this population
was confirmed ORR, defined as PN decrease 2 20% compared to baseline in consecutive scans
within 2-6 months, using independent central review of MRI volumetric analysis. DOR was a key
secondary endpoint. The primary efficacy population consisted of 114 patients, including 56
