14.2.4. Trial 2001 (Dose-Ranging Trial in Subjects
With HAE)

Title

A multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel-arm trial to investigate the efficacy, PKs,
and safety of garadacimab in subjects with hereditary angioedema.

Objectives

The primary objective was to evaluate the efficacy of garadacimab in the prevention of HAE
attacks in subjects with C1-INH HAE. Characterization of PK in subjects with HAE was
included as a secondary objective, and evaluation of PD was an exploratory objective.
