NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

In general, the applicant’s population PK (PopPK) analysis is considered acceptable for the
purpose of characterizing the PK profile of ziftomenib and its metabolites KO-516 and KO-
739 in adult patients with R/R AML. There is no clinical meaningful impact for covariates
including body weight, sex, race, age (18-86 years old), renal and hepatic impairment (mild
or moderate), and p-gp inhibition. Based on simulation, fed status and strong CYP3A4
inhibitors can cause ~6 and ~2-fold increase in exposure, respectively. The applicant’s
analyses were verified by the reviewer, with no significant discordance identified.
