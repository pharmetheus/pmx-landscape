Patient Disposition

Data:

The primary efficacy analysis was performed on the Phase 2 mITT population consisting of

92 patients, with 9 (9.8%) of patients remaining on ziftomenib treatment as of the 28 October
2024 data cutoff date. Of the 92 patients, 34 (37.0%) discontinued treatment due to progressive,
12 (13%) due to lack of efficacy, 17 (18.5%) due to AEs, and 3 (3.3%) due to death. In total,
54 (58.7%) patients discontinued the study due to death, predominantly related to AML. As of
28 October 2024, 26 (28.3%) remained on study.

The Applicant’s Position:

The main reasons for treatment discontinuation were related to a patient's underlying disease
(progressive disease, lack of efficacy, and AEs). The main reasons for discontinuation from the
study was death and withdrawal by patient.
