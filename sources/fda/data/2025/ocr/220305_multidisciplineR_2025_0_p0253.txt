Human Carcinogenicity or Tumor Development

Given the short median exposure of ziftomenib, the single-arm study design, and the extensive
past cancer treatments for this population with R/R AML, it is not possible to attribute these
events to ziftomenib. However, the review team notes that lymphoma did occur in a mouse
during the 13-week toxicity study (see Section 5), and as such, this information is disclosed in
Section 13.1 of the ziftomenib USPI.
