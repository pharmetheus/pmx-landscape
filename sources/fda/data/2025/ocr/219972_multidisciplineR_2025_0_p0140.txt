The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of study endpoints and that ORR as
assessed by BICR per RECIST v1.1 supported by DOR is appropriate to support accelerated
approval in this disease setting. FDA notes that DCR is not considered to be a clinically
relevant endpoint for efficacy evaluation given the inability to attribute stable disease to an
effect of the study drug. Time-to-event endpoints such as PFS and OS in non-comparative
trials are not interpretable and are considered descriptive only.

Statistical Analysis Plan and Amendments

The Applicant’s Description:

The statistical analyses were based on the SAP version 2.0, dated 14 OCT 2024, which was
finalized before the database lock for the primary completion analysis of Group D. Additionally,
a supplemental SAP version 1.0, dated 11 FEB 2025, was prepared. Details regarding the SAP
versions are summarized in the Study 21607 CSR (Module 5.3.5.2, CSR B003047, Section
10.1.9.). The end of the study is defined as the date of the LPLV.

This NDA includes efficacy results from the primary and supportive populations of participants
with NSCLC harboring HER? activating mutations (including ex20ins) treated with sevabertinib
monotherapy in Study 21607. The primary efficacy population is Group D, which includes
participants who have received prior systemic therapy but were naive to HER? ex20ins-targeted
therapy. Supportive efficacy data are provided by Group E, which includes participants who
have received prior systemic therapy and had documented progression to HER2-targeted ADCs.

The safety analyses in support of the proposed indication are based on the data from Study
21607. The primary safety population is Group D. Safety data from participants who received at
least 1 dose of sevabertinib at 20 mg BID (RP2D dose) and participants who received at least 1
dose of sevabertinib at any dose level are also presented (see Section 8.2).

Analysis Populations

participants who received at least 1 dose of study treatment at 20 mg BID. In addition, sensitivity
analyses of ORR were performed using the EFS, including all participants who received at least
1 dose of study treatment at 20 mg BID, had a measurable disease at baseline and either at least 1
