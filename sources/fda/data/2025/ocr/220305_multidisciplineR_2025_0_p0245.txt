Most laboratory shifts from baseline were mild, except for potassium decreased which showed a
grade 3-4 shift in 22% of ziftomenib treated patients. However, it has been noted for decades that
uncontrolled acute leukemia is associated with hypokalemia and total body potassium
depletion. Therefore, it is hard to ascertain if the shifts seen on Study KO-MEN-001 are due to
uncontrolled AML or ziftomenib treatment. Since electrolyte abnormalities have secondary
consequences, they were included in ziftomenib labeling. Additionally, since uncontrolled or
worsening acute leukemia is associated with elevations of lactate dehydrogenase and urate,
worsening cytopenias, and coagulopathy, these labs shifts are shown in FDA Table 49 but were
not included in the ziftomenib label.
