NDA/IND Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219379/219389
Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant’s description of the protocol amendments is consistent with what was provided
in the submission. FDA notes that in Amendment 1, the inclusion criteria were revised so that
patients with progressive PN and no associated morbidities were not eligible to enroll. We also
note that while inclusion criterion number 5 was modified in Amendment 2 to specify that he
PN must be analyzable by volumetrics using the REINS criteria, a modified version of REINS
criteria was utilized in this study as described in Section 8.1, Efficacy results. As described by the
Applicant in Amendment 4, the primary endpoint language was modified to specify that the
ORR will be assessed at the end of the Treatment Phase, therefore, patients with objective
responses during the long-term follow-up phase were not included in FDA’s analysis.
