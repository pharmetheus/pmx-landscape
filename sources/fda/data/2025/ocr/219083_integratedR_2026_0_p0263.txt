Results

©® was not mutagenic in any conditions used for this study. Revertant
colony number slightly exceeded the 98% tolerance threshold for negative control data
at 50 ug/plate and 158 ug/plate with S9 for strains TA98 and TA1537 respectively.
However, revertant colony number did not exceed the >=2-fold increase cutoff above
negative controls for any strain to be considered positive, and there was no dose-
dependent increase in revertant colonies for any strain.
