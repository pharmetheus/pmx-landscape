PK Analysis

The 38 C1-INH subjects who received garadacimab in Treatment Periods 1 and 2 were included
in the PK population, which comprised all subjects for whom at least one measurable
concentration of garadacimab was reported. All the six FXII/PLG HAE subjects were included in
the PK population in Treatment Period 1. Both FXII/PLG HAE subjects were included in the PK
population in Treatment Period 2.
