A summary of efficacy data in subjects with HAE who tested positive for ADAs (both
pretreatment and treatment-emergent) is shown in Table 98. Among all subjects, there was a
reduction in the number of HAE attacks per month during the Treatment Period relative to that
during the Run-in Period. The observed efficacy in ADA-positive subjects was consistent with
that observed for the overall population. Overall, there was no apparent impact of ADAs on the
efficacy of garadacimab.
