Komzifti (ziftomenib)

5.5.4. Reproductive and Developmental Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

Reproductive and developmental toxicity studies conducted with ziftomenib are limited to dose-
tange finding and definitive embryo-fetal studies in mice. As the definitive study was positive
for teratogenicity, a confirmatory study in a second species (eg, rabbit) was not warranted.

Per the ICH S9 guideline, studies of fertility and early embryonic development or pre- and
postnatal toxicology are not warranted and therefore were not performed. In the 13-week repeat
dose toxicity study in dogs test article-related microscopic findings in the reproductive tracts
were observed after dosing at 6, 20, or 60 mg/kg/day indicating a potential adverse effect of
ziftomenib on human fertility. Juvenile animal studies were not conducted, consistent with the
guidance provided in the ICH S9 guideline.
