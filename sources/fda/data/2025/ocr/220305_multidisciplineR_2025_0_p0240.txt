Overall, the spectrum of TESAEs with ziftomenib were generally consistent with expected
events in patients with R/R AML and those from therapy that induce differentiation.

Dropouts and/or Discontinuations

FDA conducted an analysis of TEAEs leading to dose interruptions in the main safety population
of Study KO-MEN-001 as shown in FDA Table 46 below. This shows the results for TEAEs
with a frequency of 2% or greater. TEAEs leading to dose interruptions occurred in 54% of
patients in the main safety population of Study KO-MEN-001. The most frequent TEAEs leading
to dose interruptions were infection, DS, febrile neutropenia, leukocytosis, pyrexia,
electrocardiogram QT prolonged, and bacterial infection.
