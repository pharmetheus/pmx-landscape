NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 218881

Inluriyo (imlunestrant)

The primary endpoints were investigator assessed progression free survival (PFS) in the ESRJm
population and the ITT population (ESR/m and ESR1m-not detected). The secondary endpoints
were overall survival (OS) in the ESR/m and ITT populations. Randomization was stratified by
previous treatment with a CDK4/6 inhibitor (yes vs no), presence of visceral metastasis (yes vs
no), and region (East Asia vs North America/Western Europe vs Others). ESR/ mutational status
was determined by blood circulating tumor deoxyribonucleic acid (ctDNA) using the
Guardant360 CDx assay and was limited to specific ESR/ mutations in the ligand binding
domain.

EMBER-3 demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement in PFS
by investigator in the ESR/m population in this replacement trial. Median PFS was 5.5 months
versus 3.8 months (HR 0.62, 95% CI: 0.46-0.82; p-value 0.0008). Results from the secondary
endpoints of PFS by blinded independent review committee (BIRC), objective response rate
(ORR) and duration of response (DoR) in the ESR/m population appeared to be supportive of the
primary endpoint results. The PFS endpoint in the ITT population did not cross the prespecified
significance alpha level and therefore was not statistically significant. The secondary OS
endpoint in the ESR/m population was immature (31% maturity), and the interim analysis of OS
at the time of final PFS analysis was not statistically significant. However, there was no evidence
of detriment in the ESR/m population (HR 0.91, 95% CI: 0.56-1.47). OS could not be tested in
the ITT population since PFS in the overall population failed to meet the prespecified threshold.

The FDA review team conducted an exploratory analysis of investigator-assessed PFS for the
comparison of the imlunestrant arm and TPC arm in the ESR/m-not detected (ESR/m-nd)
population and excluded 20 patients whose ESR/ mutation status was failed or missing (n=385).
The HR was 1.04 (95% CI: 0.82, 1.33). The observed median PFS was 5.6 (95% CI: 3.8, 7.3)
months in the imlunestrant arm and 5.6 (95% CI: 5.5, 7.4) months in the TPC arm.
