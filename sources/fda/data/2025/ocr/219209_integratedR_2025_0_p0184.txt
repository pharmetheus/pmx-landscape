14.3. Bioanalytical Method Validation and
Performance

For the determination of plasma SUZ and M6-SUZ concentrations, the Applicant used a liquid
chromatography with tandem mass spectrometric detection (LC-MS/MS) method. The Applicant
submitted bioanalytical method life cycle information to support SUZ and M6-SUZ

quantification in human plasma from various clinical studies (validation reports Q136, Q137,
Q222, R116, and T411).

To quantify SUZ and M6-SUZ from plasma samples, a 50-1L aliquot of plasma sample is
fortified with 50-1L stable labeled internal standard working solution. The analytes are isolated
through liquid/liquid extraction. The solvent is evaporated under nitrogen, then reconstituted
with acetonitrile:water (50:50, v/v). The final extract is analyzed via high-performance liquid
chromatography with MS/MS detection using positive ion electrospray. The concentration of the
samples was determined from a linear regression of the standard curve with 1/x? weighting. This
