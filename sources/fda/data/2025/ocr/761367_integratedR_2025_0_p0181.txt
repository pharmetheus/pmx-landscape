Andembry / garadacimab

The mean (SD) garadacimab concentration-time profiles for adults versus adolescents are shown
in Figure 39. The PK of garadacimab in adolescents appears consistent with that in adults. The
mean (SD) garadacimab trough concentrations in adolescents from Day 60 through the
remainder of the Treatment Period ranged from 7297 (2085) ng/mL to 9688 (2421) ng/mL. In
adults, trough concentrations ranged from 7969 (4257) ng/mL to 8593 (4006) ng/mL (data
derived from Table 14.2.5.1.2, CSR for trial 3001).

The final pooled population PK model was used to simulate steady state PK parameters for all
subjects in trial 3001. Refer to Section 14.5 for additional details on the population PK analysis.
Parameter estimates are shown in Table 81. Exposure following the initial 400 mg SC loading
dose was also predicted using the final population PK model. The mean (SD) model predicted
AUCiau and Cmax following the 400 mg SC loading dose were 13700 (4140) ng*h/mL and 28.5
(9.0) g/mL. Garadacimab exposure after the loading dose is thus about 133% of that at steady
state following administration of 200 mg SC once monthly (refer to Applicant’s response to IR,
submitted September 20, 2024).
