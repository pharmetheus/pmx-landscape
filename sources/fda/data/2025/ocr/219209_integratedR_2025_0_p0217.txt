Model Development

The PBPK analyses were performed using the PBPK software Simcyp® V22 (Simcyp Ltd.,
Sheffield, UK).

preclinical and clinical PK data. The SUZ 50-mg! tablet is an immediate-release tablet with

based on using PK data from VX19-548-001 (single ascending dose study), VX20-548-002
(multiple ascending dose study), and VX21-548-008 (DDI study with itraconazole).

The full PBPK distribution model was utilized, and steady-state volume of distribution (V,,) was
predicted via Method 2 in Simcyp software. Furthermore, a tissue-to-plasma partitioning
coefficient scalar (Kp scalar) of 0.55 was applied to fit the observed SUZ PK data from the
following clinical studies: VX21-548-008 (itraconazole DDI study), VX22-548-013 (rifampin
DDI study), VX21-548-011 (relative bioavailability study).

SUZ intravenous clearance (CL;,) was set as 5.8 L/h based on the PBPK model-predicted.
bioavailability value (F) and the observed apparent oral CL values in Study VX21-548-011 and
VX22-548-013, assuming minimal first-pass elimination in the gut (Response to the IR received
on July 24, 2024). SUZ’s fm (fraction metabolized) values for CYP3A4, CYP2C19, and
CYP2C9 were set to 0.85, 0.10, and 0.05, respectively, based on CYP reaction phenotyping
studies in recombinant CYP enzymes in human liver microsomes (Report R161). M6-SUZ’s fm
values for CYP3A4 and CYP2C9 were set to 0.85 and 0.15, respectively, according to Report
R184. These fmCYP (fraction of hepatic clearance of the substrate mediated by an individual
CYP enzyme) values were confirmed by two DDI study results with rifampin (VX22-548-013)
or itraconazole (VX21-548-008).
