7.6.3. Safety Results, Trial 2001

Treatment Period 1 of trial 2001 was a phase 2, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled,
parallel-group, dose-ranging trial in 44 adults with HAE. The trial randomized 32 subjects to one
of three dosage regimens (75 mg Q4 weeks, 200 mg Q4 weeks, 600 mg Q4 weeks) and assigned
an additional six subjects to open-label garadacimab 400 mg Q2 weeks and an additional six
subjects with Type III HAE to open-label garadacimab 600 mg Q4 weeks. A total of eight
subjects received the proposed dose of garadacimab 200 mg Q4 weeks. See Section 15.3.1 for
details of the study design. Overall, the safety results from Treatment Period 1 of trial 2001 were
consistent with those of trial 3001; no new risks were identified.

7.6.4. 120-Day Safety Update

The Applicant submitted a 120-day safety update for this BLA with a data cutoff of June 13,
2023, that consisted solely of data from study 3002, the only ongoing clinical investigation.
Updated safety data from study 3002 included no deaths, one drug rash SAE due to dipyrone,
and one treatment discontinuation due to an ISR. The ISR consisted of a large itchy wheal,
starting within 24 h of garadacimab administration, that was mild in severity and resolved after
5 days. Commonly reported TEAEs were generally consistent with the safety profile observed to
date. The safety update did not identify any new or concerning safety signals for garadacimab.
