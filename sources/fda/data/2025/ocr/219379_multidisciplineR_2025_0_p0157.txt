Actual dose intensity = cumulative dose received/number of prescribed dosing days.

Source: ADSL, ADEX, ADEX2

The Applicant's Position:

A total of 56 (100%) participants in the pediatric cohort received at least 1 dose of study
treatment. The median duration of study treatment exposure was almost 2 years with a
maximum duration of over 3 years, suggesting sustained tolerability over an extended period of
treatment in the pediatric cohort.

A total of 58 (100%) participants in the adult cohort received at least 1 dose of study treatment.
The median duration of study treatment exposure was almost 2 years with a maximum
duration of almost 4 years, suggesting sustained tolerability over an extended period of
treatment in the adult cohort.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s description of concomitant medication use and treatment
compliance.
