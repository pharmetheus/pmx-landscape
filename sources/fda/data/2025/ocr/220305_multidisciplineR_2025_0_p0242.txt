Significant Adverse Events

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that blood cytopenias, infection, and DS were
the most common severe TEAEs. The FDA conducted an analysis of all-grade and grade 3-4
TEAEs with FDA grouped terms in FDA Table 48 below, with infection further clarified as:
bacterial, fungal, or viral infection, or infection without an identified pathogen. The FDA also
notes that in the context of a single-arm study of patients with R/R AML, severe cytopenias are
expected as the underlying disease progresses or fails to respond to treatment. With that context
in mind, the rates of cytopenias observed on the study were not considered to occur at a rate
higher than expected for the enrolled population.

Treatment Emergent Adverse Events

FDA tabulations of TEAEs agreed with the Applicant’s with regard to the rate of any TEAE,
grade 3 or 4 TEAEs, grade 5 TEAEs, and TEAEs leading to either dose discontinuations, dose
reductions, or dose interruptions. FDA Table 48 shows the FDA tabulation of the most common
grade all grade and grade 3- 4 TEAEs that occurred in at least 10% of subjects or were grade 3-4
in at least 5% of subjects on Study KO-MEN-001.

Due to the high rate of TEAEs observed in patients with R/R AML, the FDA identified those
TEAEs that occurred in at least 20% of subjects or were grade 3-4 in at least 5% of subjects as
those clinically relevant for ziftomenib labeling. These are marked in FDA Table 48 below with
(*) following the preferred term and were included in labeling. Most laboratory TEAEs were not
included in the USPI as they were better handled with the lab shift analysis discussed below. As
the Applicant’s analysis similarly showed, the most common TEAEs were GI toxicity, infectious
