PK Results

Mean (SD) garadacimab concentration-time profiles by dose group for SC cohorts are shown in
Figure 30. PK parameters by dose group are shown in Table 74. Across SC cohorts, Tmax Was
approximately 6 to 7 days, and the mean half-life was approximately 18 to 19 days. These data
are consistent with observations in study 1001. Between doses of 200 and 600 mg SC, Cmax and
AUCo in increased approximately two-fold for a three-fold increase in dose.
