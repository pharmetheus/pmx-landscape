FDA agrees with the description of the inclusion and exclusion criteria for the NF-106 study.

Study Endpoints
The Applicant’s Description:

Primary Endpoint:

e The primary efficacy endpoint was confirmed ORR defined as the proportion of
participants who have a BOR of PR or CR by the end of treatment with 24 weeks
duration.

Secondary Endpoints:

e Safety endpoints included TEAEs, serious adverse events (SAEs), adverse event of special
interest (AESIs), changes from baseline in laboratory parameters, vital signs and retinal
exam abnormalities

e Duration of response for participants whose best response was CR or PR

e@ Change in pain and quality of life scores from baseline as measured by NRS-11, BPI, and
PedsQL NF1 module.
