Effects on Reproductive Organs
Study Title/ Study Number: Submission to the Licensing Authority in Respect to Marketing a
New Drug Treosulfan Leo, Vol. 2, Experimental and Biological Studies/ Leo-1978b

Key Study Findings
e Treosulfan demonstrated dose-dependent effects on male and female reproductive
organs. Effects on spermatogenesis and ovary function indicate that treosulfan can
impair fertility in both males and females.

GLP compliance: Unknown

Methods

months

Route of administration: Stomach tube

Formulation/Vehicle: Dissolved in saccharose solution (2 mL/kg)

Species/Strain: Rat/ Wistar

Number/Sex/Group: 10/sex/group
