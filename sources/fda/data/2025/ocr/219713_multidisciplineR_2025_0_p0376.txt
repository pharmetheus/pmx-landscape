Effects of taletrectinib on OATP1B substrate pravastatin

The PBPK analysis cannot rule out a potential inhibition effect of taletrectinib on OATP1B
substrates for reasons discussed below:

Drug interactions of pravastatin with OATP1B1/3 inhibitors were frequently not
predicted using the pravastatin model in the Simcyp library, even when the lowest in vitro
transporter inhibition parameters of the inhibitors in the Certara DDI Database were applied in
the simulations. The observed interactions with pravastatin could only be reproduced, within 0.8
-1.25 of clinical observations, when the lowest experimentally generated unbound Ki or ICso
values of OATP1B1/3 inhibitors were reduced approximately 10- to 100-fold (2024 ISSX
webinar on Challenges in Predicting PK for Transporter Substrates ). These lowest Ki or ICso
parameters were mostly generated using estradiol-17B-glucuronide as an OATP1B1 probe
substrate in HEK293-OATP1B1 cells with a 15- to 60-minute preincubation (data not shown).

Taletrectinib 600 QD was predicted to increase pravastatin AUC and Cmax up to 1.7-fold
when K; values for both OATP1B1 and OATP1B3 were reduced up to 200-fold (Table 97).
Notably, taletrectinib OATP1B1 inhibition study for taletrectinib utilized estrone-3-sulfate as a
probe substrate, which is known to be 6- to 15-fold less sensitive to OATP1B inhibition
compared to using estradiol-17B-glucuronide (PMID: 23920221). Additionally, the use of a
predicted rather than measured nonspecific binding value in the PBPK model introduces another
layer of uncertainty to the predicted results.
