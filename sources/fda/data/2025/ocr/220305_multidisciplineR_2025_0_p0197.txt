Clinical Benefit Endpoint(s)

For nonmyelosuppressive drugs given in settings without curative intent, the FDA has accepted
durable CR + CRh for regulatory decision making, with adequate blood count recovery and
transfusion independence as corroborating evidence. Ziftomenib is considered a
nonmyelosuppressive agent (see Section 8.3). The KO-MEN-001 SAP specified CR + CRh rate
as the primary efficacy endpoint. Secondary endpoints included duration of response and
achievement of transfusion-independence. The review team established efficacy on the basis of
CR + CRh, the duration of CR + CRh, the rate of conversion from transfusion dependence to
transfusion independence, and the rate of maintenance of transfusion independence in
participants who were independent of transfusions at study enrollment (FDA Table 37).
