NDA/IND Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219379/219389
Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

Precautions.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The safety databases for Pool 1 and the ReNeu study were analyzed at each level of the
MedDRA hierarchy for TEAEs. TEAEs are defined as AEs that occurred from the time of the first
dose up to 30 days after the last dose of mirdametinib or the data cutoff date if that date was
earlier. All patients in the adult and pediatric population that were exposed to mirdametinib
had at least one AE during treatment. FDA’s analysis of adverse events in Pool 1 and the ReNeu
study did not consider relatedness as part of the safety review.

Table 60 represents the most frequent AEs (220%) observed in the ReNeu study. The most
common adverse reactions (>25%) in the adult population were rash, diarrhea, nausea,
musculoskeletal pain, vomiting, and fatigue. The most common adverse reactions (>25%) in the
pediatric population were rash, diarrhea, musculoskeletal pain, abdominal pain, vomiting,
headache, paronychia, left ventricular dysfunction, and nausea.
