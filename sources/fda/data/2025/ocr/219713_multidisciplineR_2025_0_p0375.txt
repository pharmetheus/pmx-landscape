Effects of taletrectinib on CYP2D6 substrates desipramine and dextromethorphan

Taletrectinib is expected to have weak to moderate inhibitory effects on CYP2D6 substrates in
general cancer patients for reasons discussed below.

The Applicant assessed the inhibition potential of taletrectinib on CYP2D6 by applying
the in vitro ICSO value generated using bufuralol as a CYP2D6 substrate (VPT1184/2013).
Because substrate-dependent inhibition with CYP2D6 was observed in vitro (PMID21976621),
the predicted drug interactions with desipramine or dextromethorphan need to be interpretated
with caution.

The verification of the desipramine model has been discussed in the PBPK review of
capivasertib (NDA218197 PBPK review). Although there is a trend towards underprediction, the
overall performance of the desipramine model seems reasonable. Similarly, the
dextromethorphan model can reasonably predict its interaction with quinidine, fluoxetine and
cinacalcet (CYP2D6 V21 Qualification). During the capivasertib PBPK review, the reviewer
found that the in vitro Ki, values were approximately 2- to 40-fold greater than those used in the
PBPK models of CYP2D6 inhibitors. In other words, the in vitro Ki, value of an inhibitor needs
to be reduced by 2- to 40-fold to reproduce the observed effects of this inhibitor on the exposure

of CYP2D6 substrates (NDA218197 PBPK review).
