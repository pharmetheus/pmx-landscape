E-R analysis for diarrhea

Age was identified as a covariate in the ER analysis for diarrhea of grade >3 where a prominent
increase in diarrhea event onset was observed in 4" quartile of age bin [69-91]. Therefore, geriatric
subgroup with 65- and 75-year cutoff was evaluated. When age was subgroup to be groups of >
75 and < 75, the categorized age is also identified a covariate in Cox proportional hazard model
with similar OFV value as that of age as a continuous covariate that produced comparable AIC.
Furthermore, the Kaplan-Meier plot of diarrhea of grade >3 stratified by age groups showed a clear
difference between two groups, shown in Figure 21. Based on CoxPH analysis, patients aged >
75 years was associated with 2.32 folds risk of grade >3 diarrhea than those aged <75 years. The
wide CI reflects the limited subjects in this age subgroup.
