NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

Dosing in patient | Intrinsic factors /Section 6.3.2.3]

subgroups Organ impairment: No dosage adjustments are recommended in mild-to-

moderate RI or HI.

There is no clinically significant effect of mild-to-moderate RI
(creatinine clearance [CLc;] 30 to 89 mL/min) or HI (bilirubin less than
or equal to upper limit of normal [ULN] and aspartate aminotransferase
[AST] greater than ULN or bilirubin less than or equal to 3 x ULN) on
ziftomenib PK.

The impact of severe RI or HI is unknown. Post-marketing requirements
(PMRs) will be issued to evaluate the impact of severe RI and HI on
ziftomenib PK.

Other intrinsic factors: No dosage adjustments are recommended based
on age, sex, race, ethnicity, body weight or body surface area (BSA).

There is no clinically significant effect of age (18 to 86 years), sex (51%
female), race (75% White, 5.3% Black or African American, 3.2%
Asian), ethnicity (6.3% Hispanic or Latino), body weight (41 to 135 kg),
or BSA (1.40 to 2.46 m?) on ziftomenib PK.

Extrinsic factors [Section 6.3.2.4]
Metabolic DDIs

Strong or moderate CYP3A inhibitors: No dosage adjustments are
recommended in patients receiving strong or moderate CYP3A
inhibitors; however, additional safety monitoring is recommended.

Ziftomenib is primarily metabolized by CYP3A. Concomitant use of
strong CYP3A inhibitors increases ziftomenib exposure by ~2-fold,
which may increase risk of adverse reactions such as QTc prolongation.

The Applicant has an ongoing study, Study KO-MEN-001 Sub-study 2,
to evaluate the impact of itraconazole (strong CYP3A and P-gp inhibitor)
on ziftomenib PK. Given that the study is ongoing, a PMR will be issued
for its completion.

Strong or moderate CYP3A inducers: Concomitant use of strong or
moderate CYP3A inducers should be avoided.

Ziftomenib is primarily metabolized by CYP3A. Concomitant use of
strong or moderate CYP3A inducers may decrease ziftomenib exposure,
which may decrease efficacy.
