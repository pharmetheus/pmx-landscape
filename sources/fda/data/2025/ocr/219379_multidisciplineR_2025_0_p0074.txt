Bone findings in rats at 1 mg/kg in the 1-month study included minimal to moderate
hypocellularity of the metaphyseal region of the femur and distal tibia and findings at 20.3
mg/kg in the 3-month study, included minimal to mild dysplasia and thickening of the
trabeculae. While these bone findings occurred in both sexes, males appeared to be more
sensitive to these effects. Findings in long bone are relevant considering the intended
population includes pediatric patients and were included in section 8.4 of the GOMEKLI label.
These findings were not noted at the end of the recovery period in the 1-month study in rats.

Male and female reproductive organs were target organs in the 3-month study in rats. See
Section 5.5.4 for details. Findings noted in male reproductive organs (epididymis, prostate, and
testis) in the 3-month study in dogs were not added to the label as the relationship of these
findings to mirdametinib was uncertain given that descriptions of these findings in the study
report did not clearly distinguish differences in severity.
