e Impact of early censoring in PFS analysis

For the comparison of imlunestrant vs TPC in the ZSR1m population, more patients in the TPC
arm were censored early in the PFS analysis compared to those in the imlunestrant arm: 12
patients (10.2%) in the TPC arm were censored within 4 months after randomization compared
to 7 patients (5.1%) in the imlunestrant arm. Per FDA’s request, the Applicant conducted a
tipping point analysis for PFS (Figure 8.7). The results suggest that even if the early censored
patients in the TPC arm were to have at least 75% lower risk of PFS compared to the other
chemotherapy arm patients, the 96% CI of the estimated PFS HR still won’t cross 1. Based on
the tipping point analysis, early censoring in the TPC arm were unlikely to change the statistical
significance conclusion for PFS in the ESR/m-detected population.
