Grafapex (treosulfan)

Victim drug models

Midazolam

The midazolam PBPK model was used to predict the DDI potential of treosulfan as a CYP3A4
inhibitor. The Applicant’s midazolam PBPK model was based on Hanke et al.*. The performance
of midazolam PBPK model as a CYP3A substrate was validated with 32 clinical studies.
Representative model validation and DDI evaluation results were shown in Figure 23.
