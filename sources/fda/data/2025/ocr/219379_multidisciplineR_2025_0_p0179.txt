Additional Analyses Conducted on the Individual Trial

Data:

Analysis of exploratory endpoints showed the following for the pediatric cohort:

The overall median (min, max) change from baseline at Cycle 13 for the PROMIS
mobility scale score was 7.0 (3, 11) by self-report and 3.0 (-3, 11) by parent proxy.

The median (min, max) change from baseline at Cycle 13 for the PROMIS upper
extremity scale score was 11.5 (9, 14) by self-report and 9.5 (7, 12) by parent proxy.

The median (min, max) change from baseline at Cycle 13 was 4.7 (1, 95) Ibs in
strength assessment using MicroFET2 dynamometer. The median (min, max) change
from baseline at Cycle 13 in strength assessment using MRC grading was 0.1 (0, 1);
given the median score at baseline was 4.4 out of 5, there was not much room for
improvement in this assessment.

The median (min, max) change from baseline at Cycle 13 in range of motion was
15.0 (-189, 70) degrees.

The median (min, max) change from baseline at Cycle 13 in the 6-minute walk test
distance was 15.0 (-99, 55) meters.

The median TTP was not reached for all participants or for participants with a
progressive tumor at baseline due to few events of disease progression.
