The FDA’s Assessment:

Based on individual population PK analysis, FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that no
dosage adjustment is required for age (18 to 91 years), race (27% White, 65% Asian), sex, body
weight (29 to 155 kg), smoking status, renal impairment (eGFR 30 to < 90 mL/min), or mild
hepatic impairment (AST > ULN and total bilirubin < ULN; or total bilirubin > 1 to 1.5x ULN
and any AST).

Given the small sample size of Black/African American patients (n=9) included in the pivotal
study 21607, a definitive conclusion of comparable PK cannot be made for this patient
population. Therefore, FDA does not agree with Applicant’s assessment that no dosage
adjustment is required for Black/African American patients based on current data. FDA
anticipates that additional data to inform recommendations may be available from SOHO-02
which is intended to verify the clinical benefit of sevabertinib (refer to Section 13).

In addition, FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s DDI assessment using the population PK
model to evaluate the effect of moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors and moderate CYP3A4 inducers on
the PK of sevabertinib given these population PK estimates are not robust or reliable. Refer to
Section 6.3.2.4 for detailed DDI evaluation.
