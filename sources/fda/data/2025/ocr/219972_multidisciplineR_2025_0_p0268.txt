NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0
10 Pediatrics

The Applicant’s Position:

The Applicant plans to request a full waiver of the pediatric study requirement for sevabertinib
for all pediatric patients (i.e., neonates, infants and children aged 0 to <18 years) because studies
are impossible or highly impractical to execute (because the number of pediatric patients is so
small or geographically dispersed) (Section 505B(a)(4)(A)(i) of the Pediatric Research Equity
Act).

Further reference is made to the FDA’s “Agreed Initial Pediatric Study Plan — Agreement” letter,
dated 26 NOV 2024 (Reference ID 5486478), in which the FDA agreed with Bayer’s plan not to
develop sevabertinib for the proposed indication in the pediatric population.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. The approval letter will state that the requirements of
PREA will be waived.
