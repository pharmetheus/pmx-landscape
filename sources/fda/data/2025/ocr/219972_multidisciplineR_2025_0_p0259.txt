Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0
Pediatrics and Assessment of Effects on Growth

Data:

Please see The Applicant’s Position below.

The Applicant’s Position:

Sevabertinib has not been studied in a pediatric population. The Sponsor plans to request a full
waiver for sevabertinib for pediatric patients. On 26 NOV 2024, the FDA agreed with the
Agreed iPSP that was submitted on 24 OCT 2024 (Reference ID 5486478).

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position.
Overdose, Drug Abuse Potential, Withdrawal, and Rebound

Data:
Please see The Applicant’s Position below.

The Applicant’s Position:

The highest dose of sevabertinib studied clinically was 40 mg BID, equivalent to a TDD of
80 mg. At this dose, the DLTs observed were stomatitis, vomiting, and diarrhea. There is no
specific antidote for sevabertinib overdose. In the event of an overdose, the patient should be
closely monitored, and general supportive treatment should be considered based on signs and
symptoms.

No data are available on the drug abuse or dependency potential for sevabertinib. Withdrawal
effects were not evaluated upon discontinuation of treatment.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
