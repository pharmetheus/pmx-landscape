Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

General Information

Goal of ER analysis PKPDx models were developed to evaluate the ERR for
mirdametinib and PD-0315209 in participants with NF1 PN,
which included the following objectives:

@ Characterize the ERR between mirdametinib and PD-
0315209 exposure and the incidence events of Grade 2
or higher with at least 5% incidence and at least 10
events, the ER relationship was evaluated using logistic
regression models or ordered categorical regression
models.

Study Included MEK-NF-201 and NF-106 included adult and pediatric

participants with NF1 PN receiving a target dose of 2 mg/m?

BID mirdametinib for 3 weeks on and 1 week off with a

maximum dose of 4 mg (Mirdametinib E-R Report Table 1).

Population Included 133 participants were included in safety analyses.

Endpoint Thirteen selected TEAEs were evaluated for grade 1+
occurrence and grade 2+ occurrence: Acneiform rash, Rash,
left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) decrease, Paronychia
(in pediatric cohort only), Fatigue & Malaise, Headache &
Migraine, Leukopenic events, Eye disorders, Gastrointestinal
disorders, alkaline phosphatase (ALP) elevation, alanine
aminotransferase (ALT) elevation, aspartate
aminotransferase (AST) elevation, and creatine
phosphokinase (CPK) increase. Exposure response of dose
reduction (binary) and time to first dose reduction were also

explored.
