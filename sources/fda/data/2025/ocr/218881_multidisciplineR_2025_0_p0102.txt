2. Effect of Imlunestrant on Other Drugs:
a) P-gp substrates: Digoxin (P-gp substrate) AUCo int increased 1.4-fold and Cmax

increased 1.6-fold following concomitant use of single dose imlunestrant 400 mg
(Table 6.9).

b) BCRP substrates: Rosuvastatin (BCRP substrate) AUCo-ing increased 1.5-fold and
Cmax increased 1.7-fold following concomitant use of single dose imlunestrant 400
mg (Table 6.10).

FDA disagrees with the Applicant’s proposal a

. Concomitant use of P-gp and BCRP substrates should be avoided unless otherwise
recommended in the Prescribing Information of these substrates given that concomitant use with
imlunestrant leads to an increase in exposure of these substrates which may increase the risk of
adverse reactions related to substrate exposure.
