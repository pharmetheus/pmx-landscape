TEAEs by Race and Ethnicity
Analysis of TEAEs by race and ethnicity is challenging due to the small number of patients in

each category present in the key safety population. To investigate any large variations by race,
patients were broken down by white (n=88) versus non-white (n=24); the rates of TEAEs in the
key safety population were then analyzed with a focus of a risk difference greater than 10
between groupings (FDA Table 54). Overall events were similar between white patients and all
others, and no clear signal for safety difference was observed between groupings.
