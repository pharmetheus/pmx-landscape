NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

In order to complement the rat EFD studies, the ReproTracker assay (Study T106172-7) was
conducted. In this assay, differentiation of hiPSCs into cardiomyocytes, hepatocytes, and neural
rosettes was investigated using morphology. Biomarker gene expression was assessed using
qRT-PCR. The ReproTracker assay’s validity was established by exposing hiPSCs to known
teratogenic (thalidomide/retinoic acid) and non-teratogenic (saccharin) compounds. Sevabertinib
did not significantly affect the mesodermal marker but decreased MYH6 expression in a
concentration-dependent manner. Changes in MYH6 expression were noted starting at 1.0 1M,
indicating disrupted cardiomyocyte differentiation. Sevabertinib also decreased AFP expression
from 0.2 iM, disrupting hepatocyte differentiation and morphology at 2.0 1M. Additionally, it
exhibited a U-shaped response in neural rosette markers and disrupted their morphology at
higher concentrations. Ultimately, ReproTracker classified sevabertinib as a teratogen due to its
effects on stem cell differentiation.

Prenatal and postnatal development studies were not conducted and are not warranted per ICH
S9 guidance for nonclinical safety assessment of pharmaceuticals intended for the treatment of
advanced cancer.

Sevabertinib: Oral (Gavage) Enhanced Range-Finding Study of Embryo-Fetal
Development in the Rat (Module 4.2.3.5, Report B003097, GLP)

Key Drug-related Adverse Findings:

There were three sevabertinib-related mortalities in the high dose group.

The main clinical observations were piloerection, reduced body weight, liquid feces.
Only marginal increase in post-implantation loss and early resorptions were noted for
animals administered 11 mg/kg/day. This dose was considered excessive due to severe
maternal toxicity.
