PK Results

Treatment Period 1

Mean (SD) garadacimab concentration-time profiles by dose group for blinded cohorts (i.e., 75,
200, or 600 mg Q4W with IV loading doses of 40, 100, and 300 mg, respectively) are shown in
Figure 34, for the open-label C1-INH cohort (i.e., 400 mg Q2W with no IV loading dose) in
Figure 35, and for the FXI/PLG HAE cohort (300 mg IV then 600 mg Q4W) in Figure 36. For
all cohorts that received an IV loading dose, peak concentrations were reached after the end of
IV infusion. Following the beginning of SC dosing, plasma concentrations declined, with steady
state reached after three doses (approximately Day 35, Week 5). In subjects receiving 400 mg SC
Q2W with no IV loading dose, steady state appears to have been reached by Day 56 (Week 8).
