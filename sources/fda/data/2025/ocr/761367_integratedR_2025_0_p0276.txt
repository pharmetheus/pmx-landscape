Baseline Demographics and Clinical Characteristics

The demographics and clinical characteristics of the ITT set at baseline are summarized in
Table 116. Imbalances in age, sex, race, and ethnicity are noted across the treatment arms. Due
to the small size of the trial (HAE is a rare condition), these imbalances are expected. This trial
was only powered to detect a difference between 200 mg garadacimab versus placebo and
between 600 mg garadacimab versus placebo; as such, a sample size of seven to eight subjects
per treatment arm was established (see Section 15.3.8 for more details). The baseline
demographics and clinical characteristics of the subjects in this study are generally reflective of
the overall HAE population and are generally reasonable for a phase 2 HAE trial.
