. Multiple product quality deficiencies were also

Lastly, a withhold approval was recommended for the DP manufacturing facility, CSL
Behring GmbH, Marburg, Germany based on the pre-license inspection and facility
compliance review.

In this CR resubmission, the Applicant demonstrated that the manufacturing processes and
overall control strategies for garadacimab were appropriately established to ensure
consistency and quality of the final product and thus has adequately addressed the
deficiencies outlined in the CRL. The Applicant and Agency have reached agreement on the
product labeling that includes the carton & container, Prescribing Information (Pl), Patient
Information, and Instructions for Use. The recommended regulatory action from all members
of the multidisciplinary review team is Approval.
