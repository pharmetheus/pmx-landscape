The Applicant’s Position:

The overall incidence of TEAEs with Grade 3 as the worst grade in severity was consistent
across the 3 safety populations (36.1% to 42.0%) (Table 67). The most common TEAEs with
Grade 3 as the worst grade were diarrhea and hypokalemia, occurring in 23.5% and 11.1% of
participants in Group D, and in 14.6% and 7.8% of participants in the Whole Study 20 mg BID
population, respectively. In the majority of participants who experienced TEAEs with Grade 3 as
the worse grade in severity, the events were considered study drug-related by the investigator.

The incidence of TEAEs with Grade 4 as the worst grade in severity was consistent across the
safety populations (2.2% to 2.5%) (Table 67). In Group D, the Grade 4 TEAEs reported were
duodenal ulcer, dyspnea, and hypocalcemia in 1 participant each (1.2%). In the Whole Study 20
ing population, the only TEAE with a worst grade of Grade 4, occurring in >1% of participants,
was hypokalemia (1.1%). Study drug-related TEAEs with Grade 4 as the worst grade occurred in
1 participant (1.2%) in Group D and in 3 participants (1.1%) in the Whole Study 20 mg
population including dyspnea in 1 participant (in Group D) and hypokalemia in 2 participants.

Source (study drug-related TEAEs): Module 5.3.5.2 B003047, Table 8.3.1.2/2 [Datasets ADSL, ADAE and ADDS]
