V. Impact of UGT1A1 polymorphisms

In vitro studies demonstrated that uridine 5'-diphospho-glucuronosyltransferase (UGT)1A1 is
involved in the clearance of imlunestrant, however, the relative contribution of various UGT
enzymes toward the overall clearance of imlunestrant remains undetermined. The sponsor
performed a retrospective exploratory analysis to explore the association of UGTJA1
polymorphisms with imlunestrant exposure using a subset of participants enrolled in EMBER-3
and healthy women of childbearing potential enrolled in Study J2J-MC-JZLD. Paired genetic
and PK data were available for a total of 137 subjects. The following genotypes were observed:
