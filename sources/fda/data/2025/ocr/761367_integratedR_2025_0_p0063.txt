Trial 3001 enrolled only subjects with Type I or Type II HAE (subjects with Type III HAE were
excluded). However, the phase 2 trial (2001) included a nonrandomized, open-label treatment
arm for subjects with Type III HAE, which enrolled three subjects with FXII mutation and three
subjects with PLG mutation. Type III HAE, and the six aforementioned subjects in trial 2001, are
discussed further in Section 6.3.2.

Efficacy by Attack Location and Severity

A summary of the HAE attacks experienced during the 6-month Treatment Period by location
and severity is provided in Table 20. Fewer subjects experienced abdominal, cutaneous, and
laryngeal attacks in the garadacimab arm (28%, 26%, and 5%, respectively) compared to the
placebo arm (88%, 88%, and 20%, respectively). While similar conclusions can be drawn for
attack severity, a higher number of mild abdominal attacks were experienced in the garadacimab
arm compared to placebo (20 versus 14), and two subjects in each treatment arm experienced a
severe laryngeal attack.
