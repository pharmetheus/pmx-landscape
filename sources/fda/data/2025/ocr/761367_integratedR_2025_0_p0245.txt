15.1.1. Definition and Collection of HAE Attacks,

Trial 3001

The following items pertaining to an acute HAE attack event, including start and end dates and
times, were recorded by subjects during the study in an eDiary: HAE attack onset and resolution,
HAE symptoms (with location), use of on-demand therapy or medical assistance to treat HAE
symptoms, and interference with daily activities. Participants were encouraged to notify and
report HAE attacks to the study site within 72 h of symptom onset. HAE attacks were confirmed
by the investigator after reviewing the eDiary data at each visit and collecting any additional
missing or relevant information. Per the clinical study protocol (dated August 4, 2020),
investigator-confirmed HAE attacks were defined as meeting all of the following criteria:

e Associated with at least one symptom or location (see Table 108).

e Separated from the previous attack by a minimum of 24 h without symptoms.

e Consisted of symptoms typical of an HAE attack and not just prodromal symptoms.

e Lacked an alternative cause for the symptoms.

e  Lacked use of on-demand medication to only treat prodromal symptoms.
