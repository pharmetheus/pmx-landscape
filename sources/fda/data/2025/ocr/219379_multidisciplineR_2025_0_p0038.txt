NDA/IND Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219379/219389
Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

4 Significant Issues from Other Review Disciplines Pertinent to Clinical
Conclusions on Efficacy and Safety

4.1. Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI!)

Three clinical investigators and the imaging contract research organization (CRO) were
inspected. Inspections of the clinical investigators did not find significant concerns regarding
the study conduct, data integrity, Good Clinical Practice (GCP) or regulatory compliance. The
inspection of the imaging CRO revealed no significant data discrepancies, GCP or regulatory
violations. See Clinical Inspection Summary for full details (uploaded in DARRTS, 1/2/2025) .

4.2. Product Quality

The Integrated Quality Assessment Template (uploaded in DARRTS 12/30/24) supports
approval. The review noted that the original NDA submission contained CMC information for
1 mg tablets;

keeping the 1 mg strength is
acceptable based on the dosing regimen. NDA 219389, submitted simultaneously, contains
relevant data to support the capsule formulation and cross references all other modules in
mirdametinib tablet NDA 219379 for supporting data. The Applicant’s requests to designate
mirdametinib tablets for oral suspension and capsules as a BCS Class 1 drug substance/drug
product were granted. For routine quality control testing of mirdametinib tablets, the
Applicant’s proposal to use disintegration testing (instead of dissolution testing) is acceptable.
The Applicant provided sufficient information to assure the identity, strength, purity, and
quality of the proposed drug product (tablets and capsules). All associated manufacturing,
testing, packaging facilities were deemed acceptable.
