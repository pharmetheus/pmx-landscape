FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity was also evaluated in a subset of garadacimab-naive subjects
who had additional samples collected after the loading dose (n=15). FXTIa-mediated kallikrein
activity over 30 days postdose in garadacimab-naive subjects is shown in Figure 46.
Concentration-dependent inhibition was observed with approximately 40% of baseline activity
observed on Day 7, the time of peak concentration. By Day 30, approximately 75% of baseline
activity was observed. Note that the values of 40% and 75% were visually estimated from

Figure 46.

This trend of concentration-dependent inhibition of FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity was
corroborated by data in adolescent subjects who had additional samples collected following the
Month 3 dose. FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity in adults and adolescents over time is shown in
Figure 47. At the time of peak concentration on Days 3 to 7 following the Month 3 dose
(administered on Day 90), inhibition to about 50% activity was observed. Throughout the
Treatment Period, activity appeared similar between adults and adolescents.
