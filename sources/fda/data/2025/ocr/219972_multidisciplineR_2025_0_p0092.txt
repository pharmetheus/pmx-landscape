NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

Parameters Major findings
Mortality Mortality was observed at the highest dose of 11 mg/kg per day.
Clinical Signs Body weight loss, reduced food consumption, and adverse clinical observations,

such as piloerection, liquid feces, hunched posture, and a thin appearance were
observed following administration of 11 mg/kg/day. Test item-related effects
observed following administration of 7 mg/kg/day included body weight loss,
reduced body weight gain, and food consumption. Clinical observations included
piloerection and a hunched posture. At 3 mg/kg/day initial reduced body weight
gain and small reductions in food consumption were observed, which did not
adversely impact the health of the maternal animal.

Body Weight Reduced body weight and reduced body weight gain were observed starting with
the lowest dose tested.

Feed Consumption Food consumption was reduced.
