Inluriyo (imlunestrant)

e Descriptive analysis of OS in the ESRIm-detected population with additional follow-up

The Applicant submitted SAP Addendum v1 in a meeting request dated November 14, 2024
(Sequence Number 0165). SAP Addendum v1 was amended after data unblinding. In SAP
Addendum v1, the Applicant proposed to remove the anchoring of the OS testing on the OS
endpoint for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm versus the TPC arm in the ITT population,
and to update the event-driven OS interims to time-driven interims for the following OS
endpoints: OS for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm and the TPC arm in the ESR/m-
detected population, and OS for the comparison of the imluntabema arm and the imlunestrant
arm in the ITT population. The FDA advised the Applicant not to change the remaining formal
OS tests between the imlunestrant arm and the TPC arm in the ESR/m-detected population as
they were made after data unblinding and could be data driven. Additionally, the FDA stated that
changing from event-driven to calendar-based analyses would likely lead to inadequately
powered analyses. The FDA warmed the Applicant that analyses conducted under SAP
Amendment v1 would be considered post-hoc and descriptive only.

Despite FDA’s warning, the Applicant proceeded with SAP Addendum v1 and conducted
updated OS analyses with a DCO date of January 30, 2025. Topline results of updated OS
analyses based on the DCO date of January 30, 2025 were provided and discussed “
. The
FDA considered these OS analyses post-hoc and descriptive only. The descriptive analyses of
OS with a DCO date of January 30, 2025 showed that the hazard ratio was 0.60 (95% CI: 0.41,
0.88) for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm and the TPC arm in the ESR/m-detected
population. The observed median OS was 34.4 (95% CI: 25.0, NE) months in the imlunestrant
arm and 22.7 (95% CI: 18.4, 28.9) months in the TPC arm. The descriptive OS analysis results

are consistent with the OS results conducted at IA1 with a DCO date of June 24, 2024.
