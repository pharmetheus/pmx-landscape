The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees that no clinically significant differences in the PK of ziftomenib were
observed based on the following intrinsic factors: age (18 to 86 years), body weight (41 to 135
kg), height (142 to 193 cm), body mass index (BMI, 18.0 to 52.7 kg/m”), BSA (1.40 to 2.46 m’),
sex (51% female), race (75% White, 3.2% Asian, 5.3% Black of African American), ethnicity
(6.3% Hispanic or Latino, 78.5% not Hispanic of Latino), AML mutation (no mutation, VPM/-
m, KM724-t, or others), serum creatinine (SCr, 0.39 to 2.00 mg/dL), mild (CLe; 30 to 59
mL/min) to moderate (CLc, 60 to 89 mL/min) renal impairment, bilirubin (0.2 to 2.3 mg/dL),
albumin (25 to 50 g/L), alkaline phosphatase (ALP, 26 to 748 U/L), alanine transaminase (ALT,
7 to 99 U/L), AST (9 to 69 U/L), or mild (bilirubin less than or equal to ULN and AST greater
than ULN or bilirubin less than or equal to 1.5 x ULN ) to moderate (bilirubin 1.5 to 3 x ULN)
hepatic impairment. For more detailed information, see Section 14.4.2.

The effect of severe renal (CLc; less than 30 mL/min) and hepatic (bilirubin greater than 3 x
ULN) impairment have not been studied; therefore, it is unknown if therapeutic individualization
will be required for these patients and PMRs will be issued (FDA Table 22).

6.3.2.4 Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions, and what is
the appropriate management strategy?

The Applicant’s Position:

Based on clinical evaluation, there is a food effect and interaction with PPIs. To minimize the
impact of food on ziftomenib absorption, it is recommended that ziftomenib be taken on an
empty stomach to ensure consistent absorption and avoid potential overexposure. |

While
co-admunistration of ziftomenib with strong or moderate CYP3A4 inducers should be avoided,
based on modeling discussed in Section 6.2.1, no clinically meaningful interaction is indicated
with strong, moderate or weak CYP3A4, CYP1A2, or CYP2D6 inhibitors.

The FDA’s Assessment:
Food Effect

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position that ziftomenib should be taken on an empty
stomach (1 h before or 2 h after a meal).
