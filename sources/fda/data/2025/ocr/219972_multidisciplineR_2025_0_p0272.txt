12 Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies (REMS)

The FDA’s Assessment:

There were no significant safety concerns identified during the NDA review requiring risk
management beyond labeling or warranting a consideration for a REMS. Sevabertinib will be
prescribed by oncologists who are trained in monitoring, diagnosing, and managing serious
toxicities caused by antineoplastic drugs, including tyrosine kinase inhibitors. Safety concerns
are adequately addressed in the Warnings and Precautions and Dosage and Administration
sections of product labeling.
