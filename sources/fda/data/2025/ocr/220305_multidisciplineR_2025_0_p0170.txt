NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

The FDA’s Assessment:

For the Phase 1 portion, no participants from 1a dose escalation had a confirmed NPM/
mutation; 20 participants from the Phase 1b cohort had a confirmed NPM/ mutation and
received 600 mg of ziftomenib. Therefore, this population (n=20) was considered in the FDA’s
review of efficacy. In the Phase 2 portion, 92 participants with R/R AML with a confirmed
NPM1 mutation were enrolled and treated with 600 mg of ziftomenib. In total, 112 patients were
included in the Pooled Efficacy Cohort, whose disposition is summarized in FDA Table 29 (data
cutoff date 28 October 2024).
