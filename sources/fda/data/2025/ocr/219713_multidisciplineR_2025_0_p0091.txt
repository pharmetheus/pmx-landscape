Sperm analysis

Sperm morphological abnormalities were found in all treatment
groups: 3.4% at 4 mg/kg, 2.5% at 25 mg/kg, and 4.0% at 60 mg/kg,
which were 2.31, 1.71, and 2.72 times higher than vehicle control
group.

No abnormal changes in sperm motility and number were found in
any dose group.

Estrous cycle analysis

No adverse effects of taletrectinib on the estrus cycle.
