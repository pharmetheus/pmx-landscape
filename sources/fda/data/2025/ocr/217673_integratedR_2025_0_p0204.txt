Method description

In this method. INS1007 and internal standard INS10075C¢, were extracted from human plasma
using liquid-liquid extraction. After extraction, the residue was chromatographed using reversed
phase HPLC with a Pursuit C18 analytical column. INS1007 and INS10074Co were detected by
monitoring the precursor and product ions (m/z 421.2100.1 for INS1007 and m/z 427.2100.1
for INS1007#°Css) using an Applied Biosystems Sciex APTS000 or APISS00 LC-MS/MS. The
quantitation was based on setting a calibration graph using the internal standard.
