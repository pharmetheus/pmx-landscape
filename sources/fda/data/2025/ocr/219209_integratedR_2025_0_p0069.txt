7.1.3. Mutagenesis and Carcinogenesis

SUZ was evaluated for genotoxic potential in a full battery of genotoxicity studies, including an
in vitro Ames, an in vitro micronucleus assay, and an in vivo micronucleus assay in rats. SUZ
was not considered mutagenic nor clastogenic in these assays.

The major human metabolite, M6-SUZ, was also negative in an Ames assay and posed minimal
concern for clastogenicity.

7.1.4. Other Toxicity

SUZ drug substance was nonirritant in an in vitro skin irritation test and was not a skin sensitizer
in a local lymph node assay in the mouse. SUZ was not considered an eye irritant based on an
eye hazard potential evaluation using the reconstructed human comea-like epithelium model.
SUZ was nonphotoreactive based on in vitro and in vivo studies.

7.1.5. Impurities
The proposed specifications for drug substance impurities and drug product degradants are

acceptable. Additional impurities of concern were adequately justified for safety.

7.2. Potential Risks or Safety Concerns Based on
Drug Class or Other Drug-Specific Factors

SUZ is a selective Nay1.8 blocker that represents a new pharmacologic class of analgesic
medications. Aside from the safety data for SUZ that were reviewed in this NDA, there are
limited data available to inform safety concerns based on drug class.
