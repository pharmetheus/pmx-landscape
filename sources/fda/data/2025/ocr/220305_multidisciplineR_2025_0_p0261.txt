NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305

Komzifti (ziftomenib)

10 Pediatrics

The Applicant’s Position:
There is currently no data regarding the use of ziftomenib in the pediatric population.

Ziftomenib is directed at inhibiting the menin-MLL(KMT724A) interaction, which is considered a
relevant molecular target for the purposes of Section 505B of the Federal Food, Drug, and
Cosmetic Act. To comply with the pediatric study requirements in Section 505B(e), an initial
pediatric study plan (iPSP) to was agreed to on 16 August 2024; a partial waiver for patients

<1 month of age and a deferral for patients >1 month of age is included in this submission.
