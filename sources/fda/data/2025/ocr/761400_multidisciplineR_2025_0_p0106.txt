The Applicant's Position:

Review and Evaluation Biologics License Application 761400

Overall, the clinical pharmacology data adequately characterized the PK, PD, and
immunogenicity of linvoseltamab. The E-R analyses for efficacy and safety support the
proposed 5 mg/25 mg/200 mg dosing regimen in patients with RRMM.

The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, the FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. General pharmacology and PK
characteristics of linvoseltamab are summarized in Table 20.
