NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

There was no clear relationship between extent of H2RA use and CR + CRh. Efficacy of
ziftomenib in patients receiving H2RA at least 75% of study time appeared comparable to
patients receiving H2RA at 0-10% of study time. Therefore, a staggered dosing of H2RAs (10
hours before or 2 hours after KOMZIFTI administration) appears reasonable, which is also
consistent with ziftomenib dosing guidance with regard to H2RA during KO-MEN-001 (as of
Protocol Version 6 [05/12/2022]).

Antacids: The FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s position that administration of locally-
acting antacids |
The FDA is recommending that administration of locally-acting antacids be staggered 2 hours
before or 2 hours after ziftomenib administration.

There was no dedicated DDI study evaluating the impact of coadministration of ziftomenib with
locally-acting antacids on ziftomenib PK. However, given that coadministration of ziftomenib
with PPIs decreases ziftomenib exposure by ~60 to 70%, it is reasonable to stagger ziftomenib
dosing with locally-acting antacids. Staggering by 2 hours before or 2 hours after ziftomenib
administration may be reasonable given the short duration of action for antacids.

Additional DDIs

QTe-prolonging drugs: The FDA does not agree that ziftomenib does not have clinically
meaningful impact on QTc prolongation, and therefore PD-mediated DDIs with other QTc-
prolonging drugs may occur. The FDA recommends that concomitant use of QTc-prolonging
drugs should be avoided. If concomitant use cannot be avoided, additional safety monitoring is
recommended. Interrupt administration if QTc interval is over 500 ms or change from baseline is
over 60 ms.

Patients receiving ziftomenib 600 mg displayed a concentration-dependent increase in QTc
interval (largest mean increase in QTc predicted to be 7.7 ms), with 8% of patients experiencing
QTcF over 500 ms and QTc increase over 60 ms over baseline.
