Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

Model Structure The relationships between Acceptable
mirdametinib exposure and efficacy
endpoints were evaluated as PKPDx or
ERR models. The PKPDx model
development for tumor size used
individual parameter estimates from
the final PopPK model added to the
PKPDx datasets. For the ERR analyses
of DOR, BOR, and TTP the final PopPK
model was used to calculate individual
estimates of exposure at nominal
steady state.

For the TGI model, a disease
progression model was first developed
based on data from 01-C-0222 and 08-
C-0079. The model was based on a
Gompertz growth model and was
expanded with a natural tumor decay
rate (KD), an age-effect on tumor
growth rate (KG) and KD, and an effect
of baseline tumor volume on
maximum tumor volume (TSmax).

A drug effect for mirdametinib (KDrug)
was then estimated for treated
participants in MEK-NF-201 and NF-
106 with disease progression model
parameters fixed. KDrug is mudeled as
a decaying linear effect as a function
of Cavg,ss with a categorical age effect
on the decay rate for KDrug (A).
Proportional error is included for
Study 80079, Study 10222, and for
each reader in MEK-NF-201. IIV is
included for KG, TSMAX, KD, KDrug,
and A.

Exposure-response relationship with
BOR was evaluated by logistic
regression with participants reaching
stable disease, progressive disease,
and with missing evaluations
categorized as non-responders. AUCO-
12,ss was the best predictor of
response and no statistically
significant covariates were identified.
