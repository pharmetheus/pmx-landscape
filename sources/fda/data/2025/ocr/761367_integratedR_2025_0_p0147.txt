PK and Immunogenicity Sampling

Blood PK samples were collected at predose on Day 1, and postdose at Hours 8, 24, 32, and 48
and Days 5, 8, 15, 22, 29, 43, 64, and 85.

Immunogenicity samples were collected at predose on Day 1, and on Days 29 and 85.

Subject Disposition and Demographics
A total of 132 subjects were randomized to treatment and received garadacimab, including 66
subjects each who received the AI and PFS-NSD (AI: N=22 per injection site; PFS-NSD: N=21

to 23 per injection site). Overall, 131 subjects completed the study. One subject in the AI group
was lost to follow-up and discontinued the study (Table 63).

The mean (SD) body weight at baseline was 73.0 (9.8) kg in the PFS-NSD group and 75.2
(11.5) kg in the AI group. In each device group, most subjects had body weight <80 kg: 46/66
(70%) subjects in the AI group, and 47/66 (71%) subjects in the PFS-NSD group.

PK Analysis

The 132 subjects in the PK Analysis Set comprised those who received garadacimab and had at
least one quantifiable concentration measurement. PK parameters were estimated using
noncompartmental analyses. Point estimates and 90% CIs for geometric mean ratios were
derived from log-transformed PK parameters.
