The FDA’s Assessment:
The FDA agrees with the Applicant's assessment of the primary pharmacology studies

as described above. Additional details and clarifications regarding the studies are
provided in the review below.

Surface plasmon resonance (SPR) studies demonstrated linvoseltamab bound
recombinant human BCMA protein with high affinity (Ko = 437 pM) and to recombinant
cynomolgus monkey BCMA protein with lower affinity (Kp = 24.6 nM); linvoseltamab did
not bind to rodent BCMA under the conditions tested.

The binding affinity of linvoseltamab to CD3 expressed endogenously on human and
cynomolgus monkey T-cells was determined by flow cytometry; linvoseltamab bound
