In the dose range-finding embryo-fetal development study in mice, pregnant females received
twice-daily ziftomenib doses of 20, 60, or 200 mg/kg/day on gestation days 6-15, and the dosing
period was followed by a period of no dosing on gestation days 16-18. Lower matemal body
weights were seen at the high dose by the end of the dosing period compared to controls. The
high dose group only had a single female with live fetuses compared to 7 or 8 in the other
groups. External malformations included open eye and cleft palate. These were observed in all
the test article groups. Based on the results, dose levels of 6, 20, and 60 mg/kg/day were selected
for a definitive embryo-fetal development study.

5.5.5. Other Toxicology Studies
The Applicant’s Position:
Phototoxicity

In a 3T3 neutral red uptake (NRU) phototoxicity assay, ziftomenib demonstrated phototoxic
potential. However, in an in vivo phototoxicity study conducted in hairless mice, no evidence of
cutaneous phototoxicity was noted indicating that ziftomenib does not present a phototoxic
hazard to humans.
