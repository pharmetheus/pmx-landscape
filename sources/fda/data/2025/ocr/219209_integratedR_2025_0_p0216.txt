Apparent volume of distribution based on the terminal phase (Vz/F) of SUZ is 495 L. Plasma
protein binding was 99% for SUZ and 96% for M6-SUZ. Both albumin and alpha-1 acid
glycoprotein contribute to SUZ and M6-SUZ binding in human plasma. The in vitro blood-to-
plasma concentration ratio was approximately 0.6 for both SUZ and M6- SUZ in humans. SUZ
is not a substrate for transporters (P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1 or OATP1B3). M6-SUZ is a substrate
for P-gp, but not other transporters.

Following [!4C]SUZ oral administration, ~94% of the administered dose was recovered, out of
which ~50% was recovered in feces (primarily as metabolites, with 9.1% unchanged parent and
<0.9% unchanged M6-SUZ) and ~44% in urine (as metabolites). The mean plasma apparent
clearance is 13.9 L/h. Effective half-lives of SUZ and M6-SUZ are 24 hours and 33 hours,
respectively. SUZ and M6-SUZ are primarily metabolized by CYP3A, with minor contributions
from CYP2C9, CYP2C19, and CYP2C8. SUZ was mainly cleared by oxidative metabolism.
After a single oral dose of [!4C]SUZ, 44.0% and 49.9% of the dose was excreted in urine and
feces, respectively.

Based on in vitro data, SUZ and M6-SUZ are not expected to result in clinically significant
inhibition of CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, and CYP3A
enzymes. SUZ decreased the AUC of midazolam (a sensitive CYP3A substrate) by 48% and
Cmax by 37% (Study 010). Compared to CYP3A, SUZ has lesser potential to induce CYP2B6,
CYP2C8, CYP2C9, and CYP2C19 based on the in vitro data. SUZ does not induce CYP1A2
according to the in vitro data. SUZ has no significant effect on the PK of levonorgestrel and
ethinyl estradiol.

A clinical DDI study (Study 010) showed that SUZ did not affect the PK of digoxin (P-gp
substrate). Based on in vitro data, SUZ and M6-SUZ do not inhibit transporters (OATP1B1,
OATP1B3, OAT3 BCRP OAT1, organic cation transporter (OCT) 2, multidrug and toxin
extrusion protein [MATE] 1, and MATE2/K).

The Applicant also used PBPK analysis to assess the effects of hepatic impairment. At the
current stage, the application of PBPK analysis to assess the effects of hepatic impairment
without clinical data is considered exploratory. The Applicant’s PBPK analysis for severe
hepatic impairment was not reviewed.
