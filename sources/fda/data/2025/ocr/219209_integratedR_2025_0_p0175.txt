Reviewer Analysis of Food Effect

SUZ is for an acute pain indication; hence, the attainment of sufficient initial concentrations after
dosing is expected for the onset of pain relief. In the phase 3 studies of SUZ (Studies 104, 105
and 107), the first dose (100 mg) was administered under fasting conditions and subsequent
doses were administered without regard to food. The median time to onset of perceptible pain
relief in abdominoplasty (Study 105) was 34 minutes, while in bunionectomy (Study 104), time
to onset was 60 minutes. >]

The Applicant conducted two food-effect studies, 011 and 016. Study 011 evaluated the effect of
a high-fat meal on the PK of SUZ and M6-SUZ versus fasted state. Study 016 evaluated various
meal types (low-fat, moderate-fat, and high-fat) and timings of meal administration (60 minutes
predose, at Oh, and 60 minutes postdose*) on SUZ and M6-SUZ PK versus fasted state.

The effect of food on the first dose of 100 mg and the subsequent doses of 50 mg from 12 hours
onwards was evaluated separately. >|
