e Patient ID ©). This is an event of Grade 4 ILD/pneumonitis which occurred 180

Treatment was permanently discontinued and the patient expired one day after the start of

concerning the etiology with no imaging data.

ILD/pneumonitis is a known adverse reaction associated with anti-HER2 therapy and many
kinase inhibitors. Given the potential severity, the occurrence of cases resulting in study drug
withdrawal and need for provider awareness of this adverse reaction, a warning for
ILD/pneumonitis is warranted in Section 5 of the USPI.

Ocular Toxicity

Ocular toxicity is a potential class effect of anti-EGFR/HER2 therapy. FDA’s analysis used a
GT as detailed in Table 74. The median time to any Eye disorder (GT) was 21 and 21.5 days in
the two safety populations with a range of 3 to 504 days. The GT Eye disorder occurred in 14%
of the pooled 20 mg safety population of which 0.4% of patients had a Grade 3 event. The most
common PT within the GT was Dry Eye (6% of patients). Narratives for serious or severe cases
of ocular toxicity are described below.
