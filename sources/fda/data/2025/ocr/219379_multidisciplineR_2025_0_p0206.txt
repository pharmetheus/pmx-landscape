Durability of Response
Data:

Median DoR for participants with a confirmed response was not reached.
The Applicant's Position:

Response was durable among participants with a confirmed response.
The FDA’s Assessment:

Among the 19 patients in NF-106, only 6 (32%) had a confirmed response of PR. The small
number of patients, lack of events and follow-up limit the ability to interpret DOR and durability
of response.

Persistence of Effect
Data:

Refer to efficacy results — secondary and other relevant endpoints above.

The Applicant's Position:

The study demonstrated persistence of effect over time.
The FDA’s Assessment:
Persistence of effect cannot be verified.

Efficacy Results — Secondary or exploratory COA (PRO) endpoints
Data:

Of 19 participants, 9 completed the NRS-11 at Cycle 12 and 4 participants completed the NRS-
11 at Cycle 24. The median (min, max) change in score from baseline at Cycle 12 for the FAS
was -1 (-9, 0). At Cycle 24, the median (min, max) change from baseline was 0 (-3, 1). There was
statistically significant improvement in the mean NRS-11 change from baseline at Cycle 12

(LS mean=-1.81; p=0.030), indicating decreased pain severity. At Cycle 24, the LS mean change
from baseline was -0.30 (p=0.740).
