Data (presented by FDA):

In the 28-day toxicity study, rats were given imlunestrant lot# ALL-D00218-026 at 100 mg/kg
(600 mg/m? Human Equivalent Dose [HED]) orally once daily for 28 days without any early
mortalities. In the 3-month toxicity study, rats were given imlunestrant lot # CR-C18071754-
J21001 at doses up to 150 mg/kg (900 mg/m? HED) without any early mortalities. Based on body
surface area (mg/m/) in rats at the dose level that did not result in severe toxicities, these
impurities were administered at higher levels than would be administered to patients receiving
the recommended clinical dose of 400 mg (two 200 mg tablets) taken orally, once daily (400
mg/60 kg x ©® mg/m?) at the proposed specification limits for these impurities in the
imlunestrant drug substance and drug product and are considered qualified.
