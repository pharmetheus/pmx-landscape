NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

6.3.2.2 Is the proposed dosing regimen appropriate for the general patient population
for which the indication is being sought?

Data:

Sevabertinib 20 mg BID resulted in rapid, deep, and durable responses in advanced NSCLC
participants with HER? activating mutations, who had received prior systemic therapy, but are
naive to HER2 ex20ins-targeted therapy, as assessed by ORR by BICR (see Section 8.1.2). In
addition, sevabertinib 20 mg BID presented a manageable safety profile. Most common drug-
related TEAEs were of low grade (Grade 1 or 2), reversible, and manageable with standard of
care (see Section 8.2). Out of 268 participants treated with 20 mg BID, 6 (2.2%) discontinued
treatment due to a TEAE, 73 (27.2%) experienced TEAEs leading to a dose reduction, and 113
(42.2%) had TEAEs leading to dose interruption/delay.

Results of exposure-response analyses confirm that the efficacy benefit of sevabertinib was
highest in the targeted patient population (see Question 6.3.2.1 above), regardless of baseline
demographic and clinical characteristics.

The Applicant’s Position:

Taken together, the efficacy and safety data as well the results from exposure-response analyses,
support a favorable benefit/risk profile of the 20 mg BID sevabertinib dosing regimen for the
general patient population for the proposed indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the PK, PD, activity, and safety data as well the
results from the E-R analyses, support a favorable benefit/risk profile of the 20 mg BID
sevabertinib dosing regimen for the general patient population for the proposed indication. Refer
to Sections 6.3.2.1 and 19.4.4 for additional details.

6.3.2.3 Is an alternative dosing regimen or management strategy required for
subpopulations based on intrinsic patient factors (e.g. race. ethnicity. age.

performance status. genetic subpopulations. etc.)?

Data:

Data related to the effect of intrinsic (and extrinsic) factors on the PK of sevabertinib are
presented in Section 6.2.2.2.

The Applicant’s Position:

The population PK analysis demonstrated that age, sex, body weight, race, geographic region,
mild or moderate renal impairment, mild hepatic impairment, and smoking status do not affect
the PK of sevabertinib. Therefore, no alternative dosing regimen or management strategy is
required for these intrinsic (and extrinsic) patient factors.
