metastasis.

Assessment: FDA considers the SAE of abnormal hepatic function as possibly related to
the study treatment, although her retroperitoneal lymph node metastasis, liver metastasis
and abnormal liver function at baseline could be contributing factors.

The assessment of liver-related TEAEs and liver-related laboratory abnormalities indicates an
