NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

The Applicant’s Position:

Carcinogenicity studies have not been conducted. Consistent with the guideline ICH S9,
carcinogenicity studies are not warranted to support clinical trials for therapeutics intended to
treat patients with advanced cancer.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees that carcinogenicity studies have not been conducted and limited long-term data
preclude definitive evaluation of carcinogenicity.

Human Reproduction and Pregnancy
Data:

Please see The Applicant’s Position below.

The Applicant’s Position:

There are no human data available on the effect of sevabertinib on fertility or on the use of
sevabertinib in pregnant women.

Available animal studies do not provide sufficient information regarding reproductive toxicity
(Module 2.6.6, Section 6). The absence of HER2 and/or EGFR signaling has been shown to
result in impairment of embryo-fetal development, and embryolethality, as well as post-natal
death in animals (46). Based on its mechanism of action and findings in animal models (Module
2.6.6, Section 9), sevabertinib may cause fetal harm when administered during pregnancy.

Sevabertinib should not be given during pregnancy, unless the benefit of treatment of the woman
is considered to outweigh the potential risks to the fetus. Female patients taking sevabertinib
during pregnancy or who become pregnant while taking sevabertinib should be advised of the
potential hazard to the fetus.

There is no data on the presence of sevabertinib or its metabolites in human milk. In rats,
sevabertinib or its metabolites are excreted in milk. A risk to the breast-fed child cannot be
excluded. Breastfeeding should be discontinued during treatment with sevabertinib and for 1
week following the final dose. The elimination half-life of sevabertinib is approximately a
hours. The recommended post-dose period of 1 week is based on 5 half-lives after completion of
treatment, along with an additional buffer period.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. Refer to Section 5 for FDA’s review of data to
support recommendations for males and females of reproductive potential, lactation and

pregnancy.
