The Applicant's Position:
The clinical pharmacology of mirdametinib and PD-0315209 has been adequately characterized

by clinical evaluations. The primary metabolites in humans were identified, results from
nonclinical assays demonstrate that contribution of the metabolites to pharmacological activity
is not clinically relevant. No clinical evaluations of DDIs were conducted, however, in vitro
assessments support low risk of clinically relevant interactions with major CYP enzymes or
transporters. The PopPK analysis was sufficient to evaluate expected effects from mild and
moderate renal impairment and mild hepatic impairment. No dose adjustments are necessary
in these populations and a clinical study is planned to further evaluate the effects of moderate
and severe hepatic impairment. The overall evaluation of clinical pharmacology supports the
selection of 2 mg/m? BID as an appropriate dose for adult and pediatric patients with NF1 PN.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position. FDA notes that the effect of moderate and severe
hepatic impairment on mirdametinib PK will be addressed as a PMR (instead of a PMC, as
stated by the Applicant above). The DDI assessment based on the PopPK model alone is
