Correlation Between Responses and Clinical Improvements

Tumor responses based on imaging were supported by signs of clinical improvements, per
investigator assessment, in all responders (Table 8.10), most with improving focal motor
weakness, cranial neuropathies or ataxia, and many with other broader improvements in
functioning such as being able to work or travel again. Patients with minor responses had
clinically meaningful improvements and based on the totality of data, FDA determined it was

reasonable to include patients with minor responses per RANO 2.0 criteria in the calculation of
ORR.
