5.4. Embryo-Fetal Toxicity

FDA revised text to include human data of a pregnancy
reported 31 days after the last dose of GOMEKLI that
resulted in a first trimester spontaneous abortion and to
provide data from studies of rats and rabbits
demonstrating embryo-fetal mortality, structural
abnormalities and alterations to growth.

7 Drug Interactions

FDA removed this section as non-actionable, consistent
with 21 CFR 201.56(d)(4).

8.1. Pregnancy

FDA revised this section for consistency with the
Guidance Pregnancy, Lactation, and Reproductive
Potential: Labeling for Human Prescription Drug and
Biological Products-Content and Format (July 2020).
FDA added data from studies of rats and rabbits
demonstrating embryo-fetal mortality, structural
abnormalities and alterations to growth. FDA added the
subsection, Human Data to include human data of first
trimester spontaneous abortion where a pregnancy was
reported 31 days after the last dose of GOMEKLI.
Additional details from the rat and rabbit studies were
provided that demonstrate nonclinical embryo-fetal
toxicity.

Contraception
Females

FDA revised the Applicant’s proposed requirement of
female contraception for 1 week to 6 weeks, based on
human and animal demonstration of embryo-fetal
toxicity.

Contraception
Males

FDA revised the Applicant’s proposed requirement of
male contraception to 3 months, based on nonclinical
studies.

8.4. Pediatric Use

FDA provided information on the data supporting the
pediatric indication and included nonclinical toxicity
findings that were not demonstrated in the clinical
studies.

8.5 Geriatric Use

FDA revised text for consistency with 21 CFR
201.57(c)(9)(v)(B)(1).

8.6 Renal Impairment

FDA revised text for clarity and brevity.

8.7 Hepatic Impairment

FDA revised text for clarity and brevity.
