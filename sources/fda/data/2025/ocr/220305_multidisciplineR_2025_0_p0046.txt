The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s statements. Ziftomenib activity on the hCavl.2,
hERG, and hNav1.5 ion channels was evaluated at concentrations of 0.5, 3, 10, and 50 1M by the
patch clamp method when the genes were expressed in HEK293 or CHO cells (study KO-TOX-
0021). The resulting ICso values were 1.6 uM for hERG and 2.8 uM for hCav1.2, but the ICso
could not be determined for hNav1.5 because it was higher than the highest dose used. Ina GLP
study with the hERG channel (study KO-TOC-0023), ziftomenib at 66 nM and 1.62 1M
inhibited at 71% and 95%, respectively. The ICso value could not be calculated but was
determined to be less than 66 nM.

Telemetered dogs were used to determine the effects of single oral ziftomenib doses (40, 120,
and 200 mg/kg; study KO-TOX-0022) on heart rate, arterial blood pressure, pulse pressure, body
temperature, and electrocardiogram activity (PR, QRS, QT, and QTc; study KO-TOX-0022).
Observations were recorded over 24 hours. Notable observations occurred between 6 and 24
hours after dosing and included dose-dependent lower blood pressure and longer PR intervals.
The observations were no more than 10% different from control levels. Cardiovascular
assessments were made during the 28-day and 13-week GLP repeat-dose toxicology studies in
dogs. While there were no notable observations attributed to ziftomenib in the 13-week study,
heart rate decreases resulting from increases in the RR interval were observed in the high-dose
animals (200 mg/kg/day) during the 28-day study.
