NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

14.4.3.3. ER (safety) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant conducted ER safety analysis in all patients with at least one measurable
concentration of ziftomenib (N=174). The analysis is conducted on the following endpoints
based on the occurrence >10% criteria: 1) any TEAE grade 3+ AEs, 2) any TEAE grade 4+
AEs, 3) any TRAE grade 3+, 4) any TRAE grade 4+, 5) grade 3+ treatment-emergent
anemia, 6) grade 3+ treatment-emergent febrile neutropenia, 7) grade 3+ treatment-emergent
neutropenia, 8) grade 3+ treatment-emergent pneumonia, 9) grade 3+ treatment-emergent
sepsis, 10) grade 3+ treatment-emergent thrombocytopenia, 11) treatment-emergent
differentiation syndrome, and 12) treatment-related differentiation syndrome. There is no
positive trend identified between ziftomenib exposure parameters and ER safety endpoints.
Overall, the Applicant’s ER analysis is considered acceptable for the purpose of assessing the
ER relationship of ziftomenib and its safety endpoints.
