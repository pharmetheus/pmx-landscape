A summary of steady state PK parameters following SC dosing in blinded treatment arms
(C1-INH HAE subjects) and in subjects with FXII/PLG HAE is shown in Table 79. Across all
treatment groups, median Tmax was approximately 6 to 7 days postdose, and the mean half-life
ranged from 14.5 to 18.5 days. These values are consistent with those reported for healthy
subjects following a single SC dose in studies 1001, 1003, and 1004. Cmax and area under the
concentration-time curve during the dosing interval appeared to increase in a slightly less-than-
dose proportional manner between doses of 75 and 600 mg, with 5.3-fold and 5.9-fold increases,
respectively, for an 8-fold increase in dose. There was no apparent difference in garadacimab
exposure between subjects with C1-INH HAE and subjects with FXII/PLG HAE.
