The FDA pooled the population of patients slightly differently than presented in by the Applicant
(FDA Table 39). The FDA considered the pooled population of patient with R/R AML with an
NPMIu treated at the proposed monotherapy dose as the key safety population for labeling (N=112,
N= 20 Phase 1b + N=92 Phase 2). The FDA then considered the whole population of R/R AML
patients treated with ziftomenib monotherapy from KO-MEN-001 (N=175) as another key population
to review. Lastly, the FDA considered the population of all ziftomenib treated patients in both
monotherapy and combination therapy studies as the ISS population (N=353).
