Model Structure The relationships between Acceptable

(mirdametinib and PD-0315209)
exposure and safety endpoints
were evaluated as PKPDx or ERR
models. For the ERR analyses of
safety endpoints evaluated by
logistic regression, the final
PopPK model was used to
calculate individual estimates of
exposure on the day of onset, as
available. For each safety
endpoint, nominal steady state
exposure was used for
participants with no event.

Exposure-response relationships
with safety events were
evaluated by logistic regression.
For significant relationships,
covariates were tested in a
stepwise forward search at a

p < 0.05 level of significance.
Only covariates that were
significant in a univariate search
were included in multivariate
testing. Mirdametinib and/or PD-
0315209 AUCO-12,ss were found
to be the best exposure
predictors for all significant
TEAEs. Of the grade 1+ binary
endpoints, the probability of a
ALT elevation, AST elevation, or
CPK increase event were found
to have statistically significant
relationships with mirdametinib
exposure. The probability of
acneiform rash, ALT elevation,
and AST elevation were found to
have statistically significant
relationships with PD-0315209
exposure.
