NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

The Applicant’s Position:

In heavily pre-treated patients with R/R NPM/-m AML, ziftomenib treatment induced a
clinically meaningful response to treatment. The KO-MEN-001 Phase 2 study met the primary
efficacy endpoint for CR/CRh rate (21.7%) at the planned primary analysis.

In the Phase 2 mITT population, CR responses occurred in 14.1% (95% CI: 7.7, 23.0) of patients
(historical control of low-intensity chemotherapy from 2% to 6%; Perl et al, 2019; Issa et al,
2023) and accounted for approximately two-thirds of the overall CR/CRh response rate (21.7%).
Sixty percent of patients (6 of 10 patients) with a BOR of CR who were locally evaluated for
MRD achieved MRD-. In patients who achieved a CR/CRh response and were tested for MRD in
the Phase 2 population (n=14), there were 9 (64.3%) patients who achieved MRD- with
ziftomenib 600 mg QD treatment, demonstrating a deep and robust efficacy response to
treatment. These data demonstrate that ziftomenib’s pharmacologic activity against the menin-
MLL(KMT2A) complex is sufficient to induce deep clinical responses in heavily pretreated
patients who have been refractory to or relapsed following multiple lines of therapy, thus
validating the menin protein as a critical drug target in these patients.

The positive results of CR/CRh were supported by 4 additional patients who achieved CRi in the
Phase 2 mITT population leading to a CRe rate of 26.1% (95% CI: 17.5, 36.3; Applicant Table
9). While not a regulatory endpoint, achievement of CRi indicates positive treatment effect on
underlying disease and clinical benefit. Additionally, MRD- based on local readings was
observed in 62.5% (10 of 16 patients with available MRD data) in patients who achieved CRe.

In the Phase 1b EAS population, 35% (95% CI: 15.4%, 59.2%) of patients achieved CR/CRh
(Applicant Table 9). An additional patient in the Phase 1b EAS population achieved a BOR of
CRi/complete remission with incomplete platelet recovery (CRp), rendering a CRe rate of 40%.
The treatment effects in the Phase 1b EAS population (N=20) population confirmed the
clinically meaningful response rates observed in the Phase 2 mITT population.

In totality, these findings on the primary efficacy analysis are evidence that ziftomenib provides
a clinically meaningful therapeutic option for patients with R/R NPM1-m AML for the proposed
indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The results of response rate based on the FDA’s adjudicated responses are summarized in FDA
Table 32 for the Pooled Efficacy Cohort. The CR + CRh rates were similar between the Phase 1b

and 2 cohorts, although the lower bound of 95% CI excluded the proposed benchmark of 12%
only in the Phase 1b and the Pooled Efficacy Cohorts.
