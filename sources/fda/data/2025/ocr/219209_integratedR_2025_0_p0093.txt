7.7.3. Risk of Reproductive and Developmental Effects

Issues

Toxicities of SUZ were observed in rat reproductive and developmental studies such as increased
pre-implantation loss, increased post-implantation loss, lower fetal body weights, and increased
pup mortality.

Background

Ina female fertility study in rats, increased pre-implantation loss was observed at oral SUZ doses
of 15 mg/kg when administered prior to mating and through gestation day (GD) 7. These effects
on fertility in female rats appear to be reversible as pre-implantation loss was not observed when
female rats were mated following discontinuation of SUZ for 4 weeks.

In the rabbit EFD study, higher post-implantation loss and lower fetal body weights were
observed at 200 mg/kg/day when administered on GDs 7 to 20. However, these effects occurred
at a dose that also caused maternal toxicity so it is unclear if they are SUZ related. In the rat EFD
