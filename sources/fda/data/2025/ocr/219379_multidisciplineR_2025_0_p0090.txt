Phototoxicity

Mirdametinib absorbs light in the 290 to 700 nm range; therefore, an in vitro phototoxicity
study was conducted (05075). In the in vitro Balb/c 3T3 mouse fibroblast neutral red uptake
assay, mirdametinib exhibited a weak positive signal. However, mirdametinib was not retained
in the skin or eye based on the [14C] mirdametinib tissue distribution study in rats.
