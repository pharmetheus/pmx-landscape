The primary endpoint of investigator-assessed PFS for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm
and TPC arm in the ESR/m-detected population crossed the initially allocated alpha of 0.04 (2-
sided) and was statistically significant. The graphical representation of testing schema was
updated with alpha levels recalculated, and the investigator-assessed PFS for the comparison of
the imlunestrant arm vs TPC arm in the ITT population was formally tested at 0.038 (2-sided)
alpha level. The HR for the investigator-assessed PFS for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm
vs TPC arm in the ITT population was 0.87 (95% CI: 0.72, 1.04), the observed median PFS was
5.6 (95% CI: 5.3, 7.3) months in the imlunestrant arm and 5.5 (95% CI: 4.6, 5.6) months in the
TPC arm. The 2-sided stratified log-rank test p-value was 0.1158, which didn’t cross the
significance alpha level of 0.038 (2-sided).

e Lack of PFS benefit of imlunestrant over TPC in the ESRIm-nd population

The Applicant conducted an exploratory analysis of investigator-assessed PFS for the
comparison of the imlunestrant arm and TPC arm in patients without ESR/ mutation (n=405,
61.3% of the ITT population). The HR was 1.00 (95% CI: 0.79, 1.27). The observed median PFS
was 5.6 (95% CI: 4.4, 9.1) months in the imlunestrant arm and 5.7 (95% CI: 5.5, 7.4) months in
the TPC arm. The Applicant’s analysis in patients without ESR/ mutation included 20 patients
whose ESR/ mutation status was failed or missing (13 from the imlunestrant arm and 7 from the
TPC arm). The FDA review team conducted an exploratory analysis of investigator-assessed
PFS for the comparison of imlunestrant arm and TPC arm in the ESR/m-not detected (ESR/m-
nd) population by excluding these 20 patients whose ESR/ mutation status was failed or missing
(n=385). The HR was 1.04 (95% CI: 0.82, 1.33). The observed median PFS was 5.6 (95% CI:
3.8, 7.3) months in the imlunestrant arm and 5.6 (95% CI: 5.5, 7.4) months in the TPC arm
(Table 8.19). No treatment benefit for PFS was observed in the ESR/m-nd population.
