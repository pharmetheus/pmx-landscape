NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 218881

Inluriyo (imlunestrant)

FDA agrees that the proposed recommended imlunestrant dosage of 400 mg PO QD on an empty
stomach is appropriate for the indicated population.

In EMBER-3, the proposed recommended dosage demonstrated a favorable benefit-risk profile
compared to the control arm (fulvestrant or exemestane) in patients with ER-positive, HER2-
negative, ESR/J-mutated advanced or metastatic breast cancer. Imlunestrant improved median
PFS by 1.7 months (5.5 months for imlunestrant vs 3.9 months for control arm with HR of 0.62)
in the proposed patient population with an acceptable safety profile. In EMBER-3, permanent
discontinuation, dosage interruption, and dosage reduction of imlunestrant due to an AE occurred
in 4.6%, 10%, and 2.4% of patients, respectively. Refer to Section 1.3 for additional assessment
of the benefit-risk.

With regard to safety, the MTD was not reached at up to 1,200 mg QD in EMBER and there
were no apparent E-R relationships for safety of imlunestrant. Higher rates of nausea and
diarrhea were observed for 800 mg QD compared to 200 and 400 mg QD in EMBER-?2, despite
the lack of E-R relationships for safety, which contributed to the selection of 400 mg QD for
further evaluation.

With regard to activity, there was no apparent dose-response relationship for ORR in patients
treated with imlunestrant monotherapy in EMBER (Table 6.2), similar ER degradation was
observed across 200 mg to 800 mg QD dosages in EMBER-2, and there was no apparent E-R
relationship for PFS among patients treated with 400 mg QD in EMBER-3.

The proposed administration of imlunestrant without food is appropriate given that
administration of imlunestrant 400 mg with a low-fat meal increased Cmax 3.6-fold and AUCo int
2-fold as compared to administration under fasting conditions (Refer to Section 6.3.2.4 for
details). In addition, all three studies were conducted using the proposed food conditions (i.e.,
imlunestrant at least 2 hrs before or 1 hr after food); therefore, the observed safety and efficacy
in EMBER-3 is representative of the recommended dosage and administration instructions.
