7 DRUG INTERACTIONS

CYP3A Substrates

The Applicant did not have any proposed language for CYP3A
substrates. Since SUZ decreases the exposure, it can thereby
compromise the efficacy of certain CYP3A substrates. The
review team included following labeling recommendations:

« Avoid concomitant use of JOURNAVX with CYP3A
substrates where minimal concentration changes may lead to
loss of efficacy unless otherwise recommended in the
Prescribing Information for CYP3A substrates.

« When using JOURNAVX with sensitive CYP3A substrates,
consider increasing the dose of substrate drugs based on
their Prescribing Information if needed or use alternative
medications.

During labeling negotiations, the Applicant proposed the
following language, which was acceptable:

Refer to the prescribing information me

Suzetrigine is an inducer of CYP3A. Concomitant use with
JOURNAVX may reduce the exposure of sensitive CYP3A
substrates which may decrease the efficacy of these
substrates. Discontinuation of JOURNAVX may increase the
exposure of sensitive CYP3A substrates.
