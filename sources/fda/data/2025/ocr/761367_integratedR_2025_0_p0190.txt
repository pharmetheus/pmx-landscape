Adolescent Subjects

Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time

Per the Applicant, the normal range for aPTT values is between 24.3 and 43.4 seconds. Mean
(SD) aPTT at baseline was 29.46 (11.1) seconds. From Month 3 through Month 12, mean (SD)
aPTT ranged from 28.55 (3.76) to 30.73 (11.3) seconds. There were 21/161 subjects (13.0%)
with 25 cases of aPTT prolongation during the study, including 5 cases at Screening, 5 cases at
Day 1, 6 cases at Month 3, 8 cases at Month 6, and 1 case at Month 12. Values above normal
tange ranged between 45.1 and 126.0 seconds. Most cases were transient (i.e., occurring at a
single time point), except in three subjects who had prolongation at multiple time points,
including before the first dose of study treatment. Prolongation of aPTT was observed in three
adolescent subjects (data derived from Listing 16.2.8.5.1, CSR for study 3002). In two cases,
prolongation was observed at Screening only. In the remaining case, prolongation was observed
transiently at Month 6.
