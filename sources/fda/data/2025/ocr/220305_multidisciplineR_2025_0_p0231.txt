Clinical Comment: The safety populations as delineated by the FDA show enrollment of a
sufficient number of patients treated at the intended dose who had R/R AML with an NPM1
mutation. The ISS population including the remaining patients from Study KO-MEN-001 adds
241 ziftomenib-treated patients to the overall safety database. Comparison of demographics
between these groupings shows a similar breakdown by age, sex, race, and ethnicity. The United
States was the most common country for enrolled patients across both the population of
participants with NPM1-m AML and the ISS. Finally, the exposure across these population were
also similar (table exposure above).
