NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

Expectations on Safety in the Postmarket Setting

The Applicant’s Position:

The safety profile in the postmarketing setting is expected to be similar that observed in the
clinical trial setting, anticipated to be acceptable and manageable with appropriate labeling, in
the indicated population.

8.3.11. Integrated Assessment of Safety

Data:

The pivotal study, Study KO-MEN-001, was the primary source for the evaluation of the safety
of ziftomenib 600 mg QD as monotherapy for the treatment of R/R AML in patients with
NPM1-m (N=112). Sponsor data and positions for the Integrated Summary of Safety (ISS) is
provided in the preceding sections of this document.

The Applicant’s Position:

The overall safety profile of ziftomenib at the recommended dosage is acceptable for its intended
use as palliative treatment of patients with R/R AML NPM/ mutations. Risk management can
adequately be managed through clear product labeling and monitoring for the important
identified risk of DS and embryo-fetal risk (based only on nonclinical data). Labeling will
include a warning for the serious risks, instructions for monitoring, and dose modification for
toxicities. A non-REMS medication guide will inform and educate patients of the risk of DS and
when to seek immediate medical attention. Ziftomenib had no clinically relevant effect on liver
function and QTc or other ECG parameters. The safety, tolerability, and lack of
myelosuppression of ziftomenib in this frail population support its role as a palliative therapy,
reinforcing a favorable risk-benefit profile.
