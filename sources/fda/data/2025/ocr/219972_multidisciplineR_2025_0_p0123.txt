NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0
The Applicant’s Position:

The clinical pharmacology program provides supportive evidence of the effectiveness of 20 mg
sevabertinib for the treatment of patients with advanced NSCLC harboring HER2 activating
mutations as demonstrated in Study 21607. The results of the exposure-response analyses
support the effectiveness in the proposed indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees with the Applicant’s proposed dosage of sevabertinib 20 mg BID for the targeted
indication given the following reasons:

1) In Study 21607 dose escalation phase, multiple dosages have been studied using different
formulations (LSF: 10 mg, 20 mg, or 40 mg QD, and tablets: 40 mg QD, 60 mg QD, 20
mg BID, 30 mg BID, and 40 mg BID). The PK linearity range was established over the
dose range of 20 mg to 80 mg total daily with the determined MTD of 60 mg total daily
dose. The tablet formulation was selected due to higher bioavailability over the LSF
formulation. The PD estimate suggested that BID regimens could achieve more sustained
pEGFR reduction as compared to QD dosing, and dosages >20 mg BID are likely to
achieve >80% pEGFR reduction. In addition, a higher ORR (i.e., 38%) was reported at
20 mg BID over other dosages evaluated in the dose escalation phase, which supported
further evaluation of 20 mg in the expansion phase.

2) Expansion/extension Groups D and E in Study 21067 evaluated 20 mg BID in patients
who were naive to HER2 ex20ins-targeted therapy (N=81) and who progressed to HER2-
targeted ADCs (N=52), respectively. In addition, a separate expansion Group D1
evaluated 10 mg BID in the same patient population as Group D (N=36), which allowed
for a comparison of activity and safety data between the two dosages.

Activity: As shown in Table 23, 20 mg BID exhibited higher ORR (64% vs 36%) and
longer median DOR (9 months vs 7 months) as compared to 10 mg BID in the dose
expansion and extension phase. In addition, E-R analysis for efficacy also identified
positive trends for ORR and BTR with higher exposure (refer to Section 19.4.4 for
details).
