The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of OS Interim Analysis 1 (IA1)
results for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm vs TPC arm in the ESR/m-detected
population. There were 80 deaths observed in the ESR/m-detected population (52% information
fraction of the OS final analysis in the ESR/m-detected population). The updated alpha allocated
for interim and final analyses of OS in the ESR/m-detected population is 5.5<10° (2-sided). The
p-value for the OS IA1 for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm vs the TPC arm in the
ESRIm-detected population did not cross the efficacy boundary of 2.2x107° (2-sided). There
was no potential detriment in overall survival in the ESR/m-detected population at IA1.

Landmark analyses for time-to-event endpoints are considered exploratory since specific time
points are used as opposed to assessing the entire survival distribution. Therefore, any
differences between treatment arms at these landmarks must be interpreted with caution.

The OS curves for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm and the TPC arm in the ESR/m-
detected population overlapped for the first 3 months, favored to the TPC arm from about 3 to 7
months, and began to separate in favor of the imlunestrant arm from Month 7 onward. The
Applicant provided a table showing the number of OS events by time interval, and it was noted
that the number of events that occurred before Month 7 was small.

Although the OS curves began to separate from Month 7 in favor of the imlunestrant arm, the
censoring for OS is heavy starting from Month 7-8 since many patients were administratively
censored, therefore, there is still uncertainty in the OS result and further review of the preplanned
remaining interim and final OS analyses for the comparison of the imlunestrant arm and the TPC
arm in the ESR/m-detected population with more mature data is important for both efficacy and
safety. The Applicant committed to submit SAP version 5 pre-specified interim and final OS
results as a PMC (Section 13).
