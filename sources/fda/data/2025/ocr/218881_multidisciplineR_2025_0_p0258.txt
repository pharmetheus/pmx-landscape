The safety analysis included 473 patients from monotherapy arms of Studies EMBER and
EMBER-3. Four key safety endpoints of interest were evaluated: serious adverse events (SAEs),
nausea, diarrhea, and fatigue. These endpoints were selected based on their frequency and

clinical importance.

There were no significant relationships between imlunestrant exposure and selected safety
endpoints. The Applicant’s E-R analysis for safety is acceptable.
