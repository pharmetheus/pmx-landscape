The Applicant’s Position:

Consistent with the expected patient population, the study enrolled patients who were heavily
pre-treated and relapsed and/or refractory. These baseline disease characteristics and the prior
antineoplastic therapies reflect those of patients with R/R NPM1-m AML, including involvement
of additional high-risk co-mutations, expected bone marrow and peripheral blood blast counts,
and the number of prior antineoplastic therapies.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Most participants (83%) had a performance status of 0-1, de novo AML (85%), and prior use of
venetoclax (60%). The median number of prior lines of therapy is 2 (range: 1, 7) and 23% had
prior HSCT (FDA Table 31). There were 59% patients who were transfusion dependent at
baseline.
