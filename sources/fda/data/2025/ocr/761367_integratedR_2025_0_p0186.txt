Additional PK samples were collected following administration of the 400 mg SC loading dose
in a subgroup of garadacimab-naive subjects (n=15). Peak concentrations were achieved on
Day 7 postdose. The mean (SD) Cmax was 24.5 (11.0) 1g/mL, and the mean (SD) AUCo-30 was
10685 (5064) ,tg-h/mL. These values are comparable to model-predicted steady state PK
parameters from trial 3001 (mean [SD] Cmax=21.2 [6.6] pg/mL; mean [SD] AUC tau s;:=10,300
(3380) tg-h/mL). Refer to Table 81.

The mean (SD) garadacimab concentration-time profiles for adults (N=151) versus adolescents
(N=10) are shown in Figure 43. Garadacimab trough concentrations in adolescents were greater
than those in adults, possibly due to lower body weight in adolescent subjects. The mean (SD)
garadacimab trough concentrations from Month 3 through Month 12 are summarized in

Table 82. From Month 3 through Month 12, garadacimab trough concentrations were 1.2- to
1.8-fold greater in adolescents than in adults. Exposure was 1.8-fold greater at Month 12, a time
point at which only three adolescent subjects provided data. Data at Month 12 should therefore
be interpreted with caution.
