NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that no dosage adjustment is required for age (18 to
91 years), race (27% White, 65% Asian), sex, body weight (29 to 155 kg), smoking status, eGFR
30 to < 90 mL/min, or mild hepatic impairment (AST > ULN and total bilirubin < ULN; or total
bilirubin > 1 to 1.5x ULN and any AST).

HER2 genetic alternations and treatment effect:

FDA explored the treatment efficacy by HER2 genetic alteration in the primary efficacy
population, Group D, in study 21607. In study 21607, group D, 81 patients with activating HER2
mutations were enrolled. HER2 mutations were detected at a laboratory accredited by Clinical
Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA) or equivalent standard. The local HER2 results
were retrospectively confirmed by the Thermo Fisher Oncomine™ Dx Target Test (ODxTT) as
part of the clinical bridging study for companion diagnostic development. Based on an
information request response (dated 05/21/2025), there was no pre-defined list of HER2 variants
eligible for enrollment. Variants known to contribute to carcinogenesis and activate the HER2
pathway, based on publications and public databases including ClinVar
(https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/) and COSMIC (https://cancer.sanger.ac.uk/cosmic), were
considered as activating HER2 mutations. Consistent with literature (Loeffler et al. 2023), the
majority of identified HER2 genetic alterations were exon 20 insertions (N=64, 83%), with
HER2 Y722_A775 dup (YVMA) identified as the most common exon 20 insertion (N=48, 59%).
Treatment response was observed across HER2 genetic alterations (Table 26). Responses were
numerically lower in patients with point mutations, compared to patients with exon 20 insertions.
However, the results for patients with point mutations need to be interpreted with caution due to
small sample size. This analysis suggests sevabertinib treatment is effective for patients with
activating HER2 mutations, and did not identify efficacy concems for any mutation subtypes or
specific mutations based on the limited available data.
