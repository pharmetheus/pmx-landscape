Abbreviation: ADA, antidrug antibody

The impact of ADAs on PD was assessed by comparing FXII-mediated kallikrein activity by
ADA status (Figure 49). There was no apparent difference in the percent change from baseline in
FXII-mediated kallikrein activity among ADA-positive subjects relative to subjects who did not
develop ADAs. As noted in Sections 14.2.5 and 14.2.6, after accounting for variability, little
change from baseline in FXII-mediated kallikrein activity was observed at predose timepoints.
As all subjects with TE-ADAs were derived from trial 3001 and study 3002, in which FXII-
mediated kallikrein activity was measured at time points associated with garadacimab trough
concentrations, the data are not sufficiently sensitive for determining whether ADAs had an
impact on PD.
