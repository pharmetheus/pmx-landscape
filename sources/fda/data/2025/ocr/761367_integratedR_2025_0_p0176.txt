PD Results

FXIa-mediated kallikrein activity was assessed as a PD marker. In the blinded treatment arms,
garadacimab showed dose-dependent inhibition of FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity (Table 80).
Following administration of the IV loading dose, inhibition of FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity
was observed within 8 h postdose. Inhibition at all doses was maintained at trough throughout
the Treatment Period, with the lowest activity observed among subjects receiving 600 mg Q4W.
Changes in FXIIa-mediated kallikrein activity were comparable between C1-INH and FXII/PLG
HAE subjects who received 300 mg IV then 600 mg SC Q4W.
