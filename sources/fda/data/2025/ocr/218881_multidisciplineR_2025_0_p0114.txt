Study Endpoints

The Applicant’s Description:

Choice of investigator-assessed PFS as the primary endpoint

Investigator-assessed PFS is an appropriate endpoint in MBC as demonstrated by recent pivotal
studies supporting approvals in this setting (FDA 2019). Meta-analyses indicate that investigator-
assessed PFS provides a reliable estimate of treatment effect (Amit et al. 2011; Dello Russo and
Navarra 2022). Importantly, PFS as assessed by BIRC was a secondary endpoint to assess
potential for bias in investigator assessments (Amit et al. 2011).
