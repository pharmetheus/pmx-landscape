19.4.4.6. Reviewer’s Independent Analysis

E-R analysis for BTR

0 and the approach to estimate EC50 that had inherent limitations (i.e., grid search to find EC50
value with smallest AIC). Therefore, the reviewer conducted independent Bayesian Emax model
assessment. As shown in Table , the estimated EC50 had a large credible interval, suggesting that
the data are not informative to robustly estimate ECS5O and the efficacy is likely near plateau.
