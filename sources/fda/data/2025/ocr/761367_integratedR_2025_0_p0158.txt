14.2.3. Study 1003 (SAD Study in Healthy Japanese
and Caucasian Subjects)

Title

A two-part, phase 1, single-center, open-label, single ascending dose study to investigate the
PKs, PDs, safety, and tolerability of SC and IV garadacimab in healthy adult Japanese and
Caucasian subjects.

Objectives

The primary objectives of this study were to characterize the PK of garadacimab in Japanese
subjects after SC dosing and to compare the PK of garadacimab in Japanese and Caucasian
subjects at the 200 mg SC dose. Further characterization of PK (including in IV cohorts), PD
(FXIa-mediated kallikrein activity), and immunogenicity were the secondary objectives.
