The Applicant’s Position:

In Group D, 24 participants (29.6%) had at least 1 important protocol deviation. The most
commonly reported important protocol deviations were related to the study procedures (27.2%).
There were 20 participants (36.4%) in Group E with any important protocol deviation; procedure
deviations were the most common of those (25.5%).

None of these protocol deviations were considered to have effect on the overall interpretation of
the efficacy and safety results of the study.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position that the described protocol deviations do not appear
likely to have a significant impact on the interpretation of safety or effectiveness.
