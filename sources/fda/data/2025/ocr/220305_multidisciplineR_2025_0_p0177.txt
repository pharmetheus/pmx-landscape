Treatment Compliance, Concomitant Medications, and Rescue Medication Use

Data:

Treatment Compliance

The mean (SD) and median relative dose intensity were 90.37% (15.318) and 99.03% in the
Phase 2 mITT population used for primary efficacy and safety analyses.

Concomitant Medications

All 92 patients in Phase 2 reported a concomitant medication. The most frequently used
medications were typical of those used by the patient population under study and included
antibacterials for systemic use (96.7%), antimycotics for systemic use (94.6%), and antivirals for
systemic use (82.6%).
