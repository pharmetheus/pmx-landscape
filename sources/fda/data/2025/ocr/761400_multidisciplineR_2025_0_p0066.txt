The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, the FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment of current treatment of patients who have received an anti-
CD38 antibody, Pl, and IMiD (triple class exposed). The FDA notes that the three bispecific antibodies approved for
RRMM (teclistamab, talquetamab, and elranatamab) remain under accelerated approval. The FDA also notes that in
addition to the therapies that are specifically indicated for patients who are triple class exposed, patients may also
respond to therapies or combinations of therapies of the same classes already received in subsequent lines of therapy.
