19.4.3.7. Overall benefit-risk evaluation based on E-R analyses

The Applicant's Position:

Mirdametinib has a clear exposure-response relationship with tumor volume, with increasing
exposure being predictive of greater reductions in tumor response, increased probability of
response, and shorter time to response. Exposure-response relationships were identified for
both mirdametinib and PD-0315209 with some Grade 1+ TEAEs, however these relationships
were not apparent when severity was limited to Grade 2+. Additionally, the events for which
significant relationships are identified have not been correlated to observations of clinical
intolerability. The models were sufficiently qualified to provide predictive application and
simulations of various target dose levels support the conclusion that 2 mg/m? BID is an optimal
dose level for treatment of NF1.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Positive trend between mirdametinib exposure and efficacy endpoints were observed.
However, the prediction of efficacy for untested dosing regimen should be interpreted with
caution as it has not been verified in the clinical trial.
