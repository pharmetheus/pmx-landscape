The Applicant's Position:
The overall TEAE profile in Pool 1 was similar to what was observed in Study MEK-NF-201.
Overall, the TEAE profile between the pediatric and adult cohorts of Pool 1 was similar.

Safety analysis included TEAEs, treatment-related TEAEs, Grade 23 TEAEs, Grade 23 treatment-
related TEAEs, SAEs, treatment-related SAEs, TEAEs by maximum severity, deaths, TEAEs that
led to treatment discontinuations, dose modifications, dose interruptions, dose reductions,
safety topics of special interest (including hypersensitivity, liver function, hematologic
disorders, opportunistic infections, effects on cardiac conduction, cardiac effects,
gastrointestinal effects, stomatitis, skin disorders, eye disorders, central nervous system (CNS)
effects, musculoskeletal effects, upper respiratory infections, and bone effects), clinical
laboratory evaluations, ECG parameters, cardiac function (multigated acquisition scan [MUGA]
and echocardiogram [ECHO)]), and vital signs.

AEs were re-coded for all integrated studies to Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities
(MedDRA) Version 24.0 and displayed in tables and data listings using system organ class (SOC)
and preferred term (PT). Analyses of AEs were performed for those events that were
