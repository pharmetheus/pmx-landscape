14.2.1. Study 1004 (Relative Bioavailability Study
Comparing PFS-NSD and AI)

Title

A phase 1, randomized, open-label, parallel-group study to compare the PK properties of
garadacimab administered by SC prefilled syringe assembled to AI to prefilled syringe
assembled to needle safety device in healthy adult subjects.

Objectives

Primary objectives of the study were to characterize and compare the PK of a single SC injection
of garadacimab administered via garadacimab AI or garadacimab PFS-NSD. Safety and
tolerability were also assessed as secondary objectives.
