The Applicant’s Position:

At baseline, the majority of participants in Groups D and E never had any smoking habits and
had an ECOG PS of 1. Most participants had no history of brain metastases, were classified with
NSCLC adenocarcinoma, NOS, had Stage IV disease at baseline, and a HER2 Y772_A775dup
(YVMA) ex20ins mutation. All participants in Groups D and E received prior systemic anti-
cancer therapy as required by the protocol, with 23.5% and 47.3% of participants receiving 3 or
more prior lines of systemic therapy, respectively.

Overall, the primary efficacy population treated in Study 21607 is representative of the intended
patient population, and the baseline cancer characteristics are consistent with those described for
US patients with advanced NSCLC harboring HER? mutations (40, 41). The majority of
participants (93.8% in Group D and 78.2% in Group E) had received platinum-based.
chemotherapy with or without anti-PD(L)1 treatment, reflecting the US standard practice (18).
