NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972

Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0
Review Issue Recommendations and Comments

Per population PK analysis, no dosage adjustment is required based on age
(18 to 91 years), sex, race (27% White, 65% Asian), body weight (29 to 155
kg), or smoking status.

HER? genetic alternations: Sevabertinib treatment response was generally
observed across HER? alterations assessed.

Drug Interactions CYP3A is the primary CYP450 enzyme involved in sevabertinib
metabolism. Sevabertinib AUC increased 2.3-fold and Cmax 1.6-fold following
concomitant use of itraconazole (strong CYP3A4 inhibitor) 200 mg once
daily. Sevabertinib AUC decreased by 79% and Cmax by 57% following
concomitant use of carbamazepine (strong CYP3A inducer) 600 mg once
daily.

Sevabertinib displays a net inhibitory effect on PK of CYP3A4 substrates.
Midazolam (CYP3A4 substrate) AUC increased 2-fold and Cmax 1.8-fold
following concomitant use of sevabertinib 20 mg twice daily.

Sevabertinib is an inhibitor of P-gp. Dabigatran etexilate (P-gp substrate)
AUC increased 1.4-fold following concomitant use of sevabertinib 20 mg
twice daily.

Based on the above clinical DDI results, the review team agrees with the
proposed labeling recommendations to avoid concomitant use with strong
CYP3A inhibitors and inducers (dose reduction if concomitant use of strong
CYP3A inhibitors cannot be avoided), revises the instructions for certain
substrates of CYP3A4 and P-gp, and recommends additional instructions for
moderate CYP3A inhibitors and inducers based on the anticipated drug
interactions.

e Monitor patients for increased HYRNUO-associated adverse reactions
when used with a moderate CYP3A4 inhibitor.

e Avoid concomitant use with a moderate CYP3A4 inducer.

e Avoid concomitant use with certain CYP3A4 substrates where minimal
concentration increase may lead to serious adverse reactions.

e Refer to the Prescribing Information of P-gp substrates where minimal
concentration increase may lead to serious adverse reactions.

Based on the in vitro DDI results, the review team agrees with the proposed
labeling recommendations to refer to the Prescribing Information for
CYP1AI substrates when concomitant use of sevabertnib with these
substrates.
