NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 218881

Inluriyo (imlunestrant)

Study title/number: A 3-month toxicity and toxicokinetic study of LY3484356 tosylate
(compound 3513322) given daily by oral gavage to Cynomolgus monkeys/ 8003147

Methods: Monkeys received daily oral imlunestrant (LY3484356; lot# CR-C18071754-J21001,
89.8% purity) at 0, 15, 100, and 600 mg/kg for 3 months (n = 4/sex/group).

Key Study Findings:

e The major target organs were the hematopoietic/lymphatic systems, female reproductive
organs, gastrointestinal tract, liver, and kidneys with similar severity to those observed in
the one-month repeat-dose toxicity study.

Observations and Results

Parameters Major findings
Mortality All animals survived to the end of the study.
Clinical Signs At 600 mg/kg, imlunestrant-related clinical signs included vomitus during Days 5-102 in

both males and females, salivation in males between Days 95-105, abdominal distension
in females during Days 30-35. Abnormal feces (pale, soft, liquid) were observed at 600
mg/kg in both males and females during Days 4-106.
