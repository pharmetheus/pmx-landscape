A total of 58 participants in the adult cohort were exposed to at least one dose of study
treatment. During the Treatment Phase, 27 (47%) participants prematurely discontinued
treatment. The most common (> 10%) reasons for premature treatment discontinuation were
AE (13 [22%] participants) and participant decision (8 [14%] participants). Of the 31 (53%)
participants in the adult cohort eligible for the LTFU Phase, 26 (84%) participants entered the
LTFU Phase. As of the data cutoff, 22 (85% of those who entered the LTFU Phase) are ongoing
treatment in the LTFU Phase and 4 (15%) discontinued treatment in the LTFU Phase; reasons for
treatment discontinuation included participant decision (2 [8%] participants), non-compliance
(1 [4%] participant), and other (1 [4%] participant). There was 1 death in the adult cohort during
Study MEK-NF-201.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the description of patient disposition in MEK-NF-201 presented in
Table 35. There appears to be differential rates of treatment discontinuation prior to
completion of the treatment phase between the pediatric and adult cohorts, particularly for
reasons of radiological progression: pediatric cohort (16.1%) compared to the adult cohort
(5.2%) and adverse events: pediatric cohort (9%) compared to the adult cohort (22.4%). The
higher rate of discontinuation due to PN progression in the pediatric may be attributable to the
more rapid growth rate in childhood as compared to adulthood.

Protocol Violations/Deviations

Data:

In the pediatric cohort, 3 (5%) participants had at least 1 major protocol deviation and were
therefore excluded from the per-protocol set. These deviations included study eligibility; 1
participant did not meet inclusion criterion #5 (their target PN volume at baseline was < 5 mL)
and 2 participants did not meet inclusion criterion #3 (reported no morbidity at baseline) and
both were enrolled in the study.

In the adult cohort, 6 (10%) participants had at least 1 major protocol deviation and were
therefore excluded from the per-protocol set. These deviations included study inclusion (2 [3%]
participants; both participants did not meet inclusion criterion #5 and were enrolled in the
study with baseline target PN volumes measured < 5 mL) and investigational product (4 [7%]
participants; all 4 participants received < 60% prescribed study treatment during the Treatment
Phase).
