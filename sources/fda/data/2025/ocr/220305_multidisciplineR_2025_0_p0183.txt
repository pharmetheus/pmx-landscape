Note that for the population of patients with NPM1 mutated AML, the inclusion criteria are
identical. While there are minor differences in the baseline characteristics and response rates,
no overt signals suggest that the populations are meaningfully different. Pooling appears to be
justified and even under scenarios in which the Phase 1b data are not directly applicable to
Phase 2, the probability that the CR + CRh rate exceeds 12% is high.

It should be noted that while it is helpful to contextualize the total data relative to the pre-
specified efficacy threshold, this threshold is modest. Merely excluding a 12% CR + CRh rate is
not sufficient to establish substantial evidence of efficacy on its own. Products intended to
establish efficacy based on CR + CRh should also be tolerable, have sufficient durability of
response, and a meaningful rate of transfusion independence. These measures are discussed
below.
