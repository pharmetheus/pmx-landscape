Risk

Although taletrectinib can cause serious
adverse reactions, these safety concems are
adequately addressed by information in the
Warnings and Precautions and Dosage and
Administration sections of product labeling.

There were no significant safety concerns
identified during NDA review requiring risk
management beyond labeling or warranting
consideration for a Risk Evaluation and
Mitigation Strategy (REMS). Taletrectinib will
be prescribed by oncologists who are familiar
with monitoring, identifying, and managing the
toxicities described in the USPI.
