Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

Source: ADSL, ADEFF, ADRS

The Applicant’s Position:

At the time of the data cut-off in Study MEK-NF-201 (20Sep2023) 88% of pediatric participants
with a confirmed objective response during the 24 cycle Treatment Phase had been followed
for at least 12 months from the onset of response, or discontinued study. The K-M estimated
median DoR was not reached for the pediatric cohort due to no events occurring as of the data
cutoff. The responses were durable, with 22 (76%) of pediatric responders maintaining a
confirmed response for at least 12 months, and no disease progression or deaths were
observed following a confirmed response. These data demonstrate continuing effect of
mirdametinib over time in pediatric participants with NF1-PN.

At the time of the data cut-off in Study MEK-NF-201 (20Sep2023) 86% of adult participants with
a confirmed objective response during the 24 cycle Treatment Phase had been followed for at
least 12 months from the onset of response, or discontinued study. The K-M estimated median
DoR was not reached for the adult cohort due to a low number of events after achieving a
confirmed objective response as of the data cutoff (1 event of PD). The responses were durable,
with 18 (75%) of adult responders maintaining an observed response for at least 12 months; 1
PD and no deaths were observed following a confirmed response. These data demonstrate
continuing effect of mirdametinib over time in adult participants with NF1-PN.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA generally agrees with the description of secondary and other relevant endpoints.

The median DOR was not reached in either the pediatric or adult cohorts due to lack of events.
Therefore, the Applicant provided DOR estimates for 8-, 12-, 16- and 24-month landmarks
based on the KM rate as shown in Table 41. The Applicant has also included DOR based on
observed time which provides additional critical information, as it reflects the conservative
estimate.

A 90-day safety update for DOR, based on a data cut of June 12, 2024, indicates the Kaplan-
Meier estimated DOR was still not reached in either pediatric or adult cohorts. Additionally,
there was no change in estimated ORRs for both cohorts. A summary of the updated efficacy
results including observed DOR is provided in Table 42.
