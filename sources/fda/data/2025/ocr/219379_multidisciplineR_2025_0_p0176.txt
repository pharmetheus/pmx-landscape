A post-hoc exploratory analysis was conducted to assess the correlation of change from
baseline in NRS-11 score and percent change from baseline in tumor volume, by cycle, in
participants with a baseline NRS-11 score 2 2. The Spearman rank correlation coefficient (r) was
used to measure the strength of this relationship and was assessed as per Cohen (Cohen 1988)
using the following ranges (for both positive and negative correlations): r = 0 to 0.3 indicates a
weak correlation, r = 0.3 to 0.5 indicates a moderate correlation, and r = 0.5 to 1.0 indicates a
strong correlation.

In the pediatric cohort, a strong positive correlation was observed between percentage change
from baseline in tumor volume and change from baseline in NRS-11 score at Cycle 13 (n=6, r=
0.714) (Figure 14). However, small sample sizes at this time point limit the interpretation of
these data.

In the adult cohort, a weak positive correlation was observed between percent change from
baseline in tumor volume and change from baseline in NRS-11 score at Cycle 13 (n= 11, r=

0.164) (Figure 15). However, small sample sizes at this time point limit the interpretation of
these data.
