NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation

NDA 214759
Grafapex (treosulfan)

REVIEW ISSUE

RECOMMENDATIONS/COMMENTS

Pivotal or supportive evidence
of effectiveness

In Study MC-FLudT.14/L Trial Il, treatment with the proposed dosing
regimen of treosulfan + fludarabine improved overall survival (OS) with a
hazard ratio (HR) and 95% confidence interval (Cl) of 0.65 (0.48, 0.88)
compared to treatment with busulfan + fludarabine. However, the Agency
was not able to independently verify the primary endpoint of event-free
survival (EFS) for Study MC-FludT.14/L Trial II (Refer to Section 8).

General dosing instructions

The recommended dosage regimen of treosulfan is 10 g/m?/day for 3
consecutive days (Days -4, -3, and -2) in combination with fludarabine 30
mg/m?/day for 5 consecutive days (Days -6 through -2) prior to allogeneic
hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (Day 0).

Dosing in patient subgroups
(intrinsic and extrinsic factors)

Pediatric Patients >1 year of age: The recommended dosage regimen of
treosulfan is the same for all patients >1 year of age. Efficacy in pediatric
patients >1 year of age was extrapolated from adults and dose selection
was supported by PK and safety data from Studies MC-FludT.16/NM and
MC-FludT.17/M. Treosulfan exposure after administration of 10 g/m? in
pediatric patients with BSA <0.7 m? and with BSA 0.7 to <1.1 m? are 11%
and 5% higher, respectively, compared to adults.

Renal Impairment: No dosage modification is recommended for patients
with mild renal impairment. An additional study will be necessary to
identify a safe dose of treosulfan for patients with moderate hepatic
impairment. A study in patients with severe renal impairment is not
feasible and will not be required.

Hepatic Impairment: No dosage modification is recommended for
patients with mild hepatic impairment.
