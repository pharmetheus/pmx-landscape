8.2. Extrinsic Factors

8.2.1. Effect of Food

The proposed indication for SUZ is acute pain and therefore, the attainment of sufficient initial
concentrations after dosing is an expectation for the onset of pain relief. In the phase 3 studies of
SUZ (104, 105 and 107), the first dose (100 mg) was administered in fasting condition, and
subsequent doses were administered without regard to food. In the phase 3 studies, with the first
dose under a fasted state, the median time to onset of perceptible pain relief in abdominoplasty’
(Study 105) and bunionectomy (Study 104) was 34 minutes and 60 minutes, respectively. ae

The effect of food on the pharmacokinetics of SUZ and M6-SUZ was evaluated in two studies,
011 and 016, using the 50 mg to-be-marketed formulation at a dose of 100 mg. Study 011
evaluated the effect of a high-fat meal (800 to 1000 calories, 50% derived from fat) on the
pharmacokinetics of SUZ and M6-SUZ. Study 016 evaluated the effects of various meal types
(low-fat meal [up to 500 calories with no more than 25% derived from fat], moderate-fat meal
[600 calories, 30% derived from fat], and high-fat meal) and the timing of meal administration
(60 minutes predose, at 0 hours, and 60 minutes postdose) on SUZ and M6-SUZ
pharmacokinetics.

Administration of SUZ after consuming high-fat meals and moderate-fat meals resulted in
considerable reduction in SUZ concentrations in initial time points. This decrease in initial
concentrations was well represented in individual level PK data where multiple subjects showed
very low concentrations for the first 2 to 3 hours postdose in the fed state versus fasted state
(Figure 18 and Figure 19). The high-fat meal and moderate-fat meal delayed the median time to
maximum concentration (T,,..) of SUZ from 3 hours in the fasted state to 5 hours in the fed state
and that of M6-SUZ from 8 to 10 hours in the fasted state to 24 hours in the fed state. The Cmax
and AUC of SUZ and M6-SUZ were not affected by any of the meal conditions.

A high-fat meal consumed 1 hour after the administration of SUZ did not affect the systemic
exposures of SUZ and M6-SUZ. Administration of the second dose of 50 mg with or without a
meal is also predicted to not affect the systemic exposures of SUZ and M6-SUZ.
