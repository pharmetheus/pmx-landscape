Detection of mutations, including ESR/

e Patients were required to provide blood samples of circulating tumor DNA analysis.
Samples were analyzed using Oncoscreen520 Plus (Burning Rock - China) and
Guardant360 (Guardant Health — rest of the world). Results were not required before
randomization since this was not a stratification factor. As a result, some patients on
EMBER-3 had an unknown ESR/m status. According to the Applicant’s analyses
patients were designated “ESR/m-detected” if either test showed a ligand binding domain
mutation or “ESR/m-not detected” if no ESR/ mutation(s) was detected, if the test did
not detect ctDNA, or test results were missing.

No tissue testing for ESR/m was performed.
