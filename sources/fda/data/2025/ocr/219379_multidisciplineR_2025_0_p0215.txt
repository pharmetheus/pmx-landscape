NDA/IND Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219379/219389
Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

FDA agrees with the exposure data presented in Table 48 [Mirdametinib Exposure for Pool 1
(n=133)], the pediatric population (n=58) and the adult population (n=75). Among 133 patients
who received mirdametinib, 62% were exposed one year or longer, 38% were exposed for 2
years or longer and 12% were exposed for more than 3 years.

For adult patients treated with mirdametinib in the ReNeu study, the median duration of
treatment was 22 months (range: 0.4 to 46 months. For pediatric patients treated with
mirdametinib in the ReNeu study, the median duration of treatment was 22 months (range: 1.6
to 40 months).
