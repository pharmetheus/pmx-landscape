Study Design

Study 1004 was designed as an open-label, parallel-group study in healthy adult subjects

(Figure 21). Subjects were stratified by body weight (i.e., <80 kg or >80 kg) and then
randomized 1:1:1:1:1:1 to one of six groups defined based on device (i.e., PFS-NSD or AI) and
injection site (i.e., abdomen, thigh, or upper arm). All subjects received a single 200 mg SC dose
of garadacimab.

The study enrolled male or female subjects aged 18 to 55 years, with body weight between 50
and 100 kg, inclusive, and body mass index between 18 and 30 kg/m”, inclusive. The only
penmitted therapies included hormonal contraceptives, as required; therapies to treat adverse
events (AEs); and all non-live vaccines, including vaccines against COVID-19. Administration
of any other investigational agent within five half-lives of 30 days, whichever was longer, before
administration of garadacimab was prohibited during the study.
