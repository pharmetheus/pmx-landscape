Komzifti (ziftomenib)

Dose-Dependency for Adverse Events

FDA looked at the most common TEAEs by dose level in the table below (FDA Table 56). Of
note, columns for dose levels of 50 mg, 100 mg, and 1000 mg QD were not included since only 1
patient was treated at each of those dose levels. However, those patients are still included in the
overall tabulations (n2=175). Any grade TEAEs occurred in 100% of patients at all dose levels.
There was a lower rate of grade 3-4 TEAEs in the 200 mg QD dose level compared to higher
dose levels, which could be interpreted as a dose-dependent effect. However, this observation is
confounded by the duration of treatment, as there was a shorter median duration in the 200 mg
and 400 mg QD cohorts (31 and 28 days) versus the 600 mg QD (67 days) cohort of patients, in
addition to the small sample size of patients treated at doses < 600 mg daily. Interestingly, DS
appeared to have similar rates of occurrence in this analysis. However, as noted above, the
different genetic makeup of the 200 mg versus 600 mg cohort confounds this analysis since
participants with KM724-1 AML made up high proportion of the 200 mg cohort and had a higher
tate of DS occurrence. Lastly, QTc prolongation was only reported in the 600 mg cohort. It is
unclear if this represents a dose effect with too few patients treated at the 800 mg or 1000 mg
dose level to observe this adverse rection or due to some other factor.
