The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA does not use inferential testing procedures to assess single arm trial results, including for
PRO-based endpoints. There was no control for multiplicity in any of the hypothesis tests
included in this report, therefore, claims of statistical significance reported by the Applicant
should be interpreted with caution.

FDA notes some observed improvements in functioning relative to baseline in patients treated
with mirdametinib, however notes the following limitations in the PRO data:
e@ The PRO data from a single arm trial with a small, heterogenous (age, location of PN
targets) population may be difficult to accurately interpret.
@ Compliance at Cycle 13 for all PROs except the PedsQL was below 70%. In the pediatric
population, compliance with the NRS-11 was 41% at cycle 13 and for pain interference it
was 57%. Compliance rate for the PedsQL was high at cycle 13 for all patients (>90%).
