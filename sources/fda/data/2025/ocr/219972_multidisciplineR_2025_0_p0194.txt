8.1.5. Integrated Assessment of Effectiveness
Data:

Results from the pivotal Study 21607, supporting the efficacy of sevabertinib in the treatment of
patients with advanced NSCLC, whose tumors harbor HER? activating mutations, are provided
in Section 8.1.2.

Applicant’s Position:

The primary efficacy data supporting this application are from 81 participants treated with 20 mg
BID sevabertinib who qualified for Group D in the Phase 1/2 Study 21607. This includes
participants with advanced NSCLC whose tumors harbor HER? activating mutation and who
received at least 1 prior line of systemic therapy but are naive to HER2 ex20ins-targeted therapy.

Treatment with sevabertinib 20 mg BID resulted in rapid, deep, and durable responses in this
primary efficacy population, as assessed by ORR, DOR and TTR by BICR. The confirmed ORR
assessed by BICR in Group D was 60.5% (FAS) as of 14 OCT 2024, with a median DOR of

6.8 months and a median TTR of 1.4 months. Updated data as of 28 MAR 2025 for Group D
show a confirmed ORR of 64.2% and median DOR of 9.2 months, assessed by BICR. The
increased ORR and median DOR in the updated analysis highlights the potential clinical benefit
of sevabertinib for participants with HER2-mutant NSCLC who have received a prior systemic
therapy.
