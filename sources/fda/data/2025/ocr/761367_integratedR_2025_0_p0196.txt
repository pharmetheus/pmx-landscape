Note: Precision and accuracy were demonstrated by interpolating the concentration of five controls and examining the relative error
(RE) and coefficient of variation (CV). Based on the concentrations of the controls (i.e., 1, 5, 40, 80, and 100 ng/mL), the range of
the assay was shown to be 1 to 100 ng/ml.

The relative error of 40% under selectivity was noted as not meeting acceptance criteria. This value was obtained in 1 out of 5
replicates at a concentration of 200 g/mL. Across all remaining replicates (n=9), the relative error ranged from -13 to 17%.
Acceptance criteria of relative error <30% for dilutional linearity is not consistent with FDA’s guidance for industry, M10 Bioanalytical
Method Validation and Study Sample Analysis (November 2022). The calculated mean concentration should be within 20% of the
nominal concentration. The observed relative error for dilutional linearity meets acceptance criteria.

Repeatability refers to three sets of controls assayed on one plate; interassay precision refers to three sets of controls assayed on a
different plate and performed on different days by one operator, interoperator precision refers to three sets of controls assayed on
three separate days by two different operators.

In response to the Agency’s inquiry, the Applicant submitted an amended bioanalytical method

validation report for method RnD-VAL-000141302 (v. 8.0, submitted on February 20, 2024).

The following information was provided on selectivity, benchtop stability and long-term

stability:

e Selectivity was assessed in plasma from subjects with HAE (N=20 lots). Individual lots were
spiked with garadacimab at concentrations of 1, 5, and 80 ng/mL. Note that 10 lots were

spiked at 5 ng/mL, while 10 separate lots (N=20 total) were spiked at 1 and 80 ng/mL. At all
concentrations, the %RE and %CV were <20% for at least 8/10 lots.

e Sample stability in 100% pooled healthy human plasma (sodium citrate) at temperatures
<-60°C is established for up to 3 years, 5 months and 25 days (~41 months).

e Long-term stability in plasma from subjects with HAE is ongoing. Samples are spiked with
garadacimab at concentrations of 15,000 and 7,500 ng/mL. Per the Applicant, at the time of
response (i.e., February 20, 2024), 10 months stability assessment has been completed and
shown to pass acceptance criteria.
