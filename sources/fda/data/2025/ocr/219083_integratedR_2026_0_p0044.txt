The Applicant’s proposed echocardiographic assessments during titration and maintenance
appeared reasonable based on the population PK/PD modeling and simulation:

1. Assessment during titration phase: The Applicant’s proposed flexible echocardiographic
assessment every 2 to 8 weeks (instead of at Weeks 2, 4, and 6 that was done in the Phase 3
trial) after initiation of treatment appeared reasonable. The pharmacometrics reviewer
evaluated the frequency of echocardiographic assessment during the titration phase by
performing model-based simulations at different titration frequencies of echocardiographic
assessments ranging from every 2 weeks to every 8 weeks. Figure 2 and Table 11 summarize
the percent of subjects with Valsalva LVOT-G <30 mm Hg and LVEF <50% based on
various echocardiographic assessment frequencies during the titration phase. Briefly, from a
safety perspective, every 2-week echocardiographic assessment (orange color) and every 8-
week echocardiographic assessment (yellow color) provided similar percentages of subjects
with LVEF <50% at 24-weeks. For efficacy, compared to every 2-week echocardiographic
assessment, every 8-week echocardiographic assessment resulted in 7% fewer subjects with
LVOT-G <30 mm Hg at 24 weeks. Beyond 24 weeks, a similar number of subjects achieved
the titration goals for LVEF >50% or Valsalva LVOT-G <30 mm Hg irrespective of titration
frequences.
