2.5 Proposed Clinical Population and Dosing Regimen

The recommended dose of Garadacimab (CSL312) is an initial loading dose of 400 mg
administered subcutaneously as two 200 mg injections on the first day of treatment
followed by a monthly dose of 200 mg to prevent attacks of hereditary angioedema
(HAE) in adult and pediatric patients (aged 12 years and older).
