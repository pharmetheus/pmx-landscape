The Applicant’s Position:

Lilly reviewed the protocol deviations and considered them unlikely to have substantially
affected patient safety or the results and conclusions in this report.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s characterization of important protocol deviations
in the ITT and ESR/m populations. The FDA also performed additional analyses of important
protocol deviations by subcategories for the ITT population.

The most common protocol deviations under visit schedule criteria were follow up imaging out
of window or not performed (9.4% vs 9.0%) and imaging for suspected progression not
performed within 14 days (1.5% vs 0.9%).

The most common protocol deviations under inclusion and exclusion criteria were timing and
prior lines of therapy (Inclusion Criteria #3: 5.1% vs 3.0%), measurable disease by RECIST v1.1
or non-measurable bone-only disease (Inclusion Criteria #8: 3.3% vs 1.5%), and presence of
symptomatic or untreated brain metastasis (Exclusion Criteria #20: 2.7% vs 2.4%). The FDA
requested further detail on the patients that did not meet eligibility criteria 3 (Table 8.10).
