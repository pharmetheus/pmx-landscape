The DOR estimates, based on observed time, indicate that 90% of patients in the pediatric
cohort and 88% of patients in the adult cohort are still in response at 12 months or longer.
Additionally, 48% of patients in the pediatric cohort and 50% of patients in the adult cohort
remain in response for 24 months or longer.

Dose/Dose Response

The Applicant’s Position:

Mirdametinib was dosed in Studies MEK-NF-201 and NF-106 by BSA. The median (minimum,
maximum) of actual doses received by participants with NF1 PN was 2.21 mg/m? (0.806, 2.86)
(Figure 4).

In addition, population and trial simulations were conducted using exposure-efficacy and
exposure-safety models to predict the impact of alternative target dose levels from 1.0 to 3.0
mg/m? BID (1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.2, and 3.0 mg/m?). The simulations support that exposures
associated with the proposed marketing dose of 2 mg/m? of mirdametinib provide an optimal
balance of robust efficacy with a tolerable incidence of low severity exposure-related AEs for
patients with NF1 PN. Please refer to Section 6.2.2.1 for additional simulation details in support
of the proposed 2mg/m2 dose used in Study MEK-NF-201, Figure 4.

The FDA’s Assessment:
Refer to Section 6, 19.4.2, and 19.4.3 for clinical pharmacology review details.
