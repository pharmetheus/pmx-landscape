NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

Strong or moderate CYP3A inhibitors: Monitor patients more frequently
for KOMZIFTI-associated adverse reactions.

Strong or moderate CYP3A inducers: Avoid concomitant use of
KOMZIFTI with strong or moderate CYP3A4 inducers.

PPIs: Avoid concomitant use of KOMZIFTI with PPIs.

H2RAs: Administer H2RAs either 10 hours before or 2 hours after
KOMZIFTI administration.

Antacids: Administer antacids either 2 hours before or 2 hours after
KOMZIFTI administration.

QTe-prolonging drugs: Avoid concomitant use of KOMZIFTI with other
product(s) with a known potential to prolong the QTc interval. If
concomitant use cannot be avoided, obtain ECGs when initiating, during
concomitant use, and as clinically indicated. Interrupt KOMZIFTI if the
QTc interval is > 500 ms or the change from baseline is > 60 ms.

Bridge between The Applicant utilized the same formulation throughout the clinical
formulations development process; therefore, bridging to the to-be-marketed
formulation is not necessary.
