NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s description of the statistical analysis plan. The
Supplemental SAP version 1.0 is based on protocol version 6.0 (dated 02 FEB 2024) and main
SAP version 2.0 (dated 14 OCT 2024). It described additional analyses that were not included in
the main SAP but used in the reports (e.g., CSR, Summary of Clinical Efficacy) for marketing
application. The additional analyses include concordance and discordance rates of ORR by BICR
and investigator, summary of DOR, and ORR subgroup analysis.

Although the supplemental SAP was created after the initial data cutoff date, it only documented
additional exploratory analyses and did not modify any prespecified primary analyses. These
analyses were descriptive in nature and did not influence regulatory decisions or labeling claims.

Protocol Amendments

The Applicant’s Descriptio

There were 4 global protocol amendments to the original Study 21607 protocol, dated

19 AUG 2021. A description of the key modifications in the 4 global amendments is presented in
Table 28. In addition, there were 8 local amendments to the protocol. In the local protocol
amendment 5 for the US, dated 12 DEC 2023, the Extension part of the study was removed.
However, based on Health Authority feedback, the Extension part is applicable to the US and,
therefore, this change was reversed in global protocol Amendment 6.

The changes made to the protocol amendments did not impact the integrity of the study or the
interpretation of the results.
