19.4.1. Population PK Analysis
19.4.1.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant’s final population PK (popPK) model is adequate for the purpose of
predicting total linvoseltamab concentration in patients with RRMM. Following the
proposed 5/25/200 mg dosage regimen, no clinically relevant differences in total
linvoseltamab exposure were identified according to age, weight, sex, race, mild to
moderate renal impairment, or mild hepatic impairment.
