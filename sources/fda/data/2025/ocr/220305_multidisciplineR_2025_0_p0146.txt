H2RAs: The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position that H2RAs can be
concomitantly used but that staggered dosing of H2RAs is appropriate (10 hours before or 2
hours after KOMZIFTI administration).

The Applicant’s PopPK analysis indicated that concomitant use with H2RA inhibitors on the
same day of ziftomenib administration has no clinically significant effect on ziftomenib PK
[Section 19.4.2.2]. However, this analysis may be limited, as dosing time of H2RA inhibitor to
ziftomenib is not recorded, and approximately 50% of patients were treated when there was not
restriction on ARA use in KO-MEN-001. Therefore, the FDA assessed the effect of H2RAs on
ziftomenib PK at C1D1, as shown in

FDA Table 26. Patients were stratified by azole antifungal use, given that ziftomenib exposure
increases when ziftomenib is coadministered with strong CYP3A inhibitors. There was a limited
sample size and high variability in ziftomenib PK, resulting in wide 90% CIs. However, the
analysis demonstrated that there is no evidence that coadministration of ziftomenib with H2RAs
decreases ziftomenib PK (GMRs ranged from 1.37 to 1.71).

Additionally, the FDA evaluated the effect of duration of H2RA use on response in NPM/-m
R/R AML (n=112, Phase 1b EAS + Phase 2; FDA Table 27). Patients were classified based on if
they received strong CYP3A-inhibiting azoles for minimum (1 - 10%), some (11-75%), or the
majority (more than 75%) of study time.
