Clinical chemistry

Significant increases in potassium levels were observed for males
(up to 18%) and females (up to 17%) at 230 mg/kg/day
garadacimab (IV) relative to control animals. A significant increase
in potassium levels were also observed for males (up to 13%) in
both SC treatment groups. Clear dose-dependent effects were not
observed. These findings are clinically monitorable.

Slight increases in total protein levels were observed for males (up
to 4%) in all IV treatment groups relative to control animals. Clear
dose-dependent effects were not observed. These findings were
considered clinically monitorable.

Significant increases in A1 and gamma globulin protein fractions
were observed for males (up to 100%) in all treatment groups (A1)
and at 230 mg/kg/day garadacimab IV (gamma) relative to control
animals.

Significant increases in gamma globulin protein fractions were
also observed for males (up to 100%) and females (up to 100%) in
SC treatment groups relative to control animals. Clear dose-
dependent effects were not observed. These findings are clinically
monitorable.

Significant decreases in phosphorous levels were observed for
females (up to 20%) in the SC treatment groups relative to control
animals. These findings are clinically monitorable.

Gross pathology

An increased incidence of enlarged axillary and/or inguinal lymph
nodes were observed in males and females at 260 mg/kg/day
garadacimab (SC) relative to control animals. These findings
correlated with histopathological findings of minimal to slight
increased generalized cellularity in the inguinal and axillary lymph
nodes in the SC treatment groups.
