Histopathology Linvoseltamab target organs of toxicity included the spleen, thymus,
mesenteric and mandibular lymph nodes, bone marrow, urinary
bladder and GALT/Peyer’s patch. Increased cellularity was shown in
the bone marrow with an increase in CD3 and decrease in CD20
staining. Linvoseltamab also induced inflammation as observed by the
increased inflammatory cell infiltrates in various tissues across all dose
groups. Cellular inflammation was also observed in the submucosa of
the Gl tract and urinary bladder. These findings were generally
reversible at the end of the recovery phase.
