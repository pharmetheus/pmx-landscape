July 27, 2018: Pre-IND Written Responses Only (WRO) responses were issued.
Topics addressed in the meeting included key elements of the R5458-ONC-1826
study, including the patient population, starting dose and dose escalation plan,
and DLT criteria.

September 21, 2018: New IND was submitted.

October 19, 2018: Study was determined to be safe to proceed.

March 11, 2021: Preliminary Breakthrough Therapy Designation (BTD) Request
Advice Meeting was held.

o The Agency informed the Sponsor that BTD would be premature, given
the small sample size, lack of data in patients treated at the RP2D, and
limited follow-up. The Agency advised the sponsor to request an EOP1
meeting to discuss future development and dose selection.

April 23, 2021: WRO responses with feedback on the completed 5-week
toxicology study and reproductive toxicology package were issued.
