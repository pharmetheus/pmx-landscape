NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

22 FEB 2024

BTD was granted for the proposed indication of “the treatment of adult patients with unresectable or
metastatic NSCLC whose tumors have activating HER2 (ERBB2) mutations, as detected by an
FDA-approved test, and who have received a prior systemic therapy”.

14 MAR 2024

The IND 170680 for Phase 3 confirmatory Study 22615 was submitted. Study may proceed was
granted on 12 APR 2024.

31 MAY 2024

Type B meeting to discuss how to expedite the development of sevabertinib in the target indication
(Reference ID: 5395385).

The Sponsor agreed to the Agency's proposal to provide an efficacy update for Group D (naive to
HER?2-targeted treatment) during the NDA review; the Sponsor and the Agency agreed that the
update would include the duration of response follow-up data of 26 months for approximately 100%
of responders. The Agency also acknowledged the Sponsor's proposal to revise the interim analysis
for PFS to 80% from 70% of events in the Phase 3 confirmatory study. The Agency noted this may
be acceptable depending on the results observed at this timepoint and the risk, even though still
present, would be reduced with greater maturity of the data. The Agency also provided additional
feedback on CMC, regulatory and toxicology topics.

10 JUN 2024

Thermo Fisher Scientific in partnership with Bayer Healthcare Pharmaceuticals, Inc., submitted a
Pre-Submission (Q241442) to obtain feedback from the Agency regarding the proposed clinical
validation strategy for the ODxTT for use as a CDx for patients with NSCLC harboring activating
mutations in HER2 (ERBB2) for selection of treatment with sevabertinib.

23 SEP 2024

Thermo Fisher Scientific in partnership with Bayer Healthcare Pharmaceuticals, Inc., submitted a
Pre-Submission (Q242491) to obtain feedback from the Agency regarding the proposed clinical
validation strategy for the ODxET for use as a CDx for testing tumor and plasma specimens from
patients with NSCLC to determine whether they harbor activating mutations in HER2 (ERBB2) for
selection of treatment with sevabertinib.
