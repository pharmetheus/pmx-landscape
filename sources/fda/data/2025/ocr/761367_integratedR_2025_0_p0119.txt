Major Findings

Mortality

There were two unscheduled deaths during the Treatment Period.

One female animal (Animal #537) in the 200 mg/kg/week SC group
was euthanized for welfare reasons on Day 66 (Week 10). The
Applicant's report stated that Animal #537 exhibited rapid
development of a large swelling starting on the right-hand mandible,
extending across the right-hand side of the throat and down the right
shoulder immediately prior to termination. The animal also had
clinical signs of a distinct raspiness on vocalization, an elevated body
temperature (i.e., 39.3°C), and its breathing was reportedly slightly
pyrexic. The animal exhibited macroscopic abnormalities of dark
colored lungs, discolored (pale and dark areas) and enlarged
kidneys, abnormal shape of the gallbladder, enlarged mandibular
lymph node, raised areas of the skeletal muscle, thyroids cysts,
edematous mandibular salivary gland, and dark areas at the
subcutaneous injection sites. This animal exhibited histopathological
findings of marked myofiber necrosis and slight regeneration of the
skeletal muscle, minimal plasmacytosis of the mandibular lymph
node, minimal inflammatory cell infiltration of the mandibular and
sublingual salivary glands and slight hemorrhage and inflammatory
cell infiltration of the subcutaneous injection sites which correlated
with observed macroscopic findings. This animal was confirmed as
ADA-positive.
