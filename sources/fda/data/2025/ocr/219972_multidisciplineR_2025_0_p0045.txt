NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

Applicant performed a human-induced pluripotent stem cell-based assay (NAM), in which
thalidomide/retinoic acid were used as the positive controls for adverse reproductive effects. In
this assay, sevabertinib disrupted stem cell differentiation into cardiomyocytes and hepatocytes.
Fertility studies were not conducted with sevabertinib nor are they warranted for the advanced
cancer indication.

Based on findings in animals and its mechanism of action and consistent with current
recommendations for non-genotoxic compounds that are teratogenic and/or embryo-fetal lethal
described in the FDA Guidance for Industry, “Oncology Pharmaceuticals: Reproductive Toxicity
Testing and Labeling Recommendations,” effective contraception use is recommended for male
and female patients during treatment with HYRNUO and for 1 week after the last dose, based on
five times the half-life of sevabertinib measured in patients (° hours). Following
administration of [!4C]-sevabertinib to lactating rats, sevabertinib-derived radioactivity was
approximately 13- to 26-fold higher in milk than maternal plasma. During the 32-hour
monitoring period, approximately 1.3% of the administered dose of sevabertinib was excreted
into the milk. Thus, women are advised not to breastfeed during treatment with sevabertinib and
for 1 week after the last dose.

Recommendation:
The nonclinical pharmacology and toxicology data submitted to this NDA are adequate to
support the approval of HYRNUO for the proposed indication.
