Recommendations and Comments

Based on data from Study 21607, the observed largest mean increase in the
QTc interval for sevabertinib was 3.9 msec (upper confidence interval of 5.8
msec) at steady state Cmax after administration of 60 mg QD, which covers
the anticipated therapeutic sevabertinib Cmax by 2.2-fold. According to
FDA’s QT-IRT analysis (DAARTS ID: 5634071), given the absence of a
positive control and a placebo group in Study 21607, there is reluctance to
conclude a lack of an effect of sevabertinib on the QTc interval. FDA
proposes to describe the observation from Study 21067 in the label. i.e., at 2
times the maximum recommended dose, a mean increase in the QTc interval
>20 ms was not observed.

FDA provided labeling revisions following the guidance and current labeling
practice. Overall, the proposed labeling language in the Clinical
Pharmacology pertinent sections are generally acceptable upon the
Applicant’s agreement to the FDA revisions.
