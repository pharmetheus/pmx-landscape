15.2.1. Study Design, Study 3002

Study 3002 was a multicenter, open-label, phase 3b study to investigate the long-term safety and
efficacy of garadacimab when administered once monthly for at least 12 months. Subjects
eligible to participate in this study were:

e Subjects from the phase 2 trial (2001).
e Subjects from the phase 3 trial (3001).

e Garadacimab-naive subjects (i.e., newly enrolled subjects with C1-INH [Type I or Type II]
HAE).

The study comprised four periods (see Figure 62):

e Screening Period (up to 1 month; for garadacimab-naive subjects).
e Run-in Period (up to 2 months; for garadacimab-naive subjects).

e Treatment Period (12 months minimum; open-label).

e Follow-up Period (2 months after End of Treatment [EoT] Visit).

Following informed consent, garadacimab-naive subjects underwent a Screening Period of up to
1 month to determine eligibility for enrollment in the study. Screened subjects who met all
inclusion criteria, and none of the exclusion criteria, entered the Run-in Period. If a subject did
not meet the criteria for entering the Run-in Period within 30 days, the subject could have been
tescreened once.
