Serious Adverse Events

The FDA performed an analysis of all-grade treatment-emergent SAEs occurring in >2 % of
subjects in the main safety population of Study KO-MEN-001, which is presented in FDA Table
44 below. The results agreed with the Applicant’s analysis overall but differed in the rates of
grouped events. Most of the SAEs are expected during the care of patients with R/R AML such
as infections and bleeding events. DS and leukocytosis are the two SAEs whose rates are likely
due to ziftomenib treatment and its mechanism of action as an agent that releases the genetic
block to blast differentiation.
