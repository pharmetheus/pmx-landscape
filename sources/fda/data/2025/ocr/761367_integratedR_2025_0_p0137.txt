Andembry / garadacimab
Parameter Major Findings
Toxicokinetics Increases of systemic exposures were dose proportional. Plasma Cmax and
AUCo.s values increased in a dose-proportional manner with dose
following multiple dosing. The mean half-life (t12) values were in the range
of 29-49 h. The mean accumulation ratios for Cmax (RACmax) were in the
range 1.24-1.65. The mean accumulation ratios for AUCo4s (RAAUCo-48)
were in the range of 1.33-1.63. An accumulation of up to 1.81-fold was
observed.

Excerpted from Applicant's submission
ADA The ADA results were inconclusive as the control animals were tested
positive. In the control, 4/22 (approx. 18.2%) animals tested positive on
Day 19, and 3 of these 4 animals remained positive on Day 29. These
results are considered false positive due to unknown reasons, as no
garadacimab was detected in plasma (toxicokinetic) samples taken from
the control. garadacimab plasma concentration at the time of ADA
sampling remained above the drug tolerance level in the assay system.
Since exposure to garadacimab and pharmacodynamic activity (ie., aPTT
prolongation) was confirmed throughout the study, potential ADA formation
was not considered to have impacted the outcome of the study.
APTT Mean aPTT values determined approximately 5 min after the first injection

were more than three-fold higher in garadacimab-treated animals than in
the control. The mean aPTT on Day 18 (approximately 5 min after the fifth
and final administration) showed mean values approximately 4- to 5-fold
higher than the control. On Day 29 after mating (11 days after the last
dose), mean aPTT values of treated animals remained approximately 4- to
6-fold higher than the control. There was no clear dose-relationship shown.
