The FDA’s Assessment:

The updated results for DOR in the revised population are presented below. Among 50
responders in Group D, median DOR by BICR was 9.2 months (95% CI: 6.3, 15.0), witha
slightly shorter median DOR follow-up of 9.0 months (95% CI: 8.2, 19.3). Among 20 responders
in Group E, median DOR by BICR was 7.0 months (95% CI: 5.6, NR), with a median DOR
follow-up of 9.7 months (95% CI: 6.8, NR).
