IV. ESRI Mutations

In EMBER-3, circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) was analyzed using the FDA-approved
Guardant360 CDx to detect the presence of ESR/ mutations at baseline prior to initiating study
treatment. A total of 37.9% (323/853) of patients had an ESR/ mutation detected. Among these
patients, the four most common ESR/ mutations detected were D538G (21.8%), Y537S (12.9%),
