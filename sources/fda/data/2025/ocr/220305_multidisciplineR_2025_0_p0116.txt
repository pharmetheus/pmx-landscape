Komzifti (ziftomenib)

Neutral Red Uptake Phototoxicity Assay of KO-539 in BALB/c 3T3 Mouse Fibroblasts /
KO-TOX-0019 / Section 8.1 of Module 2.6.6 and Section 17 Module 2.6.7.

Key Findings: ziftomenib was demonstrated to have phototoxic potential

Study Type In vitro photosafety testing with BALB/c 3T3 mouse fibroblasts
GLP Yes
compliance

Test System

The phototoxicity potential of ziftomenib was measured by the relative
reduction in viability of BALB/c 3T3 mouse fibroblasts exposed to
ziftomenib and ultraviolet radiation (UVR) as compared to the viability of
fibroblasts exposed to ziftomenib in the absence of UVR.

OECD 432-recommended cell survival and OD540 criteria and promethazine
cytotoxicity and phototoxicity criteria were met indicating that the assays
were valid.
