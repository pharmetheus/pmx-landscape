Acceptance criteria of relative error <30% for dilutional linearity is not considered with FDA’s Guidance for Industry, M10
Bioanalytical Method Validation and Study Sample Analysis (November 2022). The calculated mean concentration should be within
20% of the nominal concentration. The observed relative error of 25.4% was derived from 1/6 individual plasma lots. Among the
remaining five lots, relative error ranged from -6.9 to 11.4%.
