Key Study Findings

e In this study, pregnant rabbits (FO) were administered garadacimab doses of 0, 10 (LD), 30
(MD), and 100 (HD) mg/kg/dose by SC, and 100 (HD) mg/kg/dose by IV, once every 5 days
from implantation (Gestation Day 7) through weaning (Lactation Day 38). FO toxicity
animals were dosed on Gestation Day 7, 12, 17, 22, and 27 and Lactation Days 3, 8, 13, 18,
23, 28, 33, and 38. FO placental transfer animals were dosed on Gestation Day 7, 12, 17, 22,
and 27. The F1 generation were not directly administered the test article.

e For FO dams, no garadacimab-related effects were observed on mortality, clinical signs, body
weight, natural delivery, litter data, or maternal macroscopic observations.

e For the F1 generation, no garadacimab-related effects were observed on mortality, clinical
signs, body weight, developmental signs, functional observation battery, eye blink response,
presence of motile sperm, reproductive performance, cesarean section parameters, or
preweaning or postweaning macroscopic observations.

e Garadacimab-related prolongation in aPTT was observed in all treated FO females. It was an
expected pharmacological effect of garadacimab.

than dose proportional in dose levels from 10 to 100 mg/kg SC. Accumulation was
