Andembry / garadacimab

Additionally, the phase 3 PK data were incorporated into the E-R analyses for efficacy based on
the relative risk of HAE attacks. The model-predicted relative risk of an HAE attack as a
function of simulated steady-state garadacimab exposure (area under the concentration-time
curve (AUC) over one dosing interval at steady state) is shown in Figure 2. Simulated steady-
state exposure was derived from the proposed dosing regimen consisting of a 400 mg SC loading
dose followed by 200 mg SC once monthly. Results indicate that at steady state, the area under
the plasma concentration-time curve over one dosing interval at steady state (AUC tauss)
following the 200 mg once monthly maintenance dose yielded a reduction in the relative risk of
HAE attacks near the plateau of the curve. Also, population predictions of exposure indicated
that about 75% of subjects will achieve an at least 90% reduction in the HAE attack rate
compared to baseline.
