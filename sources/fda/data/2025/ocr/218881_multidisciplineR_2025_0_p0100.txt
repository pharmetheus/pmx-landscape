The FDA’s Assessment:

L Food effect:

Administration of a single dose of imlunestrant 400 mg with a low-fat meal (approximately 475
calories with 13% fat, 16% protein, and 71% carbohydrates) increased imlunestrant Cmax 3.6-fold
and AUCo int 2-fold as compared to administration under fasting conditions. Therefore, FDA
agrees with the Applicant that imlunestrant should be administered in the fasted state (at least 2
hours before and at least 1 hour after food). In EMBER-3, patients were instructed to take
imlunestrant at least 2 hours before or 1 hour after food (i.e., the recommended administration
instructions) therefore, EMBER-3 evaluated safety of imlunestrant under the recommended
administration instructions with regard to food.
