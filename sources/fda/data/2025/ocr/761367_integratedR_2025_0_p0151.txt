14.2.2. Study 1001 (SAD Study in Healthy Subjects)

Title
A phase 1, single-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, single ascending dose

study to investigate the safety, tolerability, and PKs of IV and SC garadacimab in healthy
subjects.

Objectives

Primary objective of this study was to assess the safety and tolerability of escalating doses of
garadacimab after a single IV infusion or SC injection in healthy subjects. Characterization of
PK and immunogenicity were also included as secondary objectives.

Study Design

Study 1001 was designed as a single-center, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled,
single ascending dose study in healthy adult subjects (Figure 24). All subjects received a single
IV or SC dose of garadacimab and were followed until 85 days postdose. A total of eight cohorts
(n=6/cohort; 4 active, 2 placebo) were enrolled, including five IV cohorts in which subjects
received placebo or garadacimab at doses of 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, or 10 mg/kg, and three SC cohorts in
