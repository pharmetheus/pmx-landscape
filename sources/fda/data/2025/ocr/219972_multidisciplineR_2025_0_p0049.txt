In vivo antitumor efficacy of sevabertinib in the patient-derived NSCLC xenograft model
CIG-2842 (EGFR ex20insASV) in mice

The therapeutic efficacy and tolerability of sevabertinib was assessed in the patient-derived
xenograft model, CTG-2842, carrying an EGFR V769_D770insASV insertion (ex20insASV)
(26). Female athymic Nude-Foxn/™ mice were subcutaneously transplanted with CTG-2842
tumor fragments. At a tumor size of 150 mm?-300 mm”, treatment with either vehicle (control)
or sevabertinib at 10 mg/kg, 25 mg/kg, or 50 mg/kg QD was initiated and continued for 28 days.
Animal body weight and tumor size were measured twice weekly and at study end, tumors were
collected for tumor weight analyses.

Treatment with sevabertinib was well tolerated and resulted in significant anti-tumor activity
compared to the vehicle control and tumor regression with final T/Cvorweight values of 0.06/0.02
