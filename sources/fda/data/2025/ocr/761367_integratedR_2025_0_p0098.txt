The proposed population for garadacimab includes adolescent subjects with HAE aged 12 years
and older. Adolescent subjects with HAE were enrolled in trial 3001 (N=6) and study 3002
(N=10, including 5 rollover subjects from trial 3001). In both studies, the median body weight
among adolescents receiving garadacimab was lower than the overall median body weight
among all subjects receiving garadacimab (trial 3001: 60.3 kg and 78.0 kg for adolescents and
overall, respectively; study 3002: 55.7 kg and 78.3 kg for adolescents and overall, respectively).

Despite the lower body weight in adolescents relative to the overall population enrolled in

trial 3001, there was no apparent difference in the observed garadacimab trough concentrations

throughout the Treatment Period (Figure 16). In study 3002, garadacimab trough concentrations
were 1.2 to 1.8-fold greater in adolescents relative to adults from Month 3 through Month 12

Figure 17). Note that only three adolescent subjects provided data at Month 12, the time point
that showed 1.8-fold greater exposure among adolescents. Data at Month 12 should be

interpreted with caution.
