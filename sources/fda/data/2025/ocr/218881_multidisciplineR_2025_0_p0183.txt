5. Patient aa:

Tumor mutation status: ESR/m- not detected

This 72-year-old white female in the United States with metastases in the lung and a history of
hypertension, diabetes mellitus 2, hypercholesterolemia, and pulmonary embolism on apixaban
died on day 54 of cardiac arrest. The last dose of imlunestrant was on day 54 and she had no
dose omissions or reductions. On day 54 she reported heart racing, vomiting and profuse
sweating. She died in the ambulance on the way to the hospital within 30 minutes of the onset of
her symptoms. Imaging done two days prior revealed progression of disease in multiple bone
lesions. The cardiac arrest was thought not to be related to imlunestrant by the investigator and
the Applicant. The FDA considers that there is insufficient information to determine if the study
treatment contributed to death but cannot exclude the possibility.

1. Patient Oe
Tumor mutation status: Not evaluable
This 68-year-old white female from Mexico with metastases to lung, liver, and bone, and a
history of hypertension and diabetes mellitus type 2 complicated by diabetic retinopathy, died of
elevated calcium levels on day 62. The patient had previously been treated with
cyclophosphamide, epirubicin, docetaxel, and anastrozole. On day 28 she was advised by the
investigator to present to a health system because she had been noted to have clinical
deterioration and poor performance status. She had also stopped attending scheduled activities
and was non-compliant with denosumab treatment. When she presented to a health system for
care she was told her calcium was significantly elevated, however, no laboratory results or
additional details are available. She received her last dose of fulvestrant on day 29 and on day 62
the patient was admitted to the hospital due to elevated calcium levels; she died the same day.
