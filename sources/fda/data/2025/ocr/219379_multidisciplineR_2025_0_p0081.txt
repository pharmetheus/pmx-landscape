NDA/IND Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219379/219389
Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

Based on the totality of data from the genotoxicity studies, mirdametinib is not genotoxic.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s interpretation of the in vivo micronucleus assay. In this
study, female rats treated with mirdametinib showed dose-related increases in percent of
micronucleated polychromatic erythrocytes (%MNPCE) compared to concurrent controls and
achieved statistical significance at the high dose of 3 mg/kg, suggesting that mirdametinib may
have clastogenic potential. However, the results of the 3 mg/kg dose level did not meet the
criteria for a positive response because the results were within the levels of historical controls
albeit closer to the higher limit (historical control range % MNPCE = 0.16 to 0.59; at 3 mg/kg %
MNPCE = 0.54). The Applicant attributes this increase to be potentially related to bone marrow
toxicity, but this endpoint was not measured in the study. Overall, the FDA considered the
results of the micronucleus assay to be equivocal. Follow-up testing demonstrated
mirdametinib was negative for clastogenicity in the in vivo chromosomal aberration assay in
rats, but this study did not rule out the potential for aneugenicity. Therefore, FDA determined
that mirdametinib is genotoxic with an aneugenic mode of action.

See section 5.5.1 Other Toxicology Studies for discussion of genetic toxicology of metabolites.

5.5.3. Carcinogenicity

The Applicant's Position:
Data:

The carcinogenic potential of mirdametinib was assessed in a 6-month study in transgenic
rasH2 mice (20196651). The dose levels established for this study (0, 2, 5, 15 mg/kg/day) were
based on findings in the 28-day mouse study and feedback provided by the Carcinogenicity
Advisory Council at FDA.

Based on microscopic evaluation, there were no carcinogenic effects of mirdametinib at any
dose level in this study.

A 2-year rat carcinogenicity study (20435943) will be conducted (start of dosing planned in
Aug2024), and the Applicant intends to submit the results post-approval.
