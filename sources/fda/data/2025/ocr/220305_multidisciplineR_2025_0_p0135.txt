NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

Based on the FDA analysis, elimination half-life (t12) was 190 (23%) hours when
coadministered with strong CYP3A inhibitors and 94 (21%) hours when administered without
CYP3A inhibitors. CL/F was 42 (29%) L/h when coadministered with strong CYP3A inhibitors
and 101 (27%) L/h when administered without CYP3A inhibitors

Excretion: The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position that the role of renal elimination of
ziftomenib is minimal, given that 0.5% total radioactivity was excreted in the urine and renal
clearance (CL) was low (0.1 L/h).

The FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s position that “
In study KO-MEN-005, 86% of total radioactivity was excreted in the feces, with

73% as unchanged drug. The majority of unchanged drug in the feces following a single oral

dose of 400 mg ['4C]-ziftomenib likely represents unabsorbed drug due to low bioavailability.

Transport: The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that ziftomenib is a substrate of P-
gp and BCRP in vitro, which likely play a role in ziftomenib absorption given low bioavailability
despite moderate permeability. Ziftomenib is not a substrate of OATP1B1, OATP1B3, BSEP, or
MRP? in vitro. It is unlikely that ziftomenib is a substrate of renal transporters (e.g., OAT1/3,
OCT2) due to the minimal role of renal clearance in ziftomenib elimination.

Intrinsic & Extrinsic Factors

For detailed information on the FDA’s Assessment of intrinsic factors, see Section 6.3.2.3. For

PD

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the in vitro menin-KMT2A ICs0 for
ziftomenib is 37 nM in vitro. The Applicant did not submit clinical PD data.

QTc Prolongation: The FDA does not with the Applicant’s assessment that ziftomenib has no
clinically relevant effect on QTc.

The FDA evaluated the effect of KOMZIFTI on the QTc interval in n=133 R/R AML patients
treated at 600 mg QD. Ziftomenib exhibited a concentration-dependent increase in QTc interval
with largest mean increase in QTc interval predicted to be 7.7 ms (upper confidence interval
[CI]=12.6 ms). 8% (n=10) patients had QTc > 500 ms and increase in QTc > 60 ms over
baseline.

Metabolite PK/PD

PK: The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment that the major circulating metabolites KO-
516 and KO-739 are formed via CYP3A-mediated metabolism. After a single dose of
ziftomenib, KO-516 and KO-739 were minor metabolites (metabolite-to-parent ratio [M:P] for
AUCins approximately equal to 0.1 and 0.2, respectively).
