15.3.1. Trial Design, Trial 2001

Results from this study support the efficacy of garadacimab in preventing HAE attacks. This was
a two-period (Treatment Periods 1 and 2), multicenter (10 U.S. sites and 9 ex-U.S. sites),
randomized, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, dose-ranging, phase 2 trial among 38 adult
subjects (20 to 65 years of age) with C1-INH (Type I or Type Il) HAE or FXII/PLG (Type III)
HAE. Treatment Period 1 was the randomized (1:1:1:1), double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-
ranging portion of the study, and Treatment Period 2 was an open-label portion (see Figure 65).
Before entering the trial, subjects with Type I or Type II HAE had to demonstrate >4 HAE
attacks over a consecutive 2-month period during the 3 months prior to Screening.
