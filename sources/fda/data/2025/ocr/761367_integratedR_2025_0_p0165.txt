Andembry / garadacimab

PD Results

FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity was assessed as a PD marker. Garadacimab showed dose-
dependent inhibition of FXTla-mediated kallikrein activity (Figure 32 and Figure 33). In SC
cohorts, the time of peak response was approximately correlated with the garadacimab Tmax.
Mean inhibition was greater in Japanese relative to Caucasian subjects. However, there was large
variability in measured kallikrein activity in both cohorts. Among SC cohorts, the most potent
inhibition was observed at the 600 mg dose. The 3 and 10 mg/kg IV cohorts showed inhibition of
FXIla-mediated kallikrein activity. Activity retumed to baseline by about Day 64 for the 200 mg
SC and 3 mg/kg IV cohorts, whereas activity did not return to baseline by Day 85 for the 600 mg
SC or 10 mg/kg IV cohorts. In study 1001, a return to baseline in FXIla-mediated kallikrein
activity was observed by Day 85 for cohorts receiving 10 mg/kg IV. The differences observed
across studies may be due to variability of PD.
