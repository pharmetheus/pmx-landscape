ADA Positive ADA results were noted for the control and HD groups. The

reason for ADA-positive response in control group samples was unclear.
There was no evidence that control animals received garadacimab (all
concentration values of garadacimab in the vehicle-control group were
below the lower limit of quantitation, <500 ng/mL). The presence of ADA
did not appear to have a neutralizing effect or consistently cause a
reduction in observed exposure (concentration results from ADA-positive
and ADA-negative animals were generally similar at comparable doses
and timepoints) to garadacimab. Results from ADA-positive animals were
not excluded from descriptive statistics or toxicokinetic analysis.
