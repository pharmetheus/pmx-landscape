Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

Neoplastic Changes | * Based on microscopic evaluation, no carcinogenic effect related to mirdametinib

administration was observed at any dose level in this study.

* In both sexes, the NMU positive control group exhibited the expected neoplastic
(increased incidence of malignant lymphoma, squamous cell papilloma, and squamous

Mirdametinib showed no evidence of carcinogenicity in the 6-month CByB6F1/Tg rasH2
hemizygous mice study.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA previously reviewed the 6-month carcinogenicity study in CByB6F1/Tg rasH2
hemizygous mice under IND 139883. The review focused on neoplastic findings. FDA agrees
with the Applicant’s conclusion that no carcinogenic effects related to administration of
mirdametinib were observed at any dose level tested in the 6-month mouse study.

The Applicant submitted a special protocol assessment (SPA) for the 2-year rat study under IND
139883, which was reviewed by the FDA on June 25, 2024. The final study report will be
submitted to the NDA as a post marketing requirement (PMR).

5.5.4. Reproductive and Developmental Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

The developmental and reproductive toxicology assessment of mirdametinib consisted of a
definitive fertility study in male and female rats and preliminary EFD toxicity studies in pregnant
rats and rabbits.

Given the significant EFD toxicities observed in both the rat and rabbit range-finding studies,
and the requirement for MEK in embryonic development (Giroux 1999), neither definitive
embryo-fetal toxicity studies nor pre- and post-natal development studies were conducted for
mirdametinib; this was agreed upon with the FDA (email, 24Feb2023).
