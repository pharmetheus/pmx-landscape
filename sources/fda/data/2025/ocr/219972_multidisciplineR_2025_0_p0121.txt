NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

0. The effect of concomitant use of moderate CYP3A
inhibitors on sevabertinib Cmax and AUC are
unknown. Monitor patients for increased HYRNUO-
associated adverse reactions.

e Effect of Strong and Moderate CYP3A4 Inducers on
HYRNUO:

o Sevabertinib AUC decreased by 79% and Cmax
decreased by 57% following concomitant use of
carbamazepine (strong CYP3A4 inducer) 600 mg
once daily. Avoid concomitant use with a strong or
moderate CYP3A4 inducers with HYRNUO.

Effect of HYRNUO on Other Drugs

e Midazolam (CYP3A4 substrate) AUC increased 2-fold
and Cmax increased 1.8-fold following concomitant use
of HYRNUO 20 mg twice daily. Avoid concomitant use
of HYRNUO with CYP3A4 substrates where minimal
increases in the concentration may lead to serious adverse
reactions unless otherwise recommended in the
Prescribing Information of the CYP3A substrate.

e Dabigatran etexilate (P-gp substrate) AUC increased 1.4-
fold following concomitant use of HYRNUO 20 mg twice
daily. Refer to the Prescribing Information of P-gp
substrates where minimal increases in the concentration
may lead to serious adverse reactions.

e  Sevabertinib is an inhibitor of CYP1A1 in vitro. Refer to
the Prescribing Information of CYP1A1 substrates when
concomitant use with these substrates (see Section
6.3.2.4).
