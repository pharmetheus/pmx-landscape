 Elacestrant, on oral SERD, was approved January 2023 for
treatment of postmenopausal women or adult men with ER-
positive, HER2-negative, ESRJ-mutated advanced or
metastatic breast cancer with disease progression following at
least one line of endocrine therapy.

For patients in the ESR1-mutated
population, imlunestrant demonstrated
a statistically significant and clinically
meaningful improvement in PFS by
investigator, which was supported by a
favorable trend in OS and secondary
endpoints such as PFS by BICR.

e The EMBER-3 trial enrolled 874 patients randomized to
imlunestrant monotherapy, a treatment of physician’s choice
(TPC) arm of endocrine therapy of either fulvestrant or
exemestane, or a third arm of an additional investigational
combination regimen, in patients with ER+, HER2- locally
advanced or metastatic breast cancer previously treated with
an AI, with or without a CDK4/6 inhibitor.

e There were 323 patients enrolled with ESR/m disease across
all 3 arms, with 256 patients with ESR/m in total enrolled in
the imlunestrant monotherapy arm and TPC arm.

¢ In the ESR/m population, median PFS by investigator was 5.5
months versus 3.8 months (HR 0.62, 95% CI: 0.46-0.82; p-
value 0.0008).

e PFS by BICR results were supportive of the primary endpoint
