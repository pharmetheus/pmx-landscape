After review, in the WRO meeting minutes dated December 20, 2024, the FDA advised the
Applicant not to change the remaining formal OS tests between imlunestrant arm and TPC arm
in the ESR/m population as they were made after data unblinding and could be data driven.
Additionally, the FDA stated that changing from event-driven to the proposed calendar-based
analyses would be likely to lead to inadequately powered analyses. FDA warned the Applicant
that analyses conducted under the SAP Amendment v1 would be considered post-hoc and
descriptive only.

Against FDA advice, the Applicant proceeded with SAP Addendum 1 and conducted updated
OS analyses with DCO date of January 30, 2025. Topline results of updated OS analyses based,
on the DCO date of January 30, 2025, were provided and discussed by the Applicant

meeting held on May 23, 2025. The
FDA considered these OS analyses post-hoc and descriptive only. Per meeting minutes, “The
FDA agreed with the proposal to return to the plan of the future OS interim and final analyses as
pre-specified initially in SAP Version 5 before data unblinding, .... The FDA reiterated that the
Sponsor should only keep and perform the preplanned, event-driven OS analyses and revise the
SAP back to Version 5. Sponsor acknowledged.”

Per FDA’s Information Request dated February 6, 2025, the Applicant stated that the interim
futility analysis of PFS for the comparison of imlunestrant arm and TPC arm in the ITT
population was conducted based on a data cutoff date of February 27, 2023, under the guidance
of an independent DMC, who reviewed unblinded data prepared by the independent Statistical
Analysis Center.

least one line of endocrine therapy. In addition, the designs of several registrational intent trials
