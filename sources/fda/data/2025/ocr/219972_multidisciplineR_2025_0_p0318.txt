Of note, none of the statistically
significant covariate effects on the risk
of selected AEs (diarrhea and rash)
were found to have an effect on dose
modifications due to TEAEs also.
Hence, a statistically significant
baseline covariate effect on AE risk
does not necessarily indicate that a
higher AE risk in a specific sub-
population, e.g. elderly, can be
mitigated by dose modifications. For
instance, the incidence of all grade
diarrhea grouped by exposure quartile
was similar in elderly (265 years)
compared to participants <65 years, i.e.
in both age groups the incidence of all
grade diarrhea was lower for subjects
with the lowest exposure (Q1) vs
participants with higher exposures (Q2-
Q4) (See Figure 15-1 in Module 5.3.3.5,

Report B003834).
