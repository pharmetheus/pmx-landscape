Clinical Signs

Fecal and vomitus abnormalities incidence was higher in 1 and 10
mg/kg monkeys during the dosing phase. The incidence decreased
during the recovery phase except for 10 mg/kg monkeys. Numerous
veterinary interventions occurred during the study to control GI
disruption, dehydration and to provide extra food supplements. Scabs
were present in various locations on the body for several monkeys at
all dose levels during the dosing and recovery phases with uncertain
relationship to linvoseltamab.

Body Weights

No linvoseltamab-related alterations in body weight or body weight
gain occurred during the dosing or recovery phases.

Food Consumption

Low qualitative food consumption was noted in 10 mg/kg male
monkeys with no correlating effect on body weights.
