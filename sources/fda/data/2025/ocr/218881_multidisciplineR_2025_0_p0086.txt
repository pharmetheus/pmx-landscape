6.2 Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1 Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:

Imlunestrant has been administered orally at doses ranging from

e 200 to 1200 mg QD in patients with ER+ locally advanced or MBC and ER+ recurrent,
persistent, or metastatic EEC, and
e 200 to 800 mg QD in healthy participants.

Exposures of imlunestrant increased proportionally across the 200 mg to 1200-mg dose range.

Patients with ER+, HER2- locally advanced or MBC in the EMBER-3 Phase 3 pivotal study
were administered 400 mg QD imlunestrant. The Cmax.ss, Cmin,ss, aud AUC o-24,s5 were

141 ng/mL (45% CV), 71.2 ng/mL (54% CV), and 2400 ng*h/mL (46% CV), respectively.

Absorption

e The absolute oral bioavailability of imlunestrant was 10.5%.
e Maximal plasma concentrations of imlunestrant were reached approximately 4 hours
postdose with an elimination half-life of approximately 30 hours.

Distribution

e The mean apparent oral volume of distribution for imlunestrant was 9 T in patients
with locally advanced or MBC.
e The percent of imlunestrant bound in human plasma was greater than 99.9%.

Metabolism

e The primary clearance pathways involved in imlunestrant metabolism are sulfation,
glucuronidation, and CYP3A4-mediated oxidation.
