NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s efficacy results for the primary endpoint of confirmed ORR by
BICR at both data cutoffs. Following review of the subgroup analyses of ORR at the updated
data cutoff, FDA determined that given the limited data, there was insufficient evidence of
efficacy in patients with non-TKD mutations or in patients with squamous histology, with
extremely limited numbers of patients in both groups. Four patients with squamous histology
were included in Group D (with one responding patient); no patients with squamous histology
were included in Group E. Two of eight patients with non-TKD mutations in Group D
demonstrated an objective response, and one of three patients with non-TKD mutations in Group
E demonstrated an objective response. Therefore, the primary efficacy population was limited to
include only patients with both TKD mutations and non-squamous histology. The updated results
for this revised population are presented in Table 41 below. Among 70 patients in Group D, the
confirmed ORR by BICR was 71% (95% CI: 59, 82). Among 52 patients in Group E, the
confirmed ORR by BICR was 38% (95% CTI: 25, 53).

ORR results in Group D were consistent across prespecified subgroups. ORR results in Group E
were generally consistent across subgroups, however, limited number of patients and responses
in each subgroup resulted in wide confidence intervals. These subgroup results are considered
exploratory and should be interpreted with caution, particularly those with fewer patients or
responses.
