Toxicokinetics

The increases in Cmax and AUCo-72 values were generally dose proportional
except Cmax on Premating Day 15, where values were greater than dose
proportional from the LD to MD and less than dose proportional from MD to
the HD. Slight accumulation of garadacimab was observed after multiple
doses in rabbits on premating Days 15 and 27.

ADA

Animals administered 0, 10, 30 or 100 mg/kg/dose had ADA incidences, at
least one time after dosing, of approximately 28, 32, 16, or 72%,
respectively, tested positive for ADA. The Applicant considered the ADA
incidence of 28% from the control group to be false positives for unknown
reasons. There was no evidence that control animals had received
garadacimab (all concentration values of garadacimab in the vehicle-control
group were below the lower limit of quantitation, <500 ng/mL). The positive
ADA results from the treated animals were therefore hard to interpret.
