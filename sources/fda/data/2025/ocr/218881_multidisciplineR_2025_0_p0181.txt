discontinuation. Of note, a sixth death occurred in a patient who received imlunestrant within 30

1. Patient oer

Tumor mutation status: ESR/m-detected

This 39-year-old Asian female in India with baseline anemia and thrombocytopenia, and
metastases in the liver and bones, died on day 107 from right ventricular failure, two weeks after
the last dose of imlunestrant. The patient’s baseline anemia and thrombocytopenia worsened
while on treatment with imlunestrant and on day 29 imlunestrant was held for 5 days. The patient
was also treated with romiplostim. Imlunestrant was re-started at a lower dose on day 35.
However, on day 92 the patient again experienced worsened anemia and thrombocytopenia and
imlunestrant was held and not re-started. Five days later the patient went to the hospital with
fatigue, breathlessness, cough, and symptoms of ongoing cardiac failure. She was admitted for
severe thrombocytopenia and received a platelet transfusion. The severe thrombocytopenia was
not considered related to imlunestrant by the investigator. An echocardiogram suggested
moderate pulmonary hypertension considered to be secondary to right cardiac failure. FDA
requested results of the echocardiogram and cardiac evaluation; however, the Applicant stated
that echocardiogram results were not provided and a cardiology evaluation was not done. A bone
marrow biopsy was done and confirmed progression of disease in the bone marrow. The patient
was discharged 9 days after this admission, and on that same day she experienced breathlessness
and fatigue. She ultimately died the following day. The cytopenias noted were likely secondary
to bone marrow involvement of the tumor and this may have contributed to the ultimate cardiac
failure. Chronic anemia may have led to the development of pulmonary HTN. The right
ventricular failure was assessed as related to disease progression and there is insufficient
evidence to suggest a causal association between the patient’s right ventricular failure and
imlunestrant. FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment.
