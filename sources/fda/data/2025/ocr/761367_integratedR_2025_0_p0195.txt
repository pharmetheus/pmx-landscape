The minimum required dilution (MRD) of the assay is 1:100. Standard curves used a five-parameter curve fit without weighting.
Precision and accuracy were demonstrated by interpolating the concentration of five controls and examining the relative error (RE)
and coefficient of variation (CV). Based on the concentrations of the controls (ie., 1.25, 5, 40, 80, and 100 ng/mL), the range of the
assay was shown to be 1.25 to 100 ng/mL.

The mean relative error of -23.84% and %CV of 22.8% were both assessed for a theoretical concentration of 1.01 ng/mL, which is
below the LLOQ of 1.25 ng/mL. The mean % relative error and %CV for theoretical values in between the assay limits ranges

from -9.2 to 10.4% and 2.8 to 8.5%, respectively

Repeatability refers to three sets of controls assayed on one plate; interassay precision refers to three sets of controls each assayed
on independent assay runs on the automated platform; interoperative precision is included in error and refers to evaluation of
overlapping process iterations meaning three sets of controls assayed on overlapping process iterations on the automated platform.
