In the current section, the presented exploratory analyses were not planned to formally test the
specified hypotheses given their exploratory aims.

16.1. Subgroup Analyses for the Primary
Endpoint

The same analysis of covariance model and imputation methods as described in Section 6.2.2.3
were used, but it was fit separately for each subgroup.

Results from subgroup analyses for the primary endpoint are shown in Figur for Study 104
and Figure 52 for Study 105. In both studies, due to low numbers of subjects in the group of >65
years of age, the Applicant performed ad-hoc subgroup analyses with age groups categorized by
median age instead of the originally planned age groups (=18 to <65 years of age or >65 years of
age). Additionally, in Study 105, the Applicant did not include results from subgroup analysis by
sex in the original submission.
