8.2.4. Safety Results
Deaths

Data:

In Study MEK-NF-201, there were no deaths in the pediatric cohort and there was 1 death on
study in the adult cohort due to COVID-19 which was considered by the investigator to be
unrelated to study treatment. No additional deaths were reported in Pool 1.

In Pool 2, with the exception of 1 death in the <2 mg BID dose group, the majority (11/12) of
deaths were observed in the higher dose groups and none were observed at the 4 mg BID dose
group. The TEAEs resulting in deaths included death (7%), hypoxia (1%), respiratory arrest (1%),
respiratory failure (1%), cardiac arrest (1%), myocardial infarction (1%), colon cancer metastatic
(1%), metastases to the CNS (1%), and cerebrovascular accident (1%). No deaths were
attributed to mirdametinib by the investigator.

The Applicant's Position:
Mirdametinib was not considered a cause of death for any reported event.

The increase in number of deaths in Pool 2 compared to Pool 1 was driven by the older
population of participants in these early studies who had advanced cancers, including
metastatic malignant melanoma and non-small cell lung cancer.

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s analysis of 1 death in Pool 1 (adult cohort) due to COVID 19
and not related to study treatment. This patient was a 68-year-old male with NF1 PN who
