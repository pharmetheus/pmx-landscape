Applicant agreed to describe the imbalances and state that

The review team initially recommended expanding the common
adverse events table to include those occurring in =1% (from
22%) of JOURNAVX treated patients to provide a more robust
representation of the safety profile of this first-in-class drug.
However, the table was ultimately revised to reflect adverse
reactions in > 1% of JOURNAVX-treated patients.

The laboratory abnormalities regarding elevations in creatine
phosphokinase and a decrease in estimated glomerular
filtration rate were added to Section 6 and not to Section 5 as
described above. No clinically significant adverse events were
associated with these laboratory abnormalities.

The review team also revised the description of Trial 3 (Study
107) from © to “open-
label study” considering the limitations for evaluation of safety
or efficacy in a single-arm trial. The Applicant also proposed
including a description of the rescue medication regimen in
Trial 3, which was acceptable.
