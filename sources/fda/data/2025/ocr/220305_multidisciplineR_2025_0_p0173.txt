Other Baseline Characteristics (e.g., disease characteristics, important concomitant drugs)

Data:

A summary of baseline disease characteristics in the Phase 2 portion of KO MEN 001 is
provided in Applicant Table 7. The baseline disease characteristics in the Phase 2 population of
patients in Study KO MEN 001 are reflective of the population of patients with R/R NPM1-m
AML seen in clinical practice and are representative of the proposed indication population. The
majority of patients in the Phase 2 population at baseline had de novo AML (83.7%), and 16.3%
of patients presented with secondary AML, of which 13.0% had antecedent hematological
disease-AML. In this population of patients with R/R NPM1-m AML, 64.1% of patients had
NPMI subtype A mutation. At baseline, the most common AML co-mutations included FLT3-
ITD mutation (30.4%), DNMT3A mutation (23.9%), and IDH2 mutation (12.0%). Similar
