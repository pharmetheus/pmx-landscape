Study Design
This was a two-part, single-center, multiple-dose, dose-escalation study in healthy male subjects.
Part A

Each subject was randomized to receive either LY686017 (n=6) or matching placebo (n=6) for
28 consecutive days.

Part B

Four groups of two subjects were enrolled sequentially. All subjects received LY686017, there
was no placebo control. In addition, each subject was scheduled to undergo three PET scans for
receptor occupancy assessment.
