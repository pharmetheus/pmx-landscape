The FDA categorized these requests into several categories of concerns: agree, agree with DS as
fatal AR, agree with cause but consider the event a fatal AR, disagree with cause and consider
DS as a possible fatal AR, disagree with cause and consider it a fatal AR, and unclear, please
provide additional details. Given the context of the study, which enrolled patients with R/R AML
without other available therapy, infectious events were ultimately attributable to persistent
disease unless the patient had evidence of disease control (defined as < 5% bone marrow blasts).
Based on this approach and the information, the Applicant was able to provide to the original
narratives, we adjudicated these 35 events to 23 events of infection in the setting of persistent
disease, 3 events of persistent disease, 1 event of stroke/persistent disease, 6 events of AR-DS, 1
event of AR-cardiac arrest, and 1 event of AR-infection (FDA Table 43). In the key safety
population (n=112, NPM1-m AML treated at 600 mg QD), 4 fatal AEs occurred: 2 fatal ARs of
DS, 1 fatal AR of infection, and 1 fatal AR of cardiac arrest. These were included in the USPI.
