Major Findings

Mortality

There were 23 unscheduled deaths during the study (16 males
and 7 females). The Applicant's report stated that four of the
deaths (i.e., 100 mg/kg IV male, 60 mg/kg SC male, and two

60 mg/kg SC females) were due to the blood sampling procedure
and not test article related.

Five males in the 3 mg/kg/day IV group and one female in the

30 mg/kg/day IV group were found dead during or immediately
following the dose administration. An additional four animals were
found dead on dosing days (3 mg/kg IV female, three 10 mg/kg IV
females). A further nine animals were prematurely euthanized for
welfare reasons, because of clinical signs observed (i.e., eight

3 mg/kg IV males and one 10 mg/kg IV male). Clinical signs prior
to termination consisted of mainly swollen limbs and muzzle
and/or reddening of the limbs or muzzle sometimes associated
with signs of decreased activity, shallow or slow breathing and
flattened posture. These deaths were most likely due to
immunogenicity based on adverse clinical signs that occurred
shortly after dosing. The remaining 14 low dose males and 29 low
dose females were terminated on Day 18 following the sixth dose
and Days 12-13 after the fourth dose, respectively. Terminal
procedures were conducted on low dose males and females after
sacrifice.
