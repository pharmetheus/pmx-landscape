8.1.3. Integrated Review of Effectiveness

The FDA’s Assessment:

The ReNeu study is a single arm, multi-center study which served as the primary basis for
establishing the efficacy of mirdametinib in the treatment of patients with NF1 PN. Study NF-
106, an open-label, single arm study that included 2 pediatric and 17 adult patients, provided
supportive evidence of mirdametinib’s efficacy.

All 114 patients enrolled in the ReNeu study had measurable disease at baseline as assessed by
BICR. The analysis of tumor response included 56 patients in the pediatric cohort and 58
patients in the adult cohort. In the pediatric cohort, the confirmed ORR was 52% (95% Cl: 38,
65) and in the adult cohort, confirmed ORR was 41% (95% Cl: 29, 55). All the responses
observed in the study were partial responses. The ORR subgroups analysis based on

demographic and clinical characteristics were mostly consistent across subgroups in both
cohorts.

As of data cutoff date of June 12, 2024 (90-day safety update) the median DOR was not reached
in either cohort. The percentage of patients with response who have observed DOR 2 12
months and 2 24 months were 90% and 48% in the pediatric cohort and 88% and 50% in the
adult cohort, respectively.

The median time to onset of response was 7.8 months (range: 4.0 months to 19.0 months) for
the adult cohort and 7.9 months (range: 4.1 months to 18.8 months) for the pediatric cohort.
