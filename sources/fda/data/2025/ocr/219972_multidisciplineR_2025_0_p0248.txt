The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA performed an independent analysis of GI toxicity, skin toxicity, ILD/pneumonitis, ocular
toxicity, and cardiac toxicity as specific safety issues but also identified hepatotoxicity and
elevated pancreatic enzymes as detailed respectively in the following sections . FDA’s analysis
used grouped terms as described in Table 74.

Hepatotoxicity

In the pooled safety population, there were three cases of Grade 3 hepatic function abnormal and
one case of Grade 3 cholecystitis. A total of 1.5 and 1.1% of patients in the combined Groups D
+E and pooled 20 mg population had an event of Grade 3 hepatic function abnormal. One case
(0.4%) of Grade 3 cholecystitis occurred in the pooled 20 mg population. As described under
Laboratory Findings, FDA reviewed the narratives and agrees there were no Hy’s law cases.
The median time to onset for the GT hepatotoxicity (comprised of hepatic function abnormal,
ALT, and AST increased) was 42 days for the combined Group D + E while increased bilirubin
occurred at 70.5 days. For the pooled 20 mg population the median onset of hepatic function
abnormal was 35.5 days, ALT increased was 42 days, AST increased was 22 days, and total
bilirubin 64 days. Narratives for severe events in the hepatobiliary system were reviewed, with
select narratives briefly discussed below.

e Patient ID ©)- This is a case of Grade 3 hepatic function abnormal with elevated
AST and ALT aminotransferases; both the Investigator and Sponsor attributed this to
study drug resulting in dose reduction and interruption. This was treated with
ursodeoxycholic acid, cysteine hydrochloride, glycine, and glycyrrhizic acid and no other
clear etiology could be determined from the narrative. The last reported assessment noted
this as resolving which is corroborated by the pattern of provided liver function tests
(LFTs). Approximately 3 weeks after this hepatic event, the patient experienced a Grade
