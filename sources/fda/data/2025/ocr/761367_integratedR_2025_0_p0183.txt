Activated Partial Thromboplastin Time

Per the Applicant, the normal range for aPTT values is between 24.3 and 43.4 seconds. For
subjects in the garadacimab arm, there was little change in mean aPTT values throughout the
Treatment Period. Mean (SD) aPTT at baseline, Day 91, and Day 182 were 29.26 (9.07) seconds,
28.84 (5.59) seconds, and 33.36 (19.8) seconds, respectively (data derived from Table 14.3.4.4,
CSR for trial 3001). There were three subjects noted to have aPTT prolongation following

administration of garadacimab:
e One adult subject with aPTT values of 59.4 and 95.5 seconds on Days 91 and 182,
respectively.

¢ One adolescent subject (aged 17 years) with an aPTT of 67 seconds on Day 182, and
e¢ One adult subject with an aPTT of 126 seconds on Day 182.

The Cyough values from these three subjects at the four time points listed above were not outliers
or consistently at the higher end of the distribution of population Crougn values observed from
trial 3001 (see Section 7.7.1).

Immunogenicity Results

Two subjects in the garadacimab arm tested positive for ADAs, including one subject at baseline
(prior to receiving garadacimab) and one subject on Day 182, both with a titer value of 10. The
subject who tested positive at baseline did not have detectable antibodies at subsequent visits.
