Type B Interaction, Clinical
24 Oct 2023

The Agency concluded that in general the proposed plan for key
elements of the marketing application for taletrectinib for
traditional approval is acceptable.

The Agency stated the Sponsor’s plan to submit an NDA to seek
traditional approval for taletrectinib for the treatment ROS1+
NSCLC based on pooled efficacy data from Studies G208 and
C203 appeared reasonable.

The Agency recommended that the Sponsor include a dose-
randomized cohort in Study G208 (randomized 1:1) with
20 participants per dose at 2 dose levels (Cohort 5).

The Agency agreed that the overall clinical pharmacology plan
presented is acceptable and recommended that the Sponsor
submit the hepatic impairment study plan/protocol to EDA for
review prior to the planned NDA submission.

The Agency agreed that the proposed ISE plan is acceptable.
