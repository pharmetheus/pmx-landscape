Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

Treatments at 25 mg/kg QD and 50 mg/kg QD significantly reduced tumor growth, but to a
lesser extent than in the EGFR mutant tumors models tested. The weak to moderate antitumor
activity is reflected by the final T/Cvovweight of 0.46/0.49 (25 mg/kg QD) and 0.36/0.32 (50 mg/kg
QD), respectively. sevabertinib treatment at 10 mg/kg QD, the dose that has been determined as
fully efficacious in the HER2- and EGFR mutant tumor models, shows no significant antitumor
activity in the EGFRwt model, A431, with final T/Cvovweight values of 0.77/0.75. All treatments
were well tolerated (Figure 3) (Module 4.2.1.1, Report PH-42102).
