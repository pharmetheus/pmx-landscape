SC doses were administered using a 170 mg/mL solution in 1.2 mL PFS-NSD devices. SC
injections were administered as 1<1.2 mL (200 mg) or 3=1.2 mL (600 mg), with preferred site of
injection in the abdomen. IV doses were administered using a 100 mg/mL formulation in a vial
presentation. IV doses were given as an infusion at a volume of 60 mL over 60 min.

The study enrolled healthy male or female subjects aged 18 to 55 years, with body weight
between 50 and 100 kg, and body mass index between 18 and <30 kg/m”, all inclusive.
Caucasian subjects were weight-matched +15% 1:1 with Japanese subjects. Use of prescription
or over-the-counter medications, including herbal products and dietary supplements,
anticoagulant therapy or drugs with antiplatelet action were prohibited within 21 days before
administration of the investigational product. All medications were prohibited during the study
except for acetaminophen, and oral or intranasal medications to treat seasonal allergies.
Hormonal contraceptives were also permitted.
