Treatment Compliance, Concomitant Medications, and Rescue Medication Use — as of
14 OCT 2024

Data and the Applicant’s Position:

Treatment compliance

Treatment compliance was high. The median percentage of the actual administered dose of
sevabertinib was 98.51% of the planned dose in the primary efficacy population (Group D) and
95.22% in Group E (Module 5.3.5.2, B003047, Table 8.1.8/4 [Datasets ADSL and ADEXSUM]).

Concomitant medications

In Group D, 98.8% of participants received at least 1 concomitant medication. In Group E, all
participants received at least 1 concomitant medication. The most commonly used concomitant
medications, by ATC subclass, taken by >50% of participants in either Group D or Group E,
included: (Module 5.3.5.2, B003047, Table 8.1.7/8 [Datasets ADSL and ADCM)]).
