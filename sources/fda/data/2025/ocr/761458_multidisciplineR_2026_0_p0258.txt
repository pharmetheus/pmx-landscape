Methods

The drug product for all 3 batches (GD9B, GD8W, GD8U) was manufactured 1 year
prior to TO (September 22, 2023). They were stored inverted at 5°C, 25°C, and 40°C.
Samples were taken at 3, 6, 9, and 12 months with 24 and 36 time points being planned
for September 2025 and 2026. The samples stored at 40°C (accelerated degradation)
were only stored for up to 6 months. Volatiles analysis was conducted using Headspace
GC-MS while semi-volatiles analysis was conducted using direct injection GC-MS.

An elemental impurities analysis was conducted using samples from depemokimab
batches that were inverted for 6 months under accelerated degradation (40°C/75% RH).
