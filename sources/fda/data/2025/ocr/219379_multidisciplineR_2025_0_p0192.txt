The Applicant’s Description:

There were 2 versions of the SAP that were submitted to the FDA. The initial SAP (v1.0) was

The results of this study are presented overall and by pediatric and adult cohorts.

Primary Efficacy Endpoint

The primary efficacy endpoint was confirmed ORR defined as the proportion of participants
who had a BOR of CR or PR by the end of Cycle 24 with 24 weeks duration.

The target PNs were centrally evaluated by MRI by a single reader at the |

Level of target PN response was defined as follows:

e CR:Acomplete resolution of the target PN for 24 weeks

e PR: A 220% reduction in the volume of the target PN lesion compared to the
pretreatment volume for 24 weeks

e Stable Disease (SD): Between a <20% increase, and <20% decrease in the volume of the
target PN lesion compared to the pretreatment volume for 24 weeks

e PD:A 220% increase in the volume of the target PN compared to the pretreatment
volume

To assess the primary efficacy endpoint, the confirmed ORR was presented alongside the
corresponding 2-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson Cls and the p-values from a one-sample two-sided
binomial test evaluating the null hypothesis that the ORR = 5%. Best overall confirmed response
was summarized according to the categories presented above. Tumor volume (mL), change
from baseline in tumor volume, and percentage change from baseline in tumor volume were
summarized. Additionally, the best observed percentage volumetric change from baseline was
also summarized.

For calculation of the ORR, all participants with missing data who were not previously
presented as confirmed PR or CR were imputed as non-responders for the primary endpoint
