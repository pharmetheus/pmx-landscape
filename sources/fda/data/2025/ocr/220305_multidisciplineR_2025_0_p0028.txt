The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees that at the time of the submission of the marketing application, there was no

therapy approved for patients with R/R NPM1-m AML that specifically targeted this mutational

pathway. For patients unable to tolerate intensive reinduction chemotherapy, options include
lower intensity chemotherapy, including a hypomethylating agent with or without venetoclax. In
addition, other targeted agents are approved for patients with coexisting mutations, such as

FLT3-m (gilteritinib), JDHJ-m (ivosidenib and olutasidenib), and JDH2-m (enasidenib) (FDA

Table 1). Of note, only gilteritinib has shown a long-term survival benefit for patients with
R/R AML in a randomized study.*

Prior to the PDUFA date for this application, the FDA approved revumenib, a menin inhibitor,

for adult and pediatric patients with relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia with a
susceptible NPM/ mutation who have no satisfactory alternative treatment options. However, the
review team concurs that there remains a high unmet medical need for patients with R/R NPM1-

m AML.
