10 Pediatrics

The Applicant's Position:

Pediatric studies for linvoseltamab were not performed, given that the molecular target
has been identified to be non-relevant to the growth or progression of pediatric cancers
and that linvoseltamab has been granted orphan drug designation for the treatment of

MM.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s position.
