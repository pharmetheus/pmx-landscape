NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

Observations made during the FDA’s assessment of the 13-week toxicology study in dogs, some
of which were not captured by the Applicant, are included in the tables below. All animals
survived to the scheduled euthanization dates. Clinical observations included abnormal feces,
reduced activity, hunched posture, wet fur, salivation, and thin appearance. Electrocardiogram
readings identified a decrease in heart rate that correlated with increased RR intervals. Notable
hematology findings included dose-dependent reversible elevations in basophil, large unstained
cell, leukocyte, monocyte, and neutrophil counts. Erythrocyte, hematocrit, and hemoglobin levels
were reduced dose-dependently. Alanine aminotransferase, alkaline phosphatase, and aspartate
aminotransferase, bilirubin, and creatine kinase levels were reversibly elevated. Reversible organ
weight increases were observed in the adrenal gland, pituitary gland, thyroid gland, kidney, and
liver, while decreases were noted in the prostate, testes, ovaries, uterus, and thymus.
Macroscopic findings in the liver, gallbladder, lymph nodes, thymus, and thyroid included
reversible discoloring. Microscopic findings were principally noted in the liver and included
cholestasis and biliary hyperplasia. Other notable tissues of toxicity included the lymph node,
spleen, gall bladder, thymus, ovaries, bone marrow, adrenal glands, parathyroid gland, pituitary
gland, and epididymis. A high-dose group female (number 4506) had findings of hyperplasia in
several tissues, including the liver, pancreas, thymus, heart, aorta, adrenal glands, and mammary
glands.
