PK, PD, and Immunogenicity Samplin:

Blood PK samples were collected at predose on Day 1, and postdose at Hours 0.5 (IV only), 1, 6,
12, 24, 32 (SC only), and 48 and Days 5, 8, 15, 22, 29, 36, 57, and 85. Samples to assess FXIIa-
mediated kallikrein activity were collected at the same time points as PK samples.
Immunogenicity samples were collected at predose on Day 1, and on Days 29 and 85.

Subject Disposition and Demographics

A total of 48 subjects were randomized, including 32 subjects who received garadacimab and 16
subjects who received placebo. Among subjects receiving garadacimab, 20 received an IV
infusion (n=4 per IV cohort) and 12 received a SC injection (n=4 per SC cohort). All the subjects
completed the study.
