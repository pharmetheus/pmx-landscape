7.8. Abuse Potential

The Controlled Substance Staff (CSS) conducted a review of the abuse potential of SUZ. The
abuse- and dependence-related TEAE data provided by the Applicant suggest that SUZ did not
produce abuse- or dependence-related effects under the tested conditions. CSS does recommend
that TEAEs be monitored for dependence if SUZ is investigated for chronic use. In summary,
SUZ does not have a potential for abuse and should not be controlled under the Controlled
Substances Act. CSS’s review dated December 6, 2024, is excerpted below:

Conclusions

CSS has reviewed the nonclinical and clinical abuse-related data submitted in NDA
219209 for JOURNAVX (suzetrigine) and concludes that the drug does not have abuse
potential and should not be controlled under the CSA [Controlled Substances Act]. This
conclusion is based on the following:

e Suzetrigine is a new molecular entity with a novel mechanism of action proposed
for the indication of moderate to severe acute pain.

e Invitro binding and activity studies indicate suzetrigine and its active metabolite,
M6-SUZ, function as highly selective Na,1.8 channel blockers. Suzetrigine is
also an antagonist at the gamma-aminobutyric acid A (GABAa) receptor,
dopamine transporter (DAT), and norepinephrine transporter (NET), however,
not at clinically relevant doses. Similarly, M6-SUZ is an antagonist at the
GABAg and Orexin 1 (OX1) receptors, however, not at clinically relevant doses.
As a result, suzetrigine does not appear to bind or have activity at neuronal
targets associated with having a potential for abuse.

e Behavioral studies using suzetrigine in rats and nonhuman primates produced no
CNS mediated behaviors, and no signs associated with abuse potential.

e An analysis of CNS-mediated AEs that can be indicative of abuse liability or
physical dependence was conducted on the clinical studies provided by the
Applicant. This analysis indicated that the most prevalent AEs were dizziness
and somnolence. There were no concerning reports of AEs that suggest that
suzetrigine has a potential for abuse or physical dependence.

e Based on the data provided above, there is no indication that suzetrigine has a
potential for abuse. As a result, the Applicant was not asked to conduct animal or
clinical studies to directly assess the abuse potential of suzetrigine.
