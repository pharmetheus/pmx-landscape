Durability of Response
Data:
DOR is presented as a secondary endpoint in Section 8.1.2.

The Applicant's Position:

Responses were durable; among All 200 mg Patients the KM estimated median DOR
by IRC (per IMWG criteria) as of the data cutoff date was NR (95% Cl: 14.2, NE). In
Phase 2 50 mg Patients, the KM estimated median DOR by IRC (per IMWG criteria)
was 31.2 months (95% Cl: 18.6, NE).

A comparison with the Phase 2 50 mg Patient data suggests that the DOR in patients
treated with 200 mg will be similar to, or longer than, patients treated with 50 mg.
