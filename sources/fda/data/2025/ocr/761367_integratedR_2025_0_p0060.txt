6.2.4.6. Subgroup Analyses, Trial 3001

Garadacimab met its prespecified primary efficacy endpoint of time-normalized number of HAE
attacks per month (see Section 6.2.4.3) in adults and adolescents with C1-INH (Type I or

Type II) HAE in trial 3001. However, when evaluating the efficacy of garadacimab, we also
considered whether the primary endpoint was consistent across clinically relevant subgroups that
were not considered by the Applicant, including by sex, age, race, ethnicity, geographic location,
HAE subtype, and HAE attack location and severity.

Efficacy by Sex

For both males and females, there was a clinically meaningful and statistically persuasive
reduction in the mean time-normalized number of HAE attacks per month relative to placebo
(Figure 4). These results are similar to the overall primary analysis results (Table 10).
