NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 218881

Inluriyo (imlunestrant)

The FDA agrees with the Applicant’s description of injection site reaction in patients who
received fulvestrant and also notes that no patients who received imlunestrant experienced
injection site reaction.

The FDA agrees with the Applicant that the incidence of any grade TEAEs and Grade >3 TEAEs
was similar across treatment arms. The majority of TEAEs were Grade 1 or 2. FDA disagrees
with the Applicant that the safety profile of imlunestrant monotherapy was consistent with the
mechanism of action of SERDs and events commonly experienced by patients with HR+/HER2-
advanced breast cancer. Since imlunestrant is a new molecular entity (NME) this is the first
assessment of this drug’s safety database and all safety findings are new. The FDA agree with
the Applicant that the safety profile of imlunestrant was manageable through routine monitoring.
