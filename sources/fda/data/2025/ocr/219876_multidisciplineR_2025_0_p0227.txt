Dordaviprone

Results
Can the PBPK model adequately describe the PK profiles of dordaviprone?

Yes. The model was developed and optimized using the data from the itraconazole DDI study
(Figure 19.9 and Table 19.9). Clinical PK data that had not been used in model development
was used to verify the ability of the dordaviprone PBPK model to describe the PK profiles of
dordaviprone. The model could reasonably describe the plasma concentration-time profiles of
dordaviprone following single and multiple oral doses of dordaviprone in adult patients with
cancer. Majority of the model-estimated AUC and Cmax were within 2-fold of the observed
values. This predictive performance was considered reasonable considering the high variability
in dordaviprone PK (CV% >100%) reported in patients. Simulated and observed dordaviprone
PK profiles and parameters are summarized in Figure 19.10 and Table 19.10.
