1.1 Introduction

The Sponsor is developing CSL312 (garadacimab), an activated Factor XII (FXlla)
inhibitor monoclonal antibody for prophylaxis to prevent attacks of hereditary
angioedema (HAE). CSL312 has been developed as a combination product
incorporating a prefilled syringe which contains 200 mg of the formulated CSL312 active
substance, defined as the CSL312 semi-finished product (SFP) 200 mg. Extractables
and leachables studies were conducted with the container closure system that were
evaluated in the present review.
