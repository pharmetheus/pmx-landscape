Study Endpoints

The Applicant’s Description:

Given the single-arm nature of Study 21607, the evaluation of antitumor activity was performed
using the primary endpoint of confirmed ORR, as assessed by BICR, according to RECIST
v1.1. ORR is defined as the proportion of participants who achieved a BOR of CR or PR, with
the requirement that CR and PR must be confirmed at least 4 weeks apart.

There was an agreement with the FDA that the primary endpoint of ORR along with the
secondary endpoint of DOR as assessed by BICR could be used for accelerated approval
provided the results are provided in the context of a positive benefit-risk profile (FDA Type B
End-of-Phase 1 Meeting [30 JAN 2024]).

The study used established RECIST v1.1 criteria (Eisenhauer et al. 2009) for response and
progression assessments, based on standard imaging (CT or MRI scans), assessed by both the
BICR and the investigator. Radiological tumor assessments were performed every 6 weeks (+7
days) for the first 36 weeks and every 9 weeks (+7 days) thereafter.

The primary endpoint of confirmed ORR was supported by the following secondary endpoints:
e ORR by investigator assessment

e DCR by BICR and investigator assessments, defined as the proportion of participants
whose BOR was either CR, PR or SD of at least 12 weeks following the first
administration of study treatment, taking into account the requirement for confirmation of

CR and PR at least 4 weeks apart.

e DOR by BICR and investigator assessments, defined as the time from the first
observed PR or CR to the date of disease progression assessed by the investigator or
BICR per RECIST v1.1 or death due to any cause (if death occurs before progression is
documented).

e PFS by BICR and investigator assessment, defined as the time from the start date of
study treatment to the date of first documented disease progression (radiological tumor
assessment) or death due to any cause (if death occurs before progression is documented).

e OS, defined as the time from the start of study treatment to death, due to any cause.
