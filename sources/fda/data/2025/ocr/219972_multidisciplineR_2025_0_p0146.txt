The Applicant’s Position:

Updated data as of 28 MAR 2025: 37.0% of the participants in Group D were on sevabertinib
treatment. Progressive disease (assessed radiologically) was the primary reason (50.6%) for
discontinuing study treatment. In the supportive efficacy population Group E, 27.3% of the
participants were ongoing with treatment, with progressive disease (assessed radiologically) as
the primary reason (49.1%) for treatment discontinuation. AE was the primary reason for
discontinuation of 4 participants (4.9%) in Group D and in 2 participants (3.6%) in Group E.

As of 14 OCT 2024: Approximately half of the participants in Group D (50.6%) were on
sevabertinib treatment at the time of the database cut-off date. Progressive disease (assessed
radiologically) was the primary reason (37.0%) for discontinuing study treatment. In the
supportive efficacy population, Group E, 45.5% of the participants were ongoing with treatment
at the time of the cut-off with progressive disease (assessed radiologically) as the primary reason
(32.7%) for treatment discontinuation. AE was the primary reason of discontinuation for 4
participants (4.9%) in Group D and in 1 participant (1.8%) in Group E.
