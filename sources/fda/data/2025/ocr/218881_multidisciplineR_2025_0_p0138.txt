While the trial excluded patients that were HER2-positive by ASCO/CAP Guidelines, the data to
perform an analysis of patients that may have had HER2-low or HER2-ultralow breast cancer

Treatment Compliance, Concomitant Medications, and Rescue Medication Use
Data:

Treatment compliance

Treatment compliance for imlunestrant and exemestane was assessed through pill counts at each
cycle.

Compliance for fulvestrant was assured as the drug was administered at the investigator site.
Concomitant medication

Any medication or vaccine (including over-the-counter or prescription medicines, vitamins,
and/or herbal supplements) received while on study and during the short-term follow-up was
recorded. Anticancer therapies (including, chemotherapy, immunotherapy) were not permitted
while on study treatment.

The frequency of patients who received GnRH and bone-modifying agents while on treatment
was similar across treatment arms within both the overall safety population and the ESR1m-
detected safety population.
