Study title / number: A Dose Range-Finding Embryo-Fetal Development Study of ONC201
by (Oral) Gavage in Rabbits / 20218012

Key Study Findings

e Dordaviprone administered at > 62.5 mg/kg resulted in early termination of all the
animals due to dordaviprone-related maternal toxicities including adverse clinical signs
and maternal body weight loss.

e Incidences of fetal external and visceral abnormalities including short snout, absent teeth
and misshapen brain were noted at 10 mg/kg and 25 mg/kg.

GLP compliance: No
