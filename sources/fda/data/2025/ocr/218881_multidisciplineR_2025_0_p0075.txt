Fetal weights

e A decrease in fetal weight >10% was observed in both male and female fetuses when
pregnant females were treated at > 3 mg/kg; however, these changes were within the
historical control range. The observed changes were similar across both male and female;
thus, the data were presented as a combined analysis of both sexes.

Fetal malformations

Statistically significant increase in fetuses with malformations including small jaw (mandible,

when compared to litters in the control group.
