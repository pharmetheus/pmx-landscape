NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 220305
Komzifti (ziftomenib)

SUMMARY AND CONCLUSIONS

8.4. Statistical Issues
The FDA’s Assessment:

Study KO-MEN-001 demonstrated a numerically higher than historically reported response rate
in adult participants with R/R AML with an NPM/ mutation. The CR + CRh rate was 21.4%
(95% CI: 14.2%, 30.2%) in the Pooled Efficacy Cohort and 18.5% (95% CI: 11.1%, 27.9%) in
its Phase 2 subset (pivotal cohort). The lower bound of 95% CI of the Phase 2 cohort did not
exclude the prespecified benchmark of 12% based on the FDA’s adjudication; however, the
response of Phase 2 patients based on Applicant’s assessments as well as the Pooled Efficacy
Cohort did exclude this benchmark. The median duration of response was 5.0 (95% CI: 1.9, 8.1)
months, and the rate of conversion to transfusion independence was 21.2% (95% CI: 12.1%,
33.0%) in Pooled Efficacy Cohort.

One issue identified during the FDA’s review of response data was that there was no pre-
specification of a time window between the marrow sampling and the CBC for remission
assessment. As a result, some patients did not have marrow sampling in adjunction with CBC
assessment to support the adjudication of remission after clearance of bone marrow blasts, at
which point participants stopped monthly marrow assessments. For the adjudication process for
this application, we used a window of -7 to +14 days along, in addition to clinical judgement.
While the initial results excluded the benchmark rate of 12%, the adjudicated results did not.
This raises questions about the robustness of the results.

Another change in the FDA’s efficacy analysis from what was specified in the SAP was to
establish the effectiveness on the pooled population of Phase 1 and 2. This pooling was
supported by the same eligibility criteria used in two phases and the similarity of two study
populations in terms of baseline characteristics. The pooled results appeared to confirm the initial
inference based on the Applicant’s assessments for Phase 2 patients only. The FDA evaluated the
impact of borrowing from Phase 1 data by Bayesian analysis under various effective sample size
assumptions. These results suggested that the probability to exceed the benchmark rate was over
97.5% as long as the Phase 1 data were considered at least relevant enough to borrow half of the
formation.

8.5. Conclusions and Recommendations
The FDA’s Assessment:

identified by local testing. The median DoR was 5.0 months. However, the rate of conversion
