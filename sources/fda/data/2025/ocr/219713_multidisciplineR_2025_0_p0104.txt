REVIEW ISSUE RECOMMENDATIONS/COMMENTS

The primary evidence of effectiveness comes from two Phase 2
single-arm studies, AB-106-G208 and AB-106-C203, conducted
in patients with locally advanced or metastatic ROS/-
positiveNSCLC. The primary endpoint of overall response rate
(ORR) was 89% (95% CI: 83, 93) in ROS1 TKI naive patients
and 56% (95% CI: 46, 65) in patients with one prior ROS1 TKI.
The intracranial ORR was 77% (95% CI: 50, 93) in ROS1 TKI
naive patients and 66% (95% CI: 47, 81) in patients with one
prior ROS1 TKI. See Section 8.1.2 for details.

Note that a flat E-R relationship for efficacy was observed across
the dose range of 400 mg to 600 mg QD with a small portion of
data from 400 mg dose level. Positive E-R relationships were
identified for various safety endpoints, including Grade>3
TEAEs, AE leading to dose modification, and Grade>3
AST/ALT elevation.

The recommended dosage is taletrectinib 600 mg QD
administered on an empty stomach (i.e., no food intake at least 2
General dosing hours before and 2 hours after taking the drug). Given that the
instructions proposed recommended dosage could be optimized with regard to
food intake, a PMR study is to be issued for further dosage
optimization.
