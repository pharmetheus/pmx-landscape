19.4.3. Population PK Analysis
19.4.3.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The Applicant is seeking approval for sevabertinib, an oral, reversible human epidermal growth
factor receptor 2 (HER2) and epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR) inhibitor, for the treatment
of adult patients with advanced non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumors have HER2
activating mutations, and who have received a prior systemic therapy. The key pharmacometrics
findings are summarized below:

e Sevabertinib PK was adequately characterized using a 2-compartment model with
transit first-order absorption and first-order elimination.

e Administration of liquid service formulation is found to increase absorption rate by
49% and reduces relative bioavailability by 36% compared to tablet administration,
which is clinically significant. Tablet formulation is the to-be-marketed formulation.

e Increasing albumin (as a fraction of total protein) and increasing body weight are
associated with lower relative bioavailability, or equivalently, higher adjusted
apparent clearance and adjusted volume of distribution of the central compartment,
which is not clinically significant.

e No significant impact on sevabertinib exposure was found for mild and moderate
hepatic impairment or mild renal impairment.

e No conclusions on the impact of severe renal impairment or moderate or severe
hepatic impairment could be drawn due to a no patient in these categories
