Note that an assay to measure neutralizing antibodies is still in development. Thus, at the time of

Individual dose-normalized garadacimab concentration-time profiles by ADA status are shown
in Figure 48. Overall, there was no apparent impact of ADAs on the PK of garadacimab.
Individual concentration-time profiles indicate that garadacimab concentrations among subjects
who tested positive for ADAs were within the range of concentrations observed among subjects
who were negative for ADAs.
