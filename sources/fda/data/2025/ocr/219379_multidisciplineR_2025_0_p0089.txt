Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

Additional relevant data is presented below:

In an EFD study, administration of mirdametinib to pregnant rats caused decreases in maternal
body weight gains and food consumption at the dose of 5 mg/kg (approximately 8-times the
human clinical dose of 2 mg/m? twice daily based on BSA). A summary of the Cesarean section
data in rats is shown in Table 20. The increases in percentage mean post-implantation loss and
decrease in mean number of live fetuses and fetal body weights were observed at doses 23
mg/kg (25-times the human clinical dose of 2 mg/m? twice daily based on BSA). One fetus at the
3 mg/kg dose had multiple malformations including micromelia, adactyly, brachydactlyly, and
blunt-tipped tail and another one had a limb hyperflexion variation.
