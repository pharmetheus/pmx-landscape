6.2. Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Data:

Taletrectinib has been investigated in 10 clinical studies and in more than 400 participants to
date. This includes 5 Phase 1 healthy volunteer studies to investigate drug-drug interactions,
mass balance, and food effect; 2 completed Phase 1 studies in locally advanced or metastatic
participants with cancer; and 3 Phase 2 studies in adult participants with ROS1+ NSCLC and in
solid tumors with NTRK fusion genes.

Tn participants with cancer, the geometric mean (CV%) of taletrectinib steady state peak
concentration (Cmax) is 476.3 (36.5%) ng/mL and the area under the time concentration curve
(AUCau) is 9649 (35.8%) ng*h/mL following the recommended 600 mg once daily (QD) dosage.
Taletrectinib Cmax and AUC increase approximately proportional to dose over the range of 50 to
1200 mg QD. Steady state is achieved within 7 days of daily administration of 600 mg.
Accumulation ratios after 600 mg QD dosing were approximately 3.3 to 4.5 based on area under
the concentration-time curve overdosing interval tau (AUC).
