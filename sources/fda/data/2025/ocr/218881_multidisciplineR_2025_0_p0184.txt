2. Patient oT ]

Tumor mutation status: ESR/m-detected

This 78-year-old White female from Greece with metastases to liver and bone and history of
diabetes mellitus, hyperlipidemia, hyperthyroidism, and chronic kidney disease died on day 165
after a 10-day hospitalization due to chronic kidney disease. No other information or details
regarding the fatal event were provided. She received her last dose of fulvestrant on day 113 and
had been noted to have chronic kidney disease after signing consent but prior to receiving the
first dose of study drug. The investigator did not consider the event related to study drug. FDA
agrees with this assessment. Of note, this death occurred after the FDA 30day definition (52 days
after last dose of therapy).

3. Patient oer

Tumor mutation status: ESR/m-not detected

This 59-year-old Asian female from India with metastases to liver and no known hypertension,
diabetes mellitus, or cardiac disorder died on day 15 after collection of blood samples from
cardiac arrest. This was a witnessed cardiac arrest after which cardiopulmonary resuscitation was
started and the patient was admitted to the intensive care unit. Cardiac activity resumed;
however, the patient experienced a second cardiac arrest in the intensive care unit.
Cardiopulmonary resuscitation was started and the patient was placed on a ventilator. She
deteriorated and two days later was comatose. On day 18 the family opted for no further
escalation of treatment and comfort care. On day 26 after one fulvestrant dose the patient had
another cardiac arrest and resuscitation was not successful. The investigator’s opinion is that the
cardiac arrest was not related to the study drug. FDA generally agrees with this assessment.

4. Patient ane

Tumor mutation status: ESR/m-not detected

This 47-year-old American Indian/Alaskan Native female from Mexico with metastases to liver
and bone died on day 103 of unknown cause. Her last dose of fulvestrant was on day 57 and she
was discontinued from the study on day 85 due to progression of disease in her bone metastases.
The patient died at home and it was not possible to acquire more information. In the opinion of
the investigator the death was not related to study drug. FDA agrees with this assessment. Of
note, this death occurred after the FDA 30-day definition (46 days after last dose of therapy).
