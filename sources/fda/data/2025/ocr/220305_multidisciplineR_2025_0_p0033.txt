Komzifti (ziftomenib)

4 Significant Issues from Other Review Disciplines Pertinent to Clinical Conclusions on
Efficacy and Safety

4.1. Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI)

The Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI) conducted inspections for Study KO-MEN-001 at
Clinical Sites 105 and 112. The sites were chosen on the basis of enrollment of large numbers of
participants, a high proportion of participants with response to treatment, and a high number of
protocol deviations per participant. Following inspection, Site 105 was determined to be No
Action Indicated (NAI), and Site 112 was determined to be Voluntary Action Indicated (VAI).
The VAI observations were mainly attributed to reporting and record accuracies and were
considered to have little impact on the evaluation of the primary efficacy results. Site 112
provided an acceptable response and corrective action plan.

The Applicant (Kura) was also audited; the final classification for the Applicant’s inspection was
NAIL. OSI concluded that the study data submitted to the Agency were acceptable for review in
support of the proposed indication.
