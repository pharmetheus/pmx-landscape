The covariates in the model were visualized by varying single covariates individually while keeping all other conditions constant
(ceteris paribus). Results are presented relative to a reference subject, i.e., a non-Japanese, non-Chinese, healthy volunteer with
baseline weight of 70 kg, baseline SCR of 0.75 mg/dL, baseline ALT of 25 U/L and baseline BILI of 8umol/L. The colored diamonds
represent the median and the solid horizontal lines represent the 95% confidence interval. The grey shaded reference range of 80-
125% was analogous to the region of practical equivalence (ROPE). For continuous covariates, the upper and lower values used in
the simulations were determined based on the 10th and 90th percentiles in the observed dataset.

Abbreviations: AUC, area under the concentration-time curve; BLWT, baseline weight (kg), BLCREAT, baseline serum creatinine
(mg/dL), BLBILI, baseline bilirubin (umol/L), BLALT, baseline alanine aminotransferase (U/L), HAE, hereditary angioedema, Cl,
