19.4.4. Exposure-Response Analysis

19.4.4.1. ER (Efficacy) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:
e There are two dosage (10 mg and 20 mg QD) in the D/D1 subset (105 patients) in the
efficacy ER assessment, in which there are limited data for the lower dosage and large
between-cohort variability under the same dosage. No positive exposure-response
relationship was observed for objective response rate (ORR).

e Participants with the HER2 activating mutations were more likely to have an objective
response, which is exposure independent.
