Fertility and Early Embryonic Development

Fertility and early embryonic development studies are not needed to support the use of MK-
375A in the currently proposed indication per ICH S9; however, the Applicant conducted a
fertility and early embryonic development study with MK-5180 in rats. FDA generally agrees
with the Applicant’s conclusions. FDA’s assessment is below.
