Drug-Disease Interactions

Almost all patients enrolled on ziftomenib trials in the ISS were diagnosed with either
telapsed/refractory AML or untreated AML. Only 5 patients were treated with ALL into KO-
MEN-001 SS1. Therefore, there is insufficient experience outside of AML to comment on
potential drug-disease interactions.
