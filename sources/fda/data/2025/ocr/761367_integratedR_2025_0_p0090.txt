Pooled PK Analysis by aPTT

Further analyses were conducted exploring the real-time relationship between garadacimab
plasma concentrations and aPTT prolongation using data from subjects with HAE in trial 3001,
which evaluated the proposed maintenance dose of 200 mg SC once monthly. The relationship
between garadacimab plasma concentration and aPTT was generally flat in trial 3001 when
subjects received the proposed maintenance dose of 200 mg SC once monthly (see Figure 12).
Some data points fell above the general trend, and these represent subjects with prolonged aPTT.
These subjects have been described above.

Prothrombin Time

As presented in Table 36, there were a total of six subjects with prolonged prothrombin time
(PT) >1.3x ULN (normal range 9.8 to 12.1) in the garadacimab group (two of whom are
mentioned above) and one subject in the placebo group (not shown). Most prolongations were
singular events that resolved without garadacimab dose modification or treatment
discontinuation. There was no clear correlation with other concomitant medications or other
underlying conditions or risk factors. None of the subjects experienced clinical manifestations of
bleeding diatheses or thromboemboli. Again, of note, subject ©) reported taking
apixaban (Factor X inhibitor), which may prolong PT.
