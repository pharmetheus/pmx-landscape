NDA/IND Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 219379/219389
Mirdametinib (GOMEKLI)

Based on the results of the ReNeu study, the totality of information submitted to this NDA, and the favorable benefit:risk profile for this
population with a serious and life-threatening disease, the review team recommends regular approval for mirdametinib for the following

indication: the treatment of adult and pediatric patients 2 years of age and older with neurofibromatosis type 1 (NF1) who have symptomatic
plexiform neurofibromas not amenable to complete surgical resection.

Dimension

Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and Reasons

Analysis of
Condition

e NF1 is a progressive multi-system disease caused by loss of
NF1 function and characterized by progressive cutaneous,

neurological, skeletal, and neoplastic manifestations early in

life and which can be associated with severe morbidity.

e PNs are benign nerve sheath tumors that occur in 40-50% of
patients with NF1. PNs may develop along nerves
throughout the body and may be located around the orbit,
face, upper and lower limbs, back, thorax, abdomen, neck
brachial plexus and/or lumbosacral plexus, which result in
clinical symptoms such as disfigurement, motor
dysfunction, pain, respiratory impairment, visual
dysfunction, and neurological dysfunction.

e Malignant transformation to malignant peripheral nerve
sheath tumors (MPNST) can occur in 8-16% of patients with
NF1-PN.

NF1 is a rare, progressive, and highly morbid condition
that can lead to fatal outcomes depending upon the
location of the tumor. It is a serious and life-
threatening disease.

Current
Treatment
Options

e Selumetinib is approved for pediatric patients 2 years of age
and older with symptomatic, inoperable NF1-PN, and
demonstrated an ORR of 44% as assessed by independent
central review. Selumetinib is available in a capsule
formulation.

e There are no available systemic therapies for adult patients

There is an unmet medical need for adult patients with
progressive or symptomatic NF1-PN, and for pediatric
patients who are unable to swallow capsules.
