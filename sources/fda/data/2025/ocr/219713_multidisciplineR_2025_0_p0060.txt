5.5. Toxicology

5.5.1. General Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

In the Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) 28-day repeat-dose rat toxicity and TK study with
administered taletrectinib doses of 0, 10, 30 or 100 mg/kg/day, the STDio for taletrectinib was
estimated to be >100 mg/kg/day. In the pivotal 85-day repeat-dose rat toxicity and TK study with
administered taletrectinib doses of 0, 20, 65 or 100 mg/kg/day, the STDio for taletrectinib was
not determined but was estimated to be >100 mg/kg/day. At the 100 mg/kg/day dose, the
taletrectinib Day 85 sex combined mean Cmax was 1,855.52 ng/mL and AUCo-72n was

62,588.16 ng*h/mL.

In the GLP 28-day repeat-dose monkey toxicity and TK study with administered taletrectinib
doses of 0, 10, 30 or 100 mg/kg/day, the HNSTD was 100 mg/kg/day. In the pivotal GLP 85-day
tepeat-dose monkey toxicity and TK study with administered taletrectinib doses of 0, 10, 30, or
60/48 mg/kg/day. Initially, a dose of 60 mg/kg/day was the highest dose but was reduced to

48 mg/kg/day following deaths early in the study. The HNSTD for taletrectinib was

30 mg/kg/day with the sex combined mean Cmax and AUCo-24 values on Day 85 of 813.04 ng/mL
and 13,941.54 ng*h/mL, respectively.
