Body weight showed statistical significance but limited clinical impact, with exposure

dosing interval.

The Applicant’s dataset and analyses were reviewed and verified by the pharmacometrics
reviewers. The final model was deemed adequate to characterize the PK profile of imlunestrant
in healthy participants and patients with ER+ breast cancer and endometrial endometroid cancer
(EEC). The Applicant's popPK analysis is acceptable.
