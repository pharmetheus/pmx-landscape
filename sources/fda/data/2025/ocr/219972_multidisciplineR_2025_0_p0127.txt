6.3.2.4 Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions, and what is
the appropriate management strategy?
Data:

Clinical data related to the effect of food, acid-reducing agents, and other drugs (CYP3A4
inhibitors and CYP3A4 inducers, P-gp inhibitors and inducers, and BCRP inhibitors and

inducers) on the PK of sevabertinib are provided in Section 6.2.2.2.
Clinical data related to the potential effects of sevabertinib on other drugs are presented below:

CYP3A4 substrates: In Study 22251, co-administration of a single dose of 20 mg sevabertinib
with 1 mg midazolam (a CYP3A4 probe substrate) resulted in a mean midazolam AUC and Cmax
ratio of 1.10 compared to administration of 1 mg midazolam alone. The results indicate that a
single dose of sevabertinib has only a minor effect on the PK of midazolam. Pre-treatment and
co-administration of multiple doses of sevabertinib (20 mg BID) with 1 mg midazolam resulted
in an increase in a mean midazolam AUC ratio of 1.95 (reflecting a 95% increase) and a mean
midazolam Cmax ratio of approximately 1.8 (reflecting an 80% increase) compared with
administration of 1 mg midazolam alone.

P-gp substrates: In Study 22252, pre-treatment and co-administration of 20 mg sevabertinib BID
with 75 mg dabigatran etexilate (a sensitive P-gp substrate) resulted in a mean dabigatran AUC
ratio of 1.4 (reflecting a 40% increase) while the Cmax was unchanged, compared with
administration of 75 mg dabigatran etexilate alone.

BCRP substrates: In Study 22252, pre-treatment and co-administration of 20 mg sevabertinib
BID with 10 mg rosuvastatin (a sensitive BCRP substrate) resulted in a mean rosuvastatin AUC
ratio of 1.25 (reflecting a 25% increase) and a mean rosuvastatin Cmax ratio of approximately 1.4
(reflecting a 40% increase) compared to administration of rosuvastatin alone.
