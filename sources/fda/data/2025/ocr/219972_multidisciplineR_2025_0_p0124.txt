NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

Safety: Higher rates of Grade >3 TEAEs, TEAEs leading to dose modifications (i.e., dose
reductions and interruptions) were observed at 20 mg BID compared to 10 mg BID in the
dosage expansion/extension part (Table 24). Particularly, higher rates of all grade and
Grade 3/4 diarrhea and earlier onset of diarrhea were reported at 20 mg BID (Table 25).
In alignment with the safety observations, FDA analysis identified significant positive E-R
relationships for rash, any Grade and Grade > 3 diarrhea and AEs leading to dose
reduction or interruption (refer to Section 19.4.4 for details). However, further analysis
using safety data of individual patients suggested that diarrhea was generally manageable
and did not necessarily lead to dose reduction, regardless of severity and recurrence of
diarrhea, patient age, and dose level (see Section 19.4.4 for details).
