T-cell dependent antibody response (anti-KLH IgM)
¢ Compared to the vehicle control, changes were not dose-dependent, except a dose-dependent increase in

anti-KLH IgM in the second challenge on Days 176 and 183 in males administered 30 to 150 mg/kg
imlunestrant. Although it was not dose-dependent, a decrease in the primary anti-KLH IgM response
was noted 7 to 14 days after the first KLH injection, but increased during Day 21 post-KLH
administration. However, the magnitude of changes in T-cell dependent antibody response did not
correlate with histopathology findings. Overall, imlunestrant had no effect on the primary immune
response to KLH challenge.

T-cell dependent antibody response (anti-KLH IgG)
© Changes were not dose-dependent when compared to the VH control; except a dose-dependent increase
in anti-KLH IgG in the second challenge on Days 176 and 183 in males administered 10-150 mg/kg
imlunestrant. Changes in anti-KLH IgG responses also decreased at 7 to 14 days after the initial KLH
injection, though this effect was not dose-dependent. However, the magnitude of changes in T-cell

General toxicology, additional studies in rats:

GLP-compliant 4-week and 3-month daily oral repeat dose toxicology studies were conducted in
tats. These studies were reviewed under IND 145311. The main findings including target organs
of toxicity generally reflected those observed in the GLP-compliant 6-month study. Additional
findings are noted below.

3-month study (#8003221/GLP compliant): Rats were administered daily oral imlunestrant
(LY3484356; lot# CR-C18071754-J21001, 89.8% purity) at doses of 0, 10, 30, and 150
ing/kg/day for 3 months followed by a 6-week recovery period (n = 10/sex/group [main],
5/sex/group [recovery for only control and high dose groups]).

e No early deaths. Clinical observations of salivation and abnormal liquid/soft/mucoid
feces were observed at > 10 mg/kg in both males and females, and minimal nerve fiber
degeneration in the optic nerve in a female administered 150 mg/kg.

4-week study (#8002568/GLP compliant): Rats were administered daily oral imlunestrant (lot#
JR-C18071754-J19001, 92.7% purity) at doses of 0, 30, 100, and 300 mg/kg for 4 weeks
followed by a 2-week recovery period (n = 10/sex/group [main], 5/sex/group [recovery for only
control and high dose groups]).

e Early deaths (n=3 males) occurred at 300 mg/kg due to imlunestrant related
hepatocellular and/or muscular damage, toxicities that were not observed in the 3- and 6-
month studies up to 150 mg/kg.
