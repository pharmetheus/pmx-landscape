5.5.2. Genetic Toxicology

The Applicant’s Position:

Ziftomenib was not genotoxic in the GLP-compliant standard genotoxicity testing battery
conducted to support registration. Ziftomenib was demonstrated to be nonmutagenic in a
bacterial reverse mutation assay and nonclastogenic/aneugenic in an in vitro micronucleus assay
conducted in human peripheral blood lymphocytes and in an in vivo micronucleus assay
conducted in CD-1 mice.
