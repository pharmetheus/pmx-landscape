The FDA’s Assessment:

In general, FDA agrees with the Applicant’s description of the trial design. While the Applicant
tefers to Group D as the primary efficacy population and Group E as the supportive efficacy
population, FDA is evaluating data from Groups D and E as the primary evidence of efficacy in
support of the indication. Due to limited data and insufficient evidence of effectiveness in
patients with non-TKD mutations and squamous histology, FDA revised the primary efficacy
population to include only patients with TKD mutations and non-squamous histology. Please
refer to the later section - Efficacy Results for Primary Endpoint for details.

Eligibility Criteria

The Applicant’s Description:

Key eligibility criteria included:

¢ Females or males aged >18 years, with documented histologically or cytologically

confirmed locally advanced NSCLC, not suitable for definitive therapy, or recurrent or
metastatic NSCLC. Documented activating EGFR and/or HER? mutation and
documented disease progression after treatment with at least 1 prior systemic therapy for
advanced disease (except for participants eligible for Group F [Expansion or Extension]

who should have received no prior systemic treatment for locally advanced or metastatic
disease).

e Measurable disease by RECIST v1.1.

e Group D: NSCLC with HER? activating mutations (including ex20ins) and naive to
treatment with a HER? ex20ins-targeted therapy (e.g. poziotinib, trastuzumab
deruxtecan). Participants who had prior treatment with HER2 ex20ins-targeted therapy
for < 2 months and stopped treatment due to reasons other than progressive disease were
also eligible.

¢ Group E: NSCLC with HER? activating mutations (including ex20ins) and documented
progression to HER2-targeted ADCs (e.g. trastuzumab deruxtecan). Prior treatment with
HER2 ex20ins-TKIs (i.e. poziotinib) was exclusionary unless treatment lasted for < 2
months and was stopped due to reasons other than progressive disease.

e Backfill, Expansion, Extension: Participants with a known history or presence of the
EGFR T790M mutation were excluded.
