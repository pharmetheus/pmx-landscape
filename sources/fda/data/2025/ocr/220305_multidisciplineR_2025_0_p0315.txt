14.4.4.4.3. Effects of ziftomenib on the substrates of CYP3A, CYP2Cs and
UGTIA1

Ziftomenib is expected to have a moderate inhibitory effect on the sensitive CYP3A substrate
midazolam and have a minimal effect (AUC ratio <1.25) on the UGT1A1 substrate raltegravir
and substrates of CYP2C isoforms in patients with cancer (FDA Table 67).
