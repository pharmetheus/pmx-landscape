The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s assessment of the nonclinical ADME/PK data.
FDA notes that following a single oral dose of 10 mg/kg, [**C]-mirdametinib-derived
radioactivity distributed widely to rat tissues, with greatest uptake in the liver, followed by
kidney, blood, adrenal, lung, salivary gland, heart, and pancreas.

Because the FDA calculated animal to human exposures based on their own analysis of
nonclinical and clinical PK data, the FDA did not verify the results presented by the Applicant for
Table 4 titled Calculated Safety Margins from Definitive Repeat-Dose Toxicity Studies.
