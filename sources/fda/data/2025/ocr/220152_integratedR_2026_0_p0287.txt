Eli Lilly, the initial Sponsor for tradipitant, conducted nonclinical and clinical studies to
determine the metabolite profile of tradipitant early in the development program, with
“four major metabolites being identified (M2, M3, M4, and M8)’ in humans.®® However,
nonclinical data to assess the safety of the four major human metabolites were limited
during development, as these metabolites were not prospectively measured in general
toxicology studies with tradipitant in Fischer rats (up to 6 months) or in dogs (up to 3
months). Eli Lilly did measure M2, M3, and M4 retrospectively in stored samples from

1317-105 Amendment 2) was never submitted to any IND for tradipitant. Eli Lilly also
