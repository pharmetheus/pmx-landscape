Andembry / garadacimab

8.3. Plans for Pediatric Drug Development

Garadacimab was granted Orphan Drug Designation in April 2020 for bradykinin-mediated
angioedema (which includes hereditary angioedema) and is therefore exempt from Pediatric
Research Equity Act study requirements.

8.4. Pregnancy, Lactation, and Females/ Males of
Reproductive Potential

The potential reproductive toxicity of garadacimab was evaluated in fertility and early embryonic
development studies with male and female rabbits, an embryofetal development study with
female rabbits, and a pre- and postnatal development study with female rabbits. Rabbits were
determined to be a pharmacologically relevant species.

Fertility and Developmental Toxicology

mating period, and continuing to Postpairing Day 8. Females were not dosed and assigned to
