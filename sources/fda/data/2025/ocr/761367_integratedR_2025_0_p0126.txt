13.1.4.2. Carcinogenicity

An assessment of the carcinogenic potential associated with chronic inhibition of FXIIa by
garadacimab was conducted by the Applicant and the Nonclinical Reviewers.

e The mouse was demonstrated to be a pharmacologically relevant species with garadacimab.
A 4week study with garadacimab was conducted in the mouse; however, this study showed
that mice developed an immunogenic response to treatment with this heterologous
humanized monoclonal antibody. Therefore, a 2-year mouse carcinogenicity study was not
considered technically feasible.

e A 2-year carcinogenicity study in rats would not provide meaningful information for the
safety assessment of garadacimab as the rat was not a pharmacologically relevant species
(ie., no binding of garadacimab to rat FXTIa).
