NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0
Updated results as of 28 MAR 2025:

In Group D, the updated confirmed ORR by BICR was 64.2% (95% CI: [52.8%, 74.6%])
among the 81 participants in the FAS (including 2 CRs and 50 PRs), exceeding the ORR
of 60.5% reported for participants at the 14 OCT 2024 database cut-off date.

The majority of participants in Group D (FAS) experienced a reduction in tumor size as
the best change from baseline, with 19.8% of participants having a >70% reduction.

Sensitivity analyses of ORR by BICR, performed in 80 evaluable participants in the EFS,
were consistent with the results from the main analysis in the FAS.

The ORR results were consistent and >57%, and the lower limit of the 95% CI exceeded
30% across the pre-specified subgroups in Group D.

The data from Group E (N=55) aligns with and further supports the findings from
Group D, demonstrating efficacy in a participant population that had received prior
systemic therapy and progressed to HER2-targeted ADC therapy.

Results as of 14 OCT 2024:

In Group D, the confirmed ORR by BICR was 60.5% (95% CI: [49.0, 71.2]) among the
81 participants in the FAS, including 1 CR and 48 PRs.

The majority of participants in Group D (FAS) experienced a reduction in tumor size as
the best change from baseline, with 16.0% participants having a >70% reduction.

Sensitivity analyses of ORR by BICR, performed in 80 evaluable participants in the EFS,
were consistent with the results from the main analysis in the FAS.

The ORR results were consistent and >57% across the pre-specified subgroups in Group
D, including demographics (age, sex, and race), ECOG PS at baseline, geographical
region, smoking status, histology (adenocarcinoma yes vs. no; squamous vs. non-
squamous), prior surgery (pneumonectomy), number of prior systemic anti-cancer
therapy regimens, presence/absence of brain metastases at baseline, prior platinum
therapy, prior anti-PD(L)1 treatment, and HER2 TKD mutations status at baseline.

Based on data from Study 21607, no clinically meaningful differences are anticipated in
the efficacy of sevabertinib in the US vs. non-US NSCLC patients with HER2 activating
mutations.

The data from Group E (N=55) aligns with and further supports the findings from Group
D, demonstrating efficacy in a participant population that had received prior systemic
therapy and progressed to HER2-targeted ADC therapy.
