Overall, the data from trials 2001 and 3001 supported the proposed dosing regimen of a 400 mg
loading dose administered SC followed by a 200-mg dose administered SC once monthly.

6.2. Clinical Studies/Trials Intended To
Demonstrate Efficacy

6.2.1. Design and Conduct, Trial 3001

Results of the pivotal trial (3001) served as the basis for efficacy evaluation. This was a 6-month,
randomized (3:2 to garadacimab: placebo), double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group,
multicenter (9 U.S. sites and 19 ex-U.S. sites), phase 3 trial evaluating 200 mg garadacimab
administered SC once monthly in adults (aged 18 to 69 years) and adolescents (aged 12 to

17 years) with C1-INH (Type I or Type II) HAE who had >2 HAE attacks during the

Run-in Period. This trial had a Screening Period of up to 1 month, a Run-in Period of up to

2 months, a 6-month Treatment Period, and a 2-month Follow-up Period (Figure 3).
