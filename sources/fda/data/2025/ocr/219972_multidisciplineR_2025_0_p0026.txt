Conclusions and Reasons

e There are no products specifically targeting HER2-mutated NSCLC
currently under traditional approval in the U.S. Treatments used for
patients with HER2-mutated NSCLC are the same as those used for
patients with NSCLC without a specific driver mutation.

e Initial systemic therapy in the metastatic setting generally consists of
platinum-based doublet chemotherapy, with or without anti-PD-(L)1
antibody.

For patients with disease progression following initial systemic
therapy, available treatment options include chemotherapy (single
agent or docetaxel with ramucirumab), associated with ORRs of 10-
23% with median DORs of 4-9 months, or anti-PD-(L)1 antibody as a
single agent for patients who are anti-PD-(L)1 naive, associated with
ORRs of 15-20% and median DORs of 16-17 months.

« Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki (T-Dxd), a HER2-directed antibody
and topoisomerase inhibitor conjugate, which demonstrated an ORR of
58% (95% CI: 43-71) and median DOR of 8.7 months (95% CI: 7.1,
NE), was granted accelerated approval for NSCLC with activating
HER2 mutations in August 2022.

Treatment options are limited for patients with
previously treated NSCLC harboring HER2
(ERBB2) mutations whose disease has
progressed after prior systemic therapy and an
unmet medical need exists for more effective
therapies.
