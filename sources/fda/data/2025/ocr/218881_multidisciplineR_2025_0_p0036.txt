4.1 Office of Scientific Investigations (OSD

For clinical inspections, 3 clinical sites were selected in conjunction with OSI for FDA
inspection based on being high enrolling sites for the EMBER-3 trial. In addition, the Applicant
(Eli Lilly and Company), was also selected for inspection. The full report was completed by Dr.
Leigh Marcus from FDA OSI and is summarized below. Overall, the inspection findings were
acceptable and did not alter the conclusions from the NDA review.

1. Dr. Monica Lis Casalnuovo (Site #33334)
Buenos Aires, Argentina

Inspection Dates: 4/1/2025, 4/3/2025 — 4/4/2025, 4/7/2025

This was the first FDA inspection for this clinical investigator. At this site, 15 patients
were screened and 9 were enrolled to the trial (5 with tumors that were ESR1-mut).
During the inspection, the primary efficacy endpoint data were verifiable. There was no
underreporting of adverse events (AEs) or serious AEs (SAEs). It was noted that
Informed consent Documents (ICDs) did not contain the required statement related to
ClinicalTrials.gov in any of their versions of the main ICD document. There was a
discussion to ensure the site has the required statement related to clinicaltrials.gov in all
versions of the main informed consent form going forward.

Clinical Reviewer: The findings from this site did not alter the benefit: risk of this
application.
