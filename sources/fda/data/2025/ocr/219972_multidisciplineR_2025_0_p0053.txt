NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA disagrees with the Applicant’s use of vague, subjective, and promotional language a

©® when describing
study results. FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s conclusions, with additional pertinent
details below.

+ FDA notes that the activity of sevabertinib against WT EGFR and EGFR mutants is not
televant to the proposed clinical indication; however, WT EGFR inhibition may have
safety implications and was therefore included in Section 12.1 of the label.

+ A homology model of HER2 was built using an X-ray crystallographic structure of
EGFR in complex with sevabertinib as a template. The predictive X-ray crystallography
showed that sevabertinib noncovalently interacts with the HER2 kinase domain. In
addition, it did not interact with the Cys805 in the HER2 kinase domain, which is
associated with irreversible binding. A cell viability assay showed that sevarbitinib
maintained activity in a Ba/F3 cell line expressing a HER2 C805S mutation (Siegel et al.
Cancer Discovery, 2025).

+ In vitro cellular assays in Ba/F3 murine cells stably expressing WT HER2 and a panel of
HER2 exon 20 insertion mutations and activating point mutations showed that
sevabertinib exhibited antiproliferative activity against all HER2 mutants tested, with
IC5O0 values generally within 2-fold for WT HER2 (ICso = 5.30 nM) and the HER2
mutants, with the exception of the activating point mutations S310Y (IC50 = 0.98 nM),
V777L (ICs0 = 0.54 nM), and T798M (ICs0 = 13.9 nM) (Study No. EXP-GDPWI-06962
(ELSA); Report No. B003066). The ICso concentrations for WT and mutant HER2 are
below the free Cmax of 88 nM at the intended clinical sevabertinib dose of 20 mg based on
~95% protein binding.

+ Inaddition to xenograft models bearing EGFR mutants, the antitumor activity of
sevabertinib was evaluated in inmunocompromised mice bearing NSCLC patient-
derived xenografts expressing the HER2 ex20ins YVMA mutation (Study No. 1212-104;
Report No. R-13868). Oral administration of sevabertinib at doses of 10, 25, and 50
mng/kg once daily for 28 days led to tumor growth inhibition of 40%, 67%, and 74%,
respectively, compared to the control group. There were 3/10 partial responders (PRs) in
the 25 mg/kg group and 6/10 PRs in the 50 mg/kg group. There were no mortalities or
adverse effects on body weights.

+ Immunoblot analysis showed that treatment of human cancer cell lines with increasing
concentrations of sevabertinib (1 nm to 10 uM) led to inhibition of p-HER2 as well as
downstream signaling components including p-ERK, p-AKT, and p-MEK in NCI-H1781
lung cancer cells with a HER20 exon 20 insertion, and NCI-H2170 lung cancer cells and
NCI-N87 gastric cancer cells with WT HER2 amplification (Study No. STY-25-00593;
Report No. B004515).
