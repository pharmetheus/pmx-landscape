NDA Multidisciplinary Review and Evaluation
NDA 214759
Grafapex (treosulfan)

6.2.2 General Dosing and Therapeutic Individualization
General Dosing

The recommended regimen of treosulfan in adults and pediatric patients >1 year of age is
treosulfan 10 g/m?/day for 3 consecutive days (Days -4, -3, and -2) in combination with
fludarabine 30 mg/m?/day for 5 consecutive days (Days -6 through -2) prior to allogeneic
hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (Day 0).

Therapeutic Individualization

No dosage adjustment is recommended for patients with mild renal impairment (CLcr 60-89
mL/min) or mild hepatic impairment [total bilirubin less than or equal to upper limit of normal
(ULN) with AST greater than ULN or total bilirubin greater than 1 to 1.5 times ULN with any
AST]. No information is provided or requested for patients with moderate or severe hepatic
impairment as they are typically excluded from transplantation. Additional data will be
necessary to inform the dosing of treosulfan in patients with moderate renal impairment (CLcr
30-59 ml/min).

Outstanding Issues

The following issues were not adequately addressed by the available data:

e Determination of an appropriate safe dose of treosulfan for patients with moderate
renal impairment (CLcr 30-59 mL/min)
Evaluation of the effects of treosulfan on the QTc interval
Evaluation of treosulfan and its epoxide metabolites as substrates of metabolizing
enzymes in vitro

e Evaluation of the PK of treosulfan active metabolites in adult patients

e Determination of the effects of race and ethnicity on treosulfan PK
