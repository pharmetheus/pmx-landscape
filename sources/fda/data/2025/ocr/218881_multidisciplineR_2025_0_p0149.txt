Inluriyo (imlunestrant)

In the ESR/m-detected population, the PFS Kaplan-Meier curves overlapped throughout the first
4 months and started to separate thereafter (Figure 8.4), which indicated that the assumption of
exponential distribution was not met. Late separation in the KM curves persisted and led to an
observed benefit in the PFS hazard ratio. The Applicant conducted an exploratory analysis of
PFS using the restricted mean survival time (RSMT) to further quantify the treatment benefit for
PFS (Table 8.13). The estimated RSMT at 19.4 months was 7.9 months (95% CI: 6.8, 9.1) in the
imlunestrant arm vs 5.4 months (95% CI: 4.6, 6.2) in the TPC arm [difference 2.6 months (1.2.-
3.9)]. It is noted that RMST results could change if a different cutoff timepoint was chosen;
therefore, the RMST analysis is considered to be supportive only.

e Discordance between PFS per investigator assessment and PFS per BIRC assessment

The review team assessed the discordance rate of PFS assessments between investigator and
BIRC (Table 8.20). In the ITT population, more patients in the TPC arm were censored by BIRC
but considered as having a PFS event by investigator than patients in the imlunestrant arm

(31.5% in the TPC arm vs 26.9% in the imlunestrant arm).

investigator progressions. LDR was the frequency of investigator-assessed progressions after
