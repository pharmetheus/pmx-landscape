The Applicant’s Position:

Routine clinical tests were conducted as directed by the Schedule of Activities in the EMBER-3
protocol. A central laboratory analyzed clinical chemistry, fasting lipid panel, PK, and biomarker
samples. Local laboratories were used to analyze hematology, coagulation, and hormone levels.
The study monitors evaluated the local laboratories’ use of internal and external quality control,
quality assurance, and accreditation schemes.

The Applicant’s Position:

EMBER-3 was designed with routine scheduled visits. Tests were appropriate for monitoring
safety at the designated intervals. The testing standards were suitable for the indicated
population, and the assessments performed were appropriate.

The FDA’s Assessment:
In general FDA agrees with the Applicant’s assessment for categorization of adverse events.

FDA categorization of adverse events used preferred terms (PTs) to group terms and further
consolidate synonymous adverse events, as outlined in section 8.2.1. The FDA PTs for
abdominal pain, vomiting, diarrhea, and musculoskeletal pain were slightly different than the
ones used by the Applicant as previously noted in this review. The FDA PT for abdominal pain
included hepatic pain, the PT for vomiting included hematemesis, the PT for diarrhea included
enterocolitis and colitis, and the PT for musculoskeletal pain included cancer pain and back pain.
