Supportive efficacy results in Group E — as of 14 OCT 2024

In the supportive efficacy population, Group E, the ORR by the investigator assessment was
38.2% (95% CI: [25.4, 52.3]), which was consistent with the results by BICR (34.5%; 95% CI:
[22.2, 48.6]) (Table 48).

The median DOR by BICR was 8.5 months (95% CTI: [4.1, not estimable] in the FAS with a
median DOR follow-up time of 4.1 months from initial radiological response. Of the 19
responders, 5 participants (26.3%) had observed DOR of >6 months, 3 participants (15.8%) had
an observed DOR of >9 months, and 1 participant (5.3%) had an observed DOR of >12 months,
as of the database cut-off. The median DOR by the investigator assessment was 9.5 months (95%
CI: [4.1, A]) (Module 5.3.5.2, B003047, Tables 8.2.2/2, 8.2.2/1s1 and 8.2.2/3 [Datasets ADTTE, ADSL and
ADFACE)).

The median PFS was 5.4 months (95% CI: [4.1, 9.7]) by BICR and 6.5 months (95% CI: [4.2,
10.7]) by the investigator assessment. The median OS was 12.9 months (95% CI: [11.1, not
estimable]), with 41 participants (74.5%) censored. The range in OS (including censored values)
was 1.9 months to 14.9 months.
