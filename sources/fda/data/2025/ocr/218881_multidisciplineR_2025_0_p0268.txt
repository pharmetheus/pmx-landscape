Assessment Conclusions

The Applicant has sufficiently validated bioanalytical methods for determination of imlunestrant
concentration in human plasma by HPLC with MS/MS detection (8415876) as well as in
KEDTA plasma by LC-MS/MS (400008-213865-PMV_1).

The precision and accuracy of quality control (QC) were within the prespecified acceptance
limits. The Applicant validated the selectivity, matrix effect, hemolysis effect, lipemic effect,
dilution linearity, bench-top and process stability, and freeze-thaw stability. The long term
stability was established for concentration control was 1393 days at -70°C and 960 days at -70°C
for methods 8415876 and 400008-213865-PMV_1, respectively.
