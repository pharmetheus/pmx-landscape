The Applicant’s Position:

Imlunestrant demonstrated high selectivity as an ERa antagonist with >1734-fold selectivity over
other nuclear hormone receptors such as androgen receptor, glucocorticoid receptor,
mineralocorticoid receptor, and PGR. Imlunestrant was highly selective against 33 kinases and 8
human G-protein coupled receptors relevant for human safety when tested in enzymatic assays in
vitro. Imlunestrant displayed no uterotrophic effects or adverse microscopic findings in the
uterus, cervix, or vagina of immature female rats, indicating no agonist activity in these tissues.
