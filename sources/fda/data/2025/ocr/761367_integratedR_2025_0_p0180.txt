PK Analysis

All the 39 subjects who received garadacimab were included in the PK population, which
comprised all the subjects with at least one measurable concentration of garadacimab.

Plasma concentrations were summarized by nominal time points and plotted on linear and
semilogarithmic scales. Noncompartmental analysis was not conducted. PK data were included
in analyses of population PK, PK/PD, and E-R.

PK Results

The mean (SD) garadacimab concentration-time profile for all subjects is shown in Figure 38.
Following administration of the SC loading dose, the highest trough concentrations were
observed on Day 30 prior to the second dose (mean [SD]=10,533 [4004] ng/mL). Plasma
concentrations declined by Day 60 (prior to administration of the third dose) and remained
consistent for the remainder of the Treatment Period. Mean (SD) trough concentrations from Day
60 through the remainder of the Treatment Period ranged from 8087 (4277) ng/mL to 8692
(3934) ng/mL. Steady-state for Ctrough was reached by Day 60. This behavior is similar to
observations in trial 2001, in which subjects received an IV loading dose prior to initiation of SC
dosing.
