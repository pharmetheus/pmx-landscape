Based on the results of the phase 2 dose-ranging trial (2001) and assessment of exposure-
response (E-R) relationships for efficacy, selection of the 200-mg dose for the phase 3 trial
(3001) appears to be reasonable.

Trial 3001

The 200 mg monthly maintenance dosing regimen (with a 400 mg loading dose) was further
evaluated in the 6-month, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, phase 3 trial (3001).
The 200-mg dose met the primary endpoint of time-normalized number of HAE attacks per
month and all key secondary efficacy endpoints, demonstrating an improvement over placebo
(see Sections 6.2.4.3 and 6.2.4.4).
