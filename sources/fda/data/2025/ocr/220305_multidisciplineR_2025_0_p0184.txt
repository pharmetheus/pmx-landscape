Clinical TL Comment: Of interest, the CR + CRh rate appears to be numerically higher in study
participants with a Type D NPM1 mutation. The interpretation of this is limited by small overall
numbers, but this will be interesting to investigate in the postmarket setting and in the future
randomized Phase 3 study.

The impact of comutation on the efficacy of ziftomenib was explored as a subgroup analysis in
the Phase 2 cohort, as this data was not directly collected at the time of relapse prior to
initiation of Phase 2. While the FDA acknowledges the small sample size of each individual
comutation subgroup, the presence of a FLT3-ITD appears to portend an inferior response rate,
compared to the other mutations, which were generally in line with the broader treatment
population. With that being said, the broad confidence interval around the response rate of the
FLT3-ITD population includes the point estimate for the broader population, and the
recommended indication ensures that patients should have received FLT3 targeted therapies
prior to ziftomenib. No other mitigation strategy is warranted at this time.
