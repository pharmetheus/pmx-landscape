Secondary Pharmacology

The Applicant’s Position:

The selectivity of ziftomenib (10 tM) was evaluated in a radioligand binding assay against a
panel of 55 purified receptors, ion channels, and transporters. In this assay, > 50% inhibition of
ligand binding to the human 5-HT>z receptor was observed in the presence of 10 1M ziftomenib
(KO-PD-0028). Subsequently, an ICso value of 10 1M against the human 5-HT>x receptor (an
antagonist effect) was determined from concentration-response curves in a cell-based functional
assay (KO-PD-0029). Given that ziftomenib is highly plasma protein bound, the observed off-
target in vitro inhibitory activity against the human 5-HT>p receptor at 10 tM is considered
unlikely to be clinically relevant in humans.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the statements above. Ziftomenib (FAA-02) at 10 uM was used
to screen a panel of 55 receptors, ion channels, and transporters for activity modulation.
Ziftomenib decreased activity of the 5-hydroxytryptamine 2B (5-HT2 8) receptor by 96%, by 39%
for the C-C chemokine receptor type 1 (CCR1) and by 24% for the neuropeptide Y receptor type

1 (Y1, study KO-PD-0028). In another study (study KO-PD-0029), the ICso for ziftomenib
inhibition of the 5-HTog receptor was determined to be 10 1M.
