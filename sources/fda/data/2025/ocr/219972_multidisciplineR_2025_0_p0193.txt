NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation — NDA 219972
Sevabertinib (BAY 2927088) — Version 2.0

Persistence of Effect

Data and The Applicant’s Position:

Participants in Study 21607 received sevabertinib until disease progression according to RECIST
v1.1, unacceptable toxicity, or any other withdrawal criteria were met. The persistence of effect
is reflected in the durability of response. See discussion of DOR as aforementioned under
Secondary and other relevant endpoints.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant; the study design and drug administration schedule do not permit
an assessment of persistence of effect beyond drug discontinuation.
