13.1.8.3. Phototoxicity Assessment

The Applicant conducted a chemical photoreactivity assay per International Council for
Harmonisation (ICH) guideline $10 Photosafety Evaluation of Pharmaceuticals (November
2013). The molecular extinction coefficient of SUZ was 1339 Lmol!cm” at the maximum
absorbance observed of 290 nm. However, SUZ was nonphotoreactive (no generation of singlet
oxygen/superoxide anion) with UVA/B and visible light. In addition, radioactivity concentrations
in melanin-containing tissues (the uveal tract, pigmented skin, and meninges) declined to
undetectable levels by 72 hours after peaking between 1 and 4 hours postdose in rats, suggesting
no irreversible binding to melanin-containing tissues. Therefore, SUZ has low potential for
phototoxicity and no further assessment is necessary.
