3.1. Overview of the Product and Regulatory Background

The clinical development program for IMCIVREE comprised of 13 studies: 6 Phase 1/1b/2a studies,

4 Phase 2 studies, 2 Phase 3 studies, and 1 long-term extension study. A total of 424 subjects participated
in these clinical studies, including healthy obese volunteers (n = 313), patients with POMC, PCSK1 or
LEPR (n = 64), patients with Prader-Willi syndrome (n = 39) and Bardet-Bied1 syndrome and Alstrém
syndrome patients (n=7).
