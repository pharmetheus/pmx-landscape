14.1. In Vitro Studies

14.1.1. Protein Binding

Rezafungin (REZ) plasma protein binding (PPB) has been studied in mouse, rat, monkey,
chimpanzee, and human plasma (Studies NC-008, NC-121, NC-137, CD101.IV.1.15, RESTORE,
CD101.IV.1.06, STRIVE).
