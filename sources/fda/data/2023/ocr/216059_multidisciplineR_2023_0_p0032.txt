The binding reversibility of pirtobrutinib to BTK in studies of purified protein/kinase was
supported by data for the inhibition of B-cell activation by pirtobrutinib (LOXO-305-PHARM-
016). Pirtobrutinib reversibility was tested in the peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMC) B-
cell activation assay using a washout method. In the assay, data obtained from the washout and
non-washout plates were compared. Less than 10% inhibition of B-cell activation was observed
in the washout plate by pirtobrutinib up to the highest concentration of 10 4M, demonstrating
the reversibility of pirtobrutinib induced inhibition on CD69+B-cell activation (data not shown).

Binding kinetics of BTK inhibitors on the targets
The inhibitory effect against BTK C4815, illustrated as binding kinetics in the Proteros Receptor

Displacement Assay, was compared among pirtobrutinib, ibrutinib and acalabrutinib.

The table below summarizes the binding kinetics:

¢ The dissociation kinetic constant (kof) values for pirtobrutinib from BTK and BTK C481S
were greater for BTK, indicating a slower rate of dissociation from BTK C481S compared
to BTK.

¢ Pirtobrutinib’s residence time (=1/ko#) on BTK C481S was 3.8 times longer than
ibrutinib’s residence time and greater than 14 times longer than acalabrutinib’s
residence time.

e  Pirtobrutinib had similar Kp values for WT and BTK C481S; however, ibrutinib and
acalabrutinib showed increased Kp values for BTK C4815, indicating decreased affinities
of ibrutinib and acalabrutinib towards C481S mutant BTK.
