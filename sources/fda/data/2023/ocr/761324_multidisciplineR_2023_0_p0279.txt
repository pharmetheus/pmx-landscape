The final epcoritamab model used a quasi-steady-state approximation of a target-mediated
drug disposition model structure (Gibiansky et al. 2008). Elimination half-life is summarized in
Table 98. Total apparent clearance (CL/F) was equal to the sum of target-mediated CL/F and
target-independent CL/F. Median total CL/F decreases as epcoritamab concentrations increase
over time, as shown in Figure 23. The geometric mean (CV%) apparent total clearance is

3.34 L/day (102%) at the first full treatment dose and 0.53 L/day (39.7%) at the 11th full dose.
Median target-mediated CL/F was between 85 to 90% of total CL/F during administration of the
proposed priming dose, intermediate dose, and first full dose. By Week 12, target-mediated
CL/F was <15% of total CL/F.
