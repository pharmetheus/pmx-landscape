Assessment

e High incidence of ADA antibodies in subjects treated with somatrogon

In this development program, in both Trials CP-4-006 and CP-4-004, the incidence of
somatrogon-induced ADA was higher compared to the Genotropin comparator.

In the main period of Trial CP-4-006, 84 (77%) subjects in the somatrogon group developed
ADA compared to 18 (16%) subjects in Genotropin group. In the OLE period, a total of 114/212
subjects (54%) were ADA-positive: 78/104 subjects (75%) from the original somatrogon group
and 36/108 subjects (33%) from the original Genotropin group (Table 20). This observation is
consistent with a conclusion that the majority of the seroconverted subjects in the somatrogon
arm of the main period continued to be ADA-positive during the subsequent year in OLE. The
incidence of positive ADA in the somatrogon group originally treated with Genotropin also
increased in the OLE, but it is unclear why subjects treated with Genotropin during the main
period had a lower incidence of seroconversion in OLE compared to somatrogon-treated
subjects. Somatrogon is nearly identical to the native hGH. Cross-reactivity of the ADA was
demonstrated with the endogenous protein. Samples positive in the ADA screening were further
tested in the confirmatory assay for anti-somatrogon antibody and if confirmed positive were
subsequently tested for CTP and hGH specificity (refer to Section 7.7.4 for discussion on anti-
CTP safety). In addition, confirmed anti-somatrogon Ab samples were tested for neutralizing
activity. Thus, ADA-positive subjects include those who also tested positive for NAb and/or
anti-CTP.

ADA that blocks somatrogon activity by directly blocking the binding of somatrogon to the hGH
receptor are called neutralizing antibodies (Nabs). NAb are a concern for long-term treatment
because anti-somatrogon NAbs may reduce both drug efficacy and endogenous hGH activity.
