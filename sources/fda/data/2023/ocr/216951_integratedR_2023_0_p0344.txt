14.6. Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic
Modeling Review

Executive Summary

The objective of this review is to evaluate the adequacy of the Applicant’s PBPK report
regarding evaluation of daprodustat’s (GSK1278863) drug-drug interaction with a moderate
CYP2C8 inhibitor clopidogrel using physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) modeling to
support the intended uses. Specifically, the Applicant applied the PBPK modeling approach to
assess the effects of clopidogrel on the exposure of daprodustat, following multiple dose
administration of daprodustat. The Division of Pharmacometrics has reviewed the PBPK report,
supporting modeling files, and the Applicant’s responses to FDA’s information request (IR)
submitted on July 15, 2022, and concluded that the effects of clopidogrel on the PK profiles of
daprodustat following multiple oral dose administration in healthy subjects were potentially
underpredicted because the clopidogrel PBPK model was not reliably validated.

Background

Daprodustat, a HIF PHI, is proposed for the treatment of anemia due to chronic kidney disease in
adults on dialysis and not on dialysis. The starting dose of daprodustat is based on the patient’s
dialysis status and hemoglobin level. For those switching from an erythropoiesis stimulating
agent (ESA) to JESDUVROQ, the dose is based on their existing ESA dose. For details of the
proposed dose regimen, please refer to the labeling of daprodustat. The dosage form of
daprodustat is a tablet with different strengths (1 mg, 2 mg, 4 mg, 6 mg, and 8 mg).
