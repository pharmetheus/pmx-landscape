Covariate Effect on PK Parameters

A Forest plot showing the covariate-parameter relationships of the final bimekizumab
population PK model is presented in Figure 22 to Figure 23, for CL/F, V/F and exposure metrics,
respectively. The plots are based on 200 bootstrap samples. Six bootstrap samples terminated
unsuccessfully due to rounding errors (NONMEM error code = 134) but were retained in all
calculations. The plots indicate that WT seems to be the most clinically significant covariate on
CL/F and V/F.
