Acid-reducing
Agent Effect

Repotrectinib is a neutral compound where pKa cannot be experimentally determined.
Solubility of repotrectinib at 37°C is pH independent ranging from 0.008 mg/mL at pH 1.2 to

0.006 at pH 7.4. Therefore, acid reducing agents are not expected to affect the bioavailability
of repotrectinib.
