NDA 216059 Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation
Jaypirca (pirtobrutinib)

Dimension Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and Reasons

e MCL is generally incurable and relapse is near universal
e Remission duration is generally longest after frontline therapy and
successively shortens with subsequent lines

MCL is a serious and life-threatening
condition

e Treatment options for relapsed or refractory MCL include covalent
BTK inhibitors (under accelerated approval), chemoimmunotherapy,
lenalidomide, bortezomib alone or in combination, and hematopoietic
stem cell transplantation (HSCT) in selected patients. Allogeneic HSCT
is potentially curative but generally limited to younger, fit patients

¢ BTK inhibitor resistance and intolerance are common and limit the
ability to retreat with a second covalent BTK inhibitor.

There is an unmet need for effective and
tolerable treatment options for patients
with relapsed or refractory MCL who have
received a prior BTK inhibitor

e Ina single-arm trial of pirtobrutinib monotherapy of 120 BTK
inhibitor-pretreated patients with MCL treated with pirtobrutinib
200mg daily, the IRC-assessed ORR was 50% (95% Cl: 41, 59) and CR
rate was 13% according to Lugano criteria. The estimated median
DOR was 8.3 months.

Based on an evaluation of response rate
and durability, pirtobrutinib demonstrates
an advantage over available therapies for
patients with relapsed or refractory MCL
with prior BTK inhibitor exposure

e In 583 patients with hematologic malignancies in the BRUIN trial, the
most common AEs (incidence >20%) were neutropenia, anemia,
thrombocytopenia, fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, lymphopenia,
bruising, and diarrhea.

e Events of special interest with pirtobrutinib include serious and
opportunistic infections; bleeding; grade 3-4 cytopenias; SPMs; and
atrial fibrillation or flutter.

e Duration of exposure was limited (median of 7.5 months in the pooled
safety population and 3.8 months in the MCL safety population).

The risks are acceptable in patients with
relapsed or refractory mantle cell
lymphoma who have an indication for
treatment

Precautions for include infections,
hemorrhage, cytopenias, atrial fibrillation
or flutter, and SPMs.
