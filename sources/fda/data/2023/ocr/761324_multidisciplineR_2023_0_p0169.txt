Adequacy of the safety database:

Data:

An integrated safety database was created to support the integrated safety analyses described
in this application.

The evaluation of safety is based on the primary safety analysis pool comprising 167 subjects
with LBCL from the pivotal GCT3013-01 trial, supplemented with safety data from the GCT3013-
04 trial. All subjects in the safety analysis pool were assigned to receive the 48 mg full dose and
received at least 1 dose of epcoritamab. The data cutoff date was 31 Jan 2022.

The Applicant's Position:
The safety database is considered adequate to evaluate the safety of epcoritamab in patients
with R/R LBCL after 2 or more lines of systemic therapy.

The FDA’s Assessment:
