Velsipity (etrasimod)

As mentioned above, in the phase 3 trials, etrasimod 2 mg QD was supported by a significantly
higher proportion of subjects achieving clinical remission at Week 12 and Week 52. Refer to
Section 8.1 for more details.

Exposure-Response Relationship

The exposure-response relationships for the observed rates of clinical remission at Week 12
and several Applicant-proposed secondary efficacy endpoints (i.e., endoscopic improvement,
symptomatic remission, and mucosal healing)? at Week 12 were explored using data from the
phase 2 trial APD334-003 and the phase 3 trials APD334-301 and APD334-302 in adult subjects
with UC using population PK/PD analysis, with individual PK parameter estimates from the final
PopPK model. The exposure-response relationships are presented in Figure 6.
