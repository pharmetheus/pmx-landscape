13.1.4. Other Toxicology/Specialized Studies

Phototoxicity: As sparsentan does not absorb light (between 290 nm and 700 nm) and no
sparsentan-derived radioactivity was noted in eye or in melanin-containing tissues in pigmented
tats, phototoxic potential is considered negligible and an in vivo phototoxicity study with
sparsentan was not required.
