Sensitivity analysis was conducted using only evaluable Hb data. The difference in response rate
(daprodustat-placebo) was 0.65 (95% CT: 0.58, 0.72; one sided p<0.0001). Results were
supportive of the analysis of proportion of participants with a >1 g/dL increase in Hb between
Baseline and over the EP (Weeks 24 to 28) using all Hb values.

The SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain was scored on a 0-100 scale (refer to Section 16.1 for more
details on the SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain). Daprodustat was superior to placebo in mean change
in SF-36 Vitality domain-score between Baseline and Week 28 with a mean difference of 5.4
(one-sided p=0.0005) (Table 60). Although statistical significance has been reached, this small
difference between the two amms raised a concern of clinical meaningfulness (refer to Section
6.3.2 for more details).
