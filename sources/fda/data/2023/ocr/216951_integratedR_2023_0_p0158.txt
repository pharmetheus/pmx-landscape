7.6.1.4, Deaths

Adjudicated causes of death in the On-Study analysis are summarized in Table 75. In the
ASCEND-ND study, all-cause mortality, a prespecified secondary endpoint, was similar between
arms (HR 1.03; CI 0.87, 1.20) (see Section 17 for survival curves). There were 301/1937 deaths
[8.3 deaths/100 PY] in the daprodustat arm and 298/1935 deaths [8.3 deaths/100 PY] in the
darbepoetin alfa arm. The incidence of death from adjudicated cardiovascular causes was higher
in subjects who received daprodustat. Death from undetermined cause was lower with
daprodustat.

Importantly, undetermined deaths need to be interpreted with caution — as many of these are
likely to be cardiovascular related. Thus, treatment effect estimates of adjudicated, cause specific
mortalities was interpreted cautiously. Overall, we did not observe meaningful trends in
mortality causes, and in aggregate, the rates of all-cause mortality are comparable between the
two treatment arms
