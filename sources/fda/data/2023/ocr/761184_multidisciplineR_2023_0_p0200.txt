13.1.3. Toxicology

Single-Dose Toxicology

Non-Good Laboratory Practices (GLP) single-dose toxicology studies were performed where
somatrogon was administered subcutaneously to rats (0, 3, 6, 36, or 180 mg/kg) and monkeys
(1.8 or 90 mg/kg). No adverse findings were noted.
