Lamzede (velmanase alfa-tycv)

Vital Signs

The review team focused on the following vital signs heart rates, and systolic and diastolic blood
pressures for patients in the treatment group. Of note oxygen saturation was not routinely
measured during velmanase administration. Because abnormalities in respiratory rate and body
temperature may be more reflective of hypersensitivity reactions (including anaphylaxis) and
IARs, discussion of these abnormalities when clinically significant is included in the adverse
event of special interest (AESI) narratives in Section 7.7.1.

At baseline, the mean systolic pressure, diastolic pressure, and pulse values were within normal
ranges for pediatric age group. No clinically meaningful changes in these vital signs were
observed over time.

The review team concluded vital signs should be monitored during infusions, especially due to
the risk for hypersensitivity reactions (including anaphylaxis) and ARs.
