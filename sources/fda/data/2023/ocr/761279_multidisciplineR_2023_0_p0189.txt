8.2.5.5. Treatment Emergent Adverse Events

An overview of treatment emergent adverse events (TEAEs) for study AMBG is shown in Table
58. Overall, the proportion of subjects with at least one TEAE was higher in the placebo group
(132 subjects, 68.8%) compared to the mirikizumab group (251 subjects, 64.5%). The majority
of the TEAEs were mild or moderate in severity. The proportion of subjects with a severe TEAE
was higher in the placebo group (12 subjects, 6.2%) compared to the mirikizumab group (16
subjects, 4.1%).

Table 58 summarize the common TEAEs reported at a frequency of at least 2% of subjects in
the mirikizumab group and greater than placebo group. The most commonly reported TEAEs
were upper respiratory infections, injections site reactions, arthralgia, headache, and rash.
