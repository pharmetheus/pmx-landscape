Immunogenicity Results

Serum samples collected from all subjects prior to dosing and at 14 days and 30 days following
MOD-4023 administration were negative for anti- MOD-4023 antibodies. Therefore,
immunogenicity was nota factor in evaluating PK and PD in this study.

Pharmacodynamic Results

on IGF-1 change from baseline concentrations in healthy volunteers following subcutaneous
administration of 4, 7, or 21 mg MOD-4023 are shown in Figure 14 and Table 48.
