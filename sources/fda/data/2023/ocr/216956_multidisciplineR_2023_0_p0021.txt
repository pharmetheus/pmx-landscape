NDA/BLA Multi-Disciplinary Review and Evaluation NDA 216956
Velsipity (etrasimod)

Dimension

Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and Reasons

@ In APD334-301, efficacy was demonstrated on the coprimary
endpoints of clinical remission (defined as a mMS score of 0 to 2 with
a stool frequency subscore [SFS] of 0 or 1, rectal bleeding subscore
[RBS] of 0, and ES of 0 or 1 [excluding friability]) at Week 12 and Week
52. The treatment difference in the proportion of subjects achieving
clinical remission between etrasimod and placebo was 20.1% (p
<0.0001) and 25.7% (p<0.0001) for Week 12 and Week 52,
respectively.

oO The coprimary endpoint results were further investigated in
subgroup analyses for subjects with prior biologic/JAK
inhibitor exposure. Efficacy results were consistent across
both subgroups. The treatment difference for clinical
remission between etrasimod and placebo was 15.1% and
16.4% for Week 12 and Week 52, respectively, in the prior
biologic/JAK inhibitor exposure subgroup.

O. The majority of the secondary endpoints were statistically
significant, including endoscopic improvement (Week 12 and
52), histologic-endoscopic mucosal improvement (Week 12
and 52), corticosteroid-free clinical remission (Week 52), and
maintenance of clinical remission (Week 52).

¢ In APD334-302, efficacy was demonstrated on the primary endpoint
of clinical remission at Week 12. The treatment difference in the
proportion of subjects achieving clinical remission between etrasimod
and placebo was 11.2% (p=0.010). Although the treatment difference
observed for APD334-302 was smaller than that observed for
APD334-301, baseline demographic and disease characteristics were
similar between APD334-301 and APD334-302. The difference
observed was attributed to the intrinsic variability of the placebo

treatment with etrasimod.
