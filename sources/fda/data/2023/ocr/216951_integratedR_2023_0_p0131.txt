ASCEND-ND

The proportion of participants stopping randomized treatment due to meeting rescue criteria was
2% in the daprodustat arm compared to 3% participants in the darbepoetin alfa arm. One subject
met the rescue criteria but did not discontinue randomized treatment (Table 63).

The daprodustat arm showed an observed reduced risk of stopping randomized treatment due to
meeting rescue criteria compared to the darbepoetin alfa arm (nominally significant one-sided
p=0.01) (Zable 64), but this endpoint was not pre-specified as a major efficacy endpoint under
the testing hierarchy and, therefore, was not included in the study-wise type I error control.
