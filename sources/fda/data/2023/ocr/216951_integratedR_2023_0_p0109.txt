Subgroup Analysis

Subgroup analyses, including age, sex, and race were performed for the primary ITT analysis of
post-randomization mean change in Hb between Baseline and over the EP (Weeks 28 to 52).
Subgroup analysis were performed using all available observed and imputed Hb values (on and
off-treatment). Rubin’s rules were used to combine results of the imputed datasets.

6.2.4.3. Results of Analyses

Patient Disposition

Participants were considered to have completed the study if they remained on study to Week 52
and completed the Week 52 visit irrespective of whether they discontinued randomized
treatment. Participants were also considered to have completed the study if they died while on
study. Of 312 participants in the ITT population, 306 (98%) participants completed the study to
Week 52 or died while on study. 2 (1%) participants in the daprodustat group and 4 (3%)
participants in the darbepoetin alfa group discontinued randomized treatment and withdrew from

the study (Table 40).
