The FDA’s Assessment:
The Applicant uses terminology such as “potent” to describe study results; such terms should
be avoided as they are vague, subjective, and promotional.

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s conclusions on the pharmacology of
repotrectinib, with additional pertinent details below. In vitro in enzymatic assays, repotrectinib
showed kinase inhibitory activity against wild type ROS1 and ALK and their mutants along with
wild type TRKA/B/C (Table 3). Repotrectinib bound to ROS1, ALK, TRKA, TRKB, and TRKC with Ka
values of 0.19, 5.7, 0.019, 0.054, and 0.088 nM, respectively.
