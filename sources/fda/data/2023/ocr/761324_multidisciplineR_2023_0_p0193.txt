Drug-induced Liver Injury

The FDA concurs with the Applicant in that 5 (3%) of the patients in the primary safety
population experienced AST or ALT >3x upper limit of normal (ULN) and total bilirubin >2xULN
within 30 days of the first dose of epcoritamab. The FDA independently confirmed that 4 of
these cases did not meet the formal definition of Hy’s law given concurrent elevation of serum
alkaline phosphatase >2x ULN, leaving one case that met criteria based on labs alone.
