Subgroup Analysis

Subgroup analyses, including age, sex, region, current ESA use, and race were performed. For
both the Hb change from baseline to Weeks 28 to 52 and the Hb responder during EP, the region
and current ESA use at randomization subgroups were conducted in a method similar to that
described for the primary and secondary ITT analyses.

6.2.2.3. Results of Analyses

Patient Disposition

Participants were considered to have completed the study if they completed all visits through the
End of Study visit irrespective of whether they discontinued randomized treatment. Participants
were also considered to have completed the study if they died while on study. Of 3872
participants in the ITT population, 3743 (97%) participants completed the study through the End
of Study visit or died while on study. A total of 64 (3%) participants in the daprodustat group
and 65 (3%) participants in the darbepoetin alfa group discontinued randomized treatment and
withdrew from the study (see Table 17). There were no differences between the treatment
groups in time to withdrawal from the study.
