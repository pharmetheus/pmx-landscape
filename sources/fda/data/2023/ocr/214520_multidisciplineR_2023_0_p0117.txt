Reviewer’s comment: Extensive revisions were made to Section 6 based on the Adverse Reactions Section of Labeling (final
guidance). Sections were added as discussed above to describe the demographics of the safety population, to report the deaths,
overall numbers of serious adverse reactions, and adverse reactions leading to discontinuation that were reported in each study
arm. The findings of the safety analysis and important safety signals that emerged were also reported in this section in Tables 1
and 2 of the labeling. The most important safety signal emerging in the Phase 3 trial was loss of catheter patency in the
Defencath arm and this is described in Table 2 of the labeling.
