8.2.4.1.2 Hemorrhage

In the pooled safety population, major bleeding, defined as serious or Grade 23 bleeding, or any
grade of CNS or retinal bleed, occurred in 2.7%, with gastrointestinal bleeding as the leading
site. Excluding Grade 2 nonserious retinal bleeding, this incidence was 2.4%.
