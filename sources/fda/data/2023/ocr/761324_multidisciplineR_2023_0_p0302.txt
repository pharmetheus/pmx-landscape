19.4.5.3. ER (safety) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The E-R safety analysis did not identify any safety issues with the proposed epcoritamab dosage
regimen. Patients with higher exposure were more likely to experience injection site reactions,
but no other E-R safety associations with higher Cycle 1 AUC were identified.
