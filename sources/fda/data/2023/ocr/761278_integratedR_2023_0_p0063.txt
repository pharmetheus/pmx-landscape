6.3. Key Efficacy Review Issues

6.3.1. Establishment of Efficacy in Trial rhLAMAN-05

Issue/Background

The single adequate and well-controlled clinical trial in the velmanase development program,
Trial rhLAMAN-05, assessed two primary endpoints, the change from baseline in a biomarker,
serum oligosaccharide levels, and the change from baseline in a clinical endpoint, the 3MSCT, as
well as two key secondary endpoints, the 6MWT and FVC%. Although the results for the serum
oligosaccharide biomarker endpoint were statistically significant, this surrogate biomarker has
not been validated for FDA regulatory purposes for AM or any other indication. Although the
results for the clinical endpoints (the 3MSCT, the 6MWT and FVC%) were considered clinically
