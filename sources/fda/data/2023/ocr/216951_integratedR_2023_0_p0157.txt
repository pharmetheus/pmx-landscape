7.6.1.3. Overview of Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events
Summary

Table 74 provides a frequency-based overview of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs)
reported in the safety population during the on-study period for ASCEND-ND. Based on the
frequency-based analysis, daprodustat is associated with a higher rate of AEs leading to
permanent discontinuation and AEs leading to dosage modification of study drug. However, the
clinical relevance of these treatment differences cannot be interpreted without more granular
data, which are presented and discussed in more detail in the next several sections. Otherwise,
there were no remarkable differences between study arms.
