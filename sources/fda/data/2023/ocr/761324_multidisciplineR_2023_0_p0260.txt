19.4.3. Bioanalytical Method

Two bioanalytical assays, namely, SMCIA-139 and ECLIA-139, were used to quantify
epcoritamab (free; not bound to CD3 and/or CD20) in human plasma for PK evaluation.

The SMCIA-139 method (quantitation range: 60-500 pg/mL) is a fluorescent sandwich
immunoassay technique while ECLIA-139 method (quantitation range: 10-1000 ng/mL) is an
electrochemiluminescence sandwich immunoassay (see Figure 18).
