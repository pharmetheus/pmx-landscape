Primary Efficacy Sensitivity Analysis

For the analysis of the co-primary efficacy endpoint, a multiple imputation approach was used to
handle missing Hb values. Missing scheduled Hb data were imputed based on the MAR
assumption. However, it is important to examine the sensitivity of inferences to departures from
the MAR assumption for the monotone missing data. A tipping point analysis was performed as
a sensitivity analysis for the assumptions of missing data, that is, to mimic MNAR in which the
missing observations depend on unobserved values. Results of the tipping point analysis suggest
that the extent of missing data would unlikely impact the conclusions of the co-primary efficacy

analysis.

Efficacy Results—Secondary Endpoints

A supportive analysis was performed for the primary ITT analysis of post-randomization mean
change in Hb between Baseline and over the EP, repeating the analysis using a shorter EP (Week
28 to 36). For each study the lower bound of the 95% CI for the treatment difference was greater
than the prospectively defined non-inferiority margin of -0.75 g/dL, thus supporting the overall
conclusion of non-inferiority of daprodustat (see Table 21). Results were consistent with the
conclusion from the primary analysis.
