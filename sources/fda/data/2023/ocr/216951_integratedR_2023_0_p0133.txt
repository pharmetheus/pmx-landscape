NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

6.3.2. Lack of Clinical Meaningfulness on Key
Secondary Endpoint of SF-26 V2.0 Vitality
Domain Score in ASCEND-NHQ

Issue

Lack of Clinical Meaningfulness on Key Secondary Endpoint of SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain
score in ASCEND-NHQ.

e Sub-Issue 1: The Applicant’s proposed 6-point improvement threshold in the 0-100
scaled SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain score did not represent a clinically meaningful within-
patient improvement.

e Sub-Issue 2: A meaningful treatment effect was not demonstrated upon review of the data
from the secondary PRO endpoint of change from baseline to Week 28 in the 0-100
scaled SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain score.

Background

Sub-Issue J

The Applicant proposed a 6-point reduction (improvement) in the 0-100 scaled SF-36 v2.0
Vitality domain score as a clinically meaningful within-patient change based on literature review
and distribution-based and anchor-based methodologies. During the IND phase (IND 101291),
we had advice regarding the Applicant’s approach to deriving the threshold(s) for meaningful
within-patient score change for the SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain (i.e., identified several limitations
with using a literature review to derive threshold(s) for meaningful within-patient score change,
recommended using an anchor-based analysis supplemented with both empirical cumulative
distribution function [eCDF] and probability density function [PDF] curves). Ultimately, we
concluded at that time that there was insufficient evidence to support that a 6-point improvement
in the SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain score is a meaningful within-patient change. For additional
details, refer to the Final Written Response dated December 4, 2019.

Sub-Issue 2

Although statistically significant results were reported on the key secondary endpoint of change
from baseline to Week 28 in the 0-100 scaled SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain score, the difference
between the daprodustat and placebo means was small. Minimal magnitude of changes was
observed at the item- and domain-level of the SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain-score, using both raw
and transformed score scales.

Assessment

Sub-Issue J

As part of the NDA, the Applicant submitted the “Patient-Reported Outcome Evidence Dossier
for SF-36 Vitality domain and CKD-AQ in anemia due to Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)”
Version 4.0 dated August 31, 2021 (hereafter referred to as the SF-36 PRO Dossier), which
included findings from the literature review and anchor-based analyses.
