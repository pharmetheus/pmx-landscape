Of the 2964 participants in the ITT population, 1390 (47%) completed randomized treatment.
The proportion of participants who discontinued randomized treatment was high and similar in
the daprodustat group [785 (53%) participants] compared to rhEPO [781 (53%) participants]. Of
the participants who discontinued randomized treatment 114 (8%) in the daprodustat group and
119 (8%) in the rhEPO group died while on randomized treatment (see Table 10). The primary
reasons for discontinuation were similar in both treatment groups and were mostly due to adverse
events or reaching protocol-defined stopping criteria.
