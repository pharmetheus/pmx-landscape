Assay Method Validation Report 179714 — Determination of LY3074828 [mirikizumab] in Human Serum
by ELISA ( me)

The assay method is based on binding of mirikizumab to IL-23 that has been immobilized onto a
microtiter plate. After blocking un-adsorbed sites, analyte in the samples, including standards,
quality control samples (QCs), and trial samples, are dispensed onto the plate and incubated. A
mouse anti-human IgG4 horseradish peroxidase (HRP) conjugate is added. The reaction is
initiated via the addition of tetramethylbenzidine, which leads to the development of color
proportional to the amount of mirikizumab present. Runs are read on a plate reader at 450 nm
(detection) and 620 nm (background), and concentrations are determined on a standard curve.
