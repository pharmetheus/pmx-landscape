The proportion of participants with a first occurrence of RBC or whole blood transfusion during
the on-treatment time period was similar in the daprodustat and darbepoetin alfa groups.
Participants on daprodustat had a numerically lower risk for time to first occurrence of receiving
an on-treatment RBC or whole blood transfusion compared to darbepoetin alfa, but the reduction
in risk was not statistically significant (Table 50).
