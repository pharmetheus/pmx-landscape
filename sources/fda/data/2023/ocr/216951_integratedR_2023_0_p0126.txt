on-treatment analysis using only evaluable Hb values and a post-randomization (on- and off-
treatment) observed case analysis. Results were consistent with the superiority conclusion from
the primary analysis (p value <0.0001) (see Table 58).

Based on the disposition distribution, the placebo arm had higher dropout as compared to the
treatment arm. Thus, we were concerned that the MAR might not be valid. To study the
robustness of the primary efficacy results, we requested that the Applicant perform a tipping
point analysis. This analysis showed that the extent of missing data is unlikely to have had an
impact on the conclusions of the primary Hb analysis and SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain analysis.
