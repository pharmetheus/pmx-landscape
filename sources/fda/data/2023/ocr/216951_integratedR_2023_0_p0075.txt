In ASCEND-ND, subjects were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to either oral daprodustat or to
darbepoetin alfa SC. Daprodustat was dosed daily, while the control darbepoetin alfa was dosed
weekly, every 2 weeks, or every 4 weeks, depending on the subject’s prior ESA use.

An anemia rescue algorithm, which included a provision for the use of intravenous iron, an RBC
transfusion, or both, was provided in the protocols (details are provided in Section 16.4).
Additionally, the protocol required subjects to be iron replete throughout the study — and initiate
iron therapy (investigator’s choice) if ferritin was less than or equal to 100 ng/mL, transferrin
saturation was less than or equal to 20%, or both.

Study Population Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria

Inclusion Criteria

The study recruited male and female (non-pregnant, non-lactating) participants, >18 years to <99
years of age with anemia associated with CKD not on dialysis (Kidney Disease Outcomes
Quality Initiative [KDOQI] stages 3, 4, or 5 defined by eGFR using the CKD Epidemiology
Collaboration [CKD-EPI] formula) and who were either not using ESAs or were current ESA
users:

e Group | (not using ESAs): No ESA use within the 6 weeks prior to screening and no
ESA use between screening and randomization (Day 1).

e Group 2 (ESA users): Use of any approved ESA for the 6 weeks prior to screening and
continuing between screening and randomization.

Exclusion Criteria

¢ On dialysis or clinical evidence of impending need to initiate dialysis within 90 days after
study start (Day 1).
Planned kidney transplant within 52 weeks
