The Applicant's Position:

In pivotal Cohort A, elranatamab administered at the RP2D (b) (4)

met the primary endpoint with an ORR of 61.0% (95% Cl: 51.8, 69.6)
(Table 38). A consistent ORR benefit as assessed by BICR was observed across prespecified
subgroups; however, ORR interpretation in some subgroups was limited by small sample size.
Although differences in ORR were observed in participants with poor prognostic features,
including EMD at baseline, R-ISS Stage III disease, and penta-refractory disease, the ORR in these
subgroups was clinically meaningful (Figure 9).

In Cohort B, a clinically meaningful efficacy was observed in a heavily pretreated population
(median of 7.5 prior lines of therapy) and met the primary endpoint with an ORR of 34.4% (95% Cl:
22.9, 47.3).
