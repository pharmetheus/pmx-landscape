7.6.1. Safety Results, ASCEND-ND
7.6.1.1. MACE

7.6.1.1.1. Overview

Results for the pre-specified primary analysis of time to first MACE are shown in Table 71. The
HR (95% CI) from the primary analysis (on-study analysis) for time to first MACE was 1.03
(0.89, 1.19) comparing daprodustat to darbepoetin alfa. The upper bound of the 95% CI was
lower than the prespecified risk margin of 1.25, i.e., the study ruled out a relative risk for MACE
greater than 1.25. Kaplan-Meier survival curves for MACE overlapped throughout the follow-up
period (Figure 27). The IRD was 0.2 events/100 PY (95% CI -1.3, 1.8) between daprodustat and
darbepoetin alfa. The incident events of the MACE (daprodustat 378/1937 [10.9 events/100 PY],
darbepoetin alfa 371/1935 [10.6 events/100 PY]) composite endpoint comprised mostly all-cause
mortality (daprodustat 252/378, darbepoetin alfa 259/371), followed by nonfatal MI (daprodustat
96/378, darbepoetin alfa 91/371), and nonfatal stroke (daprodustat 30/378, darbepoetin alfa
21/371).

Because both co-primary endpoints (mean change in Hb and time to first MACE) ruled out their
respective risk margins, the principal secondary endpoints were tested for superiority by
comparing the one-sided superiority p-value against the rank-associated alpha derived from the
Holm-Bonferroni method. None of the principal secondary endpoints (MACE, MACE or
thromboembolic events, MACE or HHF, progression of CKD) were significant (results not
shown).
