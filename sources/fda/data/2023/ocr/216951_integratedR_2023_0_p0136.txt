The Applicant additionally provided the response rate of patients achieving different levels of
improvement for each arm, and the difference in response rates between arms of ASCEND-
NHQ. Table 65 shows the Applicant’s post-hoc responder analysis results using different cutoffs.
At the thresholds of >12, >18, and >21, the treatment difference was 12%, 8% and 6%,
respectively, and the corresponding 95% confidence intervals include 0 at the thresholds of >18
and >21. These modest to small treatment differences were not considered as representative of a
clinically meaningful improvement to patients.
