Study Design

This was a 28-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, multi-center
Phase 3 study in participants with anemia associated with CKD who were not on dialysis. This
study enrolled participants who had a Hb of 8.5 to 10.0 g/dL, inclusive, as measured by a point-
of-care Hb analyzer (HemoCue). These participants had limited history of IV iron administration
and were rhEPO naive prior to Screening and Randomization.

The study included a 4-week Screening period, a 28-week Treatment period, and a 4-week
Follow-up period (Figure 20). The total duration of study participation for each participant was
approximately 36 weeks. Rescreening was allowed up to 2 additional times.

Participants were randomized 1:1 to receive either daprodustat or matching placebo tablets.
Daprodustat and placebo doses for participants were titrated based upon HemoCue Hb. Dose
modifications for these participants followed a protocol-specified dose adjustment algorithm to
achieve and maintain Hb within the target range of 11.0 to 12.0 g/dL, inclusive. Dose changes
were made programmatically in a blinded fashion by the IRT system in order to ensure that
investigators and participants stayed blinded to the study treatment and the dose adjustments.

A rescue algorithm was provided to minimize participants having an inadequate response to the
treatment for their anemia for an extended period of time and to enable rescue therapy to be
provided to the participants based on local clinical practice (Section 16.4).

A Clinical Events Committee adjudicated any event suspected to be MACE.

Study Population

Participants were eligible for inclusion in this study only if all of the protocol-specified criteria
applied at Screening (Week -4 and Week -2) and Randomization (Day 1), unless otherwise
