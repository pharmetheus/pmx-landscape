The proportion of participants with a first occurrence of RBC or whole blood transfusion during
the on-treatment time period was an exploratory endpoint and was numerically lower in the
daprodustat group compared with darbepoetin. Participants on daprodustat had a numerically
lower risk for time to first occurrence of receiving an on-treatment RBC or whole blood
transfusion compared to darbepoetin and no statistically testing was performed (Table 25).
