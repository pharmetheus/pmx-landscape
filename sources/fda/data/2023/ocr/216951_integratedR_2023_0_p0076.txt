6.2.2.2. Statistical Analysis Plan

Sample Size Determination

The size of this event driven trial was based on the co-primary CV safety objective and
determined by a fixed event target of 664 adjudicated first MACE. This provided approximately
90% power to establish non-inferiority assuming a true underlying 3% lower relative risk of
MACE in favor of daprodustat compared to darbepoetin alfa (i.e., HR=0.97) and 82% power for
the pre-specified non-inferiority margin of 1.25 under the assumption that the true underlying
tisk of MACE was the same in both arms (i.e., HR=1.00).

For 90% power to test the co-primary non-inferiority Hb hypothesis, with a two-sided 95% CI,
pre-specified margin of -0.75 g/dL and a two-sample t-test, a total of 86 evaluable participants
per treatment group was required. The planned study size far exceeded this requirement and
provided more than 99% power for non-inferiority and a high level of precision to estimate the
treatment effect (two-sided 95% CI half width of 0.105 g/dL, assuming 30% of participants
would be non-evaluable for efficacy). The largest (most negative) difference between arms that
would meet the statistical criterion for non-inferiority would be -0.645 g/dL. If the two-sided
95% CI is completely negative (i.e., lies fully within the range -0.75 to <0 g/dL) non-inferiority
would still be concluded.

Interim Analysis

There were no prospectively defined interim analyses planned to stop the study early for benefit.
While the planned futility analysis described below had a small impact on reducing study-wise
Type I error rate, there were no plans to adjust the alpha level used for the final analysis.
