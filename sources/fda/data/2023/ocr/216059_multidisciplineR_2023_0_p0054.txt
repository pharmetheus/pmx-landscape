Study title/study number: In Vitro human lymphocyte micronucleus assay with mechanistic

fluorescent in situ hybridization (FISH) Analysis of Micronuclei/LOXO-305-TOX-014 (GLP
compliant)

Key findings:

e  Pirtobrutinib induced increases in the number of micronucleated (MN) cells at
concentrations of 12 and 16 pg/mL without S9 activation following the exposure to
pirtobrutinib for 24 hours.

e@ The use of FISH with pan-centromeric DNA probes demonstrated that micronuclei were
generated via a predominantly aneugenic mechanism at 12 and 16 pg/mL.

Test system: Human lymphocyte cells; pre-treatment with phytohemagglutinin (PHA) for
mitogen stimulation 48 hours prior to pirtobrutinib treatment; exposure to pirtobrutinib of 24
hours without $9 activation; fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) with pan-centromeric DNA
probes
