Ngenla (somatrogon)

Benefit

e The first approved hGH formulations
were studied in clinical trials until final
adult height was achieved and
demonstrated that improved annualized
height velocity (AHV) was sustained over
the years of treatment and ultimately
improved/normalized final adult height.

e AHV at Week 52 was the primary
endpoint in Trial CP-4-006. The effect of
somatrogon on AHV at the end of 12-
month treatment was noninferior to the
effect of Genotropin (somatropin daily
injection): the mean treatment difference
between somatrogon and Genotropin
was 0.34 cm/year (95% confidence
interval -0.23, 0.91). Somatrogon was not
superior to Genotropin (lower limit of 95%
confidence interval was less than zero).

e Similar changes in increase of mean
height standard deviation score (HT
SDS) at 12 months were observed in
somatrogon and Genotropin groups: 0.91
and 0.86, respectively.

e The proportion of subjects who
underwent dose reduction during Study
CP-4-006 was higher in the somatrogon
group (24/109 subjects) than in the
Genotropin group (9/105 subjects ). Of the
24 subjects in somatrogon group, 12 had
a dose according to pre-specified criteria
of 2 consecutive measurements of IGF-1
SDS >2. Dose reductions were
temporary, except for 3 subjects whose
changes were close to the end of the
study. The IGF-1 SDS levels decreased
in all subjects following dose reduction.
There were 5 subjects in the somatrogon
group and 2 in the Genotropin group who
had dose reductions due to adverse
events and not related to IGF-1.
