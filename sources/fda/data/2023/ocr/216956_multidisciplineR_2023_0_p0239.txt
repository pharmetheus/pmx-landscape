16.4. Additional Efficacy Information
16.4.1. Multiple Testing Procedure

A prespecified gatekeeping multiple testing approach was implemented to control the overall
Type | error rate at 2-sided alpha of 0.05 for all co-primary and multiplicity-controlled
secondary endpoints for APD334-301 and APD334-302, as shown in Figure 29 and Figure 30,
respectively. First, the whole alpha was spent on testing family 1 (F1) consisting of the co-
primary endpoints. If both null hypotheses in F1 were rejected, the testing with the whole alpha
proceeded to F2. Truncated Hochberg procedure with a truncation fraction 0.8 was
implemented for testing hypotheses in F2. Since all null hypotheses in F2 were rejected, the
whole alpha was spent on testing F3 based on a traditional Hochberg procedure. The Hochberg
method may not control type | error when endpoints within a family are negatively correlated;
however, given the efficacy results for these studies, the review team is not concerned with
potential type | error inflation in this instance.
