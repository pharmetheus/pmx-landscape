The Applicant's Position:

There were no intrinsic patient factors identified which, when accounting for the wide
therapeutic window of pirtobrutinib in patients with MCL, necessitate a dose adjustment or
management strategy. The effect of moderate and severe hepatic impairment is currently being
studied.
