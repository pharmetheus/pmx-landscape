5.5.5. Other Toxicology Studies

The Applicant's Positiot

Repotrectinib was not phototoxic in an in vivo rat study in Long-Evans pigmented rats up to and
including the limit dose of 1000 mg/kg for three consecutive days followed by a single exposure
to UVR approximately 4 hours postdose.
