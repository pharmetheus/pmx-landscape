Pharmacokinetic Results

When the small number of subjects and associated variability are taken into account, there does
not appear to be a difference between Japanese and Caucasian subjects with respect to PK
parameters for the 2.5 mg and 7.5 mg cohorts (Figure 16 and Table 50). For the 15 mg cohort,
the geometric mean Cmax Was lower and the median Tinax was longer in Japanese compared to
Caucasian subjects although the geometric mean values for AUC (ng were comparable, possibly
suggesting a slower rate of absorption.

For both Caucasian and Japanese subjects, there was a greater than proportional increase in
AUC fap with dose.
