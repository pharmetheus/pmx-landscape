14.4.5. Impact of Immunogenicity on Safety

Among 5 subjects in studies rhLAMAN- 10 and rhLAMAN-08 who were reported with IARs,
four subjects ( OO and a) were ADA positive (80%) and one subject (ia)
was ADA negative (20%).

In Trial rhLAMAN-05, mild to moderate [ARs occurred in a higher incidence in ADA positive
patients compared to ADA negative patients. Two subjects who had developed severe

hypersensitivity was ADA positive and one of the subjects developed highest levels of ADA.
One ADA negative patient developed anaphylactic type reaction.

In Trial rhLAMAN-08, 2 out of 4 ADA positive pediatric patients experienced mild to moderate
IARs. Among these 2 patients, one patient experienced an anaphylactic reaction during treatment
