1.1 Regulatory History

The Applicant, Pfizer, Inc. (Arena Pharmaceuticals was acquired by Pfizer Inc. in 2022),
submitted a Type B pre-IND meeting request on January 29, 2015 (IND 125154, SDN
2). No nonclinical questions were included in this meeting; however, preliminary
comments from the Agency included recommendations to conduct a study in an animal
model of inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) to show the potential effectiveness of
etrasimod in the intended study population and to conduct a fertility and early embryonic
development (FEED) study prior to the initiation of the proposed phase 2 clinical trial.
The Applicant addressed these comments in a subsequent submission (SDN 6; May 4,
2015). The Applicant stated that a study in an animal model of IBD would be conducted
concurrently with clinical development. Additionally, the Applicant referenced previously
submitted 3-month toxicology studies in rats and dogs ]
and indicated that these data would be supplemented with a study of sperm
morphology and motility to adequately address all FEED study endpoints prior to the
initiation of the phase 2 clinical study. The final report of a 28-day study of the effects of
