NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

Key Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria

Inclusion Criteria

Male or female participants >18 years of age, at the time of signing the informed consent.
CKD confirmed at Screening: Kidney Disease Outcomes Quality Initiative (KDOQI)
CKD stages 3, 4, or 5 defined by estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) using the
CKD Epidemiology Collaboration (CKD-EPI) formula.

Stable HemoCue Hb from 8.5 to 10.5, inclusive, at the Screening visit (Week -4) and
from 8.5 to 10.0 g/dL, inclusive, at Randomization (Day 1).

Treatment with up to one IV iron dose within the 8 weeks prior to Screening and no IV
iron use between the Screening visit and Randomization (Day 1). If needed, stable oral
iron supplementation was permitted (<50% change in overall dose maintenance and no
change in type of iron prescribed doses in the 4 weeks prior to Day 1 Randomization
visit).

Exclusion Criteria

Dialysis or clinical evidence of impending need to initiate dialysis within 180 days after
Randomization (Day 1).

Planned living-related or living-unrelated kidney transplant within 28 weeks after
Randomization (Day 1).

Low values of ferritin or transferrin saturation (TSAT) at Screening.

History of rhEPO or rhEPO analogue use within the 8 weeks prior to Screening and
thEPO use between Screening and Randomization (Day 1).

History of transfusion within the 8 weeks prior to Screening and transfusion between
Screening and Randomization (Day 1).

History of bone marrow aplasia or pure red cell aplasia.

Megaloblastic anemia (untreated pernicious anemia and folate deficiency), thalassemia
major, sickle cell disease, or myelodysplastic syndrome.

Evidence of actively bleeding gastric, duodenal, or esophageal ulcer disease or clinically
significant GI bleeding <8 weeks prior to Screening through Randomization (Day 1).
QTcB >500 msec or QTcB >530 msec in participants with Bundle Branch Block. There
was no QTc exclusion for participants with a predominantly paced rhythm.

For participants residing in Italy, QTcB exclusion criteria were replaced with QTcF
criteria: QTcF >470 ms (females) and >450 ms (males) pre-dose prior to Randomization
(Day 1) and presence of second or third degree AV block.

Malignancy within 2 years of Screening through Randomization (Day 1), currently
receiving treatment for cancer, or had a known complex kidney cyst >3 cm.

Any prior treatment with daprodustat for >30 days.

Ferric citrate use within 4 weeks prior to Randomization (Day 1).

Significant CV or hepatic disease.
