Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

Sensitivity Analyses and Supportive Analyses for Hemoglobin Change From Baseline

Additional supportive analyses included an on-treatment analysis using only evaluable Hb
values, a per-protocol analysis using only evaluable Hb values, analysis with a shorter EP
(Weeks 28 to 36), and an observed case analysis (on- and off- treatment). Results were consistent
with the non-inferiority conclusion from the primary analysis (See Section 16.1.1)

Results of the tipping point analysis suggest that the extent of missing data would unlikely
impact the conclusions of the primary efficacy analysis. Therefore, missing data in these studies
are unlikely to have had an impact on the primary results.

Efficacy Results—Key Secondary Endpoints

Daprodustat did not demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in average monthly IV iron
dose from baseline to Week 52 compared to rhEPO (see Table 13). The observed p-value was
greater than the rank-associated alpha threshold as prescribed by the Holm-Bonferroni
adjustment.
