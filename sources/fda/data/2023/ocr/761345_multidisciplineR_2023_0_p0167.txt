Incidence and Severity of Neurologic Toxicity

Using the above methodology, FDA analysis of neurologic toxicity showed that neurologic TEAEs
occurred in 59% of patients who received the recommended dosing regimen, with Grade 3-4
neurologic AEs occurring in 7.1%. FDA also notes that one patient treated with the recommended

dosing regimen experienced Grade 3 Guillain-Barre Syndrome. FDA’s analysis of neurologic toxicity
is provided in Table 62 below.
