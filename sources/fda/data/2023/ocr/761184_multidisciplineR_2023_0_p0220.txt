Open-Label Extension Periods

Summaries of IGF-1 SDS at the end of Period III (Year 1 OLE) and Periods IV to V (Years 2-4
and PEN) are provided in Table 59 and Table 60, respectively. The observed mean IGF-1 SDS
was lowest for the 0.25 mg/kg/week treatment group at the end of Year 1. The mean IGF-1 SDS
for all cohorts in Period III and subjects in Year 2 were similar and increased at Year 3 and the
end of Period V (PEN). Mean IGF-1 SDS values were consistently within the target therapeutic
range and remained below 2 SDS across all time points in Periods III to V.
