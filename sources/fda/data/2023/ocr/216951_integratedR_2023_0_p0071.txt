The proportion of participants with a first occurrence of RBC or whole blood transfusion during
the on-treatment time period was an exploratory endpoint and was numerically lower in the
daprodustat group compared with rhEPO. Participants on daprodustat had a numerically lower
risk for time to first occurrence of receiving an on-treatment RBC or whole blood transfusion
compared to rhEPO and no statistically testing was performed.

Blood transfusion events to Week 52 are summarized in Table 16. The proportion of participants
who received RBC or whole blood transfusions was similar between the treatment groups.
