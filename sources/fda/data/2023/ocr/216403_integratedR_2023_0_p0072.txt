The subject was a 42-year-old white male with FSGS. At baseline, his ALT was 11 U/L, AST
was 20 U/L, and ALP and TB were “normal.” He started sparsentan on oe) - On

© (Day 83), his ALT increased to 64 U/L and AST increased to 45 U/L. ALP and

on alcohol use was not provided. Assessment of CMV, EBV, or HEV was not performed. The
patient remained asymptomatic throughout.

DHN Assessment

This case is thought to be probably due to sparsentan given the resolution after discontinuation of
sparsentan and no obvious alternate etiology.
