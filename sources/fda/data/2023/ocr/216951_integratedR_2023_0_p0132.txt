Conclusion

The above results support the non-inferiority conclusion of the efficacy of daprodustat on Hb.
We expect the similarity in Hb response between daprodustat and the ESA control arms to
translate into similar reductions in the need for RBC transfusions, an accepted clinical benefit for
treatments for anemia of CKD, because of avoiding the procedure and its attendant risks.
