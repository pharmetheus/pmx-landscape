NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

Daprodustat’s evidence of safety and effectiveness for the treatment of anemia due to CKD in
adults is based primarily on ASCEND-ND and ASCEND-D, which are event-driven,
multicenter, multinational, open-label, active-controlled, randomized, parallel-group trials.

ASCEND ND assessed the efficacy and safety of daprodustat once daily compared to
darbepoetin alfa in the NDD population. Subjects not currently receiving ESAs and subjects
currently receiving ESAs were included. Those on an ESA continued their ESA therapy during
the screening and run-in periods.

ASCEND-D assessed the efficacy and safety of daprodustat once daily compared to intravenous
(IV) epoetin alfa in subjects on hemodialysis and compared to SC darbepoetin alfa in subjects on
peritoneal dialysis. Subjects switched their ESA to these study drugs on the day of
randomization.

The following three additional smaller, fixed duration studies are considered supportive for
efficacy and safety:

e ASCEND-NHQ was a double-blind, placebo-controlled study in a non-dialysis
population with anemia with objectives of providing evidence of efficacy, safety, and
improved quality of life versus placebo.

e ASCEND ID was a 1-year, open-label, active-controlled study that evaluated subjects
with anemia associated with CKD who were not regularly using an ESA and who were
initiating dialysis (unplanned or planned) to provide evidence for the efficacy and safety
of daprodustat in the incident dialysis population.

e ASCEND-TD was a 1-year, double-blind, active-controlled study that enrolled subjects
who were hemodialysis dependent (hemodiafiltration and hemofiltration with
hemodialysis) who had anemia of CKD and were being treated with ESAs or its
analogues to provide support for a three-times a week dosing regimen.

6.2.1. Study 200807/ASCEND-D

6.2.1.1. Design

Overview

ASCEND-D (NCT02879305): The Applicant conducted a randomized, Phase 3 Study,
200807/ASCEND-D, titled “A phase 3 randomized, open-label (sponsor-blind), active
controlled, parallel-group, multi-center, event driven study in dialysis subjects with anemia
associated with chronic kidney disease to evaluate the safety and efficacy of daprodustat
compared to recombinant human erythropoietin, following a switch from erythropoietin-
stimulating agents.”

Coprimary Safety and Efficacy Endpoint

Safety: Time to first occurrence of adjudicated MACE (composite of all-cause mortality,
nonfatal MI, and nonfatal stroke)

Efficacy: Mean change in Hb between Baseline and Evaluation Period (EP) (Weeks 28 to 52)
