Efficacy Results—Primary Endpoint

Mean Hb at Baseline and over the EP was similar between treatment groups and remained within
the Hb analysis range (10 to 11.5 g/dL) during the EP (Weeks 28 to 52) (Figure 17). Also, mean
Hb was below the analysis range at baseline and increased during the first 12 weeks of
randomized treatment (daprodustat or darbepoetin alfa). Thereafter Hb remained stable and
within the analysis range (10 to 11.5 g/dL) through Week 52.
