7.6.1.2.3. Hospitalization for Heart Failure Subgroup
Analyses

Exploratory subgroup analyses by history of heart failure (Yes, No) showed a higher incidence
of HHF in the daprodustat arm compared to the control arm in the subgroup with a history of
heart failure (Table 73). The HR for the subgroup with history of heart failure was 1.51 (95% CI
1.01, 2.25). In contrast, the HR for the subgroup without a history of heart failure was 0.99 (95%
CI 0.72, 1.36).
