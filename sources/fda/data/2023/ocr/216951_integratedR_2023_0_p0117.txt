Efficacy Results—Other Subgroups

Overall, the results of the subgroup analyses were consistent with the primary analysis with little
or no heterogeneity between subgroups. The adjusted mean differences were consistently >--0.75
for most of the subgroups except for Age >75 and Black or African American (see Figure 19).
Because of small sample sizes in these subgroups, which is reflected by their very large
confidence intervals, results should be interpreted with caution.
