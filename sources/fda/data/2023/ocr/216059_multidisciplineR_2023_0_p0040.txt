Distribution of [**C]pirtobrutinib-related material to the testis and regions of the brain was

The in vitro and in vivo metabolism studies indicated the primary clearance pathways of
pirtobrutinib in rats involved biliary elimination, oxidation, direct glucuronidation, direct
sulfation, and oxidation with glucuronidation. In vitro studies in human hepatocytes indicated
that the metabolic pathways were mainly via CYP3A4 and 3A5, and were consistent with
hydroxylation, N-glucuronidation, O-demethylation followed by glucuronidation, and
hydroxylation followed by glucuronidation. The in vitro human metabolites were detected in
the rat and/or dog. The most abundant metabolite circulating in human plasma, M1 (at
approximately 87%), is present in rat plasma.
