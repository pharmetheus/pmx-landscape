Sensitivity Analysis

For the analysis of the Hb co-primary efficacy endpoint, a multiple imputation approach was
used to handle missing Hb values. Missing scheduled Hb data were imputed based on the MAR
assumption. However, it is important to examine the sensitivity of inferences to departures from
the MAR assumption for the monotone missing data. A tipping point analysis was performed as
a sensitivity analysis for the assumptions of missing data, that is, to mimic MNAR in which the
missing observations depend on unobserved values.

Results of the tipping point analysis suggest that the extent of missing data would unlikely
impact the conclusions of the primary/co-primary efficacy analysis. Therefore, missing data in
these studies are unlikely to have had an impact on the primary results.
