The only somatrogon dose level tested in the phase 3 study was
0.66 mg/kg/week.
The highest dose tested in pediatric patients with GHD was 0.66 mg/kg/week.

In pediatric patients with GHD, somatrogon exposure increases in a dose
proportional manner for doses of 0.25, 0.48, and 0.66 mg/kg/week.

There is no accumulation of somatrogon after once weekly administration.
Steady state is achieved with the first dose because there is no drug
accumulation with once weekly dosing
