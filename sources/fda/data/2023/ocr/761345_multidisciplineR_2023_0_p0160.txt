The Applicant’s Position:

As a CD3-targeting bispecific antibody, elranatamab stimulates cytokine release and CRS has been

identified as an ADR. ma)
The 12/32 priming dose regimen led to a predictable

CRS profile, with most events occurring after the first or second dose of elranatamab. CRS was

fully reversible and was managed with standard supportive care and in some cases with

tocilizumab and/or corticosteroids.

To mitigate risk of CRS, the proposed USPI will include a boxed warning and will provide guidance
on the appropriate use of elranatamab, and on monitoring and treatment of CRS. The Sponsor is
also proposing a REMS with ETASU to mitigate the risk of CRS and neurologic toxicity including
ICANS.
