Bioanalytical Methods for Immunogenicity

Immunogenicity samples were assessed using a tiered approach. For subjects receiving
somatrogon, samples were first screened for anti-somatrogon binding antibodies (ADA). Those
tested positive for anti-somatrogon ADA were then tested using competitive confirmatory assay
with somatrogon. Confirmed anti-somatrogon ADA-positive samples were assessed for
specificity using hGH and CTP, followed by titer and assessed for neutralizing (NAb) to both
somatrogon and hGH (Figure 59). For subjects randomized to Genotropin treatment group,
samples screened positive for anti-hGH ADA were then tested using confirmatory assay with
hGH. Confirmed anti-hGH ADA-positive samples were followed by titer and assessed for NAb
only to hGH.
