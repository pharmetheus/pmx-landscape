The nonserious treatment differences not favoring daprodustat of pruritis, dyspepsia, nausea, and
vomiting are reversible, showed small treatment differences (i-e., less than 2% difference in
proportion of subjects reported), and were generally manageable. Importantly, these adverse
events did not emerge as leading to treatment discontinuation. For these reasons, we felt that the
level of concern for these common AEs did not raise to a safety review issue (i.e., contribute
substantially to the benefit-risk assessment of the drug), but rather, could be labeled and
considered in individual patient/physician decision making.

There were no clinically significant differences between study arms in the remainder of the
TEAE groupings.
