No imbalance was noted between treatment groups with respect to other baseline comorbidities

penicillin, which are commonly used in this age population of GHD.

Efficacy Results
Main Period (12 Months)

The analyses of data demonstrated improvement in all mean (SD) growth parameters and
biomarkers at Week 52.

months of treatment (Table 18). The increase in AHV with somatrogon treatment was dose-
dependent. All somatrogon groups had numerically smaller AHV compared to the Genotropin

group.
