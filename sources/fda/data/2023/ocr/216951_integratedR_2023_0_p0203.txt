Conclusion

It is unclear whether the cardiovascular safety of daprodustat in the ID population more closely
resembles that of the DD population or the NDD population. The available cardiovascular safety
data from ASCEND ID are too limited to draw definitive conclusions. Exploratory analyses from
the subset of patients transitioning to dialysis during ASCEND-ND show some signals but also
have limitations that preclude definitive conclusions. Therefore, |

we cannot reliably differentiate these patients from the ND
population. With regard to initiating daprodustat after chronic dialysis has been started,
ASCEND-D enrolled patients who had been on dialysis for more than 90 days prior to the
screening visit, and were treated with daprodustat after a 4-week run-in period (totaling 118
days).
