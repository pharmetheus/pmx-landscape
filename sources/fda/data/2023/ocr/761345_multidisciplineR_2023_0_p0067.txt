elranatamab

Commonly found impurities include (m4)

. These impurities were spiked (levels of MMe) into elranatamab and
cytokine release was evaluated in an in vitro CRS assay using PBMCs. Elranatamab induced a
concentration-dependent increase in TNFa, IL2 and IFNy levels. Spiked impurities did not induce
significant increases in the cytokine levels compared to elranatamab alone.

In addition to the toxicology studies mentioned above, the Applicant also conducted an in vitro
CRA using whole blood from 8 healthy human donors. Elranatamab concentrations of 0.005,
0.5, and 50 pg/mL were incubated with whole blood samples for 24 hours and the release of
TNFa, IL6 and IFNy was evaluated. Elranatamab induced cytokine release primarily from
samples treated with concentrations of 0.5 and 50 pg/mL. IFNy release was detected in all
samples with 0.5 and 50 pg/mL elranatamab (402-2313 pg/mL and 719-3009 pg/mL,
respectively); 5 of the 8 donors had IFNy levels similar to or greater than levels elicited by the
lipopolysaccharide positive control. TNFa (ranged from below the lower limit of quantitation
up to 124 pg/mL) and IL6 levels (ranged from below the lower limit of quantifation up to 204
pg/mL) were detected from a subset of samples; TNFa and IL6 levels were lower than those
from the positive control.
