Of the 13 subjects with positive ADA at the end of OLE period while on treatment, 3 remained
ADA-positive after the drug discontinuation at the time of the post-OLE sample assessment. The
ADA titer in these 3 subjects was lower compared to the end of OLE period (Figure 4). The
median (range) time between the end of OLE and post OLE sampling time was 663 days (range
363 -1029 days). None of the 3 subjects who had positive ADA at the post OLE testing had
neutralizing antibodies.

Four out of the 13 subjects with positive ADA at the end of OLE period reported taking another
hGH product (Table 13).
