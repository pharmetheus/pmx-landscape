19.4.3.3 PPK Review Issues

The FDA’s Assessment:

Although there were some overprediction for elranatamab concentrations under 20 ng/mL, the
PopPK analysis is acceptable in general for describing the PK of elranatamab and deriving
exposure metrics for subsequent exposure-response analyses.
