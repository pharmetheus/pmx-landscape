Efficacy Results—Primary Endpoint

The mean Hb at Baseline and over the EP was similar between treatment groups and remained
within the Hb analysis range (10 to 11.5 g/dL) during the EP (Weeks 28 to 52) (Figure 4). Also,
following a switch to daprodustat or active control (epoetin alfa in hemodialysis participants) Hb
remained relatively stable and within the analysis range (10 to 11.5 g/dL) from Baseline through
Week 52.
