Efficacy Results—Subgroups

A subgroup analysis was performed for the primary ITT analysis of post randomization mean
change in Hb between Baseline and over the EP (Weeks 28 to 52) by ESA use at baseline. The
analysis by ESA use at Baseline had an interaction p-value of 0.0025, however, mean Hb
remained in the analysis range (10 to 11.5 g/dL) during the EP regardless of ESA use at Baseline

in both groups (see Table 27).
