1.2. Conclusions on the Substantial Evidence of Effectiveness

The FDA review team recommends accelerated approval of epcoritamab for the following
indication:

Epcoritamab is a bispecific CD20-direced CD3 T-cell engager indicated for the treatment of adult
patients with relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), not otherwise
specified, including DLBCL arising from indolent lymphoma, and high-grade B-cell lymphoma,
after two or more lines of systemic therapy.

This recommendation is based on the efficacy of epcoritamab from the pivotal Study GCT3013-
01, an open-label, multi-cohort, single-arm trial that included an expansion cohort of 157 adult
patients with relapsed or refractory large B-cell lymphoma who had received at least two prior
lines of systemic therapies, including an anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody.

Patients received epcoritamab subcutaneously in 28-day cycles, at step-up dosing in Cycle 1
(0.16 mg on Day 1, 0.8 mg on Day 8, and 48 mg on Day 15 and Day 22), 48 mg weekly in Cycles
2-4, 48 mg every other week in Cycles 5-9, and 48 mg every four weeks from Cycles 10
onwards. Epcoritamab was administered until progressive disease or unacceptable toxicity.
