Jesduvroq (daprodustat)

Study Design

Study 201410/ASCEND-ID was a Phase 3, randomized, open-label (sponsor blind), active-
controlled, parallel-group, multi-center study in participants with anemia associated with CKD
who were initiating dialysis. The study comprised 3 periods: a screening period of 2 weeks
(which could be extended by an additional 4 weeks for kidney ultrasound examination, IV iron
supplementation and/or vitamin B12 treatment as needed), a 52-week active treatment period,
and a follow-up period of 4 to 6 weeks (Figure 16).

Participants were stratified by dialysis type to either hemodialysis, or peritoneal dialysis and by
whether their dialysis start was planned or unplanned (urgent). Following stratification,
participants were randomized 1:1 to receive daprodustat or darbepoetin alfa. During the
treatment period, iron therapy was supplied and administered locally.

The treatment period consisted of:

e The SP, defined as the period from Day 1 to Week 28 during which randomized
treatment was dose titrated to achieve the appropriate Hb target of 10.0 tol1.0 g/dL.

e The EP, defined as the period from the end of the stabilization period (Week 28) to Week
52, to assess safety and efficacy. If required, randomized treatment could be dose titrated
to achieve or maintain Hb level in the target range.
