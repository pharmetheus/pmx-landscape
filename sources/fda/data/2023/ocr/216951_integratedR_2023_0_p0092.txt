6.2.3.2. Statistical Analysis Plan

Sample Size Determination

Approximately 402 participants were planned to be randomized in a 2:1 ratio to daprodustat TIW
or rhEPO (268 to the daprodustat TIW arm and 134 to the rhEPO arm), to provide at least 100
participants exposed to daprodustat for one year. Participants were treated to achieve and
maintain Hb between 10 and 11 g/dL. The expected difference in mean Hb change from baseline
and the EP, between arms, was 0 g/dL and the anticipated between participant standard deviation
(SD) was 1.5 g/dL, based on historical rhEPO trials and daprodustat clinical trial experience to
date. With a prespecified non-inferiority margin of -0.75 g/dL, a two-sample t-test and assuming
that up to approximately 10% of participants had no Hb values measured during the EP, 402
randomized participants would provide >90% power to test the primary hypothesis.

With 402 randomized participants, it was anticipated that the difference in mean Hb change from
baseline between arms would be estimated with a precision of 0.33 g/dL (half width of the two-
sided 95% CI) and the largest (most negative) difference between arms that would meet the
statistical criterion for non-inferiority would be -0.42 g/dL.

Interim Analysis

No interim analyses were performed.

Missing Data

For the analysis of the primary efficacy endpoint, a multiple imputation approach was used to
handle missing Hb values. Both intermittent missing and monotone missing scheduled Hb data
were imputed based on the MAR assumption. However, it is important to examine the sensitivity
of inferences to departures from the MAR assumption for the monotone missing data. A tipping
point analysis was performed as a sensitivity analysis for the assumptions of missing data, that is,
to mimic MNAR in which the missing observations depend on unobserved values.
