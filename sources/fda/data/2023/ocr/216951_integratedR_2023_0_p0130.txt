6.3. Key Efficacy Review Issues

6.3.1. Use of Rescue Therapy in the ASCEND Program

Issue

Impact of Rescue Therapy Use on the Non-Inferiority Efficacy Conclusion of Daprodustat

Background

Hb response and reduction in transfusion need have been accepted endpoints for establishing
clinical benefit in patients with anemia of CKD. The ASCEND-D and ASCEND-ND trials used
Hb response at the primary efficacy period of 28-52 weeks as the primary efficacy endpoint. The
pre-specified non-inferiority Hb comparisons used -0.75 g/dL as the non-inferiority margin,
which was appropriately justified based on previous applications for drugs intended to treat
anemia of CKD and statistical reasoning. An evaluation of the time to stopping randomized

treatment due to meeting rescue criteria was evaluated as a secondary efficacy endpoint in the
ASCEND-D and ASCEND-ND study to further evaluate the robustness of the efficacy results.

Assessment

ASCEND-D

The cumulative incidence for time to stopping randomized treatment due to meeting rescue
criteria was similar between the treatment groups (Table 61).
