The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the Applicant’s conclusions on the secondary pharmacology of
repotrectinib, with additional pertinent details below. The Cmax of unbound repotrectinib was
calculated by the FDA clinical and nonclinical team to be 0.092 uM at the recommended dose of
160 mg BID; thus, the inhibition of adenosine Aza receptor, Ca2* channel, and LCK were at
concentrations 68-, 38-, and 1.2-fold higher than the unbound Cmax in patients. Additional
secondary kinase targets inhibited by repotrectinib at clinically relevant concentrations are

Safety Pharmacology

Inhibition of the hERG potassium channel by repotrectinib was investigated using a stably
transfected CHO cell line expressing the hERG mRNA at concentrations up to 30 uM. The ICso
value for the inhibitory effect of repotrectinib on hERG potassium current was 18 4M, which
corresponds to a 249-fold multiple to the clinical mean Cmax of unbound repotrectinib

(0.072 LM). The inhibition of human Nav1.5 sodium channel and hCav1.2 calcium channel by
repotrectinib was minimal (< 15% and 35% inhibition, respectively, at 30 uM). ECG
