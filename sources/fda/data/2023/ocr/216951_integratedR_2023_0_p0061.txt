NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

Study Population Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria

Inclusion Criteria

The study recruited male and female (non-pregnant, non-lactating) participants, aged >18 years
to <99 years of age with anemia of CKD who were being treated with an ESA for at least the
6-weeks prior to screening and up to randomization. Participants were required to have been on
dialysis for greater than 90 days prior to screening and were either receiving hemodialysis

>2 times per week or peritoneal dialysis >5 times per week. Hb concentration (measured by
HemoCue) for inclusion are defined as follows:

e Week -8: Hb 8 to 12 g/dL (5 to 7.4 mmol/L); If Hb is 12.1 to 12.4 g/dL (7.5-7.7 mmol/L),
up to two retests are allowed; the retest value must be between 8 to 12 g/dL (5 to
7.4 mmol/L).

e Day 1: Hb 8 to 11 g/dL (5 to 6.8 mmol/L) and receiving at least the minimum ESA dose;
Hb >11 to 11.5 g/dL (6.8 to 7.1 mmol/L) and receiving greater than the minimum ESA
dose.

Exclusion Criteria

Planned kidney transplant within 52 weeks
Ferritin <100 ng/mL (<100 j1g/L) at screening
Transferrin saturation (TSAT) <20% at screening (If TSAT was 18-20%, a retest using a
new blood sample could be obtained within 7 days of the final laboratory report; the final
retest value must be >20% to confirm eligibility)

e History of bone marrow aplasia or pure red cell aplasia
Anemia other than anemia of CKD.
MI, acute coronary syndrome, stroke, or transient ischemic attack within 4 weeks prior to
screening through to randomization (Day 1)

¢ QTcB >500 msec, or QTcB >530 msec in participants with bundle branch block at
randomization (Day 1)
Chronic Class IV heart failure
Uncontrolled hypertension

e Prior (within 2 years) or current history of cancer (with the exception of localized
squamous cell or basal cell carcinoma of the skin), or complex kidney cyst (e.g., Bosniak
Category II F, III or IV) >3 cm.; current unstable liver or biliary disease or known hepatic
or biliary abnormalities; or gastrointestinal bleeding.

e Participants requiring treatment with strong inhibitor of CYP2C8 or a strong inducer of
CYP2C8 were excluded.
