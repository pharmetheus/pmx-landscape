PTA Analyses at the Initially Proposed Dosage Adjustments

Using the PK/PD targets of SUL (%fT > MIC of 50%) and DUR (fAUC:MIC ratio of 10)
associated with a 1-log;9 colony-forming units (CFU) reduction, PTA analyses based on free-
drug plasma concentration or total-drug concentration in ELF was performed at the initially
proposed dose regimens (Table 34) in the simulated subjects across various renal function
categories and global surveillance MIC distribution. As shown in Figure 3, the initially proposed
dose regimens resulted in >90% PTA at MICs <4 mg/L across all renal function categories.
