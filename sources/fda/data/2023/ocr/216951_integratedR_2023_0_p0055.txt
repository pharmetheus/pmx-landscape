Following oral solution administration, approximately 80% of daprodustat is absorbed across the Gl tract. The oral absolute bioavailability

Clearance Average renal clearance (CL) of daprodustat is 0.05 mL/min, with a urinary recovery of 0.01% of unchanged daprodustat.

Half-life Half-life of daprodustat ranges from 1-4 h.

Metabolic Daprodustat is metabolized by hydroxylation primarily by CYP2C8, with a minor contribution of CYP3A4, producing 6 predominant

pathway(s) circulating metabolites, of which, 3 (M2, M3, M13) are major (210% drug related material) metabolites.
Intrinsic Factors and Specific Populations

Body weight Using population PK modeling, the geometric mean ratio (GMR) of Cmax for the 5" and 95" percentile body weights relative to the median
was 0.73 and 1.28, and the GMR for AUC was 0.76 and 1.24, respectively. Body weight does not have a clinically significant effect on
daprodustat PK and dose adjustment based on body weight is not needed.

Age The PK of daprodustat has not been evaluated in children and adolescents less than 18 years of age. The PopPK analyses using data

from Phase 2/3 trials in adults with anemia associated with CKD (ranging in age in from 22 to 93 years (median 59 years) did not show
age as significant covariate.

Renal impairment

Renal impairment does not have a clinically significant effect on daprodustat PK. Daprodustat is not significantly cleared by hemodialysis.

experience.
Drug Interaction Liability (Drug as Perpetrator)

Inhibition/induction In vitro, daprodustat and the 6 predominant circulating metabolites are not competitive or time-dependent inhibitors of CYPs 1A2, 2A6,

of metabolism 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, 2D6, and 3A4. Daprodustat is not an in vitro inducer of CYP1A2, CYP2B6, or CYP3A4.

Inhibition/ Daprodustat is not an inhibitor of transporters P-gp or BCRP in vitro. Daprodustat is an in vitro inhibitor of human OATP1B1 and

induction of OATP1B3. The 6 daprodustat metabolites did not inhibit OATP1B1 at concentrations up to 50M. Daprodustat and the 6 metabolites are

transporter not inhibitors of the human renal transporters OAT1, OAT3, OCT2, MATE1 and MATE2-K.
