OPQ Recommendation:

The Applicant of this 505(b)(1) new drug application has provided sufficient CMC (chemistry,
manufacturing, and controls) information to assure the identity, strength, purity, and quality of
the drug substance, miglustat and the drug product, Opfolda (miglustat) Capsules, 65mg.

assessment has been granted.

Therefore, from the OPQ perspective, this NDA is recommended for approval with the

configurations and 36 months for 24-count capsules packaging configuration.
