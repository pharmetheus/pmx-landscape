Action or Recommended Action: | Approval

This memo serves as the decisional memo to support the regulatory actions for BLA 761204
(cipaglucosidase alfa-atga) and NDA 215211 (miglustat). See the integrated review dated
October 17, 2022, for detailed review of these applications. The applicant proposed the use of a
novel biologic (cipaglucosidase alfa) co-administered with miglustat for the treatment of adult
patients with late-onset Pompe disease (lysosomal acid alpha glucosidase [GAA] deficiency).

Pompe disease (PD) is an autosomal recessive, lysosomal storage disease that results in deficient
activity of acid alpha-glucosidase (GAA), the enzyme that degrades glycogen in lysosomes.
GAA deficiency leads to myopathy, respiratory weakness, physical disability, and premature
death.

Cipaglucosidase is a modified recombinant human GAA intended for use as enzyme replacement
therapy (ERT) in patients with LOPD. Miglustat is an N-alkylated iminosugar (a synthetic
analog of D-glucose) and is the active ingredient in Zavesca, which is approved for the treatment
of adult patients with mild/moderate Type 1 Gaucher disease for whom ERT is not a therapeutic
option. Given that cipaglucosidase and miglustat will be co-administered, we are taking action
on these applications at the same time.
