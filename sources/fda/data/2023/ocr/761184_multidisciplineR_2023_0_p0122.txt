Intrinsic Factors and Specific Populations
Based on population PK, body weight had a significant effect on clearance after
oral administration. Based on simulation, the dose of 0.66 mg/kgWveek should
provide comparable exposures over the anticipated weight range of potential
patients (8-52 kg).
Age was not a clinically relevant covariate for somatrogon PK based on
population PK analysis.
Subjects with renal impairment were excluded from enrolling in either of the
pediatric clinical studies and so no information about the requirement for
alteration in dosing for children with both GHD and renal impairment can be
made.
As a therapeutic protein, somatrogon is expected to be degraded by non-
specific proteases and therefore hepatic impairment study was not conducted.

Immunogenicity (if Applicable)

Bioanalysis Per the Office of Biotechnology Products reviewers, immunogenicity assays
were validated for semiquantitative measurement of antidrug antibodies (ADA),
including assays for antisomatrogon binding antibodies, anti-somatrogon
neutralizing antibodies, anti-hGH binding antibodies, and anti-hGH neutralizing
antibodies.

Incidence In the phase 3 safety and efficacy study, among 109 patients treated with
somatrogon, 84 (77.1%) tested positive for ADA.

Impact on PK/PD The mean clearance of somatrogon decreases ~26% after patients developed

ADA. The increase in exposure in ADA-positive subjects did not appear to
affect the IGF-1 response.
