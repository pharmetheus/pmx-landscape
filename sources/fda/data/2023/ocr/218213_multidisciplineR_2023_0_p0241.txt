19.4.3.4. ER (Safety) Assessment Summary
The Applicant's Position:

There was a statistically significant relationship between repotrectinib exposure (either Cmax,
Cavg, or both) for 11 out of the 15 safety endpoints evaluated (grade 2 2 dizziness, headache,
muscular weakness, ataxia, AST increased, ALT increased, paresthesia, anemia, constipation,
dysgeusia, and dizziness).

Repotrectinib Cmax was significantly associated with an increased probability of

grade > 2 dizziness. Increasing age also increased the probability of grade > 2 dizziness.
Simulations conducted at different doses and regimens indicated that the probability of
experiencing grade 2 2 dizziness increases with the dose. However, the absolute risk of
grade 2 2 dizziness was still relatively low.
