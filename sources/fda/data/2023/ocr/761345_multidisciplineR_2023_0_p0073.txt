NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation BLA 761345
elranatamab

The Applicant's Position:
The clinical pharmacology findings including PK, PD, and exposure-response for efficacy and

safety analyses support the recommended dosing regimen; 76 mg QW administered as a SC
injection with a 2-dose step-up priming regimen (12 mg on Day 1 and 32 mg on Day 4)
administered during the first week of treatment. The dosing interval should transition to 76 mg
Q2W after at least 24 weeks for patients who have achieved a response.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees that proposed dosing regimen of elranatamab 76 mg QW administered as a SC
injection with a 2-dose step-up priming regimen (12 mg on Day 1 and 32 mg on Day 4) for the
indicated patient population is acceptable.

e Step-up dosing regimen
The proposed 12/32 step-up dosing regimen is acceptable based on the following assessments:

1) The proposed RP2D regimen of 12/32/76 mg resulted in a lower incidence of all Grade and
Grade 22 CRS compared to the single step-up dosing regimen 44/76 mg. The single step-up
dose regimen resulted in rates of overall CRS and Grade > 2 CRS of 83% and 47%
respectively in patients with RRMM, compared to 57.9% and 13.7% in the RP2D regimen
(Table 9).
