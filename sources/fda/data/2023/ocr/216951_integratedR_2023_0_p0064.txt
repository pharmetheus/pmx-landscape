Multiple Comparison

The multiplicity strategy for this trial used a combination of a gatekeeper approach on the co-
primary endpoints, followed by a closed-test multiplicity procedure wrapped around the family
of superiority hypotheses consisting of MACE and the key secondary endpoints.

Figure 3 illustrates the structure of the statistical testing plan. First, the co-primary endpoints
were evaluated for non-inferiority by comparing each two-sided 95% CI to the appropriate non-
inferiority margin. Conditional on both co-primary endpoints achieving non-inferiority (i-e.,
passing the gatekeeper), the family of MACE and the key secondary endpoints were formally
tested for superiority using the widely known Holm-Bonferroni procedure. The procedure was
conducted based on a familywise Type I error rate set at the one-sided 2.5% level.

Subgroup Analysis

A subgroup analysis for age, sex, region, and race were performed for the primary ITT analysis
of post-randomization mean change in Hb between Baseline and over the EP (Weeks 28 to 52).
