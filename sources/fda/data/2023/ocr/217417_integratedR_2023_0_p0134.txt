Toxicokinetics

Weekly intravenous injections of rezafungin at 5, 15, and 30 mg/kg resulted in approximately
dose-related increases in rezafungin area under the concentration-time curve (AUC) and Cmax.
Terminal elimination half-life ranged from 50.9 to 78.8 hours in individual animals. Clearance
values ranged from 5.30 to 12.7 mL/h/kg in individual animals and Vz values ranged from 456 to
1,000 mL/kg in individual animals.
