DHN Assessment

The pattern of liver enzyme elevation after restarting sparsentan appears to be consistent with a
positive rechallenge followed by the development of tolerance. Figure 12 shows that the liver
enzymes appeared to modestly decrease even before sparsentan was initially discontinued. This
case is notable for possible tolerance to the event of DILI with sparsentan.
