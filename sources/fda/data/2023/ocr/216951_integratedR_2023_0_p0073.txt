NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

6.2.2. Study 200808/ASCEND-ND

6.2.2.1. Design

Overview

To support an indication for the treatment of anemia due to CKD in patients not on dialysis, the
Applicant conducted a randomized, Phase 3 Study, 200808/ASCEND-ND, titled “A phase 3
randomized, open-label (sponsor-blind), active controlled, parallel-group, multi-center, event
driven study in non-dialysis subjects with anemia associated with chronic kidney disease to
evaluate the safety and efficacy of daprodustat compared to darbepoetin alfa.”

Primary Safety and Efficacy Endpoints

Safety: Time to first occurrence of adjudicated MACE (composite of all-cause mortality,
nonfatal MI, and nonfatal stroke)

Efficacy: Mean change in Hb between Baseline and EP (Weeks 24 to 52).

Secondary Efficacy Endpoints

Hb change from Baseline to Week 52

N (%) responders, defined as having a mean Hb within the Hb analysis range 10 to
11.5 g/dL during EP

% time Hb in analysis range during the EP

Time to stopping randomized treatment due to meeting rescue criteria

Exploratory Endpoints

e Number (%) of participants who receive at least one RBC or whole blood transfusions by
Week 52 and by end of treatment
e Number of RBC and whole blood transfusions per 100 person years

The key secondary safety endpoints tested for superiority and adjusted for multiplicity included:

Time to MACE*

Time to first occurrence of MACE or a TEE

Time to first occurrence of MACE or HHF

Time to progression of CKD among subjects with baseline eGFR>15 mL/min/1.73m?
defined as: (a) 40% decline in estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) from baseline
(confirmed 4 to13 weeks later), or (b) ESRD as defined by either initiating chronic
dialysis for > 90 days or not initiating chronic dialysis when dialysis is indicated or
kidney transplantation

Conditional on both co-primary endpoints ruling out their risk margins, key secondary endpoints
would be tested for superiority by comparing the one-sided superiority p-value against the rank-
associated alpha derived from the Holm-Bonferroni method.

4 Tested for noninferiority as a coprimary endpoint and tested for superiority as a key principal secondary endpoint.
