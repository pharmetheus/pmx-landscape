6.2.5.3. Results of Analyses

Patient Disposition

Participants were considered to have completed the study if they completed the 28 Week
treatment period and the follow-up visit irrespective of whether they discontinued randomized
treatment. Participants were also considered to have completed the study if they died while on
study. Of 614 participants in the ITT population, 590 (96%) participants completed the study to
Week 28 or died while on study. Seven participants (2%) in the daprodustat group and 17
participants (6%) in the placebo group discontinued randomized treatment and withdrew from

the study (Table 52).
