14.3.1.2. Introduction

The Applicant developed two population PK models to describe the same PK data collected from
children 6 to 12 years old who participated in the phase 3 study. The models were developed at
different periods and for different purposes. The first PopPK model (dated May 26, 2020) was

model after a previously developed PopPK model based on phase 2 adult and children PK data

children only data. In this model clearance and volume parameters were normalized by body
