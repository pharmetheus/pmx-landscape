14.3.3.2.5. Assessment of Factors Associated With IGF-1
SDS 22 Over Time

Factors associated with IGF-1 SDS >2 were assessed using the individual predicted IGF-1 data
for patients in both phase 2 and 3 studies. The good agreement between observed and predicted
trough IGF-1 concentration over time (Figure 51) justifies the use of the predicted IGF-1 for
assessment of factors associated with SDS >2.
