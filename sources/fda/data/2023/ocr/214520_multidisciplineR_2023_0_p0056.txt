Protocol Violations/Deviations

As shown in Table 6 above, approximately 2% of subjects withdrew from the study early
because of a protocol violation. A lower proportion of subjects in the Defencath group
withdrew due to protocol violations compared with the heparin group (0.5% versus 3.0%).
These subjects were censored in the primary analysis either at three days after their last study
drug dosing date or at the time they withdrew from the study but were considered as events in
a sensitivity analysis conducted by the reviewer.
