Reversible binding of pirtobrutinib toward BTK targets

Binding kinetics of pirtobrutinib toward WT BTK and C481 substitution mutant (resistance
mutation) BTKs:

The in vitro equilibrium binding affinities of pirtobrutinib for WT BTK and the resistance
mutations, the BTK C481 substitution mutants (i.e., C481S, C481R and C481T), were determined
further using a surface plasmon resonance (SPR)-based assay. The data indicate that
pirtobrutinib binds WT and C481S mutant BTK with Kp values in the low nM range. Pirtobrutinib
demonstrated reversible binding to WT BTK and BTK C4815 with off-rates corresponding to a
pirtobrutinib dissociation half-life of 1.9 -2.6 hours. While the binding affinities were
comparable across the BTK mutants tested, the complex half-lives were higher for the C481R
(7.5 h) and C481T (28 h) mutations compared to C4815 (1.9 h).
