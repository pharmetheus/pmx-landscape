NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

6.2.3. Study 204837 /ASCEND-TD

6.2.3.1. Design

Overview

To support a three times a week dosing regimen in patients on hemodialysis, the Applicant
conducted a randomized, Phase 3 Study, 204837/ASCEND-TD, titled “A Phase 3 randomized,
double-blind, active-controlled, parallel-group, multi-center study in hemodialysis participants
with anemia of chronic kidney disease to evaluate the efficacy, safety and pharmacokinetics of
three-times weekly dosing of daprodustat compared to recombinant human erythropoietin,
following a switch from recombinant human erythropoietin or its analogues.”

Primary Efficacy Endpoint
Mean change in Hb between Baseline and EP (Weeks 28 to 52)

Key Secondary Efficacy Endpoint
Average monthly IV iron dose (mg)/participant to Week 52

Secondary Efficacy Endpoints

e Hb change from Baseline to Week 52

e N (%) responders, defined as mean Hb within the Hb analysis range 10 to 11.5 g/dL
during EP
% time Hb in analysis range during the EP
Time to stopping randomized treatment due to meeting rescue criteria

Study Design

Study 204837/ASCEND-TD was a randomized, double-blind, active-controlled, double-dummy,
parallel-group, multi-center study in hemodialysis-dependent participants, including combination
methods such as hemodiafiltration or hemofiltration with hemodialysis, who had anemia of CKD
and were being treated with rhEPO or its analogues.

This study comprised a 4-week Screening Period, a 52-week Treatment Period and a Follow-up
Period (Figure 12).
The 52-week Treatment Period consists of:
e SP, defined as the period from Day 1 to Week 28 (up to but excluding the Week 28 visit),
during which study treatment was dose-titrated to achieve and maintain Hb in the target
Tange.
e EP, defined as the period from Week 28 to Week 52, to assess long-term efficacy and

safety. Throughout the trial, including during the EP, randomized treatment was titrated
(in a double-blind fashion) according to a prespecified algorithm.
