The disease and treatment characteristics for patients in both the primary and supportive
safety populations are shown in Table 47 below. Analysis focused on the primary safety
population, which consisted of patients with R/R LBCL consistent with the proposed indication.
Among the 157 patients in the primary safety population, the majority had DLBCL (89%) and
advanced stage disease (Stage III/IV: 75%). The population was overall heavily pretreated with a
median of 3 lines of prior therapy and 39% of patients treated with at least 4 or more. Over half
of the patients (61%) were considered to have primary refractory disease and 39% and 20% of
patients had had prior CAR-T therapy or autologous stem cell transplant, respectively.
