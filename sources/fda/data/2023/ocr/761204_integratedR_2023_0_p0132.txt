8.2. Drug Interactions

8.2.1. Cipaglucosidase Alfa

No specific drug-drug interaction studies were conducted with cipaglucosidase alfa.

8.2.2. Miglustat

Based on previous in vitro studies under the Zavesca NDA, miglustat does not inhibit CYP
enzymes, including CYP1A2, CYP2A6, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP2E1, CYP3A4 and
CYP4A11. We recommend the Applicant conduct a postmarketing study to further evaluate
whether miglustat is a substrate, inhibitor, or inducer of metabolizing enzymes and transporters
as outlined in the FDA guidance for industry Jn Vitro Drug Interaction Studies - Cytochrome
P450 Enzyme- and Transporter-Mediated Drug Interactions (FDA 2020).
