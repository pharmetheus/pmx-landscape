Interference and specificity

The selectivity of the method in the presence of APD334-glucnronide (a
metabolite of APD334 not quantified by the method) was evaluated.
APD334/AR401967 QC samples at low and high concentrations were
fortified with APD334-glucuronide at 40.0 ng/mL and analyzed after
incubation on the bench (in an ice water bath) for 1 hour and 2 hours,
respectively. The samples were assessed against calibration standards in
control matrix without APD334-glucuronide. The selectivity of APD334 and
AR401967 from APD334-glucuronide was demonstrated.
