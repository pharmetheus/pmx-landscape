The Applicant submitted an amended agreed initial pediatric study plan (iPSP). In the amended
agreed iPSP, the Applicant requested a waiver for pediatric assessment for the birth to <2 years
of age pediatric age group due to studies being impossible or highly impracticable. The
Applicant also requested a deferral of pediatric studies because the adult studies were
completed prior to completion of the pediatric studies. The review team agreed with the waiver
and deferral requests.

In the amended agreed iPSP, the Applicant proposed two pediatric clinical studies. At the time
of the review of the NDA submission, a one-year, phase 2, multicenter, open-label, single-arm,
sentinel dose cohort study to evaluate the efficacy, PK, and safety of etrasimod in adolescent
subjects 12 to <18 years of age with moderately to severely active UC was ongoing (i.e.,
APD334-207). °

(refer to Section 7.2).

Given the lack of data available for etrasimod in pediatric subjects with UC, the review team
anticipates that data from APD334-207 may be necessary to inform the design of the future
pediatric program, particularly to inform dose selection for the second pediatric study (i.e.,
APD334-208). Therefore, the PREA PMRs will include one PMR to finish the ongoing pediatric
study APD334-207 and a second PMR to conduct a one-year study to evaluate the safety,
efficacy, pharmacokinetics, and pharmacodynamics of etrasimod in pediatric patients 2 to 17
years of age with moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis. The PREA PMRs are described
in Section 13.
