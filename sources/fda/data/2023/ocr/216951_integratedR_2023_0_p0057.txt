Starting doses of 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 12 mg were selected. These doses were estimated to maintain

analogue or for participants not receiving prior rhEPO to increase the Hb concentration from
Baseline (Day 1) to the target Hb range of 10 to 11 g/dL (11 to 12 g/dL in 205270/ASCEND-
NHQ). As CKD patients receiving hemodialysis are typically treated three times a week (TIW),
thEPO treatment regimens are often TIW, coincident with the hemodialysis schedule. Therefore,
a TIW regimen was assessed for daprodustat in the hemodialysis population. For TIW dosing in
204837/ASCEND-TD the once daily doses used in Study 200807/ASCEND-D were converted to
corresponding TIW doses using the estimated dose conversion ratio of approximately 2.0 that
was determined in the Phase 2A TIW daprodustat dose ranging study. For TIW dosing, starting
doses of 8, 12, 16 and 24 mg were selected. These doses were estimated to maintain equivalent
Hb concentrations to that achieved in the participants on prior rhEPO therapy and its analogues.
