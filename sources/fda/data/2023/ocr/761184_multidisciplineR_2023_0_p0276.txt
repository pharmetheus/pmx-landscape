e Serum levels of insulin growth factor binding protein 3 IGFBP3)

15.2.3. Eligibility Criteria

Key Inclusion Criteria

Children aged 3-10 year (girls) and 3-11 years (boys)

Confirmed diagnosis of GHD (by 2 separate GH provocative tests)

Bone age (BA) is not older than chronological age (CA); BA <10 for females and <1 1 for
males

Naive-to-treatment for GHD

Impaired growth, defined as:

— Height velocity (HV) <25 percentile for CA (HV <-0.7 SDS) and gender

— Height (HT) of atleast 2 standard deviation (SDs) below the mean HT for CA and
gender. The interval between 2 HT measurements should be 6 to 18 months prior to
inclusion

e Baseline IGF-1 value at least 1 standard deviation (SD) below the mean IGF-1 for age and
gender

¢ Normal calculated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) based on updated “bedside” Schwartz
formula for pediatric patients.
