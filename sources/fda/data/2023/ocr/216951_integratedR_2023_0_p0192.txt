Conclusion

Limitations of the CV analyses included lower precision (compared to MACE) because of lower
event rates (and hence wider 95% CIs) and no Type I error control for treatment arm
comparisons of the CV endpoints. However, in ASCEND-ND, the consistently increased risk
estimates across different CV endpoints measuring related aspects of CV risk in a CV outcomes
trial raised concems as to whether daprodustat is safe relative to darbepoetin alfa, which itself
carries an increased CV risk. In ASCEND-D, there was not a consistent pattern of increased risk
estimates in the CV endpoints (Section 7.6.2.2.1). The reasons for discrepant trials results for CV
safety are not clear.

7.7.3. Higher Incidence of Cardiovascular Endpoints in
the USA Subgroup - ASCEND-ND

Issue

A higher incidence of CV endpoints was observed in the daprodustat arm compared to the
darbepoetin alfa arm in the USA region in the ASCEND-ND study. Except stroke, the HRs of
the non-USA subgroup analyses were approximately 1.0, and the HRs of the USA subgroup
analyses were elevated (HRs ranging from 1.21 to 2.03).
