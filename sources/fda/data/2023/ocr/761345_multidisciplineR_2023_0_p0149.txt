The FDA's Assessment:

FDA analysis of SAEs was based on FDA’s grouped terms (See Appendix 19.6) and included analysis
of SAEs that occurred in Cohorts A and B independently, as well as in the entire safety population.
Overall, 68% of patients experienced a SAE, and there was no significant difference in the
incidence of SAEs that occurred in Cohort A (68%) and Cohort B (69%). Based on FDA’s analysis,
the most frequent SAEs included pneumonia (25%), sepsis (13%), and cytokine release syndrome
(13%). Serious TEAEs that occurred in >2% of patients are shown in Table 45. This information will
be included in Section 6.1 of the USPI. FDA agrees with the Applicant’s statement that, in general,
the most common SAEs were associated with identified risks for elranatamab.
