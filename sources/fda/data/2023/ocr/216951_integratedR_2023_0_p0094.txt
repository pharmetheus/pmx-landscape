Multiple Comparison

The primary endpoint was tested first for non-inferiority, using the lower limit of the two-sided
95% CI. Conditional on achieving statistical significance (i.e., establishing non-inferiority) the
single key secondary endpoint was tested for superiority using a one-sided 2.5% significance
level. This two-step hierarchical strategy preserved the study-wise Type I error rate at a one-
sided 2.5% level.

The additional secondary/exploratory endpoints, if tested, were not adjusted for multiplicity. A
nominal one-sided 2.5% significance level was applied per test.

Subgroup Analysis

Post hoc subgroup analyses, including age, sex, region, and race were performed for the primary
ITT analysis of post-randomization mean change in Hb between baseline and over the EP
(Weeks 28 to 52).

6.2.3.3. Results of Analyses

Patient Disposition

Participants were considered to have completed the study if they remained on study to Week 52
and completed the Week 52 visit irrespective of whether they discontinued randomized
treatment. Participants were also considered to have completed the study if they died while on
study. Of 407 participants in the ITT population, 404 (+99%) participants completed the study to
Week 52 or died while on study. One (<1%) participant in the daprodustat TIW group and 2
(1%) participants in the epoetin alfa group discontinued randomized treatment and withdrew
from the study (see Table 28).
