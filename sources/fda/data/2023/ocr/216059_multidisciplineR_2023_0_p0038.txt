Safety Pharmacology
The Applicant's Position:

The safety pharmacology package indicates that there is little risk of pirtobrutinib having
negative consequences in systems that are vital for life: cardiovascular, CNS, and respiratory.
While there was a slight increase in QTc interval in the 1-month dog study, this finding was
likely spurious. It was not repeated in the dog cardiovascular safety pharmacology study or the
3-month dog study. In addition, this QTc increase is not consistent with the findings from the
GLP in vitro hERG study.
