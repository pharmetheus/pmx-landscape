Somatrogon was evaluated in hypophysectomized rats. Following subcutaneous administration
of a single dose or repeated doses of somatrogon every 4 days for 12 days, or every 7 days for 14
days, a dose-dependent increase in body weight gain was observed. Body weight gain in rats
administered 0.48 mg/kg somatrogon every 4 days was similar to that seen with daily
administration of 0.1 mg/kg recombinant human growth hormone. A single subcutaneous
injection of somatrogon (0.6 or 1.8 mg/kg) produced higher levels of circulating human growth
hormone (somatrogon) and IGF-1 levels when compared to a single subcutaneous administration

of recombinant human growth hormone (0.35 or 1.05 mg/kg), consistent with its significantly
longer half-life.
