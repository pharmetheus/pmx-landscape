NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

FDA identified limitations in the PGI-S and PGI-C anchor scales that limit the interpretability of
the results of the anchor-based analyses. Specifically, the concepts measured in the SF-36 v2.0
Vitality domain (tiredness, weakness, full of life, energy) were not aligned with the concepts
measured in the PGI-S and PGI-C anchor scales (overall CKD symptoms). Due to these
limitations, FDA focused on the Applicant’s anchor-based analyses using items 1 and 2 from the
CKD-AQ, which measure tiredness and energy that are better aligned with the SF-36 v2.0
Vitality domain. As such, FDA considered the range of 18- to 21-point improvement appropriate
based on the Applicant’s anchor-based analyses results. Refer to the full COA review by the
Division of Clinical Outcome Assessment (DCOA) for additional details on the adequacy of the
anchor scales.

Of note, while PRO data (e.g., SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain, CKD-AQ items 1 and 2) were
collected in Studies ASCEND-ND and ASCEND-ID, the open-label study design of these two
studies was a limitation to the interpretation of PRO data and subsequently the evaluation of
clinically meaningful-within patient change, as it is unknown whether patients’ knowledge of
treatment assignment may lead to systematic overestimation or underestimation of the treatment
effect, the magnitude of which is currently unknown. Therefore, FDA requested additional
anchor-based analyses using Study ASCEND-NHQ data, which suggested a threshold range of
12.5- to 18.75-point improvement in the SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain score as clinically
meaningful to patients. Of note, the Hb target in ASCEND-NHQ was higher than the other
studies (11 to 12 g/dL, in contrast to 10 to 11 g/dL, which reflects current labeling for ESAs).
The relevance of any efficacy conclusions to a population treated to a lower target is unclear.

Refer to Section 16.1 of this review for more discussion on the anchor-based analyses.

Sub-Issue 2

FDA reviewed the Applicant’s eCDF curves to further evaluate the observed changes in the SF-
36 v2.0 Vitality domain-score. The eCDF plot below (Figure 26) displays a continuous view of
the cumulative proportion of patients reporting any amount of change from baseline to Week 28
in the 0-100 scaled SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain score between treatment arms of ASCEND-
NHQ. Note that a positive change (>0) to the left represents an improvement in the score and a
negative change (<0) to the right represents a worsening in the score. There is not a clear and
consistent separation between daprodustat and placebo arms within the range of 12- to 21-point
improvement, which encompasses all threshold ranges derived using data from Studies
ASCEND-ND, ASCEND-ID and ASCEND-NHQ.
