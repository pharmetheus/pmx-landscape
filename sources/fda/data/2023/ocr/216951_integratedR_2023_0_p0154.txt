7.6.1.2. CV Endpoints

7.6.1.2.1. Overview

To better understand CV risk, adjudicated CV endpoints, not including all-cause mortality, were
assessed. Results of the primary (on-study) time-to-event analysis of CV endpoints in ASCEND-
ND are shown in Figure 29. As shown in the figure, the estimated HRs for CV MACE, CV
mortality, MI, stroke, HHF, TEE, and VAT are all increased for the comparison of daprodustat to
darbepoetin alfa, ranging from 1.06 to 1.49. The corresponding IRDs (per 100-person years)
ranged from 0.2 to 0.7 (Appendix Section 17.6).
