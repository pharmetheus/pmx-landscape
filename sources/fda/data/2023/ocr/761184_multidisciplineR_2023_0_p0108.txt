No new safety signals were identified
during the 12-month treatment with
somatrogon in trials CP-4-006 and CP-4-
004 followed by uncontrolled periods of
these trials in a small number of subjects.
The most common (25%) adverse events
(AEs) that occurred with higher frequency
in the somatrogon group compared to
Genotropin group were local
administration reactions (43% versus 25
%), followed by nasopharyngitis (33%

15%), respectively.

AEs of hypoadrenalism were observed in
4 subjects treated with somatrogon. No
adrenal crisis was reported.
