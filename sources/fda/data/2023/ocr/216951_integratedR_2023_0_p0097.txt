Efficacy Results—Primary Endpoint

For Study 204837/ASCEND-TD, mean Hb at Baseline and over the EP was similar between
treatment groups and remained within the Hb analysis range (10 to 11.5 g/dL) during the EP
(Weeks 28 to 52) (Figure 13). Also, Hb remained relatively stable and within the analysis range
(10 to 11.5 g/dL) from Baseline through Week 52.
