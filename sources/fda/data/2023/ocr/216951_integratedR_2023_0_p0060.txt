NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

This study comprised four study periods: a 4-week screening period, a 4-week placebo run-in
period, a treatment period, and a follow-up period (Figure 2). Prior ESA therapy continued
during the screening and run-in periods.

The Treatment Period consists of:

e Stabilization Period (SP), defined as the period from Day 1 to Week 28 (up to but
excluding the Week 28 visit), during which study treatment was dose-titrated to achieve
and maintain Hb in the target range.

e Maintenance Period (MP), defined as the period from the end of the SP to the end of
treatment (variable per participant), to assess long term safety and efficacy

e EP, defined as the period from Week 28 to Week 52, during which Hb efficacy was
primarily assessed.

e Alternative EP: Because of the importance of patients not spending undue time with
suboptimal Hb levels, a shorter EP, defined as the period from Week 28 to Week 36, was
also assessed for Hb efficacy.

In ASCEND-D, subjects were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to oral daprodustat or to IV epoetin alfa
(hemodialysis) or SC darbepoetin alfa (peritoneal dialysis). In the ASCEND-D study,
daprodustat was also dosed daily while the control was dosed weekly, every 2 weeks, or every
4 weeks (darbepoetin alfa) or every 2 days or weekly (epoetin alfa), depending on the subject’s
prior ESA use.

An anemia rescue algorithm, which included a provision for the use of intravenous iron, an RBC
transfusion, or both, was provided in the protocol. Additionally, the protocol required subjects to
be iron replete throughout the study — and initiate iron therapy (investigator’s choice) if ferritin
was less than or equal to 100 ng/mL, transferrin saturation was less than or equal to 20%, or
both.
