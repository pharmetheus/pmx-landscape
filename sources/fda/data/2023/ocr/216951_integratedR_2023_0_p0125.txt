For the primary ITT analysis of mean change in Hb between Baseline and over the EP (mean
over Weeks 24 to 28) daprodustat was superior to placebo. The adjusted mean treatment
difference (daprodustat - placebo) was 1.40 g/dL (95% CI: 1.23, 1.56; one-sided p value
<0.0001) (see Table 57).
