12. Summary of Regulatory History

The original IND for sparsentan for the treatment of IgAN was submitted on October 25, 2018.
The opening IND was a phase 3 study, 021IGAN17001, entitled “A Randomized, Multicenter,
Double-blind, Parallel-group, Active-control Study of the Efficacy and Safety of Sparsentan for
the Treatment of Immunoglobulin A Nephropathy”, also known as the PROTECT study.

The Sponsor cross-referenced the existing IND | (sparsentan indicated for the treatment of

FSGS) and an inactivated IND OF for
chemistry, manufacturing, and control data, as well as nonclinical and supportive clinical data.
