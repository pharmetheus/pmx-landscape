NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

Secondary Efficacy Analyses

The planned key secondary efficacy analysis was the on-treatment average monthly IV iron dose
(mg)/participant from Baseline to Week 52 excluding IV iron use following a participant's first
RBC or whole blood transfusion and was based on the ITT population. An ANCOVA model was
used to compare the difference in average monthly IV iron dose per participant between
treatment groups, adjusting for: treatment; Baseline monthly IV iron dose; dialysis type; and
dialysis start manner. The number and percentage of participants with Baseline IV iron use, on
treatment EP IV iron use, and on-treatment IV iron use to Week 52 was summarized by
treatment.

The secondary analysis of Hb change from baseline to Week 52 Hb was tested for non-inferiority
using the -0.75 g/dL margin used in the primary analysis. A MMRM approach was used with an
unstructured covariance matrix to compare the difference in means between groups. The model
included factors for treatment, time, prognostic randomization factors (dialysis type and dialysis
start manner), Baseline Hb, and the Baseline Hb by time and treatment by time interaction terms.
This analysis was performed using all available Hb values (on- and off-treatment) and separately
using evaluable Hb values only.

For the analysis of % time Hb in range (10.0 tol 1.5 g/dL) during the EP (Weeks 28 to 52), the
method by Rosendaal was used to calculate the percentage of time (days) a participant’s Hb was
below, within and above the Hb analysis range during the EP. Non-inferiority with the margin of
-0.75 g/dL was assessed using the Hodges-Lehmann estimate of the median treatment difference
and associated 95% CI, comparing the percentage of time in range between treatment groups.
Superiority was assessed using the 1-sided p-value from a van Elteren test (stratified Wilcoxon
rank sum test). The stratified Mann-Whitney estimator stratified by dialysis type and dialysis
start manner, and 95% CI for the probability of daprodustat achieving a greater percentage of
time with Hb in the analysis range than darbepoetin alfa provided a supportive treatment
comparison estimate for the van Elteren test.

For the Hb responder analysis, responders were participants with a mean Hb during the EP that
fell within the Hb analysis range of 10.0 to 11.5 g/dL. A Cochran-Mantel- Haenszel chi-squared
test, adjusting for treatment, dialysis type, and dialysis start manner, was used to compare the
number and % of responders between the treatment groups.

For the analysis of time to stopping randomized treatment due to meeting rescue criteria, a Cox
proportional hazards model, adjusting for treatment, dialysis start type, and dialysis start manner
(planned or unplanned) was used to generate the hazard ratio and associated 95% CI for the
treatment group comparison. A 1-sided superiority p-value was generated using a Wald test. The
incidence rate per 100 PY and associated 95% CI for each treatment group, as well as the
absolute rate difference per 100 PY and associated 95% CI were also provided.

Multiple Comparison

The primary endpoint was tested first for non-inferiority, using the lower limit of the 2-sided
95% CI. Conditional on achieving statistical significance (i-e., passing the primary gate by
establishing non-inferiority) the single key secondary endpoint was tested for superiority using a
1-sided 2.5% significance level. This 2-step hierarchical strategy was implemented to preserve
the study-wise Type I error rate at a 1-sided 2.5% significance level.
