Bosentan PBPK model

The bosentan PBPK model was developed according to Posada et al. (2020). The model
included hepatic and non-hepatic clearance as observed at therapeutic bosentan plasma
concentrations (Volz et al. 2017) and auto-induction of CYP3A4-mediated bosentan clearance.

The Applicant validated the bosentan PBPK model by comparing the observed and predicted
bosentan PK following a single dose of 125 mg or 62.5 mg, as well as after single ascending
doses of 100, 200 and 500 mg in healthy volunteers. The predicted versus observed Cmax and
AUC ratios are within 0.66 to 1.02 and 0.80 to 1.05, respectively (The Applicant’s PBPK report).
