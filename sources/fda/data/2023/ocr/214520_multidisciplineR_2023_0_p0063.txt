Efficacy Results — Primary Endpoint

The planned interim analysis was conducted when 28 CAC-adjudicated CRBSIs had occurred.
The results are summarized in Table 11. The treatment difference was statistically significant in
favor of Defencath. Subjects in the Defencath group had a 72% reduction in risk of CAC-
adjudicated CRBSIs as compared to those in the heparin group. The Data and Safety Monitoring
Board recommended early termination of the study based on the results of the interim analysis.

Additional subjects were randomized and treated between the date that the database was
locked for the interim analysis and the date when the study was stopped. Also, additional
subjects experienced CAC-adjudicated CRBSI events between the database lock and the date of
the last patient completing the study. As shown in Table 11, the results using all data through
the study completion were similar to the findings from the interim analysis. Figure 2 displays
the cumulative incidence of the CAC-adjudicated CRBSIs at study completion per treatment
group using the Kaplan-Meier approach generated by the statistical reviewer.
