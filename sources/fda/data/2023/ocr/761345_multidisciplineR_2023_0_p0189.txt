19.4.1 Pharmacokinetics

Population predictions of Cmax, dose-normalized Cmax, AUC, dose-normalized AUC of

elranatamab following intravenous and SC dose in the first week are presented in Figure 14.

Excluding data with 3 evaluable measurements, dose normalized AUCiau and Cmax were
generally consistent across the dosing groups for SQ administration, which suggests that
elranatamab exposure is approximately dose proportional (Table 68).
