NDA 216951
Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

Missing Data

For the analysis of the co-primary efficacy endpoint, a multiple imputation approach was used to
handle missing Hb values. Both intermittent missing and monotone missing scheduled Hb data
were imputed based on the MAR assumption. However, it is important to examine the sensitivity
of inferences to departures from the MAR assumption for the monotone missing data. A tipping
point analysis was performed as a sensitivity analysis for the assumptions of missing data, that is,
to mimic MNAR in which the missing observations depend on unobserved values.

Primary Efficacy Analysis

The primary Hb efficacy analysis assessed the estimand defined as the comparative treatment
effect in mean Hb change between Baseline and EP (i.e., Weeks 28 to 52 inclusive) in all
randomized participants 1.e., the ITT population. An ANCOVA model adjusted for
randomization stratification factors (dialysis type and dialysis start manner), Baseline Hb, and
treatment was used. All Hb data (on- and off-treatment) recorded during the EP was included in
the primary efficacy analysis, regardless of adherence to randomized treatment including
interruptions and discontinuations, the use of non-randomized ESA medication for any reason
including rescue therapy, or the use of blood transfusions. Multiple imputation was applied in the
case of missing Hb data.

Participants were analyzed according to the treatment to which they were randomized using an
ANCOVA model. The analysis tested whether daprodustat was non-inferior to darbepoetin alfa.
The non-inferiority margin was pre-defined as -0.75 g/dL, determined based upon a combination
of clinical judgment, statistical reasoning, and regulatory guidance for designing non-inferiority
trials. Non-inferiority would be established if the lower limit of the 2-sided 95% CI for the
treatment difference in mean Hb change between Baseline and EP (daprodustat - darbepoetin
alfa) was greater than 0.75 g/dL.
