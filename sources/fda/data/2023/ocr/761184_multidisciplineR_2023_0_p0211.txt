Pharmacodynamic Results

There were no apparent differences in the arithmetic mean plasma concentrations of IGF-1,
either uncorrected or corrected for baseline, between Japanese and Caucasian subjects for the
vehicle, 2.5 mg, and 7.5 mg cohorts, although those for the Japanese were lower than for the
Caucasians after a dose of 15 mg (Figure 17). However, within each dosing cohort, the geometric
mean values for baseline corrected C,,,, and AUEC (area under the effect curve) were
comparable (Table 51), suggesting no difference in response between the two ethnic groups.

Immunogenicity Results

Overall, all but 1 subject had negative result on MOD-4023 binding specificity test. At the EOS
visit (Day 30), 1 Japanese subject © from the 15 mg dose group had a positive result for
the MOD-4023 binding specificity (value 24.8%) and negative results for the NAb for MOD-
4023, native hGH and CTP Abs (-43% inhibition).
