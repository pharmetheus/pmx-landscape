Exposure-Response Relationship for Safety

Exposure-response relationships for safety were investigated in phase 3 induction study AMAN
and maintenance study AMBG. No clear exposure-response associations were observed
between average mirikizumab concentration quartile during induction treatment or
maintenance treatment and incidence of TEAEs leading to dose modifications, infusion site
reactions, injection site reactions, infections, serious infections, opportunistic infections, and
hypersensitivity reactions. See Section 16.3.5 for the detailed E-R safety analysis.
