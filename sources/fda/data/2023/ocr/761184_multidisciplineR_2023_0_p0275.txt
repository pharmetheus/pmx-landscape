15.1.5. Trial CP-4-006 Protocol Deviations

The majority of the protocol deviations were minor. There were 6 (5.5%) subjects with major
deviations in the somatrogon group (4 missing doses and 2 stadiometer calibration) and 5 (4.3%)
subjects in the Genotropin group (3 missing doses and 2 stadiometer calibration).
