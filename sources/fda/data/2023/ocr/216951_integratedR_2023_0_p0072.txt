Efficacy Results - Subgroups

Overall, the results of the subgroup analyses were consistent with the primary analysis with little
or no heterogeneity between subgroups. The adjusted mean differences were consistently >-0.75
for all subgroups (see Figure 6). In general, the treatment effect of daprodustat compared to
thEPO was generally consistent across all prespecified subgroups, including regional subgroup
results.
