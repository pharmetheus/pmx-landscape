Randomization was stratified by region and participants were randomized in a 2:1 ratio to either:

¢ Daprodustat TIW + saline IV injection (placebo), or:
e Epoetin alfa once weekly or TIW (depending on dose level) + placebo tablets.

Study Population Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria

Inclusion Criteria

The study recruited male and female (non-pregnant, non-lactating) participants, aged >18 years
to <99 years of age with anemia of CKD who were being treated with rhEPO or its analogues.
Participants were hemodialysis-dependent (including hemofiltration or hemodiafiltration) for
greater than 90 days and receiving hemodialysis (in-center) >3 times per week. Hb eligibility
range is defined as:

e Week -4: Hb 8 to 11.5 g/dL (5 to 7.4 mmol/L); If Hb is 11.6 to 11.9 g/dL (7.2 to
7.4 mmol/L), up to two retests are allowed; the retest value must be between 8 to
11.5 g/dL (5 to 7.1 mmol/L).

e Day 1: Hb 8 to 11 g/dL (5 to 6.8 mmol/L) and receiving at least the minimum rhEPO or
analogue dose; Hb >11 g/dL to 11.5 g/dL (6.8 to 7.1 mmol/L) and receiving greater than
the minimum rhEPO or analogue dose

Exclusion Criteria

e Planned kidney transplant within 52 weeks
e Ferritin <100 ng/mL (<100 pg/L) at screening
e TSAT <20% at screening
