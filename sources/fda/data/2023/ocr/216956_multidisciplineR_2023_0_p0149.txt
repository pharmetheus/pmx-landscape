8.2.4.5. Treatment Emergent Adverse Events and Adverse Reactions

An overview of treatment emergent adverse events (TEAEs) for APD334-301 is shown in Table
51. Overall, the proportion of subjects with at least one TEAE was greater in subjects who
received etrasimod 206/289 (71%) compared to placebo 81/144 (56%). The majority of TEAEs
were mild or moderate in severity. The proportion of subjects with a severe TEAE was similar
between etrasimod 21/289 (7%) and placebo 11/144 (8%).

Table 55 summarizes the common TEAEs reported at a frequency of at least 2% of subjects in
the etrasimod group and greater than placebo during APD334-301. The most common TEAEs
were headache, elevated liver tests, dizziness, and arthralgia.
