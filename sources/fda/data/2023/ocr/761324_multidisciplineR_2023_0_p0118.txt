LBCL subtypes and Indication:

Although the entire population of 157 patients with LBCL is used for the FDA efficacy analysis,
the exact breakdown by subtype is of importance, especially in support of the indicated
population. The FDA did not agree with the Applicant that there was sufficient representation
of multiple other subtypes of R/R LBCL to justify extension of the indication to all R/R LBCL. The
initial breakdown shown in Table 32 was based on report at entry and demonstrated few
patients with HGBCL (6%; 9/157), FL Grade 3B (3%; 5/157), or PMBCL (3%, 4/157). However,
following reassessment of 88 patient samples by central laboratory FISH analysis for MYC, BCL2,
and/or BCL6 rearrangements, 12 patients initially diagnosed as having DLBCL were reclassified
as having HGBCL. Thus, as shown in Table 33, the final population contained 13% HGBCL rather
than 6% and 81% DLBCL instead of 89%. Based on this reclassification, the final population
supports an adequate evaluation in DLBCL NOS, DLBCL transformed from indolent lymphoma,
and HGBCL.
