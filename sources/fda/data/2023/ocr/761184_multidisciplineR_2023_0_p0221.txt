Immunogenicity Results

ADA Incidence
Main Study Period (First Year)

Among 42 somatrogon-treated subjects, 10 subjects (23.8%) tested ADA-positive for
somatrogon, five from the 0.48 mg/kg dose group and five from the 0.66 mg/kg dose group. No
subjects receiving the lowest dose (0.25 mg/kg/week) developed ADAs. No somatrogon-treated
subjects tested positive for NAbs.
