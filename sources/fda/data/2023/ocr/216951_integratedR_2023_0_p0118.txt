6.2.5. Study 205270/ASCEND NHQ

6.2.5.1. Design

Overview

The Applicant conducted a randomized, Phase 3 Study, 205270/ASCEND NHQ, titled “A 28-
week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, multi-center, study in
recombinant human erythropoietin (rhEPO) naive non-dialysis participants with anemia
associated with chronic kidney disease to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and effects on quality of
life of daprodustat compared to placebo.”

Primary Efficacy Endpoint
Mean change in Hb between Baseline and EP (Weeks 24 to 28)

Key Secondary Efficacy Endpoints

% of participants having a Hb increase of >1.0 g/dL from Baseline to EP (Weeks 24 to 28)
Mean change in SF-36 v2.0 Vitality domain between Baseline and Week 28
