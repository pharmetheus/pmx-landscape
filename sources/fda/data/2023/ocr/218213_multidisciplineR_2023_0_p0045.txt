The anti-tumor effect of repotrectinib alone against the CD74-ROS1 G2032R fusion protein was
also evaluated in female athymic nude mice bearing NIH3T3 CD74-ROS1 G2032R xenograft
tumors. Mice (5-8 weeks old) were subcutaneously inoculated with NIH3T3 CD74-ROS1 G2032R
cells (5 x 10°), and dosing was initiated when the average tumor volume reached approximately
200 mm}. Mice (n = 6/group) were dosed for 7 days BID with vehicle or three dose levels of
repotrectinib. BID dosing with 3 mg, 15 mg, or 75 mg/kg/dose of repotrectinib resulted in a
dose-dependent inhibition of tumor growth with TGls of 35%, 64%, and 91%, respectively
(Figure 2). The TGI was statistically significant at the 15 mg/kg/dose and 75 mg/kg/dose groups
(p < 0.001 and p < 0.0001, respectively, compared to vehicle). Treatment of tumor-implanted
mice with repotrectinib was well tolerated.
