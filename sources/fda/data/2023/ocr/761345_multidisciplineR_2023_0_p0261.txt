General Information

Goal of QSP analysis

QSP modeling was used to explore: i) whether there is a predicted
increase in efficacy over different dose and regimens for virtual
patient sub-cohorts with high versus low sBCMA, ii) whether the
simulated virtual patient response for SC QW differed from a
simulated regimen with a QW to Q2W regimen change after 6 Cycles
for participants with response of PR or better for at least 2 Cycles.
