The primary endpoint results were further investigated in
subgroup analyses for subjects with prior biologic/JAK
inhibitor exposure. The treatment difference for clinical
remission between etrasimod and placebo was 5.8% at
Week 12 in the prior biologic/JAK inhibitor exposure

Several secondary endpoints were statistically significant at
Week 12, including endoscopic improvement and histologic-
endoscopic mucosal improvement.

¢ The safety of etrasimod was evaluated in two adequate and well-
controlled trials, APD334-301 (52-week duration) and APD334-302
(12-week duration). Additionally, data from APD334-003, a phase 2,
12-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled induction trial
was pooled with data from APD334-302 because the two trial designs
and populations were sufficiently similar, to better assess the adverse
event profile for the 12-week trials.

¢ The safety profile of etrasimod for the treatment of moderately to
severely active UC appears to be consistent with the labeled safety
profile of other S1P receptor modulators, including ozanimod, which
is approved for the same indication.
