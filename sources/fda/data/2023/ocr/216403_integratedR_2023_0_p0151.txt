14.3. Bioanalytical Method Validation and
Performance

Two bioanalytical methods were used to quantify sparsentan in human plasma during drug
development. Table 89 highlights the key features of these methods. The primary method used in
most clinical studies was RE-021-Report037-2014-MVA, with the exception of DDI studies
021HVOL16006, 021HVOL16007, and 021HVOL16008, and hepatic impairment study
021HFX16009004 which used the assay M8349671. Table 90 and Table 91 lists the validation
and performance results of methods RE-021-Report037-2014-MVAL and M8349671,

respectively.
