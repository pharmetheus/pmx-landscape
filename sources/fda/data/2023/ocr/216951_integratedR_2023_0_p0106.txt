6.2.4.2. Statistical Analysis Plan

Sample Size Determination

The size of this study was determined based on the following criteria: (1) to be sufficient to meet
the International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) E1 guideline for participant exposure,
number, and duration; and (2) to provide at least 90% power to test the primary non-inferiority
hypothesis with a 2-sided 95% CI.

With a pre-specified non-inferiority margin of -0.75 g/dL and a 2-sided 95% CI, approximately
300 randomized participants (150 per treatment group) were deemed sufficient to provide >90%
power to test the primary hypothesis and to provide safety data on at least 100 participants
exposed to daprodustat for 1 year.

Interim Analysis

The IDMC periodically received unblinded safety reports containing, at a minimum, clinical
endpoints (whether adjudicated or pending adjudication) and serious adverse events (SAEs) from
an independent statistical data analysis center while the study was ongoing. There were no
prospectively defined interim analyses planned to stop the study early for Hb efficacy or futility.
