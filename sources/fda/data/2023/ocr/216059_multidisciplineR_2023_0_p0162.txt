Jaypirca (pirtobrutinib)

The FDA’s Assessment:

Table 66 summarizes all-cause treatment-emergent AEs, excluding laboratory terms, in each
safety population within the primary safety window, using a threshold of 210% in the MCL
safety population. Slight differences in some AE incidences by FDA and Applicant analysis are
not unexpected due to methodologic differences; refer to the USPI for final agreed-upon
numbers.

In the MCL safety population, the most common non-laboratory AEs (> 15%) were fatigue,
musculoskeletal pain, diarrhea, edema, dyspnea, pneumonia, and bruising. In the pooled safety
population, the most common non-laboratory AEs (2 15%) were fatigue, musculoskeletal pain,
diarrhea, bruising, rash, and edema.
