Metabolism studies indicate that the major human metabolites of daprodustat [M2; M3, M4, MS,
M6 (both stereoisomers); M13] have comparable activity to the parent drug.

While the sponsor did not conduct studies in animal models of anemia, overall, these
pharmacology data support the Applicant’s therapeutic rationale for the use of daprodustat as a
treatment for anemia, especially with regards to the drug’s ability to potently induce EPO
expression and increase production of red blood cells. However, given that pharmacology
models described above consisted of healthy animals lacking either anemia or features of chronic
kidney disease, these data do not directly address the impact of underlying disease on the
efficacy of daprodustat.

The established pharmacologic class term for daprodustat is hypoxia inducible factor prolyl
hydroxylase inhibitor, which is based on extensive prior internal discussions for previous HIF-
PHD drug candidates.
