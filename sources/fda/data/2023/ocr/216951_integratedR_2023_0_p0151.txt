Two other considerations related to the MACE findings are discussed in subsequent sections:

e The supportive OT+28 days analysis of MACE provided HR estimates (HR 1.40; 95% CI
1.17, 1.68) inconsistent with the primary analysis (on-study analysis). See the discussion
in Section 7.6.1.1.2.
