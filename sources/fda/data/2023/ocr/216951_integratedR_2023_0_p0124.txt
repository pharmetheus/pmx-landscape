Efficacy Results—Primary Endpoint

Mean Baseline Hb was similar between the treatment groups (See Figure 21). In the daprodustat
group, increases in mean Hb were observed within 4 weeks of initiating treatment and continued
through Week 28; mean Hb values were in the Hb analysis range (11 to 12 g/dL) during the EP
(Week 24 to Week 28). Numerically small changes in mean Hb were seen in the placebo group
from Baseline through Week 28 and mean Hb remained below the analysis range.
