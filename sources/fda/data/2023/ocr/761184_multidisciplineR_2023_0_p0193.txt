ADA Status

Observed PK data from Trial CP-4-004 and CP-4-006 including open-label extension showed
increased somatrogon concentrations in ADA-positive subjects. See Section 14.3.1.4.2.

Based on the population PK analysis, the clearance of somatrogon decreases ~26% after patients
developed antidrug antibodies. Table 40 summarizes central tendency and ranges of the selected
