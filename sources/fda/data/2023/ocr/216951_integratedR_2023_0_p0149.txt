7.5. Adequacy of the Clinical Safety Database

Each CVOT was adequately powered to rule out the risk of MACE according to the prespecified
NI margin. Based on these calculations, the number of subjects and total exposure met ICH E1A
recommendations and were sufficient to assess unexpected risks (i.e., signal detection) and
general safety. For this reason, the safety database is adequate for the comprehensive safety
assessment of daprodustat for the proposed indication, patient population, dosage regimen and
duration.

Exposure within each of the 2 large CV outcomes studies was similar across the daprodustat and
ESA comparators (Table 70). Greater than 50% of participants were on treatment for at least 18
months (ASCEND-ND) and 2 years (ASCEND-D). It is notable that the overall exposure and
overall follow up are balanced between randomized treatment arms for both studies. Of note, a
majority (77 to 85%) of the time on study was also on treatment (last row in Table 70).
