Efficacy Results—Secondary Endpoints

A supportive analysis was performed for the primary ITT analysis of post-randomization mean
change in Hb between Baseline and over the EP, repeating the analysis using a shorter EP (Week
28 to 36). For each study the lower bound of the 95% CI for the treatment difference was greater
than the prospectively defined non-inferiority margin of -0.75 g/dL, thus supporting the overall
conclusion of non-inferiority of daprodustat (see Table 228 in appendix 16.1). Results were
consistent with the conclusion from the primary analysis.

Additional primary endpoint supportive analyses included an on-treatment analysis using only
evaluable Hb values, a PP Population analysis using only evaluable Hb values, a shorter EP
(Weeks 28 to 36) analysis using only evaluable Hb values, and a post-randomization (on- and
off-treatment) observed case analysis. Results were consistent with the non-inferiority
conclusion from the primary analysis (Table 219 in Section 16.1).

The proportion of participants with mean Hb within the analysis range during the EP was higher
in the daprodustat arm as compared to rhEPO arm (Table 15).
