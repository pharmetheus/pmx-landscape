7.6.1.6. Adverse Events Leading to Treatment
Discontinuation

In ASCEND-ND, daprodustat was associated with higher rate of AEs leading to permanent
discontinuation, compared to darbepoetin alfa (15.3% versus 13.3%). As shown in Table 78,
most of this 2% treatment difference is attributable to the following AEs: thrombosis
(Stroke/TIA and M1), bacterial infection, and hemorrhage. In aggregate, the treatment difference
not favoring daprodustat is relatively small, and based on the 95% CI, could very likely be due to
chance alone. When inspecting lower-level terms, such as the PT or FMQ, we did not find any
revealing trends. Nonetheless, the isolation of the treatment difference to thrombosis and
hemorrhage are addressed as major safety review issues given similar findings in other analyses
(e.g., SAEs, MACE endpoint analyses). Bacterial infection, while imbalanced (22 vs. 11 subjects
reported to have discontinued treatment due to this adverse event class), did not rise to the level
of a review issue, because the imbalance was not isolated to a specific medical concept (e.g.,
fungal, bacterial, or viral, or infection loci), and supporting queries (such as all TEAEs, SAEs)
did not establish a convincing signal.
