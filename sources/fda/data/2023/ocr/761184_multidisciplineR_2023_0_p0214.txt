Immunogenicity
No subject was tested positive for ADAs during the study period.

Conclusions

While AUCs met the prespecified bioequivalence criteria, the 90% confidence interval of Cmax
did not fall within 80 to 125%. Cmax of pen presentation was 10% lower than that of vial, based
on the ratio of geometric means. This lower Cmax is not a concern because similar PD response
were observed between pen and vial presentations and pen presentation was used in the phase 3
study (Trial 006).
