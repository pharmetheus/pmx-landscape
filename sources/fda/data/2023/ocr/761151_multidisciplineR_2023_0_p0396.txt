Reviewer’s comments: Body weight seems to have a significant impact on PK and efficacy. For
some patients (patients with body weight >120kg), continued dosing with 320mg Q4W after
Week 16 may be considered. Body weight was found to be an intrinsic factor that could impact
clinical response. Patients with a higher body weight may have a lower clinical response due to
lower plasma concentrations with the same dose compared to a lower body weight patient.
Therefore, Q4W interval is recommended for dosing regimen in body weight > 120 kg PSO adult
patients.
