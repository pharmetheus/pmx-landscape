Jesduvrog (daprodustat)

Efficacy Results—Secondary Endpoints

Because of the importance of patients not spending undue time with suboptimal Hb levels, a
supportive analysis was performed for the primary ITT analysis of post-randomization mean
change in Hb between Baseline and over the EP, repeating the analysis using a shorter EP
(Week 28 to 36). For each study the lower bound of the 95% CI for the treatment difference was
greater than the prospectively defined non-inferiority margin of -0.75 g/dL, thus supporting the
overall non-inferiority results of daprodustat (see Table 46). Results were consistent with the
conclusion from the primary analysis.
