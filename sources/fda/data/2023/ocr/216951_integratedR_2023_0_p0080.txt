Of the 3872 participants in the ITT population, 2417 (62%) completed randomized treatment.
The proportion of participants who discontinued randomized treatment was high and similar in
the daprodustat group [727 (38%) participants] compared to darbepoetin alfa [726 (38%)
participants]. Of the participants who discontinued randomized treatment, 156 (8%) in the
daprodustat group and 166 (9%) in the darbepoetin alfa group died while on randomized
treatment. The primary reason for discontinuation was similar in both treatment groups (see

Table 18).
